## Conversion gaps

1. **Zero commerce link infrastructure**  
   189 articles, 56 commerce mentions (proxy), but 0 outbound links. You're naming products without linking to them—leaving 100% of affiliate upside on the table. Every mention is a missed $0.50–$5 per click.

2. **Tip jar is invisible/end-of-funnel only**  
   Readers who find value early have no friction-free way to support. Tip jar buried at article end means 80%+ drop-off before they see it. No mid-article or sidebar placement = low conversion.

3. **No lead magnet or email capture**  
   14 days old, small traffic, no email list. You're not building recurring revenue or owned audience. Every visitor is a one-time transaction with zero LTV extension.
