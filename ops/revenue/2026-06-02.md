## Daily revenue snapshot
**(no payout data yet)** — Site is pre-revenue. Zero articles = zero traffic = zero monetization surface. This is not a revenue problem yet; it's a content problem masquerading as one.

---

## Conversion gaps

1. **No content = no monetization surface**  
   0 articles means 0 commerce mentions, 0 affiliate opportunities, 0 tip jar triggers. Skimlinks and Ezoic are idling. This is the binding constraint.

2. **Tip jar is friction-heavy for cold traffic**  
   Buy Me a Coffee requires account creation and payment intent. With small traffic, conversion will be <0.5%. Need micro-friction alternatives (email capture, newsletter signup) to build audience first.

3. **AdSense approval timing is a trap**  
   Applying Day 14 (May 20) assumes content exists. If we have <5 articles by then, AdSense will reject or deprioritize. We're burning a one-time approval opportunity on an empty site.

---

## [BUILD-NOW] actions for the queue

1. **(Content scaffolding)** Auto-generate article stub templates with pre-positioned Skimlinks anchor points (e.g., "Best [Product Category]" sections with empty `<a class="skimlinks-link">` tags ready for manual product insertion). Reduces friction for Ada to publish first 10 articles.

2. **(Monetization)** Add lightweight email capture modal (exit-intent, non-blocking) with Mailchimp integration. Decouple audience-building from payment friction. Tip jar stays, but email list becomes primary conversion funnel.

3. **(Ezoic optimization)** Implement Ezoic header bidding wrapper on all future article templates now, before content launches. Don't wait for Incubator approval—ensure ad stack is live the moment traffic arrives.

---

## Risks

- **AdSense rejection on Day 14:** Applying with <5 articles = automatic decline. Wastes approval slot for 6+ months.
- **Skimlinks without commerce volume:** Commission sits at $0 indefinitely if no product mentions ship.
- **Ezoic Incubator limbo:** If rejected, we lose 30+ days and must pivot to AdSense (which we'll have already burned).
- **Tip jar fatigue:** Cold traffic will see CTA as aggressive; conversion will crater if it's the only ask.

---

## One-line recommendation for Ada

**Ship 5 SEO-optimized articles with pre-templated Skimlinks anchors and Ezoic header bidding live by Day 35; defer AdSense application until Day 45 with proven 100+ daily sessions.**
