## Conversion gaps

1. **Zero outbound links despite 56 commerce mentions**  
   You're mentioning products but not linking them. Skimlinks can't monetize what isn't clickable. Every unlinked mention is lost affiliate revenue.

2. **Tip jar placement unknown; likely end-of-article only**  
   With small traffic, you need multiple ask points. Tip jar should appear: mid-article (after value delivery), sidebar (persistent), and exit-intent (last chance).

3. **No inline affiliate context or urgency**  
   Commerce mentions exist but lack "where to buy" CTAs. Readers don't know a link is clickable or why they should click it. No price comparison, no "best deal" callout, no scarcity.

## [BUILD-NOW] actions for the queue

1. **(Skimlinks) Auto-linkify all commerce mentions**

   Insert `<div id="ezoic-placeholder-{N}">` tags in article template (top, mid, end) so Ezoic can inject ads immediately upon approval. Don't wait for approval to build the real estate.

## Risks

- **AdSense rejection (May 20 deadline):** 4-day-old site with thin content and no organic traffic history is high-risk. Ezoic approval is your safety net; don't rely on Google.

- **Tip jar fatigue:** Too many ask points = reader friction. A/B test placement; remove if conversion drops below $0.10/1K impressions.
