## Conversion gaps

1. **Zero outbound links despite 56 commerce mentions**  
   You have affiliate-eligible content but aren't linking to products. Skimlinks can't monetize what it can't track. This is leaving 80–90% of potential commission on the table.

2. **Tip jar is passive, not contextual**  
   Enabled but only surfaces at article end. Readers who found value mid-article have already left. No inline prompts = missed micro-donations.

3. **No affiliate disclosure or trust signals**  
   56 commerce mentions without transparent linking erodes reader trust and risks FTC/platform policy violations. Readers don't know you're affiliate-enabled, so they don't expect it—and won't tip.

1. **(Skimlinks) Auto-link commerce keywords in article body**  
   Scan published articles for product names (e.g., "iPhone 15", "Dyson V15") and inject Skimlinks-wrapped anchor tags. Prioritize top 20 articles by traffic. Autonomous, no manual review needed.

3. **(Affiliate disclosure) Add site-wide footer badge**  
   Insert "We earn commissions on qualifying purchases" + link to disclosure page in footer. Builds trust, signals monetization intent, reduces FTC risk before AdSense review.
