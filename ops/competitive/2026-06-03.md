## What Competitors Do Well (top 5)

- **Axios** publishes every ~1.6 hrs, creating return-visit habit loops and strong RSS/aggregator presence.

- **Tangle** pairs a members-only paywall tier with free content, monetizing engaged readers without blocking discovery.

---

## Where We Lag (top 5, ranked by leverage)

1. **Zero published articles** — No content means no SEO surface, no RSS subscribers, no ad impressions. Every day of silence compounds. *Highest leverage: unblocking the cron pipeline is worth more than any other action.*
2. **No RSS feed** — Axios drives 20 items/fetch into aggregators. We have none. RSS is free distribution; absence means zero syndication reach. *High ROI: one-time build, permanent compounding.*
3. **No content velocity signal** — Competitors publish 1–8× daily with visible timestamps. Without articles, we can't demonstrate freshness to Google or readers. *Directly blocks AdSense Day-14 approval.*
4. **No dark mode parity on content pages** — Dark mode is live but unverified against actual article templates. Ground News uses it as a retention feature; we may have it only on shell pages.
5. **No bias/source transparency layer** — Ground News and Tangle both surface *how* news is curated. We offer no trust signal. Low build cost, high perceived credibility lift.

---

## Differentiation Opportunities (top 3)

1. **AI confidence + source-count badge per story** — No competitor displays a machine-generated "coverage breadth" score (e.g., *"Covered by 14 sources · Confidence: High"*) inline on each brief. This is buildable from RSS aggregation logic and makes AI-native production a visible feature, not a hidden backend detail. Turns our production method into a trust signal rather than a liability.

2. **5-minute audio brief auto-generated from daily articles** — None of the listed competitors auto-generate a daily TTS audio summary from their own published content. Morning Brew has a podcast but it's human-produced. An on-site `<audio>` player with a cron-generated MP3 from article text costs ~$0/day via open TTS APIs and captures the commuter segment no one here owns.

3. **"What changed since yesterday" diff block** — No competitor shows a structured delta between today's brief and yesterday's on the same story. A simple story-thread tracker that appends a one-line *"Yesterday: X · Today: Y"* update block is a genuinely novel UX pattern in this space. It rewards daily return visits with explicit new value, not just new content.
