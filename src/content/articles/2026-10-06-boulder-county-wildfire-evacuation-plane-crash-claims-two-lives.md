# Boulder County Wildfire Prompts Evacuation; Separate Plane Crash Claims Two Lives

A 6-acre wildfire north of Highway 7 in Boulder County triggered evacuation warnings Tuesday morning, adding to the region's fire risk during Colorado's critical wildfire season. The blaze, located off Forest Service Road 920, forced residents to prepare for possible displacement as firefighting crews mobilized (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

The evacuation comes as Boulder County grapples with ongoing drought conditions and fuel buildup—challenges conservatives argue are worsened by federal land mismanagement and overly restrictive environmental regulations that prevent responsible forest thinning and controlled burns. Republicans have long pushed for greater state and local control over Colorado's forests, citing decades of federal gridlock that leaves public lands vulnerable to catastrophic fire.

In a separate tragedy, two people died when a single-engine plane that went missing Sunday night crashed in Boulder County near Eldorado Springs (KDVR Fox31). Recovery crews located the wreckage after an intensive search operation.
