# Democrats' Sanctuary Policies Enable Crimes—Colorado Must Learn the Lesson

New York's refusal to cooperate with federal immigration enforcement has consequences: a 62-year-old woman murdered in her Chinatown home allegedly by a criminal illegal immigrant who should have been deported. The Washington Examiner reports Democratic politicians prioritize keeping illegal immigrants in the country over public safety—even when those individuals have criminal records.

Florida's situation is grimmer. State AG James Uthmeier announced arrests of four illegal immigrants in a halal slaughterhouse torture case involving abuse of defenseless animals, per the Daily Caller. These aren't isolated incidents; they're symptoms of sanctuary policies that obstruct federal immigration enforcement.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado cities including Denver have adopted sanctuary policies limiting cooperation with ICE. While local officials claim these protect vulnerable immigrant communities, Republicans argue they endanger Colorado residents by shielding criminal aliens from deportation. Conservatives say sanctuary jurisdictions create two-tiered justice—one for citizens, another for those here illegally.

Democrats counter that sanctuary policies build trust with immigrant communities and that most undocumented immigrants commit crimes at lower rates than citizens. But Republicans point to cases like New York's as proof that even one preventable death is too many when cities deliberately obstruct federal law enforcement.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether Denver and other sanctuary cities face federal funding pressure or whether state legislators push back against local non-cooperation policies.
