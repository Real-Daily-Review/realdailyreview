# Democratic Socialism's Radical Agenda Threatens Constitutional Limits, Conservatives Warn

Colorado conservatives are sounding the alarm over the Democratic Socialists of America's platform, which they argue represents a direct assault on constitutional governance and free-market principles. According to Complete Colorado, the DSA's agenda would systematically dismantle the nation's checks-and-balances framework—a warning that resonates as progressive ideology gains traction on college campuses nationwide.

The concern extends beyond theory. Fox News reports that socialist primary victories are reshaping Democratic positions on key issues, signaling a leftward drift within the party establishment. While some Gen Z voters show resistance to outright communism, the normalization of "democratic socialism" as a political stepping stone troubles conservatives who see it as incrementalism toward state control of the economy.

For Colorado specifically, this ideological shift threatens the state's business climate, energy independence, and property rights. If DSA-aligned policies gain ground in state legislatures, Coloradans could face expanded government control over land use, energy production, and wealth redistribution through aggressive taxation.

Republicans argue the Constitution's separation of powers exists precisely to prevent the concentration of government authority that socialism demands. Democrats counter that democratic socialism merely seeks to expand social programs and worker protections within a democratic framework.
