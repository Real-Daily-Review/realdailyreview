# Morocco's Border Chaos Is a Warning for America's Open-Door Advocates

Spain's Ceuta enclave is descending into crisis—a cautionary tale for Colorado and the nation about what happens when border security collapses.

Sixty thousand migrants, with apparent Moroccan state cooperation, have flooded into the Spanish territory in recent weeks. The human cost has been staggering: at least 67 deaths, including drownings and stampede victims at a breakwater barrier, according to Breitbart reporting. More migrants are reportedly preparing additional crossing attempts.

National Review notes that Morocco appears to be deliberately testing Spanish sovereignty—weaponizing migration as a geopolitical tool. This isn't accidental chaos; it's calculated pressure.

For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: when governments signal they won't enforce borders, they invite catastrophe. Advocates for open-border policies dismiss such warnings as fearmongering, but Ceuta proves that mass, uncontrolled migration kills people and destabilizes communities. Spain's experience underscores why border enforcement isn't cruel—it's necessary.

The Biden-Harris administration's catch-and-release policies and refusal to secure America's southern border follow a similar playbook of weakness. Colorado taxpayers foot the bill for illegal immigration's costs while border communities absorb the chaos.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation demands the federal government learn from Spain's failure—or continues enabling the same reckless policies.
