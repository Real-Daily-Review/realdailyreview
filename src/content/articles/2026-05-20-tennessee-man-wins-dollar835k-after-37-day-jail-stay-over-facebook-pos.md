# Tennessee Man Jailed Over Facebook Post Wins $835,000 Settlement

A Tennessee man has won an $835,000 settlement after being jailed for more than a month over a Facebook post about conservative activist Charlie Kirk, according to CBS News and The Hill.

Retired law enforcement officer Larry Bushart, 61, was arrested in September and held for 37 days after sharing memes on Facebook that accused Kirk's organization, the Turning Point USA, of involvement in Kirk's assassination, according to Ars Technica. The sheriff's office pursued the case despite no evidence that Bushart posed an actual threat.

The settlement resolves a lawsuit challenging the legality of his detention. Bushart's case hinged on First Amendment protections for online speech, even when that speech makes inflammatory accusations. The prolonged jailing over social media content raised questions about the bounds of law enforcement authority in policing speech online.

Tennessee officials agreed to the settlement, effectively conceding the case. The outcome represents a legal victory for free speech advocates who argued the arrest and extended detention violated constitutional protections.
