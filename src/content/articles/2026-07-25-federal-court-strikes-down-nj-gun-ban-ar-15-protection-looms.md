# Federal Court Signals AR-15s Get Constitutional Protection; Left Panics

A federal appeals court has struck down New Jersey's "assault weapons" ban, giving Second Amendment advocates a major win and Colorado gun owners reason for optimism about their own rights. The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit's decision directly challenges state-level bans modeled on similar restrictions, and progressive outlets like Slate are already warning the ruling could give the Supreme Court "cover" to expand constitutional protections for AR-15s nationwide (Breitbart).

For Colorado, this matters. The state has wrestled with its own gun regulations, and this ruling signals courts are increasingly skeptical of blanket bans on commonly owned firearms. Conservatives argue the Second Amendment protects individual ownership of modern rifles for lawful purposes—a position gaining judicial traction.

Democrats and gun-control advocates counter that states retain authority to regulate firearms in the interest of public safety, but the Third Circuit's reasoning undermines that argument by emphasizing constitutional text and historical tradition.

Meanwhile, a separate controversy erupted when a federal judge in Massachusetts—appointed by Biden—blocked deportations of Ethiopian nationals despite a Supreme Court ruling clearing the way for those removals (Fox News). The judge's defiance of SCOTUS authority highlights ongoing judicial resistance to immigration enforcement.
