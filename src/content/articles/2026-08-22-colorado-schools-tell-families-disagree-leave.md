# School Disputes Heat Up as Colorado Families Face Pressure Over Teacher Conflicts

Colorado families objecting to school policies are being told to leave the public system entirely—a troubling sign of how far progressive education ideology has moved from serving all students.

According to Fox News, a father of three reported that families raising concerns about a transgender teacher were advised that "public school may not be for them." One speaker went further, suggesting children of "bigots" could be relocated. The message is clear: disagree with progressive curriculum or personnel decisions, and you're unwelcome.

This reflects a broader pattern where school districts prioritize ideological conformity over parental input and educational fundamentals. Colorado parents—who fund public schools through property taxes—are increasingly finding their concerns dismissed rather than addressed. When families can't voice legitimate questions about classroom policies without being told to leave, the system has abandoned its duty to serve the entire community.

Meanwhile, the Lakewood school community is mourning art teacher Lindsey Barnett, whose death has devastated colleagues and students (KDVR Fox31 Denver). Her husband has been charged with her murder, and a GoFundMe has been established for her children.

Colorado conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether school boards begin addressing parental concerns respectfully—or double down on the message that disagreement means expulsion.
