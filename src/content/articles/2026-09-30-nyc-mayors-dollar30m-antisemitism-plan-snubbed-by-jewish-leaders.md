# NYC Mayor's $30M Antisemitism Plan Draws Fire From Jewish Leaders

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is spending $30 million to combat antisemitism—but three leading Manhattan rabbis say they were shut out of the planning and the money misses the mark.

The initiative comes as Mamdani faces mounting criticism from Jewish leaders over his vocal support for Palestinian statehood and his record of Israel criticism, which opponents argue reveals bias against Jewish people. Mamdani denies antisemitic intent (Fox News Politics).

The rabbis' complaint cuts to the heart of the problem: Mamdani's plan, they argue, avoids addressing what they see as a key driver of antisemitic hate—his own political positions and rhetoric (Just The News). No Jewish community leaders were consulted on funding allocation or strategy, according to the rabbis' statement.

This is classic progressive theater: announce a splashy spending program to address a problem while ignoring the actual source of community concern. Mamdani wants credit for fighting antisemitism without confronting his own record on Israel or including the people most affected in the solution.
