# Stolen Bison Sculpture Recovered in Pueblo

A bronze bison sculpture stolen from a Native youth organization over the weekend has been recovered by Pueblo police, ending a brief but concerning theft that highlighted vulnerabilities in protecting cultural assets across Colorado.

The sculpture and its trailer were reported missing and later located by authorities, according to reporting from 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31. Police confirmed the artwork and trailer sustained no apparent damage during the theft, a fortunate outcome given the cultural and historical significance of the piece to the Native community it serves.

The recovery underscores a broader issue conservatives and community leaders alike should monitor: the protection of private property and cultural heritage in Colorado. While this case ended well, it raises questions about security measures for organizations serving vulnerable populations and the need for swift law enforcement response—areas where Colorado communities depend on adequate police resources and funding.
