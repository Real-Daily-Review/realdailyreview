# Trump DOJ Reopens Nixon-Era Prosecutorial Conduct Review

The Trump Justice Department is scrutinizing how federal prosecutors handled the Watergate investigation, arguing the special prosecutor's office may have weaponized the legal system against a sitting president—a concern conservatives say echoes modern threats to executive authority.

According to *The Daily Wire*, the DOJ review examines whether the special prosecutor's conduct during Watergate crossed ethical and legal lines. Conservatives argue the move highlights a pattern of prosecutorial overreach against Republican presidents, from Nixon to Trump himself. They contend that unchecked special counsels can become political instruments rather than impartial investigators.

*National Review* offers a more measured take, acknowledging Nixon's accomplishments while cautioning against making him a conservative hero—a signal that even right-leaning outlets recognize the complexity of defending Watergate-era conduct.

Democrats and the left will likely frame this review as Trump attempting to rewrite history and undermine the rule of law. They argue Watergate represented legitimate constitutional accountability, not weaponization.
