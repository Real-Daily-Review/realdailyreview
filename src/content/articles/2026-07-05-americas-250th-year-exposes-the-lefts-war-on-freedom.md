# America's 250th Birthday Reveals a Nation Forgetting What Made It Free

Colorado conservatives should be alarmed: as America marks 250 years of constitutional self-government, a rising generation of young socialists and progressive activists are actively working to dismantle the very freedoms that made this nation exceptional.

National Review editors warn that today's Democratic Socialist Alliance adherents "understand just as little as their predecessors did" about the failures of centralized government—yet they push the same failed ideology anyway. Meanwhile, millions of Americans take for granted the blessings of liberty that immigrants and those who've lived under dictatorship recognize instantly.

The contrast is stark. Those who've experienced authoritarian rule understand "what that first breath of freedom feels like." Yet here at home, progressive activists push for ever-larger government, higher taxes, and regulatory control over energy, speech, and property rights—precisely the tools of tyranny.

On energy specifically, the Daily Caller notes that nuclear power—not wind and solar mandates—has powered American prosperity for generations. Yet Colorado's Democratic legislature continues blocking nuclear development while forcing expensive renewables on taxpayers and businesses.

This gratitude crisis matters for Colorado: as the left pushes socialism-lite policies through state government, taxpayers and energy workers must remember what made America prosperous and demand their elected officials defend, not surrender, constitutional limits on government power.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans will mount serious opposition to the next round of progressive spending and regulatory overreach in 2027.
