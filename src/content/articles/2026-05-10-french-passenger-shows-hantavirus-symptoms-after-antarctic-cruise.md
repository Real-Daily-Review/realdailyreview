# French Passenger Shows Hantavirus Symptoms After Antarctic Cruise

A French national who returned from the MV Hondius cruise ship displayed symptoms consistent with hantavirus while on a repatriation flight Sunday, according to the Washington Examiner. The passenger is among five people from the vessel who will be quarantined in Paris "until further notice," France's prime minister announced, per BBC World.

The MV Hondius had been operating in an area with confirmed hantavirus cases. Hantavirus is a potentially serious rodent-borne illness that can cause fever, muscle aches, and in severe cases, respiratory complications.

French health authorities have not yet released details on the severity of the passenger's condition or whether other quarantined individuals are showing symptoms. The decision to quarantine five passengers suggests broader exposure concerns aboard the ship, though officials have not specified how many total passengers were aboard or whether non-French nationals are also being monitored.

Watch for updates on whether additional cases emerge among the quarantined passengers or other cruise participants, and any official guidance from French health authorities on hantavirus exposure protocols for Antarctic tourism.
