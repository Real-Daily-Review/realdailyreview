# NYC Mayor's Netanyahu Arrest Threat Exposes Progressive Hostility to Israel

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's call to arrest Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu during his U.N. General Assembly visit this week reveals how far progressive politics has drifted from defending democratic allies. According to the Washington Examiner, Israel's U.N. ambassador responded by calling for Mamdani's arrest instead, citing the mayor's inflammatory rhetoric ahead of Netanyahu's arrival.

Mamdani, a progressive Democrat, initially demanded Netanyahu's arrest on war crimes allegations—a position that contradicts U.S. law and international protocol protecting visiting heads of state. Fox News Politics reports the mayor later walked back his more extreme remarks, but the damage was done: his initial stance signals how anti-Israel sentiment has metastasized within Democratic urban leadership.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. Progressive activists and elected officials increasingly treat Israel—America's most reliable Middle East ally—as a pariah state. Meanwhile, they champion policies that weaken U.S. strength abroad while expanding government control at home. The contrast is stark: defending a democratic nation under siege versus performative activism that undermines American interests.

Coloradans should watch whether Democratic leaders here echo Mamdani's hostility toward Israel or distance themselves from such rhetoric—a telling test of where state party leadership actually stands.
