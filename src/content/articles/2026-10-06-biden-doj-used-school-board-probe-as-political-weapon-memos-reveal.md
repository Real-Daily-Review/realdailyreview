# Biden DOJ's School Board Probe Became Political Weapon, Internal Memos Show

The Biden Justice Department weaponized a school board investigation to target political opponents, according to internal documents released by Trump's Weaponization Working Group this week.

Attorney General Merrick Garland's 2021 memo directing federal prosecutors to investigate threats against school board members sparked alarm among DOJ officials themselves. According to *Fox News* and the *Washington Examiner*, career Justice Department lawyers warned the initiative risked becoming an "Anti-MAGA Task Force"—using federal law enforcement to suppress conservative parents and political speech.

The revelation matters for Colorado. Federal overreach into local education disputes sets a precedent for how Washington treats parents challenging school curricula, mask mandates, or gender ideology in classrooms. If the DOJ can mobilize against one group of parents, Colorado families face the same risk.

Republicans argue the memo exemplified Biden-era politicization of law enforcement—using the FBI and DOJ to intimidate citizens exercising First Amendment rights. Democrats have defended the initiative as necessary to protect school officials from genuine threats.

The documents underscore a larger conservative concern: unelected bureaucrats weaponizing federal agencies against political opponents. Colorado taxpayers and parents should watch whether the Trump administration's findings lead to accountability measures or structural reforms preventing future abuse.
