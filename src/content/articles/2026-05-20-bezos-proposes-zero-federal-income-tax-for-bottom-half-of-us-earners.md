# Bezos Calls for Zero Federal Income Tax on Bottom Half of Earners

Jeff Bezos said the bottom half of U.S. earners should pay no federal income tax, arguing the policy could ease financial pressure and encourage entrepreneurship.

The Amazon founder made the statement Wednesday, according to CNBC. Bezos did not elaborate on how the federal government would offset lost revenue or which specific income thresholds would qualify under such a system.

The proposal aligns with broader tax-cut arguments made by some business leaders and conservative policymakers, though it represents a significant departure from current tax policy, where lower-income households already benefit from credits and deductions that reduce or eliminate their tax burden.

Bezos did not specify implementation details, including whether the proposal would apply to payroll taxes, state income taxes, or only federal levies. He also did not address how such a change might affect Social Security and Medicare funding, which rely partly on payroll tax revenue.

The statement comes as tax policy remains a contentious issue in Washington, with lawmakers divided over whether to raise, lower, or restructure rates across income brackets.
