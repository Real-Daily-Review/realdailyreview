# Trump Education Department Rolls Back Biden's Transgender Title IX Protections

The Trump administration's Education Department has finalized revised Title IX rules that scrap Biden-era language protecting students based on gender identity and sexual orientation—a move conservatives say restores clarity to sex-based discrimination law while progressives argue strips protections from vulnerable students.

Education Secretary Linda McMahon announced the rescission Monday, following a federal court decision that struck down Biden's 2024 interpretation. The final rules are set for formal publication Tuesday (Washington Examiner, Just The News).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Biden's expansion of Title IX conflated sex with gender identity, creating regulatory chaos in schools and athletics. The original statute addressed biological sex discrimination, not ideological categories. Restoring that distinction, they contend, protects women's sports and privacy rights while preventing federal overreach into local education.

**The progressive counter:** Democrats and education advocates claim the rollback abandons students facing discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity, leaving them without federal recourse.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado schools have grappled with transgender policy disputes for years. This federal shift will likely influence how Colorado districts—many already progressive on gender issues—navigate Title IX compliance and athletic eligibility rules going forward.
