# Colorado Conservatives Should Welcome SpaceX IPO as Antidote to Anti-Success Politics

Elon Musk's SpaceX is preparing what *The Daily Wire* reports will be "the biggest IPO in history," with unprecedented retail investor access—a direct rebuke to the gatekeeping that typically reserves wealth-creation opportunities for the connected few.

The offering matters beyond Wall Street. SpaceX represents American innovation, risk-taking, and entrepreneurship unencumbered by the regulatory suffocation that progressives champion. *National Review* notes the deeper question: what America are we actually celebrating as we approach 2026? One that rewards success and enables ordinary citizens to participate in it, or one that wages war on achievement itself?

For Colorado, this cuts sharply. The state hosts aerospace and defense contractors, space-tech startups, and workers whose livelihoods depend on a competitive, innovation-friendly economy. A SpaceX IPO that democratizes investment sends a powerful signal: success isn't a sin to be taxed away or regulated into submission.

Democrats and progressive activists routinely frame wealth creation as exploitation. They push higher capital-gains taxes, expanded SEC regulations, and ESG mandates that constrain business growth. Republicans counter that broad-based investment access—letting the "man on the street" own SpaceX stock—is the real path to shared prosperity.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature attempts to impose new capital-gains or investment taxes that would discourage retail participation in future offerings.
