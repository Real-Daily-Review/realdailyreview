# U.S. and Iran Edge Toward Ceasefire Extension Deal

President Trump said the U.S. is "getting a lot closer" to an agreement with Iran, as officials from both sides discuss terms that could extend the current ceasefire by 60 days, according to mediators cited by the Financial Times.

The proposed deal centers on gradually reopening the Strait of Hormuz, a critical waterway for global energy supplies, according to U.S. officials and negotiators briefed on the conversations (CBS News). The Financial Times reported that mediators have indicated the two countries are moving closer to the ceasefire extension, though neither side has confirmed final terms.

Details remain sparse. Neither the Trump administration nor Iranian officials have publicly disclosed the full scope of negotiations or a timeline for reaching a final agreement. The discussions represent a significant diplomatic engagement between the two nations, which have faced escalating tensions in recent years.

Watch for official statements from either government confirming the deal's terms and any announcement of a signing date.
