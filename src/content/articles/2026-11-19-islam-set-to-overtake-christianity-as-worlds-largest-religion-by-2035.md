# Islam Projected to Surpass Christianity as World's Largest Religion by 2035

A Pew Research Center analysis projects that Islam will become the world's largest religion by 2035, overtaking Christianity due to higher Muslim birth rates and demographic trends. The Daily Wire reports the shift will occur within the next decade, driven primarily by population growth patterns rather than conversion rates.

The demographic realignment reflects broader global population shifts.
