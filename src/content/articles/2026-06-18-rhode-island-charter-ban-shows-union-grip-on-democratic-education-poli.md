# Rhode Island's Charter School Ban: A Cautionary Tale for Colorado Parents

Rhode Island's Democratic governor faces a choice that should alarm Colorado families: capitulate to teachers unions by signing a three-year ban on new charter schools, or protect educational choice. According to the *Washington Examiner*, Gov. Dan McKee appears poised to cave to union pressure—prioritizing campaign support over the school choice promises he made five years ago.

This is union power politics at its clearest. Teachers unions oppose charter schools because they threaten their monopoly on public education funding and membership rolls. When Democrats depend on union support for reelection, families lose leverage.

Colorado conservatives should take note. While Colorado has a stronger charter school framework than Rhode Island, the same union-backed pressure exists here. Progressive legislators and union allies regularly push restrictions on charter expansion, arguing they "drain resources" from traditional public schools—a framing that ignores parental demand for alternatives and the reality that per-pupil funding follows students.

The Rhode Island case shows what happens when Democratic politicians choose union loyalty over educational freedom. McKee's capitulation would eliminate options for families seeking alternatives to failing district schools—precisely the families progressives claim to champion.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature attempts similar restrictions on charter growth in the 2025 session.
