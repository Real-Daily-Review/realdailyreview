# Senate Blocks War Powers Resolution as 2026 Midterms Loom

Republicans are drawing a line on executive war authority ahead of the midterm elections, with the Senate shooting down a final war powers resolution on Iran before voters head to the polls (Daily Caller). The move reflects GOP concern about military entanglement and congressional oversight—issues that could shape races across Colorado and the nation.

The vote comes as lawmakers grapple with the proper constitutional balance between presidential authority and legislative approval for military action. According to the Washington Examiner, the debate echoes the 2002 Iraq War authorization process, when Congress conducted extensive deliberation before authorizing force. Republicans argue that robust congressional review protects both constitutional limits on executive power and prevents costly foreign interventions that drain resources from domestic priorities.

For Colorado voters, the stakes include how federal spending on military operations affects state budgets and whether Congress maintains meaningful oversight of war decisions. The 2026 midterms will test whether voters reward or punish lawmakers based on their positions on military authority and fiscal restraint.
