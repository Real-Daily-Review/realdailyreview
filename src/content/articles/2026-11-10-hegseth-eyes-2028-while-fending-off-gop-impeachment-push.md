# Hegseth Signals 2028 Run While Fending Off Impeachment

Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth is already positioning himself for a 2028 presidential bid, using a Pentagon press conference Tuesday to defend against Rep. Thomas Massie's (R-KY) impeachment effort while casting himself as President Trump's loyal lieutenant, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

Hegseth's first public defense since May signals he's eyeing the Republican nomination even as he battles congressional criticism. By emphasizing his fealty to Trump while subtly differentiating himself from Vice President JD Vance, Hegseth is laying groundwork for a potential primary challenge in four years.

The impeachment push from Massie—a libertarian-leaning Republican—reflects ongoing tensions within GOP ranks over Pentagon leadership and defense priorities. Hegseth's strategy of doubling down on Trump loyalty while the impeachment effort fizzles suggests he believes the base will reward unwavering support for the president's agenda.
