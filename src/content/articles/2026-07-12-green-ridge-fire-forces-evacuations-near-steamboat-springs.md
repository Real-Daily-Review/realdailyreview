# Green Ridge Fire Forces Evacuations South of Steamboat Springs

Colorado's wildfire season is hitting hard again—the Green Ridge Fire ignited Sunday afternoon in Routt County, forcing evacuations for areas south of Steamboat Springs near Stagecoach Reservoir.

According to CPR News and 9News Denver, the blaze is burning southeast of the reservoir in a region that includes ranches, rural properties, and outdoor recreation areas critical to northwest Colorado's economy. Evacuations are in place for affected communities, though specific acreage and containment percentages were not immediately available from initial reports.

This fire underscores a persistent problem conservatives have flagged for years: Colorado's forests remain dangerously overstocked with dead and dying timber due to decades of fire-suppression policies and environmental restrictions on timber harvesting and forest management. Republicans and forestry experts argue that aggressive thinning, controlled burns, and responsible logging are essential to reducing catastrophic wildfire risk—yet environmental groups and progressive regulators have long blocked such measures on public lands.

The Green Ridge Fire also threatens water infrastructure and ranching operations in a county where energy development and agriculture remain economic anchors. Conservatives emphasize that sound forest stewardship protects both lives and livelihoods.

Watch for how state and federal land managers respond to this fire and whether they'll finally embrace the forest-thinning strategies Republicans have championed.
