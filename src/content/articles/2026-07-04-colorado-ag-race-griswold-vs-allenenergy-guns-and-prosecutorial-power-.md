# Colorado AG Race Set: Griswold vs. Allen in November Showdown

Colorado Democrats have nominated Secretary of State Jena Griswold to defend the attorney general's office, while Republicans will back El Paso County District Attorney Michael J. Allen in November's general election (Denver Post).

The matchup pits Griswold's progressive record—marked by aggressive litigation against energy companies and gun manufacturers—against Allen's law-and-order platform emphasizing public safety and constitutional rights. Republicans argue Griswold has weaponized the AG's office for partisan ends, citing her high-profile lawsuits targeting Colorado's oil and gas industry and her push for stricter gun regulations. Conservatives say Allen offers a reset: a prosecutor focused on violent crime, border security, and defending Colorado's energy sector from federal overreach.

The race carries real stakes for Colorado's economy. The next AG will shape policy on water rights, land use, energy development, and Second Amendment litigation—issues directly affecting ranchers, oil workers, and rural Colorado. Griswold's office has pursued aggressive climate litigation; Allen has signaled skepticism of such suits.

Democrats counter that Griswold's tenure has strengthened consumer protections and environmental enforcement, framing the election as a choice between progressive accountability and conservative deregulation.

Taxpayers should watch whether either candidate commits to reining in the AG's litigation budget and regulatory scope.
