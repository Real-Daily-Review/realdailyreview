# Maine Democrat's Consent Allegations Highlight Party's Credibility Crisis

A former girlfriend of Democratic Maine Senate nominee Graham Platner accused him Tuesday of repeatedly removing condoms without her consent during their 2013-2015 relationship in Washington, D.C., according to *The Washington Post*. Lyndsey Fifield, who has previously accused Platner of physical abuse, is the second woman to come forward with serious allegations against the candidate in as many days.

The allegations follow a Monday report detailing separate sexual assault accusations from another ex-girlfriend. In response, major Democratic institutions and figures have begun distancing themselves from Platner's candidacy, with some rescinding endorsements.
