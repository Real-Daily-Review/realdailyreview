# Tom Steyer's Billions Can't Buy Voters—A Lesson for the Left

Billionaire activist Tom Steyer's latest electoral defeat proves that no amount of personal wealth can overcome voter rejection of a candidate's message. After spending hundreds of millions of dollars, Steyer failed to advance to California's top-two runoff this week, conceding defeat once again.

The Washington Examiner notes the irony: Democrats routinely claim billionaires are "buying elections," yet voters have repeatedly rejected Steyer despite his massive financial advantage. The lesson cuts both ways—money matters far less than voters' actual preferences, and Steyer's repeated failures suggest Californians simply don't want him in office, regardless of his checkbook.

For Colorado conservatives, this is worth watching. As progressive mega-donors like Steyer continue pouring cash into left-wing causes and candidates nationwide—including efforts to reshape Colorado's energy and gun policies—voters should remember that grassroots opposition and clear messaging still carry weight. Steyer's spending spree didn't move the needle because voters saw through the agenda.

Watch whether national Democrats learn this lesson or double down on billionaire-funded campaigns in 2024 and beyond.
