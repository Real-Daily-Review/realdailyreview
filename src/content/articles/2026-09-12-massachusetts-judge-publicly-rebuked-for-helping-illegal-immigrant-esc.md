# Massachusetts Judge Publicly Rebuked for Helping Illegal Immigrant Dodge ICE

A Massachusetts judge has been publicly reprimanded by the state's highest court for allegedly enabling an illegal immigrant to escape Immigration and Customs Enforcement custody during a 2018 courthouse proceeding.

Judge Shelley Joseph faced the formal rebuke after accusations that she colluded with the defendant's attorney and a court officer to allow the man—facing drug possession charges—to slip out a back exit while ICE agents waited to take him into custody (Fox News, Just The News).

The case underscores a broader conservative concern: judicial activism that prioritizes sanctuary-city ideology over immigration law enforcement. Republicans argue that judges who obstruct federal immigration authorities undermine the rule of law and endanger public safety by shielding criminal aliens from deportation.

Democrats and progressive legal advocates counter that courts should scrutinize ICE enforcement tactics and that judges have discretion to manage their courtrooms—though such arguments ring hollow when a judge allegedly orchestrates an escape.
