# Kennedy Center Removes Trump's Name After Court Blocks Appeal

A federal appeals court has denied the Kennedy Center's emergency request to block a judge's order requiring the removal of Donald Trump's name from the performing arts venue's facade and materials. Workers have already begun removing the signage.

The Kennedy Center board filed an emergency appeal seeking an administrative stay, but the court rejected the request (Fox News). The name had been added to the building months earlier, and its removal now stands as final.

The decision has drawn sharp reactions. CNN's Jim Acosta compared the removal to major historical events, calling it part of standing "up against tyranny" (Daily Caller). Critics on the right argue the move reflects the broader cultural effort to erase Trump from public life, regardless of his administration's support for the arts.
