# Google Settles U.K. Review Probe; DeepMind Taps EVE Online for AI Testing

Google has agreed to penalize British businesses that use fake online reviews to inflate their ratings, according to the Wall Street Journal. The search giant's settlement with the U.K. competition watchdog will involve placing warnings on business listings that post fraudulent reviews and targeting the accounts responsible for submitting them, effectively ending the regulator's investigation into the practice.

In a separate development, Google DeepMind is partnering with EVE Online, the long-running space MMO, to test AI models in complex virtual environments, Ars Technica reports. The collaboration comes as EVE Online's developer, CCP Games, secured $120 million in funding to become independent and rebranded itself as Fenris Creations.

The Google review settlement addresses a persistent problem for consumers and legitimate businesses navigating online marketplaces. The company's willingness to implement enforcement measures suggests growing pressure on tech platforms to police user-generated content more aggressively.

The DeepMind partnership underscores the gaming industry's emerging role as a testing ground for advanced AI systems, where complex multiplayer environments offer rich datasets and unpredictable scenarios.
