# Google Settles U.K. Review Probe; DeepMind Taps EVE Online for AI Testing

Google has agreed to penalize British businesses that post fake reviews and boost their star ratings, according to the Wall Street Journal, ending a U.K. competition watchdog investigation into the practice. The search giant will issue warnings to offending businesses and the users posting fraudulent reviews, addressing long-standing concerns about review manipulation on its platforms.

In a separate development, Google DeepMind has partnered with EVE Online, the long-running space MMO, to test AI models in complex virtual environments, according to Ars Technica. The partnership comes as EVE Online's developer, CCP Games, announced a $120 million funding round to become independent and rebranded itself as Fenris Creations.

The U.K. settlement represents a rare enforcement win for regulators scrutinizing tech platforms' role in combating misinformation and fraud. Google's warning system aims to deter both businesses and review-posting services from gaming ratings, a persistent problem affecting consumer trust.

The DeepMind-EVE Online collaboration signals growing interest in using complex game environments as testing grounds for AI systems, where agents must navigate intricate social and economic dynamics.

Watch for whether Google's warning system effectively reduces fake reviews, and whether other platforms adopt similar enforcement measures.
