# NY AG James Faces Ethics Heat Over Prejudicial Statements in Cornell Case

New York Attorney General Letitia James is under fire for potential prosecutorial misconduct—a cautionary tale for Colorado voters watching how progressive prosecutors wield power.

An ethics complaint filed with the New York State Bar Association challenges James's fitness to lead the criminal investigation into the alleged Cornell University gang rape case, commonly called the "Cornell 7" matter (*Just The News*, *The Daily Wire*). The complaint centers on public comments James made about the case before her office took over the prosecution, raising questions about whether she prejudged the defendants before reviewing evidence.

The National Legal and Policy Center filed the complaint, arguing James should recuse herself or face removal. Defense attorneys have separately moved to disqualify her from the case entirely.

**Why Colorado should care:** This case illustrates how partisan prosecutors can abuse their offices when ideology trumps impartiality. Colorado voters should demand that state and local prosecutors—from Denver's DA to the Attorney General's office—maintain professional distance from high-profile cases and avoid inflammatory public statements that suggest predetermined outcomes. Defendants deserve neutral arbiters, not politicians grandstanding for headlines.
