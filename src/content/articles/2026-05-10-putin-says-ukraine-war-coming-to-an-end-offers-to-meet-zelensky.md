# Putin Says Ukraine Conflict 'Coming to an End,' Signals Willingness to Negotiate

Russian President Vladimir Putin said he believes the Ukraine war is "coming to an end," according to BBC World and Al Jazeera English, while signaling openness to direct talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky.

Putin's comments came as Russia observed Victory Day, and according to the Washington Examiner, coincided with a three-day ceasefire. Al Jazeera reported that Putin said he would be willing to meet Zelensky in a third country if a long-term peace deal is reached.

However, Putin also condemned Western support for Ukraine, according to BBC World, suggesting continued friction over international backing for Zelensky's government.

The remarks represent a potential shift in rhetoric from Moscow, though they come without concrete details on negotiation terms or conditions. Neither the timing nor the likelihood of actual peace talks has been confirmed by Ukrainian officials or Western governments.

Watch for Ukrainian responses to Putin's overture and whether any formal diplomatic channels open in coming weeks.
