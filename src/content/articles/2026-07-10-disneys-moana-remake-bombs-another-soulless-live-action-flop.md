# Disney's Live-Action 'Moana' Flops: Another Soulless Remake Bombs at Box Office

Disney's live-action remake of "Moana" is tanking—and for good reason. The film, which 9News Denver describes as "a lifeless carbon copy of an animated classic," is tracking toward one of the lowest opening weekends for a Disney live-action adaptation, with a dismal 35 percent review rating on Rotten Tomatoes (Breitbart).

The problem is obvious: Disney shot the remake nearly note-for-note from the original animated film, adding only scattered new material. Rather than create something fresh, the studio opted for a cynical cash grab that strips away the charm and artistry that made the 2016 original work.

This is emblematic of Hollywood's broader creative bankruptcy. Instead of investing in original storytelling, major studios recycle existing intellectual property, betting audiences will show up out of nostalgia. When the execution is lazy—as it clearly is here—audiences rightfully stay home.

The box office failure sends a message: Colorado families and moviegoers nationwide are tired of soulless remakes. They want genuine creativity, not corporate assembly-line filmmaking.

Watch whether Disney learns anything from this disaster or doubles down on more hollow live-action retreads.
