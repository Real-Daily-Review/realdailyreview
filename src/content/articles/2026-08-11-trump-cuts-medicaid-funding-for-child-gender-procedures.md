# Trump Administration Halts Medicaid Funding for Child Gender Procedures

The Trump administration has moved to strip Medicaid and CHIP funding for puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones, and sex-change surgeries for minors—a sharp reversal of Biden-era policy that conservatives have long opposed as reckless medical experimentation on children.

CMS Administrator Mehmet Oz announced the decision, characterizing these treatments as experimental and inappropriate for government-funded programs (Fox News). The move aligns with Republican arguments that such procedures lack sufficient long-term safety data and that children cannot meaningfully consent to irreversible medical interventions.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's Medicaid program, which serves roughly 1.5 million low-income residents, will no longer be required to fund these treatments—potentially freeing state resources while shielding taxpayers from subsidizing contested medical procedures.

Democrats and progressive medical groups argue these treatments are evidence-based care for transgender youth experiencing gender dysphoria. They contend the ban will harm vulnerable children and restrict parental choice. Conservatives counter that government shouldn't fund experimental treatments on minors, period—and that protecting children from premature medical decisions is a legitimate state interest.

The decision reflects the Trump administration's broader skepticism of progressive gender ideology in federal programs. Watch for Colorado legislative Democrats to potentially move to protect state-level funding for these procedures, setting up a direct clash over taxpayer dollars and parental rights.
