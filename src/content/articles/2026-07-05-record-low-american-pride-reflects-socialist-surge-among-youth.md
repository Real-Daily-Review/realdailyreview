# Young Americans' Pride in Country Hits Record Low as Socialism Gains Ground Among Youth

A new NBC News poll reveals a troubling trend: record-low numbers of Americans say they're "extremely proud" to be American—a crisis of gratitude that conservatives blame on progressive education and cultural messaging that downplays American exceptionalism.

National Review warns that today's young socialists, energized by Democratic Socialist of America (DSA) organizing, understand the failures of socialism no better than their predecessors did. Meanwhile, The Daily Wire notes that too few American schoolchildren hear the actual story of American achievement, constitutional liberty, and the blessings of free markets that built the world's greatest economy.

Conservatives argue the problem runs deeper than polling numbers. Unlike immigrants who've fled dictatorship and tyranny, many native-born Americans take constitutional rights and prosperity for granted—a dangerous vulnerability when progressive activists actively work to discredit capitalism and American institutions.

For Colorado specifically, this cultural drift threatens the state's business climate and workforce. If young Coloradans absorb anti-American, anti-capitalist messaging instead of learning why free enterprise and limited government created their opportunities, the state risks brain drain and declining entrepreneurship.

**Watch whether Colorado schools adopt curricula that actually teach American history and constitutional principles—or continue the progressive narrative that frames the nation's founding as primarily a story of oppression.**
