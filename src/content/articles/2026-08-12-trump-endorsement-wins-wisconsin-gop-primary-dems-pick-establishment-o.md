# Wisconsin GOP Picks Trump Ally; Democrats Nominate Establishment Over Socialist

Trump's endorsement muscle delivered again: Michael Alfonso, the 26-year-old son-in-law of former Rep. Sean Duffy, won Wisconsin's 7th Congressional District Republican primary in a crowded field, signaling continued MAGA dominance in GOP nomination contests (Fox News).

On the Democratic side, Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley pulled off an upset, narrowly defeating socialist state legislator Francesca Hong for the gubernatorial nomination—defying pollsters who favored Hong throughout the race (Breitbart, Just The News). Hong subsequently endorsed Crowley, uniting the fractured Democratic base.

**The Colorado angle:** Wisconsin's results underscore how Trump-backed candidates continue reshaping Republican primaries nationwide, while Democrats are retreating from their progressive flank in competitive races. Colorado Republicans should note the playbook: Trump endorsements remain potent in primary battles, even in crowded fields. Meanwhile, Colorado Democrats watching Wisconsin may recalculate whether their own progressive candidates can win statewide in 2026—a lesson relevant as the state's gubernatorial race takes shape.
