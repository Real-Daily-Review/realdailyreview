# Federal Government's Immigration Failures Leave Colorado Hospital $4M in the Red

The unpaid tab underscores a broader pattern of federal negligence: while Washington stiffs hospitals for immigrant care, it simultaneously botches basic security protocols, as evidenced by a Virginia case where an illegal alien convicted of murder was allowed unsupervised mental hospital passes and fled the country (New York Post, Daily Wire).

UCHealth's situation illustrates how sanctuary-friendly policies and bureaucratic incompetence cascade into real costs for Colorado taxpayers and healthcare providers.
