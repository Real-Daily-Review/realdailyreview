# Meta Cuts 8,000 Jobs as Zuckerberg Pivots to AI

Meta is laying off roughly 8,000 employees—about 10% of its workforce—and canceling plans to hire for 6,000 additional roles, according to CNBC and Al Jazeera English.

CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced the cuts Wednesday, framing the move as necessary for competing in artificial intelligence development. In a memo to staff, Zuckerberg said "success isn't a given" in the AI era, signaling the company's strategic shift away from its current operational structure.

The layoffs represent Meta's latest workforce reduction as the tech giant reallocates resources toward AI initiatives. The company is simultaneously freezing new hiring plans, effectively doubling the impact on headcount growth.

Zuckerberg's memo emphasized the competitive pressures facing Meta in the rapidly evolving AI landscape, though the company did not detail severance packages or transition support in the initial announcement.
