# Colorado Businesses Split on July 4th Holiday Hours; CPW Ramps Up Boating Enforcement

Colorado retailers and banks are taking divergent approaches to the July 4th holiday this year, with some observing the federal holiday Friday while others remain open for business as usual.

According to 9News Denver, because Independence Day falls on Saturday, some—but not all—Colorado businesses are closing Friday or adjusting hours. Banks, post offices, and major retailers are split on whether to recognize the holiday early, leaving consumers to verify hours before heading out.

Meanwhile, Colorado Parks and Wildlife is using the holiday weekend to crack down on boating under the influence (BUI), KDVR reports. CPW is warning boaters to stay sober and avoid operating watercraft while intoxicated—a timely reminder as Coloradans flock to lakes and rivers over the long weekend.

The enforcement push reflects a broader state focus on water safety during peak recreation season. Conservatives generally support CPW's public-safety messaging, though some argue the agency should prioritize education over aggressive enforcement tactics that can burden law-abiding recreationists.
