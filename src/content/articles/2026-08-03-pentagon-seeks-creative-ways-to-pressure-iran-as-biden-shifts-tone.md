# Pentagon Seeks 'Creative' Iran Pressure Tactics as Biden Admin Signals Tougher Line

A senior U.S. Central Command official has asked military colleagues for "new creative and unconventional ways to pressure and punish Iran," according to emails obtained by the *Washington Examiner*. The request, sent by an officer in CENTCOM's intelligence branch, suggests the Pentagon is exploring options beyond traditional diplomatic and economic channels to counter Iranian aggression.

CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Timothy Hawkins defended the inquiry, telling *Just The News* that the agency "has a long history of thinking and working in innovative ways."

The move marks a potential shift from the Biden administration's earlier posture toward Iran, which included attempts to resurrect the Obama-era nuclear deal. It also comes as Iranian proxies continue attacks on U.S. forces and interests across the Middle East, and as Tehran accelerates its nuclear program.
