# Trump Administration Deports 46,000 in July as Mullin Faces MAGA Blowback

President Trump's immigration enforcement machine hit a record stride in July, deporting 46,000 illegal aliens and arresting 51,000 more through ICE—an average of 1,645 daily arrests—yet DHS Chief Markwayne Mullin is drawing fire from hardline Republicans over perceived enforcement gaps.

According to the Washington Examiner, the deportation numbers represent an unprecedented pace compared to prior administrations. Over the past 18 months, more immigrants have received judicial deportation orders than during the entire four-year Obama-Biden tenure. The scale of enforcement action underscores Trump's stated commitment to border security and interior removal operations.

However, Breitbart reports that MAGA supporters are criticizing Mullin, suggesting frustration within the Trump base over the pace or scope of enforcement. The criticism signals internal disagreement on whether the administration is moving aggressively enough on immigration—a core Trump campaign promise.

For Colorado, which has absorbed significant illegal immigration flows into Denver and northern Front Range communities, these enforcement numbers carry direct implications for local law enforcement resources, housing pressure, and labor market dynamics. Conservative Coloradans have long argued that federal enforcement failures shift costs to states and municipalities.
