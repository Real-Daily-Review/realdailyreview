# Democratic Party in Freefall as Moderates Lose Ground to Radical Wing

Colorado Democrats should pay attention: the national party is fracturing, and moderates are losing control to the socialist left.

Sen. Elissa Slotkin (D-Mich.) recently declared that the Democratic Party needs "new leadership" because "old models are no longer working" (Fox News). Her admission signals panic among establishment Democrats who built their brand on centrist messaging—a brand that's collapsing.

The Washington Examiner argues the shift runs deeper. Conservative analysts describe the primary victories of Democratic Socialists as a "coming-out party" for what they call "DINOs"—Democrats in name only—who now control major cities like New York, Chicago, and Seattle. Candidates openly backing socialized medicine, rent control, and wealth taxes are no longer fringe; they're the party's future (Washington Examiner).

**The Colorado angle:** As Democrats dominate state politics, this ideological lurch leftward will shape Colorado policy. Expect pressure for higher taxes, aggressive climate regulations, and expanded government programs—all funded by Colorado taxpayers and businesses already burdened by one of the nation's highest cost-of-living increases.

Republicans see opportunity. While Democrats cannibalize themselves over ideology, conservatives can hammer home a simple message: limited government, lower taxes, and freedom work. Colorado voters tired of progressive overreach have a clear alternative.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats embrace or resist the national party's socialist turn—and whether that choice costs them swing districts in 2024 and beyond.
