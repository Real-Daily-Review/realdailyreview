# Democrats Can't Escape the Woke Reckoning They Built

Colorado Democrats should brace for what's coming: their party's progressive wing is frantically distancing itself from the radical rhetoric of 2019–2021, but the receipts are permanent.

Across the country, Democratic candidates are attempting what conservatives call a "memory-hole" operation—pretending they never embraced the defund-the-police slogans, gender ideology mandates, and anti-energy zealotry that defined their recent platform. National Review and The Daily Wire note that Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and allies can't outrun their own words, captured forever on video and in legislative records.

The pattern is unmistakable. Democratic candidates who once championed radical climate lockdowns, attacked fossil fuels, and pushed progressive social experiments are now repositioning themselves as moderates. Republicans argue this exposes the hollowness of progressive conviction—these weren't principled stands but political theater that evaporates when electoral winds shift.

For Colorado specifically, this matters enormously. State Democrats pushed aggressive energy regulations and climate mandates that hurt workers and businesses. Now, as the political tide turns, they'll likely claim they were "misunderstood." Conservatives say voters deserve accountability: Did these policies work? Did they harm Colorado's economy? Will Democrats own the consequences, or simply rebrand and move on?

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic establishment distances itself from the state's most radical environmental and social policies—and whether Republicans effectively remind voters of what was actually promised and delivered.
