# Colorado Universities Embrace AI—While National GOP Fumbles the Message

Colorado's higher education institutions are moving fast on artificial intelligence, but Republicans are squandering an opportunity to shape the narrative around this transformative technology.

Six Colorado colleges and universities have earned a new state "AI Engaged Campus Designation," recognizing intentional deployment of AI in teaching and research (CPR News). The move reflects Colorado's growing role in the tech economy and signals that state universities are positioning themselves competitively in an AI-driven future.

Meanwhile, national Republicans are botching the conversation. The Washington Examiner's right-leaning opinion section notes that GOP leadership is wasting midterm messaging bandwidth on trivial rebranding efforts—like Trump's push to rename AI "super intelligence"—instead of articulating a coherent conservative vision for innovation, economic growth, and responsible AI governance.

**The Colorado angle:** As universities integrate AI into curricula and research, state policymakers should ensure these initiatives don't become vehicles for progressive ideology or wasteful spending. Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and defense contractors depend on AI innovation; Republicans should champion market-driven development over heavy-handed regulation.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado's AI initiatives include transparency requirements and whether taxpayer dollars fund ideologically captured research.
