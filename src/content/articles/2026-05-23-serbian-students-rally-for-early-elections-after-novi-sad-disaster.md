# Serbia's Student-Led Movement Demands Early Elections After Novi Sad Disaster

Tens of thousands of Serbians rallied in antigovernment demonstrations demanding early elections, according to Al Jazeera English. The student-led movement, which began following the Novi Sad disaster in November 2024, has grown into a sustained push for political change. The protests reflect broader discontent with the current government's handling of the incident and its aftermath.

The demonstrations underscore mounting pressure on Serbian leadership to accelerate the electoral timeline. Student organizers have framed early elections as essential to addressing systemic governance failures they say contributed to the Novi Sad event.

Watch for whether the government responds to protest demands or attempts to manage the movement through other means.
