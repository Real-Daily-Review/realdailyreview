# Canadian Wildfires Choke Western Air—While Democrats Blame Trump Instead of Ottawa

Colorado's air quality has taken a backseat to partisan theatrics as smoke from Canadian wildfires continues to drift south, but Democrats are more interested in attacking President Trump's 51st-state quip than demanding Ottawa get its forest management under control. *Just The News* reports that hazardous smoke concerns have now shifted to severe thunderstorm and flood threats across the eastern U.S., yet the root problem—Canada's failure to manage its forests responsibly—remains unaddressed by the Biden-Harris administration.

Rather than pressure Canada to invest in wildfire prevention and suppression, Democratic House members, including U.S. Rep. Linda Sánchez, have seized on Trump's tongue-in-cheek suggestion about Canadian statehood to warn that his "rhetoric threatens to leave us alone to face other challenges we cannot overcome on our own," according to *The Daily Wire*. 

**Republicans argue** that instead of deflecting with political grievances, the Biden-Harris administration should have held Canada accountable for transboundary air pollution affecting millions of Americans—particularly Coloradans who endure weeks of unhealthy air quality each fire season.

Conservatives should watch whether the incoming Trump administration will actually pressure Canada on forest management or use trade leverage to force real solutions.
