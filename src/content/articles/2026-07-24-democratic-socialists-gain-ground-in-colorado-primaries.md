# Democratic Socialists Gaining Ground in Colorado and Beyond

Colorado Democrats are watching their party shift further left as Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) candidates notch wins across the country, including in Colorado, New York, and Washington, D.C., defeating establishment Democratic incumbents and capturing open seats.

The DSA's momentum reflects a broader ideological realignment within Democratic politics. According to the Washington Examiner, these candidates have been "on a roll lately," signaling that socialist-aligned voices are no longer fringe players in Democratic primaries. The trend carries real implications for Colorado: DSA-backed candidates winning statewide or legislative races could accelerate the state's drift toward higher taxes, expanded government programs, and stricter regulations on energy development and business.

Republicans and conservatives argue the DSA's rise exposes the Democratic Party's abandonment of moderate governance. They contend that candidates openly embracing democratic socialism will push for wealth redistribution, aggressive climate mandates that threaten Colorado's energy sector, and expansive social spending that Colorado taxpayers cannot afford.

Democrats counter that DSA candidates represent grassroots energy and respond to voter demands for bold action on inequality and climate change.
