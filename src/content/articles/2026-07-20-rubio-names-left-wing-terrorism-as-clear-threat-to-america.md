# Secretary Rubio Sounds Alarm on Left-Wing Terrorism Threat

Secretary of State Marco Rubio is calling out what he characterizes as an epidemic of left-wing violence and extortion targeting Americans, with the State Department now taking action to address the threat.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Rubio has directly named far-left extremism as "a clear and present danger to the United States and its values," invoking Ronald Reagan's definition of terrorism as "the preferred weapon of weak and evil men." The *National Review* notes that even Democrat Adam Smith has acknowledged the severity of the threat, suggesting the issue transcends partisan lines.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that progressive activists have normalized political violence and intimidation as acceptable tactics, creating a chilling effect on free speech and lawful dissent. Conservatives contend the mainstream left has been reluctant to condemn these acts forcefully, instead offering qualified rhetoric or deflecting blame.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's history of left-wing activism—from environmental extremism to Antifa presence in Denver—makes this federal focus relevant to state residents concerned about public safety and political intimidation.

Taxpayers and conservatives should monitor whether the Biden-Harris administration follows through on State Department action or whether progressive allies in Congress attempt to block or undermine these efforts.
