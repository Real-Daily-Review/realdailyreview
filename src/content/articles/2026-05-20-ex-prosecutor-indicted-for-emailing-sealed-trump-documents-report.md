# Former Prosecutor Indicted for Emailing Herself Sealed Trump Documents Report

A former federal prosecutor in Florida has been indicted for violating a judge's order by sending herself a sealed special counsel report on President Donald Trump's classified documents investigation, according to charging documents unsealed Wednesday.

Carmen Mercedes Lineberger, 62, of Port St. Lucie, faces four criminal counts, according to The Hill. She allegedly emailed herself Volume II of Special Counsel Jack Smith's report and used misleading file names—disguising the documents as bundt and chocolate cake recipes—to avoid detection, the Washington Examiner reported.

The indictment alleges Lineberger sent the confidential communications to her personal email account despite a judge's explicit order that the materials remain sealed, according to PBS NewsHour. Lineberger pleaded not guilty before a federal court.

The case underscores ongoing scrutiny of how sensitive Justice Department materials related to Trump investigations have been handled. Jack Smith's office concluded its work following Trump's return to office, and portions of his report have faced legal battles over public disclosure.
