# Judge Blocks NYC Socialist Mayor's Luxury Home Tax—A Warning for Colorado

New York City's newly elected socialist mayor, Zohran Mamdani, just got a hard lesson in the limits of confiscatory taxation. A state judge slapped a temporary restraining order on Mamdani's pied-à-terre tax Monday, blocking the rollout of a surcharge targeting roughly 1 million residents who own second homes (Washington Examiner, Just The News). Judge Wayne Ozzi ordered the city to remove its published tax roll of over 900,000 properties and barred further action on approximately 17,000 warning notices.

Mamdani, who worked with Gov. Kathy Hochul to implement the tax after taking office, filed an appeal immediately—signaling the left's determination to squeeze wealth wherever it finds it.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Punitive taxes on property ownership punish success, drive capital flight, and shrink the tax base. New York's experience shows that even activist judges recognize the overreach.

**Colorado should pay attention.** As progressive lawmakers eye wealth taxes and property-based levies to fund their spending agenda, this New York precedent matters. Colorado's business community and property owners should watch whether similar schemes gain traction in Denver or the legislature—and prepare legal challenges if they do.
