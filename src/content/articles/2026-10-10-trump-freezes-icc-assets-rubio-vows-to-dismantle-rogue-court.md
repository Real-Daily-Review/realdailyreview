# Trump Administration Freezes ICC Assets, Vows to Dismantle 'Rogue' Court

The Trump administration is taking direct aim at the International Criminal Court, freezing its assets and barring all U.S. transactions with the institution—a dramatic escalation that signals Washington's refusal to bend to what conservatives view as an illegitimate, anti-American tribunal.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced the sweeping financial sanctions Friday, vowing to cripple the ICC "piece by piece" unless it abandons what the administration calls threats against the United States (Fox News). The move comes just hours after the Norwegian Nobel Committee awarded its 2026 Peace Prize to Navi Pillay, whom The Daily Wire describes as a "virulently anti-Israel ideologue"—a decision that appears to have crystallized the Trump team's resolve.

Conservatives argue the ICC has become a rogue institution hostile to American interests and Israeli sovereignty, operating without legitimate accountability to any democratic electorate. By freezing assets and blocking U.S. financial flows, the administration is weaponizing America's economic leverage to constrain what it sees as judicial overreach by an unaccountable international body (Breitbart).

The sanctions represent a sharp break from the Biden-era posture of ICC deference and signal the Trump administration's willingness to confront multilateral institutions it views as threats to U.S. sovereignty and allies.

Watch whether Congress moves to codify these sanctions into law or whether the ICC capitulates to American pressure.
