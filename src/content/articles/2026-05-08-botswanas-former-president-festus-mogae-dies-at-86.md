# Botswana's Former President Festus Mogae Dies at 86

Festus Mogae, who led Botswana for a decade and earned international recognition for his governance and public health initiatives, has died at age 86, according to Al Jazeera English and Deutsche Welle.

Mogae served as Botswana's president and became known as a pioneer in combating HIV/AIDS during his tenure, a crisis that had devastated the southern African nation. His administration was widely praised for good governance practices that helped stabilize the country's economy and institutions.

The former leader's legacy centers on his efforts to address one of the region's most pressing health challenges during his presidency. Deutsche Welle characterized him as a "pioneer in the fight against AIDS," underscoring the public health dimension of his political career.

Details on funeral arrangements or immediate succession matters were not provided in available reports.
