# Body Found in Park County Believed to Be Missing Littleton Employee

A body discovered Thursday night in Park County is believed to be Tyler Barton, a Littleton city employee missing since November 16, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

Barton was last seen the morning of November 16. Search efforts have been underway since his disappearance, with authorities focusing on the Park County area where the remains were located Thursday evening.

The discovery marks a tragic conclusion to a weeks-long search that engaged local law enforcement and community resources across the Denver metro region. Formal identification is pending, though authorities expressed confidence in the preliminary identification based on available evidence.

Details surrounding the circumstances of Barton's death remain under investigation. Park County Sheriff's Office and Littleton Police are coordinating the ongoing probe.
