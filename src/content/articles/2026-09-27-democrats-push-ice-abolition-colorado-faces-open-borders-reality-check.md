# Democrats' ICE Abolition Push Signals Open-Borders Agenda, Conservatives Warn

Colorado voters should brace for what a Democratic Congress might actually do with immigration enforcement—and it's far more radical than campaign rhetoric suggests.

Rep. Delia Ramirez (D-IL), who has publicly identified as a "proud anchor baby," introduced the *Reimagining Safety Act*, the most comprehensive legislation yet to abolish Immigration and Customs Enforcement, according to the *Washington Examiner*. While Democrats have floated ICE abolition before, Ramirez's bill represents the most detailed blueprint for dismantling the agency.

Republicans and conservatives argue the proposal exposes the Democratic Party's true immigration stance: effective open borders. Eliminating ICE would strip the federal government of its primary interior enforcement tool, leaving states like Colorado vulnerable to unchecked illegal immigration, strain on social services, and labor market disruption in agriculture and construction sectors where Colorado employers operate.

The *National Review* notes that defending classical liberalism requires clearly defining its enemies—and progressives pushing ICE abolition represent a fundamental rejection of the rule of law and national sovereignty that undergird constitutional governance.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the stakes are direct: who pays for emergency room visits, education, and social services when federal enforcement collapses? Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from or embrace Ramirez's agenda ahead of 2026.
