# France's Immigration Crisis Should Alarm Colorado Policymakers

France is burning—literally—and the culprits aren't hiding their allegiances. Rioters carrying Algerian, Moroccan, and Palestinian flags have spent over two weeks torching schools, vandalizing storefronts, and assaulting teachers, according to the *Washington Examiner*. When President Trump identified mass migration as the root cause, France's leadership reflexively denied reality rather than confront the problem.

The lesson for Colorado is stark: uncontrolled immigration and failed assimilation create social chaos that no amount of progressive rhetoric can paper over. France's establishment refused to acknowledge what citizens could plainly see—that rapid demographic change without integration breeds resentment, lawlessness, and the breakdown of civic order.

Colorado conservatives should ask hard questions about our own sanctuary policies, catch-and-release practices, and whether state and local governments are adequately vetting and integrating newcomers. The Left claims immigration restrictions are xenophobic; Republicans counter that nations have the right—and duty—to control their borders and ensure newcomers embrace American values and law.

France's crisis didn't happen overnight. It's the predictable result of decades of permissive policies and denial. Colorado voters should watch closely whether state Democrats move toward similar policies or whether they'll learn from Europe's cautionary tale.
