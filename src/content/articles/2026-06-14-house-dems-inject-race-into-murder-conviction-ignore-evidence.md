# House Democrats Push Race Narrative in Murder Case, Ignore Facts

House Democrats are weaponizing the Karmelo Anthony murder conviction to advance a pre-written racial grievance script—ignoring that a jury convicted him of fatally stabbing Austin Metcalf based on evidence, not skin color (Fox News). Rather than accept the verdict, Democratic lawmakers have blamed jury composition and alleged systemic racism, a move National Review rightly calls an attempt to "make an open-and-shut murder case into a race-based morality play."

The facts matter. Anthony received a 35-year sentence. Meanwhile, activists and some elected officials have rallied to his defense, drawing comparisons to other high-profile cases in ways that strain credulity. A Texas Department of Criminal Justice employee was terminated after posting about the case on Facebook—a cautionary tale about speaking freely in today's climate (The Daily Wire).

One of Anthony's most vocal street supporters, Jerome Winston Parker, was arrested this week on an outstanding warrant for unlawful carrying of a weapon, further undercutting the narrative that this case is about systemic injustice rather than criminal conduct.

Conservatives argue that Democrats' race-first approach to criminal justice corrodes public confidence in courts and distracts from actual accountability. Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state Democrats adopt similar talking points to undermine jury verdicts or push soft-on-crime policies disguised as racial equity.
