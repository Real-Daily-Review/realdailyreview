# Bernie's Wealth-Grab Scheme Flops—Again

Bernie Sanders's latest push for a government-controlled "wealth fund" recycled a failed Swedish experiment that should alarm Colorado taxpayers wary of socialist schemes masquerading as innovation.

According to *National Review*, Sanders's AI-era wealth fund proposal mirrors Sweden's disastrous attempt at state-managed investment funds—a cautionary tale of government overreach and market distortion. The Vermont senator's plan would essentially nationalize portions of corporate assets, funneling returns to government coffers rather than letting workers and investors keep their earnings.

Conservatives argue the scheme represents naked wealth confiscation dressed up in progressive rhetoric. It penalizes capital formation, discourages business investment, and hands bureaucrats control over markets they've proven incompetent to manage. For Colorado's energy sector, tech startups, and small businesses, such policies would chill growth and job creation.

Meanwhile, *Washington Examiner* reports progressives are fracturing over Sanders's apparent 2028 exit, with figures like Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez positioning themselves as heirs to his far-left mantle. The Democratic Party's internal battle over who carries the socialist torch underscores how far left the party's base has drifted.
