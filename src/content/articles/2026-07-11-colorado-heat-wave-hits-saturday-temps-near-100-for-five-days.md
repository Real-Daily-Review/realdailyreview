# Colorado Faces Five-Day Heat Wave Starting Saturday; Temps Near 100 Expected

A dangerous heat wave is bearing down on Colorado, with the Front Range bracing for temperatures near 100 degrees starting Saturday and potentially lasting five days, according to 9News Denver. The broader system sweeping the Southwest and Great Plains will bring triple-digit highs across much of the region, trapped under a dome of high pressure that meteorologists warn could persist for a week or more (Washington Examiner).

For Colorado families and businesses, the timing matters. Peak demand on the grid will strain power supplies just as summer cooling loads spike. Energy producers and utilities will face pressure to meet demand—a reminder that reliable baseload power from natural gas and coal remains essential, despite progressive push-back against fossil fuels. Coloradans should ensure air conditioning systems are serviced and stock up on water and electrolytes.

The heat also threatens outdoor workers, ranchers managing livestock in extreme conditions, and rural communities dependent on consistent power. Forecasters are urging residents to stay hydrated and seek air-conditioned shelter during peak afternoon hours.

Watch whether Colorado's grid operators invoke emergency protocols or whether rolling blackouts occur—a test of whether the state's aggressive renewable-only mandates can actually deliver when demand peaks.
