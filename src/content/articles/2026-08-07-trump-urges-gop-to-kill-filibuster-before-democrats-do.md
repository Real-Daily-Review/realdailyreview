# Trump Warns GOP: Don't Let Democrats Kill the Filibuster First

Former President Trump is sounding the alarm that Republicans must end the Senate filibuster before Democrats do—or risk permanent Democratic dominance in Washington.

Trump's warning comes after Michigan Democrat Abdulrahman El-Sayed won his party's Senate primary this week. According to *Just The News* and *Breitbart*, Trump labeled El-Sayed a "communist" and noted that both he and the Democratic candidate support eliminating the 60-vote threshold that currently protects the minority party from majoritarian overreach.

The stakes are stark: if Democrats nuke the filibuster first while controlling the Senate, Trump argues, Republicans will face an insurmountable disadvantage. "It could make me the last Republican President ever," Trump warned, per *Breitbart*.

**The conservative case:** Republicans have long defended the filibuster as a check on executive and legislative power. But Trump's argument flips that script—he's saying Republicans must strike first to prevent Democrats from using a filibuster-free Senate to impose their agenda unilaterally. Conservatives are divided: some see the filibuster as essential protection against progressive overreach; others agree with Trump that Democrats will kill it anyway, so Republicans shouldn't unilaterally disarm.

**Colorado angle:** Any filibuster change would affect how federal energy policy, water law, and gun regulations flow downstream to Colorado.
