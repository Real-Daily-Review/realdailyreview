# Judge Blocks Lindsay Clancy Defense Bid; Trial Moves Forward

A Massachusetts judge has rejected a defense motion seeking to dismiss murder charges against Lindsay Clancy, the Colorado mother accused of killing her three young children in their Duxbury home. Judge William Sullivan ruled Thursday that prosecutors presented sufficient evidence for a jury to consider the charges, dealing a significant setback to the defense strategy (9News Denver, Washington Examiner).

Clancy's attorney Kevin Reddington had argued that both prosecution and defense expert witnesses agreed she suffered severe mental illness, attempting to use that agreement as grounds for a not-guilty finding on the basis of lack of criminal responsibility. The judge rejected that approach, determining the evidence warranted proceeding to trial (The Daily Wire).

The case involves the deaths of Cora, 5; Dawson, 3; and Callan, 8 months old. A mistrial was declared last month, but Sullivan's ruling clears the path for the case to move forward rather than being dismissed outright.

The decision represents a major procedural victory for prosecutors and suggests the judge found their evidence sufficient to proceed—a critical threshold in any criminal case. Conservatives and crime victims' advocates typically support judicial decisions that allow cases to reach juries rather than being dismissed on technical or evidentiary grounds.

Watch whether the defense appeals this ruling or pivots to a mental-health-based insanity defense at trial.
