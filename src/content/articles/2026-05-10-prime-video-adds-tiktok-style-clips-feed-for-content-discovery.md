# Prime Video Launches TikTok-Style 'Clips' Feed to Boost Discovery

Amazon is adding a vertical short-form video feed to Prime Video, joining Netflix and Disney Plus in adopting the scrollable format to drive engagement.

The new "Clips" feed will display short snippets from shows and movies available on the platform, according to The Verge. Users can scroll through the feed on the app's homepage and jump directly to full titles, or rent or purchase content from individual clips. Prime Video says the experience will personalize based on viewing history, showing something new with each visit.

The move mirrors strategies already deployed by competitors. Netflix and Disney Plus have both introduced similar vertical feeds in recent months as streaming services compete for user attention in an increasingly crowded market. Amazon previously tested a TikTok-style feed featuring NBA game highlights, but the new Clips feature expands that approach across the broader catalog.

The feature represents a broader industry shift toward short-form discovery mechanisms, as traditional browse-and-search interfaces face pressure to compete with algorithmic feeds popularized by social media platforms. Streaming services are banking on the format to surface content users might otherwise miss and reduce friction between discovery and playback.
