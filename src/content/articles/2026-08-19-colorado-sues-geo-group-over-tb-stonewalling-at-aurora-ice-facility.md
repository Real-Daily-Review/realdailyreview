# Colorado Sues GEO Group Over TB Outbreak at Aurora Detention Center

Colorado's Attorney General is taking the GEO Group to court over its refusal to cooperate with state health investigators probing a tuberculosis outbreak at the Aurora ICE detention facility. According to the Colorado Sun and 9News Denver, an active TB case surfaced in June, but the facility operator has stonewalled the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment's investigation—prompting the state to file suit in Adams County court.

This is a rare moment where government enforcement and private detention operations align with public health necessity. Conservatives generally oppose federal immigration detention expansion, but they also expect operators contracted to house detainees to meet basic health and safety standards. The GEO Group's resistance to a straightforward TB investigation undermines that expectation and invites exactly the kind of regulatory overreach progressives will use to justify tighter government control of detention facilities.

For Colorado taxpayers, the real question is whether the state's lawsuit will force genuine transparency or simply become another vehicle for the left to attack private detention operators—potentially paving the way for full government takeover of immigration enforcement infrastructure.

Watch whether the GEO Group cooperates once sued, or whether Colorado Democrats use this case to push for state-run detention facilities.
