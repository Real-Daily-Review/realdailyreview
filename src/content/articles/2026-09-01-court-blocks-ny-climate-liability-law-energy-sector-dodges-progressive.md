# Court Blocks New York's Fossil Fuel Liability Grab—A Win for Energy Industry and Colorado Workers

A court has struck down New York's 2024 law that would have made fossil fuel companies liable for climate change damages, dealing a blow to Democratic efforts to weaponize the courts against the energy sector (Just The News).

The law represented a coordinated progressive strategy: use state litigation to impose massive financial liability on oil, gas, and coal producers without going through legislatures or voters. Democrats have long sought to replicate this playbook across blue states, including Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature.

Republicans and industry advocates argue such laws amount to backdoor regulation—imposing costs and compliance burdens that elected officials never approved. They say the real target is energy companies that power American prosperity, not genuine climate policy. The court's decision protects companies operating in Colorado's energy sector, which employs thousands and generates substantial tax revenue.

Meanwhile, a separate study cited by 9News Denver claims El Niño events have intensified over the past 40 years due to climate change. Conservatives note that weather patterns have always fluctuated; attributing every weather event to human activity conflates correlation with causation and often justifies expansive government control over energy production.
