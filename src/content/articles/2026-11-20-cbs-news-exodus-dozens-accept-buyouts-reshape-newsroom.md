# CBS News Exodus: Dozens of Left-Wing Staffers Take Buyouts and Leave

Dozens of CBS News employees are heading for the exits after accepting voluntary buyout packages following Skydance's merger with Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery, marking what insiders are calling an "extinction level event" at the network.

The departures come as new leadership under CBS News editor-in-chief Bari Weiss and Skydance chief David Ellison reshape the newsroom. According to the New York Post, Wednesday was the deadline for staffers to accept the offers, and a significant number took the deal.

Conservative observers see the exodus as a welcome housecleaning. Breitbart's John Nolte characterized the departures as removing "dozens of villains" from what he describes as a "far-left" operation, though some leftist outlets have spun the staff reductions as a loss for the network's credibility and institutional knowledge.

The buyout reflects broader turbulence in legacy media as ownership changes and cost pressures force newsrooms to downsize. Whether CBS News emerges leaner and more balanced—or simply diminished—remains to be seen.
