# Ilhan Omar Under Federal Investigation for Immigration Fraud—and Fundraising Off It

Minnesota Democrat Ilhan Omar faces a serious federal criminal investigation into her immigration paperwork as Homeland Security Investigations agents raided her Washington, D.C. home, with her husband Tim Mynett spotted at the scene (Fox News). The probe deepens questions about Omar's background and entry to the United States that conservatives have raised for years.

Rather than wait for facts, Omar's campaign immediately weaponized the investigation for donations, firing off fundraising emails claiming the probe is "baseless" and "partisan" (The Daily Wire). That's classic progressive playbook: when facing legal trouble, cry persecution and shake down supporters.

National Review notes the awkwardness conservatives face: the Trump DOJ's investigation raises legitimate questions about Omar's past, yet politicized prosecutions of political opponents—regardless of party—threaten rule of law.

Colorado Republicans should pay attention to how national Democrats respond. If Omar faces charges and the left reflexively defends her on partisan grounds rather than facts, it signals they've abandoned principle for tribal loyalty. If the investigation proves overreaching, it's a cautionary tale about DOJ independence.
