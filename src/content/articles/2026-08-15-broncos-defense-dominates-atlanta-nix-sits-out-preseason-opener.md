# Broncos Defense Dominates in Preseason Opener; Nix Sits Out

Denver's defense is sending an early message: this team can win games without relying on Bo Nix's arm. The Broncos crushed Atlanta 27-7 in their preseason opener, with the defense recording two interceptions—including a pick-six—while the offense scored 24 points in a dominant first half (KDVR Fox31). Coach Sean Payton kept star quarterback Nix on the sidelines for the matchup, instead starting Jarrett Stidham, who managed the game effectively.

The lopsided victory suggests Denver's defensive overhaul is paying dividends early. A strong pass rush and ball-hawking secondary could be the foundation for a competitive AFC West campaign—good news for Broncos fans tired of recent mediocrity and for Colorado's economy, which benefits from a winning team's draw on tourism and local spending.

Nix is scheduled to see action Aug. 21 against Green Bay (CPR News), giving fans their first real look at the franchise's investment at quarterback. Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Payton's system can sustain this defensive intensity into the regular season—and whether Denver's front office has finally built a roster capable of playoff football.
