# Real Daily Review — The Brief
### Saturday, September 26, 2026

Colorado's fall weekend arrives with sunshine and a full plate of news — from cartel drone swarms at the southern border to a landmark child-safety settlement that should have every state attorney general paying attention.

---

## Denver Sees a Late-Season Return to the 80s

Good news for Coloradans heading outdoors this weekend: temperatures are bouncing back into the 80s with sunnier skies across the Denver metro. After a stretch of cooler fall weather, the brief warm-up is a welcome break before winter sets in. Enjoy it while it lasts — and if you're planning a drive on I-70 through Wheat Ridge, read the next item before you leave the driveway.

---

## CDOT Closing I-70 Eastbound Lanes at Kipling This Weekend

The Colorado Department of Transportation is warning drivers that I-70 eastbound lanes at Kipling will be shut down over the weekend as construction continues on the bridgeway. Wheat Ridge commuters and weekend travelers should plan alternate routes now. This is a reminder that Colorado's infrastructure projects — however necessary — carry real costs in time and disruption for the working Coloradans who depend on these corridors every single day.

---

## DHS: 378 Cartel Drones Detected at Southern Border in Just Over a Month

The Department of Homeland Security is sounding the alarm after detecting 378 hostile cartel drones at the southern border in just over one month — a stark illustration of how sophisticated and brazen transnational criminal organizations have become. DHS says the drones are failing, but the sheer volume is the story. For Colorado, this isn't an abstract border-state problem: cartel networks run drug and human trafficking operations deep into the state's cities and rural communities, and a porous, drone-surveilled border makes every Colorado community less safe.

---

## Alabama Wins $100 Million and Child Safety Concessions From TikTok

Alabama Attorney General Steve Marshall secured a landmark settlement with TikTok on Friday — $100 million and concrete new safety features on the platform — just days before a scheduled trial. The state's lawsuit centered on TikTok's impact on child safety. This is exactly the kind of aggressive, results-oriented action conservatives want to see from state attorneys general: using existing legal tools to hold Big Tech accountable without waiting for Washington to act. Colorado parents should be asking what their own AG is doing on this front.

---

## Colorado Ballot Watch: Proposition 133 and Human Trafficking Penalties

Colorado voters will face Proposition 133 this cycle, a ballot measure dealing with penalties for human trafficking of a minor — and, according to reporting, participation restrictions tied to school sports. The details matter enormously here. Conservatives should read this measure carefully before November: tough penalties for child predators are long overdue, but any ballot language that touches school sports and minors will draw intense scrutiny from both sides. Know what you're voting on before you vote.

---

## What to Watch

Keep your eyes on the southern border drone story — if cartel surveillance operations are this active, the pressure on Congress and the administration to respond with force will only grow, and Colorado's delegation will need to answer for where they stand. On the state level, Proposition 133 deserves a close read from every Colorado conservative voter before early ballots hit mailboxes. And watch for any movement from the Polis administration on energy, spending, or regulatory actions — the governor rarely lets a quiet news weekend go to waste.
