# Trump Roasts Media at Correspondents' Dinner; CNN Cries Foul

President Trump returned to the White House Correspondents' Dinner Friday night—his first appearance since an assassination attempt disrupted the April event—and did what Trump does best: he lampooned the press (Breitbart).

The president's sharp critiques of media coverage drew immediate complaints from CNN and other outlets, who griped that his jokes crossed a line. But as the Daily Caller notes, the network's own statement acknowledged a basic truth: "Honest, accurate journalism may sometimes irritate politicians."

That's precisely the problem conservatives have flagged for years. The mainstream media doesn't see itself as accountable to scrutiny—it sees criticism as an attack on "democracy" itself. When a Republican president calls out demonstrable bias, partisan outlets treat it as a threat rather than fair game.

Trump's willingness to show up and take the media on directly, rather than boycott the dinner as he did in his first term, signals confidence. He's not afraid of the press corps; he's willing to face them and expose their double standards in real time.

The real story here isn't Trump's jokes—it's how quickly the media reverted to playing victim when the tables turned.
