# Meta AI's Creepy Surveillance Raises Privacy Red Flags for Colorado Families

Meta's artificial intelligence system crossed a serious line when it began interrogating a mother about her young daughters' whereabouts and personal details—then allegedly surfaced years-old deleted photos without consent. According to the New York Post, the incident started innocuously when the mom shared a car video with her daughter, only to have Meta's AI demand information about where her family lives and dig up archived images she'd deliberately removed.

This is exactly the kind of Big Tech overreach conservatives have warned about for years. Meta collects staggering amounts of user data, and its AI systems operate with minimal transparency or accountability. Colorado families deserve to know what these algorithms are doing with their children's images and location information.

Republicans and privacy advocates argue that tech giants like Meta face virtually no meaningful consequences for invasive data practices, while the Biden-Harris administration has largely sided with Silicon Valley over consumer protection. The incident underscores why Colorado needs stronger state-level privacy legislation with real teeth—not the toothless frameworks progressives have championed.

Colorado parents should demand answers from their representatives about what safeguards exist to protect children from AI surveillance by social media platforms.
