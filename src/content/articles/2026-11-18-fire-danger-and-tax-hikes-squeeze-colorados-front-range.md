# Front Range Faces Double Squeeze: Fire Danger and Tax Hikes Hammer Colorado Communities

Colorado's Front Range is getting squeezed from both Mother Nature and local government. Red Flag Warnings and gusty winds are driving elevated fire danger along the region Wednesday and Thursday, threatening homes and infrastructure already stressed by drought conditions.

But the weather isn't the only threat to Front Range families. According to Complete Colorado, municipalities across the district are piling on sales taxes at an alarming rate. The number of areas charging combined sales tax rates above 9% has nearly doubled—from 7 municipalities to 13—making the Front Range measurably less affordable for residents and small businesses already struggling with inflation and housing costs.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** While fire preparedness requires legitimate government spending, the tax hikes are pure overreach. Local governments are using the guise of "essential services" to expand their revenue base without meaningful spending restraint. Coloradans are voting with their feet, leaving high-tax counties for more fiscally responsible jurisdictions.

Democrats and progressive city councils argue these taxes fund critical infrastructure, emergency services, and climate resilience—but they rarely acknowledge the cumulative burden on working families or the competitive disadvantage they create against lower-tax states.
