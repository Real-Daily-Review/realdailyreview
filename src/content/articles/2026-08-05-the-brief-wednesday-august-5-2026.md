## Fort Morgan Meatpackers Reject Contract, Lockout Continues

More than 1,700 Cargill workers in Fort Morgan have been locked out since May 20 after rejecting a tentative contract over wages and benefits. This is a real-world reminder that labor disputes have consequences for Colorado's agricultural economy — Cargill's Fort Morgan plant is one of the largest beef processing facilities in the state. Every week this drags on, ranchers, supply chains, and rural communities feel the pinch. Both sides need to get back to the table and get this resolved.
