# Trump Endorsement Can't Stop South Carolina GOP Senate Scramble

Trump's backing of Darline Graham for South Carolina's open Senate seat is hitting immediate headwinds from establishment Republicans hungry for the nomination.

Gov. Henry McMaster appointed Graham—sister of the late Sen. Lindsey Graham—to fill the seat temporarily, a move that drew swift criticism. The *Washington Examiner* noted Graham remains largely unknown to voters, while the *Fox News* report shows serious GOP challengers, including Rep. Russell Fry and Rep. Ralph Norman, are pressing ahead with their own campaigns despite Trump's endorsement.

**The conservative angle:** This race tests Trump's sway within the Republican Party. While Trump's endorsement carries weight in GOP primaries, South Carolina Republicans are signaling they won't simply fall in line—a sign that primary voters may prioritize experience and electability over a single endorsement, even from the former president.

Republicans argue that McMaster's quick appointment of an untested candidate undermines the democratic process and that voters deserve a genuine competitive primary. Conservatives want to see a nominee who can hold the seat and advance a conservative agenda, not a placeholder riding family connections.

**Watch next:** Whether Trump doubles down on Graham or pivots to back a frontrunner as the primary heats up—and whether South Carolina Republicans ultimately reject or embrace his choice.
