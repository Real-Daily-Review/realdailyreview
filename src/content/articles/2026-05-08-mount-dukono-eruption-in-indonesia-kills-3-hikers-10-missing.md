# Indonesia Volcano Erupts, Kills Three Hikers; 10 Missing

Mount Dukono on Indonesia's Halmahera island erupted early Wednesday, killing three hikers and leaving 10 missing, according to police.

The volcano spewed an ash cloud approximately six miles into the air, according to The Guardian. Two of the three dead are foreigners; the third is a resident of Ternate, an island in east Indonesia, North Halmahera district police chief Erlichson Pasaribu told Kompas TV.

The hikers were in the area despite Mount Dukono being closed to the public since April 17, according to Al Jazeera. Search and rescue operations are underway for the missing hikers.

Mount Dukono is one of Indonesia's most active volcanoes. The country sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire, a region prone to seismic and volcanic activity.
