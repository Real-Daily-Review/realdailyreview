# Ex-Bodyguard's Home Invasion Exposes Sinema's Security Lapses

Former Arizona Senator Kyrsten Sinema's ex-bodyguard and ex-boyfriend, Matthew Ammel, was arrested after allegedly breaking into her home and destroying property in a fit of rage (Just The News, Daily Wire). According to court documents, Ammel demanded Sinema post a video on X, threatening to damage one of her paintings every 10 minutes until she complied. When she refused, he allegedly threw approximately 22 paintings into her swimming pool and trashed her residence.

The incident raises uncomfortable questions about Sinema's judgment in hiring and maintaining close professional relationships. Ammel's ex-wife had already filed suit against Sinema earlier this year under North Carolina's "alienation of affection" law—a red flag that apparently went unheeded.

While this is primarily a personal matter, it underscores a broader conservative concern: government officials often operate with inadequate accountability and poor vetting practices. Sinema, who positioned herself as a moderate Democrat and independent voice, now faces public embarrassment stemming from what appears to be a serious lapse in personal security protocols and relationship management.
