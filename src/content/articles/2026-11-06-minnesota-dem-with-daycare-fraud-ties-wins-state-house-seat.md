# Minnesota Dem's Daycare Fraud Ties Raise Questions About Vetting Standards

A Minnesota Democrat with documented ties to daycare fraud just won a state House seat, raising conservative concerns about Democratic Party vetting and government accountability.

Abdi Daisane, owner of Blooming Kids Child Care Center in St. Cloud, won election to the Minnesota Legislature this week despite his daycare receiving millions in state and federal funding while facing multiple violations, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire. The center has been connected to a major fraud settlement.

Republicans argue the victory signals troubling priorities: a candidate with serious compliance and fraud questions advanced through Democratic ranks without apparent scrutiny. Conservatives point to the pattern as emblematic of progressive governance—where government money flows freely to connected operators with minimal oversight, and accountability takes a backseat to demographic representation.

The case echoes concerns about how federal and state subsidies for childcare and social services often lack rigorous fraud prevention. Colorado taxpayers should note the relevance: state-funded daycare programs and early-childhood initiatives operate under similar funding structures, raising questions about how thoroughly Colorado vets providers before distributing public dollars.

Democrats have not publicly addressed the fraud connections or explained their vetting process.
