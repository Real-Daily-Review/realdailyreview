# AOC Dismisses 'Defund Police' as a Joke—Conservatives Demand Real Accountability

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is trying to laugh off the left's most destructive policy blunder in a generation. In an ABC interview, the New York congresswoman characterized the radical "defund the police" movement—which devastated urban crime rates and public safety across America—as merely a "crazy, Woke 1" era that her side has now moved past (The Daily Wire).

But conservatives aren't buying the casual dismissal. National Review argues that if progressives have truly recognized their errors, they owe Americans far more than an "oops"—they owe accountability for the real damage inflicted on communities that embraced these policies.

The stakes matter for Colorado. Denver and other blue-led cities pushed police-defunding rhetoric that contributed to rising crime and business flight. Taxpayers footed the bill for the chaos, and residents—particularly in lower-income neighborhoods—paid the price in safety.

Ocasio-Cortez's framing reveals the left's playbook: radical experiments get rebranded as youthful mistakes when they fail, with no reckoning for the harm done or the public resources wasted. Colorado voters should watch whether national Democrats double down on this "move on, nothing to see here" approach or actually acknowledge the cost of progressive policy overreach.
