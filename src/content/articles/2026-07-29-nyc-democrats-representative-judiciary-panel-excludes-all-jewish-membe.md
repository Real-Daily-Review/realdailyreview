# NYC Democrat's Judiciary Panel Snubs Jewish Representation, Drawing Fire

A New York City progressive politician's claim to build a "truly representative" judiciary advisory panel rings hollow after Jewish bar associations flagged the complete absence of Jewish members from his 19-person slate.

Zohran Mamdani, a Democratic state senator, faced immediate backlash from four Jewish bar associations over the exclusion. "Excluding Jewish representation from this committee is neither reflective nor inclusive," the groups wrote, according to *Just The News*. "It follows a troubling pattern that has left many Jewish New Yorkers feeling increasingly marginalized."

The controversy underscores a broader tension within progressive politics: the gap between diversity rhetoric and actual inclusive practice. When Democrats champion "representation" while systematically excluding specific groups—in this case, a historically prominent legal community—it exposes the selective nature of their identity-politics framework.

The incident also highlights how left-wing activists and politicians often prioritize ideological alignment over genuine pluralism. Mamdani's panel composition suggests that "truly representative" means something narrower than the word implies—likely reflecting his own political coalition rather than the actual demographics and professional expertise of New York's legal community.
