# Colorado's 3rd District Tightens as Hurd Distances Himself from Trump

Rep. Jeff Hurd, once positioned for a comfortable reelection, is now scrambling to separate himself from President Trump as his 3rd Congressional District race unexpectedly tightens, according to reporting from the *Colorado Sun* and CPR News.

Hurd entered 2026 appearing to coast to victory. But Trump's declining national approval ratings have suddenly put the seat back in play—a sharp reversal that has forced the congressman into a defensive posture. Rather than run on his record or local issues, Hurd is now emphasizing his disagreements with the president, a tactical shift that underscores how toxic Trump's brand has become even in Republican-leaning districts.
