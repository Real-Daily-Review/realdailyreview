# Colorado College Offers Academic Credit for Left-Wing Activism—Taxpayers Footing the Bill

Colorado College is now awarding course credit for students engaged in progressive activism, including efforts to "shut down immigration detention centers," raising questions about whether higher education has abandoned academic rigor for ideological advocacy.

The designation comes as Colorado colleges pursue "Basic Needs Campus" and "Thriving Institution" status—labels that appear designed to address student hardship but, conservatives argue, often mask mission creep toward activist programming. Complete Colorado reports that students can earn credit for summer work on left-wing causes rather than traditional coursework.

Republicans and taxpayers should ask hard questions: Are Colorado's public and private colleges using tuition dollars and state funding to subsidize political organizing? Does offering academic credit for activism serve students' educational interests or the college's progressive agenda?

The Colorado College model reflects a broader trend in higher education where ideological conformity increasingly trumps intellectual diversity. When institutions reward activism over rigorous study, they betray their educational mission and justify skepticism from families and donors who expect colleges to teach critical thinking—not predetermined conclusions.

Watch whether Colorado legislators demand transparency on how colleges spend education dollars and whether accreditors hold institutions accountable for academic standards versus activist recruitment.
