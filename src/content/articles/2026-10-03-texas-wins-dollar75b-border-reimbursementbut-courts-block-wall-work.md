# Texas Secures $7.5B Border Reimbursement—While Courts Block Wall Construction

Texas Gov. Greg Abbott won a major federal victory this week: the Department of Homeland Security reimbursed the state $7.5 billion for its border security operations under Operation Lone Star, launched in March 2021 as the Biden administration's southern border crisis spiraled (Washington Examiner). Abbott celebrated the payment at the governor's mansion during Law Enforcement Appreciation Month, marking vindication for Texas taxpayers who footed the bill for federal enforcement failures.

But the win is incomplete. A federal judge simultaneously blocked border wall construction in a Texas national park, ruling that environmental plaintiffs were likely to succeed on their claims and barring the administration from using emergency waivers until litigation concludes (Just The News).

**The conservative takeaway:** Republicans argue Washington should never have forced states to bankroll border security in the first place—that's a federal responsibility. Abbott's reimbursement, while welcome, is a band-aid on a broken system. Meanwhile, activist courts continue tying the administration's hands on wall construction, even as cartels exploit gaps.

**Colorado angle:** If federal border enforcement remains broken, migrant flows and trafficking networks will continue pressuring Colorado's southern border and straining state resources.

**Watch next:** Whether the incoming administration accelerates wall construction and whether other border states demand similar reimbursements.
