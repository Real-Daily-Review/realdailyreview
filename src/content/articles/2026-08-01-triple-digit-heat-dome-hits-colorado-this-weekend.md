# Colorado Braces for Triple-Digit Heat Dome This Weekend

Another dangerous heat wave is bearing down on Colorado, with temperatures expected to crack 100 degrees across the Front Range through the weekend.

The National Weather Service has issued heat advisories for Sunday and Monday, with Denver and surrounding areas forecast to hit triple digits both days (9News Denver). CPR News reports temperatures climbing into the high hundreds across the state, with the heat dome expected to persist through the period.

This marks the latest in a series of extreme heat events Colorado has experienced. While weather patterns are natural and cyclical, the timing underscores why reliable, affordable energy infrastructure matters—air conditioning demand will spike, and the grid must deliver. Colorado's energy mix, including natural gas and coal plants alongside renewables, will be tested to keep the lights on and homes cool when residents need it most.

Coloradans should take standard precautions: stay hydrated, limit outdoor activity during peak heat hours, check on elderly neighbors, and never leave children or pets in vehicles. Those with air conditioning should use it freely—this is not the time for energy rationing that leaves families vulnerable.

Watch whether Colorado's grid operators can meet demand without rolling blackouts or emergency measures that might hint at future reliability concerns.
