# Texas Jury Convicts Karmelo Anthony in Stabbing Death of High School Athlete

A Texas jury has delivered justice for Austin Metcalf's family: Karmelo Anthony, 19, was found guilty of murder and sentenced to 35 years in prison for fatally stabbing the 17-year-old high school athlete at a track meet in Frisco (Breitbart, Just The News). Anthony faced up to life imprisonment; the jury's verdict came after a week-long trial in Collin County.

Metcalf's parents delivered powerful victim impact statements in court. "You should feel lucky you got 35 years because I've been given a life sentence without my son," his mother told Anthony (9News Denver). The conviction represents a decisive outcome in a case that drew national attention.

According to the Daily Caller, some protesters attempted to use the verdict as a flashpoint, with activists shouting inflammatory rhetoric at the courthouse. The Daily Wire reported Anthony broke down in tears when the sentence was announced.

The case underscores the reality that violent crime destroys families and communities—a truth that should inform Colorado's own criminal justice debates. As state lawmakers continue pushing soft-on-crime policies and bail reform, Coloradans should watch whether our courts and prosecutors will maintain the resolve to hold violent offenders accountable the way Texas did here.
