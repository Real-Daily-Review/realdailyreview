# American Airman's 50-Hour Iran Rescue: A Testament to Military Excellence Under Fire

A U.S. Air Force colonel's harrowing survival story after his F-15E Strike Eagle was shot down over Iran in April stands as a stark reminder of the threats our military faces and the caliber of personnel defending American interests abroad.

In his first public interview with "60 Minutes," the weapons systems officer—call sign "Bravo"—detailed his ordeal: ejecting at up to 100 mph, surviving a broken back and other injuries, and enduring 50 hours hunted by Iranian forces before a daring rescue extraction (Breitbart). Bravo described the experience as a "modern-day miracle," crediting prayer and exceptional military training for his survival (The Daily Wire).

The rescue underscores why America needs a strong, well-equipped military capable of operating in hostile territory. It also highlights the real dangers posed by adversaries like Iran—a regime that continues threatening U.S. forces and regional stability.
