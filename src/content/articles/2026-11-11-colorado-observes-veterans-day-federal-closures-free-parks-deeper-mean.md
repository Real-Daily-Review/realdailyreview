# Veterans Day in Colorado: Federal Closures, Free Parks, and a Reminder of Service

Colorado will observe Veterans Day with the typical federal shutdowns—USPS and most banks closed—while retail largely stays open for business as usual, according to 9News Denver. National parks across the state offer free admission to honor those who served.

Beyond the logistics, Veterans Day carries weight that transcends holiday schedules. Vietnam veterans featured in the documentary *Where We Once Stood* gathered in Washington, D.C., to share their stories with younger generations, underscoring what many in uniform want Americans to remember: the sacrifice and duty that defined their service (Washington Examiner). An 82nd Airborne veteran similarly emphasized that Veterans Day shouldn't be reduced to another calendar celebration—it's a moment to reflect on what service actually means, not just another excuse for sales and promotions (The Daily Wire).

For Colorado, this is a chance to honor the veterans in our communities—from those who returned home to build lives here to those still serving. Too often, government reduces military service to talking points while underfunding VA benefits and veteran support programs. Conservatives rightly argue that honoring veterans means more than a day off; it means ensuring those who served get the care, respect, and opportunity they've earned.
