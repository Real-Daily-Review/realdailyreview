# Trump DOJ Opens Gun Rights Restoration Path—Colorado Gun Owners Should Pay Attention

The Trump administration's Justice Department, under Attorney General Todd Blanche, has launched a new process allowing Americans barred from firearm ownership to petition for restoration of their Second Amendment rights (Fox News Politics, Just The News).

Under the new pathway, applicants must demonstrate the circumstances of their prohibition, prove they pose no public safety risk, and submit to DOJ evaluation of their record, reputation, and subsequent conduct. The move represents a sharp reversal from the Biden administration's posture on gun restrictions.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado has roughly 500,000 gun owners and a robust Second Amendment community. This restoration process could affect Coloradans who lost their rights through felony convictions, domestic violence restraining orders, or mental health adjudications—categories that have expanded under progressive state legislation in recent years.

Conservatives argue that many Americans barred from gun ownership decades ago no longer pose genuine threats, and that due process demands a mechanism for rights restoration. The Second Amendment is not a one-time revocation, they contend.

Democrats and gun-control advocates typically oppose broad restoration pathways, arguing that existing prohibitions protect public safety and that case-by-case reviews burden law enforcement.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado's Democratic legislature attempts to block or complicate the restoration process through state law—a likely flashpoint between state and federal authority.
