# Progressive Policy Failures Echo Warning for Colorado Democrats

Oregon's lone Republican congressman just handed Democrats a roadmap of their own failures—and Colorado progressives should take notes. Rep. Cliff Bentz told Fox News Digital that Oregon voters rejected the state's failed progressive agenda, citing crushing taxes and suffocating regulation as the culprits driving businesses and residents away (Fox News Politics).

The pattern repeats across blue states. Vermont's abandoned single-payer health insurance experiment from over a decade ago stands as a cautionary tale: ambitious government overreach that collapsed under its own weight (Washington Examiner). Democrats promised universal coverage; voters got higher costs and a dead program.

Colorado faces similar pressures. The state's Democratic supermajority has pursued aggressive tax hikes, regulatory expansion on energy development, and government-run healthcare schemes that echo Vermont's failed model. Conservatives argue these policies strangle business growth, inflate living costs for working families, and drive talent and investment to freer states.

Republicans counter that Oregon and Vermont prove voters eventually reject progressive experiments when they hit their wallets. The question: will Colorado voters learn from those mistakes before the damage compounds?

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado's 2024 legislative session brings renewed pushes for state-level healthcare mandates or energy regulations—and whether Republicans can effectively message the Oregon and Vermont cautionary tales to suburban swing voters.
