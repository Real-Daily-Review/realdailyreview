## Mississippi Shocker Tightens the Senate Map

Democrat Scott Colom defeated Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi's U.S. Senate race — a result that would have been unthinkable in a state this red just a cycle ago. The AP called the race after a prolonged ballot count. Republicans argue this is a wake-up call about candidate quality and turnout infrastructure, not a mandate for the left's agenda. For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: no seat is safe, and every race — from the courthouse to the Capitol — demands full engagement.

## Mesa County Deputy Disciplined for ICE Cooperation — Now Works for ICE

A former Mesa County Sheriff's deputy, Alexander Zwinck, who was disciplined and sued by Colorado Attorney General Phil Weiser after collaborating with ICE, has landed a job at ICE. Democrats and the Weiser AG office treated his cooperation with federal immigration enforcement as a scandal. Conservatives say it's the opposite — a law enforcement officer did the right thing and is being rewarded for it. This is what Colorado's sanctuary-state politics look like in practice: punish the deputies, protect the policy.

## SpaceX's Data Center Standoff Is a Preview of What's Coming Everywhere

SpaceX is pushing to build a $24 billion data center outside a Texas city in a move that would circumvent a local construction moratorium, drawing a notice of alleged violations. The dispute is already amplifying calls from the left to pause or restrict data center development more broadly. Colorado conservatives should pay close attention: the state's Front Range is prime territory for data center expansion, and the same regulatory instincts that would strangle this kind of private investment in Texas are alive and well in the Polis administration and the Democrat-controlled legislature.

## Nvidia Scandal Raises Alarms on China Tech Transfers

Reports indicate Nvidia's board has opened an independent investigation and is preparing to consider removing CEO Jensen Huang, as a broader scandal over Chinese efforts to obtain Nvidia's restricted technology comes into focus. Conservatives say the episode is a warning shot: the lengths China is going to in order to acquire American technology make the case for tighter export controls, not looser ones. Colorado's defense and aerospace sector — anchored along the Front Range — has a direct stake in how seriously Washington takes the threat of technology transfer to adversaries.

## United Airlines Blocks AI Booking Agents — and Markets Notice

United Airlines announced it will block personal AI assistants from its app, website, and travel agency channels, citing data security concerns and federal rules. The move sent Meta shares tumbling. Whether United's call is smart business or a short-sighted overreaction, the episode underscores how fast AI is reshaping commerce — and how quickly corporate and regulatory friction can follow. Colorado's growing tech economy will be navigating these same tensions between innovation and institutional resistance for years to come.
