# Apple and Intel in Preliminary Chip-Making Deal

Apple and Intel have reached a "preliminary agreement" for Intel to manufacture chips for Apple hardware, according to The Wall Street Journal. The deal marks a significant reversal after Apple's highly successful transition to its own Apple Silicon chips, which began phasing out Intel processors from Mac computers years ago.

The timing coincides with major changes at Intel, which appointed Lip-Bu Tan as CEO in March 2025 and saw the US government take a 10 percent stake in the company in August. Neither Apple nor Intel immediately responded to requests for comment.

The scope of the agreement remains unclear, according to WSJ reporting. It's still unknown which Apple products would receive Intel chips under the deal, or what role Intel would play in Apple's broader chip strategy.

The preliminary nature of the agreement suggests negotiations are ongoing and terms remain subject to change. Watch for official announcements from either company clarifying which product lines might be affected and the commercial terms of the arrangement.
