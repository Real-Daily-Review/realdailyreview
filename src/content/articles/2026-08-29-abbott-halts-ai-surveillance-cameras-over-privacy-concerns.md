# Texas Governor Halts Flock Camera Funding Over Privacy Overreach

Gov. Greg Abbott is pumping the brakes on government surveillance technology that even conservatives should question—a rare moment of restraint from state officials on privacy grounds.

Abbott ordered Texas agencies to pause funding for Flock Safety's AI-powered license plate readers after cities began canceling contracts over privacy concerns (Fox News Politics). The cameras, which use artificial intelligence to track vehicles across jurisdictions, have drawn fire from civil libertarians and privacy advocates who argue they enable mass surveillance without adequate oversight.

**The conservative case:** While law enforcement argues the cameras aid crime-fighting, Republicans increasingly recognize that unchecked government surveillance tools—even well-intentioned ones—threaten individual liberty and Fourth Amendment protections. Abbott's move reflects growing skepticism about AI-powered monitoring systems that can track citizens' movements without warrants or clear legal guardrails.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado cities and counties have deployed similar surveillance technology. Taxpayers and gun owners should ask whether their local police departments are using Flock cameras and whether city councils have approved adequate privacy safeguards and public oversight.

The broader lesson: technology that expands government's surveillance capacity moves fast; accountability and constitutional protections lag behind. Coloradans should demand their elected officials apply Abbott's caution to any AI-powered tracking systems before contracts lock in.
