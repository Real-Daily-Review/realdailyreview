# Washington Wildfire Crisis Exposes Failures of Progressive Forest Management

Record-breaking wildfires consuming over 200,000 acres across eastern Washington underscore the catastrophic consequences of decades of misguided environmental policy that has left forests choked with dangerous fuel loads.

Washington Gov. Bob Ferguson (D) declared a state of emergency Saturday as "unusually high temperatures and high winds" drove 12 large fires through Spokane County, forcing mass evacuations (Washington Examiner). Video footage shows the inferno advancing through residential neighborhoods at terrifying speed, with police racing toward flames as residents fled (New York Post).

Conservatives have long warned that radical environmentalists' opposition to controlled burns, timber harvesting, and forest thinning creates tinderboxes. Instead of managing forests actively, progressive states prioritize litigation and regulation that paralyze land managers. The result: catastrophic megafires that destroy homes, displace families, and emit far more carbon than responsible forestry ever would.

Colorado faces identical risks. Our forests are similarly overgrown and vulnerable to record fires—a direct consequence of federal and state policies that restrict logging and fuel reduction on public lands. While Democrats lecture about climate change, their own policies guarantee the next megafire will be worse.

Watch whether Washington's disaster prompts Colorado Democrats to finally embrace forest management solutions conservatives have championed for years—or whether ideology trumps common sense once again.
