# Democratic Party Fracturing Over Antisemitism, Socialist Influence, Union Leaders Say

Colorado Democrats should pay attention: the national party is tearing itself apart over antisemitism and anti-capitalist ideology, and it's exposing deep rifts that could reshape Colorado politics.

A CNN host recently suggested Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro "reads too Jewish" for the Democratic presidential nomination (The Daily Wire), a stunning admission of the party's tolerance for antisemitic gatekeeping. Meanwhile, union leaders—traditionally Democratic stalwarts—are abandoning ship. One union official announced his departure, citing the party's capture by Democratic Socialists of America and activist networks that prioritize ideological transformation over working people's interests (Washington Examiner).

Republicans argue this reveals what conservatives have long contended: the Democratic Party has been hijacked by anti-capitalist radicals hostile to business, energy development, and traditional American values. For Colorado, where union workers in energy, construction, and manufacturing still matter politically, this fracture could reshape electoral math in 2026 and beyond.

Democrats counter that these are isolated incidents and that the party remains committed to working families, though they've struggled to reconcile pro-Palestinian activism with Jewish voters' concerns.
