# Real Daily Review — The Brief
### Wednesday, July 1, 2026

Independence Day week arrives with Congress in recess, a Chinese fraudster heading to prison, and Colorado's own backyard dealing with arson and highway chaos. Here's what every Colorado conservative needs to know this morning.

---

## Congress Punts on SAVE America Act, Heads Home for the Fourth

House members dismissed themselves for the July 4 holiday with the SAVE America Act still unresolved — a frustrating pause for conservatives who want the legislation across the finish line. Republicans argue Democrats who voted against the bill are standing in the way of commonsense policy, and the holiday recess gives the opposition more time to organize against it. Colorado taxpayers and voters who sent conservatives to Washington to deliver results should be watching closely when the House reconvenes. Recess is not a substitute for results.

---

## Bipartisan Majority Swats Down Tlaib's War Powers Stunt

In a lopsided vote, Democrats joined Republicans to crush Rep. Rashida Tlaib's war powers resolution — a rare moment of bipartisan pushback against the progressive left's most radical foreign policy instincts. The White House and military leadership were clear: there are no U.S. combat forces in the relevant theater. Tlaib's resolution was less about constitutional principle and more about political theater. Colorado's military families and the veterans community, which numbers in the hundreds of thousands statewide, have no patience for congressional grandstanding that undermines American strength abroad.

## Chinese Billionaire Who Bought Political Access Gets 30 Years

Miles Guo — also known as Ho Wan Kwok and Guo Wengui — has been sentenced to 30 years in federal prison after being convicted of defrauding his followers of more than $1 billion. The case is a stark reminder that foreign influence operations targeting American political and media institutions are a genuine national security threat, not a talking point. Conservatives have long warned about Chinese infiltration of American civic life. This conviction should prompt a harder look at every foreign-linked donor, media operation, and political network operating on U.S. soil.

---

## New Mexico Man Accused of Arson Along Colorado's Highway 74

A New Mexico man has been arrested on suspicion of intentionally setting a brush fire along Highway 74 in Jefferson County, according to the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office. With Colorado's fire season already a source of anxiety for ranchers, rural homeowners, and energy operators across the Front Range and foothills, an alleged act of deliberate arson is infuriating — and should be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law. Colorado cannot afford to treat wildfire arson as anything less than a serious violent crime against communities and livelihoods.

---

## Wrong-Way Chase on I-70 Ends in Semi Crash, Diesel Spill

A high-speed wrong-way pursuit on I-70 near Denver ended in a multi-vehicle crash and a diesel fuel spill, according to the Colorado State Patrol. Details on the suspect and circumstances are still developing, but the incident is another reminder of the real public safety costs when law enforcement is forced into dangerous pursuits on Colorado's most critical freight and commuter corridor. Colorado businesses and truckers who depend on I-70 as an economic lifeline deserve roads that are safe and secure.

## What to Watch

Keep your eyes on Washington when the House returns from recess — the SAVE America Act needs to move, and every day of delay is a win for the opposition. In Colorado, watch for any escalation in wildfire activity as the July 4 holiday brings increased human traffic into fire-prone areas. And as the Guo sentencing reverberates, pay attention to whether Congress uses the moment to push stronger foreign influence disclosure requirements before the next election cycle.
