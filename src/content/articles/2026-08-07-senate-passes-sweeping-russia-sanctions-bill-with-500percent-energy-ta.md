# Senate Passes Russia Sanctions Bill—But Colorado Energy Workers Should Worry About Blowback

The Senate has approved a sweeping Russia sanctions package featuring tariffs up to 500% on Russian energy imports, a measure championed by the late Sen. Lindsey Graham and passed in his honor (Fox News, Daily Caller, Just The News).

The bill targets Moscow's oil and gas revenue and penalizes countries that continue buying Russian energy. While Republicans broadly support punishing Putin's regime, conservatives warn the measure could have unintended consequences for American energy markets and Colorado's oil and gas sector.

**The conservative concern:** Aggressive tariffs on Russian energy can disrupt global supply chains and potentially drive up domestic energy costs. Colorado's energy industry—already facing regulatory headwinds from the Biden-Harris administration—could face tighter margins if international energy markets tighten further. Republicans argue targeted sanctions are justified, but blanket tariffs risk harming American consumers and workers dependent on stable energy prices.

Democrats and the broader foreign-policy establishment frame the bill as necessary deterrence against authoritarian aggression. Graham, a longtime hawk on Russia and Ukraine, made the measure a priority before his unexpected death weeks ago.

**Watch next:** Whether the White House implements the tariffs aggressively or negotiates exemptions—and how Colorado's energy sector adapts to potential market volatility.
