# Democrats Target Hegseth as Partisan Distraction From Failed Agenda

Democrats are laying groundwork to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, a move conservatives say exposes the left's desperation to distract from economic failures and affordability crises hammering American families.

According to the Washington Examiner, Democrats view Hegseth as an easier political target than pursuing a third impeachment of President Trump—a strategy that would backfire spectacularly during a year when voters are focused on grocery prices, rent, and jobs. The left needs a scalp, and Hegseth fits the bill.

Republicans counter that impeaching the Defense Secretary over unproven allegations represents pure partisan theater. House Republicans, per the Daily Caller, are divided on the merits, with some like Rep. Thomas Massie questioning whether the case meets constitutional standards for removal.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado hosts significant military installations and defense contractors. An impeachment circus targeting the Defense Secretary could distract from legitimate oversight of Pentagon spending and readiness—issues that matter to Colorado's defense workforce and taxpayers footing the bill.

Conservatives argue this is exactly what Democrats do when their policies fail: manufacture outrage and pursue vendetta politics instead of governing. Coloradans should watch whether House Republicans hold the line on constitutional standards or cave to media pressure.
