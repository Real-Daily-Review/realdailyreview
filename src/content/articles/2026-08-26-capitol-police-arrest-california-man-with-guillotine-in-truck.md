# Capitol Police Arrest California Man With Guillotine in Truck Bed

U.S. Capitol Police arrested a 35-year-old California man after discovering a guillotine in his pickup truck near the Capitol building Tuesday, raising fresh questions about security threats to federal lawmakers and institutions.

Philan-Tam-Duy Le was taken into custody and charged with carrying a dangerous weapon, according to the Washington Examiner and New York Post. Capitol Police seized the device—a guillotine, historically used for executions by decapitation—from the truck bed after spotting the vehicle in the area near Capitol Hill and the Supreme Court.
