# Democrats Can't Escape Their Own Radical Record

Democrats are scrambling to distance themselves from the progressive agenda they championed just years ago—and voters shouldn't let them get away with it.

From Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez to Wisconsin gubernatorial candidate Francesca Hong, left-wing politicians are now walking back the radical rhetoric that defined 2019 to 2021 (Washington Examiner). The problem for Democrats: their words are permanently recorded. As National Review notes, if progressives have truly recognized errors in their approach, they owe Americans more than a casual "oops."

Republicans argue this retreat exposes the fundamental weakness of woke ideology—it was never popular with mainstream voters, only with activist bases and coastal elites. The Daily Wire points out that Democratic Socialists of America can't outrun their own rhetoric; every radical statement, every captured syllable, circles back to haunt them during election season.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. State Democrats have pushed similar progressive experiments—from energy mandates to regulatory overreach—often echoing the same national talking points now being quietly abandoned. If national Democrats are admitting their 2019-2021 agenda was a mistake, Colorado voters should ask: why are our state legislators still doubling down on it?

Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from state-level progressive policies as 2026 elections approach—and whether Republicans hold them accountable for the damage already done.
