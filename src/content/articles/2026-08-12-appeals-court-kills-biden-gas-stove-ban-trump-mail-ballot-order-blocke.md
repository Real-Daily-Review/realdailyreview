# Federal Judge Blocks Trump Mail-Ballot Order; Appeals Court Kills Biden Gas-Stove Ban

A federal judge in Boston has blocked the Trump administration from enforcing an executive order restricting mail-in voting rules ahead of the 2026 midterm elections, according to Fox News Politics. The ruling halts USPS enforcement of the order, dealing a setback to Trump's election-integrity agenda.

Meanwhile, a federal appeals court delivered a decisive win for energy freedom and limited government: a 3-0 ruling struck down the Biden administration's gas-stove ban, according to Just The News. The court sided with Louisiana, Mississippi, Montana, Nebraska, Tennessee, Texas, and Utah—states that challenged the regulatory overreach.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado consumers and small businesses dodged a bullet. The gas-stove rule would have driven up appliance costs and forced unnecessary replacements in thousands of Colorado homes and restaurants. Republicans argue the Biden ban exemplified progressive government intrusion into household choices; Democrats countered it was necessary for air quality and climate goals.

The mail-ballot ruling, however, complicates Republican efforts to tighten election security. Conservatives argue ballot integrity requires voter ID and signature verification; Democrats claim such measures suppress turnout.
