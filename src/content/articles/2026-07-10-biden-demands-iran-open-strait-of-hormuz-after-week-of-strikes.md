# U.S. Demands Iran Open Strait of Hormuz After Escalating Attacks

The Biden administration is demanding Iran publicly declare the Strait of Hormuz open to international shipping and halt attacks on commercial vessels following a week of military strikes between the two countries, according to the Washington Examiner and 9News Denver.

Senior U.S. officials told reporters Friday that Washington has made the demand explicit: Tehran must permit passage through the critical waterway and cease targeting merchant ships. The move follows escalating tensions and Iranian strikes that have threatened one of the world's most vital shipping lanes.

However, officials acknowledged internal Iranian power struggles have complicated efforts to reach and maintain any agreement, raising questions about whether Tehran can—or will—honor such a commitment.

**The Colorado angle:** Disruptions to Middle East shipping lanes drive up energy costs nationwide, directly hitting Colorado consumers and businesses at the pump and heating bill. Colorado's energy sector also faces uncertainty when geopolitical instability roils global oil markets.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether the Biden administration will enforce this demand with credible military deterrence or allow Iran to continue testing American resolve without consequence.
