# California Elects Biden-Era Democrat as Governor—Colorado Should Take Note

California voters have chosen Xavier Becerra, President Biden's former Health and Human Services Secretary, to succeed Gavin Newsom as governor, defeating Republican Steve Hilton 65% to 34% (The Daily Wire). The race centered on housing costs, homelessness, and Trump—issues that reveal the left's policy failures under Democratic governance.

Becerra's victory signals California's commitment to Biden-era progressive governance despite the state's well-documented crises: skyrocketing housing unaffordability, rampant homelessness, and business exodus. Republicans argue that Becerra's record as HHS secretary—overseeing pandemic lockdowns and vaccine mandates—demonstrates he will double down on the regulatory overreach that has crippled California's economy (Fox News Politics).

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't yet elected a full-blown progressive like Becerra statewide, Democrats control the governor's office and legislature. Becerra's win underscores how the left doubles down on failed policies—higher taxes, aggressive regulation, and government-centered solutions—rather than learning from California's cautionary tale. Colorado's energy sector, water rights, and business climate remain vulnerable to similar progressive experimentation.

Conservatives should watch whether Becerra's administration accelerates California's regulatory agenda on energy, housing, and healthcare—and whether Colorado Democrats cite his policies as a blueprint for further state intervention.
