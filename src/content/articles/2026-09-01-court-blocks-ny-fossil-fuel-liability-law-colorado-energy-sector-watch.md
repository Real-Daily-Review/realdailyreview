# Court Blocks New York's Fossil Fuel Liability Law—A Win for Energy Industry and Colorado's Economy

A court has struck down New York's 2024 law that would have made fossil fuel companies liable for climate change damages, dealing a significant blow to Democratic efforts to weaponize the courts against the energy sector (Just The News).

The ruling matters for Colorado. New York's law represented a coordinated left-wing strategy to impose massive liability on oil, gas, and coal companies through state courts—a model that could spread to Democratic-controlled legislatures nationwide, including pressure on Colorado's energy-dependent economy. Such laws would effectively criminalize fossil fuel production without congressional action, bypassing democratic processes.

Republicans and energy advocates argue these liability schemes are backdoor climate regulation designed to bankrupt energy companies and eliminate affordable power. The court's decision protects businesses from activist litigation that ignores established law and due process.

Meanwhile, climate activists point to a new study (9News) suggesting El Niño events have intensified over 40 years due to climate change, using weather patterns to justify aggressive climate policy. Conservatives counter that weather variability is natural and that draconian regulations harm working families and energy workers without meaningful climate impact.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado Democrats attempt similar fossil fuel liability legislation in the 2025 state session—and whether the energy industry mobilizes to block it before it reaches the courts.
