# Colorado Election Security Amendment Faces Media Bias, Conservative Critics Say

Colorado's Amendment 87—a ballot measure on election integrity—is being undermined by incomplete media coverage and legislative resistance, according to conservative observers at *Complete Colorado*. The amendment aims to tighten voting safeguards, but critics argue the state's left-leaning media establishment and Democratic-controlled legislature are obscuring its merits through selective framing.

The debate arrives as Colorado grapples with documented election vulnerabilities. The *Denver Post* reported that three fraudulent ballots were counted in 2024 after passing signature verification—a gap that Amendment 84 (a related security measure) could have prevented. Though Colorado's multi-layered verification system caught the fraud eventually, conservatives argue the cases demonstrate why tighter ballot security remains necessary.

Republicans contend that Amendment 87 would close loopholes and restore voter confidence in Colorado's election system. Democrats and progressive outlets counter that Colorado's current safeguards are robust and that stricter measures risk disenfranchising legitimate voters or creating unnecessary bureaucratic burden.

For Colorado taxpayers and election integrity advocates, the amendment represents a direct test of whether the state will prioritize ballot security or defer to progressive resistance. Watch whether the legislature's Democratic supermajority moves to actively oppose or undermine the measure before November's vote.
