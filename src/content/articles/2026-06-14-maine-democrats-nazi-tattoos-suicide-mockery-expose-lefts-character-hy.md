# Maine Democrat's Baggage Exposes Left's Moral Hypocrisy on Character

Democrats are scrambling to defend Maine Senate candidate Graham Platner after a cascade of damaging revelations—Nazi-inspired tattoos, mocking a teenager's suicide attempt on Reddit, and a troubling behavioral history—that would trigger immediate calls for resignation if a Republican faced similar scrutiny.

Hunter Biden himself waded into the mess, offering a weak "show me your phone" deflection while speaking with Governor Gavin Newsom (D-CA), essentially arguing that critics should reveal their own digital dirt before questioning Platner's conduct (The Daily Wire). The New York Post unearthed Reddit posts in which Platner cruelly mocked a teen's suicide attempt—a level of callousness that should disqualify any serious candidate.

Even the Denver Post's editorial board couldn't ignore the stench, noting that Democrats "cannot ignore Graham Platner's red flags and hold the moral high ground" while the party positions itself as the arbiter of decency and character standards.

The contrast is stark: Republicans face relentless media pressure over far lesser controversies, while Democrats nominate candidates with Nazi tattoos and a history of mocking suicide victims. This double standard reveals what conservatives have long argued—the left's moral posturing is selective theater, deployed only when politically convenient.

Watch whether national Democrats distance themselves from Platner or double down on defending him through the general election.
