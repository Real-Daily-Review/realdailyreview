# Senate Republicans Blast Trump Iran Deal as Giveaway Dwarfing Obama's Mistake

Senate Armed Services Chair Roger Wicker is hammering a Trump administration Iran deal, warning that a $300 billion reconstruction fund makes Obama's 2015 nuclear agreement look trivial by comparison.

Wicker (R-Miss.) says the memorandum of understanding "negotiates away the victories of Operation Epic Fury in ways that are completely out of step with the President's goals" (New York Post). The $300 billion commitment, Republicans argue, represents a massive capitulation that undercuts U.S. leverage and rewards Tehran despite its regional aggression and support for terror proxies.

The criticism signals fractures within GOP ranks on Trump's foreign policy. While the administration frames the deal as pragmatic, Senate Republicans are demanding accountability—particularly over the scale of financial commitments to a hostile regime.

Separately, Sen. Jim Banks (R-Ind.) secured an NDAA amendment reversing Biden-era DEI policies at the Pentagon, backing Defense Secretary Hegseth's push for merit-based military promotions (Fox News). Conservatives argue that ideological hiring undermines military readiness and unit cohesion.

**What to watch:** Whether Republican senators force votes on Iran deal provisions and whether the Pentagon fully implements merit-based promotion standards without bureaucratic resistance.
