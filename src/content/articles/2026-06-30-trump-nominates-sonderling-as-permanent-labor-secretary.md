# Trump Nominates Sonderling as Permanent Labor Secretary

President Trump has tapped acting Labor Secretary Keith Sonderling for the permanent role, pending Senate confirmation. Trump announced the pick on Truth Social, citing Sonderling's track record across both his administrations in advancing pro-worker, pro-business labor policy.

Sonderling's nomination signals Trump's intent to reshape the Labor Department away from the Biden administration's aggressive union-friendly stance and expansive regulatory agenda. During Biden's tenure, the department pursued aggressive organizing campaigns, stricter wage-and-hour enforcement, and expanded overtime rules—policies conservatives argue strangled small businesses and inflated labor costs.

For Colorado, the nomination matters. The state's energy sector, agriculture, and small-business community have chafed under Biden-era labor regulations. A Sonderling-led department focused on reducing red tape and protecting worker choice over forced unionization could ease hiring and operational costs for Colorado employers struggling with labor shortages and compliance burdens.

Republicans expect Sonderling to prioritize voluntary association over union coercion and to roll back regulations that conservatives say killed job creation. Democrats will likely oppose the nomination as anti-worker, though they lack the votes to block confirmation.

**Watch for:** Senate confirmation hearings and whether Sonderling moves quickly to rescind Biden-era labor rules that hit Colorado's energy and agricultural employers hardest.
