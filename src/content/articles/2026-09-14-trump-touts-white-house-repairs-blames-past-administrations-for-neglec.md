# Trump Highlights White House Restoration Work, Blames Past Administrations for Neglect

President Trump is touting extensive White House renovations, claiming the building was in severe disrepair when he took office. According to Fox News, Trump said the White House looked like a "dump" before his work began, with the exterior skin "practically falling off." Just The News reports Trump included photos of restoration efforts and blamed previous administrations for failing to maintain the historic structure.

The renovation includes construction of a new White House ballroom, part of a broader modernization effort Trump says was overdue. Trump's framing emphasizes fiscal stewardship—arguing that past presidents neglected basic maintenance on the nation's most iconic building.

**The conservative take:** Republicans see this as Trump taking responsibility for infrastructure that Democratic and Republican administrations alike had allowed to deteriorate. The message: competent management matters, even at the highest levels of government.

**What to watch:** Whether Democrats challenge Trump's characterization of the building's condition or the scope and cost of ongoing renovations—and whether media outlets scrutinize the total expenditure on these projects.
