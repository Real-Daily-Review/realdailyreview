# Pro-Palestinian Protester Booed at Yankee Stadium on 9/11 Anniversary

A man waving a Palestinian flag sprinted onto the field at Yankee Stadium at 9:11 p.m. Friday during the Yankees-Mets game, marking the 25th anniversary of the September 11 terrorist attacks. Security tackled him in center field as fans erupted in fury—chanting "USA" and pelting him with beer cans, according to the Daily Wire and Daily Caller.

The stunt drew immediate backlash from attendees who saw the timing and symbolism as deeply offensive. A firefighter near the 9/11 memorial at Ground Zero similarly confronted a pro-Palestinian agitator, delivering a sharp rebuke of the protest, Breitbart reported.

The incident underscores the sharp cultural divide over Middle East politics in America. While the left frames pro-Palestinian activism as legitimate political speech, conservatives argue that exploiting the anniversary of a terrorist attack that killed nearly 3,000 Americans—to advance a cause unrelated to 9/11—crosses a line from protest into deliberate provocation designed to inflame.
