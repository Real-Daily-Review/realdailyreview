# China's Exports Surge 14% Ahead of Trump Summit

China's exports jumped 14% in recent trade data, according to the Financial Times, demonstrating resilience in the country's manufacturing sector ahead of a planned summit between Xi Jinping and Donald Trump. The robust trade surplus suggests that U.S. tariffs have had limited impact on China's export performance so far.

The timing underscores the economic backdrop for high-level talks between the two leaders. China's strong export numbers could factor into negotiations over trade tensions that have defined U.S.-China relations in recent years.

Meanwhile, Germany also reported stronger-than-expected export growth, according to Deutsche Welle, signaling broader momentum in global trade despite ongoing economic uncertainties.
