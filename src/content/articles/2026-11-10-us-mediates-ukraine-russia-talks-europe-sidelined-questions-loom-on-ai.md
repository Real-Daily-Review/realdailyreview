# Ukraine-Russia Peace Talks Begin in Abu Dhabi as U.S. Mediates

As American resources and attention remain locked on the Ukraine-Russia conflict, the U.S. is now directly mediating trilateral peace talks in Abu Dhabi between Moscow and Kyiv—a shift that raises questions about how long Washington will bankroll this foreign war while domestic priorities languish.

Russia, Ukraine, and the United States held nearly four hours of talks this week (9News Denver), with Ukrainian President Zelensky calling the discussions "constructive and quite substantive" (New York Post). The talks mark a potential opening for negotiated settlement after years of grinding conflict.

However, the diplomatic process is already showing friction. European leaders, hoping to participate, were reportedly told by Russian officials to "sit there in the hotel" and wait in the lobby rather than join substantive discussions (Washington Examiner). The snub underscores Europe's diminished role as the U.S. takes the lead.

Meanwhile, South African authorities arrested nine foreign nationals aged 20 to 26 attempting to travel to Russia to join the war effort (Breitbart), illustrating the conflict's continued draw for foreign fighters despite peace overtures.

**What conservatives and Colorado taxpayers should watch:** whether these talks produce genuine de-escalation or simply extend U.S. military aid commitments indefinitely while Washington neglects domestic infrastructure, border security, and fiscal responsibility.
