# Chinese Bot Farm Wages Coordinated Attack on U.S. Data Center Energy Policy

A massive Chinese disinformation campaign is actively working to block American AI data center development—and Colorado should pay attention.

X uncovered approximately 200,000 inauthentic Chinese accounts running a coordinated influence operation against U.S. data centers, according to Fox News Politics. The bot farm flooded social media with claims that AI facilities drive up household electricity prices and strain the power grid. Some posts featured AI-generated cartoons portraying data-center operators as profiteers exploiting the public.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's energy sector and tech economy stand to benefit significantly from data center expansion. Foreign adversaries understand that blocking American AI infrastructure development weakens U.S. technological competitiveness—and they're willing to manipulate American public opinion to achieve it.

Conservatives argue this disinformation campaign exposes how hostile regimes exploit environmental and energy concerns to sabotage American economic growth. Rather than fall for Beijing's narrative, policymakers should recognize data centers as engines of innovation and job creation that Colorado needs.

Democrats have raised legitimate grid-capacity questions, though critics contend the left often amplifies worst-case scenarios about energy demand without acknowledging technological solutions or market-driven efficiency gains.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado regulators and lawmakers resist foreign pressure and embrace data center development as a genuine economic opportunity—or whether anti-energy sentiment, foreign-amplified or otherwise, continues blocking projects.
