# Front Range Rail Tax Faces Conservative Backlash Over Wasteful Spending and Influencer Campaign

Colorado voters are being asked to bankroll a multibillion-dollar Front Range rail project—and taxpayers are footing the bill for a slick "education" campaign designed to sell them on it.

Complete Colorado argues the Colorado Connector represents classic government waste: billions in spending for a train few Coloradans will actually ride. The outlet identifies 10 reasons to reject the tax, framing the choice as straightforward—either blow billions on rail infrastructure or let taxpayers keep their own money.

The pitch is getting expensive help. CPR News reports that millions in tax dollars have already funded a controversial campaign featuring paid Instagram influencers and targeted mailers promoting the project with "positive messages" about the Colorado Connector. That's government using public funds to convince the public to tax itself further—a practice conservatives say exemplifies how progressive projects bypass honest debate.

Republicans argue the rail project represents the kind of top-down, big-government thinking that ignores market realities and Colorado's actual transportation priorities. Supporters counter that regional rail reduces congestion and serves long-term growth, but skeptics note the project's cost-benefit analysis remains murky.

Watch whether voters reject this tax in November—and whether the rail authority's spending practices face legislative scrutiny afterward.
