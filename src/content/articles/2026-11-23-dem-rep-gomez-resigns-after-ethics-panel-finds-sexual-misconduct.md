# Democratic Rep. Gomez Resigns After Ethics Panel Finds Sexual Misconduct

California Democrat Jimmy Gomez is stepping down following a House Ethics Committee report finding "substantial evidence" he made unwelcome sexual advances toward a congressional staffer, according to Fox News and 9News Denver.

The committee's investigation concluded Gomez's conduct could implicate D.C. laws on criminal sexual assault and potentially constitute criminal misdemeanor sexual abuse, the Daily Caller reports. Rather than face further proceedings, Gomez announced his resignation.

The case underscores a persistent problem on Capitol Hill: members of Congress operating without meaningful accountability until ethics probes force their hand. While the misconduct alleged here is serious, conservatives have long argued that Congress polices itself poorly and that taxpayers foot the bill for settlements and cover-ups that would end careers in the private sector.

Democrats have positioned themselves as champions of workplace safety and #MeToo accountability—yet another member of their caucus has been caught engaging in exactly the behavior they claim to oppose. The contrast between progressive rhetoric and Democratic conduct on sexual misconduct remains stark.

Watch for whether Democratic leadership addresses the broader pattern of ethics violations among its members or simply moves on.
