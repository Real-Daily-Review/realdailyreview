# Trump Sets July 4, 2026 Deadline for EU Trade Deal

President Donald Trump has given the European Union until July 4, 2026—the United States' 250th birthday—to implement a trade agreement or face higher tariffs, according to the Washington Examiner. Trump said he had a "great call" with Ursula von der Leyen, president of the European Commission, and described the potential deal as "historic," according to Deutsche Welle.

The deadline represents a roughly 18-month window for EU negotiators to finalize terms. Trump has not detailed the specific tariffs that would apply if the deadline passes unmet, nor has he outlined the precise terms of the proposed agreement.

The move signals Trump's intent to use tariff threats as leverage in trade negotiations with the bloc. The EU has not yet publicly responded to the deadline or commented on the substance of discussions with Trump.

Watch for the EU's formal response and whether negotiators can narrow differences on key trade issues—including agricultural products, industrial goods, and digital services—within the timeframe.
