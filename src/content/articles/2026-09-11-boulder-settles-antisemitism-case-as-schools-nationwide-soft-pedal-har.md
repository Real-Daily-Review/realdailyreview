# Colorado Schools Must Guard Against Woke Revisionism on Antisemitism and Terror

Colorado parents should watch closely: while Boulder's school district settles a civil rights complaint over antisemitic harassment (CPR News), the broader national trend shows public schools increasingly tiptoeing around uncomfortable truths to avoid offending activist constituencies.

Boulder agreed to "concrete steps" to address antisemitism after the complaint, a tacit admission the problem festered under progressive leadership. Meanwhile, Virginia's Fairfax County schools face backlash over a sanitized 9/11 curriculum guide that names al Qaeda but warns teachers against "centering the extremists"—a euphemism for avoiding honest discussion of Islamic terrorism (Fox News). A Pentagon witness called the approach "vanilla."

The pattern is clear: left-leaning school boards prioritize identity politics and cultural sensitivity over factual history and student safety. Antisemitic bullying and terror attacks deserve unvarnished treatment in classrooms, not diluted narratives designed to spare feelings.

Colorado taxpayers fund these districts. Parents deserve curricula that teach history straight, protect all students from harassment regardless of religion, and don't sanitize evil in the name of progressive pieties.

Watch whether Colorado school boards—particularly in Boulder and Denver—adopt similar "sensitivity-first" frameworks that obscure rather than illuminate.
