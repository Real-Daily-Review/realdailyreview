# Colorado Conservatives Should Reject Moral Relativism on Hamas and Israel

Three years after October 7, 2023, the world has gained hard clarity about who our enemies are—and it's time Colorado conservatives stopped entertaining false equivalencies about terrorism.

The Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire both mark this grim anniversary by noting a simple truth: Hamas's founding charter and October 7 attacks revealed the organization's true intentions with unmistakable clarity. The Daily Wire emphasizes that three years of debate over ceasefires and international law have obscured the fundamental reality—Hamas is a terrorist organization committed to Israel's destruction, not a political actor open to negotiation.

This matters for Colorado. As progressive activists and some Democratic voices domestically push "ceasefire now" rhetoric and moral relativism between democracies and terror groups, Colorado voters should recognize the stakes. Conservatives argue that clarity about evil actors—whether Hamas abroad or hostile regimes—must precede any diplomatic framework. Surrendering that moral clarity in pursuit of false consensus weakens America's resolve and emboldens adversaries.

The left counters that humanitarian concerns and civilian casualties demand immediate ceasefires regardless of Hamas's stated goals. But conservatives say that position ignores how terrorist organizations exploit humanitarian pauses to rearm and regroup.

**Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly Democratic members—continues echoing ambiguous language about Israel's right to defend itself, or clarifies their position on Hamas's explicit genocidal charter.**
