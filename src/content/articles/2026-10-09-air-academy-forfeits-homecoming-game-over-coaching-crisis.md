# Air Academy Forfeits Homecoming Game Over Coaching Shortage Amid Harassment Claims

Academy School District 20's decision to forfeit Air Academy High School's homecoming football game against Lutheran High School exposes how Colorado schools are struggling to field basic athletic programs—and raises questions about what's really driving the coaching shortage.

District officials cited insufficient coaches willing to participate as the reason for the forfeit (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31). The move comes amid racial harassment allegations, though the district has not detailed the specific incidents or their connection to the coaching exodus.

The forfeit is a stark reminder that Colorado's public schools face real operational challenges. Whether those challenges stem from legitimate safety concerns, coaching burnout, or administrative mismanagement remains unclear from available reporting. What's certain: when a school can't field enough coaches for a homecoming game, something systemic is broken.

Conservatives should watch whether District 20 provides transparent accounting of what happened—and whether similar staffing crises are spreading across Colorado's school districts. Taxpayers deserve to know if schools are losing experienced coaches due to hostile work environments, inadequate support, or ideological pressures that discourage participation in student athletics.
