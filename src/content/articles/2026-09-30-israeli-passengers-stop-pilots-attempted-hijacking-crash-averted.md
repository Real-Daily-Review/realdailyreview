# Heroic Passengers Stop Pilot's Attempted Hijacking of Israel-Bound Flight

An Israeli passenger and crew member thwarted a catastrophic attack Wednesday when a Flydubai copilot stabbed a colleague and attempted to crash the aircraft carrying Israelis from Dubai to Tel Aviv, according to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

Flight data shows the plane plummeted from 33,000 feet to 17,000 feet in roughly one minute during the incident. The quick action by passengers—including one who stormed the cockpit while still covered in blood—prevented disaster. Netanyahu released a video statement praising the heroic intervention, calling it "a serious security incident" that could have ended tragically (The Daily Wire, Just The News).

All passengers and crew safely landed in Saudi Arabia for an emergency stop, then continued to Ben Gurion Airport in Israel on a rescue flight, where celebrations erupted upon arrival. One hero passenger, Ynav Sharir, met with Netanyahu after landing (New York Post).

The incident underscores the reality that ordinary citizens—not government alone—often stand between civilization and catastrophe. When seconds count and lives hang in the balance, armed, alert passengers make the difference. It's a lesson worth remembering in any discussion about security, self-defense, and individual responsibility.
