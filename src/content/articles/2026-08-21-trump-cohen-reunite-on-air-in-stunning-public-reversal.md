# Trump, Cohen Reunite on Air—But Colorado Voters Should Focus on What Matters Here

Former President Trump sat down with ex-fixer Michael Cohen on Cohen's radio show this week, marking a stunning public reconciliation between two men locked in bitter legal combat just months ago. According to the Washington Examiner, Trump praised Cohen for retracting his 2024 testimony in the hush money trial, calling the justice system "weaponized" against him.

The interview represents a dramatic reversal. Cohen previously testified against Trump both in court and before Congress, but has since walked back his trial testimony. Trump used the platform to argue the legal system was deployed as a political weapon—a claim conservatives have made repeatedly about prosecutions against the former president.

For Colorado Republicans, this spectacle underscores a broader concern: while national media obsesses over Trump's legal drama and personal feuds, Colorado faces real challenges—water scarcity, energy policy uncertainty, and tax-and-spend governance at the state level that directly hits working families and businesses.
