# Listeria-Tainted Soup Kits Pulled From Colorado Walmart Shelves

Federal health officials have ordered a recall of soup kits sold at Walmart stores across Colorado due to possible listeria contamination, a serious foodborne pathogen that poses particular risk to pregnant women, elderly consumers, and those with weakened immune systems.

The Maryland-based manufacturer initiated the nationwide recall after detecting the contamination risk. According to KDVR Fox31 Denver and 9News, consumers are being urged to discard the affected tomato bisque soup kits immediately rather than consume or donate them.

Listeria monocytogenes can cause severe illness and, in vulnerable populations, potentially fatal infections. The recall underscores ongoing food-safety challenges in the supply chain—a concern conservatives have long flagged as an argument *against* consolidating food production into fewer, larger facilities dependent on complex distribution networks. Local Colorado producers and smaller-scale operations often face less regulatory burden while maintaining closer quality control.

Walmart has not yet issued a public statement on the scope of affected inventory in Colorado stores, though the recall applies to products sold nationwide.

Coloradans should check their pantries immediately and monitor the FDA website for updated recall details and product lot numbers.
