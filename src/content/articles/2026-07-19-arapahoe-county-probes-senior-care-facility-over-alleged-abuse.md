# Arapahoe County Investigators Probe Assisted Living Facility Over Alleged Abuse

A criminal investigation into All Saints Home Care in Arapahoe County is underway, with the sheriff's office and district attorney asking former residents to come forward with allegations of mistreatment.

According to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31, authorities are actively seeking possible victims and witnesses connected to the facility. The scope and specific nature of the alleged violations remain under investigation, though the coordinated law enforcement response suggests serious concerns about resident safety and care standards.

This case highlights a critical gap in Colorado's regulatory oversight of senior care facilities. While state agencies oversee licensing and compliance, investigations like this one expose how vulnerable elderly residents can be when accountability mechanisms fail. Conservatives and taxpayers should demand transparency about what regulatory failures allowed alleged abuse to persist, and whether state oversight boards acted swiftly once complaints surfaced.

The investigation also raises questions about whether Colorado's regulations adequately protect seniors and hold facilities accountable—or whether bureaucratic red tape actually obscures problems rather than preventing them.
