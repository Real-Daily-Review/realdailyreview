# Colorado Democrats Push County Takeover While Missing Key Votes

Colorado Democrats are doubling down on a "county comeback" strategy to flip GOP-held seats at the local level—even as party members fumble high-stakes votes in Washington.

According to Complete Colorado, state Democrats have made their long-standing goal of infiltrating Republican county strongholds a priority, targeting local offices where conservative control has been strongest. The effort reflects Democrats' recognition that county-level power translates to control over land use, zoning, spending, and local regulations that directly affect Colorado families and businesses.

Meanwhile, the Denver Post reports that U.S. Rep. Brittany Pettersen (D-Colorado) was among Democrats who missed a critical Pete Hegseth impeachment vote, allowing Republicans to block the effort 211-204. Fellow Democrats criticized the absences, which handed Republicans a procedural win.

**The conservative concern:** While Democrats chase county seats—where they can expand local government reach and regulation—their own members are AWOL on marquee votes. Republicans argue this reveals Democratic priorities: win power locally to regulate and tax, but skip tough votes in Congress.

Coloradans should watch whether this county strategy translates to ballot-box gains in 2026, and whether Democratic control of counties means higher property taxes and more land-use restrictions for rural and suburban Colorado.
