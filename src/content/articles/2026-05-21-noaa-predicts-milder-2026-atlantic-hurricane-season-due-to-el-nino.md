# NOAA Forecasts Milder Atlantic Hurricane Season in 2026

The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration is predicting a less active Atlantic hurricane season for 2026, driven by a developing El Niño pattern expected to strengthen significantly, according to PBS NewsHour and CBS News.

El Niño conditions typically suppress Atlantic hurricane activity by increasing wind shear and altering atmospheric patterns that fuel storm formation. Federal and outside meteorologists say the phenomenon will likely dampen the upcoming season, though it won't eliminate the threat of potentially deadly storms entirely.

NOAA's forecast comes as the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season approaches. The agency has released its outlook detailing what residents and emergency officials should expect in the coming months.

While El Niño is expected to provide some relief from typical hurricane activity, meteorologists caution that even a milder season can still produce significant storms capable of causing serious damage and loss of life.
