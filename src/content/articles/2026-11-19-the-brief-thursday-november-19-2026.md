## Adams County Commissioners Eye Higher-Density Housing Rules

Adams County commissioners are taking up a housing density plan that would allow more duplexes, townhomes, and accessory dwelling units in urban areas, while also reducing minimum property setbacks. Republicans argue that local zoning decisions belong to communities and property owners — not to planners chasing density targets handed down from Denver or Washington. Colorado conservatives should watch whether this plan expands individual property rights or simply opens the door to top-down development mandates that override neighborhood character and homeowner preferences.
