# House Delays Iran War Powers Vote Ahead of Recess

House Republicans blocked a vote Thursday on a Democratic-led resolution that would have limited President Donald Trump's military authority against Iran, according to The Hill and the Washington Examiner.

The measure, which Democrats said was assured passage, was pulled using a procedural mechanism ahead of Congress's Memorial Day recess. Rep. Gregory Meeks (D-NY), ranking member of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, accused GOP leadership of deliberately delaying the vote to prevent its passage, the Washington Examiner reported.

House Republicans cited attendance issues as the reason for postponing the vote, according to The Hill.

The resolution reflects a fundamental disagreement over executive war powers. Trump and GOP leaders argue the president has unilateral authority to conduct military operations against Iran without congressional approval. Democrats contend that such actions require legislative authorization under the War Powers Resolution.

The war powers measure represents an ongoing tension between Congress and the executive branch over military decision-making. The vote's postponement leaves the question unresolved as lawmakers depart for the holiday recess.

Watch whether Democrats attempt to bring the resolution to a vote after the recess or if the procedural delay effectively shelves the measure for the remainder of the session.
