# AOC Inherits Socialist Lane as Democrats Eye 2028

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez has consolidated the far-left wing of the Democratic Party as Bernie Sanders exits the presidential stage, signaling that 2028 will pit moderate Democrats against an unapologetic socialist faction. According to the Washington Examiner, a University of New Hampshire poll shows Ocasio-Cortez commanding 22% support in early primary testing, ahead of Pete Buttigieg and other centrist contenders.

The shift matters for Colorado conservatives and taxpayers watching national Democratic direction. AOC's ascendancy represents the party's leftward lurch on energy policy, wealth redistribution, and government expansion—positions that directly threaten Colorado's oil and gas economy, agricultural interests, and middle-class families already squeezed by inflation and taxes.

Republicans argue that AOC's socialist platform—Medicare for All, the Green New Deal, massive tax hikes on businesses and earners—would devastate working Americans and Colorado's energy sector. Democrats counter that her policies address inequality and climate urgency, though they've struggled to explain how such spending avoids crushing middle-class wallets.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from or embrace AOC's 2028 candidacy as the primary season heats up—that alignment will reveal how far left the state party has drifted.
