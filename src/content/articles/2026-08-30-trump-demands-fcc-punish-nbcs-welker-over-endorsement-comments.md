# Trump Demands FCC Action Against NBC's Welker Over Endorsement Critique

President Trump is escalating his war with legacy media by demanding the Federal Communications Commission punish NBC News anchor Kristen Welker for on-air comments challenging his endorsement record.

Trump posted on Truth Social Sunday that Welker deserved "rebuke and punishment" from the FCC after she characterized his endorsements as having "mixed results" during "Meet the Press." The president's demand marks another flashpoint in his ongoing conflict with mainstream outlets he views as hostile to his agenda.

The move reflects Trump's willingness to weaponize regulatory agencies against perceived media enemies—a tactic that raises questions about press freedom and government overreach, even among some conservatives who share Trump's frustration with biased coverage.

**The Colorado angle:** While this is a national media story, it underscores the broader conservative complaint that establishment journalists routinely misrepresent Republican accomplishments and candidates. Colorado voters increasingly distrust network news; Trump's direct confrontation appeals to his base but risks reinforcing perceptions of authoritarian impulses that could hurt Republicans in swing states.

**Watch:** Whether the FCC entertains Trump's complaint and what precedent it sets for executive pressure on broadcasters.
