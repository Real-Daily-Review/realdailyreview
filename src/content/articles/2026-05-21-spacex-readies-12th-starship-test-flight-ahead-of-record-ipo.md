# SpaceX Prepares 12th Starship Test Flight as IPO Prospectus Released

SpaceX is preparing its 12th test flight of the Starship rocket as the company moves toward what could be a record initial public offering, according to CNBC.

The launch comes shortly after SpaceX released its IPO prospectus, setting the stage for the offering. In its filing, Elon Musk's space conglomerate acknowledged that its strategy is "highly dependent" on the massive rocket's success, according to the Financial Times.

The timing underscores the high stakes surrounding both the test program and the company's public market debut. Starship represents SpaceX's next-generation fully reusable launch system designed for deep space missions, including lunar and Mars objectives.

The prospectus disclosure signals investor focus will likely remain on Starship's development trajectory and test results as the company pursues its IPO. Each successive test flight provides data on the rocket's capabilities and readiness for operational missions.
