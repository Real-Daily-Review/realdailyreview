# Capitol Testimony Blitz: Trump Officials Face Hill Scrutiny on Economy, Security, and Aviation

Trump administration officials are taking the hot seat on Capitol Hill this week—FBI Director Kash Patel, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, and FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford all testifying before House and Senate committees on economy, national security, and federal operations.

Bessent faces questioning on economic policy and the international financial system before the House Financial Services Committee, while Patel defends the FBI's direction before the Senate Judiciary Committee (Just The News). Meanwhile, FAA head Bryan Bedford is discussing a $12.5 billion air traffic control overhaul, including software reforms, following a government watchdog's report on funding shortfalls (Washington Examiner).

For Colorado, the FAA testimony matters: Denver International Airport is a major hub, and any overhaul of air traffic control systems directly affects the state's aviation infrastructure and economic connectivity. The Treasury testimony also carries weight—Bessent's economic testimony will signal the administration's stance on inflation, growth, and fiscal policy that ripple through Colorado's energy sector and small-business economy.

Republicans are using these hearings to advance Trump's deregulation agenda and fiscal discipline. Democrats will likely press on government accountability and funding adequacy.

**Watch whether these officials commit to specific timelines and cost controls on the FAA overhaul—Colorado taxpayers deserve transparency on how federal dollars are spent.**
