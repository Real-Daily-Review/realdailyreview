# Ship Attacked in Hormuz as Trump Asserts U.S. Control, Iran Escalates

The Trump administration's aggressive posture in the Strait of Hormuz is being tested as Tehran ramps up military threats and an unidentified projectile damaged a commercial vessel early Tuesday, killing a crew member.

President Trump has doubled down on declaring the strategic waterway U.S. territory, asserting Washington maintains "total control" of the chokepoint through which roughly one-fifth of global oil passes. The move reflects a hardline approach to Iran after a 60-day peace negotiation window expired (Breitbart).

Iran has responded with threats to break what Tehran calls a U.S. naval blockade and signaled a shift toward more offensive military operations. The attack on the ship's engine room underscores the real-world stakes of escalating tensions (9News Denver).

**The Colorado angle:** Energy markets tied to Middle East stability directly affect Colorado's oil and gas sector. Sustained conflict or supply disruptions could reshape domestic energy policy and investment—potentially benefiting Colorado producers but also risking economic volatility.

Conservatives argue Trump's assertive stance deters further Iranian aggression and protects American interests and global commerce. Critics worry the rhetoric could provoke unintended escalation.
