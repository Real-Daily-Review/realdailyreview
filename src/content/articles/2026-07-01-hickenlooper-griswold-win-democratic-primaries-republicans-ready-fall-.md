# Colorado Democrats Nominate Hickenlooper, Griswold in Primary Sweep—Republicans Set for Fall Fights

Colorado Democrats solidified their ticket Tuesday, with incumbent Sen. John Hickenlooper defeating challenger Julie Gonzales in the primary (CPR News). Hickenlooper declared victory a sign of a Democratic sweep coming in November—a claim Republicans will test aggressively in the general election.

Elsewhere, current Secretary of State Jena Griswold won the Democratic primary for Attorney General (9News), while Jeffco clerk Amanda Gonzalez secured the Democratic nod for Secretary of State unopposed (CPR News). In the 3rd Congressional District, Democrat Dwayne Romero edged fellow Aspenite Alex Kelloff with 55.2% of the vote (Colorado Sun), setting up a general election matchup against Republican incumbent Jeff Hurd in a district spanning Craig to Durango (CPR News).

Republicans counter that Hickenlooper's primary victory masks vulnerability on inflation, energy policy, and government overreach—issues that resonate in rural Colorado counties. The 3rd District race will test whether Republicans can hold ground in a region dependent on energy development and ranching, where Biden-era regulations have sparked backlash.

In the 8th District, Democrat Manny Rutinel won his primary and will face Republican incumbent Gabe Evans in another competitive race (9News).
