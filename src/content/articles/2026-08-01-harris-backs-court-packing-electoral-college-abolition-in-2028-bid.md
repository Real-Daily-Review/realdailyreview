# Harris Pushes Court-Packing, Electoral College Abolition—Signals Radical 2028 Agenda

Kamala Harris is openly campaigning on dismantling constitutional guardrails, endorsing Supreme Court expansion and abolishing the Electoral College at the National Urban League Conference as she weighs a 2028 presidential run (Fox News).

The move signals Harris intends to run hard left. Court expansion—packing the bench with liberal justices—has long been a progressive priority rejected by Republicans as a naked power grab that would destroy judicial independence. Abolishing the Electoral College would eliminate the federalist system that gives smaller states and rural America meaningful representation, concentrating power in coastal urban centers.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's electoral votes and influence in presidential contests would evaporate under Harris's scheme. Rural Colorado counties—energy-producing regions dependent on oil, gas, and mining—would lose what little leverage they retain against progressive urban supermajorities.

Notably, Harris dodged naming Joe Biden among America's "most effective presidents," instead citing FDR, JFK, and LBJ (Breitbart, Daily Caller)—a telling snub of her former boss that underscores her pivot away from the Biden record.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Harris's radical constitutional agenda becomes the Democratic Party's official 2028 platform, and how Colorado Democrats respond to court-packing and Electoral College abolition proposals.
