# Supreme Court Ruling Clears Path for Data Center Boom—and Colorado Could Benefit

A three-year-old Supreme Court decision narrowing the EPA's regulatory reach is finally paying dividends for American builders, unblocking a data center construction surge that progressive activists had been working overtime to stop.

According to the Washington Examiner, socialists led by Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) and Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) have pushed hard to halt data center projects nationwide. But the high court's decision made it significantly harder for environmental activists to weaponize federal regulations against construction, clearing the way for rapid expansion in the sector.

**The Colorado angle:** Data centers represent high-value infrastructure investment, job creation, and tax revenue for Colorado counties and municipalities. The ruling removes a major regulatory barrier that progressive groups have exploited to delay or kill projects—a tactic that had stalled development across the West.

Conservatives argue this is exactly what happens when courts rein in unelected bureaucrats at the EPA. The Left counters that weakening environmental review endangers ecosystems and climate goals, but Republicans point out that blocking infrastructure in the name of climate activism simply pushes investment—and jobs—to other countries with weaker standards.

Watch for Colorado environmental groups to challenge incoming data center proposals through state-level permitting and water-rights disputes, the next frontier for project obstruction.
