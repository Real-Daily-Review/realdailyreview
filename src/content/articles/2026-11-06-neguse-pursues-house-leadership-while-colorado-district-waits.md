# Colorado Democrat Neguse Seeks House Leadership Post—But What About His District?

Rep. Joe Neguse is running unopposed for assistant speaker of the House, a move that would make him the youngest lawmaker to hold the No. 4 Democratic leadership position (CPR News). The Boulder-based congressman sees the role as a springboard for continued ascent within the Democratic caucus.

Here's what conservatives should note: Neguse's climb up the Democratic leadership ladder raises questions about his focus. While he pursues national party power, Colorado's 2nd Congressional District—which includes Boulder, parts of Denver, and surrounding areas—deserves representation from someone whose primary job is serving constituents, not climbing the leadership ladder in Washington.

Republicans argue that career politicians prioritize party advancement over local accountability. Neguse's unopposed bid suggests limited internal Democratic challenge, but Colorado voters should ask whether their congressman is spending more time on Democratic strategy than on the kitchen-table issues affecting his district: housing costs, energy policy, and water security.

The assistant speaker role, while prestigious within Democratic circles, is fundamentally a party management job—not a position that brings resources or influence back to Colorado.

Watch whether Neguse's expanded leadership duties affect his constituent services and voting record on energy and water issues critical to Colorado's economy.
