# Colorado Conservatives Should Reject Campus Antisemitism in Their Own Backyard

Three years after October 7, 2023, American college campuses remain battlegrounds for anti-Israel activism—a trend Colorado universities cannot ignore.

Since Hamas's attack on Israel, progressive activists have weaponized U.S. campuses to advance anti-Israel causes, according to National Review. The movement has metastasized from coastal elites into mainstream academic life, where students face pressure to adopt anti-Western, anti-Israel positions as the price of social acceptance.

**The conservative argument:** Clarity matters. As the Washington Examiner notes, three years of debate have revealed hard truths about the actors involved—Hamas's founding charter explicitly commits to Israel's destruction, yet American progressives routinely sanitize this reality. The Daily Wire emphasizes that the left has spent years parsing "international law" and "ceasefire logistics" while ignoring Hamas's own stated objectives. This rhetorical gymnastics serves no one but those hostile to Western civilization.

**Colorado's stake:** University of Colorado, Colorado State, and other state-funded institutions have hosted pro-Hamas demonstrations and antisemitic rhetoric. Colorado taxpayers fund these campuses. Conservative parents and donors should demand that university leadership enforce actual free speech standards—not the progressive version that silences pro-Israel voices while amplifying antisemitic agitation.
