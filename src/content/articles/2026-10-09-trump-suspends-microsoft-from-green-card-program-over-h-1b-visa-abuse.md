# Trump Admin Suspends Microsoft From Green Card Program Over H-1B Abuse

The Trump administration is cracking down on what it calls systematic visa fraud, suspending Microsoft and seven other tech giants from federal green-card programs over allegations they've displaced American workers with cheaper foreign labor.

Vice President JD Vance and the Department of Labor announced the enforcement action Thursday, citing evidence that companies like Microsoft laid off American workers while simultaneously importing foreign H-1B visa holders—effectively replacing U.S. employees with what critics call "indentured servants" willing to work for less. According to the Daily Caller, the administration argues that "for every worker that Microsoft laid off, they replaced that worker with one and a half foreign" visa workers.

The crackdown extends beyond tech: the White House also launched investigations into nine major universities over their use of J-1 visas, signaling a broader assault on what conservatives view as systematic labor-market manipulation by elites.

**The Colorado angle:** Tech companies operating in Colorado's growing software and aerospace sectors may face similar scrutiny. If enforcement tightens visa access, Colorado employers relying on H-1B workers could face pressure to hire domestically—potentially raising labor costs but creating opportunities for American workers.

Conservatives argue this targets a rigged system that undercuts American wages; the tech industry will likely counter that visa restrictions harm innovation and competitiveness.
