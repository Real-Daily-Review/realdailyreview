# Colorado's New Gun-Control Law Takes Effect Saturday—Here's What Owners Need to Know

Colorado's latest assault on Second Amendment rights kicks in this weekend. Senate Bill 003, effective Saturday, August 1, imposes mandatory training requirements before purchasing certain semiautomatic firearms—a backdoor way to restrict gun ownership under the guise of "safety" (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

The law requires buyers to complete a training course before taking possession of regulated semiautomatic weapons. Supporters—including some law enforcement and progressive advocates—frame this as a commonsense measure. **Republicans and gun-rights advocates counter that the requirement amounts to an unconstitutional tax on exercising a fundamental right.** Training mandates create barriers for working Coloradans and low-income residents who can least afford additional costs and bureaucratic hurdles.

Meanwhile, Colorado lawmakers also fast-tracked a separate 72-hour social media warrant law taking effect in August, backed by law enforcement, healthcare providers, and youth advocates (9News Denver). While framed as protecting minors, conservatives worry about government overreach into digital privacy and the precedent of expedited warrant processes.

**Colorado gun owners should prepare for compliance and monitor whether these restrictions survive legal challenge—and whether the legislature moves to expand them further.**
