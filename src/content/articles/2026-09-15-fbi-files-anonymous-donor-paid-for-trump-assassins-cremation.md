# Mystery Donor Funded Cremation of Trump Assassin—FBI Files Raise Questions

Federal investigators have uncovered that an anonymous donor paid for the cremation of Thomas Crooks, the would-be assassin who attempted to kill former President Donald Trump, according to FBI records reviewed by the New York Post and reported by the Daily Wire and Daily Caller.

The revelation emerged from FBI interview notes with Matthew Crooks, the shooter's father. The files describe Crooks as emotionally detached since childhood—"emotionless," per Just The News—yet academically gifted, scoring 1400 on his first SAT attempt without studying and later reaching a near-perfect 1500.

The identity of the anonymous donor remains unknown, fueling questions about who bore the costs and why. The FBI's notation offers no explanation for the donor's motivation or relationship to the Crooks family.

This disclosure adds another layer of mystery to an already scrutinized assassination attempt. Conservatives and Republicans have pressed for full transparency into the circumstances surrounding the shooting and any potential connections or financial arrangements that preceded it.

Taxpayers and watchdogs should demand complete disclosure of the FBI's investigative findings and clarification on the donor's identity and motives.
