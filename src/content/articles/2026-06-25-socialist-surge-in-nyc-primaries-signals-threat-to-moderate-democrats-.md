# Socialist Wave Threatens to Expand Beyond NYC—Colorado Conservatives Should Take Note

Far-left candidates backed by radical Democratic operatives just swept New York primaries, defeating mainstream incumbents and signaling a troubling shift in the party's direction that could ripple westward to Colorado.

Three socialist-aligned candidates—Darializa Avila Chevalier, Brad Lander, and Claire Valdez—won Democratic primary races with support from NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani, according to Fox News Politics. Their victories came despite opposition from House Democratic leadership, including Hakeem Jeffries, revealing a widening rift between the party establishment and its activist base.

National Review warns these candidates represent a genuine ideological threat, not mere political theater. The magazine characterizes their rhetoric as fundamentally hostile to American constitutional principles and free-market values.

**The Colorado angle:** While these races occurred in New York, the same activist networks and funding mechanisms that powered these victories have already targeted Colorado races. Progressive primary challengers backed by socialist organizations have run against moderate Democrats in Colorado legislative races, and the playbook is identical—mobilize young voters, attack incumbents as insufficiently radical, and push candidates further left on taxes, energy policy, and government spending.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado Democrats face similar primary pressure from their left flank in 2026, and whether national socialist organizations begin targeting Colorado House and Senate races.
