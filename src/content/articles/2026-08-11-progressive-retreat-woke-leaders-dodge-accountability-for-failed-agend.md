# Progressive Darlings Face Reckoning Over Failed Woke Agenda

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her progressive allies are quietly distancing themselves from the radical policies that defined their movement—but they're offering no real accountability to the Americans who paid the price.

According to *National Review*, the progressive left has begun acknowledging errors in their approach, yet refuses to offer genuine contrition. "If Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her friends have recognized the errors of their ways, they owe the American people more than an 'oops,'" the outlet argues. The retreat signals that even Democrats recognize the unpopularity of their agenda, but without admitting fault, they risk simply rebranding the same failed policies under new language.

*Daily Caller* columnist Duke raises the fundamental question: what exactly was wrong with the original woke agenda that now requires a version 2.0? The implication cuts deep—if progressives can't articulate what went wrong, how can voters trust course correction?

For Colorado, this matters. Woke policies have driven energy regulations that threaten our oil and gas workers, education mandates that bypass parental rights, and spending sprees that inflate costs for families already squeezed by inflation. When progressive politicians rebrand without reckoning, Coloradans should demand specifics: which policies are actually being abandoned, and which are simply being repackaged?

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from the progressive agenda ahead of 2026—or double down.
