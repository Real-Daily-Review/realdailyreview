# Tesla Recalls 1.2M Vehicles in China; Waymo Suspends Freeway Service Over Safety Issues

Tesla is recalling 1.2 million China-made and imported vehicles to address software issues, according to the Wall Street Journal.

The recall represents a significant safety action in Tesla's largest market outside the United States. The company did not specify the nature of the software defects or their potential risks, though the recall encompasses both domestically produced and imported models.

Separately, Waymo has suspended freeway driving across all U.S. markets due to safety concerns related to construction zones, according to The Verge. The autonomous vehicle company also paused service in Atlanta and San Antonio over issues with flooded roads. The move came after Waymo robotaxis were spotted driving through flooded areas at elevated speeds in Texas.

The suspensions affect customer experience immediately—trips that previously used freeways now route through local roads, extending travel times. Waymo has not announced a timeline for restoring freeway service.

Both actions underscore mounting scrutiny of autonomous and semi-autonomous vehicle safety systems. Tesla has not disclosed when repairs will be completed or whether the software issue affected vehicle performance or user safety.
