# U.S. Must Hold Firm Against Russian Deception as Ukraine Talks Loom

As diplomatic negotiations with Russia edge closer, Washington must reject Moscow's battlefield narratives and propaganda—a lesson Colorado's defense contractors and military-connected workforce should monitor closely.

Russia is pushing a false story of inevitable victory while committing documented atrocities in Ukraine. According to the *National Review*, the evidence is clear: Russia's battlefield position is not the foregone conclusion Moscow claims, and capitulating to Russian framing would betray both Ukrainian civilians and American credibility.

The *Washington Examiner* documents how Russia's own Orthodox Church leadership has been co-opted into the propaganda machine. Patriarch Kirill, head of the Russian Orthodox Church, has blessed Russian military actions and mischaracterized Ukrainian defensive strikes as "cynical acts" by Kyiv—a stunning corruption of religious authority for geopolitical ends.

Republicans argue that any negotiated settlement must be built on facts, not Russian disinformation. Surrendering territory or accepting Moscow's false narratives would reward aggression and embolden future adversaries. The Biden-Harris administration's inconsistent messaging on Ukraine has already muddied American resolve; a Trump administration must enter talks with steel-backed clarity.

For Colorado, this matters: defense spending tied to NATO commitments and weapons production supports thousands of state jobs. Weakness invites Chinese aggression over Taiwan and destabilizes global markets Colorado businesses depend on.

Watch whether the incoming administration will demand verifiable Russian concessions or fold to diplomatic theater.
