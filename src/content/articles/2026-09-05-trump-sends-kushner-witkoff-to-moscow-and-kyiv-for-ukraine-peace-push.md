# Trump Envoys Head to Moscow and Kyiv in Latest Push for Ukraine Peace Deal

President Trump is doubling down on diplomatic outreach to end the Ukraine war, sending Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff to meet Putin this weekend before traveling to Kyiv—but conservatives are split on whether the mission strengthens America's hand or plays into Russian propaganda.

Kushner and Witkoff, who previously visited Moscow in January, are tasked with exploring whether a negotiated settlement is possible after four-and-a-half years of conflict (Just The News). This marks their first Kyiv trip under Trump's second term.

The move reflects Trump's stated priority of ending the war quickly. However, the Washington Examiner's opinion section warns that meeting Putin again risks lending credibility to what it calls the Russian leader's "false sincerity" on peace—essentially enabling Kremlin propaganda while Ukraine remains skeptical of Moscow's intentions.

For Colorado, the outcome matters: prolonged instability in Europe affects energy markets, defense contractor operations, and broader geopolitical stability that impacts the state's economy and military installations.

Conservatives should watch whether these talks produce concrete movement toward a settlement or simply become another diplomatic theater that leaves Ukraine vulnerable and Russia emboldened.
