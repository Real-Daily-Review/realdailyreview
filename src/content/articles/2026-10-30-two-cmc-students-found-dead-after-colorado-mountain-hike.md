# Colorado Mountain College Students Found Dead After Mountain Search

Two Colorado Mountain College students reported missing after attempting an ice climb in the high country have been found dead, the Chaffee County Sheriff's Office confirmed following a multi-day search operation.

The students, from the Leadville Campus, failed to return from their planned hike, prompting staff to alert authorities. Search and rescue teams mobilized across the challenging alpine terrain to locate them.

The incident underscores the serious risks posed by Colorado's unforgiving mountain environment, particularly during winter conditions when ice climbing and high-altitude hiking demand expert preparation and equipment. Outdoor recreation in Colorado's backcountry—while a draw for residents and visitors—requires respect for weather, elevation, and terrain that can turn deadly without proper planning.

The Chaffee County Sheriff's Office has not yet released details on the exact circumstances of the deaths or whether safety lapses played a role. Colorado Mountain College has not issued a public statement on whether the expedition followed institutional safety protocols or whether additional safeguards for student outdoor activities will be reviewed.

Coloradans should watch for the sheriff's final investigation report and any policy changes CMC implements regarding student backcountry activities.
