# Supreme Court Blocks Trump's Birthright Citizenship Order in Major Immigration Setback

The Supreme Court dealt President Trump a stinging 6-3 defeat, rejecting his executive order to end birthright citizenship and reaffirming the 14th Amendment guarantee of automatic U.S. citizenship for children born on American soil (Fox News Politics).

Three conservative justices sided with the court's liberal wing, preserving what immigrant advocates call a cornerstone protection for future generations (CPR News). Trump responded sharply, calling the decision "too bad for our Country" (9News Denver).

**The conservative case against the ruling:** Justice Clarence Thomas issued a forceful dissent, arguing the decision "devalues American citizenship" and questioning whether it "will stand the test of time" (Daily Caller). Republicans contend birthright citizenship incentivizes illegal immigration and undermines sovereignty—a core Trump administration priority that now faces a legal dead end.

**Colorado angle:** The ruling affects Colorado's growing immigrant communities and sanctuary-city policies in Denver and Boulder, while frustrating conservatives who view birthright citizenship as a magnet for illegal border crossings and chain migration.

**What to watch:** Whether Republicans pursue a constitutional amendment—the only remaining path to overturn birthright citizenship—or focus immigration enforcement efforts elsewhere.
