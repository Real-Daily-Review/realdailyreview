# Tennessee GOP Approves New Congressional Map, Dismantling Majority-Black District

Tennessee Republicans on Thursday approved a new congressional map that redraws a majority-Black district in Memphis, according to PBS NewsHour. The move came after lawmakers voted to overturn the state's decades-old ban on mid-decade redistricting, according to CBS News.

The map reshapes the Memphis district to Republican advantage as part of what PBS describes as President Donald Trump's strategy to maintain GOP control of the House ahead of November midterm elections. Democrats protested the vote, locking arms in the chamber, according to PBS NewsHour.

The redistricting effort is part of a broader multistate Republican push that followed the Supreme Court's reinterpretation of the Voting Rights Act, according to the Washington Examiner. The legislature first repealed the state law preventing redistricting between censuses before approving the new map.

Republicans control the Tennessee legislature, enabling them to pass the measure without Democratic support. The new map will govern House races in the state going forward.

Watch for legal challenges to the map, which voting rights advocates may contest on grounds that it dilutes Black voting power in Memphis.
