# Democrats' Catch-and-Release Policies Enable Crimes Against Americans

Democratic sanctuary policies are creating a revolving door for criminal illegal immigrants, leaving American citizens vulnerable. A 62-year-old woman, Jin Yu, was murdered in her New York City home—allegedly by an illegal immigrant who should have been deported (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, Florida authorities arrested four illegal immigrants in a slaughterhouse torture case involving animal abuse (Daily Caller).

Republicans argue that Democratic politicians prioritize protecting illegal immigrants over public safety. "These policies prove Democrats value open borders more than American lives," conservatives say, pointing to sanctuary city rules that prevent local law enforcement from cooperating with federal immigration authorities.

Democrats counter that such cases are statistical outliers and that immigrants—documented or not—commit crimes at lower rates than native-born citizens. They defend sanctuary policies as protecting vulnerable populations from deportation over minor infractions.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's major cities, including Denver and Boulder, operate as de facto sanctuaries, limiting cooperation with ICE. This means Colorado taxpayers fund services for illegal immigrants while criminal cases slip through cracks.

Conservatives and Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state lawmakers push to strengthen cooperation between local police and federal immigration enforcement—or whether Democratic majorities double down on sanctuary protections.
