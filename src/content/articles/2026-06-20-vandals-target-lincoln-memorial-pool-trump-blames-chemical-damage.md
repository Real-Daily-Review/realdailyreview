# Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool Hit by Vandals; Trump Blames Chemical Damage

Federal workers are battling both vandalism and an algae bloom at the newly renovated reflecting pool near the Lincoln Memorial, President Trump announced Friday. According to Trump, vandals used chemicals to damage the fresh surface coating, an act he characterized as an attempt to "destroy and demean" the restoration work (Fox News, Breitbart).

The incident comes as the pool's new sealant has begun peeling, complicating federal efforts to control the algae bloom (Just The News). Trump defended the administration's response to the environmental problem while pinning blame for the coating damage on deliberate sabotage.

The vandalism underscores a broader problem: the difficulty of maintaining federal monuments and public infrastructure when they become targets for activists hostile to Trump administration priorities. While the Left often frames such damage as political protest, conservatives argue it represents lawlessness that erodes public property and wastes taxpayer dollars on repairs.

Police are investigating the incident.

**What to watch:** Whether federal authorities identify and prosecute the vandals, and whether the reflecting pool restoration becomes a flashpoint in debates over monument security and activist accountability heading into 2026.
