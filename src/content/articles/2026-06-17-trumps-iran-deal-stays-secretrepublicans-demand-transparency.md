# Trump's Iran Deal Stays Secret—And That's a Problem

The Trump administration's vaunted Iran agreement remains locked in a vault, and nobody outside the inner circle knows what was actually promised. Vice President Vance is blaming "delicate diplomatic things" for keeping the text under wraps (Just The News), while Fox News reports that core issues—sanctions relief, nuclear concessions—are apparently still on the table for "future talks." That's not a deal; that's a framework wrapped in opacity.

National Review demands transparency: "It's the only way to get past the conflicting claims over what Trump and Iran agreed to." Republicans and conservatives are right to be skeptical. If this agreement is as strong as the administration claims, why hide it from Congress and the American people?

Even some Democrats smell trouble. Rep. Yassamin Ansari (D-AZ) called the deal "abysmal" on CNN, though she argued the U.S. needs to "cut our losses" and end the conflict (Breitbart).

For Colorado: any Iran deal affects energy markets and oil prices at the pump—critical for rural communities and working families already squeezed by inflation.

**Watch whether Congress forces the administration to release the full text before ratification.**
