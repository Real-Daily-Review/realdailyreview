# Colorado Parks and Wildlife Clears Massive Arkansas River Logjam Before Labor Day

Colorado Parks and Wildlife crews have successfully cleared a 100-yard logjam on the Arkansas River ahead of the Labor Day weekend recreation rush, according to reports from 9News Denver and the Colorado Sun.

The unprecedented blockage posed a hazard to boaters and rafters navigating the popular waterway. CPW crews worked from boats and riverbanks to dismantle the jam without explosives, clearing the passage in time for holiday weekend traffic.

The agency had previously warned the public to avoid the affected section while removal operations were underway.
