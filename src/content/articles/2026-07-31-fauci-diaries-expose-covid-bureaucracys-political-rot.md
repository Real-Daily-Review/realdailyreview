# Fauci's Diaries Expose the Rot at the Heart of COVID Bureaucracy

Anthony Fauci's personal diaries—now public—reveal what conservatives have long suspected: the nation's top pandemic official operated as a political tool rather than a straight-shooting scientist. According to the *Washington Examiner*, key players including HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. are offering conflicting accounts of how the journals surfaced, raising questions about transparency even in their release.

The diaries corroborate what Fauci stonewalled during Senate testimony. Sen. Rand Paul's homeland security panel documented Fauci invoking the Fifth Amendment 111 times—an extraordinary evasion for a public health official spending taxpayer dollars. *National Review* notes the left has already abandoned Fauci, treating him as a disposable political asset now that his usefulness has expired.

For Colorado, this matters. State and local health officials followed Fauci's guidance during lockdowns that shuttered small businesses, schools, and churches—decisions that cost jobs and livelihoods across the state. The diary revelations underscore how federal health bureaucrats operated without meaningful accountability, imposing one-size-fits-all mandates on diverse communities.

Conservatives argue this episode demands structural reform: term limits for unelected officials, mandatory transparency in public health decision-making, and real congressional oversight with teeth.

Watch whether Colorado's Republican delegation demands answers from federal health agencies about which Fauci directives shaped state policy—and whether taxpayers get a full accounting.
