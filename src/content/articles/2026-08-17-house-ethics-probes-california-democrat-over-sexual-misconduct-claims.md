# House Ethics Committee Opens Investigation Into California Democrat Over Sexual Misconduct Allegations

Another California Democrat faces the ethics gauntlet: the House Ethics Committee has opened an investigation into Rep. Jimmy Gomez over allegations of inappropriate sexual contact with a House staffer, according to Fox News and the Daily Caller.

The bipartisan committee announced Monday that it is reviewing claims Gomez "may have engaged in sexual misconduct," marking the latest scandal to engulf a California Democrat in Congress. The Daily Wire notes this adds to a growing series of misconduct allegations roiling the chamber.

**The conservative angle:** While this scandal doesn't directly affect Colorado taxpayers, it underscores a broader pattern: Democratic leadership's inconsistent handling of ethics violations and workplace misconduct. Republicans have long argued that Congress's ethics enforcement remains toothless and selective—investigations often drag on without meaningful consequences, and party loyalty frequently trumps accountability. The bipartisan Ethics Committee's involvement here is noteworthy, but conservatives remain skeptical that real consequences will follow, particularly for a sitting Democrat.

Colorado voters should note how their own congressional delegation responds to ethics questions and whether they demand consistent standards regardless of party affiliation.
