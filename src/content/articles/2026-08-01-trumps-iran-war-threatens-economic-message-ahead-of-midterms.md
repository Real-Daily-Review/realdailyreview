# Trump's Iran Conflict Threatens Economic Message as Midterms Loom

President Trump's escalating military engagement with Iran is consuming political capital he built on economic growth—and Colorado voters who backed him on jobs and low taxes are watching closely.

Fox News reports Trump vowed the U.S. will "win" against Iran even as diplomatic talks fade, predicting the conflict will "peter out" despite admitting the five-month campaign could extend through November's midterm elections. National Review, a conservative outlet, argues bluntly that "pursuing tariffs and the Iran war" has caused Trump to forfeit "his signature issue of economic stewardship."

For Colorado, the stakes are real. Prolonged Middle East conflict typically drives energy prices higher—bad news for families already squeezed by inflation and for the state's oil and gas workers facing regulatory headwinds from Democratic state leadership. Military spending also crowds out the tax relief and deregulation agenda conservatives expected.

Republicans defending Trump argue military strength deters broader regional conflict. Critics on the right counter that endless foreign entanglements drain resources from the domestic economy Trump promised to rebuild.

**Watch whether Trump can thread the needle: maintaining military credibility while pivoting back to the economic messaging that won Colorado in 2024.**
