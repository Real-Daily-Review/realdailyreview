# Colorado Should Reject Single-Payer Socialism Disguised as Healthcare Reform

Colorado Democrats are watching national single-payer pushes with interest—and conservatives need to sound the alarm now.

Michigan Democratic Senate nominee Abdul el Sayed recently downplayed his support for government-run healthcare in a podcast interview, according to the Washington Examiner. But his actual position is clear: he backs single-payer systems that nonpartisan and left-leaning analysts estimate would cost over $3 trillion annually *on top of* existing federal spending.

This matters for Colorado. If Democrats gain ground nationally on healthcare centralization, state-level pressure will follow. Single-payer schemes sound compassionate in theory; in practice, they mean rationed care, longer wait times, and massive tax increases to fund the federal takeover.

Republicans argue that government-run healthcare destroys market competition, kills innovation, and leaves patients waiting months for procedures. The left counters that universal coverage justifies the cost and inefficiency—a trade-off conservatives reject outright.

National Review has warned against similar government overreach in other sectors, noting that Washington's appetite for control extends far beyond healthcare (National Review). The pattern is consistent: Democrats promise affordability and access; they deliver bureaucracy and bills.

**Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature attempts to pilot single-payer models or pushes federal mandates that would bypass state taxpayers' consent.**
