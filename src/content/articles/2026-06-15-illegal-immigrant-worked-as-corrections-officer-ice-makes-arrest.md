# Colorado Border Security Spotlight: Illegal Immigrant Arrested While Working as Corrections Officer

Federal immigration enforcement has exposed a serious vetting failure: an illegal immigrant from Congo worked as a corrections officer in Maine after overstaying his student visa by more than a decade, according to The Daily Wire. ICE arrested Billy Maclarant Bassikissa Bounguiena on June 9 after Department of Homeland Security investigators discovered the breach.

The arrest underscores what conservatives have long argued—that inadequate background checks and lax enforcement create dangerous gaps in public safety. An individual with no legal status gained access to a law enforcement position, raising questions about how state and local agencies vet employees and whether they properly verify immigration status.

Meanwhile, the Daily Caller reports that ICE is investigating a separate incident in New Jersey where an illegal immigrant allegedly struck an ICE agent with a car while fleeing arrest, further illustrating the risks officers face during enforcement operations.

Colorado taxpayers should note: these cases highlight why border security and interior enforcement matter beyond the southern border. States and municipalities that fail to cooperate with federal immigration authorities—or that don't verify employment eligibility—risk similar breaches in critical positions.

Watch for whether Colorado law enforcement agencies are auditing their own hiring practices and immigration-status verification procedures.
