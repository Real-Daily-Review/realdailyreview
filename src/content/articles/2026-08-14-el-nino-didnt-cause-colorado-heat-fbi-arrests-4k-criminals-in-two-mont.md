# Colorado Summer Heat Wave Has Nothing to Do With El Niño—And That's Not the Real Story

Colorado's brutal summer heat wave isn't being driven by El Niño, contrary to some climate-focused explanations circulating in progressive media. According to the Colorado Sun, experts confirm El Niño won't impact continental U.S. weather until later this year, debunking the narrative that distant ocean patterns are responsible for current conditions.

But while the left obsesses over climate attribution, federal law enforcement is actually delivering results on public safety. The FBI's Operation Summer Heat has netted 4,000 arrests in just two months, including three members of the Mexican mafia convicted of murder in 2023 and members of the Houston street gang Rich Kings (Just The News). This is the kind of aggressive federal action conservatives have long demanded—targeting violent criminal organizations that threaten American communities.

For Colorado specifically, the distinction matters. Our state faces real public safety challenges from transnational criminal networks and gang activity. While progressives debate weather patterns and push for more environmental regulations, Republicans are focused on the enforcement operations that actually protect taxpayers and neighborhoods.
