# FBI Investigates Suspected Homicide Aboard Cruise Ship in Texas

Federal authorities are treating a death aboard the MSC Seascape as a potential homicide after the vessel returned to Galveston, Texas, following what the Washington Examiner reports was a directive from authorities to cut short its Caribbean voyage.

The FBI has launched a formal investigation into the incident, according to 9News Denver. Details remain sparse as federal investigators board the ship to gather evidence and interview passengers and crew. The vessel was en route when authorities ordered it back to port, disrupting the cruise itinerary.

Cruise ship incidents involving federal crimes fall under FBI jurisdiction when they occur in international waters or involve U.S. citizens. The investigation's scope—whether it involves negligence, foul play, or other circumstances—has not been disclosed by authorities.

This case underscores ongoing questions about maritime law enforcement and the adequacy of safety protocols aboard commercial vessels operating from U.S. ports. Passengers and crew aboard cruise ships operate in a legal gray zone where jurisdiction can be murky and accountability sometimes unclear.
