# National Debt Hits $40 Trillion: America's Fiscal Crisis Demands Spending Cuts, Not Tax Hikes

America's national debt has crossed the $40 trillion threshold—a fiscal catastrophe that demands immediate action, yet Washington continues to spend as if the bill will never come due.

The milestone, reported by the *Washington Examiner* and *National Review*, represents a staggering failure of fiscal discipline across both parties. Conservatives argue that runaway federal spending—not insufficient taxation—is the root cause. Republicans contend that Washington must slash wasteful programs, eliminate redundant agencies, and enforce hard budget caps rather than raise taxes on already-burdened Americans and businesses.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado taxpayers and small businesses are already squeezed by state and local taxes. A federal fiscal collapse would trigger either massive tax increases, severe spending cuts to programs Coloradans depend on, or both. Energy workers in Colorado's oil and gas sector face regulatory headwinds; a debt-driven economic contraction would devastate job creation further. Retirees and savers watch their purchasing power erode as inflation pressures mount from deficit spending.

The *Washington Examiner* warns that absent structural reform, America is "heading for bankruptcy." Conservatives say the math is simple: you cannot spend $40 trillion you do not have without consequences.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly Republicans—will demand real spending cuts or cave to bipartisan spending deals that mortgage Colorado's future.
