# Out-of-State Billionaire Drops $1.5M to Defeat Boebert as Colorado's 4th District Heats Up

A California tech billionaire's super PAC is flooding Colorado's 4th Congressional District with $1.5 million in attack ads against Rep. Lauren Boebert, signaling national Democrats' determination to flip this Republican-held seat (Colorado Sun). The Invest in Tomorrow Coalition, funded predominantly by Chris Larsen, is resurrecting old controversies rather than focusing on policy—a sign that Democrats lack a strong affirmative case for their candidate, Eileen Laubacher.

Meanwhile, Boebert and Laubacher clashed during a televised debate Monday over tariffs, inflation, and diesel prices (Denver Post), with both candidates staking out their positions on kitchen-table issues affecting Colorado families and energy workers.

**The conservative take:** Out-of-state money flooding Colorado elections to prop up progressive candidates undermines local representation. Larsen's millions suggest Democrats fear Boebert's message resonates with eastern Colorado voters who care about energy jobs, border security, and economic freedom—not celebrity gossip. Laubacher should defend her own record and vision instead of hiding behind billionaire-funded character attacks.

**What taxpayers should watch:** Whether Boebert's ground game and message discipline can overcome the spending disadvantage in a district where energy and agriculture still matter more than coastal progressive priorities.
