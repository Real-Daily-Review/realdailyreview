# 9/11 Families Demand Mayor Skip Memorial Over Radical Past

Over 101,000 people have signed a petition demanding New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani stay away from Friday's 25th anniversary 9/11 memorial service, citing his documented history of radical activism and anti-American rhetoric.

The petition, delivered to City Hall, reflects deep concern among 9/11 victims' families about Mamdani's fitness to represent the city at one of America's most solemn commemorations. Former Mayor Rudy Giuliani—who led the city through the attacks—joined the chorus of former officials calling on Mamdani to skip the event, according to *Just The News*.

Mamdani's record includes controversial statements and associations that conservatives argue disqualify him from presiding over a ceremony honoring nearly 3,000 Americans murdered by terrorists. The families' message is unambiguous: your presence dishonors the dead.

This controversy exposes the left's broader problem: elevating politicians whose worldview fundamentally conflicts with American security and patriotism. Mamdani's election reflects how far progressive New York has drifted from the values that defined the city's response to 9/11—strength, clarity, and unwavering commitment to victims.

Watch whether Mamdani respects the families' wishes or doubles down, revealing whether he views the memorial as sacred ground or political theater.
