# Colorado's Senate and Treasurer Races Set: Hickenlooper Faces Baisley; Bridges Challenges Grantham

Colorado voters will choose between starkly different visions for state and federal government this November, with Democratic incumbent John Hickenlooper defending his U.S. Senate seat against Republican challenger Mark Baisley, while state Treasurer Jeff Bridges faces former Senate President Kevin Grantham in a race that will shape Colorado's fiscal future.

The Senate matchup pits Hickenlooper's progressive agenda—backed by Colorado's Democratic establishment—against Baisley's conservative platform emphasizing limited government and free-market solutions. Republicans argue Hickenlooper's record reflects Washington's big-spending, regulatory overreach that burdens Colorado's energy sector and small businesses. The treasurer's race is equally consequential: Bridges, a Democrat, and Grantham, a Republican and Fremont County commissioner, diverge sharply on how Colorado should manage its budget, pension obligations, and taxpayer dollars.

Both races will test whether Colorado voters are ready to reject progressive policies or double down on them. The treasurer's office, in particular, holds significant sway over state investment decisions and fiscal accountability—critical for Colorado taxpayers watching government spending balloon.

Watch for how candidates address Colorado's energy economy, water rights, and tax burden as the campaign intensifies.
