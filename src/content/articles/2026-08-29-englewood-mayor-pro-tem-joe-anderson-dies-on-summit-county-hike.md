# Englewood Mayor Pro Tem Joe Anderson Dies on Summit County Hike

Englewood has lost a sitting city leader: Mayor Pro Tem Joe Anderson died Friday morning while hiking in Summit County, according to reports from KDVR Fox31 Denver and 9News Denver.

Anderson had served on the Englewood City Council since 2019 and held the position of Mayor Pro Tem at the time of his death. The circumstances surrounding his death while on the trail remain under investigation.

His passing marks a significant loss for the south Denver suburb, which now faces questions about succession and leadership continuity on the council. Anderson's tenure on the council spanned a period of significant growth and policy decisions affecting the metro area.

The city of Englewood has announced his death but has not yet released additional details about memorial services or the process for filling his seat.

Englewood residents and the broader Colorado political community should watch for the city council's appointment process to replace Anderson and any special election that may follow.
