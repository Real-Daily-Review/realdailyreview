# Colorado Conservatives Eye Planned Parenthood Funding as Leverage Point

Republicans see an opening to defund Planned Parenthood at the state level, with national conservative outlets framing the moment as critical for protecting minors from what they call the organization's "one-way pipeline" toward gender ideology and medical interventions.

National Review argues Republicans "should not squander" the current political opportunity, while The Daily Wire features testimony from Chloe Cole, who describes herself as a victim of cross-sex hormone treatments, alleging Planned Parenthood facilities failed to provide alternatives when she sought help.

Colorado conservatives have long opposed state funding flowing to Planned Parenthood, citing both abortion concerns and now—increasingly—objections to the organization's gender-affirming care protocols for minors. Taxpayer groups argue Colorado dollars should not subsidize what they view as ideologically-driven medical decisions affecting children.

Democrats defend Planned Parenthood as a critical healthcare provider offering contraception, cancer screenings, and gender-affirming care to underserved populations. They contend defunding efforts are politically motivated attacks on reproductive freedom.

The Colorado legislature will face pressure from conservative activists and GOP lawmakers to strip state funding or impose restrictions on Planned Parenthood clinics operating in the state—a fight that could reshape healthcare access in rural and urban Colorado communities.

Watch for legislative proposals in the 2027 session targeting Planned Parenthood funding streams and parental notification requirements for minors seeking gender-related care.
