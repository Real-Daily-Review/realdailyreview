# Blue State Model Crumbles While Red States Surge Ahead

Progressive governance is failing across America, and Colorado should take note before doubling down on the same tax-and-regulate playbook.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, red states are decisively outperforming blue states on education and economic competitiveness. Florida is "eating New York's lunch" on schooling outcomes, while Mississippi and other conservative-led states demonstrate that limited government and lower tax burdens attract talent and investment—the opposite of what progressive mayors and governors promise.

The contrast is stark. Blue states cling to expansive government spending, heavy regulation, and progressive social experiments. Red states prioritize fiscal restraint, business-friendly policies, and parental choice in education. The results speak for themselves: people and capital are fleeing high-tax, high-regulation blue states for freer, more prosperous red alternatives.

**The Colorado angle:** Coloradans already face some of the nation's highest housing costs and rising tax burdens. If state lawmakers continue mimicking California and New York—more spending, more regulation, more progressive mandates—expect more working families and businesses to relocate to Texas, Florida, and Wyoming. The blue state model doesn't just fail; it empties wallets and erodes opportunity.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Colorado's legislature learns this lesson or doubles down on progressive policies that chase prosperity elsewhere.
