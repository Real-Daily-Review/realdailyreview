# Fourth of July Surveillance: Feds Deploy Drones to Police Fireworks

Federal bureaucrats are using America's 250th birthday as cover to expand government surveillance over holiday celebrations.

The Federal Aviation Administration has loosened drone regulations, enabling cities and counties to deploy unmanned aircraft to monitor Fourth of July gatherings under the guise of spotting illegal fireworks (The Daily Wire). The so-called Drone as First Responder (DFR) initiatives cut through normal approval processes, accelerating what conservatives warn is creeping surveillance infrastructure.

**The conservative concern:** Republicans and limited-government advocates argue this represents mission creep—using public safety as justification to normalize aerial monitoring of Americans exercising their rights. Once deployed for fireworks enforcement, these systems remain in place for other purposes. Colorado taxpayers should ask whether their local governments are funding these programs and what safeguards exist against expanded use.

**What's at stake:** Colorado's outdoor recreation culture and Fourth of July traditions shouldn't become testing grounds for federal surveillance apparatus. The precedent matters: today's "emergency" drone deployment becomes tomorrow's routine monitoring infrastructure.

Coloradans should demand transparency from their county commissioners and city councils about any DFR participation—and whether taxpayer dollars are funding federal overreach disguised as holiday safety.
