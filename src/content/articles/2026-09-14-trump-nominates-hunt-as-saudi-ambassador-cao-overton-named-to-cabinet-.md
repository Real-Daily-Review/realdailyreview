# Trump Taps Rep. Wesley Hunt as Saudi Ambassador; GOP Fills Key Diplomatic Posts

President Trump has nominated Rep. Wesley Hunt (R-TX) to serve as U.S. ambassador to Saudi Arabia, a critical diplomatic post that has sat vacant throughout his second term, according to *Just The News* and the *Washington Examiner*. Hunt, a Texas Republican and longtime Trump ally, previously served as a diplomatic liaison officer in Saudi Arabia, giving him direct experience in the region.

The nomination is part of a broader slate of Trump administration appointments. The president also formally nominated acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao for the permanent Navy Secretary position and Heidi Overton to lead the Food and Drug Administration.

Hunt's diplomatic background in the Middle East positions him to manage U.S.-Saudi relations at a time when energy markets, regional stability, and counterterrorism cooperation remain central to American interests. For Colorado, stable Middle East policy and predictable global energy markets matter directly—oil and gas production supports state jobs and tax revenue, while geopolitical uncertainty can spike energy prices affecting consumers and businesses statewide.

Republicans view these nominations as strengthening Trump's cabinet with experienced, loyal operatives. No Democratic opposition has been formally articulated in available reporting.

Watch for Senate confirmation hearings and whether Democrats challenge Hunt's nomination or Overton's FDA pick—the latter typically draws scrutiny over regulatory philosophy.
