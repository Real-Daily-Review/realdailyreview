# FBI Shelved Omar Investigation After Biden Took Office

A federal investigation into whether Minnesota congresswoman Ilhan Omar married her brother—launched under the Obama administration in 2016—was shut down in 2021 by a Biden-appointed attorney, according to Fox News and the Daily Caller. Obama-era investigators reportedly gathered evidence suggesting the marriage was fraudulent, but the probe went nowhere after the change in administrations.

The case centers on Omar's 2009 marriage to Ahmed Nur Said Elmi. According to the reporting, federal agents found indications the union was improper, yet the investigation stalled under Biden's Justice Department.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans and watchdog groups have long questioned the circumstances of Omar's marriage and alleged campaign finance violations tied to the relationship. Critics argue the abrupt closure of a credible federal investigation—particularly one that had already gathered evidence—raises questions about selective prosecution and political protection. The timing, they contend, suggests the Biden administration prioritized shielding a progressive ally over pursuing potential fraud.

Democrats have dismissed the allegations as conspiracy theories without merit. Omar herself has denied wrongdoing.

The broader issue for Colorado conservatives: this case exemplifies how federal law enforcement decisions can appear politically motivated depending on which party controls the Justice Department—a concern that should alarm anyone worried about equal application of the law.

Watch whether congressional Republicans demand documents and testimony about why the investigation was closed.
