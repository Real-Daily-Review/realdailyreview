# Colorado Governor-Elect Weiser Taps Top GOP Senator for Agriculture Post

Phil Weiser's incoming administration is already signaling a willingness to work across the aisle—nominating Cleave Simpson, the top Republican in the state Senate, as Colorado's next agriculture commissioner, according to the *Denver Post*.

Simpson's selection is notable in a state where agriculture, water rights, and land-use policy directly affect ranchers, farmers, and rural economies. Republicans have long argued that agriculture policy should prioritize property rights, water access for irrigation, and resistance to federal overreach on public lands. Simpson's appointment suggests Weiser may be open to conservative input on these issues—or at least wants to avoid early conflict with GOP lawmakers.

The move comes as national security concerns continue to dominate headlines. Attorney General Todd Blanche has been relocated to a military base due to ongoing threats against Trump administration officials, *Breitbart* reports—a reminder that the new administration faces serious security challenges even as it settles in.

For Colorado, the agriculture commissioner role carries real weight: the position oversees livestock regulations, water compacts, and agricultural licensing. Conservatives should watch whether Simpson's appointment translates into actual deference to rural interests on water policy and land management, or whether it's merely symbolic.
