# Trump's Election Integrity Speech Faces Network Blackout

President Trump is set to deliver a primetime address Thursday on "free and fair elections" and the SAVE America Act, but major television networks are stonewalling on whether they'll broadcast it—a telling sign of media gatekeeping that should alarm Colorado voters concerned about election transparency.

According to Fox News, Trump's speech will focus on election integrity and address the Iran conflict. However, The Daily Wire reports that ABC, NBC, and CBS have refused to commit to airing the 9 p.m. address, leaving the president's message dependent on cable and digital platforms.

**The conservative concern:** This is classic media bias. Networks routinely granted airtime to Democratic addresses during the Biden administration, yet now won't confirm coverage of a sitting president discussing election security—a legitimate policy matter affecting every American voter, including Coloradans. Republicans argue the networks' hesitation reveals their preference for controlling the narrative rather than letting voters hear directly from elected officials.

Democrats and their media allies counter that Trump's election claims lack credibility and that networks have editorial discretion over what to broadcast.

**What Colorado taxpayers should watch:** Whether the networks' refusal to air this speech becomes a flashpoint in the broader debate over media bias and election integrity messaging heading into 2024.
