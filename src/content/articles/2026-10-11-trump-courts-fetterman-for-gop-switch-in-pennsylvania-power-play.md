# Trump Courts Fetterman as GOP Eyes Pennsylvania Democrat Defection

President Trump is openly courting Sen. John Fetterman (D-Pa.), calling him a "great guy" and signaling he'd welcome the Pennsylvania Democrat into Republican ranks, according to Fox News and the New York Post. The overture reflects growing speculation about Fetterman's political future and suggests GOP confidence in flipping a seat in a crucial swing state.

Republicans see an opening. Fetterman, a working-class Democrat who won statewide in 2022, has positioned himself as a moderate willing to break with his party on key votes. For conservatives, a Fetterman switch would represent a symbolic and practical victory—gaining a Senate seat without a costly campaign and demonstrating that Trump's populist, pro-worker messaging resonates beyond traditional GOP voters.

Democrats will likely frame any defection as opportunism, though they've grown increasingly frustrated with Fetterman's independent streak on issues like energy and fiscal policy.

The Colorado angle: Pennsylvania's political realignment matters to Western Republicans. A successful GOP recruitment of a blue-state Democrat signals that Trump's anti-establishment, pro-growth message can crack Democratic strongholds—a model Colorado Republicans hope to replicate in purple counties as they rebuild statewide competitiveness.
