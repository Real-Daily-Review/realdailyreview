# Trump Creates 'AI Force,' Names Czar to Keep U.S. Ahead of China

Trump is betting American innovation—not bureaucratic caution—will dominate the artificial intelligence race.

The president announced plans to establish an "AI Force" and appoint a high-level "AI czar" to oversee the sector, mirroring his first-term creation of the Space Force (Fox News). Trump emphasized that "only High I.Q. individuals need apply," signaling his intent to staff the initiative with top talent rather than career bureaucrats.

In a statement, Trump framed AI as potentially worth 25% of U.S. GDP and positioned the move as essential to maintaining American leadership over China and global competitors (Just The News). The announcement comes as industry titans have urged caution on AI regulation, though Trump's approach prioritizes growth and market leadership over restrictive oversight (Daily Caller).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that protecting American AI dominance requires removing regulatory barriers and empowering private innovation, not strangling the sector with red tape. A government-led czar role should facilitate industry growth, not impose progressive mandates on development.

**What Colorado should watch:** Whether the AI Force prioritizes Colorado's tech hubs and whether the czar appointment includes voices skeptical of climate-driven AI restrictions that could hamstring data centers and energy-intensive operations critical to the state's economy.
