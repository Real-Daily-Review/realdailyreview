# Two Boulder County Communities Win Firewise Designation—But County Seeks to Expand Government

Gold Hill and Wondervu have earned Firewise USA status through practical wildfire prevention work, but Boulder County commissioners are simultaneously pushing ballot measures to expand the size of county government itself.

The two communities reduced wildfire risk by clearing vegetation with help from youth-based crews, a straightforward approach to protecting property and lives in Colorado's fire-prone foothills (9News Denver). That's the kind of community-led initiative conservatives support: local action, minimal bureaucracy, results.

But Boulder County Commissioner Ashley Stolzmann is now arguing that "as the size of the county has grown over time, there's more of a need for more representation," according to the Denver Post. Translation: more commissioners, more government payroll, more layers of bureaucracy.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue that county government bloat—not representation gaps—drives inefficiency and higher taxes. They point out that existing commissioners can hold more meetings, use technology, or streamline processes without expanding the payroll. Expanding commissions typically means more staff, more overhead, and more spending on county operations that Coloradans already fund.

The irony is sharp: Boulder County residents solve wildfire problems through grassroots effort while their commissioners pitch bigger government as the answer to growth.

**Watch whether Douglas County voters reject a similar commission-expansion ballot measure—a potential sign that Colorado taxpayers are tired of government-size solutions.**
