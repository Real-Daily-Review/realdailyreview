# NYC Lawmaker's Anti-Police Record Catches Up With Him at NYPD Event

A New York City progressive politician learned a hard lesson about public accountability when crowds booed him off stage in under a minute at an NYPD National Night Out celebration on Staten Island, according to Fox News and Just The News.

State Senator Zohran Mamdani, who has faced sustained criticism for his hostile stance toward law enforcement, attempted to address the pro-police gathering but was met with immediate, sustained booing from attendees who chanted against him. The public rejection underscores growing frustration with progressive politicians who campaign against police while crime remains a serious concern in urban centers.

Mamdani's anti-police record includes previous attempts to downplay incidents of violence against officers—including dismissing a February incident where a group pelted NYPD officers with snowballs, according to Just The News. His forced exit from the event reflects what conservatives argue is a broader rejection of the defund-the-police movement and anti-law-enforcement rhetoric that has damaged Democratic credibility on public safety.

While Colorado hasn't seen the same level of organized anti-police activism as New York, Denver progressives have pushed similar soft-on-crime policies. Taxpayers and law enforcement advocates should watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from Mamdani-style anti-police positioning ahead of 2024, or double down on policies that undermine police recruitment and retention.
