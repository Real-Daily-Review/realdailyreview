# SpaceX IPO Filing Reveals Massive AI Spending, Musk's Overlapping Ventures

SpaceX has disclosed its IPO filing ahead of what is expected to be the largest public offering ever, with Elon Musk set to serve as CEO, CTO, and chairman, according to TechCrunch.

The filing exposes significant financial commitments to artificial intelligence. xAI, Musk's AI company, lost $6.4 billion in 2025 while planning a major expansion of its Grok product, according to TechCrunch. The IPO documents reveal deep financial entanglement across Musk's portfolio of companies: Tesla appears 87 times in the filing, xAI 356 times, and X 267 times, with smaller mentions of the Boring Company and Neuralink, according to The Verge.

The filing highlights Musk himself as a central risk factor. The Verge notes the document details how Musk's companies interact and overlap, "shuffling money around in ways that are often difficult to keep track of." This concentration of control and capital flows across multiple ventures underscores investor exposure to a single individual's decision-making.

The IPO filing also emphasizes SpaceX's ambitions beyond rockets, with significant focus on Starship development and AI integration into the company's operations.

Watch for regulatory scrutiny of the capital flows between Musk's companies and how investors respond to the concentration-of-risk disclosure.
