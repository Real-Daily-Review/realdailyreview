# New York Swing Races Heat Up Over Immigration, ICE Enforcement

Republican Rep. Mike Lawler and Democrat Cait Conley traded sharp blows in a House debate over sanctuary city policies and ICE enforcement—a clash that could determine control of Congress in 2026 (Fox News Politics). Lawler fact-checked Conley on New York's sanctuary laws, pressing her on the state's refusal to cooperate with federal immigration enforcement, while Conley defended the policies as protecting vulnerable residents.

The immigration fight isn't confined to the House race. New York's gubernatorial contest also centered on the issue, with Republican challenger Lee Zeldin's successor and Governor Kathy Hochul clashing over how the state handles migrant arrivals and ICE operations (Breitbart).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that sanctuary city policies obstruct federal law enforcement, endanger public safety, and shift costs to taxpayers. Lawler's aggressive fact-checking signals GOP confidence that immigration enforcement resonates with swing voters tired of Democratic permissiveness on the border.

**Democrats counter** that sanctuary protections prevent racial profiling and encourage immigrant communities to cooperate with local police on unrelated crimes.
