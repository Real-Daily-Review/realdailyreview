# Jack Smith's Festival Appearance Draws Trump Fire Over Prosecutorial Bias

Former Special Counsel Jack Smith appeared at a Maryland "Power to the People Fest" wearing a Bruce Springsteen "No Kings" shirt—and Trump immediately seized on the optics as evidence of partisan overreach (Just The News).

The event, headlined by Springsteen, was billed as a get-out-the-vote rally emphasizing "the power everyday human beings have when they come together — through music, art, community and action — to shape our country" (Just The News). Springsteen himself railed against ICE and delivered anti-Trump messaging (Breitbart).

Trump responded sharply, calling Smith "Deranged" and a "Crooked Prosecutor" on social media, seizing on what conservatives see as damning visual evidence: a federal prosecutor who indicted Trump attending a partisan Democratic rally.

**The conservative argument:** Smith's appearance at an overtly anti-Trump political event undercuts claims of prosecutorial independence and impartiality. When the official tasked with investigating a former president shows up at opposition campaign rallies in activist wear, it reinforces Republican arguments that the indictments were politically motivated, not legally grounded.

Colorado Republicans and Trump allies will likely cite this moment as proof that the Justice Department weaponized itself against political opponents—a central campaign theme heading into 2026.
