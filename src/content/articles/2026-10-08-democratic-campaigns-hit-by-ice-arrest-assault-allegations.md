# Democratic Campaigns Roiled by Security Breaches and Criminal Allegations

A pair of troubling incidents have exposed vulnerabilities in Democratic campaign operations just weeks before the general election. In Pennsylvania, Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrested Chimaobim Adedayo Igbojekwe, a 35-year-old Nigerian national allegedly working as a canvasser for Democrat Janelle Stelson's congressional campaign in the competitive 10th District (Washington Examiner). Igbojekwe, who pleaded guilty to disorderly conduct after allegedly exposing himself in public, was detained on Sept. 27. Stelson's campaign denied the staffer's association with her operation, though Just The News reported ICE identified him as working directly for her campaign at the time of arrest.

Meanwhile, in Arizona, Democratic congressional nominee Bernadette Greene-Placentia reported being raped in August while repairing campaign signs in a parking lot around 2 a.m. (The Daily Wire). The Maricopa County Sheriff's Office is investigating the allegation.

Republicans argue these incidents underscore the chaos and poor judgment plaguing Democratic campaigns heading into November. The Pennsylvania case raises fresh questions about vetting procedures and immigration enforcement priorities under a Democratic administration.

**Watch for:** Whether mainstream media covers these stories with the same intensity applied to Republican campaign controversies, and whether Democratic leadership addresses campaign security standards.
