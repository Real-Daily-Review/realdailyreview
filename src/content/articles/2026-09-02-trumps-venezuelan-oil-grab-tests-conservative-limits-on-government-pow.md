# Trump's Venezuelan Oil Play Raises Questions About Government Overreach

President Trump's move to position the federal government as a "hedge fund" seizing Venezuelan oil assets marks a troubling expansion of executive power that conservatives should scrutinize carefully. National Review warns the strategy amounts to "American nationalization" of foreign resources—precisely the kind of government intervention conservatives typically oppose when Democrats propose it domestically.

The irony is sharp: Republicans rightly criticize progressive wealth seizures and regulatory takings at home, yet a Republican administration is now deploying similar logic abroad. If government can nationalize Venezuelan assets without congressional authorization, what's the principled conservative objection when the left argues for nationalizing domestic energy reserves or seizing "excess" corporate profits?

Meanwhile, Colorado energy workers and producers should pay attention. Any precedent normalizing federal asset seizure—even of foreign oil—weakens the constitutional guardrails protecting American property rights and private enterprise. The energy sector, already battered by years of regulatory hostility from the Biden administration, needs clarity that a Republican White House won't simply swap targets while keeping the same expansionist playbook.

**Watch for:** Congressional Republicans' response—whether they demand formal authorization or quietly endorse executive overreach when it serves their foreign-policy goals.
