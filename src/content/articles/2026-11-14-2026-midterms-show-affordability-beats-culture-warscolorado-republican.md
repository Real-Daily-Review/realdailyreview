# Colorado Conservatives Eye 2026: Affordability Beats Culture Wars

Republicans won the 2026 midterms by focusing on what matters most to working families—not divisive social issues. According to the *Washington Examiner*, voters cared about whether they could afford food, housing, and basic living expenses. That message trumped abortion and climate rhetoric, even among millions of voters who hold strong views on those topics.

For Colorado, this is a crucial lesson heading into the next cycle. State Republicans should hammer Democrats on the cost of living—energy prices, housing affordability, and grocery bills—rather than getting pulled into secondary battles. Colorado's economy has been battered by progressive policies on energy regulation and housing restrictions that drive up costs for working families and small businesses.

Conservatives argue that when elections focus on kitchen-table economics, voters reject the left's spending-heavy, regulation-heavy agenda. Democrats counter that social issues remain motivating for their base, but the 2026 results suggest that message discipline on affordability wins elections.

The takeaway: Colorado Republicans must stay laser-focused on how state and federal policies hit family budgets—and hold Democrats accountable for inflation, energy costs, and housing shortages.
