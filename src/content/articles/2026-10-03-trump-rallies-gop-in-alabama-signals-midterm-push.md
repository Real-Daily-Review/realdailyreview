# Trump Rallies for GOP in Alabama, Eyes College Football Spotlight

President Trump is hitting the campaign trail hard ahead of the midterms, making stops across red states to boost Republican candidates—and he's mixing politics with football. Trump rallied in Mobile, Alabama on Friday alongside Sen. Tommy Tuberville and state GOP candidates, continuing a swing that included Texas and Oklahoma to support Republican Senate nominee Ken Paxton (Washington Examiner). The president announced he'll return to Alabama next week to watch the Crimson Tide face Georgia, adding high-profile sporting events to his midterm push.

For Colorado Republicans, Trump's aggressive campaign schedule underscores the party's focus on reclaiming momentum before November. While Democrats control the state legislature and governor's office, Colorado conservatives are watching whether Trump's endorsement strategy and grassroots energy can help GOP candidates in competitive races—particularly in congressional districts where Republican challengers are targeting vulnerable Democratic incumbents.

The rallies signal Trump's intent to remain the party's dominant force heading into 2024, a move that will shape Colorado's Republican primary landscape and general election dynamics.
