# Trump White House Bars CNN From Air Force One Pool

The Trump White House is enforcing media discipline by excluding CNN from the presidential press pool aboard Air Force One for the president's Tennessee trip, replacing the network with conservative outlet Real America's Voice News, according to reporting from the New York Post and Washington Examiner.

CNN, along with MSNBC and Politico, had been barred from White House grounds earlier in Trump's term. While those outlets have since regained general access to the executive complex (9News Denver reports), the Air Force One exclusion signals a more targeted approach: the White House is rotating which outlets travel with the president on Air Force One, effectively controlling which networks get direct access to breaking news and presidential moments.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue major media outlets like CNN have abandoned objective reporting in favor of partisan opposition coverage. Controlling press pool access—a presidential prerogative—is a legitimate response to what conservatives see as coordinated hostility from legacy media. Real America's Voice News represents an alternative voice.

**The counterargument:** Democrats and press freedom advocates contend that excluding major news organizations from the presidential pool undermines press freedom and public access to information about the president's activities.

**Colorado angle:** This reflects broader national tensions over media credibility that resonate with Colorado conservatives skeptical of mainstream outlets.

Watch whether the White House expands or formalizes these media restrictions, or if legal challenges emerge.
