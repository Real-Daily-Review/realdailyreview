# WNBA Star Stands Firm: Girls' Sports Must Stay Female-Only

Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham is refusing to back down from her defense of biological sex categories in women's sports—a position that has drawn fire from progressive corners of the WNBA but earned quiet support from conservatives.

Speaking to ESPN this week, Cunningham doubled down on her conviction that female athletics should remain protected spaces for biological women, according to *The Daily Wire* and *Breitbart*. "I said what I said," she declared, making clear she has no apologies for prioritizing fairness and safety in girls' competition.

Her stance puts her at odds with the WNBA's institutional position and some fellow players who have embraced transgender athlete participation policies. The backlash has been swift—critics have labeled her a "MAGA Barbie" for daring to voice a mainstream conservative view on sex-based sports categories.

Notably, the *Washington Examiner* reported that White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt confirmed President Trump is monitoring the controversy, signaling the issue remains a flashpoint in the broader culture war over sex and gender ideology.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado schools and universities operate under state athletic association rules that increasingly blur sex-based categories. Cunningham's public stand may embolden parents and coaches here demanding clarity on fairness standards.

**Watch next:** Whether other athletes—particularly in Colorado—will publicly support sex-based sports protections or remain silent under institutional pressure.
