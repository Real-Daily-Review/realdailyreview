# Federal Judge Blocks Attempt to Stop UFC Freedom 250 at White House

An Obama-appointed federal judge has rejected an emergency motion to halt the UFC Freedom 250 event scheduled for the White House and Lincoln Memorial, ruling that plaintiffs lacked legal standing to challenge the fight (Fox News Politics). The decision clears the way for UFC fighters to compete on the White House lawn this weekend, with fighters holding a press conference at the Lincoln Memorial ahead of the event (Washington Examiner).

The ruling represents a win for the Trump administration's effort to host the high-profile combat sports event as part of its "Freedom 250" celebration. Opponents of the event had sought to block it through the courts, but the judge found their challenge legally insufficient.

For Colorado conservatives, this outcome underscores the importance of judicial appointments and the limits of activist litigation to overturn executive decisions. The decision also signals that courts may be less receptive to last-minute injunctions against administration events—a shift from the Trump-era litigation playbook that saw judges frequently block executive orders.
