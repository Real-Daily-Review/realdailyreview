# Maine Democrats' Vetting Disaster Shows Perils of Rush-Job Candidate Screening

A slapdash vetting process has blown up in Maine Democrats' faces, exposing how party operatives ignored repeated red flags before their Senate candidate imploded. According to Fox News, Dan Moraff and Morris Katz—who oversaw vetting for Democrat Graham Platner's challenge to Sen. Susan Collins (R-ME)—repeatedly dismissed warnings from their own vetting firm that the candidate needed deeper investigation. Platner dropped out Wednesday after an ex-girlfriend accused him of rape.

The collapse reveals Democratic desperation to unseat Collins. Before Platner's exit, his campaign polled potential replacements, per the Washington Examiner, suggesting party brass knew trouble was brewing. Yet Maine's Democratic Secretary of State Shenna Bellows, now a Senate candidate herself, told CNN it's "important" to preserve "Platner's movement" to beat Collins—because Democrats need to "abolish ICE," according to Breitbart.

Republicans argue the fiasco demonstrates how progressive activists prioritize ideology over basic due diligence. The rush to field a challenger willing to embrace radical positions like immigration enforcement abolition apparently trumped elementary background screening.
