# Construction Worker Dies at Broncos Training Facility; OSHA Opens Investigation

A construction worker died Monday at the Denver Broncos' new training facility site, prompting an Occupational Safety and Health Administration investigation into workplace safety protocols at the project.

According to 9News Denver, the deceased worker was employed by a subcontractor rather than Turner Construction, the lead contractor managing the project. Turner Construction confirmed the fatality but did not immediately disclose additional details about the circumstances or the subcontractor involved.

OSHA investigations into construction deaths typically examine whether employers met federal safety standards, including fall protection, equipment maintenance, and worker training requirements. Colorado's construction industry has faced scrutiny in recent years over workplace safety compliance, particularly on large commercial projects.

The incident underscores a broader conservative concern: while safety regulations serve a legitimate purpose, the regulatory burden on construction firms—especially smaller subcontractors—can drive up costs and complexity. Colorado taxpayers and businesses should monitor whether OSHA's investigation leads to reasonable findings or becomes another example of federal overreach that inflates project timelines and expenses without meaningfully improving outcomes.

Watch for OSHA's final report and whether Turner Construction or the subcontractor faces citations that could affect future Denver-area construction bids.
