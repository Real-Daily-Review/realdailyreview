# Michigan Democrat's Israel Stand Exposes Party's Radical Drift

Rep. Haley Stevens (D-MI) just did what few Democrats dare: she endorsed Republican Mike Rogers over her own party's Senate nominee, Abdul El-Sayed, citing irreconcilable differences over Israel policy. Her defection, announced hours before Election Day, signals deepening fractures within Democratic ranks over antisemitism and anti-American radicalism.

Stevens said El-Sayed "shut her out" and that "loyalty to a party should never require abandoning your values or judgment" (The Daily Wire). National Review reports Stevens is far from alone—many Democrats harbor serious reservations about El-Sayed's record. Her breaking point: his documented hostility toward Israel, a red line for voters who won't tolerate progressive antisemitism masquerading as foreign policy.

The left's response proved her point. Jon Stewart raged on *The Daily Show*, calling Rogers a "nightmare" and attacking Stevens for her conscience (Breitbart). That fury—directed at a Democrat for refusing to rubber-stamp a radical nominee—illustrates how thoroughly progressive activists have captured party machinery.

For Colorado: this Michigan implosion previews what happens when Democrats nominate candidates hostile to American allies and dismissive of Jewish concerns. Voters nationwide are watching whether their party will course-correct or double down on the El-Sayed wing.

Watch whether national Democrats punish Stevens or quietly acknowledge she read the room.
