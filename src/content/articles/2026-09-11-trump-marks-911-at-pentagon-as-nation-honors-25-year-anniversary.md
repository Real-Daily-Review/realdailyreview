# Trump Honors 9/11 at Pentagon as Nation Marks 25 Years Since Terror Attacks

President Donald Trump and First Lady Melania Trump attended a memorial service at the Pentagon on Friday to mark the 25th anniversary of the September 11 terrorist attacks (Breitbart). The ceremony was one of several observances across the nation, including a tribute at the Flight 93 Memorial in Shanksville, Pennsylvania, where passengers and crew members fought back against hijackers (9News Denver).

The 9/11 remembrances serve as annual reminders of the attacks that killed nearly 3,000 Americans and reshaped national security policy, defense spending, and federal authority. For Colorado conservatives, the anniversary underscores enduring questions about the balance between security and civil liberties—particularly regarding government surveillance programs that expanded in the post-9/11 era. Republicans have long argued that robust defense spending and intelligence capabilities are essential to preventing future attacks, while critics across the political spectrum have raised concerns about overreach by federal agencies.

The Pentagon ceremony reflected the nation's continued commitment to honoring those lost, though conservatives remain vigilant about ensuring that security measures don't become permanent fixtures of government overreach.
