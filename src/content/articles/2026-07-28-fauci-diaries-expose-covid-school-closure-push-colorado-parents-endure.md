# Fauci's COVID Defense Crumbles as Diaries Reveal Pandemic Overreach

Anthony Fauci's freshly released diaries undermine his repeated denials that he pushed for school closures and lockdowns during the COVID-19 pandemic—a reckoning Colorado parents and taxpayers should demand answers about.

While Fauci has insisted to Congress that he "never shut anything down," the diaries paint a starkly different picture, according to *National Review*, which reports the documents "enhance Fauci's already deeply tarnished reputation" in the opposite direction. The *Washington Examiner* notes Fauci's technical defense—that he didn't literally wield the shutdown authority himself—rings hollow when his documented advocacy for school closures is weighed against the real-world damage inflicted on American education.

**Conservatives argue** Fauci's fingerprints are all over the pandemic policies that devastated Colorado schools, disrupted learning for hundreds of thousands of students, and enriched remote-learning vendors while harming working families. Republicans point out that Fauci's influence over public health guidance gave political cover to governors and school boards who implemented the most draconian restrictions.

**Democrats counter** that Fauci was following evolving science and that state and local officials made their own decisions.

The diaries vindicate what Colorado parents have long suspected: federal health bureaucrats shaped the lockdown consensus that shuttered classrooms and damaged a generation of students.

Watch for whether Colorado's Republican legislators demand accountability from state health officials who followed Fauci's lead.
