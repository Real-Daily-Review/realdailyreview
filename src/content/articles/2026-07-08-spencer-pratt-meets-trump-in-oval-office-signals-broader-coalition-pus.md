# Spencer Pratt's Oval Office Visit Highlights Trump's Outreach Beyond Traditional GOP

Reality TV personality Spencer Pratt scored a face-to-face meeting with President Trump at the Resolute Desk this week, signaling the administration's willingness to engage unconventional voices in its political coalition.

Pratt, a former Los Angeles mayoral candidate, posted photos from the Tuesday meeting showing him alongside Trump and White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles. The visit underscores Trump's strategy of building a broader political base that extends beyond establishment Republican circles—a tactic that proved effective in 2024 and could shape coalition-building heading into 2026 midterms.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Pratt shared a photo of his son in the Oval Office, accompanying it with a post on X: "You must love your country the way you love your child. Unconditional love." The message reflects themes of patriotism and family values that resonate with Trump's base.

The meeting carries symbolic weight for conservatives who view Trump's accessibility to diverse supporters—from media personalities to grassroots activists—as evidence of his anti-establishment credentials. It also demonstrates the administration's comfort with high-profile figures outside traditional political hierarchies.

Coloradans should watch whether this outreach strategy extends to state-level GOP candidates and whether it influences how Trump's team engages with Colorado Republicans ahead of 2026 races.
