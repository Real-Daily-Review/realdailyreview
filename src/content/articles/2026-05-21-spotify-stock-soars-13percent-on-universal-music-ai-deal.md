# Spotify Surges on AI Music Deal with Universal

Spotify's stock jumped 13% after the streaming giant announced a partnership with Universal Music Group to let Premium subscribers create AI-generated song covers and remixes, according to CNBC. Under the deal, participating artists will receive a share of revenue generated from fan-made content.

The announcement came during Spotify's first investor day in four years, where co-CEOs Gustav Söderström and Alex Norström outlined the company's direction. The partnership represents a significant step in integrating generative AI into Spotify's platform while addressing rights concerns that have complicated AI adoption across the music industry.

The deal signals a potential model for how major labels and streaming services might collaborate on AI music tools—a space that has faced legal and licensing uncertainty. By securing Universal's participation, Spotify gains legitimacy with one of the world's largest music publishers and removes a major barrier to rolling out AI features at scale.

Watch for announcements from other major labels about similar arrangements, and whether other streaming platforms pursue comparable partnerships.
