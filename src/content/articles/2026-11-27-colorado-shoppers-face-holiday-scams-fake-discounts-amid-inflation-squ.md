# Holiday Shopping Season: Beware Scams and Fake Deals as Inflation Squeezes Colorado Wallets

Coloradans heading into Black Friday and Cyber Monday should watch their wallets twice—once for scammers, once for inflation masquerading as savings.

Consumer groups are flagging a surge in holiday fraud, including fake discounts, tampered gift cards, and phishing delivery messages designed to steal personal information and payment data (9News Denver). The scams are particularly effective because they exploit the shopping frenzy and time pressure of the season.

But there's a deeper problem: even legitimate retailers are playing games. A marketing professor cited by Fox News warns that many advertised "Black Friday deals" aren't real discounts at all. Instead, retailers use shrinkflation—smaller products at the same price—and inflate baseline prices before the sale to create the illusion of savings. With inflation still eating into Colorado household budgets, shoppers are more vulnerable to these tactics than ever.

The result: consumers think they're getting deals when they're actually paying more for less, or falling victim to outright theft.

**What to watch:** Colorado Attorney General's office enforcement actions against retailers engaging in deceptive pricing practices, and whether state consumer protection laws adequately address shrinkflation tactics.
