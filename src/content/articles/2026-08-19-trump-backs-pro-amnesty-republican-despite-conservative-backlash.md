# Trump Endorses Florida Rep. Salazar Despite Her Pro-Amnesty Record

President Trump has endorsed Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-Fla.) for reelection, calling her "fantastic," even as conservative outlets flag her as a leading voice for mass amnesty within Republican ranks. The Daily Caller reports Trump's endorsement contradicts his hardline immigration stance, noting Salazar "routinely undermines" the president on border security and deportation policy.

Conservatives argue the endorsement signals troubling compromise on immigration—a core Trump issue. The Daily Wire notes Salazar is "at the center of the amnesty fight" on the Right, suggesting internal GOP fracture over legal status for undocumented immigrants.

For Colorado, this matters: immigration policy directly affects labor markets, housing costs, and public services. Colorado Republicans have largely aligned with Trump's border-first agenda, making Salazar's amnesty push a potential model conservatives here want to avoid.

The endorsement raises questions about whether Trump will enforce immigration discipline within his party or tolerate open-borders Republicans under the GOP banner. Watch whether Colorado's Republican delegation distances itself from Salazar's position or follows Trump's lead.
