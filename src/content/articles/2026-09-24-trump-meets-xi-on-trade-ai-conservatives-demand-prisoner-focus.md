# Trump Meets Xi on Trade, AI—But Colorado Should Watch the China Angle

President Trump says he had a "great meeting" with Chinese leader Xi Jinping, with bilateral talks focused on trade and artificial intelligence (Just The News). Trump has also floated rebranding AI as "super intelligence" rather than "artificial intelligence" during the discussions.

While the White House frames this as a cooperation opportunity, conservatives argue the agenda should prioritize harder issues. The Daily Wire notes that securing the release of political and religious prisoners held by the Chinese Communist Party should be a top Trump administration priority—not buried beneath trade pleasantries.

For Colorado, the stakes are real. China's theft of intellectual property and forced tech transfers have cost American energy companies, manufacturers, and defense contractors billions. Colorado's aerospace, tech, and energy sectors depend on protecting proprietary innovation from Beijing's predatory practices. Republicans and conservatives worry that focusing talks narrowly on "AI cooperation" risks legitimizing Chinese tech dominance while ignoring human rights abuses and espionage.

The Trump administration's willingness to engage directly is a departure from Biden-era posturing, but Colorado taxpayers and business owners should demand clarity: Are we trading away IP security and values for a photo op?

Watch whether Trump's team secures concrete commitments on prisoner releases and IP protection—or settles for vague "cooperation" language that leaves American innovators exposed.
