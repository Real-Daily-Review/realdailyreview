# U.S. Awaits Iran's Response to Peace Proposal as Regional Tensions Persist

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Friday the U.S. is expecting Iran to respond to Washington's proposal to end the war, according to CNBC. The timeline for Tehran's reply remained unclear, though Rubio suggested a response could come imminently.

The diplomatic overture comes amid continued fighting in the region. Israel killed 31 people in southern Lebanon on Friday, including a rescue worker, according to Lebanon's National News Agency, as reported by Al Jazeera. Clashes were also reported in the Strait of Hormuz, a critical shipping chokepoint.

The U.S. proposal represents a potential shift toward negotiated settlement after months of escalating tensions. However, Iran has not yet publicly committed to engaging with the terms, and the specifics of Washington's offer remain undisclosed in available reporting.

Watch for Iran's formal response and whether any breakthrough in talks can reduce military operations across the region.
