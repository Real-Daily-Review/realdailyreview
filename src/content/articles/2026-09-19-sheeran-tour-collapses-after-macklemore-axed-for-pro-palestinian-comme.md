# Ed Sheeran's Tour Implodes After Macklemore Removal Over Pro-Palestinian Remarks

The mass exodus of supporting acts leaves the headliner scrambling to salvage what was supposed to be a full bill at Philadelphia's Lincoln Financial Field (9News Denver).
