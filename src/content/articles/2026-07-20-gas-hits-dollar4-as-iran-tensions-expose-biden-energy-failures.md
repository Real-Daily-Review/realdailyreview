# Gas Hits $4 Again as Biden Energy Policy Leaves Americans Vulnerable to Geopolitical Shocks

National gas prices have climbed back to $4 per gallon, according to AAA data cited by 9News Denver, as Middle East tensions with Iran escalate. The New York Post reports the average had dipped in mid-June but has since rebounded sharply.

The price spike exposes a hard truth conservatives have warned about for years: dependence on unstable global energy markets leaves American families and businesses hostage to foreign conflicts. When the Biden administration killed the Keystone XL pipeline, restricted domestic drilling permits, and strangled energy development on federal lands, it surrendered energy independence for ideological purity.

Regional disparities tell the story. Just The News notes Indiana drivers pay $3.35 per gallon while California—strangled by its own progressive regulations—faces $5.50. Colorado sits somewhere in between, but energy workers and families filling up trucks and RVs feel every penny of this squeeze.

Republicans argue that ramped-up domestic oil and natural gas production would buffer Americans from Middle East volatility. Democrats counter that renewable energy transition is the answer—but that transition won't happen overnight, and it won't lower your gas bill next week.
