# Trump's DC Golf Course Renovation Blocked by Biden Judge—For Now

A Biden-appointed federal judge has slapped a temporary restraining order on President Trump's plan to renovate the East Potomac Golf Course in Washington, D.C., blocking closure of the course and surrounding park for two weeks (Washington Examiner, Breitbart).

U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes issued the order Saturday, preventing the Trump administration's Interior Department from proceeding with the redevelopment. The ruling represents the kind of judicial obstruction conservatives have long warned about: unelected judges appointed by the previous administration using procedural delays to block executive initiatives.

Republicans argue that Trump, as president, has clear authority to manage federal properties and pursue infrastructure improvements that benefit the public. The temporary restraining order is a tactical delay tactic—a two-week holding pattern that could stretch into months of litigation, tying up the administration's agenda in courtroom battles rather than allowing it to govern.

The broader pattern is familiar to Colorado conservatives: progressive judges appointed during Democratic administrations routinely block Republican policy through injunctions and restraining orders, weaponizing the courts to override electoral outcomes. Whether it's energy development, land management, or now golf course renovation, the playbook remains consistent.
