# America 250: Republicans Celebrate Freedom, Work, and Sacrifice Ahead of Semiquincentennial

Colorado conservatives should take note: as America marks 250 years, Republican leaders are reminding voters what made this nation exceptional—and what's at stake if progressive policies continue eroding those foundations.

Sen. James Lankford (R-OK) highlighted "freedom of faith" as a radical concept that became America's cornerstone, while Senate Majority Whip John Barrasso (R-WY) credited the Armed Forces with preserving the freedoms Americans enjoy today (Breitbart). The message is clear: constitutional liberty and national defense aren't abstractions—they're earned through sacrifice and vigilance.

Meanwhile, Mike Rowe's "Build Freedom" initiative, covered by the Washington Examiner, aims to restore dignity to American work and skilled trades. Standing on the banks of the Monongahela River near Pittsburgh, Rowe reflects on a nation that once built things—a stark contrast to today's progressive obsession with deindustrialization and green mandates that have hollowed out manufacturing communities across the country, including Colorado's energy sector.

For Coloradans, the subtext matters: while Democrats push climate regulations that strangle oil, gas, and coal jobs, Republicans are celebrating the work ethic and self-reliance that built America. The question isn't nostalgia—it's whether Colorado will protect the industries and freedoms that sustain working families.
