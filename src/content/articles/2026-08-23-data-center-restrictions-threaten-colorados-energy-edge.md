# Data Center Boom Faces Regulatory Headwinds as States Restrict AI Infrastructure

Colorado's energy advantage could be threatened by the same anti-development sentiment sweeping blue states. Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro recently signed an executive order restricting data center development, prompting even centrist Democrat Sen. John Fetterman to push back, arguing that "AI supremacy and energy dominance underpins our national security" (Washington Examiner). Yet National Review reports that despite widespread public anxiety about data centers, regulatory opposition persists regardless of the evidence presented.

**The Colorado angle:** Data centers represent a genuine economic opportunity for the state—they create jobs, generate tax revenue, and leverage Colorado's abundant hydroelectric power and cool climate. But if progressive governors elsewhere succeed in blocking development through executive fiat, Colorado could face similar pressure from environmental groups and anti-growth activists who view AI infrastructure as a threat.

**The conservative case:** Republicans and industry advocates argue that restricting data centers amounts to unilateral disarmament in the AI race. Fetterman's willingness to break ranks with his governor signals that even Democrats recognize the folly of ceding technological and energy leadership to competitors. Blocking development doesn't stop AI—it simply moves infrastructure (and jobs) elsewhere.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership follows Shapiro's restrictive model or embraces the economic upside of responsible data center development.
