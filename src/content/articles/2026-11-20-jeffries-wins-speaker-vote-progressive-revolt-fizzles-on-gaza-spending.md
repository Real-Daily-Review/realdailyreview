# Jeffries Wins House Democrats' Speaker Vote Despite Progressive Revolt Over Spending, Gaza

House Democrats coalesced behind Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker-designate with 251 of 262 secret ballots, crushing a progressive revolt that demanded floor votes on ICE enforcement, Gaza policy, and Citizens United (Fox News Politics). Eleven progressive holdouts staged the rebellion, but their leverage evaporated—a stark reminder that Democratic unity trumps ideological purity when leadership is at stake.

The real story for Colorado taxpayers: Jeffries has committed to "massively increase entitlements," according to National Review, a reckless agenda that will balloon federal spending and debt while working families foot the bill. Republicans argue his spending blueprint contradicts any serious fiscal restraint and will fuel inflation that already hammers Colorado's middle class and retirees on fixed incomes.

Meanwhile, progressive Democrats who thundered about getting tough with Israel on Gaza folded anyway, backing Jeffries despite his pro-Israel record (Daily Caller). The left's moral grandstanding collapsed the moment party leadership was secured—a pattern Colorado voters should remember when Democrats campaign on "fighting for you."
