# White House Fractures with MAGA Allies Over Iran Deal

The Trump administration is turning on its own conservative base over a controversial Iran nuclear agreement, signaling a dangerous split within the right at a moment when unity matters most.

According to the *Daily Caller* and *New York Post*, prominent MAGA influencers and conservative commentators—including Batya Ungar-Sargon and David Reaboi—have become targets of White House hostility after publicly opposing the Iran deal. One administration official reportedly expressed "utter contempt" for the influencers, marking an extraordinary breach between a president and his core supporters.

**The conservative case against the deal is straightforward:** Republicans argue the agreement repeats the failures of the Obama-era JCPOA by enriching Iran, weakening U.S. leverage, and threatening Israel. Conservative media outlets have hammered the White House for capitulating to Tehran rather than maintaining maximum pressure.

The *Washington Examiner* gave the administration failing grades this week, citing the Iran deal as a key reason for the poor performance.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this matters: a weakened Middle East posture invites instability that drives energy prices higher and complicates domestic energy independence—directly hitting household budgets and Colorado's oil and gas sector.
