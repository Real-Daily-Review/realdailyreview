# California Halloween Store Shooting Kills 1, Injures 6; FBI Joins Manhunt

Another mass shooting has struck California—this time at a Spirit Halloween store in Fairfield near the Bay Area—underscoring the state's failure to protect citizens despite some of the nation's strictest gun laws.

One person was killed and at least six others wounded Friday night in the attack (9News Denver). The Fairfield Police Department has zeroed in on a single suspect as investigators work the case, with the FBI now deployed to assist (New York Post).

**The Colorado angle:** While California has spent decades imposing magazine bans, background-check expansions, and waiting periods, violent crime persists. Colorado conservatives have long argued that criminals don't follow gun laws—they follow their own. This incident reinforces that argument: California's regulatory approach hasn't stopped determined shooters.

Democrats typically respond to such tragedies by calling for *more* restrictions on law-abiding gun owners. Republicans counter that the focus should be on prosecuting violent criminals, securing public spaces, and addressing mental health and gang violence—not disarming citizens who follow the law.

For Coloradans, the lesson is clear: no amount of progressive gun control prevents determined criminals from obtaining weapons or committing violence. The real question is whether law enforcement can catch suspects quickly—and whether citizens retain the right to defend themselves.

Watch for how California's political class responds: expect calls for additional gun restrictions rather than accountability for failed public safety.
