# Trump Education Department Rolls Back Biden's Transgender Title IX Protections

The Trump administration's Education Department has finalized revised Title IX rules that scrap Biden-era language protecting students based on gender identity and sexual orientation—a move conservatives say restores common sense to federal education policy.

Education Secretary Linda McMahon announced the rescission Monday, following a federal court decision that struck down the 2024 Biden regulations. The final rules are set for formal publication Tuesday (Just The News, Washington Examiner).

**The conservative argument:** Republicans contend that Title IX, enacted in 1972 to address sex-based discrimination, was being weaponized by the Biden administration to impose ideological mandates on schools. Conservatives argue the original statute addressed biological sex, not gender identity, and that schools should focus on academics rather than social engineering. The rollback aligns with Trump's broader push to limit federal overreach in education.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** The state's progressive-led education establishment has embraced gender-identity policies in schools. This federal shift could create friction with Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature and education bureaucracy, potentially forcing Colorado policymakers to choose between federal compliance and their ideological commitments—or sparking litigation over state versus federal authority.

Watch for Colorado Democrats' response and whether the state attempts to maintain Biden-era protections through state law, forcing Colorado taxpayers into another culture-war funding battle.
