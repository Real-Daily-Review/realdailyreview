# Democratic Primary Spending Spiral Tests Colorado Voters' Patience

Colorado Democrats are drowning their own primary in cash, and voters are fed up. The Denver Post reports that scorched-earth spending in the race for legislative seats has left ordinary Coloradans cynical about politics itself—one Uptown voter told the paper the money chase has made him question whether his vote matters at all.

The spending blitz reflects a deeper ideological battle within the Democratic Party. The Daily Caller notes that democratic socialists have notched major victories in recent cycles, signaling a leftward shift that's forcing establishment Democrats to spend heavily just to compete in their own primary.

Here's the Colorado angle: while national Democrats battle over the party's direction, Colorado taxpayers are watching their state legislature become a proving ground for increasingly expensive political warfare. The primary spending arms race doesn't just drain candidate coffers—it sets expectations for general election spending that ultimately gets passed along to voters through policy.

Republicans see an opening. While Democrats cannibalize each other's resources and credibility, the GOP can focus on core messaging about fiscal responsibility and limited government—messages that resonate when voters are already disgusted by political money-grubbing.

Watch whether this primary spending backlash carries into the general election and whether Colorado Republicans capitalize on voter frustration over campaign finance excess.
