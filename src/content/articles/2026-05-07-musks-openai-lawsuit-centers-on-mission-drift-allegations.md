# OpenAI Trial Centers on Musk's Allegations of Mission Drift

Elon Musk's high-stakes lawsuit against OpenAI is underway, with testimony revealing details about the billionaire's early vision for the AI startup. Shivon Zilis, a former OpenAI board member who has four children with Musk, testified Wednesday that Musk offered Sam Altman a Tesla board seat, according to CNBC, suggesting he wanted OpenAI to align more closely with his electric vehicle company.

Musk filed the lawsuit in 2024, accusing OpenAI of abandoning its founding mission to develop AI for humanity's benefit and instead prioritizing profits, according to The Verge. The trial could significantly alter OpenAI's future and that of ChatGPT, its widely used product.

Testimony has already been given by Musk, his financial manager Jared Birchall, and OpenAI cofounder Greg Brockman. According to TechCrunch, the case may ultimately hinge on whether OpenAI's for-profit subsidiary enhances or undermines the organization's original commitment to ensuring humanity benefits from artificial general intelligence.

The trial represents a rare public reckoning over OpenAI's transition from nonprofit research lab to a hybrid structure with significant commercial operations—a shift that has drawn scrutiny from its co-founder.

Watch for closing arguments and the jury's verdict on whether OpenAI violated its founding principles.
