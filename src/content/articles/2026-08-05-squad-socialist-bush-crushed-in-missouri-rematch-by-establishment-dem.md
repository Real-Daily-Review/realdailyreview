# Squad Socialist's Comeback Bid Crushed by Establishment Democrats

Missouri Democrats have dealt a decisive blow to the far-left insurgency, with Rep. Wesley Bell decisively defeating former "Squad" member Cori Bush in their rematch for Missouri's 1st Congressional District. This marks the second consecutive victory for Bell over Bush, signaling that even deep-blue districts are rejecting the DSA-aligned socialist wing of the Democratic Party.

Bush, a prominent voice for defunding police and radical progressive causes, lost her seat to Bell two years ago and attempted a comeback on Tuesday. With commanding margins reported early, Bell's victory demonstrates that establishment Democrats—not socialists—still control the party machinery in safe seats (Fox News Politics, The Daily Wire).

Bush's defeat suggests voters care more about competence and public safety than radical ideology—a message that resonates far beyond Missouri.
