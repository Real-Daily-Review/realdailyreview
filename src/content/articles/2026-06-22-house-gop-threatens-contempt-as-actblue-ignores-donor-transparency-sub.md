# House Republicans Threaten Contempt Over ActBlue's Refusal to Answer Subpoenas

House Republicans are escalating pressure on ActBlue, the Democratic Party's dominant online fundraising platform, after the company ignored congressional subpoenas demanding transparency on its fraud-prevention practices and donor vetting procedures.

Republicans argue ActBlue has stonewalled legitimate oversight by claiming baseless privilege protections, according to the *Daily Caller*. The GOP is now threatening contempt charges—a serious escalation in the standoff.

The scrutiny centers on reports that foreign nationals have used ActBlue to funnel money to Democratic candidates, raising questions about whether the platform adequately screens donors for legal eligibility. *Just The News* reports lawmakers have grown increasingly concerned about ActBlue's fraud-prevention methods.

**The Colorado angle:** ActBlue processes donations to Colorado Democrats and progressive causes. If the platform lacks robust donor verification, Colorado's campaign finance integrity—and federal law compliance—could be compromised. Voters deserve assurance that money flowing into state races meets legal requirements.

Democrats have not publicly addressed the specific allegations; the company's silence itself has become the story.

Conservatives say this stonewalling exemplifies how progressive institutions resist accountability when their fundraising mechanics face scrutiny. Watch whether House Republicans follow through on contempt charges and whether ActBlue finally produces the subpoenaed documents.
