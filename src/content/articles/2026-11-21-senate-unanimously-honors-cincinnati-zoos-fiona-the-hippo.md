# Senate Passes Unanimous Resolution Honoring Cincinnati Zoo's Fiona the Hippo

Ohio Republican Sen. Bernie Moreno introduced a bipartisan resolution this week honoring Fiona, the beloved Nile hippopotamus at the Cincinnati Zoo who died Thursday following a medical emergency. The Senate unanimously passed the tribute, recognizing Fiona as "a symbol of resilience and perseverance" and praising the zoo staff's dedication to her care.

According to Fox News and the Washington Examiner, Moreno brought the resolution to the Senate floor to commemorate the hippo's life and the work of Cincinnati Zoo personnel. The unanimous passage reflects rare bipartisan agreement in a polarized Congress—a moment of levity and shared sentiment across party lines.

Fiona had become a cultural icon since her premature birth in 2017, drawing visitors and attention to the zoo's conservation efforts. Her death marks the end of an era for the institution and its supporters nationwide.

While the resolution carries no fiscal impact, it underscores how Congress occasionally sets aside partisan gridlock for ceremonial tributes. Coloradans watching their own state's wildlife management and zoo operations may note this as a reminder that bipartisan support for animal welfare and public institutions remains possible—even if such unity proves elusive on weightier fiscal and regulatory matters.
