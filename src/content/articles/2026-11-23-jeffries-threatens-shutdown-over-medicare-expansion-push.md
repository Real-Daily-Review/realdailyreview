# Democratic Leader Signals Government Shutdown Threat Over Medicare Expansion

House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries is already threatening to shut down the government to force through a Medicare expansion—a reckless gambit that would hold taxpayers hostage to progressive spending ambitions.

In recent interviews, Jeffries signaled willingness to weaponize government funding deadlines if Republicans resist Democratic demands, according to *The Daily Wire*. The threat centers on a proposed Medicare expansion that *National Review* warns would balloon federal spending and entrench another entitlement program.

Republicans argue the move exemplifies Democratic priorities: expand government, raise costs on working Americans, and use procedural threats to bypass fiscal restraint. *National Review* editors contend Republicans "should be prepared to fight it at all costs, even if President Trump is game"—a signal that GOP unity on spending restraint cannot be assumed.

For Colorado, this matters directly. Medicare expansion means higher payroll taxes on Colorado workers and businesses, while federal debt service crowds out resources for infrastructure, defense, and economic growth. Colorado's energy sector and small businesses already face regulatory headwinds; new entitlement spending will only increase pressure for tax hikes or energy restrictions to fund Washington's ambitions.

Watch whether Republican leadership holds firm on fiscal boundaries or caves to shutdown threats—a test of whether the party will actually constrain government growth.
