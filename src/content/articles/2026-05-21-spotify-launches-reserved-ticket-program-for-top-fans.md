# Spotify Reserves Concert Tickets for Top Fans

Spotify is launching a "Reserved" program to allocate concert tickets to its most engaged users, according to The Verge. The streaming service will identify top fans based on metrics including streams, shares, and other activity on its platform, then offer them presale access to shows from participating artists.

The initiative aims to reduce the friction and stress typically associated with securing tickets for popular acts, according to TechCrunch. However, eligibility comes with a requirement: users must hold Spotify Premium memberships to participate in the program.

The criteria for determining who qualifies as a "real fan" remains somewhat opaque. Spotify has outlined that it will monitor streaming behavior and engagement, but the exact algorithmic weighting of these factors is unclear. The company has not disclosed which artists or venues will participate initially, or when the program will launch broadly.

The move reflects Spotify's broader push to deepen artist-fan relationships and drive platform engagement beyond music streaming itself. Watch for announcements about which major touring acts will participate first, and whether other streaming services adopt similar presale models.
