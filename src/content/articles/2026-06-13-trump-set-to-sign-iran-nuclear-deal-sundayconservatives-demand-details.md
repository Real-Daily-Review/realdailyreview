# Trump Poised to Sign Iran Nuclear Deal—But Details Remain Murky

President Trump says he'll sign a new Iran nuclear agreement Sunday, June 14, promising it will reopen the Strait of Hormuz and block Tehran's nuclear ambitions. According to the Washington Examiner, Trump declared the deal would create a "WALL TO NO NUCLEAR WEAPON," signaling a hardline approach to Iran's weapons program.

Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif told reporters Saturday that a Middle East peace deal is "closer than ever before" and expected finalization imminently (KDVR/AP).

**What conservatives should demand:** Republicans have long been skeptical of Iran deals—the 2015 JCPOA was widely criticized by the right as toothless and insufficiently verified. Before celebrating, conservatives need specifics: Does this agreement include robust inspections? Are sanctions relief conditions ironclad? Does it truly prevent nuclear weapons development, or does it repeat the mistakes of the Obama-era accord?

Colorado energy workers and taxpayers should watch whether any deal affects U.S. oil markets, gas prices, or domestic energy independence—issues that directly hit Colorado's economy and household budgets.

The full text and verification mechanisms must be public before judgment. Coloradans deserve to know exactly what their government is signing away.

**Watch for:** The actual deal text and Republican verification that inspections are truly enforceable.
