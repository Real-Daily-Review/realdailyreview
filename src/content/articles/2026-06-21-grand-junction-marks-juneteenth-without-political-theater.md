# Grand Junction Marks Juneteenth with Community Celebration

Grand Junction is hosting its annual Juneteenth celebration Saturday at Lincoln Park—a straightforward community event highlighting local history, games, and food without the heavy-handed progressive politics that often hijack such occasions elsewhere.

The gathering, covered by CPR News, brings together residents to commemorate the June 19, 1865 emancipation of enslaved people in Galveston, Texas. The event features family-friendly activities and historical programming rooted in actual American history rather than revisionist narratives.

Golden State Warriors guard Moses Moody is among notable attendees, according to the New York Post, showcasing talents beyond the basketball court at the celebration.

For Colorado conservatives, this is a reminder that communities can honor historical milestones authentically—celebrating freedom and American progress—without the divisive identity politics that dominate progressive-controlled urban centers. Grand Junction's approach respects the significance of emancipation while keeping the focus on unity and shared civic values rather than grievance narratives.

Watch whether local organizers maintain this balanced, history-focused approach or whether future iterations drift toward the activist messaging that has consumed similar events in Denver and Boulder.
