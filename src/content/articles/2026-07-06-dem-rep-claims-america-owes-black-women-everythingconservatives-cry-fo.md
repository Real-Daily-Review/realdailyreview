# Rep. Crockett's 'America Owes Everything' Claim Reignites Divisive Identity Politics

A Texas Democrat's Fourth of July weekend declaration that America owes Black women "everything" has reignited the left's identity-politics playbook—and drawn sharp conservative pushback on what should be a unifying national holiday.

Rep. Jasmine Crockett made the remarks at the Essence Festival, according to Fox News and the Daily Caller, sparking fierce online backlash. The comment exemplifies how progressive politicians increasingly frame American gratitude and obligation through racial and gender categories rather than shared citizenship.

Conservatives argue this framing divides rather than unites. Republicans counter that America's debt is owed to all citizens who built the nation—regardless of race or gender—and that reducing national gratitude to demographic grievance undermines the civic bonds that hold the country together. The left's obsession with identity-based claims of reparation and special obligation, conservatives say, replaces merit and individual achievement with group-based entitlements.

While Crockett represents Texas, her rhetoric reflects the progressive ideology increasingly dominant in Democratic circles—ideology that shapes policy debates affecting Colorado taxpayers, from DEI mandates in state government to identity-focused spending priorities.
