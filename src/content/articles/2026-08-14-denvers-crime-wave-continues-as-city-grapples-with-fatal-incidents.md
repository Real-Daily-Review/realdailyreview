# Denver's Public Safety Crisis Deepens as City Struggles With Crime and Disorder

Denver's deteriorating public safety situation worsened Thursday with a fatal crash in Park Hill and a shooting death in West Denver, underscoring the city's inability to maintain basic order and protect residents (9News Denver).

A 19-year-old was killed in a shooting on the 4100 block of West Custer Place Thursday night, while a separate fatal crash occurred near East 17th Avenue Parkway and Glencoe Street in the Park Hill neighborhood (9News Denver). The incidents reflect an ongoing pattern of violence in Colorado's capital.

Adding to neighborhood frustrations, Denver residents are now battling a license application for an escort service in their community—a fight that reveals how permissive city policies have enabled commercial disorder (KDVR Fox31 Denver). Conservatives argue that Denver's progressive approach to crime, combined with lenient enforcement and regulatory decisions that prioritize business licensing over neighborhood quality of life, has created an environment where criminals operate with impunity and residents lose faith in city government.

Republicans and neighborhood advocates contend that Denver's leadership has failed to prioritize public safety, instead focusing on progressive social experiments while violent crime and quality-of-life issues plague residents.

Watch for whether Denver's next city budget prioritizes police staffing and enforcement—or continues the progressive pattern of defunding and de-prioritizing law enforcement.
