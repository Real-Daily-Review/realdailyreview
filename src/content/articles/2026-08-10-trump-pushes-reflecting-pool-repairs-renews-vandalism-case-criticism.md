# Trump Pushes Reflecting Pool Reopening as Vandalism Case Drags On

President Trump is pressing forward with repairs to the Lincoln Memorial's Reflecting Pool while continuing to spotlight what he views as prosecutorial failures in the vandalism case against former Olympian David Hearn. Trump shared court documents on Truth Social citing a National Park Service employee's account alleging Hearn "violently" damaged the pool, and announced Sunday that security cameras have been installed with reopening "shortly" expected (Washington Examiner).

The case has become a flashpoint in Trump's broader criticism of the D.C. U.S. Attorney's office. Trump previously stated that U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro "choked" by dismissing the indictment against Hearn, signaling his frustration with what he sees as weak prosecution of the incident.

**The conservative angle:** While this is primarily a D.C. story, it reflects broader Republican concerns about inconsistent law enforcement and prosecutorial discretion under the Biden-era Justice Department—issues that resonate with Colorado voters skeptical of federal overreach and selective justice.

Conservatives should watch whether the case is ultimately refiled and how Trump's Justice Department handles similar vandalism or property-damage cases going forward.
