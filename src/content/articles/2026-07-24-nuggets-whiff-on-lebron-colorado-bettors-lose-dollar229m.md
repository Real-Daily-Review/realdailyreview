# Nuggets Strike Out as LeBron Heads to Philly—and Colorado Bettors Lose Big

Denver's championship hopes took a hit this offseason when LeBron James spurned the Nuggets and signed with the Philadelphia 76ers on a two-year, $8 million deal with a player option, according to ESPN reporting cited by KDVR Fox31 Denver.

The move caps months of speculation that had the defending champs in the mix for the superstar. James is betting on breaking Philadelphia's 43-year championship drought, leaving Colorado fans and the Nuggets' front office empty-handed.

The real story for Colorado? The betting angle. According to the New York Post, the "Last Decision" generated a staggering $229 million in speculative wagers this offseason as bettors tried to predict James's destination. While some programmers and sharp bettors cashed in big on the surprise Philly signing, most of that $229 million represents losses for everyday Colorado sports bettors who got it wrong.

It's a reminder that sports betting—now legal and aggressively marketed across Colorado—can drain wallets fast when the odds shift unexpectedly. The Nuggets will have to chase another title without the aging superstar, and Colorado's betting public learned an expensive lesson about the house edge.
