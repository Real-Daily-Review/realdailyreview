# Democrats' Open-Border Policies Put Americans at Risk—Colorado Must Demand Better

Colorado conservatives are watching a national catastrophe unfold: Democratic-led sanctuary policies are enabling criminal illegal immigrants to prey on American citizens with impunity. From New York's Chinatown murder of Jin Yu to animal torture cases in Florida, the pattern is unmistakable—and it's a direct result of progressive refusal to enforce immigration law.

The Washington Examiner reports that Democrats prioritize keeping illegal immigrants in the country over protecting American lives. Former White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt (via Breitbart) has called out the hypocrisy: while celebrities visit immigration courts for photo ops, "Angel Families"—Americans who lost loved ones to illegal alien criminals—are ignored and forgotten.

Colorado taxpayers foot the bill for this chaos. Illegal immigrants convicted of crimes strain our courts, prisons, and social services—costs borne by hardworking Coloradans. Meanwhile, our southern border remains a revolving door.
