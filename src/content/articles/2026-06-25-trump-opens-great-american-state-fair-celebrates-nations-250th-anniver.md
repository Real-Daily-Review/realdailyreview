# Trump Opens 'Great American State Fair' on National Mall, Celebrates Nation's 250th

Trump kicked off the Great American State Fair on the National Mall Wednesday night, using the 250th anniversary celebration to reinforce his political message and patriotic themes.

Speaking 10 days before Independence Day, the president delivered remarks comparing his movement to the American Revolution while praising the nation's founding ideals (Washington Examiner). The event featured military flyovers and transformed the National Mall into an exhibition ground complete with a Ferris wheel and other attractions (9News Denver).

Trump used the commencement to reprise central themes of his presidency, framing his political movement within the broader context of American history and national renewal (Washington Examiner). Fox News reported Trump declared "America is back," emphasizing restoration of American strength and values.

The Great American State Fair runs through the Fourth of July weekend, serving as the centerpiece of Washington's Independence Day celebrations.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether this patriotic messaging and focus on American founding principles resonates with Colorado voters heading into the 2024 cycle, particularly among independents and suburban voters who've drifted from Republican support in recent years.
