# Pakistan Car Bomb Attack Kills at Least 12 Police Officers

A car bomb detonated near a security checkpoint in northwest Pakistan on Friday, killing at least 12 police officers in a coordinated assault. According to Al Jazeera, the attack occurred in Bannu, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province, where a bomber drove an explosives-laden vehicle toward a security post before detonating it. Several fighters then launched an ambush on the site, Deutsche Welle reported.

The incident marks another deadly strike in Pakistan's volatile northwest region, where militant groups have conducted periodic attacks on security forces. Bannu, located in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, has been a flashpoint for insurgent activity in recent years.

No group immediately claimed responsibility for the attack. Pakistani security officials have not yet released statements on the incident or indicated whether they have identified suspects.
