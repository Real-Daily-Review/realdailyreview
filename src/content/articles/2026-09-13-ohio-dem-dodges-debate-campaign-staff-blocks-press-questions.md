# Ohio Democrat Dodges Debate Challenge; Campaign Staff Shuts Down Press

An Ohio Democratic gubernatorial candidate's refusal to face her Republican opponent on stage is raising red flags about progressive politicians' willingness to defend their records in open debate.

Dr. Amy Acton's campaign handlers confronted a cameraman asking why she won't debate Republican Vivek Ramaswamy, with staff demanding "no pictures" and blocking further questioning (Fox News, New York Post). The incident underscores a broader pattern: when Democrats can't win on ideas, they retreat from public scrutiny.

Ramaswamy, a businessman and conservative, has called for open debate as a test of leadership. Acton's evasion suggests her progressive agenda—likely heavy on regulation and government expansion—can't withstand direct challenge from a free-market alternative.

**The Colorado angle:** Coloradans should watch how our own Democratic candidates handle debate invitations this cycle. Will they face tough questions on taxes, energy policy, and government overreach, or will they follow Acton's playbook of avoidance? Voters deserve to hear both sides defend their vision for the state's future without handlers running interference.

**What to watch:** Whether Acton ultimately accepts debate or continues dodging—and whether Colorado's left learns from her mistake or repeats it.
