# Russell Brand Convicted of Rape, Sexual Assault in London

A London jury has convicted actor and comedian Russell Brand of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, delivering a guilty verdict that caps a trial centered on allegations spanning more than two decades. The 51-year-old faces a "significant" prison sentence following the two-day jury deliberation at Southwark Crown Court (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire). Prosecutors characterized Brand as a "sexual predator" during proceedings (Daily Caller).

The conviction involved four women, though jurors acquitted Brand on two additional rape counts and one other charge (Daily Wire). The case marks a rare high-profile criminal conviction in the UK involving a prominent entertainer.

Adding to Brand's legal jeopardy, two additional rape accusations have surfaced following the conviction, with accusers describing him as a "danger to women" (New York Post). Brand's sentencing remains pending.
