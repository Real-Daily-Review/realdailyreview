# State Department Dumps DEI Ideology From Foreign Service

The Trump administration is scrubbing diversity, equity, and inclusion mandates from the State Department's diplomatic corps—a move conservatives say restores merit-based hiring and professional standards to American diplomacy.

The State Department announced it will overhaul Foreign Service personnel policies, eliminating DEI training protocols that (Washington Examiner) required employees to address "unconscious bias" and diplomats to discuss "allyship" in foreign negotiations. Just The News reports the DEI ideology had so thoroughly "infected" the department during the previous administration that it became embedded in institutional culture.

Republicans argue DEI programs prioritize identity politics over competence, wasting taxpayer dollars on ideological training while undermining diplomatic effectiveness. The administration's move signals a broader rollback of progressive personnel policies across federal agencies—a shift that could ripple through Colorado's federal workforce and contractor ecosystem.

Democrats and progressive advocates contend DEI initiatives improve workplace inclusion and address systemic bias, but conservatives counter that merit, not demographic checkboxes, should determine who represents America abroad.

**Watch for:** Whether other federal agencies follow suit, and how Colorado's Democratic congressional delegation responds to further DEI rollbacks in agencies with significant state presence.
