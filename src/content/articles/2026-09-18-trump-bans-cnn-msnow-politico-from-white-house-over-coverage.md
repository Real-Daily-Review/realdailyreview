# Trump Bans CNN, MSNOW, Politico From White House Over 'Fake News' Coverage

President Trump has barred CNN, MSNOW, and Politico from White House access, citing what he calls their repeated publication of false reporting. According to the Daily Wire and Just The News, Trump announced the ban effective immediately, accusing the three outlets of consistently pushing "FAKE NEWS" rather than factual coverage of his administration.

Trump has long feuded with these networks—particularly CNN—over their political coverage of his career. The Daily Caller reports Trump stated that "Media Outlets shouldn't be able to constantly write or report FICTION and LIES," framing the ban as a response to what conservatives view as systematic bias in mainstream journalism.

However, 9News Denver notes this move "is the administration's latest test of constitutional First Amendment protections," raising legal questions about government restrictions on press access. Critics will argue that barring outlets from the White House—regardless of editorial slant—sets a troubling precedent for executive power over the press.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether this escalates into broader media access restrictions or becomes a model other Republican administrations adopt.
