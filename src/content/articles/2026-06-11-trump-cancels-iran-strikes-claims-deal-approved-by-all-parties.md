# Trump Cancels Iran Strikes, Claims Deal "Approved by All Parties"

President Donald Trump has canceled planned military strikes against Iran, announcing via Truth Social that high-level diplomatic discussions have advanced and a deal to end the conflict has been "approved by all parties involved," with formal signing "to be announced shortly" (Fox News, Breitbart).

The reversal comes after months of Trump signaling a nuclear agreement was imminent. According to Fox News, Trump appears to be testing whether military pressure—combined with diplomacy—can succeed where negotiations alone have stalled. Trump has insisted the deal includes a ban on nuclear weapons development (Just The News).

The timing is significant: Trump renewed strikes this week after Iranian forces shot down an Apache helicopter, then pivoted to negotiations when Tehran signaled willingness to talk (Just The News).

**The conservative case:** Skeptics will want hard proof that Iran has genuinely committed to verifiable nuclear restrictions and hasn't simply bought time. The Obama-era JCPOA collapse taught that Iranian promises require ironclad enforcement mechanisms, not diplomatic theater.

**Colorado angle:** Any Iran deal affects energy markets and oil prices—critical for Colorado's economy and energy workers dependent on stable fuel costs.

Watch whether Trump releases the actual deal text and whether Congress demands a vote before any agreement takes effect.
