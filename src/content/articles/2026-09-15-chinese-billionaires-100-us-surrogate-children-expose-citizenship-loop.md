# Chinese Billionaire's U.S. Surrogate Army Exposes Birthright Citizenship Loophole

A Chinese billionaire has reportedly fathered over 100 children through American surrogates, exploiting U.S. birthright citizenship law to effectively purchase American citizenship for his offspring—a practice that should alarm Colorado taxpayers and policymakers alike.

According to CBS News reporting covered by *The Daily Wire* and *Daily Caller*, the scheme works like this: wealthy foreign nationals hire American women as surrogates, the children are born on U.S. soil, and instant citizenship follows. One surrogate told CBS she felt blindsided after learning the intended father's identity and scope of the operation. "It feels like he's creating an army," she said.

Republicans and conservatives argue this exposes a critical flaw in birthright citizenship policy: the 14th Amendment was never intended to grant automatic citizenship to children of foreign nationals simply because they were born here. The practice raises questions about anchor babies, chain migration, and whether America's citizenship should be effectively for sale to the highest bidder.

Democrats have largely remained silent on the issue, but the left generally opposes restricting birthright citizenship, viewing it as a humanitarian protection.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's schools, hospitals, and social services could absorb costs if such children remain in-state; state lawmakers should clarify whether Colorado has any legal recourse or reporting requirements.

**Watch:** Whether Congress moves to close this loophole or if states like Colorado pursue their own citizenship-related legislation.
