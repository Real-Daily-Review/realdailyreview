# Colorado Voters Face Tax Refund Extinction While Weighing Redistricting Reform

Colorado taxpayers may never see another TABOR refund under current spending trajectories, even as voters weigh Initiative #256 to impose new guardrails on congressional redistricting (Complete Colorado).

Proposition NN, a tax measure on the ballot, threatens to permanently alter Colorado's Taxpayer Bill of Rights by redirecting surplus revenue that would otherwise trigger automatic refunds. Conservatives argue the measure represents a backdoor assault on TABOR protections that have limited government growth for decades. The initiative would lock in higher spending baselines, making future refunds mathematically unlikely—a permanent tax hike by another name.

Meanwhile, Initiative #256 would ban congressional redistricting designed to favor either political party, imposing new procedural constraints on the process. Republicans and Democrats disagree on whether such guardrails strengthen democracy or create unintended consequences for representation.

For Colorado taxpayers already squeezed by inflation and rising property taxes, the TABOR threat is the more immediate concern. Losing refund protections would mean government keeps more of your money—permanently.

Watch whether voters reject Prop NN and whether Initiative #256's redistricting rules actually prevent partisan gerrymandering or simply shift advantage to one side.
