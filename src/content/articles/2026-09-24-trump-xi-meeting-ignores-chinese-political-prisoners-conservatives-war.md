# Trump Meets Xi: Trade and AI Talks Miss the Mark on Chinese Oppression

President Trump called his meeting with Chinese leader Xi Jinping "great," but conservatives say the administration missed a critical opportunity to demand Beijing release political and religious prisoners held by the Communist regime (The Daily Wire).

The bilateral talks focused on trade and artificial intelligence—Trump even floated rebranding AI as "super intelligence" (Just The News)—yet human rights advocates note the absence of serious pressure on China's systematic detention of political dissidents and religious minorities.

For Colorado, this matters. Chinese trade policies directly affect our agriculture, energy, and manufacturing sectors. Weak negotiating positions on intellectual property and forced technology transfer harm Colorado businesses and workers. Republicans have long argued that engaging China requires leveraging human rights concerns alongside economic demands; ceding moral authority undermines America's negotiating strength.

The Daily Wire's critique underscores a conservative concern: that transactional diplomacy without principled stands on freedom emboldens authoritarian regimes and abandons persecuted populations. Trump's team should clarify whether prisoner releases will be a condition of any trade deal.

**Watch whether the Trump administration ties future trade agreements to concrete Chinese concessions on religious freedom and political prisoners—or whether economic expediency wins out.**
