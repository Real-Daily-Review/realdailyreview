# ABC Challenges FCC Over "The View" Scrutiny, Citing Free Speech Concerns

ABC has filed a petition with the Federal Communications Commission claiming the agency's scrutiny of daytime talk show "The View" violates First Amendment protections, according to CBS News.

The network argues that the FCC's recent actions represent "major shifts in policy and practice" that threaten to "chill critical protected speech," according to The Verge. ABC is asking the FCC to reaffirm its long-standing exemption for bona fide news interviews on daytime talk shows and its support for public interest broadcasting.

The petition suggests ABC views the FCC's approach as a departure from established regulatory practice. The network contends that the agency's actions require action from the full Commission to clarify its stance on broadcast journalism protections.

The filing comes amid broader tensions between media outlets and federal regulators over editorial independence and government oversight of broadcast content. ABC has not detailed specific FCC actions it considers problematic, though the petition references a series of recent Commission moves.

Watch for the FCC's formal response to ABC's petition and whether the full Commission takes up the matter, which could set precedent for how federal regulators approach editorial decisions at broadcast networks.
