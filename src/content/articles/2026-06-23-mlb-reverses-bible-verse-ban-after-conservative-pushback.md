# MLB Backs Down on Bible Verse Ban After Conservative Pressure

Major League Baseball caved to public pressure and reversed course on threatening San Francisco Giants pitchers who wrote Bible verses on their Pride Night caps, vindicating conservatives who said the league was trampling religious expression.

MLB Commissioner Rob Manfred promised in a letter to Sen. Josh Hawley (R-Mo.) that the league would not fine or discipline the players, according to *The Daily Wire*. Hawley characterized the reversal as MLB admitting it was "wrong to threaten the Giants players over Bible verses," *Daily Caller* reports.

The incident exposed how corporate America increasingly polices speech in the name of progressive ideology. The Giants players faced potential discipline simply for expressing their Christian faith during a league-sanctioned event—a stark reminder that even America's pastime isn't immune to woke corporate overreach.

Conservatives have long warned that tolerance is a one-way street in corporate America: progressive causes get celebrated and protected, while religious expression gets threatened. This backdown, forced only by public outcry and political pressure, proves the point.
