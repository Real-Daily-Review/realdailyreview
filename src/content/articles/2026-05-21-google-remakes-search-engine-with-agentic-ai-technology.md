# Google Overhauls Search With AI-Powered Agents

Google is fundamentally reshaping its search engine with agentic artificial intelligence, marking a significant departure from its traditional keyword-based model, according to reporting from its I/O 2026 developer conference.

The shift builds on Google's existing AI Overview feature, which summarizes search results using generative AI. The new direction will push further into autonomous AI agents capable of performing tasks beyond summarizing information, according to Ars Technica.

The changes are prompting some users to explore alternatives. TechCrunch reports that dissatisfaction with AI Overviews is driving interest in competing search engines, with the publication highlighting six alternatives worth considering as Google's transformation accelerates.

The exact capabilities and rollout timeline for the agentic AI features remain unclear from available details. Google has not specified which tasks the new agents will handle or when they will become widely available to users.

The overhaul reflects Google's broader bet that AI-driven search will become the dominant paradigm, even as it risks alienating users who prefer traditional search results. Watch for announcements about specific agentic features and their availability in coming weeks.
