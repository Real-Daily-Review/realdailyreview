# Democrats' Midterm Win Sets Stage for Tax Hikes, Abortion Push That Could Hit Colorado Hard

Democrats' midterm victories have emboldened the party to pursue a sweeping agenda that threatens Colorado taxpayers and pro-life voters alike. According to *National Review*, if Democrats prevail in upcoming legislative battles, expect a push for a nationwide right to abortion—directly challenging Colorado's current restrictions and energizing the state's pro-life movement ahead of 2026. Meanwhile, *The Daily Wire* reports that Democratic control could resurrect billions in proposed tax increases, with the party targeting federal income tax rates and dismantling President Trump's tax-cut framework.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that higher federal taxes will squeeze Colorado families and small businesses already burdened by state-level spending. Pro-lifers warn that a national abortion mandate would override Colorado's existing laws and values held by many rural and conservative communities across the state.

**Democrats counter** they're targeting tax relief for lower-income families and expanding credits—a framing conservatives reject as cover for broader tax-and-spend expansion.

For Colorado, the stakes are clear: a Democratic Congress could force federal abortion policy onto the state while raiding household budgets through tax hikes. Watch closely whether Colorado's Republican delegation mounts effective opposition to these proposals in 2025 and beyond.
