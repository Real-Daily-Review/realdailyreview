# Chinese Bot Farm Exposed Pushing Anti-Data Center Lies to Block Colorado Energy Development

China is running a coordinated disinformation campaign to kill American AI data center projects—and Colorado is squarely in the crosshairs.

X uncovered approximately 200,000 inauthentic Chinese accounts flooding U.S. social media with false claims that AI data centers drive up household electricity prices and strain the grid, according to Fox News and Just The News. The bot farm deployed AI-generated cartoons depicting data-center operators as greedy profiteers enriching themselves at the public's expense.

The timing is no accident. Colorado is emerging as a prime location for major data center development, which promises jobs, tax revenue, and energy demand that benefits rural communities and power producers. Chinese competitors have every incentive to block U.S. technological advancement and energy infrastructure expansion.

National security experts are sounding alarms over the operation (Breitbart), recognizing it as foreign interference in American energy and technology policy debates. Republicans and conservatives argue this exposes how hostile foreign powers exploit American environmental and anti-corporate sentiment to sabotage domestic economic growth.

Democrats have largely echoed genuine concerns about grid capacity and electricity costs—concerns that, conveniently, align with the Chinese messaging. Whether by design or happenstance, the left's resistance to data center expansion serves Beijing's interests.

Coloradans should watch whether state regulators and lawmakers acknowledge this foreign influence campaign when evaluating data center permits and energy policy.
