# French Court Finds Airbus, Air France Guilty in 2009 Crash

A French court has convicted Airbus and Air France of manslaughter over a 2009 plane crash that killed 228 people, reversing acquittals issued in 2023, according to Al Jazeera.

The ruling marks a significant legal shift in the case surrounding Air France Flight 447, which crashed in the Atlantic Ocean en route from Rio de Janeiro to Paris. The court's decision overturned the previous year's not-guilty verdicts for both companies, according to BBC reporting.

Airbus has indicated it plans to appeal the conviction, Al Jazeera reported. The companies now face potential penalties following the manslaughter finding, though the sources do not specify sentencing details or financial damages at this stage.

The case has spanned years of litigation, with the 2023 acquittals representing a temporary legal victory for both firms before this week's reversal. The crash itself occurred in June 2009 and remains one of the deadliest aviation accidents involving an Airbus aircraft.

Watch for Airbus's formal appeal filing and any sentencing announcement from the French court in coming weeks.
