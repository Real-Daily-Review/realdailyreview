# Colorado Voters to Decide 'Right to Natural Gas' Constitutional Amendment

Colorado voters will decide this fall whether to enshrine a "right to natural gas" in the state constitution. Initiative 177, backed by Advance Colorado, qualified for the ballot according to the Colorado Secretary of State's office (Denver Post).

The measure represents a direct conservative pushback against progressive energy mandates and regulatory overreach. Republicans and energy advocates argue Colorado's economy and working families depend on reliable, affordable natural gas—and that the state shouldn't be forced into premature energy transitions that spike utility costs and kill jobs in oil and gas communities.

Democrats and environmental groups will likely counter that enshrining natural gas rights locks Colorado into fossil fuels and undermines climate commitments. The left claims such amendments prevent the state from transitioning to renewables and meeting emissions targets.

For Colorado taxpayers and energy workers, this is a critical fight. Natural gas heats homes, powers businesses, and supports thousands of jobs across the Western Slope and Front Range. A constitutional guarantee protects that industry from future legislative bans or regulatory strangulation—the kind of green-energy overreach that's already crippled California's grid and spiked electricity bills nationwide.

Watch whether environmental groups mount a well-funded campaign to defeat Initiative 177 before November.
