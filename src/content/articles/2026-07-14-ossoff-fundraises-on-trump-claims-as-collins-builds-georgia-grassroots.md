# Georgia Senate Race: Ossoff Leans on Trump Fearmongering as Collins Builds Grassroots Momentum

Sen. Jon Ossoff (D-GA) is mining the 2020 election grievance well again, using reports that Trump may revisit fraud claims to juice fundraising ahead of his November rematch against Republican Mike Collins, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Democrats have made this playbook their default: invoke Trump, activate the base, collect checks.

But here's what the mainstream narrative misses. While Ossoff enjoys a cash advantage, Collins is building genuine grassroots energy that traditional fundraising totals don't capture. The *Examiner's* opinion analysis notes that Georgia Republicans shouldn't concede the race simply because Ossoff's war chest looks bigger—grassroots engagement and voter enthusiasm often outpace raw dollars, especially in a cycle where Trump's presence energizes the GOP base.

For Colorado conservatives watching national races, this Georgia contest matters: it's a bellwether for whether Republicans can compete in purple states despite the left's media-amplified fundraising edge. Collins' path requires converting grassroots momentum into turnout, something the GOP has struggled with in recent cycles.

Watch whether Collins can close the fundraising gap while maintaining the enthusiasm advantage heading into fall.
