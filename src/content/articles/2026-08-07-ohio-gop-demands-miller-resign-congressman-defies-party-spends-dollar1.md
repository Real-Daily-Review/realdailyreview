# Ohio Republican Defies Party Pressure, Dumps $1M Into Doomed Reelection Bid

Rep. Max Miller (R-OH) is burning his own cash to stay in a race Republicans want him to abandon. The embattled congressman has pumped another $1 million of personal funds into his reelection campaign despite mounting domestic abuse allegations and growing calls from GOP leadership to step aside, according to Breitbart and The Daily Wire.

The pressure is intensifying. U.S. Sen. Jon Husted (R-OH) joined the chorus Thursday, publicly demanding Miller resign and end his campaign—a significant break from party silence (The Daily Wire). Sen. Bernie Moreno has also called for Miller's exit.

**Republicans argue** Miller's continued defiance undermines party credibility heading into the general election and wastes resources that could support stronger candidates in competitive races. The allegations and his refusal to withdraw create a liability that could drag down the ticket.

Miller, however, is showing no signs of backing down, betting his personal fortune that he can survive the storm.
