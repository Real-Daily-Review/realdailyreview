# Michigan Democrat Who Impeached Trump Loses Primary to Even More Progressive Challenger

Rep. Shri Thanedar, the Michigan Democrat who made national headlines by introducing articles of impeachment against President Trump early in 2025, has been ousted in his primary by a more left-wing challenger—a sign that even Trump-impeachment credentials aren't enough to satisfy the progressive base anymore.

Thanedar, who represented Michigan's 13th District, lost to an anti-establishment progressive opponent in what *Just The News* and the *Daily Caller* describe as a pivotal primary upset in the deep-blue district. The result underscores a broader Democratic Party dynamic: the party's activist wing is pushing candidates further left, not moderating them.

For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: Democrats' internal civil war between establishment and radical wings offers Republicans an opening. While Michigan's 13th is safely Democratic, similar primary dynamics are playing out in purple and swing districts nationwide—and Colorado's competitive races could benefit if progressive challengers pull Democratic nominees further from the center on taxes, energy, and border security.

The real question for Colorado taxpayers: Will our state's Democratic representatives face similar primary pressure from the left, and if so, will that push them toward even more aggressive spending and regulation?
