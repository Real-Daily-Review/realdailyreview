# Senegal's President Fires Prime Minister Sonko, Dissolves Government

Senegal's President Bassirou Diomaye Faye has dismissed Prime Minister Ousmane Sonko and dissolved the government, according to BBC World, marking an escalation of months-long tensions between the two leaders.

The move comes as Senegal grapples with a debt crisis. Faye, who took office in March, had previously clashed with Sonko over policy direction and governance. The dismissal creates immediate uncertainty about the country's political direction and economic management during a period of fiscal strain.

Supporters of the ousted prime minister have begun rallying outside his home in Dakar, according to Al Jazeera, signaling potential public backlash to the decision. The dissolution of the entire government means Faye will need to appoint a new cabinet, a process that could take weeks and further complicate efforts to address Senegal's debt challenges.

The rift between Faye and Sonko had been widening for months, though specific policy disagreements have not been detailed in available reporting. The timing of the dismissal—during an economic crisis—raises questions about whether the government can maintain stability while restructuring its leadership.

Watch for Faye's announcement of a new prime minister and cabinet composition, which will signal the direction of his administration's economic and political priorities.
