# Ohio Baby Shower Shooting Kills Three in Domestic Dispute

A shooting at a baby shower in Parma, Ohio left three dead and one wounded Saturday evening, authorities said, in what investigators are treating as a domestic dispute that escalated to gunfire.

According to reports, the incident occurred around 6 p.m. at Pandora's Magical Events. Those killed included a father-to-be, a grandfather, and a teenage girl, with a fourth person injured in the exchange of gunfire (Breitbart).

Details remain limited as investigators work to establish the sequence of events and motive. Ohio authorities have not yet released the shooter's identity or confirmed charges.

**The Colorado angle:** While this tragedy occurred out of state, it underscores a recurring conservative argument in the gun-rights debate: violent crime often stems from criminal behavior and domestic disputes—not lawful gun ownership. Second Amendment advocates argue that prosecuting criminals and enforcing existing laws matters far more than imposing new restrictions that burden law-abiding Coloradans and gun owners nationwide.

Progressives typically use such incidents to call for stricter gun regulations, though conservatives counter that criminals ignore gun laws by definition.
