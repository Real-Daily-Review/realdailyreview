# Trump Considers Firing Ambassador Guilfoyle Over Misconduct Allegations

President Trump is weighing the removal of U.S. Ambassador to Greece Kimberly Guilfoyle over mounting allegations of misconduct and donor pressure. "We're looking at it. We're not happy," Trump told reporters Monday, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

The allegations include pressuring a donor to cover a substantial credit card bill, accepting improper gifts and services, and threatening a Greek official, *Fox News* reports. Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio have reportedly weighed in on the controversy.

Guilfoyle, a former Fox News host and Trump ally, has faced scrutiny over her conduct as ambassador. The emerging details suggest potential abuse of diplomatic authority and improper financial entanglements—precisely the kind of ethical lapses conservatives rightly condemn regardless of party affiliation.

**The conservative takeaway:** Trump's willingness to consider firing a loyalist over credible misconduct allegations demonstrates accountability. Republicans have long criticized the left's selective enforcement of ethics standards; Trump's apparent openness to holding his own appointees to account is the right move, even when politically inconvenient.
