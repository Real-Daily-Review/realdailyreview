# Trump Announces $3.7B Defense Investment in Baltimore Shipyard

President Trump is betting on American manufacturing muscle to rebuild U.S. submarine production capacity—a sharp contrast to years of defense-industrial neglect under the Biden administration.

At a Tuesday event in Baltimore County, Trump announced that defense contractor Anduril will invest $3.7 billion into construction of the largest new shipyard since World War II, dedicated to manufacturing components for Virginia-class submarines (Just The News). The facility leverages Baltimore's storied shipbuilding heritage and addresses a critical gap in America's naval readiness as China accelerates its own submarine fleet.

The Pentagon is simultaneously standing up its first-ever Autonomous Warfare Command under Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, signaling a comprehensive modernization push across defense technology (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative case:** This represents smart, market-driven defense investment—private capital flowing into critical national security infrastructure without massive new government spending. It creates high-skilled manufacturing jobs and restores American industrial capacity in a sector where we've fallen dangerously behind.

**Colorado angle:** While this shipyard is Maryland-based, Colorado's aerospace and defense contractors should watch closely. Hegseth's autonomous warfare initiative could drive demand for Colorado-based defense tech and engineering talent.

Conservatives should monitor whether this momentum extends to other defense-industrial gaps and whether regulatory barriers get cleared to accelerate production timelines.
