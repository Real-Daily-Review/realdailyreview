# Denver Police Shooting Highlights Public Safety Crisis in Progressive City

Denver's weekend violence—including a police shooting of a knife-wielding suspect and a separate stabbing that left one woman dead—underscores the public safety collapse plaguing Colorado's capital under progressive leadership.

On Sunday, Denver Police officers shot a man reported to be harassing people with a knife in north Denver (KDVR Fox31). Hours earlier, another shooting occurred in the Globeville neighborhood when officers responded to a separate incident on East 51st Avenue (9News Denver). The incidents follow a Saturday stabbing spree that killed one woman and hospitalized multiple victims (KDVR Fox31).

**The conservative case:** These incidents reflect the consequences of progressive policies that have gutted law enforcement morale, reduced police presence in high-crime neighborhoods, and prioritized criminal justice "reform" over victim protection. Denver's defund-the-police movement and soft-on-crime approach have emboldened violent offenders while leaving residents vulnerable.

Democrats and progressive advocates counter that police shootings require scrutiny and that root causes—poverty, mental illness, substance abuse—demand social investment rather than enforcement alone.

The reality for Colorado taxpayers: Denver residents are paying the price for an experiment in progressive governance that has failed to deliver safety. Coloradans should demand accountability from city leadership on whether current public safety strategies are actually protecting neighborhoods or enabling criminals.
