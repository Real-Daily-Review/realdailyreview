# Colorado Should Embrace AI Growth, Not Strangle It With Regulation

Colorado has a rare opportunity to lead the artificial intelligence economy—but only if policymakers resist the urge to over-regulate an industry still finding its footing.

A Magellan Strategies survey released this month shows Coloradans are warming to AI development, according to *Complete Colorado*. The state's tech corridor and data center capacity position it competitively for high-wage jobs and business investment that could diversify Colorado's economy beyond energy and tourism.

Conservatives argue that premature regulation and AI "panic" risk driving companies and talent to friendlier states. Colorado's business climate already faces headwinds from progressive tax and regulatory policies; doubling down on AI restrictions would be self-defeating.

The *Colorado Sun* has pitched AI apprenticeships as a workforce solution, framing skills training as a public-good investment. While skills development has merit, conservatives should scrutinize whether this becomes another government-funded program with strings attached—or a genuine private-sector partnership that doesn't burden taxpayers.

The real question: Will Colorado compete for AI jobs and tax revenue, or will it cede this sector to Texas, Arizona, and other states playing it smart?

Watch whether the state legislature attempts to impose AI-specific regulations or tax incentives that pick winners and losers rather than letting market forces work.
