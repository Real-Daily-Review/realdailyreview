# Trump's Fourth of July Salute Showcases American Military Might

Colorado families celebrating Independence Day this weekend have plenty of local options, but the nation's capital is hosting what organizers call the largest aerial military celebration in American history.

President Trump is returning to Mount Rushmore for Fourth of July fireworks before heading to Washington for "Salute to America 250" on the National Mall Saturday (Fox News Politics). The centerpiece: hundreds of military aircraft roaring over D.C. in an unprecedented aerial display (The Daily Wire).

The Denver metro area is offering its own slate of festivities to mark America's 250th birthday, with events and activities across the region (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

For conservatives, Trump's emphasis on military pageantry and patriotic symbolism stands in sharp contrast to years of progressive efforts to downplay American exceptionalism and military strength. The "Salute to America 250" frames the nation's founding and defense capabilities as something to celebrate openly—a direct rebuke to the left's tendency to lead with national guilt and apologies.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether the Biden-Harris administration's final months attempt to restrict similar patriotic military displays or impose new regulations on future Independence Day celebrations.
