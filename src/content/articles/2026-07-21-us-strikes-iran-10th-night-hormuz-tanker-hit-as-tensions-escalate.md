# U.S. Strikes Iran for 10th Night as Hormuz Shipping Faces New Threats

The U.S. military is conducting sustained strikes against Iranian targets for the tenth consecutive night, escalating tensions in the Persian Gulf as commercial shipping faces mounting risks. According to the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations center (9News Denver), a tanker was hit by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz, underscoring the threat to global energy supplies and trade routes critical to American interests.

The offensive follows the deaths of three American service members in recent days, including one killed while detonating an Iranian drone and two others killed in strikes in Jordan (Just The News). The sustained campaign signals a hardened U.S. posture against Iranian aggression in the region.

**Colorado angle:** Disruptions to Middle Eastern oil and gas markets directly affect energy prices at Colorado pumps and heating costs for households and businesses across the state. Any prolonged conflict could drive up fuel and utility costs for Colorado families already squeezed by inflation.

Republicans argue the strikes are a necessary response to Iranian provocation and protect American lives and strategic interests. Democrats have raised concerns about escalation risks and the need for diplomatic off-ramps, though specifics remain limited in available reporting.
