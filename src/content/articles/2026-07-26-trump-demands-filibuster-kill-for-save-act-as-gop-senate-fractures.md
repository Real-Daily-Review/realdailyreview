# Trump's SAVE Act Stalls as GOP Senate Fractures Over Filibuster Fight

Donald Trump is demanding Senate Republicans nuke the filibuster to pass his SAVE America Act, but the party's divided leadership is blocking the move—and Colorado conservatives should care about what this infighting means for actual legislative wins.

Trump has repeatedly called on GOP leadership to eliminate the 60-vote threshold, telling Republicans to "do it now, before it is too late" (Just The News). The problem: Sen. Mitch McConnell's extended absence from the Senate has complicated the party's ability to corral votes, and Republicans remain fractured on whether torching the filibuster is worth the long-term cost (Fox News Politics).

**The conservative case for caution:** While Trump's SAVE Act aims at spending cuts and regulatory rollback—priorities Colorado taxpayers support—eliminating the filibuster would hand Democrats the same nuclear option when they return to power. Republicans argue the Senate's 60-vote rule, though frustrating, protects the minority and prevents wild policy swings.

**The Trump argument:** Filibuster reform is necessary to pass an agenda before Democrats obstruct everything. Without it, Republicans waste their majority.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether McConnell's return shifts the math—and whether Senate Republicans find a reconciliation path that avoids the filibuster fight altogether.
