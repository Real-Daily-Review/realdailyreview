# Biden's Debate Denial Insults Voters Who Saw the Truth

Former President Joe Biden is gaslighting the American people about his catastrophic June 2024 debate performance, claiming it was "not very consequential" in remarks to *People* Magazine. Biden told the magazine he doesn't "know anybody who hasn't made a mistake in a debate," treating the worst presidential debate performance in modern memory as a minor stumble. *Fox News* and the *Washington Examiner* report Biden is downplaying what became the turning point of his political career, despite his own party abandoning him shortly after.

The debate mattered enormously: it shattered the media's protective narrative around Biden's fitness and forced Democrats to confront reality.

Republicans counter that Biden's attempt to minimize the debate's significance shows contempt for voters' own eyes and judgment. If he won't acknowledge what happened then, why should Americans trust anything he says now?
