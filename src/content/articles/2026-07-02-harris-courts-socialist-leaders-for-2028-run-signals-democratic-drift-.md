# Harris Courts Socialist Wing as 2028 Ambitions Emerge

Former Vice President Kamala Harris is privately courting far-left figures to build a 2028 presidential apparatus, signaling the Democratic Party's continued drift toward socialism—and raising questions about whether Colorado Democrats will follow suit.

According to *Axios*, Harris recently called New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani, a prominent socialist, to discuss the party's future direction. *Washington Examiner* columnist Joe Concha characterized the outreach as Harris "kissing the ring" of the socialist faction now steering Democratic politics.

The move underscores a fundamental strategic choice: Harris is consolidating support among the party's most ideologically rigid wing rather than repositioning toward the center. For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats have already embraced aggressive climate mandates, gun control, and tax-and-spend governance aligned with the progressive agenda. If Harris emerges as the 2028 frontrunner, expect Colorado's Democratic establishment to double down on those policies heading into 2026 state elections.

Republicans argue Harris's embrace of socialist allies proves the Democratic Party has abandoned working-class voters and fiscal responsibility. They contend the party's leftward tilt creates an opening for Republicans to reclaim Colorado's independent voters and suburban moderates who've grown weary of progressive overreach.
