# Democratic Socialism's Radical Agenda Threatens Constitutional Order, Conservatives Warn

Colorado conservatives are sounding the alarm over the Democratic Socialists of America's explicit platform, which they argue represents a direct assault on constitutional governance and free-market principles. According to Complete Colorado, the DSA's agenda would systematically dismantle the nation's checks-and-balances framework—the very foundation of limited government that protects individual liberty and property rights.

The concern extends beyond abstract ideology. Republicans argue that DSA-aligned politicians are gaining influence within Democratic ranks, particularly among younger voters. Fox News reports that socialist primary victories signal a "leftward shift" reshaping Democratic policy positions, including stances on Israel and likely economic policy affecting Colorado's energy sector, small businesses, and job creators.

**What conservatives say:** The DSA platform represents an opening gambit toward outright communism, not merely a competing economic philosophy. Its rejection of constitutional checks and balances threatens the rule of law itself—the bedrock protecting Coloradans' property, business freedom, and Second Amendment rights.

**The left claims:** Democratic socialism is a legitimate policy alternative focused on economic equity and addressing inequality.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the real risk lies in how these ideas filter into state and local Democratic platforms. Watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt DSA-style proposals on taxes, regulation, or energy policy in the 2026 cycle.
