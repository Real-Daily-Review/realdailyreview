# Colorado Ski Season Opens as Flu Concerns Mount Heading Into Holidays

Colorado's ski resorts are ramping up for the 2026-2027 season with fresh terrain and upgraded infrastructure, but public health officials are sounding the alarm about a potentially severe flu outbreak as the state heads into the holiday travel rush.

Purgatory Resort near Durango is opening a new chairlift accessing over 40 acres of expert terrain, part of broader season upgrades across Colorado's mountain destinations (Colorado Sun). Meanwhile, CPR News reports that flu hospitalization numbers are climbing, with roughly three out of four Coloradans remaining unvaccinated heading into peak travel season.

The timing is problematic: ski resorts draw thousands of visitors from across the country and internationally, creating ideal conditions for respiratory illness transmission. Colorado doctors are bracing for a repeat of last year's congested holiday season, according to CPR News.

Public health advocates are pushing vaccination campaigns, though conservatives have long emphasized individual choice and skepticism of one-size-fits-all health mandates. The state should focus on transparent risk communication rather than coercive measures, allowing Coloradans to make informed decisions about their own health.

Watch whether state health officials attempt new vaccine mandates or restrictions on businesses and gatherings as flu cases climb.
