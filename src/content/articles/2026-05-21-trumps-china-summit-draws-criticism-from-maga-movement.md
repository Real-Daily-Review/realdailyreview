# Trump's Beijing Summit Draws Fire From Right-Wing Critics

President Donald Trump's recent summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping in Beijing has sparked backlash from the MAGA movement, which views engagement with China as a strategic mistake.

Right-wing activists, including commentator Mike Cernovich, criticized the talks on social media, questioning whether the summit represented a capitulation to Beijing (according to the Washington Examiner). The MAGA movement, which has prioritized confronting China over Russia, expressed disappointment with the diplomatic overture.

The criticism reflects a broader ideological divide within conservative circles over how to approach U.S.-China relations. Supporters of Trump's engagement argue that direct dialogue with Xi is necessary for managing great-power competition and addressing shared challenges. Critics within the MAGA wing counter that the summit risks legitimizing Chinese interests in global affairs without securing concrete concessions on trade, technology, or military posturing.

The summit comes as global energy markets face mounting pressures, with governments struggling to respond adequately to the current crisis, according to the Financial Times. The convergence of geopolitical tensions and economic instability underscores the stakes of U.S.-China diplomacy.
