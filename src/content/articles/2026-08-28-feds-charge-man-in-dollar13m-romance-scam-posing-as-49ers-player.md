# Federal Charges Filed in $1.3M Romance Scam Targeting Women

A Colorado angle on a cautionary tale: federal prosecutors have charged a man for allegedly impersonating an NFL player to defraud women of over $1.3 million in a romance scam that ran from February 2022 through August 2024, according to 9News Denver and the Daily Caller. The scheme exploited victims' trust by posing as a San Francisco 49ers investor, extracting money under false pretenses of romantic involvement and business opportunity.

A second man faces federal charges as an alleged co-conspirator in the fraud operation. The case underscores how romance scams—often targeting vulnerable individuals—continue to proliferate across state lines, leveraging social engineering and false identities to bypass victims' defenses.

For Colorado residents, this serves as a stark reminder: verify identities independently before sending money to anyone claiming investment credentials or romantic interest online. Scammers routinely exploit social media and dating platforms to build false credibility. Victims should report suspected fraud to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) and local law enforcement immediately.

Coloradans should watch whether federal prosecutors pursue asset forfeiture and restitution orders—critical tools for recovering victims' losses in romance fraud cases.
