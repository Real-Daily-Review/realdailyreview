# Trump Courts Fetterman: Pennsylvania Democrat Signals GOP Openness

President Trump is openly courting Sen. John Fetterman (D-Pa.), calling him a "great guy" and saying he'd "love" to see the Pennsylvania Democrat switch to the Republican Party—a striking signal that even Trump-skeptical Democrats may be reconsidering their political home as the GOP consolidates power.

Fetterman, elected in 2022 as a Democrat but known for bucking his party on crime and fiscal restraint, has shown willingness to work across the aisle. Trump's public recruitment suggests the GOP sees opportunity to peel off moderate Democrats frustrated with progressive overreach on spending, regulation, and cultural issues.

For Colorado Republicans, the Fetterman flirtation underscores a broader realignment: voters nationwide are abandoning the Democratic Party over inflation, government waste, and ideological rigidity. Colorado's own moderate Democrats—particularly in rural and energy-producing counties—face similar pressure as Biden-era policies hammer energy workers and ranchers while driving up costs.

Republicans argue Fetterman's potential defection proves Democrats have drifted too far left. Democrats counter that Trump is simply trying to exploit divisions and that Fetterman remains committed to his party.

**Watch whether Colorado's own moderate Democrats—especially those representing energy and agricultural districts—face similar recruitment pressure or primary challenges from their left flank.**
