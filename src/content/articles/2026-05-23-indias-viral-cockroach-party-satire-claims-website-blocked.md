# India's Satirical 'Cockroach Party' Claims Website Blocked

A Gen Z satirical political movement in India called the "Cockroach Janta Party" has gone viral on social media, claiming its website was blocked, according to BBC World.

The parody group, which has amassed millions of followers, describes itself as "the voice of the lazy and unemployed," according to BBC World. The movement emerged as a humorous commentary on Indian politics and has resonated with younger audiences through social media, according to Al Jazeera English.

The group's assertion that its website faced blocking has drawn attention to questions about online speech and government regulation in India, though neither outlet provides details on who allegedly blocked the site or the stated reasons.

The viral nature of the satirical movement reflects broader Gen Z engagement with political discourse through humor and parody rather than traditional activism. The incident underscores the growing role of internet-based satire in shaping political conversation among younger Indian voters.
