# Treasury Yields Hit 19-Year High as Washington's Debt Binge Catches Up

Washington's reckless spending spree is finally hitting the wallet of every American—and Colorado taxpayers should brace for impact.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, the *Wall Street Journal* reported that a punishing bond selloff has pushed the 30-year Treasury yield to a 19-year high, with the benchmark 10-year yield approaching 5%. For Coloradans already squeezed by rising living costs and energy prices, this signals a dangerous reality: the federal government's borrowing binge is becoming unsustainable.

**Republicans argue** that Washington must pivot toward fiscal responsibility—cutting wasteful spending, eliminating regulatory bloat, and restoring market confidence in U.S. debt. Without serious spending restraint, higher yields will ripple through Colorado's economy, raising borrowing costs for small businesses, farmers, and homebuyers already struggling with inflation.

**Democrats counter** that federal spending supports critical infrastructure and social programs, though they offer little concrete plan to address the debt crisis.

The bond market isn't ideological—it's mathematical. When the federal government borrows at unsustainable levels, investors demand higher returns, crowding out private investment and strangling economic growth. Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small-business community will feel the squeeze first.

Watch whether Republican leadership actually demands spending cuts or simply talks about fiscal responsibility while Washington keeps the spigot open.
