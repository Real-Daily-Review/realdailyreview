# GOP Closes Ranks Behind Hegseth as Massie's Impeachment Bid Collapses

Rep. Thomas Massie's last-minute attempt to impeach War Secretary Pete Hegseth has backfired spectacularly, with House Republicans rallying decisively behind the defense chief and rejecting the Kentucky libertarian's parting shot as a transparent grab for attention.

House Speaker Mike Johnson (R-LA) dismissed Massie's filing as a "political gambit for attention" (The Daily Wire), while the GOP-controlled chamber moved swiftly to table the impeachment resolution (Daily Caller). The unified Republican response underscores deep frustration with Massie's contrarian posture as he exits Congress—a stark contrast to the party's broader support for Hegseth's confirmation and leadership.

Christian evangelist Franklin Graham weighed in on the infighting, questioning why Republicans "eat each other" over Hegseth's nomination (Breitbart). The comment captures conservative concern that internal GOP feuds distract from the administration's agenda and hand ammunition to Democrats.

For Colorado Republicans, the episode highlights the difference between principled constitutional conservatism and performative obstruction. While Colorado's GOP delegation has largely backed Hegseth, the broader lesson is clear: party unity matters when defending conservative appointees against coordinated Democratic attacks.
