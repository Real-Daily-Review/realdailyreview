# Trump Pushes GOP Unity on Blanche AG Pick as Democrats Ready Opposition

President Trump is demanding unified Republican Senate support for Todd Blanche's confirmation as attorney general, signaling zero tolerance for party defections on his cabinet picks. Blanche faces Senate Judiciary Committee hearings this week, where Democrats are already mobilizing opposition—particularly over his prior legal work, according to *The Daily Wire*.

The push reflects Trump's tightening grip on Republican messaging and priorities. Political analysts note the GOP continues to embrace what *The Denver Post* describes as Trump's "unique brand of religious, conservative populism," cementing his influence over party direction regardless of internal disagreements.

For Colorado Republicans, the Blanche confirmation battle tests whether the state's GOP senators will fall in line or chart independent courses—a tension that has defined Colorado politics since Trump's 2016 rise. Conservatives backing Blanche argue his legal credentials and prosecutorial experience qualify him for the role; Democrats counter that his prior representation of Trump in legal matters disqualifies him as insufficiently independent.

The real question: will Colorado's Senate delegation prioritize party unity under Trump's demands, or will they risk primary challenges by breaking ranks? Watch whether Sen. John Hickenlooper signals any bipartisan hesitation—or whether Colorado Republicans present a unified front.
