# Republican Evans Pivots to Colorado's 4th District After 8th District Loss

Colorado Republicans are already reshuffling the deck for 2028. Just days after Democrat Eileen Laubacher narrowly defeated Rep. Lauren Boebert in the 4th Congressional District, Republican Gabe Evans—who lost his own reelection bid in the 8th District—announced he's running for the 4th seat instead (Colorado Sun, CPR News).

Evans' move gives him an early organizational advantage over other Republicans who may eye the seat. Laubacher, a retired Navy rear admiral, won with just under 1 percentage point over Boebert, signaling the district remains competitive (Denver Post).

The timing underscores a larger conservative concern: Colorado's congressional map. Conservatives have long argued that partisan gerrymandering tilts the state's districts unfairly. Initiative #256, which would ban congressional redistricting designed to favor either party, reflects growing GOP frustration with how district lines are drawn (Complete Colorado).

For Republicans, the 4th District represents a genuine pickup opportunity in 2028—if they can unite behind a candidate and capitalize on Laubacher's razor-thin margin. Evans' early entry signals the GOP sees blood in the water.
