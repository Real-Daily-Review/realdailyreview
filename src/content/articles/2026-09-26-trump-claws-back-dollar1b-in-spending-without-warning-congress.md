# Trump Uses Rare Authority to Claw Back $1B in Federal Spending

The Trump administration is wielding an obscure but powerful tool to reclaim nearly $1 billion in congressionally approved spending—a move that has caught even Republican lawmakers off guard.

Using "pocket rescissions," Trump can withhold federal funds for 45 days while Congress decides whether to approve, reject, or ignore the clawback, according to federal law (Just The News). The administration notified lawmakers of the action without advance warning or consultation, prompting pushback from U.S. Sen. Susan Collins, the Republican chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee (9News Denver).

**The conservative case:** Republicans and fiscal hawks have long argued that Congress bloats budgets with wasteful spending. Trump's use of rescission authority—rarely invoked by recent presidents—represents a direct challenge to unchecked federal spending. Conservatives view this as a legitimate executive tool to enforce fiscal discipline.

**The counterargument:** Even some Republicans, including Collins, object to the unilateral approach and lack of transparency. Democrats predictably oppose any clawback of approved funds.

For Colorado taxpayers and businesses relying on federal contracts or grants, the 45-day window creates uncertainty about project funding and timelines. Watch whether Congress acts to block or approve Trump's rescission requests—and whether this becomes a recurring tactic that reshapes federal spending dynamics.
