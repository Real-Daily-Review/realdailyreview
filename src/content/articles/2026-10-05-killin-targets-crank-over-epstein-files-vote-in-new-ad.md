# Killin Campaign Targets Crank Over Epstein Files Vote

Jessica Killin's congressional campaign is hammering Republican incumbent Jeff Crank for his previous vote against forcing the release of federal files on Jeffrey Epstein's crimes and connections.

In a new TV ad, Sky Roberts and his wife Amanda—relatives of Epstein victim Virginia Giuffre—directly condemn Crank for refusing to meet with them about legislation to unseal the documents (Colorado Sun, CPR News). The Roberts family claims Crank's office brushed off their meeting request (Washington Examiner).

Crank's position puts him in a difficult spot heading into the general election. The ad frames his vote as complicity in a "cover-up" of Epstein's ties to powerful figures—a charge that resonates across the political spectrum. Conservatives generally support transparency in government and accountability for elites; Crank's refusal to back file release and his staff's apparent dismissal of the family's request undercut that principle.

The congressman has not publicly responded to the allegations. Republicans typically argue for government transparency on matters of public interest, making Crank's stance vulnerable to criticism from both left and right.

Colorado voters should watch whether Crank addresses the Epstein files issue directly or whether this becomes a defining liability in a competitive race.
