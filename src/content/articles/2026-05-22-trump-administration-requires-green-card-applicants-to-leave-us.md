# Trump Administration Requires Most Green Card Applicants to Leave U.S.

The Trump administration announced Friday that most foreign nationals seeking permanent residency must leave the United States to apply for green cards, according to CBS News and PBS NewsHour. The policy, announced by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services, represents a significant shift in how the government processes green card applications for people already in the country on temporary visas.

Under the new rule, authorities say green cards obtained from within the U.S. are discretionary rather than automatic rights, according to Al Jazeera. The policy effectively closes a pathway that previously allowed some visa holders to adjust their status without departing.

The administration frames the move as part of a broader effort to restrict legal immigration. PBS NewsHour reports this is the latest step making it harder for foreigners already in the U.S. and those hoping to enter to obtain permanent residency.

The policy applies to most applicants, though the sources do not specify which categories, if any, remain exempt. The announcement comes as the Trump administration continues rolling out immigration restrictions across multiple agencies.
