# Kristi Noem Files for Divorce After 34 Years of Marriage

Former Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem, a prominent conservative voice and South Dakota governor, has filed for divorce from her husband Bryon after 34 years of marriage, citing "irreconcilable differences," according to documents obtained by Dakota News Now and reported by the Washington Examiner and Just The News.

The couple married in 1992 and has three adult children: daughters Kassidy, 31, and Kennedy, 29, and son Booker, 23. Noem, who served as DHS secretary under President Trump and remains influential in conservative circles, described herself as "devastated" following recent allegations regarding her husband's personal conduct.

The filing marks a significant personal development for one of the Republican Party's most visible female leaders. Noem has maintained a high profile in GOP politics and was previously considered for vice presidential consideration before controversies surrounding her memoir derailed that prospect in 2024.

The divorce documents do not detail financial arrangements or custody matters at this stage. Conservatives and political observers will be watching how this personal matter affects Noem's political standing and any future role in Republican leadership or governance.
