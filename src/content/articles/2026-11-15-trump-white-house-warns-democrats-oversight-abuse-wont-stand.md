# White House Counsel Warns Democrats: Oversight Abuse Won't Go Unanswered

The Trump administration is drawing a hard line against what it sees as Democratic threats to weaponize congressional oversight. White House Counsel Will Scharf promised a "vigorous response" to incoming House Oversight Committee Chairman Robert Garcia (D-CA) after Garcia signaled plans for aggressive investigations into the Trump administration, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

Scharf's warning reflects a core conservative concern: that Democrats, now in the minority, will use oversight powers not for legitimate accountability but as a political cudgel. Republicans argue the left has a track record of using committee authority to harass and distract Republican administrations rather than conduct serious legislative business.

The Colorado angle matters here. If Democrats pursue scorched-earth oversight tactics in Washington, it could embolden Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature to follow suit—using state-level investigations and regulatory pressure against Republican-led energy companies, agricultural operations, and other industries that fuel Colorado's economy. Coloradans who depend on oil and gas jobs, ranching, and small business should pay attention to whether Democratic overreach in Congress signals similar tactics coming to the state Capitol.

This sets up a crucial test: will Democrats use their minority status responsibly, or will partisan warfare consume Congress?
