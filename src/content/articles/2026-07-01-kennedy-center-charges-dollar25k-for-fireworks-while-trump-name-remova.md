# Kennedy Center Cashes In on July 4 While Scrubbing Trump's Name

The Kennedy Center is hawking $25,000 VIP fireworks packages this Independence Day—even as the taxpayer-funded venue scrambles to recover from financial damage caused by its decision to strip President Trump's name from the building.

According to the Washington Examiner, the performing arts center is offering premium rooftop tickets with air-conditioned lounges for its July 4 celebration, a premium pricing strategy that underscores how the institution is trying to offset revenue losses. The move comes as the Kennedy Center faces the fallout from removing Trump's name—a decision that prompted major donors to reconsider their support.

National Review reports that despite the "building name wars," the venue continues hosting high-profile events, including Bill Maher's show, drawing Trump impressionists and MAGA-friendly crowds.

**The conservative take:** The Kennedy Center receives federal funding and operates as a quasi-public institution. Rather than bow to progressive pressure and alienate donors, leadership should have recognized that erasing a president's name—regardless of party—sets a dangerous precedent for politicizing cultural institutions. Now taxpayers are subsidizing a venue that's simultaneously charging elites $25,000 for fireworks while begging for donations to cover self-inflicted financial wounds.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether similar "naming disputes" emerge at state-funded cultural venues in Denver or Boulder.
