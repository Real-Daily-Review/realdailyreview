# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Broader Progressive Push

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's full-throated defense of unrestricted gender-transition procedures for minors reveals how far the left has moved on medical decisions involving children—and signals what Colorado voters may face if progressive candidates gain ground in statewide races.

Abdul El-Sayed, a physician running for U.S. Senate, argued at Michigan State University that doctors, not government, should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments. He cited his medical credentials as qualification to support such procedures without legislative guardrails (Fox News, The Daily Wire).

Republicans counter that parents and elected representatives have a legitimate role protecting minors from irreversible medical interventions. They argue that allowing physicians alone to green-light these procedures—without parental consent requirements or age restrictions—amounts to government abdication of its duty to safeguard children.

**Colorado angle:** Several Democratic-backed bills in recent legislative sessions have sought to shield gender-transition procedures from parental notification and to classify restrictions as "discrimination." If similar candidates gain traction in Colorado's 2024 and 2026 cycles, expect renewed fights over parental rights, medical autonomy, and the scope of state regulation.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado Democrats embrace El-Sayed's "doctors decide, government stays out" framing in upcoming campaigns.
