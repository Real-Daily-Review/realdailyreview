# GOP Senator Murkowski Tanks Blanche AG Nomination, Handing Democrats a Win

Alaska Republican Lisa Murkowski announced Friday she'll vote against Todd Blanche's confirmation as Attorney General, potentially derailing the nomination and handing Senate Democrats a crucial obstruction point. Though Murkowski called Blanche "decent and capable," she said she lacks confidence he would serve as an independent check on the administration—language that echoes progressive talking points about executive overreach.

Republicans counter that an Attorney General's job is to enforce the law and execute the president's lawful agenda, not to obstruct it. Conservatives argue that Murkowski's framing conflates legitimate policy disagreement with constitutional duty. The nomination now faces an uphill climb with Republicans holding a narrow Senate majority and no Democratic votes expected.

For Colorado, the AG confirmation matters: federal enforcement priorities directly affect energy development, water rights, public lands policy, and Second Amendment enforcement. A weakened or delayed confirmation could leave the Justice Department in limbo during critical regulatory decisions affecting Colorado's oil and gas industry and water compacts.

Watch whether other Senate Republicans join Murkowski's defection—or whether GOP leadership can secure her reversal before a floor vote.
