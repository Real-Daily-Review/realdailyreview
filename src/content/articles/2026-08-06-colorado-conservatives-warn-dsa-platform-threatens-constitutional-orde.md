# Democratic Socialism Agenda Threatens Constitutional Limits, Conservatives Warn

Colorado conservatives are sounding the alarm over the Democratic Socialists of America platform, which they argue represents a direct assault on constitutional governance and free-market principles. According to Complete Colorado, the DSA's platform would undermine the nation's system of checks and balances—a core conservative concern about radical left overreach.

Former President Trump has amplified the warning, calling communism "the greatest threat facing the U.S. in its history" (Fox News Politics). Trump specifically targeted Abdul El-Sayed, a Democratic socialist candidate, during a Las Vegas speech, arguing that Democrats are abandoning even socialism for outright communism.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** DSA policies—which typically include wealth redistribution, government control of production, and elimination of private enterprise—are incompatible with the Constitution's protections for property rights and individual liberty. Republicans argue that Colorado voters should reject candidates aligned with these radical ideologies, which would devastate the state's energy sector, agriculture, and small-business economy.

The left counters that "democratic socialism" simply means stronger social safety nets and corporate regulation—not Soviet-style authoritarianism. But conservatives remain skeptical of the semantic distinction.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from DSA-aligned candidates ahead of 2026 elections, or double down on the socialist messaging.**
