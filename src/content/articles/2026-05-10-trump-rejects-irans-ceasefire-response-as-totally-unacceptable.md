# Trump Rejects Iran's Response to US Peace Proposal

President Trump on Sunday rejected Iran's response to his administration's ceasefire proposal, calling it "totally unacceptable" in a Truth Social post, according to The Hill. Iran submitted its response to the latest U.S. proposal via Pakistani mediators, but the administration quickly dismissed it without releasing details of either the American proposal or Tehran's counter-response.

Trump also issued a veiled threat, saying Iran will be "laughing no longer" as he continues negotiating a peace framework with Tehran, according to The Hill. He cited decades of Iranian actions, including what he characterized as attacks on U.S. forces.

According to Al Jazeera, Iran has indicated that negotiations should focus on ending hostilities and securing guarantees against future attacks. However, no specifics of Iran's formal response have been made public by either side.

The rejection marks another stall in talks aimed at ending the conflict. Neither the substance of the U.S. proposal nor Iran's detailed response has been disclosed to the public, leaving the core issues in dispute unclear.

Watch for whether either side releases details of their proposals and whether mediation efforts through Pakistan continue.
