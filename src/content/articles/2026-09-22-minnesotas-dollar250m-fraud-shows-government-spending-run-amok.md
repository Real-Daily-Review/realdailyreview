# Minnesota's $250M Fraud Fiasco Shows Why Government Can't Be Trusted With Your Money

Minnesota's Department of Education handed scammers a quarter-billion dollars through the "Feeding Our Future" nonprofit—and Gov. Tim Walz's administration either knew something was wrong or should have. According to the *Washington Examiner*, federal prosecutors are investigating how the state failed to catch obvious red flags while managing taxpayer funds meant for child nutrition programs.

This is what happens when government agencies prioritize speed and feel-good programs over basic oversight. The Walz administration's negligence—or worse—left Minnesota taxpayers on the hook for massive fraud that could have been stopped with elementary due diligence.

Colorado conservatives should take note: this is exactly why we need strict limits on government spending, robust audit requirements, and accountability mechanisms that actually work. When bureaucrats control hundreds of millions in public money with minimal scrutiny, fraud follows. The lesson is simple: government agencies cannot police themselves, and progressive administrations that prioritize program expansion over fiscal responsibility create the conditions for waste and theft.

Watch whether Minnesota's legislature demands real consequences for the officials who failed their oversight duties—and whether Colorado's own state agencies learn anything about tightening controls on grant programs and nonprofit partnerships.
