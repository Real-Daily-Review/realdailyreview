# Canadian MAID Fiasco Warns Colorado: Don't Let Democrats Normalize Assisted Suicide as Healthcare

A Canadian cancer patient's desperate flight to the U.S. exposes the dystopian endpoint of progressive healthcare policy—and warns Colorado voters what's coming if Democrats gain full control here.

Kristin Logan, diagnosed with cancer, was offered Medical Assistance in Dying (MAID) as her initial treatment option by Canada's government health system, according to the New York Post. Rather than exhaust conventional care, Canada's bureaucrats presented assisted suicide as a cost-effective solution. Logan and her husband fled to the U.S. for actual cancer treatment.

The Daily Caller warns that Democratic-controlled state legislatures are now pushing similar assisted-suicide laws across America, framing them as "end of life decisions." What Canada's experience reveals: once government controls healthcare, rationing becomes policy, and the cheapest option—death—gets marketed as compassionate choice.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado already permits medical aid in dying under limited circumstances. Conservatives worry that Democratic supermajorities in the legislature could expand those provisions without meaningful safeguards, turning assisted suicide into a pressure tactic for expensive patients—especially the elderly, disabled, and uninsured.

Republicans argue that normalizing government-sanctioned suicide inverts medicine's core purpose: healing, not killing. The left counters that autonomy and dignity justify the practice.
