# Colorado's 3rd District Stays Red as Hurd Edges Out Democrat Challenger

Republican Jeff Hurd held the Western Slope and southern Colorado for the GOP in a tight 3rd Congressional District race, defeating Democrat Dwayne Romero and extending Republican control of the seat since 2011 (Denver Post). The close contest underscores how competitive Colorado's traditionally conservative regions have become as Democrats pour resources into flipping rural seats.

The 3rd District—spanning from the rugged Western Slope through ranching country and into the San Juan region—remains a bellwether for Republican strength in Colorado's interior. Conservatives argue the district's energy workers, ranchers, and small-business owners depend on policies Hurd supports: responsible oil and gas development, water rights protection, and limited federal land grabs. Democrats countered with messaging on healthcare and climate action, but Romero's challenge fell short.

The result matters for Colorado's broader political map. Democrats have aggressively targeted rural Republican districts nationwide, and this district's narrow margin signals the GOP cannot take Western Slope voters for granted. Meanwhile, a Kentucky county's tied election decided by coin flip (Breitbart) highlights how razor-thin margins are becoming the norm—a reminder that turnout and ground game will decide 2028.
