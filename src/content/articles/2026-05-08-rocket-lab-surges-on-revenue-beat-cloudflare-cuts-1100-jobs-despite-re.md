# Space Stocks Surge; Cloudflare Cuts 1,100 Jobs Despite Record Revenue

Rocket Lab jumped 34 percent in its best trading day on record after beating revenue expectations and securing a major launch deal, according to CNBC, as investor appetite for space stocks intensifies ahead of SpaceX's anticipated IPO.

In a separate tech sector development, Cloudflare announced its first large-scale layoff of 1,100 employees—roughly 20 percent of its workforce—even as the company posted record revenue, according to TechCrunch. CEO Matthew Prince attributed the cuts to artificial intelligence efficiency gains that have reduced demand for support roles.

The two moves illustrate divergent trajectories in tech: Rocket Lab's gains reflect bullish sentiment around commercial space ventures, while Cloudflare's layoffs underscore how AI adoption is reshaping labor demand within the sector. Prince's framing suggests companies view AI-driven productivity as justification for workforce reductions despite strong financial performance—a pattern likely to intensify as more firms integrate AI tools.

Watch for whether other software and cloud companies cite similar AI-driven efficiency arguments in upcoming earnings calls, and whether space sector momentum sustains if SpaceX's IPO proceeds.
