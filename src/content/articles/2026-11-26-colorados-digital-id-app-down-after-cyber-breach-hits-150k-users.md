# Colorado's Digital ID App Down Ahead of Holiday Travel—Data of 150K at Risk

Colorado's myColorado digital ID app has gone dark just as thousands of Coloradans prepare for Thanksgiving travel, leaving residents unable to access the state's digital identification system following a cybersecurity incident. The Colorado Department of Revenue confirmed the breach may have compromised personal data for up to 150,000 users, though state officials have offered no timeline for restoration.

The outage compounds travel headaches already mounting from a massive coastal storm battering the eastern U.S., which has delayed over 550 flights and canceled 366 as of Thursday morning (Washington Examiner). While Colorado's airports face less direct impact than eastern hubs, the timing underscores a broader problem: government digital systems remain vulnerable to attack, and state agencies often lack contingency plans.

Conservatives have long warned that expanding government digital infrastructure—from vaccine passports to state ID apps—creates centralized targets for hackers and privacy risks for citizens. The myColorado incident validates those concerns. Coloradans now face the prospect of traveling without reliable state digital ID access while the state scrambles to secure compromised data.

**Watch for:** whether the state discloses full details on the breach's scope and whether lawmakers demand accountability for cybersecurity failures in state IT systems.
