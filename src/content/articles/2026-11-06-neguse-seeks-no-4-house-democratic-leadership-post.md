# Colorado Democrat Neguse Seeks House Leadership Post, Unopposed

Colorado's Joe Neguse is running unopposed for House assistant speaker—the No. 4 Democratic leadership slot—positioning himself as the youngest lawmaker ever to hold the position, according to the Colorado Sun and CPR News.

The move signals Neguse's ambitions within Democratic caucus ranks and his consolidation of power among House Democrats heading into the next Congress. Running unopposed suggests he has secured backing from party leadership and rank-and-file members alike.
