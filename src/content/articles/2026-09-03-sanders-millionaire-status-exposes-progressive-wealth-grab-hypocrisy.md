# Bernie's Billionaire Hypocrisy Exposes Left's Wealth-Grab Agenda

Bernie Sanders has become a millionaire—yet continues railing against billionaires as threats to democracy, according to *Complete Colorado*. The Vermont socialist's sudden shift in target, now that his own net worth has climbed, reveals the fundamental dishonesty behind progressive wealth-redistribution rhetoric.

Sanders' transformation underscores a core conservative argument: the left doesn't oppose wealth itself—it opposes *others'* wealth. Once progressives accumulate their own fortunes through book deals and political power, the moral crusade conveniently narrows its scope. For Colorado taxpayers already burdened by state income taxes and property assessments, this hypocrisy matters. When Democratic politicians preach about "taxing the rich" while protecting their own assets, they're signaling that wealth-grab policies will ultimately hit the middle class and small business owners who can't afford elite accountants.

Meanwhile, *National Review* reports that the Trump administration is pursuing what amounts to a government hedge fund strategy abroad, including moves on Venezuelan oil assets. The nationalization approach raises questions about market intervention that conservatives typically oppose—though energy independence advocates argue domestic energy security justifies the strategy.
