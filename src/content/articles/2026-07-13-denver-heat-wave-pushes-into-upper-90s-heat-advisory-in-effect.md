# Denver Heat Wave Pushes into Upper 90s; Coloradans Urged to Prepare

Colorado's summer heat wave is intensifying, with Denver facing a heat advisory Monday as temperatures climb into the upper 90s and threaten near-record highs through the week, according to 9News and KDVR Fox31.

The statewide heat event is expected to persist through the coming days, with afternoon highs consistently in the upper 90s. Medical professionals are sounding the alarm: Dr. Joseph Ponce warned that outdoorsy Coloradans—a significant portion of the state's population—often underestimate heat risks and fail to take adequate breaks during peak summer conditions (9News).

Heat-related illnesses pose real dangers, particularly for those working outdoors in agriculture, construction, and energy development. Colorado's outdoor economy depends on workers staying safe in extreme conditions, making hydration and rest breaks essential.

While weather events are natural, conservatives should note that Colorado's energy grid will face strain during peak demand hours. Reliable baseload power—whether from natural gas or nuclear—becomes critical when air conditioning demand spikes. Renewable-only energy mandates could complicate grid stability during heat waves like this one.

Coloradans should monitor local heat advisories, stay hydrated, and check on elderly neighbors and outdoor workers throughout the week.
