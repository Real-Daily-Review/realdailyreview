# Seattle Police Chief Resigns After Bungled Mass Shooting Response

Seattle's police leadership collapsed this week when Chief Shon Barnes resigned following widespread criticism of the department's catastrophic response to a mass shooting at the Bite of Seattle food festival Sunday. According to the Washington Examiner and Just The News, Barnes stepped down Thursday after Mayor Katie Wilson announced his departure—just four days after the incident that exposed serious failures in the city's public safety apparatus.

The resignation came amid damaging revelations that Barnes was out of town during the shooting and subsequently confronted a journalist over coverage of the botched response (Just The News). The Daily Wire reported that scrutiny intensified as details emerged about the department's handling of the emergency.

This collapse of Seattle's police leadership underscores a broader conservative concern: progressive cities that have spent years undermining law enforcement through budget cuts, anti-police rhetoric, and activist pressure now face the consequences when crises hit. When departments are demoralized and leadership is weak, public safety suffers—and citizens pay the price.
