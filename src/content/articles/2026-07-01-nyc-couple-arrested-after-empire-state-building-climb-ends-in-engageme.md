# Empire State Building Stunt Ends in Arrests—and an Engagement

Two daredevils turned a dangerous publicity stunt into an engagement announcement Wednesday, scaling New York's iconic Empire State Building and unfurling a banner about "the power of love" before NYPD shut down their aerial romance.

Angela Nikolau, 33, and Ivan Kuznetsov, 32, climbed to the building's antenna in what appeared to be a coordinated stunt, with the man proposing mid-climb (The Daily Wire reports). Helicopter footage captured the pair at the spire's peak, waving their banner before officers reached them and made arrests (9News Denver).

Bodycam video released by NYPD showed the moment officers detained the couple at the antenna (New York Post). The pair, dressed in dark outfits with one wearing a Catwoman-style mask, faced charges for the unauthorized climb.

While the stunt grabbed headlines for its romantic angle, it underscores a broader problem: security vulnerabilities at America's most recognizable landmarks and the ease with which determined individuals can breach restricted areas. New York officials will face questions about how two people accessed the building's upper reaches undetected—a concern that extends far beyond social media theatrics.

Conservatives and security experts should watch whether city and federal authorities tighten access protocols or simply chalk this up to harmless romance gone viral.
