# Boulder County's Tax-Funded Trump Anxiety Session

Boulder County officials are using taxpayer dollars to run what amounts to group therapy for residents obsessed with Trump, according to *Complete Colorado*. The county's self-styled "election champions" held a presentation that felt less like civic education and more like a therapeutic exercise for Trump-anxious progressives—all on the public dime.

This is vintage Boulder County: taking a straightforward government function (election administration) and turning it into a progressive anxiety management program funded by local taxpayers. Conservatives argue that election offices should focus on secure, efficient voting procedures—not become platforms for partisan emotional processing.

The move reflects a broader pattern in deep-blue Boulder County, where government resources routinely serve ideological ends rather than core functions. Residents frustrated with bloated government and wasteful spending should take note: this is what happens when one-party rule goes unchecked.

Colorado taxpayers watching their own counties should demand accountability: Are your election offices doing their jobs, or running political therapy sessions? Watch for similar initiatives creeping into other Colorado counties as progressive activists normalize using public funds for partisan purposes.
