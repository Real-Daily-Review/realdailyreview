# Missing Colorado Baby Found Safe in Texas

A two-week-old Westminster infant, Zuriel Calloway, who vanished Thursday and triggered a Colorado Bureau of Investigation Endangered Missing Alert, has been located safely in Texas, authorities confirmed Friday.

The newborn was last seen in a vehicle near 128th Avenue and Huron Street in Westminster with his father. The CBI's swift alert—distributed across state lines—helped mobilize law enforcement and the public to track the child's whereabouts as he was believed headed toward Texas.

The case underscores both the speed of modern missing-child protocols and the critical importance of interstate coordination when a child's safety is at immediate risk. Colorado's law enforcement agencies, working with Texas authorities, successfully resolved the situation without harm to the infant.

Details surrounding the circumstances of the disappearance and recovery remain limited, but the safe return marks a positive outcome in what could have been a tragedy. Parents and guardians across Colorado should remain vigilant about child safety and familiar with how to report suspicious activity to local law enforcement or the CBI.
