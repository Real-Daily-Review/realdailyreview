# Nine Deportees Arrive in Sierra Leone Under US Third-Country Deal

Nine migrants deported from the United States landed in Sierra Leone on Wednesday, marking the latest in a series of third-country deportation agreements the Trump administration has negotiated with African and Latin American nations, according to PBS NewsHour.

The deportations are part of the administration's broader crackdown on illegal immigration. Sierra Leone becomes the newest African country to receive deportees under these arrangements, according to BBC World.

The third-country agreements have drawn widespread criticism, though the sources do not detail the specific objections or who has raised them. The deals allow the US to send migrants to countries other than their nations of origin—a practice that has expanded under the current administration's immigration enforcement push.

The arrangement with Sierra Leone reflects a broader pattern: the Trump administration has pursued similar agreements with multiple nations across Africa and Latin America to manage deportations.
