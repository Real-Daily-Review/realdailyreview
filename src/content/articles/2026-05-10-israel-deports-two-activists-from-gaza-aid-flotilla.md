# Israel Deports Two Activists From Gaza Aid Flotilla

Israel has deported two activists detained after attempting to breach the naval blockade of Gaza with an aid flotilla, according to PBS NewsHour and BBC World.

The deportees are Spanish national Saif Abu Keshek and Brazilian Thiago Ávila, who were held for slightly over a week following their detention aboard the Gaza-bound vessel. Both were leading the flotilla effort, which aimed to circumvent Israel's blockade of the Palestinian territory.

The deportations underscore ongoing tensions over humanitarian access to Gaza. Israel maintains the blockade as a security measure, while activists and aid organizations argue it restricts the flow of essential supplies to civilians.

The incident reflects a broader pattern of confrontation between Israel and international activists seeking to challenge the blockade through maritime routes. Previous flotilla attempts have also resulted in detentions and deportations.
