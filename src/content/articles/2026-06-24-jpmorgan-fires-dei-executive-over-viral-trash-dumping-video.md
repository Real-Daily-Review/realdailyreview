# JPMorgan Chase Fires DEI Executive After Trash-Dumping Viral Video

A JPMorgan Chase diversity officer has been terminated after appearing in a viral video dumping garbage in the street during New York's Knicks championship parade—apparently to steal an orange-and-blue trash can as a souvenir.

Angela Báez, identified by sources cited in the *Daily Caller* and *Daily Wire*, was caught on camera engaging in the behavior while attending the parade in her personal capacity. The incident sparked immediate backlash, with JPMorgan Chase moving swiftly to fire her from her DEI (diversity, equity, and inclusion) role.

The episode underscores a broader conservative critique of corporate DEI programs: that they often prioritize ideological hiring over merit and judgment, resulting in employees who lack basic professional standards. Republicans and conservatives have long argued that DEI initiatives distract from competence-based advancement and create resentment among workers hired on traditional qualifications.

JPMorgan's rapid termination suggests even major corporations recognize the reputational liability when DEI-focused staff become public embarrassments. The bank's swift action may reflect pressure from shareholders and customers tired of corporate wokeness backfiring spectacularly.

Colorado investors and employees at major financial institutions should monitor whether JPMorgan and similar firms begin reassessing their DEI spending and hiring practices in response to public backlash.
