# Woman Convicted in Phantom Canyon Double Homicide

A Fremont County jury has convicted a woman of murder in the 2022 deaths of two men whose bodies were found dumped down a steep cliff in Phantom Canyon, according to the 12th Judicial District Attorney's Office (KDVR). The bodies of Rodulfo Santillan-Reyes and Ryan Roth were discovered in February 2022 after being thrown down the rocky ravine (9News Denver).

The case proceeded to conviction after the original prosecutor was disqualified, requiring reassignment to complete the prosecution. The conviction marks the resolution of a high-profile case that drew significant attention across the region.
