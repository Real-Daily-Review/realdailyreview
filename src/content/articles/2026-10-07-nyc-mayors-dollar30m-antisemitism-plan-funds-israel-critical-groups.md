# NYC Mayor's 'Antisemitism' Plan Funds Groups Critical of Israel

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's $30 million antisemitism initiative reveals the left's fundamental confusion about what antisemitism actually is—and who gets to define it.

According to Fox News, Mamdani's plan indirectly funnels money to the Arab American Association of New York, an organization openly critical of U.S.-Israel ties. The irony is thick: a program ostensibly designed to combat antisemitism ends up supporting groups that blur the line between legitimate criticism of Israeli policy and hostility toward Jewish people.

The Washington Examiner reports that Mamdani himself called Israel's actions in Gaza "genocide" on the third anniversary of October 7, even while acknowledging Hamas's "horrific war crimes" that killed over 1,100 Israelis and abducted 251 others. This rhetorical sleight of hand—condemning Hamas while equating Israeli self-defense with genocide—has become standard progressive playbook.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this matters: similar ideological capture is already embedded in our own state institutions, from university DEI programs to municipal "equity" spending that often prioritizes activist narratives over actual community safety and cohesion.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership adopts similar "antisemitism" frameworks that effectively shield anti-Israel activism from scrutiny.
