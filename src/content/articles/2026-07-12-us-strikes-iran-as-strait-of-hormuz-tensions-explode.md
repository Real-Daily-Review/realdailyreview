# U.S. Strikes Iran as Middle East Tensions Spiral Over Strait of Hormuz

The U.S. has launched fresh military strikes against Iran following an Iranian attack on a container ship in the Strait of Hormuz that left a crew member missing, according to 9News Denver. The escalation marks a dangerous expansion of regional hostilities, with Iran now targeting neutral Gulf states it had previously spared.

Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz closed and unleashed a barrage of missiles and drones across the Gulf, hitting targets in Oman, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, and Kuwait—the broadest strike campaign since May, the Washington Examiner reports. The Iranian regime has dramatically widened its target list beyond traditional adversaries.

**The Colorado angle:** Energy markets remain volatile. Disruptions to global oil shipping through the Strait of Hormuz directly affect Colorado energy prices and the competitiveness of American oil and gas producers. Conservatives argue the Biden-Harris administration's weak posture toward Iran—including the failed nuclear diplomacy and sanctions relief that preceded this crisis—emboldened Tehran's aggression. Republicans contend that strength and deterrence, not appeasement, prevent regional wars.

The U.S. response signals a necessary show of force, but Colorado taxpayers should watch whether this conflict expands into a broader regional war that destabilizes energy markets and stretches American military resources thin.
