# Democrats Plan Dallas Counterprogramming as GOP Midterm Convention Looms

Colorado Republicans heading to the GOP's midterm convention in Dallas next week should expect a well-funded Democratic circus outside the gates.

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries and the Democratic National Committee announced Friday they're deploying top messengers to shadow the two-day GOP confab, focusing on affordability messaging and their 2026 agenda (Washington Examiner). Democrats are also weaponizing data centers as a midterm political issue, attempting to frame GOP support for tech infrastructure development as reckless deregulation (Daily Caller).

Republicans counter that Democrats are manufacturing outrage over policies that drive economic growth and job creation. The GOP argues that data center expansion—particularly relevant to Colorado's growing tech corridor—brings high-wage employment and tax revenue without the bloated government spending Democrats favor.

This is classic Democratic playbook: show up, complain about "affordability," and attack business-friendly policies without offering serious alternatives beyond tax hikes and red tape.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado GOP delegates and candidates adopt a unified message defending pro-growth energy and tech policies against Democratic attacks heading into 2026.
