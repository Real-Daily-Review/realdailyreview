# Republican Nominee Marx Overstates Owens Endorsement as General Election Looms

Victor Marx, Colorado's Republican gubernatorial nominee, claimed he had secured an endorsement from former Gov. Bill Owens—but Owens himself says that's premature. According to the Colorado Sun, Owens stated he wants to observe Marx's general election campaign "over the next month or so" before deciding whether to back him, directly contradicting Marx's assertion of an endorsement already in hand.

Marx, a first-time political candidate with an unconventional background, narrowly won the GOP primary (Denver Post). His thin primary margin and now-disputed endorsement claim raise questions about the strength of his candidacy heading into the general election against Democrat Jared Polis.

**The conservative angle:** Republicans need a unified, credible nominee to challenge Polis's progressive agenda on taxes, energy policy, and government overreach. Marx's credibility gap—overstating support from Colorado's last Republican governor—could undermine GOP turnout and donor confidence when conservatives should be mobilizing against Democratic control of state government.

Owens's cautious stance suggests even establishment Republicans are taking a wait-and-see approach. Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Marx can clarify his actual endorsements and build momentum, or whether the primary's narrow victory signals deeper problems with his viability in November.
