# GOP Rep. Massie Forces Impeachment Vote Against Defense Secretary Hegseth Over Iran Operations

Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.) introduced eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth on the House floor, alleging violations of the War Powers Resolution in Iran operations (Fox News). The Kentucky lawmaker, who lost his reelection bid to a Trump-backed challenger earlier this year (Just The News), forced a vote on the measure despite Pentagon pushback and expectations the articles will not clear the lower chamber (Just The News).

Massie's move represents a rare intra-Republican clash over executive war powers—a constitutional tension that has plagued administrations of both parties for decades. While conservatives generally support a strong defense posture, some libertarian-leaning Republicans like Massie argue that military actions abroad require explicit congressional authorization under the War Powers Act, not just executive discretion.

The Pentagon's response underscores the administration's confidence that the impeachment effort lacks support. With Trump-aligned Republicans controlling the House, Hegseth faces no real removal risk.
