# Trump Secures Iran Deal From Position of Strength—Unlike Obama's Capitulation

President Trump has negotiated an end to hostilities with Iran after demonstrating American military resolve, a stark contrast to the Obama administration's failed diplomacy that enriched Tehran without securing lasting peace.

Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth emphasized Thursday that the agreement was "born of strength of American action," underscoring that Trump's willingness to use force—not appeasement—brought Iran to the negotiating table (The Daily Wire). The deal comes after Operation Epic Fury eliminated dozens of senior Iranian military officials and commanders, including Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, fundamentally weakening Tehran's negotiating position.

National Review notes that Trump proved his own adage: "Iran has never won a war, yet never lost a negotiation"—until now. This agreement stands in sharp relief to the 2015 Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action, which conservatives argue handed Iran sanctions relief and cash while the regime continued destabilizing the Middle East.

Hegseth made clear the Pentagon remains "ready to go" if Trump decides to restart military operations, signaling that American strength, not weakness, underpins the accord. Republicans argue this demonstrates that peace through strength works where Obama-era engagement failed.

Watch whether Congress will ratify this deal and whether Iran honors its commitments—or whether the left will resurrect its failed talking points about diplomacy over deterrence.
