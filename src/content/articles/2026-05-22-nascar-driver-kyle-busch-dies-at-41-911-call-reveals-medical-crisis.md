# NASCAR Star Kyle Busch Dies at 41; 911 Call Reveals Medical Emergency

Kyle Busch, a prominent NASCAR driver, died Thursday at age 41, according to PBS NewsHour and CBS News. A 911 call released by authorities reveals that the day before his death, Busch was experiencing shortness of breath and coughing up blood while in a racing simulator in North Carolina, CBS News reported.

Busch's family said earlier he had been hospitalized with a "severe illness" three days before he was scheduled to compete in the Coca-Cola 600 at Charlotte Motor Speedway, according to PBS NewsHour. No official cause of death has been announced.

The 911 call, which described the medical emergency Busch was experiencing, provides the first public details about the circumstances leading up to his death. The exact nature of his illness remains undisclosed by authorities and his family.

Watch for an official cause of death announcement from medical examiners or a formal statement from Busch's family or NASCAR.
