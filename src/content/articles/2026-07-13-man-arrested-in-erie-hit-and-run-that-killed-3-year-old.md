# Man Arrested in Erie Hit-and-Run Death of 3-Year-Old

Colorado law enforcement made an arrest this week in a case that should remind every parent why accountability matters—and why our courts must deliver justice swiftly.

Seth Kirkegaard, 32, was arrested Friday in connection with a February hit-and-run crash in Erie that killed a 3-year-old child, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31. The driver fled the scene, leaving a family devastated and a community demanding answers.

Details remain limited as the case moves through the system, but the arrest represents a critical step toward closure for the victim's family. Colorado's hit-and-run laws carry serious penalties precisely because leaving the scene of a fatal crash is an act of cowardice that compounds tragedy.

This case underscores a broader concern: Colorado's criminal justice system must ensure that suspects are prosecuted vigorously and sentences reflect the severity of such crimes. Victims' families deserve a legal process that doesn't drag on indefinitely.

Conservatives and law-and-order advocates will be watching whether prosecutors pursue the strongest possible charges and whether the courts move this case to resolution without delay.
