# GOP Senator Blasts 'Hit Job' Report as Schumer-Backed Political Attack

Kansas Republican Senator Roger Marshall is accusing Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer of orchestrating a New York Times investigation into lawsuits against his former obstetric patients, calling it a coordinated "hit job" ahead of his reelection bid (Fox News Politics).

Marshall, who practiced obstetrics before entering politics, contends the timing and sourcing of the report reflect Democratic opposition research rather than legitimate journalism. Republicans argue the story represents a pattern of using media allies to damage GOP candidates through personal attacks unrelated to policy or governance.

The allegations underscore broader conservative skepticism of mainstream media's role in Democratic campaigns. Schumer's office has not publicly responded to Marshall's claims.

Meanwhile, Denver Health's NICU unit offers a counterpoint to the controversy: former patients recently reunited with caregivers who saved their lives, with one mother now pursuing nursing as a career inspired by her experience (9News Denver). The reunion highlights the positive impact of quality medical care and dedicated healthcare workers.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether national media outlets scrutinize the sourcing and timing of political hit pieces with the same rigor they apply to Republican-critical stories.
