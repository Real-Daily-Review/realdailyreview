# Trump Touts White House Repairs; Democrats Resort to Name-Calling

President Trump is highlighting necessary renovations to a deteriorating White House, while Democrats offer nothing but inflammatory rhetoric instead of substantive criticism.

Trump told Fox News the executive mansion was in "very bad condition" before his restoration work began, with the exterior skin "practically falling off." According to Just The News, the president included photographic evidence of the repairs, which encompass White House ballroom construction and broader restoration efforts. Trump argued that "past Presidents did not take care of it," pointing to decades of deferred maintenance.

Rather than engage on the merits, Michigan Democratic Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed resorted to name-calling on CBS's "Face the Nation," calling Trump "the Barbarian in the White House" (Breitbart). The comment exemplifies how progressives avoid policy debates by attacking character.

**The conservative case:** Maintaining federal property is a basic government responsibility. If the White House infrastructure was genuinely neglected, documenting and fixing it demonstrates competent stewardship—not excess. Democrats' refusal to discuss the actual condition of the building or the scope of necessary repairs suggests they have no serious alternative vision.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether mainstream media will cover the documented condition of the White House before and after repairs, or continue amplifying Democratic personal attacks instead of reporting facts.
