# Ukraine Strikes Russian Data Centers; Zelensky Demands Better Intel Sharing

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky claims drone strikes on three Yandex data centers—Russia's Google equivalent—have crippled the Kremlin's online disinformation apparatus, according to *The Daily Wire*. Automated traffic from the company's network collapsed almost immediately following the four-day campaign.

Yet Zelensky is simultaneously pressing Western allies for better intelligence coordination. In remarks reported by the *Washington Examiner*, he lamented receiving "no early warning from our partners" on recent Russian ballistic missile strikes against Kyiv, calling real-time intelligence sharing "one of the most critical issues" for saving Ukrainian lives.

The dual message underscores Ukraine's precarious position: scoring tactical wins against Russian infrastructure while remaining dependent on U.S. and NATO intelligence feeds to prevent catastrophic civilian casualties. For American policymakers, it raises hard questions about the depth and speed of intelligence-sharing commitments to Kyiv.

**Colorado angle:** As Congress continues debating aid packages to Ukraine, taxpayers should track whether expanded intelligence-sharing operations require new spending or create long-term commitments beyond current appropriations.
