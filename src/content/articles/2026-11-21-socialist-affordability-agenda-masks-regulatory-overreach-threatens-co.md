# Socialist 'Affordability' Push Masks Real Cost to Taxpayers

New York City's socialist leadership is rebranding government overreach as "affordability"—a rhetorical sleight of hand that should alarm Colorado taxpayers watching similar policies gain traction locally.

According to National Review, the left's affordability agenda conflates government mandates with genuine cost relief, obscuring the reality that price controls and regulatory expansion typically backfire. When politicians impose new fees on businesses and then blame companies for "making customers aware" of those costs, they're practicing what conservatives call demagoguery—shifting blame for government-created problems onto the private sector.

The Washington Examiner notes that New York's socialist mayor is simultaneously pursuing soft-on-crime policies while claiming fiscal responsibility, a contradiction that exposes the incoherence of progressive governance. Disbanding police units and reducing incarceration while claiming to address public safety mirrors the defund-the-police movement that Colorado Democrats have flirted with.

**The Colorado angle:** As progressive activists push similar "affordability" schemes in Denver and Boulder—from rent controls to energy mandates—taxpayers should recognize the pattern. Government doesn't create affordability through regulation; it creates scarcity, higher costs, and unintended consequences that hurt the working families it claims to help.

Watch whether Colorado legislators adopt New York-style affordability rhetoric without addressing the actual cost-drivers: overregulation and government spending.
