# America's Energy Boom Stalled by Regulatory Red Tape—Colorado Must Break Free

Colorado's energy workers and taxpayers are watching Washington bungle what should be a no-brainer: unleashing American energy abundance to power an AI-driven economic boom.

According to the Washington Examiner, America faces an unprecedented energy expansion opportunity as data centers and AI infrastructure demand skyrocket—yet regulatory paralysis is strangling it. The problem isn't partisan: even a self-described Democrat contributor to the Examiner admits that "energy paralysis isn't Trump's fault," pointing instead to the maze of permits, lawsuits, and environmental delays that block pipeline construction, transmission lines, and critical mineral mining.

Breitbart notes that for 250 years, Americans have found the next energy source and built industries around it. Today's obstacle isn't geology or economics—it's government red tape.

For Colorado specifically, this matters enormously. Our state sits atop vast natural gas reserves and mineral wealth critical for battery production and grid infrastructure. Yet permitting delays and litigation—often weaponized by environmental groups—kill projects before they start. Colorado energy workers lose jobs. Taxpayers miss revenue. And the state falls behind in the race for data center investment and manufacturing.

Republicans and conservatives argue that streamlining permitting timelines and limiting frivolous litigation is essential to compete globally and secure American energy independence.
