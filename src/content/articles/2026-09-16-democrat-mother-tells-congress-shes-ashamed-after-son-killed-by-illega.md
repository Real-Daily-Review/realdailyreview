# Democrat Mother Breaks with Party Over Son's Death in Illegal Immigrant Crash

A lifelong Democrat testified before Congress that she is "ashamed" of her party after her son was killed by an illegal immigrant truck driver in California—a stark indictment of open-border policies from an unexpected source.

Elizabeth Carter told lawmakers that her son Billy, his wife Jenny, and their cat were killed just 16 days after the couple's wedding. "They lost the Russian roulette game our government is forcing them to play," Carter testified, according to the New York Post.

The case underscores a core conservative argument: lax immigration enforcement and sanctuary policies put American lives at risk. Republicans have long warned that inadequate border security and lenient deportation practices enable preventable tragedies. Democrats counter that illegal immigration's overall crime rate remains lower than native-born citizens and that comprehensive immigration reform—not enforcement—is the answer.

Carter's testimony carries weight precisely because she is a Democrat. Her willingness to publicly condemn her own party's immigration stance signals growing frustration among voters who feel abandoned by progressive leadership on public safety.

Colorado voters should watch whether this narrative gains traction in upcoming state and federal races, particularly as border security and immigration enforcement become defining campaign issues.
