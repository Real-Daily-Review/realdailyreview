# Front Range Rail Tax: Colorado Voters Face Billion-Dollar Boondoggle Dressed Up as Progress

Colorado taxpayers are being asked to bankroll yet another government megaproject that will drain billions while delivering minimal return—the Front Range rail tax masquerading as the "Colorado Connector."

Complete Colorado lays out the conservative case plainly: voters face a choice between "blowing billions on a train few will ride" or keeping that money in their own pockets. The project's backers aren't confident in honest debate. Instead, CPR News reports, they've deployed paid Instagram influencers and targeted mailers to sell what amounts to an "education" campaign—taxpayer-funded propaganda dressed in positive messaging.

This is textbook government overreach: massive spending on infrastructure most Coloradans won't use, funded by regressive taxation that hits working families hardest. The rail project promises connectivity and progress, but conservatives rightly ask: at what cost, and who actually benefits? Urban planners and transit bureaucrats, certainly. Colorado families already squeezed by inflation and housing costs? Less clear.

Republicans argue the state should prioritize fiscal restraint and let private enterprise—not government—drive transportation innovation. Democrats counter that public transit investment is essential for regional growth and emissions reduction, though they've offered no credible ridership projections or cost controls.

Watch whether Colorado voters reject this tax grab or whether slick influencer campaigns and mailers succeed in selling another generation of debt.
