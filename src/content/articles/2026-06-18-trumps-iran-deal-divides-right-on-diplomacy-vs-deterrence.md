# Trump's Iran Deal Splits Conservatives on Pragmatism vs. Caution

President Trump's framework agreement with Iran has divided the right over whether dealmaking with Tehran serves American interests or risks emboldening a hostile regime.

According to the Reagan Institute Summer Survey cited by Fox News, Americans are split: 39% favor a negotiated settlement while 36% back regime change—a narrow margin reflecting deep uncertainty about Iran policy.

Trump's supporters argue the agreement is a pragmatic diplomatic achievement that reduces war risk and opens communication channels with Washington's adversary. The Washington Examiner notes that Trump is presenting the deal as a "historic peace breakthrough" that lowers Middle East tensions.

Skeptics within conservative ranks worry the agreement may not go far enough or could strengthen Iran's hand. The Daily Wire questions whether negotiations have truly begun in earnest, framing Trump's approach as pragmatism rather than ideological victory—a characterization some hawks reject.

**The Colorado angle:** While Iran policy doesn't directly hit Colorado's energy sector like Middle East oil markets might, conservatives here should watch whether this agreement affects gas prices or energy independence—issues that matter to Colorado's economy and working families.

Watch whether Congress demands oversight of the agreement's implementation and whether Iran honors its commitments.
