# Stephen Colbert Signs Off From 'The Late Show' After Decade-Long Run

Stephen Colbert took the stage of "The Late Show" for the final time Thursday night, ending a decade-long tenure that reshaped late-night television, according to CBS News. The exit marks the conclusion of the "Late Show" franchise after 33 years on air.

During his time hosting, Colbert entertained and provoked audiences from the Ed Sullivan Theater stage in ways that transformed the comedic landscape, according to PBS NewsHour. His departure represents a significant moment for the late-night format, which has undergone considerable shifts in recent years as viewership patterns and media consumption habits have evolved.

The finale comes as the late-night landscape continues to experience transitions, with several hosts and programs undergoing changes. Colbert's exit will leave a notable gap in the nightly talk-show ecosystem that has long served as a platform for comedy, celebrity interviews, and political commentary.

Watch for announcements regarding who will helm "The Late Show" next and how the network plans to reshape its late-night programming strategy.
