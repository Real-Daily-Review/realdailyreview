# El Paso County Backs Education Agency Despite Christian School Controversy

El Paso County School District 49 has renewed its contract with a regional education service agency that backed a "public Christian school" proposal—a decision that keeps the organization afloat but leaves its future uncertain, according to the *Colorado Sun*.

The board's move signals tolerance for the agency's involvement in religiously-oriented education initiatives. Conservatives argue that public funding for explicitly Christian education models raises legitimate questions about secular governance and parental choice in a diverse district. The agency's survival now depends on whether other districts follow suit.

This comes as Colorado and nationwide debates intensify over what belongs in public schools. While the Christian school proposal itself remains contentious among parents and administrators, the district's decision to retain the agency suggests some board members see value in exploring alternative education models—even those with religious dimensions.

The broader Colorado angle: as families increasingly question progressive curricula and seek alternatives, regional education agencies become crucial gatekeepers. Whether districts embrace or reject religiously-informed education options will shape what Colorado families can access without leaving the public system.
