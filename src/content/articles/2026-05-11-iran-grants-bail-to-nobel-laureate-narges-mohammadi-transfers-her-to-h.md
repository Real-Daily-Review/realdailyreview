# Iranian Nobel Laureate Granted Bail, Hospitalized After Prison Collapse

Iranian authorities have granted bail to Nobel Peace Prize laureate Narges Mohammadi and transferred her to a Tehran hospital, according to her foundation and BBC World, following her collapse in prison more than a week prior.

Mohammadi, a prominent human rights activist, had been detained in Iran. The decision to release her on bail and move her to hospital care comes amid mounting international concern over her deteriorating health while incarcerated, according to PBS NewsHour.

The activist has long been a vocal critic of Iran's government and advocate for human rights, particularly women's rights. Her imprisonment and subsequent health crisis have drawn attention from international human rights organizations and governments worldwide.

Details on the specific charges against Mohammadi or the conditions of her bail were not immediately available in the reporting. Her foundation announced the hospital transfer Sunday, signaling the first major development in her case in recent days.
