# Autonomous Aircraft Completes Cross-Country Flight—What's Next for Colorado Aviation?

Joby Aviation has completed the first fully autonomous flight across the continental United States, with an aircraft traveling from California to North Carolina's Outer Banks, according to reports from 9News Denver and the New York Post. The milestone marks a significant step in autonomous aviation technology.

The achievement raises important questions for Colorado, home to major aviation hubs and a growing aerospace sector. While the technology promises efficiency gains, conservatives should scrutinize how federal regulators will oversee autonomous aircraft operations—and whether new rules will burden Colorado's aviation industry with costly compliance mandates.

Republicans have long warned that regulatory overreach can stifle innovation. The Federal Aviation Administration will need to balance safety with allowing American companies to compete globally without drowning in red tape. Colorado businesses and workers in aerospace manufacturing and operations should watch closely: overzealous regulation could drive development and jobs to less-regulated states or overseas.

The autonomous flight itself is an American engineering win worth celebrating. The real test comes next: whether Washington lets innovation flourish or uses this breakthrough as an excuse to expand government control over the skies.

**Watch for:** FAA rulemaking on autonomous aircraft certification and whether Colorado's aviation sector gets a seat at the table before bureaucrats lock in restrictive standards.
