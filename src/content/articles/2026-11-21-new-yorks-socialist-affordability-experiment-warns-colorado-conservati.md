# New York's Socialist Playbook Shows Why Government 'Affordability' Plans Backfire

New York City's socialist mayor Zohran Mamdani is running an experiment in progressive governance—and conservatives say it's a cautionary tale for Colorado voters watching similar rhetoric take hold locally.

Mamdani has banned tourist attractions, rebranded new government fees as "affordability" measures, and announced plans to close the city's central jail while dramatically reducing the incarcerated population, according to the *Washington Examiner*. He's also dismantling an elite NYPD unit. The *National Review* argues this exposes a fundamental truth: "affordability and socialism are incompatible."

Republicans counter that progressive "affordability" schemes—which typically involve expanded government control, new regulations, and higher taxes dressed up in compassionate language—consistently fail to deliver lower costs. Instead, they argue, such policies drive up expenses for businesses and working families while expanding government dependency.

For Colorado, this matters. The state has seen similar affordability rhetoric from progressive lawmakers pushing rent control, expanded regulations, and tax increases. Conservatives warn that New York's experience shows these policies don't solve housing or cost-of-living crises; they worsen them by discouraging investment and job creation.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt New York's playbook—or whether voters reject it first.
