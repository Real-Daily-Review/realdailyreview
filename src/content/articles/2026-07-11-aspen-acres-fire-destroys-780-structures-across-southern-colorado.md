# Aspen Acres Fire Destroys 780+ Structures; Colorado Faces Massive Rebuild

Colorado's Aspen Acres Fire has obliterated 780 structures across Pueblo and Custer counties—far exceeding initial official counts—according to aerial analysis by Vexcel Data. The toll includes homes, garages, barns, and sheds, with damage assessments still incomplete as crews continue surveying the devastation (Colorado Sun).

Among the losses: a historic lodge in the Arvada area that served generations of Coloradans and held deep community significance (9News Denver). The destruction underscores the mounting toll of Colorado's wildfire season and raises urgent questions about forest management, prevention, and recovery resources.

**The conservative case:** Republicans and taxpayers should demand accountability on why Colorado's forests remain tinderboxes despite decades of environmental litigation blocking sensible timber management and fuel reduction. While federal and state agencies blame climate, critics point to regulatory gridlock that prevents landowners and agencies from thinning overgrown forests—the primary driver of catastrophic fire spread. Recovery will require massive state and federal spending; conservatives should insist on transparent accounting and prioritize private-sector rebuilding over government dependency.

Watch for: How state and federal agencies respond to calls for streamlined forest management and whether Colorado's legislature will finally unblock fuel-reduction projects.
