# Democrats' Midterm Win Could Mean Higher Taxes and Abortion Push—Colorado Taxpayers Should Brace

Democrats' sweeping midterm victories could resurrect billions in proposed tax increases targeting higher earners and corporations, threatening to unwind the Trump tax cuts that have benefited Colorado businesses and workers, according to *The Daily Wire*. The party has signaled plans to expand tax credits for lower-income families while simultaneously pursuing significantly higher rates on top earners—a classic redistribution play that conservatives argue kills job creation and investment.

Simultaneously, *National Review* warns that pro-lifers should expect Democrats to pursue a nationwide right to abortion if they consolidate power, reversing state-level protections that Colorado conservatives have fought to maintain.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that raising taxes during economic uncertainty stifles growth, particularly harmful to Colorado's energy sector, small businesses, and working families already squeezed by inflation. Tax hikes on corporations and high earners don't stay there—they cascade down through reduced hiring, lower wages, and fewer opportunities. Combined with a federal abortion mandate, Democrats would be imposing a radical progressive agenda that most Coloradans reject.

Democrats counter that their proposals target only the wealthy and corporations, offering relief to working families while funding social programs.
