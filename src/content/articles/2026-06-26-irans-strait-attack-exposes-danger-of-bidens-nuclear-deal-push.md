# Iran's Brazen Strait Attack Shows Why Biden's Nuclear Deal Talk Is Reckless

Iran's drone strike on a Singaporean cargo ship in the Strait of Hormuz this week—hitting a vessel on a U.N.-designated "safe route"—exposes the folly of the Biden administration's push toward a new nuclear agreement with Tehran, according to conservative analysts.

The attack, which endangered over 11,000 stranded seafarers, demonstrates Iran's contempt for international norms and U.S. diplomatic overtures. As the *Washington Examiner* reported, Gulf nations are now demanding any peace deal address far more than nuclear weapons—they want security guarantees covering maritime attacks and regional aggression.

Republicans argue that rewarding Iran with sanctions relief while it wages attacks on commercial shipping invites further provocation. The regime has shown it will strike American interests and allies regardless of diplomatic engagement. Conservatives warn that a weak nuclear accord—one that fails to address Iran's ballistic missile program, proxy militias, and maritime terrorism—amounts to appeasement.

For Colorado, this matters: energy markets remain vulnerable to Persian Gulf disruptions, and higher oil prices ripple through state economies and household budgets. Any deal that emboldens Iranian aggression threatens global energy stability.

Watch whether the Biden team conditions any nuclear agreement on Iran ceasing attacks on shipping and regional destabilization—or caves to Tehran's maximalist demands.
