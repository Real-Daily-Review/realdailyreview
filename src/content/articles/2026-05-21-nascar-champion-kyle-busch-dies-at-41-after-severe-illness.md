# NASCAR Champion Kyle Busch Dies at 41

Kyle Busch, a two-time NASCAR Cup Series champion, has died at age 41 following a severe illness, according to his family. Busch's family announced Thursday morning that he had been hospitalized with the illness and would miss racing at Charlotte Motor Speedway that weekend. He died later that day. The specific nature of the illness has not been disclosed.

Busch was one of NASCAR's most accomplished drivers, having won more races than anyone across the sport's three national series, according to PBS NewsHour. His death came suddenly, with the family's hospitalization announcement preceding his death by hours, according to the Washington Examiner.

The racing community and broader sports world are expected to respond in the coming days with tributes and reflections on his career and legacy.
