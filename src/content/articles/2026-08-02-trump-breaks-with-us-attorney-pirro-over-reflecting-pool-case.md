# Trump Breaks With U.S. Attorney Over Reflecting Pool Case

Former President Donald Trump publicly disagreed "100%" with U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro over how the Justice Department handled charges related to damage at the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool, according to Fox News Politics. Trump called the incident "VANDALISM" on Truth Social, signaling frustration with the DOJ's approach to the case.

The Daily Caller reports that the Justice Department dismissed charges in what it characterized as a "botched" prosecution, throwing the Interior Department under the bus in the process. The move marks another chapter in what has become a contentious saga, with Trump—who appointed Pirro to her DOJ role—now at odds with his own appointee over case strategy and outcome.

The dispute underscores tensions within Trump's administration over how aggressively to pursue vandalism charges and raises questions about coordination between federal agencies on high-profile cases. Conservatives have long argued that government agencies should take property damage and vandalism seriously, particularly at national monuments.
