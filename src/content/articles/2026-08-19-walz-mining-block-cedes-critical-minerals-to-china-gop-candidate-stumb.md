# Minnesota Governor's Mining Block Risks U.S. Energy Independence While GOP Senate Candidate Stumbles on National Security

Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz's executive order blocking mining permits near the Boundary Waters is drawing fire from Republicans who say it abandons domestic mineral production to China's advantage. Rep. Tom Emmer called the move "stupid," arguing that blocking access to critical minerals needed for defense and technology manufacturing weakens American security and hands Beijing a strategic win (Fox News).

The timing underscores a broader GOP concern: as the U.S. competes with China for rare-earth and battery minerals essential to military systems and energy infrastructure, progressive governors are choking off domestic supply. Colorado conservatives should note the parallel risk here—environmental restrictions on mining and energy development in our state similarly threaten domestic mineral independence and good-paying jobs.

Meanwhile, the GOP's own messaging discipline took a hit when South Carolina Republican Senate candidate Darline Graham Nordone admitted during a televised debate that national security is "not my thing" when asked about Taiwan and the South China Sea (Breitbart). The candid admission—however refreshingly honest—handed Democrats a soundbite on Republican preparedness.
