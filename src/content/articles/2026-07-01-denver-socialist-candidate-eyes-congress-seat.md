# Denver Socialist Candidate Eyes Congress Seat as Voters Face Ideological Choice

A 29-year-old Democratic Socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress, forcing Colorado voters to decide whether they want someone openly aligned with the DSA's far-left agenda in Washington.

According to CPR News, Melat Kiros—a Democratic Socialist—is positioning herself as Denver's next congressional representative. The DSA platform typically advocates for wealth redistribution, expanded government control over markets, and policies that conservatives argue would burden Colorado's economy and individual liberty.

Republicans and conservatives warn that electing a self-identified socialist to Congress would push Colorado further left on taxes, regulation, and government spending. They argue Denver voters should consider whether DSA-backed policies—higher taxes on businesses and earners, expanded entitlements, and aggressive climate mandates—align with Colorado's energy sector, small-business community, and working families already squeezed by inflation and cost-of-living pressures.

Democrats frame Kiros as a progressive voice for working people and climate action. They argue her candidacy reflects growing frustration with establishment politics.

The race reflects a broader ideological split: whether Colorado remains a state that welcomes business investment and energy development, or one that embraces socialist economic models that have failed elsewhere.
