# Texas Gov. Abbott Kills Airport Wudu Stations; Colorado Should Ask Why Federal Dollars Fund Religious Facilities

Texas Gov. Greg Abbott has forced Dallas-Fort Worth and Houston airports to scrap plans for Islamic foot-washing stations, calling the facilities an improper use of state and federal grants. Abbott directed Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy and airport officials to review how public money was being spent on religious infrastructure (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

Conservatives argue that taxpayer-funded airports should remain neutral public spaces—not venues for subsidizing religious practices of any faith. Abbott's position: if airports want to accommodate religious ablution needs, private funding or voluntary airport fees should cover it, not grants tied to state and federal transportation dollars.

**The Colorado angle matters here.** Denver International Airport and other Colorado facilities receiving federal transportation grants should clarify their own policies on religious accommodation projects. Taxpayers deserve transparency: are Colorado airports considering similar facilities? What's the legal and fiscal standard?

Democrats typically frame such accommodations as inclusive pluralism; Republicans counter that government neutrality means not funding *any* religion's infrastructure—Christian, Jewish, Muslim, or otherwise.

Watch whether Colorado airports face similar pressure or whether DIA proactively clarifies its grant-funded spending priorities before controversy arrives.
