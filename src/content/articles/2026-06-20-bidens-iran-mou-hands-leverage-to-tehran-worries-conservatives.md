# Biden's Iran Deal Hands Leverage to Tehran—Colorado Should Demand Answers

The Biden administration's Memorandum of Understanding with Iran represents a capitulation that weakens American leverage and strengthens a regime hostile to U.S. interests, according to conservative analysts. National Review editors argue the deal gives "momentum to Iran" while offering little benefit to Americans, with Trump-era negotiators noting that Iran "has never won a war, yet never lost a negotiation."

The timing raises questions about political calculation. Vice President JD Vance's involvement in the agreement during a book tour signals the administration is prioritizing diplomatic optics over substance, The Daily Wire reports.

**Why Colorado cares:** Energy markets respond to Middle East stability. Weakened U.S. leverage with Iran could destabilize oil prices and complicate Colorado's energy sector recovery. Additionally, any agreement that reduces pressure on Iranian nuclear ambitions affects national security—a concern Colorado's defense contractors and military installations take seriously.

Republicans argue the deal repeats the failures of the 2015 JCPOA, which Iran systematically violated while the U.S. remained constrained. Democrats counter that diplomacy prevents conflict, though they've offered no public defense of this specific MOU's terms.

**Watch next:** Whether Congress demands a full briefing on the agreement's verification mechanisms and whether Colorado's congressional delegation pushes for transparency on how this affects U.S. energy independence.
