# House Democrat Launches Third Impeachment Push Against Trump—Wasting Time While Colorado Families Struggle

Rep. Al Green (D-TX) is forcing another House-wide vote to impeach President Trump, marking his third impeachment attempt in as many years—a move Republicans say is pure partisan theater divorced from any legitimate constitutional purpose.

Green announced Monday he'll use a privileged resolution to bypass committee review and drag the full House into yet another impeachment circus. According to *Just The News*, Green insisted Democrats don't need to wait for midterm results to proceed, signaling this is about energizing the base, not governing.

**The Republican argument:** This is exactly the kind of wasteful partisan grandstanding that infuriates Colorado voters. While families struggle with inflation, energy costs, and water scarcity, House Democrats are burning floor time on impeachment stunts that go nowhere. Colorado's delegation—particularly moderate Democrats—should be focused on kitchen-table issues: securing water rights, supporting responsible energy development, and protecting jobs. Instead, they're enabling circus acts that undermine Congress's credibility.

Democrats counter that impeachment is a constitutional duty when they believe presidential conduct warrants it. But even liberal media has largely dismissed Green's previous attempts as lacking serious support.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado's Democratic representatives vote with Green or break ranks—a telling sign of whether they're answering to their districts or the party's activist wing.
