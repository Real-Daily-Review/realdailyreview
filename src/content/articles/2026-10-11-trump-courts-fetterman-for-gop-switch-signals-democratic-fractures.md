# Trump Signals Door Open for Fetterman Party Switch

Trump is openly courting Pennsylvania Sen. John Fetterman to abandon the Democratic Party, calling him a "great guy" and suggesting he'd thrive as a Republican (Fox News Politics). The former president told reporters Fetterman "does better as a Republican than he does as a Democrat" and said he would "love" to have him join GOP ranks (Just The News).

The overture reflects Trump's confidence in Republican momentum and hints at fractures within Democratic ranks. Fetterman, a working-class Democrat who won his 2022 Senate race in a swing state, has occasionally broken with progressive orthodoxy—a pattern that may have caught Trump's attention.

For Colorado conservatives, the subtext matters: if a Democrat senator from a purple state is being actively recruited across the aisle, it signals weakness in the left's coalition and strength in Republican messaging on kitchen-table issues like inflation, crime, and government overreach. Fetterman's potential defection would also shift Senate math, though Pennsylvania Democrats would likely mount fierce resistance to any party switch.

Republicans see this as evidence that moderate Democrats are increasingly out of step with their party's progressive wing. Democrats dismiss it as political theater.

Watch whether Fetterman responds publicly and whether other swing-state Democrats face similar recruitment pressure from the right.
