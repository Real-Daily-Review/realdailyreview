# Colorado's New Governor Weiser Signals Big-Government Agenda From Day One

Phil Weiser's victory as Colorado's next governor comes with a troubling blueprint: expanded state control over data centers, social media regulation, and youth programs—priorities that signal more government spending and intervention ahead (CPR News).

The Democrat governor-elect outlined his legislative agenda at a post-election press conference, pledging to "work across the aisle" despite Democrats holding a supermajority in both chambers (CPR News). Republicans and conservatives should be skeptical. Weiser's focus on regulating data centers and social media represents exactly the kind of government overreach that stifles business innovation and free speech. Colorado's tech sector—a genuine economic bright spot—doesn't need state bureaucrats micromanaging operations or content moderation.

His emphasis on youth programs signals another round of taxpayer-funded expansions, though specifics remain vague. Conservatives argue that bloated government social initiatives rarely deliver results and often create dependency rather than opportunity.

Weiser defeated his Republican rival decisively (Denver Post), inheriting a state legislature controlled by Democrats. While he gestures toward bipartisanship, the supermajority means Republicans will have minimal leverage to block tax hikes, regulatory expansion, or spending increases.

**Watch for:** Weiser's first 100 days and which bills Democrats prioritize—they'll reveal whether his "across the aisle" talk is genuine or just election-year rhetoric.
