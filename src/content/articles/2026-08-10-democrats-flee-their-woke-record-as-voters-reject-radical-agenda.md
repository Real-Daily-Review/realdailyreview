# Democrats Can't Run From Their Woke Record—And Colorado Should Remember

Democratic candidates nationwide are desperately trying to distance themselves from the radical rhetoric they championed just years ago. Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and other progressive leaders who spent 2019-2021 pushing aggressive woke policies are now hoping voters have short memories—but their words are permanently on the record.

The Washington Examiner notes that Democratic candidates, including Wisconsin gubernatorial front-runner Francesca Hong, are actively walking back statements from the recent past. Republicans argue this represents a cynical pivot, not genuine change. As The Daily Wire points out, modern technology has made it impossible for progressives to escape their own rhetoric; every radical proposal, every inflammatory statement lives forever in digital archives.

For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats have pushed similar woke agendas—from aggressive climate regulations that threaten energy jobs to progressive education policies that have drawn fierce parent backlash. If national Democrats are now admitting their 2019-2021 positions were mistakes, Colorado voters deserve clarity on whether state Democrats agree.

Conservatives say this retreat proves the woke experiment failed with ordinary Americans. The question now: Will Colorado's Democratic establishment own their own progressive overreach, or will they try the same memory-hole strategy?
