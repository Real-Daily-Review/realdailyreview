# Pentagon Acknowledges 153 Civilian Deaths in Yemen Strikes

The Trump administration's military campaign against Houthi rebels in Yemen resulted in 153 civilian deaths and over 200 wounded across three strikes in April 2025, according to a Pentagon assessment released this week.

The acknowledgment marks a rare moment of transparency from the Defense Department on collateral damage—a persistent challenge in counterterrorism operations. The strikes targeted Houthi positions amid the Iranian-backed militia's attacks on commercial shipping in the Red Sea, a campaign that has disrupted global trade and threatened U.S. naval operations.

The Pentagon's disclosure comes as the Trump administration balances aggressive counterterrorism with growing international scrutiny over civilian casualties in military operations. The administration has argued that strikes against Houthi infrastructure are necessary to protect American interests and global commerce, though critics—including some foreign governments and human rights organizations—have questioned the proportionality of the response.
