# Aspen Acres Fire Containment Grows as Thousands Return Home

Firefighters have doubled containment on Colorado's massive Aspen Acres blaze, allowing residents in Pueblo County to begin returning home—but don't expect this fight to end soon.

Nearly 2,000 firefighters are battling the now 97,000-acre inferno in Southern Colorado, with crews anticipating weeks of continued effort ahead, according to CPR News. The Colorado Sun reports that progress on the Aspen Acres fire and other blazes across the state has accelerated containment efforts, offering some relief to evacuated families.

The scale of the operation underscores a persistent challenge for Colorado: massive wildfires that strain resources and threaten communities, property, and livelihoods. While firefighting crews deserve credit for their aggressive response, conservatives have long argued that overly restrictive forest management policies—driven by environmental regulations that limit timber harvesting and controlled burns—have left Colorado's forests dangerously overgrown and fuel-laden. Proper land stewardship, not just reactive firefighting, prevents catastrophes like this.

Residents returning home face weeks of uncertainty as containment continues.
