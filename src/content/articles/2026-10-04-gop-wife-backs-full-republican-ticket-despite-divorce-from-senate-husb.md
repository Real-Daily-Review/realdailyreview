# GOP Wife Backs Full Republican Ticket Despite Divorce From Senate Candidate

Texas State Senator Angela Paxton is voting straight Republican in November—including for her estranged husband Ken Paxton's Senate race—signaling that party loyalty trumps personal marital breakdown in high-stakes elections.

In a weekend advertisement, Angela Paxton told voters she's backing the full GOP slate, framing the choice as bigger than individual candidates. "It's not about the individual players," she said, according to Breitbart. "It's about getting the whole team across the finish line." She filed for divorce from Ken Paxton last year but stopped short of naming him directly in her endorsement.

The move underscores a hard political reality: even when personal relationships fracture, Republican operatives and family members often prioritize unified party messaging in competitive races. Ken Paxton faces Democrat James Talarico in what sources describe as a tight Texas Senate contest.

The Paxton endorsement carries weight in suburban Texas, where Angela holds her own state Senate seat. Her public backing of the GOP ticket—despite ongoing divorce proceedings—sends a clear signal to Republican voters that family disputes won't derail party unity.
