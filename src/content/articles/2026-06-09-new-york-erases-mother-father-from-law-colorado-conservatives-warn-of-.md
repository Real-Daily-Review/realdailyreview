# New York Erases 'Mother' and 'Father' From Law—A Warning for Colorado on Progressive Overreach

New York Democrats just proved that no institution is too sacred for progressive social engineering: the state legislature has systematically scrubbed the words "mother" and "father" from family court statutes, domestic relations law, child support rules, and education law, replacing them with clinical bureaucratic terms like "gestating parent" and "non-gestating parent" (Washington Examiner).

Conservatives argue this represents exactly the kind of ideological mission creep that threatens common sense and parental rights. By erasing foundational family language from law, Democrats are imposing a radical worldview on courts, schools, and child welfare systems—all without meaningful public debate.

The Colorado angle matters here. While Colorado hasn't yet followed New York's path, progressive legislators in Denver have shown appetite for similar gender-ideology experiments in schools and state policy. This New York precedent signals what's coming if conservatives don't push back: the systematic replacement of biological and legal reality with activist terminology that confuses courts, undermines parental authority, and treats mothers and fathers as outdated concepts.

Coloradans should watch whether Denver Democrats attempt to import similar language into state family law, education statutes, or child welfare rules—and be ready to fight it.
