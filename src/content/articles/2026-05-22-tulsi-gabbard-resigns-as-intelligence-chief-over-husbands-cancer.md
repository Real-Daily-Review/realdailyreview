# Tulsi Gabbard Resigns as Director of National Intelligence

Tulsi Gabbard has resigned as director of national intelligence, citing her husband's diagnosis with a rare form of bone cancer, according to CBS News and The Hill.

Gabbard said she must step away from public service to support her husband, Abraham, through the medical challenges ahead. The former congresswoman's departure marks the latest in a series of high-profile exits from President Trump's cabinet, according to the Financial Times.

No successor has been named. The timing and circumstances of Gabbard's resignation come as the intelligence community faces ongoing leadership transitions.
