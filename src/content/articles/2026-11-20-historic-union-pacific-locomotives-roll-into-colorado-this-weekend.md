# Historic Steam Locomotives Roll Into Colorado This Weekend

Union Pacific's two legendary steam engines—the world's largest "Big Boy" No. 4014 and the fastest "Living Legend" No. 844—are steaming into Colorado for a rare holiday tour, marking the first time in six years the Living Legend has run the rails.

The locomotives will make stops in Greeley, Denver, and La Salle this weekend, drawing railroad enthusiasts and families across the state (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31). The Big Boy, a massive Union Pacific workhorse from the steam era, pairs with No. 844 for what amounts to a moving piece of American industrial heritage—a reminder of the rail infrastructure that built the West and still connects Colorado's economy to national markets.

For Colorado, these vintage engines represent more than nostalgia. They underscore the state's deep ties to transportation, energy, and commerce. Union Pacific remains a critical player in moving Colorado coal, agricultural products, and goods to market, even as the railroad industry navigates modern regulatory pressures and environmental mandates from Washington.

The tour offers Coloradans a tangible connection to the era when private enterprise and engineering ingenuity—not government mandates—drove American infrastructure forward.

Watch for how many Coloradans turn out: strong attendance signals enduring public appetite for free-market industrial achievement over green-energy rhetoric.
