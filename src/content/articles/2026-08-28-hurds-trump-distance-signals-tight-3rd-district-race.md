# Colorado's 3rd District Tightens as Hurd Distances Himself from Trump

Rep. Jeff Hurd's once-comfortable reelection path in Colorado's 3rd Congressional District is suddenly competitive, forcing the incumbent to publicly break with President Trump as national GOP approval sags (Colorado Sun, CPR News).

Hurd entered 2026 expecting a smooth runway to another term. But Trump's declining approval ratings have reopened what looked like a safe Republican seat, prompting Hurd to highlight his disagreements with the president—a tactical shift that underscores the political headwinds facing GOP candidates in purple districts.

**The Conservative Angle:** Republicans argue that distancing from Trump on specific issues can be a legitimate strategy in swing districts, but conservatives worry that wholesale repudiation of the president's agenda—particularly on energy, border security, or regulatory rollback—could demoralize the base. Colorado's 3rd District, which spans rural and exurban terrain dependent on energy development and agriculture, will likely turn on whether voters see Hurd as a principled independent or a fair-weather ally.

**Watch:** Whether Hurd's Trump separation strategy actually moves the needle in a district where rural voters still favor the president's policies on energy and immigration.
