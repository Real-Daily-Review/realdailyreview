# Loveland Police Justified in Shooting Armed Domestic Violence Suspect

Loveland police officers were right to use deadly force against a man who fired on them during a domestic violence call—a stark reminder that law enforcement must have the freedom to protect themselves and the public without second-guessing from activists and prosecutors.

According to Loveland Police, officers responded to a domestic violence report and confronted the suspect on Banyan Avenue. The man was armed and opened fire on officers, who returned fire in self-defense, fatally wounding him (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31 Denver).

This is textbook justified use of force. An armed suspect who shoots at police officers has made his choice. Officers have a constitutional right to go home to their families, and Colorado law—like most states—permits lethal force when officers face imminent threat of death or serious bodily injury.

Conservatives have long argued that demonizing police for defending themselves against armed suspects undermines public safety and officer morale. When officers hesitate in split-second decisions, innocent people die. Colorado should continue backing its law enforcement and resisting calls from the left to criminalize self-defense by cops.

Watch whether progressive activists or prosecutors attempt to second-guess this shooting or file charges against officers acting within their legal authority.
