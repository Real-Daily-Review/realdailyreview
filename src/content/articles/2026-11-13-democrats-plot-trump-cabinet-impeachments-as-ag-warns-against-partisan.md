# Democrats Target Trump Officials With Impeachment Threats as AG Blanche Warns Against Partisan Witch Hunts

Colorado voters should brace for another round of partisan warfare: Democrats are openly plotting investigations and potential impeachment of Trump's cabinet nominees, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio, border czar Tom Homan, and commerce secretary Howard Lutnick (Fox News). The threat signals Democrats' intent to obstruct Trump's agenda through congressional harassment rather than legislative debate.

The distinction matters: Blanche is drawing a line between legitimate oversight and what conservatives argue amounts to a coordinated campaign of political retaliation.

Republicans counter that Democrats are abandoning their constitutional duty to govern in favor of scorched-earth opposition. The pattern echoes the left's failed impeachment efforts against Trump during his first term—costly theater that divides the country and wastes taxpayer resources on political vendettas rather than solving real problems.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers: watch whether Senate Democrats, including Colorado's Michael Bennet, participate in these obstruction efforts or instead focus on actual legislative work that benefits constituents.
