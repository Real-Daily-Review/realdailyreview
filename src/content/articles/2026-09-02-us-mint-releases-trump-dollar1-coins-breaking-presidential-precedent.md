# Trump $1 Coins Now in Circulation—A Commemorative First

The U.S. Mint has released $1 coins featuring President Trump's likeness to mark America's 250th birthday, making Trump the first sitting president to appear on circulating U.S. coinage, according to 9News Denver and Just The News.

The coin design places Trump's image on the "heads" side, with the Great Seal and a "250" marking on the reverse. While former presidents have historically appeared on coins after leaving office, Trump breaks that precedent while still in the White House—a distinction that underscores his outsized cultural footprint in American politics.

The New York Post notes that collectors seeking these coins will pay a premium above face value, as the Mint sells them at collector prices rather than standard circulation rates. This is standard practice for commemorative coinage, though it means everyday Americans won't encounter these coins casually in their wallets.

The move reflects Trump's enduring influence within the Republican Party and among his base, who view such commemorations as fitting recognition of his presidency and policy agenda. Critics on the left have predictably objected to honoring a sitting president on currency, but the Mint's authority to issue commemorative coins gives the administration broad discretion here.
