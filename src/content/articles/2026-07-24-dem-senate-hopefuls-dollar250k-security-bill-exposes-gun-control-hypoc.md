# Democratic Senate Hopeful's Six-Figure Security Tab Exposes 'Soft-on-Crime' Hypocrisy

Former North Carolina Gov. Roy Cooper, the Democratic frontrunner for a U.S. Senate seat, has spent more than a quarter-million dollars on private security this election cycle—a striking contradiction that Republicans are weaponizing against his "soft-on-crime" record.

According to Fox News Politics, the expense fuels GOP arguments that Democrats demand stricter gun control and reduced police funding for ordinary Americans while surrounding themselves with armed protection. Cooper's lavish security detail stands in sharp contrast to his progressive messaging on criminal justice reform and gun restrictions.

Republicans argue the disparity exposes the fundamental hypocrisy of Democratic leadership: they lecture working families about public safety while insulating themselves behind private armed guards—a luxury most Colorado taxpayers and small-business owners cannot afford.

Democrats have not publicly addressed the contradiction, though the optics are damaging heading into the general election.

The contrast matters for Colorado voters watching similar rhetoric from state Democrats pushing gun-control measures and police-reform agendas while crime remains a kitchen-table concern in Denver, Boulder, and Colorado Springs.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from Cooper's approach or double down on anti-gun messaging despite their own security arrangements.
