# Rubio's Venezuela Role Draws Left-Wing Fire—But Conservatives See Smart Statecraft

Secretary of State Marco Rubio's aggressive Venezuela strategy is drawing fierce criticism from the left, who view his hands-on approach to sanctions and oil-revenue management as overreach (National Review). Democrats and progressive outlets are targeting Rubio as a threat to their foreign-policy vision, but conservatives argue his Venezuela stance reflects necessary pushback against socialist regimes and their U.S. sympathizers.

According to The Daily Wire, Rubio has taken an "extraordinary role" in shaping Venezuela policy from Washington, including overseeing sanctions regimes and maintaining direct contact with the country's acting leadership. The approach represents a sharp departure from the Obama-Biden era's diplomatic accommodation of hostile regimes.

**The conservative case:** Rubio's Venezuela policy reflects a clear-eyed view that American interests demand confronting socialist governments that threaten regional stability and harbor anti-American actors. His willingness to use economic leverage—sanctions and oil-revenue controls—is exactly the kind of principled, results-oriented diplomacy conservatives have long demanded.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** Energy markets and oil prices affect Colorado's economy and energy workers directly. A tougher Venezuela stance could influence global crude supplies and domestic energy independence—issues critical to Colorado's oil and gas sector.

Watch whether Congress moves to constrain Rubio's Venezuela authority or whether his approach becomes the template for future hostile-regime policy.
