# Colorado AG Race Heats Up as Griswold Faces Primary Challenge Over Litigation Strategy

Jena Griswold's prominence as Colorado's Democratic attorney general—and her aggressive courtroom battles against the Trump administration—have made her a target in her own party's primary race.

According to the *Denver Post*, Griswold's Democratic opponents acknowledge shared opposition to Trump but argue her litigation approach has been counterproductive. The dispute centers on *how* Colorado should challenge federal policy, not *whether* it should. Meanwhile, conservatives note that Griswold's office has pursued costly legal campaigns funded by Colorado taxpayers—a pattern her primary rivals are now weaponizing.

The race reflects a broader Democratic anxiety: Griswold's high-profile AG tenure has kept Colorado in constant litigation mode, racking up legal fees and staff resources. Republicans argue this represents exactly the kind of government overreach and wasteful spending that should concern Colorado voters—using the attorney general's office as a political cudgel rather than a neutral enforcer of law.

The primary outcome will signal whether Colorado Democrats want to dial back the confrontational approach or double down. Conservatives should watch whether the eventual Democratic nominee commits to restraint on litigation spending or continues the Griswold model of aggressive, taxpayer-funded courtroom activism.
