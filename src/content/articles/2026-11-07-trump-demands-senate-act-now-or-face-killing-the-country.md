# Trump Demands Senate Action After Midterms, Warns of Legislative Gridlock

President Trump is demanding the Republican-controlled Senate move aggressively during the lame-duck session, warning that legislative paralysis is "killing our country," according to the Washington Examiner. Trump's rebuke signals frustration with Senate Republicans over stalled priorities heading into the new Congress.

The midterm results handed Republicans control of both chambers, but Trump's criticism underscores internal GOP tensions over legislative strategy and pace. National Review notes the results present a critical moment for the party's direction going forward.

**The conservative case:** Republicans won the midterms on a mandate for action—border security, energy independence, tax relief, and rollback of Biden-era regulations. Conservatives argue the Senate must capitalize on this momentum immediately, not waste time on procedural delays or bipartisan compromise that waters down conservative priorities. The clock is ticking before the new Congress convenes, and every day of inaction squanders political capital.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** Energy development, water policy, and Second Amendment protections all depend on Senate follow-through. Colorado Republicans expect swift action on permitting reform and energy independence—not endless committee hearings.

Watch whether Senate Republicans actually move legislation during the lame-duck session or punt major bills to 2027.
