# Rep. Max Miller Faces Federal Lawsuit Over Alleged Abuse, Nude Photo of Daughter

A sitting Republican congressman's reelection campaign is imploding under serious criminal allegations that should disqualify him from office.

Rep. Max Miller (R-Ohio) was sued Wednesday by his ex-wife, Emily Moreno, alleging he posted a nude photograph of their 2-year-old daughter online and physically abused Moreno herself—including holding a gun to her head and pouring hot water on her, according to *Just The News* and the *Washington Examiner*.

The federal lawsuit, filed in Ohio, claims Miller and his attorney violated federal laws protecting against disclosure of intimate images when Miller shared a Dropbox link containing a photograph depicting the child's genitalia, the *New York Post* reports. Moreno previously sought a restraining order against the congressman last month.

Miller has denied the allegations. His legal team has not yet responded to the latest lawsuit.
