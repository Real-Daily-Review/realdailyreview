# Navy Sailor Sentenced to 44 Years for Murder of Fellow Petty Officer

A Navy sailor has been handed a 44-year military prison sentence for the 2024 killing of Petty Officer Angelina Resendiz in his Virginia barracks room, according to *Just The News* and the *New York Post*.

The sailor pleaded guilty Monday to unpremeditated murder, aggravated assault by strangulation, indecent recording, obstruction of justice, and making a false official statement. In court, he admitted to the crime, stating: "I strangled her with my hands."

The case underscores persistent concerns among military readiness advocates about discipline, command accountability, and the safety of service members—particularly women—within the armed forces. While the military justice system delivered a conviction, conservatives have long argued that the Pentagon must prioritize unit cohesion, rigorous vetting, and swift accountability to maintain force effectiveness and protect personnel.

The conviction and sentencing represent the military court system functioning as intended, though questions remain about whether command-level oversight prevented warning signs from escalating into tragedy.
