# U.S. Mint Marks 250th Anniversary With Limited-Edition Quarters as Americans Split on National Pride

The U.S. Mint is striking 250,000 limited-edition 2026 Declaration of Independence quarters with a special "July 4th" privy mark ahead of America's semiquincentennial, according to 9News Denver.

The commemorative coins arrive as Americans remain divided on national optimism. A Fox News poll finds 53% of voters say they're proud of America, while 54% believe the nation's best days lie ahead as it approaches its 250th birthday.

The quarter release reflects official efforts to mark the milestone, though the modest print run—250,000 coins—suggests limited collector appeal. For Colorado numismatists and patriotic Americans, the coins offer a tangible keepsake of the founding document's principles: limited government, individual liberty, and constitutional restraint on federal power.

The broader polling snapshot reveals a nation at philosophical crossroads. While a slim majority expresses pride, the numbers underscore how progressive governance and regulatory overreach have eroded confidence in American institutions among nearly half the electorate. Conservatives argue that restoring constitutional limits on government and returning to free-market principles—not expanding federal programs—will genuinely restore American greatness.

Watch whether the Biden-Harris administration uses the 250th anniversary as a platform for additional spending or regulatory initiatives under the guise of "national renewal."
