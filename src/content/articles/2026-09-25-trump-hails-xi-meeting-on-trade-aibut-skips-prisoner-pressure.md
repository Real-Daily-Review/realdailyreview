# Trump Meets Xi on Trade, AI—But Where's the Pressure on Chinese Prisoners?

President Trump called his meeting with Chinese leader Xi Jinping a "great" encounter, discussing trade and artificial intelligence—but conservatives say the administration missed a critical opportunity to demand Beijing release political and religious prisoners held by the Communist regime.

According to *Just The News*, Trump told reporters the bilateral talks went well as he escorted Xi from the building. The president also floated rebranding AI as "super intelligence" during discussions, signaling a focus on tech competition with China.

Yet *The Daily Wire* notes a glaring absence: human rights. While trade and AI cooperation dominate the agenda, the Trump administration should prioritize securing the release of political and religious detainees languishing in Chinese custody—a moral and strategic imperative conservatives argue cannot be sidelined for commercial deals.

For Colorado, this matters. Chinese competition in AI and energy sectors directly impacts Colorado tech workers and energy development policy. Republicans have long warned that engagement with Beijing must come with strings attached: market access in exchange for demonstrable improvements in human rights and religious freedom.

Conservatives should watch whether Trump's team circles back to prisoner releases in follow-up negotiations—or whether commercial interests eclipse moral clarity.
