# Trump Rallies for Paxton in Texas; Colorado Republicans Watch Playbook

Donald Trump is doubling down on his 2024 midterm strategy, hitting the campaign trail hard across multiple states to shore up Republican nominees facing competitive races. Wednesday's rally in San Antonio for Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton—who is battling Democrat James Talarico—marks another stop on Trump's multi-state blitz that includes Nebraska, New York, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Ohio (Just The News).

The former president's aggressive ground game underscores Republican confidence heading into the general election cycle. Trump's willingness to personally campaign for down-ballot candidates signals the party's intent to nationalize races and mobilize his base in key battlegrounds.

Protesters disrupted the San Antonio event, though Trump used the moment to energize supporters, directly calling out demonstrators during his remarks (Just The News).

**The Colorado angle:** Trump's endorsement strategy and rally-driven turnout model are templates Colorado Republicans are studying as they prepare for 2024 statewide races. The question for Colorado conservatives: Can similar grassroots energy and high-profile endorsements help Republicans recapture ground lost in recent cycles, particularly in purple suburban counties?

Watch whether Trump commits to campaigning in Colorado before November—a signal of where national Republicans see the state's competitive landscape.
