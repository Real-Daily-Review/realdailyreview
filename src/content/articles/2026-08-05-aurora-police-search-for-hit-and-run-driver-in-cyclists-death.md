# Aurora Police Hunt Hit-and-Run Driver After Cyclist Killed

Aurora police are searching for a driver who fled the scene after striking and killing a cyclist on South Powhatan Road near East Jewell Avenue, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The fatal crash occurred last week. Police have not yet identified a suspect vehicle or released details on the driver's description. Investigators are asking the public for information about the incident.

Hit-and-run fatalities underscore a broader public safety challenge facing Colorado's Front Range communities. Aurora, like Denver and surrounding areas, has seen traffic-related deaths climb in recent years—a trend conservatives and law enforcement attribute partly to inadequate enforcement of existing traffic laws and insufficient consequences for dangerous driving behavior.

The case remains active. Anyone with information about the vehicle or driver is urged to contact Aurora Police.

Coloradans should watch whether Aurora PD secures leads quickly and whether prosecutors pursue maximum penalties if the driver is apprehended—a test of whether local criminal justice systems hold dangerous drivers accountable.
