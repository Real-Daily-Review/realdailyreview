# Trump Warns Iran Over Alleged Dirty Bomb Plot Against Washington

President Trump is taking reported Iranian threats to deploy a dirty bomb or chemical weapon against Washington, D.C., seriously—and promising severe consequences if Tehran follows through. The intelligence assessment remains divided among U.S. agencies on the credibility and immediacy of the threat, but the Trump administration is treating it as a genuine national security concern.
