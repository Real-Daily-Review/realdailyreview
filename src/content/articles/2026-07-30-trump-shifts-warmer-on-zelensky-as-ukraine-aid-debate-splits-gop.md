# Trump Warms to Zelensky as Ukraine Aid Debate Heats Up

President Trump's relationship with Ukrainian President Zelensky has shifted markedly positive, but the warming ties mask a deeper conservative divide over continued U.S. spending on the Ukraine conflict.

Trump recently received Zelensky at the White House, signaling improved rapport after months of skepticism (National Review). The meeting came days after Sen. Lindsey Graham's funeral—Graham, a Ukraine hawk, had been negotiating a Russia sanctions package with the administration before his sudden death (Washington Examiner).

**Here's the catch for Colorado taxpayers:** Republicans remain split on Ukraine aid. While some conservatives, like Graham, backed robust support and sanctions on Russia, others argue the U.S. has already committed substantial resources to the conflict and should prioritize domestic priorities—border security, infrastructure, and tax relief.

Trump's apparent shift toward Zelensky suggests the president may be moving closer to the Graham wing of the GOP, which favors maintaining pressure on Putin through sanctions and military support. However, the broader Republican base remains wary of open-ended commitments abroad.
