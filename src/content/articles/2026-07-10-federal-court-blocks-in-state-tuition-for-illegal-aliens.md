# Federal Court Blocks States From Subsidizing Illegal Aliens' College Tuition

A federal appeals court has ruled that states cannot offer in-state tuition rates to undocumented immigrants, dealing a blow to progressive sanctuary policies that drain taxpayer resources (Daily Caller, Breitbart). The decision affirms that federal law bars such subsidies—a critical win for fiscal conservatives who've long argued that American citizens and legal residents shouldn't foot the bill for benefits extended to those in the country illegally.

The ruling comes as immigration enforcement reveals the real-world costs of lax border security. ICE agents in Houston arrested 735 criminal illegal aliens in May alone, accounting for nearly 1,200 violent convictions including murderers, rapists, and gang members (Breitbart). Meanwhile, sanctuary jurisdictions like Massachusetts face their own reckoning: 11 illegal aliens were charged in a $1.4 million benefits fraud scheme involving stolen food stamps and disability payments.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Taxpayer-funded benefits—whether tuition subsidies or welfare—should prioritize citizens and legal immigrants. States that ignore federal law to extend in-state tuition undermine rule of law and reward illegal entry.
