# Trump Hints at Obama-Era Iran Secrets as Tehran Escalates Threats

President Trump is teasing classified information about former President Obama's handling of Iran—but won't say what—just as the Iranian regime reportedly plots his assassination and realigns Middle Eastern power dynamics against U.S. interests.

During a Fox News segment this week, Trump began discussing Iran policy across administrations before abruptly stopping himself, suggesting Obama and previous presidents failed to confront Tehran's decades-long aggression. "Every president got tapped along, didn't do anything," Trump said, then went silent (The Daily Wire).

Meanwhile, Iran's regional strategy is shifting dangerously. Oman, long a Western-aligned Gulf partner, is now openly coordinating with Tehran over the Strait of Hormuz—proposing to split shipping traffic into Iran-controlled and Oman-controlled routes (Washington Examiner). The move signals Iran's growing influence and America's weakening position in a critical chokepoint for global energy supplies.

More alarming: Iran has reportedly attempted to assassinate Trump aboard Air Force One, with the regime's assassination efforts intensifying since Trump terminated Obama's Iran nuclear memorandum of understanding (Washington Examiner). The escalation suggests Tehran views Trump's hardline stance as an existential threat.

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets and gas prices hinge on Strait of Hormuz stability. Iranian aggression and regional realignment could spike fuel costs for Colorado drivers and businesses.
