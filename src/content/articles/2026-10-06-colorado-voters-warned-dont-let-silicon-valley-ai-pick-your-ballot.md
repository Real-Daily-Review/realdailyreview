# Midterm Voters Outsourcing Judgment to Silicon Valley AI—A Recipe for Trouble

Colorado voters heading to the polls should think twice before letting Big Tech chatbots decide their ballot. According to Breitbart, Americans are increasingly turning to AI tools created by left-leaning Silicon Valley companies to guide their midterm voting decisions—outsourcing political judgment to algorithms designed by the same crowd that routinely censors conservative voices.

The trend raises a fundamental question: who's really voting? When citizens rely on ChatGPT and similar platforms to "make sense" of their ballots, they're trusting opaque systems built by companies with a documented progressive bias. These aren't neutral tools—they're products of an industry hostile to free speech and skeptical of limited government.

Meanwhile, Republicans and conservatives worry that polling data itself is being weaponized to suppress turnout (Just The News), while Trump's personal diplomacy with China's Xi Jinping (Washington Examiner) could shift U.S. trade policy in ways that ripple through Colorado's energy and agriculture sectors.

Colorado taxpayers and business owners should demand transparency: What exactly are these AI systems telling voters about energy policy, water rights, and tax proposals? And will November's results reflect genuine Colorado values—or Silicon Valley's?

Watch whether Colorado's election officials take steps to educate voters on the risks of algorithmic voting guidance before ballots are cast.
