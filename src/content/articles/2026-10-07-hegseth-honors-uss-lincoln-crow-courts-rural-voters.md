# Defense Secretary Hegseth Honors USS Lincoln After 300+ Days Defending American Interests Abroad

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth awarded the Presidential Unit Citation to the USS Abraham Lincoln Carrier Group this week, recognizing the crew's 300-plus consecutive days of deployment supporting U.S. military operations in the Pacific, South China Sea, and Middle East (Washington Examiner). The roughly 5,000 sailors and Marines aboard the Lincoln have been instrumental in countering Iranian threats and maintaining American naval superiority in contested waters.

Hegseth traveled to San Diego to welcome home the carrier group, underscoring the Trump administration's commitment to honoring military service and projecting strength abroad. The citation reflects the crew's sustained operational tempo under demanding conditions—a stark contrast to the Biden-Harris years, when military readiness and morale faced persistent challenges.

Meanwhile, Colorado Democrat Jason Crow is positioning himself as a bridge-builder to rural and working-class voters, telling the Denver Post he wants to "reintroduce" Democrats to America's heartland. Conservatives should note: Crow's recruitment push for House Democrats signals the left's recognition that it has lost ground with working Americans—the very voters who support strong defense, energy independence, and limited government overreach.
