# Trump Warns Iran: Assassination Attempt Will Trigger Predetermined U.S. Response

President Trump has made clear that any Iranian attempt on his life will not go unanswered—he's already left standing orders for a significant military response, he told the New York Post Friday.

"I've been on their list for a long time. That's what we're dealing with," Trump said, adding that he has "left instructions" for how the U.S. should retaliate if Iran succeeds in carrying out its long-running assassination threats (The Daily Wire, Breitbart).

The warning comes as Iran appears to be reigniting tensions with the Trump administration. National Review reports that Tehran's "erstwhile 'very rational people'" may have rekindled the conflict after a period of relative restraint.

Trump's preemptive deterrent—essentially a dead-man's-switch doctrine—signals that the administration will not tolerate Iranian aggression against sitting U.S. leadership. The move reflects a hardline posture toward Tehran that differs sharply from the Obama-era appeasement approach that conservatives have long criticized.

For Colorado energy interests, renewed Iran tensions could affect oil markets and domestic energy policy priorities. Conservatives argue that strengthening U.S. energy independence—through expanded domestic drilling and production—remains the best hedge against Middle Eastern volatility.

Watch whether Iran escalates further or backs down in response to Trump's explicit warning.
