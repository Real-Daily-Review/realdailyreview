# GOP Fracture Over Immigration Enforcement Threatens Trump's 2026 Agenda

Trump's hard-line immigration stance is splintering Republican unity just as the party needs it most. A Fox News poll shows only 43% approve of the president's immigration enforcement, with 53% of voters saying ICE operations have crossed the line—a dramatic erosion of what should be a GOP strength heading into 2026.

The crack widened when Trump-backed GOP Rep. Carlos Salazar (R-Fla.), representing a majority-Hispanic district, publicly broke ranks in a 30-second spot, warning the president that Latino supporters "feel betrayed" by enforcement tactics (New York Post).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that border security and lawful deportation are essential to sovereignty and rule of law. But the messaging problem is real. When Hispanic Republicans—the demographic the GOP needs to expand—feel compelled to distance themselves from Trump on immigration, it signals the administration may be losing the persuasion battle on *how* enforcement is conducted, not whether it should happen.

Colorado conservatives should note: Hispanic voters in swing counties like El Paso and Pueblo will be crucial in 2026. If the GOP hemorrhages support among working-class Latino families over immigration optics, it could cost Republicans winnable races statewide.
