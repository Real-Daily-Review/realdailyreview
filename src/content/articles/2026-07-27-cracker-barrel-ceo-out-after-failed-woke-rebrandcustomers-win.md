# Cracker Barrel CEO Out After Woke Rebrand Fiasco—A Win for Free-Market Pushback

Julie Masino's departure as Cracker Barrel CEO marks a rare corporate victory for Americans who reject corporate "woke" rebranding campaigns. Masino stepped down Monday after just three years, following a botched attempt to strip the chain's iconic folksy identity—including ditching Uncle Hershel from the logo in favor of a sleeker, corporate aesthetic (Daily Wire, Daily Caller).

The rebrand sparked swift backlash from loyal customers who saw the move as abandonment of the company's rural, Americana roots. Within eleven months, Cracker Barrel reversed course, restoring the original logo and aesthetic. Masino's exit signals that consumers still have power: when corporations ignore their base to chase progressive credentials, the market corrects itself.

This matters beyond restaurant branding. It demonstrates that Americans—including Colorado families and rural communities—won't tolerate corporate contempt for traditional values. Companies that chase ESG talking points and abandon their core identity do so at their peril. Shareholders and customers alike punished the experiment.

The lesson for Colorado's business community: authentic connection to your customer base beats trendy corporate virtue signaling every time.
