# Union Bosses Skimming Medicaid Funds Meant for Disabled Caregivers, Trump Admin Eyes Crackdown

The Trump administration has identified a brazen Medicaid fraud scheme: politically connected union bosses in blue states are siphoning dues directly from federal payments meant for home care providers serving disabled Americans, according to the Washington Examiner. The scam diverts roughly $100 million that should reach frontline caregivers—many of them low-income workers—into union coffers instead.

This is textbook government waste enabled by Democratic-controlled states and their union allies. Medicaid funds are taxpayer dollars. When union operatives intercept them as dues before they reach workers, disabled patients lose care quality and funding, while union leadership enriches itself.

The Trump administration has already flagged the problem as part of its broader Medicare and Medicaid fraud crackdown. Conservatives argue the administration must now deploy enforcement tools—audits, benefit recapture, and criminal referrals—to stop the theft and redirect every dollar back to caregivers and patients.

Colorado taxpayers should care: Medicaid fraud anywhere inflates the federal-state cost-sharing burden that eventually lands on state budgets and property taxes. If blue-state unions are stealing from disabled care, it's a warning sign for how progressive states mismanage federal health dollars.

Watch whether the Trump administration follows through with enforcement actions against the unions and states involved.
