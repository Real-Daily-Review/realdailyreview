# Glass Family Honors Son With Arts Center in Lyons

Colorado's Glass family is channeling grief into community legacy. Christian Glass, who was fatally shot by a sheriff's deputy, loved art—and now his memory lives on through a new youth arts center in Lyons bearing his name.

The center, which opened this week, displays some of Glass's own work alongside programming for young artists in Boulder County. It represents a deliberate choice by the family to transform tragedy into something constructive for the community rather than pursue prolonged legal battles or political grandstanding.

The case itself remains sensitive. Glass was shot during a 2022 encounter with law enforcement that sparked debate over police protocols and use-of-force standards. Rather than weaponize their son's death for political gain—a temptation many grieving families face—the Glass family opted for direct community investment.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this approach offers a refreshing contrast to the reflexive calls for sweeping police reform or expanded government oversight that typically follow officer-involved shootings. The family's decision to build rather than litigate demonstrates that private citizens and families can address community needs without waiting for bureaucratic solutions or new regulations.
