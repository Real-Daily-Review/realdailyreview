# Out-of-State Billionaire Cash Floods Colorado Governor's Race

Michael Bloomberg, the New York billionaire and failed presidential candidate, is pouring millions into Colorado's 2026 governor's race—a stark reminder that Democrats rely on coastal elites to bankroll their campaigns while claiming to represent working Coloradans.

According to Complete Colorado, Bloomberg's spending aims to shape the Democratic primary and general election. Meanwhile, U.S. Sen. Michael Bennet, the leading Democratic candidate, is leveraging his government resume and elected experience to secure the nomination (Denver Post).

**The conservative case:** Bloomberg's intervention exemplifies how progressive politics depends on billionaire patronage rather than grassroots support. His money will amplify left-wing positions on energy regulation, gun control, and tax policy—priorities that don't align with Colorado's energy workers, ranchers, or gun owners. Bennet's record in Washington includes support for Biden's regulatory agenda, which has harmed Colorado's oil and gas sector.

Democrats counter that Bloomberg's involvement reflects legitimate political engagement and that Bennet's experience makes him qualified to lead the state.

The real question: Will Colorado voters reject a governor-in-waiting bankrolled by out-of-state billionaires and committed to policies that undermine Colorado's energy independence and economic growth?
