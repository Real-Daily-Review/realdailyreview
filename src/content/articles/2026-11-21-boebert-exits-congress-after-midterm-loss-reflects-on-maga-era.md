# Lauren Boebert Exits Congress After Midterm Loss, Reflects on MAGA Era

Colorado's firebrand conservative Rep. Lauren Boebert is closing the book on her congressional career after losing her seat in the midterms, marking a significant turnover in the state's Republican delegation.

Speaking from a U-Haul van while packing up her office, Boebert reflected on her six-year tenure in Congress and the rise-and-fall arc of her MAGA-era prominence (CPR News). The 3rd District representative, who became a national conservative media fixture during her time in Washington, now faces life outside elected office after Colorado voters chose a different direction.

The loss underscores broader Republican struggles in Colorado, a state trending purple in statewide races. Conservatives will debate whether Boebert's polarizing national profile helped or hindered GOP prospects in a swing district—and whether her departure signals a shift in how Colorado Republicans approach messaging and candidate selection going forward.

For Colorado taxpayers and conservative voters, the question now centers on who fills the 3rd District seat and whether the next representative will better reflect the district's energy-sector interests, Second Amendment priorities, and fiscal concerns that have historically anchored Western Slope politics.

Watch for special-election timing and the Republican primary field that emerges to replace Boebert.
