# Trump's Iran Deal Sparks GOP Revolt: Where's the Text?

President Trump claims he's signed a historic Iran agreement, but Republicans are demanding answers—and a congressional vote—before the ink dries on what may not be a binding deal at all.

The core problem: nobody knows what Trump actually agreed to. Most GOP senators say they've been kept in the dark on details, while conflicting claims swirl about whether sanctions relief, nuclear concessions, and other major issues are locked in or punted to future negotiations (Fox News Politics).

National Review calls for transparency, arguing the only way past the spin is releasing the full text. Former Vice President Mike Pence went further, telling CNN the arrangement "smacks of appeasement" (Breitbart).

Republicans are unified on one demand: if this is a real deal with Iran, Congress gets a vote. That's not obstruction—it's constitutional duty. The War Powers Act and treaty law exist precisely to prevent executives from making sweeping foreign commitments in secret.

For Colorado, the stakes include energy markets (Iran sanctions affect oil prices and domestic production) and national security posture. Coloradans deserve to know whether their government just handed Tehran a lifeline or genuinely neutralized a nuclear threat.

Watch whether Trump releases the agreement text—and whether Senate Republicans hold firm on forcing a floor vote.
