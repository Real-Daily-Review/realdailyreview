# Colorado Republican Presses DHS on Sanctuary City Enforcement, Visa Processing

Colorado Rep. Gabe Evans is demanding answers from federal immigration authorities on why sanctuary cities get a pass while law-abiding immigrants jump through hoops. Evans questioned DHS chief Markwayne Mullin about streamlining visa renewal for lawful immigrants in Colorado—a direct challenge to the sanctuary city status quo that Republicans argue undermines immigration law and rewards jurisdictions that obstruct federal enforcement.

The exchange reflects growing GOP frustration with Democratic-led cities that shield illegal immigrants from deportation. According to Fox News, Republican senators are rallying behind a DHS proposal to pull Customs and Border Protection officers from sanctuary city airports, effectively punishing those jurisdictions where it counts: travel processing lines. "They should pay a price for what they do," one GOP senator said.

**The conservative argument:** Sanctuary policies create a two-tiered system—lawful immigrants face delays while illegal immigrants get protection. Colorado taxpayers foot the bill for services while federal immigration law sits ignored. Republicans say consequences are overdue.

**Democrats counter** (per Daily Caller reporting) that some Republicans worry the airport proposal goes too far, though the left's full objection remains unclear from these sources.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: Evans is pushing back against the state's own sanctuary protections while demanding efficiency for legal immigrants. Watch whether the DHS proposal advances and whether Colorado Democrats face political heat for harboring illegal aliens while legal applicants wait.
