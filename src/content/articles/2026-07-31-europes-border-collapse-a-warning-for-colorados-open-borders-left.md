# Europe's Open-Borders Experiment Collapses as Migration Crisis Spirals

Europe's permissive immigration policies are unraveling in real time—and Colorado conservatives should take note of what happens when governments abandon border enforcement.

France has deployed four mobile military units, aircraft, and drones to its border with Spain following a massive illegal migrant surge into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, according to *The Daily Wire*. French President Emmanuel Macron ordered the deployment after tens of thousands of foreigners stormed the territory bordering Morocco, overwhelming local authorities.

The crisis is so severe that multiple European leaders are now calling to expel Spain from the EU's Schengen open-borders zone entirely, *Breitbart* reports. Spain's conservative Vox party has gone further, demanding prosecution of Socialist Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez for what they characterize as catastrophic border mismanagement.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Open-borders ideology without enforcement creates humanitarian and security disasters. When governments prioritize political correctness over sovereignty, they lose control of their own territory—and citizens pay the price.

Colorado voters should watch how this unfolds. Progressive politicians here regularly cite European immigration models as inspiration for looser border policies and sanctuary-city ordinances. This European meltdown is a cautionary tale: border security isn't xenophobia; it's governance.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats acknowledge this crisis or double down on their open-borders rhetoric.
