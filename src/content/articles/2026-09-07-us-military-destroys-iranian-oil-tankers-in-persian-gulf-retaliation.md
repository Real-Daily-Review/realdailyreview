# U.S. Military Strikes Iranian Oil Tankers in Persian Gulf Retaliation

American military forces destroyed three Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps crude oil tankers in the Persian Gulf on Saturday in direct retaliation for ballistic missile attacks on U.S. Navy vessels, according to footage released by Central Command.

The strikes followed Iran's assault on two American warships, escalating tensions in one of the world's most critical energy chokepoints. Video shows massive explosions and thick black smoke rising from the targeted tankers as U.S. aircraft executed the operation.
