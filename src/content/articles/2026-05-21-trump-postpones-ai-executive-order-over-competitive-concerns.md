# Trump Postpones AI Executive Order, Citing Competitive Concerns

President Trump canceled a scheduled signing ceremony Thursday for an executive order on artificial intelligence testing, saying he "didn't like certain aspects of it," according to The Hill.

The White House notified invited tech leaders just hours before the planned afternoon signing that the event was off. Trump cited concerns that the order could undermine American competitiveness in AI development.

"AI is causing tremendous good," Trump said, according to CNBC, adding that the executive order "could have been a blocker." PBS NewsHour reported Trump worried the measure could dull America's technological edge.

The order would have required pre-release government security reviews of AI models, according to TechCrunch. Trump indicated he did not want to "get in the way" of AI industry leadership.

The postponement marks a reversal from the administration's earlier push for the testing framework. No new signing date has been announced, and it remains unclear whether the order will be revised or abandoned entirely.

Watch for whether the White House revises the order's language or shelves it altogether as Trump weighs regulatory caution against industry pressure.
