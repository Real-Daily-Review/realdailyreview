# Mexico Fairground Fire Kills Five During Concert

A massive fire erupted at a fairground in Villahermosa, southeastern Mexico, killing at least five people during a concert attended by roughly 135,000 people, according to BBC World and Al Jazeera English.

The blaze broke out at the packed venue, though details on the cause, timeline, and circumstances of the deaths remain limited in initial reports. Emergency response and evacuation procedures were not detailed in available accounts.

The incident marks a significant tragedy at a major public gathering in Mexico. Authorities have not yet released comprehensive information about the fire's origin or the full scope of injuries sustained.
