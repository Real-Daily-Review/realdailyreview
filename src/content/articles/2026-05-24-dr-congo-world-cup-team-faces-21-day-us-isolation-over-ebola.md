# DR Congo World Cup Squad Faces 21-Day Isolation Over Ebola Outbreak

The Democratic Republic of Congo's World Cup team must isolate for 21 days before entering the United States, according to the BBC, as an Ebola outbreak threatens the nation's participation in the tournament next month.

The World Health Organization upgraded its risk assessment for the DRC to "very high" on Friday, according to the Washington Examiner. The US isolation requirement stems directly from the WHO's escalated threat level for the central African nation.

The outbreak has created a significant logistical challenge for the Congolese squad, which will need to complete the mandatory quarantine period before the team can travel to compete in the World Cup. The timing is tight, with the tournament scheduled to begin in roughly four weeks.

The isolation requirement applies specifically to players and staff entering American territory, reflecting heightened caution around disease transmission across borders during international sporting events.
