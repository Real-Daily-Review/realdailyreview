# Former White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt Joins Fox News as Contributor

Karoline Leavitt, who stepped down as White House Press Secretary after maternity leave, is landing a high-profile media gig at Fox News starting in November as an on-air contributor, according to *The Daily Wire* and *Just The News*.

Leavitt departed the Trump administration following the birth of her second child. She briefly returned from leave before deciding to exit the administration entirely, according to *Just The News*.

Her move to Fox News as a contributor—a non-full-time role typically structured as a contract or recurring agreement—positions her to leverage what *Daily Caller* describes as her "firsthand experience in the White House." The role capitalizes on her recent tenure in one of the administration's most visible positions.

The appointment reflects a common Washington-to-media pipeline, particularly among Republican operatives and administration officials seeking platforms to shape conservative messaging and commentary on national politics.
