# Fatal Wrong-Way Crash on I-70 Near Utah Border Kills Woman, Injures Five

A wrong-way driver on Interstate 70 near the Utah border killed one woman and left five others seriously injured in a head-on collision, Colorado State Patrol confirmed. Five people from Thornton were hospitalized with serious injuries following the crash.

The incident underscores ongoing safety concerns on I-70, a critical corridor for Colorado commerce, tourism, and energy transport. The highway connects Denver to Western Slope communities and serves as a vital artery for the state's economy—making roadway safety and infrastructure maintenance essential priorities for state officials.

State Patrol is investigating the cause of the wrong-way entry. Details on the driver responsible remain limited at this reporting.
