# Officer Injured, Suspect Dead in Golden Police Shooting

A Saturday morning shooting at a Circle K in Golden left one person dead and a Golden Police officer injured, authorities said. The incident occurred at the store located at 17691 W. Colfax Ave., according to reports from 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

Police have not yet released details about what prompted the confrontation or the circumstances leading to the shooting. The suspect was pronounced dead at the scene, while the officer sustained injuries during the exchange.

Golden Police are continuing their investigation into the incident. No additional information about the officer's condition or the identity of the suspect has been released at this time.
