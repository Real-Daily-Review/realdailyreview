# Democrat Fracture Over Israel Exposes Party's Real Priorities

Former Democratic Senate candidate Haley Stevens has broken ranks with her party over Abdul El-Sayed's anti-Israel record, a move that reveals deep fissures within the Democratic coalition—and exposes the left's selective commitment to its stated values.

Stevens, who nearly won a Michigan Senate race, has publicly objected to El-Sayed's hostility toward Israel, according to *National Review*. Rather than engage her concerns substantively, Democrats have responded by attacking Stevens herself, according to the *Washington Examiner*, contradicting years of rhetoric about "trusting women" and "elevating women's voices."

The episode underscores a core conservative argument: the Democratic Party's commitment to democracy and women's empowerment is conditional—applied selectively when convenient, abandoned when inconvenient. Stevens's willingness to challenge party orthodoxy on a matter of principle has triggered the very demonization Democrats claim to oppose.

For Colorado voters, this matters. It signals that Democratic unity depends less on shared values than on enforced conformity. When a Democrat steps out of line on issues the party leadership prioritizes—whether Israel, energy policy, or gun control—dissent is punished, not debated.

Watch whether other Democrats follow Stevens's lead or fall back in line under pressure.
