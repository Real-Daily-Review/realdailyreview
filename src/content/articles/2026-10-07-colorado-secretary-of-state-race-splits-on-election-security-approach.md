# Colorado Secretary of State Race Heats Up as Candidates Spar Over Election Integrity

Colorado's secretary of state race is shaping up as a referendum on how the state's top election official should handle voting procedures and election security—with the two major candidates offering starkly different visions for the office.

Both James Wiley and Amanda Gonzalez say they want to depoliticize the role, but their approaches differ widely, according to the *Denver Post*. The office has become increasingly prominent in recent years amid disputes over mail ballots, election security, and lingering questions about the 2020 presidential election, the *Colorado Sun* reports.

Republicans argue that Colorado's election administration needs tighter security protocols and greater transparency to restore voter confidence. Conservatives have raised concerns about mail-ballot vulnerabilities and the need for robust chain-of-custody procedures. Democrats counter that Colorado's election system is secure and that Republican skepticism fuels unfounded conspiracy theories.

The race matters because Colorado's secretary of state oversees voter registration, ballot procedures, and election certification—decisions that ripple across the state's voting infrastructure and affect how Coloradans cast ballots.
