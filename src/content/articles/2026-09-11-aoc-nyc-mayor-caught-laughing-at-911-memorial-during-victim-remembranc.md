# AOC Caught Laughing at 9/11 Memorial While Families Mourn

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani were photographed grinning and laughing at the 9/11 Memorial on Friday morning as mourners wept nearby during the reading of victims' names, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire.

Video footage captured the two Democrats smiling together at Ground Zero while a young man sobbed in front of the memorial's names. The moment sparked immediate backlash online, with critics questioning the appropriateness of their conduct at one of America's most solemn sites during the annual remembrance of nearly 3,000 lives lost on September 11, 2001.

Neither Ocasio-Cortez nor Mamdani has publicly addressed the incident or explained the context of their interaction. The footage raises questions about decorum and respect at memorial events—particularly for elected officials who hold positions of public trust.
