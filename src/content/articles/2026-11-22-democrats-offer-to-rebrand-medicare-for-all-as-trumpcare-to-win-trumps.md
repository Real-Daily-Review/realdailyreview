# Democrats Court Trump on Medicare for All—With a Rebranding Twist

Incoming Minnesota Senator Peggy Flanagan has signaled Democrats are willing to rebrand a government takeover of American healthcare as "TrumpCare" if the president will sign on to Medicare for All, according to remarks on CNN's *State of the Union*. Flanagan, joined by Michigan's Abdul el Sayed, is explicitly courting Trump to back the single-payer scheme—essentially offering him political cover through a name change.

This is a transparent political calculation: Democrats know Medicare for All polls poorly when voters understand it means eliminating private insurance and raising taxes. By slapping Trump's name on it, they hope to neutralize opposition and ram through what conservatives have long warned against—a government-run healthcare monopoly that would devastate choice, innovation, and quality of care.

**The conservative case is straightforward.** Republicans argue Medicare for All means rationing, longer wait times, and crushing tax increases on working Coloradans and small businesses. It would eliminate the private insurance market that covers millions of Coloradans and destroy competition that drives down costs. Whether it's called "TrumpCare" or anything else, the policy remains a socialist experiment that expands government control over one-sixth of the economy.

Colorado taxpayers and employers should watch whether Trump entertains this pitch—and demand clarity on where Republican leadership stands on preserving market-based healthcare.
