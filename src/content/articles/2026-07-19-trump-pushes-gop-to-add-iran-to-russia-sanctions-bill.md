# Trump Pushes GOP to Expand Russia Sanctions Bill to Iran

Trump is doubling down on confronting Tehran, urging Republicans to add Iran to the pending Russia sanctions legislation—a move that could reshape U.S. foreign policy and Colorado's energy sector in one stroke.

Hours after the eighth consecutive night of U.S. Central Command strikes on Iranian targets, the president posted on Truth Social Sunday morning calling on the GOP to expand the sanctions package. "That's what Lindsey wanted to do, and it was going to happen," Trump wrote, invoking the late Sen. Lindsey Graham's hawkish Iran stance (Washington Examiner, Just The News).

**The Colorado angle:** Broader Iran sanctions could tighten global oil markets and potentially lift domestic energy prices—affecting Colorado consumers and strengthening the case for expanded U.S. oil and gas production on federal lands, a conservative priority the Biden administration had throttled.

Republicans have long pushed for tougher Iran policy. Conservatives argue that economic pressure, combined with military strikes, forces Tehran to the negotiating table without committing ground troops. The strategy aligns with Trump's "maximum pressure" doctrine from his first term.

Democrats and the Biden administration had pursued diplomatic engagement with Iran, a posture conservatives say emboldened Tehran's nuclear ambitions and regional aggression.

**Watch next:** Whether Senate Republicans fold Iran sanctions into the Russia bill or face Trump pressure to pass separate, tougher Iran legislation before the 2024 cycle closes.
