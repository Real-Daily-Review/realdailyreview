# Colorado College Hands Out Academic Credit for Left-Wing Activism

Colorado College is now awarding course credit to students for summer work explicitly framed as left-wing political activism, raising questions about whether higher education dollars are funding partisan organizing rather than education.

According to Complete Colorado, the college's summer offerings include assignments to "shut down immigration detention centers" and other activist projects. The arrangement effectively subsidizes progressive political work while students earn academic credentials—a practice conservatives argue conflates education with advocacy.

The move reflects a broader pattern of Colorado's higher-education institutions blending classroom instruction with partisan causes. While colleges have long hosted activism, awarding credit for explicitly ideological summer work crosses a line that troubles taxpayers and parents footing tuition bills.

Meanwhile, the state announced it awarded Colorado Public Radio a $1.5 million Community Revitalization Tax Credit for its new Denver headquarters (CPR News). While the tax credit program aims to spur development, conservatives should scrutinize whether public broadcasting outlets—which lean left on editorial judgment—warrant state subsidies when private media operate without government support.
