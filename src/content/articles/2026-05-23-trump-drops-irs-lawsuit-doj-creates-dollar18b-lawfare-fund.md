# Trump Drops IRS Lawsuit, DOJ Creates $1.8B 'Lawfare' Compensation Fund

President Trump has withdrawn a $10 billion lawsuit against the IRS in exchange for the Department of Justice establishing an $1.8 billion fund to compensate alleged victims of "lawfare," according to CNBC.

The fund represents a significant shift in how the administration is addressing Trump's long-standing grievances over what he characterizes as politically motivated prosecutions. Trump has lashed out at Republican senators who oppose the initiative, calling them "screwing the Republican Party," according to the Financial Times.

The arrangement consolidates Trump's legal strategy: rather than pursue the IRS case through courts, the administration is using executive authority to create a compensation mechanism. The fund's scope—which alleged victims it will cover and what criteria will determine eligibility—remains unclear from available reporting.
