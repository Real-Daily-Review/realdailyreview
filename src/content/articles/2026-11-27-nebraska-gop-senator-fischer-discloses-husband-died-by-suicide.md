# Senator Fischer Discloses Husband's Death by Suicide

Nebraska Republican Senator Deb Fischer announced Friday that her husband, Bruce Fischer, died by suicide—a tragic update to her initial statement from November 22, when she said he had "passed away peacefully" while outdoors. According to Fischer's office (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire), further investigation revealed the true cause after the family initially believed his death was peaceful.

Fischer, a conservative stalwart on energy policy and a defender of Nebraska's agricultural interests, has represented the state since 2013. The senator has not issued additional public statements beyond the corrected announcement.

This deeply personal tragedy underscores the silent crisis of suicide affecting Americans across all walks of life—a reality that transcends partisan lines. Mental health resources remain critically underfunded and underutilized, even as awareness campaigns proliferate.

Colorado conservatives and all Americans should keep Fischer and her family in their prayers during this difficult time.
