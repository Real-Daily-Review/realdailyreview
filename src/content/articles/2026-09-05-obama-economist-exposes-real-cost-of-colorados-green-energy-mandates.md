# Green Energy Mandates Cost More Than Politicians Admit, Obama's Own Economist Warns

Colorado's renewable energy mandates are about to get a reality check from an unlikely source: Barack Obama's former chief economist.

Michael Greenstone, who led Obama's Council of Economic Advisers, and his University of Chicago research team have studied Renewable Portfolio Standards—the state-level mandates forcing utilities to source power from renewables. Their findings directly contradict the rosy promises from green-energy advocates and progressive lawmakers who've championed these rules across the country, including Colorado.

Conservatives have long argued that renewable mandates drive up electricity costs for families and businesses while delivering minimal environmental benefit. Greenstone's research validates that concern. The study examined the actual cost burden of these mandates, which now cover most U.S. states.

Colorado's own renewable energy requirements—among the nation's most aggressive—have already increased utility bills for ratepayers. When an Obama administration economist confirms what free-market advocates have warned for years, it's harder for the left to dismiss the criticism as partisan fearmongering.

Democrats typically counter that green mandates spur innovation and job creation, but Greenstone's work suggests the economic trade-offs deserve far more scrutiny than state legislators have given them.

Watch for whether Colorado's Public Utilities Commission uses this research to reconsider the cost-benefit analysis of future renewable expansion.
