# Gas Prices Climb as Summer Travel Season Begins

Gas prices are averaging around $4.50 per gallon nationally as Memorial Day weekend approaches, according to CBS News, putting pressure on American consumers heading into the summer travel season. The price surge comes as the nation enters peak driving months, when demand for fuel typically increases.

The timing coincides with broader inflationary pressures affecting household budgets across multiple sectors. Meanwhile, some companies are capitalizing on higher prices: Verizon reported that its latest quarterly earnings beat Wall Street forecasts, with the telecommunications giant crediting elevated pricing and gains in wireless customers for the revenue boost, according to the Wall Street Journal.

The dual trend—rising consumer costs paired with corporate pricing power—underscores the uneven impact of inflation across the economy. Consumers face steeper expenses at the pump and potentially higher service bills, while some large corporations report strong financial performance tied to their ability to raise prices.

Watch for whether gas prices stabilize or continue climbing through peak summer travel season, and whether consumer spending patterns shift in response.
