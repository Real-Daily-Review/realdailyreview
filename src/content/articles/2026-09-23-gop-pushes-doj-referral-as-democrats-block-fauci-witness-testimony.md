# Democrats Block GOP Bid to Question Witness in Fauci Records Case

Senate Republicans' push to compel testimony from a key witness in the Anthony Fauci investigation hit a Democratic wall this week. Sen. Rand Paul (R-KY), chairman of the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee, sought to force a mystery witness to testify about alleged destruction of COVID-19 origin records—but Democrats blocked the effort, according to the Daily Caller.

Undeterred, Paul announced he would refer Fauci's executive assistant to the Department of Justice for investigation into potential record destruction related to the pandemic's origins, the Washington Examiner reported. Republicans argue the alleged destruction of documents obstructs accountability and prevents Americans from learning the truth about COVID-19's genesis.

Democrats have consistently resisted GOP-led investigations into Fauci's conduct and the National Institutes of Health's funding of gain-of-function research, framing such inquiries as politically motivated witch hunts.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado taxpayers funded NIH grants; transparency about how federal research dollars were spent and what records were kept—or destroyed—directly affects public trust in government science spending.

**What to watch:** Whether the DOJ referral gains traction under current leadership, and whether Republicans can sustain pressure on the record-destruction allegations heading into 2026.
