# Bezos Denies Tax Strategy, Praises Trump's 'Maturity'

Amazon founder Jeff Bezos said there is "no truth" to reports that he uses the "buy, borrow, die" tax strategy, according to CNBC. The strategy involves wealthy investors borrowing against assets and using loan proceeds as income to minimize tax obligations.

In the same interview, Bezos praised President Trump's second-term performance, telling CNBC that Trump is "a more mature, more disciplined version of himself than he was in his first term," according to The Hill. The comments came as Trump faces record-low approval ratings among voters.

Bezos's remarks on the tax strategy appear aimed at addressing longstanding scrutiny of how ultra-wealthy individuals minimize their tax burden. The "buy, borrow, die" approach has drawn attention from lawmakers and tax-reform advocates concerned about wealth concentration and tax avoidance among billionaires.

The timing of Bezos's praise for Trump is notable given Amazon's regulatory relationship with the federal government and ongoing antitrust scrutiny of the company. Bezos has historically maintained a cautious public stance toward political figures.
