# OpenAI Trial Hears Testimony on Musk's Merger Proposal

Elon Musk proposed merging OpenAI with Tesla and offered Sam Altman a board seat at the EV company, according to testimony from Shivon Zilis, a former OpenAI board member who has four children with Musk.

Zilis took the stand Wednesday in the ongoing trial between Musk and OpenAI leadership, according to The Verge. The high-stakes case centers on Musk's 2024 lawsuit, which accuses OpenAI of abandoning its founding mission to develop AI for humanity's benefit and instead prioritizing profit, according to CNBC.

The trial has already heard from Musk, his financial manager Jared Birchall, Neuralink CEO, and OpenAI cofounder Greg Brockman. Zilis's testimony provided new details about Musk's strategic vision for the startup during his time as a board member.

The case carries significant implications for OpenAI's future direction and its most prominent product, ChatGPT, according to The Verge. The outcome could reshape how the company balances its nonprofit mission against commercial operations.

Watch for closing arguments and the jury's verdict on whether OpenAI has fundamentally shifted away from its original charter.
