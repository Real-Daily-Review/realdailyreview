# Trump Facing Political Constraints as Second Term Pressures Mount

President Trump is operating under significant political and institutional constraints that are blunting his policy ambitions, according to *National Review*, even as former President Obama claims Trump maintains an unhealthy "obsession" with him—a charge that underscores the ongoing partisan warfare consuming Washington.

Trump's second-term agenda is colliding with congressional dynamics, judicial skepticism, and bureaucratic resistance that limit executive action. *National Review* reports Trump faces three major retreats from his stated goals, suggesting the president cannot simply govern by decree despite controlling the White House.

Meanwhile, Obama's recent podcast comments—that Trump "obviously has a room in his head, a suite in his head"—reveal the personal animosity still driving Democratic messaging. Obama's claim that Trump "acts differently" in private than on social media is classic Democratic spin: portraying the president as unstable or duplicitous rather than engaging his actual policies.

**The Colorado angle:** Coloradans should watch whether Trump's constraints affect his promised rollback of federal land-use restrictions and energy regulations that have hampered Wyoming and Utah development—policies that could unlock economic opportunity across the Mountain West.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether congressional Republicans will actually fight for Trump's deregulation agenda or cave to institutional pressure.
