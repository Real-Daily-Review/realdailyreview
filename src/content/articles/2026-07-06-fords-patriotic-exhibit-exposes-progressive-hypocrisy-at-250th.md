# Ford's Patriotic Gesture Overshadowed by Progressive Hypocrisy at Nation's 250th

While Ford Motor Company honored America's 250th anniversary with a free public exhibit of historic vehicles at Union Station—including a Model T and vintage pickups from the Ford Heritage Vault—progressive activists used the milestone to attack the very capitalism that built the nation (Fox News Politics). The contrast was stark: Democratic socialist New York City official Zohran Mamdani spent the celebration criticizing American institutions, capitalism, and ICE, while his wife jetted off to a luxury Islamic retreat in Spain, skipping the nation's birthday entirely (The Daily Wire).

The Ford exhibit exemplifies what conservatives argue should define America's quarter-millennium: private enterprise celebrating innovation, free markets, and American ingenuity without government subsidy. The vehicles on display represent generations of American manufacturing excellence and individual achievement—the engine of prosperity that built Colorado's economy.

Meanwhile, the left's response to the 250th reveals the progressive playbook: use national moments not to celebrate American achievement, but to lecture about grievances and attack the economic system that enabled their own comfortable lives. Coloradans should note the contrast as they evaluate which party actually values American exceptionalism versus which one sees the nation as a vehicle for ideological complaint.

Watch for how Colorado's Democratic leadership marks—or exploits—future national celebrations.
