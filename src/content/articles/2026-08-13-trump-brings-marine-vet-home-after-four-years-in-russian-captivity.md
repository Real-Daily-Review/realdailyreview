# Trump Administration Secures Release of Marine Veteran Robert Gilman After Four Years in Russian Captivity

The Trump administration has delivered on its "no American left behind" pledge, bringing former Marine Robert Gilman back to U.S. soil after four years in captivity. Gilman landed in the United States on Tuesday, met by White House officials Steve Witkoff and Sebastian Gorka, marking a swift diplomatic win for the new administration (Fox News, Just The News).

President Trump secured Gilman's release without detailing the specific terms of the agreement with Russia, characterizing the deal as "very much on a humanitarian basis." The case had languished under the previous administration, drawing conservative criticism that the Biden-Harris team had deprioritized American citizens held abroad.

The successful negotiation reflects Trump's stated commitment to prioritizing American interests in foreign policy and his willingness to engage directly with adversaries to resolve disputes. Conservatives view the outcome as validation of a more assertive diplomatic posture that puts American lives first.

The case underscores ongoing concerns about Americans detained overseas and the administration's capacity to resolve such situations swiftly—a contrast conservatives argue with the previous administration's record on hostage negotiations.
