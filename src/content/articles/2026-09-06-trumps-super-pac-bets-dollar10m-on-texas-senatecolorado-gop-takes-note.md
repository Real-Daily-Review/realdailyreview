# Trump Super PAC Dumps $10M Into Texas Senate Race—Colorado Republicans Watch Closely

Donald Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is flooding $10 million into Ken Paxton's Texas Senate bid against Democrat James Talarico, signaling Trump's willingness to flex financial muscle in competitive races that could reshape the midterms. The move comes as Trump headlines both nights of the GOP convention in Dallas, cementing his grip on the party apparatus and its fundraising machinery.

For Colorado Republicans, the Texas race matters. A Paxton victory would strengthen the conservative bloc in the Senate and validate Trump's endorsement strategy—a model Colorado GOP candidates are watching as they prepare their own 2026 bids. Conversely, if Democrats flip a red seat in Texas, it signals weakness in Republican strongholds heading into the next cycle.

The $10 million infusion underscores a hard reality: Trump controls the money. Candidates seeking his backing—or avoiding his opposition—must align with his vision. Colorado's Republican establishment, already fractured between Trump loyalists and traditional conservatives, faces pressure to choose sides as primary season approaches.

Democrats will argue the spending reflects Trump's outsized influence over the GOP and threatens party independence. Republicans counter that strong leadership and unified fundraising are exactly what Republicans need to compete in a hostile media environment.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans seeking statewide office in 2026 seek Trump's MAGA Inc. endorsement—or keep their distance.
