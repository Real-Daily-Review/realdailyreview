# Federal Judge Blocks Trump Citizenship Voting Requirement

A federal judge has permanently barred the Trump administration from enforcing its executive order requiring proof of citizenship to register to vote, dealing another blow to the president's election security agenda.

U.S. District Judge Denise Casper, an Obama appointee, issued the injunction Wednesday against the March 2025 executive order (Washington Examiner). The ruling marks the latest judicial setback for Trump's election policies in recent months (Washington Examiner).

Republicans and conservatives argue that citizenship verification is a basic safeguard to prevent non-citizens from voting—a concern that resonates in Colorado, where election integrity remains a flashpoint in statewide races. Supporters of the requirement contend that proof of citizenship is a reasonable, common-sense measure to protect ballot access for American citizens.

The judge's decision reflects the ongoing legal battles over voting access and election administration. Democrats and progressive groups have opposed citizenship requirements, arguing they create barriers to voter registration and disproportionately affect certain populations.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether the Trump administration appeals this decision or pursues alternative election security measures through Congress or state-level action—and whether Colorado's Republican legislature moves to strengthen voter verification standards independently.
