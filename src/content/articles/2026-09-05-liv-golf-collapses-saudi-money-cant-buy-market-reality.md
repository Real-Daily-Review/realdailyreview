# LIV Golf's Collapse Shows the Limits of Saudi Money and Woke Backlash

LIV Golf is imploding under mounting losses, lawsuits, and unpaid bills—a cautionary tale about throwing unlimited foreign capital at a cultural grievance. The Saudi-backed upstart, which promised to shake up professional golf by challenging the PGA Tour's dominance, is collapsing despite its deep pockets and celebrity player roster.

According to the Washington Examiner, the league's demise shouldn't be mourned by golf fans or the PGA Tour. Instead, conservatives argue LIV Golf's real legacy is forcing the sport to confront its own institutional bloat and woke overreach. The venture exposed how the golf establishment had become stale and preachy—and that mattered more than the Saudi funding's eventual exhaustion.

The collapse underscores a broader conservative principle: you can't simply buy your way past cultural and market realities. LIV Golf had the money but lacked the sustainable business model, fan base, and competitive legitimacy to dethrone an entrenched institution. It also highlights the risks of accepting foreign capital tied to geopolitical agendas, no matter how appealing the anti-establishment pitch sounds.

For Colorado golf enthusiasts and business-minded conservatives, the lesson is clear: disruption requires more than cash and celebrity—it demands real competitive advantage and customer loyalty.

Watch whether the PGA Tour actually implements the reforms LIV Golf's challenge forced it to consider, or simply returns to business as usual.
