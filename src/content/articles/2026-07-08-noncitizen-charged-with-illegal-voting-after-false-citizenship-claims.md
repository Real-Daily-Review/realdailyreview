# Noncitizen Charged With Illegal Voting After Falsing Citizenship Claims

A lawful permanent resident from Australia has been charged with illegally casting federal ballots by falsely claiming U.S. citizenship during voter registration, according to Fox News and the New York Post. The noncitizen allegedly made fraudulent statements to register and voted in both the 2022 and 2024 elections.

**The conservative concern:** This case underscores what Republicans have long argued—that voter rolls lack adequate citizenship verification, creating openings for fraud. While Democrats typically dismiss election security concerns as overblown, conservatives point to cases like this as evidence that ballot integrity safeguards remain inadequate. The incident raises questions about how many noncitizens may slip through registration processes nationwide, and whether Colorado's voter verification systems are sufficiently rigorous.

Colorado has faced its own election integrity debates. Republicans have pushed for stricter voter ID requirements and citizenship checks, while Democratic officials have resisted, citing accessibility concerns. This case provides fresh ammunition for conservatives arguing that tighter verification protocols—not looser ones—should be the priority.

**What to watch:** Whether Colorado election officials will tighten citizenship verification procedures in response, or whether Democratic-controlled state leadership will resist such measures as unnecessary.
