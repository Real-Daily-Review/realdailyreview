# Colorado Gun Rights Lawsuit Challenges State's Carry Restrictions

Colorado's Second Amendment advocates are taking aim at the state's patchwork of gun carry restrictions through fresh legal action. The lawsuit, detailed by *Complete Colorado*, represents the latest in an escalating series of constitutional challenges to Colorado's state and local firearm regulations—a sign that gun rights groups see opportunity in the current legal landscape.

The complaint targets Colorado's carry limitations head-on, arguing they violate constitutional protections. Republicans and Second Amendment advocates contend that Colorado's restrictions—layered across state law and local ordinances—infringe on law-abiding citizens' right to self-defense and are increasingly vulnerable to legal challenge following recent Supreme Court precedent favoring gun rights.

Democrats and gun-control advocates counter that reasonable restrictions protect public safety and that local jurisdictions should retain authority to set their own firearm policies.

The timing matters: Colorado has become a battleground for gun rights litigation, with conservative legal groups testing state regulations in federal court. For Colorado gun owners and businesses, the outcome could reshape carry laws statewide—potentially invalidating restrictions in Denver, Boulder, and other anti-gun municipalities.

Watch for federal court rulings on whether Colorado's restrictions survive constitutional scrutiny under current Second Amendment jurisprudence.
