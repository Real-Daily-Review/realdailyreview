# Trump's Iran Diplomacy Gambit Tests GOP Base Trust

President Trump is pivoting toward diplomatic negotiations with Iran over the Strait of Hormuz, a shift that has Republicans divided on whether dealmaking beats the military deterrence that defined his first term.

The Daily Wire reports Trump claims Iran made "key concessions" on Hormuz access, though Tehran disputes the characterization. Meanwhile, the Washington Examiner notes that congressional Republicans' willingness to back any emerging deal hinges directly on whether the GOP base maintains confidence in Trump's Iran strategy as he moves away from the "maximum pressure" and military posturing of 2017-2021.

The tension is real: rank-and-file Republicans who supported Trump's hawkish Iran stance—including Colorado conservatives who backed energy independence and strong deterrence—may balk if they perceive weakness in negotiations. A fracture in GOP voter confidence could embolden House and Senate Republicans to resist any agreement Trump brokers.

For Colorado, Iran policy matters. Sanctions relief could flood global oil markets, undercutting domestic energy producers and workers. Conversely, a destabilized Middle East threatens energy prices and American security.
