# House Republicans Brace for Hegseth Impeachment Vote This Week

The move reflects deep conservative divisions over Hegseth's fitness for the Pentagon's top job. Some Republicans have raised concerns about his background and statements, though the specific grounds for impeachment remain contested within GOP ranks.

**Conservative concerns** center on whether Hegseth possesses the temperament and experience to lead the world's largest military.
