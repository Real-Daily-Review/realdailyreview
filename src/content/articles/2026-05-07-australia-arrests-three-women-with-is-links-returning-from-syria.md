# Australia Arrests Three Women Linked to Islamic State Upon Return from Syria

Three women with alleged ties to Islamic State were arrested in Australia after returning from Syria for the first time in years, according to the BBC and The Guardian.

The women were part of a group of 13 that arrived in separate flights to Sydney and Melbourne on Thursday evening, having spent years in Syrian detention camps. One of those arrested, Janai Safar, 32, has been charged in Sydney with allegedly entering a declared conflict zone and joining Islamic State, The Guardian reports.

The arrests mark a significant moment in Australia's handling of citizens and residents who traveled to Syria to support or join IS during the group's territorial control. Australian authorities have faced ongoing questions about how to manage the return of women and children from conflict zones, balancing security concerns with humanitarian considerations.

The three women face potential charges related to their alleged involvement with the designated terrorist organization. Details on the specific charges against the other two arrested women were not immediately disclosed in available reports.
