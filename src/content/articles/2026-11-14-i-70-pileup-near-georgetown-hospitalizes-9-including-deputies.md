# Multi-Vehicle Pileup on I-70 Near Georgetown Injures Nine, Including Law Enforcement

A major traffic incident on Interstate 70 near Georgetown Thursday morning left nine people hospitalized, including two Clear Creek County Sheriff's Office deputies who were assisting a stranded motorist when an 11-vehicle crash struck the area.

According to Colorado State Patrol and local reports (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31), the deputies were conducting roadside assistance when the collision occurred, underscoring the hazards law enforcement faces during routine traffic stops on Colorado's busiest mountain corridor. Both deputies were transported to hospitals for treatment of injuries sustained in the impact.

The exact cause of the multi-vehicle pileup remains under investigation by state authorities. I-70 through Clear Creek County—a critical commercial and commuter route connecting the Front Range to Western Slope communities—experiences heavy traffic, particularly during morning and evening peaks, and weather-related incidents are common in the mountain passes.

The crash highlights ongoing concerns about highway safety and the risks deputies and first responders encounter while protecting motorists on Colorado's roadways. Conservatives and law enforcement advocates have long emphasized the need for adequate staffing and resources to keep Colorado's transportation infrastructure safe and functional.
