# Kansas Democrat's Anti-Beef Sermon Signals Trouble for Rural America

A Kansas Democrat running for U.S. Senate once told his megachurch congregation to repent for eating beef—a stunning disconnect in a state built on cattle ranching that should alarm Colorado conservatives watching similar progressive overreach creep westward.

Adam Hamilton, a pastor-turned-politician, framed beef consumption as a moral failing requiring spiritual atonement, according to reporting from Fox News and Breitbart. In Kansas—one of America's largest cattle-producing states—such rhetoric amounts to cultural hostility toward the agricultural backbone of rural economies.

Republicans argue this exemplifies the left's contempt for working Americans and traditional industries. Democrats counter that climate and environmental concerns justify dietary messaging, though they rarely frame it as bluntly as Hamilton did.

**The Colorado angle:** Western states from Kansas to Colorado depend on ranching, energy development, and agricultural exports. If anti-beef, anti-energy progressives gain Senate seats, they'll push carbon taxes, methane regulations, and land-use restrictions that directly threaten Colorado ranchers, oil workers, and rural communities. Hamilton's sermon reveals the ideological core driving Democratic environmental policy—not pragmatism, but moral condemnation of how rural Americans live.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from or embrace this anti-agriculture messaging ahead of 2026 Senate races.
