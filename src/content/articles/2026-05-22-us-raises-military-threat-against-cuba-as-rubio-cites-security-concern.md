# US Raises Military Threat Against Cuba as Tensions Escalate

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Thursday that Cuba poses a national security threat to the United States, according to Al Jazeera, as the Trump administration renewed threats of military intervention against the island nation.

Rubio characterized Cuba as a threat during remarks that came a day after the administration announced criminal charges against Raúl Castro, the island's former leader, according to The Guardian. President Trump told reporters the U.S. would "look at" military options, suggesting his administration may be "the one" to act after decades of inaction by previous presidents.

The secretary of state expressed skepticism about diplomatic prospects with Havana, telling reporters that while Trump would prefer a negotiated agreement, the likelihood of one succeeding "is not high," according to The Guardian.

Cuba's foreign minister fired back, accusing Rubio of attempting to "instigate a military aggression," according to BBC World. Havana characterized the U.S. statements as "lies."

The rhetoric marks an escalation in U.S.-Cuba tensions under the Trump administration, which has taken a harder line on the communist-led island. Watch for whether the administration follows through with concrete military preparations or diplomatic overtures in coming weeks.
