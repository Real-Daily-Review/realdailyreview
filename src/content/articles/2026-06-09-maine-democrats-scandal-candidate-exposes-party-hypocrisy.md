# Maine Democrats' Scandal-Plagued Candidate Exposes Party's Moral Hypocrisy

Colorado Democrats should take note: the national party's willingness to nominate a scandal-ridden candidate in Maine reveals how little principle guides their politics.

Graham Platner, the Bernie Sanders-backed Democratic challenger to Sen. Susan Collins, brings a troubling record that even the *Denver Post* editorial board won't ignore. According to the opinion piece, Platner carries "more baggage than Denver International Airport on Thanksgiving weekend"—yet Democrats are preparing to nominate him anyway (Denver Post).

Collins has hammered Platner as too extreme for Maine voters, while Platner fires back that Collins is the "radical" (Fox News). But the substance tells a different story. National Review notes Platner's "mendacity" and lack of a credible record, leaving voters with only his word—a word he has given them no reason to trust.

Republicans argue this exposes Democratic hypocrisy: the party claims the moral high ground on character and integrity while nominating a candidate whose past disqualifies him by their own stated standards. If Democrats can't hold their own candidates accountable in Maine, why should Colorado voters trust their judgment on anything else?

Watch whether Colorado Democratic leaders distance themselves from Platner or double down on party loyalty over principle.
