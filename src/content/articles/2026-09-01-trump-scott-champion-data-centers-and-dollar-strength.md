# Trump, Scott Push Pro-Growth Economic Vision—Data Centers and Dollar Strength

President Donald Trump and Sen. Rick Scott (R-FL) are making the case for straightforward economic growth, and Colorado should be paying attention.

Trump has mounted a full-throated defense of data centers—critical infrastructure for AI development and computing power that drives innovation and jobs. Scott, meanwhile, is defending the U.S. dollar's role as the global reserve currency, a position that protects American economic leverage and purchasing power.

Both moves signal Republican commitment to pro-growth policy over regulatory obstruction. Data centers represent real economic opportunity for Colorado communities seeking tax base expansion and high-wage employment. The push to maintain dollar dominance safeguards the stability that Colorado businesses, farmers, and energy producers depend on for trade and investment.

The contrast is sharp: Republicans are championing infrastructure and monetary strength; the Biden-Harris administration spent four years strangling energy development, imposing green mandates, and weakening the dollar through reckless spending. Colorado taxpayers and employers felt that squeeze acutely.

Watch whether Colorado's state legislature and local governments will embrace data center development or throw up regulatory roadblocks in the name of environmental concerns—a test of whether state GOP leadership will match Trump's pro-growth clarity.
