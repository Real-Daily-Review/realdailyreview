# Trump's China Diplomacy Raises Questions Among Conservative Hawks

President Trump's surprisingly warm reception in Shenzhen—where Xi Jinping personally greeted him at the tarmac—is drawing fire from right-wing skeptics who worry the administration is going soft on Beijing.

Fox News reports the gesture reciprocated Trump's own tarmac welcome from months earlier, signaling diplomatic thaw. But the Washington Examiner's conservative opinion section warns this cordiality contradicts Trump's long-standing hawkish stance on China. The outlet argues Trump's second administration has been "surprisingly soft" on the communist regime and calls on Congress to use oversight authority to investigate what it frames as policy failures.

**The conservative concern:** Trump built his political brand on confronting Chinese trade practices, intellectual property theft, and military expansion. A shift toward ceremonial diplomacy without corresponding pressure on Beijing's behavior risks squandering leverage and disappointing the base that expects hardball tactics.

**Colorado angle:** Energy and agriculture matter here. Any China deal affecting tariffs, trade terms, or agricultural exports directly impacts Colorado farmers and energy producers already navigating market uncertainty.

Republicans and conservative hawks should watch whether Trump uses the APEC meeting to extract concrete concessions—or whether this becomes another example of diplomatic theater without results.
