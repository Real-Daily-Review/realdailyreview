# Denver Shooting Highlights Crime Crisis in Progressive-Run Cities

A 25-year-old man was killed in a seemingly random shooting on East Colfax Avenue on June 7, with a suspect now in custody following the homicide investigation, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The incident underscores a troubling pattern in Denver and other major cities led by progressive administrations. While Democrats have pushed policies like reduced police funding, lenient bail reform, and catch-and-release prosecution strategies, violent crime has surged. Republicans and law-and-order advocates argue these soft-on-crime approaches have emboldened criminals and left law-abiding Coloradans—particularly in urban cores—less safe.

Denver's homicide rate has climbed in recent years, a reality conservatives attribute directly to progressive criminal-justice policies that prioritize offender rehabilitation over victim protection and community safety. The city's district attorney's office has faced criticism from law enforcement and victims' advocates for declining to prosecute certain crimes aggressively.

Coloradans deserve leadership that prioritizes public safety, enforces laws consistently, and holds criminals accountable—not policies that treat crime as a social-work problem. Watch whether Denver's next municipal elections shift toward candidates willing to reverse the progressive experiment that has made the Mile High City less safe.
