# Iran Escalates Attacks on US Forces in Iraq; Colorado Should Brace for Broader Conflict

The Middle East is spiraling toward direct US-Iran confrontation, and Colorado's military installations and defense contractors need to prepare for potential consequences.

Drones carrying explosives targeted Irbil, the capital of Iraq's Kurdish region, where American forces are based, according to reporting from 9News Denver and the New York Post. An Associated Press journalist on the ground reported at least seven blasts Friday morning local time. Sirens also sounded in Bahrain, where the US Navy's Fifth Fleet operates.

This marks the latest Iranian provocation in an escalating cycle of tit-for-tat strikes that threatens to drag the US into a broader regional war. The Biden-Harris administration's weak posture toward Tehran—from the failed Iran nuclear deal revival to hesitant responses to previous Iranian attacks—has emboldened Tehran to test American resolve.

**The Colorado angle:** Peterson Space Force Base near Colorado Springs hosts critical US Central Command operations. Fort Carson and other state military installations could face deployment surges or heightened readiness requirements. Colorado defense contractors supporting Middle East operations may see supply chain disruptions or contract acceleration.

Republicans have consistently warned that appeasement invites aggression. Democrats' diplomatic overtures to Iran have failed spectacularly.

Watch whether the administration finally enforces red lines or continues the pattern of measured, ineffectual responses that invite further Iranian aggression.
