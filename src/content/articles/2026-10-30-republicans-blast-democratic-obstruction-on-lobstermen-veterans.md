# Senate Democrats Block Protections While Abandoning Veterans, Republicans Say

Senate Republicans are calling out Democratic obstruction on two fronts: Sen. Ted Cruz has pledged to advance the Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act after Sen. Susan Collins accused a top Democratic senator of blocking the bill (Fox News Politics), while a Purple Heart Vietnam veteran who was invited as Jon Ossoff's personal guest to Trump's March 2025 Joint Address is now accusing the Georgia Democrat of abandoning veterans entirely in his reelection campaign (Breitbart).

Republicans argue the pattern reflects Democratic priorities misaligned with working Americans and those who've sacrificed for the nation. Cruz's commitment to push the lobsterman protections forward signals GOP resolve to fight regulatory overreach affecting small industries—a principle that resonates with Colorado's own struggles against federal land and water restrictions.

The Ossoff controversy underscores a broader conservative critique: Democrats court veterans for political theater but fail to deliver meaningful support when cameras fade. That disconnect matters to Colorado's veteran community, which has grown increasingly skeptical of Democratic-led budget priorities that favor progressive social spending over defense and veteran benefits.

Watch whether Senate Republicans can build bipartisan momentum on the lobsterman bill—and whether Colorado's delegation will join Cruz in demanding Democratic accountability on veterans' issues ahead of 2026.
