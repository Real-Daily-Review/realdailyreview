# mRNA Flu Vaccine Raises Safety Questions as FDA Oversight Questioned

Colorado families deserve transparent data on vaccine safety—and they're not getting it. Moderna's new mRNA flu vaccine shows a significantly higher injury rate than traditional shots, according to the company's own research cited by *Just The News*, yet the FDA approved it with minimal scrutiny.

The numbers are troubling. Moderna's data indicates 5,000 novel jabs would be required to prevent just one hospitalization compared to standard vaccines. Industry observers warn the FDA has become a "rubber stamp" for pharmaceutical interests following the departure of officials with stricter approval standards, *Just The News* reports.

*National Review* argues the Vaccine Injury Compensation Program—which has served patients for decades—should be strengthened, not dismantled, suggesting confidence in the system is eroding. But strengthening a compensation program doesn't address the core problem: why are riskier vaccines being fast-tracked in the first place?

For Colorado, this matters. State health officials promote vaccines aggressively, and if federal oversight is compromised, Coloradans have no backup. Parents and doctors need honest risk-benefit analysis, not regulatory capture serving pharmaceutical profits over public health.

Watch whether Colorado's health department demands independent safety reviews before recommending the mRNA flu vaccine to residents.
