# Real Daily Review — The Brief
### Sunday, October 11, 2026

Colorado wakes up to a warm October Sunday, but the political climate is anything but mild — from a landmark Supreme Court case that could gut Colorado's energy economy to a ballot measure that puts parents back in charge of their children's medical decisions.

---

## SCOTUS Climate Case Threatens Colorado's Energy Industry

The Supreme Court's *Suncor v. Boulder* case is shaping up to be one of the most consequential energy rulings in a generation. Energy experts warn a ruling against the oil companies could open the floodgates to thousands of climate liability lawsuits nationwide — driving gas prices through the roof and threatening the livelihoods of Colorado's energy workers. Republicans argue this is exactly the kind of judicial weaponization of climate politics that punishes producers and consumers alike. Colorado's oil and gas sector, one of the state's economic pillars, cannot afford to become a litigation target for every progressive city attorney in the country.

---

## Colorado Voters to Decide on Gender Surgery Ban for Minors — Proposition 135

Colorado's Proposition 135 will ask voters whether to ban surgeries aimed at altering the biological sex characteristics of minors. The measure applies broadly — to transgender patients and non-transgender patients alike — for anyone under 18. Conservatives say this is straightforward: the government has no business standing aside while children undergo irreversible surgical procedures, and parents deserve the protection of law, not the pressure of an activist medical establishment. Colorado voters will have the final word, and this one matters.

---

## Pennsylvania Mass Shooting Leaves Nine Dead, Including Two Children

A gunman killed eight people Friday night in Erie, Pennsylvania, before also dying — leaving nine dead total, including two children, according to police. Authorities called the situation "deeply disturbing." Conservatives will rightly note that no law-abiding Coloradan should be left defenseless against this kind of violence, and that the answer is not stripping Second Amendment rights from responsible gun owners — it is holding violent criminals accountable and ensuring citizens can protect themselves and their families.
