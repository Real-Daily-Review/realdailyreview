# Democratic Nominee's Radical Ally Exposes the Damage-Control Game

Abdul El-Sayed's attempt to distance himself from far-left Twitch streamer Hasan Piker—who has praised Hamas and claimed "America deserved 9/11"—is falling apart, with Piker himself bragging that the Michigan Democratic Senate nominee is faking moderation to deceive voters, according to *The Daily Wire*.

El-Sayed spent months actively campaigning alongside Piker, whose antisemitic rhetoric and extremist positions were well documented, *National Review* reports. Now that the association has drawn national backlash, El-Sayed is attempting damage control—but Piker is having none of it, insisting the candidate's core positions remain unchanged and that the distancing act is purely electoral theater.

This matters for Colorado Democrats. As *Washington Examiner* opinion notes, every Democrat in a competitive race should face the same question: Are you aligned with this militant-left ideology, or not? Piker wasn't a casual endorser; he was a key surrogate with direct access to millions of young voters. El-Sayed's playbook—radical positioning during the primary, sudden moderation for general election voters—is the Democratic blueprint nationwide.

Colorado voters should watch whether Democratic candidates running statewide this cycle get asked directly about their ties to far-left influencers and whether they'll disavow the antisemitic, anti-American rhetoric now mainstream on the progressive left.
