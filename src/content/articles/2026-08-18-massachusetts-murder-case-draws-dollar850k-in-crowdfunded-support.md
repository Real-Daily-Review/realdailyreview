# Colorado Connection Absent in National Murder Trial Fundraising Surge

A GoFundMe campaign for the parents of Lindsay Clancy—a Massachusetts woman charged with murdering her three children—has raised over $850,000 in less than a week, according to the Washington Examiner. The "Musgrove Family Fund" lists Mike and Paula Musgrove as beneficiaries, ostensibly to support their daughter's legal defense.

The Daily Wire reports that Paula Musgrove testified Monday for the defense, describing her daughter's mental deterioration in the weeks before the alleged strangulation deaths. Her testimony focused on "disturbing thoughts" that preceded the crimes.

The case has drawn national attention and significant crowdfunding support, though it remains unclear whether Colorado has any direct connection to the defendant or her family. The trial underscores how high-profile criminal cases can mobilize online fundraising networks across state lines.
