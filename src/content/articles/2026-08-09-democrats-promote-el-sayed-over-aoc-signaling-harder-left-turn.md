# Democrats Eye El-Sayed as AOC Stays Loyal to Party Line

Colorado Democrats should brace for a harder left turn: national party strategists are actively promoting Abdul El-Sayed over Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez for higher office, signaling the left's preference for ideological purity over pragmatism.

According to Democrat strategist Evan Barker on "The Alex Marrow Show" (Breitbart), AOC won't run for president because "she's a team player" who "wants to get along" — code for someone willing to compromise with the party establishment. El-Sayed, by contrast, represents the uncompromising progressive wing Democrats increasingly favor.

El-Sayed's Michigan Senate bid is gaining traction with key demographics (Daily Caller), even as he faces headwinds in general-election territory. Republicans argue this signals Democrats doubling down on far-left economic and social policies that have failed in swing states and suburbs.

**The Colorado angle:** If El-Sayed-style progressivism becomes the Democratic Party's north star, expect Colorado's left-leaning legislature to accelerate spending, regulation, and energy restrictions that burden taxpayers and energy workers across the state.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado Democrats embrace El-Sayed's model or resist it — a telling sign of how far left the state party intends to drift before 2026.
