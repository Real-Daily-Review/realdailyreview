# Colorado Voters Keep Democrats on a Short Leash—With Ballot Initiatives

Colorado voters are sending a clear message: elect Democrats for statewide office, then use citizen initiatives to block their policies. According to Complete Colorado, Coloradans have developed a pattern of ticket-splitting that amounts to a built-in check on Democratic governance—voting for Democratic candidates while simultaneously approving ballot measures that constrain progressive agendas on taxes, regulation, and spending.

This dynamic reveals deep skepticism among Colorado voters about one-party Democratic control. Rather than trust the legislature to restrain itself, voters are essentially saying they want divided government by other means. The strategy works: ballot initiatives have repeatedly blocked or modified Democratic priorities that voters feared would raise costs, expand government, or threaten property rights and energy development.

Conservatives see this as voters instinctively protecting themselves from the progressive overreach that follows Democratic electoral victories. The pattern suggests Colorado's electorate remains fundamentally center-right on fiscal and regulatory matters, even as it votes for Democratic personalities and branding.

**Watch:** Whether Democrats respond by pushing harder on their agenda or moderating to respect voter intent on the ballot measures.
