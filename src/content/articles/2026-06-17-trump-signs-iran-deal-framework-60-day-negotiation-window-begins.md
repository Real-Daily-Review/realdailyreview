# Trump's Iran Deal Framework: 60-Day Negotiation Window, Full Text Finally Disclosed

President Trump signed a memorandum of understanding with Iran this week, committing both nations to negotiate a final deal within 60 days—though Trump clarified the timeline is not a "hard" deadline and can be extended with mutual consent, according to *Just The News*.

The full text of the agreement was revealed to reporters after initial secrecy surrounding the framework. *Breitbart* confirmed the White House shared the complete Memorandum of Understanding with news outlets, ending weeks of speculation about the deal's contents.

**The conservative concern:** Right-leaning outlets have questioned the administration's handling of the agreement's disclosure. The *Washington Examiner* editorial board criticized Vice President JD Vance's explanation for the delayed public release, calling it "scandalous" and arguing transparency should have come sooner. Conservative critics worry that vague timelines and negotiation frameworks leave room for Iranian stalling tactics and fail to lock in concrete commitments upfront.

Republicans argue the U.S. should demand ironclad terms—not aspirational 60-day windows—before legitimizing further Iranian diplomacy. They point to the failed 2015 nuclear deal as a cautionary tale of American concessions without lasting results.

**What Colorado taxpayers should watch:** Whether this framework leads to actual sanctions relief or military commitments that affect defense spending and energy markets tied to Middle East stability.
