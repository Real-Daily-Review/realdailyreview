# Trump Administration Defends White House UFC Event Against Legal Challenge

The Trump administration is fighting back against a lawsuit seeking to block a UFC event at the White House, arguing in court filings that the challenge came too late and would disrupt months of planning, according to 9News Denver and Just The News.

Justice Department attorneys told the judge that halting the event would upend extensive preparation work already underway. The administration's position: the lawsuit is procedurally flawed and filed after the die was cast.

The case highlights the broader tension between executive authority and judicial intervention in White House operations. Conservatives generally support the administration's argument that presidents retain broad discretion over events held on their own grounds, and that last-minute legal challenges shouldn't derail planned activities.
