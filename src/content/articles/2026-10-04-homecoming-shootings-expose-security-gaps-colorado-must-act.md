# Violence Plagues Homecoming Events Across Nation; Colorado Communities Should Harden Security

Two separate mass shooting incidents at homecoming celebrations—one in Georgia leaving two dead and 25 hospitalized, another near Minneapolis with gang involvement—underscore a troubling trend that Colorado event organizers and law enforcement must address head-on.

The Vienna, Georgia shooting erupted around 1:20 a.m. at a block party despite deputy presence monitoring the crowd (9News Denver). Meanwhile, Somali gangs have been blamed for a shooting outside a Minneapolis homecoming game that wounded two and forced event cancellations (New York Post).

**The conservative concern:** These incidents reveal the failure of progressive soft-on-crime policies and gang enforcement. While Democrats push gun-control measures targeting law-abiding citizens, actual violent criminals—including organized gang members—operate with impunity in jurisdictions that have gutted police budgets and prosecution resources. Colorado communities hosting homecoming events, football games, and block parties face similar risks, yet some local governments remain reluctant to adequately fund law enforcement and security infrastructure.

Republicans argue the solution isn't restricting Second Amendment rights for responsible Coloradans, but rather aggressive prosecution of gang violence, sufficient police presence, and hardened event security. Democrats typically counter with calls for stricter gun laws and community programs—approaches conservatives say ignore the root problem: criminal accountability.

Colorado school districts and event organizers should demand their local officials prioritize visible security and swift prosecution of violent offenders.
