# Establishment Democrats Crush Squad Socialist in Missouri Rematch

Cori Bush's far-left comeback collapsed Tuesday as Missouri Democrats rejected the DSA-linked firebrand for a second time, handing establishment rival Wesley Bell a commanding victory in the state's deep-blue 1st Congressional District.

Bush, a former member of the radical "Squad," lost decisively to Bell, who first ousted her two years ago. With roughly 26% of the vote counted, Bell held a commanding lead, according to *Fox News* and *The Daily Wire*.

The result marks a significant rebuke of the socialist wing within the Democratic Party—and a win for moderate Democrats willing to challenge progressive incumbents on their home turf. Bell's victory suggests that even in safely Democratic districts, voters are tiring of Squad-style radicalism and the anti-police rhetoric that defined Bush's tenure.

While this race occurs in Missouri, not Colorado, it signals a broader pattern: establishment Democrats are fighting back against their party's far-left flank. Colorado Democrats should take note—if moderates in deep-blue districts are rejecting socialism, the appetite for progressive overreach may be weakening nationwide.
