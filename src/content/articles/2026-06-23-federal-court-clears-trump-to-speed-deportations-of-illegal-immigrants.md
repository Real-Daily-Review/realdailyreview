# Federal Court Clears Trump to Accelerate Deportations of Illegal Immigrants

A federal appeals court has handed the Trump administration a major win, allowing the Department of Homeland Security to resume expedited deportations of illegal immigrants who cannot prove continuous U.S. residence for two years or longer, according to *The Daily Wire* and *Just The News*. The DC Circuit Court of Appeals ruling Tuesday removes a legal barrier that had blocked the process.

The expedited removal authority, originally designed for recent border crossers, was expanded to cover all migrants unable to document their continuous presence—a significant broadening of enforcement reach. The decision clears the way for what *Breitbart* describes as a "restart" of the program after legal challenges had stalled it.

For Colorado, this matters: illegal immigration strains state resources, from emergency rooms to schools, and competes with legal workers in construction, agriculture, and service industries. Republicans argue expedited deportations are a lawful, necessary tool to enforce immigration law and reduce the pull factor of sanctuary policies. Democrats and immigration advocates contend the process denies due process and separates families, though they offer no alternative enforcement mechanism.

The ruling reflects judicial recognition that immigration enforcement authority lies with the executive branch, not activist courts. Conservatives should watch whether the Biden-era legal challenges resurface or whether Democrats in Congress attempt legislative workarounds to hamstring enforcement.
