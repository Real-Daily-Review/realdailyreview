# California's DEI Bureaucracy Collides With Newsom's Rebranding Act

California Governor Gavin Newsom is quietly dismantling the state's bloated DEI apparatus—a tacit admission that progressive overreach has become a political liability, even for Democrats eyeing higher office. According to *National Review*, Newsom's pivot toward "normal" governance directly contradicts years of DEI expansion across California's bureaucracy, signaling that the governor recognizes the policy has become electoral poison.

The contradiction runs deeper than optics. While Newsom positions himself as a pragmatist, he simultaneously blamed President Trump for rejecting a bipartisan housing bill, claiming (*Washington Examiner*) the president was unfamiliar with California's own housing reforms. Yet California's housing crisis—driven partly by regulatory red tape and zoning restrictions—remains one of the nation's worst, suggesting Newsom's "reforms" haven't solved the problem they promised to fix.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats should take note. As progressive policies pile up—from energy regulations to housing mandates—Coloradans are watching whether their own leaders will admit failure or double down. California's DEI retreat shows even blue-state politicians recognize voters are tired of bureaucratic bloat masquerading as progress.
