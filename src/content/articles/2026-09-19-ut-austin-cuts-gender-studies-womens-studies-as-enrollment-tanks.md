# UT Austin Axes Woke Studies Programs as Enrollment Collapses

University of Texas at Austin is shuttering its women's studies, gender studies, and ethnic studies majors—a rare victory for conservatives tired of ideological bloat in higher education.

The move comes after sustained political pressure from Texas state leaders and reflects what critics call the inevitable reckoning with "leftist delusions" that have hollowed out humanities departments nationwide. Low enrollment numbers made the case: fewer students want degrees in grievance studies when job prospects are bleak.

**The conservative argument:** These programs represent taxpayer-funded ideological capture masquerading as education. They prioritize activist messaging over rigorous scholarship and leave graduates with unmarketable credentials. Texas Republicans have long argued that state universities should serve workforce development and intellectual merit, not progressive social engineering.

**Democrats counter** that eliminating these majors erases important perspectives and disciplines, though they've offered no compelling defense of enrollment numbers or graduate outcomes.

**Colorado angle:** While UT Austin isn't Colorado's fight directly, the state's own universities—CU Boulder, CSU—host similar programs. Conservative Colorado taxpayers should watch whether Republican legislators here demand similar accountability for bloated humanities departments that drain resources from STEM and practical fields.

Watch whether other conservative-led states follow Texas's lead in defunding ideological programs.
