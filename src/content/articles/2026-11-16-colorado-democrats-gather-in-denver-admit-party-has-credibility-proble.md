# Democrats Gather in Denver, Scramble for 2028 as Party Faces Credibility Crisis

Colorado Democrats convened in Denver this week for what amounted to a reality check wrapped in Obama-era nostalgia—a stark admission that the party's brand is damaged heading into 2028. According to the *Denver Post*, the defining message from Thursday's gathering was blunt: "People still don't like you."

The concert-style event, covered by *Fox News*, showcased Democratic leaders jockeying for position in the next presidential race while attempting to recapture the energy of the Obama years. But the optics tell the real story: a party searching for answers after electoral setbacks and struggling to rebuild trust with voters.

**The conservative takeaway:** Colorado Republicans should note that Democrats are openly acknowledging a credibility problem—not a policy problem, but a fundamental trust deficit with the American people. This suggests voters are rejecting progressive governance, not merely the messengers. The Denver gathering's backward-looking nostalgia (invoking Obama) rather than forward-looking vision underscores the left's intellectual bankruptcy on new ideas.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the question is whether Republicans will capitalize on Democratic weakness by offering a clear, pro-growth, limited-government alternative—or squander the opportunity with infighting.
