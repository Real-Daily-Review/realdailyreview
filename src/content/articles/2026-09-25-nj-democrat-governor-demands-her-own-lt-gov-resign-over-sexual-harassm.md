# New Jersey Democrat Governor Demands Her Own Lieutenant Governor Resign Over Sexual Harassment

New Jersey Gov. Mikie Sherrill (D) has called for the resignation of Lt. Gov. Dale Caldwell after an investigation substantiated sexual harassment and ethics violations, according to Fox News and the Washington Examiner. Sherrill gave Caldwell until Friday to step down.

The investigation found that Caldwell engaged in "serious, repeated violations of state policy," the New York Post reported, including attempting to secure a promotion for his girlfriend, making inappropriate sexual comments to staff, and ignoring repeated warnings about his conduct. Caldwell has denied the allegations.

If Caldwell refuses to resign, Sherrill can pursue impeachment through the state legislature, though Just The News notes the governor cannot unilaterally remove him.

**The Colorado angle:** This scandal underscores a broader conservative concern—that Democratic administrations often promote loyalty over accountability. Sherrill hand-picked Caldwell as her 2025 running mate, yet failed to vet or supervise conduct that investigators say was flagrant and repeated. It's a cautionary tale for Colorado voters about the importance of executive competence and proper oversight.

**What to watch:** Whether the Democratic-controlled New Jersey legislature actually holds one of their own accountable, or shields Caldwell from consequences.
