# Trump Rallies Against Omar as DOJ Probe Intensifies

Federal agents raided Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington home this week, and President Trump wasted no time weaponizing the moment at an Ohio rally, where crowds chanted "deport" as he attacked the Minnesota Democrat. Trump stopped short of endorsing deportation himself, telling reporters he thinks Omar is "a terrible person" and "a terrible congresswoman" who "doesn't love our country," but declined to take an explicit position on removal (The Daily Wire).

The raid has sparked sharp debate even among conservatives. While Trump's base seized on the investigation as vindication of long-standing criticism of Omar's record—her controversial statements on Israel, campaign finance questions, and anti-American rhetoric—National Review's editors urged caution, noting they find themselves "rooting against both sides" when it comes to DOJ prosecutions of political opponents.

**The Colorado angle:** Omar's far-left positions on energy, gun control, and federal land management align with policies Colorado Democrats have pushed. Her record opposing domestic energy development mirrors efforts by progressive groups to restrict oil and gas on public lands here.

Conservatives should watch whether the DOJ investigation produces charges or fizzles—and whether Trump's DOJ becomes a tool for political payback rather than impartial justice.
