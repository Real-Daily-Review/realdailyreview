# Michigan Democrat's Alliance With Antisemitic Influencer Exposes Left's Tolerance Problem

Michigan's Democratic Senate nominee Abdul El-Sayed has spent months campaigning alongside Hasan Piker, an online personality known for antisemitic and anti-American rhetoric—and refuses to distance himself from him. According to *National Review* and *The Daily Wire*, newly circulated video shows El-Sayed defending his refusal to condemn Piker's inflammatory remarks, sitting casually with the influencer while dismissing calls for disavowal.

**Republicans argue** this alliance reveals the Democratic Party's willingness to tolerate extremism when it serves electoral purposes. The refusal to condemn antisemitic speech, they contend, reflects a broader pattern of progressive politicians ignoring bigotry from their base when politically inconvenient.

**Democrats have not offered a substantive public defense** of El-Sayed's position, instead remaining largely silent on the controversy.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: it underscores how national Democratic figures operate without meaningful accountability from their own party. As Colorado's Democratic establishment remains influential in state politics, voters should ask whether similar tolerance for extremist rhetoric exists closer to home.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic leaders distance themselves from El-Sayed's approach or remain silent—their response will signal how seriously they take antisemitism within their coalition.
