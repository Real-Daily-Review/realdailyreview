# Colorado Football: Sanders Cleans House After First Loss

Coach Deion Sanders is making roster moves at the University of Colorado following the Buffaloes' first loss of the season to Northwestern, dismissing several players for what he cited as a "pattern of behavior" — though the school says the process remains incomplete.

Sanders did not specify which players were dismissed or the exact nature of the conduct violations (KDVR, 9News). The university said an updated roster will be released later in the week, suggesting the final roster decisions are still being finalized.

The move signals Sanders' willingness to enforce team discipline and accountability standards early in his tenure. For Colorado taxpayers and boosters invested in CU athletics, the question is whether roster turnover will improve performance or create instability as the season progresses.

Colorado's football program has been a point of pride and significant institutional investment. How Sanders manages player discipline and team culture will directly affect fan engagement, donor confidence, and the program's competitive standing in the Big 12.
