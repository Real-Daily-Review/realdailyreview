# Rand Paul Alleges Cover-Up of Fauci Gain-of-Function Research; Cotton Accused of Concealment

Senator Rand Paul is accusing fellow Republicans—specifically Senator Tom Cotton—of burying explosive evidence about Dr. Anthony Fauci's involvement in gain-of-function research and document destruction, according to *Just The News* and the *Daily Caller*.

Paul claims new revelations from Fauci's phone, computer, and diary records show undisclosed destruction of documents and deliberate attempts to weaponize the virus through gain-of-function research. His committee reportedly sought prosecutorial immunity for a former Fauci aide to secure testimony, but the effort failed.

The intra-GOP dispute centers on whether damaging information will reach public view. Paul contends that Democrats—and some Republicans like Cotton—are blocking transparency on what conservatives argue represents a catastrophic government failure that contributed to the pandemic's origins and severity.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado voters and taxpayers funded the National Institutes of Health grants that allegedly supported this research. Transparency on federal bioweapon research directly affects public trust in government health agencies and future pandemic preparedness.

Conservatives argue the American people deserve full disclosure of how federal health bureaucrats may have enabled the creation of COVID-19. The stonewalling, they say, is a textbook example of institutional self-protection over accountability.

Watch for whether Paul's committee can force document release and compel testimony before the 2026 election cycle reshuffles congressional power.
