# Real Daily Review — The Brief
## Wednesday, May 20, 2026

A wave of primary results reshapes both parties' near-term futures, while a landmark IRS settlement and a high-stakes summit between Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin add weight to an already consequential morning.

---

## Massie Ousted in Most Expensive House Primary Ever

President Trump's endorsee Ed Gallrein defeated Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.) in Tuesday's GOP primary, extending Trump's winning streak against Republicans who have defied him. The race became the most expensive House primary on record. Massie had been one of Trump's sharpest Republican critics. The result signals that the president's intraparty retribution campaign remains potent heading into the general election cycle.

---

## Google Overhauls Gemini at I/O 2026

Google used its annual I/O developer conference to announce significant updates to its Gemini app, positioning the product more directly against OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude. The announcements coincided with Singapore signing new AI partnerships with both Google and OpenAI, as the city-state moves to cement its role as a global AI hub. The moves reflect accelerating competition among major AI platforms for enterprise and government contracts across Asia and beyond.

## IRS Drops Tax Claims Against Trump in Sweeping Settlement

The U.S. government will drop tax claims against President Trump as part of a settlement agreement, according to a document posted to the Department of Justice website. The agreement states the U.S. is "forever barred and precluded" from examining or prosecuting Trump, his sons, and the Trump Organization over current tax issues. Critics argue the settlement represents an extraordinary use of executive power to shield the president from financial scrutiny; the administration has not publicly addressed those concerns.

## Xi and Putin Meet in Beijing Days After Trump's China Visit

Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin held talks in Beijing on Wednesday, just days after President Trump completed an official visit to China. The timing underscores the complexity of great-power diplomacy, with Beijing navigating relationships with both Washington and Moscow simultaneously. No details on specific agreements reached in the Xi-Putin meeting were immediately available from the sources.

## Senate Advances War Powers Resolution on Iran

The Senate voted to advance a War Powers Resolution aimed at ending U.S. military action against Iran, a rare legislative rebuke of the president. Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-La.), following his loss in a primary contest, flipped to support the measure. Supporters argue Congress must reassert its constitutional war-making authority; the administration has not publicly indicated whether it would accept or resist the resolution's terms.
