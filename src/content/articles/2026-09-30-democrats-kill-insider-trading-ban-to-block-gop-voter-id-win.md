# Democrats Block Insider Trading Ban to Protect Voter ID Push

Senate Democrats, led by Chuck Schumer, killed a GOP bill combining an insider trading ban with voter ID requirements—blocking a measure that had enjoyed bipartisan support. Schumer called the Republican approach "trickery" and "skullduggery," signaling Democrats will tank even popular legislation rather than hand Republicans a legislative win ahead of the midterms.

Republicans argue Democrats are playing pure politics, sacrificing good governance to deny the GOP any momentum. The insider trading ban itself had bipartisan appeal, but Democrats refused to let it pass if paired with voter ID protections—a core conservative priority to secure election integrity.

The pattern extends beyond this single bill. Democrats have blocked the Ratepayer Protection Act and other previously bipartisan measures, according to reporting, in a coordinated effort to starve Republicans of legislative victories before November.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado voters and taxpayers should care that partisan gridlock is killing bills that could protect both market integrity and election security. When either party weaponizes procedural votes to block popular measures, Coloradans lose.

**Watch next:** Whether Republicans reintroduce these bills separately—forcing Democrats to vote directly against insider trading bans or election security measures on the record.
