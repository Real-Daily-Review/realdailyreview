# LIV Golf's Collapse Shows the Limits of Saudi Money and Woke Backlash

LIV Golf is imploding under mounting losses, lawsuits, and unpaid bills—but conservatives argue the Saudi-backed upstart accomplished what the PGA Tour couldn't: forcing the golf establishment to confront its own hypocrisy and stale product.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, LIV Golf's collapse matters less than what it exposed. The tour's aggressive challenge to the PGA's monopoly rattled an institution that had grown complacent, bloated with corporate correctness, and boring to fans. LIV forced the PGA to compete on player compensation and tournament format—changes that benefited the sport regardless of the Saudi venture's financial viability.

Conservatives see LIV's failure not as vindication of the PGA's woke gatekeeping, but as proof that even well-funded challenges to entrenched establishments face structural headwinds. The real lesson: the PGA's grip on professional golf remains nearly unshakeable, and the sports media's reflexive hostility to LIV revealed more about progressive bias than about the tour's merits.

For Colorado golf fans and business owners in the hospitality sector, the consolidation means fewer high-profile tournaments competing for venues and sponsorship dollars—a missed opportunity for mountain resort communities.

Watch whether the PGA uses its renewed dominance to actually reform its product or simply revert to the same stale formula that made LIV's challenge plausible in the first place.
