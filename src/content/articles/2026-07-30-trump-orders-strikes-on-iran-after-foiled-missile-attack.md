# U.S. Strikes Iran After Foiled Attack on American Forces

Trump administration delivers promised response to Iranian missile assault on U.S. troops in Jordan.

The Pentagon launched a "heavy wave" of strikes against Iranian targets Wednesday evening, following an attempted Iranian missile attack on American forces stationed in Jordan, according to U.S. Central Command (9News Denver). President Trump had committed to giving Iran "a beating" for the surprise assault, and the strikes represent that promised retaliation (Washington Examiner).

U.S. Central Command characterized the operation as "a powerful response to yesterday's attempted Iranian attack," with strikes beginning at 8 p.m. EDT Wednesday. The action marks the Trump administration's willingness to respond decisively to Iranian aggression—a sharp contrast to years of restraint under the previous administration that conservatives argue emboldened Tehran.

For Colorado, the broader implications matter: sustained Middle East instability can affect energy markets and military readiness at installations like Fort Carson and the Air Force Academy.
