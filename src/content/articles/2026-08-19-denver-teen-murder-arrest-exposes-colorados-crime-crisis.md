# Denver Murder, Border Security Failures Highlight Crime Wave Across America

Denver police arrested three teenagers on suspicion of murder after a 19-year-old man was found shot dead in a vehicle, allegedly lured to a specific location (KDVR Fox31). The arrest underscores Colorado's ongoing struggle with violent crime in urban centers.

Meanwhile, federal immigration enforcement failures continue to endanger Americans nationwide. A Salvadoran national was arrested in Virginia after a mother was found fatally stabbed near a jogging trail in Great Falls—another preventable tragedy tied to border security breakdown (The Daily Wire, Daily Caller).

**The conservative argument is clear:** Soft-on-crime policies and lax immigration enforcement create conditions where violent criminals—whether homegrown or foreign nationals—operate with impunity. Colorado Democrats have pushed bail reform, reduced penalties, and sanctuary policies that critics say prioritize criminal defendants over public safety. Meanwhile, the Biden-Harris administration's open-border approach has allowed known criminal aliens to remain in the country.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Colorado needs tougher prosecution, meaningful bail conditions, and cooperation with federal immigration authorities—not more social programs or criminal-justice "reform" that leaves law-abiding citizens vulnerable.
