# New York's Soft-on-Crime Experiment: A Warning for Colorado

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has dismantled the NYPD's Strategic Response Group—the elite counterterrorism unit created in 2015 to handle terrorist attacks, violent protests, and civil unrest—signaling that progressive ideology trumps public safety (Washington Examiner). Florida's Sen. Ashley Moody is already recruiting the displaced officers, recognizing talent when she sees it.

This move exemplifies the left's war on law enforcement. By gutting specialized riot-response capability, Mamdani has chosen fealty to far-left activists over the safety of New Yorkers—a calculation that should alarm Colorado voters watching similar defund-the-police rhetoric gain traction in Denver and Boulder.

Colorado conservatives should note the pattern: progressive mayors and city councils nationwide are dismantling police units under the guise of "reimagining public safety." The result? Emboldened criminals, weakened response to civil unrest, and officers fleeing to states that back the badge. Colorado's law enforcement recruitment is already competitive; if Denver's leadership follows New York's playbook, we'll lose experienced cops to states that value their work.

**Watch whether Denver's progressive city council moves to restrict or eliminate specialized police units—and whether Colorado's Republican legislature will preempt such moves with state-level protections for law enforcement.**
