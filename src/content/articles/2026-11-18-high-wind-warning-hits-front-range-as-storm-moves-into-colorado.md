# Front Range Braces for High Wind Warning as Storm System Moves In

Colorado's Front Range is about to get hammered with dangerous winds and mountain snow, with the National Weather Service issuing a High Wind Warning for the mountains and foothills ahead of an approaching storm system.

The system will bring strong winds Tuesday night into Wednesday, with light snow expected in higher elevations. Denver and the metro area will experience the brunt of the wind event, while mountain communities should prepare for accumulating snow and hazardous conditions.

The timing is critical for Colorado's fire season recovery. High winds in fall and spring create dangerous fire conditions across the state, and this warning underscores why water management and forest health remain urgent priorities for Colorado communities and ranchers dependent on reliable weather patterns.

Coloradans should secure loose outdoor items, monitor road conditions in mountain passes, and prepare for potential power outages in exposed areas. The storm should clear by Thursday.
