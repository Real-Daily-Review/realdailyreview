# Washington Honors Lindsey Graham as Senate Prepares Final Vote on His Russia Sanctions Bill

Senator Lindsey Graham's funeral services unfolded Tuesday at the National Cathedral in Washington, D.C., as world leaders and colleagues gathered to pay respects to the longtime South Carolina Republican. Vice President JD Vance eulogized Graham in the Capitol Rotunda, calling him "a true American original" and a great statesman who "deeply loved his fellow Americans" (The Daily Wire).

The timing proved symbolic: hours after Graham's funeral, the Senate prepared for a key vote on his Russia sanctions legislation—a final legislative mission the senator had championed before his unexpected death earlier this month. Graham's sister, Darline Graham, succeeded him in the Senate and is running for a full term.

The Air Force has initiated the process to rename Joint Base Charleston in South Carolina after Graham, according to an internal memo from Secretary of the Air Force Troy Meink (The Daily Wire). The renaming represents an unusual honor for a sitting senator's legacy.

Graham's death marks the loss of a prominent Republican voice on foreign policy and military affairs—positions that shaped defense and national security debates for decades.

Watch for confirmation of Darline Graham's Senate seat in the 2026 midterms and whether his Russia sanctions bill passes the chamber this week.
