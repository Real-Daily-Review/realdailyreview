# Federal Court Wins One for Second Amendment: DOJ Forces LA County to Stop Burying Concealed Carry Permits

The Department of Justice has secured a settlement against the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department over systematic delays in issuing concealed carry permits—a victory for Second Amendment rights that highlights how bureaucratic obstruction can function as a de facto gun ban.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, the DOJ sued LASD in September 2025, alleging the department denied citizens their constitutional right to bear arms "not through outright refusal, but through" excessive delays. The settlement forces the department to process permits more efficiently and respect statutory timelines.

This case underscores a conservative argument: progressive jurisdictions often use administrative delay as a workaround to circumvent Second Amendment protections when outright bans face legal challenge. Rather than deny permits directly—risking immediate court intervention—they simply bury applications in bureaucratic limbo, effectively disarming law-abiding citizens.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's sheriffs maintain relatively permissive concealed carry policies compared to coastal progressive strongholds, but this federal precedent strengthens legal arguments against any future attempts by Democratic-controlled counties to weaponize permit delays. It also signals the Trump DOJ will aggressively defend gun rights.
