# Democrats Flip Script on Trans Issue as GOP Questions Authenticity

Democrats are suddenly reversing course on transgender policy ahead of the midterms, with South Carolina Democratic Senate nominee Annie Andrews now opposing biological males in women's sports—a stark shift from her 2022 positions. Republicans argue the reversal exposes the left's willingness to abandon core progressive commitments for electoral gain (Fox News Politics).

The move reflects broader Democratic anxiety over Hispanic voter retention. Republicans made historic gains with Hispanic voters in 2024, and Democrats are now aggressively courting that bloc back, according to the Washington Examiner. The question looming over the midterm cycle: Can Republicans hold ground they've already won, or will Democratic messaging—including repositioning on cultural issues—peel away support?

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. The state's Hispanic population is growing, and both parties are competing hard for those votes in statewide races and congressional districts. Democrats' sudden moderation on transgender issues in sports signals they know the old messaging failed with working-class and Hispanic voters who prioritize kitchen-table economics and traditional values.

Republicans argue Democrats are simply flip-flopping to win elections rather than standing on principle—a credibility problem that could haunt them if they regain power and revert to progressive orthodoxy.
