# Aurora ICE Facility Employee Charged in Shooting of Protester

An Immigration and Customs Enforcement detention facility employee in Aurora faces charges after shooting a protester in the foot during a demonstration, according to an affidavit obtained by CPR News. Brandon Booth, who worked at the GEO Group-operated ICE facility, allegedly told investigators he "did some dumb sh*t" following the incident involving Emma Landis.

The shooting underscores the volatile environment surrounding immigration detention operations in Colorado—a flashpoint where progressive activism, federal enforcement, and public safety collide. Aurora's GEO facility has long drawn protests from immigration advocates demanding its closure, while conservatives argue that secure detention is essential to immigration enforcement and public order.

Republicans and law enforcement advocates contend that federal immigration detention serves a legitimate function in processing and holding individuals pending deportation or legal proceedings. They argue that disrupting these operations—or the security personnel managing them—undermines border security and rule of law.

Progressives counter that private detention facilities like GEO's Aurora operation prioritize profit over humane conditions and demand their closure as part of broader immigration reform.

The incident raises questions about security protocols at Colorado's ICE facilities and whether federal detention operations can function safely amid sustained activist pressure. Watch whether this case prompts legislative scrutiny of private detention contracts or renewed calls from the left to shutter the Aurora facility entirely.
