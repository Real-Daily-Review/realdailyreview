# Pentagon's AI Blunder Nearly Sparked China Confrontation

The Pentagon's reckless faith in artificial intelligence nearly triggered a military confrontation with China—a stark warning about automating critical national security decisions without human oversight.

According to CNN reporting cited by the *Washington Examiner*, American military aircraft were already airborne and armed personnel were preparing to board a Chinese vessel when more experienced analysts reopened the intelligence file and discovered the operation's central claim was false. A military special operations command analyst had relied on AI analysis that proved unreliable when subjected to proper human review.

This incident exposes a dangerous trend: government agencies deploying unvetted AI systems to drive consequential decisions while bypassing seasoned expertise. The Pentagon's blind spot isn't artificial intelligence itself—it's the institutional failure to maintain human verification checkpoints before committing military assets to potentially catastrophic operations.

Conservatives have long warned against government overreach and bureaucratic incompetence. This episode proves that adding AI to the mix without rigorous safeguards doesn't solve institutional problems; it accelerates them. When lives, national security, and international relations hang in the balance, no algorithm should replace experienced human judgment and accountability.

Coloradans should watch whether Congress demands transparency into which federal agencies are deploying AI without adequate human oversight—and whether the Pentagon implements mandatory verification protocols before AI-driven intelligence reaches operational commanders.
