# Colorado Must Reject Socialist Overreach as America Marks 250 Years of Freedom

Colorado conservatives face a stark choice: embrace the free-market principles that built America, or slide toward the socialist experiments gaining traction among the nation's youth.

As the country marks its 250th anniversary, National Review warns that today's Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) understand economics no better than their Cold War predecessors—yet their influence in progressive circles continues to grow. The left's push for expanded government control, higher taxes, and regulatory expansion threatens the individual liberty and limited government that made America exceptional.

For Colorado specifically, this matters enormously. The state's energy sector—oil, gas, and increasingly nuclear power—depends on free-market development and reasonable regulation, not socialist mandates that would cripple production and kill jobs. The Daily Caller notes that nuclear energy represents America's energy future, but only if government gets out of the way and lets private industry innovate.

Republicans argue that Colorado's prosperity flows from entrepreneurship, property rights, and low-tax policies—not government planning. Democrats counter with calls for "green energy transitions" and wealth redistribution that would devastate rural Colorado economies and working families.

Coloradans should watch whether state Democrats embrace or reject DSA-style policies in the next legislative session—particularly on energy, taxes, and land use.
