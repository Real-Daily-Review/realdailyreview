# Trump Preps for U.N. Assembly While Bilateral Talks Heat Up

Trump is heading to the United Nations General Assembly this week after meeting with French President Macron at Trump Tower to discuss the wars in Iran and Ukraine, according to the New York Post. The president debuted a new White House helipad ahead of the trip, using Marine One to depart for the assembly, *Just The News* reports.

The U.N. General Assembly's high-level debate begins Tuesday—a platform where global leaders can address the world stage on their terms, *Breitbart* notes. The gathering comes as Trump continues bilateral diplomacy on two major foreign conflicts.

**The Colorado angle:** While foreign policy rarely dominates state politics, Colorado voters—especially those with military ties or energy sector interests—watch how administrations handle Ukraine and Middle East stability. Energy markets and defense contracting tied to these regions can ripple through Colorado's economy.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Trump uses the U.N. platform to challenge the institution's bloat and anti-American bias, or if the assembly becomes another venue for progressive grandstanding on climate and "global governance."
