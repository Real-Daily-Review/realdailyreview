# Trump's Iran Deal Draws Fire From Right-Wing Hawks Over Vance's Messaging

President Trump's new Iran memorandum of understanding is already dividing conservatives, with hawkish Republicans questioning Vice President JD Vance's defense of the agreement against claims it mirrors Obama's failed JCPOA approach.

Vance has pushed back hard, arguing the Trump deal reverses Obama-era logic by flipping the carrot-and-stick dynamic (Fox News). Trump himself doubled down at the G7 summit, calling the JCPOA "a catastrophe" and claiming he "finished the job" that Netanyahu couldn't complete under the previous administration (Fox News).

But the Washington Examiner's opinion section flagged a messaging problem: Trump's unpredictable PR strategy is leaving Vance exposed as the public face of the Iran deal, even as the administration's own senior officials are still clarifying the memorandum's terms to reporters. The lack of unified messaging undercuts confidence in the agreement's details and rationale.
