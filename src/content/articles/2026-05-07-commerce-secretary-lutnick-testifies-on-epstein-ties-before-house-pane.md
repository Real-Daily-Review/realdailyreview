# Commerce Secretary Lutnick Testifies on Epstein Ties; Democrats Demand Answers

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick testified Wednesday before the House Oversight Committee in a closed-door, transcribed interview about his relationship with convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein.

The nearly four-hour session drew sharp partisan divides. Democrats argued Lutnick's claimed lack of knowledge about Epstein's crimes constituted an "egregious cover-up," according to The Hill. Rep. James Comer told reporters that Lutnick had not been "100% truthful" about visiting Epstein's private island, per PBS NewsHour.

Democratic Rep. Ro Khanna went further, telling reporters that President Trump would fire Lutnick if he reviewed the testimony, according to The Hill.

Republicans offered a contrasting assessment. The Washington Examiner reported that GOP members said Lutnick was "forthcoming" during the interview.

The investigation centers on Lutnick's past statements about his connection to Epstein and visits to the financier's island property. The exact contents of Wednesday's testimony remain sealed, with only lawmakers and staff present during the closed-door session.

Watch for whether Republicans and Democrats release portions of the transcript and whether the committee pursues additional testimony or enforcement actions.
