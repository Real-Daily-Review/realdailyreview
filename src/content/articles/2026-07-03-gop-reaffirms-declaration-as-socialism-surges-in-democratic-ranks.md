# Colorado Republican Pushes Declaration of Independence Reaffirmation as Socialism Gains Left-Wing Ground

As America marks 250 years since the Declaration of Independence, Rep. Matt Van Epps is introducing legislation to reaffirm the nation's founding document—a direct response to socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primaries, according to Fox News Politics.

Van Epps's move reflects conservative alarm over the left's drift toward anti-capitalist ideology. Republicans argue that the Declaration's core principles—individual liberty, limited government, and economic freedom—stand in direct opposition to the socialist agenda now mainstream in progressive politics.

Breitbart notes the Founders' original economic grievances remain strikingly relevant: control over taxation, immigration policy, trade, and restraining government growth. These same battles define today's conservative movement, particularly in Colorado, where taxpayers face relentless pressure from state Democrats to expand spending and regulation.

The 250th anniversary celebrations—from the Freedom Train touring historic landmarks to scholarly visits to Jefferson's Monticello—offer conservatives a cultural moment to remind Americans of the anti-government principles embedded in our founding.

**Watch for:** Whether Van Epps's Declaration reaffirmation bill gains co-sponsors among Colorado's Republican delegation and whether it becomes a rallying point against progressive overreach heading into 2026.
