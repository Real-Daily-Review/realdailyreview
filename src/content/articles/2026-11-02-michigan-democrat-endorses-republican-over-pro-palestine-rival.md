# Michigan Democrat's GOP Endorsement Exposes Deep Party Fracture Over Israel

A sitting Democratic congresswoman just endorsed a Republican for Senate—a stunning rebuke that reveals how badly the left's anti-Israel wing has alienated even its own. Rep. Haley Stevens (D-MI) backed Republican Mike Rogers over Democrat Abdul El-Sayed, citing El-Sayed's escalating hostility toward Israel, according to National Review and The Daily Wire.

Stevens told CNN that El-Sayed has been getting "angrier and angrier," suggesting his rhetoric crossed a line she couldn't ignore. National Review reports that Stevens is far from alone—many Democrats harbor serious reservations about El-Sayed's positions but have stayed silent until now.

The move carries real political consequences. El-Sayed raised $1.2 million after Stevens's endorsement, per the Daily Caller, signaling that the progressive base is energized by anti-establishment defiance. But Stevens's willingness to publicly back a Republican underscores a fracture in Democratic ranks: establishment moderates versus a vocal progressive faction increasingly defined by hostility to Israel.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. It shows how identity politics and single-issue litmus tests are splintering the Democratic coalition. As the left doubles down on divisive cultural battles, Colorado Republicans should capitalize on moderate Democrats' growing discomfort with their party's direction.

Watch whether other Democratic incumbents follow Stevens's lead—or whether party pressure forces them back in line.
