# GOP Lawmakers Demand Federal Probe Into Planned Parenthood's Taxpayer Funding

A coalition of 128 Republican lawmakers is pressing the HHS Inspector General to investigate whether Planned Parenthood has misused federal funding, citing concerns about potential fraud or abuse (Fox News Politics).

The push comes as conservatives intensify scrutiny of taxpayer dollars flowing to the nation's largest abortion provider. According to the Washington Examiner, the organization performs over 50 abortions for every prenatal visit—a ratio Republicans argue demonstrates misaligned priorities for an entity receiving federal support.

**The conservative argument:** GOP lawmakers contend that American taxpayers shouldn't subsidize an organization whose primary business is abortion. They point to the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which imposed a one-year defunding of Planned Parenthood effective July 4, 2025, as evidence that Congress can act decisively to redirect federal resources.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** Colorado taxpayers fund federal programs that support Planned Parenthood clinics operating statewide. A successful federal probe could reshape how federal dollars flow to reproductive health providers nationwide, affecting Colorado's healthcare landscape and state-level policy debates.

Republicans argue the defunding period provides a critical window to demonstrate that other providers can deliver prenatal care and family planning services without the abortion focus.

Watch whether the HHS Inspector General's office launches a formal investigation and what findings emerge during the 12-month defunding window.
