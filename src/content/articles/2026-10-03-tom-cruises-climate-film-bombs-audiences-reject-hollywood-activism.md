# Tom Cruise's Climate Sermon Bombs at Box Office—Audiences Reject Hollywood Preaching

National Review calls it a "climate hoax," while The Daily Wire notes the film saddles Cruise with "a belief system even crazier than Scientology"—a pointed jab at how far the actor has strayed from apolitical star power.
