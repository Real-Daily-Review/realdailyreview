# DOJ Probes Texas Democratic PAC Over Alleged Vote-Buying Scheme

A Trump Department of Justice is investigating Texas Majority PAC after an undercover video caught a fired organizing manager describing what appeared to be a "Paid Relational Program"—raising serious questions about whether Democrats are literally buying votes.

In the viral footage, Sky MacAdams, a since-terminated organizer for the Democratic group, suggested the PAC was "paying people" for votes. MacAdams himself acknowledged the optics were damning: "If I was a Republican, I would be pissed. I would be like, that's shady as f–k," according to the New York Post.

Republicans argue this is exactly the kind of voter manipulation and potential election fraud that demands federal scrutiny. The investigation signals the Trump administration's willingness to pursue allegations of Democratic misconduct that legacy media outlets have largely ignored.

Democrats have not yet offered a detailed public response to the allegations, though the PAC's swift termination of MacAdams suggests damage control.
