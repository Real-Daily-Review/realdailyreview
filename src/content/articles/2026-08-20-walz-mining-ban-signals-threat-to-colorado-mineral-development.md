# Colorado Should Watch Mining Permit Fight as Biden Admin Blocks Key Mineral Development

Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz's executive order blocking mining permits near the Boundary Waters has ignited a national security debate with direct implications for Colorado's energy and mineral sectors.

Republicans argue the move cripples domestic production of critical minerals needed for defense and technology, handing advantage to China. Rep. Tom Emmer (R-MN) called the decision "stupid," warning that blocking U.S. mining forces reliance on hostile foreign sources (Fox News). Conservatives contend that environmental restrictions on domestic extraction—whether in Minnesota, Colorado, or elsewhere—undermine energy independence and national security while enriching adversaries.

Democrats frame such restrictions as environmental protection, but the GOP counterargument is straightforward: America cannot afford to outsource mineral production to countries with no environmental or labor standards while simultaneously claiming to support green energy, which requires massive quantities of copper, lithium, and rare earths.

For Colorado, this matters enormously. The state sits atop molybdenum, uranium, and other critical minerals. If the Biden-Harris administration continues blocking or delaying permits on federal lands—a pattern conservatives say is already happening—Colorado mining jobs and tax revenue face real pressure. Meanwhile, China expands its mineral monopoly unchecked.

**Watch whether the incoming Trump administration reverses these permit blocks and whether Colorado Republicans push for streamlined mining approval on state and federal lands.**
