# Record Fourth of July Travel Expected as Americans Hit the Road

Colorado families planning Independence Day getaways will be part of a historic travel surge: AAA projects a record 72.2 million Americans will travel for the Fourth, with road trips dominating the exodus (9News Denver). The TSA expects to screen nearly 19 million air travelers between June 30 and July 6 alone (KDVR Fox31).

Road travel remains the preferred option for most Americans, though cruises, trains, and buses are seeing the fastest growth this holiday week. The surge reflects continued consumer confidence and spending power—a bright spot for Colorado's tourism-dependent economy, which relies heavily on summer travel to mountain destinations, national parks, and Front Range attractions.

For Colorado conservatives watching government overreach, the silver lining is clear: private enterprise—airlines, rental car companies, hotels, and restaurants—is handling this record demand without federal micromanagement. No government stimulus required; Americans are simply choosing to spend their own money on freedom and family time.

The flip side: expect crowded highways, packed airports, and potential delays. Gas prices and travel costs remain elevated, which will pinch middle-class families most.

Watch whether Colorado tourism boards and businesses capitalize on this surge to push back against over-regulation that threatens hospitality and outdoor recreation sectors.
