# Trump's Iran Conflict Threatens to Overshadow Economic Agenda Ahead of Midterms

President Trump is doubling down on military action against Iran even as the five-month conflict threatens to derail his core economic message before November's midterm elections. Trump vowed the U.S. will "win" and predicted Iranian resistance will "peter out," but acknowledged the fighting could drag on through the campaign season (Fox News).

The problem for Republicans: the prolonged conflict is consuming political oxygen. National Review argues Trump has "forfeited his signature issue of economic stewardship" by pursuing both tariffs and sustained Iran operations simultaneously, risking voter focus on kitchen-table issues that typically favor the party in power.

For Colorado conservatives, the stakes are clear. Extended military engagement abroad means sustained defense spending and potential pressure on federal budgets—complicating GOP arguments for fiscal restraint and tax relief. Energy markets remain volatile, affecting Colorado's oil and gas sector. Meanwhile, the midterm messaging window is closing: Republicans need to pivot hard to inflation, border security, and economic growth if they hope to hold Congress.

**Watch whether Trump recalibrates messaging to emphasize domestic wins—or whether the Iran conflict continues to dominate headlines through Election Day.**
