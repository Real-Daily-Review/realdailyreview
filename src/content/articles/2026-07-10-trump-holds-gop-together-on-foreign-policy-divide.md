# Trump's Grip on GOP Tightens as Party Wrestles with Foreign Policy Divide

Donald Trump has solidified his hold on the Republican Party by bridging a fundamental schism over America's role abroad—a fracture that could determine the GOP's direction for years to come.

The party faces a sharp divide between foreign policy hawks who favor confronting adversaries militarily and restrainers who want the U.S. out of endless wars (The Daily Wire). Trump has emerged as the unifying force holding these factions together, according to analysis, even as he continues reshaping the party in his image with what observers call a "unique brand of religious, conservative populism" (Denver Post).

Political scientist Seth Masket notes that while Trump didn't originate the populist movement within the GOP, "he certainly added his own flavor to it." This consolidation matters for Colorado Republicans, who will face pressure to align with Trump's foreign policy stance—whether restraint or selective confrontation—as primary season approaches.

**For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers:** Watch whether Trump's unifying role holds if the party fractures over specific military interventions or defense spending priorities, which could reshape Colorado's defense-industry footprint and military installations.
