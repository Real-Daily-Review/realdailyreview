# Toronto Festival Shooting Kills 2, Injures 4; Gunfire Exchange Between Targeted Individuals

A shooting at Toronto's Salsa on St. Clair Latin music festival Saturday evening left two dead and four wounded, police said. According to Toronto Police Operations, officers responded at 8:12 p.m. to reports of an active shooter at St. Clair and West Arlington Avenues (9News Denver, Washington Examiner). 

Police determined the initial active-shooter warning stemmed from an exchange of gunfire between two individuals targeting each other, rather than a random mass shooting (9News Denver). The incident occurred during a crowded public gathering, underscoring how quickly violence can erupt in urban centers even in neighboring countries with strict gun control laws.
