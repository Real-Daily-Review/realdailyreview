# Trump Warns GOP: Don't Let Democrats Kill Filibuster First

Former President Donald Trump is sounding the alarm that Republicans must act on the filibuster before Democrats do—or risk permanent Democratic control of the Senate.

Trump's warning comes after Michigan Democrat Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed won his party's Senate primary this week. According to *Just The News* and *Breitbart*, Trump characterized El-Sayed as a "Communist Dumocrat" who also supports ending the 60-vote filibuster rule. Trump argues that if Democrats eliminate the filibuster first, Republicans will never regain meaningful power in the chamber, potentially making him "the last Republican President."

**The conservative case:** Republicans contend the filibuster is a critical check on one-party rule. If Democrats abolish it while holding the Senate majority, they could ram through radical legislation—from gun confiscation to energy mandates—without Republican input. Trump's position: Republicans should preempt that move.

**Democrats counter** that the filibuster is an obstruction tool that blocks popular legislation and judicial confirmations.

For Colorado, this matters. A filibuster-free Senate could fast-track federal gun bans, aggressive climate regulations, and water-rights overreach that would hit energy development and ranching hard.

**Watch:** Whether Senate Republicans actually move on filibuster reform—or whether Trump's warning becomes just another campaign talking point.
