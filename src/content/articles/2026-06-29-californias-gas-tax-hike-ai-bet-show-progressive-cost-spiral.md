# California's Gas Tax Hike Hits Pump Prices as Newsom Doubles Down on AI Spending

California's government just made driving more expensive while betting state resources on unproven technology—a cautionary tale for Colorado policymakers watching their own energy and tech agendas.

Gov. Gavin Newsom is facing GOP pushback as California's gas tax rises 2.2 cents per gallon this week, adding to pump prices already burdened by state policy (Fox News). Republicans, led by Rep. David Valadao, are urging Newsom to suspend the hike, but the Democratic governor is moving in the opposite direction: he's now signing a major deal with San Francisco-based Anthropic to deploy the company's Claude AI platform across California state agencies and local governments (Washington Examiner).

The moves reflect a broader pattern: progressive governments raising costs on working families while spending on experimental initiatives. Meanwhile, a Federal Reserve working paper found that unauthorized immigration during the Biden administration drove up home prices by 2.2% and rents by 1.4%—compounding affordability crises (Fox News).

**The Colorado angle:** As Colorado lawmakers consider their own energy and tech policies, Newsom's playbook offers a warning. Higher gas taxes and regulatory costs hit energy workers and rural communities hardest. Coloradans should watch whether state officials pursue similar AI spending commitments or energy taxes that would follow California's lead.
