# Colorado Holiday Markets Boom as Local Shoppers Reject Big-Box Retail

Colorado communities are doubling down on local holiday markets this season, giving shoppers a genuine alternative to corporate chain stores and Amazon's stranglehold on retail. Cities across the state—including Arvada—are rolling out expanded lineups of Christmas markets, tree lightings, and community events designed to keep holiday spending in local hands.

Arvada's slate includes multiple holiday markets and festive gatherings throughout the season, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver. CPR News reports that Colorado is hosting some of the biggest and most unique holiday markets in years, offering everything from artisan goods to locally made crafts.

For conservatives and Colorado taxpayers, this is a win: local markets strengthen small businesses, reduce dependence on corporate monopolies, and keep money circulating in communities rather than flowing to Silicon Valley billionaires. These events also celebrate genuine community gathering—something increasingly rare in our digital age.

The trend reflects what many Coloradans already know: supporting local isn't just good economics; it's good citizenship. When families shop at these markets, they're voting with their wallets for the kind of Colorado they want to live in.

Watch whether local governments continue removing regulatory barriers that make it easier for small vendors to participate in these markets without crushing permit fees and red tape.
