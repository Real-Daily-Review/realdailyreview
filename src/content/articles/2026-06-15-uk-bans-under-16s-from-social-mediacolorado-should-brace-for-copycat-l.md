# UK Moves to Ban Under-16s From Social Media—What Colorado Should Learn

Britain's government just did what American progressives keep threatening: a blanket ban on social media for minors. UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced Monday that Parliament will vote on legislation before Christmas to prohibit children under 16 from accessing TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, and similar platforms (9News Denver, The Daily Wire).

The move mirrors Australia's recent crackdown and signals a global trend toward heavy-handed tech regulation that Colorado lawmakers should watch carefully. While protecting kids from social media's documented harms—addiction, mental health damage, predatory content—is legitimate, conservatives worry about the precedent: government deciding which legal services citizens can access.

Republicans argue such bans, however well-intentioned, represent creeping state control over personal choice and parental authority. If Washington or Denver follows Britain's lead, expect similar legislation to land in Colorado's legislature within two years, likely framed as "child protection" while expanding government's regulatory footprint over tech companies and family decisions.

The real question: Should parents and market competition solve this problem, or should government mandate it? Colorado conservatives should prepare for that debate now—because it's coming.

**Watch for:** Colorado Democrats introducing copycat social media restriction bills in the 2025 legislative session.
