# Georgia Senate Candidate Fires Staffer Over Inflammatory Social Media Post

Rep. Mike Collins (R-GA) fired a campaign staffer Friday after they posted inflammatory content on X targeting an adviser to rival Derek Dooley's supporting PAC, according to The Hill. The post alluded to accusations against former "Today" anchor Matt Lauer involving the adviser's wife, The Hill reported.

Collins called the post "despicable and unauthorized," according to the Washington Examiner. The staffer had posted from the Mike Collins War Room account in response to polling data shared by Luke Thompson, a Dooley adviser, showing Dooley ahead in the Republican runoff primary matchup.

Collins's campaign apologized for the content, The Hill reported. The incident occurred as the two candidates compete in what appears to be a closely contested primary race.

The firing underscores tensions in the Georgia Republican primary and the campaign's effort to distance itself from the controversial post. Watch for whether the incident affects polling or donor dynamics in the runoff race.
