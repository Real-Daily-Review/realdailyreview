# Sanctuary Policies Under Fire as Illegal Immigrant Crimes Surge Into National Spotlight

Colorado and other states should brace for the immigration enforcement debate to intensify after a string of violent crimes allegedly committed by illegal immigrants has reignited Republican calls to dismantle sanctuary city policies.

A 24-year-old illegal immigrant from Honduras stands accused of breaking into a Polk County, Florida home and sexually assaulting a 77-year-old woman (Fox News, Daily Wire). In New Jersey, an illegal immigrant allegedly struck a family in a grocery store parking lot, killing a nine-month-old child and reigniting debate over the state's sanctuary protections (Fox News). At a Capitol Hill hearing, Marcus Coleman—whose daughter was permanently disabled in a crash involving an illegal immigrant—rejected Democratic sympathy, calling her injuries "the end result" of sanctuary city policies.

Republicans argue that sanctuary jurisdictions—which limit cooperation with federal immigration enforcement—create dangerous gaps in public safety and enable preventable crimes. Democrats counter that sanctuary policies protect vulnerable immigrant communities and that violent crime rates among immigrants remain statistically lower than native-born populations.

Colorado has its own sanctuary provisions in state law, and Denver maintains sanctuary city status. These cases will likely fuel GOP pressure on Democratic Gov. Jared Polis to tighten immigration enforcement cooperation—a politically charged issue in a swing state heading into 2024.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans introduce legislation to restrict sanctuary protections or demand greater ICE cooperation.
