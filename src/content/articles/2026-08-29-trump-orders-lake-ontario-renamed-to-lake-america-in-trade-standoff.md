# Trump Orders Federal Rename of Lake Ontario to Lake America

President Trump has signed an executive order directing the federal government to rename Lake Ontario to Lake America, escalating tensions with Canada amid a broader trade dispute.

The move, announced Thursday, represents an unconventional negotiating tactic as trade discussions with Ottawa have stalled. Illinois Governor J.B. Pritzker, a Democrat, mocked the decision, according to *Just The News*, though the full scope of his criticism remains unclear from available reporting.

**The conservative case:** Trump supporters argue the administration is using all available levers—including symbolic ones—to pressure Canada on trade terms favorable to American workers and businesses. Rebranding a Great Lake signals resolve and dominance in negotiations where traditional diplomacy has faltered.

**Democrats counter:** The move appears performative and potentially damaging to U.S.-Canada relations, which affect cross-border commerce, energy flows, and security cooperation.

**Colorado angle:** While Colorado has no direct stake in Lake Ontario, the broader U.S.-Canada trade dispute carries weight for Colorado energy producers, agricultural exporters, and manufacturers dependent on cross-border supply chains. Any escalation in trade tensions could ripple through the state's economy.

Watch whether this executive order triggers legal challenges or congressional pushback—and whether it actually moves the needle on trade negotiations or remains symbolic theater.
