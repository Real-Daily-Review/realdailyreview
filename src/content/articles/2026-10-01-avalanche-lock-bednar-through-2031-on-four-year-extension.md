# Colorado Avalanche Lock in Coach Bednar Through 2031—Good News for Fans, Not Taxpayers' Wallets

The Colorado Avalanche have secured head coach Jared Bednar with a four-year extension through the 2030-31 season, according to CPR News and KDVR Fox31 Denver. Bednar, who holds the franchise record for most wins, will remain behind the bench as the team opens its season.

The move signals organizational stability and confidence in Bednar's leadership. Fans at the practice facility are energized by the news, which arrives just as the season kicks off.

**The conservative angle:** While this is good news for Avalanche Nation, Colorado taxpayers should note that the team plays at Ball Arena—a venue built with significant public financing. Any long-term coaching commitment affects the franchise's payroll structure and, indirectly, the economics of a publicly-supported facility. Bednar's extension likely means higher coaching costs absorbed by ownership, which could influence future negotiations over arena operations or public subsidies.

The Avalanche organization is making a private-sector decision about its own money, which is appropriate. The real question for taxpayers: what are the terms of Colorado's ongoing financial relationship with this team, and are they getting a fair return on public investment?

Watch for any future discussions about arena funding, lease renewals, or public subsidies tied to the team's performance or payroll commitments.
