# Colorado Democrat Neguse Pursues House Leadership Post, Unopposed

Rep. Joe Neguse is running unopposed to become assistant speaker—the No. 4 leadership position for House Democrats—a move that would make him the youngest lawmaker ever to hold the role, according to the Colorado Sun and CPR News.

The Boulder-area congressman's ascent reflects Democrats' confidence in his political machinery and messaging ability. If elected, Neguse would solidify Colorado's outsized influence in Democratic caucus leadership, continuing a pattern of the state punching above its weight in the minority party's internal power structure.

Republicans have not mounted a public challenge to his bid, and no alternative candidate has emerged from within Democratic ranks. The position carries significant influence over floor strategy, messaging, and party discipline—tools Neguse would wield as Democrats navigate a Republican-controlled House.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, Neguse's elevation matters: he has consistently backed higher federal spending, climate regulations that threaten Colorado's energy sector, and gun-control measures that clash with Second Amendment rights across the state. His leadership role would amplify his influence over Democratic strategy on issues directly affecting Colorado's economy and freedoms.
