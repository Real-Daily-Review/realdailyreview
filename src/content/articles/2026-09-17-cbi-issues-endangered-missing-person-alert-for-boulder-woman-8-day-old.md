# Colorado Bureau of Investigation Issues Endangered Missing Person Alert for Boulder Woman, Infant

A 24-year-old Boulder woman and her 8-day-old newborn have been reported missing and endangered, triggering a statewide alert from the Colorado Bureau of Investigation.

According to 9News Denver, Kylie Rucker was last seen Tuesday afternoon in Boulder. Authorities believe she is with 35-year-old Julio Cardona III and their infant. KDVR Fox31 Denver reports the CBI has classified the case as an endangered missing person alert, elevating the urgency of the search.

The specifics surrounding the disappearance remain limited in initial reports. Boulder Police and the CBI are actively investigating and coordinating the search effort.

Endangered missing person alerts are issued when authorities have reasonable cause to believe a missing person faces a credible threat to their safety—a critical distinction from standard missing person reports. Given the age of the child and the circumstances, law enforcement is treating this case with high priority.

Coloradans with information are urged to contact the Boulder Police Department or the Colorado Bureau of Investigation immediately.

Watch for updates on whether additional details emerge about the circumstances of the disappearance or the whereabouts of Rucker, Cardona, and the infant.
