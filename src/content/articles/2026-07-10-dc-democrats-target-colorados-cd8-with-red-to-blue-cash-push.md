# National Democrats Flood Cash Into Colorado's CD8, Signaling Desperation on House Control

Washington Democrats are doubling down on Colorado's 8th Congressional District, adding Democrat Manny Rutinel to the DCCC's "Red to Blue" program in a bid to flip the seat and shore up their House majority (Complete Colorado, CPR News). The move underscores how competitive—and crucial—CD8 has become in the national fight for congressional control.

Republicans argue the Democratic investment reveals panic: they're forced to spend heavily in a district that should lean Republican, diverting resources from safer Democratic seats. The seat remains genuinely competitive, with both parties treating it as a top-tier battleground.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. CD8 spans the state's eastern plains and exurban areas—ranching, energy, and agricultural country where limited government and property rights resonate. A Democratic flip would mean another vote for higher taxes, regulatory overreach on water and land use, and restrictions on energy development that Colorado's rural economy depends on.

Rutinel's inclusion in the DCCC's flagship program guarantees a flood of out-of-state money and coordinated messaging. Republicans will need to match that intensity and remind CD8 voters what Democratic control of Congress has meant: inflation, border chaos, and anti-energy zealotry.

Watch whether national Republican groups match Democratic spending in CD8 over the next election cycle.
