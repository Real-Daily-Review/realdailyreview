# Failed Trump Prosecutor Jack Smith Urges States to Sue Trump Over Elections

Jack Smith, the special counsel who failed to convict President Trump in two separate cases, is now urging state attorneys general to "initiate litigation" against Trump to "protect" the upcoming midterm elections, according to reporting from the Washington Examiner and Breitbart.

Smith's cases—one involving alleged classified document mishandling, another centered on Trump's 2020 election efforts—were both dismissed after Trump's 2024 victory. Rather than accept those defeats, Smith is now calling on Democratic state officials to become his litigation proxies, urging them to "be proactive" in court challenges ahead of the midterms.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** A failed prosecutor with a documented political vendetta is essentially asking Democratic AGs to weaponize state legal systems against a political opponent. This is precisely the kind of institutional abuse conservatives warned about during the Trump prosecutions—using the machinery of government to target political rivals rather than pursue genuine justice.

For Colorado specifically, this matters. Democratic Attorney General Phil Weiser could face pressure to join coordinated multi-state litigation against Trump, using Colorado taxpayer resources and the AG's office for what amounts to opposition research masquerading as law enforcement.

**Watch whether Colorado's AG joins any coordinated state litigation against Trump—and whether Republican lawmakers demand accountability for using state resources this way.**
