# Crowdfunding Campaign to Revive Spirit Airlines Surpasses $337 Million

A content creator has launched a crowdfunding effort to purchase defunct Spirit Airlines, attracting over $337 million in pledges, according to the Washington Examiner. Hunter Peterson, who gained attention last year by flying Spirit for 24 hours straight, initiated the campaign the same day the budget carrier shut down.

The collapse of Spirit Airlines may benefit larger competitors. According to The Hill, a YouGov analysis found that former Spirit customers rank United Airlines, Delta Air Lines, and American Airlines among their top alternative choices for future bookings.

Spirit Airlines ceased operations after years of financial struggles, leaving a gap in the ultra-low-cost carrier market. Peterson's grassroots campaign represents an unconventional attempt to fill that void, though the feasibility of reviving the airline through crowdfunding remains untested.

Watch whether the crowdfunding campaign gains regulatory approval and whether Peterson can convert pledges into actual capital for an airline acquisition.
