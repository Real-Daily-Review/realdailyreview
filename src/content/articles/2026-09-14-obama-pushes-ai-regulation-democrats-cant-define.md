# Democrats Push AI Regulation Without a Plan—Republicans Offer No Alternative

Former President Barack Obama is pressuring Democrats to make artificial intelligence regulation a centerpiece of their 2024 campaign agenda, yet offers no concrete policy framework to back it up, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The vagueness is telling: Democrats want to regulate AI but haven't decided how, leaving voters and businesses in limbo.

Republicans aren't faring much better. The GOP has articulated no coherent AI strategy either, ceding ground to Democrats on an issue that will shape American competitiveness, innovation, and economic growth for decades.

For Colorado, this matters. The state hosts growing tech hubs in Denver and Boulder, plus emerging AI applications in energy optimization, agriculture, and aerospace. Heavy-handed federal AI regulation—the likely Democratic approach—could stifle the startups and research institutions driving Colorado's economy forward. Conversely, a regulatory vacuum invites the kind of patchwork state-level rules that burden businesses.

Conservatives should be wary of Democrats' instinct to regulate first and ask questions later. AI innovation thrives under light-touch oversight, not bureaucratic micromanagement. Republicans need to develop a pro-growth AI framework that protects legitimate concerns without strangling the technology that could keep America—and Colorado—competitive.

Watch for whether Colorado's congressional delegation takes a principled stand on AI policy, or simply follows national party talking points.
