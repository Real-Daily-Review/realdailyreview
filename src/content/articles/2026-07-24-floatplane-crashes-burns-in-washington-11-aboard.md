# Floatplane Crash in Washington Kills Multiple; Safety Questions Loom

A single-engine floatplane carrying 11 people caught fire and sank Thursday evening near Sucia Island in Washington's San Juan Islands, according to 9News Denver and the New York Post. Photos show the aircraft completely engulfed in flames and torn apart, resting in shallow water near rocky shoreline. Emergency responders confirmed the crash occurred north of Seattle.
