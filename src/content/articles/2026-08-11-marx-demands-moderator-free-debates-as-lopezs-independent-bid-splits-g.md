# Colorado GOP Governor Race Fractures as Marx Demands Moderator-Free Debates

Republican Victor Marx is drawing a hard line on debate format—refusing to participate unless moderators are removed entirely—just as fellow conservative Greg Lopez's independent candidacy threatens to split the right-wing vote in Colorado's 2026 gubernatorial race.

Marx, a former Navy SEAL and political outsider, announced Marine veteran George Markert as his running mate, emphasizing both candidates' non-politician credentials (Denver Post). But his debate ultimatum signals confidence—or concern—about controlling the narrative without traditional media gatekeeping. CPR News reports Marx will only accept unmoderated forums, a stark departure from standard campaign practice.

The real problem for Republicans: Lopez, a former GOP congressman who's run for governor before, just qualified for the ballot as an independent (Denver Post). That move could fracture conservative turnout in a state Democrats have been steadily turning blue. With an anti-establishment Republican, a moderate independent, and presumably a Democrat nominee all competing, Colorado's gubernatorial race is shaping up as a three-way slugfest where the divided right hands victory to the left.

Conservatives should watch whether Marx's outsider appeal and debate strategy can consolidate GOP voters before Lopez siphons support—or whether Colorado Republicans end up splitting the difference and losing the governor's mansion.
