# Hawaii Supreme Court's Attack on SCOTUS Signals Judicial Activism Run Amok

Hawaii's state supreme court has launched a rhetorical assault on the U.S. Supreme Court that exposes how far progressive judges will go to undermine constitutional authority—and it could have ripple effects for Second Amendment cases nationwide.

According to National Review, the Hawaii justices' inflammatory language risks cementing the view that judging is "just politics by another name." Their attack on the federal bench signals a troubling willingness to delegitimize SCOTUS rather than accept unfavorable rulings.

The timing matters. As the Daily Caller reports, a case involving a law that criminalizes conduct protected under Second Amendment precedent could soon reach the Supreme Court. The case centers on an arrest during vehicle travel—precisely the kind of fact pattern that tests Hawaii's aggressive gun restrictions against constitutional limits.

**The Colorado angle:** If Hawaii's judiciary can openly attack SCOTUS and push boundaries on gun rights, Colorado's Democratic-controlled courts may follow suit. Colorado gun owners and Second Amendment advocates should brace for similar judicial activism in state courts, where progressive judges increasingly ignore federal constitutional constraints.

**Watch for:** Whether SCOTUS accepts the Hawaii gun case and how aggressively the Court responds to state judicial defiance of constitutional limits.
