# Fauci Diaries Expose Government Health Bureaucrat's Media Manipulation During COVID

Anthony Fauci's newly released diaries reveal a public health official more concerned with media celebrity than scientific integrity during the pandemic—a damning portrait that should alarm Coloradans skeptical of unaccountable federal bureaucrats.

The entries show Fauci suppressing dissenting scientific opinions and cultivating favorable press coverage, according to reporting from the *New York Post* and *National Review*. Former FDA Commissioner Scott Gottlieb, hardly a Fauci critic, called the diary entries "disappointing," citing evidence of the longtime NIAID director enjoying media notoriety while the nation grappled with lockdowns and economic devastation.

The *Washington Examiner* notes the diaries also expose how legacy journalists abandoned objectivity to become activists amplifying Fauci's messaging rather than scrutinizing his evolving guidance.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue these revelations vindicate critics who questioned Fauci's one-size-fits-all pandemic policies that devastated Colorado's small businesses, schools, and energy sector. The diaries suggest a federal health bureaucrat prioritized his own reputation over open scientific debate—precisely the kind of unaccountable government overreach conservatives warned about.

Colorado taxpayers funded this apparatus. Watch for legislative efforts to strengthen oversight of federal health agencies and prevent similar media-driven policymaking during future crises.
