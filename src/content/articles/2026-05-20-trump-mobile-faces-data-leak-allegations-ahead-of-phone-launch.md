# Trump Mobile Faces Data Leak Allegations as T1 Phone Nears Release

Trump Mobile is allegedly leaking customer personal information including email addresses and home addresses, according to security researchers who say the company has not responded to breach notifications.

YouTuber Coffeezilla first reported the apparent vulnerability on his secondary channel after being tipped off by an anonymous person who discovered insecure data storage on the Trump Mobile website, according to The Verge. Two YouTubers told TechCrunch they verified their own leaked data as authentic, though Trump Mobile has not publicly acknowledged the exposure or responded to alerts.

The timing is significant: the company's T1 Phone was expected to begin shipping last week, according to The Verge. The alleged leak has also revealed actual order numbers, which the outlet reports are substantially lower than figures that circulated on social media.

Neither source indicates Trump Mobile has issued a statement about the breach, the scope of affected customers, or remediation steps. The company has not disclosed whether it notified affected users or regulators.

Watch for whether Trump Mobile acknowledges the vulnerability, issues a public statement, and what regulatory scrutiny may follow.
