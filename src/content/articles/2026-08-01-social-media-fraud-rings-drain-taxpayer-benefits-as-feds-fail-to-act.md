# Social Media Fraud Rings Exploit Government Benefits While Biden Admin Looks the Other Way

A troubling new threat is draining taxpayer dollars: "fraudfluencers" are openly recruiting Americans into government benefit scams via social media, according to a stark warning from Department of Labor Inspector General Anthony D'Esposito (Fox News Politics).

These schemes exploit unemployment insurance, pandemic relief programs, and other federal benefits—money that ultimately comes from Colorado workers' pockets. The fraudsters use TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube to teach others how to file false claims, creating a coordinated criminal enterprise that operates in plain sight.

**What conservatives say:** This represents catastrophic government waste and a failure of Biden-era oversight. When federal agencies can't police their own programs, it's a damning indictment of bloated bureaucracy and lax enforcement. Republicans argue the solution isn't more spending on audits—it's shrinking these programs to their core mission and holding bad actors accountable.

**The left's response:** Democrats typically frame fraud as a minor problem relative to program size, emphasizing that most beneficiaries are legitimate. They often call for more funding for fraud detection rather than program cuts.

The scale remains unclear from available reporting, but the brazenness of social-media recruitment suggests enforcement has collapsed. Colorado taxpayers should watch whether the Trump administration actually prosecutes these "fraudfluencers" or if this becomes another forgotten scandal.
