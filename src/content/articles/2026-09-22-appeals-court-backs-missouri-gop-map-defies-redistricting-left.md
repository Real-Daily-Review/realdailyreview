# Federal Court Backs GOP-Friendly Missouri Map, Defying Redistricting Activists

A federal appeals court has revived Missouri's Republican-leaning congressional map—a 7-1 GOP advantage—despite months of legal challenges from Democrats and progressive redistricting groups. The map, passed by Missouri's GOP-controlled legislature in 2025 and used in state primaries, erases Democratic Rep. Emanuel Cleaver's Kansas City-based district (Just The News).

The ruling sets up another Supreme Court battle. Democrats argue the map violates fair representation principles; Republicans counter that legislatures have constitutional authority to draw districts and that the map reflects legitimate political geography. The Washington Examiner notes this is the latest in a series of reversals on the issue, with courts ping-ponging between competing legal theories.

**The Colorado angle:** While Missouri's fight doesn't directly affect Colorado redistricting, it signals how courts are increasingly deferring to state legislatures on map-drawing—a win for GOP-controlled statehouses nationwide. Colorado's own 2022 independent redistricting commission produced Democratic-friendly maps; conservatives have long argued that process stripped legislative authority.

The broader lesson: redistricting remains a high-stakes partisan battleground. Expect the Supreme Court to weigh in again, and watch whether this ruling emboldens Republican legislatures in other states to defend their maps more aggressively.
