# Colorado Fire Department Warns: Deep-Fried Turkey Can Burn Your House Down

North Metro Fire Rescue District put on a dramatic show this week—intentionally torching a turkey to drive home a dangerous holiday truth.

During a Tuesday demonstration, fire crews showed Coloradans exactly what goes wrong when deep-frying turkey indoors or without proper precautions. The wrong-way demo went up in flames, illustrating why turkey fryers rank among the fastest ways to start a house fire during Thanksgiving week (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

The lesson is straightforward: deep-fried turkey demands respect. Improper oil temperature, inadequate clearance from structures, and indoor cooking create a perfect storm for disaster. Fire departments across Colorado see preventable Thanksgiving fires every year—many tied to amateur frying operations that seemed like a good idea at kickoff.

The right way? Proper outdoor setup, thermometer discipline, and keeping the fryer away from your deck, siding, and anything flammable. It's not complicated, but it requires attention.

Colorado families planning holiday meals should heed the warning: a burnt turkey is a minor inconvenience. A burnt house is a catastrophe. Follow the fire department's guidance, or stick to the oven.

Watch whether Colorado fire departments see a measurable drop in Thanksgiving-week structure fires following this public education push.
