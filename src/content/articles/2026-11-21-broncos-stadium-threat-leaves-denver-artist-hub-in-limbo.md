# Denver's Prism Artist Studios Survives Broncos Stadium Squeeze—For Now

Denver's Prism Artist Studios dodged displacement when the Broncos' Burnham Yard stadium project threatened the warehouse creative hub, but uncertainty lingers a year after the property sale (9News Denver). Dozens of artists feared losing their studios as the NFL team advanced plans for its new facility, yet the art space remains operational—though the lingering threat has made recruiting new tenants harder.

The situation highlights a familiar tension in Denver: private development and major projects can upend established communities, even when they don't ultimately deliver the worst-case scenario. While the Broncos' stadium project represents significant private investment in the city, the collateral damage to small creative businesses underscores how large-scale development can create uncertainty for entrepreneurs and workers who lack the resources to weather prolonged limbo.

For now, Prism Artists Studios has breathing room. But without clear long-term security from the Broncos organization or city planners, the studio remains vulnerable—a reminder that Denver's growth agenda doesn't always account for the smaller players who make neighborhoods distinctive before the cranes arrive.
