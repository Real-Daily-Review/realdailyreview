# Trump Blames Ukraine War, Blue-State Regs for Gas Prices Ahead of Midterms

President Trump is attributing elevated gas prices to Ukraine's strikes on Russian oil refineries and restrictive energy policies in Democratic-led states—not Middle East tensions—as Republicans gear up for November midterm elections Democrats currently favor.

According to the Washington Examiner, Trump argued Monday that "record numbers of barrels" are flowing from the Strait of Hormuz, shifting blame away from Iran conflict toward Ukraine's bombing campaign and what he calls energy-killing regulations in blue states. Just The News reports Trump made the claim ahead of midterms when Democrats hold electoral advantage.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans contend that progressive states' restrictions on domestic oil production, refining capacity, and pipeline development have artificially constrained U.S. energy supply, driving prices higher. Trump's framing suggests Democratic energy policy—not global geopolitics alone—bears responsibility for consumer pain at the pump.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's oil and gas industry, already facing federal leasing restrictions and state-level pressure from Democratic lawmakers, stands to benefit if Republicans successfully argue that domestic energy development—not foreign wars—should drive U.S. energy policy. Higher gas prices directly hit Colorado families and small businesses.
