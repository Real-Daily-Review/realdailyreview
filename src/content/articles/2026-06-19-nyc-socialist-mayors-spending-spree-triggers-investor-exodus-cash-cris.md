# Socialist NYC Mayor's Spending Spree Spooks Investors—A Warning for Colorado

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's hard-left agenda is colliding with economic reality as investors flee and a looming cash shortage threatens financial crisis, according to the New York Post. Mamdani, who has allied with Sen. Bernie Sanders to push out moderate Democrats and remake the city's congressional delegation in a socialist image (Fox News), is discovering what conservatives have long warned: you can't spend your way to prosperity.

The lesson for Colorado: progressive politicians who prioritize ideological purity over fiscal responsibility eventually run out of other people's money. Mamdani's vision—backed by Sanders and aimed at consolidating power over three city elections—mirrors the spending-first, consequences-later approach favored by Colorado's Democratic legislature. While our state hasn't yet hit NYC's breaking point, the pattern is identical: expand government, raise taxes and regulations, watch businesses and investment capital head for the exits.

Republicans argue that Mamdani's crisis proves socialism works only until the money runs out. Democrats counter that the real problem is insufficient federal aid and wealth redistribution—not reckless spending.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether our own Democratic supermajority learns from Mamdani's impending financial "Armageddon" or doubles down on the same failed model.
