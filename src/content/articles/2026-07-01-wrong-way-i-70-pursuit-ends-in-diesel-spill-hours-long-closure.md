# Wrong-Way I-70 Pursuit Ends in Diesel Spill, Hours-Long Closure

Colorado State Patrol's high-speed pursuit of a wrong-way driver on I-70 near Silver Plume ended Tuesday in a multi-vehicle crash and diesel fuel leak that shut down the interstate for hours, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The pursuit—spanning roughly 20 miles—culminated when the suspect's vehicle collided with a semitrailer carrying fuel, triggering the spill and forcing emergency responders to close eastbound I-70 while crews contained the environmental hazard and cleared wreckage.

Colorado State Patrol did not immediately release details on the suspect's identity, charges, or why the driver was traveling the wrong direction. The incident underscores the risks posed by dangerous driving on mountain corridors that serve as critical commercial and commuter arteries for the state.

I-70 reopened after cleanup and investigation concluded, though no timeline for full restoration of normal traffic flow was provided by authorities.

Taxpayers and Colorado drivers should monitor whether CSP reviews pursuit protocols on congested interstate stretches where collateral damage—fuel spills, commerce delays, environmental cleanup costs—can ripple across the state's economy.
