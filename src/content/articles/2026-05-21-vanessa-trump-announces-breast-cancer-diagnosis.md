# Vanessa Trump Announces Breast Cancer Diagnosis

Vanessa Trump, 48, announced Wednesday that she has been diagnosed with breast cancer, according to CBS News. The ex-wife of Donald Trump Jr., with whom she shares five children after a 12-year marriage, disclosed the diagnosis on Instagram.

"I've recently been diagnosed with breast cancer," Trump posted, according to the Washington Examiner. "While this isn't news anyone expects, I'm working closely with my medical team on a treatment plan."

Trump said she is grateful to her doctors and thanked them for their support as she moves forward with treatment.
