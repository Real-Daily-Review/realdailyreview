# America's 250th Birthday: D.C. Plans Massive Celebration While States Chart Their Own Course

Washington is pulling out all the stops for the nation's quarter-millennium milestone, planning what Breitbart reports will be the "largest" fireworks display in U.S. history—a 40-minute spectacle of 850,000 fireworks over the capital on July 4th.

The timing carries symbolic weight. National Review notes that America's 250th falls on Shabbat, drawing a parallel to the Founders' reliance on Judeo-Christian principles that undergirded the republic.

For Colorado conservatives, the real question isn't whether D.C. can light up the sky—it's whether the federal government's bloated footprint will overshadow what should be a decentralized celebration of American liberty. The Founders didn't envision a centralized capital dictating the terms of national commemoration. Communities across Colorado, from mountain towns to the Eastern Plains, have their own traditions and their own reasons to mark independence.

While federal planners spend lavishly on pyrotechnics, Coloradans should remember that true patriotism isn't measured in fireworks budgets. It's measured in defending the Constitution, protecting individual liberty, and keeping government in its proper place—limited and accountable.

Watch whether Colorado's 250th celebrations emphasize local self-governance and constitutional principles, or whether progressive organizers use the milestone to push federal overreach narratives.
