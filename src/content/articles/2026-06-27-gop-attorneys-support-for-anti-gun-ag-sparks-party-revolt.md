# GOP Attorney's Gun-Control Stance Draws Fire From Party Faithful

A Republican attorney's public support for Colorado's anti-gun Attorney General Jay Jones has triggered backlash from fellow GOP members questioning his party loyalty, according to the Daily Caller. The unnamed GOP attorney defended his position in heated remarks, saying he found himself "in the middle" of competing pressures—a telling admission that reveals fractures within Colorado's Republican establishment over Second Amendment priorities.

The controversy underscores a broader tension: some establishment Republicans are willing to work across the aisle with progressive prosecutors on issues conservatives view as non-negotiable. Jones, Colorado's Democratic AG, has pursued aggressive gun-control litigation and regulatory action—positions fundamentally at odds with Republican principles of constitutional rights and limited government overreach.

Meanwhile, at the federal level, former Attorney General Bill Barr is publicly urging Senate Republicans to confirm Todd Blanche as the next U.S. Attorney General, arguing Blanche is the right choice to lead the Justice Department (Washington Examiner). Barr's endorsement carries weight among Senate GOP members weighing confirmation votes.

The Colorado dust-up matters locally because it signals whether state Republicans will maintain principled opposition to Jones's anti-gun agenda or continue accommodating Democratic priorities. Taxpayers and gun owners should watch whether this GOP attorney's cooperation translates into weakened resistance to future gun restrictions in Colorado.
