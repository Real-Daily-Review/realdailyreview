# Britain's Decline and California's Warning: What Colorado Must Learn

America is watching two cautionary tales unfold—and Colorado voters should pay attention before progressive policies take deeper root here.

Britain's economic stagnation offers a stark lesson in what happens when government inertia replaces dynamism. As *National Review* reports, even the U.K. press now acknowledges the island's structural decline—a warning about the costs of regulatory sclerosis and policy paralysis that should alarm any free-market conservative.

Closer to home, *New York Post* analysis of deep-blue California reveals what happens when Democrats control both chambers of government without meaningful opposition. The state has become a cautionary exhibit: runaway spending, regulatory overreach, and policies that punish business investment and individual liberty. California's trajectory—high taxes, mass exodus of productive citizens, and deteriorating public services—mirrors the progressive agenda Democrats would impose nationwide if they retake Congress.

Republicans argue that Colorado's competitive advantage depends on resisting this model: keeping taxes low, regulations reasonable, and government accountable. The state's energy sector, outdoor economy, and growing tech corridor thrive because Colorado has (so far) avoided the worst excesses of coastal progressivism.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado voters recognize that midterm elections will determine whether the state follows California's path or charts a different course toward growth and opportunity.
