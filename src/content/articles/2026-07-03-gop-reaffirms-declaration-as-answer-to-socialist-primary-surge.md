# Republican Pushes Declaration of Independence Reaffirmation Bill Amid Socialist Primary Surge

Colorado and the nation face a stark ideological choice as America approaches its 250th birthday—and one House Republican is forcing the issue directly.

Rep. Matt Van Epps unveiled legislation reaffirming the Declaration of Independence, explicitly framing it as a response to socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primaries (Fox News Politics). The move signals Republican concern that progressive forces are abandoning foundational American principles of limited government and individual liberty.

The timing is deliberate. As National Review notes, the Declaration's 250th anniversary offers a moment to revisit the Founders' core grievances—many of which remain strikingly relevant. Breitbart Business Digest highlights the economic DNA of 1776: control over taxation, immigration, trade policy, and restraint on government growth. Those same battles define today's Colorado and national politics.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the subtext is clear: Democrats' push for higher taxes, regulatory expansion, and government-directed "solutions" directly contradict the Declaration's animating principles. Republicans argue the bill serves as a rhetorical and constitutional anchor against progressive overreach.

Conservatives should watch whether this Declaration reaffirmation gains co-sponsors and whether it becomes a rallying point for 2026 messaging on limited government and economic freedom.
