# Trump Vows End to Columbus Statue Removals, Blasts 'Dumocrats' Over Cultural War

President Donald Trump used Columbus Day to declare war on what he calls the left's assault on American history, posting a fiery message to Truth Social condemning the removal of Columbus statues and blaming Democrats for leading the charge.

"The days of plunder of radical left Democrats, socialists, and communists ripping down statues of Christopher Columbus are over," Trump wrote, according to Breitbart. In a separate post captured by The Daily Wire, Trump attacked "Dumocrats" directly, framing statue removals as part of a broader progressive campaign to erase American heritage.

The message signals Trump's intent to make cultural battles—particularly disputes over monuments and historical figures—a centerpiece of his political messaging. Conservatives argue that removing Columbus statues represents an ideological crusade by the left to rewrite American history and delegitimize Western civilization. They contend that Columbus, whatever his flaws, deserves recognition as a pivotal historical figure whose voyages shaped the modern world.

Democrats and progressive activists counter that Columbus statues glorify colonialism and the suffering inflicted on Native Americans, and that removing them reflects historical accuracy and respect for indigenous peoples.

For Colorado, where several cities have grappled with monument disputes in recent years, Trump's hardline stance may energize conservative voters who view cultural preservation as a litmus test for political leadership.

Watch whether Trump follows rhetoric with concrete federal action to protect federal monuments from removal.
