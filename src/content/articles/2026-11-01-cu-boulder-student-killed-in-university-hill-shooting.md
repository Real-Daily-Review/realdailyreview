# Boulder Shooting Leaves CU Student Dead, Another Critical

A midnight shooting in Boulder's University Hill neighborhood Sunday claimed the life of a 19-year-old CU Boulder student and left a second student in critical condition, according to Boulder Police Chief Stephen Redfearn (KDVR). The incident occurred near 7th and Marine streets just after midnight.

Details remain sparse as police investigate. No suspect information has been released, and the motive is unclear. The shooting underscores ongoing public safety concerns in Boulder, a city that has experienced multiple high-profile violent incidents in recent years.

**The conservative angle:** Colorado's progressive cities—particularly Boulder—have pursued aggressive anti-police rhetoric and defunding initiatives while crime persists. Residents and business owners have repeatedly warned that such policies undermine law enforcement's ability to prevent and respond to violent crime. This incident will likely reignite debate over whether soft-on-crime policies and reduced police funding have left communities less safe.

Republicans and law-and-order advocates argue that adequate police resources, swift prosecution, and support for officers—not social experiments—are essential to protecting citizens. Democrats typically counter that root causes like poverty and mental health require investment, though such arguments ring hollow to families grieving lost loved ones.

Watch for Boulder police's investigation results and whether city leadership acknowledges any connection between their anti-police stance and public safety outcomes.
