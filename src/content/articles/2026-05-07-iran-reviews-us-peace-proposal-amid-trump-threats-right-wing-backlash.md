# Iran Reviews U.S. Peace Proposal as Trump Threatens New Bombing Campaign

Iran is reviewing a U.S. proposal to end the two-month conflict, according to multiple reports, after President Trump threatened Tehran with renewed bombing if it rejects a deal (PBS NewsHour). The proposal reportedly includes reopening the Strait of Hormuz to international shipping and addressing Iran's nuclear program, according to The Hill.

Trump said the war would be "over quickly" if Iran agrees, while Pakistan—acting as mediator—said it is "endeavouring to convert this ceasefire into a permanent end to this war" (BBC World).

The push for a deal has fractured the American right. Conservative radio host Mark Levin called the reported 14-point proposal "disastrous" if implemented, casting doubt on Trump's approach (The Hill). The Financial Times reported that rightwing nationalists and influencers have accused the president of betraying his "America first" pledge, describing the divide as a "'Maga divorce.'"

Iran has not yet formally responded to the latest proposal. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has been engaged in diplomatic efforts alongside the peace initiative.

Watch for Iran's formal response to the U.S. proposal and whether the reported 14-point framework becomes the basis for ongoing negotiations.
