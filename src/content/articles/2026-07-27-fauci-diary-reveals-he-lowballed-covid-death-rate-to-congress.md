# Fauci's Private Diary Reveals Massive Gap Between Private COVID Estimates and Public Testimony

Dr. Anthony Fauci's personal diary—compiled and released by GOP Sen. Rand Paul—exposes a troubling disconnect: Fauci privately estimated COVID-19's case fatality rate at 0.2–0.3%, yet weeks later told Congress the figure was around 1%, according to Fox News and Just The News.

Republicans argue the discrepancy raises serious questions about the credibility of guidance that shaped lockdowns, school closures, and vaccine mandates across America—policies that devastated Colorado's small businesses, schools, and economy. If Fauci knew the death rate was substantially lower than his public statements suggested, conservatives say, why did federal health authorities push for the most restrictive measures?

The diary also reportedly documents Fauci's apparent enthusiasm for media attention during the pandemic and includes anecdotes suggesting he may have downplayed the lab-leak theory—claims that align with GOP criticism of his role in shaping the pandemic narrative.

Democrats have defended Fauci's evolving statements as reflecting updated scientific understanding, not deception. The left argues that early uncertainty about COVID justified a precautionary approach.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners still recovering from pandemic-era shutdowns, this raises a critical question: Were the most economically destructive policies based on inflated risk assessments? Watch whether congressional Republicans demand accountability and whether mainstream media covers the diary's findings fairly.
