# Front Range Rail Plan Targets November Sales Tax Vote—Colorado Taxpayers Footing the Bill

Colorado's regional rail planners are pushing toward a November ballot measure that would impose a one-third penny sales tax across the Front Range to fund an ambitious passenger rail corridor from Fort Collins to Pueblo (Colorado Sun). The Front Range Passenger Rail District has unveiled station plans, including stops at sports venues like the Broncos stadium and Summit FC facilities (9News Denver).

**The conservative case against it:** Republicans and taxpayers should scrutinize whether this project represents genuine transportation need or another government spending scheme. A new sales tax—however modest it sounds—is still a tax hike on Colorado families and businesses already burdened by inflation and rising costs of living. The rail district must prove ridership projections are realistic, not wishful thinking, and that private investment or federal dollars can't shoulder more of the load. Colorado's economy thrives on free enterprise and low taxes; permanent tax increases should face intense scrutiny.

The August decision point gives the rail agency time to make its case—or for taxpayers to demand accountability on costs, timelines, and actual demand before voting in November.
