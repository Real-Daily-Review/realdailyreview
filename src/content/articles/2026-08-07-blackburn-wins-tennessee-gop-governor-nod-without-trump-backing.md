# Tennessee GOP Picks Blackburn for Governor Without Trump Backing

Sen. Marsha Blackburn (R-TN) won Tennessee's Republican gubernatorial nomination Thursday, defeating two conservative rivals by over 10 points despite lacking former President Donald Trump's endorsement. Blackburn garnered 43.8% of the vote, according to the Washington Examiner, ahead of Rep. John Rose's 32.7% and State Rep. Monty Fritts's 23.4%. The Daily Wire noted she clinched the race with more than 60% reporting.

Blackburn is running to replace term-limited Republican Gov. Bill Lee. Her victory comes exactly one year after announcing her gubernatorial campaign, per Just The News.

The race underscores a notable dynamic in GOP primaries: Trump's endorsement, while influential, is not determinative. Blackburn's win without his backing suggests Tennessee Republicans are confident in her conservative credentials and Senate record. She ran on a platform appealing to the party's base without relying on the former president's machinery.

Tennessee's gubernatorial race will be one to watch as Republicans seek to hold the governorship in a solidly red state heading into the 2026 midterms.
