# Biden DOJ Weaponization Reaches Alaska—and Colorado Should Take Note

The Biden Justice Department's selective prosecution playbook just landed in Alaska, where an Obama-appointed judge blocked the Trump administration's bid to delay Senate election certification in the "Decoy Dan" scheme—a coordinated Democratic effort to confuse voters and tank Republican incumbent Dan Sullivan (R-AK).

Hans von Spakovsky, former Federal Election Commission chair, told *Fox News* that Jack Smith's Trump indictments set the precedent for the DOJ's criminal probe into Alaska's voter-confusion operation. The irony is sharp: the same department that weaponized the courts against Trump now shields Democratic election interference from judicial review.

Judge Sharon Gleason, an Obama appointee, dismissed the Trump administration's lawsuit seeking an election certification delay (*The Daily Wire*). The ruling signals that federal courts—stacked with progressive judges—won't intervene when Democrats orchestrate electoral schemes, only when Republicans challenge them.

**The Colorado angle:** If the DOJ can ignore Democratic election manipulation in Alaska while prosecuting Trump, what's to stop similar schemes here? Colorado Republicans should demand transparency on whether the Biden administration investigated any coordinated Democratic efforts to confuse voters in 2020 or 2022.
