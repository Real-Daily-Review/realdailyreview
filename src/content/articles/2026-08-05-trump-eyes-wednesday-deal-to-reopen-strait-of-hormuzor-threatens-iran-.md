# Trump Signals Imminent Strait of Hormuz Deal—or Military Action Against Iran

President Trump is pressing for a quick resolution to the Strait of Hormuz standoff, signaling that an agreement could materialize as early as Wednesday or Thursday, according to 9News Denver. The administration, working with Iran and Oman, is reportedly closing in on an interim deal aimed at reopening one of the world's most critical shipping chokepoints.

Trump has made clear the stakes: the waterway will open "very soon," or Iran will face serious consequences. As Breitbart reports, the President warned Tehran it will get "hit very hard" if negotiations fail—a direct ultimatum backing diplomacy with credible military threat.

For Colorado energy producers and consumers, a reopened Strait of Hormuz matters. Roughly one-third of global seaborne oil passes through those waters. Prolonged closure drives up energy costs nationwide, hitting Colorado families and businesses at the pump and heating bill. A swift resolution would ease energy markets and reduce inflationary pressure on the state's economy.

Republicans view Trump's approach—negotiation backed by strength—as the right formula. The contrast with the previous administration's Iran appeasement is stark.

**Watch for:** whether Trump actually announces a deal by week's end, and what terms emerge on Iranian nuclear activity and regional proxy operations.
