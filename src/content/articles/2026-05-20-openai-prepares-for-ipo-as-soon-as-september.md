# OpenAI Prepares for IPO as Early as September

OpenAI is moving forward with plans to go public, potentially as soon as September, according to reporting from TechCrunch and the Financial Times. The AI lab is preparing IPO filings with investment banking support from Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, and legal counsel from Cooley, according to the Financial Times.

The timing comes a day after Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI—which had threatened the company's structure, leadership, and finances—was dismissed, TechCrunch reports. The company is reportedly targeting a valuation around $1 trillion, according to the Financial Times.

The move marks a significant milestone for the AI startup, which has grown rapidly since the public release of ChatGPT. A public listing would make OpenAI one of the most valuable companies to debut on U.S. markets.

Watch for regulatory filings and any updates on the company's financial performance and governance structure ahead of a potential listing.
