# Democratic Socialist Legislator Begs for Rent Money While Pocketing $142K Salary

Colorado voters are about to learn what "democratic socialism" really means: one thing for thee, another for me.

Brian Romero, a self-described democratic socialist headed to the state legislature, posted a GoFundMe plea seeking $2,000 for rent and groceries—then quietly deleted it after backlash (Fox News). The timing is rich: Romero is about to earn $142,000 annually as an Assembly member, a salary most working Coloradans would consider a windfall.

The hypocrisy cuts deeper. While Romero begs strangers for basic living expenses, Hollywood democratic socialists like actor John Leguizamo brag about using their capitalist earnings to buy multiple homes (Breitbart). The pattern is unmistakable: progressive politicians and celebrities preach wealth redistribution while hoarding their own gains and seeking handouts.

Republicans and conservatives argue this exposes the fundamental dishonesty of the left's economic agenda. Romero will soon vote on spending Colorado taxpayer dollars—money he apparently believes should flow to him personally before it reaches working families. His deleted post suggests he knows how bad it looks.

Colorado taxpayers should demand transparency: which other progressive legislators are quietly seeking government assistance while voting to expand government spending?
