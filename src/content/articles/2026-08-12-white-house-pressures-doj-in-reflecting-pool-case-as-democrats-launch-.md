# White House Pressure on DOJ Signals Politicization of Justice System

The Biden-Harris White House is pushing the Justice Department to pursue a misdemeanor prosecution against Olympic canoeist David Hearn after federal prosecutors dropped a felony charge related to the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool incident—a move that reeks of political interference in law enforcement.

According to Fox News, officials are now exploring lesser charges following the prosecutors' decision to abandon the original felony case. Meanwhile, House Judiciary Democrats, led by Ranking Member Jamie Raskin, have launched a probe into Interior Secretary Doug Burgum and Judge Jeanine Pirro, demanding communications between their offices and the White House regarding the case (Just The News).

The sequence is troubling: prosecutors conclude the felony charge lacks merit, then the White House steps in to demand DOJ consider alternatives. This is exactly the kind of politicization conservatives have warned about—using federal law enforcement as a cudgel against perceived opponents while simultaneously investigating Republicans who question the process.

Democrats claim they're investigating potential "interference," but their real concern appears to be that Trump appointees and allies might be involved in the case's handling. The irony is lost on them: they're investigating alleged interference while the White House itself pressures the DOJ.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether this case becomes a template for weaponizing federal prosecution against political adversaries.
