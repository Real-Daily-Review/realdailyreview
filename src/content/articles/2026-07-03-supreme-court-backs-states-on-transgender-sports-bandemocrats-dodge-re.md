# Supreme Court Affirms Biological Sex in Sports—Democrats Silent on Response

The Supreme Court has dealt a decisive blow to progressive gender ideology by ruling that states may bar transgender athletes from competing on girls' sports teams, grounding the decision in biological sex rather than gender identity (National Review). This represents a major legal victory for women's rights and state sovereignty—two principles conservatives have fought to preserve against the left's relentless reframing of sex as merely a social construct.

The ruling upholds laws in West Virginia and Idaho that protect female athletes from competing against biological males, a commonsense safeguard that Democrats have largely avoided addressing publicly (Fox News Politics). Republicans are now pushing for nationwide legislation to codify these protections across all states, with Sen. Jim Justice calling for federal action to prevent a patchwork of conflicting rules.

Colorado has its own stake in this fight. The state's high school athletic associations and universities will face pressure to align policies with this ruling—or risk legal challenges from girls' athletes and their families demanding equal protection. Expect progressive activists and education bureaucrats to resist, claiming "inclusivity" trumps fairness, but the Court has now made clear that biological reality matters in law.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature attempts to circumvent this ruling through creative regulatory language, or whether state athletic bodies voluntarily comply with the Court's logic.
