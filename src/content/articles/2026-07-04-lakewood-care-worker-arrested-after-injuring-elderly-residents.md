# Colorado Care Facility Worker Arrested After Injuring Elderly Residents

A Lakewood assisted living employee has been arrested after allegedly causing serious injuries to two elderly residents, including a 100-year-old memory care patient whose skin was torn from elbow to wrist (KDVR Fox31 Denver). The arrest occurred in June, within the worker's first 30 days on the job.
