# Trump DOJ Sues California Over Glock Ban—A Warning for Colorado's Gun-Grabbing Lawmakers

The Trump administration's Department of Justice has filed suit against California over its ban on commercial sales of semiautomatic Glock-style handguns, signaling serious federal pushback against state-level gun confiscation schemes that Colorado Democrats should take note of.

Assistant Attorney General for Civil Rights Harmeet Dhillon announced the lawsuit Wednesday after giving Gov. Gavin Newsom and California's attorney general one week to settle (Washington Examiner). The DOJ's core argument: "California cannot ban the most popular type of handgun in America" (Daily Caller).

This matters for Colorado. While the Centennial State hasn't yet pursued a full Glock ban, Democrats in the legislature have repeatedly pushed magazine restrictions, "red flag" confiscation laws, and other Second Amendment infringements that gun owners rightly view as a foot in the door. The Trump DOJ's aggressive defense of the right to bear arms—and willingness to litigate—sends a clear message that federal courts may finally side with gun owners against state overreach.

Republicans and Second Amendment advocates argue that California's ban violates the Second Amendment as interpreted by *District of Columbia v. Heller* and *New York State Rifle &amp; Pistol Association v. Bruen*. Democrats counter that states have authority to regulate firearms in the name of public safety.

Watch whether this lawsuit succeeds and whether Colorado's gun-control caucus gets the hint.
