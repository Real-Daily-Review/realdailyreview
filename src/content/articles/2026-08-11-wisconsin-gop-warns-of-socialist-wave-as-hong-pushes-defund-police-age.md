# Wisconsin GOP Sounds Alarm on Socialist Challenger as Defund-Police Agenda Gains Left-Wing Traction

Republicans are sounding the alarm over Democratic socialist Francesca Hong's Wisconsin gubernatorial bid, warning that her platform of defunding police and abolishing prisons represents a dangerous lurch toward the radical left. Trump-backed Rep. Tom Tiffany and Gov. Glenn Youngkin are framing the race as "common sense against crazy," according to Fox News, as Hong's policies threaten public safety and law-and-order governance.

Tiffany is expected to secure the GOP nomination without serious primary opposition (Just The News), positioning him for a general-election showdown against Hong's progressive agenda. Republicans argue that Hong's defund-the-police stance and prison abolition proposals would cripple law enforcement and endanger communities—a stark contrast to GOP priorities on crime prevention and victim protection.

The Wisconsin race signals a broader pattern: Democrats continue pushing socialist-aligned candidates and policies that threaten economic freedom and public safety. For Colorado conservatives, this should serve as a cautionary tale. Progressive activists have already gained ground in Colorado cities and statewide offices; similar radical candidates could emerge in upcoming state races if Republicans don't mobilize early.

Watch whether Colorado GOP candidates adopt the "common sense vs. socialism" messaging that's resonating in the Midwest—and whether similar defund-police rhetoric gains traction in Denver or Boulder.
