# ABC Host's Flag Comments Reveal Elite Contempt for Patriotic Colorado Communities

An ABC "View" co-host's recent claim that American flags make her feel "unsafe" in neighborhoods crystallizes the cultural divide between coastal progressives and heartland America—including Colorado communities where Old Glory flies proudly on front porches and Main Streets.

Sunny Hostin told viewers Monday that neighborhoods "all over" with American flags trigger feelings of unsafety, repeating similar remarks from 2021 (Breitbart, New York Post). The comments drew swift backlash from conservatives who see the statement as emblematic of progressive disdain for traditional patriotism and working-class America.

Republicans argue such rhetoric exposes the left's hostility toward symbols of national unity and constitutional governance. In Colorado—a state with deep military roots, ranching heritage, and strong patriotic traditions—Hostin's remarks land poorly. Rural and suburban Coloradans routinely display flags as expressions of love of country, not intimidation.

Democrats have not meaningfully responded to defend Hostin's specific claim, though progressive media outlets have largely ignored the story. The left's broader argument, when pressed, centers on concerns about "extremism," but conservatives counter that equating patriotic flag display with danger is itself extreme and divisive.

The real story: mainstream media figures openly express contempt for ordinary Americans' patriotic expression, signaling how far cultural progressivism has drifted from mainstream values.
