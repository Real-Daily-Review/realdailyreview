# NASA Names Artemis III Crew for Moon Mission Test Flight

NASA announced its crew for Artemis III, the next crewed spaceflight in the agency's lunar program—a mission that will test equipment in Earth's orbit rather than land on the moon itself (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31). The crew includes Coast Guard Reserve Commander Andre Douglas and Army Colonel Frank Rubio, among others (Washington Examiner).

The Artemis III mission represents a critical stepping stone in NASA's broader effort to return astronauts to the lunar surface later this decade. Rather than an immediate moon landing, the flight will validate hardware and procedures in orbit—a more measured approach than the original Apollo-era timeline.
