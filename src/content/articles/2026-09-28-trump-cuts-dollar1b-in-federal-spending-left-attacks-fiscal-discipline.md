# Trump Halts Nearly $1 Billion in Federal Spending; Democrats Cry Foul

President Trump has done what fiscal conservatives have long demanded: he actually cut government spending. Over the weekend, Trump canceled nearly $1 billion in unspent federal funding earmarked for what the Washington Examiner describes as "illegal aliens, race-obsessed programs, and other waste" — a move that has Democrats and the progressive establishment in open revolt.

This is exactly the kind of fiscal discipline Colorado taxpayers have been demanding. While state and federal governments bloat budgets year after year, Trump's rescission order demonstrates that waste can be eliminated without gutting essential services. The Left will inevitably claim these cuts harm vulnerable populations, but conservatives counter that government should stop funding programs that don't serve American citizens and that wasteful spending ultimately harms working families through inflation and debt.

The real test comes next: whether Congress and future administrations have the spine to sustain this spending discipline. Democrats will fight tooth and nail to restore these funds, and the media will paint every cut as heartless. Colorado conservatives should watch whether our state's delegation — particularly Democratic members — pushes to reverse these rescissions or stands with taxpayers demanding fiscal responsibility.
