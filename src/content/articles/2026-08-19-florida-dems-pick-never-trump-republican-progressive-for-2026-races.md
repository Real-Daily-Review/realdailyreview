# Florida Democrats Choose Never-Trump Republican and Progressive Firebrand in 2026 Primary Contests

Florida Democrats have signaled their 2026 strategy: nominate a former Republican-turned-critic of Trump for governor, and elevate a progressive state lawmaker to challenge for Senate. Neither choice suggests Democrats are confident in traditional party messaging.

David Jolly, a never-Trump former Republican congressman, won the Democratic gubernatorial primary (Fox News), while progressive state Rep. Angie Nixon upset retired Army Lt. Col. Alex Vindman in the Senate primary race—a stunning result given Vindman's national profile and fundraising advantage (Fox News). In Florida's 24th Congressional District, Democrats selected a successor to Rep. Frederica Wilson in a crowded seven-candidate field (Fox News).

**The conservative takeaway:** Democrats are banking on anti-Trump sentiment rather than affirmative policy arguments. Jolly's nomination signals weakness—the party couldn't find a Democrat credible enough to carry the gubernatorial banner. Nixon's upset over Vindman, despite his advantages, shows primary voters rejecting establishment-backed candidates in favor of hard-left messaging.
