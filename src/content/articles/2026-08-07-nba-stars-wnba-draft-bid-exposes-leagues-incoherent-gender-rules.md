# Former NBA Star's WNBA Draft Stunt Exposes the Left's Selective Rule-Making

Former NBA player Enes Kanter Freedom declared himself eligible for the 2027 WNBA Draft Friday, directly challenging the league's eligibility standards and forcing a reckoning over how it applies its own rules.

Freedom, posting on social media, stated he meets "every requirement necessary" under current WNBA guidelines and is "officially declaring myself a @WNBA prospect" (Just The News). He framed the move not as mockery but as a test of consistency: the league should apply its rules "equally to everyone," Freedom argued.

The declaration puts the WNBA in a bind. If the league denies Freedom entry based on sex, it contradicts its stated commitment to inclusion. If it accepts him, it admits its eligibility criteria are incoherent—a problem the league has ducked by avoiding clear public statements on biological sex requirements.

Conservatives see this as exposing the logical collapse of progressive gender ideology when applied uniformly. Freedom's challenge forces the WNBA to either enforce biological reality or abandon any pretense of objective standards. Either way, the league loses credibility with one constituency or another.

**Watch for:** Whether the WNBA formally responds and what its official eligibility statement actually says—or doesn't say—about sex-based categories.
