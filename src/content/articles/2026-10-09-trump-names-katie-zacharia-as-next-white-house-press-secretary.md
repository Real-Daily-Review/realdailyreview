# Trump Taps Katie Zacharia as New White House Press Secretary

The White House has offered Katie Zacharia the role of Press Secretary, replacing Karoline Leavitt who departed in August, according to Fox News, the New York Post, and the Washington Examiner. Zacharia currently serves as senior communications adviser for Truth Social, President Trump's social media platform.

The move signals a shift in Trump's communications strategy as he navigates his second term. Zacharia's appointment from the president's own media company underscores the administration's focus on controlling its message through both traditional press briefings and direct digital channels—a strategy that has defined Trump's approach to media relations.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the broader takeaway is straightforward: the administration's personnel decisions will shape how federal policy affecting the state—from energy development to regulatory rollback—gets communicated to the public. Zacharia's background in Trump's inner circle suggests continuity in the president's combative stance toward mainstream media, which conservatives have long criticized for bias.

Watch whether Zacharia's tenure brings more aggressive pushback against hostile press questioning and clearer articulation of the administration's pro-growth, deregulation agenda—or whether the revolving door at the podium continues.
