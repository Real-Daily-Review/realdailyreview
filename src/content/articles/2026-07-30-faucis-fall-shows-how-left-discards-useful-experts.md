# Fauci's Fall From Grace: The Left's Disposable Expert

Dr. Anthony Fauci's abrupt exile from progressive favor reveals how the left weaponizes experts when convenient, then discards them when politically inconvenient. Once the media's pandemic oracle, Fauci now faces serious questions about his credibility on masks, gain-of-function research, and the lab-leak theory—claims he long dismissed or deflected (National Review).

The contrast is stark: during COVID lockdowns, questioning Fauci's guidance was treated as heresy by mainstream media and Democratic leaders. Now that the political winds have shifted, those same allies have moved on, leaving the controversial scientist to answer for his record. As National Review notes, Fauci "was only a tool in his political allies' hands."

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado families endured some of the nation's strictest lockdowns and school closures, policies justified by appeals to Fauci's authority. Coloradans who lost jobs, businesses, or educational opportunities for their children based on guidance now under scrutiny deserve accountability—not just from Fauci, but from state officials who enforced those mandates without question.

The broader lesson: when government health bureaucrats operate without meaningful oversight or skepticism from the press, taxpayers pay the price. Conservatives should demand transparency from Colorado's Department of Public Health and Environment about which Fauci-era policies shaped state lockdown decisions.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats acknowledge their role in enforcing pandemic policies that scientific consensus now questions.
