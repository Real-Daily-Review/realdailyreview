# Thornton Pool, Rec Center Evacuated Over Threats

Thornton police evacuated the city pool and Margaret Carpenter Recreation Center Tuesday afternoon after receiving threats against the facilities, forcing families out mid-visit while officers investigated.

According to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31, both municipal facilities were cleared as a precaution. Details on the nature or source of the threats remain limited, though law enforcement responded to secure the scene and determine credibility.

The evacuation disrupted afternoon programming and left residents wondering about the safety protocols at publicly funded recreation centers—a legitimate concern for families in the northern Denver suburb. Thornton taxpayers fund these facilities and deserve transparency about what triggered the threat assessment and how quickly police can respond to similar incidents.

City officials have not yet released a full incident report or statement on whether the threats were deemed credible or resulted in any arrests.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether Thornton city leadership provides a complete public accounting of the incident, the response timeline, and any security upgrades planned for municipal facilities.
