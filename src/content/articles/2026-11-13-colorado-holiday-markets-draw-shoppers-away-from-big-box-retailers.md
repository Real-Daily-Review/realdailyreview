# Colorado Holiday Markets Bring Local Shopping Back Into Focus

Colorado communities are ramping up holiday markets and seasonal festivities as families look to support local businesses rather than rely on big-box retailers and online giants. Arvada and communities across the state are hosting tree lightings, craft markets, and special events designed to keep holiday spending in local hands—a refreshing counterweight to the consolidation of retail power in the hands of a few mega-corporations.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, Arvada's lineup includes tree lightings and holiday markets featuring local vendors. CPR News reports that Colorado is home to some of the biggest and most unique holiday markets taking place this season, offering shoppers alternatives to Amazon and chain stores.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the message is clear: supporting local markets strengthens community economies, keeps tax revenue circulating locally, and reduces dependence on corporate behemoths that often dodge state taxes through legal loopholes. Small retailers and artisans benefit directly when Coloradans choose to shop at farmers markets and craft fairs rather than shipping their dollars out of state.

Watch whether Colorado cities will continue removing regulatory barriers—parking requirements, permitting fees, zoning restrictions—that make it harder for small vendors and pop-up markets to operate affordably.
