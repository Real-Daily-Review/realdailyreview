# GOP Midterm Convention in Dallas to Showcase Trump, Vance Agenda

The Republican National Committee is staging an unprecedented midterm convention in Dallas next month—a direct signal that Trump and his allies are seizing the political initiative heading into the 2026 cycle. President Trump and Vice President JD Vance will headline the September 9-10 event, with Trump appearing both nights and delivering the keynote on night two, according to *The Daily Wire*.

The convention marks a departure from tradition. Rather than waiting for a presidential election year, Republicans are using the platform to highlight administration accomplishments and energize the party base during a midterm window—a calculated move to build momentum for congressional races and state-level contests.

For Colorado Republicans, the convention offers a high-profile venue to rally support around energy development, border security, and Second Amendment protections—issues where Trump's policies diverge sharply from the Biden-Harris approach that dominated Colorado's recent politics. The event also signals GOP confidence in messaging ahead of 2026 state elections, where Colorado's governorship and Senate seat will be contested.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado GOP leadership uses this platform to recruit strong candidates and articulate a clear counter-agenda to Democratic control of the statehouse.
