# Biden DOJ Weaponizes Alaska Election Case While Ignoring Democrat 'Decoy Dan' Scheme

The Biden Justice Department is pursuing a criminal probe into Alaska's "Decoy Dan" Senate race—but only against Republicans, while Democrats orchestrated the voter-confusion scheme itself, conservatives argue.

An Obama-appointed federal judge (The Daily Wire) dismissed a Trump administration lawsuit seeking to delay certification of Alaska's Senate election, rejecting arguments that the Democratic "decoy candidate" operation warranted judicial intervention. The scheme involved Democrats recruiting a fake Republican candidate to split the GOP vote against incumbent Sen. Dan Sullivan (R-AK).

Hans von Spakovsky, former Federal Election Commission chair, told Fox News that Jack Smith's Trump indictments set a dangerous precedent: the DOJ now weaponizes election law selectively. Republicans argue the Biden administration is prosecuting Republicans for alleged election interference while ignoring the documented Democratic operation that actually confused voters in Alaska.

Conservatives say this reveals a two-tiered justice system. The Left claims the "Decoy Dan" scheme was legal political hardball; Republicans counter it was voter manipulation that should trigger equal DOJ scrutiny—not one-sided prosecution.

**What to watch:** Whether the DOJ's Alaska criminal probe targets Democratic operatives equally, or confirms conservative fears of partisan weaponization ahead of 2024.
