# Colorado's Natural Gas Amendment 82 Heats Up Ballot Fight

Amendment 82 would enshrine a constitutional right to natural gas use in Colorado—a direct response to progressive efforts to phase out gas appliances statewide.

The measure protects Coloradans' ability to use natural gas for heating, cooking, and other household needs. Supporters argue that banning natural gas, as environmental activists have proposed, would spike energy costs and damage Colorado's economy and energy sector jobs (Complete Colorado). Natural gas remains a cornerstone of affordable, reliable home energy across the state.

Opponents frame the amendment as unnecessary constitutional overreach. The Denver Post notes the measure would essentially lock in gas-stove cooking and home heating as protected rights, suggesting it's a preemptive strike against future regulations rather than a response to imminent bans.

The real issue: Colorado's progressive legislature and local governments have pursued aggressive electrification mandates and building codes that effectively discourage or ban new gas connections. Amendment 82 forces voters to decide whether energy choice belongs in the state constitution—a question that cuts to the heart of Colorado's energy future and affordability.

Conservatives and energy advocates say this protects working families from government-mandated energy poverty. Watch whether Amendment 82 passes and whether the legislature respects the outcome.
