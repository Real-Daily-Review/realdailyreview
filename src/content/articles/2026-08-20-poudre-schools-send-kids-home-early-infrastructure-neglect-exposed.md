# Poudre School District Sends Kids Home Early as Taxpayers Foot the Bill for Inadequate Infrastructure

Colorado's Poudre School District is dismissing thousands of students two hours early Thursday due to lack of air conditioning during heat conditions—a stark reminder that school infrastructure spending hasn't kept pace with Colorado's growing climate demands (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

The early dismissal affects classroom instruction and working parents' schedules, yet reflects a broader problem: districts across Colorado have deferred maintenance and facility upgrades despite steady budget increases. Conservatives argue that bloated administrative overhead and top-heavy spending on programs unrelated to classroom basics have starved capital projects that directly serve students.

Meanwhile, the DEA is warning parents about emoji-coded drug trafficking targeting students as the school year begins (KDVR Fox31 Denver). Fentanyl and other synthetic drugs continue infiltrating Colorado schools, underscoring that no amount of curriculum expansion addresses the real threats students face.

**What conservatives should watch:** whether Poudre and other districts demand accountability for how existing taxpayer dollars are allocated before asking voters for new bond measures.
