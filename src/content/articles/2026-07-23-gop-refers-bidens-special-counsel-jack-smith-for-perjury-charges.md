# GOP Moves to Hold Biden's Special Counsel Accountable for Congressional Testimony

House Judiciary Committee Chairman Jim Jordan has issued a criminal referral against former special counsel Jack Smith, accusing him of perjury before Congress—a significant escalation in Republican efforts to hold the Biden DOJ apparatus accountable for what conservatives view as weaponized prosecution of the former president.

Jordan's referral (Washington Examiner) alleges Smith lied under oath about the scope of subpoenas his team issued for lawmakers' communications. Smith testified that requests for congressional "toll records" did not include message content, but Republicans argue the evidence contradicts that claim (Just The News). The referral marks a formal accusation that Smith made false statements to Congress—a federal crime.

Republicans have long questioned Smith's conduct as special counsel, viewing his investigations into Trump as politically motivated. The referral represents their attempt to shift from defensive litigation to offensive accountability, demanding the DOJ prosecute one of its own.

While this case centers on federal matters, Colorado Republicans should note the broader pattern: the weaponization of federal law enforcement against political opponents undermines the rule of law that protects all Americans, including Colorado citizens and businesses from arbitrary government power.
