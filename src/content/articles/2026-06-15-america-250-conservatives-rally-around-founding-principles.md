# America 250 Celebration Underway as Nation Marks Founding Milestone

Colorado conservatives should view the nation's 250th birthday as a moment to reassert founding principles against progressive drift.

As America marks its 2026 semiquincentennial, conservative outlets are framing the milestone as an opportunity to recommit to constitutional governance and limited government. National Review emphasizes spiritual renewal—"In God we trust and rest and renew"—while The Daily Wire frames the celebration as a reassertion of American strength and constitutional order under conservative leadership.

The America 250 commemoration comes as the nation grapples with expanding federal authority, regulatory overreach, and progressive policy experiments that conservatives argue undermine the Founders' vision of limited government and individual liberty. Republicans see the anniversary as a rallying point to challenge the administrative state's growth and restore constitutional constraints on executive power.

For Colorado specifically, the 250th anniversary provides a platform to highlight how state and federal overreach—from water regulations to energy restrictions to gun control measures—deviates from the Founders' intent. Colorado's ranchers, energy workers, and gun owners have particular stakes in whether the nation returns to constitutional first principles or continues down the progressive path.

Watch for how conservative leaders use America 250 messaging to mobilize voters around constitutional restoration and limited-government reform heading into 2026 elections.
