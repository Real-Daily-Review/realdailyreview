# Texas Dem's Campaign Collapse Signals Trouble for Left's Senate Push

A Texas Democratic Senate nominee's mysterious 12-day disappearance from the campaign trail is raising red flags about Democratic momentum heading into the final stretch—and giving Republicans an opening in a race Democrats hoped to flip.

James Talarico, the Democratic nominee for Texas Senate, has been largely absent from public view, with reports indicating he is "really sick" following what *The Daily Wire* reports was the flu contracted after a New York City fundraiser. Trump seized on the absence, calling Talarico "MISSING IN ACTION" on social media, according to *Fox News Politics*.

The timing is brutal for Democrats. With the race entering its critical final weeks, Talarico's extended absence has left Democratic volunteers worried about losing campaign momentum and message discipline. For Republicans, the gaffe underscores a larger narrative: Democratic candidates struggling to maintain focus and energy as Election Day approaches.

While Democrats will likely frame this as a health issue requiring rest, conservatives see a cautionary tale about a nominee unable to sustain the grueling demands of a statewide race—or worse, a campaign in disarray at a pivotal moment.

**Watch for:** Whether Talarico can mount an effective comeback in the final weeks, or if his absence becomes a permanent drag on Democratic turnout and volunteer enthusiasm in Texas.
