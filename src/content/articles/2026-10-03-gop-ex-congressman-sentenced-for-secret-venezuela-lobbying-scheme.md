# GOP Corruption Scandal Shows Why Colorado Voters Should Demand Accountability From All Parties

A former Republican congressman and ally of Secretary of State Marco Rubio was sentenced to 10 years in prison Friday for running a secret $50 million lobbying operation on behalf of Venezuela during the first Trump administration, according to the New York Post. David Rivera, a onetime Miami representative, concealed the foreign influence campaign in violation of federal law.

The case underscores a hard truth conservatives must face: corruption and backroom dealing aren't exclusive to the left. When Republicans betray the public trust—especially by secretly advancing foreign interests over American ones—they deserve the same scrutiny and consequences as any Democrat caught in similar misconduct.

Colorado Republicans and taxpayers should take note. Backroom lobbying schemes, whether foreign or domestic, corrode the limited-government principles conservatives claim to champion. If the GOP is serious about draining the swamp and restoring faith in institutions, it must police its own ranks without excuse or favoritism.

The contrast is stark: voters demand transparency, accountability, and leaders who put America first—not hidden agendas financed by hostile regimes.

Watch whether Colorado's Republican leadership uses this moment to recommit to transparency and ethical governance, or whether it quietly moves past the scandal.
