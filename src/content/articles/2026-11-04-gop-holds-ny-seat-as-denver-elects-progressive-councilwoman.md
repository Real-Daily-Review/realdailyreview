# Trump Ally Holds NY Seat; Denver Voters Pick Progressive for Council

Republicans secured a decisive win in New York's 21st Congressional District, with Trump-backed candidate Constantino claiming victory in the race to replace retiring Rep. Elise Stefanik—a significant hold for the GOP in a deep-red district (New York Post). Meanwhile, in Colorado's capital, Denver voters elected progressive Melissa Mejía to an at-large City Council seat in a special election Tuesday night, her campaign manager confirmed (Denver Post).

The New York result underscores continued Republican strength in upstate strongholds and Trump's enduring influence over GOP candidate recruitment. The Denver outcome reflects the city's leftward tilt on municipal governance, where progressive candidates maintain electoral advantage in at-large races.

For Colorado conservatives, the Denver result signals the ongoing challenge Republicans face in urban centers where progressive organizing and turnout operations dominate local politics. Mejía's victory adds another progressive voice to a council already skeptical of business-friendly development, police funding, and limited-government approaches to homelessness and housing policy.
