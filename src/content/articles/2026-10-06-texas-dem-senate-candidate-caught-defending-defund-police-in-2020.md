# Texas Democrat's Police Defunding Comments Resurface, Campaign Scrambles

A resurfaced 2020 video shows Texas Democratic U.S. Senate candidate James Talarico questioning why "defund the police" remained off-limits in political debate—a position his campaign now frantically denies (Fox News, Breitbart). In the call with progressive activists, Talarico told supporters it was "rich" that the defunding debate faced stigma, according to CNN reporting cited by Breitbart.

Talarico's team issued a damage-control statement: "James did not call for and does not support defunding the police, and he strongly supports Texas law enforcement and first responders" (New York Post). The contradiction between his 2020 rhetoric and current messaging underscores a broader Democratic vulnerability on law-and-order issues heading into 2026.
