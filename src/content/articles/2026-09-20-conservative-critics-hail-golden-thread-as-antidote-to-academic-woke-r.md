# Conservative Book Critics Hail 'Golden Thread' as Antidote to Academic Woke Revisionism

A sweeping new tome is drawing praise from right-leaning outlets as a necessary corrective to what conservatives call the systematic erasure of Western civilization from American classrooms.

*National Review* calls "The Golden Thread" [the book of the year](https://www.nationalreview.com/2026/09/the-book-of-the-year/), noting that every page "brims with insight about the roots of Western Civ." The *New York Post* frames the 12-pound volume as a [counter-cultural act](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/opinion/book-of-the-year-this-12-pound-tome-can-save-western-civilization/) at a moment when Western civilization has been deliberately "de-centered" in academia and pushed to the margins of curricula.

Conservatives argue that universities and K-12 schools have systematically downplayed or reframed Western intellectual, legal, and cultural achievements in favor of grievance-focused narratives. They contend that students graduate without understanding the philosophical and institutional foundations—rule of law, individual rights, free markets—that underpin American prosperity and liberty.

The book's reception signals growing conservative frustration with what they see as ideological capture of education. Colorado parents and school board members have echoed these concerns in recent years, pushing back against curricula they view as hostile to American history and values.
