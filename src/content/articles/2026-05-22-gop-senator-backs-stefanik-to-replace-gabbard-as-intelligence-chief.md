# GOP Senator Floats Stefanik as Replacement for Gabbard at DNI

A Republican senator is backing Rep. Elise Stefanik to succeed Tulsi Gabbard as Director of National Intelligence after Gabbard announced her resignation Friday.

Sen. Jim Banks (R-Ind.), a Trump ally, said in a post on X that Stefanik would be a "great replacement" for Gabbard and would "easily" clear the Senate confirmation process, according to The Hill and the Washington Examiner. Banks did not elaborate on Stefanik's qualifications or why he believed she would face no significant obstacles to confirmation.

Stefanik, a House Republican from New York, previously served as Trump's ambassador to the United Nations. The timing of Banks's endorsement comes as the Trump administration navigates the sudden departure of Gabbard, a former congresswoman and 2024 presidential candidate who had been confirmed as DNI in 2025.

The recommendation underscores ongoing discussions within Republican circles about filling key national security positions. No formal announcement has been made by the White House regarding Gabbard's successor.

Watch for whether Trump endorses Stefanik or considers other candidates for the intelligence chief role, and whether Democrats raise concerns during any confirmation hearing.
