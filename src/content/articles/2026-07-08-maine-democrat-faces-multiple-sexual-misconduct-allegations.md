# Maine Democrat's Misconduct Allegations Expose Party's Credibility Problem

A Democratic Senate candidate in Maine faces mounting sexual misconduct allegations that underscore the left's selective outrage on women's issues. Graham Platner, the Democratic nominee, now faces accusations from multiple ex-girlfriends—including claims he removed condoms without consent and allegations of physical abuse, according to reporting by the Washington Post cited by the Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire.

The allegations are particularly damaging because they've prompted Democratic institutions and party figures to distance themselves from Platner, effectively acknowledging the severity of the claims. This stands in sharp contrast to how Democrats have handled similar allegations against their own in the past, raising questions about whether the party applies consistent standards or simply abandons candidates when the political cost becomes too high.

Conservatives argue this episode reveals the hypocrisy embedded in progressive politics: Democrats leverage women's issues as a cudgel against Republicans while quietly jettisoning their own when scandal strikes. The pattern suggests principle takes a backseat to political calculation.

While this race is in Maine, Colorado voters should note how national Democratic leadership responds to misconduct within its ranks—and whether that standard ever applies equally.
