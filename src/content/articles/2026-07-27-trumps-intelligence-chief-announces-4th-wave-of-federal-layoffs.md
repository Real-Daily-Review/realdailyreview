# Federal Intelligence Agency Slashes Payroll as Trump Pushes Downsizing Agenda

The Office of the Director of National Intelligence is shrinking fast. Acting DNI William Pulte announced a fourth round of layoffs Sunday, continuing an aggressive cost-cutting campaign that has already drawn fire from both parties (Washington Examiner). The timing is notable: Pulte's announcement came just as the Senate prepared to vote on his replacement's nomination, signaling the Trump administration's commitment to trimming what conservatives see as bloated federal bureaucracy.

Pulte, described by the Washington Examiner as a "controversial figure" and Trump loyalist, has become the public face of intelligence community downsizing. Republicans argue that the federal workforce has grown too large and that cutting redundant positions saves taxpayer money. Democrats and some establishment Republicans worry about gutting institutional capacity and national security expertise.

For Colorado, the impact depends on how many intelligence jobs are based in the state—particularly around federal contractors and defense operations in the Denver metro area and beyond. If layoffs extend to regional offices or contractor positions, Colorado's defense-dependent economy could feel the ripple.

**Watch:** Whether the Senate confirms Pulte's replacement and whether the new DNI continues the layoff pace—or whether Congress moves to protect intelligence positions from further cuts.
