# South Park's Trump Jab Shows Hollywood's Predictable Playbook

The animated series *South Park* announced it will rename itself "South America" in a mocking response to the Trump administration's geographic rebranding efforts—a move that perfectly captures how the entertainment left defaults to snark when it disagrees with policy.

The show's creators, citing "bravery and patriotism," sarcastically thanked Paramount and "Skydance Capitulation" for enabling the name change. The stunt references Trump's recent directives renaming the Gulf of Mexico to the Gulf of America and similar geographic rebranding initiatives (Just The News, Washington Examiner).
