# Biden DOJ's School Board Crackdown Exposed as Political Targeting, Internal Docs Reveal

The Biden Justice Department weaponized federal law enforcement against parents opposing progressive school policies, according to newly released internal documents that should alarm every Colorado family concerned about government overreach.

Attorney General Merrick Garland's 2021 memo directing the FBI to investigate school board protests sparked alarm within the DOJ itself. Internal officials warned the initiative risked becoming an "Anti-MAGA Task Force"—a direct admission that the investigation targeted political opponents rather than genuine threats (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

The scheme hit Colorado hard. Parents across the state faced federal scrutiny for attending school board meetings and voicing concerns about curriculum, mask mandates, and gender ideology. What Garland framed as protecting schools from "threats" was actually a coordinated effort to silence conservative dissent.

Republicans argue this represents a dangerous politicization of the Justice Department—using federal power to intimidate citizens exercising First Amendment rights. The Trump administration's Weaponization Working Group released these findings Monday, exposing the operation's true intent.

Conservatives say this case exemplifies why federal law enforcement must be stripped of partisan mission creep and why Colorado voters should demand accountability from elected officials who remained silent while parents were targeted.
