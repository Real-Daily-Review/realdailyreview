# Market Manipulation: How Big Finance Rigs the Game Against Colorado Investors

Colorado savers and retirees are getting fleeced by Wall Street's rigged playbook—and the financial media won't tell you the truth.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, institutional investors deliberately engineer stock "dips" to trigger panic selling among retail traders, then scoop up shares at artificially depressed prices. When mega-cap stocks drop a few percentage points on minor headlines, the financial press spins it as rational market correction. In reality, conservatives argue, it's mechanical manipulation orchestrated through dark pools and algorithmic trading designed to separate ordinary Americans from their wealth.

The scheme works like this: major institutions coordinate selling pressure, triggering stop-loss orders from retail investors who bail out in fear. Once small investors are flushed out, the big players buy the dip cheap and ride the recovery—pocketing the difference while mom-and-pop investors miss the gains.

For Colorado families saving for retirement or college, this rigged system means their 401(k)s and IRAs are being systematically disadvantaged. Republicans and free-market conservatives argue that regulators have allowed dark-pool trading and high-frequency manipulation to flourish unchecked, betraying the principle of fair markets.
