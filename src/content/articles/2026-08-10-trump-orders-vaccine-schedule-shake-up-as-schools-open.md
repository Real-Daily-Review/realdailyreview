# Trump Orders Vaccine Schedule Overhaul as Schools Open

Trump is pushing federal health agencies to rethink childhood vaccine protocols, a move that will ripple through Colorado schools and spark fierce debate over parental choice versus public health mandates.

The executive order directs the nation's health department to recommend separating combination vaccines—particularly the MMR shot—into individual doses administered across different visits, and to expand vaccine research (Just The News). The timing is pointed: school years are starting nationwide amid a measles outbreak.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Americans deserve flexibility and transparency on vaccine schedules, especially post-COVID skepticism of one-size-fits-all health mandates. The Daily Caller reports the order emphasizes that "Americans … are asking questions" about vaccine protocols. Conservatives contend parents should have genuine choice in how and when their children receive immunizations, not federal diktat.

**The counterargument:** Public health officials and Democrats warn that separating proven combination vaccines could reduce vaccination rates, leave children vulnerable to preventable disease, and undermine herd immunity—particularly dangerous during an active measles outbreak.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado schools enforce vaccine requirements; this order could reshape state health policy and parental rights in classrooms statewide.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic-led health department resists the federal guidance or whether Republican legislators push state law to align with the new flexibility.
