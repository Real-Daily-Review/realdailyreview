# Ukraine Anti-Corruption Agencies Accused of Sabotaging Trump Peace Efforts

Ukraine's Western-backed anti-corruption authorities are being weaponized to undermine President Trump's diplomatic push to end the Russia-Ukraine war, according to the country's former top prosecutor.

Ruslan Kravchenko, who resigned as Ukraine's prosecutor general in September 2026, told the Washington Examiner that the National Anti-Corruption Bureau of Ukraine and the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor's office are deliberately weakening President Zelensky to sabotage peace negotiations—benefiting Russia in the process.

The allegation fits a broader pattern. Former Trump adviser Casey Means told Joe Rogan that "bureaucrats are trying to subvert" the Trump administration, describing it as "an unruly government" (Daily Caller). Meanwhile, another former MAHA adviser alleged that White House staffers blocked pesticide reform investigations by invoking "national security" concerns—suggesting deep-state resistance to Trump's deregulatory agenda across multiple fronts.
