# Trump's Iran Strategy Divides Conservatives as Strikes Mount

President Trump's escalating military campaign against Iran is fracturing his own coalition, with some conservatives warning that the conflict is overshadowing his economic agenda and draining resources better spent at home.

According to National Review, Trump's pursuit of both tariffs and Iran strikes has cost him his "signature issue of economic stewardship"—a core strength that won him Colorado and other swing states in 2024. Meanwhile, 9News reports Trump has threatened additional strikes while pushing to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, signaling no near-term exit from the conflict.

**The conservative split is sharp.** Some Republicans argue that sustained military engagement abroad contradicts Trump's "America First" platform and diverts attention from domestic priorities like border security, tax reform, and deregulation. Others contend that confronting Iran's regional aggression is necessary to protect U.S. interests and energy security—critical for Colorado's oil and gas sector.

The broader concern: every dollar spent on Middle East operations is a dollar not available for tax cuts or infrastructure that benefits Colorado workers and businesses. Energy markets, too, remain volatile as long as the Strait of Hormuz stays contested.

**Watch whether Trump signals a genuine off-ramp or digs in further—his economic credibility with voters depends on it.**
