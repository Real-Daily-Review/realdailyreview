# Boulder County Wastes Taxpayer Cash on Trump Anxiety Sessions

Boulder County officials are hosting what amounts to tax-funded therapy for residents obsessed with Trump, according to *Complete Colorado*. The county's so-called "election champions" program has devolved into what observers describe as a "group therapy session" rather than legitimate civic engagement.

This is classic progressive Boulder: using public resources to process political grievances instead of focusing on core county functions. Residents footing the bill for these sessions—many of whom don't share the Trump-anxiety worldview—have every right to ask why their tax dollars are funding what amounts to partisan emotional support.

The program illustrates a broader problem in blue-county governance: mission creep. What likely started as election administration has morphed into ideological activism on the public dime. Meanwhile, actual county priorities—infrastructure, public safety, fiscal responsibility—take a back seat to therapeutic venting sessions.

Conservatives argue this is exactly the kind of wasteful government spending that drives up property taxes and erodes public trust. If Boulder County wants to host political discussion groups, fine—but do it on private time and private dime, not with money extracted from taxpayers who may hold entirely different views.

**Watch for:** Whether county commissioners face pushback at budget hearings over continued funding for these programs.
