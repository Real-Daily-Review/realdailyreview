# Russia's Victory Day Parade Stripped of Military Hardware

Russia will hold its annual Victory Day parade on Saturday without tanks or other military equipment for the first time in nearly two decades, according to BBC World, signaling potential strain on Moscow's war effort in Ukraine.

The parade in Moscow will feature only soldiers, marking a notable departure from tradition. BBC World's analysis suggests the absence of hardware indicates the war in Ukraine may not be proceeding according to plan for Russia.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has warned Russia's allies against attending the event, according to Deutsche Welle. The warning underscores Kyiv's diplomatic pressure campaign amid the ongoing conflict.

Victory Day, celebrated annually on May 9, typically showcases Russia's military might through displays of tanks, missiles, and other weaponry. The decision to exclude hardware this year comes as Russia faces sustained Ukrainian resistance and international sanctions related to its 2022 invasion.
