# Denver Could Send Democratic Socialist to Congress

A 29-year-old democratic socialist is positioned to represent Denver in Congress, raising concerns among Colorado conservatives about the direction of the state's political center.

Melat Kiros, according to CPR News, could become Denver's next congressional representative. The Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) member represents a sharp leftward turn for a district that has already trended heavily Democratic.

Republicans and conservatives argue that DSA-aligned candidates push radical economic agendas—including wealth redistribution, government control of key industries, and expansive entitlements—that would burden Colorado taxpayers and small businesses. The DSA platform fundamentally rejects free-market capitalism in favor of centralized economic planning, a stark contrast to Colorado's entrepreneurial tradition and energy sector.

Democrats counter that Kiros represents grassroots activism and responds to constituent demands for stronger social programs and climate action.

The race underscores a broader pattern: Denver's congressional seat has become a proving ground for the Democratic Party's progressive wing. If Kiros wins, she would join a growing caucus of socialist-aligned lawmakers pushing federal spending and regulation to historic levels.
