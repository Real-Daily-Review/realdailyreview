# Democrat Breaks Ranks, Endorses Republican Over Party's Senate Pick

A Michigan Democrat's last-minute endorsement of a Republican Senate candidate over her own party's nominee reveals deep fractures in the left over antisemitism and radical ideology.

Rep. Haley Stevens (D-MI) endorsed former Rep. Mike Rogers (R-MI) instead of Abdul El-Sayed, the Democratic nominee, citing what she characterized as El-Sayed's hostility toward Israel. "Loyalty to a party should never require abandoning your values or judgment," Stevens said in a statement released hours before Election Day (The Daily Wire).

The move triggered fury among progressives. Jon Stewart used his platform on *The Daily Show* to denounce Stevens for backing what he called a "nightmare" Republican (Breitbart). But National Review reported that Stevens is far from alone—many Democrats harbor serious reservations about El-Sayed's record and rhetoric.

Stevens's defection underscores a widening rift: Democrats increasingly divided between those willing to tolerate anti-Israel positions within their coalition and those who view such stances as disqualifying. Republicans, meanwhile, have capitalized on the chaos, with Rogers benefiting from the intra-party implosion.

**The Colorado angle:** While this race unfolds in Michigan, Colorado Democrats face similar pressures as their base splinters over Middle East policy and party loyalty—tensions that could reshape state races in 2026 and beyond.
