# Unknown Substance Kills Three in New Mexico; First Responders Sickened

Three people are dead after exposure to an unknown substance in Mountainair, New Mexico, according to PBS NewsHour and The Hill. First responders who arrived at a home in the town began experiencing symptoms including nausea and dizziness after possible exposure to the same material.

More than a dozen first responders were taken to hospitals, The Hill reports. Authorities decontaminated those affected as part of the emergency response.

The identity of the substance remains unclear. Officials have not yet disclosed what caused the deaths or provided details about the initial exposure that led to the emergency call.

The incident underscores the hazards first responders face when called to scenes involving unknown materials. Authorities are continuing to investigate the source and nature of the substance.
