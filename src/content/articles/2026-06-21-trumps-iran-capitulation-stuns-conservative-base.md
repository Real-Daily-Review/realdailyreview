# Trump's Iran Deal Collapse Sparks Conservative Backlash Over Capitulation

President Trump's sudden reversal on Iran negotiations has drawn sharp criticism from the right, with conservatives arguing he abandoned core campaign promises on the world stage. National Review reports that Trump capitulated on "all the key issues of contention" in the conflict, reversing his previous hardline stance (National Review). The move contradicts Trump's 2024 messaging on confronting Iranian aggression and maintaining American strength in the Middle East.

Colorado Sun columnist Mike Littwin frames the shift as desperation, claiming Trump now relies on "lies" to justify the policy U-turn, citing Vice President JD Vance's inflammatory rhetoric about the truce as evidence of the administration's credibility problem.

For Colorado conservatives, the Iran capitulation raises questions about energy policy. Trump's previous energy agenda included aggressive oil and gas development partly justified by reducing dependence on hostile Middle Eastern regimes. A weakened Iran posture could undermine that rationale and embolden OPEC, affecting Colorado's energy sector competitiveness.

Republicans must now explain how this reversal aligns with "America First" foreign policy—or whether Trump's negotiating leverage simply evaporated faster than expected.
