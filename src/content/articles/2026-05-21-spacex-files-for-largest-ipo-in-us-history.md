# SpaceX Files for Record-Breaking IPO

SpaceX has formally filed for what could become the largest initial public offering in U.S. history, according to The Verge. The company plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX.

The filing reveals SpaceX generated $18.67 billion in revenue in 2025, with its Starlink satellite internet service accounting for more than $11 billion of that total, according to The Wall Street Journal reporting cited by The Verge. The company posted a loss of over $4.9 billion last year while capital expenditures jumped to $20.7 billion, up from $11.2 billion in 2024.

The IPO would surpass previous record-setting debuts. According to CNBC, major U.S. IPOs have included Alibaba, Facebook, and Visa, which each raised historic sums when they went public.

SpaceX's filing outlines the company's broader ambitions beyond its current rocket and satellite operations, according to the Financial Times, which notes the prospectus details plans spanning asteroid mining and artificial intelligence ventures.

The company's path to public markets marks a significant milestone for Elon Musk's space venture, which has grown from a private rocket manufacturer into a diversified aerospace and technology enterprise.
