# Speaker Johnson Launches Anti-Socialism Campaign Ahead of 2026 Midterms

House Speaker Mike Johnson is betting that Americans are ready to reject the left's socialist drift—and he's building a digital war room to prove it.

Johnson unveiled a new campaign website Saturday designed to educate young voters about socialism and communism as he kicks off a nationwide "Contrast for America" tour ahead of the 2026 midterm elections, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The Republican leader is framing the choice starkly: "common sense vs. crazy," positioning the GOP as the party defending free markets and individual liberty against Democratic embrace of government overreach.

The move reflects Republican strategy to nationalize midterms around ideology rather than local issues. Republicans argue Democrats have abandoned fiscal restraint and constitutional limits on government power, pushing policies that expand the state's reach into energy, healthcare, and education—areas where Colorado voters have shown skepticism of progressive experimentation.

Democrats counter that Republicans mischaracterize their positions and that voters care more about kitchen-table economics than abstract ideological battles.

The *Examiner* also notes both parties seem oddly unfocused heading into 2026, raising questions about whether either side truly understands what voters demand.
