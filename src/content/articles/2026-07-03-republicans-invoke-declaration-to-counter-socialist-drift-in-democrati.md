# Republicans Dust Off Declaration of Independence as Pushback Against Socialist Drift

As America marks 250 years since the Founders declared independence from Crown overreach, Republicans are weaponizing Jefferson's words against what they see as creeping socialism in Democratic ranks.

Rep. Matt Van Epps unveiled legislation reaffirming the Declaration of Independence, framing it directly as a response to socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primaries (Fox News Politics). The move reflects conservative anxiety that the party has drifted far from the Founders' vision of limited government.

Republicans argue the Declaration's core economic principles—control over taxation, immigration, trade policy, and restraint on government growth—remain urgently relevant today (Breitbart). These weren't abstract ideals; they were grievances against arbitrary state power that resonate with modern taxpayers tired of regulatory bloat and federal overreach.

The timing matters for Colorado. As the state grapples with rising energy regulations, water disputes, and tax-and-spend proposals from Democratic leadership, the Founders' skepticism of unchecked government power offers a philosophical counterweight. Jefferson's Monticello, now a vibrant scholarly center (National Review), serves as a reminder that the American experiment was built on the principle that government exists to protect liberty, not expand its reach.

Watch whether this rhetorical push translates into actual legislative pushback against progressive policies in Congress and state capitals.
