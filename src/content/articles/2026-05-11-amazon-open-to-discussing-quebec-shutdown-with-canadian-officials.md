# Amazon Open to Quebec Talks as Shutdown Threatens 1,700 Jobs

Amazon said it is willing to discuss its decision to shut down operations in Quebec with Canadian and provincial officials, according to the Wall Street Journal. The closure would result in approximately 1,700 job losses in the French-speaking province.

The company has not detailed the reasoning behind the planned exit or specified conditions under which it might reconsider. Canadian officials have not yet responded publicly to Amazon's offer to engage in talks.

The shutdown marks a significant pullback from Amazon's Canadian operations and comes amid broader labor and regulatory pressures facing the e-commerce giant across North America.

Watch for whether negotiations between Amazon and government officials produce any agreement to reverse or modify the closure plan.
