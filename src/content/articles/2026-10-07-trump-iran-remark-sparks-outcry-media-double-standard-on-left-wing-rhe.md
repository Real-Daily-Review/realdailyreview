# Trump's Iran Remark Sparks Debate; Left-Wing Streamer's 9/11 Comment Resurfaces

President Trump's comment that Iran "can take out a city" like Los Angeles or San Diego has ignited a firestorm—but Republicans are pointing out a glaring double standard in media coverage.

At a Nebraska rally, Trump made the remark about Iranian capability, prompting swift White House pushback against critics who seized on the statement (Breitbart). The controversy, however, has resurfaced past comments from left-wing Twitch streamer Hasan Piker, who in 2019 said America "deserved" 9/11—remarks he later claimed were "far less damaging" than Trump's Iran comment (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative argument:** Republicans argue the media's selective outrage exposes a double standard. Piker's inflammatory rhetoric—suggesting America deserved a terrorist attack that killed nearly 3,000 Americans—drew minimal mainstream condemnation, yet Trump's geopolitical observation about Iranian threats triggers wall-to-wall criticism. Conservatives say this hypocrisy reveals how progressive figures and their media allies operate under different rules.
