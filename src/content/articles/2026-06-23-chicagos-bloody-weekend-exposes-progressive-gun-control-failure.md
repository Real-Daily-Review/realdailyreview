# Chicago's Deadly Weekend Exposes Progressive Failure—and the Gun-Control Myth

Chicago's Juneteenth weekend bloodbath—seven dead, nearly 40 wounded—is a damning indictment of Democratic governance and the left's gun-control fantasy.

Over a single weekend, Chicago descended into chaos under Mayor Brandon Johnson's watch, with shootings leaving the city reeling (Fox News). Trump seized on the violence to shame Democratic Gov. J.B. Pritzker for inaction, calling on him to request federal assistance.

The response from Chicago's progressive leadership? Tone-deaf misdirection. Mayor Johnson faced ridicule for declaring a "state of emergency" over transfemicide violence while his city burned from gang and street violence (Fox News). Meanwhile, a Chicago priest demanded Trump "shut up" and restore gun-violence prevention funding—the same failed approach that hasn't stopped Chicago's decades-long murder crisis.

Here's what conservatives know: Chicago has some of the nation's strictest gun laws. It also has some of the nation's highest murder rates. The left's solution—more regulations, more spending on "prevention programs"—has failed spectacularly. What's missing isn't gun control; it's law enforcement, consequences for criminals, and leadership willing to name the real problem: lawlessness in Democrat-run cities.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether our own progressive leaders learn anything from Chicago's collapse—or double down on the same failed policies.
