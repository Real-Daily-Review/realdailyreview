# Maine Dem Senate Nominee Faces Family Ties Controversy as Campaign Shuffles Staff

A Maine Democratic Senate nominee is drawing fire over personal relationships and campaign finance entanglements that raise questions about Democratic vetting standards. According to the Daily Caller and Washington Examiner, Troy Jackson, the Democratic candidate in Maine's Senate race, fathered two children with his cousin—a detail that emerged as his campaign scrambled to distance itself from a fundraising official also tied to another progressive candidate's super PAC.

The Washington Examiner reported that Jackson's campaign manager, Peter Sarasohn, was simultaneously serving as treasurer of the main super PAC backing Michigan Senate candidate Abdul el Sayed. Jackson's campaign removed Sarasohn hours after the Examiner's inquiry, suggesting damage control rather than transparent governance.

Republicans argue the episode illustrates how Democratic campaigns operate with loose ethical standards and opaque fundraising networks. The family relationship revelation compounds questions about Jackson's judgment and the Democratic Party's due diligence on nominees.
