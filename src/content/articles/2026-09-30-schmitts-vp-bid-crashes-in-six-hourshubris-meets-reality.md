# Schmitt's VP Gambit Implodes in Six Hours—A Cautionary Tale for Ambitious Republicans

Sen. Eric Schmitt (R-MO) learned a hard lesson about premature political positioning: his shadow campaign for the 2028 vice presidency collapsed before lunch. According to the *Washington Examiner*, Axios published a favorable profile of the three-term senator early Wednesday morning, but the effort unraveled just hours later, undone by what observers called sheer hubris and poor political timing.

The episode underscores a broader Republican challenge heading into 2028: ambitious senators jockeying for national office risk looking desperate and out-of-touch when they leak favorable coverage to friendly media outlets. Schmitt's rapid retreat suggests party insiders and Trump allies weren't interested in the narrative Axios was selling.

For Colorado Republicans, the cautionary tale is clear: focus on delivering results in your current role rather than signaling ambition through the press. Voters and party leadership reward competence and loyalty, not self-promotion.

Watch whether other Republican senators attempt similar positioning—and how quickly the Trump orbit shuts them down.
