# Outgoing GOP Rep. Mace Pivots to YouTube After Political Losses

Rep. Nancy Mace (R-SC) is launching a new YouTube talk show, "Get Maced," as her congressional career winds down following recent electoral setbacks. The outgoing congresswoman has unveiled a heavily tattooed new look for the venture, displaying full sleeves she had largely kept private during her time in office (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire).

Mace, 48, has promoted the show through social media and promotional materials featuring her previously concealed arm tattoos. According to the Washington Examiner, she described the ink as giving her "the pain that I need to feel"—suggesting a personal reinvention as she exits electoral politics.

The move marks a notable shift for the South Carolina Republican, who gained attention as a firebrand conservative voice in Congress but faced political headwinds that appear to have accelerated her departure from the House.

**The Colorado angle:** While Mace represents South Carolina, her pivot to digital media reflects a broader trend among departing GOP figures seeking alternative platforms—a model some Colorado Republicans may consider as the state's political landscape continues to shift leftward.
