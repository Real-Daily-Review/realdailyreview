# Declassified FBI Files Expose Swalwell's Compromising Ties to Chinese Operative

Newly declassified FBI documents reveal that former California congressman Eric Swalwell—a vocal critic of Republican security practices—had illegal financial entanglements and a sexual relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence operative, raising serious questions about foreign influence on Capitol Hill.

The memos show the FBI discovered evidence that Swalwell received illegal donations from Christine Fang, known as "Fang Fang," a woman U.S. intelligence assessed as a Chinese spy attempting to cultivate influence among American officials (Just The News). Swalwell confessed to FBI agents in 2015 that he had "physical relations" with Fang on multiple occasions while serving in Congress (New York Post).

The relationship went undetected for years. The FBI initially attempted to recruit Fang as a source on Chinese influence operations before discovering her intelligence connections and her ties to Swalwell.

**The Colorado angle:** Swalwell served on the House Intelligence Committee during this period—the same panel overseeing counterintelligence operations. His compromise by a foreign operative while accepting illegal donations underscores conservative warnings about security vulnerabilities among progressive lawmakers who often downplay foreign threats while pushing aggressive investigations of Republicans.

Conservatives argue this case exemplifies why vetting and accountability matter; Democrats have largely remained silent on the revelations.

Watch for whether House Republicans demand a formal review of Swalwell's access to classified information during his compromised period.
