# Colorado Fire Crews Warn: Deep-Fried Turkey Can Turn Your Holiday Into a Disaster

North Metro Fire Rescue District put on a dramatic show this week—intentionally torching a turkey to prove a point about kitchen safety.

Firefighters demonstrated both proper and improper deep-frying techniques on Tuesday, with the wrong-way version erupting in flames to illustrate just how fast a turkey-frying accident can spiral out of control (KDVR). The message: one mistake with hot oil and your Thanksgiving dinner becomes a house fire.

Colorado homeowners planning to deep-fry this year should take note. Turkey-frying accidents send thousands to emergency rooms annually and destroy homes. The North Metro demonstration showed exactly why: oil temperature spikes, moisture from thawing poultry causes violent reactions, and a single oversight turns a holiday meal into a liability nightmare.

Fire departments across Colorado have run similar public-safety campaigns for years, recognizing that preventable kitchen fires spike during the holiday season. If you're set on deep-frying, follow manufacturer instructions precisely—keep the fryer outside, away from structures, never leave it unattended, and ensure your turkey is completely thawed.
