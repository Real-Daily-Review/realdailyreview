# Trump Administration to Admit More White South African Refugees

The Trump administration plans to significantly increase admissions of white South African refugees, raising the annual intake from roughly 7,500 to 17,500, according to PBS NewsHour and The Guardian.

The administration cites discrimination against Afrikaners and claims "unforeseen developments in South Africa created an emergency refugee situation," per The Guardian. Trump has repeatedly asserted that white Afrikaners face "white genocide"—a claim South Africa's government has rejected.

The policy shift marks a notable expansion of refugee admissions under an administration that has generally pursued restrictive immigration policies. The Guardian reports estimated resettlement costs at $100 million.

**Perspectives differ sharply:** Supporters of the policy argue it addresses genuine persecution of a vulnerable minority group. Critics contend Trump's "white genocide" framing is factually unfounded and that the emergency designation lacks credible basis. South Africa's government has furiously rebutted the genocide claims.

The move comes as the administration continues reshaping U.S. refugee and immigration policy in its second term. Watch for responses from South African officials and humanitarian organizations assessing whether the intake numbers and emergency justification withstand scrutiny.
