# Justice Alito Commits to Staying Put as GOP Fears Court's Future

Justice Samuel Alito has made clear he's not stepping down anytime soon—a move that matters enormously for Colorado conservatives worried about the Supreme Court's ideological balance.

Alito confirmed he intends to serve through the end of the current Court term, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire. The announcement comes as Republicans confront a sobering reality: the GOP's midterm performance has raised serious concerns about whether the party will control the Senate long enough to confirm conservative replacements if any current justices retire.

The stakes are real. If Democrats maintain Senate control, any vacancy on the Court could mean a liberal replacement—a nightmare scenario for conservatives who've fought for decades to build a constitutionally sound majority on issues from abortion to gun rights to religious liberty.

**The conservative argument:** Alito's commitment to stay is reassuring, but Republicans can't rely on current justices to outlast Democratic Senate control. The party needs to win back the Senate decisively in future elections to protect the Court's conservative wing.

**What to watch:** Whether other conservative justices make similar public commitments to remain on the bench, and whether GOP Senate candidates in 2024 make Court preservation a central campaign issue.
