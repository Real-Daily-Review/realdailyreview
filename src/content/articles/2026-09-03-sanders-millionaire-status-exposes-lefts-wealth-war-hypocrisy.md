# Bernie's Billionaire Hypocrisy Exposes Left's Wealth-Obsession Trap

Bernie Sanders has become a millionaire—and promptly pivoted his class-warfare rhetoric to attack billionaires instead, exposing the fundamental dishonesty of progressive wealth-targeting politics.

According to Complete Colorado, Sanders' shift from railing against "the millionaire class" to focusing exclusively on billionaires reveals a convenient goalpost-moving that undermines the left's entire anti-wealth crusade. The contradiction is stark: once Sanders crossed the millionaire threshold himself, the definition of "the problem" conveniently changed.

This matters for Colorado because it illustrates how progressive tax-and-spend ideology operates in practice. Democrats don't oppose wealth accumulation on principle—they oppose *other people's* wealth. When their own politicians become rich through book deals and political careers, the moral outrage mysteriously evaporates. Meanwhile, Colorado small-business owners and entrepreneurs face the real-world consequences of policies designed by millionaires who claim to despise the wealthy.

The left's wealth obsession, as Complete Colorado argues, rests on myth rather than economic reality. Targeting billionaires makes for good rhetoric but terrible policy—and Colorado taxpayers foot the bill when those policies translate into higher taxes, regulatory burdens, and slower economic growth.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from Sanders' hypocrisy or double down on billionaire-bashing as midterm messaging.
