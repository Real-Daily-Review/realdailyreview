# Kevin Warsh Sworn In as Federal Reserve Chair

Kevin Warsh was sworn in Friday as chair of the Federal Reserve, taking the helm of the central bank at a time when its independence faces heightened political scrutiny.

The ceremony took place at the White House, with Supreme Court Justice Clarence Thomas administering the oath. President Trump attended and said he wanted Warsh to "be totally independent" and "just do a great job," according to PBS NewsHour.

Warsh, who was confirmed by the Senate last week, has pledged to protect the Fed's autonomy. In remarks to lawmakers, he vowed never to "predetermine" interest rates at the president's request, CBS News reported.

The timing of Warsh's appointment comes as the Federal Reserve navigates a complex economic environment. Al Jazeera noted that the central bank's independence has "come under scrutiny amid political pressure"—a reference to Trump's past calls for lower interest rates and criticism of Federal Reserve decisions.

Warsh replaces Jerome Powell, whose term as chair ended. The new Fed chair will oversee monetary policy decisions that affect inflation, employment, and broader economic conditions.

Watch for Warsh's first policy decisions and any signals about the Fed's interest-rate trajectory in coming weeks.
