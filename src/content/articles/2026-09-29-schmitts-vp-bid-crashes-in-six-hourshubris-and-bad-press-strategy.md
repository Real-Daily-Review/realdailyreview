# Missouri Senator's VP Bid Implodes in Six Hours

Sen. Eric Schmitt (R-MO) learned a hard lesson in political humility this week: don't leak your ambitions to the press and expect them to stay buried.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Schmitt's shadow campaign for the 2028 vice presidency collapsed almost as quickly as it launched. Around 6 a.m., *Axios* published what the *Examiner* called a "puff piece" promoting the three-term senator as a potential running mate. By midday, the whole thing had unraveled—a cautionary tale in premature self-promotion and media manipulation gone wrong.

The *Examiner's* take was blunt: Schmitt's gambit was undone by "hubris" and a press corps eager to amplify political theater. The episode underscores a broader conservative frustration with how establishment Republicans sometimes court media attention for personal gain rather than focusing on legislative wins or party priorities.

For Colorado Republicans, the lesson is straightforward: focus on delivering results for your constituents, not positioning yourself for higher office through coordinated media drops. The voters notice the difference.

Watch whether other Republican senators learn from Schmitt's misstep or continue the same pattern of leaked ambitions and premature VP speculation.
