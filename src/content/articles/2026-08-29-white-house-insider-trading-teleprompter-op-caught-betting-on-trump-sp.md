# White House Insider Trading Case Shows Swamp Still Thriving Under Trump

A former White House teleprompter operator has been ordered to surrender roughly $100,000 in profits after federal regulators caught him using advance knowledge of presidential speeches to make prediction-market trades—a textbook case of insider abuse that undercuts promises to drain the swamp.

According to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission settlement reported by *Just The News*, the operator earned $107,500 between December 2025 and February 2026 by trading on language he knew would appear in Trump's remarks before the public did. The scheme is straightforward: access to classified information, personal profit, zero accountability until caught.

*9News Denver* reports that federal regulators claimed the teleprompter operator exploited his position to make profitable bets on prediction markets—essentially betting on outcomes he had insider knowledge of. The CFTC settlement requires him to disgorge most of the ill-gotten gains.

The case raises hard questions about White House vetting and internal controls. If a teleprompter operator can run an insider-trading scheme from the West Wing, what other staffers are exploiting classified access? Colorado taxpayers fund this apparatus and deserve assurance that government employees aren't using their positions for personal enrichment.

Watch whether the White House implements stricter conflict-of-interest rules and financial-disclosure requirements for all staff with access to advance presidential communications.
