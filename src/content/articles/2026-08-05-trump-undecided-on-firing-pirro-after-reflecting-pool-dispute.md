# Trump Weighs Pirro's Future After Reflecting Pool Clash

Trump is reportedly undecided on whether to retain U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro after a heated dispute over the Reflecting Pool, according to *Just The News*. When asked before boarding Air Force One whether he planned to fire Pirro, Trump said he "hasn't yet decided."

The Colorado Sun's Mike Littwin reports that Pirro brought documented evidence to the Oval Office challenging Trump's position on the matter—and notably, Trump did not immediately terminate her, despite reportedly being in "no mood to hear the facts."

The incident underscores tension within the Trump administration over how to handle internal disagreements. Conservatives watching this situation should note the broader question: whether Trump's team can maintain cohesion when senior officials push back on presidential directives, or whether dissent triggers swift personnel changes.

The outcome could signal how the administration handles future policy disputes and whether experienced officials like Pirro retain influence when they challenge the president directly.
