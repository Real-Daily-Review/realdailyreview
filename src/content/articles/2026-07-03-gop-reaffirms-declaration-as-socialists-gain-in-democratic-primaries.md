# Republican Pushes Declaration Reaffirmation as Socialism Gains in Democratic Primaries

As America approaches its 250th birthday, Rep. Matt Van Epps is forcing a debate about first principles—introducing legislation to reaffirm the Declaration of Independence, directly responding to what he sees as a troubling trend: socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primary races (Fox News Politics).

The move signals Republican concern that progressive politics has drifted so far left that core American founding documents need explicit congressional restatement. Van Epps's bill is a pointed reminder that the party of limited government, individual liberty, and free markets still claims the mantle of America's founding vision.

Democrats have not yet responded publicly to the legislation, but the underlying tension is clear: while Republicans invoke Jefferson and the Declaration's defense of unalienable rights and limited government, the left increasingly embraces expansive state power and wealth redistribution—positions fundamentally at odds with 1776's core message.

For Colorado conservatives, this is a proxy battle over whether America's founding principles—property rights, individual liberty, skepticism of centralized power—will survive the progressive assault on them. The question isn't academic: it shapes debates over gun rights, energy development, land use, and taxation that directly affect Colorado families and businesses.

Watch whether Van Epps's bill gains co-sponsors and whether any Democrats dare oppose it publicly without offering a competing vision of American founding principles.
