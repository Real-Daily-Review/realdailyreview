# Federal Charges Filed Against College Students Over Trump Rally Threats

College campuses are becoming staging grounds for left-wing activism that federal authorities now view as criminal. Three University of South Alabama students face federal charges over alleged social media threats against President Trump ahead of his campus rally in Mobile, according to Fox News Politics.

The charges underscore a troubling pattern: pro-Hamas and terrorism-linked activist groups operate openly on American college campuses with minimal consequences. The Washington Examiner reports that Students United for Palestinian Equality and Return UW (SUPER UW)—a group tied to pro-Hamas organizing—continues operating at the University of Washington despite the U.S. sanctioning its foreign affiliate as a terrorism front.

Republicans and conservatives argue that college administrators have abdicated their duty to maintain order and protect free speech from intimidation. They contend that progressive campus leadership tolerates radical left-wing activism while cracking down on conservative speech, creating a two-tiered system of accountability.

Democrats counter that student activism is protected political expression, though they rarely address why terrorism-linked groups operate with impunity on campuses.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's universities—CU Boulder, CSU, and others—host similar activist networks. Taxpayers should demand their boards of regents enforce consistent conduct standards and transparency about which groups operate on state-funded campuses.

Watch whether federal prosecutors expand charges beyond individual students to hold activist organizations accountable.
