# Texas Judge Removed in High-Profile Stabbing Case; Defendant Seeks New Trial

A Texas judge has been removed from the case of Karmelo Anthony, 19, who was sentenced to 35 years in prison for fatally stabbing Austin Metcalf at a high school track meet in 2025. Judge Sid Harle ruled that a reasonable outside observer could question whether Judge John Roach, who presided over Anthony's June trial, appeared impartial (Just The News). The removal bolsters Anthony's bid for a new trial.

The case has drawn scrutiny over judicial impartiality concerns. Anthony's legal team argued that Roach's conduct raised questions about fairness, prompting the removal request. Harle's decision to remove Roach suggests the appellate process found merit in those concerns, though the specific grounds for questioning impartiality were not detailed in available reporting.

Anthony's path forward now depends on whether a new trial is granted. The removal of the original judge typically signals a significant procedural setback for prosecutors and raises the stakes for retrial proceedings.
