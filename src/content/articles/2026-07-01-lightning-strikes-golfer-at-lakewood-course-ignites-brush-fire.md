# Lightning Strike at Lakewood Golf Course Injures Golfer, Sparks Brush Fire

A golfer learned the hard way why Colorado's afternoon thunderstorms demand respect: a lightning strike near the sixth hole at Fox Hollow Golf Course in Lakewood sent one person to the hospital with injuries, while simultaneously igniting a brush fire that drew West Metro Fire Rescue crews to the scene.

The incident underscores a reality Colorado residents know well—summer lightning is no joke in the high country and metro areas alike. The strike occurred during what appears to have been an afternoon thunderstorm, the kind that rolls across the Front Range with little warning.

West Metro Fire Rescue confirmed the golfer was transported to a local hospital with injuries from the strike. Crews responded to contain the brush fire sparked by the lightning, a common hazard during Colorado's dry season when vegetation is primed to ignite.

The good news: emergency responders were on scene quickly, and the fire was contained. The reminder: Colorado golfers and outdoor enthusiasts should take lightning warnings seriously and seek shelter when storms approach.
