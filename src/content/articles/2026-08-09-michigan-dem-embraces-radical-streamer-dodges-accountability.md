# Michigan Democrat's Radical Alliance Raises Red Flags for Voters

Michigan's Democratic Senate nominee Abdul el Sayed is banking on far-left streamer Hasan Piker's audience—but refusing to distance himself from Piker's inflammatory rhetoric, including comments suggesting America deserved 9/11.

On NBC's "Meet the Press," el Sayed dodged questions about Piker's past statements, claiming he cared about reaching the streamer's audience rather than addressing the controversial comments themselves. Republicans argue this evasion reveals poor judgment. "It isn't Islamophobia to say that Hasan Piker, the live streamer, who [has made incendiary remarks], raises legitimate questions about el Sayed's judgment," *Washington Examiner* senior editor Peter Laffin wrote, pushing back against Democratic claims that scrutiny amounts to unfair targeting.

The alliance underscores how far left the Democratic Party has drifted. El Sayed's willingness to campaign alongside someone with a track record of extreme rhetoric—while refusing to clearly repudiate those views—signals where his priorities lie: appealing to the socialist fringe rather than mainstream voters.

For Colorado conservatives watching Senate races nationwide, this is a cautionary tale about what happens when Democrats embrace their radical wing without pushback. Watch whether Michigan voters punish el Sayed at the ballot box for this calculation.
