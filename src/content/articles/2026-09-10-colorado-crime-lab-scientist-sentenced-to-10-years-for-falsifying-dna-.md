# Colorado Crime Lab Scientist Gets 10 Years for Falsifying DNA Evidence in Hundreds of Cases

A massive breach of public trust just landed a former Colorado Bureau of Investigation scientist in prison. Yvonne "Missy" Woods pleaded guilty to four felonies and was sentenced to 10 years for systematically falsifying DNA test results across hundreds of criminal cases—potentially freeing guilty defendants and destroying legitimate prosecutions.

Woods, who worked in the state crime lab, altered DNA samples without explanation, according to CPR News. The Colorado Sun reports she entered guilty pleas in June before her sentencing this week. Her misconduct has cast serious doubt on countless cases that relied on her lab work, creating a nightmare scenario for Colorado law enforcement and victims' families alike.

This isn't just a personnel failure—it's a catastrophic indictment of government oversight. Taxpayers funded a state agency that was supposed to ensure evidence integrity, yet one analyst operated unchecked long enough to compromise hundreds of investigations. Questions loom: How many guilty people walk free? How many innocent people were convicted based on her work? What systemic failures allowed this to happen?

Colorado's criminal justice system now faces years of case reviews and potential retrials, draining resources and reopening old wounds.

Conservatives and law-and-order advocates should demand a full audit of CBI protocols and accountability measures to prevent future sabotage of evidence.
