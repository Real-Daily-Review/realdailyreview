# Federal Judge Warned DOJ on Omar Raid—Constitutional Concerns Emerge

Unsealed court documents reveal a federal judge flagged serious constitutional risks before the DOJ raided Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington, D.C. home, according to Fox News. Judge Matthew Sharbaugh warned prosecutors that the search warrant could violate the Speech and Debate Clause—a constitutional protection shielding lawmakers from executive overreach on legislative matters.

The heavily redacted 169-page filing (per Breitbart) shows Homeland Security Investigations seized Omar's phone and passport in what court records indicate was part of a decades-long investigation. The judge's pre-raid concerns suggest the DOJ proceeded despite explicit constitutional warnings.

**The conservative angle:** This case underscores how government power can be weaponized against political opponents when judicial safeguards are sidelined. Even judges appointed to rubber-stamp warrants recognized the constitutional problems here. Republicans argue the Biden-Harris DOJ's willingness to raid a sitting congresswoman's home—over a judge's objections—demonstrates the politicization of federal law enforcement that conservatives have long warned about.

Democrats have remained largely silent on the specifics, focusing instead on Omar's foreign policy record rather than defending the raid's legality.
