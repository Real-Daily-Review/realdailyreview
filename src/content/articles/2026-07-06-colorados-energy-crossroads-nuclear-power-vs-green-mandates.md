# Colorado's Energy Future Hinges on Nuclear Power, Not Green Mandates

America's 250-year energy success came from embracing reliable, scalable power sources—and Colorado's economy depends on rejecting the Left's ideological war on fossil fuels and nuclear innovation.

According to the Daily Caller, nuclear energy represents the lesson of America's industrial dominance: affordable, abundant power that built the nation. Yet progressive policymakers continue strangling energy development through regulation and subsidy-driven mandates favoring unreliable renewables.

Colorado faces a critical choice. The state's economy—from manufacturing to data centers to agriculture—runs on cheap, dependable electricity. Democrats' push for rapid coal phase-outs and their resistance to next-generation nuclear plants threatens both jobs and grid stability. Republicans argue that energy policy should prioritize affordability and reliability over climate ideology.

National Review warns that today's younger generation increasingly embraces socialist economics, which would accelerate government control over energy markets and impose punitive carbon taxes that would devastate Colorado's working families and small businesses.

Conservatives contend that Colorado should lead on energy innovation by permitting advanced nuclear reactors, protecting natural gas infrastructure, and rejecting federal overreach that strangles domestic production.
