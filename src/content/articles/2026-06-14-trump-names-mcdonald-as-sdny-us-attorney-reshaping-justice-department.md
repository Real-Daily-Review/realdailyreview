# Trump Names James McDonald U.S. Attorney for Powerful SDNY Post

President Trump is moving to install a seasoned conservative litigator in one of the nation's most consequential federal prosecutor roles—the U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York—signaling his intent to reshape the Justice Department with trusted allies.

Trump announced his intention to appoint James M. McDonald, a senior partner at Sullivan &amp; Cromwell and former law clerk to Chief Justice John G. Roberts Jr., to replace Jay Clayton, who is departing to serve as director of national intelligence (Fox News). McDonald holds a Harvard College degree and graduated from the University of Virginia School of Law (Just The News).

The SDNY post carries outsized influence over financial crimes, securities fraud, and high-profile prosecutions affecting Wall Street and Manhattan's elite. McDonald's appointment reflects Trump's broader effort to install loyalists throughout the executive branch—a strategy conservatives view as necessary to restore institutional integrity after what they argue was weaponized prosecutorial overreach under the Biden administration.

For Colorado, the appointment has limited direct bearing, though conservatives should monitor how the SDNY office handles cases involving progressive activists and whether the Justice Department pursues investigations into alleged misconduct by federal agencies that affected Colorado energy development and water rights.

Watch whether Senate Republicans swiftly confirm McDonald and whether the appointment signals a broader realignment of federal prosecutorial priorities.
