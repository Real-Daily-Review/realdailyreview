# Pentagon Releases Second Batch of Classified UFO Files Under Trump Order

The Pentagon released 64 previously classified files related to UFO sightings on Friday, marking the second tranche of records made public under an executive order by President Trump, according to CBS News and Al Jazeera.

The files include documents and videos from Pentagon observations that have drawn reactions of surprise from officials. CBS News quoted one source as saying they were "speechless after these observations," though the specific contents and nature of the newly released materials remain unclear from the available reports.

The releases follow Trump's directive to declassify UFO-related records, part of a broader effort to increase transparency around unidentified aerial phenomena. The administration has not detailed what conclusions, if any, the Pentagon has drawn from the observations contained in the files.

The move continues a years-long shift toward public disclosure of UFO incidents, which the Pentagon now refers to as unidentified anomalous phenomena. Previous releases have included military pilot accounts and sensor data from encounters with unexplained objects.
