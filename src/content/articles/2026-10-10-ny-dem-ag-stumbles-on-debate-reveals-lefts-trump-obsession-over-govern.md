# New York AG Debate Exposes Left's Obsession With Trump Over Governing

New York Democratic Attorney General Letitia James stumbled badly in Thursday's debate when asked to name a former AG she admired—drawing several seconds of silence before pivoting awkwardly (Fox News). Her Republican challenger, meanwhile, delivered what observers called a sharp contrast: when asked what she'd recently read, James cited a January 6 memoir by Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD), while her GOP opponent named a children's book she reads to her toddler (The Daily Wire).

The exchange crystallizes a core conservative critique: progressive Democrats remain fixated on Trump-era grievances while neglecting bread-and-butter governance. James, who has spent considerable political capital on Trump-related litigation, appeared unprepared to articulate her own prosecutorial philosophy or admiration for legal predecessors.

For Colorado voters, the lesson cuts close. State Democrats have similarly prioritized culture-war litigation and progressive activism over kitchen-table issues—energy policy, water rights, business regulation—that directly affect working families and job creators. When Democratic officials can't name their governing heroes, it signals priorities are elsewhere.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic AG and state officials face similar accountability on what they've actually accomplished for taxpayers versus what investigations they've launched.
