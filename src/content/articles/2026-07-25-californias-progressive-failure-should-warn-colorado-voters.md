# California's Election Shows Perils of Ideology Over Results—Colorado Must Take Note

California voters just doubled down on the progressive policies that have made their state a cautionary tale of government overreach, and Colorado conservatives should pay attention to what happens next.

The Washington Examiner reports that California's election results demonstrate a troubling pattern: when ideology trumps outcomes, failure becomes irrelevant. Voters in the nation's most left-leaning state rejected course correction despite years of deteriorating conditions—homelessness, business flight, regulatory burden, and fiscal mismanagement.

The conservative argument is straightforward: California proves that progressive governance prioritizes ideological purity over practical results. When voters won't hold their leaders accountable for failure, government spending and regulation expand unchecked, harming the very people they claim to help.

Colorado faces a similar crossroads. The state has trended blue in recent elections, and progressive policies—from energy restrictions to gun regulations to tax increases—are gaining traction. If Colorado follows California's path, Colorado taxpayers and energy workers will pay the price through higher costs, fewer jobs, and diminished opportunity.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Colorado must learn from California's mistakes: ideology-driven policy fails. Limited government, free markets, and accountability work.

Watch whether Colorado's 2024 legislative session embraces California-style progressive expansion or pivots toward pro-growth, limited-government reforms.
