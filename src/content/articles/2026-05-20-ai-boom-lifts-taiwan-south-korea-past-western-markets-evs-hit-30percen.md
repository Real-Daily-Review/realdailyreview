# Global Markets Shift as AI Boom Reshapes Rankings

Taiwan and South Korea are surging past long-established Western economies in global stock-market rankings, driven by the artificial intelligence boom, according to CNBC. The reshuffling reflects how AI investments are reordering the hierarchy of major markets worldwide.

Separately, electric vehicles are gaining ground globally. The International Energy Agency reported that nearly 30% of vehicles sold worldwide this year will be electric, with global EV sales expected to reach 23 million units—exceeding last year's growth rate of 20%, according to the Washington Examiner.

The twin developments underscore a broader economic transition: Asian tech-heavy markets are capitalizing on AI demand, while the automotive sector is undergoing a structural shift toward electrification. Taiwan and South Korea's gains reflect their dominance in semiconductor manufacturing and chip design, sectors central to AI infrastructure.

The EV milestone signals accelerating adoption of cleaner vehicles, though the market remains concentrated in developed economies and China. Watch for how Western markets respond to competitive pressure from Asia, and whether EV adoption rates continue accelerating in emerging markets.
