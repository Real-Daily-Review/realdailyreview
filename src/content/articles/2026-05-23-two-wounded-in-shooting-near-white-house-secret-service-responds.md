# Shooting Near White House Leaves Two Wounded

Two people were wounded in a shooting near the White House on Saturday evening, prompting a lockdown of the complex and an immediate response from Secret Service.

According to law enforcement sources cited by CBS News, a man armed with a pistol opened fire near 17th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue NW. Secret Service returned fire in response, according to The Hill's reporting via NewsNation. The incident resulted in two people being wounded, including a possible suspect, sources told CBS News.

The shooting occurred while reporters were present on White House grounds. CBS News reporters said they heard several shots and were immediately directed by Secret Service to take cover, with staff rushed into the press briefing room, according to The Hill.

A Secret Service spokesperson told the Washington Examiner the agency "is gathering information and will have more on the incident shortly." The U.S. Secret Service is leading the investigation into the reported gunfire, according to Al Jazeera.

The White House was secured following the incident. FBI Director Kash Patel acknowledged the shooting on social media immediately after it occurred, according to the Washington Examiner.

Watch for official statements from Secret Service and law enforcement detailing the suspect's identity and the circumstances that led to the exchange of gunfire.
