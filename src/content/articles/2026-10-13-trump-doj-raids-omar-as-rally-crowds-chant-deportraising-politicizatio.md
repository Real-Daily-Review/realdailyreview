# Trump DOJ Raids Omar's Home as Rally Crowds Demand Deportation

President Trump used a federal raid on Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington home to fuel campaign rhetoric, with Ohio rally crowds chanting "deport" as he attacked the Minnesota Democrat—raising questions about the politicization of law enforcement that conservatives themselves are divided over.

Vice President JD Vance told reporters the administration had no advance notice of the Monday morning search, according to The Daily Wire, suggesting the DOJ acted independently. But the timing—hours before Trump's public attack on Omar—fueled concerns about weaponized justice.

National Review's conservative editors expressed skepticism of both sides: the Omar investigation's optics and the inflammatory political response. Republicans have long criticized Omar's anti-Israel rhetoric and campaign finance practices, but using federal raids as campaign fodder risks the very government overreach conservatives claim to oppose.

The raid's legal basis remains unclear from public statements. If the DOJ is investigating genuine campaign finance violations, that's legitimate law enforcement. If it's retribution dressed in prosecutorial robes, it validates progressive claims that Trump weaponizes the Justice Department—a charge that undermines conservative arguments for limited, neutral government.

Colorado Republicans should watch whether this investigation produces actual charges or becomes a political sideshow that damages the credibility of future DOJ actions.
