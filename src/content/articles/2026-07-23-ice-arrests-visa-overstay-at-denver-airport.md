# ICE Detains Visa Overstay at Denver Airport

Immigration enforcement worked as intended when federal agents arrested a 27-year-old woman accused of overstaying her visa while boarding a Southwest Airlines flight at Denver International Airport this week.

ICE confirmed that Chantal Alejandra Morales Rojas was detained on the jetway after attempting to board, according to 9News Denver. The agency stated she entered the country on a valid visa that has since expired—a straightforward immigration violation that underscores why border security and visa tracking matter.

The arrest sparked emotional reactions from onlookers at DIA, as reported by KDVR Fox31 Denver, but the core issue remains: federal law requires foreign nationals to maintain valid immigration status. When someone overstays a visa, they are in the country illegally, regardless of circumstances or sympathies.
