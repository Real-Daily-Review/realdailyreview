# Minnesota Governor's Mining Ban Risks U.S. Energy Independence, Conservatives Warn

Gov. Tim Walz's executive order blocking mining permits near Minnesota's Boundary Waters has drawn sharp Republican fire over national security grounds. Rep. Tom Emmer (R-MN) called the move "stupid," arguing it surrenders critical mineral production to China at a moment when domestic supply chains remain dangerously dependent on foreign sources (Fox News).

**The conservative case:** Republicans contend that blocking domestic mining for rare earth elements and other key minerals weakens American manufacturing, defense capabilities, and energy independence. With China controlling much of global mineral processing, they argue, environmental restrictions that halt U.S. extraction hand geopolitical leverage to Beijing.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado sits atop significant rare earth and mineral deposits. If the Biden-Harris administration follows Walz's precedent—restricting mining on federal lands or near sensitive areas—Colorado energy workers, mining operations, and the state's resource-based economy face direct pressure. Walz's order signals the left's willingness to sacrifice domestic production capacity for environmental activism, a pattern that threatens Colorado's economic base.

Democrats typically frame such restrictions as environmental protection, but conservatives see them as ideological overreach that damages American competitiveness and jobs.

Watch whether the incoming Trump administration reverses similar federal mining restrictions and whether Colorado's congressional delegation pushes back against Biden-era environmental rules that block resource development.
