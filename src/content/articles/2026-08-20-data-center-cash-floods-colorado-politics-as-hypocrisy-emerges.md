# Data Center Backlash Hits Colorado as Tech Money Floods Politics

Colorado faces mounting pressure over data center expansion as tech industry cash floods state politics—and politicians on both sides are caught in the crossfire.

National Review reports widespread public anxiety about data centers, noting that despite extensive efforts to address concerns, skepticism persists (National Review). Meanwhile, the Daily Caller reveals that politicians attacking data center interests often receive substantial funding from the same industry themselves, exposing what conservatives see as rank hypocrisy in the debate (Daily Caller).

For Colorado, the stakes are real. Data centers promise jobs and tax revenue but demand massive water and power resources—scarce commodities in the state. Republicans argue that blanket opposition to data centers ignores their economic benefits and energy reliability contributions. Democrats counter that unchecked expansion threatens Colorado's water supply and rural character.

The deeper conservative concern: politicians accepting tech industry donations while publicly opposing data center projects signals either corruption or cowardice. Coloradans deserve transparency about who's funding their elected officials and why.

Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado's legislature demands full disclosure of data center industry contributions to state candidates—and whether politicians will actually follow the money.
