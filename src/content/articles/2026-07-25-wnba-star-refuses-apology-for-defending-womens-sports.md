# WNBA Star Cunningham Stands Firm Against Woke Sports Ideology

A professional athlete willing to state biological reality without apology is becoming rare—and refreshing. Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham has doubled down on her common-sense position that men should not compete in women's sports, brushing off predictable outrage from the progressive sports media establishment.

After her initial comments sparked the usual social-media firestorm from the left, Cunningham posted to Instagram Thursday with a defiant message: "I said what I said," accompanied by a saluting emoji (The Daily Wire). She's refusing to grovel or walk back her stance, a stark contrast to the cowardice we've seen from corporate sponsors and league officials who cave to activist pressure.

Cunningham's willingness to speak plainly about sex-based fairness in athletics reflects what most Americans actually believe—that women's sports exist to protect opportunities for female athletes. Yet the progressive left continues to weaponize "inclusivity" language to erase biological sex distinctions, all while claiming the moral high ground.

This isn't a Colorado-specific issue, but it matters to Centennial State parents, coaches, and female athletes watching their own opportunities shrink under similar ideological pressure in schools and recreational leagues statewide.

Watch whether other athletes find the courage to follow Cunningham's lead, or whether corporate fear keeps the sports world locked in progressive groupthink.
