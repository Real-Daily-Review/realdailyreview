# Colorado's Heat Pump Rebate: Another Green Mandate Dressed as Consumer Choice

Colorado is rolling out a $1,500 rebate for heat pump installations across the nine-county Denver metro region, available to homeowners and businesses retrofitting existing buildings. The program also includes free energy advising (CPR News).

**The conservative concern:** This is government picking winners and losers in the energy market. Rather than letting Coloradans choose their own heating solutions based on cost and efficiency, the state is using taxpayer dollars to subsidize one technology—heat pumps—over natural gas, propane, or other alternatives. It's a backdoor way to phase out reliable fossil fuels without a direct vote.

Republicans and free-market advocates argue that if heat pumps made economic sense, homeowners wouldn't need government bribes to install them. The real cost—including grid upgrades, reliability concerns in Colorado winters, and higher upfront expenses—gets buried while the rebate takes center stage.

**The Colorado angle:** This targets the Front Range, where state regulators and Denver's progressive leadership have been aggressive on emissions mandates. Expect this to expand statewide, raising costs for rural Coloradans who depend on affordable heating and have fewer alternatives.

Watch whether this rebate program expands beyond the metro area or morphs into a ban on gas furnaces—the left's endgame in states like California.
