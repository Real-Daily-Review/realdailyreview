# Federal Funding Scandal: Taxpayer Cash Flowed to CCP Training, Democrat Candidate's Seven-Year Student Gig

Colorado taxpayers should be outraged: federal dollars may have bankrolled the education of over 1,500 future managers for Chinese state-owned enterprises, according to a Daily Caller investigation. Meanwhile, the Daily Wire reports that a Democratic congressional candidate spent seven years as a "professional student" on the taxpayer dime while claiming financial hardship motivated her run for office.

The CCP education pipeline raises serious national security questions. Republicans argue that American universities receiving federal funding should face strict scrutiny over partnerships with hostile foreign regimes. Democrats have largely remained silent on the specifics, though they typically defend academic freedom and international exchange programs.

Conservatives say this exemplifies how the left talks about helping ordinary Americans while enriching themselves through the bureaucratic machine.

For Colorado: watch whether our delegation demands audits of federal education funding flowing to universities with CCP ties, and whether state lawmakers push for transparency in how federal student aid is spent.
