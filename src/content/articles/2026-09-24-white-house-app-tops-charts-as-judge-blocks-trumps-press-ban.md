# White House App Surges as Trump Restricts Press Access—Then Loses Court Battle

The White House's official app has rocketed to the top of Apple's News category, according to Just The News, as the Trump administration simultaneously launched "TRUMP TV," a YouTube livestream featuring administration highlights. But the victory proved short-lived: a federal judge ordered the White House to restore press credentials for CNN, MS NOW, and Politico after Trump had banned them as "fake news" outlets.

The competing moves reveal the administration's dual strategy—promoting its own media channels while restricting traditional press access. Conservatives argue the mainstream media's coverage of Trump has been relentlessly hostile, justifying skepticism of outlets that have repeatedly misreported on the administration. Republicans counter that banning credentialed journalists from the White House briefing room crosses a constitutional line, even if the outlets are unfair.

The judge's ruling underscores a fundamental tension: while Trump supporters champion the right to build alternative media platforms and bypass hostile press, federal courts have consistently held that the First Amendment protects journalists' access to government proceedings, regardless of editorial slant.

**What to watch:** Whether the administration appeals the ruling or accepts the decision—and whether it signals a broader retreat from press restrictions or a pivot to other methods of controlling the narrative.
