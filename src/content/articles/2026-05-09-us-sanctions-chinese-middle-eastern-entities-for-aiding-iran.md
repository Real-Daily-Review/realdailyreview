# U.S. Sanctions Chinese, Middle Eastern Entities for Aiding Iran

The U.S. imposed sanctions on 11 entities and three individuals across Iran, China, Belarus, and the United Arab Emirates for allegedly assisting Tehran's military operations, according to CNBC.

The State Department said the targeted groups provided satellite imagery that enabled Iran to strike American forces in the Middle East, according to the Financial Times. The sanctions represent an escalation in U.S. efforts to disrupt Iran's ability to conduct strikes against American personnel and interests in the region.

The move targets both Chinese companies and Middle Eastern intermediaries accused of facilitating the flow of intelligence to Iranian military planners. Officials did not immediately detail the specific sanctions mechanisms—whether asset freezes, trade restrictions, or other penalties—or name all entities involved.

The action comes amid heightened tensions between the U.S. and Iran over Tehran's ballistic missile and drone capabilities. Iran has previously acknowledged conducting strikes on American bases in Iraq and Syria, citing retaliation for U.S. military actions in the region.

Watch for whether China responds diplomatically to sanctions on its companies, and whether the U.S. expands the designations to additional entities.
