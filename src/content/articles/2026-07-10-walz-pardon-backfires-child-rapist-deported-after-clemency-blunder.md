# Rubio Confirms: Walz-Pardoned Child Rapist Deported After Minnesota Governor's Reckless Clemency

Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz's decision to pardon an illegal alien convicted of repeatedly sexually assaulting a 10-year-old girl has backfired spectacularly—and exposed the dangers of progressive clemency politics.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced Friday that the offender, Tou Lue Vang, has been deported after Walz's pardon removed the criminal conviction that triggered his deportation order (Washington Examiner). The three-person Minnesota Board of Pardons, which Walz sat on, approved the clemency in June—just days before the deportation machinery could proceed.

Republicans argue Walz's pardon exemplifies how soft-on-crime Democratic governance endangers children and undermines immigration enforcement. The case underscores a broader conservative concern: when governors and pardon boards prioritize rehabilitation narratives over victim protection, violent criminals—especially those here illegally—slip through the cracks.

For Colorado, this serves as a cautionary tale. State pardon and clemency decisions carry real consequences. Coloradans should scrutinize how Gov. Jared Polis's administration handles similar cases and whether clemency is being granted to violent offenders with inadequate vetting.

Watch whether this embarrassment prompts Democrats to tighten pardon standards or whether progressive clemency advocates double down on their ideology.
