# Blue Wave Sweeps Statehouses While Colorado Watches Its Own Borders

Democrats notched decisive gubernatorial wins across six states Tuesday, flipping Wisconsin, Texas, Ohio, and California while holding Pennsylvania and New Mexico—a stark reminder that Republican weakness in suburban and urban corridors remains a structural problem heading into 2028.

David Crowley (Fox News) defeated Wisconsin Rep. Tom Tiffany by tying him to Trump's tariffs. Gina Hinojosa (Fox News) ousted Texas Gov. Greg Abbott by campaigning against school vouchers and corporate influence. Amy Acton won Ohio (Breitbart), Josh Shapiro secured Pennsylvania (Breitbart), Xavier Becerra took California (Breitbart), and Deb Haaland—Biden's Interior Secretary—claimed New Mexico (Breitbart).

Republicans argue these losses reflect candidate quality and messaging failures rather than ideological rejection. The tariff and Trump-association attacks proved potent in swing states, suggesting GOP candidates struggled to defend or distance themselves effectively.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Republicans should study these defeats closely. Democrats used anti-Trump messaging and education/voucher opposition to peel suburban voters. With Colorado's own 2026 gubernatorial race looming, GOP strategists must decide whether to embrace or recalibrate Trump alignment—and whether education policy remains a liability in purple precincts.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans learn these lessons or repeat them.
