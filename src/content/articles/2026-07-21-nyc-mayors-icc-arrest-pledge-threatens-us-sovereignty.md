# NYC Mayor's ICC Arrest Pledge Exposes Globalist Overreach—And Threatens American Sovereignty

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's repeated promises to arrest Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on behalf of the International Criminal Court represent a dangerous surrender of American sovereignty to unaccountable foreign bureaucrats, according to conservative critics.

The ICC, which the United States has never joined, claims authority to prosecute American officials and servicemen anyway—a power Secretary of State Marco Rubio is now working to dismantle. Mamdani's pledge to enforce ICC warrants violates U.S. law and treats a globalist organization as superior to American constitutional authority, the *Washington Examiner* argues.

For Colorado conservatives, this episode illustrates a broader pattern: progressive politicians subordinating American interests to international bodies and ideological commitments that bypass voters and constitutional limits. Mamdani's stance treats the ICC's authority as legitimate despite the U.S. never ratifying its jurisdiction—a troubling precedent for any American official, let alone a major city's mayor.

The real question isn't whether Netanyahu should face arrest, but whether American mayors should pledge allegiance to foreign courts over their own Constitution. Conservatives say no.

Watch whether other Democratic officials distance themselves from Mamdani's position or quietly embrace it—a telling indicator of how far the progressive left will go to subordinate national sovereignty to international progressivism.
