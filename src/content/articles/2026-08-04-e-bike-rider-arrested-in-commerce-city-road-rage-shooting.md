# E-Bike Road Rage Shooting in Commerce City Highlights Colorado's Growing Urban Crime Problem

A Commerce City man riding an e-bike was arrested Monday after allegedly shooting another man in the head during a road rage confrontation, leaving the victim critically injured (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31). The incident occurred near the 6200 block of East 62nd Avenue.

The shooting underscores a troubling trend: Colorado's urban centers are experiencing escalating violent crime, even as progressive prosecutors and local officials push soft-on-crime policies that prioritize rehabilitation over accountability. Commerce City, like Denver proper, has seen prosecutors decline to pursue serious charges aggressively, and bail reform policies have made it easier for dangerous offenders to remain on streets pending trial.

While Democrats argue that addressing "root causes" like poverty and mental health will reduce violence, conservatives point out that criminals—armed or otherwise—must face swift, certain consequences. Colorado's permissive approach to criminal justice has coincided with rising violent crime rates that threaten public safety and quality of life for law-abiding residents and businesses.

The arrest is a start, but taxpayers should watch whether Commerce City prosecutors pursue maximum charges and whether bail is set appropriately to keep a violent suspect off the streets pending trial.
