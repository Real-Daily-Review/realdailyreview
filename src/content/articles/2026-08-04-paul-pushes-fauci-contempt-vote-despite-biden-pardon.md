# Rand Paul Moves to Hold Fauci in Contempt—Biden Pardon Won't Stop It

Sen. Rand Paul (R-KY), chairman of the Senate Homeland Security Committee, is pressing forward with a contempt-of-Congress vote against Dr. Anthony Fauci despite the former NIAID director's presidential pardon from Joe Biden, according to reports from *9News Denver* and the *Washington Examiner*.

Paul's committee will vote to refer Fauci to the Department of Justice for contempt, effectively bypassing a full Senate floor vote. Republicans argue Fauci improperly invoked his Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination during congressional testimony—a move they say obstructed legitimate oversight of pandemic policy decisions that shaped lockdowns, school closures, and federal health mandates affecting millions of Americans, including Colorado families and businesses.

The Biden pardon shields Fauci from criminal prosecution for any underlying conduct, but Republicans contend it does not erase the contempt charge itself or prevent DOJ referral. Conservatives say the move represents accountability for a government official who wielded extraordinary power during COVID-19 without adequate congressional scrutiny.

Democrats have largely remained silent on the matter, with the left characterizing the effort as partisan retribution rather than legitimate oversight.

**Watch:** whether the DOJ under the new administration actually pursues charges, or whether this becomes a symbolic Republican statement on executive overreach.
