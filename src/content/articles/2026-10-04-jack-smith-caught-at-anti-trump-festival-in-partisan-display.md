# Former Special Counsel Jack Smith Caught at Anti-Trump Music Festival in Partisan Display

Former Special Counsel Jack Smith abandoned any pretense of impartiality Saturday when he appeared at a left-wing "Power to the People" music festival in Maryland wearing a Bruce Springsteen-inspired "No Kings" shirt—an anti-Trump message shirt—while mingling with celebrities including Dave Grohl, according to photos shared online (Breitbart).

Trump responded sharply to the images, calling Smith a "Crooked Prosecutor" and labeling him "Deranged" (Just The News). The appearance underscores conservative concerns about the weaponization of the Justice Department under the Biden-Harris administration and the politicization of federal prosecutors who should maintain strict neutrality.

Smith led the special counsel investigation into Trump's handling of classified documents and January 6th. Republicans have long argued that Smith's prosecutions were politically motivated and part of a broader effort to sideline Trump as a political rival rather than pursue genuine justice. The festival appearance—headlined by Springsteen, who railed against ICE enforcement (Breitbart)—reinforces that narrative by showing Smith openly associating with anti-Trump activists and entertainers rather than maintaining the professional distance expected of a federal prosecutor.

The optics are damaging to the credibility of federal law enforcement and validate conservative warnings about institutional bias at Justice.

Watch whether congressional Republicans call for investigations into Smith's conduct and whether media outlets covering Trump's legal cases acknowledge this apparent partisan alignment.
