# Former Boulder Deputy Arrested After Shooting at Law Enforcement Buildings

A former Boulder County Sheriff's Office deputy has been arrested following suspected shootings at the Boulder County Sheriff's Office headquarters and Broomfield Police Department, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

Tyler Reilly resigned from the Boulder County Sheriff's Office in 2024 in lieu of termination for cause, the sheriff's office confirmed (CPR News). He was taken into custody after firing rounds at both facilities overnight Monday.

The incident raises questions about law enforcement vetting and personnel management in Boulder County. Conservatives have long argued that government agencies need tighter accountability measures and clearer protocols for removing problematic employees before situations escalate. The fact that Reilly was allowed to resign rather than face formal termination—a common practice that protects agency reputation at taxpayers' expense—underscores the need for transparent, consequence-driven personnel systems in public agencies.

Details remain limited, but the case highlights vulnerabilities in how law enforcement handles internal discipline and separation procedures. Colorado taxpayers deserve to know whether proper safeguards were in place and why a deputy facing termination for cause was permitted an exit that may have obscured the full record.
