# Boulder's Climate Lawsuit Against Oil Giants Heads to Supreme Court—With Justices Skeptical

Boulder and Boulder County are asking the U.S. Supreme Court to let them sue Exxon Mobil and Suncor Energy for damages tied to climate change impacts, including costs from the Marshall wildfire. But the justices appeared wary during oral arguments, with key members questioning whether cities and states should be able to pursue such sweeping litigation against energy companies over a global issue (Colorado Sun, 9News Denver).

**The conservative concern:** Republicans and energy advocates argue this represents "climate lawfare"—using courts to impose costs on the oil and gas industry that voters and legislatures won't approve through normal channels. The Trump administration has urged the Court to reject the case entirely (CPR News). Conservatives worry that allowing such suits would expose Colorado energy workers and companies to endless liability and chill domestic energy production at a time when American oil and gas development should be encouraged, not punished.

**What's at stake:** A ruling for Boulder could trigger a wave of similar lawsuits nationwide, destabilizing the energy sector and raising costs for Colorado businesses and consumers. The Court is unlikely to issue an opinion for several months (CPR News).

**Watch for:** Whether the Supreme Court sides with energy producers or opens the courthouse door to municipal climate litigation that could reshape Colorado's energy economy.
