# Maine Democrat's Sexual Misconduct Allegations Mount as Party Distances Itself

Democratic Maine Senate nominee Graham Platner faces mounting sexual misconduct allegations that have prompted his own party to abandon him. An ex-girlfriend, Lyndsey Fifield, accused Platner on Tuesday of repeatedly removing condoms without her consent during their 2013–2015 relationship in Washington, D.C., according to *The Washington Post*. Fifield's allegations follow a separate bombshell report Monday in which another ex-girlfriend accused Platner of sexual assault—claims that triggered Democratic institutions and party figures to rescind their endorsements.

The cascade of allegations underscores a persistent problem: Democratic leaders' selective application of accountability standards. When allegations surface against Republican candidates, the left demands immediate withdrawal and swift condemnation. Yet when Democrats face similar or worse accusations, the party's response has historically been slower and more equivocal—though Platner's case shows some willingness to cut ties when political damage becomes unavoidable.
