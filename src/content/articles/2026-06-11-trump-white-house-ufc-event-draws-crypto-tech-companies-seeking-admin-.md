# Trump White House UFC Event Draws Crypto, Tech Companies Seeking Administration Access

President Trump is hosting UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn Sunday—a spectacle that reveals which corporations are betting on his second term.

The event has attracted major logos from crypto, tech, and alternative finance firms that have cultivated close ties to Trump's political movement, according to the *Washington Examiner*. These companies see the White House octagon as prime real estate to align themselves with the administration and gain influence on policy.

The weekend includes a press conference Friday at the Lincoln Memorial and live viewing on the National Mall, per *Just The News*.

**The conservative angle:** This event underscores Trump's willingness to blur traditional White House decorum—using the presidential residence as a corporate branding platform. While some conservatives celebrate Trump's unconventional style and his appeal to younger, tech-savvy voters, others worry about the optics of corporate sponsors essentially buying face time with the president on federal property. The crypto and fintech companies bidding for cage space are precisely the industries seeking lighter regulation from a Trump administration.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether this White House-corporate partnership model extends to energy, agriculture, or Second Amendment-friendly industries—and whether it sets a precedent for future administrations to monetize the people's house.
