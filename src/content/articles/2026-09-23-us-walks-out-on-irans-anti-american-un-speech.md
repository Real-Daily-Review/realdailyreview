# U.S. Delegation Walks Out on Iran's Anti-American Tirade at UN

America's diplomatic representatives did what too few do anymore: they refused to sit and listen to an adversary's lies. During Wednesday's UN General Assembly session, the U.S. delegation walked out as Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian accused America and Israel of terrorism while claiming Iran acts only in self-defense—a claim so absurd that audible boos erupted from the crowd before he even spoke (The Daily Wire).

The walkout signals a welcome return to clarity in U.S. foreign policy. For years, the Obama and Biden administrations bent over backward to appease Tehran, culminating in the catastrophic Iran nuclear deal that enriched the regime while leaving America less safe. Conservatives have long argued that moral clarity—not endless negotiation with hostile regimes—protects American interests.

Iran's nuclear ambitions, ballistic missile programs, and support for terrorist proxies across the Middle East remain existential threats. The regime's rhetoric at the UN changes nothing about its intentions.

Colorado voters should watch whether our congressional delegation—particularly those in purple districts—stands firm on Iran policy or caves to the progressive wing's appetite for renewed appeasement talks.
