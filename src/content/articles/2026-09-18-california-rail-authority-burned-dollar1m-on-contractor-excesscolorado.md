# California's High-Speed Rail Boondoggle: A Warning for Colorado Taxpayers

California's High-Speed Rail Authority squandered more than $1 million on unjustified travel expenses—private jets, premium airline tickets, and cigar lounges—according to an inspector general report cited by *Just The News*. The waste underscores a brutal truth: government-run megaprojects attract bureaucratic excess and contractor padding that taxpayers foot.

Colorado should take note. As state lawmakers and transit advocates push rail and transit expansion, this California cautionary tale reveals how quickly oversight collapses when government controls the purse strings. Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue that bloated public-sector projects inevitably breed waste because there's no market discipline—no profit motive to cut costs or fire incompetent managers.

The rail authority's lavish spending also coincides with broader California dysfunction driving businesses out. *Breitbart* reports that Paramount Skydance is relocating to Nashville, joining a exodus of companies fleeing Democrat-run states with high taxes, regulation, and government mismanagement.

Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small businesses face similar pressures from progressive taxation and regulatory overreach. The lesson: when government controls infrastructure, waste follows—and when taxes and red tape climb, businesses leave.

Watch for any Colorado push to expand state-run transit or rail projects; demand independent audits and strict contractor oversight before a dime is spent.
