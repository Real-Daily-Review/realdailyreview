# Voters Reject Socialist Label as Election Nears; GOP Messaging Gains Traction

Ahead of the midterm elections, Americans are souring on socialism—a shift Republicans have deliberately engineered through months of messaging that ties the ideology to Democratic Party leadership. According to a new poll cited by the *Washington Examiner*, voter skepticism of socialist policies is rising as President Trump and GOP figures have consistently warned that socialists are gaining influence within Democratic ranks.

The timing matters. With elections days away, Republicans are capitalizing on public unease over progressive economic proposals—a strategy that resonates particularly in Colorado, where voters in swing districts have shown sensitivity to tax-and-spend messaging and regulatory overreach.

Meanwhile, election integrity concerns are surfacing elsewhere. Alaska's GOP Sen. Dan Sullivan saw his campaign account temporarily suspended on X for appearing "inauthentic," even as his team battles Democrat operatives who reportedly recruited a same-name challenger to split votes. The *Daily Wire* reports the account has since been restored—but the incident underscores how close these races are and how vulnerable platforms can be to manipulation.

Additionally, the *Daily Caller* reports the DOJ is reviewing felony charges against James O'Keefe and Cam Higby related to an undercover election operation, raising First Amendment concerns among conservatives who see it as potential intimidation of political speech.
