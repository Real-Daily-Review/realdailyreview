President Trump said the US will not allow Iran to develop enriched uranium, claiming Washington has Tehran's nuclear material "surveilled" and will "blow up" anyone who approaches it, according to Al Jazeera.

The rhetoric comes as the Trump administration announced a separate nuclear victory: the US Department of Energy removed 13.5 kilograms of highly enriched uranium from a legacy research reactor in Venezuela on Friday, according to The Guardian. The amount represents a fraction of the roughly 408 kilograms Iran currently holds.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said Sunday that Trump told him he wanted to "go in" to Iran to remove enriched uranium, the Washington Examiner reported. Trump did not elaborate on what military or diplomatic means might be employed.

The statements underscore Trump's hardline stance on Iranian nuclear capabilities, a central concern for his administration and Israeli leadership. Iran has long maintained its nuclear program is for peaceful purposes, though international inspectors have documented uranium enrichment beyond civilian energy needs.

Watch whether Trump's rhetoric translates into concrete diplomatic or military action against Iran's nuclear facilities, and how Tehran responds to the escalating US pressure.
