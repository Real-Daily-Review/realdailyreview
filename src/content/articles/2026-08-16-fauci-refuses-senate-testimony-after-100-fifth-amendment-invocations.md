# Fauci Dodges Senate Grilling on COVID Origins, Invokes Fifth Over 100 Times

Dr. Anthony Fauci's legal team has flatly refused a Senate appearance requested by Sen. Ron Johnson (R-Wis.), claiming further testimony would constitute political harassment. This comes after Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment more than 100 times during a previous COVID-19 probe, according to Fox News and the New York Post.

Johnson, who has been conducting a parallel investigation into the federal government's pandemic response, sought another round of questioning. Fauci's attorney rejected the request outright, signaling the former NIAID director intends to avoid additional scrutiny over his role in COVID policy and the lab-origin question that has shadowed his tenure.

Republicans argue Fauci's repeated Fifth Amendment invocations and now his refusal to testify again suggest consciousness of guilt and an unwillingness to answer hard questions about gain-of-function research funding, messaging shifts, and the origins of the virus. Democrats and Fauci's camp counter that the former director has already cooperated extensively and that further Senate appearances are merely partisan theater designed to relitigate settled matters.

For Colorado taxpayers and voters, this episode underscores broader conservative concerns about government accountability and the lack of transparency surrounding pandemic decisions that upended schools, businesses, and livelihoods across the state.

Watch whether Johnson pursues subpoena power to compel Fauci's testimony or whether Senate Republicans accept this stonewalling.
