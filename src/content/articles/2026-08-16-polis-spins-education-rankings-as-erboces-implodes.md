# Gov. Polis Caught Overselling Colorado's Education Ranking While State Grapples With School Governance Failures

Governor Polis is selectively touting Colorado's U.S. News education rankings without acknowledging the full picture—a classic political move that obscures real problems in the state's K-12 system, according to *Complete Colorado*. The governor's cherry-picked messaging comes as Colorado education officials face deeper credibility issues: a regional education cooperative (ERBOCES) that infamously opened a "public Christian school"—a direct violation of church-state separation—is now facing dissolution after failing to attract buyers, *CPR News* reports.

The ERBOCES debacle exposes how government agencies can drift from their mission when oversight weakens. One board member called the lack of interest in saving the organization "a collective, statewide referendum on ERBOCES," signaling taxpayer and stakeholder rejection of its governance.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Polis's selective use of education metrics while his administration oversees agencies that bungle basic constitutional compliance undermines public trust. The governor should focus on accountability and transparent metrics rather than selective spin.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether the state legislature demands a full audit of ERBOCES spending and whether similar governance failures exist elsewhere in Colorado's education bureaucracy.
