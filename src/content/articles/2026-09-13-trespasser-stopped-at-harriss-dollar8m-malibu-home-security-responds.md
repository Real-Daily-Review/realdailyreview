# Security Stops Trespasser at Harris's $8M Malibu Estate

Vice President Kamala Harris's security detail stopped a woman who twice showed up uninvited at her and Doug Emhoff's $8 million Malibu home Friday evening, prompting a sheriff response (Fox News).

According to a Harris spokesperson, security personnel intercepted the individual before she reached the residence itself (Washington Examiner). The woman allegedly trespassed on the property, triggering law enforcement involvement late Friday (9News Denver).

Details remain sparse on the intruder's identity, motive, or whether charges were filed. The incident underscores ongoing security concerns for high-profile political figures, even in gated coastal communities.
