# Maine Democrat's Consent Allegations Expose Party's Credibility Problem

A former girlfriend of Democratic Maine Senate nominee Graham Platner accused him Tuesday of repeatedly removing condoms without her consent during their 2013-2015 relationship, according to *The Washington Post*. Lyndsey Fifield's allegations follow a bombshell report Monday in which another ex-girlfriend accused Platner of sexual assault—claims that prompted Democrats and major institutions to rescind their endorsements.

Republicans argue the swift abandonment of Platner by Democratic leadership exposes the party's selective application of its own #MeToo principles. When allegations surface against their own candidates, they note, Democrats act decisively; yet the party has long shielded figures like President Biden from similar scrutiny. The contrast underscores what conservatives say is Democratic hypocrisy on sexual misconduct.

Democrats counter that they responded appropriately by withdrawing support once credible allegations emerged, distinguishing their conduct from Republican handling of misconduct claims.

For Colorado voters, the Platner saga serves as a reminder that character matters in elections—and that party loyalty should never trump accountability. Watch whether national Democrats apply the same standard to their own candidates consistently, or whether selective enforcement becomes the norm heading into 2026.
