# Iran Reviews U.S. Peace Proposal as Trump Signals Patience

President Donald Trump said he is willing to wait "a few days" for Iran to respond to a U.S. peace proposal, according to CNBC. The move signals a potential opening in diplomatic talks between the two countries.

Iran is currently reviewing the American proposal, according to Al Jazeera. Pakistan is simultaneously stepping up mediation efforts, with Pakistani military chief Asim Munir set to travel to Tehran for talks, according to Iranian media reports cited by Al Jazeera.

The timing suggests a coordinated diplomatic push, with multiple parties engaged in back-channel negotiations. Trump's willingness to wait indicates the administration believes there is room for negotiation, though no details about the substance of the U.S. proposal have been disclosed.

The outcome of Iran's review and Pakistan's mediation efforts could determine whether these talks advance to more formal negotiations or stall.
