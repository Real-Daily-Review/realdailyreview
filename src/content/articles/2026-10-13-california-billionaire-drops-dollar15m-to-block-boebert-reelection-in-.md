# Out-of-State Billionaire Drops $1.5M to Topple Boebert in Colorado's 4th District

A California tech billionaire is bankrolling a liberal super PAC's last-ditch effort to unseat Rep. Lauren Boebert, flooding Colorado's 4th Congressional District with $1.5 million in attack ads (Colorado Sun). The Invest in Tomorrow Coalition, predominantly funded by Chris Larsen, is resurrecting old controversies in hopes of swaying voters in the GOP-leaning eastern district before Election Day.

Meanwhile, Boebert and Democratic challenger Eileen Laubacher squared off in a televised debate Monday, sparring over tariffs, inflation, and diesel prices—kitchen-table issues hitting Colorado energy workers and ranchers hard (Denver Post). The debate underscored the real policy divide: Boebert's focus on border security and economic growth versus Laubacher's progressive agenda.

Republicans argue that out-of-state billionaire money flooding Colorado races proves the left's desperation to impose its coastal agenda on rural Colorado. Conservatives counter that Boebert's record on energy independence and Second Amendment rights resonates far more with 4th District voters than California cash and personal attacks.

The race remains competitive, but the timing and scale of the super PAC spending signal Democratic anxiety about holding ground in a district that's trending Republican.

**Watch for:** final polling and turnout models in the 4th District heading into the final week—this race could signal whether rural Colorado is truly slipping from Democratic reach.
