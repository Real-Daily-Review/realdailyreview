# Trump Taps Vance to Hunt Corruption in Democratic-Run States—Including California's Billions in Waste

President Trump has tasked Vice President JD Vance with investigating alleged corruption and fraud in Democratic-controlled states, specifically Minnesota, California, and Illinois. According to Fox News, Trump claims billions are being stolen monthly from government programs in these jurisdictions.

The directive reflects a broader Republican strategy to highlight mismanagement in blue-state governance. Conservatives argue that Democratic-run cities and states have become laboratories for wasteful spending, regulatory bloat, and unchecked government corruption—problems that drain taxpayer dollars and erode public trust.

However, National Review notes potential friction between Trump and Vance on policy priorities, warning that significant disagreements on major issues could complicate the vice presidency. The outlet suggests this assignment may signal either alignment or an attempt to channel Vance's focus toward Trump's anti-corruption agenda.

**Colorado angle:** While Colorado has split governance, Republicans should watch whether Vance's investigation exposes lessons applicable to state-level spending waste and whether similar accountability measures gain traction in Republican-controlled legislatures.

Taxpayers and conservatives should monitor whether this anti-corruption push produces concrete results or remains campaign rhetoric.
