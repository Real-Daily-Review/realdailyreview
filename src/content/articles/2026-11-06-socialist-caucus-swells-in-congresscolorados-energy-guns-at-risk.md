# Socialist Caucus Surges in Congress—Colorado Conservatives Must Mobilize

A swelling Democratic Socialist contingent is heading to Capitol Hill, signaling a dramatic leftward lurch in Democratic ranks that threatens Colorado's energy economy, gun rights, and tax burden.

National Review reports the socialist caucus is poised to balloon in the incoming Congress, driven by grassroots momentum among younger voters, union organizers, and urban progressives. The Daily Wire's correspondent documented the celebratory energy among Democratic Socialists of America members on election night, capturing the movement's organizational muscle and cultural dominance in blue strongholds.

For Colorado, this matters. Socialists have consistently opposed fossil fuel development, backed aggressive gun control measures, and championed wealth redistribution schemes that would hammer Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and middle-class taxpayers. Their growing influence within the Democratic caucus will intensify pressure on Colorado's congressional delegation to embrace radical climate mandates, gun bans, and tax-and-spend policies.

Republicans argue the socialist surge exposes the Democratic Party's true ideological center and presents a clear choice for voters: limited government and free markets versus centralized control and dependency. Conservatives say the GOP must articulate a compelling alternative—not just opposition, but a vision of prosperity through individual liberty and economic freedom.

**Watch for:** whether Colorado Republicans use this moment to frame 2028 races around socialism's threat to energy jobs, property rights, and Second Amendment freedoms.
