# Trump Weighs Pirro's Future After Reflecting Pool Clash

Trump hasn't decided whether to fire U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro following a heated dispute over the Reflecting Pool, leaving her job in limbo as the administration navigates internal disagreements over policy direction.

According to *Just The News*, Trump told reporters before boarding Air Force One that he remains undecided on Pirro's future. The Colorado Sun's Mike Littwin reports that Pirro brought documented evidence to the Oval Office challenging Trump's position—and notably, Trump didn't immediately fire her despite reportedly being "in no mood to hear the facts."

The standoff highlights tensions within the Trump administration over how to handle policy disputes. Conservatives argue that strong leadership requires decisive action when advisors openly challenge presidential directives, while others contend that Pirro's willingness to present contrary evidence reflects the kind of principled counsel presidents need, even when uncomfortable.

For Colorado taxpayers and business leaders watching federal policy, the outcome matters: Pirro's tenure could signal whether Trump's team will prioritize loyalty or expertise in staffing decisions that affect everything from regulatory enforcement to land and water management.

Watch whether Trump makes a final call on Pirro before Congress returns to session—and whether her potential departure signals a broader shake-up in the administration's legal and policy ranks.
