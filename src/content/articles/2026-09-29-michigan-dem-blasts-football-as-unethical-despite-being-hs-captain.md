# Michigan Democrat's Football Hypocrisy Resurfaces as Senate Race Heats Up

A resurfaced podcast clip shows Michigan U.S. Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed—a self-described progressive—questioning whether football is "ethical" given chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) risks, despite having served as his high school football team's captain.

In the audio, El-Sayed asks: "What is it about this brutal sport that keeps us coming back? Is there a version of football that's actually ethical and safe?" (Fox News Politics, New York Post). The comments expose what Republicans see as typical left-wing moralizing: progressives lecturing Americans about their traditions and pastimes while ignoring their own participation in those same activities.

The clip underscores a broader conservative concern about the Democratic Party's regulatory impulse. El-Sayed represents the activist wing of the left that questions the legitimacy of American institutions—from football to energy production—without offering workable alternatives or acknowledging trade-offs ordinary people accept daily.
