## Colorado's Proposition 134: Voters to Decide on Transgender Athletes in School Sports

Colorado voters will face Proposition 134 this cycle, a measure asking whether transgender athletes should be permitted to compete on school sports teams aligned with their gender identity. This is a straightforward question of fairness: biological females have trained their entire lives for competitive opportunities that a policy of unchecked gender-identity accommodation threatens to erase. Republicans and conservatives argue that protecting girls' sports isn't bigotry — it's basic biology. Colorado parents should read this measure carefully before they mark their ballots.
