# Electoral College Remains America's Bulwark Against Urban Tyranny

Colorado conservatives should understand why Democrats relentlessly attack the Electoral College: it prevents coastal urban centers from imposing their will on the rest of the nation.

According to *Complete Colorado*, the left recognizes that its political power concentrates in major metropolitan areas—precisely why progressives push for a national popular vote that would render flyover states and rural America irrelevant. The Electoral College forces presidential candidates to build geographically diverse coalitions, ensuring that farmers, ranchers, energy workers, and small-town Coloradans cannot be ignored by politicians chasing votes in New York and California.

Republicans argue the Founders designed this system intentionally: to preserve federalism, protect minority interests, and prevent a handful of densely populated regions from dictating policy to the entire country. Democrats counter that the Electoral College is "undemocratic" and that every vote should count equally—a framing that conveniently ignores how a pure popular vote would marginalize entire regions.

For Colorado specifically, the Electoral College means the state remains competitive and consequential in presidential races. Without it, Democrats could safely ignore rural Colorado and focus exclusively on Denver metro voters.

**Watch for:** renewed Democratic efforts to erode Electoral College influence through the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact, which could reshape how Colorado's electoral votes are allocated.
