# Trump Administration Seeks to Reassure NATO Allies on Troop Deployments

Secretary of State Marco Rubio is meeting with NATO allies to clarify U.S. troop commitments in Europe, according to PBS NewsHour, following confusion over recent statements on force levels. President Trump said this week he wants to send more troops to Poland—a reversal that came just days after his officials cancelled a similar deployment, the BBC reported.

The mixed messaging has created uncertainty among European partners about American military intentions. Rubio's diplomatic engagement appears aimed at steadying those concerns and reaffirming U.S. commitment to the alliance.

Separately, a bipartisan group of female senators is embarking on a tour of Arctic nations to reassure U.S. allies in the region, PBS NewsHour reported, underscoring broader efforts across the administration and Congress to shore up international relationships.
