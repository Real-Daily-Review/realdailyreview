# Michigan Democrat's Antisemitism Problem Exposes Progressive Movement's Blind Spot

Michigan Senate Democratic nominee Abdul El-Sayed spent months campaigning alongside Twitch streamer Hasan Piker—a figure known for antisemitic rhetoric—before abruptly distancing himself when the association drew scrutiny, according to the *Washington Examiner* and *National Review*.

El-Sayed repeatedly appeared with Piker at campus rallies across Michigan, including at Michigan State and the University of Michigan, despite Piker's well-documented controversial positions. Only after public pressure did El-Sayed claim he had "no plans" to continue campaigning with him.

**The conservative critique is sharp:** El-Sayed's months-long association with an antisemitic personality—and his apparent indifference to it until media attention forced his hand—reveals how progressive politicians will overlook extremism when it serves electoral purposes. Republicans argue this exposes the left's selective moral outrage: antisemitism from the progressive wing gets a pass, while conservatives face relentless scrutiny for far lesser transgressions.

The incident underscores a broader problem within Democratic circles, where anti-Israel activism and tolerance for antisemitic voices have become increasingly normalized among younger, activist-oriented voters.

**Colorado conservatives should watch whether national Democratic leaders distance themselves from El-Sayed or quietly accept this as business-as-usual progressive politics.**
