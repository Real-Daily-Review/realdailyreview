# Iran's Hormuz Aggression Exposes Biden-Harris Weakness on Foreign Policy

The Biden-Harris administration's failed Iran diplomacy is now costing American security and Colorado energy prices.

Iran launched drone attacks on U.S. bases while moving to choke off the Strait of Hormuz—one of the world's most critical oil chokepoints. National Review notes the regime's "Death to America" isn't mere rhetoric; it's operational doctrine. The Daily Wire reports Iranian aggression is intensifying even as the administration clings to a negotiation strategy that has plainly failed.

**The Colorado angle:** Disruptions to Middle Eastern oil flow drive up gas prices at the pump and threaten the stability of energy markets that Colorado workers and families depend on. Tighter global oil supplies mean higher heating costs this winter and elevated fuel expenses for ranchers and small businesses across the state.

Republicans argue the administration's appeasement approach—prioritizing nuclear talks over deterrence—has emboldened Tehran. Conservatives say weakness invites aggression, and that a credible military posture, not endless negotiation with a hostile regime, protects American interests and keeps energy affordable.

Democrats counter that diplomacy remains preferable to military escalation, though their track record on Iran has left the U.S. in a weaker negotiating position.
