# US Doctor With Ebola Evacuated to Germany as Congo Outbreak Spreads

An American physician who contracted Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has been airlifted to Germany for treatment, according to The Guardian, as the outbreak accelerates across the region. The doctor's wife and four children are also being monitored for symptoms.

The outbreak, which has spread into urban areas, has killed at least 134 people and infected more than 500, according to health authorities cited by The Guardian. The virus responsible—Bundibugyo—has no approved treatments or vaccines. The World Health Organization has warned of the "scale and speed" of the outbreak, which has been declared a public health emergency.

Germany's decision to accept the patient reflects its capacity to handle highly infectious diseases, though Deutsche Welle notes the country's role in the evacuation underscores the global dimensions of the crisis. The evacuation highlights both the severity of the outbreak and the limited medical infrastructure in the affected region.
