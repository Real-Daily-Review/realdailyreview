# Michigan Democrat's Radical Agenda Should Alarm Colorado Voters

A Michigan Democratic Senate nominee with ties to democratic socialism and a track record of anti-capitalist rhetoric is poised to carry the party's banner into a general election—a warning sign for Colorado conservatives about where the national Democratic Party is headed.

Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan's Democratic primary despite openly embracing DSA politics and calling for the abolition of multiple institutions, according to *National Review*. The nominee has also made bizarre public statements attacking capitalism itself; during a 2021 podcast episode, El-Sayed argued that "capitalism has become the thief of sex," blaming market economics for undermining relationships, *The Daily Wire* reported.

Republicans argue El-Sayed's nomination reflects how far left the Democratic Party has drifted—away from mainstream economics and toward fringe ideology that would tank the economy if implemented nationally. His anti-business rhetoric and institutional radicalism represent exactly the kind of progressive overreach that has already burdened Colorado with high taxes, regulatory bloat, and energy-sector uncertainty.

Democrats have not publicly distanced themselves from El-Sayed's statements, instead focusing on his healthcare background and populist messaging.

Colorado conservatives should monitor whether national Democratic leaders embrace or repudiate El-Sayed's radical platform—it will signal how aggressively they'll push similar policies in our state.
