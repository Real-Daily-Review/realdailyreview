# South Africa's Constitutional Court Orders Parliament to Revisit Ramaphosa Impeachment

South Africa's Constitutional Court has ruled that lawmakers improperly blocked impeachment proceedings against President Cyril Ramaphosa, ordering Parliament to reconsider the case.

According to the BBC, the court determined that MPs were wrong to halt the impeachment process. Deutsche Welle reports the ruling requires Parliament to revisit the proceedings, effectively overturning the legislative body's earlier decision to block them.

The decision intensifies pressure on Ramaphosa, with some calling for his resignation in the wake of the court's intervention. The ruling underscores a constitutional clash between the judiciary and Parliament over the impeachment process and presidential accountability.

Details on the specific allegations underlying the impeachment attempt were not provided in the available reporting. The next step—how Parliament will respond to the court's mandate and whether it will proceed with a full impeachment hearing—remains unclear.
