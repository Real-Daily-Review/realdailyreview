# Denver Halloween Shooting Suspect Arrested in Colorado Springs

Denver police arrested a suspect Friday in connection with a deadly Halloween shooting that claimed the life of a 46-year-old man. Deontre Yorks, 23, was taken into custody in Colorado Springs and is being held for investigation of first-degree murder, according to 9News Denver.
