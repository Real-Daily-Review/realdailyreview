# Federal Court Signals AR-15s Deserve Constitutional Protection—Colorado Gun Owners Should Take Notice

A federal appeals court has struck down New Jersey's "assault weapons" ban, handing Second Amendment advocates a major win and potentially clearing the path for the Supreme Court to affirm that the AR-15 is constitutionally protected (Breitbart). The Third Circuit's decision has alarmed progressive outlets like Slate, which worry the ruling gives SCOTUS political cover to expand gun rights nationwide.

For Colorado gun owners, this matters enormously. The state has faced relentless pressure from Denver-based Democrats to impose its own "assault weapons" restrictions—proposals that have stalled but remain on the left's agenda. A Supreme Court decision affirming AR-15 protections would likely invalidate any such Colorado ban before it reaches the statute books.

Republicans argue the Constitution's plain text protects common firearms in lawful use, and that lower courts have no authority to second-guess that right. Democrats counter that reasonable regulations serve public safety and that states retain broad police powers.

Meanwhile, a separate controversy shows judicial overreach in other domains: a Massachusetts judge appointed during the Biden administration blocked deportations of Ethiopian nationals despite a Supreme Court ruling allowing TPS terminations (Fox News), illustrating how lower courts continue defying higher authority on immigration enforcement.

**Watch for:** Colorado legislative attempts to resurrect gun bans in the 2026 session—and whether SCOTUS rules before then.
