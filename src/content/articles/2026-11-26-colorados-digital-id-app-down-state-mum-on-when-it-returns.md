# Colorado's Digital ID App Down Ahead of Holiday Travel—State Still Silent on Timeline

Colorado's myColorado digital ID app remains offline following a cybersecurity incident that may have compromised data for up to 150,000 residents, leaving travelers scrambling just as Thanksgiving travel peaks (Colorado Sun). The state has offered no timeline for restoration, compounding travel headaches already worsened by a massive coastal storm battering the East Coast with widespread flight delays and cancellations—over 550 flights delayed and 366 canceled as of Thursday morning (Washington Examiner).

For Colorado travelers relying on digital identification, the outage creates real friction. The incident underscores a conservative concern: government-run digital systems often lack the security rigor and accountability of private-sector alternatives. When the state manages sensitive personal data, failures carry direct consequences for citizens—and the state's sluggish communication about timelines only deepens mistrust.

Republicans and taxpayer advocates argue that expanding government digital infrastructure without proven security protocols is reckless. The myColorado platform was pitched as convenient and modern; instead, Coloradans are left without answers as holiday travel season peaks.

Watch for the state's full disclosure on what data was accessed, how the breach occurred, and whether officials face accountability for the slow response and lack of transparency.
