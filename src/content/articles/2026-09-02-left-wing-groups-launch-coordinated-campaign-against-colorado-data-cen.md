# Colorado's Data Center Gold Rush Faces Coordinated Left-Wing Opposition Campaign

Colorado's booming data center industry—a genuine economic engine for the state—is under siege from a coordinated campaign by progressive activist groups bankrolled by left-wing donors, according to reporting by Fox News Politics and the Daily Caller.

Groups like Public Citizen and the Sierra Club are deploying what experts call a familiar "playbook" to block data center development across the country, framing the facilities as environmental threats. Republicans and industry advocates counter that data centers represent high-wage jobs, tax revenue, and critical infrastructure for artificial intelligence innovation—economic opportunities Colorado can't afford to squander.

The backlash has intensified despite continued momentum: the data center boom persists even as activists escalate opposition. Trump administration officials have signaled strong support for data center expansion as essential to American competitiveness.

**The Colorado angle:** Data centers are already reshaping the state's economy and energy profile. Progressive environmental groups' coordinated resistance could slow job creation and investment that benefits rural communities and state coffers. The fight also highlights a broader tension: Colorado's need for economic growth versus activist pressure to restrict development.
