# Tesla Recalls 1.2 Million Vehicles in China; OpenAI Adds Safety Alert Feature

Tesla is recalling 1.2 million China-made and imported vehicles to address software issues, according to the Wall Street Journal. The recall targets safety concerns but the company has not disclosed specific details about the defects or their scope.

In a separate development, OpenAI is rolling out an optional safety feature for ChatGPT called "Trusted Contact," according to The Verge. The feature allows adult users to designate an emergency contact—such as a friend, family member, or caregiver—who will be notified if OpenAI detects discussions of self-harm or suicide. OpenAI framed the tool as part of a "simple, expert-validated premise: when someone may be in crisis, connecting with someone they know and trust can make a meaningful difference."

The ChatGPT feature is voluntary and designed to provide an additional layer of support beyond the chatbot's existing safety guardrails. OpenAI did not specify a rollout timeline or which regions would receive the feature first.

Tesla's recall in China comes as the automaker faces intensifying competition in the world's largest EV market. Watch for details on the specific software vulnerabilities and whether similar recalls are planned in other markets.
