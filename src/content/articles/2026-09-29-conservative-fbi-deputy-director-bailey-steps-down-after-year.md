# Conservative FBI Deputy Director Andrew Bailey Steps Down After Year Under Trump

FBI Deputy Director Andrew Bailey, a conservative favorite tapped by the Trump administration to help reform the bureau, announced his departure Monday, citing family reasons and a return to Missouri. Bailey's exit marks another leadership shuffle at an agency conservatives have long criticized for politicization and institutional drift.

According to The Daily Wire, Bailey posted his resignation on X, thanking Trump administration officials for their confidence in him during his tenure. His departure comes as Republicans continue pushing for FBI accountability and structural reform—concerns that gained momentum after the bureau's handling of the 2016 election, the Hunter Biden laptop story, and its role in January 6th investigations.

Conservatives have argued the FBI needs leadership committed to restoring institutional credibility and ending what they view as weaponization of federal law enforcement against political opponents. Bailey's departure raises questions about who will fill the role and whether Trump's reform agenda at the bureau will continue gaining traction.

Colorado Republicans, already skeptical of federal overreach in energy policy and land management, will be watching closely to see whether the next FBI leadership maintains focus on actual crime rather than political theater.

Watch whether Trump appoints another reform-minded deputy director or faces resistance from the FBI's institutional resistance to change.
