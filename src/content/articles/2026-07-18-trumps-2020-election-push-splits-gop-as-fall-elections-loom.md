# Trump's 2020 Election Focus Risks Dividing GOP Ahead of Fall Elections

Former President Trump's renewed push on 2020 election claims—including allegations of Chinese interference in voter databases—is drawing sharp criticism from within conservative ranks, even as some Republicans defend election integrity concerns.

Trump's Thursday primetime speech on election integrity and alleged Chinese acquisition of voter data has reignited diplomatic tensions ahead of a planned fall summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The White House says the meeting remains on track, but Beijing has already pushed back.

The problem: *National Review* argues Trump's focus on widely unpopular stolen-election claims "won't make the job of Republicans any easier this fall," signaling concern that relitigating 2020 could cost GOP candidates momentum heading into critical races—including Colorado's competitive congressional and state legislative contests.

Republicans remain divided. Some, citing *Just The News*, point to documented concerns about Chinese attempts to acquire voter databases in 2020 as legitimate election-security issues warranting scrutiny. Others worry the messaging muddies Republican efforts to win on kitchen-table issues like inflation, energy policy, and education—areas where Colorado voters have shown openness to conservative solutions.
