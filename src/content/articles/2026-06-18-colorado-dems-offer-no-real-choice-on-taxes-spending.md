# Colorado Democrats Offer Voters Little Choice on Policy

Colorado's Democratic gubernatorial primary has devolved into a Trump-focused grudge match, with Attorney General Phil Weiser and U.S. Senator Michael Bennet offering virtually identical positions on the issues that matter most to Colorado taxpayers—housing, taxes, and economic policy (Colorado Sun). Rather than debate substantive differences, both candidates are banking on anti-Trump messaging to differentiate themselves, leaving conservative voters without a clear contrast on government spending, regulatory overreach, or energy policy.

The race is tightening enough that Bennet recently loaned his campaign $1 million, signaling concern about momentum (Colorado Sun). A debate dispute has further exposed campaign anxiety, with Bennet's team questioning Weiser's commitment to face-to-face confrontation.

Bennet, leveraging his Senate tenure and government experience, has positioned himself as the establishment favorite (Denver Post). Yet neither candidate has articulated a compelling case for restraint on state spending or relief from Colorado's rising regulatory burden—issues that resonate with working families and small business owners across the state.

**Conservatives should watch whether either Democrat breaks ranks to address Colorado's affordability crisis with market-friendly solutions rather than more government programs.**
