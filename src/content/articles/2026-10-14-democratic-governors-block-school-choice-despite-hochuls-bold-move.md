# Democratic Governors Should Stop Blocking School Choice, Experts Say

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul's decision to opt into a federal tax credit scholarship program shows what happens when Democratic leaders prioritize families over union demands.

The program allows federal tax credits to fund tutoring, specialized services, after-school care, technology, transportation, school supplies, and tuition assistance—giving parents real choice in education. Teachers unions have fiercely opposed the initiative, and most Democratic governors have blocked it rather than face union pressure (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Parents, not unions, should control education dollars. Tax credits empower families to find solutions that work for their children—whether that's specialized tutoring for a struggling reader, after-school programs, or alternative schools. This is market-driven education reform that respects taxpayer money and parental rights.

**Democrats counter** that such programs drain resources from public schools, though supporters argue the credits simply redirect existing federal tax benefits to education choices families actually want.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether Gov. Jared Polis—who has positioned himself as education-friendly—will follow Hochul's lead or cave to Colorado's powerful teachers unions. School choice remains a defining issue for Colorado families tired of one-size-fits-all public education and rising tuition costs.
