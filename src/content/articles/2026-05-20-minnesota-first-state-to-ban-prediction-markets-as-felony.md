# Minnesota Becomes First State to Ban Prediction Markets

Minnesota has become the first state to pass legislation making it a felony for prediction market companies to operate within its borders, according to NPR. The law targets platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket, which allow users to bet on the outcomes of future events including elections and other real-world occurrences.

While dozens of states have pursued legal action against the prediction market industry, Minnesota's approach marks the first outright ban codified into state law. The felony designation represents a significant escalation in regulatory enforcement compared to other states' efforts.

Prediction markets have grown in popularity and prominence in recent years, drawing both investment and scrutiny from regulators and lawmakers concerned about their potential impacts. The industry has faced mounting legal challenges across multiple jurisdictions.

Minnesota's move signals a hardening stance among state governments on the sector, though the broader legal and regulatory landscape remains in flux. Watch for whether other states follow Minnesota's lead with similar bans, and how the prediction market industry responds—potentially through legal challenges or lobbying efforts.
