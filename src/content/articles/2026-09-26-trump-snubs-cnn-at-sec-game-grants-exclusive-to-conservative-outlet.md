# Trump Attends SEC Showdown; Mainstream Media Left Behind

President Trump flew into Tennessee to catch a high-stakes college football matchup, but the trip highlighted a widening rift between the White House and legacy media outlets. According to 9News Denver, Air Force One descended low over Neyland Stadium as Trump arrived for the Texas-Longhorns SEC game, where he conducted a halftime interview with Clay Travis of The Daily Wire rather than granting access to CNN, MSNBC, or Politico—outlets Trump had previously barred from White House grounds (The Daily Wire).

The media exclusion underscores Trump's ongoing conflict with what conservatives call the "hostile press." Republicans argue that outlets like CNN have abandoned objective reporting in favor of partisan activism, justifying selective press access. Democrats and mainstream journalists counter that restricting media access undermines press freedom and democratic accountability.

**The Colorado angle:** While this story plays out on the national stage, Colorado conservatives should note how Trump's media strategy—favoring independent and right-leaning outlets over establishment press—mirrors broader conservative skepticism of institutional gatekeepers. The shift toward alternative media platforms continues reshaping political communication.
