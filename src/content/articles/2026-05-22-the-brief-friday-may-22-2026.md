# Real Daily Review — The Brief
## Friday, May 22, 2026

Friday opens with U.S. foreign policy pulling in multiple directions — troops heading to Poland, a new consulate in Greenland drawing protests, and a Chinese commerce minister's conspicuous absence signaling quiet friction at a Pacific trade forum.

---

## 🪖 Trump Sends 5,000 More Troops to Poland

President Trump announced the U.S. will deploy 5,000 additional troops to Poland — a reversal in posture that comes just one week after the Pentagon cancelled a planned deployment of 4,000 troops to the country. The announcement adds to uncertainty about the administration's NATO commitments and its broader European security strategy, even as the war in Ukraine continues.

---

## 🇬🇱 Greenlanders Protest at New U.S. Consulate

The United States inaugurated a new consulate in Greenland on Friday, drawing protests from residents who gathered outside with signs reading "No means no." The demonstration reflects sustained local opposition to President Trump's push for greater U.S. control over the island. Greenland's government has repeatedly rejected any transfer of sovereignty, though Washington has continued to press the issue.

---

## 🦠 American Doctor With Ebola Evacuated to Germany

An American doctor who contracted Ebola while working in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has been flown to Germany for treatment. His wife and four children are also being monitored for symptoms. The evacuation comes amid an active Ebola outbreak in the DRC. Dr. Peter Stafford is identified as the patient. No additional details about his condition were provided in available reports.

---

## 🤝 China-Russia Summit in Beijing

Chinese President Xi Jinping welcomed Russian President Vladimir Putin to Beijing with formal ceremony, the visit coming just days after Xi hosted Donald Trump. The meeting underscores China's position as a central diplomatic player — courted by both Washington and Moscow — as Russia's war in Ukraine deepens its economic and strategic dependence on Beijing, and Western leaders cautiously re-engage with China.

---

## 🏛️ First House Democrat Calls for DNC Chair to Resign

Rep. Marc Veasey (D-Texas) became the first House Democrat to publicly call on Democratic National Committee Chair Ken Martin to step down, saying Martin should "move on." Veasey's remarks follow the botched rollout of the party's autopsy report on its 2024 election losses. Martin has faced growing criticism over the DNC's handling of the post-election review process.

---

## 📦 Also Noted

**Tesla recalls 1.2 million vehicles in China** over software safety concerns, affecting both China-made and imported cars.

**Guzman y Gomez exits the U.S. market**, with the Australian fast-food chain's shares surging after it announced it would refocus entirely on its home market — the latest casualty of what observers have called a graveyard for Australian chains attempting U.S. expansion.

**At APEC**, China's Commerce Minister Wang Wentao skipped the opening session, citing urgent official business. International trade representative Li Chenggang chaired in his place. Beijing called for expanded APEC cooperation, though the minister's absence drew notice.

## What to Watch

Monitor whether Trump's Poland troop announcement produces a formal NATO response or bilateral agreement with Warsaw, particularly given last week's cancellation of a similar deployment. The Ebola evacuation will draw scrutiny as health officials assess containment in the DRC. And watch for any readout from the Xi-Putin summit — the diplomatic signals from Beijing this week will shape how Western capitals read China's role in the Ukraine conflict going into the weekend.
