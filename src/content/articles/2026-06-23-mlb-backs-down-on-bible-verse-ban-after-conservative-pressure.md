# MLB Backs Down on Bible Verses After Conservative Pressure

Major League Baseball caved to Republican pushback this week, promising not to fine or discipline San Francisco Giants players who wore Bible verses on their Pride Night caps—a stark reminder that woke corporate overreach still faces resistance when called out directly.

MLB Commissioner Rob Manfred initially threatened the Christian players over their religious expression during the June Pride event. But after Sen. Josh Hawley (R-Mo.) and conservative outlets amplified the story, Manfred reversed course in a letter to Hawley, stating the players "were neither fined nor disciplined, nor will they ever be" (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative argument:** This was textbook corporate censorship dressed up as "rule enforcement." Hawley characterized MLB's initial stance as the league admitting it was "wrong to threaten the Giants players over Bible verses" (Daily Caller). Religious expression shouldn't be a fireable offense—especially when the league simultaneously celebrates other causes during games.

**What happened:** MLB claimed it was simply enforcing existing uniform rules, but the selective enforcement against Christian speech while permitting other messaging exposed the double standard progressives apply to religious liberty.

The broader lesson: Corporate wokeness retreats when conservatives fight back publicly and loudly. Watch whether MLB's commitment holds or if similar pressure tactics resurface under different pretexts.
