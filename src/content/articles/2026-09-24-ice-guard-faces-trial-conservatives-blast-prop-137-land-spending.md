# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Trial Over Protest Shooting; Prop 137 Land Deal Draws Fire

A Colorado judge has cleared the way for prosecution of Brandon Booth, an ex-detention center guard accused of firing at women protesters who called him a "race traitor" and threatened to locate guards' children, according to the Colorado Sun. The ruling finds sufficient evidence to proceed to trial.

Meanwhile, conservatives are mobilizing against Proposition 137, a ballot measure that would funnel Colorado taxpayer dollars to wealthy environmental nonprofits under the guise of land conservation. Complete Colorado's editorial board warns that the initiative would effectively subsidize The Nature Conservancy—the measure's primary backer—and other NGOs with public money, representing another example of government-funded corporate welfare dressed up as environmental stewardship.

The Prop 137 fight highlights a broader conservative concern: Colorado voters are being asked to approve spending that benefits well-heeled special interests while expanding government's role in land management. Taxpayers should scrutinize whether this measure genuinely serves conservation or simply enriches activist organizations with deep pockets and political influence.

Watch for the trial outcome in the Booth case and whether Prop 137 gains traction with Colorado voters ahead of the November election.
