# Former President Biden's Cancer Has Spread, Son Says

Joe Biden's health is deteriorating faster than the public has been told, according to his son Hunter Biden's recent BBC interview.

Hunter Biden disclosed Friday that his father's prostate cancer—first disclosed in 2025—has metastasized beyond bone involvement and is causing "very painful" symptoms. Yet the former president continues making public statements on policy matters, raising questions about the extent of his condition and whether Americans were fully informed about his health status during his time in office.

The revelation comes as Biden remains active in Democratic circles and continues speaking on national issues. Conservatives have long questioned the transparency surrounding Biden's health, pointing to observable gaffes, verbal stumbles, and physical incidents during his presidency as evidence that the media and his administration downplayed legitimate concerns about his fitness for office.

Republicans argue the American people deserved complete honesty about a sitting president's medical condition, especially one affecting cognitive function and stamina. The left counters that Biden's doctors cleared him for duty and that his condition developed or worsened after he left office.

The disclosure underscores a broader conservative concern: government and media institutions failed to give voters the full picture about Biden's health when it mattered most.
