# U.S. Strikes Iranian Oil Tankers After Tehran Targets Navy Warship

The U.S. military destroyed five Iranian crude oil tankers in retaliatory strikes following direct missile attacks on an American Navy warship, escalating tensions in the Middle East and raising energy-market concerns for Colorado consumers.

According to 9News Denver and Just The News, Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched ballistic missiles at a U.S. Navy vessel twice in recent days. The American ship successfully evaded both attacks with no injuries reported. In response, U.S. forces struck the Iranian tankers in the Strait of Hormuz region, with Iranian state media confirming the strikes.

The escalation matters to Colorado. Crude oil prices typically spike during Middle East conflicts, directly hitting gas pumps and heating costs for Front Range families and rural Coloradans already squeezed by inflation. Energy producers and refineries across the state face supply-chain uncertainty.

Republicans argue the Biden-Harris administration's weak posture toward Iran—including the failed nuclear deal and sanctions relief—emboldened Tehran's aggression. Conservatives say only decisive American military strength deters hostile regimes. Democrats counter that diplomatic channels remain open and military restraint prevents wider regional war.
