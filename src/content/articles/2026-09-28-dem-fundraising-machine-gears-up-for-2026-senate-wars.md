# Colorado Conservatives Should Brace for Out-of-State Dem Cash Flooding 2026 Senate Races

National Democratic operatives are already mobilizing big-dollar fundraisers for 2026 Senate challengers, signaling that left-wing groups plan to pour resources into competitive races across the country—a playbook that could reach Colorado if Sen. John Hickenlooper faces a serious Republican challenger.

According to the New York Post, allies of New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani hosted a fundraiser Monday for Democratic Senate hopefuls including Texas State Rep. James Talarico and Ohio's Sherrod Brown. The Washington Examiner reports Talarico said he'd "absolutely" accept an endorsement from Republican Sen. John Cornyn, a move that underscores how Democrats are hedging their bets and courting establishment figures even as their base mobilizes.

Republicans argue this early, aggressive fundraising apparatus demonstrates Democrats' determination to defend Senate seats and flip Republican-held chambers—and that Colorado's Senate race could become a national battleground if GOP recruitment succeeds. Conservatives should expect similar out-of-state money flowing into Colorado if Republicans nominate a credible challenger to Hickenlooper in 2024's successor race.

Watch for which national Democratic groups register in Colorado and begin ad buys targeting Republican Senate candidates.
