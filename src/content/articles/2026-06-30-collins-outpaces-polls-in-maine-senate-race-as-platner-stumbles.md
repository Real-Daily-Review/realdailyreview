# Maine Senate Race Tightens as Collins Defies Polling Skeptics

Susan Collins is running stronger than public polls suggest in her 2026 Senate reelection bid against Democrat Graham Platner—a sign that Maine voters may be more skeptical of the challenger than headline numbers indicate.

A New York Times/Portland Press Herald/Siena survey shows Platner with a slim two-point edge, but Breitbart notes Collins is already outperforming her 2020 public polling at this stage of the race. Fox News reports that despite Platner's narrow lead, controversies and character concerns surrounding the Democrat cloud his path to victory.

The dynamic mirrors a familiar pattern: mainstream media polls often underestimate Republican incumbents in purple states, particularly when challengers carry baggage. Collins, a moderate Republican who has occasionally broken with her party, appears to be benefiting from voter familiarity and concerns about her opponent's record.
