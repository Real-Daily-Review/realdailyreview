# Trump Faces Pre-Election Lawsuit Blitz While Voting by Mail

President Trump says he's "not surprised" by revelations that Democratic state attorneys general planned lawsuits against him months before his 2024 reelection victory—a coordinated legal strategy that has already yielded roughly 100 suits since January 2025, according to *Just The News*.

The aggressive litigation campaign underscores the left's determination to tie up the Trump administration in courtrooms rather than defeat it at the ballot box. Republicans argue this represents weaponized law enforcement by blue-state prosecutors bent on obstruction.

Meanwhile, Trump cast his ballot by mail in Florida's Republican primary—the second time he's voted absentee this year, *Washington Examiner* reports. The move highlights a tactical inconsistency: Trump has hammered mail-in voting as vulnerable to fraud since 2020, yet his campaign has quietly embraced it when convenient. Conservatives who've demanded election integrity standards deserve clarity on why those standards shift based on political advantage.

For Colorado, the lawsuit deluge matters. Democratic AG Phil Weiser has joined similar multi-state actions against the Trump administration. Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado resources are diverted to partisan litigation rather than protecting state interests.
