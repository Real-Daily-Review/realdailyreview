# Guzman y Gomez Exits U.S., Refocuses on Australia

Mexican-themed fast-food chain Guzman y Gomez announced Friday it will shutter its U.S. operations and concentrate on its home market in Australia, sending its shares up as much as 20 percent, according to CNBC.

The company told shareholders that performance at its American stores had fallen short of expectations. Founder Steven Marks said the chain could no longer justify the cost of competing in a crowded market already saturated with Mexican food options, according to The Guardian.

The exit marks another setback for Australian fast-food companies attempting to break into the U.S. market. The Guardian notes the country has earned a reputation as a "graveyard" for such ventures, with multiple chains struggling to establish footholds against entrenched competitors.

Guzman y Gomez will maintain its Australian business as its primary focus going forward. The company's decision to retreat from the U.S. came despite earlier high-profile expansion efforts in the American market.
