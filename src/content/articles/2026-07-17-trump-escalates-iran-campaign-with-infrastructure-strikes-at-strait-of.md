# U.S. Escalates Iran Campaign: Trump Targets Infrastructure as Tehran Threatens Global Oil Routes

The Trump administration is escalating military pressure on Iran by striking critical infrastructure—bridges and port facilities—in a direct response to Tehran's threats against the Strait of Hormuz and attacks on commercial shipping (New York Post, Just The News).

The strikes target Bandar Abbas, a key Iranian naval and IRGC stronghold, with the stated goal of disrupting supply routes and forcing Iran to ease its blockade of one of the world's most critical energy chokepoints. According to a senior U.S. official cited by Just The News, the infrastructure hits are designed to "choke off supply routes" that Iran has weaponized against international commerce.

This escalation follows days of tit-for-tat strikes and represents a sharp departure from the previous administration's Iran deal approach. The back-and-forth has "shredded the deal to end the war," according to 9News Denver, with renewed threats to global shipping and energy markets.

**The Colorado angle:** Disruptions to Middle Eastern oil flows directly impact Colorado energy prices and the broader U.S. energy sector. Any sustained conflict threatens fuel costs for Colorado drivers and businesses already burdened by state-level energy regulations.

**What to watch:** Whether Iran escalates further or whether the infrastructure strikes force Tehran back to the negotiating table—and what that means for Colorado gas prices heading into winter.
