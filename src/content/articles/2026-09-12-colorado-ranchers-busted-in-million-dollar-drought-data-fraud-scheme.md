# Colorado Ranchers Busted in Massive Drought-Data Fraud Scheme

Two Colorado ranchers conspired to tamper with rain gauges across the plains, falsifying drought data to pocket millions in fraudulent insurance claims—a brazen scheme that exposed how easily government drought programs can be gamed by those willing to cheat the system.

According to the Colorado Sun, the pair wrecked rain gauges, triggered whistleblower complaints, and sparked manhunts before the conspiracy unraveled. The scam netted million-dollar payouts based on fabricated drought numbers, underscoring a hard truth: when government creates subsidy programs tied to environmental conditions, bad actors will exploit them.

**The conservative takeaway:** This case illustrates why conservatives remain skeptical of expansive government insurance and drought-relief programs. When Washington and state capitals hand out taxpayer money based on self-reported conditions, fraud becomes inevitable. Better to let markets and private insurance handle risk than create bureaucratic programs vulnerable to manipulation.

Meanwhile, Colorado's State Fair drew over half a million attendees this year—the highest turnout since 2015 and an 18 percent jump from last year (CPR News), signaling strong consumer confidence and rural engagement heading into fall.
