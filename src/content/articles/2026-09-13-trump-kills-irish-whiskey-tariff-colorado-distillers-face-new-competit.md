# Trump Lifts Irish Whiskey Tariff—But Colorado Distillers Wonder What's Next

President Trump announced he's eliminating the remaining 10% U.S. tariff on Irish whiskey at the Irish Open trophy ceremony at his Doonbeg resort, after the rate had already been cut from 15% earlier this year (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire). Trump quipped that he'd been "bugged" into the decision by Irish officials and golfers alike.

The move signals Trump's broader push to ease trade tensions, but it raises questions for Colorado's craft distillery industry. While tariff relief on imports sounds pro-free-market, Colorado distillers—already competing in a crowded spirits market—may face stiffer competition from cheaper Irish imports. Conservatives generally favor lower tariffs as a matter of principle, but domestic producers worry about unequal trade dynamics.

Republicans argue that selective tariff cuts should benefit American workers and businesses, not just foreign competitors. The question for Colorado: does this deal level the playing field, or does it undercut local distillers who've invested in American production?

Watch whether Trump's tariff moves extend to other sectors affecting Colorado—agriculture, energy equipment, and manufacturing—or remain narrowly focused on high-profile trade gestures.
