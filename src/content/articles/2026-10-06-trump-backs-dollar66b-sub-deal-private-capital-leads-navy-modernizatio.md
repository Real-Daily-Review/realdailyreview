# Trump Backs $6.6B Submarine Push—Private Sector Leads, Not Bloated Pentagon Bureaucracy

President Trump is announcing a $6.6 billion deal to accelerate Virginia-class submarine production, with California defense tech firm Anduril Industries investing $3.7 billion to build a new Baltimore shipyard while the Navy contracts $2.9 billion in manufacturing work (Fox News, The Daily Wire). The move breaks a critical bottleneck in undersea capability—a genuine national security need conservatives support.

Here's what matters: **This is private capital doing the heavy lifting.** Rather than another bloated Pentagon procurement disaster, Anduril is risking its own $3.7 billion to solve a real problem. That's market discipline at work. The Navy's existing submarine pipeline has been strangled by inefficiency and monopolistic reliance on a single builder; competition and private investment can fix what government alone cannot.

For Colorado, this underscores why energy independence and domestic manufacturing matter. A stronger Navy protects American interests globally—and keeps defense dollars flowing to domestic production rather than foreign competitors.

Conservatives should watch whether this model—private investment + targeted government contracts—becomes the template for other defense gaps, or whether Pentagon bureaucrats reassert control and kill the competitive advantage.
