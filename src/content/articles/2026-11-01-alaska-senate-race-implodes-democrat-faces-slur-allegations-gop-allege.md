# Alaska Senate Race Descends Into Chaos as Democrats Face Scandal, GOP Alleges Ballot Fraud

Alaska's pivotal U.S. Senate race has become a cautionary tale of Democratic dysfunction and electoral shenanigans, with the AOC-backed Democratic frontrunner now fighting allegations of racial slurs while Republicans cry foul over a "Decoy Dan" ballot-confusion scheme.

Mary Peltola, the Democratic hopeful championed by Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, is defending herself against accusations from former staffers that she used the n-word in a 2024 incident, according to the New York Post. Meanwhile, the FBI has begun contacting donors to Republican candidate Dan Sullivan across Alaska and Massachusetts as part of an expanding investigation into what conservatives call a coordinated effort to confuse voters.

Conservative commentator Mark Levin characterized the "Decoy Dan" ballot strategy—reportedly involving two candidates with similar names—as a "conspiracy" designed to "steal the United States Senate from the American People" (Breitbart). Republicans argue the tactic is a deliberate attempt to siphon votes from the legitimate GOP nominee.

Alaska's Republican-run state government has moved to block a Trump-directed lawsuit seeking to invalidate Democratic ballots, calling the legal challenge "meritless" and procedurally impossible (Washington Examiner). Democrats counter that GOP legal maneuvers represent election denial.
