# Trump-Walz Reflecting Pool Spat: Minnesota Governor's Jab Backfires Over State's Own Fraud Mess

Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz's viral criticism of Trump over peeling paint at the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool has drawn sharp conservative pushback—with Republicans pointing to Minnesota's own fraud scandal as disqualifying for the lecture.

Walz mocked Trump's renovation of the pool, which was painted a deep blue under Trump's direction. The paint has since peeled in places, and Trump has blamed vandals using knives and chemicals rather than construction defects from the no-bid contractor. Walz's quip went viral, but conservatives seized the opening: *Fox News* and the *Washington Examiner* noted that Minnesota faces its own major fraud investigation, with critics arguing Walz should "sit this one out."

Trump's White House defended the contractors and attributed damage to vandalism, not workmanship. The Daily Wire reported Trump blamed the deterioration on deliberate attacks with "knives and chemicals."

The Colorado angle: While this is a national story, it highlights how Democratic leaders often dodge accountability for their own states' failures—a pattern Colorado taxpayers should recognize when evaluating state spending and oversight of major projects.
