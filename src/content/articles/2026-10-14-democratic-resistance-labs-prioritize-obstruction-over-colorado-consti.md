# Democratic Congresswoman's 'Resistance Labs' Show Left's Priority: Obstruction Over Governance

Colorado Democrats should explain whether they're focused on constituent services or joining a decade-long campaign to block the Trump administration at every turn.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Democratic congresswoman Pramila Jayapal is organizing "resistance labs" designed to systematically obstruct Trump administration policies. The effort reflects what conservatives argue is a troubling Democratic strategy: since Trump's 2016 victory, the party has made resisting his agenda the centerpiece of its political platform rather than advancing constructive alternatives.

Republicans argue this approach prioritizes partisan obstruction over governing. Instead of working across the aisle on issues affecting their districts—inflation, border security, energy policy—Democrats have institutionalized resistance as their core mission. For Colorado voters, this raises a critical question: Are our Democratic representatives in Congress working to solve problems facing Coloradans, or are they spending political capital on coordinated opposition campaigns?

The "resistance labs" model suggests the latter. This isn't bipartisan problem-solving; it's organized obstruction dressed up as activism.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado's Democratic delegation participates in these efforts and demand they prioritize constituent concerns—jobs, energy development, water rights, and fiscal responsibility—over national resistance theater.
