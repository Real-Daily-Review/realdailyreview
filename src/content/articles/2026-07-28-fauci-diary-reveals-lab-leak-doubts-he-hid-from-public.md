# Fauci's Own Diary Exposes Years of Public Deception on COVID Origins and Risk

Dr. Anthony Fauci's personal diary entries—released by Senate Homeland Security Committee Chairman Rand Paul—reveal the former NIAID director privately acknowledged concerns about COVID-19's lab origins while publicly dismissing the theory, according to reporting from the *Daily Caller* and *Just The News*. The diary shows Fauci "staring at the furin-cleavage site and talking about deliberate insertion," contradicting his repeated public statements that a natural origin was most likely.

The documents also expose what *Breitbart* characterizes as deliberate misrepresentation of the virus's fatality rate to the American public—a critical detail for Coloradans who endured some of the nation's harshest lockdowns based partly on Fauci's guidance.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans contend Fauci's diary proves he knowingly misled Americans on two fronts: the lab-leak theory's plausibility and COVID's actual danger level. This matters for Colorado because state and local officials imposed restrictions on businesses, schools, and churches largely justified by federal health guidance Fauci shaped. Taxpayers funded gain-of-function research that may have produced the pandemic itself, then were fed false reassurances by the man steering the response.

Democrats have largely avoided direct comment on the diary's contents, instead defending Fauci's overall pandemic record.

Watch for Paul's congressional questioning and whether Colorado's delegation demands accountability for the state-level damage caused by policies built on Fauci's misrepresentations.
