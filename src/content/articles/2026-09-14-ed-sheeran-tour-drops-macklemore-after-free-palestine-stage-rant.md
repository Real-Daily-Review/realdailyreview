# Macklemore Booted From Ed Sheeran Tour After "Free Palestine" Stage Rant

Ed Sheeran's tour promoter has dropped rapper Macklemore from the remainder of the "Loop Tour" following his September 4 outburst at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey, where he told the crowd to "free Palestine" (9News Denver).

The move marks a rare instance of a major entertainment venue taking swift action against political agitation on stage. Sheeran's team announced the decision Monday morning, effectively ending Macklemore's role as a tour opener.

While mainstream outlets like 9News reported the incident straightforwardly, conservative media outlets including The Daily Wire characterized the rapper's comments as "Islamist propaganda" and a "pro-Islamic terrorist rant," reflecting broader conservative concerns about anti-Israel activism gaining ground in American entertainment and culture.

The incident underscores the ongoing tension between free speech and corporate consequences—a debate that cuts across political lines. For Colorado audiences and conservative voters, it raises questions about which political messages get amplified or silenced in mainstream entertainment venues, and whether corporate decisions reflect genuine principle or market pressure.

Watch whether other entertainment venues adopt similar policies regarding on-stage political statements, and whether this becomes a flashpoint in the broader culture war over Israel advocacy in American media.
