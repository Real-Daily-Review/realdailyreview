# Socialist Ideology Gaining Ground in U.S. Elections—and Colorado Schools May Be the Pipeline

Socialist candidates are now running openly in American primaries, a shift that would have ended campaigns a generation ago, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The trend reflects what conservatives argue is decades of left-wing indoctrination in K-12 and higher education.

Republicans and conservatives warn that the platform behind this shift—abolishing borders, defunding police, seizing private property—represents radical ideology far beyond traditional Democratic politics. The concern: Colorado's public schools, like those nationwide, have become breeding grounds for progressive activism rather than civic education grounded in constitutional principles and free-market economics.

This matters for Colorado taxpayers and parents. If schools are teaching students that socialism is a viable—even desirable—political system, that directly undermines the values of limited government and individual liberty that built Colorado's economy. It also shapes the electorate that will decide Colorado's future on taxes, energy policy, gun rights, and land use.

Conservatives argue that schools should teach critical thinking about all ideologies, not advocate for any. The question now: Will Colorado parents demand transparency in curricula and push back against ideological capture of classrooms before the next generation votes?

Watch whether Colorado school boards face organized parent pressure to audit civics and social studies curricula for progressive bias.
