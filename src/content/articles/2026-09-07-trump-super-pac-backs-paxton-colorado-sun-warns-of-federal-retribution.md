# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Race; Colorado Sun Warns of 'Retribution'

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is flooding $10 million into Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign against Democrat James Talarico, according to FEC filings reported by The Daily Wire—a show of force that underscores Trump's commitment to backing loyalists in competitive races.

Meanwhile, Colorado Sun columnist Carman has sounded the alarm that Trump's administration is using federal leverage to coerce Colorado into surrendering constitutional powers, framing the dynamic as a "campaign of retribution" targeting the state.

Republicans argue Trump's financial backing for Paxton reflects legitimate party support for a conservative candidate facing a tough general election. The Left counters that Trump's deployment of super PAC resources signals authoritarian overreach and political vengeance against states—including Colorado—that resist his agenda.

The Colorado angle matters here: if the Sun's characterization holds weight, Colorado taxpayers and state officials could face federal pressure on energy policy, water rights, or other sovereignty issues. Conservatives counter that such warnings often exaggerate executive authority and ignore Colorado's own responsibility to defend its interests.

Watch whether Trump's federal agencies follow through with concrete pressure on Colorado—or whether this remains rhetorical posturing.
