# Missouri AG Fights State Court Ruling on Trump-Backed Redistricting Map

Missouri's Republican Attorney General Catherine Hanaway has escalated a redistricting battle by filing an emergency petition with the U.S. Supreme Court to restore a Trump-backed congressional map that Missouri's state supreme court blocked this week (Fox News Politics, Just The News).

The Missouri Supreme Court ruled Thursday that the map cannot take effect, prompting Hanaway's federal appeal. The dispute centers on whether the redrawn districts comply with state constitutional requirements—a fight that hinges on competing interpretations of what constitutes fair representation.

Republicans argue the map reflects legitimate redistricting principles and voter intent, while Democrats and progressive groups contend it unfairly advantages GOP candidates. The case underscores the broader national battle over congressional maps, where both parties aggressively pursue favorable district lines when they control the process.

**Colorado angle:** While this fight plays out in Missouri, Colorado Republicans and conservatives should monitor how the Supreme Court rules—the decision could set precedent for future redistricting disputes in Colorado, where Democrats currently control the independent redistricting commission. A favorable ruling for Republicans could strengthen arguments for more competitive maps in future cycles.

**Watch:** Whether the U.S. Supreme Court accepts Hanaway's emergency petition and how quickly the justices rule on the merits.
