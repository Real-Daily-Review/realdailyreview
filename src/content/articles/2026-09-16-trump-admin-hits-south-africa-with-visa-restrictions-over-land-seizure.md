# Trump Administration Targets South African Land Seizures With Visa Restrictions

The Trump administration is taking direct action against what it views as state-sponsored racial discrimination, with Secretary of State Marco Rubio announcing visa restrictions targeting officials responsible for land seizures and violence against white farmers in South Africa.

According to Fox News and the Daily Caller, the restrictions target those involved in what Rubio characterized as "not adequately addressed" anti-white farmer violence and discriminatory land grabs. The move signals the administration's willingness to use diplomatic tools against what conservatives argue amounts to government-enabled racial persecution.

For Colorado conservatives, the broader principle matters: the Trump administration is defending property rights and equal protection against ideologically driven confiscation—a stark contrast to progressive domestic policies that conservatives say similarly threaten private land ownership through regulatory overreach and eminent domain abuse.

Republicans have long warned that racial discrimination and property seizure, when state-sanctioned, represent tyranny regardless of the victims' identity. This action reinforces that principle on the world stage.

Watch whether Congress moves to tie U.S. aid to South Africa to concrete reforms in land policy and protections for all citizens regardless of race.
