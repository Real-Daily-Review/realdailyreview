# Colorado Forward Party Candidate Chew Pitches Fiscal Discipline Against Democratic Incumbent

A Colorado Navy veteran and engineering entrepreneur is spending his own money to challenge the Democratic Senate incumbent on a platform of fiscal responsibility—a direct rebuke of the spending spree that has defined the Biden-Harris years and their Colorado allies.

Bob Chew, founder and former CEO of an engineering firm, has invested more than $1 million of his personal funds in his Forward Party bid, according to CPR News. His candidacy reflects growing frustration among Colorado voters and business leaders over unchecked government spending and regulatory bloat.

Chew's "problem solver" messaging targets a core conservative concern: Washington and Denver have abandoned fiscal discipline. Democrats have responded by tying Republican and independent challengers to "socialist" policy positions—a rhetorical move that underscores how polarized Senate races have become, even as candidates like South Carolina Democrat Annie Andrews claim they'll "stand up to their party" (Fox News).

The contrast is stark. While Democrats defend their spending agenda as necessary "investment," conservatives argue it has fueled inflation, burdened Colorado families and small businesses, and expanded government beyond constitutional limits. Chew's self-funded campaign signals he's not beholden to party machinery—a pitch that may resonate with Colorado independents tired of partisan gridlock.
