# Denver Dem Establishment Braces for Socialist Primary Challenge

Colorado's Democratic establishment is girding for an internal civil war as socialist candidates eye Diana DeGette's safe Denver congressional seat, exposing deep fractures within the party over ideology and control. DeGette, the long-serving incumbent, is reportedly not taking the challenge passively, signaling real anxiety among moderate Democrats who fear losing ground to the party's far-left wing.

The tension mirrors national Democratic infighting. When pressed on whether socialist primary victories could threaten House Democratic unity, Rep. John Larson (D-Conn.) lashed out at reporters, per Fox News Politics, suggesting the question itself was inflammatory—a telling sign that party leadership views the socialist surge as a genuine threat to cohesion.

For Colorado conservatives, this is instructive: it demonstrates that Democrats' "big tent" is splitting over fundamental questions of governance and ideology. While socialists push for expanded government control and wealth redistribution, moderate Democrats scramble to defend their turf. The outcome will shape whether Colorado's 1st Congressional District—currently a Democratic stronghold—tilts further left or remains merely center-left.

**Watch whether DeGette's primary challenger forces her to adopt more radical positions to survive, or whether moderate Democrats successfully contain the socialist insurgency.**
