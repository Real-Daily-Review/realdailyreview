# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Medicine Signals Broader Left-Wing Push

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's unapologetic defense of child sex-change procedures—arguing doctors, not government, should decide—exposes the progressive playbook Colorado voters should recognize heading into 2024.

Abdul El-Sayed, the Democratic nominee, told Michigan State University students Friday that physicians should have unfettered authority to perform gender-transition treatments on minors without state oversight. He cited his own medical credentials as justification (Fox News, The Daily Wire).

**Republicans counter** that parental rights and child safety trump medical autonomy. GOP lawmakers across the country, including in Colorado, have pushed legislation requiring parental consent and age restrictions on puberty blockers and surgeries—arguing children cannot consent to irreversible medical interventions. Conservatives say the left's "follow the science" rhetoric conveniently ignores the absence of long-term safety data on these procedures in minors.

Colorado Republicans have championed similar protections. In 2023, GOP legislators backed bills restricting gender-transition treatments for children, though Democratic majorities in the state legislature blocked passage.

El-Sayed's comments reflect the Democratic establishment's resistance to any guardrails on pediatric gender medicine—a position increasingly at odds with parental instincts and medical caution.
