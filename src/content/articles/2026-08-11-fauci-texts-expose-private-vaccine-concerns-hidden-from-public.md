# Fauci's Sealed Texts Reveal Private Vaccine Concerns He Never Shared With Public

Newly released text messages from Dr. Anthony Fauci's government phone show the nation's top pandemic official privately discussed theoretical miscarriage risks tied to COVID vaccines in 2021—concerns he never disclosed to the American public, according to documents released this week by Republican Senators Ron Johnson and Rand Paul.

The texts reveal Fauci was warned about "significant cytokines storm and fever" following second doses, with potential pregnancy implications (Just The News). Yet his public messaging consistently downplayed or omitted such risks, raising questions about transparency during the federal vaccine rollout.

Republicans argue the discrepancy between Fauci's private doubts and public reassurances undermines public trust and suggests deliberate information control. Louisiana's Attorney General notes that citizens raising identical concerns were dismissed as "anti-vaxxers and liars" (Just The News).

The Daily Wire reports the newly surfaced messages contradict Fauci's public statements about vaccine safety, while Fox News notes the release has sparked fierce backlash from GOP lawmakers demanding accountability.

For Colorado: These revelations matter to state residents who faced federal vaccine mandates affecting employment, healthcare, and education—decisions made partly on assurances that now appear incomplete.
