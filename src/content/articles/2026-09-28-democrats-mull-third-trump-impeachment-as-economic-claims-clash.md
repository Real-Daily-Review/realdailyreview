# Democrats Float Third Trump Impeachment as Economic Claims Dominate Debate

House Democrats are openly discussing a third impeachment of President Trump should they retake the chamber, with Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) telling NBC's *Meet the Press* that doing so would amount to "setting a standard" for future partisan warfare. This signals Democrats' willingness to weaponize impeachment as a political tool rather than reserve it for genuine constitutional crises—a dangerous precedent that threatens to further erode institutional norms (Washington Examiner).

Meanwhile, Trump claims employment has reached historic highs, stating more Americans are working than at any point in U.S. history. The administration argues mainstream media refuses to report these economic gains fairly (Just The News).

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's energy sector and small businesses have benefited from pro-growth policies and regulatory rollback. A return to Democratic control of Congress would likely mean renewed pressure on oil and gas development, mining operations, and agricultural land use—precisely the industries that drive rural Colorado's economy.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Democrats actually pursue impeachment theater in 2025, and whether Colorado voters in swing districts recognize that endless partisan investigations distract from kitchen-table issues like inflation, energy costs, and job creation.
