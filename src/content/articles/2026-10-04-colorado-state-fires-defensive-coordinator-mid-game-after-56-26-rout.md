# Colorado State Fires Defensive Coordinator Mid-Game After Blowout Loss

Colorado State head coach Jim Mora made a dramatic move Saturday, removing defensive coordinator Tyson Summers from the sideline during the Rams' 56-26 loss to Oregon State and firing him outright after the game.

According to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31, Summers disappeared from the field early in the contest. "Tyson was relieved of his play-calling duties. He left the field, and he's no longer with this football program," Mora said postgame.

The swift dismissal signals Mora's willingness to make immediate personnel changes when performance falters—a stark contrast to the patience some coaches extend to struggling coordinators. For Colorado State athletics, already operating under budget constraints common to state universities, the mid-season coordinator change raises questions about depth on the coaching staff and the financial impact of turnover.

The Rams' defensive collapse—allowing 56 points to Oregon State—underscores the urgency. Mora's decisive action reflects the accountability standard expected in major college football, where taxpayer-funded state programs face scrutiny over coaching expenditures and results.
