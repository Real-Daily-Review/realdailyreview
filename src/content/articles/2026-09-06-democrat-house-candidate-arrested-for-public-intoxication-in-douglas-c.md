# Democrat Candidate's Public Intoxication Arrest Raises Questions About Judgment in Douglas County Race

A Lone Tree Democrat running for Colorado's House of Representatives was arrested in May for public intoxication, according to Texas court records uncovered by the *Denver Post*. The arrest raises questions about the candidate's fitness for office just months before the election.

Republicans argue the incident demonstrates poor judgment and raises concerns about who Douglas County voters are considering for the legislature. The arrest record, filed in Texas courts, suggests a pattern of behavior that should concern voters evaluating candidates' character and decision-making.

Democrats have not yet publicly responded to the arrest disclosure, though the candidate's campaign could argue the incident is isolated or unrelated to legislative duties.

Douglas County, a swing area with growing conservative strength, has become a key battleground in Colorado legislative races. Voters in the county—home to many families, small-business owners, and gun owners—typically prioritize candidates who demonstrate personal responsibility and sound judgment.

The arrest comes as Democrats face headwinds in suburban Colorado counties over concerns about crime, government spending, and progressive policies. Republicans are banking on character issues and policy disagreements to flip competitive seats.

**Watch for whether the candidate addresses the arrest publicly and how Douglas County voters respond in polling ahead of Election Day.**
