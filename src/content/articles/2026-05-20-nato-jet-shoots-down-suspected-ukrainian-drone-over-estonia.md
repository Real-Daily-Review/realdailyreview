# NATO Jet Downs Suspected Ukrainian Drone Over Estonia

A NATO fighter jet shot down an unidentified drone over Estonian airspace, according to BBC World and Deutsche Welle. Estonia suspects the aircraft was a Ukrainian projectile that had been knocked off course by Russian electronic jamming.

The incident marks a rare direct engagement between NATO forces and an airborne object in the region amid the ongoing war in Ukraine. Estonian authorities did not immediately confirm the drone's origin or provide additional details about the interception.

The downing underscores escalating tensions over airspace violations near NATO's eastern flank. Russian electronic warfare capabilities have been documented disrupting Ukrainian weapons systems throughout the conflict, and this incident suggests such interference may extend to affecting the trajectory of Ukrainian munitions near allied territory.
