# Trump Warns of Iranian Retaliation After Ceasefire Collapse; U.S. Security Threat Escalates

President Trump is threatening to eliminate additional Iranian leadership tiers following Tehran's breakdown of the Strait of Hormuz ceasefire, while acknowledging his own vulnerability to Iranian assassination plots. At this week's NATO summit, Trump offered a rare admission that he may "be gone too" after Iran placed him atop its kill list.

The threat comes as federal prosecutors have already unsealed charges alleging Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps tasked an operative with surveilling and assassinating the president—a directive issued on October 7, 2024. According to the complaint, this represents a concrete Iranian effort to target sitting U.S. leadership.

Republicans argue Trump's willingness to confront Iranian aggression directly—including potential strikes on regime officials—demonstrates resolve against state-sponsored terrorism that the Biden-Harris administration failed to deter. Democrats and Iran hawks across the spectrum have raised concerns about escalation risks in an already volatile region.
