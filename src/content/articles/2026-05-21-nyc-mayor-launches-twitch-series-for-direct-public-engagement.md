# NYC Mayor Launches Twitch Series to Engage Constituents

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is launching a Twitch streaming series called "Talk with the People" today at 4 p.m. ET, according to TechCrunch and The Verge.

The show will allow Mamdani to respond to questions submitted by viewers in the chat, bringing what he describes as direct engagement between City Hall and New Yorkers on platforms where they already spend time. "By launching the country's first recurring cross-platform" series of this kind, Mamdani told Polygon, he aims to speak directly with constituents outside traditional media channels.

The Washington Examiner characterized the initiative as Mamdani's "latest effort to use social media to propel his agenda," though the mayor framed it as a straightforward outreach tool.

The series represents a shift in how local officials communicate with the public, leveraging streaming platforms popular with younger demographics. Whether the format will become a model for other municipal leaders remains to be seen.
