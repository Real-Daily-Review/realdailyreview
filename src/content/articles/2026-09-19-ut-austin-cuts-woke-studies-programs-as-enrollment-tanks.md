# UT Austin Axes Woke Studies Programs as Enrollment Collapses

University of Texas at Austin is shuttering its women's studies, gender studies, and ethnic studies majors due to plummeting enrollment—a rare victory for conservatives tired of ideological bloat in higher education.

The move reflects what Republicans have long argued: progressive academic programs disconnected from market demand and job prospects don't survive scrutiny. Texas state leaders, including GOP officials, applied political pressure on the university to cut the underperforming departments, according to Fox News. The university confirmed low enrollment as the primary driver.

This matters for Colorado. State universities here—CU Boulder, CSU, and others—operate similar programs that drain resources while producing few graduates. Conservative taxpayers foot the bill for administrative overhead and faculty positions in fields with minimal workforce demand. If UT Austin's decision gains traction, Colorado legislators should examine whether taxpayer-funded programs at Boulder, Fort Collins, and Denver justify their existence through enrollment and employment outcomes.

Democrats typically defend such programs as essential to diversity and inclusion, though enrollment numbers tell a different story. The left claims these majors serve underrepresented students, but if students aren't enrolling, the argument collapses.
