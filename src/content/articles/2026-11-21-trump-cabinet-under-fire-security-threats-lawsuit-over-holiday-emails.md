# Trump Cabinet Members Face Legal and Security Threats as Left Escalates Pressure

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has relocated to a military base following credible Iranian assassination threats, while Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins faces a federal lawsuit over Christian-themed workplace emails—two stark examples of the legal and security gauntlet Trump's cabinet now navigates.

Bessent's move came after officials reported Iranian threats had reached "an advanced stage," according to Fox News. Meanwhile, a Biden-appointed federal judge in California issued a preliminary injunction against Rollins, ruling that her "He is risen" Easter message and Christmas greetings to USDA staff likely violated the Establishment Clause (Fox News and The Daily Wire).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue the lawsuit represents weaponized litigation by the left—using courts to harass Trump appointees over routine holiday messages that pose no constitutional threat. Rollins' emails were voluntary, non-coercive communications in a secular workplace context. The ruling signals that progressive judges will aggressively police even mild religious expression by Republican officials, chilling free speech and religious liberty.

The Iranian threats underscore a separate danger: hostile foreign actors targeting U.S. officials, a reality the Biden-Harris administration reportedly failed to adequately address before leaving office.

**What to watch:** Whether Rollins' case reaches the Supreme Court and whether Colorado's federal judiciary follows California's aggressive Establishment Clause interpretation in similar cases.
