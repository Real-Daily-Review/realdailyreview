# DOGE's Early Stumbles Hand JD Vance a Chance to Reshape Waste-Fighting Agenda

Elon Musk's Department of Government Efficiency promised bold action against federal waste and fraud—then immediately hit the wall of bureaucratic reality and political pushback.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, DOGE generated early excitement but quickly faced disappointment as initial efforts stalled. The piece suggests Vice President JD Vance now has an opening to recalibrate the anti-fraud campaign and deliver tangible results that resonate beyond Washington's revolving door.

For Colorado taxpayers and businesses, the stakes are real. Federal waste directly inflates the tax burden on working families and diverts resources from genuine priorities. If DOGE can identify and eliminate duplicate programs, bloated agency overhead, and fraudulent spending—particularly in federal land management, energy permitting, and social programs—Colorado stands to benefit from leaner government and faster economic decision-making.

Conservatives have long argued that Washington's bureaucratic machinery wastes billions annually through redundancy and poor oversight. A functional anti-fraud operation could prove the case for smaller, more efficient government. Democrats, by contrast, typically resist aggressive audits of federal spending, fearing cuts to programs they champion.

**Watch whether Vance and the administration can translate DOGE's initial momentum into concrete spending cuts and fraud recoveries—or whether the effort becomes another unfulfilled campaign promise.**
