# Lone Tree Arson: Police Hunt Suspect in Deliberate Median Fire

An arson suspect remains at large after intentionally igniting a tire fire in a Lone Tree median Saturday night, raising questions about public safety in the Denver suburb.

The fire was reported shortly before 10 p.m. near County Line Road and Chester Street, according to 9News Denver. Police say the suspect used a tire and wood blocks to start the blaze deliberately—not an accident, but a criminal act.

Lone Tree police are actively searching for the suspect (KDVR Fox31 Denver). The incident underscores a broader concern conservatives raise about property crime and public disorder in Colorado's growing metro areas: as government focuses resources on progressive priorities like climate regulations and social spending, basic law enforcement and community safety often take a backseat.

For Lone Tree residents and taxpayers, this is a direct hit.
