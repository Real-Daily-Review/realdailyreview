# Lakewood Police Officer Killed in RTD Bus Shooting; Three Colleagues Wounded

A Thursday evening shooting on a Regional Transportation District bus in Lakewood claimed the life of Lakewood police Agent Nick Graham and left three fellow officers and a civilian passenger injured, according to 9News Denver and the Colorado Sun. The incident occurred around 6:30 p.m. on Wadsworth Boulevard near Colfax Avenue.

Three of the four wounded victims have since been released from the hospital, the Colorado Sun reports. The shooting underscores Colorado's ongoing public safety challenges on mass transit—a vulnerability progressives often cite to justify expanded gun restrictions, while conservatives argue enforcement of existing laws and armed presence are the real deterrents.

In response to the tragedy, neighboring police departments from five agencies are now handling Lakewood calls to allow grieving officers time to recover, according to 9News. This mutual-aid arrangement reflects the professional solidarity of law enforcement but also highlights staffing pressures facing Colorado police departments.

The incident comes as Colorado communities grapple with violent crime on public transit systems—a reality that should prompt serious questions about RTD security protocols and whether current resources adequately protect riders and officers.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether city and state officials use this tragedy to demand more funding for police and security infrastructure, or whether they pivot toward progressive calls for gun control that do nothing to stop criminals already breaking the law.
