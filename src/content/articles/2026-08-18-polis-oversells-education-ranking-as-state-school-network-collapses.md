# Gov. Polis Caught Overselling Colorado's Education Ranking While State School Network Faces Dissolution

Governor Jared Polis is selectively citing U.S. News rankings to inflate Colorado's education performance, according to *Complete Colorado*, a move that obscures the state's actual standing and raises questions about how the administration uses data to justify education policy.

Meanwhile, the Colorado education cooperative that infamously opened a publicly funded Christian school is heading toward dissolution. The regional education service organization (ERBOCES) has failed to attract buyers or merger partners, prompting board members to declare the network's end inevitable, *CPR News* reports.

The timing underscores a broader problem: Colorado's education establishment struggles with both accountability and judgment. Polis's selective use of rankings mirrors how progressive administrators nationwide cherry-pick metrics to avoid scrutiny of spending and outcomes. Conservatives argue that Colorado's $20 billion-plus annual K-12 budget deserves transparent performance measures, not political spin.

The ERBOCES collapse—triggered by its controversial Christian school venture—reveals how government education bureaucracies often operate without meaningful oversight. Taxpayers funded an organization that overstepped its authority, and now dissolution is the only remedy.
