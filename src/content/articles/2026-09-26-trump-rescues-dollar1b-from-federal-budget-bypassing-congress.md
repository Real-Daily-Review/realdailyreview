# Trump Claws Back Nearly $1B in Federal Spending—Again

President Trump is using rare executive authority to rescind nearly $1 billion in congressionally approved spending, targeting DEI programs, immigration initiatives, and foreign aid—a move that has Republicans divided and Democrats fuming over process.

The rescission marks only the second "pocket rescission" in 50 years, both issued by Trump in his second term (Washington Examiner). Under federal law, Trump can request Congress rescind funding and withhold it for 45 days while lawmakers decide whether to approve, reject, or ignore the request (Just The News).

The cuts target $810 million in immigration and refugee programs, foreign debt-relief initiatives, and business grants. Republicans backing fiscal restraint applaud the effort to trim bloated federal spending. However, Sen. Susan Collins, the Republican chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee, criticized the lack of warning or consultation, signaling GOP fractures over executive overreach (9News Denver).

Conservatives argue the move demonstrates Trump's commitment to cutting wasteful spending and reining in progressive priorities like DEI. Democrats counter that unilateral rescissions bypass Congress's constitutional power of the purse and set a dangerous precedent.

For Colorado taxpayers and businesses, the outcome depends on which programs lose funding and whether any state-level grants are affected.

Watch whether Congress moves to block or approve the rescission—and whether Trump escalates this tactic further.
