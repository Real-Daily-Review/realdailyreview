# Democratic Rep. Stevens Needs U.S. Marshals After Crossing Party Line on Israel

A sitting Democratic congresswoman is requesting federal protection after endorsing a Republican—a stark sign of how far the Democratic Party has drifted from its historical pro-Israel stance.

Rep. Haley Stevens (D-MI) requested U.S. Marshal protection following death threats triggered by her endorsement of Republican Mike Rogers in Michigan's Senate race, according to reporting by *The Daily Wire* and *Axios*. Stevens's break came over her opposition to Democrat nominee Abdul El-Sayed, whom she views as hostile to Israel.

National Review reports that Stevens is far from alone: multiple Democrats harbor serious reservations about El-Sayed's anti-Israel positions, but party pressure and fear of backlash have kept most silent. Stevens, to her credit, finally "hit her breaking point," as *National Review* observed, willing to risk her political standing to defend a core American alliance.

This reflects a broader realignment within the Democratic Party, where progressive activists increasingly dominate foreign policy positions—pushing candidates and elected officials toward positions that alienate traditional pro-Israel Democrats. The fact that Stevens faces death threats for simply endorsing a Republican over an anti-Israel Democrat underscores how intolerant the party's activist base has become.

Colorado Democrats should take note: the national party's hostility toward Israel and its tolerance for extremism may soon force similar reckoning votes in our state.
