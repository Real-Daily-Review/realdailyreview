# Actress Selma Blair Arrested After Violent Midair Meltdown on Delta Flight

Hollywood actress Selma Blair was arrested Sunday night after a disturbing incident aboard a Delta Air Lines flight from Los Angeles to New York's JFK Airport, raising fresh questions about airline safety and passenger conduct at 35,000 feet.

According to reports from the New York Post and The Daily Wire, Blair allegedly punched one flight attendant and bit another during the altercation. The Post reported that audio captured Blair "screaming maniacally" as she was dragged off the aircraft. The Daily Wire added that Blair attempted to open the plane's door mid-flight before crew members intervened.

The incident is particularly striking given that Blair had posted on Instagram from the aircraft moments before the disturbance, according to Page Six reporting.

Delta and federal aviation authorities will likely face scrutiny over passenger screening protocols and crew safety procedures. While this incident involves a single passenger's criminal conduct—not a systemic policy failure—it underscores why airlines must maintain strict enforcement of federal air safety rules and why flight crews need robust support from law enforcement.

Colorado travelers and business leaders who rely on commercial aviation should monitor how Delta and the FAA respond to ensure that disruptive passengers face swift consequences and that crew safety remains the priority.
