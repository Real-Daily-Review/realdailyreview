# Trump Praises Cruz for 2028, But Denver Democrats Eye Convention Host Role

Donald Trump stopped short of endorsing Ted Cruz's potential 2028 presidential bid Thursday, offering only measured praise—a telling signal that the former president may be keeping his options open in a crowded Republican primary field (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, Democrats are eyeing Denver as a potential host city for their 2028 national convention, narrowing their shortlist to Denver, Boston, and Philadelphia (Denver Post).

The dual developments highlight competing visions for Colorado's political future. Republicans are focused on the 2028 succession battle, with Trump's lukewarm comments about Cruz suggesting the party's kingmaker role remains unsettled. Democrats, by contrast, are banking on hosting their convention in Colorado—a state they've locked down in recent cycles—as a show of strength heading into the general election.

Conservatives should note the strategic calculus: a Democratic National Convention in Denver would pump millions into the state's economy while cementing Colorado's status as a Democratic stronghold. Republicans counter that Colorado remains competitive if the right candidate energizes voters on border security, energy independence, and Second Amendment rights—issues that resonate with the state's rural and suburban voters.
