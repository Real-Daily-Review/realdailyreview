# House Democrats Ready Partisan Witch Hunts Against Trump

Incoming House Oversight Chairman Robert Garcia is signaling that Democrats plan to weaponize the committee against President Trump, calling him and his sons the "Trump crime family" before investigations even begin—a tactic that mirrors Republican James Comer's Biden-focused probes but promises to be far more aggressive.

Garcia's inflammatory rhetoric, reported by the *Washington Examiner*, reveals Democrats' intent to use the Oversight Committee as a political cudgel rather than a legitimate investigative body. Republicans argue this represents the worst kind of partisan abuse of congressional power: launching investigations based on predetermined conclusions rather than evidence.

The Colorado angle matters here. Colorado Democrats in Congress—including Representatives Joe Neguse and Jena Griswold—have consistently aligned with the party's most aggressive anti-Trump positions. If Garcia's committee launches sweeping investigations, Colorado taxpayers will foot the bill for staff, hearings, and legal costs that many conservatives view as purely political theater rather than legitimate oversight.

Republicans counter that Democrats learned nothing from the failed Trump impeachments and endless Russia investigations. They argue the Oversight Committee should focus on actual government waste, not settling political scores.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado's Democratic delegation publicly endorses Garcia's investigation agenda or attempts to distance themselves from the partisan approach.
