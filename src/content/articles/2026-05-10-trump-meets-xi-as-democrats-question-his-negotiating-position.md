# Trump Heads to China Amid Democratic Criticism Over Negotiating Position

President Trump is preparing to meet with Chinese President Xi Jinping, with Iran policy expected to feature prominently in discussions, according to U.S. officials cited by Al Jazeera. The administration is likely to pressure China over its purchase of Iranian oil during the ongoing conflict, officials said.

The visit comes as Democrats raise concerns about Trump's negotiating strength. Sen. Jack Reed (D-R.I.), the top Democrat on the Senate Armed Services Committee, told Fox News Sunday that Trump is entering the meeting "terribly weakened," according to The Hill. Reed did not elaborate on specific factors undermining the president's position in available excerpts.

The timing of the China visit underscores the administration's focus on constraining Iran's economic lifelines, particularly energy sales that fund its military operations. Beijing has been a significant buyer of Iranian crude despite U.S. sanctions pressure.

The meeting represents a key diplomatic engagement as Trump navigates complex relationships with major powers while managing multiple foreign policy challenges simultaneously.
