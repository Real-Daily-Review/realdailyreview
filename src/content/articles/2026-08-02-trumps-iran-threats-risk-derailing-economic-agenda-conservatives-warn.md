# Trump Threatens Iran Strikes, But War Distraction Risks Economic Agenda

President Trump is escalating military pressure on Iran while signaling willingness to halt strikes if a deal emerges to reopen the Strait of Hormuz—but conservatives warn the brewing conflict is derailing his core economic mission.

According to 9News Denver, Trump has threatened additional strikes on Iran as the administration pushes to restore shipping through the critical waterway. The move reflects a hardline approach to Iranian aggression, but it's drawing criticism from the right.

National Review argues the Iran conflict is becoming a dangerous distraction. The outlet warns that by pursuing both tariffs and military escalation in the Middle East, Trump risks surrendering his signature strength: economic stewardship. Conservatives worry that prolonged foreign entanglement will consume political capital, media attention, and resources needed to implement tax cuts, deregulation, and pro-growth policies.

For Colorado, sustained Middle East conflict could spike energy prices—already volatile under Biden's anti-oil policies—and complicate efforts to revive domestic energy production that supports thousands of state jobs.

Republicans generally support strong deterrence against Iranian threats, but fiscal conservatives and Trump's base increasingly question whether another Middle East war serves American interests or drains the presidency of focus on the economic revival voters demanded.

Watch whether Trump can negotiate a genuine de-escalation or whether Iran tensions consume his second term.
