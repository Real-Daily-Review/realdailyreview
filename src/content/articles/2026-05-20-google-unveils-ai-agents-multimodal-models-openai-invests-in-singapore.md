# Google Unveils AI Agents, Multimodal Models as OpenAI Partnership Expands

Google rolled out a suite of advanced AI tools at its I/O developer conference, including new agentic assistants and multimodal models designed to compete with OpenAI and Anthropic, while Singapore announced major AI investments from both tech giants.

The centerpiece of Google's announcement is Gemini Spark, a 24/7 personal assistant with Gmail integration built on Gemini's base models, according to TechCrunch. Google also introduced Gemini Omni, a multimodal model that reasons across text, images, audio, and video to generate and edit videos through conversation, starting with Omni Flash (TechCrunch). The updates signal Google's effort to transform Gemini from a standalone chatbot into an all-purpose AI hub, per TechCrunch.

Separately, Singapore inked partnerships with both Google and OpenAI to strengthen its position as a global AI hub. OpenAI committed $234 million to the local ecosystem, according to CNBC.

The announcements underscore intensifying competition among major AI developers. Google's focus on "agents"—AI systems designed to autonomously complete tasks—reflects industry-wide efforts to move beyond conversational chatbots toward more practical applications.

Watch for how quickly these new tools gain adoption among developers and enterprise users, and whether Singapore's AI investments yield measurable advances in regional AI capabilities.
