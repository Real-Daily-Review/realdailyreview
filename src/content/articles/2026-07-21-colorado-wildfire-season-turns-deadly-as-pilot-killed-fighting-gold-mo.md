# Colorado Wildfires Claim Pilot as State Battles Multiple Blazes

Colorado's wildfire season is turning deadly, with crews racing to contain multiple fires across the state as a pilot lost his life fighting the Gold Mountain fire.

Firefighters worked through Sunday preparing containment lines and bracing for monsoon weather that could either help or complicate suppression efforts (Colorado Sun). A procession along I-25 is scheduled Monday to honor the fallen pilot, underscoring the human cost of wildfire response.

**The conservative angle:** Colorado taxpayers foot massive bills for wildfire suppression—costs that spike when federal land-management policies prioritize environmental litigation over forest thinning and fuel reduction. Republicans have long argued that decades of fire-suppression policies and restrictions on timber harvesting have left Colorado's forests dangerously overstocked with dead wood and undergrowth, creating tinderboxes. Meanwhile, progressive environmental groups have sued to block logging and fuel-reduction projects, tying up resources that could prevent catastrophic burns.

The state is deploying crews across multiple active fires (9News), but prevention through aggressive forest management remains underfunded and legally hamstrung by environmental opposition.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado's legislature will finally loosen restrictions on private and state forest thinning, or whether environmental groups will continue blocking the fuel-reduction projects that could spare lives and property.
