# Democrats Sweep Border Districts, Vow Tariff Fight as Voters Punish High Energy Costs

Democrats captured every House seat along the Southern border—nine districts spanning Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, and California—flipping three previously Republican seats in Tuesday's midterms, according to The Daily Wire. Meanwhile, voters fed up with soaring utility bills handed Democrats control of Georgia's Public Service Commission, The Daily Caller reports, signaling public anger over energy costs that could embolden progressive regulators nationwide.

**The conservative concern:** Republicans argue Democrats will use border control of Congress to block Trump administration immigration enforcement and push open-border policies. On energy, conservatives warn that Democratic utility board victories could accelerate costly renewable mandates and grid instability—a cautionary tale for Colorado, where similar regulatory battles loom over power rates and grid reliability.

Democrats are already signaling plans to fight Trump's Canada tariffs once they assume congressional control in January (New York Post), hoping to peel off Republican votes. Conservatives counter that tariff relief would undercut domestic manufacturing and energy independence gains.

**Colorado angle:** Energy costs hit Colorado families and businesses hard. If Democratic-led utility boards elsewhere drive rates higher through aggressive green mandates, expect similar pressure on Colorado's Public Utilities Commission and state legislature.

Watch for whether Colorado Republicans will defend rate-payers against regulatory overreach in the 2025 session.
