# Democrats Can't Escape the Woke Record They Built

Progressive Democrats are scrambling to distance themselves from the radical rhetoric and policies they championed during 2019–2021, but their own words—captured on video and in print—won't let them. Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and other left-wing figures are attempting what *National Review* calls a reckoning without real accountability, while candidates like Wisconsin gubernatorial hopeful Francesca Hong are openly walking back positions they once championed (per *Washington Examiner*).

Republicans argue this rebranding exposes the fundamental dishonesty of the progressive movement. *The Daily Wire* notes that Democratic Socialists can't outrun rhetoric permanently recorded and available for public scrutiny. The left's attempt to memory-hole "Woke 1.0" reveals they know their agenda was unpopular—they're just hoping voters have short memories.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: as Democrats nationally retreat from their most radical positions, state-level progressives here are still pushing those same policies on energy, education, and gun control. Colorado voters should ask whether our Democratic-controlled legislature will follow the national retreat or double down on the agenda that's now politically toxic nationwide.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from their own 2019–2021 voting records before 2026 elections arrive.
