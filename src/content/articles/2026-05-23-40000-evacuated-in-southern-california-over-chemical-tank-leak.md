# California Chemical Tank Leak Forces 40,000 Evacuations

A hazardous chemical leak from a storage tank in Southern California has forced the evacuation of approximately 40,000 residents and prompted school closures, according to NPR and BBC World.

Officials warned that the tank contains a "highly volatile" substance that poses a rupture or explosion risk, according to NPR. The leak remained ongoing as of Friday, when evacuation orders took effect.

Authorities have not yet disclosed the specific chemical involved or provided details on the cause of the leak. The scope of the evacuation—affecting tens of thousands of residents—underscores the severity officials attribute to the hazard.

Schools in the affected area shut down in response to the emergency. No injuries have been reported as of the latest updates from either outlet.
