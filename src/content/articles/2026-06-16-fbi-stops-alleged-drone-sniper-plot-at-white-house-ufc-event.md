# FBI Thwarts Alleged Drone-and-Sniper Plot Against White House UFC Event

Federal agents disrupted what officials describe as a coordinated terror plot targeting Sunday's UFC Freedom 250 event on the White House South Lawn, with five suspects now in custody across multiple states.

According to Fox News and the New York Post, the alleged scheme involved explosive drones and snipers aimed at the high-profile sporting event. FBI Director Kash Patel stated that "planned attacks were stopped cold," per Just The News, underscoring the agency's swift intervention.
