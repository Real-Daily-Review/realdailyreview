# Democratic Socialism Gaining Ground Among Democrats While Conservatives Sound Alarm

Colorado conservatives are watching a troubling shift in the national Democratic Party: socialist ideology is moving from fringe to mainstream, and it's reshaping the party's platform on everything from economics to foreign policy.

According to Fox News Politics, socialist primary wins are signaling a leftward tilt within Democratic ranks, even as some Gen Z voters show resistance to communist framing. Senator John Fetterman's appearance alongside 800 pro-Israel students in Washington underscores internal party fractures over how far left to go.

Complete Colorado warns that "democratic socialism" is merely a gateway to full communism, with the Democratic Socialists of America platform threatening constitutional checks and balances. Conservatives argue that DSA's agenda—wealth redistribution, government control of production, and dismantling of market economies—would devastate Colorado's energy sector, small businesses, and individual liberty.

The stakes for Colorado are direct: socialist-aligned Democrats already control state legislature majorities and the governor's office. Their push for higher taxes, aggressive regulation, and government-directed "green energy" mandates reflects DSA priorities, not free-market conservatism.

Taxpayers and business owners should monitor whether Colorado Democrats embrace or distance themselves from national socialist movements ahead of 2026 elections.
