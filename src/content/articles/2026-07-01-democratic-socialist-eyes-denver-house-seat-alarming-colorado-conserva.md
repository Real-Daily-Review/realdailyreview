# Democratic Socialist Eyes Denver House Seat, Raising Concerns Over Radical Agenda

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress, alarming conservatives who worry about the direction of Colorado's urban politics. According to CPR News, Melat Kiros—a member of the Democratic Socialists of America—is positioning herself as a serious contender for the House seat, signaling the growing influence of far-left ideology in Colorado's Democratic stronghold.

Republicans and conservatives argue that DSA-backed candidates push policies antithetical to Colorado values: massive tax increases, government-run healthcare, wealth redistribution, and expanded regulatory control over energy and business. Denver voters would be electing someone committed to socialist economics at a moment when Colorado's economy depends on competitive markets, energy development, and entrepreneurship.

Democrats counter that younger progressives like Kiros represent legitimate frustration with inequality and corporate influence. They frame her candidacy as energizing the base and pushing the party leftward on healthcare, climate, and labor issues.

The race matters beyond Denver. A DSA representative from Colorado's capital would amplify pressure on state Democrats to adopt more radical policies—from carbon taxes to gun control to aggressive land-use restrictions that could ripple across the Front Range.

Conservatives should monitor whether Kiros gains traction and what her specific policy platform demands from Colorado's congressional delegation.
