# VP Vance Courts the Center—and Liberal Media Takes Notice

Vice President JD Vance's appearance on "The View" this week signals a calculated play for centrist voters ahead of potential 2028 ambitions, with even the show's liberal hosts grudgingly acknowledging his appeal.

Joy Behar, the daytime talk show's reliably progressive co-host, complimented Vance as "intelligent" and—in a striking moment of candor on the show's podcast—admitted he "isn't a bad guy," according to the *Washington Examiner*. The comment underscores Vance's ability to disarm skeptics through direct engagement, a tactic the *Daily Wire* notes caught observers off guard given the hosts' vocal anti-Trump positioning.

The broader strategy is transparent: Vance is positioning himself as a bridge figure capable of speaking to both the Trump base and moderate Republicans wary of cultural warfare. His media blitz included friendly appearances on Fox &amp; Friends and Gutfeld! alongside the hostile territory of The View—a deliberate effort to demonstrate cross-partisan credibility as he promotes his new book, *Communion: Finding My Way Back to Faith*.

For Colorado conservatives, the play matters. Vance's willingness to engage hostile media without apology reflects the kind of unapologetic conservatism that resonates in purple states. Watch whether this centrist positioning holds or fractures under pressure from the Trump wing of the party.
