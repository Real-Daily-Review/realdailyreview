# Federal Grand Jury Examines Ilhan Omar's Citizenship Claims as Court Records Reveal Name Discrepancies

A federal grand jury in Minnesota is hearing evidence in an investigation into whether Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) used false information to obtain U.S. citizenship, according to Fox News. Court records unsealed in the case reference three different versions of her name, with federal investigators repeatedly claiming her real name is "Ilhan Nur Said Elmi"—the same surname as her ex-husband, Ahmed Nur Said Elmi. Long-standing allegations suggest Ahmed is Omar's biological brother, though Omar has denied such claims.

Prosecutors have characterized the matter as a "pre-indictment investigation," though specifics on what evidence the grand jury has reviewed remain unclear. The investigation centers on potential false statements or fraudulent documentation related to her citizenship application.

Republicans argue the case underscores serious questions about vetting and accountability for public officials. Democrats have dismissed the allegations as baseless conspiracy theories and attacks on Omar's character.
