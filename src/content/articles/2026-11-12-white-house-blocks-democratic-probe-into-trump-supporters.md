# White House Warns Democrats Against Using Committee Power as Political Weapon

The Trump administration is pushing back hard against Democratic lawmakers weaponizing congressional investigations to intimidate political opponents—a stark reminder that government power, unchecked, becomes a tool of partisan revenge.

White House counsel Will Scharf has warned House Oversight Committee ranking member Rep. Robert Garcia (D-CA) against overreaching congressional authority, according to Fox News Politics. Meanwhile, a top Department of Justice attorney expressed concern that Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D-CT) is using his Senate investigations committee to intimidate Trump supporters, the Washington Examiner reports. Blumenthal's probe into White House ballroom donors has raised alarms among conservatives who see it as a textbook example of using subpoena power to punish political enemies.

Republicans argue this represents exactly the kind of government overreach conservatives have long warned about: weaponized investigations that chill free speech and political participation. When citizens fear donating to causes or candidates will trigger federal scrutiny, the First Amendment suffers in practice, even if it survives on paper.

Democrats counter that legitimate oversight requires investigating potential wrongdoing, but conservatives see a pattern of selective prosecution and intimidation masquerading as accountability.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether these investigations expand to target state-level donors or businesses—a preview of how progressive prosecutors might weaponize power if similar tactics spread westward.
