# Aurora Schools Face Water-Restriction Showdown as Mayor Demands Compliance

Aurora's mayor is calling out the school district for apparently ignoring the region's water restrictions—a rare moment of accountability that Colorado taxpayers should welcome.

Mayor Mike Coffman flagged that school district lawns appear suspiciously green despite mandatory watering limits now in effect across the metro area (KDVR). The accusation cuts to a core conservative principle: government agencies must follow the same rules they impose on residents and businesses. When schools operate under different standards, it signals either incompetence or entitlement—neither acceptable with Colorado's water crisis deepening.

Colorado's Front Range faces genuine scarcity. Residents and farmers are tightening belts. Businesses adjust operations. But if public institutions get a pass, the restrictions become theater rather than policy—and taxpayers foot the bill for both the waste and the hypocrisy.

Coffman's pushback matters because it demonstrates that even within government, someone will demand equal treatment under the law. The school district now faces pressure to either comply visibly or explain why their lawns deserve more water than yours.

Watch whether the district responds with transparency and corrective action, or doubles down with bureaucratic deflection.
