# Ohio GOP Fracture: Miller Defies Resignation Calls, Dumps $1M Into Doomed Campaign

Republican unity is cracking over Ohio Rep. Max Miller's refusal to quit despite mounting domestic abuse allegations. U.S. Sen. Jon Husted (R-OH) joined the chorus Thursday demanding Miller end his reelection bid and resign from Congress, *The Daily Wire* reports. Miller, however, is doubling down—pumping another $1 million of personal funds into his campaign, according to *Breitbart*, even as fellow Republicans including Sen. Bernie Moreno (R-OH) have called for his exit.

The standoff reveals a GOP split: establishment figures want Miller gone to protect the party's credibility and electoral prospects, while Miller himself is betting his personal fortune that he can survive the storm. Republicans argue that candidates facing serious misconduct allegations should step aside to preserve party integrity and focus resources on winnable races. Democrats, predictably, are weaponizing the controversy to paint Republicans as hypocritical on accountability.

**Colorado angle:** While this Ohio drama doesn't directly affect Colorado voters, it signals how national GOP infighting over candidate quality could weaken Republican messaging heading into 2026—a critical election year for Colorado's U.S. Senate seat and statewide races.
