# Kansas Stays Blue Despite Trump Landslide—What Colorado Republicans Should Learn

Democrat Cindy Holscher narrowly won Kansas's governor's race Tuesday, holding the statehouse for Democrats even as President Trump carried the state by 16 points over Kamala Harris in 2024. Holscher, a state senator, defeated Trump-backed Republican Ty Masterson with 50.3% to Masterson's 49.7%, according to the Associated Press (Washington Examiner).

The result extends Democrats' grip on a reliably red state and adds to their recent gubernatorial gains. Democrats now control a majority of U.S. governorships for the first time in two decades, per the New York Post.

**The conservative takeaway:** Kansas Republicans failed to capitalize on Trump's commanding 16-point state victory—a warning sign for Colorado GOP operatives. When presidential coattails don't translate to down-ballot wins, it signals either weak candidate recruitment, messaging failures, or voter ticket-splitting that favors Democratic incumbents.

Colorado Republicans should note that holding statewide office in purple-to-blue states requires more than national momentum. Masterson's narrow loss in a Trump +16 state underscores how Democratic governors can insulate themselves from national headwinds through local brand-building and superior ground operations.
