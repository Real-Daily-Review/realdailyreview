# Trump Admin Bans Gain-of-Function Research; Colorado School District Sues Over Federal Rules

The Trump Administration has formally banned gain-of-function research, a move conservatives say prioritizes public safety and national security over open-ended biomedical experimentation. The State and Health and Human Services departments argue that while such research has produced vaccines and breakthroughs, the risks—particularly around engineered pathogens—outweigh the benefits (Just The News).

Meanwhile, Jefferson County Public Schools is challenging the Trump Administration in court, claiming new federal rules conflict with Colorado law. The lawsuit, filed in federal court, centers on the district's assertion that federal mandates exceed constitutional authority and undermine state control over education policy (CPR News).

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans have long argued that federal overreach in education violates the Tenth Amendment and that states—not Washington bureaucrats—should set school policy. The gain-of-function ban aligns with conservative calls for stronger biosecurity protocols and skepticism of research that could weaponize pathogens. However, some in the biomedical industry worry the ban may chill legitimate vaccine development.

**What to Watch:** Whether Colorado courts side with Jeffco or uphold federal authority—a ruling that could reshape state-federal education battles nationwide and signal how aggressively Trump's agencies will enforce their mandates against blue-state resistance.
