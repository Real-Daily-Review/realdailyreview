# Telecom and Fitness Giants Post Earnings Gains on Price Increases

Verizon and Peloton both reported stronger-than-expected quarterly results driven by raising prices on customers, signaling that consumers are absorbing higher costs across sectors.

Verizon, the New York telecommunications giant, beat Wall Street forecasts as higher prices boosted revenue alongside gains in wireless customer additions, according to the Wall Street Journal. Peloton's stock rose following its earnings report, with CEO Peter Stern telling CNBC that the company views its subscription price increases as "value-driven" moves that helped the fitness platform return to profitability.

The earnings reports arrive as companies across industries test price sensitivity amid persistent inflation concerns. Both firms demonstrated that selective price hikes can improve margins without triggering significant customer defection—at least in the near term.

Separately, oil prices moved higher as investors monitored U.S. developments in Middle East negotiations, according to CNBC, though that story remains distinct from the earnings momentum.

Watch for consumer reaction in coming quarters as more companies report results tied to pricing strategies.
