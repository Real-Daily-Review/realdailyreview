# New York Swing Races Heat Up Over Immigration, ICE Enforcement

Republican Rep. Mike Lawler and Democrat Cait Conley clashed sharply over sanctuary city policies and ICE enforcement in a fiery House debate that could determine control of Congress, according to Fox News. Lawler fact-checked his opponent on New York's sanctuary laws, highlighting the clash between GOP demands for border security and Democratic resistance to immigration enforcement.

The immigration showdown extends to New York's gubernatorial race, where Republican Lee Zeldin challenger and Gov. Kathy Hochul battled over the same issue in their only scheduled debate (Breitbart). Republicans argue that sanctuary city policies and weak bail reform enable criminal aliens to remain on streets, endangering public safety. Democrats counter that aggressive ICE enforcement separates families and harms immigrant communities.

**The Colorado angle:** While these are New York races, the immigration and law-and-order messaging resonates with Colorado voters in competitive districts. Republicans nationwide are weaponizing sanctuary city failures and crime concerns—issues that could shape Colorado's 2026 House races and statewide contests.

**What to watch:** Whether GOP momentum on immigration and public safety translates into Colorado campaign messaging and whether Democrats can neutralize the issue before next cycle.
