# Colorado's School Choice Edge Gives Parents Real Power—But Health Survey Shows Kids Still Struggling

Colorado's decentralized education system empowers parents to choose schools aligned with their values, a competitive advantage progressives in other states are still fighting to achieve (Complete Colorado). The state's local control model allows diverse educational approaches—from traditional public schools to charter and private options—giving families genuine alternatives rather than one-size-fits-all bureaucratic mandates.

Yet a statewide health survey reveals troubling gaps in how well Colorado is protecting youth wellbeing. CPR News reports the Healthy Kids Colorado Survey found encouraging signs overall, but significant numbers of young people make unhealthy choices around weight management, reckless driving, vaping, and alcohol use. The survey identifies "protective factors"—particularly parental involvement—as critical to helping kids navigate these risks.

The takeaway: Colorado's school choice framework gives parents a tool to shape their children's educational environment, but parental engagement remains the linchpin of youth health and safety outcomes. No policy, however well-designed, substitutes for active parenting.

Conservatives should watch whether progressive education advocates use these health findings to justify expanded state intervention in family decisions—or whether policymakers recognize that parental choice and involvement, not more government programs, drive better outcomes.
