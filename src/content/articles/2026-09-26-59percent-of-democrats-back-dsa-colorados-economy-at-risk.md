# Democrats' Socialist Drift Threatens Colorado's Energy, Jobs, and Liberty

A new Quinnipiac poll shows 59 percent of Democrats now embrace Democratic Socialists of America ideology—a troubling sign for Colorado's economy and individual freedoms. The Washington Examiner warns that DSA's stated goals go far beyond Scandinavian-style welfare; they demand radical restructuring of American society that would erode both economic opportunity and democratic governance.

For Colorado, this matters enormously. DSA-aligned policies typically target fossil fuel industries, restrict Second Amendment rights, and expand government control over land use and water—all critical to Colorado's ranching, energy, and outdoor economies. Democrats claiming they want "Swedish-style" social democracy are increasingly captured by activists pushing Venezuelan-style state control.

Conservatives argue that Democratic socialism, whatever its rhetorical packaging, inevitably leads to government overreach, job destruction, and the erosion of property rights. Republicans counter that Colorado's prosperity depends on free markets, responsible energy development, and limited regulation—not ideological experiments that punish productive citizens.

The left claims democratic socialism simply means stronger social safety nets and worker protections. Conservatives respond that DSA's actual platform demands nationalization of key industries and centralized economic planning—poison for Colorado's diverse economy.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from DSA rhetoric ahead of 2026, or double down on socialist-friendly policies that could cost the state jobs and tax revenue.**
