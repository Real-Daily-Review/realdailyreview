# Republicans Face Midterm Reckoning: Show Results or Lose Base

Republicans are at a crossroads heading into the midterms, with party leaders warning that failure to deliver on core promises risks handing Democrats control and betraying the voters who put them in power.

Rep. Jim Jordan (R-OH), House Judiciary Committee chairman, told Breitbart that Republicans must do two things to maintain power: demonstrate concrete accomplishments to voters and sharply contrast GOP governance against what he calls "the socialist lunacy taking over the Democratic Party." Jordan's message reflects growing frustration within conservative ranks that Republicans haven't sufficiently highlighted their wins or drawn clear ideological lines ahead of the election cycle.

The Daily Caller echoed that warning, arguing that voters "only have one option to make them pay for betraying their base: ensure they win"—a blunt reminder that Republican turnout depends on the party proving it's fighting for its promises, not just occupying office.

For Colorado conservatives, this translates to a simple test: Will Republicans in Washington and statewide deliver on energy independence, border security, and tax restraint? Or will they compromise away the agenda that energized the base in the first place?

Watch whether Colorado Republicans running for Congress and statewide office clearly articulate their accomplishments and draw sharp contrasts with progressive opponents on inflation, regulation, and government overreach.
