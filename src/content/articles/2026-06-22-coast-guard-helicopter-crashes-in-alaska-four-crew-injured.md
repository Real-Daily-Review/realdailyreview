# Coast Guard Helicopter Crashes in Alaska; Four Crew Injured

A U.S. Coast Guard MH-60 Jayhawk helicopter crashed near Harbor Mountain in Sitka, Alaska on Monday during a training flight, injuring all four crew members aboard, according to multiple reports (Daily Caller, New York Post, The Daily Wire). The crash occurred at 10:07 a.m. local time, triggering an immediate search-and-rescue response. No deaths were reported.

The cause remains under investigation. The Coast Guard has not yet disclosed details about the severity of injuries or the circumstances that led to the incident during the routine training mission.

While this incident occurred in Alaska, it underscores ongoing questions about military readiness and equipment maintenance across all branches—issues that ripple through defense spending debates in Congress. Colorado defense contractors and military installations depend on robust federal procurement and maintenance budgets for Coast Guard and broader military operations.
