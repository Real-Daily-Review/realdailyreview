# Serbia's Student-Led Movement Demands Early Elections

Tens of thousands of demonstrators rallied across Serbia in an antigovernment protest, according to Al Jazeera, continuing a student-led movement that emerged following the Novi Sad disaster in November 2024. The demonstrations are calling for early elections and represent sustained pressure on the government over issues connected to the initial tragedy.

The protests underscore growing discontent among younger Serbians, who have mobilized around demands for political change. The movement has maintained momentum months after the triggering incident, suggesting deeper grievances beyond the initial disaster.

The scale and persistence of the rallies indicate the government faces significant domestic pressure heading into any potential electoral timeline.
