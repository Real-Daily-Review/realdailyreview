# Bolton Urges Trump to Abandon Iran Ceasefire

Former White House national security adviser John Bolton called on President Trump to end the U.S. ceasefire with Iran, arguing that diplomatic engagement with Tehran is futile. "I think it's a waste of oxygen to negotiate with the Iranians," Bolton told Bloomberg. "I don't think they're ever going to come up with something that we should find satisfactory."

The push comes as the Trump administration weighs its approach to Iran policy. According to the Financial Times, the president faces limited military options—ranging from air strikes to naval operations—to pressure Iran into concessions, with no straightforward path to force Tehran to yield.

Bolton's call reflects a hardline stance on Iran negotiations. The former adviser has long opposed diplomatic efforts with the Iranian government, positioning military pressure as the more viable alternative.

The administration has not publicly committed to ending the ceasefire, leaving the trajectory of U.S.-Iran relations uncertain as Trump's team deliberates its next steps.
