# Trump Blasts Cornyn Over Federal Judge Blockade; Colorado Republicans Watch Judicial Confirmation Fight

President Trump is publicly pressuring Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX) over the blocking of DOJ lawyer Elliot Gaiser's federal judge nomination for Dallas, calling the move a "tremendous disservice" (Fox News). Trump warns that without Cornyn's support, "we're going to have a radical left lunatic get in there" (New York Post).

The dispute highlights a rare Republican-on-Republican clash over judicial confirmations—a flashpoint conservatives have fought hard to control since Trump's first term. Republicans argue that federal judgeships are too consequential to block over intra-party disagreements, especially when the alternative risks activist judges imposing progressive policy from the bench. Cornyn's position remains unclear, but the public rebuke signals Trump's determination to reshape the judiciary with reliable conservative nominees.

For Colorado, this matters. Federal judges increasingly rule on energy permits, water rights, Second Amendment cases, and land-use disputes that directly affect state businesses and property owners. A judiciary stacked with progressive activists threatens Colorado's energy independence, water compacts, and constitutional rights. Conservatives should monitor whether Senate Republicans hold the line on judicial confirmations or allow individual senators to derail Trump's court-packing strategy.
