# Fauci's Pandemic iPhone Handed to Senate as Contempt Vote Looms

Senate investigators have obtained a copy of Dr. Anthony Fauci's pandemic-era iPhone as Congress weighs holding him in contempt for allegedly stonewalling oversight of taxpayer-funded research at a Chinese laboratory (Fox News). The move comes ahead of a Thursday contempt vote in Sen. Rand Paul's panel, which has demanded answers about why the federal government funded research at a facility lacking adequate safety standards.

Republicans argue Fauci has obstructed legitimate congressional inquiry into the origins of COVID-19 and the NIH's role in funding gain-of-function research overseas. Sen. Paul has pressed for transparency on how American tax dollars were deployed in Wuhan. Democrats counter that Fauci deserves an apology, with Illinois Sen. Durbin defending him ahead of the vote (Just The News).

For Colorado taxpayers, this matters: federal research dollars flow through grants and contracts that ultimately come from your wallet. If the government can't account for how it spends money on overseas research—especially at facilities with questionable safety records—that's a red flag for all federal spending accountability.

Watch whether the contempt vote passes and whether the iPhone data reveals communications that contradict Fauci's prior testimony.
