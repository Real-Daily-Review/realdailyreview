# Amazon's Bezos Plays Hollywood Casting Director—While Colorado Taxpayers Foot the Bill for Corporate Subsidies

Jeff Bezos is so flush with cash he's now personally casting James Bond films. The Amazon founder revealed he was "heavily involved" in screen-testing Robert Pattinson for the iconic spy role, running what he called "real old-fashioned screen tests" (9News Denver, Breitbart).

Pattinson reportedly clinched the part after performing a seduction scene from "From Russia with Love"—the same test used for every Bond hopeful (The Daily Wire).

Here's the Colorado angle: While Bezos plays Hollywood mogul, Amazon continues collecting state and local tax incentives across the country. Colorado has handed out millions in corporate welfare to Amazon warehouses and operations, money that could've gone to schools, roads, or tax relief for working families. Conservatives argue these sweetheart deals represent exactly the kind of crony capitalism that distorts free markets—billionaires get subsidies while small businesses and taxpayers get squeezed.

The broader point: mega-corporations like Amazon leverage government favor to dominate markets, then their executives dabble in entertainment ventures. It's a reminder that when government picks winners and losers through tax breaks and regulations, ordinary Coloradans lose.

Watch whether Colorado lawmakers push back on Amazon's next subsidy request—or cave again.
