# Leftist Candidates Face Arrest, Assault Allegations as Campaign Season Turns Ugly

Democratic and progressive congressional candidates are making headlines for all the wrong reasons—violent rhetoric and criminal allegations that underscore the left's increasingly hostile political climate.

Meanwhile, Arizona Democratic congressional candidate Bernadette Greene-Placentia reported being raped in August while repairing a campaign sign in Sun City, a Phoenix suburb, according to Just The News and the Daily Caller.
