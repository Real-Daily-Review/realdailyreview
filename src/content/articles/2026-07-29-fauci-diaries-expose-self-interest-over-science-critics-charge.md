# Fauci Diaries Reveal Pattern of Self-Interest Over Science, Critics Say

Dr. Anthony Fauci's newly released diaries paint a damning portrait of a government bureaucrat more concerned with media celebrity than honest pandemic guidance—a troubling reminder of why Coloradans should demand accountability from unelected health officials wielding unchecked power.

The diaries show Fauci suppressing dissenting scientific opinions and basking in media attention during COVID-19, according to *National Review*. Even Scott Gottlieb, the former FDA commissioner and no radical, called the entries "disappointing," citing evidence of Fauci's enjoyment of public notoriety and his pattern of silencing competing viewpoints (*New York Post*).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue the diaries expose how a single unelected official shaped pandemic policy while prioritizing his own reputation over scientific debate. This matters in Colorado, where state health officials followed federal guidance that shuttered businesses, closed schools, and restricted gun ranges—decisions made by people accountable to no one.

**What's at stake:** The diaries underscore why conservatives oppose concentrating health authority in federal bureaucracies. When officials answer to no one and face no consequences for poor judgment, taxpayers and citizens lose.

Watch for whether Colorado lawmakers demand greater transparency and accountability from state health officials, or whether they continue deferring to the same federal apparatus that failed during the pandemic.
