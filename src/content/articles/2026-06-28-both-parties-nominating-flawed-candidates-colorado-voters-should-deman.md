# Colorado Voters Should Demand Better Than Character-Challenged Candidates

Both parties are fielding Senate candidates with troubling personal records, a trend that should alarm Colorado voters who expect integrity from their representatives.

The Washington Examiner warns that we're entering an era of "YOLO candidates"—individuals with documented histories of poor behavior who nonetheless secure major-party nominations. Democrats nominated a problematic candidate in Maine; Republicans did the same in Texas. This pattern reflects a dangerous erosion of standards across both parties.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Character matters in elected office. Voters deserve candidates who demonstrate personal integrity, sound judgment, and respect for the rule of law. When parties prioritize partisan wins over candidate quality, they weaken democratic institutions and invite cynicism about government itself.

Colorado has a tradition of electing serious people to serious offices. That standard shouldn't be abandoned because national parties have lowered theirs. Whether it's a U.S. Senate race or a state legislative seat, Coloradans should reject the notion that "our guy" deserves a pass simply because the other side nominated someone equally flawed.

The real power lies with voters: we can refuse to ratify bad choices at the ballot box.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans and Democrats will hold their own nominees accountable—or whether character becomes just another casualty of partisan tribalism.
