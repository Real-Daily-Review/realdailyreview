# Democrats' Socialist Drift Threatens Swing States—Colorado Next?

Nearly a third of Democrats now identify as democratic socialists, according to a CNN poll cited by Fox News, and the movement is spreading beyond coastal strongholds into the Midwest swing states that decide presidential elections. Democratic Socialists of America-backed candidates have won races in New York City and Seattle, while some DSA activists have signed pledges supporting "Chinese socialism," raising alarms among conservatives about the party's ideological trajectory (Fox News Politics).

The Washington Examiner warns that socialism is no longer confined to deep-blue cities—it's infiltrating swing-state Democratic primaries, with candidates openly embracing the label. Republicans argue this represents a fundamental rejection of free-market capitalism and individual liberty in favor of government control over the economy.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's Democratic establishment has trended left for years. If national DSA momentum reaches the state's primary contests, expect pressure on Colorado Democrats to adopt higher taxes, aggressive regulation of energy development, and expanded government spending—policies that would hammer the state's oil and gas workers, small businesses, and taxpayers already burdened by rising costs.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado Democratic candidates begin openly courting DSA endorsements or adopt socialist-friendly platforms in 2024 and 2026 races.
