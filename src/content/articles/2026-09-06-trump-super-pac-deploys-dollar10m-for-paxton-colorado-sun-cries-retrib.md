# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Fight; Colorado Sun Warns of 'Retribution'

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is opening its war chest with a $10 million commitment to rescue Republican Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign against Democrat James Talarico, according to new FEC filings reported by The Daily Wire. The financial injection marks the first major Trump-backed intervention in a competitive general election race this cycle.

Meanwhile, Colorado Sun opinion writer Carman warns that Trump's administration is waging a "campaign of retribution" against Colorado specifically, claiming the state faces pressure to "relinquish its constitutional powers" or face federal consequences. Carman frames this as an attack on Colorado's autonomy.

Republicans counter that Trump's support for allies like Paxton reflects standard political backing for party members facing tough races—not retribution, but party unity. Conservatives argue that federal-state disputes typically involve legitimate policy disagreements over regulatory overreach, not coercion.

The Paxton race itself remains closely watched as a bellwether for Trump's influence over Republican primary and general election outcomes heading into 2026.
