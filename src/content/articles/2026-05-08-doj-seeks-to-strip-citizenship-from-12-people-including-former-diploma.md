# Justice Department Seeks to Strip Citizenship From 12 People, Including Former Diplomat

The Trump administration's Justice Department filed denaturalization actions this week against 12 people across U.S. District Courts, alleging they fraudulently obtained citizenship by lying during the naturalization process.

The cases include a former U.S. Ambassador to Colombia, Victor Manuel Rocha, who admitted to spying for the Cuban government. According to The Hill, federal prosecutors allege Rocha illegally obtained naturalization by misrepresenting himself during the process. The complaint was filed in the Southern District of Florida on Thursday.

The remaining 11 defendants face accusations including child sexual abuse and providing material support to terrorists, according to PBS NewsHour. The DOJ announced the broader denaturalization push on Friday, targeting individuals from various parts of the country.

Denaturalization—the revocation of citizenship—is a rare legal action. The cases represent an aggressive enforcement posture by the administration on immigration and national security matters.
