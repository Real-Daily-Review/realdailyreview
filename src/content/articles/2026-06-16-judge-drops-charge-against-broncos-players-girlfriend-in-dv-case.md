# Judge Drops Charge Against Broncos Player's Girlfriend in Domestic Violence Case

A Denver judge has dismissed a domestic violence charge against the girlfriend of Denver Broncos defensive end Jonathon Cooper, according to 9News Denver. Both were initially accused of domestic violence and misdemeanor criminal mischief following an arrest, but Cooper's girlfriend will not face charges on the domestic violence count. Cooper himself remains charged with four additional counts related to the incident.

The case highlights ongoing questions about how domestic violence allegations are prosecuted in Colorado courts and whether initial charging decisions withstand judicial scrutiny. While details remain limited, the dismissal suggests the evidence may not have supported the original charge against Cooper's girlfriend—a reminder that arrest does not equal guilt, and that Colorado's criminal justice system does include safeguards for the accused.

Cooper, a key defensive contributor for Denver's NFL franchise, continues to face his own legal exposure. The Broncos organization has not publicly commented on how the charges may affect his roster status or playing time.
