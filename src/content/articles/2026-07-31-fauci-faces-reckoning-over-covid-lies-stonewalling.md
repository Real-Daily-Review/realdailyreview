# Fauci's Reckoning: Conservative Media Demands Accountability on COVID Lies

Dr. Anthony Fauci faces mounting pressure to answer for his role in pandemic policy after invoking the Fifth Amendment 111 times before Sen. Rand Paul's homeland security panel and the release of his personal diaries, according to the Washington Examiner. National Review argues Fauci must be "exposed on his real views about masks, the wet-market theory, and everything else he lied about."

The conservative case is straightforward: Fauci's stonewalling and selective disclosure suggest he knowingly misled Americans on critical public health matters during 2020—the year that upended Colorado's economy, shuttered small businesses, and fueled government overreach at state and local levels. Colorado Gov. Jared Polis, a Democrat, implemented some of the nation's strictest lockdowns partly on Fauci's guidance; those policies devastated Main Street while enriching big-box retailers and remote-work tech companies.

Republicans argue Fauci's evasiveness and diary revelations vindicate those who questioned official narratives from day one. They contend accountability is essential to prevent similar bureaucratic capture during future health crises.

Democrats have largely remained silent on the matter, offering no public defense of Fauci's conduct.

Watch for whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly GOP members—will demand transparency on how federal health guidance shaped state policy decisions that harmed Colorado workers and families.
