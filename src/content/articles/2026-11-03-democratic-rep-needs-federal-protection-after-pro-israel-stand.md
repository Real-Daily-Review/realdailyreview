# Democratic Rep. Stevens Needs U.S. Marshals After Crossing Party Line on Israel

A sitting Democratic congresswoman is requesting federal protection after endorsing a Republican—a stark sign that the party's pro-Israel wing faces real threats from its own base.

Rep. Haley Stevens (D-MI) requested U.S. Marshal protection following death threats triggered by her surprise endorsement of Republican Mike Rogers in Michigan's Senate race, *The Daily Wire* reported. Her offense: breaking ranks over the Democratic nominee's record on Israel.

Stevens had already signaled her breaking point with Democrat Abdul El-Sayed's anti-Israel positions, according to *National Review*. The outlet noted that Stevens is "not alone"—suggesting broader Democratic anxiety about the party's leftward tilt on Middle East policy, even as party leadership remains publicly silent.

The incident exposes a brutal reality: Democrats who defend Israel face intimidation from their own coalition. Stevens's need for federal marshals underscores how far the party has drifted from its historical pro-Israel consensus, and how willing some activists are to punish dissent.

**What conservatives and taxpayers should watch:** Whether Democratic leadership condemns the threats and recalibrates party messaging, or whether they continue enabling the anti-Israel wing that's now forcing their own members into protective custody.
