# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Race as Colorado Faces Federal Pressure

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is unleashing $10 million in television spending to rescue Texas Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign against Democrat James James Talarico, according to new FEC filings reported by *The Daily Wire*. The financial intervention marks the first major deployment of Trump's war chest in a competitive general election race.

Meanwhile, Colorado faces escalating pressure from the Trump administration over state sovereignty disputes. *Colorado Sun* columnist Carman warns that Colorado is "under attack by an administration that seeks to coerce the state to relinquish its constitutional powers — or else," framing federal pressure as a campaign of retribution against the state.

Republicans view Trump's financial support for Paxton as essential reinforcement in a tightening race, while critics argue the super PAC spending signals weakness in the Republican nominee's position. The Paxton race has drawn national attention as a bellwether for GOP strength in Texas.

For Colorado, the broader question looms: how will the state navigate federal-state conflicts over regulatory authority, energy policy, and other constitutional flashpoints under renewed Trump administration pressure?

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership capitulates to federal demands or stands firm on state prerogatives—and what that means for taxpayers caught in the crossfire.
