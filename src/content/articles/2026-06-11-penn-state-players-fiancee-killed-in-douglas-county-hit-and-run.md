# Former Penn State Player's Fiancée Killed in Highlands Ranch Hit-and-Run

A tragic hit-and-run in Douglas County has left a former Penn State football player hospitalized after his fiancée was killed and two others seriously injured. Kyle Vasey, his fiancée Corrine More, and Dianne Windes were struck on June 1 about 30 minutes south of Denver in what authorities are treating as a criminal matter. Vasey has undergone six surgeries and remains hospitalized, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver.

The Douglas County Sheriff's Office is investigating the crash. Details on the suspect vehicle and driver remain limited as the investigation continues.

This incident underscores a persistent public safety concern for Colorado communities: hit-and-run crashes that leave victims and families devastated while perpetrators evade accountability. Conservatives and law-and-order advocates have long argued that swift prosecution and meaningful consequences deter such crimes and protect law-abiding Coloradans.

Taxpayers and residents should monitor whether Douglas County law enforcement identifies and prosecutes the responsible driver with the full force of Colorado law.
