# Colorado Conservatives Should Watch EPA's Next Move on Glyphosate

The Supreme Court's decision in *Monsanto v. Durnell* has handed a win to pesticide manufacturers—but Colorado farmers and ranchers need to understand what comes next.

The high court ruled against consumers seeking to hold pesticide makers accountable for alleged harms from glyphosate, the active ingredient in Roundup. While the decision protects manufacturers from certain liability claims, it shifts responsibility to federal regulators. The EPA now faces pressure to tighten oversight of the widely used herbicide, which Colorado's agricultural sector depends on for crop management across the state's vast farming regions.

**The conservative case:** Excessive litigation against agricultural products drives up costs for farmers and threatens food security. Regulatory clarity beats courtroom chaos. Colorado's agricultural economy—worth billions annually—benefits from stable, science-based pesticide policy rather than jury verdicts driven by emotion.

**What the left argues:** Consumers deserve legal recourse when corporations profit from potentially dangerous chemicals. The ruling weakens accountability and favors corporate interests over public health.

The real issue for Colorado taxpayers: Will the EPA use this opening to impose costly new restrictions on glyphosate, or will it maintain the balanced approach that lets farmers operate efficiently? Watch the EPA's next regulatory move closely—it could reshape Colorado agriculture's bottom line.
