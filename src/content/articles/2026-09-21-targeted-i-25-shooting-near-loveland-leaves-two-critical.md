# Targeted Shooting on I-25 Near Loveland Leaves Two Critically Injured

Colorado's I-25 corridor near Loveland became a crime scene overnight as preliminary investigations suggest a targeted shooting—not random violence—left two people critically injured and triggered a highway crash.

According to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31, authorities are treating the incident as a targeted attack rather than a random act of road rage or highway violence. The shooting apparently caused a vehicle crash on the interstate, compounding the immediate danger to motorists in the area.

Details remain limited as investigators work the scene, but the "targeted" designation raises questions about gang activity, personal disputes, or organized crime along one of Colorado's busiest transportation corridors. I-25 between Fort Collins and Denver has seen recurring safety concerns, from accidents to criminal activity.

For Colorado drivers and commuters, the incident underscores the reality of crime on major highways and the unpredictability of travel safety—even in daylight hours on heavily trafficked routes. Law enforcement response times and investigative capacity will be critical to identifying suspects and preventing further incidents.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether state and local authorities adequately resource highway patrol presence and whether prosecutors pursue charges aggressively to deter similar violence.
