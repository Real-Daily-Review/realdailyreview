# Country Music Legend Dolly Parton Dies at 80

Dolly Parton, the iconic country music star who embodied American entrepreneurship and charitable spirit, has died peacefully at age 80 in Nashville, Tennessee, according to her family and publicist (9News Denver).

Parton had stepped back from public life in recent months to focus on her health. She notably missed the unveiling of Dollywood's latest attraction due to health issues, though she sent a pre-recorded message to fans, according to 9News Denver.

Her family released a statement saying, "May God bless Dolly Parton, and may God bless you all. She will always love you" (9News Denver).

Tributes poured in from across the political and entertainment spectrum. President Trump and fellow country legends including Reba McEntire and Lily Tomlin shared remembrances of the music icon (9News Denver).

Beyond her 60-year music career, Parton was known for her business acumen—building Dollywood into a major Tennessee employer and tourist destination—and her philanthropy, including her Imagination Library, which has distributed millions of books to children nationwide. She represented a distinctly American model of success: individual talent, hard work, and voluntary charity rather than government dependence.

Coloradans who admired Parton's independence and generosity lost a cultural touchstone this week.
