# I-70 Closure at Kipling Will Snarl Denver Metro Traffic This Weekend

Colorado Department of Transportation is closing eastbound I-70 at Kipling Street in Wheat Ridge for the second round of bridge repair work, creating predictable gridlock for commuters and commerce during peak travel hours.

CDOT's weekend closure—beginning Friday night—reflects the ongoing infrastructure maintenance required on the aging bridgeway. While road maintenance is necessary, the timing and frequency of these closures highlight a broader challenge: Colorado's transportation infrastructure is aging faster than funding mechanisms can reliably address it.

The closure will force eastbound traffic onto surface streets, compounding congestion in an already traffic-heavy corridor serving the Denver metro area. Drivers heading east should plan alternate routes or delay travel until the lanes reopen.

This repair cycle underscores what conservatives have long argued: government must prioritize efficient spending and timely project completion rather than extended, piecemeal construction that disrupts the economy and frustrates taxpayers footing the bill. Repeated weekend closures suggest either poor project planning or insufficient upfront funding—both failures of government stewardship.

Coloradans should watch whether CDOT provides a realistic timeline for completing these repairs and whether state leadership addresses the broader infrastructure funding gap without resorting to new taxes or tolls on already-burdened drivers.
