# French Open Players Limit Media Duties Over Prize Money Dispute

Top tennis players are restricting their French Open media appearances to 15 minutes as part of a broader protest over prize money distribution at the tournament.

Leading competitors including Aryna Sabalenka, Coco Gauff, and Jannik Sinner have scaled back their media obligations, according to BBC World. The action follows a boycott-impacted media day that disrupted the tournament's usual schedule.

Roland Garros organizers have responded by engaging directly with player representatives. The French Open director held what Al Jazeera described as a "positive" meeting with players to discuss greater revenue sharing—a key demand underlying the dispute.

The protest reflects broader tensions in professional tennis over how tournament revenue is distributed between organizers and competitors. Players have increasingly pushed back against what they view as inadequate compensation relative to the events' commercial value.

Neither side has disclosed specific terms discussed in the recent talks, leaving the outcome of negotiations unclear. Watch whether the French Open commits to concrete changes in prize money or revenue-sharing arrangements in coming weeks.
