# New York Mayor's Israel Genocide Claim Draws Fire from UN Ambassador and Pro-Israel Protesters

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's October 7 anniversary post accusing Israel of genocide has ignited a firestorm, drawing sharp rebukes from Israel's UN Ambassador Danny Danon and pro-Israel demonstrators at a New York memorial event.

Danon compared Mamdani's framing to "turning September 11 into a day of mourning for al-Qaeda," according to Fox News Politics, underscoring the inflammatory nature of equating Israel's response to Hamas's October 7, 2023 attack—which killed roughly 1,200 people and took about 200 hostages—with genocide.

At an October 7 memorial in New York City, pro-Israel protesters confronted Mamdani directly, with keffiyeh-clad counter-protesters shouting "You coward!" and "How do you let this happen?" (New York Post). The dual protests highlight deepening divisions over how American leaders characterize Israel's military operations.
