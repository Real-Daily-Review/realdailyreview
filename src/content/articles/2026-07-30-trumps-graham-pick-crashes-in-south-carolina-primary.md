# Trump's Graham Endorsement Flops in South Carolina Senate Race

Trump's hand-picked successor to late Sen. Lindsey Graham—the senator's own sister, Darline Graham—is tanking in the Republican primary despite the president's full-throated backing. According to polling cited by *The Daily Wire* and *Just The News*, South Carolina Rep. Ralph Norman has surged ahead of Trump's favored candidate, suggesting that even Trump's endorsement carries limits in a competitive GOP primary.

Trump enthusiastically encouraged Darline Graham to "RUN, DARLINE, RUN!" after she entered the race in July, but Republican strategists now report growing trouble for her campaign. Norman's lead underscores a broader reality: primary voters don't automatically fall in line behind presidential endorsements, especially when alternative candidates offer deeper roots in the state's Republican establishment.

The race matters beyond South Carolina. It's a test of Trump's influence over GOP primary outcomes heading into 2026—a year when Republicans will defend Senate seats in hostile terrain. If Trump can't move the needle for his own pick in a reliably red state, his endorsement may prove less valuable to candidates in purple or blue-leaning districts nationwide.

**Watch:** whether Norman's momentum holds through the primary and whether Trump doubles down on backing Graham or pivots to the frontrunner.
