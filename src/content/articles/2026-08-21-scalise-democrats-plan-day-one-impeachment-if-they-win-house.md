# Democrats Signal Impeachment Threat if They Retake House

House Republican leadership is sounding the alarm: if Democrats win the majority this fall, impeachment of President Trump is coming on day one—not as justice, but as partisan revenge.

House Majority Leader Steve Scalise (R-LA) warned that Democrats have made clear their intent to weaponize impeachment the moment they gain power, according to *Fox News* and *Breitbart*. Scalise characterized the move as evidence that socialist insurgents have reshaped the Democratic Party into a purely partisan force bent on using impeachment as a political cudgel rather than a constitutional remedy.

**Republicans argue** this represents the normalization of impeachment as a tool of partisan warfare—a dangerous precedent that erodes the gravity of the constitutional process and signals Democrats view it as a political weapon rather than a serious check on executive power.

**Democrats counter** (implicitly, through their silence in these reports) that impeachment concerns are justified based on Trump's record, though these sources do not capture the Democratic response directly.

For Colorado voters, this reflects the broader partisan breakdown in Washington. As midterm campaigns heat up, the question becomes whether voters want a Congress focused on kitchen-table issues—inflation, energy costs, border security—or one consumed by impeachment theater.

Watch whether Colorado's competitive House races (particularly in the 3rd and 8th districts) turn on this impeachment threat or remain focused on local economic concerns.
