# Gabbard Declassifies Fauci Documents, Exposes COVID-Origins Stonewalling

Outgoing intelligence chief Tulsi Gabbard released declassified documents this week alleging that Anthony Fauci improperly influenced the intelligence community's investigation into COVID-19's origins—a move conservatives say vindicates years of skepticism about the pandemic's official narrative.

According to Fox News and Just The News, Gabbard's statement accused Fauci and allies in the scientific and intelligence establishments of deploying "deep state playbook" tactics to suppress inquiry into the virus's origins. The declassified findings from the Office of National Intelligence reportedly detail how Fauci's influence shaped conclusions that should have remained independent.

Republicans have long argued that the lab-leak theory was prematurely dismissed and that institutional pressure silenced legitimate scientific debate. Gabbard's move—her final act before leaving office—signals renewed GOP determination to hold accountable those who, conservatives argue, prioritized narrative control over truth-seeking during the pandemic.

For Colorado, this matters: state health officials and institutions followed federal guidance that may have been compromised by the very political dynamics Gabbard is now exposing. Taxpayers funded research and policies built on potentially corrupted intelligence assessments.

Watch for congressional Republicans to demand a full accounting of which Colorado institutions and officials relied on Fauci-influenced guidance when implementing state pandemic mandates.
