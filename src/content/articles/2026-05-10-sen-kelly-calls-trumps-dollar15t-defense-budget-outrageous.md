# Sen. Kelly Calls Trump's $1.5 Trillion Defense Budget 'Outrageous'

Democratic Sen. Mark Kelly of Arizona criticized the Trump administration's proposed $1.5 trillion Pentagon budget request, calling it "outrageous" and urging the White House to submit a proposal "that makes sense for the moment we're in," according to CBS News and the Washington Examiner.

Kelly, who has served in the Senate for five and a half years, did not elaborate on specific objections or propose an alternative figure. His comments represent rare pushback from a Democratic senator on defense spending levels, though the sources do not indicate whether other lawmakers share his concerns or if his criticism signals broader party opposition to the request.

The Trump administration has not yet responded to Kelly's remarks. The $1.5 trillion figure encompasses the full scope of Pentagon spending and related defense outlays.

Defense budgets have historically drawn bipartisan support in Congress, though debates over spending levels and priorities persist. Watch for whether other Senate Democrats echo Kelly's criticism and how Republican leadership responds to the challenge from within the chamber.
