# Colorado Primary Races Heat Up With Record Spending as Republicans Eye Attorney General Pickup

Congressional candidates across Colorado are burning through cash in competitive primaries, with Democrats particularly aggressive in the 8th District race to challenge Republican Rep. Gabe Evans. Shannon Bird and Manny Rutinel are locked in a costly battle for the Democratic nomination in a district spanning northern suburbs, agricultural land, and oil fields stretching to Greeley, according to the Denver Post.

The spending surge reflects the stakes: Democrats see the 8th as winnable territory, while Republicans are positioned to make gains elsewhere. Most notably, political experts told the Denver Post that Republicans have a genuine shot at flipping the Colorado attorney general's office—a seat Democrats have held and used to advance progressive litigation against energy companies and conservative causes.

The primary spending frenzy underscores a broader reality: Colorado remains a battleground state where both parties are willing to invest heavily. For conservatives, the attorney general's race represents a critical opportunity to elect a chief law officer who won't weaponize the office against businesses, energy development, or Second Amendment rights—issues that have defined recent Democratic AGs' tenures.

Watch whether Republican primary winners can maintain fundraising momentum and messaging discipline heading into the general election.
