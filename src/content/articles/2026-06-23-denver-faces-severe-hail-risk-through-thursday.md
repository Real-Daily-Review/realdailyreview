# Denver Braces for Severe Storm Risk Through Thursday

Denver-area residents should batten down the hatches: severe thunderstorm warnings are in effect through Thursday, with large hail posing the primary threat to homes, vehicles, and property across the metro region.

According to 9News Denver, multiple chances for stronger storms will develop in and near the Denver metro through Thursday, with large hail as the main concern. KDVR Fox31 reports thunderstorms will continue moving east Monday evening, followed by clearing overnight before conditions deteriorate again Tuesday and Wednesday.

Coloradans in the path should secure outdoor equipment, park vehicles in covered areas if possible, and monitor local weather alerts. Large hail can cause significant damage to roofs, windows, and vehicles—costs that often fall on property owners when insurance deductibles apply.

The timing is worth noting for Colorado farmers and ranchers in the region, who face potential crop damage during a critical growing season. Homeowners should document any storm damage promptly for insurance claims.

Watch for updated forecasts and hail reports as the system moves through; conditions could shift rapidly.
