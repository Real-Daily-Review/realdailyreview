# Colorado Teen Killed by Reckless Driver With Revoked License Highlights Enforcement Gaps

A 19-year-old Colorado Springs resident, Evan Suppes, was killed in an I-25 crash by a driver traveling at 144 mph with a revoked license—raising hard questions about why dangerous drivers remain on the road.

According to 9News Denver, Suppes was remembered by family as selfless and kind. The driver now faces charges, but the case underscores a critical gap: how does someone with a revoked license operate a vehicle at triple-digit speeds without intervention?

Colorado conservatives argue that enforcement of existing traffic and licensing laws must be prioritized before expanding regulations. The state's Department of Transportation and Highway Patrol face scrutiny over whether revocation systems are adequately monitored and whether repeat offenders face sufficient consequences to deter dangerous behavior.
