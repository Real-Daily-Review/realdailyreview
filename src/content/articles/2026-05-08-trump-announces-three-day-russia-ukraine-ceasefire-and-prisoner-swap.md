# Trump Announces Three-Day Russia-Ukraine Ceasefire

President Trump announced Friday that Russia and Ukraine have agreed to a three-day ceasefire spanning May 9–11, according to The Hill. The pause in fighting coincides with Russia's Victory Day celebrations, which commemorate the Soviet Union's victory over Nazi Germany in World War II, PBS NewsHour reported.

The ceasefire will also include a prisoner swap of 1,000 prisoners of war from each country, Al Jazeera reported. Trump made the announcement on Truth Social, framing the pause as honoring both nations' commemorative occasions.

The agreement comes as the two countries have accused each other of violating separate ceasefires during the same holiday period, according to the BBC. The Financial Times noted that skepticism persists about whether Russian President Vladimir Putin is willing to commit to a more lasting peace deal beyond this temporary truce.

The announcement marks a notable diplomatic intervention by the Trump administration in the ongoing conflict, though the durability of the arrangement remains unclear.

Watch for whether the ceasefire holds through the weekend and any statements from Moscow or Kyiv regarding longer-term peace negotiations.
