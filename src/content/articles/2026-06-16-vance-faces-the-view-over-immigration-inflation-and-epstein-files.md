# Vance Faces 'The View' Hosts Over Immigration, Affordability, and Epstein Files

Vice President JD Vance walked into hostile territory Tuesday, sitting down with ABC's "The View" to defend the Trump administration's record on immigration, cost of living, and the newly released Jeffrey Epstein files. According to *The Daily Wire*, the interview proved "intensely one-sided," with Vance sparring directly with the hosts over core policy disagreements.

Vance told *Fox News Digital* he hoped the panel would "meet him halfway" as he prepared to discuss his new book and the administration's Iran peace agreement. Instead, he faced pointed questioning on issues where Democrats and the mainstream media have hammered Republicans relentlessly: border security and inflation.

The Vice President's appearance underscores a broader conservative strategy: taking the case directly to left-leaning media audiences rather than preaching to the converted. Republicans argue the Biden-Harris administration's open-border policies and reckless spending fueled the affordability crisis now crushing Colorado families and workers. Democrats counter that inflation was a global pandemic-driven phenomenon and that Republican tax cuts for the wealthy worsen inequality.

For Colorado voters, the stakes are clear: which party can actually deliver lower costs and secure borders?

Watch whether Vance's media offensive shifts independent voters in swing states—or whether ABC's audience remains unmoved by conservative arguments.
