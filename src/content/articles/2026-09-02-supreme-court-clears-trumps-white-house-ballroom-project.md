# Trump Clears Legal Hurdle on White House Ballroom Project

The Supreme Court this week sided with President Trump, vacating lower court stays that had blocked construction on a White House East Wing ballroom renovation. The decision allows the project to move forward despite ongoing litigation from the National Trust for Historic Preservation, which argues the work threatens the building's historical integrity.

Trump declared victory in the court fight, according to *Just The News*. The ballroom project has divided Americans—some view it as a necessary modernization of executive facilities, while preservationists contend it compromises architectural heritage.

The ruling clears a significant legal obstacle, though the underlying lawsuit continues. Conservatives argue the President should have broad authority to manage and improve federal property without endless litigation from activist groups. The National Trust and its allies counter that the White House is a national treasure requiring strict preservation standards.

Meanwhile, Trump is also planning a 9/11 tribute at the White House marking the 25th anniversary, featuring NYPD first responders and representatives from the Tunnel to Towers Foundation, per *Fox News*.
