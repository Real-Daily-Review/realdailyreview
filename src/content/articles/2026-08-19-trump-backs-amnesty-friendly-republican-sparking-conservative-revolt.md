# Trump Endorses Florida Republican Amid Conservative Backlash Over Immigration Stance

President Donald Trump has endorsed Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-Fla.) for reelection, drawing sharp criticism from immigration hawks who say the congresswoman has consistently pushed amnesty policies that undermine the administration's border-first agenda.

Trump praised Salazar as "doing a truly fantastic job" representing Florida's 27th Congressional District in a Truth Social post Monday, according to The Daily Wire. However, the Daily Caller reports that conservatives view the endorsement as problematic, noting Salazar has routinely championed mass amnesty measures—a direct contradiction to Trump's signature immigration platform.

The endorsement highlights a fault line within the Republican Party over immigration policy. While Trump has made border security and deportation enforcement central to his political brand, Salazar has positioned herself as an advocate for legalization pathways, frustrating conservatives who see amnesty as rewarding illegal entry and undercutting American workers competing for jobs.

For Colorado Republicans watching national immigration debates, the Salazar endorsement signals potential tension between Trump's rhetoric and his actual support for candidates whose voting records diverge from hardline border enforcement. Colorado's own immigration battles—from sanctuary city policies in Denver to labor market impacts on construction and agriculture—make this national GOP split directly relevant to state politics.
