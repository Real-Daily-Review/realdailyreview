# Senate GOP Leader Hails July 4th as Moment to Defend American Freedoms—and Fair Competition

Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD) is using the nation's 250th anniversary to remind Americans what's at stake: the freedoms our founders enshrined and the basic fairness that protects them.

Speaking ahead of Independence Day, Thune emphasized that the "incredible freedoms" Americans enjoy demand reflection and vigilance—a pointed reminder as the left continues pushing policies that erode both liberty and common sense. The Daily Wire notes that a recent Supreme Court ruling upholding bans on transgender athletes in girls' and women's sports vindicated what conservatives have argued all along: fairness in women's sports isn't bigotry, it's biology.

For Colorado, this matters. The state has become a battleground over transgender policy in schools and athletics. Conservative parents and taxpayers have fought to preserve single-sex sports and protect girls' opportunities to compete fairly—fights that will intensify as progressive school boards push back against common-sense protections.

Republicans argue that defending women's sports categories is fundamental to equal opportunity; Democrats counter that such bans discriminate against transgender youth. The Supreme Court's decision signals judicial recognition that biological sex in athletics serves a legitimate purpose.
