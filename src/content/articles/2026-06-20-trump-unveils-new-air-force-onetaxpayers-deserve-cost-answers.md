# Trump Unveils Refurbished Air Force One—But Questions Linger on Taxpayer Cost

President Trump rolled out a newly refurbished Boeing 747-8i at Joint Base Andrews Friday, showcasing a larger aircraft with updated red, white, and blue livery and expanded luxury features. The move marks a significant upgrade to the presidential fleet, which had relied on aging Boeing 747-200s for decades.

The administration touts the aircraft's enhanced size and modern capabilities as befitting America's commander-in-chief. Trump emphasized the scale difference during the unveiling, positioning the upgrade as a necessary modernization of Air Force One.

However, conservatives and fiscal hawks should demand transparency on the total cost to Colorado taxpayers and the broader federal budget. Presidential aircraft upgrades routinely balloon beyond initial estimates—and the public deserves a clear accounting of how much this refurbishment actually cost, who footed the bill, and whether competitive bidding occurred.

The Daily Wire and Fox News covered the unveiling, though neither outlet provided detailed cost breakdowns or timeline specifics for the project.
