# Israeli Minister Condemned for Taunting Detained Activists

Israeli far-right minister Itamar Ben-Gvir has drawn international criticism after a video showed him taunting dozens of activists detained at an Israeli port, according to the BBC. France and Italy are among countries that have condemned the incident, which sparked broader global outrage over Israel's treatment of aid flotilla activists, according to Deutsche Welle.

The video's release comes as Donald Trump joked about potentially running for Israeli prime minister, claiming he enjoys 99 percent support in Israel, according to Al Jazeera.

The detention and treatment of the activists has become a flashpoint for international concern over Israel's handling of aid operations and detainees. The incident underscores tensions surrounding humanitarian access and detention practices in the region.
