# Denver Shooting Underscores Need for Enforcement, Not More Gun Restrictions

A man died Friday following a shooting in Denver's Green Valley Ranch neighborhood, the latest violent incident highlighting Colorado's public safety crisis—one that gun-control advocates blame on firearms rather than criminal enforcement (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

The shooting comes as Colorado Democrats push for stricter gun regulations, including magazine limits and universal background checks. Republicans and Second Amendment advocates argue these measures fail to address the real problem: criminals who ignore existing laws. A recent Asheville, North Carolina shooting that left two dead and suspects still at large illustrates the enforcement gap—police presence and swift apprehension matter far more than adding regulations that law-abiding gun owners follow anyway.

Colorado's violent crime surge demands focus on prosecuting offenders, improving police response times, and addressing root causes like drug trafficking and gang activity. Instead, progressive lawmakers continue targeting gun owners' rights rather than criminal behavior.

Conservatives argue Denver and state leaders should prioritize funding law enforcement, supporting prosecutors who actually pursue violent criminals, and enforcing existing statutes before crafting new restrictions that burden responsible citizens.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats use this tragedy to advance their 2026 gun-control agenda—or finally acknowledge that enforcement, not regulation, stops violence.**
