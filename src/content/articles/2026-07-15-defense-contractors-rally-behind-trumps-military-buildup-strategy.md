# Defense Industry Mobilizes Behind Trump's Peace-Through-Strength Strategy

Sen. Dave McCormick (R-PA) convened hundreds of defense contractors at Pennsylvania's Defense and Innovation Summit this week, signaling Republican commitment to rebuilding U.S. military capability under Trump's "peace through strength" doctrine. The gathering at the Army War College near Harrisburg brought together companies seeking partnership opportunities with the Department of Defense and each other—a direct pivot from years of defense-sector uncertainty under the Biden administration.

McCormick framed the summit as reinforcement of Trump's military modernization agenda, according to *Breitbart News*. The message is clear: after years of progressive budget constraints and regulatory friction, the defense industry sees opportunity in Republican leadership prioritizing warfighting readiness over green-energy mandates and social experimentation.

For Colorado, this matters. The state hosts significant aerospace and defense manufacturing, including operations tied to Space Force and weapons systems development. A revitalized defense sector could mean jobs and economic growth in Colorado's Front Range corridor—but only if the Trump administration actually follows through with streamlined procurement and reduced regulatory burden on contractors.

Conservatives argue robust defense investment deters adversaries and protects American interests; the left typically counters that such spending diverts resources from social programs.

Watch whether McCormick's summit translates into actual contract acceleration and whether Colorado defense firms gain meaningful access to new opportunities.
