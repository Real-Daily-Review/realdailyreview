# GOP Rep. Max Miller Faces Restraining Order Over Alleged Domestic Abuse

A domestic dispute involving Ohio Republican Rep. Max Miller has escalated into court proceedings, with his ex-wife Emily Moreno filing a restraining order Thursday in Cuyahoga County alleging physical abuse.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Moreno's filing details an alleged encounter in which Miller grabbed her attorney. The *Just The News* report indicates Moreno has made more serious allegations, claiming Miller physically abused her, including placing a gun to her head and pouring hot water on her.

The case underscores a recurring problem within both parties: elected officials facing serious personal conduct allegations while serving in Congress. Republicans have consistently called for accountability and due process in such matters, arguing that credible allegations deserve thorough investigation and that elected officials must maintain standards befitting public office.

Miller, a first-term representative, has not yet publicly responded to the allegations. The restraining order filing suggests the dispute has moved beyond private negotiation into the legal system.

**What to watch:** Whether House Republican leadership addresses the matter and what Miller's response will be when the case proceeds through Ohio courts.
