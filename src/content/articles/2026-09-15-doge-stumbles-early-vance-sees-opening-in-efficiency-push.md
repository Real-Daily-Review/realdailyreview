# DOGE's Early Stumbles Hand Opening to Vance as Efficiency Push Falters

Elon Musk's Department of Government Efficiency arrived with fanfare but has already disappointed conservatives expecting swift action against federal waste and fraud, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The special government employee's effort—billed as a bold assault on bloated bureaucracy—quickly ran into headwinds, leaving Republicans questioning whether the administration's efficiency agenda will deliver real results or devolve into another Washington talking point.

For Colorado taxpayers and businesses already burdened by federal overreach, DOGE's stumbling start raises stakes. Conservatives have long argued that Washington wastes billions annually through redundant agencies, fraud, and regulatory excess. A credible efficiency push could trim the federal footprint and reduce the regulatory burden on Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small business. Failure to follow through would validate skeptics who see government reform as perpetually stalled.

Vice President JD Vance appears positioned to capitalize on DOGE's early struggles, the *Examiner* suggests, potentially repositioning himself as the administration's true efficiency champion. That political realignment could reshape how aggressively the Trump team pursues spending cuts and regulatory rollback—priorities Colorado conservatives view as essential to restoring fiscal sanity.

Watch whether Vance moves to take ownership of efficiency reforms and whether the administration produces measurable cuts to federal spending and Colorado-specific regulations by mid-year.
