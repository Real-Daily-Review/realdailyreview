# Trump's Nuclear Doctrine Shifts U.S. Strategy Away from Biden's Brink

President Trump has fundamentally reshaped America's nuclear posture, moving away from what conservatives argue was the Biden administration's dangerously passive approach to strategic deterrence.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Trump's doctrine emphasizes strengthening America's credible nuclear defenses while leveraging diplomacy, deterrence, and strategic pressure to reshape adversary calculations. Conservatives argue this represents a return to "peace through strength"—the principle that robust military capability, not accommodation, prevents conflict.

The shift carries implications for Colorado's defense and energy sectors. The state hosts significant nuclear weapons production and research facilities, including the Rocky Flats legacy cleanup and ongoing work at the Department of Energy complex. A more assertive nuclear posture could affect federal spending priorities, workforce demands, and the pace of environmental remediation projects tied to Cold War-era production.

Republicans contend that Biden's approach—characterized by restraint and diplomatic overtures to adversaries—emboldened hostile actors and invited miscalculation. Trump's strategy, they argue, restores deterrent credibility by making clear that America will defend itself and its allies decisively.

Conservatives should watch whether Trump's doctrine translates into sustained federal investment in Colorado's nuclear infrastructure and whether it accelerates or complicates the state's Cold War cleanup obligations.
