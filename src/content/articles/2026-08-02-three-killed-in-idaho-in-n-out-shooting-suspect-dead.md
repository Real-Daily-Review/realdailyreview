# Idaho Shooting Leaves Three Dead, Five Wounded; Suspect Among Fatalities

A gunman opened fire Saturday afternoon in a Twin Falls, Idaho shopping center near an In-N-Out restaurant, killing three people and wounding five others before police say the shooter was killed at the scene.

The shooting occurred around 2:30 p.m. local time in the parking lot, according to the Twin Falls Police Department (9News Denver). The Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire both reported the suspect is among the dead, though authorities indicated the final casualty count may rise.

Twin Falls is roughly 300 miles north of the Nevada border and sits in a region where Idaho's relatively permissive Second Amendment protections contrast sharply with neighboring states' restrictions. The incident remains under investigation. Police have not yet released details on the shooter's motive or background.
