# Democratic Socialists Push AOC for 2028—A Preview of the Left's Direction

New York City's Democratic Socialists of America chapter just passed a resolution urging Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez to run for president in 2028, signaling where the party's radical wing wants to take the country.

The DSA resolution, reported by the *Washington Examiner* and *Breitbart*, commits the organization to "building a stronger democratic socialist movement" and creating conditions for an AOC campaign. This isn't fringe noise—it's the organized left laying out its 2028 playbook.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. AOC represents the vanguard of the progressive agenda that has already reshaped Democratic energy policy, gun control messaging, and tax-and-spend priorities. A 2028 presidential bid would amplify calls for aggressive climate regulations that threaten Colorado's oil and gas industry, federal gun confiscation schemes that undermine Second Amendment rights, and wealth-redistribution policies that would hammer Colorado's middle class and small businesses.

Republicans should watch whether mainstream Democrats distance themselves from or embrace this socialist endorsement. If the party's establishment tolerates—let alone welcomes—a DSA-backed presidential campaign, it confirms what conservatives have long argued: the Democratic Party has been captured by its far-left wing.

Colorado voters deserve clarity on whether their Democratic representatives support democratic socialism or stand against it.
