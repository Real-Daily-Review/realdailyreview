# Former Trump Press Secretary Exits for Family—and That's Worth Noting

Karoline Leavitt, who served as White House press secretary under President Trump, has stepped down from the role to prioritize motherhood and family time. In recent interviews with *Fox News* and *The Daily Wire*, Leavitt praised Trump as "the best boss" and opened up about the grueling demands of the position—travel-heavy weeks that pulled her away from her two children.

Leavitt's departure highlights a tension conservatives rarely discuss openly: the personal cost of high-stakes government service. Unlike the left's performative embrace of "work-life balance," Leavitt made a clear-eyed choice about her priorities without apology or corporate-speak. She didn't blame the administration or demand policy changes; she simply decided family came first.

The move also underscores Trump's appeal to younger conservatives willing to sacrifice for the cause. Leavitt's willingness to serve, combined with her candid acknowledgment that the job wasn't sustainable long-term, reflects the real-world trade-offs of executive-branch work that media narratives often gloss over.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether Leavitt pursues state or national office down the road—her profile and Trump connection could make her a formidable candidate if she decides public service remains in her future.
