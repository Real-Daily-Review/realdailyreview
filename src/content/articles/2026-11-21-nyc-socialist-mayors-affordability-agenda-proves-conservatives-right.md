# New York Socialist Mayor's 'Affordability' Agenda Exposes Left's Economic Illiteracy

New York City's socialist Mayor Zohran Mamdani is discovering what conservatives have long known: you can't regulate your way to affordability without destroying the economy that makes things affordable in the first place.

Mamdani, according to the *Washington Examiner*, is simultaneously banning tourist attractions, attacking businesses for passing along government-imposed fees (which he rebrands as "affordability"), closing the city's central jail, and dismantling the NYPD's elite crime-fighting unit. The *National Review* cuts to the chase: "affordability and socialism are incompatible."

**The conservative argument is straightforward:** Price controls, regulatory overreach, and anti-business policies don't lower costs—they shrink supply, kill investment, and ultimately make goods and services *less* affordable for ordinary people. When government mandates fees then blames businesses for transparency, it's pure demagoguery masking failed policy.

**Colorado connection:** While Colorado hasn't elected an avowed socialist mayor, progressive Denver and Boulder leaders have pursued similar playbooks—aggressive regulations on housing, energy, and business that drive up costs for working families while claiming to fight "affordability." The Mamdani experiment offers a cautionary tale.

Taxpayers should watch whether Mamdani's policies trigger the predictable economic deterioration conservatives predict—or whether New York becomes a laboratory for the left's latest experiment in managed decline.
