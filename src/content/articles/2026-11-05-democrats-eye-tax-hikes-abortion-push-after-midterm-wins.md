# Democrats' Midterm Win Sets Stage for Tax Hikes, Abortion Push

Colorado taxpayers and pro-life voters face a potential two-front assault if Democrats capitalize on midterm gains to pursue a nationwide abortion right and resurrect billions in federal tax increases.

According to *National Review*, Democrats are positioning to reinstate a nationwide right to abortion should they prevail in upcoming legislative battles—a direct reversal of the Supreme Court's decision returning abortion regulation to the states. For Colorado, where voters approved a constitutional amendment protecting abortion access in 2024, this represents a federal-level attempt to override state sovereignty on the issue.

On taxes, *The Daily Wire* reports that Democratic midterm victories could revive "billions of dollars in proposed tax increases," potentially raising federal income tax rates for some Americans. While Democrats frame their agenda around expanding tax credits for lower-income families, conservatives argue the party's real aim is to dismantle President Trump's tax-cut legacy and fund expanded government spending—ultimately hitting Colorado's middle class and small-business owners.

Republicans counter that higher federal taxes will slow economic growth, reduce job creation, and drain resources from Colorado families already squeezed by inflation and state-level tax burdens. They argue Democrats are using class-warfare rhetoric to mask a fundamental expansion of government dependency.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado's Republican delegation can block federal tax increases and defend state-level abortion restrictions from Democratic federal overreach in the next Congress.
