# UK Moves to Ban Social Media for Under-16s—What Colorado Should Learn

Britain's government is about to do what American progressives keep threatening: ban children under 16 from TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, and similar platforms, with legislation expected before Christmas (9News Denver, The Daily Wire). Prime Minister Keir Starmer framed the move as child protection, aligning Britain with Australia's aggressive tech regulation playbook.

This matters for Colorado because it signals where the Left wants U.S. policy headed. While conservatives and tech advocates warn that age-verification mandates create privacy risks, enable government surveillance creep, and hand bureaucrats power over speech platforms, progressives argue social media harms teen mental health and warrants state intervention.

The British approach—blanket bans enforced through legislation—sidesteps parental choice and market solutions. Colorado parents and families should ask: Do we trust government or families to decide what's appropriate? Republicans typically oppose such sweeping restrictions, citing free-speech concerns and the impracticality of enforcement without invasive ID systems.

Watch whether Colorado lawmakers introduce copycat legislation next session—and whether they'll demand the same age-verification infrastructure that would track minors across the internet.
