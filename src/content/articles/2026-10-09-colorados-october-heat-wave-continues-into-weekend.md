# Colorado's October Heat Wave Continues—But Relief Is Coming

Colorado is experiencing near-record October temperatures that feel more like August than fall, with the Denver metro staying warm and dry through the weekend, according to 9News and KDVR. Forecasters say fall-like conditions are approaching, but the unseasonably hot stretch persists for now.
