# Three States Now Investigating Fauci for Alleged Perjury Over COVID Origins

Anthony Fauci's credibility is collapsing across state lines. Louisiana has joined Alabama and Florida in launching investigations into potential criminal charges against the former federal health official, following a Senate hearing where Louisiana Attorney General Liz Murrill says he committed perjury under deposition.

The allegations center on Fauci's statements about COVID-19's origins. Murrill claims Fauci "lied" during sworn testimony—a serious charge that could expose him to state-level prosecution. Even liberal commentator Bill Maher acknowledged Fauci was "caught in actual lies" about the pandemic's genesis, calling the origin question "very important" (New York Post).

**Why Colorado should care:** Coloradans endured some of the nation's harshest lockdowns and business closures under policies justified by federal health guidance. If Fauci deliberately misled Congress about COVID's origins, it raises hard questions about whether those restrictions were built on false pretenses—and whether state and local officials who followed his guidance bear any accountability.

Republicans argue the investigations are overdue accountability for a public health bureaucrat who wielded enormous power while allegedly deceiving elected officials. Democrats dismiss the probes as partisan theater.

**Watch next:** Whether these state investigations produce charges, and whether Colorado's Democratic leadership will acknowledge any role Fauci's guidance played in the state's pandemic policies.
