# ActBlue CEO Clams Up as Congress Probes Foreign Donations to Democratic Fundraiser

Republicans are demanding answers about how a major Democratic fundraising platform accepted foreign-linked donations—but the outfit's CEO isn't talking. ActBlue CEO Regina Wallace-Jones repeatedly invoked her Fifth Amendment rights during a congressional hearing Wednesday, refusing to answer questions about allegations the platform processed contributions tied to foreign sources (Breitbart). The probe, requested by President Trump in April, has intensified GOP scrutiny of Democratic fundraising practices and potential foreign interference in U.S. elections.

House Democrat Terri Sewell fired back, claiming the GOP investigation is part of a pattern of Trump DOJ harassment targeting Black women in power—a charge that sidesteps the core question: did ActBlue knowingly process foreign donations (Fox News)? Republicans argue the silence itself is damning. If the platform's operations were above board, they contend, Wallace-Jones should have testified openly rather than hide behind constitutional protections.

For Colorado Democrats and donors who've funneled money through ActBlue, this raises uncomfortable questions about vetting and compliance. Conservatives say the Fifth Amendment invocation suggests potential criminal exposure—and voters deserve transparency about where Democratic campaign cash originates.

Watch whether congressional Republicans subpoena ActBlue's transaction records and whether Colorado's Democratic establishment distances itself from the platform.
