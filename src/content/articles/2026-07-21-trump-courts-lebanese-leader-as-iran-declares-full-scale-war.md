# Trump Meets Lebanese Leader as Iran War Escalates

President Trump is engaging directly with Middle Eastern allies while Iran's leadership declares itself in "full-scale war" with the United States, signaling a sharp departure from the previous administration's appeasement-focused diplomacy.

Trump met with Lebanese President Joseph Aoun at the White House on Tuesday, July 21, as fierce fighting between Israel and Hezbollah continues in Lebanon's southern regions (Breitbart). The meeting underscores the administration's strategy of strengthening partnerships with regional governments facing Iranian-backed threats.

Meanwhile, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian declared Monday that Iran is in "full-scale war" with the U.S., citing the collapse of what he called a "memorandum of understanding" toward peace (Breitbart). The rhetoric reflects Tehran's escalating hostility following the Trump administration's rejection of the Obama-era Iran nuclear deal framework.
