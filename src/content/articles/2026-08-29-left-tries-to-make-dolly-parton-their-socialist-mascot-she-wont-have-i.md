# Dolly Won't Be the Left's Socialist Poster Child—and Conservatives Should Keep It That Way

Dolly Parton has spent a lifetime staying above the political fray, and the left shouldn't get to rewrite her legacy as a socialist icon just because she's generous.

The country music legend has famously resisted becoming anyone's political mascot. In 2018, when ABC's David Wright pressed her to criticize Donald Trump, Dolly shut it down with characteristic grace: "I don't do politics," she said flatly (The Daily Wire). That's not fence-sitting—that's principle.

Yet progressives are now attempting to co-opt Dolly's genuine charitable work—her Imagination Library, her pandemic donations, her support for various causes—as evidence she's secretly one of them. The Left claims her philanthropy proves she backs their big-government, redistributionist agenda.

Conservatives counter that Dolly's generosity flows from *private* virtue and personal choice, not government mandates or tax-funded programs. She gives because she wants to, not because bureaucrats force her to. That's the American model worth celebrating—individual liberty, not state dependency.

National Review argues we should "strive to live up to Dolly's vision for what the country can be," emphasizing her values of hard work, independence, and voluntary giving.

Coloradans should watch whether progressive activists continue trying to conscript Dolly into their political messaging—and whether she continues to politely decline.
