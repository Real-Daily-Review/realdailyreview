# Trump DOJ Raids Omar's Home as Rally Crowds Demand Deportation

President Trump seized on a federal raid of Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington home to attack the Minnesota Democrat at an Ohio rally, where supporters chanted "deport"—a moment that exposes the political minefield surrounding the Justice Department's investigation into the congresswoman.

Vice President JD Vance told reporters the administration had no advance notice of the Monday raid, claiming Trump and his team "try to stay out of such affairs" (The Daily Wire). That denial matters: it suggests either genuine separation between the DOJ and political leadership, or careful distance-keeping for optics.

**The conservative split:** National Review's editors expressed caution, noting they find themselves "rooting against both sides"—wary of both Omar's record and the appearance of weaponized prosecution. Republicans broadly argue the investigation reflects legitimate DOJ scrutiny of potential campaign finance violations. Democrats counter that Trump is using federal law enforcement as a political cudgel against critics, a charge that echoes progressive claims of authoritarian overreach.

**Colorado angle:** While Omar represents Minnesota, her case signals how the Trump administration will handle congressional opponents—a preview relevant to Colorado's delegation and voters concerned about DOJ independence.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether the investigation produces charges or dissolves into political theater.
