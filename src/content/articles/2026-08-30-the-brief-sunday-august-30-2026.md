## Colorado Springs Fights Back Against Election-Law Overreach

A lawsuit has been filed against Colorado Springs for holding its municipal elections in April — the first enforcement action since the 2025 voting law took effect. Republicans argue the state is trampling on local control by dictating when cities can hold their own elections, a classic case of Denver bureaucrats deciding they know better than local communities. Colorado conservatives should watch this one closely: if the state can dictate municipal election timing, what's next?

## Denver Mayor Johnston's Support Eroding

A new poll shows Denver Mayor Mike Johnston's appeal is declining, while more voters are viewing potential challenger Melat Kiros favorably. Most voters have consistently said Johnston is moving the city in the right direction but that he still needs to do more — a damning verdict for a mayor who has presided over a city that spent lavishly on homelessness programs with little to show for it. Denver taxpayers deserve leadership that delivers results, not progressive experiments funded on their dime.
