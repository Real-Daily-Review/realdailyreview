# Trump Keeps Huang Pardon Door Open as Nvidia CEO Faces Hill Scrutiny

President Trump said he's open to pardoning Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, calling it "always an option," though he suggested it probably won't be necessary (Washington Examiner). Huang has drawn fire from lawmakers over his ousting and reinstatement following an affair, and faces congressional testimony scrutiny.

Trump also signaled plans to meet with Michigan's incoming Sen.-elect Abdul el Sayed, a progressive Democrat, describing him as a "smart guy"—a move that underscores Trump's willingness to engage across party lines on business and policy matters.

**The Colorado angle:** Nvidia's fortunes matter to Colorado's tech corridor and semiconductor supply chain interests. Any pardon or regulatory relief for the chip giant could affect state tech employment and innovation investment.

**Conservative take:** Republicans have generally supported Huang and Nvidia's business operations, viewing the personal scandal as separate from the company's strategic importance to U.S. tech dominance against China. However, some conservatives argue that executive accountability—regardless of political connections—matters for corporate governance.

Watch whether congressional Republicans push Trump to condition any pardon on Huang's testimony and cooperation with Hill investigations into Nvidia's practices.
