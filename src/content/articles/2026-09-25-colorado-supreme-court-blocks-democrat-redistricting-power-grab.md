# Colorado Supreme Court Blocks Democrat Gerrymandering Scheme

Colorado's high court has halted a Democrat-backed redistricting power grab that would have tilted legislative maps in the party's favor—a rare win for election integrity in a state trending blue.

The Colorado Supreme Court's decision keeps the state out of the national gerrymandering wars, at least temporarily, according to *Complete Colorado*. Republicans have long argued that Democrats weaponize redistricting to entrench power and dilute conservative voting strength. This ruling suggests Colorado's courts, unlike those in other states, won't rubber-stamp partisan map manipulation.

The timing matters. While Democrats tout "voter rights" as a rallying cry—*CPR News* notes Attorney General Jena Griswold has made voting access central to her political messaging—conservatives argue the real voter right is having districts drawn fairly, not rigged for partisan advantage. Gerrymandering, whether by red or blue teams, undermines representative government and voter confidence.

For Colorado, this decision protects the state's credibility on elections at a moment when trust in the process is fragile. It also signals that not every progressive priority will sail through unchallenged, even in a Democratic-controlled statehouse.

Watch whether Democrats attempt to resurrect redistricting schemes through the legislature or ballot measures in 2026 and beyond.
