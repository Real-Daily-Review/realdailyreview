# Denver Weather Stays Mild; Alterra Mountain Co. Gets Capitol Hill Building

Colorado's Front Range will enjoy above-average temperatures and sunshine through midweek before a weekend cooldown brings mountain snow—a welcome break for the state's water-starved reservoirs.

Meanwhile, Denver City Council approved Alterra Mountain Company's lease of the former Denver Post building on Capitol Hill, though one councilmember dissented. The deal marks another chapter in the Post's decline and signals continued commercial real estate repositioning in the capital city.

The weather pattern reflects the high-pressure ridge typical of early-season Colorado conditions. Residents should expect pleasant daytime highs before the system weakens, ushering in cooler air and precipitation by weekend—particularly beneficial for Colorado's snowpack and drought-stressed water supplies heading into spring runoff season.

Alterra's Capitol Hill foothold could reshape the neighborhood's commercial corridor, though details on the company's specific plans for the historic building remain limited. The narrow council vote suggests some concern about the deal's terms or the building's future use.
