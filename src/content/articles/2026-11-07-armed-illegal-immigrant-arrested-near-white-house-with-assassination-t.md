# Armed Venezuelan Illegal Immigrant Arrested Near White House With Three Firearms

A Venezuelan national illegally in the country was arrested Thursday near the White House after Secret Service found him armed with three firearms and ammunition, according to the Department of Homeland Security. Ronny Yordrix Guanipa Chrinos was spotted acting suspiciously on Constitution Avenue and allegedly told officers that voices instructed him to kill President Trump (Daily Wire, Washington Examiner).

The arrest underscores what conservatives have long warned: open border policies create security vulnerabilities that endanger Americans. Republicans argue that inadequate vetting and enforcement allow dangerous individuals to reach sensitive locations and pose direct threats to national leadership. The incident contradicts Democratic claims that illegal immigration poses no meaningful security risk.

Colorado has absorbed thousands of migrants under Biden-era policies, straining local resources and raising questions about background checks and mental health screening. Denver and other Front Range cities have struggled to process and house migrants, diverting taxpayer funds from schools, infrastructure, and public safety.

Conservatives say this arrest validates demands for stronger border enforcement, expedited deportations, and accountability for sanctuary city policies that limit cooperation with federal immigration authorities.

Watch for Republican calls to tighten White House security protocols and renewed pressure on the Biden administration to explain how a potentially dangerous illegal immigrant reached the nation's capital.
