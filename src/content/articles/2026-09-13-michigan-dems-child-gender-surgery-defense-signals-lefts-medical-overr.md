# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Broader Left-Wing Medical Overreach

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's full-throated defense of child gender-transition procedures—without parental consent or government safeguards—exposes the progressive left's willingness to override parental rights in the name of medical autonomy.

Abdul El-Sayed, invoking his medical credentials, argued Friday at Michigan State University that doctors, not government, should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments (Fox News, The Daily Wire). Republicans counter that such decisions demand parental involvement and legislative guardrails, especially given the irreversible nature of these procedures on minors.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have largely aligned with the national progressive stance on transgender medical access. If El-Sayed's framing gains traction in Democratic circles, expect renewed pressure on Colorado lawmakers to block parental-notification bills and expand Medicaid coverage for gender procedures—directly affecting Colorado taxpayers and families.

Conservatives argue that government has a legitimate role protecting children from experimental medical interventions, particularly when parents are excluded from the decision-making process. The left dismisses such concerns as discrimination, framing parental involvement as "obstruction" of medical care.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado Democrats adopt El-Sayed's rhetoric in the 2024 legislative session and attempt to further restrict parental rights in gender-medicine decisions.
