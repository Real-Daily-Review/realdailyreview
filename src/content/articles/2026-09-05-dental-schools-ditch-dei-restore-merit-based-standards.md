# Dental Schools Ditch DEI Requirements—Merit Returns to the Chair

The Commission on Dental Accreditation has scrapped diversity, equity, and inclusion mandates from its accreditation standards, reversing a years-long push to prioritize identity politics over clinical competence.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, CODA moved beyond a temporary suspension to permanently removing DEI requirements from dental education standards. The shift signals a broader recognition among accrediting bodies that patients care about whether their dentist can fix a tooth—not whether admissions committees met demographic quotas.

Conservatives argue that DEI mandates in professional licensing and education distort merit-based selection, potentially compromising patient outcomes and eroding public trust in institutions. They contend that excellence in healthcare demands rigorous standards applied uniformly, regardless of an applicant's background. The removal of these requirements represents a win for competency-first hiring and training.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado dental schools and practices operate under CODA accreditation. This shift means Colorado's dental education programs can now prioritize clinical skill and knowledge without navigating conflicting DEI bureaucracy—a relief for institutions tired of compliance theater that doesn't improve patient care.

Watch whether other healthcare accrediting bodies follow CODA's lead in deprioritizing DEI mandates.
