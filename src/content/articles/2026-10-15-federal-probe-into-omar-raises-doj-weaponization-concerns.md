# Federal Probe Into Omar's Immigration Status Raises Questions About DOJ Overreach

A federal investigation into Rep. Ilhan Omar's immigration paperwork—reportedly aimed at potentially stripping her citizenship—illustrates how easily government power can be weaponized against political opponents, even when conservatives have legitimate concerns about her record.

Federal authorities raided Omar's Washington, D.C., residence as part of what the *Washington Examiner* reports is a criminal probe seeking grounds for denaturalization and deportation. The Minnesota Democrat has faced months of scrutiny over her immigration history.

The investigation presents a genuine conservative dilemma. Omar has been a vocal critic of U.S. foreign policy, Israel, and law enforcement—positions many conservatives reject outright. Her voting record aligns with the progressive wing of the Democratic Party, and Colorado Republicans have criticized similar left-wing representatives' positions on energy, Second Amendment rights, and fiscal responsibility.

Yet conservatives must acknowledge the danger: if a presidential administration can use the DOJ to investigate and potentially remove a political opponent from office through immigration proceedings, that same power exists for future administrations targeting conservative lawmakers. *National Review* editors themselves expressed ambivalence, noting the troubling precedent regardless of Omar's politics.

The core question isn't Omar's record—it's whether the rule of law means equal application or selective prosecution.

Watch whether congressional Republicans demand transparency into the investigation's scope and whether Democrats call for DOJ independence from political pressure.
