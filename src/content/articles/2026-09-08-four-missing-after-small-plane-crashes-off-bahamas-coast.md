# Search and Rescue Underway After Small Plane Disappears Off Bahamas

A small aircraft carrying four people—two grandparents and two grandchildren from Miami—has gone missing off the Bahamas coast following an apparent crash, according to 9News Denver and the New York Post. The family had been vacationing at their home in Great Harbor Cay over the Labor Day weekend before attempting to return to Florida when the plane went down.

Search and rescue operations are active, with authorities working to locate the aircraft and passengers. Details remain limited at this stage, though reports indicate the family was en route home when the incident occurred.

The incident underscores ongoing concerns about small aircraft safety and the importance of robust emergency response protocols for aviation incidents over open water. As rescue efforts continue, authorities will likely focus on determining the cause of the crash and locating survivors.
