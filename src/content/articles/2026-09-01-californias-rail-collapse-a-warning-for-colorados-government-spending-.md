# California's High-Speed Rail Fiasco Should Warn Colorado Against Wasteful Government Megaprojects

California's high-speed rail boondoggle is a cautionary tale Colorado lawmakers need to heed before green-lighting their own infrastructure fantasies. After burning billions in taxpayer dollars, California's project remains years behind schedule and nowhere near completion—while China has laid enough track to circle America three times, according to the *Daily Caller*. The *National Review* notes the state's regulatory overreach extends beyond rail to tire regulations, exemplifying how progressive governments weaponize bureaucracy to engineer outcomes rather than let markets function.

Republicans argue that California's failure proves government-led megaprojects are inherently wasteful, plagued by cost overruns, regulatory bloat, and political mismanagement. Democrats counter that such projects require long-term investment and that setbacks are inevitable in large infrastructure undertakings.

For Colorado, the lesson is stark: ambitious government spending schemes often deliver pennies on the dollar while enriching consultants and contractors. Colorado's own transportation and energy projects should prioritize private-sector efficiency and market-driven solutions over government central planning.

Watch whether Colorado legislators cite California's rail disaster when evaluating proposed state spending initiatives and whether they demand accountability metrics before greenlit projects break ground.
