# Colorado DA Anne Kelly Turns Around Troubled Office While Progressive Prosecutors Nationwide Face Backlash

Anne Kelly inherited a district attorney's office in crisis—and unlike her progressive counterparts in major cities, she actually fixed it. According to CPR News, Kelly has spent four years cleaning up misconduct and learning the realities of rural crime in Colorado's Alamosa County, turning around an office that was deeply troubled when she arrived.

The contrast is stark. While Kelly has focused on restoring public trust and accountability, progressive DAs in major cities like Philadelphia are facing fierce criticism. The Daily Wire reports that Philadelphia's Larry Krasner has drawn a Pennsylvania Supreme Court rebuke for seeking to free a convicted murderer, invoking "King's bench jurisdiction" to overturn his conviction—a move that underscores how far-left prosecutors have strayed from their core duty: protecting public safety.

Kelly's approach—restoring institutional credibility and taking crime seriously—reflects what conservatives argue works: competent, principled law enforcement that serves victims and communities rather than ideology. Her success in a rural Colorado county demonstrates that DA offices can be reformed without embracing the soft-on-crime policies that have devastated urban centers.

Coloradans should watch whether Kelly's pragmatic model influences other state prosecutors, or whether progressive ideology continues to dominate DA races statewide.
