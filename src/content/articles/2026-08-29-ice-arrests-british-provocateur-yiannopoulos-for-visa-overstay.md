# ICE Detains British Provocateur Milo Yiannopoulos for Visa Overstay

Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrested British provocateur Milo Yiannopoulos at Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport Thursday, with the Department of Homeland Security confirming Friday he faces deportation, according to *The Daily Wire* and *Just The News*.

Yiannopoulos, 41, entered the United States legally in 2019 through New York City but overstayed his visa, triggering the enforcement action. DHS has him in custody pending removal proceedings.

The arrest marks a rare instance of immigration enforcement against a high-profile figure in conservative circles. Yiannopoulos, a former Breitbart editor known for inflammatory rhetoric, has maintained a controversial public presence despite being deplatformed from major social media outlets in recent years.

The case underscores a broader tension within immigration enforcement: while conservatives generally support strict visa compliance and deportation of those who violate entry terms, the application to figures within right-leaning circles occasionally creates internal friction. Some conservative commentators have questioned the timing and circumstances of the arrest.

**Watch for:** Whether DHS provides additional details on how long Yiannopoulos remained in the country beyond his visa expiration and what triggered the enforcement action now.
