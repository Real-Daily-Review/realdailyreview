# Pfizer Board Member Allegedly Downplayed Natural Immunity While Pushing Vaccines

Scott Gottlieb, the former FDA chief now sitting on Pfizer's board, may have known far more about natural immunity's effectiveness than he publicly admitted during the pandemic's critical early months.

According to CIA briefing notes obtained by watchdogs and reported by *Just The News* and the *Daily Caller*, Gottlieb estimated in May 2020 that 20–25% of Americans were already infected with COVID and that each case produced roughly three transmissions. By his own math, herd immunity would have arrived before vaccines rolled out—yet he continued "lowballing" infection rates in public statements for months afterward, critics say.

The documents raise uncomfortable questions about the credibility of pandemic messaging from officials with financial stakes in vaccine adoption. Gottlieb's dual role—advising the government while profiting from Pfizer's vaccine sales—created an obvious conflict of interest that deserves scrutiny.

Colorado conservatives should care: state and local officials relied on federal guidance from figures like Gottlieb to justify lockdowns, school closures, and vaccine mandates that devastated small businesses and families across the state. If key decision-makers were selectively presenting data, Coloradans deserve answers about what was hidden and why.

Watch whether Congress demands Gottlieb's testimony and whether mainstream media outlets covering the pharmaceutical industry finally acknowledge these credibility gaps.
