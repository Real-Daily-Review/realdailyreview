# Multi-State Fauci Investigations Intensify After Senate Testimony on COVID Origins

Anthony Fauci faces mounting legal scrutiny as Louisiana joins Alabama and Florida in launching state-level investigations into potential criminal conduct, following explosive Senate testimony on COVID-19 origins.

Louisiana Attorney General Liz Murrill announced the probe after accusing Fauci of lying under deposition. The investigations center on Fauci's statements regarding the pandemic's origins—a question that has dogged the former National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases director since 2020.

**The conservative case:** Republicans and commentators argue Fauci misled the public and Congress about gain-of-function research funding and the lab-leak hypothesis. Bill Maher, no conservative, acknowledged Fauci was "caught in actual lies" about COVID origins (New York Post). Conservatives contend that accountability matters when government officials make false statements under oath, particularly on matters affecting millions of Americans.

**What's at stake:** These state-level investigations signal that Fauci's legal exposure extends beyond federal oversight. Colorado taxpayers should note that federal health bureaucrats who directed pandemic policy—including lockdowns and vaccine mandates that affected Colorado schools and businesses—may face consequences for dishonesty.
