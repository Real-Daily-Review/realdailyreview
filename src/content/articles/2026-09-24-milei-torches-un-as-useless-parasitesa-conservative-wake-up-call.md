# Argentine President Milei Torches UN as 'Useless' Bureaucratic Parasite

Argentina's libertarian firebrand Javier Milei delivered a withering indictment of the United Nations this week, calling it a "useless" organization staffed by "fatally arrogant parasites" during his address to the General Assembly in New York (Fox News, Breitbart).

Milei's broadside reflects a growing conservative skepticism toward international institutions that consume taxpayer dollars while delivering little accountability or results. The Argentine president's willingness to name the dysfunction plainly—rather than genuflect before diplomatic niceties—resonates with limited-government conservatives who view the UN as a bloated bureaucracy divorced from the interests of ordinary citizens.

For Colorado taxpayers, the critique matters: U.S. contributions to the UN and its affiliated agencies represent real money flowing overseas with minimal oversight. Republicans have long argued that American funding props up an institution hostile to U.S. sovereignty and frequently aligned against American interests on votes involving Israel, energy policy, and trade.

Milei's speech signals that anti-establishment, anti-globalist sentiment is gaining traction among world leaders willing to challenge the international consensus. His libertarian economic reforms in Argentina—slashing government spending and deregulating markets—have made him a folk hero to American conservatives tired of Washington's own bloated bureaucracy.

Watch whether Republican leaders in Congress cite Milei's UN critique to justify deeper cuts to American contributions to international bodies.
