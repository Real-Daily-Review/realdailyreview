# Trump Signals Hardline Iran Stance as Tehran Threatens Retaliation

President Trump is taking a muscular approach to Iran negotiations, warning Sunday that he's weighing options from economic strangulation to military action. Speaking to Fox News, Trump said he's in "deciding mode" on Iran, considering "wiping Iran out, letting them rot economically or making a deal"—and complained that Iranian officials are "hiding like rats," making diplomacy difficult (The Daily Wire).

The threat comes as Iran's military fired back with its own warning, promising "sustained, effective and painful" attacks on U.S. bases and interests across the Middle East if Washington strikes again (Breitbart).

**The Colorado angle:** While this is primarily a national security matter, energy markets and oil prices directly affect Colorado's economy and gas prices at the pump. A military escalation with Iran could spike energy costs, hitting Colorado families and businesses already dealing with inflation concerns.

Trump's willingness to consider maximum-pressure economic sanctions aligns with conservative skepticism of endless Middle East entanglements—but also signals he won't tolerate Iranian aggression. Republicans argue strength deters conflict; Democrats typically warn military posturing risks miscalculation.
