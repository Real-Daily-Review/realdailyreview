# Colorado Weather Brief: Enjoy Sunshine Now, Brace for Weekend Snow

Colorado's front range is catching a break before winter reasserts itself this weekend. Expect warm, sunny conditions through Friday—well above seasonal averages—before a weather system moves in Saturday and Sunday bringing rain to lower elevations and snow to the mountains, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The timing matters for ranchers, outdoor workers, and anyone planning weekend activities. Mountain communities should prepare for accumulating snow, while Denver-area residents can expect lighter precipitation. Temperatures will drop noticeably as the system approaches.

This pattern is typical late-season volatility for Colorado—a reminder that spring weather remains unpredictable at elevation. Farmers and ranchers monitoring soil conditions ahead of planting season should note the moisture arrival, though the brief nature of this system means it won't solve any lingering drought concerns.

Coloradans should monitor updated forecasts through the week for specific snow totals and timing, particularly if travel to the high country is planned.
