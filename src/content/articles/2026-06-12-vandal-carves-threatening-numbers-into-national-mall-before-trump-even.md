# Vandal Etches Threatening Numbers Into National Mall Ahead of Trump Birthday Event

A vandal carved "8647" into the grass of Washington, D.C.'s National Mall this week—numbers associated with threats against President Trump—just days before a UFC event scheduled for Trump's birthday, according to *Daily Caller*, *Just The News*, and *Breitbart*.

The U.S. Park Police called the incident "deranged vandalism" and stated that "any threat against the President is taken very seriously by the Department." The timing is notable: large crowds are expected to gather in the nation's capital for the birthday celebration.

**Colorado angle:** While this incident occurred in D.C., it underscores the security concerns facing high-profile events nationwide. Colorado has hosted major Trump rallies and political events; state law enforcement and event organizers must remain vigilant against similar threats and vandalism targeting political figures and public spaces.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether federal authorities identify and prosecute the vandal, and whether media outlets acknowledge the apparent threat or downplay it as mere "protest art."
