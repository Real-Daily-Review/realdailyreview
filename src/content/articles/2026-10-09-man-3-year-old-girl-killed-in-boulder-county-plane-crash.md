# Small Plane Crash Near Eldorado Springs Kills Two

A small single-engine aircraft crashed near Eldorado Springs in Boulder County over the weekend, killing a 43-year-old man and a 3-year-old girl aboard.

Emergency responders located the wreckage Monday after an emergency beacon activated Sunday night, according to 9News Denver. The aircraft, a fixed-wing plane, went down in a remote area that required search teams to conduct recovery operations.

Authorities have identified the two victims, though details about the flight's origin, destination, and cause remain under investigation. Boulder County officials are working to determine what led to the crash.

Small aircraft accidents in Colorado's mountainous terrain underscore the inherent risks of private aviation, particularly in areas with challenging weather and rugged topography. While such incidents remain statistically rare, they highlight the importance of proper maintenance, pilot training, and emergency communication systems—the beacon that alerted rescuers likely proved critical to locating the wreckage.

The investigation into the cause is ongoing.
