# California Democrat's Anti-Israel Stunt Exposes Left's Dangerous Foreign Policy Drift

Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) is using Israel as a political prop to launch his presidential bid—and his reckless position on military aid should alarm anyone concerned about American security interests.

Khanna announced support for Rep. Thomas Massie's (R-KY) amendment to eliminate all U.S. foreign aid to Israel, including funding for the Iron Dome defensive system, according to the Washington Examiner. National Review characterized the move as a transparent "stunt" designed to generate media attention for his long-shot presidential campaign, noting Khanna had recently staged a confrontation in the West Bank.

The Examiner reports Khanna apparently didn't bother reading the amendment carefully—a telling sign of performative politics over serious policy work.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Eliminating aid to Israel's defensive capabilities weakens a critical American ally in a volatile region and signals weakness to adversaries. The Iron Dome saves Israeli civilian lives. Democrats who embrace this position are signaling that anti-Israel activism now drives their foreign policy, not strategic thinking.

Colorado voters should note this reflects the broader leftward drift on Middle East policy within the Democratic Party—a shift that could reshape how America engages with allies and threats alike.

Watch whether other Democratic presidential hopefuls embrace Khanna's position to compete for the activist base.
