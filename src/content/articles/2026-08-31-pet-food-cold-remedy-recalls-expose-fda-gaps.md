# Pet Food and Cold Remedy Recalls Highlight FDA Oversight Gaps

Colorado pet owners and consumers should brace for another round of product recalls—this time spanning raw pet food and over-the-counter cold remedies.

Northwest Naturals is recalling raw chicken pet food products sold nationwide due to salmonella and listeria contamination risks, according to the New York Post. Separately, Zicam has initiated a nationwide recall of cold remedy nasal swabs, with a refund website now operational (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

These recalls underscore a persistent conservative concern: federal agencies like the FDA struggle to catch safety problems before products reach shelves. While food safety oversight is a legitimate government function, Republicans argue the current regulatory apparatus is bloated, slow-moving, and often reactive rather than preventive. The agency's track record of late-stage recalls—after consumers have already purchased and used products—raises questions about whether existing regulations actually protect Americans or simply create compliance burdens on legitimate businesses.

Colorado families relying on pet nutrition and cold remedies now face the hassle of checking product batches, seeking refunds, and finding alternatives. The recalls also highlight why conservatives push for transparency and market accountability: informed consumers and company reputation matter more than bureaucratic rubber-stamping.

Watch whether Colorado health officials issue specific guidance for state residents and whether retailers proactively pull affected inventory from shelves.
