# UFC Fighter's Michelle Obama Jab Draws Rebuke From Gillis, Dana White

A crude joke about Michelle Obama at Sunday's White House UFC event has sparked pushback from the very figures who brought the sport to President Trump's residence. UFC fighter Josh Hokit made disparaging remarks about the former first lady during the Freedom 250 fight, prompting comedian Shane Gillis to tell reporters he "didn't like" the comment (Fox News). UFC CEO Dana White also condemned the fighter, calling the remark "nasty" and noting that while public figures deserve scrutiny, such personal attacks cross a line (Washington Examiner).

The incident underscores a broader tension within conservative circles: while Trump's UFC partnership signals the sport's growing political alignment with Republicans, not all conservative figures embrace every rhetorical excess that emerges at these high-profile events. Gillis and White's quick condemnations suggest an effort to maintain decorum and separate legitimate political criticism from personal insults.

For Colorado conservatives and Trump supporters, the episode offers a reminder that winning political favor doesn't require—and often shouldn't include—crude personal attacks on political opponents, regardless of their prominence. The focus should remain on policy differences, not character assassination.
