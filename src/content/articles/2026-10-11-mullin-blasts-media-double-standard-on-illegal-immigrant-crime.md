# DHS Chief Mullin Fires Back at Media Over ICE Shooting, Highlights Double Standard on Illegal Immigrant Crime

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin is refusing to apologize for ICE enforcement actions, instead turning the tables on progressive politicians and media figures who he says ignore crimes committed by illegal immigrants. When pressed by CNN's Jake Tapper and CBS's Margaret Brennan over last week's ICE shooting of an illegal immigrant in New York City, Mullin countered with a stark challenge: where was the outrage when an NYPD officer was stabbed seven times by illegal aliens in the same Bronx neighborhood days earlier? (New York Post)

"Not one of them said anything," Mullin told Fox News, accusing Gov. Hochul and other Democrats of selective moral grandstanding (New York Post). The Daily Caller reports Mullin clashed with Brennan over the incident, with critics arguing the shooting endangered a child present at the scene.

Mullin's pushback reflects a core conservative argument: the media and Democratic establishment obsess over isolated law enforcement actions while downplaying the real crime wave fueled by open borders and sanctuary policies. For Colorado, where illegal immigration strains local law enforcement and social services, Mullin's willingness to defend ICE operations signals the Trump administration won't be intimidated by media narratives that prioritize illegal immigrant welfare over American citizen safety.
