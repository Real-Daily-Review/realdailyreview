# Colorado State Patrol Honors 9/11 Victims; National Controversy Over NYC Memorial Attendance

Colorado State Patrol is doing what government should do—honoring sacrifice without politics—by rolling out special vehicle wraps commemorating the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks (9News Denver).

Meanwhile, back East, the 9/11 memorial has become a flashpoint. New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani attended Ground Zero's anniversary ceremony despite a petition signed by over 110,000 people—including 9/11 families—urging him to stay away (Fox News). The controversy centers on Mamdani's appointment of Ramzi Kassem to a key position; critics argue Kassem's record on national security concerns makes his presence at the memorial inappropriate.

Mamdani proceeded anyway, greeting former Mayor Rudy Giuliani at the ceremony (Washington Examiner), while family members of victims spoke at the National September 11 Memorial &amp; Museum (Breitbart).

The contrast is stark: Colorado's State Patrol keeps the focus where it belongs—on remembrance and respect for those lost. In New York, political calculation overshadowed solemnity. For Coloradans, the lesson is clear: when government officials prioritize ideology over the dignity of victims and their families, trust erodes fast.

Watch whether Colorado's leadership maintains this standard of respectful commemoration or follows the national trend of politicizing tragedy.
