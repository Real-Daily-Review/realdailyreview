# Dennis Haskins, 'Saved by the Bell' Star, Dies at 75

Actor Dennis Haskins, best known for playing Principal Richard Belding on the 1990s teen sitcom "Saved by the Bell," has died at 75. Haskins became a cultural fixture as "America's Favorite Principal," anchoring the comedy series from 1989 to 1992 on NBC, with the character continuing through spinoffs that extended his run through 2000 (New York Post). He made his final public appearance nearly a decade before his death, according to reports (New York Post). Haskins died in Tennessee, according to multiple outlets (9News Denver, Just The News).

The actor's portrayal of the no-nonsense principal became iconic for a generation of viewers who grew up watching the show's blend of high school drama and comedy. His deadpan delivery and memorable one-liners made the character instantly recognizable across American households during the show's peak popularity.

Haskins' death marks the loss of another recognizable figure from 1980s and 1990s television, a period that defined entertainment for millions of Americans now in their 40s and 50s.
