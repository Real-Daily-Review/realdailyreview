# Unqualified Pilot Flew 900+ Boeing Jets for 17 Years—Regulatory Failure Raises Safety Questions

A stunning regulatory failure north of the border has Colorado travelers asking hard questions about aviation oversight: An Air Canada captain allegedly flew over 900 large Boeing aircraft for 17 years without proper licensing, authorities revealed this week.

Geoffrey Wall, 59, operated as a commercial airline captain from 2009 through 2025 despite never obtaining the required pilot certification, according to Canadian police. Investigators discovered "anomalies" in his licensing documentation during a routine 2025 credentials check—raising the alarming question of how such a massive gap went undetected for nearly two decades (Just The News, The Daily Wire).
