# Colorado Conservatives Should Watch: Democrats' Shell-Game Strategy in 2024 Senate Races

Democrats are running a playbook that should alarm Colorado voters: deploying "independent" candidates as Trojan horses to split Republican votes and hand seats to the left.

In Nebraska, the *Washington Examiner* reports that Democratic operatives are backing Senate candidate Dan Osborn, who claims independence from both parties while consistently echoing Democratic talking points. The strategy is transparent—if it walks like a Democrat and talks like a Democrat, it probably is one.

Meanwhile, Michigan Democrats are amplifying fringe arguments about immigration enforcement. Senate candidate Amin El-Sayed told Fox News that ICE "is not working" because the U.S. had "far fewer" illegal migrants under the old INS system, according to *Just The News*. This is the left's backdoor case for gutting immigration enforcement entirely.

Colorado Republicans should recognize this pattern. Democrats lack the votes to win outright in competitive races, so they're manufacturing "independent" cover and pushing radical policy through sympathetic candidates. It's not about principle—it's about power.

The real test: Will Colorado voters see through the charade, or will they fall for the "independent" label while voting for Democratic priorities on immigration, energy, and taxes?
