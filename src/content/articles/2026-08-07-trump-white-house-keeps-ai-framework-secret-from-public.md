# White House Keeps AI Framework Under Wraps While Trump Courts Olympic Athletes

President Trump's White House is refusing to release its artificial intelligence regulatory framework to the public, according to the Daily Caller, keeping the administration's tech policy roadmap locked behind closed doors even as it claims to champion free-market innovation.

Meanwhile, Trump hosted Team USA's Winter Olympians at the White House this week, celebrating the athletes who brought home 33 medals—12 gold, 12 silver, and nine bronze—at the January Games, finishing second overall behind Norway's 41-medal haul (Just The News, Breitbart).

The secrecy around the AI framework raises questions conservatives should scrutinize: if the Trump administration believes its tech policy is sound, why hide it from taxpayers, businesses, and Colorado's growing tech sector? Transparency matters, especially when government is shaping rules that affect innovation and competition. Colorado's tech community and venture capital ecosystem deserve to know what regulatory guardrails—or lack thereof—the White House is building.

The Olympic celebration underscores Trump's focus on American achievement and national pride heading into the midterm elections, even as the White House navigates questions about Iran policy and economic messaging.
