# Trump Judge Blocks DOJ Sanctuary Cities Suit; ACLU Retreats on Trans Bathroom Push

A Trump-appointed federal judge has gutted the Department of Justice's lawsuit against Minnesota's sanctuary immigration policies, ruling the state cannot be forced to assist ICE enforcement—a major setback for immigration hawks hoping courts would rein in sanctuary jurisdictions (Fox News Politics).

Meanwhile, the ACLU is quietly walking back its aggressive push to allow biological males in girls' restrooms after the Supreme Court's decisive ruling against trans athletes in sports (Just The News). The civil rights group is now asking courts to narrowly interpret Title IX precedents—a sharp reversal from its own earlier arguments.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado cities including Denver have adopted sanctuary policies limiting local police cooperation with federal immigration enforcement. This ruling suggests Colorado's sanctuary ordinances face minimal legal jeopardy, even under a Republican administration. Conservatives argue sanctuary policies obstruct immigration law and endanger public safety; Democrats defend them as protecting vulnerable immigrant communities.

The ACLU's retreat on bathroom access signals weakness in the transgender rights agenda after judicial losses, though progressive Colorado lawmakers may still push state-level protections regardless of federal court skepticism.
