# RNC Challenges Griswold's Overseas Voter Residency Rule

The Republican National Committee is suing Colorado Secretary of State Jena Griswold over voting eligibility rules that could allow people who've never lived in Colorado to cast ballots through family proxies.

According to *Complete Colorado*, the RNC's complaint argues that Griswold's policy—which permits residency to pass by proxy through a parent or spouse—violates the Constitution. Republicans contend the rule opens the door to fraudulent registrations and dilutes the voting power of actual Colorado residents.

The lawsuit centers on a fundamental conservative principle: voting eligibility should require genuine, verifiable residency. Allowing someone with no personal ties to Colorado to vote through a family member's claim undermines election integrity and the basic requirement that voters have a real stake in the communities they're voting to govern.

Griswold, a Democrat, has championed expansive voting access policies. The RNC's challenge suggests Republicans believe her administration has crossed the line from convenience into constitutional overreach.

This case will test whether Colorado courts agree that residency requirements mean what they say—or whether progressive election officials can redefine them through administrative fiat.

**Watch for:** whether Colorado courts rule on the merits before the 2026 election cycle heats up.
