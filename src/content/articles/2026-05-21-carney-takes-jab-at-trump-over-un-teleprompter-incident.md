# Canadian PM Carney Jabs Trump Over Teleprompter Malfunction

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney made a lighthearted dig at President Trump on Wednesday after his own teleprompter failed during a speech to the Greater Vancouver Board of Trade.

When the device went down mid-speech, Carney laughed and said, "Happens, it happens"—an apparent reference to Trump's teleprompter malfunction at the United Nations last fall. According to the Washington Examiner, Trump had faced criticism over that incident, which Carney's quip seemed to characterize as a "conspiracy theory."

The exchange marks a moment of public ribbing between the two leaders, with Carney using his own technical difficulty as an opening to needle the U.S. president over a previous high-profile mishap. Carney handled the situation with humor, continuing his remarks despite the equipment failure.

The incident underscores the occasional friction in U.S.-Canada relations, expressed here through comedy rather than confrontation. Watch for whether Trump or his team respond publicly to Carney's jab.
