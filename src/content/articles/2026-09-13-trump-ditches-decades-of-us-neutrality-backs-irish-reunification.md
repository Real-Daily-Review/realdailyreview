# Trump Breaks US Neutrality on Ireland, Signals Support for Reunification

President Trump has abandoned decades of American diplomatic neutrality by publicly endorsing Irish reunification during a Dublin visit, a significant shift in U.S. policy toward the island's constitutional future.

Speaking alongside Irish Taoiseach Micheál Martin, Trump stated he would "love to see" Ireland unified as one nation, according to *Fox News*. The comment marks a departure from longstanding U.S. practice of remaining officially neutral on Northern Ireland's status—a careful balance maintained through multiple administrations to avoid inflaming sectarian tensions or alienating either British or Irish interests.

*The Daily Wire* notes Trump explicitly broke with this decades-long neutrality framework, saying "I don't want to cause any problem. I will tell you, I'd love to see it unified."

Such moves can complicate foreign policy, alienate allies (Britain), and set precedents for other regional disputes where American neutrality serves strategic interests.
