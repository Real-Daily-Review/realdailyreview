# Foreign Money Infiltrating U.S. Academia and Climate Activism, House Republicans Investigate

Colorado taxpayers should be alarmed: foreign influence is seeping into American universities and activist groups shaping policy that directly affects Colorado's energy sector and economy.

The University of Washington scrubbed a Pentagon-funded professor's ties to a Chinese military firm, according to reporting by the Daily Caller. Meanwhile, House Judiciary Chair Jim Jordan (R-OH) is investigating ClientEarth USA, a foreign-funded climate activist group, over concerns it's unduly influencing American courts through climate-focused lawsuits—the kind that have repeatedly targeted Colorado's oil and gas industry, per The Daily Wire.

**Republicans argue** this represents a dangerous blind spot: foreign entities are bankrolling groups that weaponize the courts against American energy development while universities hide compromising foreign ties from federal funders. The pattern suggests coordinated efforts to undermine U.S. energy independence and Colorado's energy workforce.

**Democrats and climate advocates** contend that environmental litigation and academic research serve the public interest regardless of funding sources, and that scrutinizing foreign-backed climate groups amounts to suppressing legitimate advocacy.

For Colorado, the stakes are concrete: foreign-funded litigation has already targeted state oil and gas operations. If foreign money is flowing through activist groups suing Colorado energy companies while hiding its origins, taxpayers deserve transparency.

Watch whether Jim Jordan's investigation produces documents revealing how much foreign capital is funding climate suits against Colorado's energy sector.
