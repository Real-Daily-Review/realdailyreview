# South Carolina GOP Primary Shows Trump's Grip on Party Remains Iron-Clad

Trump's endorsement proved decisive in South Carolina's Republican gubernatorial primary, as Lt. Gov. Pamela Evette—backed by the former president—advanced to a runoff against Attorney General Alan Wilson (Washington Examiner, Breitbart). Evette won the initial round with roughly 29.1% of the vote, while Wilson placed second.

The real story: Nancy Mace, the incumbent congresswoman who sought the governorship, finished fifth and conceded without Trump's support—a stark reminder of the former president's stranglehold on GOP primary voters. After her loss, Mace endorsed Wilson, her former political rival, signaling party unity (Washington Examiner). Wilson had previously called Mace an "entitled, spoiled brat," but both camps have now "buried the hatchet."

Meanwhile, South Carolina's 1st Congressional District—Mace's current seat—heads to separate runoffs. On the GOP side, Jenny Honeycut and Mark Smith advanced (Fox News). On the Democratic side, retired Navy Vice Admiral Nancy Lacore, who was fired by Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, won her primary and will face the Republican nominee in the general election (Fox News).

**What conservatives should watch:** The Evette-Wilson runoff will test whether Trump's endorsement alone carries weight or if Wilson's executive experience and anti-establishment credentials resonate with South Carolina Republicans.
