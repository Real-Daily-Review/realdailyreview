# Trump Pursues End-Run on Birthright Citizenship After Supreme Court Loss

President Trump signed executive orders Thursday targeting birthright citizenship and "birth tourism"—a direct challenge to the Supreme Court's June ruling that struck down his earlier attempt to deny citizenship to children born to noncitizens on U.S. soil (9News Denver, Fox News).

The new orders deny visas to foreign nationals entering the country specifically to give birth and crack down on organized birth tourism operations, according to Fox News. Trump told reporters the Supreme Court's decision was "very unfortunate" and "very unfair," signaling his administration will pursue alternative legal strategies after the high court rejected his initial executive order interpreting the 14th Amendment narrowly (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative argument:** Republicans contend that birthright citizenship as currently practiced incentivizes illegal immigration and exploits the 14th Amendment's original intent. Conservatives argue the amendment was designed to grant citizenship to formerly enslaved people, not to create automatic citizenship for children of foreign nationals who entered illegally or temporarily (The Daily Wire, Just The News).

**The left's position:** Democrats and immigration advocates defend birthright citizenship as settled constitutional law and a cornerstone of American identity, warning that restrictions would create a two-tiered citizenship system.

Colorado voters and taxpayers should watch whether these orders survive judicial challenge—and whether the administration's visa restrictions affect Colorado's tourism and international business sectors.
