# Bloomberg's Millions Flood Colorado Governor's Race

A New York billionaire is pouring cash into Colorado's Democratic primary to shape the next governor—a stark reminder that out-of-state money and progressive mega-donors now drive Colorado politics.

Michael Bloomberg, the former New York mayor and 2020 presidential also-ran, is backing candidates in the race, according to *Complete Colorado*. The move underscores how national progressive donors view Colorado as a battleground worth controlling, even in primary elections meant to reflect local values.

The Democratic field includes U.S. Senator Michael Bennet, who is banking on his record as an elected official and government insider (per the *Denver Post*), and Attorney General Phil Weiser, whose tenure has centered on litigation against the Trump administration—now a centerpiece of his gubernatorial pitch.

**The conservative concern:** Bloomberg's intervention signals that Colorado Democrats increasingly answer to coastal billionaires and national progressive networks rather than Colorado voters. Weiser's record of aggressive litigation against Republican administrations and business-friendly policies raises questions about what his governorship would mean for Colorado's energy sector, water rights, and regulatory environment.

Republicans argue Colorado needs a governor focused on kitchen-table issues—affordability, water security, and job creation—not culture-war litigation and progressive social experiments funded by out-of-state elites.

**Watch for:** Whether Bloomberg's spending translates to primary victories and what that reveals about Democratic primary voters' actual priorities versus national donor agendas.
