# Marxist Activists Block AI Infrastructure While Colorado Faces Energy Crunch

A Marxist-aligned organizer in Virginia is leading opposition to an AI data center project, raising questions about who's really driving anti-development activism across the country—and whether Colorado will face similar obstruction as demand for power-hungry computing surges.

According to Fox News, the campaign against the Frederick County facility is being led by someone tied to the Party of Socialism and Liberation, a Marxist-Leninist organization. The effort highlights a troubling pattern: radical activists using environmental and local-control rhetoric to block infrastructure that America needs.

For Colorado, this matters enormously. Data centers require massive, reliable electricity. As AI computing explodes, demand for power will follow—and Colorado's energy sector, already under assault from progressive regulators and green mandates, will face mounting pressure to expand capacity. Yet if ideological activists can weaponize community opposition to block projects, Colorado's ability to meet that demand—and keep energy costs competitive—will suffer.

Republicans argue that blocking AI infrastructure in the name of socialism amounts to economic sabotage dressed up as grassroots activism. Conservatives warn that foreign adversaries and radical groups are exploiting legitimate local concerns to cripple American technological and economic dominance.

Watch whether similar Marxist-backed campaigns emerge against Colorado data center or energy projects—and whether state Republicans will push back hard against regulatory delays that serve radical interests, not Colorado families.
