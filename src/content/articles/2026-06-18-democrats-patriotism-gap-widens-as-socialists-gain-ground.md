# Democrats' Patriotism Problem Signals Deeper Ideological Shift

The Democratic Party's embrace of socialist candidates and declining national pride reveals a fundamental realignment away from mainstream American values—a warning sign for Colorado voters watching their state drift left.

A new analysis from *National Review* shows Democrats express less pride in America than Republicans, even during Democratic presidencies under Obama and Biden. Meanwhile, avowed socialist Janeese Lewis George's commanding victory in Washington, D.C.'s Democratic primary—virtually guaranteeing her election as mayor—underscores the *Washington Examiner's* observation that the party has become "the party of socialism now."

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue this ideological drift reflects a Democratic base increasingly hostile to American exceptionalism and free-market capitalism. Lewis George's DSA membership and radical policy platform represent the party's activist core, not a fringe element. When socialists win Democratic primaries in major cities, it signals where party energy and money flow.

**What Colorado should watch:** The state's Democratic establishment has trended similarly left on energy policy, taxes, and gun control. If national Democrats continue nominating candidates further from the American center, Colorado's purple-county voters—particularly in energy-dependent regions—may finally reject the progressive experiment.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from national socialism rhetoric or embrace it heading into 2026.
