# Trump Names Immunity Lawyer Will Scharf as White House Counsel

President Donald Trump has elevated Will Scharf, a key architect of his Supreme Court immunity victory, to White House Counsel, effective September 1, replacing David Warrington. Trump announced the move on Truth Social, praising Scharf's legal work defending him "during the Biden Administration's corrupt and illegal Weaponization" (Washington Examiner).

Scharf, who previously served as White House Staff Secretary, represented Trump in multiple cases including the landmark Supreme Court immunity ruling that shielded him from prosecution on charges related to January 6. The appointment signals Trump's intention to staff his legal operation with loyalists who have proven their commitment to his defense against what conservatives view as politically motivated prosecutions.

For Colorado Republicans and Trump supporters, the move reinforces the administration's focus on dismantling what they see as weaponized federal law enforcement. Scharf's elevation suggests the White House will pursue aggressive legal strategies against ongoing investigations and regulatory overreach—priorities that align with conservative calls for restrained federal power.

Warrington, a Marine veteran who served in the role since Trump's return to office, departs as the administration faces continued legal challenges. Watch whether Scharf's appointment accelerates efforts to investigate and potentially prosecute officials involved in Trump-era investigations.
