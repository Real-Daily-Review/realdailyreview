# Federal Trade Court Strikes Down Trump's 10% Global Tariffs

A federal trade court has ruled against President Trump's 10 percent global tariffs imposed in February, days after the Supreme Court struck down his earlier "Liberation Day" tariffs. The U.S. Court of International Trade issued a permanent injunction against the levies in a decision that sided with small businesses challenging the tariffs, according to CBS News and Al Jazeera. The three-judge panel ruled in a 2-1 majority that the tariffs, which applied to nearly all U.S. imports, were unauthorized under Section 122 of the trade act, the Financial Times reported. The injunction blocks the tariffs for the plaintiffs in the case, per the Washington Examiner. Trump had implemented the 10 percent tariffs as a replacement after the Supreme Court invalidated his initial tariff package earlier this year. The ruling represents a second major legal setback for the administration's tariff strategy in recent months. Watch for potential appeals or new tariff measures from the White House.
