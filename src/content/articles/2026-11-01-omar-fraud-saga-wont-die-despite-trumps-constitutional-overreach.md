# Omar Scandal Persists Despite Constitutional Limits on Trump's Removal Calls

President Trump has called for Rep. Ilhan Omar's resignation over alleged immigration fraud, but the Constitution reserves congressional expulsion powers to the House alone—a legal reality that hasn't stopped Republicans from pressing the issue (Fox News Politics).

Minnesota Republican State Rep. Kristin Robbins told The Daily Wire that Omar was "thick as thieves" with individuals tied to a massive fraud scandal, describing connections the congresswoman has yet to address publicly. The New York Times recently detailed fresh allegations in the ongoing controversy.

**Republicans argue** Omar's alleged ties to fraudsters and unresolved questions about her immigration history demand accountability and transparency. They contend the pattern of unanswered inquiries reflects a broader credibility problem.

**Democrats** have largely dismissed the allegations as politically motivated attacks, framing them as part of a coordinated effort to discredit a progressive voice.

While this scandal centers on a Minnesota congresswoman, Colorado conservatives should note how national Democratic figures escape serious media scrutiny when allegations surface—a double standard that affects how Colorado's own representatives are covered and held accountable.

Watch whether House Republicans formally pursue ethics investigations or whether the story fades as media attention shifts.
