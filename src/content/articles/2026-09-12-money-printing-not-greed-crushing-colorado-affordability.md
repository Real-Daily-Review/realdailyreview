# Federal Money-Printing, Not Corporate Greed, Is Crushing Colorado Wallets

Coloradans struggling to afford groceries, rent, and gas should stop blaming "greedy corporations"—the real culprit is Washington's reckless money-printing, according to Sen. Rand Paul's recent analysis covered by the *Washington Examiner*.

Paul argues that across-the-board price inflation stems not from corporate price gouging, deregulation, heavy regulation, or environmental restrictions, but from currency debasement itself. When the federal government floods the economy with newly printed dollars, the purchasing power of every dollar in your wallet shrinks—a hidden tax on savers and working families.

**The conservative case:** Republicans and fiscal hawks say Democrats and the Federal Reserve have weaponized monetary policy to fund massive spending programs, driving inflation that hits Colorado families hardest. Blaming corporations for rising prices is political theater that ignores the root cause: too much money chasing too few goods.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** Energy workers, ranchers, small-business owners, and retirees on fixed incomes face eroded savings and higher borrowing costs. The state's cost of living has surged alongside national inflation.

Watch for whether Colorado's Republican delegation will demand congressional oversight of Federal Reserve policy and push back against further deficit spending in Washington.
