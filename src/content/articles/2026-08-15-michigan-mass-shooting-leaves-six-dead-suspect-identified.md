# Michigan Mass Shooting Kills Six; Suspect Dead

A 39-year-old gunman opened fire across multiple locations in Michigan, leaving six dead including the suspect before law enforcement ended the rampage.

Michigan State Police reported that Chad Hickman carried out the killings at three separate scenes. Five victims' identities remain unreleased pending family notification. The suspect was killed, ending the attack.

Details on the exact timeline, locations, and circumstances remain limited as investigators piece together the incident. State police have not yet released comprehensive information about the victims or a clear motive.
