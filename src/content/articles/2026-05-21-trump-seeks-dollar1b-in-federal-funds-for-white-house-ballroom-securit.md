# Trump Seeks $1 Billion in Federal Funds to Secure White House Ballroom

President Trump is requesting $1 billion in taxpayer money to protect a White House ballroom from drone strikes and other security threats, according to Ars Technica. The ballroom was privately funded at a cost of $400 million.

The request underscores ongoing security concerns at the presidential residence. Details on the specific threats or the scope of the proposed protective measures were not disclosed in available reports.

The announcement comes as the Trump administration continues rolling back Biden-era policies across multiple agencies. On Thursday, Trump and EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin were scheduled to make an announcement at the White House, according to the Washington Examiner. The EPA has already repealed numerous environmental regulations as part of the administration's broader deregulation agenda.

Separately, the White House is moving to reshore fertilizer production amid price pressures linked to regional conflicts, the Examiner reported.

Watch for details on the ballroom security project's scope, timeline, and whether Congress approves the funding request.
