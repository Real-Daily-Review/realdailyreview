# Trump Pressures GOP Holdouts on Blanche AG Pick as Weaponization Fight Intensifies

Trump is publicly calling out Republican senators blocking his attorney general nominee Todd Blanche, vowing to keep him as acting AG and revive a stalled anti-weaponization bill if confirmation stalls (Fox News Politics). The Senate Judiciary Committee postponed Blanche's vote this week after two GOP senators—identified as John Cornyn and Thom Tillis—raised concerns (Just The News).

The standoff reflects a deeper conservative priority: rolling back what Republicans argue is the Biden-era Justice Department's weaponization against Trump and his allies. Trump's threat to resurrect the $1.8 billion anti-weaponization fund signals he won't abandon the issue even if Blanche faces confirmation delays.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. A Blanche-led DOJ could reshape federal prosecutions affecting Colorado energy development, land-use disputes, and Second Amendment cases that have drawn scrutiny from progressive prosecutors. Republicans argue the department needs leadership committed to restoring institutional integrity and ending selective prosecution based on politics.

The holdout GOP senators risk primary challenges and donor backlash if they're seen as obstructing Trump's cabinet. Democrats, meanwhile, oppose Blanche as too partisan and loyal to Trump.

**Watch whether Cornyn and Tillis cave to pressure—or whether Trump follows through on his threat to use executive power to bypass the Senate confirmation process.**
