# Trump's Endorsement Doesn't Clear the Field in South Carolina GOP Primary

Trump's backing of Rep. Darline Graham isn't stopping other Republicans from challenging her in South Carolina's special Senate primary set for August 11. Former Governor Mark Sanford announced his candidacy this week, joining a crowded GOP field despite the former president's endorsement of Graham.

Sanford, who lost reelection in 2018 and mounted a primary challenge against Trump in 2020, is betting that South Carolina Republicans will look past the endorsement and focus on his record. The move signals that Trump's influence over GOP primaries—while substantial—doesn't automatically clear the field, even in a red state where his endorsement typically carries weight.

For Colorado conservatives watching national GOP dynamics, Sanford's entry underscores a broader reality: primary challenges to Trump-backed candidates remain viable when candidates can credibly argue they represent the party's establishment or offer a different vision. Whether Graham's endorsement proves decisive will tell us something about Trump's actual primary power heading into 2026.
