# Trump Debuts White House Helipad as U.N. General Assembly Convenes

President Trump has unveiled a new White House helipad ahead of this week's United Nations General Assembly, using the facility to depart in Marine One for the international gathering (Just The News).

The timing underscores Trump's engagement with the global stage as world leaders prepare for the high-level debate beginning Tuesday—an annual forum where nations present their positions on international affairs (Breitbart). The new helipad represents a logistical upgrade to presidential operations at 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue.

Meanwhile, conservative voices are raising sharp questions about America's continued involvement with the U.N. itself. A Washington Examiner opinion piece argues that Trump should serve the United Nations an eviction notice, citing the organization's dysfunction and European pressure campaigns—such as the EU's demand that the U.S. lift its visa ban on Palestinian Authority leader Mahmoud Abbas so he can address the assembly. The piece characterizes such demands as "empty gestures of European virtue-signaling" that undermine American sovereignty.

For Colorado taxpayers and conservatives skeptical of international institutions, the broader debate centers on whether the U.S. should continue hosting and funding an organization many view as hostile to American interests and ineffectual at its stated mission.

Watch whether Trump uses this week's assembly to signal a harder line on U.N. reform or American withdrawal.
