# Federal Probe Into Rep. Omar's Citizenship Raises Questions About Due Process and Political Weaponization

A Trump administration investigation into Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) is reportedly aimed at stripping her citizenship and deporting her, according to reporting from the Wall Street Journal cited by the Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire. Federal authorities raided her Washington, D.C. residence as part of a probe examining her immigration paperwork and how she obtained U.S. citizenship after arriving from Somalia.

The investigation centers on potential irregularities in her naturalization process. Omar has faced scrutiny for months over immigration-related questions.

**The conservative split:** National Review's analysis captures the tension here—some conservatives support rigorous enforcement of immigration law and citizenship requirements, while others worry the investigation risks appearing politically motivated or setting a dangerous precedent for weaponizing the DOJ against political opponents, even controversial ones.

Republicans argue that if Omar obtained citizenship through fraud or misrepresentation, the law must apply equally regardless of political status. Democrats will almost certainly frame this as authoritarian overreach and selective prosecution.

**Colorado angle:** While Omar represents Minnesota, the case underscores broader conservative concerns about immigration enforcement priorities and the politicization of federal law enforcement—issues that resonate with Colorado voters skeptical of government power.

Watch whether this investigation produces formal charges and how congressional Republicans respond to Democratic accusations of DOJ weaponization.
