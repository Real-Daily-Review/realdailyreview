# Texas Court Blocks Abortion, Protects Unborn Life—While Colorado Remains Abortion Sanctuary

A Texas court has intervened to protect an unborn child with a severe heart defect after a California couple allegedly sought to end the pregnancy. Surrogate McKenna West fled to Texas, where Attorney General Ken Paxton secured a legal victory ensuring the child—named Gabriel—received life-saving surgery after birth Wednesday (Fox News).

The case highlights the stark divide between states on abortion policy. Texas law protects unborn life; California and Colorado permit abortion through birth for any reason. Colorado has positioned itself as a sanctuary for abortion access, enshrining the practice in state law and rejecting restrictions that other states impose.

**Republicans and pro-life advocates argue** that cases like Gabriel's demonstrate why legal protections for unborn life matter—that judicial intervention can preserve viable pregnancies and save lives. They contend that abortion-on-demand policies, which Colorado embraces, eliminate such protections entirely.

**Democrats and abortion-rights supporters counter** that surrogate arrangements involve complex contractual and bodily-autonomy questions, and that Texas overreached by blocking parental choice in medical decisions.

The case underscores Colorado's commitment to unrestricted abortion access—a policy that will likely feature prominently in 2024 state races as Republicans push back against the state's extreme abortion stance.
