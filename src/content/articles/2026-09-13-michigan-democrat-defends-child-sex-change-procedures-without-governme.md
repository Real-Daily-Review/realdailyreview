# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Medicine Signals Broader Progressive Push

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's blunt defense of pediatric sex-change procedures—arguing doctors, not government, should decide—exposes the ideological gulf between progressives and conservatives on medical autonomy for minors.

Abdul El-Sayed, a physician running for U.S. Senate, told Michigan State University students Friday that his medical credentials qualify him to support unrestricted gender transition treatments for children without government oversight (Fox News, The Daily Wire). He argued physicians should have final say on the procedures.

**Republicans counter** that children cannot consent to irreversible medical interventions, and that parents—not doctors alone—should retain authority over minors' healthcare decisions. They argue progressive states have rushed to normalize puberty blockers and surgeries without long-term safety data, effectively conducting experiments on vulnerable youth.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't passed sweeping restrictions on pediatric gender medicine like some red states, the state's Democratic legislature has moved to protect gender transition access. El-Sayed's unapologetic stance signals national Democratic strategy to defend these procedures against parental and state oversight—a position that could influence Colorado policy debates and ballot measures.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt similar rhetoric defending pediatric gender medicine against parental consent requirements or age restrictions.
