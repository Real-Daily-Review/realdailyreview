# Nashville Airport Board Votes to Rename Hub After Dolly Parton

Nashville's airport authority unanimously approved renaming the city's international airport to honor late country music icon Dolly Parton, who died last month at 80 after a brief illness.

The Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority voted 6-0 to begin the renaming process (Washington Examiner), though the final official name will be determined later. Parton had previously declined the honor while alive, making the posthumous tribute a reversal of her earlier wishes.

The move marks a straightforward civic decision with broad support—no partisan divide or taxpayer cost dispute has emerged. Tennessee officials are moving forward with what amounts to a ceremonial gesture recognizing Parton's cultural significance and her deep roots in Nashville and East Tennessee.

For Colorado readers, this is a reminder that airport naming decisions remain local matters.
