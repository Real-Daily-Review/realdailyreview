# Federal Judge Blocks Biden Voter-Verification Tool; Trump Admin Pushes Back

A federal judge has struck down the Trump Administration's effort to strengthen voter verification through the SAVE program, handing a win to left-wing advocacy groups challenging the common-sense safeguard.

U.S. District Court Judge Sparkle Sooknanan sided with activists opposing Trump's executive order to expand the Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements tool—a program designed to cross-reference voter rolls against federal immigration databases. The move blocks what conservatives argue is a straightforward measure to prevent non-citizens from voting, a concern that resonates with Colorado Republicans who have long pushed for election integrity protections.

The Daily Wire reports the judge's decision halts the administration's upgrades to the verification system, which would have tightened connections between the Department of Homeland Security and Social Security Administration to flag ineligible voters.

**Republicans argue** the SAVE expansion represents basic election security—verifying citizenship before ballots are cast. **Democrats and progressive groups counter** that such measures disproportionately burden eligible voters and constitute unnecessary barriers to the ballot box.

Colorado conservatives, already frustrated by the state's mail-in voting system and ballot-harvesting rules, should watch whether the Trump Administration appeals this ruling or pursues alternative pathways to strengthen voter verification without judicial obstruction.
