# Denver-Area Crime Wave Continues as Police Scramble to Restore Order

Denver and Aurora are reeling from a surge in violent crime that exposes the failure of progressive law-and-order policies. Over recent days, the metro area has seen a hit-and-run that left a pedestrian seriously injured, a targeted shooting at an Aurora gas station that hospitalized two women, and arrests stemming from an August "unlawful gathering" that spiraled into chaos (KDVR Fox31).

The incidents underscore a troubling pattern: criminals operate with apparent impunity while police play catch-up. In the hit-and-run case, the driver stopped briefly before fleeing the scene—a callous disregard for consequences that mirrors the broader breakdown in accountability plaguing Colorado's urban centers (9News Denver). The Aurora gas station shooting, described as "targeted," suggests gang or criminal-network activity that residents increasingly fear.

Aurora police have made arrests and submitted warrants in the August takeover case, but conservatives argue the real problem runs deeper. Soft-on-crime policies, reduced prosecution, and bail reform have emboldened repeat offenders. Meanwhile, law-abiding Coloradans—especially in Denver and Aurora—face rising violence with diminishing confidence in government protection.

Republicans and taxpayers should watch whether District Attorney Beth McCann's office aggressively prosecutes these cases or continues the permissive approach that has made Colorado's cities less safe.
