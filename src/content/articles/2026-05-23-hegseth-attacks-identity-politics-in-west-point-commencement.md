# Defense Secretary Hegseth Attacks 'Identity Politics' in West Point Commencement

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth delivered a commencement address at West Point on Saturday, criticizing what he characterized as identity politics in the military under previous administrations. Speaking in heavy rain, Hegseth accused former leaders of being "foolish and feckless" for prioritizing diversity initiatives over military readiness, according to The Hill.

Hegseth praised the graduating cadets as "not an army of woke," the Washington Examiner reported, and made a pointed critique of diversity efforts, saying "you can't throw your pronouns at the enemy" and that "the battlefield does not grade on a curve." The remarks marked his first visit to the academy since joining the Trump administration.

The speech reflected Hegseth's stated focus on military effectiveness as the primary measure of institutional success. He did not elaborate on specific policies or initiatives he plans to reverse, nor did he provide details on how diversity programs have affected military readiness.

The address signals how the new Defense Department leadership intends to reshape military culture and priorities in its opening months.
