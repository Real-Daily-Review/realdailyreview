# Red-State Democrats' Open-Borders Talk Sparks Backlash Over American Workers

A Democratic candidate in a red state is facing fierce criticism for suggesting illegal immigrants should replace American workers—a revealing moment that exposes the left's true priorities on immigration and labor.

Hallie Shoffner, a Democrat, claimed farmers would "trade five Americans just for one" illegal immigrant worker, according to Fox News Politics. The remark drew sharp condemnation from Sen. Tom Cotton's campaign, which seized on the comment as evidence that Democrats view American workers as expendable.

The controversy underscores a core conservative argument: progressive politicians prioritize cheap labor and open-borders ideology over the interests of American citizens and legal workers. Republicans argue that mass illegal immigration depresses wages for working-class Americans and undermines labor market integrity—concerns that resonate across red states where agriculture and construction depend on workers.

Meanwhile, The Daily Wire reported that Democratic Texas Senate candidate James Talarico's longtime church has harbored illegal immigrants facing deportation orders, further illustrating how some Democratic-aligned institutions actively obstruct immigration enforcement.

For Colorado, where agriculture, construction, and energy sectors face workforce pressures, these statements matter: they signal how far the Democratic Party will go to avoid enforcing immigration law and protecting American workers' interests.
