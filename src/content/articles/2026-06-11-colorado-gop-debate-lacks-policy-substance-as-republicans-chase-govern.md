# Colorado GOP Debate Offers Little Policy Substance as Republicans Seek to Dethrone Democrat Governor

Colorado Republicans finally faced off this week in a gubernatorial primary debate that exposed the party's struggle to articulate a coherent conservative agenda against Democratic incumbency.

The three GOP contenders shared a stage Tuesday in what the *Denver Post* described as "light on policy but full of questions and answers"—a diplomatic way of saying the debate lacked the hard specifics Colorado taxpayers need to hear. With Democrats maintaining their grip on the governor's mansion, Republicans have a narrow window to present a compelling alternative on taxes, energy policy, water rights, and regulatory overreach.

The debate's lack of policy depth matters. Colorado faces real challenges: progressive tax-and-spend policies, anti-energy regulations that threaten jobs in rural counties, and water scarcity driven partly by government mismanagement. Voters deserve to know which Republican candidate will actually fight to rein in state spending, protect Colorado's oil and gas industry, and defend Second Amendment rights—not just trade barbs on stage.

Meanwhile, House Democrats like Ilhan Omar and Sarah McBride are busy mocking Republican divisions (per *Fox News*), a reminder that the left smells blood in the water when the right can't get organized.
