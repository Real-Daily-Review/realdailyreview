# Small Plane Crashes on Texas Highway; Bystanders Save Five Passengers

A Cessna Citation Latitude business jet crashed on a busy Laredo, Texas highway Tuesday evening, and quick-thinking bystanders and first responders pulled five of six passengers from the burning wreckage.

Witnesses rushed to the fiery scene immediately after impact, working frantically to break open the windshield and pry open doors while the aircraft burned (Breitbart). Video posted on social media captured the chaotic rescue effort as motorists and emergency personnel coordinated to extract survivors from the twin-engine jet engulfed in flames moments after the crash (9News Denver).

One passenger did not survive the accident. Rescuers attempted to free those trapped inside, with bystanders and first responders working in tandem to reach occupants before the fire spread further (Just The News).

The incident underscores both the dangers of general aviation and the value of alert citizens willing to act in emergencies—a reminder that sometimes the fastest help comes not from government, but from neighbors stepping up when seconds count.

Watch for federal aviation authorities' investigation into the cause of the crash and whether any mechanical or pilot-error factors emerge.
