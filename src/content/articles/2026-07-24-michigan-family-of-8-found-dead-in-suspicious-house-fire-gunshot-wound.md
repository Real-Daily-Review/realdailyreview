# Michigan Family Deaths Under Investigation; Foul Play Suspected

A Michigan sheriff's office is investigating the deaths of eight people—two adults and six children—found after a house fire, with some victims bearing gunshot wounds that suggest foul play rather than accident.

Firefighters had responded to the area earlier that morning after reports of smoke between 8:30 a.m. and 9 a.m., but were unable to locate the source at that time, according to the New York Post. The subsequent discovery of the family—dead in what authorities are treating as a "suspicious" incident—has launched a full criminal investigation.
