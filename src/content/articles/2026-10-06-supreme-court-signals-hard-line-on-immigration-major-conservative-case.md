# Supreme Court Takes Hard Line on Immigration; Major Cases Loom Over Gun Rights, Religious Liberty

The Supreme Court is signaling it won't tolerate judicial activism on immigration—a sharp rebuke to progressive judges who've blocked Trump administration enforcement efforts (National Review). The Court's new term opens with blockbuster cases on AR-15 bans, religious liberty, election rules, and climate lawsuits, with the justices facing political pressure from both sides (The Daily Wire).

For Colorado conservatives, the stakes are clear. A ruling that strips lower courts of immigration jurisdiction could empower federal enforcement in sanctuary-friendly jurisdictions—critical for states tired of illegal immigration's fiscal and security costs. The AR-15 case directly threatens Second Amendment rights for Colorado gun owners, who've already endured magazine-capacity restrictions under state law. Religious liberty rulings could protect Colorado faith-based organizations from progressive overreach on hiring and conscience protections.

Democrats are threatening Court reforms—code for court-packing—if rulings don't go their way. Trump has publicly criticized his own appointees, adding noise to the bench's independence. The midterms could reshape the political pressure the Court faces.

**Watch whether the Court actually follows through on limiting lower-court activism, or whether political pressure causes justices to hedge.**
