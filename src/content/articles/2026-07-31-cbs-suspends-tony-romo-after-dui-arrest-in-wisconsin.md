# Tony Romo Benched: CBS Suspends Star Analyst After DUI Arrest

CBS Sports has sidelined its lead NFL analyst Tony Romo following his arrest on suspicion of operating a vehicle while under the influence in Wisconsin last week, according to reports from 9News Denver and The Daily Wire. Police say Romo performed poorly on roadside sobriety tests before being taken into custody, booked, and released.

The network announced Friday that Romo is on leave "until further notice," though CBS has not specified a timeline for his return or whether his future at the network remains secure. 9News reports that JJ Watt has been announced as his replacement.
