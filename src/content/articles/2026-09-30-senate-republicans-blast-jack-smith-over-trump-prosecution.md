# Senate Republicans Grill Jack Smith Over Alleged Political Prosecution of Trump

Senate Republicans used a high-profile hearing to hammer Special Counsel Jack Smith over what they argue was a weaponized prosecution dressed up as criminal justice. Smith's investigations into Trump—spanning classified documents, 2020 election challenges, and January 6—resulted in felony indictments that were abandoned after Trump's 2024 election victory, Republicans note, raising questions about the probe's true motivation.

"This looks like politics, not law enforcement," the GOP argument runs. Lawmakers split sharply along party lines throughout the hearing, with Republicans pressing Smith on why cases collapsed once Trump regained power, while Democrats defended the investigations as legitimate.

The hearing underscores a broader conservative concern: that the Justice Department under the Biden administration weaponized federal power against political opponents. For Colorado Republicans and taxpayers, the spectacle reinforces skepticism about government institutions and their impartiality—a sentiment that resonates in a state where federal overreach on energy, water, and land-use policy already fuels distrust.

Smith's testimony offered no new evidence that the investigations were anything but what Republicans suspected: a coordinated effort to damage Trump politically before the election.

Watch whether congressional Republicans push for DOJ reforms or inspector general investigations into Smith's conduct and funding.
