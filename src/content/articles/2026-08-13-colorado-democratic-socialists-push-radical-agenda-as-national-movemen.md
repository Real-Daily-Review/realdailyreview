# Colorado's Democratic Socialists Push Radical Agenda While National Movement Stumbles

Colorado's Democratic Socialist Alliance is channeling hard-left ideology that echoes Bolshevik revolution rhetoric, even as the DSA movement suffers embarrassing defeats nationwide.

Complete Colorado reports that state DSA activists are adopting "vengeful dogma" reminiscent of the Russian Revolution's class-warfare narrative. Meanwhile, the national DSA brand took a hit in Wisconsin, where radical chef-turned-candidate Francesca Hong lost her gubernatorial primary bid to Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley—a stunning rebuke to the far-left insurgency (National Review).

Democrats are pushing back on the "democratic socialist" label. Rep. Mark Pocan (D-WI) claimed on Bloomberg that Republicans misuse the term and "don't understand definitions" (Breitbart). Republicans counter that the DSA's actual platform—wealth redistribution, government control of production, and class-based grievance politics—matches the label precisely, regardless of semantic games.

**The Colorado angle:** State DSA chapters are gaining influence in Denver and Boulder politics, pushing for rent control, wealth taxes, and aggressive land-use restrictions that would hammer Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small business. Their ideology directly threatens Colorado's energy independence and property rights.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado DSA candidates gain traction in 2026 state races—and whether Republican candidates will clearly articulate the cost of their agenda to Colorado taxpayers and workers.
