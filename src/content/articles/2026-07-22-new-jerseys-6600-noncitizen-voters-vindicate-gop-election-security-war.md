# New Jersey Noncitizen Voter Scandal Exposes Election Security Gaps

A software glitch at New Jersey's Motor Vehicle Commission registered roughly 6,600 noncitizens to vote—and about 400 of them actually cast ballots—triggering a DOJ crackdown and GOP demands for an overhaul of automatic voter registration systems.

The Trump administration's Justice Department, led by Assistant Attorney General Harmeet Dhillon, gave Democratic Gov. Mikie Sherrill five days to hand over unredacted voter data as part of an escalating lawsuit (Fox News, Washington Examiner). Sherrill initially blamed a tech company for the error, but the vendor pinned responsibility back on the state, undermining the governor's damage-control effort.

Republicans erupted over the disclosure, calling the scope "worse than anyone expected" and demanding an immediate halt to automatic voter registration through the MVC (Fox News). The scandal vindicates conservative warnings that lax registration procedures invite fraud and compromise ballot integrity—concerns Democrats have long dismissed as overblown.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado operates automatic voter registration through the DMV and relies on software systems to verify citizenship. If New Jersey's glitch exposed 6,600 bad registrations, Colorado taxpayers should demand a full audit of our own voter rolls to ensure noncitizens aren't registered here.

Watch for whether Colorado Democrats resist similar DOJ scrutiny of our voter database.
