# Republicans Invoke Founding Principles as America Marks 250 Years

As America approaches its 250th birthday, Republicans are doubling down on the Declaration of Independence as a cudgel against progressive overreach. Rep. Matt Van Epps unveiled legislation reaffirming the Declaration, explicitly framing it as a response to socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primaries (Fox News).

The conservative movement is mining the Founders' economic grievances for contemporary relevance. Breitbart notes that the Declaration's core complaints—excessive taxation, unchecked government growth, and loss of economic control—remain the animating force of America First economics today. That's a direct indictment of the Biden-Harris spending agenda and regulatory state expansion that has hammered Colorado energy workers and small businesses.

Meanwhile, celebrations are underway: a Freedom Train rolled through D.C., and classic American icons gathered at Union Station (Daily Caller). The National Review highlighted Jefferson's Monticello as a scholarly center keeping the Founders' vision alive.

Republicans see the 250th as a moment to reclaim the Declaration from progressive reinterpretation. The Founders' warnings about unchecked government power and confiscatory taxation speak directly to Colorado conservatives' fight against state spending bloat and federal overreach on energy and water policy.

Watch whether Democrats attempt their own 250th messaging—or cede the Founders entirely to the right.
