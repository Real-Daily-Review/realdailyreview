# Teachers Unions Push DEI Into AI Classroom Tools—Colorado Schools in the Crosshairs

America's largest teachers unions are quietly embedding diversity, equity, and inclusion mandates into artificial intelligence guidance for classrooms, according to a new report cited by Fox News and the New York Post. The effort aims to shape how schools adopt AI technology through "equity frameworks" and algorithmic bias protocols—language conservatives recognize as ideological gatekeeping.

Colorado school districts, already grappling with parental backlash over progressive curriculum choices, could face pressure to adopt these DEI-laden AI systems. Republicans argue this represents another attempt by union leadership to prioritize identity politics over academic rigor and student achievement. Rather than letting teachers and administrators choose AI tools based on educational merit and cost-effectiveness, unions are pre-loading them with progressive assumptions about "algorithmic bias" that may limit how the technology actually functions in classrooms.

Conservatives say this is precisely the kind of institutional capture that wastes taxpayer dollars and distracts from core instruction. Colorado parents and school board members should demand transparency: Which AI vendors are being recommended? What exactly do these "equity frameworks" require? And who decided unions—not elected school boards—should dictate technology policy?

Watch whether Colorado's Republican-led legislature moves to require school districts to disclose DEI requirements embedded in any AI contracts or vendor agreements.
