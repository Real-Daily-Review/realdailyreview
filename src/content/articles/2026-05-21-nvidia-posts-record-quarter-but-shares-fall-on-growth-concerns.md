# Nvidia's Record Quarter Fails to Lift Stock as Growth Concerns Mount

Nvidia reported another record revenue quarter after market close Wednesday, yet shares fell in after-hours trading as investors grew skeptical about the chip giant's ability to sustain its growth trajectory, according to the BBC.

The company announced record results and lifted its dividend, according to the Financial Times, but also forecast that revenue growth would slow in the following quarter, according to TechCrunch. The slowdown forecast appears to have triggered the market's lukewarm response despite the better-than-expected figures.

Nvidia also revealed $43 billion in holdings in startups, TechCrunch reported, underscoring the company's broad bets across the artificial intelligence ecosystem.

The sell-off highlights a shift in investor sentiment around the world's most valuable company. While Nvidia continues to post stellar results, Wall Street's focus has shifted from absolute performance to whether the firm can maintain its explosive growth amid intensifying competition in the chip market.

Watch for Nvidia's next earnings call and any commentary on competitive pressures and the sustainability of its AI-driven revenue growth.
