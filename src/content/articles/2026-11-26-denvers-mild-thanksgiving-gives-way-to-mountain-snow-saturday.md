# Denver Set for Mild Thanksgiving Before Weekend Snow

Colorado's Front Range will enjoy unseasonably warm weather through Thanksgiving Day—potentially the mildest in eight years—before a winter storm brings snow to the mountains and Front Range on Saturday, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

Thursday's forecast calls for dry conditions and above-normal temperatures across the Denver metro area, making it an ideal day for outdoor holiday gatherings. The pleasant stretch won't last: a significant weather system moves in over the weekend, with mountain snow expected and potential precipitation reaching lower elevations by Saturday afternoon and evening.

Coloradans taking advantage of the mild conditions Thursday should prepare for the abrupt shift. Those traveling over the holiday weekend should monitor forecasts closely, as mountain passes could see accumulation and reduced visibility.

Meanwhile, community organizations like the Denver Rescue Mission continued their tradition of serving Thanksgiving meals to those in need, hosting its annual banquet Wednesday ahead of the holiday.

Watch for updated forecasts Friday morning to confirm weekend snow timing and accumulation amounts for your area.
