# House Passes $70B Immigration Enforcement Bill Over Unified Democratic Opposition

Republicans muscled through a $70 billion immigration enforcement package that funds ICE and Border Patrol through 2029, delivering Trump a major border-security win despite every Democrat voting no.

The **Secure America Act** cleared the House 214-212 after a dramatic last-minute switch by Rep. Tim Walberg (R-Mich.), who flipped from no to yes on the final vote (Just The News). The measure now heads to the Senate, where Democrats have vowed unified opposition.

Republicans argue the funding is essential to sustain interior enforcement operations and border security infrastructure through the end of Trump's presidency. Conservatives say sustained ICE resources are critical to enforcing immigration law and protecting American workers—particularly in Colorado, where illegal immigration strains local services and competes with citizens for jobs.

Democrats unanimously opposed the bill, framing it as excessive spending on enforcement. The Left argues resources should instead fund asylum processing and humanitarian services.

The narrow margin—just two votes—underscores how fragile Republican control remains in the House. One GOP defection could have killed the bill outright.

**Watch whether the Senate takes up the measure before recess and whether any moderate Democrats peel off to support it.**
