# Trump to Deliver 'Historic' America 250 Speech as Left Uses Milestone to Attack Border Security

President Trump will deliver a major speech celebrating America's 250th anniversary on the National Mall, promising to highlight American heroes and resilience despite weather threats—but the left is using the occasion to attack immigration enforcement and free enterprise.

Trump vowed Saturday to deliver the address regardless of thunderstorm warnings, telling supporters on Truth Social that "storms bring luck to whatever the occasion" (Washington Examiner). The speech will precede a massive fireworks display marking the nation's founding.

Meanwhile, progressive figures are hijacking the America 250 celebration for their own agenda. New York City socialist Zohran Mamdani delivered a speech painting America in dark terms, blasting ICE agents, Elon Musk, and what he called "supremacy" in the country (Fox News). The Daily Wire reports Mamdani described America's founding in sinister language, inverting the nation's immigrant heritage into a critique of current border enforcement.

Pope Leo XIV also weighed in virtually, accepting the 2026 Liberty Medal while calling for the U.S. to welcome more immigrants—before departing to visit Lampedusa, a European migrant hotspot (Fox News).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that celebrating American achievement and enforcing immigration law are entirely compatible—and that the left's attempt to reframe border security as un-American on the nation's birthday reveals their true priorities.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado's congressional delegation embraces Trump's patriotic message or aligns with Mamdani's anti-enforcement rhetoric.
