# Karmelo Anthony Convicted, Sentenced to 35 Years—But Media Narrative Crumbles Fast

A Texas jury has convicted 19-year-old Karmelo Anthony of murder in the death of 17-year-old Austin Metcalf, sentencing him to 35 years in prison—but the case reveals how quickly progressive activists and sympathetic media outlets abandon inconvenient stories when the facts don't fit their preferred narrative.

The presiding judge defended both the conviction and his decision to ban courtroom cameras, telling the *New York Post* he "sleeps well at night" with the verdict. Yet Anthony's parents have launched a coordinated media campaign claiming their son acted in self-defense and that witnesses lied—assertions the jury rejected after hearing evidence.

Most telling: Anthony's family raised over $635,000 through crowdfunding for his legal defense, yet he now claims poverty and requests a court-appointed attorney from prison (*Daily Wire*). The *Daily Wire* notes the mainstream press has largely moved on, no longer finding the case useful for activism.

As former ESPN commentator Jemele Hill observed (*Daily Caller*), this case offers "a lesson to us" about rushing to judgment based on incomplete information and identity politics rather than facts.

Coloradans should watch whether activist groups that amplified Anthony's case without scrutiny face any accountability—or simply pivot to the next cause.
