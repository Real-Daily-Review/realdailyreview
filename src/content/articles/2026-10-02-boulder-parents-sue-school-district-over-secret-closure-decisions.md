# Boulder Parents Sue Over Secret School Closures—Another Win for Government Accountability

Boulder Valley School District parents are fighting back against what they call closed-door decision-making on school closures, filing suit over the board's handling of enrollment decline. According to CPR News, the lawsuit challenges the district's process for deciding which schools to shutter, alleging the board bypassed proper transparency and community input.

The closure plan addresses real enrollment drops, but conservatives and parents argue that *how* the district made these decisions matters as much as *what* they decided. Government agencies—including school boards—must operate in the open, not in backrooms. When districts make major decisions affecting families and property values without genuine public deliberation, they erode trust and invite legal challenge.

This reflects a broader pattern: parents nationwide are demanding a seat at the table on school governance. From curriculum choices to facility decisions, families are rejecting the notion that bureaucrats know best. Colorado parents should take note—school board transparency and parental involvement aren't partisan issues; they're foundational to accountable government.

Watch whether BVSD settles quietly or whether this lawsuit forces the district to restart its closure process with real community input.
