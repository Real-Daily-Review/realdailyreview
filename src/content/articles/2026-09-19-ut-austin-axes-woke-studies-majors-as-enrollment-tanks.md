# UT Austin Dumps Woke Studies Programs as Enrollment Collapses—A Sign Conservative Pushback Works

The University of Texas at Austin is eliminating women's studies, gender studies, and ethnic studies majors due to low enrollment and political pressure from Texas state leaders, signaling that ideological academic bloat cannot survive scrutiny (Fox News).

This move reflects growing conservative momentum against what critics call "leftist delusions" embedded in higher education. Republicans and state officials have long argued these programs represent wasteful spending on ideological indoctrination rather than rigorous scholarship—a position gaining traction as enrollment numbers tell the story themselves.

The decision matters for Colorado. State universities here face similar pressure to justify sprawling DEI bureaucracies and identity-focused curricula as taxpayers demand accountability. Colorado's Republican-led legislature has already begun questioning university spending on diversity initiatives; UT Austin's move provides a template for how enrollment data and political will can force institutional change without litigation.

Democrats typically defend such programs as essential for inclusive education and underrepresented student voices, though they rarely address the enrollment problem directly.

**Watch whether Colorado's Board of Education and university regents follow Texas's lead in trimming low-enrollment ideological programs—or double down on progressive priorities despite the fiscal case against them.**
