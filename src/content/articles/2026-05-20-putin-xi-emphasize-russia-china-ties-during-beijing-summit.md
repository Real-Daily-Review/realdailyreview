# Putin, Xi Cement Russia-China Ties as Trump Courts Beijing

Vladimir Putin arrived in Beijing for talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping, with both leaders emphasizing deepening cooperation between their nations. Xi welcomed Putin and the two discussed strengthening energy and technology partnerships, according to Al Jazeera English.

The summit comes days after Donald Trump visited Beijing, underscoring competing efforts to secure Chinese alignment. Xi used the occasion to warn against what he called the "law of the jungle" in international affairs, according to the Financial Times, an apparent critique of unilateral power dynamics.

Putin characterized the Russia-China relationship as having "unshakable foundations," signaling resolve in their partnership amid broader geopolitical tensions. The leaders' focus on energy and technology cooperation reflects efforts to deepen economic ties independent of Western markets.

The timing of the Putin visit—following Trump's Beijing diplomacy—suggests both Russia and the U.S. are competing for Chinese support and cooperation on key strategic issues. Watch for announcements on specific energy deals or technology agreements that may emerge from the summit.
