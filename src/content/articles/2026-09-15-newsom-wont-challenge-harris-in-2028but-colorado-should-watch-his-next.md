# Newsom Clears 2028 Field for Harris—But Colorado Conservatives Should Remain Skeptical

California Gov. Gavin Newsom announced Monday he will not run for president in 2028 if Kamala Harris enters the race, telling CNN's Jake Tapper that a primary battle would be "mutual assured destruction" and a "gift" to Republicans (Just The News). Tapper reported being "stunned" by the answer; Newsom added, "Why would I? I wouldn't waste everyone's time" (The Daily Wire).

The move appears calculated. By deferring to Harris now, Newsom positions himself as a loyal Democrat while keeping his own 2028 options open should Harris decline to run. Conservative observers note the political theater: Newsom, who has positioned himself as a national Democratic leader, is essentially betting Harris won't be viable by 2028—or that he'll be better positioned to run if she stumbles.

**Why Colorado matters:** California's regulatory and spending model has influenced progressive policies creeping into Colorado—from energy mandates to housing restrictions. Newsom's national profile amplifies those ideas. Conservatives should watch whether he uses the 2028 cycle to push climate and gun-control agendas that could reach Colorado Democrats.

Watch whether Harris actually runs in 2028 and whether Newsom honors this pledge or finds a loophole.
