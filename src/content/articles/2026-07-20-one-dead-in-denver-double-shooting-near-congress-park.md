# Denver Shooting Near Congress Park Leaves One Dead

Denver's crime problem struck again Sunday morning when a double shooting near Congress Park left one person dead, according to reports from 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31. The incident occurred in the 1100-block of Columbine Street, just a block from the popular residential area.

Details remain limited as police continue their investigation. No suspect information has been released, and authorities have not disclosed a motive.

The shooting underscores a persistent challenge for Denver's progressive leadership: violent crime continues despite years of increased police funding debates and social programs. While Democrats argue that root causes like poverty and mental health require more government spending, Republicans counter that Denver needs stronger law enforcement presence, swift prosecution, and an end to soft-on-crime policies that have emboldened criminals.

For Colorado gun owners, this incident highlights why Second Amendment rights matter—law-abiding citizens must be able to defend themselves when government fails to maintain public safety. Denver's homicide rate remains a serious concern for residents and businesses alike.
