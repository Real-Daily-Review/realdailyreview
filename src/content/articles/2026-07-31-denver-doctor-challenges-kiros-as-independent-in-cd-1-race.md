# Independent Doctor Challenges Kiros in CD-1 Race

A Denver physician just scrambled Colorado's 1st Congressional District by qualifying as an independent challenger to Democrat Melat Kiros—signaling real fractures in the left's own backyard.

Dr. Shimon Blau, a Jewish doctor, filed to run against Kiros after the Democrat's primary victory last month, telling the *Denver Post* he was "saddened and alarmed" by her win. Complete Colorado reports that Blau's entry gives voters uncomfortable with Kiros' "antisemitism or 'democratic socialism' policy positions" a third option in November.

Kiros, who won the Democratic primary, now faces a two-front battle: holding the district against Republican opposition while managing an independent challenger who's explicitly running to her left on some issues and directly against her record on others. Blau's entry suggests the progressive wing of Colorado Democrats isn't monolithic—and that concerns about Kiros' rhetoric and far-left economic positions resonate even among Democratic-leaning voters.

For conservatives, this is a reminder that Colorado's 1st District remains competitive. A fractured Democratic base could hand Republicans an opening, or Blau could simply split the anti-Kiros vote. Either way, Republicans should monitor whether Blau gains traction and what that tells them about voter appetite for moderate alternatives to progressive Democrats.

Watch whether Blau's independent campaign gains funding and endorsements—it'll signal whether mainstream Democrats view Kiros as a liability.
