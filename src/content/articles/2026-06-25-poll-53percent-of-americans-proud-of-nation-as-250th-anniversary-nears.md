# America at 250: Majority Sees Pride in Past, Hope for Future

A solid majority of Americans remain optimistic about the nation's trajectory as it approaches its 250th anniversary, according to a Fox News poll, offering a counterweight to relentless progressive narratives of national decline and systemic failure.

The survey found 53% of voters express pride in America, while 54% believe the nation's best days lie ahead. President Trump echoed that sentiment, framing the milestone as "a time to be proud of our past" while calling Americans to "expand our ambitions, and raise our expectations of what America can be" (Breitbart).

For Colorado conservatives, this polling matters. While progressive activists and Democratic politicians have spent years apologizing for American history and pushing divisive identity politics, most voters—including many in purple Colorado—still believe in American exceptionalism and forward momentum. That's the silent majority that wins elections.

The data suggests appetite for a patriotic, forward-looking agenda centered on growth, freedom, and national strength rather than guilt-driven social engineering. It's a reminder that Americans are fundamentally optimistic when given permission to be.
