# Starbucks Shutters 250 Stores as CEO Niccol Tightens Operations

Corporate America is finally reckoning with the cost of woke management and bloated operations—and Starbucks is the latest casualty. CEO Brian Niccol, who took the helm in 2024, is closing 250 stores nationwide this week as part of a broader restructuring that already included 300 corporate layoffs in May (Just The News). The closures represent the second major round of cuts under Niccol's watch, signaling that even the left's favorite coffee chain can't ignore the math of unsustainable labor costs and declining foot traffic.

For Colorado consumers and workers, this is a reminder that progressive labor policies and activist management have real consequences. Starbucks' struggles—driven partly by unionization pushes and demands for higher wages—illustrate why businesses must prioritize efficiency over ideology. When companies prioritize social messaging over profitability, shareholders and employees pay the price.

The chain's troubles also reflect broader economic headwinds: consumer spending is tightening, and retailers are cutting excess capacity. Colorado's retail and hospitality sectors should take note: the era of unlimited growth fueled by cheap money and government stimulus is over.

Watch whether Starbucks' restructuring accelerates or stabilizes—and whether other major retailers follow suit with their own painful corrections.
