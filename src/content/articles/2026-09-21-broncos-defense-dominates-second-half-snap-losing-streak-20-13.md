# Broncos Defense Stiffens in Second Half, Snap Losing Streak With 20-13 Win Over Jaguars

Denver's defense finally showed up when it mattered most, shutting out Jacksonville in the second half to fuel a comeback victory that snapped a nine-game losing streak (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

Bo Nix bounced back from a first-half end zone interception to lead the rally, connecting with receiver Jaylen Waddle and rookie running back Jonah Coleman as the Broncos dominated the final 24 minutes. The Jacksonville offense managed just three points after halftime—none in the fourth quarter—a stark contrast to Week 1's tackling woes that had plagued the defense early in the season (KDVR Fox31).

The 20-13 home victory at Empower Field marks a critical turning point for a team that had been hemorrhaging games. Nix's second-half TD toss to tight end Adam Adkins proved the difference-maker, showing the rookie quarterback can execute under pressure when his defense tightens the screws (9News Denver).

For Colorado fans starved for winning football, this is the kind of defensive performance that builds momentum heading into the season's meat grinder. Watch whether Denver can sustain this defensive intensity—and whether Nix's hot second-half play signals he's ready to lead a legitimate playoff push.
