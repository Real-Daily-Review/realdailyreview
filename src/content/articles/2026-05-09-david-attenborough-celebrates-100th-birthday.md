# David Attenborough Turns 100, Celebrated Across Britain

Naturalist David Attenborough is marking his 100th birthday, with the BBC hosting a celebration at the Royal Albert Hall and cinemas screening his nature films, according to PBS NewsHour and Al Jazeera English.

The British natural historian, known for his distinctive hushed and excited narration on wildlife documentaries, has become a cultural icon spanning decades of broadcasting. Despite the widespread accolades and formal tributes, Attenborough has maintained a consistent philosophy: the animals, not the presenter, should be the stars of nature programming.

The milestone reflects both his longevity and his outsized influence on how generations have experienced the natural world through television. His work has shaped public understanding of wildlife and ecosystems globally.
