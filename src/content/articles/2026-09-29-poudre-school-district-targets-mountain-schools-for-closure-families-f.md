# Mountain Families Fight Poudre School District's Consolidation Plan

The Poudre School District's recommendation to close or consolidate eleven schools—including three mountain campuses—is sparking fierce pushback from families who say the district is abandoning rural communities without a real plan.

The closures would hit mountain families hardest, forcing longer commutes and disrupting tight-knit school communities. Parents argue the district hasn't adequately explained the financial rationale or explored alternatives like efficiency reforms or administrative cuts before targeting schools serving remote areas.

Conservatives and taxpayers are asking the obvious question: *Why are school administrators recommending school closures instead of scrutinizing their own bloated central office budgets?* Rural Colorado families already face geographic challenges; closing their schools compounds the problem and signals that district leadership prioritizes consolidation over serving all students equitably.

The district has not released detailed financial data justifying the closures, which fuels suspicion that this is a top-down restructuring driven by bureaucratic convenience rather than educational necessity. Mountain communities deserve transparency and a seat at the table—not a fait accompli.

Watch whether the Poudre school board actually listens to parent testimony or rubber-stamps administration recommendations, and whether local media demands the district release full financial justifications for each closure.
