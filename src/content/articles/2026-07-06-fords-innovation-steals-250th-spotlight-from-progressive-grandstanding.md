# Ford's Heritage Vault Steals Show at America's 250th While Progressive Politicians Grandstand

While Ford Motor Company brought genuine American innovation to the nation's capital with a free Union Station exhibit featuring 10 historic vehicles—including a Model T and a 1934 pickup on loan from Jay Leno—progressive politicians used the milestone to lecture Americans about capitalism rather than celebrate it.

The two-week Ford tribute showcased real American achievement: the vehicles that built the middle class and transformed a nation. Meanwhile, according to *The Daily Wire*, New York City's Democratic socialist Mayor Zohran Mamdani used America's 250th birthday to criticize capitalism, ICE, and what he called an "arena of supremacy"—even as his wife, Rama Duwaji, skipped the celebration entirely for a luxury Islamic spiritual retreat in Spain.

**The conservative take:** This contrast captures the left's fundamental disconnect. While American companies celebrate genuine innovation and free enterprise, progressive leaders attack the very system that created the wealth funding their lifestyles. Mamdani's critique of capitalism rings hollow when his family enjoys the fruits of the capitalist economy he denounces.

For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: trust American business and ingenuity, not government lectures about systemic failure.

Watch whether Colorado's progressive politicians follow Mamdani's playbook during upcoming state celebrations—or actually celebrate American achievement.
