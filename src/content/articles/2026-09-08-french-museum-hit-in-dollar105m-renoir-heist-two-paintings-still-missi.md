# Art Heist on French Riviera Has No Colorado Angle—But Here's Why We're Running It Anyway

A pair of thieves made off with four Renoir paintings worth $10.5 million from a museum on the French Riviera Tuesday, abandoning two of the works as they fled, according to 9News Denver and the Washington Examiner.

Police say the perpetrators were "well-equipped" and "well-informed," entering the Renoir Museum in Cagnes-sur-Mer through the garden. Two paintings remain missing as authorities search for the suspects.

**Why conservatives should care:** This isn't a Colorado story, but it's a reminder that even in wealthy Western democracies, law and order matter. France's inability to prevent a brazen daytime heist from a public institution reflects broader failures in security and accountability—problems that plague government agencies everywhere, including here at home. When government can't protect what it's supposed to protect, taxpayers foot the bill for failures and expansions of bureaucratic "solutions."

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state and local law enforcement agencies use this incident to justify budget increases or new surveillance programs without demonstrable need or results.
