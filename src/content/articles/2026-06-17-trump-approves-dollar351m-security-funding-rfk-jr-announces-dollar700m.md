# Trump Admin Allocates $351M for White House Security as RFK Jr. Announces $700M Addiction Initiative

The Trump administration has approved $351.6 million for Secret Service White House security measures, according to Fox News, even as questions linger over separate funding for a proposed East Wing ballroom renovation.

Meanwhile, Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. announced $700 million in federal spending to combat addiction and mental illness, which the administration says can contribute to homelessness. Kennedy made the announcement Wednesday at an Easterseals behavioral health clinic in Clinton Township, Michigan (Washington Examiner).

The security allocation underscores ongoing debate over White House capital projects and their costs. The ballroom proposal—part of Trump's broader renovation vision—has drawn scrutiny over budgeting and priorities.

Kennedy's addiction initiative represents a significant federal commitment to behavioral health, though conservatives will want clarity on how these dollars flow, whether they fund treatment infrastructure or expand government bureaucracy, and whether outcomes justify the spending. Colorado taxpayers should monitor whether these federal health dollars come with strings attached that expand state regulatory burdens or create unfunded mandates.
