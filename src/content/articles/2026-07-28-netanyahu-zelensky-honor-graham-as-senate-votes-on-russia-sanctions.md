# World Leaders Honor Lindsey Graham as Senate Prepares Final Vote on Russia Sanctions

Sen. Lindsey Graham's sudden death has drawn Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky to Washington, underscoring the South Carolina Republican's outsized influence on foreign policy and military aid (9News Denver). Both leaders plan to meet with President Trump during their visit for Graham's National Cathedral funeral this week.

The timing carries legislative weight: Graham's Russia sanctions bill faces a critical Senate vote Tuesday night, hours after his funeral concludes (Fox News). The measure reflects Graham's long-standing hawkish stance on Russia and his advocacy for robust military support to Ukraine and Israel—positions that defined his final years in the chamber.

Graham's death marks the end of an era in Republican foreign policy. His legacy centers on bipartisan support for military aid abroad and aggressive sanctions regimes, stances that increasingly divide the GOP between traditional interventionists and Trump-aligned skeptics of open-ended commitments overseas.

For Colorado conservatives, Graham's passing removes a reliable voice for defense spending and military-industrial priorities in the Senate. Watch whether his Russia sanctions bill passes and whether the incoming Senate leadership maintains or reverses his hawkish foreign-aid framework.
