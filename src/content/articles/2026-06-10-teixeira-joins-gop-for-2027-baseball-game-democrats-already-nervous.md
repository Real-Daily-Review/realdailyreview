# Democrats Nervous as Ex-MLB Star Teixeira Joins GOP Roster for 2027 Congressional Baseball Game

Democrats are already sweating the 2027 Congressional Baseball Game after learning that former MLB All-Star Mark Teixeira will suit up for the Republican team, according to the Daily Caller. The annual matchup between House and Senate Democrats and Republicans has been a Washington fixture since 1909, raising millions for local charities while giving lawmakers a chance to settle scores on the diamond rather than the House floor.

Republicans see the addition of Teixeira—a career .295 hitter with 409 home runs—as a significant talent upgrade that could tip the competitive balance. Democrats, predictably, are concerned about the optics of facing a genuine baseball talent, the Daily Caller reports.

The game remains one of Washington's few genuinely bipartisan traditions, though it's become increasingly rare to find lawmakers willing to cross the aisle for anything these days. For Colorado conservatives, it's a reminder that even in a polarized capital, there's still room for friendly competition—and a chance for Republicans to prove they can win at something other than elections.

Watch whether either party uses the game's outcome as fodder for social media bragging rights or if cooler heads prevail and the focus stays on charity.
