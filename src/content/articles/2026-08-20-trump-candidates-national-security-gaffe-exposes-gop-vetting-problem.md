# Trump-Backed Candidate Stumbles on National Security; Exposes GOP Primary Weakness

A Trump-endorsed Republican Senate candidate in South Carolina admitted during debate she isn't "that informed on national security"—a gaffe that handed Democrats ammunition and raised questions about vetting in Trump's endorsement machine (Daily Caller). 

Darline Graham later doubled down on Fox News, claiming she was "just being honest" and that most Americans can't locate Taiwan on a map anyway (Breitbart). The deflection underscores a real problem: voters expect Senate candidates to command basic geopolitical literacy, especially when running as Trump loyalists who promise strength abroad.

Meanwhile, Florida Democrats are running Angie Nixon, a Democratic Socialist of America member, on a platform of abolishing ICE and granting "swift" amnesty to illegal aliens (Breitbart). Her candidacy reveals the Left's open-borders agenda—a contrast Republicans should exploit relentlessly.

**Colorado angle:** While this race unfolds in South Carolina and Florida, Colorado Republicans eyeing statewide office should take note. Voters here punish candidates who appear unprepared on core issues, whether national security or energy policy. The lesson: Trump endorsements don't substitute for competence.
