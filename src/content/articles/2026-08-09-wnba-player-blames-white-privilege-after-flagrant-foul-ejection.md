# WNBA Player's 'White Privilege' Claim After Ejection Sparks Backlash

A flagrant foul in a WNBA game has become the latest flashpoint in America's culture wars—and it didn't stay on the court.

Chicago Sky guard DiJonai Carrington was ejected Saturday after delivering a hard forearm strike to Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham during a breakaway layup. Cunningham hit the ground hard. Rather than accept the ejection, Carrington posted on social media citing "white privilege" as the reason for the call, according to *Daily Caller* and *Breitbart* reporting.

The incident drew enough attention that Florida's Attorney General took notice, *Breitbart* reports.

**The conservative take:** This exemplifies how progressive identity politics has infected even professional sports. A player commits a flagrant foul—a clear violation of league rules—and instead of accountability, blames race. Conservatives argue this reflexive resort to racial grievance undermines personal responsibility and poisons civil discourse. The foul was objective; the response was ideological.

**What's at stake:** When athletes and public figures weaponize racial accusations to dodge consequences for their own conduct, it corrodes trust in institutions and cheapens legitimate discussions of fairness. Colorado sports fans should ask: Is this the culture we want in our entertainment?
