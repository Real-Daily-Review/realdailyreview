# New York's Reckless Police Purge Should Alarm Colorado Law-and-Order Voters

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has dismantled the NYPD's Strategic Response Group—an elite counterterrorism unit created in 2015 to handle terrorist attacks, violent protests, and civil unrest—signaling that progressive ideology now trumps public safety in America's largest city (Washington Examiner).

The move has drawn predictable lines. Far-left groups, including the Party for Socialism and Liberation, are celebrating the decision (Fox News), while Florida's Sen. Ashley Moody is actively recruiting displaced NYPD officers, recognizing the unit's value to law enforcement (Fox News).

**Why Colorado should care:** This reflects the same anti-police sentiment driving policy debates in Denver and Boulder. Progressive activists have successfully pressured cities nationwide to defund or disband specialized units, leaving communities vulnerable during civil unrest and violent crime spikes. Colorado conservatives argue that dismantling proven law-enforcement capabilities doesn't reduce crime—it emboldens it.

Republicans counter that elite police units are essential infrastructure, not symbols of oppression. The SRG's dissolution proves Mamdani's "fealty is to far-left ideologues rather than to New York City residents," as the Examiner notes. Colorado taxpayers should watch whether Denver's progressive city council moves to follow suit with similar police reductions.
