# Front Range Rail Tax: Colorado Voters Face Billion-Dollar Boondoggle Dressed Up as Progress

Colorado taxpayers are being asked to bankroll a Front Range rail project that even its boosters can't convince the public to support without spending millions on slick marketing campaigns.

The Colorado Connector—a proposed commuter rail system—is backed by a ballot measure that would tap billions in tax dollars. But rather than let the project stand on its merits, supporters have launched what CPR News calls a "controversial 'education' campaign" featuring paid Instagram influencers and targeted mailers funded by millions in tax dollars.

Complete Colorado's analysis identifies the core problem: voters are being offered a choice between "blowing billions on a train few will ride and keeping that money in their own pockets." That's the real trade-off here.

Conservatives argue the project exemplifies wasteful government spending dressed up as forward-thinking infrastructure. The money would be better left with Colorado families and businesses. Supporters counter that rail reduces congestion and serves the Front Range's growth—but their reliance on paid influencers to make that case suggests the pitch doesn't sell on substance alone.

This is a textbook example of how progressive mega-projects use taxpayer dollars to fund their own promotional machinery. Watch whether Colorado voters reject this tax grab or fall for the influencer campaign.
