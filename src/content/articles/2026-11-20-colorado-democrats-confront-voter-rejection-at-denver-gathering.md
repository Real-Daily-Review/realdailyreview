# Colorado Democrats Face Voter Backlash as National Party Reckons with Rejection

Colorado Democrats gathered in Denver this week confronting an uncomfortable truth: voters have decisively rejected their agenda. The gathering, which drew national Democratic figures for what the *Denver Post* called "a reality check," underscored deepening fractures within the party as it grapples with electoral losses and internal ideological warfare.

The tensions surfaced immediately during the first major congressional vote of the new session. According to *The Daily Wire*, Democratic Socialists of America lawmakers clashed with mainstream House Democrats over the speaker nomination, with DSA members facing intense constituent pressure—calls and emails flooding in since Election Day—signaling voter disapproval of the far-left faction's influence.

For Colorado conservatives, the message is clear: voters are rejecting progressive overreach. Colorado taxpayers have grown weary of Democratic spending priorities, regulatory expansion, and the party's lurch toward socialism. The internal Democratic civil war reflects a broader public rejection of the left's economic agenda—one that has hammered working families with inflation, energy costs, and job losses.

Republicans should capitalize on this moment by articulating a clear alternative: limited government, energy independence, and pro-growth policies that actually create jobs for Coloradans.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from the DSA wing or double down on progressive ideology heading into 2026.
