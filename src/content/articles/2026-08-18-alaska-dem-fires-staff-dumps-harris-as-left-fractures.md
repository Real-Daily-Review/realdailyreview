# Alaska Democrat Distances From Harris as Campaign Implodes

Alaska Democratic Senate candidate Mary Peltola fired campaign staff just days before Tuesday's primary—a dramatic move signaling internal chaos on the left. The shake-up came after Peltola publicly distanced herself from Vice President Kamala Harris, who had been fundraising for Peltola's PAC (Just The News).

The staff purge reflects deeper fractures within the Democratic coalition. Peltola's rejection of Harris support suggests the VP's political brand is toxic even to Democrats in competitive races—a warning sign for the Biden-Harris ticket heading into November.

Meanwhile, national Democratic groups are doubling down on abortion messaging. Planned Parenthood's super PAC is launching a historic ad blitz against Maine Sen. Susan Collins (R-ME), targeting her 2022 vote to confirm Supreme Court Justice Brett Kavanaugh (Washington Examiner). The spending underscores how abortion remains the left's primary midterm weapon, even as inflation and border chaos dominate voter concerns.
