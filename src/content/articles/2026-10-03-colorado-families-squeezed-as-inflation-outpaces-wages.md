# Colorado Families Feel the Squeeze as Inflation Outpaces Wages Nationwide

While Washington celebrates economic data, Colorado families are getting squeezed at the grocery store and gas pump—and they're not buying the "booming economy" narrative.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, the cost-of-living crisis is hitting hard across the country, with household wages failing to keep pace with inflation. The pain is real and bipartisan: whether you're Republican, Democrat, or independent, the checkout counter tells the true story of economic strain.

For Colorado specifically, this matters. Energy costs directly affect our state's working families and small businesses. Conservatives argue that inflationary pressures stem from government overspending and regulatory overreach that constrains energy production and supply chains—policies that disproportionately harm working Coloradans already struggling with housing costs and utility bills.

Republicans contend that the Biden-Harris administration's spending agenda and anti-energy regulations have fueled inflation, while Democrats argue structural factors and global supply disruptions are to blame. Either way, Colorado families are paying the price in real dollars.

Watch for how Colorado's 2024 races address cost-of-living relief and whether candidates commit to pro-growth, pro-energy policies that could ease inflationary pressure on working families.
