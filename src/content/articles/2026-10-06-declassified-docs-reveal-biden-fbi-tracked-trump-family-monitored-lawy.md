# Biden FBI's Secret Trump Surveillance Raises Abuse-of-Power Questions

Declassified records show the Biden-era FBI tracked Melania and Barron Trump's travel and monitored calls between Trump aide Susie Wiles and her lawyers while planning the Mar-a-Lago classified documents search—a stunning example of federal law enforcement weaponization that should alarm every American concerned about government overreach.

According to Fox News and the Daily Caller, the surveillance occurred during what the FBI characterized as a "secret probe." The monitoring of attorney-client communications is particularly troubling, as it breaches fundamental legal protections that shield confidential counsel from government intrusion.

Republicans argue this represents precisely the kind of political abuse of federal power that conservatives have long warned against. The targeting of a former president's family members and his advisors' legal communications suggests the Justice Department under Biden was willing to cross constitutional lines to pursue its agenda—a chilling precedent for any administration.

Democrats have not yet offered a substantive defense of the surveillance tactics, though they may argue the investigation was legally authorized. Conservatives counter that legal authorization does not excuse the *appearance* of political persecution or the breach of attorney-client privilege.

This case underscores why Colorado voters should demand accountability from federal agencies and support candidates committed to reining in the FBI's unchecked power.

Watch whether Congress launches a serious investigation into FBI conduct and whether any officials face consequences.
