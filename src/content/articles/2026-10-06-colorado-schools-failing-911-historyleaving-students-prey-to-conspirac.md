# American Schools Failing to Teach 9/11 Truth—and It's Not TikTok's Fault

Colorado classrooms are part of a national education crisis: schools are abandoning rigorous instruction on 9/11 history just as personal memory of the attacks fades and conspiracy theories gain ground among students.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, the real culprit isn't social media—it's institutional failure in our public schools. As the 25th anniversary of September 11 approaches, educators have largely abdicated responsibility for teaching fact-based, comprehensive accounts of the terrorist attacks and their consequences. This vacuum leaves students vulnerable to misinformation and revisionist narratives that flourish unchecked on platforms like TikTok.

Conservatives argue that schools have a fundamental duty to preserve historical truth and civic knowledge, especially on events that shaped American foreign policy, national security, and the post-9/11 legal framework. When curricula neglect rigorous history instruction, they fail students and weaken the nation's collective memory.

The problem runs deeper than curriculum gaps. It reflects a broader erosion of standards-based education and institutional commitment to factual accuracy—trends Colorado parents and taxpayers should scrutinize in their own school districts.

Watch whether Colorado school boards will commit to strengthening 9/11 education standards or continue defaulting to social media as the primary source of historical knowledge for the next generation.
