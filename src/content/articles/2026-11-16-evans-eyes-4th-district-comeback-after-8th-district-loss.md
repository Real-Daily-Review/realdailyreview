# Evans Seeks Redemption in Conservative 4th District After Losing Seat to Democrat

Rep. Gabe Evans is pivoting to Colorado's conservative 4th Congressional District for a 2028 comeback bid, just weeks after losing his 8th District seat to Democrat Manny Rutinel in what the *Denver Post* called a "blowout reelection loss."

The move comes as Republicans face headwinds in Colorado's purple districts. Evans's loss in the 8th mirrors a stunning upset in the 4th, where voters ousted conservative firebrand Lauren Boebert in favor of Democrat Eileen Laubacher, who campaigned as a "no drama moderate" (CPR News). The 4th District—anchored on the conservative Eastern Plains—represents more favorable terrain for a Republican comeback.

**The Conservative Angle:** Evans's strategy reflects Republican efforts to consolidate power in reliably conservative districts after losses in swing areas. The 4th's rural, energy-dependent base aligns with traditional GOP priorities: oil and gas development, Second Amendment rights, and limited federal overreach. Whether Evans can reclaim a seat in a district that just rejected Boebert's confrontational style remains an open question.

Democrats will likely argue the 4th's rejection of Boebert signals voter appetite for moderation over partisan theater—a framing conservatives reject as media spin obscuring Laubacher's left-leaning record.

**Watch:** Whether the 4th District remains competitive or swings back to Republicans in 2028 will signal whether Colorado's Eastern Plains are truly in play for Democrats or merely experiencing temporary volatility.
