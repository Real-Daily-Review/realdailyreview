# Jeffco Schools Caught Between Federal and State Law on Transgender Policies

Jefferson County Public Schools filed suit against the Trump administration this week, claiming federal rules on transgender student policies directly conflict with Colorado law—leaving the district trapped between two competing mandates.

The lawsuit asks a federal judge to resolve the collision. According to 9News Denver and CPR News, Jeffco argues it cannot simultaneously comply with both Trump administration directives and existing Colorado statutes governing student treatment.

**The conservative case:** Republicans have long opposed what they view as federal overreach on education policy. The Trump administration's directives reflect conservative concerns about parental rights, biological sex distinctions, and local control of schools. Conservatives argue Colorado's progressive transgender policies—which prioritize student preferences over parental notification—represent exactly the kind of ideological activism that should be decided locally, not imposed by either federal or state bureaucrats.

**The left's position:** Democrats and progressive advocates contend that Colorado's protections for transgender students reflect civil rights obligations and that federal rollbacks endanger vulnerable minors.

The core issue: Jeffco is essentially asking a judge to pick which government gets to dictate school policy—a lose-lose for taxpayers footing the legal bills either way.
