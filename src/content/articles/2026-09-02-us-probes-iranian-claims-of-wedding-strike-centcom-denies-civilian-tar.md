# U.S. Military Investigates Iranian Claims of Wedding Strike

The U.S. military is investigating reports from Iranian state media claiming an American airstrike Tuesday hit a wedding in southern Iran, killing four civilians—a claim CENTCOM has not substantiated and dismissed as propaganda.

Commander Tim Hawkins, U.S. Central Command spokesman, stated the military is "looking into" the reports while reaffirming that the U.S. military "never targets civilians," unlike Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (Washington Examiner). Just The News reported CENTCOM's denial, noting the stark contrast between American operational standards and Iranian state media's track record of disinformation.

The incident underscores the fog of war in the Middle East and the credibility gap between U.S. military statements and Tehran's claims. Iranian state media has a documented history of exaggerating or fabricating civilian casualty reports to inflame international opinion against American military operations.
