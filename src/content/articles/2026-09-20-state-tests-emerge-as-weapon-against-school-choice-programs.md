# State Tests Threaten School Choice in Colorado and Beyond

Colorado parents who've fought for education freedom should brace for a familiar tactic: government regulators using standardized testing mandates to strangle school choice programs before they take root.

Across Texas, Oklahoma, Arizona, and beyond, opponents of education savings accounts (ESAs) and private school choice have pushed to impose state standardized tests on families using choice programs—a backdoor way to kill parental freedom, according to the Washington Examiner. The strategy is simple: force private schools into the same one-size-fits-all assessment regime as public schools, eliminating the academic flexibility that makes choice attractive in the first place.

Proponents of school choice argue that assessment choice is fundamental to education freedom. When families select private schools or education savings accounts, they're choosing institutions with their own academic standards and accountability measures—not borrowed bureaucratic benchmarks. Forcing state tests on choice participants effectively subordinates private education to government control without the government footing the bill.

Colorado conservatives should recognize this playbook. As the state considers expanding education choice options, expect similar pressure from public education unions and progressive lawmakers to impose state testing requirements. The goal isn't accountability; it's regulatory capture disguised as oversight.

Watch whether Colorado legislators attempt to attach state testing mandates to any school choice expansion bills.
