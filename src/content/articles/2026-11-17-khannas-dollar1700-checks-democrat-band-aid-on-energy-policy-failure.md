# Democrat Pitches $1,700 Handout to Mask Inflation Caused by Foreign Policy Blunders

Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) is proposing $1,700 refund checks for every American family, claiming the payments would offset higher gas, food, and electricity costs tied to Middle East tensions (Fox News, New York Post). 

Here's the conservative problem: Khanna's scheme treats the symptom, not the disease. Rather than acknowledge that progressive energy policies and geopolitical mismanagement have driven up costs, Democrats want to throw borrowed money at voters and call it relief. That's not a solution—it's a band-aid on a self-inflicted wound.

**Republicans argue** this is classic Democratic misdirection. Instead of reversing the regulatory stranglehold on domestic energy production that has made Americans dependent on volatile global markets, Khanna wants to redistribute taxpayer dollars. Colorado energy workers and consumers know the real fix: unleash American oil, gas, and coal production to bring prices down permanently—not hand out temporary checks that inflate the deficit.

The math doesn't work either. Multiply $1,700 by 130 million households and you're looking at a massive spending bill with no offset. Coloradans already struggling with inflation don't need government checks; they need lower energy costs and stable prices.

**Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation embraces this spending proposal—it'll reveal whether they prioritize fiscal responsibility or party loyalty.**
