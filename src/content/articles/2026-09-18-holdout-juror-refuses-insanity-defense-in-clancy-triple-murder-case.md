# Holdout Juror Stands Firm in Lindsay Clancy Case—Rejects Insanity Defense

A lone juror in the Lindsay Clancy trial is refusing to back down after 11 peers voted to find the Massachusetts mother not criminally responsible for killing her three young children. Michael P. Desronvil, the sole holdout, released a statement saying "I didn't have any doubts" about his guilty verdict, according to 9News Denver, standing against intense pressure and public criticism from other jurors who favored an insanity defense.

Clancy was accused of drowning her three children—Cora, 5; Dawson, 3; and Callan, 8 months old—in 2022. The jury's split decision means the case hinges on whether jurors accept the insanity defense argument that Clancy's postpartum psychosis rendered her unable to understand the criminality of her actions.

Desronvil's refusal to join the majority reflects a fundamental conservative principle: personal accountability. While mental illness may be a mitigating factor, the argument that severe psychological distress erases criminal responsibility troubles those who believe justice demands consequences for taking innocent lives, regardless of the defendant's mental state at the time.

The Daily Wire reports that Desronvil faces "scorching attacks" from fellow jurors for his stance—a reminder that jury deliberations increasingly resemble ideological battlegrounds rather than fact-finding missions.
