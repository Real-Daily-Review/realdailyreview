# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Medicine Signals National Progressive Push

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate is openly arguing that doctors—not elected officials or parents—should unilaterally decide whether children undergo sex-change procedures, a position that crystallizes the left's medical-autonomy argument and signals how this issue could reshape races nationwide, including in Colorado.

Abdul El-Sayed, touting his medical credentials at a Michigan State University rally, told students that physicians should have "final say" on transgender medical interventions for minors without government interference (Fox News, The Daily Wire). He framed the issue as one of professional medical judgment versus state overreach.

Republicans and conservatives counter that parents—not doctors alone—must retain authority over irreversible medical decisions affecting their children. They argue that progressive states have already moved to shield minors' gender procedures from parental consent and oversight, effectively removing family input from life-altering treatments. Conservatives say this represents government-enabled medical experimentation on children, not liberation.

Colorado voters should note: Democratic candidates here will likely face similar questions on parental rights and medical autonomy as the 2024 cycle intensifies. Watch whether Colorado's Democratic establishment embraces or distances itself from El-Sayed's position on child gender medicine—the answer will reveal how far the state party is willing to go on this issue.
