# National Senate Races Tighten as Cost of Living Dominates Voter Concerns

Republicans face unexpectedly competitive Senate races in Iowa and Texas as voter anxiety over inflation and living costs reshapes the political map heading into the general election stretch.

In Iowa, Republican Ashley Hinson holds a narrow edge over Democrat Josh Turek, with independents breaking heavily and cost-of-living concerns driving voter behavior across the state (Fox News). A separate Quantus Insights poll confirms the race remains "closely divided" (Breitbart), signaling vulnerability for the GOP in a seat Republicans expected to defend comfortably.

Texas presents an even tighter contest. Democrat James Talarico holds a slim 2-point advantage over Republican Ken Paxton, with GOP enthusiasm gaps and crossover voters reshaping what should be solid Republican territory (Fox News).

**The conservative concern:** These races underscore how economic anxiety—driven by years of Democratic spending and inflation—is cutting into Republican margins even in traditionally safe districts. Voters are punishing both parties for the cost-of-living crisis, but Republicans must sharpen their message on fiscal restraint and energy independence to reclaim momentum.

**What to watch:** Whether Republican candidates can pivot messaging from defense to offense on inflation, energy costs, and government waste before November.
