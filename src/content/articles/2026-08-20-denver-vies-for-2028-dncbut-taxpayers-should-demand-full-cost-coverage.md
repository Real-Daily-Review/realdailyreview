# Denver in Running to Host 2028 Democratic Convention—But at What Cost to Taxpayers?

Denver has made the cut as one of three finalist cities to host the 2028 Democratic National Convention, alongside Boston and Philadelphia, according to reporting from CPR News and the Denver Post. Democrats announced the narrowed list Thursday after evaluating logistics, fundraising capacity, and other operational factors at potential host sites.

The move puts Colorado's capital in line to absorb the costs and disruptions of a major party convention. Republicans and fiscal conservatives should scrutinize what hosting entails: security expenses, infrastructure demands, and the likelihood that Denver taxpayers will foot a significant portion of the bill while party elites enjoy the spotlight. Convention host cities historically absorb millions in public costs for security, street closures, and services—expenses that rarely materialize as promised economic windfalls.

A final decision is expected later this year. The 2024 Democratic Convention was held in Chicago, which faced its own cost and security challenges.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the key question is simple: Will Denver's Democratic-led city government negotiate a deal that protects the public purse, or will another convention become another example of private party events subsidized by ordinary citizens?

Watch whether Denver officials demand Democrats and their convention committee cover the full security and infrastructure costs—or quietly pass the bill to Colorado taxpayers.
