# Bernie Sanders Warns Democrats: Stop Obsessing Over Trump, Focus on Kitchen-Table Issues

Even Bernie Sanders thinks Democrats are wasting time chasing Trump investigations instead of governing—a rare moment of clarity from the left that exposes the party's misplaced priorities.

The Vermont senator told NBC's *Meet the Press* that while accountability matters, Democrats "cannot spend" all their congressional time investigating the Trump administration. Sanders argued the party should pivot to affordability and working-class concerns—the issues that actually move voters (Fox News Politics, Washington Examiner).

**The conservative takeaway:** Sanders is right, though for the wrong reasons. Democrats' obsession with Trump investigations signals they have no coherent agenda for inflation, border security, or energy policy. Colorado families struggling with grocery bills and heating costs don't care about another hearing; they care about paychecks and opportunity.

Republicans should hammer this point relentlessly. If Democrats can't deliver on cost-of-living relief—and their track record suggests they can't—endless Trump probes become a convenient distraction from their failed record. Colorado voters, already skeptical of progressive economic management, will notice the difference between performative outrage and real solutions.

Watch whether Democrats actually heed Sanders' warning or double down on investigations while inflation and energy costs remain out of reach for working Coloradans.
