# Democratic Socialist Eyes Denver House Seat, Raising Concerns Over Radical Agenda

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress, alarming conservatives who worry about the district's leftward lurch. According to CPR News, Melat Kiros—a Democratic Socialists of America member—is positioning herself as a leading candidate for the seat, signaling that Denver voters may be ready to embrace openly anti-capitalist politics at the federal level.

Republicans and conservatives argue that DSA-backed candidates push policies hostile to business growth, energy development, and fiscal responsibility. Colorado's energy sector and working families could face pressure from a representative committed to radical wealth redistribution and government control of industry.

The race reflects a broader trend in Democratic strongholds: mainstream progressivism no longer satisfies the activist base. If Kiros wins the primary and general election, Denver would join a small but growing caucus of avowed socialists in Congress—a development that should concern Colorado taxpayers already burdened by state spending and regulatory overreach.
