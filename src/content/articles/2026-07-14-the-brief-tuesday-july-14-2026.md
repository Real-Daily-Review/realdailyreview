# Real Daily Review — The Brief
### Tuesday, July 14, 2026

Colorado conservatives are waking up to a full plate this morning: a heartbreaking border tragedy reshaping a congressional race, a human-caused wildfire threatening Lake County, and a state attorney general picking media-merger fights while real problems go unaddressed. Let's get into it.

---

## A Little Girl Is Dead — and the Border Is on Trial

Six-year-old Calli Toler is gone, allegedly killed when a three-time deported illegal immigrant blew through a stop sign in North Carolina. This isn't an abstraction — it's a family destroyed by a failure of enforcement that Republicans have been demanding be fixed for years. Democrats counter with process arguments; conservatives say the answer is simple: enforce the law, keep deportees out, and stop pretending the border is secure. This case is now roiling a House race, and it should. Colorado families are asking the same question North Carolina families are: who let him back in?

---

## Willow Fire Near Leadville: Human-Caused, 33% Contained

The Willow Fire burning in Lake County near Leadville was caused by humans, officials confirmed — and as of Monday morning it sat at just 33% containment. Colorado's high-country communities, ranchers, and energy workers in the region are watching conditions closely. This is a reminder that responsible land management and adequate firefighting resources aren't optional — they're life and property on the line. Conservatives have long argued that federal land mismanagement and bureaucratic red tape make wildfire response slower and more costly than it has to be.

---

## Phil Weiser Joins 11 Other AGs to Block Paramount-Warner Merger

Colorado Attorney General Phil Weiser has signed onto a 12-state lawsuit to block Paramount's $81 billion merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. Republicans and free-market conservatives argue that government regulators and activist AGs have no business picking winners and losers in private media deals — that's the market's job. Weiser, a Democrat with higher political ambitions, has made a habit of jumping into national legal fights. Colorado taxpayers should ask whether this is the best use of the AG's office and their money.

---

## New York's Socialist Mayor Has No Answer for Tent Cities

In New York City, a massive homeless encampment has sprouted near the Intrepid Museum in west Manhattan — and Mayor Zohran Mamdani, the democratic socialist who won City Hall, is offering few real answers. Conservatives say this is what happens when cities abandon enforcement, decriminalize disorder, and treat homelessness as a housing-supply problem rather than a public-safety and mental-health crisis. Colorado's own progressive-run cities — Denver chief among them — are watching the same experiment play out on their streets. The results aren't pretty anywhere.

---

## Trump Restarts War Powers Clock on Iran

President Trump formally notified Congress that the United States has resumed military operations against Iran, restarting the War Powers Act clock after a ceasefire collapsed. The July 10 letter puts Congress on notice and puts the foreign policy debate back front and center. Conservatives broadly support a strong posture toward Tehran; the question now is whether Congress — including Colorado's delegation — will assert its constitutional role or sit on the sidelines while the executive branch manages the conflict alone.

## What to Watch

Keep your eyes on the Willow Fire's containment progress — if conditions worsen, Lake County and surrounding communities could face serious consequences, and the pressure on state and federal land managers will intensify fast. Watch how Colorado's congressional delegation responds to Trump's Iran War Powers notification — their votes and statements will tell you everything about where they stand on executive authority and national security. And don't lose sight of the Calli Toler story: border enforcement is heading into the 2026 midterm stretch as one of the defining issues, and Colorado's competitive districts will feel every wave.
