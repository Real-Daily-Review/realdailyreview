# Trump's Deportation Record Hits Historic Highs—But MAGA Right Turns on DHS Chief Mullin

President Trump's immigration enforcement machine is running at full throttle: 46,000 deportations in July alone—a record no previous administration has matched—with ICE simultaneously arresting 51,000 immigrants that same month, averaging 1,645 daily arrests (Washington Examiner). Over the past 18 months, more deportation orders have been issued by judges than during the entire four-year Obama administration.

Yet even as Trump delivers on his core campaign promise, the MAGA right is fracturing over Homeland Security Chief Markwayne Mullin's leadership (Breitbart). Conservative critics are questioning whether the former Oklahoma congressman is executing the deportation agenda aggressively enough—a sign of the internal Republican tensions simmering beneath the surface.

For Colorado, where illegal immigration strains schools, hospitals, and law enforcement budgets in Denver and other sanctuary-friendly cities, these enforcement numbers matter. Conservatives here have long argued that border security and interior enforcement directly reduce the fiscal burden on state and local taxpayers footing the bill for services to undocumented populations.

The real test: whether Trump's deportation momentum holds through the fall, or whether infighting between the MAGA base and establishment Republicans undermines the enforcement push when it matters most.
