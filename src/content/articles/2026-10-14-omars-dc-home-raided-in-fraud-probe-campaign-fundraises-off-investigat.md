# Ilhan Omar's D.C. Home Raided in Federal Fraud Investigation—Campaign Fundraises Off It

Minnesota Democrat Ilhan Omar's Washington residence was raided by Homeland Security Investigations agents as a criminal fraud probe into her immigration paperwork intensifies, with her husband Tim Mynett spotted at the scene (Fox News). The investigation centers on alleged irregularities in Omar's naturalization documentation, according to reporting.

Rather than wait for facts, Omar's campaign immediately weaponized the raid for fundraising, blasting supporters with emails urging donations and framing the federal investigation as "baseless" and "partisan" (The Daily Wire). The move mirrors a familiar Democratic playbook: treat law enforcement scrutiny as a persecution narrative and monetize it.

National Review's analysis captures the conservative ambivalence here: legitimate questions about Omar's immigration status deserve answers, but using federal law enforcement as a political cudgel raises its own concerns about DOJ independence under any administration.

**Colorado angle:** While Omar represents Minnesota, her immigration controversies and the broader debate over naturalization integrity matter to Colorado voters skeptical of lax enforcement and sanctuary policies that some progressive cities have embraced.

Conservatives should watch whether the investigation produces actual charges or dissolves into partisan theater—and whether mainstream media treats this with the same scrutiny they'd apply to a Republican under similar circumstances.
