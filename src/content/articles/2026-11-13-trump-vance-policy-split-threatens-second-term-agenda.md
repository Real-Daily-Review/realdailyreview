# Trump-Vance Split on Policy Signals Trouble for Second Term Agenda

President Trump and Vice President JD Vance are already showing daylight on major policy questions, a dangerous fissure for an administration that needs unified messaging (National Review). The two diverge on issues where alignment matters most—particularly on immigration and economic policy—creating uncertainty about which direction the Trump-Vance ticket will actually govern.

Trump has floated amnesty discussions with Democrats, a move that confounds conservatives who backed him on border security. National Review notes it's unclear how any such deal gains traction with a divided GOP base. Meanwhile, Trump has tasked Vance with investigating alleged corruption in Democratic-run cities and states—Minnesota, California, and Illinois among them—claiming billions vanish monthly from government programs (Fox News Politics).

For Colorado, the stakes are real. If Trump and Vance can't align on immigration enforcement, energy policy, or fiscal restraint, Colorado's business community and energy sector lose clarity on federal support. Conservatives worry that public disagreement between the president and VP signals weakness heading into budget fights and regulatory battles.

The Washington Examiner notes Vance's ideological evolution from Trump critic to lieutenant raises questions about his actual policy convictions versus political positioning.

**Watch whether Trump and Vance resolve their differences publicly or whether internal conflict leaks damage the administration's credibility with the conservative base.**
