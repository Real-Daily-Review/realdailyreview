# GOP Senator Demands End to Citizenship for Diplomats' Children

Sen. Eric Schmitt is demanding the State Department and Department of Homeland Security revoke citizenship documentation issued to children born to foreign diplomats in the U.S., calling it an illegal back-door citizenship scheme (Fox News, Daily Wire).

In a letter to Secretary of State Marco Rubio and DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin, Schmitt argues that thousands of children born to diplomatic families have illegally obtained U.S. passports and Social Security numbers—a practice that exploits citizenship rules and undermines immigration law enforcement.

Republicans argue this represents a glaring loophole in federal immigration policy that has gone unchecked for years. The senator is demanding a federal investigation into the scope of the problem and immediate revocation of improperly issued documents.

The issue touches on broader conservative concerns about citizenship integrity and the rule of law. While Colorado isn't directly mentioned in these reports, the state's Republican delegation has consistently backed stricter immigration enforcement and citizenship verification—priorities that align with Schmitt's push.

Democrats have not yet publicly responded to the senator's allegations, though the left typically resists citizenship restrictions and emphasizes diplomatic immunity complexities.

**Watch for:** Whether the Trump administration's DHS and State Department launch a formal investigation and how many citizenship revocations actually occur.
