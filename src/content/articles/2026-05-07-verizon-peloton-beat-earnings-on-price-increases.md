# Telecom and Fitness Giants Post Earnings Gains Fueled by Price Increases

Verizon and Peloton both beat Wall Street forecasts this quarter, crediting higher prices as a key driver of revenue growth.

Verizon, the New York-based telecommunications giant, posted quarterly earnings that exceeded analyst expectations, boosted by price increases and gains in wireless customer additions, according to the Wall Street Journal.

Peloton similarly topped revenue estimates, with CEO Peter Stern telling CNBC that the company views its subscription price increases as a value-driven strategy, the network reported.

Both results underscore a broader corporate trend of raising prices to offset cost pressures and sustain margins. The earnings suggest that at least some consumers have absorbed higher charges without significantly reducing their spending on telecom and fitness services.

Watch for investor reaction to whether these price-hike strategies remain sustainable as economic conditions shift and consumer sensitivity to costs potentially increases.
