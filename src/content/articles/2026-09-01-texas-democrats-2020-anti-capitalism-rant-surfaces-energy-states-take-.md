# Texas Democrat's Anti-Capitalism Rhetoric Resurfaces—Bad News for Energy States Like Colorado

A resurfaced 2020 video shows Texas Democratic Senate nominee James Talarico calling free-market capitalism an "oppressive system" alongside white supremacy, according to *The Daily Wire* and *Just The News*. The remarks, made at a Sierra Club virtual forum, reveal Talarico's hard-left positioning before he softened his messaging for his 2024 Senate bid against GOP opponent Paxton.

Talarico also predicted the Texas oil industry won't last—a position that should alarm Colorado energy workers and taxpayers who depend on responsible oil and gas development. Republicans argue that candidates like Talarico represent the progressive wing that would strangle American energy independence and kill good-paying jobs in energy-rich states. Democrats counter that Talarico is simply acknowledging climate science and the need to transition away from fossil fuels.

The contrast between Talarico's 2020 radicalism and his current campaign messaging underscores a broader pattern: progressive Democrats moderate their rhetoric for general elections while their actual policy positions remain hostile to free enterprise and energy development. Colorado conservatives should watch whether similar anti-energy candidates gain traction in state races this cycle.
