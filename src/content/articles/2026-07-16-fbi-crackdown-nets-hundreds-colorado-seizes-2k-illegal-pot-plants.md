# Federal Crackdown Nets Hundreds of Arrests, 2K Pot Plants in Colorado Bust

FBI Director Kash Patel's "Operation Summer Heat 2.0" is delivering measurable results against crime and drug trafficking across the country, with Colorado law enforcement joining the push to dismantle illegal operations. Last week, Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office and Englewood Police seized nearly 2,000 marijuana plants from an illegal grow operation—a reminder that Colorado's permissive cannabis licensing regime continues to create cover for criminal enterprises (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

The broader federal initiative has netted hundreds of arrests and significant drug seizures nationwide, according to Breitbart's exclusive reporting. The operation also located 120 children in trafficking situations, underscoring the real-world human cost of unchecked criminal networks.

Colorado conservatives have long warned that the state's rush to legalize and commercialize marijuana—marketed as a tax windfall—opened the door to black-market grows operating under the radar of regulators. Illegal operations undercut legitimate businesses, evade taxes, and often involve organized crime and human trafficking. While the state collects licensing fees, taxpayers foot the bill for law enforcement cleanup.

Republicans argue that stronger enforcement of existing cannabis regulations and tighter licensing oversight could prevent such operations from taking root in the first place.

Watch for whether Colorado's marijuana licensing board tightens oversight or doubles down on permissiveness in the name of "social equity."
