# Water World Evacuated Over False Bomb Threat; Park to Reopen Sunday

A full evacuation of Water World in Federal Heights Saturday turned out to be much ado about nothing—law enforcement found no credible threat after an extended investigation, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver and 9News.

Federal Heights Police cleared the popular amusement park following what appears to have been a hoax call. The evacuation disrupted operations for the day, forcing the closure of one of Colorado's busiest summer attractions during peak season.

The park announced it would reopen Sunday with normal operations resuming. No injuries were reported, and authorities did not publicly identify a suspect or release details about the nature of the threat.

The incident underscores the real costs of false alarms: lost revenue for the business, wasted law enforcement resources, and disrupted plans for families who had paid for admission. Colorado businesses operating in the tourism and recreation sector already face thin margins and regulatory pressures; unsubstantiated threats add unnecessary economic friction.

Conservatives should watch whether Federal Heights or state authorities pursue charges against whoever made the false report—and whether Colorado considers tougher penalties for hoax calls that waste taxpayer resources and harm private enterprise.
