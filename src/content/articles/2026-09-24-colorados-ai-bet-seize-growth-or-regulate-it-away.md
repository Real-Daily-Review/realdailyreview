# Colorado Should Seize AI Opportunity Before Regulators Strangle It

Colorado has a genuine chance to lead in artificial intelligence and data centers—but only if state leaders resist the urge to over-regulate a nascent industry.

The Colorado Sun recently pitched AI-enabled apprenticeships as a workforce solution, recognizing that AI skills training could create real jobs for young workers. That's sound thinking. But the real story is broader: Colorado sits at the intersection of energy infrastructure, tech talent, and data-center capacity that makes the state naturally competitive in AI development.

Complete Colorado reports that a Magellan Strategies survey shows solid reasons for Colorado to "bet on artificial intelligence" rather than join the panic chorus. The state's energy resources and growing tech ecosystem position it to attract major AI investment—if policymakers don't kill the goose with heavy-handed regulation.

The national risk is real. The Daily Wire warns that America risks repeating its China-dependency mistakes with AI, allowing foreign competitors to dominate critical technology while U.S. companies chase cheaper overseas alternatives. Colorado can't afford to let that happen here.

Conservatives argue that Colorado's competitive advantage lies in light-touch regulation, reliable power, and business-friendly policy—not government mandates or AI "safety" bureaucracies that stifle innovation.

Watch whether Colorado's legislature resists calls for restrictive AI regulation that could drive investment and jobs to friendlier states.
