# Democrats' Maine Senate Candidate Faces Mounting Scrutiny Over Troubling Background

Democrats are scrambling to defend Graham Platner, their Maine Senate nominee, as his Nazi-inspired tattoos and documented behavioral red flags threaten to undermine the party's self-proclaimed moral authority.

Hunter Biden, the embattled former First Son, mounted a public defense of Platner while speaking with Governor Gavin Newsom (D-CA), dismissing critics with a "show me your phone" challenge rather than addressing the substance of the allegations (The Daily Wire). The deflection strategy highlights Democrats' inability to credibly explain away Platner's baggage.

The Denver Post's editorial board—hardly a conservative outlet—acknowledged the obvious: "Democrats' moral double standard is being exposed in Maine as the party prepares to nominate Graham Platner, an extremist Senate candidate with more baggage than Denver International Airport on Thanksgiving weekend." When center-left media outlets are calling out Democratic hypocrisy, the candidate's problems run deep.

Republicans argue this exposes the left's selective outrage on character and fitness for office. For years, Democrats lectured Republicans about "decency" and "norms"—only to nominate candidates with serious personal and behavioral liabilities when it serves their electoral interests.

Colorado Republicans should watch whether national Democrats double down on Platner or quietly distance themselves—a tell on how far the party will bend its stated principles for Senate seats.
