# Russell Brand Sentenced to 10+ Years for Rape, Sexual Assault

British comedian Russell Brand is heading to prison for over a decade after a London jury convicted him of rape and sexual assault—a stark reminder that no amount of celebrity status shields predators from justice.

Brand, 51, was sentenced Monday to 10 years and six months at Southwark Crown Court following convictions involving four women (The Daily Wire). Justice Joel Bennathan characterized Brand as a "sexual predator" with "staggering arrogance" and contempt for his victims, according to reporting from multiple outlets including the Washington Examiner and Breitbart.

Throughout the trial, Brand maintained his innocence and showed little emotion as the sentence was handed down, per the Examiner's account.

The case underscores a broader cultural reckoning: high-profile figures face real consequences when credible allegations surface and evidence meets the standard of proof.
