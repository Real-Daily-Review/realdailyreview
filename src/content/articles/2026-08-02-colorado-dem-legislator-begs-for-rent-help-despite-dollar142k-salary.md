# Colorado Democrat's Salary Hypocrisy: $142K Legislative Post, Begging Bowl Out

A Colorado Democratic socialist legislator-elect is learning a hard lesson about the gap between radical rhetoric and personal responsibility. Brian Romero, headed for a $142,000 annual Assembly salary, posted a GoFundMe plea seeking $2,000 for rent and groceries—then scrubbed it after public backlash (Fox News Politics).

The move crystallizes what conservatives have long argued about the left's economic agenda: progressive politicians demand wealth redistribution while protecting their own paychecks. Romero will earn six figures representing Colorado taxpayers, yet claimed inability to cover basic living expenses before taking office.

Republicans argue this exemplifies the hypocrisy embedded in socialist politics: demand others surrender earnings to government, then cry poverty when personal finances tighten. For Colorado voters already burdened by rising taxes and living costs, the optics are toxic—a newly elected official seeking handouts while positioned to vote on spending that affects working families statewide.

The deleted post raises questions about judgment and financial literacy among those seeking to manage state budgets. Conservatives note that if a legislator-elect can't manage a $142,000 salary without crowdfunding, how can voters trust him to steward Colorado's $30-billion-plus budget?

Watch whether Colorado media holds Romero accountable or moves on—a telling measure of local press independence.
