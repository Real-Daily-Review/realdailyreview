# Illegal Immigrant Accused of Murder in Virginia; Biden Admin's Vetting Record Under Fire

A Salvadoran national allegedly released by the Biden administration is now accused of murdering a Virginia mother of a young child—the latest case fueling conservative demands for stricter border enforcement and vetting protocols.

Alexis Antonio Cedillos-Campos, 19, is accused of fatally stabbing Carmen Lizet Puch, 42, at a park in Great Falls, Virginia (The Daily Wire). According to authorities, Cedillos-Campos allegedly grabbed a weapon and filled a bottle with gasoline before the attack. Puch was a mother of a 3-year-old daughter.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and immigration hawks argue the case exemplifies the dangers of the Biden administration's lax border policies and inadequate vetting of released migrants. They point to the alleged murderer's prior release into the U.S. system as evidence that sanctuary policies and catch-and-release protocols endanger American citizens—particularly vulnerable women and children.

**Democrats' position:** The left typically resists linking individual crimes to broader immigration policy, arguing that crime rates among immigrants (legal and illegal) remain lower than native-born populations and that sweeping policy changes shouldn't hinge on isolated incidents.
