# White House Counsel Pushes Back Against Democratic Congressional Overreach

The White House is drawing a line: congressional Democrats are abusing their investigative powers to intimidate Trump supporters, not pursue legitimate oversight. White House counsel Will Scharf warned House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform ranking member Rep. Robert Garcia against exceeding congressional authority, according to Fox News Politics. Meanwhile, a top Department of Justice attorney expressed concern that Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D-CT) is using his Senate investigations committee to target people who donated to the White House ballroom project—a probe the DOJ frames as intimidation of Trump supporters, per the Washington Examiner.

The pattern is clear to conservatives: Democrats are weaponizing committee power. Rather than focus on actual government waste or fraud, they're investigating citizens for their political beliefs and financial support of the administration. This echoes longstanding Republican complaints that the left uses congressional committees as political cudgels rather than legitimate oversight bodies.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the lesson is stark: when one party controls investigative machinery, political enemies become targets. The precedent being set today could affect how future committees treat Colorado conservatives, donors, and business leaders.

Watch whether House Republicans reciprocate with their own aggressive investigations, or whether they take the high road—a decision that will define the tone of congressional oversight for years.
