# Democrats Demand Trump Briefings Over Tech Outages; Conservatives Say Government Overreach Is the Real Problem

A series of recent tech outages has Democrats demanding the Trump administration explain itself to Congress, but conservatives argue the real issue isn't federal response—it's decades of regulatory bloat that left critical infrastructure vulnerable.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani told Fox News the administration's response was "far from what many wanted to hear," while House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-NY) called for Trump officials to brief congressional leadership on the outages affecting Americans over the past six days (Washington Examiner).

**Here's what conservatives are saying:** Republicans and free-market advocates counter that government meddling—not insufficient federal oversight—created the conditions for cascading failures. They point to how decades of regulatory complexity, compliance costs, and government-mandated "resilience" standards often force companies to patch systems rather than rebuild them properly. The Left's instinct to demand more federal briefings and oversight misses the mark: private companies need *less* red tape and *more* freedom to invest in infrastructure without Washington's permission slip.

For Colorado, this matters. Tech outages ripple through our energy sector, water management systems, and financial markets. If Democrats push for expanded federal tech regulation, Colorado businesses and energy producers could face new compliance costs that drive up operating expenses.

**Watch for:** Whether the Trump administration resists Democratic calls for expanded tech regulation, or caves to pressure for new federal "oversight" agencies.
