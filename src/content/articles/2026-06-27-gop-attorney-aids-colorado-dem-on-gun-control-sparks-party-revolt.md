# GOP Attorney's Gun-Control Stance Sparks Party Backlash Over Loyalty

A Republican attorney has drawn fire from fellow GOP members for assisting Colorado's Democratic Attorney General Jay Jones on gun-control initiatives, according to the Daily Caller. The unnamed GOP lawyer faced pointed questions from Republican colleagues about why he's lending credibility to an administration pursuing anti-Second Amendment policies—a core concern for Colorado conservatives who view gun rights as non-negotiable.

The tension reflects a broader conservative frustration: when Republicans cross party lines on constitutional issues, they risk undercutting the entire conservative agenda. Colorado's gun-owning community, already wary of Democratic overreach in the state, sees such collaboration as a betrayal of fundamental liberties.

Republicans argue that partisan loyalty on core constitutional issues—especially Second Amendment protections—isn't obstruction; it's principle. Democrats and their allies counter that bipartisan cooperation on law enforcement matters serves the public interest, regardless of ideological differences.

Meanwhile, former Attorney General Bill Barr is publicly urging Senate Republicans to confirm Todd Blanche as the next U.S. Attorney General (Washington Examiner), signaling that GOP leadership remains focused on installing reliable conservatives in Justice Department leadership at the federal level.
