# Israeli Airstrikes Kill Multiple in Southern Lebanon as Cross-Border Tensions Escalate

Israeli airstrikes killed at least five people in southern Lebanon, according to PBS NewsHour, with Al Jazeera reporting more than a dozen killed in the escalating attacks. The strikes targeted villages in Tyre province, including Toura, hours after the Israeli army's Arabic-language spokesperson issued evacuation warnings to residents of six villages in the region.

The attacks came as Hezbollah rockets struck open areas in Israel, marking the latest exchange in ongoing cross-border hostilities. Al Jazeera's correspondent reported witnessing the Israeli strikes firsthand from Tyre.

The escalation occurs ahead of a new round of talks expected next week in Washington, DC, according to Al Jazeera. The timing underscores the fragile security situation in the region even as diplomatic efforts continue.

Watch for developments from the Washington talks and whether either side signals willingness to de-escalate tensions.
