# Venezuela Rocked by Devastating Earthquakes; Widespread Damage Feared

Back-to-back powerful earthquakes have hammered Venezuela, with the U.S. Geological Survey warning of "high casualties and extensive damage" across the country. According to the Washington Examiner, a 7.1-magnitude quake struck near Moron, roughly 130 miles west of Caracas, followed by a 7.5-magnitude aftershock. The tremors rank among the strongest to hit Venezuela in over a century, collapsing buildings and triggering panic throughout the capital and surrounding regions (9News Denver).
