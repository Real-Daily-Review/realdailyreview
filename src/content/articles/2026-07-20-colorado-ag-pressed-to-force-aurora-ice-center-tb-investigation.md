# Colorado AG Faces Pressure to Investigate TB at Aurora ICE Facility

Colorado's Attorney General is under pressure to compel the Aurora ICE Processing Center to cooperate with state health officials investigating a potential tuberculosis outbreak—a routine public health matter now caught in the crossfire of immigration politics.

State health officials are legally required to investigate possible TB cases at the facility, but it remains unclear how many, if any, infections exist (CPR News). The dispute centers on whether the detention center will grant investigators full access to conduct the probe.

Meanwhile, a woman shot near the facility during a protest has returned to the scene, vowing to continue activism despite her injury (KDVR). The incident underscores the volatile environment surrounding the Aurora facility, which has become a flashpoint for immigration debates.

**The conservative angle:** Colorado taxpayers are funding detention operations while state health agencies navigate bureaucratic obstacles to do their jobs. Republicans argue that cooperation with health investigations should be straightforward—public health transcends political ideology. The real question: why would any facility resist a lawful health inspection? Conservatives also note that security concerns at the site are legitimate and deserve equal attention to protest activity.

**Watch next:** Whether the AG's office moves decisively to enforce the health investigation, and whether political pressure from immigration activists influences the outcome.
