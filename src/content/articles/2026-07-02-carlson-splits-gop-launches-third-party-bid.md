# Tucker Carlson Abandons GOP, Vows to Build Third Party

Tucker Carlson has broken with the Republican Party and declared his intention to build a new political movement, signaling a major fracture on the right ahead of 2026.

Carlson's departure reflects deepening frustration among some conservatives with Republican leadership. His move raises immediate questions about whether a third-party effort could fracture the right-leaning vote in competitive races, potentially benefiting Democrats in Colorado and nationally.
