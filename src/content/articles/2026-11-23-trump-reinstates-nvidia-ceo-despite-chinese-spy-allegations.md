# Nvidia CEO's Reinstatement Raises National Security Red Flags

Trump personally intervened to restore Jensen Huang as Nvidia CEO despite mounting concerns about alleged Chinese intelligence ties—a move that puts Silicon Valley profits ahead of American security.

According to Fox News, President Trump said he spoke directly and indirectly with Nvidia's board to reinstate Huang, claiming his return poses no national security risk. But that assurance rings hollow given the facts on the ground.

Breitbart reports that Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman romantically linked to the Nvidia chief, sits at the center of federal investigations into whether her former company, Megaspeed, funneled advanced Nvidia chips into China through underground trade networks. Other probes allege she maintained regular contact with Chinese intelligence—suggesting she may have operated as a spy.

The timing stinks worse. The Daily Caller notes that an investment firm controlled by Trump Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's sons celebrated Huang's reinstatement, raising questions about whose interests the administration is actually serving.

**The Colorado angle:** Nvidia's chips power everything from AI data centers to defense systems. If advanced semiconductor technology is leaking to China through compromised executives, Colorado's tech sector and national defense contractors face real exposure.

Conservatives should demand full transparency on what Trump knew about these investigations before intervening—and whether national security took a backseat to political favors.
