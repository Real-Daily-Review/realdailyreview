# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Prosecution; Colorado Voters Eye Land-Grab Ballot Measure

A Colorado judge has ruled sufficient evidence exists to prosecute Brandon Booth, an ex-ICE detention center guard accused of shooting toward female protesters who allegedly made inflammatory remarks about "race traitors" and threatened to locate guards' children, according to the Colorado Sun. The case underscores tensions surrounding immigration enforcement facilities in the state.

Meanwhile, Colorado conservatives are mounting resistance to Proposition 137, a ballot measure that would funnel taxpayer dollars to environmental nonprofits under the guise of land conservation. Complete Colorado reports the initiative's primary beneficiary is The Nature Conservancy—a wealthy NGO that would receive public funding while claiming to advance "conservation." Republicans argue this represents classic government overreach: using ballot measures to bypass legislative scrutiny and transfer tax revenue to special interests with progressive environmental agendas.

The measure threatens Colorado's property rights and fiscal discipline. Taxpayers would foot the bill for land acquisitions and easements that benefit wealthy foundations, not working Coloradans or ranchers managing private land responsibly.

Conservatives should watch whether Proposition 137 gains traction in November—and whether similar "conservation" schemes appear on future ballots as Democrats seek creative ways to expand government spending without direct tax increases.
