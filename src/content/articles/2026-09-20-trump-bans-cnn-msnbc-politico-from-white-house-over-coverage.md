# Trump Bars CNN, MSNBC, Politico From White House; Networks Cry Foul

President Trump has banned CNN, MSNBC, and Politico from White House access, citing what he views as unfair coverage—a move that immediately triggered constitutional pushback from the networks involved.

CNN responded Friday by declaring any ban would be "unconstitutional" and an "illegal assault on the Constitution," according to The Daily Wire. The network said it "stands fully behind our White House team and their fair and accurate reporting." Reporters from the three outlets were denied access to the White House on Saturday (9News Denver).

**The conservative case:** Trump supporters argue the mainstream media has waged a relentless campaign of hostile coverage against his administration. They contend the President has broad discretion over White House access and that networks engaging in what conservatives view as partisan activism forfeit normal press privileges. Republicans have long complained that outlets like CNN operate as Democratic opposition research operations rather than neutral news organizations.

**The media's counter:** Networks argue that denying press access violates First Amendment principles and sets a dangerous precedent for executive overreach. They claim their reporting is factual and that access to the President is essential to government accountability.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's major newsrooms will watch whether this precedent affects their own White House correspondent access and how it plays in a state with significant media markets in Denver and Boulder.

Watch whether Congress or courts intervene—and whether other outlets face similar restrictions.
