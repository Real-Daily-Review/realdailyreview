# Remains of US Soldier Missing in Morocco Recovered

The remains of 1st Lt. Kendrick Lamont Key Jr., a US soldier who went missing during a training exercise in Morocco, were recovered from the Atlantic Ocean on Saturday, according to the U.S. Army Europe and Africa.

Key, of Richmond, Virginia, disappeared on May 2 while participating in African Lion 2026, a major joint military exercise involving the US, NATO allies, and African nations, according to The Guardian. He fell off a cliff during a recreational hike near Cap Draa while off duty, The Hill reported. Moroccan searchers found his remains in the water within a mile of where he went missing, the army said in a statement.

The Washington Examiner reported that two US soldiers initially went missing during the Northern Africa training exercise, though the sources do not clarify the status of the second soldier.

The recovery concludes a week-long search operation following the soldier's disappearance during the multinational exercise.
