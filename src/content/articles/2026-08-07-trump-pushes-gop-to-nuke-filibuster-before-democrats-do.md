# Trump Warns GOP: Don't Let Democrats Nuke the Filibuster First

Former President Trump is sounding the alarm that Republicans must end the Senate filibuster before Democrats do—or risk permanent minority status. Trump's warning comes after Michigan Democrat Abdulrahman El-Sayed, whom Trump labeled a "Communist," won his party's primary and pledged to eliminate the 60-vote threshold if elected (Breitbart).

The stakes are stark: Trump argues that if Democrats abolish the filibuster first, they'll use simple-majority rule to ram through radical legislation unchecked. Republicans would then inherit a gutted chamber with no procedural defenses—potentially making Trump "the last Republican President," as he put it (Just The News).

**The conservative case:** Filibuster elimination is a double-edged sword. Yes, it would let Republicans pass legislation faster when in power. But Democrats' stated intent to use it for aggressive progressive agendas—from gun control to energy mandates to tax hikes—makes preemptive action tempting. Colorado Republicans should note: a filibuster-free Senate could fast-track federal overreach on water rights, energy development, and Second Amendment protections that currently enjoy procedural protection.

**The counterargument:** Some worry that eliminating the filibuster permanently destabilizes the Senate and invites tit-for-tat escalation, leaving no guardrails for either party.

**Watch:** Whether Senate Republicans adopt Trump's position or hold the line on preserving the filibuster as a structural check on majority power.
