# Trump Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt Steps Down to Focus on Family

President Trump announced Wednesday that White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt will depart her post at the end of August to spend more time with her family, according to Fox News and the New York Post. Leavitt, a trusted Trump aide and mother of two young children, is stepping down shortly after returning from maternity leave.

Trump praised Leavitt as "one of my most trusted aides" and said she will transition to an outside advisory role, per Fox News. The Daily Wire and Washington Examiner confirmed the end-of-month departure timeline.

Leavitt's exit marks a staffing shift in the second Trump administration, though no immediate successor has been named. Her decision to prioritize family responsibilities reflects broader workplace dynamics around work-life balance—a conversation that extends beyond Washington politics.
