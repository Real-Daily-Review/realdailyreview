# Democratic Maine Candidate Faces Serious Assault Allegations as Party Fractures Over Support

A Democratic Maine Senate candidate is imploding under the weight of sexual assault allegations, exposing deep rifts within the party over how to handle misconduct claims against one of their own.

Jenny Racicot told CNN Monday that Graham Platner, the Democratic nominee, raped her while appearing "blackout drunk"—and that he briefly stopped mid-assault to apologize before continuing (The Daily Wire). The accusation adds to what sources describe as a pattern of alleged violence against women.

The allegations have fractured Democratic unity. Sen. John Fetterman (D-PA) publicly called on Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) to rescind his endorsement of Platner, according to the Washington Examiner. Sanders has maintained his backing despite the mounting controversies, creating an awkward moment where a prominent progressive senator faces pressure from a fellow Democrat to abandon support for an accused predator.

For Colorado voters watching national Democratic politics, the Platner saga illustrates a broader problem: when progressive candidates face serious misconduct allegations, party loyalty and institutional inertia often override accountability. The question now is whether Sanders will cave to pressure or double down—either way, it's a damaging look for Democrats heading into the election cycle.
