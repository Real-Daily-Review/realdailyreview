# Polish Constitutional Crisis: Tusk's Power Grab Sparks Showdown

Poland's left-leaning Prime Minister Donald Tusk has engineered a constitutional crisis by forcibly removing the head of the nation's high court—a move conservatives warn mirrors the judicial power grabs that threaten rule of law worldwide.

PM Tusk's surprise operation to oust Constitution Tribunal President Bogdan Swieczkowski and install an interim replacement triggered overnight clashes between riot police and supporters of the conservative president, Karol Nawrocki, who rushed back to Warsaw to confront the move (Washington Examiner). Police surrounded the court complex as demonstrators threw objects at officers; three were arrested attempting to enter the building (The Daily Wire).

**The conservative case:** Republicans and constitutional scholars argue Tusk's unilateral removal of a sitting tribunal chief without proper legal process represents a dangerous assault on judicial independence. Nawrocki's supporters view it as a coup attempt by a progressive government seeking to consolidate power and silence institutional checks.

**Democrats' framing:** Tusk's coalition claims the tribunal was politicized under previous conservative leadership and needed reform—a familiar progressive argument for dismantling institutions that block their agenda.

The standoff underscores how Western democracies face mounting pressure from leaders willing to weaponize courts rather than respect constitutional limits. Colorado conservatives should watch whether similar judicial-capture tactics gain traction in American progressive strongholds.
