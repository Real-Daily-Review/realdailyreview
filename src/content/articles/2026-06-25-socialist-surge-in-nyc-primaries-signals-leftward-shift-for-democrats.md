# Socialist Wave Sweeps NYC Primaries—Warning Sign for Colorado Democrats?

Far-left candidates backed by NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani decisively won Democratic primary races, defeating incumbents endorsed by House Democratic leadership (Fox News Politics). The victories underscore a troubling shift within the Democratic Party toward openly socialist candidates—a trend that could reshape Colorado's political landscape as the state's Democratic establishment tilts further left.

The three winners, including Darializa Avila Chevalier, represent a faction openly hostile to free-market principles and constitutional governance (National Review). Republicans argue these victories expose the Democratic Party's vulnerability to its radical wing, which prioritizes government expansion, wealth redistribution, and anti-American rhetoric over pragmatic governance.

**The Colorado angle:** As Denver and Boulder lean increasingly progressive, Colorado Democrats face pressure to embrace similar socialist-backed candidates in statewide races. If the national party's center of gravity continues shifting leftward, Colorado conservatives should expect more aggressive pushes for higher taxes, expanded government programs, and regulatory overreach targeting energy development and Second Amendment rights.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from or embrace this socialist movement ahead of 2026 midterms.
