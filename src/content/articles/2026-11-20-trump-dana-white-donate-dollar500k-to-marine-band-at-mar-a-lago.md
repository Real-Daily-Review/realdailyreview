# Trump, Dana White Donate $500K to Marine Band at Mar-a-Lago Event

President Donald Trump and UFC CEO Dana White presented the U.S. Marine Band with a combined $500,000 donation Friday at Mar-a-Lago, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Trump called the gift a "big bonus" for the 170-member ensemble, America's oldest continuously active professional military musical organization, rewarding them for performances at major White House events including this summer's UFC Freedom 250 celebration.

The donation marks the first event held on Mar-a-Lago's new helipad, per *Fox News*. Trump and White each contributed $100,000 to the band, with additional funds coming from other sources.

The Marine Band, known as the "President's Own," has performed at state dinners, inaugural ceremonies, and other official functions for decades. Private philanthropy supporting military organizations reflects a conservative priority: honoring service members and their contributions without expanding government budgets or creating new federal programs.

This gesture stands in contrast to progressive calls for increased government spending on various initiatives—here, private citizens stepped up to support a cherished military institution directly.
