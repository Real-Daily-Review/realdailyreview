# Democrats Float Third Trump Impeachment as Economic Claims Dominate Debate

Colorado voters should brace for a Washington circus: House Democrats are openly discussing a third Trump impeachment, while the administration touts record employment figures that mainstream media largely ignores.

Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) told NBC's *Meet the Press* that impeaching Trump again would "set a standard"—essentially admitting Democrats view impeachment as a political weapon rather than a constitutional remedy for genuine high crimes. Republicans argue this weaponization of impeachment destroys the tool's legitimacy and deepens partisan divisions that harm actual governance.

Meanwhile, Trump claims more Americans are working than at any point in U.S. history (Just The News). The administration argues the media refuses to report positive economic data, a charge conservatives say reflects the left's bias against this administration.

**The Colorado angle:** While national Democrats pursue impeachment theater, Colorado families and workers care about jobs, wages, and whether their paychecks stretch further. Economic performance directly affects Colorado's energy sector, small businesses, and tax revenues—areas where Republicans argue Trump's policies delivered results Democrats won't acknowledge.

**What to watch:** Whether Colorado's Democratic delegation joins another impeachment push, and how that plays with moderate voters in swing districts heading into 2026.
