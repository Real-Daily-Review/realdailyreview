# Milo Yiannopoulos Deported After ICE Arrest for Visa Overstay

British political commentator Milo Yiannopoulos has been deported to the United Kingdom following his arrest by Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents, according to reporting from Breitbart and Just The News. ICE apprehended Yiannopoulos in New Orleans, Louisiana after he overstayed his visa, the outlets report.

The arrest and deportation mark a sharp turn for the controversial right-wing commentator, who has been a fixture in American conservative media circles. Daily Caller reports that some have questioned whether conservative activist Laura Loomer played a role in triggering the enforcement action, though specifics remain unclear.

The case underscores a broader tension within conservative circles: while Republicans consistently argue for strict enforcement of immigration law and visa compliance, the deportation of a prominent right-wing voice raises questions about how that enforcement is applied and whether political considerations factor into federal immigration decisions.

Conservatives should watch whether the Trump administration clarifies the timeline and reasoning behind the ICE action—and whether similar enforcement applies uniformly across the political spectrum.
