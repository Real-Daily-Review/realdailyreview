# Omar Scandal Deepens as Constitutional Questions Loom

A simmering ethics controversy surrounding Minnesota Democrat Ilhan Omar refuses to fade, even as legal and constitutional barriers limit what Congress can actually do about it. Trump has called for Omar's resignation over alleged immigration fraud connections, but as *Fox News Politics* notes, the Constitution grants only the House itself power to remove a member—a threshold Omar's Democratic colleagues show no appetite to cross.

The scandal centers on Omar's ties to individuals involved in a "massive fraud scheme," according to reporting by *The Daily Wire*. Minnesota Republican State Rep. Kristin Robbins told *The Daily Wire* Omar was "thick as thieves" with the accused fraudsters, and the outlet reports six unanswered messages from Robbins seeking comment from Omar's office.

While the Omar saga plays out in Minnesota politics, Colorado conservatives should note the broader pattern: Democratic leadership repeatedly shields its own members from accountability, regardless of the allegations. The contrast with how Republicans have handled ethics violations in their own ranks is stark.

Republicans argue Omar's connections demand serious investigation and transparency; Democrats have largely circled the wagons. The question now is whether House leadership will pursue a formal ethics inquiry or continue stonewalling.

Watch whether the House Ethics Committee takes up the matter—or whether Democratic leadership kills it quietly.
