# Defense Department Releases Decades of UFO Files, Offers Few Answers

The Defense Department released classified documents Friday detailing unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) spanning the Cold War era to recent years, according to NPR National.

The files describe reports of mysterious rotating saucers documented during the Cold War alongside more recent sightings of metallic elliptical objects hovering in mid-air. The military has adopted the term "UAP" to replace the colloquial "UFO."

However, the release offers limited concrete findings. Ars Technica notes the documents contain "no there there"—suggesting the materials lack definitive explanations or evidence of extraterrestrial origin. The outlet acknowledged it does not rule out the possibility that aliens have visited Earth, but the newly released files do not appear to provide substantive proof either way.

The timing and scope of the release remain unclear from available reporting, as does whether additional documents may follow. The disclosure represents a rare public window into decades of military observations of unexplained aerial phenomena, though analysts and observers will likely scrutinize the files for any patterns or corroborating evidence the government may have previously withheld.
