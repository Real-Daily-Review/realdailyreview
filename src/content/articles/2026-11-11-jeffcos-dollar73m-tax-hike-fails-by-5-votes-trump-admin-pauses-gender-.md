# Jeffco Schools' $73M Tax Hike Teeters on Recount as Trump Admin Pauses Gender Policy Lawsuit

Jefferson County Public Schools' $73 million mill levy override—Ballot Issue 5A—is headed to a recount after failing by just five votes out of nearly 302,000 cast, signaling Colorado taxpayers' growing resistance to unlimited school spending demands (Denver Post). The measure would have raised property taxes on Jeffco residents to fund operations in the state's second-largest K-12 system.

The recount comes as a federal judge has indefinitely paused a separate lawsuit between Jeffco and the Trump administration over the district's transgender student accommodation policies (KDVR Fox31 Denver). The pause suggests the new administration may challenge the district's approach—a potential win for conservatives who've argued schools should focus on academics, not social engineering.

Republicans have long contended that Colorado school districts prioritize ideological initiatives over fiscal restraint. The near-failure of Issue 5A reflects taxpayer fatigue with perpetual levy requests, especially when districts face questions about how existing dollars are spent.

Conservatives should monitor whether the paused lawsuit signals the Trump administration will take action against progressive school policies statewide—and whether Jeffco's recount confirms voters' rejection of higher property taxes.
