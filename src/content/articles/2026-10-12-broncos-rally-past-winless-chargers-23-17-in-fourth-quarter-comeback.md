# Broncos Outlast Chargers in Fourth-Quarter Thriller

Denver's offense finally showed up when it mattered most, as the Broncos stormed back in the fourth quarter to defeat the winless Los Angeles Chargers 23-17 on Sunday—a victory that matters far less than whether this team can sustain competence over a full 16 games.

Kicker Wil Lutz delivered the decisive blow with a go-ahead 19-yard field goal with 5:07 remaining, then sealed it with a 36-yarder with 1:08 left (KDVR, New York Post). The Broncos' fourth-quarter surge produced 13 unanswered points, lifting Denver to 3-2 on the season while handing Los Angeles its fifth consecutive loss to open the year.

Running back J.K. Dobbins, who spent time with the Chargers organization, didn't miss the chance to needle his former team after the victory (New York Post). The Broncos earned their first AFC West division win, a modest but necessary step toward relevance in a competitive conference.

The real test for Denver isn't beating a historically inept Chargers team—it's whether the offense can maintain this fourth-quarter intensity when facing playoff-caliber opponents down the stretch.
