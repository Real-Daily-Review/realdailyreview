# California Concert Shooting Exposes Failure of Gun-Free Venue Policies

A mass shooting at a private California concert that wounded nine people and a separate incident involving an unknown substance at a Don Toliver concert in Manchester highlight the inadequacy of gun-free zones to stop determined criminals. According to the New York Post, the California shooting—which sprayed gunfire into a crowd of 150—has been linked to gang violence, with new motives and details now emerging. The Daily Caller reported that the Manchester venue was evacuated after an unknown substance was released, leaving some attendees coughing up blood.

These incidents underscore a core conservative argument: criminals ignore gun bans. Private venues that prohibit lawful self-defense leave attendees defenseless against bad actors. While progressives push ever-stricter gun restrictions, criminals operate outside the law—a reality Colorado gun owners understand well as the state faces continued pressure from Denver Democrats to expand magazine restrictions and licensing schemes.

The California shooting's gang-violence connection also exposes how urban crime—not legal gun ownership—drives violence statistics. Yet Democrats consistently blame lawful gun owners and Second Amendment rights rather than addressing the root causes of criminal behavior and gang activity.
