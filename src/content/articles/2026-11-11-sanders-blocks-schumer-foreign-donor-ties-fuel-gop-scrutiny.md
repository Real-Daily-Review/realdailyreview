# Bernie Sanders Blocks Schumer, Raising Questions About Foreign Donor Network

Bernie Sanders' refusal to back Chuck Schumer as Senate Democratic leader signals deepening fractures within the left—but conservatives are raising sharper questions about who's actually funding the Vermont socialist's political machine.

According to FEC records cited by Fox News, Shanghai-based tycoon Neville Roy Singham and CodePink co-founder Jodie Evans have funneled a combined $47,000 to Sanders, progressive Squad members, and allied causes. The timing of Sanders' leadership challenge coincides with renewed scrutiny of foreign money flowing into far-left Democratic networks.

Republicans argue this pattern exposes the hypocrisy of Democrats who routinely accuse conservatives of foreign interference while remaining silent about overseas cash bankrolling their own progressive wing. The Sanders-Schumer rift also underscores how the Democratic Party is being pulled further left by activists and donors with questionable international ties—a dynamic that could reshape Senate priorities on energy, regulation, and fiscal policy.

Colorado Democrats, already struggling with voter concerns over inflation and government overreach, should brace for GOP attacks linking national progressive leadership to foreign-funded activism. Michael Bennet and John Hickenlooper—will distance themselves from Sanders-aligned causes bankrolled by overseas interests.
