# NYC Doubles Down on Gender Ideology While Colorado Watches Its Own Spending

New York City is dumping $15 million in taxpayer money into sex-change initiatives for children and adults, a move that should alarm Colorado conservatives watching their own state government's spending priorities.

NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced the funding as a defiant response to federal scrutiny, framing the expenditure as "healthcare" and a civil rights issue. Republicans argue this represents reckless use of public funds on experimental treatments—especially for minors—when cities face crumbling infrastructure, rising crime, and fiscal mismanagement.

The move reflects the progressive left's commitment to advancing gender ideology at taxpayer expense, despite growing medical and parental concerns about the long-term effects of these interventions on children. Conservatives counter that government should not be funding or promoting sex-change procedures; families and the private market—not bureaucrats—should decide healthcare priorities.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't announced similar city-level spending, progressive Denver and Boulder officials have signaled support for gender-affirming care policies. Taxpayers should monitor whether Colorado municipalities attempt comparable expenditures or whether state lawmakers push to restrict such funding—and whether Colorado's Republican-controlled legislature will act to prevent it.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans introduce legislation protecting parental rights and limiting government funding for gender-transition services.
