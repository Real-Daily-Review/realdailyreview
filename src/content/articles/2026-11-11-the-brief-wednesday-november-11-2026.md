## Supreme Court Takes Up Labor Department Migrant Worker Case

The Supreme Court is wrestling with a Labor Department case involving fines related to migrant workers, with at least one justice openly expressing confusion about the government's legal theory. Republicans and conservatives have long argued that the Labor Department under Democratic administrations overreaches its statutory authority to impose burdens on American employers. Colorado's agriculture and energy sectors — both heavy users of legal guest-worker programs — have a direct stake in how the Court ultimately draws the line on agency power.
