# Colorado Voters Face Incomplete Picture on Tax Hike Amendment 87

Colorado media outlets are downplaying the real cost of Amendment 87, a major tax increase that would reshape the state's fiscal landscape—and conservatives say voters aren't getting the full story before Election Day.

Complete Colorado reports that mainstream outlets have failed to adequately explain Amendment 87's impact on Colorado taxpayers and businesses. The amendment would significantly expand state tax obligations, yet coverage has glossed over specifics that matter to working families and job creators already burdened by Colorado's rising cost of living.

Republicans argue the media's selective reporting mirrors a broader pattern: progressive ballot measures get sympathetic framing while their fiscal consequences remain buried. Conservatives say voters deserve transparent analysis of how Amendment 87 would affect take-home pay, business competitiveness, and Colorado's ability to attract talent and investment.

Meanwhile, Amendment 81—a separate ballot measure—would loosen Colorado's sanctuary law restrictions and allow greater ICE cooperation. The Denver Post reports law enforcement remains split on the proposal, with critics warning of racial profiling risks.

**Watch closely:** How Amendment 87 polling shifts once voters encounter detailed cost breakdowns—and whether conservative media can force mainstream outlets to cover the tax hike's full price tag before November.
