# Aurora Hit-and-Run Kills Cyclist; Police Seek Driver

Aurora police are hunting for a driver who struck and killed a cyclist on Powhatan Road, leaving the victim at the scene in what authorities are treating as a hit-and-run homicide.

A good Samaritan discovered the cyclist around 9:30 a.m. Monday morning, according to the Aurora Police Department. The incident underscores a persistent problem in the Denver metro area: reckless drivers evading responsibility for serious crimes.

Hit-and-run fatalities have become a flashpoint for law enforcement across Colorado. Police say they're pursuing leads, but the case remains unsolved. Conservatives have long argued that Colorado needs stricter penalties for drivers who flee accident scenes—particularly when fatalities are involved—and better enforcement of existing traffic laws rather than endless new regulations that burden law-abiding motorists.

The victim's identity has not been released pending family notification.

Coloradans should watch whether Aurora PD makes an arrest and what charges prosecutors bring; the outcome will signal how seriously the justice system treats hit-and-run homicides in the metro area.
