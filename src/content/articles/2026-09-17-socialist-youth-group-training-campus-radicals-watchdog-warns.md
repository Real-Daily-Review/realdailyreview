# Youth Socialist Group Training Campus Radicals, Conservative Watchdog Warns

Colorado parents should know what ideology is being pushed on college campuses—and it's not traditional civics.

The Washington Examiner reports that the Young Democratic Socialists of America (YDSA) has become a pipeline for radical activism on American campuses. The group's influence grew significantly after Sen. Bernie Sanders's 2016 presidential run brought "democratic socialism" into mainstream Democratic politics, despite Sanders's eventual loss to Hillary Clinton.

According to the Examiner's investigation, YDSA operates as a training ground for student militancy, with the group's messaging explicitly framing lawbreaking as acceptable political activism. The organization's reach extends to Colorado universities, where students are being indoctrinated with anti-capitalist ideology under the guise of social justice organizing.

Conservatives argue this represents a dangerous normalization of political violence and contempt for the rule of law among the next generation. The group's tactics—which the Examiner characterizes as encouraging students to "break the law and get away with it"—directly contradict the civic values and personal responsibility that Colorado families expect schools to teach.

Republicans and taxpayers should demand transparency from university administrations about which student groups receive funding, facilities access, and institutional support—especially when those groups openly advocate lawbreaking.

Watch for whether Colorado's university boards will address YDSA's presence on campus or continue enabling radical organizing under the banner of free speech.
