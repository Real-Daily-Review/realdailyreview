# Colorado GOP Holds Four House Seats as Court Blocks Democratic Redistricting Grab

Colorado Republicans are digging in to defend their four congressional seats—the linchpin of GOP House control nationally—while Democrats' attempt to rig the map for 2028 just hit a legal wall.

The Colorado Supreme Court rejected multiple ballot measures Monday that would have allowed voters to redraw the congressional map to Democrats' advantage, according to the *Denver Post*. The ruling is a significant setback for the left's strategy to flip seats ahead of the next election cycle.

Republicans see the court decision as validation of the current map's legitimacy. "Colorado's four Republican House seats are imperative to the GOP holding on to its majority in D.C.," *Complete Colorado* reports, as GOP representatives prepare their reelection campaigns.

The rejected measures would have fundamentally altered Colorado's political landscape. Democrats argue redistricting reflects voter preferences; Republicans counter that the current map was fairly drawn and that Democrats are simply seeking a do-over because they lost ground in recent cycles.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Democrats attempt a legislative redistricting push in the 2027 session—a move that would require Democratic supermajorities in both chambers and would face fierce GOP resistance.
