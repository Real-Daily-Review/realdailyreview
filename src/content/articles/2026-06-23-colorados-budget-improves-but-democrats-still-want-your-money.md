# Colorado's Improving Finances Won't Stop Democrats From Hiking Taxes

Colorado's fiscal outlook is brightening—but don't expect the Democrat-controlled legislature to ease up on tax-and-spend ambitions. According to Complete Colorado, despite improved revenue projections, Democratic lawmakers are already eyeing new tax increases rather than pausing their fiscal expansion plans.

This pattern is classic progressive governance: revenues rise, but so do spending demands. Republicans argue that a stronger budget picture should mean tax relief for Colorado families and businesses already squeezed by inflation and cost-of-living pressures. Instead, Democrats treat surplus revenue as an invitation to fund new programs and regulatory initiatives.

The timing matters. As Colorado Sun reports, the state is gearing up for a competitive Democratic primary for attorney general—a race that will likely shape how aggressively the next AG pursues progressive litigation and regulation against energy companies, gun manufacturers, and other conservative targets.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the message is clear: a healthier state budget won't translate into relief at the pump or the grocery store. Watch whether Republicans can force meaningful tax cuts or spending restraint before the 2026 election cycle shifts the political calculus.
