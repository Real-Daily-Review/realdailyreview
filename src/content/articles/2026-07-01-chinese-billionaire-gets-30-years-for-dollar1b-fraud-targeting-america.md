# Chinese Billionaire Sentenced for $1B Fraud; Exposed Vulnerabilities in American Political Ecosystem

A Chinese businessman who cultivated ties to American right-wing figures and media has been sentenced to 30 years in prison for defrauding followers of over $1 billion, *The Daily Wire* reports. Miles Guo—who operated under multiple aliases including "Brother Seven"—was convicted of racketeering, securities fraud, money laundering, and defrauding investors through schemes targeting his own supporters.

The case underscores a troubling reality: foreign actors can exploit American political networks and conservative media ecosystems to gain influence and extract wealth from citizens.
