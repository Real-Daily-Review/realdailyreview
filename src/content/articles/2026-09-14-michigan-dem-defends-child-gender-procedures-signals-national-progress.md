# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals National Progressive Push

A Michigan Democratic Senate nominee is openly defending irreversible gender procedures for minors—a position that reveals how far the Left has moved on an issue most Americans oppose.

Abdul El-Sayed, running for U.S. Senate in Michigan, argued Friday that doctors, not government, should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments (Fox News, Daily Wire). He invoked his medical credentials as justification for keeping such decisions outside regulatory oversight.

Republicans argue this position abandons parental rights and common sense. Conservatives contend that children cannot consent to permanent medical interventions and that government has a legitimate role protecting minors from experimental procedures. The GOP has made defending children from gender ideology a centerpiece of 2024 messaging.

El-Sayed's rhetoric—positioning medical autonomy against "government interference"—mirrors talking points echoing through Democratic circles nationwide. Yet polling consistently shows most Americans, including independents, oppose gender procedures for minors without parental involvement.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have largely avoided taking explicit stances on child gender procedures, but the state's progressive legislature has moved to shield providers from legal liability. If national Democrats embrace El-Sayed's framing, Colorado conservatives should expect pressure to expand protections for medical providers—and reduced parental authority.
