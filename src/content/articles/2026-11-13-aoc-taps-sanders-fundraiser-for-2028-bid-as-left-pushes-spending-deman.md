# AOC Gears Up for 2028 Run With Sanders' Top Fundraiser

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is assembling a 2028 presidential apparatus by recruiting the strategist who engineered Bernie Sanders' record-breaking fundraising machine, signaling the Democratic Party's leftward tilt heading into the next cycle.

According to Fox News Politics, progressive groups are actively pushing AOC toward a bid, and she's responded by tapping the operative behind Sanders' fundraising juggernaut. The move underscores how the socialist wing of the Democratic Party—already influential in Congress—is positioning itself for a potential power grab at the national level.

Meanwhile, Sanders and other powerful Democratic lawmakers are demanding a freeze on Iran war funding as a condition of any government spending deal (Daily Caller), a position that reveals deep fractures within the party over foreign policy and defense spending priorities.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: a potential AOC presidency would mean aggressive climate regulations targeting energy development, gun control measures, and massive tax hikes to fund progressive social programs—all policies that would hammer Colorado's oil and gas workers, ranchers, and middle-class taxpayers. The Sanders wing's influence over Democratic spending priorities also signals that any future unified Democratic government would prioritize ideological spending over fiscal restraint.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation—currently aligned with the party's moderate wing—gets pulled further left by these emerging power dynamics.
