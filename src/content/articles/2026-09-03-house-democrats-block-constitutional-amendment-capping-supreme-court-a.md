# House Democrats Block GOP Bid to Constitutionally Cap Supreme Court at Nine Justices

House Democrats just killed a Republican amendment that would have locked the Supreme Court at nine justices—a direct rebuke to progressive court-packing schemes gaining traction on the left.

The measure failed 212-206 on Wednesday, with only one Democrat (Rep. Don Davis of North Carolina) crossing the aisle to support it. Republicans argue the amendment was necessary to prevent Democrats from expanding the Court in retaliation for conservative justices' recent rulings on abortion, gun rights, and other hot-button issues. The Left counters that court expansion is a legitimate response to what they view as Republican obstruction and ideological capture of the bench.

Though the amendment faced long odds—requiring a two-thirds majority to advance—the vote exposed Democratic willingness to keep the door open for packing the Court. Conservatives warn this signals Democrats' intent to weaponize the judiciary if they regain power and face unfavorable rulings.

For Colorado, this matters: a packed Court could overturn Second Amendment protections, expand federal land-use authority over Western states, or impose sweeping environmental regulations that threaten energy development and water rights. Republicans and conservatives should watch whether Democrats introduce formal court-expansion legislation in the next Congress—a move that would crystallize the stakes for 2026 midterms and 2028.
