# Pentagon Releases UFO Documents, Including Apollo 17 Astronaut Accounts

The US Pentagon has published transcripts, video clips, and audio recordings documenting unidentified anomalous phenomena, shedding new light on decades-old sightings by military and space personnel.

Among the released files are accounts from NASA's Apollo 17 crew, who reported seeing three mysterious dots and sparks resembling fireworks during their 1972 mission, according to CBS News. The documents include descriptions of hovering objects and flashing lights observed by various witnesses, per BBC World.

The Pentagon's disclosure represents a rare public accounting of UFO-related incidents that have long circulated in classified channels. The materials span multiple decades and involve observations from both astronauts and military personnel, though the sources do not specify the total volume of documents released or provide details on official explanations for the sightings.

The release comes amid broader Pentagon efforts to address public interest in unidentified aerial phenomena, a term the government now favors over "UFO." Neither source indicates whether the newly published materials contain definitive conclusions about the nature or origin of the observed objects.

Watch for official Pentagon statements clarifying whether these documents represent a comprehensive historical review or a selective disclosure.
