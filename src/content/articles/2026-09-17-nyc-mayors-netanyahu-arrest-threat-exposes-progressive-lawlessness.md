# NYC Mayor's Netanyahu Arrest Threat Exposes Progressive Lawlessness on Full Display

Mamdani initially signaled he would move to arrest Netanyahu on war crimes allegations when the Israeli leader arrives in Manhattan. The Israeli ambassador to the U.N., Danny Danon, fired back by calling for *Mamdani's* arrest instead, noting the mayor has no legal authority to detain a sitting head of state (Washington Examiner). Netanyahu, as a visiting dignitary, enjoys diplomatic protections that shield him from arrest.

Mamdani has since walked back his more inflammatory rhetoric ahead of Netanyahu's arrival (Fox News), but the damage to New York's credibility is done.
