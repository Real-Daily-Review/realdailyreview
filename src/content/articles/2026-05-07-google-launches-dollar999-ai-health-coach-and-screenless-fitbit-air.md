# Google Launches AI Health Coach and Screenless Fitness Tracker

Google is rolling out two new health products: a $9.99-per-month AI health coach powered by Gemini and a $100 screenless Fitbit Air wearable.

The Gemini-powered health coach, launching May 19, will function as a combination fitness coach, sleep expert, and health and wellness advisor, according to TechCrunch. The Fitbit Air, available for preorder now, represents Google's effort to streamline its wearables lineup. Ars Technica reports the device is screenless and will work alongside a new Google Health app designed to replace the existing Fitbit platform.

The moves signal Google's push to integrate artificial intelligence deeper into consumer health monitoring while consolidating its fragmented fitness hardware ecosystem. The subscription model for the AI coach suggests the company is betting on recurring revenue from health-conscious users, while the affordable price point of the Fitbit Air aims to broaden the addressable market.

Watch for user adoption rates of the Gemini health coach and whether the Google Health app successfully migrates the existing Fitbit user base without significant churn.
