# Dense Fog Advisory Blankets Denver Metro; Storm System Brewing for November

Dense fog is snarling Friday morning commutes across the Denver metro and eastern Colorado, with a Dense Fog Advisory in effect as visibility drops sharply (9News Denver). Drivers should expect changing road conditions and reduced sight lines through the morning hours.

However, meteorologists are already tracking a significant storm system moving toward Colorado as November begins, which could bring substantial weather changes early next week (KDVR Fox31).

Coloradans should prepare vehicles for the transition—check wipers, lights, and brakes before the next system arrives. The shift from October's calm finish to November's potential storm activity underscores why Colorado drivers need to stay alert during seasonal weather transitions.
