# Trump Administration Prepares Largest Mass Visa Revocation in U.S. History

The Trump administration is moving to revoke up to 200,000 visas issued to foreigners who later sought asylum, marking what would be the largest single mass revocation in American history, according to 9News Denver and The Daily Wire.

Officials say the crackdown targets what they view as systematic abuse of the immigration system—visa holders who entered legally but then filed asylum claims, effectively circumventing normal immigration procedures. The State Department is expected to announce the effort imminently.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue this closes a loophole that has allowed migrants to exploit dual pathways into the country. Tightening asylum access aligns with Trump's stated immigration enforcement priorities and appeals to voters concerned about border security and rule-of-law.

**The opposition:** Legal challenges are expected. Critics will likely argue the revocations violate due process and international treaty obligations, though the sources provided do not detail specific objections.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's agricultural and hospitality sectors rely partly on visa-holding workers. Revocations could tighten labor markets in rural counties and ski towns, potentially raising wage pressures or forcing employers to accelerate automation.

Watch whether Colorado's business community—particularly agriculture and tourism—pushes back on implementation timelines or seeks exemptions for seasonal workers.
