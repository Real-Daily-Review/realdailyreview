# Trump Administration Navigates Fragile Middle East Ceasefire as Iran Tensions Simmer

The Trump administration is walking a razor's edge in the Middle East, balancing diplomatic restraint with credible military deterrence as a 60-day ceasefire in Lebanon hangs by a thread.

Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio are executing a two-track strategy, according to the Washington Examiner. Vance is taking a measured approach in negotiations with Iranian delegations, while Rubio maintains a harder line—signaling that the administration won't tolerate Iranian provocation. The first week of the ceasefire has yielded mixed results, with both gains and setbacks.

But conservatives warn the situation remains precarious. National Review notes that as Lebanon crises continue to mount, Iran will eventually test American resolve with a provocation that could reignite full-scale conflict. The real question: will the Trump team's combination of soft talk and implicit military threat actually deter Tehran, or is this just a temporary pause before the next escalation?
