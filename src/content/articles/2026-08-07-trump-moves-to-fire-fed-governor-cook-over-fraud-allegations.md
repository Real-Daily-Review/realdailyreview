# Trump Administration Moves to Remove Fed Governor Over Fraud Allegations

The Trump administration is escalating its push to fire Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook, sending her a formal warning letter demanding she answer mortgage fraud allegations within three weeks (Washington Examiner). Cook denies the charges, but the White House has signaled it believes there is "sufficient reason" to remove her from the central bank.

Cook has publicly supported raising interest rates—a position that puts her at odds with Trump's preference for lower rates to boost economic growth. Republicans argue her removal would align the Fed with policies more favorable to business expansion and job creation. Democrats counter that ousting her would undermine the Fed's independence and represent political interference in monetary policy.

For Colorado, the stakes involve mortgage availability and borrowing costs for homebuyers and businesses. If Cook is removed and replaced with a Trump-aligned governor, Colorado's real estate market and small-business lending could shift based on the Fed's interest-rate trajectory.

The three-week deadline means a decision is imminent. Conservatives should watch whether Trump follows through and whether Senate Republicans support confirmation of a replacement who favors lower rates and pro-growth monetary policy.
