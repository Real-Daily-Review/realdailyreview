# RFK Jr. Pushes 'Make America Healthy Again' While Colorado Fire Crews Warn Against Turkey Frying Dangers

Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. is using the holiday season to promote his "Make America Healthy Again" agenda, teaming up with Indigenous chef Ray Naranjo in a cooking tutorial for *The Real Food Show* series focused on transforming Thanksgiving leftovers into traditional Native American turkey tamales (Washington Examiner). The move reflects Kennedy's broader push to reshape federal health policy around whole foods and traditional cooking methods—a departure from decades of government dietary guidance.

Meanwhile, Colorado fire crews are sending a more urgent message this Thanksgiving: deep-frying turkeys kills. North Metro Fire Rescue District demonstrated Tuesday how quickly an improperly handled turkey fryer becomes a dangerous inferno, showing both safe and catastrophically unsafe cooking methods (KDVR Fox31 Denver). The demonstration underscores a real public safety issue as families prepare holiday meals.

Kennedy's food-focused health initiative aligns with conservative skepticism of processed foods and federal overreach in dietary recommendations, though his broader HHS agenda remains controversial among conservatives over vaccine policy and other regulatory matters. The fire department's safety message, by contrast, is straightforward: follow instructions or risk burning down your home.

Colorado homeowners should heed the fire crews' warning—turkey fryer injuries spike annually during the holidays, and one preventable accident can ruin far more than Thanksgiving dinner.
