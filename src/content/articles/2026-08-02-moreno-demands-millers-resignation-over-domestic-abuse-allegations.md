# Ohio GOP Family Feud: Moreno Demands Miller's Resignation Over Abuse Allegations

A bitter Republican civil war is playing out in Ohio as Sen. Bernie Moreno publicly demands that Rep. Max Miller—his former son-in-law—resign from Congress and seek "serious psychological help" (Fox News, Washington Examiner). 

Moreno's daughter has accused Miller of domestic abuse, including allegations that he threw scalding water on her (Daily Caller). Rather than address the accusations directly, Miller reportedly claimed his ex-wife struggles with "significant mental health challenges"—a counterattack that prompted Moreno's forceful response.

In a statement posted on X, Moreno said Miller "should not" be serving in Congress and called his behavior "dangerous" (Just The News). The Ohio senator said he "holds my breath every minute" over his daughter's safety, signaling this is deeply personal, not merely political theater.

This family implosion exposes a real problem: when serious allegations of abuse emerge, they demand accountability regardless of party affiliation. Republicans rightly expect their own members to meet basic standards of conduct. Miller's attempt to deflect by attacking his accuser's mental health only deepens the credibility crisis.

**Watch for whether House Republican leadership takes action or lets this fester—silence would be deafening.**
