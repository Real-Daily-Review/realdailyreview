# Trump Endorses Florida Republican Amid Amnesty Controversy

President Trump has endorsed Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-FL) for reelection, but the move has ignited conservative backlash over her immigration stance. Trump praised Salazar as "doing a truly fantastic job" representing Florida's 27th District, according to a Truth Social post reported by *The Daily Wire*.

The endorsement, however, masks a deeper divide within the GOP. *The Daily Caller* characterizes Salazar as a "mass amnesty cheerleader" who "routinely undermines" Trump's hardline immigration agenda. Conservative critics argue that her push for amnesty policies directly contradicts Trump's core message on border security and immigration enforcement—a signature issue that energized his base.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans skeptical of Salazar contend that endorsing amnesty advocates weakens the party's credibility on immigration. They argue Trump's endorsement sends a mixed signal to voters who elected him partly on promises of strict border enforcement and deportation of illegal aliens.

**What's at Stake:** This endorsement reveals fractures in the GOP over immigration policy heading into the midterms. For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the outcome matters: immigration policy directly affects labor markets, public services costs, and school enrollment in border-adjacent states.

**Watch Next:** Whether Trump's base accepts this endorsement or whether primary challengers emerge to contest Salazar from the right on immigration.
