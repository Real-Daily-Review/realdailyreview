# WNBA's Gender Ideology Push Risks Legal Backlash Over Women's Sports Fairness

The WNBA is courting serious legal exposure by silencing fans who support women's sports fairness, according to the Alliance Defending Freedom. The league faces mounting pressure after clashes between pro-woman advocates and trans activists outside an Indiana Fever game, and after the Toronto Tempo debuted a "non-binary" turtle mascot—widely seen as a jab at Fever guard Sophie Cunningham, who has publicly questioned transgender inclusion policies in women's sports (Breitbart).

ADF warned WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert that suppressing fan speech supporting sex-based fairness in athletics could expose the league to legal liability under First Amendment and civil rights grounds. The organization cited two recent incidents involving Cunningham supporters being confronted or marginalized by league operations.
