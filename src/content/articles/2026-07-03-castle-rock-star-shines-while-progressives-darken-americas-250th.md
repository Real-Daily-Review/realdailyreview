# Castle Rock Star Shines Bright While Progressive Politicians Darken America's 250th

Colorado's Castle Rock will celebrate America's quarter-millennium the right way: with the iconic Star glowing red, white, and blue for the nation's 250th Independence Day and Colorado's 150th anniversary (9News Denver).

Meanwhile, progressive politicians are using the milestone to trash the country. New York City socialist Mayor Zohran Mamdani delivered a speech painting America as a dark, sinister place—invoking immigrant struggles while framing the nation's founding through a lens of victimhood rather than achievement (The Daily Wire). Internal edits to his remarks reveal the left's obsession with performative language: staff debated whether to use the word "slave" and demanded black people be mentioned, suggesting the speech was focus-grouped for maximum guilt-tripping rather than genuine reflection (New York Post).

The contrast is stark. Coloradans lighting up the Castle Rock Star are celebrating genuine accomplishment and freedom. Progressive activists are using America's birthday to lecture citizens about national sins.

Conservatives argue this reflects a fundamental divide: one side honors what America has achieved and aspires to do better; the other weaponizes history to delegitimize the nation itself.
