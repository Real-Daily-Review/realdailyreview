# Federal Subpoena of Brennan Signals DOJ Scrutiny of Trump-Era Intelligence Officials

Former CIA Director John Brennan has been subpoenaed to testify before a federal grand jury in a Justice Department probe examining whether government officials conspired to deprive former President Trump of his civil rights, according to Fox News Politics.

The subpoena marks an escalation in DOJ scrutiny of intelligence community conduct during the Trump administration. Conservatives have long argued that senior officials—including Brennan—weaponized federal agencies against Trump, particularly regarding the Russia investigation and subsequent impeachment proceedings. Republicans contend the probe signals potential accountability for what they view as institutional abuse of power.

Meanwhile, the Washington Examiner reports that Missouri's former election integrity director, Nick La Strada, has sued Secretary of State Denny Hoskins after being fired. La Strada alleges he was terminated for raising concerns about Hoskins's conduct and for maintaining "too much integrity"—a case that underscores conservative concerns about political interference in election administration.
