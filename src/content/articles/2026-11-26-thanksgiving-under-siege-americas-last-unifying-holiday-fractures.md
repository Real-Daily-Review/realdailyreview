# Thanksgiving Amid Division: America's Holiday Faces Culture War Headwinds

Thanksgiving remains one of America's few unifying national traditions—but even that is under strain in an era of radical cultural division.

President Trump marked the holiday Thursday with a Truth Social message wishing "ALL AMERICAN PATRIOTS WHO HAVE WORKED SO HARD TO MAKE OUR COUNTRY GREAT" a happy Thanksgiving, while taking an apparently sarcastic jab at the "Radical Far Left" (Washington Examiner). The gesture underscores how thoroughly partisan America has become.

Conservatives argue the holiday's original purpose—collective gratitude to God—has been systematically stripped away. As the Washington Examiner's opinion section notes, Thanksgiving was established as a religious observance, yet many Americans now reduce it to football, family meals, and consumerism, divorced from spiritual meaning or acknowledgment of authority beyond government.

The Daily Wire points to O. Henry's century-old short story "Two Thanksgiving Day Gentlemen" as capturing something essential about American character: the impulse toward charity and human connection that transcends class and circumstance. That spirit feels increasingly foreign in 2024.

For Colorado families gathering this week, the contrast is stark. While Coloradans still value the holiday's traditions of family and gratitude, the cultural Left's relentless secularization and politicization of American institutions—including holidays—represents exactly the kind of government-enabled cultural overreach conservatives reject.

Watch whether next year's Thanksgiving messaging becomes even more explicitly partisan.
