# California Democrat Wins Swalwell Seat; Colorado Should Note the AIPAC Spending Playbook

Democrat Aisha Wahab won California's 14th Congressional District special election—the seat vacated by Eric Swalwell after sexual misconduct allegations forced his resignation and derailed his gubernatorial run. Wahab secured 53.1% of the vote, according to the Associated Press, after winning the primary by 26 points (Washington Examiner).

The race drew national attention for a different reason: the American Israel Public Affairs Committee (AIPAC) poured $4.4 million into the contest opposing Wahab, yet she prevailed anyway (Washington Examiner). The outcome underscores a broader reality for conservatives: mega-donor spending doesn't guarantee electoral success, and grassroots energy can overcome well-funded opposition.

For Colorado, the lesson cuts both ways. Republicans should study how Wahab's coalition mobilized despite AIPAC's firepower—turnout and message discipline matter more than cash alone. Meanwhile, Colorado Democrats eyeing 2024 races should note that even in deep-blue California districts, a well-organized challenger with strong local support can overcome establishment money.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether national Democratic groups now shift resources toward defending vulnerable House seats in swing states, including Colorado's 3rd and 8th districts.
