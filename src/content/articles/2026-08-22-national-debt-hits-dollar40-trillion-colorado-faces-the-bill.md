# National Debt Hits $40 Trillion—A Reckoning Colorado Taxpayers Can't Ignore

The federal government has crossed a fiscal Rubicon: the national debt has surged past $40 trillion, a milestone that should terrify every American concerned about fiscal sanity.

The Washington Examiner notes the figure is so staggering that our brains struggle to compute it—yet the mainstream media has largely yawned. National Review calls it "America's $40 Trillion Nightmare," and they're right. This isn't abstract economics; it's a direct threat to Colorado's economy, retirement security, and the purchasing power of every paycheck.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans and fiscal hawks argue that runaway federal spending—driven by both parties but accelerated under progressive budget priorities—has created an unsustainable trajectory. Without serious spending cuts, entitlement reform, and a return to constitutional limits on government, inflation, higher interest rates, and eventual currency devaluation are inevitable. Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small businesses will bear the brunt when the bill comes due.

**Democrats' Response:** The left typically argues that the debt-to-GDP ratio matters more than the raw number, and that strategic spending on infrastructure and social programs drives growth. They resist spending cuts as "austerity."

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state leaders demand that Washington address this crisis—or continue enabling it with unfunded mandates and regulatory overreach that bleeds state coffers.
