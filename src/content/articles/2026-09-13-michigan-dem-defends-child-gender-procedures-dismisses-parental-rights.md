# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Broader Left-Wing Medical Overreach

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's unapologetic defense of child gender-transition procedures reveals the progressive playbook: invoke "medical expertise" to bypass parental rights and democratic oversight.

Abdul El-Sayed, running for U.S. Senate in Michigan, told students at Michigan State University that doctors—not government—should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments (Fox News, The Daily Wire). He cited his own medical credentials as justification, arguing physicians should operate without state interference.

Republicans counter that this framework strips parents of authority over their own children's medical decisions and sidesteps legitimate questions about the long-term effects of experimental procedures on minors. Conservatives argue that when progressives invoke "medical authority," they're often shielding ideological policy from democratic accountability.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have pushed similar language in state health debates, framing parental notification and age-restriction bills as "government interference in medicine." This rhetoric is spreading across Democratic campaigns nationwide.

El-Sayed's comments also expose a deeper left-wing strategy: delegitimize elected representatives' role in setting medical policy for minors by claiming only credentialed experts should decide. It's a backdoor way to entrench progressive positions on contested medical issues without voter consent.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt El-Sayed's framing in upcoming legislative fights over parental rights and pediatric gender medicine.
