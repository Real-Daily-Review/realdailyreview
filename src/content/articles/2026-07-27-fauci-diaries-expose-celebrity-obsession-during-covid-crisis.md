# Fauci Diaries Expose Self-Promotion While Americans Died of COVID

Newly released diary entries from Dr. Anthony Fauci reveal he was preoccupied with his own fame and celebrity status even as COVID-19 death tolls climbed—a damning portrait of misplaced priorities during a national health crisis.

According to entries released by Senate Homeland Security Committee Chairman Rand Paul (R-KY), Fauci documented calling himself "the most famous person in the country" while pandemic deaths mounted (Fox News). The diaries also show that on February 1, 2020, Fauci participated in a high-level conference call where scientists debated whether the Wuhan lab had intentionally engineered the COVID virus—contradicting his later public dismissals of the lab-leak theory (Just The News).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Fauci's diary entries expose a bureaucrat more concerned with media stardom than scientific integrity or transparent public health guidance. The lab-leak discussion documented in his own hand undermines his credibility on origins transparency and raises questions about what else he withheld from Congress and the American people.

Coloradans who endured lockdowns, business closures, and school shutdowns deserve answers about whether their trusted health advisor was leveling with them or chasing cable news appearances. Watch for whether Congress demands Fauci's full testimony under oath and whether media outlets that lionized him will reckon with these revelations.
