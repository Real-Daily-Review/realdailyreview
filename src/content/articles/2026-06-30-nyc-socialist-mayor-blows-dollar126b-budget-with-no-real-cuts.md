# Socialist NYC Mayor Blows Past Budget Reality With $126B Spending Spree

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's handshake deal with City Council Speaker Julie Menin on a $126 billion budget proves that progressive politicians still don't understand fiscal discipline—even when facing a genuine crisis.

The budget represents an 8.5% spending increase from last year's roughly $116 billion plan, according to the New York Post, with no significant cuts despite the city's acknowledged deficit crisis. Mamdani claimed the deal "balanced this budget without resorting to austerity" while "restoring honesty to the City's finances"—language that rings hollow when spending balloons nearly 10% year-over-year (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire).

The real tell: the NYPD got left out entirely. The Daily Wire reports Mamdani "visibly left out accommodations for the city's police force," even as crime remains a defining urban crisis. This is the progressive playbook—protect government spending, protect public-sector unions, and let public safety slide.

**The Colorado angle:** This is what happens when one-party rule meets socialist economics. Coloradans should watch whether our own Democratic-controlled legislature starts mimicking this spend-and-claim-balance approach to state budgets.
