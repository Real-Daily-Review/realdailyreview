# Socialist Wave Hits NYC Primaries—Colorado Should Take Note

Democratic socialists swept all three New York City congressional primaries this week, a stark reminder that the radical left's grip on the Democratic Party is tightening just as America marks 250 years of constitutional liberty.

According to the Washington Examiner, socialist candidates backed by figures like Zohran Mamdani—including Darializa Avila Chevalier, Brad Lander, and Claire Valdez—dominated Tuesday's races across multiple House districts. The Daily Wire characterized the results as a "shudder down the spine of America," signaling the transformation of once-reliably center-left urban politics toward explicit socialism.

The wins underscore a troubling national trend: Democratic primary voters are increasingly embracing candidates who openly advocate for government control of markets, wealth redistribution, and expansive state power—precisely the policies Colorado conservatives have fought to keep out of state government.

While New York City's collapse into socialist governance may seem distant, Colorado Democrats have imported similar ideological playbooks: aggressive tax hikes, regulatory overreach on energy development, and gun-control measures that treat constitutional rights as negotiable. The NYC results show what happens when the left goes unchallenged in Democratic primaries.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates begin openly embracing "democratic socialism" rhetoric in 2024 and 2026 races—and whether Colorado Republicans effectively contrast free-market, pro-growth conservatism against this expanding socialist influence.
