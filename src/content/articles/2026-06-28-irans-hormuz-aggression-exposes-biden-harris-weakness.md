# Iran's Hormuz Aggression Exposes Biden-Harris Weakness on Foreign Policy

The Biden-Harris administration's failed appeasement strategy toward Iran is now costing American security and Colorado energy prices at the pump.

Iranian forces launched drone attacks on U.S. bases and moved to control the Strait of Hormuz—a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments—signaling Tehran's contempt for diplomatic overtures (National Review). The escalation underscores what conservatives have long warned: negotiating with a regime whose leadership chants "Death to America" is folly, not statesmanship.

The Daily Wire reports that Iranian citizens themselves are demanding an end to ayatollah rule, yet the administration doubled down on engagement rather than backing those pro-freedom voices or maintaining deterrent strength.

For Colorado, this matters directly. Instability in the Strait threatens oil supplies and drives up energy costs for families and businesses already hammered by inflation. Colorado's energy sector—which supports thousands of jobs and state revenues—depends on stable global markets. Weakness invites aggression; aggression invites pain at the pump.

Republicans argue the administration's Iran policy prioritized ideology over American interests and regional stability. Democrats counter that diplomacy prevents wider conflict, though Iranian actions suggest Tehran reads restraint as permission.

Watch whether the next administration reverses course with credible deterrence and energy independence policies that insulate Colorado from Middle East volatility.
