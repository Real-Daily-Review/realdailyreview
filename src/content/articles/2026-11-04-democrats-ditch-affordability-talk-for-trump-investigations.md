# Democrats' Post-Election Pivot: Investigations Over Affordability

Colorado families were promised relief from inflation and soaring costs. Instead, Democrats are signaling their real priority: launching investigations into the Trump administration.

Within hours of securing House control, Democratic leaders—including Rep. Robert Garcia (D-CA)—began threatening probes rather than proposing solutions to the affordability crisis that dominated their campaign messaging, according to the *Washington Examiner* and *Daily Wire*. Republicans argue this reveals Democrats' true agenda: partisan theater instead of kitchen-table economics.

For Colorado voters who backed Democrats hoping for lower energy costs, cheaper groceries, and relief from state and federal tax burdens, the message is stark. While the state grapples with rising housing costs and energy prices tied to progressive regulations, House Democrats are preparing show trials instead of tackling the policies driving inflation.

Conservatives say this bait-and-switch—campaign on affordability, govern through investigation—exposes the left's real priorities: power consolidation and Trump-focused grievance politics, not the economic relief working families desperately need.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation (currently holding five House seats) joins these investigation efforts or pushes back on behalf of constituents demanding actual solutions to the cost-of-living crisis.
