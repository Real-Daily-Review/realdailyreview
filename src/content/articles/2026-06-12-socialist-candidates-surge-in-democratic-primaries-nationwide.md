# Socialist Candidates Surge in Democratic Primaries as Trump Warns of Federal Intervention

The Democratic Party's embrace of openly socialist candidates is accelerating, with New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani backing congressional candidate Darializa Avila Chevalier despite resurfaced posts attacking the U.S., police, and borders (Fox News). Meanwhile, Washington, D.C.'s Democratic primary features self-described socialist Janeese Lewis George, prompting President Trump to vow federal intervention if she wins (Washington Examiner).

Republicans argue these candidacies expose the progressive wing's radicalization. Conservatives worry that socialist economics—higher taxes, expanded government control, and anti-police rhetoric—threaten economic growth and public safety. The endorsements from establishment Democrats like Mamdani signal the party's leftward drift, not a fringe movement.

Colorado voters should note the pattern: what starts in New York and D.C. Democratic politics often reaches Colorado's urban centers within election cycles. Denver's city council and state legislature have already embraced similar progressive-socialist rhetoric on housing, policing, and energy policy. If these candidates gain national traction, expect pressure on Colorado Democrats to adopt their platform planks—higher taxes, aggressive climate mandates, and anti-energy development policies that would hit Colorado's oil and gas workers and rural communities.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic leadership distances itself from or amplifies these national socialist endorsements heading into 2026.
