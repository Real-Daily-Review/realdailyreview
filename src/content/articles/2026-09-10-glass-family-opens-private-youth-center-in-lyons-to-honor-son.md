# New Lyons Center Honors Christian Glass, Offers Youth Space

A new community center in Lyons is opening to serve local teens and young adults while memorializing Christian Glass, the 22-year-old killed during a 2022 police encounter. Sally and Simon Glass, Christian's parents, converted an old diner into an art and gathering space designed to give young people a place to create and connect (Colorado Sun, KDVR Fox31 Denver).

The center represents a private, community-driven response to a tragedy that sparked significant debate over police use-of-force protocols. Rather than waiting for government intervention or taxpayer-funded solutions, the Glass family invested their own resources into addressing a local need—a model conservatives typically champion as more efficient and accountable than bureaucratic alternatives.

The space offers arts programming and a casual hangout environment, filling what organizers say is a gap in youth services in the Boulder County town. The initiative reflects grassroots problem-solving: citizens identifying a community need and addressing it directly, without regulatory overhead or government mandates.

For Colorado conservatives, this is a textbook example of private philanthropy and family initiative solving social problems more effectively than government programs could. Watch whether the center becomes a model for other communities seeking youth engagement without expanding the public sector payroll.
