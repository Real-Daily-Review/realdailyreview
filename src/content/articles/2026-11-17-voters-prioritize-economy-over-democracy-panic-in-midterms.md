# Voters Care About Wallets, Not Democracy Panic—and Colorado Should Too

Midterm exit polls reveal what conservatives have long known: Americans care far more about their pocketbooks than Democratic doomsaying about "threats to democracy." According to the *Washington Examiner*, when voters were asked to name the single most important issue facing the country, the economy topped the list—not the state of democracy, which Democrats spent months warning was on life support.

The contrast is stark. While progressive activists and media allies hammered the "democracy in crisis" narrative heading into the 2022 midterms, ordinary voters were focused on inflation, jobs, and the cost of living. Crime, immigration, healthcare, and foreign policy also ranked higher in voter priorities than abstract constitutional concerns.

For Colorado, this matters enormously. State Democrats have pushed costly regulations, energy mandates, and tax hikes under the banner of various progressive causes. But if voters across the country are signaling that economic competence and fiscal responsibility matter most, Colorado Republicans have a clear opening: make the case that Democratic policies—from renewable energy mandates driving up electricity costs to regulatory overreach strangling small business—are the real threat to prosperity.

**Watch for how Colorado Democrats respond to 2024 messaging that leads with kitchen-table economics rather than culture-war rhetoric.**
