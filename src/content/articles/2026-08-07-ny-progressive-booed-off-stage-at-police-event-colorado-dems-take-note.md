# New York Progressive Gets Booed Off Stage at Pro-Police Event—A Warning for Colorado Democrats

A New York state legislator hostile to law enforcement got run off stage in under a minute at an NYPD National Night Out event on Staten Island, with crowds chanting him down as he attempted to speak (Fox News). Zohran Mamdani, who has faced sustained criticism for his anti-police rhetoric and his dismissal of an incident where activists pelted NYPD officers with snowballs, "walked off in shame" according to video evidence (Just The News).

The public rebuke signals growing backlash against progressive politicians who campaign against police funding and support. Mamdani's attempt to appear at a community policing event—presumably to rehabilitate his image—backfired spectacularly when constituents made clear they reject his anti-law-enforcement record.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have increasingly aligned with the national progressive movement on police reform and defunding rhetoric. As crime remains a top voter concern, this New York moment illustrates the political peril of being perceived as hostile to law enforcement. Denver and Boulder have both grappled with police staffing shortages and public safety crises partly driven by progressive anti-police sentiment.

Republicans and law-and-order conservatives argue that voters are rejecting the soft-on-crime agenda, and that Colorado Democrats should take note before pushing similar policies in 2024 and beyond.

Watch whether Colorado progressive candidates distance themselves from defund-the-police messaging ahead of the next election cycle.
