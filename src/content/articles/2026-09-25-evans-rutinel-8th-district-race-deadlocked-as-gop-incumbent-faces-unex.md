# Colorado's 8th District Tightens as Evans Faces Unexpectedly Competitive Reelection

Republican incumbent Gabe Evans is locked in a dead heat with Democrat Manny Rutinel in Colorado's 8th Congressional District, according to a new poll that signals trouble for both camps heading into the final stretch.

The survey, conducted by a Republican firm on behalf of a conservative nonprofit backing Evans, shows the race essentially tied—a warning sign for an incumbent who should be running stronger in a district stretching from Denver's northern suburbs through Weld and Larimer counties (CPR News). The 8th has become a battleground as suburban growth and shifting demographics reshape the political landscape across northern Colorado.

Republicans argue Evans' record of supporting limited government and energy development in oil-and-gas-rich Weld County should resonate with district voters. Democrats counter that Rutinel offers a fresh alternative on kitchen-table issues like healthcare costs and housing affordability.

The tightness of the race underscores how competitive Colorado's congressional map has become. Even in a district where Republicans hold the seat, complacency is no longer an option. Conservatives should watch whether Evans can consolidate GOP support and turn out rural voters in Weld—traditionally Republican strongholds that could prove decisive.
