# U.S. Strikes Iran for Ninth Straight Night After Killing of American Troops

The Trump administration is hitting back hard: the U.S. military completed its ninth consecutive night of strikes against Iranian military targets Sunday, following the deaths of three American service members in Iranian attacks on a Jordan base last week.

According to CENTCOM, American forces targeted Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps positions responsible for the July 17 assault, along with Iran's ability to threaten commercial shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. President Trump said the strikes were mounted "very hard" to honor the fallen troops.

The escalating campaign marks a forceful response to Iranian aggression—a stark contrast to the Biden administration's approach of restraint and diplomacy. Republicans and defense hawks argue that sustained military pressure deters further Iranian provocations and protects American personnel abroad. Critics on the left worry the strikes could spiral into broader regional conflict, though the Trump team frames the operation as proportional and focused on military infrastructure.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado hosts significant military installations, including Fort Carson and Peterson Space Force Base, making force readiness and troop safety direct concerns for the state's defense community and families.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Iran escalates further or whether sustained U.S. pressure forces Tehran back to the negotiating table.
