# Democrats Push AI Superintelligence Ban—Colorado Tech Workers Should Worry

Colorado's growing tech sector faces a new threat from the left: a Democratic push to ban artificial superintelligence before it even exists.

Ten House Democrats, including Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) and Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA), are backing legislation introduced by Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) that would prohibit AI models exceeding "human cognitive performance," according to the *Washington Examiner*. The bill defines the threshold so broadly it could strangle innovation in its crib.

Republicans and free-market conservatives argue the ban represents typical progressive overreach—regulating technology Democrats don't understand while ceding AI leadership to China and other competitors. The vague definition of "superintelligence" would give bureaucrats unchecked power to kill research projects, they contend, and would chill investment in the very breakthroughs that could solve real problems.

Colorado has built a competitive advantage in aerospace, energy tech, and software development. A federal AI superintelligence ban would hamstring startups and established firms alike, pushing talent and capital to friendlier jurisdictions. The state's universities and research institutions—already struggling with federal overregulation—would face new compliance burdens.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation, particularly Democratic members, will cave to Sanders-backed pressure or defend the state's tech economy.
