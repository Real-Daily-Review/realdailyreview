# Colorado's Amendment 87 Faces Conservative Pushback Over Hidden Tax Hike

Colorado voters face a deceptive ballot measure that promises to soak the rich but will inevitably hammer middle-class Coloradans through bracket creep, conservatives warn.

Amendment 87 would raise taxes on the top 3% of earners and 5% of businesses, with proponents claiming the measure generates $2.7 billion annually (Colorado Sun). But Complete Colorado argues the real danger lies in how inflation will gradually push ordinary Coloradans into higher tax brackets over time—turning what looks like a targeted levy into a stealth tax increase on everyone.

**Republicans counter** that Colorado's economy doesn't need another burden on job creators and successful earners. The state already ranks among the highest-tax states in the region, and businesses considering relocation or expansion will look elsewhere. Middle-class families, meanwhile, will eventually pay more as their wages rise with inflation, even if they weren't initially targeted.

**Democrats and the left** frame this as necessary revenue for schools, transportation, and social services—classic progressive redistribution rhetoric that ignores the economic drag of higher tax rates.

The amendment's bracket-creep mechanics represent exactly the kind of government sleight-of-hand that erodes taxpayer trust. Watch whether Colorado's business community mobilizes against this measure before the November vote.
