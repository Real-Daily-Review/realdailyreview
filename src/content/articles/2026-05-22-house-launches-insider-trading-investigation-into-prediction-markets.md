# House Launches Insider Trading Probe Into Prediction Markets

House Oversight and Government Reform Committee Chair James Comer (R-Ky.) launched an investigation Friday into prediction market platforms Kalshi and Polymarket, according to The Hill, seeking to determine whether users are trading on nonpublic or classified information.

Comer sent letters to Kalshi CEO Tarek Mansour and Polymarket CEO Shayne Coplan requesting details on how the platforms guard against insider trading, according to CBS News. The investigation also examines whether the companies are exploiting regulatory loopholes, per the Washington Examiner.

The probe focuses on the "adequacy of company safeguards to prevent access to offshore" information, though the full scope of Comer's concerns remains unclear from the initial letters. Prediction markets have grown in prominence, allowing users to bet on outcomes of elections, geopolitical events, and other developments—raising questions about whether traders with access to sensitive government information could gain unfair advantages.

Neither Kalshi nor Polymarket has publicly responded to the investigation announcement. Watch for the companies' formal responses to Comer's information requests and whether other congressional committees join the inquiry.
