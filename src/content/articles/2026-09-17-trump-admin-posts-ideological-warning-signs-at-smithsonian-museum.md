# Trump Administration Signals War on Woke Smithsonian Messaging

The Trump administration is planning to post ideological warning signs at the Smithsonian Institution's National Museum of American History, signaling a direct confrontation with what conservatives view as progressive capture of federal institutions.

According to *Just The News*, the administration will advise visitors that "the leadership of the Smithsonian and NMAH have altered the museum's role from accurately and coherently telling America's story to using the museum as a tool to advance personal, radical ideologies." The move reflects a broader conservative push to reclaim federal agencies from what Republicans argue is entrenched progressive activism.

This battle over institutional messaging matters beyond Washington. Colorado taxpayers fund these federal museums through their tax dollars—money conservatives argue should support objective history, not ideological agendas. The Smithsonian's shift toward identity-focused narratives has drawn fire from Republicans who contend museums should educate, not indoctrinate.

The administration's approach is blunt: rather than quietly reform curatorial practices, it's publicly flagging the problem for visitors. Democrats will likely cry censorship; Republicans counter that transparency about institutional bias is overdue accountability.

**Watch whether Congress moves to defund or restructure the Smithsonian's leadership—and whether other federal agencies face similar warnings.**
