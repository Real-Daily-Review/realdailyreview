# GOP Wife's Endorsement Shows Party Unity Trumps Personal Drama in Texas Senate Race

Angela Paxton's decision to back the full Republican ticket—including her estranged husband Ken Paxton—demonstrates that conservative voters and candidates prioritize principle over personal grievance when elections matter. Despite filing for divorce last year, the Texas state senator released an advertisement Saturday affirming her support for the GOP slate, framing the choice as backing "the whole team" rather than individual personalities (Breitbart, Daily Wire). 

The move underscores a disciplined Republican approach to November: party unity and platform alignment outweigh internal family conflict. Paxton faces Democrat James Talarico in what observers describe as a competitive race, making her public endorsement strategically valuable.

For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: when the stakes are high—court appointments, energy policy, tax rates, Second Amendment protections—personal disputes yield to electoral necessity. Republicans have historically struggled with circular firing squads; the Paxton family's public unity, however strained privately, signals maturity.
