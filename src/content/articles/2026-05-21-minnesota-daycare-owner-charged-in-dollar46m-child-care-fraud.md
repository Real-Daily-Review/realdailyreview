# Minnesota Daycare Owner Charged in $4.6M Fraud Scheme

A Minnesota daycare owner was charged Wednesday with defrauding the state's Child Care Assistance Program in a scheme involving millions in fraudulent claims, according to federal prosecutors.

Fahima Egeh Mahamud, who operated the Future Leaders Early Learning Center in Minneapolis, is accused of filing bogus claims to receive $4.6 million in government grants purportedly to serve meals to low-income children—meals that were allegedly never provided, according to the Washington Examiner.

The charges were unsealed as prosecutors allege Mahamud attempted to flee the United States just two days after closing the daycare center, CBS News reported. Federal authorities did not specify whether the alleged flight attempt was successful or prevented.

The case appears connected to a broader fraud investigation into Minnesota's child care assistance programs. Mahamud's name has circulated in viral social media content related to the "Feeding Our Futures" scandal, which involved multiple daycare operators accused of similar schemes.

The charges carry significant penalties, though prosecutors have not yet disclosed the specific counts or sentencing recommendations. Watch for updates on whether additional charges are filed against other operators or associates, and whether Mahamud remains in custody pending trial.
