# Denver Shooting Claims Another Life as Crime Continues Unchecked

Denver's violent crime problem claimed another victim Thursday night when a passenger was shot and killed in a vehicle in the 4100 block of West Custer Place, according to Denver Police and reports from 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The shooting is the latest in a troubling pattern of gun violence plaguing Colorado's capital city. While Democrats and progressive city leaders have pushed for stricter gun control measures and expanded social programs, violent crime continues to surge—raising hard questions about whether those policies are working.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Denver's approach has failed: catch-and-release criminal justice policies, reduced police funding, and lenient prosecution have emboldened criminals while law-abiding Coloradans and visitors face escalating danger. They contend that enforcing existing laws, supporting police, and holding criminals accountable—not restricting Second Amendment rights of law-abiding citizens—is the path forward.

The Denver Police Department is investigating the incident. No suspect information has been released.

Colorado taxpayers and business owners should watch whether city leadership will finally acknowledge that soft-on-crime policies aren't reducing violence—and whether they'll shift toward public safety over ideology.
