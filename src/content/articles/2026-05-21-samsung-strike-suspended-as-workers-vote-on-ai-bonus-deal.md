# Samsung Strike Suspended as Workers Vote on Deal; UN Court Affirms Right to Strike

Samsung workers have suspended a planned walkout to vote on a tentative labor agreement that includes demands for an artificial intelligence bonus, according to BBC World. The strike, originally scheduled to begin Thursday, is on hold pending union members' decision on the deal.

The development comes as the International Court of Justice issued a landmark advisory opinion affirming that workers' right to strike is protected under a key United Nations labor treaty, PBS NewsHour reports. The UN's top court found that a cornerstone labor convention safeguards workers' ability to walk off the job.

The timing underscores growing labor tensions in the tech sector as companies expand AI operations. Samsung's tentative agreement addresses union concerns about how artificial intelligence deployment affects worker compensation and job security—issues increasingly central to labor negotiations globally.

The union vote outcome will determine whether the Samsung agreement holds or whether workers proceed with industrial action. Watch for the union's ratification decision and whether other tech companies face similar AI-related labor demands.
