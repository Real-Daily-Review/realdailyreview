# Colorado Homeschoolers Outpacing Public Schools as Vaccine Exemptions Surge

Colorado parents are voting with their feet—and their lesson plans. As back-to-school season kicks off, homeschooling families report their children are outperforming traditional public-school peers academically, according to Complete Colorado, while vaccine exemptions have hit record highs statewide (9News Denver).

The homeschool surge reflects growing parental frustration with one-size-fits-all public education and government mandates. Conservatives argue that school choice—whether homeschooling, charter schools, or education savings accounts—empowers families over bureaucrats and delivers better outcomes. Complete Colorado highlighted one homeschool family whose son has "never attended regular school" yet remains "well ahead of most of his public-school peers academically."

Meanwhile, record vaccine exemptions signal another flashpoint: parental authority versus state mandates. While public health officials worry about disease spread, parents increasingly assert their right to medical decision-making for their children—a core conservative principle.

**The Colorado angle:** Public school enrollment pressures and regulatory overreach on health mandates directly affect state funding formulas and parental freedoms. As families explore alternatives, traditional districts face budget challenges while homeschoolers and charter operators gain ground.
