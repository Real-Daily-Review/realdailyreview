# Texas AG Uses Antitrust Settlements to Distribute 7 Million Eggs as Affordability Crisis Bites

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton is distributing 7 million eggs seized through antitrust settlements with major egg producers—a move that underscores how inflation and food-price shocks have become central campaign issues heading into midterm elections (New York Post).

The distribution stems from settlements with three national egg producers. More than 1.7 million eggs have already been delivered, with another 5.3 million scheduled for the coming weeks (Just The News).

**The conservative angle:** This egg giveaway is a Band-Aid on a self-inflicted wound. Inflation—driven by reckless federal spending, loose monetary policy, and supply-chain disruptions from government lockdowns—has crushed household budgets nationwide. Rather than address root causes, politicians resort to feel-good distributions of seized goods. Colorado families face similar affordability pressures on groceries, energy, and housing. Republicans should be hammering Democrats on the inflation crisis and demanding spending restraint and energy independence, not celebrating government handouts.

The real issue: voters are desperate for relief because purchasing power has collapsed. Conservatives should watch whether Republican candidates in Colorado and nationally pivot from sympathy theater to serious fiscal policy—or whether they'll settle for the same government-managed solutions that created the problem.
