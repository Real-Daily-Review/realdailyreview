# School Official Ignored Warnings About Armed First-Grader Before Shooting, Prosecutor Says

A Virginia elementary school assistant principal dismissed repeated warnings that a six-year-old student had a gun in his bag, according to prosecutors in the criminal trial of the former official.

The assistant principal "did nothing" after being told about the weapon, a prosecutor said Tuesday (according to PBS NewsHour). The gun was later used to shoot the student's teacher, according to CBS News.

The case centers on the 2023 school shooting and raises questions about how school administrators respond to safety concerns. Prosecutors are arguing the assistant principal failed in her duty to act on credible information about an armed student on campus.

The trial is ongoing, and the specific charges and potential defenses have not been detailed in available reporting. The case underscores the gap between reporting safety threats and institutional response in school settings.
