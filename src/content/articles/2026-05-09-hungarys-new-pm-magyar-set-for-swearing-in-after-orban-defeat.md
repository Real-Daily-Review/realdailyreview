# Hungary's New PM to Be Sworn In After Landslide Victory

Péter Magyar is set to be sworn in as Hungary's prime minister following his Tisza party's landslide election victory nearly a month ago, according to BBC World. The win ends 16 years of rule by Viktor Orbán.

Magyar's government has signaled intent to pursue swift systemic changes, according to Deutsche Welle. The incoming administration is expected to reshape Hungary's political and institutional landscape after Orbán's lengthy tenure.

The swearing-in ceremony is being framed as a "regime change" moment, marking a significant political transition in the Central European nation. Details on the specific timing and scope of the new government's planned reforms remain limited in available reporting.

Watch for early policy announcements from the Magyar administration on judicial reform, media independence, and alignment with European Union standards—areas where Orbán's government faced sustained criticism from Brussels.
