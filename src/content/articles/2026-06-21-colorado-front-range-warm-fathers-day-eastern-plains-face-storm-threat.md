# Father's Day Weather: Heat, Then Storms for Colorado Front Range

Colorado's Front Range will see warm, mostly dry conditions through Father's Day afternoon, but Eastern Plains residents should prepare for stronger thunderstorms with potential hail and gusty winds later in the day, according to forecasters.

Both 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31 report temperatures climbing into hot territory Sunday, with the greater Denver metro area staying dry through the afternoon. The more significant weather threat develops east of the Front Range, where the Eastern Plains face a real risk of severe thunderstorms capable of producing large hail and damaging wind gusts.

Coloradans planning outdoor Father's Day activities along the Front Range should have a comfortable window through mid-afternoon, but those in eastern Colorado—including the agricultural regions critical to the state's ranching economy—need to monitor conditions closely. Sudden severe weather can threaten crops, livestock, and rural infrastructure.

The pattern underscores Colorado's unpredictable late-spring weather: warm, stable conditions can flip to severe storms in hours, a reality ranchers and farmers know well when planning field work and protecting assets.

Watch whether this system brings meaningful moisture to drought-stressed eastern Colorado or passes through too quickly to help.
