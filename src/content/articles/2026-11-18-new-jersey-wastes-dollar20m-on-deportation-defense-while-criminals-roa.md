# New Jersey's Deportation Defense Spending Shows Why Border Security Matters to Colorado

New Jersey Democrats just proved why Colorado taxpayers should worry: they're spending $20.2 million in state funds to defend illegal immigrants from deportation rather than enforce the law.

Gov. Mikie Sherrill's (D-NJ) latest budget funnels taxpayer dollars into what Democrats call "deportation defense"—legal services designed to keep deportable aliens in the country. The Washington Examiner reports this is part of a coordinated strategy by Democratic politicians nationwide to obstruct federal immigration enforcement.

The stakes are real. The Daily Wire documented an illegal immigrant Uber driver in California—who crossed the border illegally in January 2023—now facing charges for molesting a 14-year-old passenger. That's the kind of public safety failure that happens when states prioritize defending illegal aliens over protecting citizens.

Republicans argue that states should cooperate with federal immigration enforcement, not undermine it. Democrats counter that they're defending vulnerable populations and that most illegal immigrants commit crimes at lower rates than citizens—though conservatives note that even one preventable crime is one too many.

Colorado hasn't gone as far as New Jersey yet, but progressive lawmakers here have signaled sympathy for similar "sanctuary" policies. Watch whether Colorado Democrats attempt to fund deportation defense or expand sanctuary protections—and whether Republican legislators will draw a line.
