# RFK Jr. Pushes 'Make America Healthy Again' While Colorado Fire Crews Warn Against Dangerous Turkey Frying

Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. is using the holiday season to promote his "Make America Healthy Again" agenda, partnering with Indigenous chef Ray Naranjo in San Diego to demonstrate how to repurpose Thanksgiving leftovers into traditional turkey tamales as part of *The Real Food Show* series (Washington Examiner).

Meanwhile, back in Colorado, North Metro Fire Rescue District is taking a more practical approach to holiday safety. The department conducted a dramatic demonstration this week showing residents the dangers of deep-frying turkeys—literally setting the wrong-way version ablaze to drive home the fire hazard (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

While Kennedy's food-focused messaging aligns with his broader health-centered platform, conservatives should note the distinction: the HHS secretary's initiative emphasizes dietary choices and food preparation education, whereas local fire departments focus on preventing the preventable accidents that send Coloradans to emergency rooms every November.

The contrast highlights how federal health messaging often emphasizes lifestyle choices while local first responders handle the real-world consequences of holiday mishaps.
