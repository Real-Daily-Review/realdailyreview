# Trump Returns to Correspondents' Dinner After Security Scare

President Trump is heading back to the White House Correspondents' Dinner stage months after an armed suspect forced an evacuation at the April event, signaling confidence in Secret Service protocols and a refusal to be deterred by security threats.

The dinner, historically held at the Washington Hilton, will take place this year at the Waldorf Astoria with a smaller guest list than the April gathering where gunfire prompted evacuation, according to *Just The News*. The Secret Service will honor Officer Gonzales for stopping the armed suspect during the earlier incident, *Fox News* reports.

Trump's return underscores the administration's commitment to public engagement despite security challenges. The move also reflects confidence in enhanced security measures and the professionalism of protective services.

The smaller venue and adjusted guest list represent practical security adjustments without capitulating to threats—a distinction conservatives will appreciate as the administration balances openness with prudent risk management.
