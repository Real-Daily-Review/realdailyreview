# Byron Donalds Wins Florida Governor's Race, Keeps State Red

Florida voters have rejected the Democratic push to flip a major battleground state, electing Republican Byron Donalds as governor with roughly 50% of the vote over Democrat David Jolly's 48.7% (Washington Examiner). Donalds, a Trump-backed congressman, will succeed Ron DeSantis and become the nation's first Black Republican governor, marking a historic win for the GOP (Fox News).

The victory underscores Republican strength in a state Democrats have long targeted. Donalds staved off what the left hoped would be a "blue wave" (The Daily Wire), cementing Florida's status as reliably red territory despite national Democratic efforts to reclaim it.

**The Colorado angle:** While Florida's governor's race doesn't directly affect Colorado policy, the outcome signals momentum for Republican candidates nationwide heading into 2026 midterms. Colorado Republicans, facing their own uphill battles in a purple state, will study Donalds's playbook—particularly his appeal to diverse voters and his alignment with Trump's America First agenda.

**What to watch:** Whether Donalds's victory energizes Colorado GOP candidates to replicate his coalition-building strategy in 2026 state races, or whether Colorado's leftward drift proves immune to national Republican tailwinds.
