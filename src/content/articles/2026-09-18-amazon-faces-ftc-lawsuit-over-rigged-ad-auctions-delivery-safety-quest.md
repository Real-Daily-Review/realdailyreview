# Amazon Faces Dual Pressure: Dead Dog, Rigged Auctions

Amazon is under fire on two fronts—a heartbreaking delivery mishap and serious allegations of marketplace manipulation that could affect Colorado consumers and small business sellers.

A Florida couple's beloved Yorkie was killed during an Amazon delivery, raising questions about whether basic safety protocols could prevent future tragedies (9News Denver). Meanwhile, Florida has joined 21 other states in an FTC lawsuit accusing Amazon of rigging its ad auction system, allegedly burying hidden charges that quietly inflate prices for everyday goods (Just The News).

**The conservative concern:** Amazon's ad-auction scheme—if proven—represents exactly the kind of market manipulation that undermines fair competition and harms consumers and small sellers. Colorado retailers and online merchants already struggle against Amazon's dominance; rigged pricing machinery makes it worse. The lawsuit signals that even center-right state attorneys general recognize the need for accountability when one company controls too much of the marketplace.

The delivery-safety issue, meanwhile, highlights how corporate efficiency protocols can ignore basic customer protections. A simple gate-check or delivery-confirmation procedure might have saved a family pet.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado's attorney general joins the multistate FTC suit—and whether Amazon faces meaningful penalties that actually restore fair-market conditions rather than just padding government coffers.
