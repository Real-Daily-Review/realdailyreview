# Canadian School Site of Mass Shooting to Be Demolished

British Columbia officials announced plans to tear down Tumbler Ridge secondary school, where a mass shooting in February killed nine people and injured dozens, according to The Guardian. The decision came after the school board met with survivors, family members, and community representatives, said Premier David Eby.

The demolition reflects a broader pattern: schools that experience mass violence often face pressure to close or be rebuilt. Officials have not yet disclosed a timeline for the demolition or details about what may replace the structure.

Watch for announcements about the school's future use and any memorial plans the community may propose.
