# Denver Travelers Face Holiday Chaos as Delta Outage Ripples Across Nation

Denver International Airport is bracing for one of the busiest travel weeks of the year, but a major Delta Air Lines outage is already snarling pre-Thanksgiving flights nationwide. According to the New York Post, hundreds of flights were delayed Sunday at Atlanta's Hartsfield-Jackson International Airport, triggering a cascading domino effect across U.S. hubs—including Denver.

TSA and DIA officials say they're prepared for the surge (KDVR Fox31), but the Delta meltdown underscores a broader vulnerability: when a single carrier's systems fail, thousands of passengers and regional economies suffer immediate consequences. Colorado's tourism and business travel sectors depend on reliable air access, and operational breakdowns like this one hit local hotels, rental car companies, and ground services hard.

For Coloradans heading out, 9News Denver recommends planning ahead and taking precautions in case disruptions worsen. The outage is a stark reminder that despite heavy investment in airport infrastructure, the airline industry remains fragile when legacy systems fail—a problem that regulation alone won't solve.
