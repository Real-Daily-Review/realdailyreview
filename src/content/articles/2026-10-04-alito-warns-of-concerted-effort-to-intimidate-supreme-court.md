# Supreme Court Opens Term Under Fire as Alito Warns of Intimidation Campaign

The Supreme Court begins its new term facing record-low public approval and coordinated pressure from the left over rulings on gun rights, religious liberty, and voter citizenship—exactly the cases conservatives need the Court to get right.

Justice Samuel Alito warned Sunday (Fox News) that a "concerted" effort to intimidate the justices threatens the Court's institutional future. Justices have faced public threats and sustained pressure campaigns following high-profile decisions, particularly the 2022 Dobbs ruling on abortion.

The left's playbook is transparent: delegitimize the Court when it rules against progressive priorities. Democrats and activist groups have called for Court-packing, term limits, and ethics reforms—all designed to punish conservative justices and reshape the bench. The intimidation campaign includes protests at justices' homes and coordinated media attacks framing originalist jurisprudence as illegitimate.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters enormously. The Court's upcoming docket includes cases affecting Second Amendment protections and religious liberty—both critical to Colorado gun owners and faith-based organizations facing state-level overreach.

Alito's warning signals the justices understand the stakes: a politicized Court serves no one. If the left succeeds in intimidating the bench into abandoning constitutional interpretation for policy preferences, judicial independence dies.

Watch whether Senate Democrats escalate Court-reform rhetoric or whether public backlash against intimidation tactics forces them to back down.
