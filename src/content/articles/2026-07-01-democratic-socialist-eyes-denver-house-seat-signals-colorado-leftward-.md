# Democratic Socialist Melat Kiros Eyes Denver Congressional Seat

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress. Melat Kiros, backed by the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA), is positioning herself as a major contender in the race (CPR News), signaling a sharp leftward shift in Colorado's political landscape.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this matters. A DSA-aligned representative from Denver—the state's largest city—would likely push for expanded government programs, higher taxes on businesses and earners, and aggressive climate regulations that could hobble Colorado's energy sector. The DSA platform historically opposes market-driven solutions and champions wealth redistribution policies that conservatives argue stifle economic growth and individual opportunity.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives should expect Kiros to champion policies that expand the welfare state, increase regulatory burdens on Colorado businesses, and pursue aggressive climate mandates. They'll argue such an agenda would drive job creators out of state and burden working Coloradans with higher costs of living.

Democrats counter that DSA-backed candidates represent grassroots energy and address inequality—though they rarely acknowledge the fiscal cost of their proposals.
