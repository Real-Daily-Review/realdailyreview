# Trump Dismisses Iranian 'Fake Deal' Claims as Regime Spreads Disinformation

Former President Donald Trump fired back Friday after Iranian state media published a draft nuclear agreement allegedly containing terms Trump says bear no resemblance to actual negotiations. Iranian officials, according to the leaked documents, claimed Iran would retain its nuclear enrichment program—a red line Trump has consistently opposed.

"No relation to the truth," Trump said of Tehran's characterization, according to *Just The News*. The Iranian regime's move appears designed to shape public perception ahead of potential talks, a tactic conservatives argue exposes the fundamental dishonesty of dealing with hostile regimes.

The leak underscores a core conservative concern: Iran cannot be trusted as a negotiating partner. Trump's 2018 withdrawal from the Obama-era JCPOA reflected this skepticism, and Friday's disinformation campaign vindicates that skepticism. The regime's willingness to fabricate deal terms and leak them to state media suggests any agreement would require ironclad verification mechanisms—not the trust-based framework progressives favored under the previous administration.
