# Trump, NYC Mayor Clash Over Immigration Enforcement at Gracie Mansion

President Trump met with New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani at Gracie Mansion amid months of escalating conflict over immigration enforcement and federal welfare rules—a showdown that underscores the deepening divide between the Trump administration and progressive urban leaders.

According to Fox News and Just The News, the meeting occurred while Trump was in New York for the United Nations General Assembly. The two discussed housing and immigration policy, though the encounter came after fierce clashes over Trump's push to enforce the public charge rule, which restricts welfare access for immigrants—a policy progressives fiercely oppose.

Conservatives argue that enforcing immigration law and tightening welfare eligibility for non-citizens is essential fiscal responsibility. The Trump administration contends that cities like New York have become magnets for illegal immigration and that federal welfare rules should prioritize American citizens and legal residents. Republicans say sanctuary city policies and generous benefits undermine border security and strain taxpayer resources.

Democrats and progressive mayors counter that immigration enforcement is inhumane and that cities need federal support to manage migrant populations. Mamdani has previously clashed with Trump over these issues, though the White House visit after his election suggested a willingness to negotiate.
