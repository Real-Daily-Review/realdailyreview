# South Carolina GOP Fracture: Trump Endorsement Doesn't Stop Norman Challenge to Graham

Rep. Ralph Norman is defying Donald Trump's endorsement of Darline Graham Nordone in the South Carolina Senate race, announcing his own bid for the seat vacated by the late Sen. Lindsey Graham. Norman, according to Fox News, is vowing to "nuke the filibuster"—a stark contrast to the establishment lane Graham represents.

Graham Nordone, appointed interim senator following her brother's death, released an emotional open letter via X addressing her commitment to the role and reflecting on the loss, per The Daily Wire. She's positioned as Trump's chosen successor in the state.

**The conservative split here matters.** Norman's filibuster-elimination pledge signals frustration among grassroots Republicans with Senate obstruction tactics—a position that aligns with Trump's 2024 agenda but threatens the institutional guardrails Democrats have repeatedly weaponized. Graham Nordone represents continuity with Lindsey Graham's more institutionalist approach.

Republicans argue the filibuster has become a tool for Democratic obstruction; others counter that eliminating it invites majority-party tyranny when power shifts. The race will test whether Trump's endorsement power holds in a contested primary or whether anti-establishment energy still drives GOP voters in red states.

**Watch whether Norman gains traction despite Trump's backing—it'll signal whether the former president's influence over South Carolina Republicans remains absolute.**
