# China Bans Nvidia's RTX 5090D V2 Chip During CEO's Visit

China added Nvidia's RTX 5090D V2 graphics processor to its list of banned goods at customs checkpoints last Friday, according to Ars Technica, timing the move to coincide with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's visit to the country.

The ban reflects Beijing's strategy to shield domestic chip manufacturers as they develop competing technologies. According to the Financial Times, China is backing homegrown players including Huawei and Cambricon to close the gap with U.S. rivals in advanced semiconductor capabilities.

The RTX 5090D V2 is a high-performance gaming chip. The ban prevents its importation into China, effectively blocking a key product line from one of the world's largest chipmakers in a major market.

The move underscores escalating tensions over semiconductor access between Washington and Beijing. The U.S. has imposed export restrictions on advanced chips to China for years, citing national security concerns. China has responded with its own restrictions on foreign technology.

Watch for Nvidia's official response to the ban and whether the company seeks exemptions or adjusts its China strategy.
