# Trump Vows Response After Iran Strikes U.S. Forces; Markets Tumble on Escalation Fears

President Trump pledged a forceful response after Iran launched a surprise ballistic missile attack on American forces in Jordan, telling Fox News the U.S. would "beat the f---ing s--- out of them." The Iranian barrage came hours after U.S. and Saudi forces struck Tehran-backed militias in Iraq (9News Denver).

The escalation spooked markets immediately. The Dow slumped 700 points Wednesday morning while oil prices jumped over 6% on fears of prolonged conflict, potential inflation resurgence, and possible Federal Reserve rate hikes (New York Post). For Colorado energy workers and consumers already squeezed by inflation, renewed Middle East tensions threaten pump prices and heating costs heading into winter.

Trump's hawkish stance contrasts sharply with the Biden-Harris administration's approach to Iran negotiations. Conservatives argue that strength deters aggression; critics worry escalatory rhetoric could trigger a costly regional war. The immediate market reaction underscores investor uncertainty about whether Trump's tough posture will deter further Iranian action or spiral into a broader conflict.

Colorado taxpayers and small-business owners should watch whether oil prices stabilize or climb further—and whether the Fed responds to inflation fears with rate hikes that could slow economic growth and job creation.
