# Federal Study Links Gender Ideology Support to Political Violence Justification

A Department of Health and Human Services-commissioned study has found an association between support for "radical gender ideology" and greater willingness to justify political violence, according to *The Daily Wire* and the *Daily Caller*. Researchers from Rutgers University, the Manhattan Institute, and the Network Contagion Research Institute conducted the preregistered survey, which was released Wednesday.

The findings align with conservative concerns that progressive gender ideology has become increasingly extreme and disconnected from mainstream values. Republicans have long argued that the left's push to normalize contested gender theories in schools, workplaces, and public institutions represents ideological overreach that corrodes social cohesion and rational discourse.

The study's release comes as Colorado continues grappling with gender-related education policy disputes. Conservative parents and school board members have pushed back against curriculum expansions and bathroom policies they view as ideologically driven rather than grounded in child welfare or parental rights.

Democrats and progressive advocates typically counter that such research mischaracterizes support for transgender rights as "radical ideology" and argue that protections for transgender individuals are matters of basic dignity and inclusion—not political violence.

The HHS report underscores a deepening cultural divide on gender policy that will likely intensify Colorado's ongoing school board battles and influence 2026 state education races.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado legislators attempt to restrict gender-related curriculum or policies based on this federal research.
