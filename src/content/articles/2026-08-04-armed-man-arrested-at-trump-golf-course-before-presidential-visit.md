# Trump Golf Course Security: Armed Man Arrested Days Before Presidential Visit

President Trump's California golf course faced a security scare when law enforcement arrested an armed man on the grounds days before the president's scheduled visit for an RNC fundraiser.

The Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department arrested the suspect on Sunday after he was observed acting "suspicious" near Trump International Golf Club in Los Angeles, according to 9News Denver. Just The News reported the man was armed at the time of his arrest, which occurred hours before Trump was set to appear at the property for a Republican National Committee fundraiser and roundtable.

Details on the suspect's identity, specific charges, and the nature of the threat remain limited in initial reporting. The incident underscores persistent security concerns surrounding the former and current president, particularly as he maintains a public schedule of campaign and official events.
