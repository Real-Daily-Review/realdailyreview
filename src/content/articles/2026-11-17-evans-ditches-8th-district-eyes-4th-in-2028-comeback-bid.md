# Republican Evans Eyes 4th District Comeback After 8th District Loss

Gabe Evans is already plotting his return to Congress—just not from the seat he just lost. Days after Democrat Eileen Laubacher narrowly unseated Rep. Lauren Boebert in Colorado's 4th Congressional District, the defeated 8th District Republican announced he'll pursue the 4th seat in 2028 (Colorado Sun, CPR News).

The move gives Evans a strategic head start over other Republicans who may eye the open seat. Laubacher, a retired Navy rear admiral, won by less than 1 percentage point—a margin thin enough to invite a rematch-adjacent challenge in a district that has become a battleground for national Democrats and Republicans alike.

Evans' pivot reflects the brutal math of Colorado's political landscape: the 8th is trending blue, while the 4th remains competitive. Whether voters will reward a politician who lost one race by immediately running in another remains an open question.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado Republicans coalesce around Evans or field a fresh candidate. The 4th's razor-thin margins suggest 2028 could hinge on turnout and messaging—and whether Republicans can recapture ground lost to Democratic gains in suburban Denver.
