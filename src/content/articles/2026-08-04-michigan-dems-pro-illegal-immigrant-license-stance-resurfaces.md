# Michigan Dem's Old Stance on Illegal Immigrant Licenses Resurfaces—Colorado Should Take Note

A resurfaced 2018 video shows Michigan Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed calling it "discrimination" to deny driver's licenses to undocumented immigrants, according to Fox News and the New York Post. The clip reveals El-Sayed's position that documentation status should not be a barrier to licensing.

Republicans argue this stance reflects the progressive left's approach to immigration enforcement—prioritizing inclusivity over border security and legal status verification. Conservatives say allowing undocumented immigrants access to driver's licenses undermines the rule of law and creates security vulnerabilities, while also rewarding illegal entry over lawful immigration pathways.

The timing matters: El-Sayed is now running for Michigan Senate, and the video suggests his immigration views remain a potential liability in a general election where swing voters prioritize border security.
