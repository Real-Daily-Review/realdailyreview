# Colorado DA Anne Kelly Turns Around Crisis-Plagued Office While Progressive Prosecutors Elsewhere Embrace Soft-on-Crime Agenda

Anne Kelly inherited a disaster—a rural Colorado district attorney's office mired in misconduct and dysfunction. Four years later, she's restored order and credibility to the Alamosa County DA's office, proving that competent leadership and accountability matter (CPR News).

Kelly's turnaround contrasts sharply with the progressive prosecutor movement sweeping urban America. While Philadelphia's Larry Krasner and other left-wing DAs have pursued catch-and-release policies that prioritize ideology over public safety—Krasner even fought to free a convicted murderer, prompting Pennsylvania's Supreme Court to intervene—Kelly has focused on doing the job: prosecuting crime, restoring institutional integrity, and serving rural Colorado communities.

Republicans and conservatives have long warned that progressive prosecutors treat the justice system as a vehicle for social experimentation rather than protecting citizens. Kelly's success in Alamosa demonstrates that voters in even rural, less-wealthy counties demand results: competent prosecutors who hold criminals accountable and restore faith in law enforcement.

The contrast is instructive for Colorado voters watching DA races statewide: experience, integrity, and a commitment to actual justice—not ideological posturing—still matter.

**Watch whether Colorado's progressive prosecutors adopt similar soft-on-crime policies that have destabilized cities like Philadelphia and Denver.**
