# Catch-and-Release Justice: Illegal Immigrants Charged in Violent Crimes After Lenient Court Releases

California and Texas courts are releasing illegal immigrants accused of serious crimes back onto the streets—with predictable and tragic results.

In Martinez, California, a judge released Marcos Iriarte-Valdez on his own recognizance. One day later, he allegedly stabbed 68-year-old Todd Stewart to death (Fox News). Meanwhile in Dallas, Jose Santiago Pantoja Morales—an illegal immigrant who crossed the border just four years ago—stands accused of shooting a Dallas ISD police officer with the officer's own weapon after a struggle (The Daily Wire).

These cases illustrate the consequences of progressive catch-and-release policies that prioritize defendants' rights over public safety. Republicans argue that judicial leniency toward illegal immigrants endangers communities and undermines law enforcement. When judges release suspects without bail or conditions, they're gambling with citizens' lives.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's criminal justice system has faced similar pressures to adopt lenient release policies. As sanctuary jurisdictions expand and progressive judges gain influence, Coloradans should ask whether their local courts are applying the same dangerous standards that freed these violent offenders.

Conservatives and taxpayers need to watch whether Colorado's judiciary follows California's path—or whether state lawmakers will demand accountability from judges who release dangerous criminals.
