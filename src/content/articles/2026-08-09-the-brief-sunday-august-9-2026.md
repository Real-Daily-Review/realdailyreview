# Real Daily Review — The Brief

## Colorado Voters Will Decide on a 'Right to Natural Gas' This November

A ballot initiative that would enshrine the right to natural gas in Colorado's Constitution has qualified for the November election. This is a direct rebuke to the progressive energy agenda that has spent years trying to ban gas appliances, strangle pipeline development, and price working Coloradans out of affordable home heating. Colorado's ranchers, rural communities, and energy workers have the most to lose from the left's war on fossil fuels — and now they have a chance to fight back at the ballot box where it counts.

## Iran War Exposes Dangerous Gaps in U.S. Munitions Stockpiles

The Trump administration is moving to accelerate production of high-end munitions after recent combat operations against Iran drew down already-depleted stocks of advanced missile interceptors. Republicans have long warned that years of defense underfunding left the military dangerously thin on the weapons that matter most in a near-peer or regional conflict. Colorado's defense contractors and military installations — including Buckley Space Force Base and the defense corridor along the Front Range — are directly tied to the national security industrial base that now urgently needs rebuilding.

## Trump's Freedom 250 Fireworks Confirmed as Largest in History
