# Colorado Town Faces Pressure Over ICE Detention Center as Health Concerns Mount

Hudson residents are demanding their town block an immigration detention facility, even as local officials say they lack legal authority to do so. The push comes as Colorado health authorities investigate a tuberculosis case at an Aurora ICE detention center—a reminder that border enforcement infrastructure raises real public health questions alongside the immigration debate.

At a Wednesday town meeting, opponents called the facility an ICE "concentration camp" and urged Hudson leaders to intervene (Colorado Sun). An ICE spokesperson confirmed a tuberculosis case at the Aurora location on June 25; the individual was treated, cleared, and removed from the country (9News Denver).

**The conservative case:** Republicans and immigration hawks argue detention facilities are necessary to process and remove illegal immigrants efficiently. However, they also acknowledge that operational standards—including health screening and disease prevention—matter for public safety and facility credibility. If Aurora's center has genuine health lapses, that's a management failure ICE must address, not an argument against detention itself.

**The left's position:** Progressive activists frame detention as inherently inhumane and demand closure; they're less focused on operational fixes than abolition.

Hudson officials' legal constraints are real, but Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state health regulators demand costly upgrades that make detention economically unviable—a backdoor closure strategy.
