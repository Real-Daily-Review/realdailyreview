# Seattle Democrat Admits What Voters Already Know: Progressive Leadership Fails When It Counts

A progressive House Democrat just said out loud what conservatives have been arguing for years: left-wing mayors bungle public safety when it matters most. Rep. Pramila Jayapal (D-Wash.) texted Washington's governor that Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson "doesn't know what she's doing" after the city's response to a mass shooting at a food festival, according to texts surfaced by *Just The News* and the *New York Post*.

When a fellow Democrat—and a prominent progressive at that—loses confidence in a mayor's competence during a crisis, it signals deeper rot in how the left governs cities. Seattle has spent years prioritizing social experiments over basic law enforcement, and the result is predictable: when violence strikes, the machinery of government fails.

This matters for Colorado. Denver and Boulder have pursued similar progressive policies—defunding police rhetoric, soft-on-crime prosecutors, regulatory overreach—that erode public safety and business confidence. Jayapal's private doubts about Wilson's leadership should prompt Colorado voters to ask hard questions about whether their own cities are headed down the same path.

Watch whether Seattle's city council and mayor respond with real reforms—or more excuses.
