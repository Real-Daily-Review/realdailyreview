# Marxist Activists Tied to Foreign Networks Blocking U.S. Tech Infrastructure

Colorado and other states should watch closely as radical activists with ties to hostile foreign interests work to block critical American AI and data infrastructure projects.

The activists are using environmental and community concerns as cover to obstruct development that would strengthen U.S. technological capacity and economic competitiveness.

This pattern matters for Colorado. The state is home to growing tech sectors and data infrastructure projects that face similar opposition from progressive activists and environmental groups. When foreign-aligned radicals infiltrate local opposition campaigns, they're not acting in Colorado's interest—they're advancing Beijing's and Moscow's strategic goal of weakening American technological dominance.

It hands competitive advantage to China and other adversaries.

Democrats and the activist left counter that such projects require rigorous environmental review and community consent, though they rarely acknowledge when foreign actors exploit these legitimate processes.

**Watch whether Colorado officials investigate foreign funding and influence in local opposition campaigns targeting energy and tech projects.**
