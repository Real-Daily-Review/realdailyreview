# Conservative Activist Charlie Kirk's Alleged Killer Cleared for Trial

A Utah judge has ruled that Tyler Robinson will stand trial for murder in the killing of conservative activist Charlie Kirk, clearing a major legal hurdle nearly a year after Kirk's assassination at a Turning Point USA rally.

Judge Tony Graf found probable cause on Tuesday, allowing prosecutors to move forward with aggravated murder charges against Robinson. According to the Washington Examiner, Utah County prosecutors presented video and DNA evidence they say proves Robinson's guilt "beyond doubt." Robinson has not entered a plea and faces a possible death penalty if convicted (New York Post).

Kirk, a prominent conservative voice and Turning Point USA founder, was shot at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. His death shocked the right-leaning activist community and sparked national attention on political violence.

The preliminary hearing's conclusion marks a turning point in the case. Prosecutors must now prepare for trial, where they'll present their forensic evidence to a jury. Robinson's defense team will have opportunity to challenge the state's case and argue for acquittal or lesser charges.
