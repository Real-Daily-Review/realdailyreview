# Denver Sees Spike in Violent Crime as Police Respond to Multiple Incidents

Denver's streets turned dangerous over the weekend as police responded to multiple stabbing and shooting incidents across the metro area, underscoring the city's ongoing public safety crisis.

A Denver police officer shot a man in the Globeville neighborhood Sunday morning after responding to reports he was harassing people with a knife (KDVR Fox31). Separately, a stabbing incident Saturday left one woman dead and multiple others hospitalized (KDVR Fox31). In Thornton, a 44-year-old man was shot and killed early Sunday outside a home where a party was being held (9News Denver).

**The conservative concern:** These incidents reflect the deteriorating conditions in Denver under progressive leadership that has prioritized defunding police rhetoric and soft-on-crime policies. Republicans argue that inadequate law enforcement resources, combined with lenient prosecution and bail policies championed by Democrats, have emboldened violent criminals and left law-abiding Coloradans vulnerable.

Denver's homicide rate and violent crime statistics have climbed as the city has grappled with gang activity, drug-related violence, and mental health crises—problems that demand robust policing, not the defund-the-police agenda that has gained traction among progressive city officials.

Coloradans should watch whether Denver's new city leadership will reverse course on public safety or continue down the path of reduced police funding and soft prosecution policies.
