# Colorado Democrats' Dramatic Flip on Israel Aid Exposes Party Fracture

Colorado's Democratic delegation has abandoned bipartisan support for Israel in just two years—a stunning reversal that reveals how thoroughly the progressive base has captured the party's foreign policy agenda.

Two years ago, every Colorado Democrat backed aid to Israel. Last month, all of them voted for an amendment to cut aid to the Middle East ally, according to CPR News. The shift reflects what the Colorado Sun describes as a "larger divide within the party" driven by activist pressure over Israeli military operations in Gaza following Hamas's October 7 attacks.

Republicans argue this capitulation to the anti-Israel left undermines a crucial U.S. ally and signals weakness on national security. The reversal also exposes how quickly Democratic elected officials abandon longstanding positions when confronted by their activist base—a pattern Colorado conservatives should note as the party continues lurching leftward on defense, energy, and spending priorities.

The Colorado Sun reports the reversal is being felt "from Denver to Washington, D.C.," suggesting this isn't isolated to the state delegation but part of a national Democratic realignment.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans use this flip-flop to challenge Democratic incumbents on consistency and national security credibility heading into 2026.
