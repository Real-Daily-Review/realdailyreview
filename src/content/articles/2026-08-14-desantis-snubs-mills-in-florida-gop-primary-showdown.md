# DeSantis Rejects Mills in Florida GOP Primary Fight

Gov. Ron DeSantis has pointedly refused to endorse incumbent Rep. Cory Mills (R-FL) ahead of a competitive Republican primary, declaring flatly: "I have not endorsed Cory Mills, and I do not support Cory Mills" (Washington Examiner). The statement, made Friday at an event near Florida's 7th Congressional District, signals a rare split among GOP leadership.

Other House Republicans have also declined to back Mills. Rep. Anna Paulina Luna (R-FL) has endorsed Ryan Elijah, one of Mills' primary opponents (Just The News). The primary contest appears tight enough that DeSantis felt compelled to weigh in—or rather, to stay out.

**Colorado angle:** While this is a Florida race, it underscores how Republican primary contests are increasingly contested on performance and record rather than party loyalty alone. Colorado Republicans face similar dynamics in their own primaries, where establishment backing no longer guarantees victory among conservative voters demanding accountability.

The Mills primary will test whether DeSantis's non-endorsement carries weight with Florida GOP voters—and whether similar fractures emerge in other Republican races heading into the general election cycle.
