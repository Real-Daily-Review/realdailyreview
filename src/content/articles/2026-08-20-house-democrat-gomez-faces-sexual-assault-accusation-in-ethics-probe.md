# House Democrat Gomez Faces Sexual Assault Accusation as Ethics Committee Investigates

A California House Democrat is now under fire for a sexual assault accusation that directly contradicts his own account of events. Rep. Jimmy Gomez (D-CA) claimed an encounter was "consensual," but a woman who spoke to the House Ethics Committee disputes that characterization entirely, according to Fox News and the Washington Examiner.

The accuser is cooperating with the committee's formal investigation, represented by lawyers Lisa Banks and Sarah Nesbitt. Gomez joins a growing list of congressional Democrats entangled in personal misconduct scandals—a pattern that undercuts the left's frequent moral posturing on workplace conduct and accountability.

The case highlights a persistent double standard: when Republicans face allegations, media outlets and Democratic leaders demand swift consequences. When Democrats do, the response is often muted, with investigations dragging on behind closed doors. The House Ethics Committee's handling of this matter will reveal whether that bias holds true here.
