# Socialist NYC Mayor Uses July 4 to Attack America, ICE, and Elon Musk

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani delivered a scathing Independence Day speech that attacked law enforcement, billionaires, and American institutions rather than celebrating the nation's founding—a stark contrast to President Trump's patriotic Mount Rushmore event (Fox News).

Speaking ahead of the July 4 weekend, Mamdani—himself an immigrant and the youngest mayor in NYC history—painted America as a "nation of contradictions" plagued by hunger and oppression. He specifically blasted ICE agents as "masked agents terrorizing our streets" and attacked Elon Musk and other oligarchs (Breitbart). The speech was flanked by naturalized citizens, framing immigration through a lens of grievance rather than gratitude.

Conservative outlets noted the irony: Mamdani's own rise from immigrant to NYC's top office contradicts his narrative of American hostility. The New York Post observed that he ignores how "this country, which he says is so unwelcoming, made him the youngest mayor in the history of its largest city."

Meanwhile, Trump returned to Mount Rushmore for a "Salute to America" event celebrating American legacy and strength—a starkly different vision of Independence Day (Fox News).
