# Penley Fire West of Sedalia Contained; Evacuations Lifted

A wildfire that forced mandatory evacuations west of Sedalia over the weekend has been brought under control, with the Penley Fire holding at three acres and 75% containment as of Monday, according to the Douglas County Sheriff's Office. No further fire growth was reported.

The blaze prompted residents to evacuate Sunday evening, but authorities lifted evacuation orders after rapid containment efforts. The small acreage and quick response underscore both the volatility of Colorado's fire season and the effectiveness of local emergency management when resources are deployed swiftly.

While this fire remained contained, Colorado's broader wildfire risk remains a persistent concern for property owners, ranchers, and rural communities across the state. Forest management practices—including controlled burns and timber thinning—remain contentious between conservation groups and those who argue environmental regulations have left forests overgrown and dangerously fuel-laden.

Conservatives and property-rights advocates have long contended that federal land-management policies and environmental litigation delay necessary forest treatments, leaving private and public lands vulnerable to catastrophic burns that destroy homes and livelihoods.
