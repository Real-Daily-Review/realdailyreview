# Colorado Voters Head to Polls in 2026 Midterms

Colorado voters are casting ballots today in the 2026 midterm elections, with polls closing between 7 and 8 p.m. across the state. The election comes as Republicans and Democrats vie for control of Congress and state offices, with energy policy, water rights, and tax issues likely to shape races across Colorado.

Conservatives are watching closely to see whether voters will reject the Biden-Harris administration's aggressive climate regulations and energy restrictions that have threatened Colorado's oil and gas industry and rural economies. Republicans argue that excessive federal environmental mandates have driven up energy costs and threatened jobs in energy-producing counties that depend on responsible resource development.

Democrats have pushed for aggressive climate action and renewable energy mandates, framing the election as a choice between progressive climate policy and what they characterize as fossil fuel obstruction.

Coloradans should verify their polling location before heading out—wait times vary by county. The Washington Examiner and Daily Wire are providing live election coverage throughout the day.
