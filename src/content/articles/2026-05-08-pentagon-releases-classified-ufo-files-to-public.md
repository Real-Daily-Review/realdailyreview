# Pentagon Begins Releasing UFO Files to Public

The Pentagon has launched a public release of previously classified documents on unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP), according to CBS News and PBS NewsHour. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said the materials "have long fueled justified speculation — and it's time the American people see it for themselves."

The initial batch of files, housed on a new Pentagon website, includes videos, photographs, and source documents from across the U.S. government, according to TechCrunch. The Department of Defense noted that while materials have been reviewed for security purposes, many "have not yet been analyzed for resolution of any anomalies."

President Trump ordered the release in February amid renewed public interest, according to Al Jazeera. A 2024 congressional report revealed hundreds of new UAP incidents but found no evidence that the U.S. government had ever confirmed a sighting of alien technology, PBS reported.

The Pentagon framed the disclosure as an opportunity for citizens to evaluate the materials independently. Al Jazeera noted that critics have claimed the release serves as a distraction, though the sources do not elaborate on those claims.

Watch for public reaction to the documents and whether additional tranches of files are released.
