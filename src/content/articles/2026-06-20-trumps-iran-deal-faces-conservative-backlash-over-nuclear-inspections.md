# Trump's Iran Deal Draws Fire From Conservative Nuclear Experts Over Uranium Oversight

President Trump's new Iran nuclear framework is drawing sharp criticism from right-leaning national security experts who warn it leaves Tehran with dangerous control over its uranium stockpile without adequate International Atomic Energy Agency inspections.

According to Fox News, nuclear experts flagging the agreement point to a critical "blind spot": the deal doesn't ensure proper IAEA oversight of Iran's enriched uranium before negotiations advance. The concern centers on verification gaps that could allow Iran to maintain leverage over its nuclear material while talks continue.

The Washington Examiner's opinion section goes further, calling Trump's Iran deal "by far the worst thing to come out of either of his two terms," arguing the president has dismissed legitimate concerns from security analysts as coming from "losers."

**The conservative case:** Republicans and defense hawks argue any Iran nuclear agreement must include ironclad inspection regimes *before* sanctions relief or concessions. They contend that trusting Tehran's compliance without real-time IAEA access repeats the failures of the 2015 JCPOA.
