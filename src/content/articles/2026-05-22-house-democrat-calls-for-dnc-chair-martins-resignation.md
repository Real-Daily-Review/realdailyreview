# House Democrat Calls for DNC Chair Martin's Resignation

Rep. Marc Veasey (D-Texas) became the first known House Democrat to publicly call for Democratic National Committee Chair Ken Martin to resign, citing the party's delayed and troubled rollout of its 2024 election autopsy report.

Veasey told Semafor on Thursday that Martin should "move on," arguing there is no clear strategy to address the party's losses. "There doesn't seem to be a plan to turn things around and the clock is ticking," Veasey said, according to The Hill.

The autopsy report, released Thursday, came more than 18 months after the 2024 presidential election, according to the Washington Examiner. The delayed and bungled unveiling has intensified scrutiny of Martin's leadership as Democrats assess what went wrong and chart a path forward.

Veasey's call marks an escalation in internal party criticism but remains isolated—no other House Democrats have publicly joined the call for Martin's ouster. The DNC chair's position has become a focal point for Democratic frustration over the party's strategic direction and messaging heading into the 2026 midterms.
