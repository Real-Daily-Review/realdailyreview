# Republican Pushes Declaration of Independence Bill as America Marks 250 Years

Rep. Matt Van Epps is leveraging America's 250th birthday to reassert constitutional first principles—introducing legislation that reaffirms the Declaration of Independence directly in response to socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primaries, according to Fox News.

The move reflects conservative concern that progressive ideology is drifting further from the nation's founding documents. Republicans argue the Declaration's core principles—individual liberty, limited government, and unalienable rights—are under sustained assault from the left's expansionist regulatory state and anti-free-market agenda.

Meanwhile, patriotic celebrations are sweeping the nation. New York City has become a hotbed of festivities (New York Post), while classic American icons are rolling into historic landmarks ahead of July 4th (Daily Caller). The Freedom Train is also making stops across the country, drawing crowds eager to commemorate the nation's founding.

The broader moment underscores a fundamental divide: conservatives see the 250th anniversary as a call to return to constitutional limits on government power, while progressives use the occasion to push for expanded state authority in the name of "progress."

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the question is whether Republican leadership will translate patriotic rhetoric into concrete action—blocking federal overreach, protecting property rights, and defending Second Amendment freedoms that the Founders enshrined.

Watch whether Van Epps's Declaration bill gains co-sponsors and whether it becomes a rallying point for constitutional conservatives heading into 2026.
