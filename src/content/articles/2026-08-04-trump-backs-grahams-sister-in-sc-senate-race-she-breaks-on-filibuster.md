# Graham's Sister Breaks With Late Brother on Filibuster—and Lands Trump's Nod

South Carolina Republican Darline Graham Nordone is running for a full Senate term with Donald Trump's endorsement—and she's already staking out territory to her brother's right on Senate procedure. During a Republican primary debate Monday, Nordone called for eliminating the 60-vote filibuster threshold rather than waiting for Democrats to do it themselves, a position that departed from her late brother Lindsey Graham's long-standing defense of the filibuster as a check on majority overreach.

Trump endorsed Nordone "for the Good of our Nation" ahead of the August 11 special Republican primary (The Daily Wire, Just The News). The endorsement signals Trump's backing for a candidate willing to break with establishment Republican orthodoxy on procedural matters—a shift that reflects the former president's frustration with Senate rules that have blocked his agenda.

Conservatives remain divided on the filibuster's utility. Some argue it protects minority rights and forces bipartisan compromise; others contend it's become a tool Democrats exploit to obstruct Republican legislation, making its elimination necessary to govern effectively.

**Watch:** Whether Nordone's filibuster position becomes a litmus test for Trump-backed Senate candidates nationwide, and whether Republicans ultimately move to eliminate it if they gain Senate control.
