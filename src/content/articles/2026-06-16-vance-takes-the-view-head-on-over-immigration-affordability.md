# Vance Faces Hostile 'View' Panel Over Immigration, Affordability

Vice President JD Vance walked into the lion's den Tuesday, appearing on ABC's *The View* to defend the Trump administration's record on immigration, affordability, and other policy fronts—only to encounter the predictable hostility conservatives have come to expect from the daytime talk show.

According to Fox News and The Daily Wire, Vance sparred with the hosts over immigration policy and cost-of-living concerns, attempting to make the case for Trump-era economic and border security approaches. The VP reportedly joked about facing a "show of MAGA Republicans," though the hosts' reception was decidedly less friendly than the quip suggested.

The appearance underscores a broader conservative strategy: taking the message directly to hostile media audiences rather than preaching to the choir. Vance's willingness to engage *The View*'s liberal panel—rather than avoid it—signals confidence in the administration's messaging on pocketbook issues that resonate with working Coloradans struggling with inflation and housing costs.

Republicans argue that the Biden-Harris administration's spending and regulatory overreach fueled inflation that crushed household budgets across Colorado and the nation. Democrats counter that Trump-era policies bear responsibility for economic disruption.

Watch for whether this media offensive gains traction with persuadable voters in swing states—Colorado included—ahead of 2026.
