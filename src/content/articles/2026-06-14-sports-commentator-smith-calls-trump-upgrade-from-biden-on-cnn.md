# Sports Commentator Smith Breaks with CNN Script, Calls Trump 'Upgrade' from Biden

Stephen A. Smith, the prominent ESPN and CNN personality, refused to play along with progressive media talking points on Sunday, telling CNN's *State of the Union* host Kasie Hunt that President Trump represents an "upgrade" from Joe Biden in terms of basic presidential alertness.

The comment came after Smith criticized Biden for appearing to doze off during Game 3 of the NBA Finals—a moment that crystallized conservative concerns about the former president's fitness for office. When Hunt pressed Smith on whether Trump is "sharp enough" to lead, Smith didn't hedge: he contrasted Trump's evident engagement with Biden's visible decline.

The exchange matters because Smith commands credibility across ideological lines as a sports media heavyweight. His willingness to state the obvious—that a president who falls asleep in public is a problem—represents a crack in the media's protective shield around Biden during his final months in office.

Republicans have long argued that Biden's mental acuity became a disqualifying issue, one the mainstream press downplayed or ignored until the June debate performance forced acknowledgment. Smith's candor on CNN suggests even liberal-leaning commentators can't indefinitely defend the indefensible.
