# Democrats Can't Outrun Their Woke Record

Democrats are scrambling to distance themselves from the radical rhetoric of 2019–2021, but their own words keep catching up.

Candidates like Wisconsin gubernatorial hopeful Francesca Hong are now walking back positions they championed just years ago, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The problem for the left: every statement was captured on camera, every tweet archived, every policy proposal documented. As the *Daily Wire* notes, Democratic Socialists can't escape their own rhetoric—it "always circles back."

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her allies face particular scrutiny. The *National Review* asks pointedly: if progressives have recognized errors in their woke agenda, do they owe Americans more than a casual "oops"? The question cuts to credibility. Voters deserve to know whether Democrats genuinely changed their minds or are simply repositioning for electoral advantage.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. Progressive candidates here—from the statehouse to Congress—have embraced similar 2019–2021 talking points on energy policy, gun control, and government expansion. When they now claim moderation, Coloradans should demand specifics: Which positions have they actually abandoned? Which do they still support?

Watch whether Colorado Democrats running in 2026 face the same accountability their national counterparts are dodging.
