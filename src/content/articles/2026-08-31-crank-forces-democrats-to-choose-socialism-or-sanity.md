# House Republican Pushes Vote to Expose Democrats' Ties to Socialism

Colorado Republican Jeff Crank is forcing Democrats to go on record about their party's embrace of democratic socialism, introducing a House resolution condemning the ideology as the Democratic Socialists of America gain real political power nationwide.

Crank's resolution cites the DSA's expanding influence, pointing to victories like Zohran Mamdani's New York City mayoral campaign as evidence that socialism is no longer fringe within Democratic ranks (Fox News). The move puts moderate Democrats in an awkward position: vote with their party's socialist wing or break ranks publicly.

President Trump amplified the pressure Sunday, calling Michigan Democratic Senate candidate Abdul el Sayed "out of it" and predicting socialist candidates will damage centrist Democrats in November's midterms (Washington Examiner). Trump argues the DSA's radical agenda—higher taxes, government control of energy and healthcare, gun restrictions—will alienate swing voters.

**The conservative argument:** Socialism is incompatible with American prosperity. Colorado voters should demand their representatives state clearly whether they support or reject the DSA's platform of wealth redistribution and government expansion.

**Democrats counter** that the DSA represents grassroots activism and that conflating democratic socialism with Soviet-style authoritarianism is a scare tactic.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation votes for or against Crank's resolution—their answer will tell you where they really stand on government overreach.
