# Soros-Backed Prosecutors Face Growing Backlash Over Soft-on-Crime Records

Soft-on-crime prosecutors funded by liberal mega-donor George Soros are facing unprecedented recall and electoral pressure as crime-weary voters and victims' families demand accountability.

Descano's lenient treatment of violent criminals has drawn fire from murder victim Stephanie Minter's mother, Cheryl, who launched an online petition Wednesday demanding his removal (Washington Examiner).

Philadelphia District Attorney Larry Krasner, another Soros beneficiary, has become a symbol of the problem. His rhetoric—including recent remarks about "blowing up" the White House ballroom—underscores conservative concerns about radical prosecutors more interested in anti-establishment posturing than protecting citizens (Daily Caller).
