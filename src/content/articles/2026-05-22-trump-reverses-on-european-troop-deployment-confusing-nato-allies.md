# Trump Reverses Course on European Troop Deployments, Confusing NATO Allies

President Donald Trump reversed course this week on U.S. military presence in Europe, announcing plans to send additional troops to Poland after his administration had recently cancelled a similar deployment.

The apparent shift came after weeks of statements from Trump and officials about reducing the U.S. military footprint in Europe, according to PBS NewsHour. Trump posted on social media Thursday that "the United States will be sending an additional 5,000 Troops to Poland," reversing the earlier cancellation of troop deployments to Poland and Germany.

The abrupt reversal has left NATO allies bewildered, PBS NewsHour reported. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has since embarked on a diplomatic mission to reassure European partners and ease tensions over the conflicting signals, according to BBC World.

The back-and-forth underscores broader uncertainty among U.S. allies about Trump's approach to NATO commitments and European security. Rubio's reassurance efforts suggest the administration recognizes the damage caused by the mixed messaging.
