# Iceland Seizes Anti-Whaling Activist Ship; Crew Detained

Iceland's coast guard and special forces have seized a vessel operated by the Paul Watson Foundation, an anti-whaling activist group, and detained its 21-member crew, according to reports from 9News Denver and the New York Post.

The foundation, founded by prominent environmental activist Paul Watson, operates ships aimed at disrupting commercial whaling operations. Iceland, which maintains a whaling industry despite international pressure, took action against the vessel in Arctic waters.
