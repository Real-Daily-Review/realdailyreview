## 10 Reasons to Kill the Front Range Rail Tax — Starting With the Bill

The so-called CoCo Front Range passenger rail measure asks Colorado voters to blow billions of taxpayer dollars on a train that, by most honest projections, few people will actually ride. Critics lay out 10 concrete reasons to reject it, and the core argument is simple: this is a massive tax hike dressed up as a transportation solution. Colorado taxpayers — especially those along the Front Range who commute by car because rail simply doesn't serve their lives — deserve to keep that money in their own pockets, not hand it to a government rail bureaucracy.
