# Midwest GOP Picks Establishment Over Trump in Stunning Primary Rebuke

Minnesota Republicans rejected Donald Trump's endorsement Tuesday, nominating House Speaker Lisa Demuth over MyPillow CEO Mike Lindell for governor—a striking setback for the former president's primary influence in a crucial swing state.

Demuth's victory (Just The News, Fox News) signals GOP establishment strength in the Upper Midwest, even as Trump-backed candidates have dominated primaries elsewhere. Lindell, a grassroots conservative favorite, couldn't overcome Demuth's institutional support and moderate positioning heading into November's general election against Sen. Amy Klobuchar, who dominated the Democratic primary with 89.1% of the vote (Washington Examiner).

The Minnesota result contrasts sharply with Wisconsin, where Republicans nominated Rep. Tom Tiffany for governor (Breitbart), while Democrats there rejected their own progressive wing—socialist Francesca Hong lost to moderate David Crowley in a tight race (New York Post).

**The Colorado angle:** These Midwest contests preview November dynamics that could reshape federal energy and tax policy. A Klobuchar governorship would likely mean aggressive climate regulations and carbon restrictions affecting Colorado's energy sector and grid reliability. Conversely, GOP victories in Wisconsin and Minnesota could preserve market-friendly energy development and resist Biden-era regulatory overreach.

**Watch:** Whether Trump's primary influence rebounds in other swing states, or if the Midwest GOP shift signals broader establishment reassertion before November.
