# Trump Faces Institutional Constraints; Obama Weighs In on Personal Rivalry

President Trump is operating under significant institutional and political constraints that repeatedly block his policy ambitions, according to *National Review*, even as former President Obama claims Trump maintains an "obsession" with him—a charge that underscores the personal animosity between the two executives.

Trump's ability to govern faces friction from Congress, courts, and bureaucratic resistance, *National Review* reports, limiting his capacity to execute his agenda as freely as he did during his first term. Meanwhile, Obama told a podcast that Trump "obviously has a room in his head, a suite in his head," suggesting the current president behaves differently in private than on social media—a characterization that reflects the deep partisan divide between the two administrations.

For Colorado conservatives, the real story is what these constraints mean for Trump's energy and regulatory agenda. If institutional resistance is slowing Trump's rollback of Biden-era environmental rules and restrictions on oil and gas development, Colorado's energy sector and workers could face prolonged uncertainty. Republicans argue that removing regulatory obstacles is essential to energy independence and economic growth; Democrats counter that environmental protections must remain intact.
