# Anthropic Poised for First Profitable Quarter

Anthropic is on track to reach profitability in its second quarter, according to disclosures to investors, marking a milestone ahead of competitors OpenAI and xAI (Financial Times). The AI lab projects revenue will more than double to around $10.9 billion in the period, according to TechCrunch.

The achievement underscores the growing commercial viability of large language model companies as enterprise demand for AI services accelerates. Anthropic, which has raised billions in funding since its 2021 founding, has positioned itself as a safety-focused alternative to rivals in the generative AI space.

The company's path to profitability comes as the broader AI sector grapples with massive infrastructure costs and questions about sustainable business models. Anthropic's ability to reach the milestone ahead of larger, better-capitalized competitors suggests its approach to scaling and monetization is gaining traction.
