# Federal Judge Blocks Trump's 75-Country Visa Freeze, Dealing Blow to Border Security Push

A Biden-appointed federal judge has struck down Secretary of State Marco Rubio's policy suspending immigrant visa processing for applicants from 75 countries, ruling the administration lacked statutory authority for the freeze (Washington Examiner, Fox News).

U.S. District Judge Jeannette Vargas, in a 61-page decision, found the visa suspension policy—implemented in January and affecting nations including Afghanistan, Iran, Russia, and Somalia—contrary to law (Just The News). The judge's language was sharp: Fox News reports she called the policy "Orwellian."

**The conservative case:** Republicans and Trump officials argue the visa freeze is a legitimate national security measure tied to vetting concerns and border integrity. The administration contends it has broad authority to manage immigration flows and protect Americans from high-risk jurisdictions.

**The judicial obstacle:** The court's ruling suggests statutory limits on executive power in immigration matters, even during a Republican administration focused on stricter border enforcement.

For Colorado, this matters: stricter vetting aligns with conservative priorities on border security and sovereignty, while judicial roadblocks complicate any Trump-era immigration enforcement agenda affecting Colorado's workforce and communities.

**Watch next:** Whether the Trump administration appeals or seeks legislative authority to reinstate similar visa restrictions.
