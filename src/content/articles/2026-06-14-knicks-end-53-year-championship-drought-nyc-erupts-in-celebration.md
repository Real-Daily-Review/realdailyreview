# Knicks End 53-Year Championship Drought; NYC Celebrates Historic Win

The New York Knicks have finally broken their half-century championship curse, defeating the San Antonio Spurs and sending Manhattan into celebration mode—a stark reminder that some Americans still find joy in sports rather than politics.

Fans poured out of San Antonio's Frost Bank Center in jubilation (New York Post), while celebrity supporters like Ben Stiller reported being "as happy as I've ever felt" in the aftermath (New York Post). Even ESPN's Stephen A. Smith, typically voluble, was nearly left speechless by the emotional magnitude of the moment (New York Post).
