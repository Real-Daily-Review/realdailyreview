# Connecticut Democrat Sen. Chris Murphy Hospitalized After Stroke

A U.S. senator known for aggressive gun-control advocacy is recovering after a serious health event. Sen. Chris Murphy (D-CT), 53, was hospitalized Saturday at Johns Hopkins Hospital after suffering a stroke while attending his son's hockey game, according to his office (Fox News, Washington Examiner). Murphy's chief of staff said the senator began experiencing arm numbness and received immediate medical attention. His team expects a full recovery.

Murphy has been one of Congress's most vocal proponents of federal gun restrictions—a position that puts him at odds with Colorado's strong Second Amendment tradition and Republican lawmakers who oppose new firearms regulations. The Connecticut Democrat has repeatedly pushed for expanded background checks and assault-weapon bans, arguments conservatives reject as unconstitutional infringements on individual liberty.

While Murphy's colleagues from both parties have offered well-wishes, his absence from the Senate highlights the unpredictable nature of legislative schedules and the importance of succession planning in Congress.
