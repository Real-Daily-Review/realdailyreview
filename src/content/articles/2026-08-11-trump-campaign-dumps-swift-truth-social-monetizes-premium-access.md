# Trump Campaign Scrubs Taylor Swift From Social Media as Truth Social Pushes Premium Access

The Trump campaign has quietly removed Taylor Swift's music from its social media accounts, marking another chapter in the pop star's public distance from the former president—a symbolic move that underscores the deepening cultural divide between Hollywood and Republican politics.

According to 9News Denver, Swift's songs previously featured on Team Trump's TikTok accounts have disappeared over recent days. Swift endorsed Vice President Kamala Harris in 2024 and has been vocal about her opposition to Trump, making the removal a straightforward business decision for a campaign managing its messaging.

Meanwhile, Trump's Truth Social platform is monetizing access to the former president's posts. The Washington Examiner reports that over 10 customers have already paid $100,000 each for early real-time access through Truth Social's new Truth API program—a premium tier launched within the past two weeks. The move reflects Trump Media's effort to generate revenue from its platform's unique value proposition: direct, unfiltered access to Trump's communications.

For Colorado conservatives, these developments illustrate the ongoing realignment of media and tech ecosystems around ideological lines. Republicans have built alternative platforms precisely because legacy media and Big Tech have marginalized conservative voices.
