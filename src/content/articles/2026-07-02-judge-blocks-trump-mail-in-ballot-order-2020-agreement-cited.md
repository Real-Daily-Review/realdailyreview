# Federal Judge Blocks Trump's Mail-In Ballot Order, Citing 2020 Agreement

A Clinton-appointed federal judge has blocked President Trump's executive order directing the U.S. Postal Service to restrict mail-in ballot handling nationwide, citing language in a 2020 agreement that constrains USPS authority on election mail (Fox News, Daily Caller).

The ruling represents another judicial obstacle to Trump administration efforts to tighten election procedures. Republicans argue that mail-in voting expands fraud vulnerabilities and that the executive order represented a legitimate exercise of presidential authority over federal agencies. Democrats and the judiciary have consistently defended broad mail-in access as essential to voter participation.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado, which conducts all-mail elections, could face pressure if federal courts ultimately side with the Trump administration on ballot procedures—though this ruling suggests courts remain skeptical of executive overreach on election administration.

The decision underscores the ongoing legal and political battle over election mechanics. Conservatives should watch whether the administration appeals or pursues legislative remedies through Congress, where Republicans control both chambers and could attempt to codify stricter mail-ballot standards.
