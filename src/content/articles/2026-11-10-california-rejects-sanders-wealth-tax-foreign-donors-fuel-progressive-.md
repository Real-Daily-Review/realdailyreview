# Voters Reject Sanders Wealth Tax; Foreign Cash Fuels Progressive Agenda

California voters just rejected a Bernie Sanders-backed wealth tax—a rare rebuke of the Left's favorite redistribution scheme—while FEC records reveal a Shanghai-based tycoon and CodePink activist have funneled nearly $47,000 to Sanders, Rep. Pramila Jayapal, and other Squad members (Fox News).

The California defeat matters for Colorado. Progressive Democrats here have long eyed wealth taxes and aggressive billionaire-targeting policies as templates for state-level confiscation. If California voters—in deep-blue territory—rejected Sanders' wealth tax by 53% to 47%, it signals even liberal voters recognize such schemes threaten economic growth and investment.

Meanwhile, the foreign funding angle raises hard questions about who's bankrolling the radical left's agenda. Neville Roy Singham, a Shanghai-connected donor, and Jodie Evans of CodePink represent the kind of outside money progressives claim to oppose—yet eagerly accept when it advances their cause (The Daily Wire).

**Republicans argue** wealth taxes are economically destructive, drive capital flight, and ultimately shrink the tax base. **Democrats counter** that billionaires must "pay their fair share"—though California voters just disagreed.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state Democrats attempt their own wealth-tax push in the 2025 legislative session, betting they can succeed where California failed.
