# Colorado Honors Former Justice Melissa Hart; Free Black Friday Options Abound

Colorado flags will fly at half-staff Friday to honor former Colorado Supreme Court Justice Melissa Hart, who served on the bench from 2017 to 2025, according to the Colorado Judicial Branch (9News Denver). Hart's eight-year tenure concluded this year, marking the end of a significant chapter in the state's judicial history.

Meanwhile, Colorado families looking to avoid the Black Friday spending trap have options. Several state organizations are offering free admission and activities throughout the holiday weekend, giving Coloradans a chance to enjoy the season without draining their bank accounts (KDVR Fox31). From museums to parks and community centers, these free-day programs provide alternatives for budget-conscious households navigating inflationary pressures that have squeezed household finances across the state.

The dual focus—honoring judicial service while highlighting cost-of-living relief—underscores persistent economic headwinds facing Colorado families. With inflation still impacting purchasing power, many residents are actively seeking ways to maintain their standard of living without taking on additional debt or cutting back on family activities.

Conservatives should monitor whether state and local governments continue expanding free-admission programs as a substitute for addressing the root causes of inflation and high costs of living in Colorado.
