# Cornell Agrees to Independent Review of Sexual Assault Handling

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul announced Wednesday that Cornell University President Michael Kotlikoff has agreed to an independent investigation into the school's response to a female student's allegations of sexual assault by seven fraternity members in 2024 (Washington Examiner).

The review comes after Hochul pressed the university to examine how it handled the case. According to 9News Denver, Hochul said she spoke directly with Kotlikoff, who committed to the third-party investigation.

**The conservative concern:** Elite universities have long faced criticism for mishandling sexual assault cases and protecting fraternity culture over student safety. Republicans and conservatives argue that independent oversight—rather than relying on university bureaucracies with institutional incentives to minimize scandal—is the only way to ensure accountability and justice for victims. The question isn't whether an investigation should happen; it's whether universities can be trusted to police themselves without external pressure.

This case underscores a broader problem: when institutions prioritize reputation over transparency, victims suffer. Kotlikoff's agreement to an independent review, while welcome, shouldn't require a governor's intervention in the first place.

Watch whether the investigation produces real consequences for administrators who failed the student, or whether it becomes another PR exercise.
