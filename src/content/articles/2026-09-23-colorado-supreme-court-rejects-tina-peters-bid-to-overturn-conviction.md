# Colorado Court Closes Door on Tina Peters' Bid to Erase Conviction

Colorado's highest court has rejected former Mesa County Clerk Tina Peters' appeal to overturn her felony conviction, ending her legal fight to clear her record (CPR News). Peters, who was released from prison but continued pursuing exoneration, sought to have the conviction reversed through the state Supreme Court—a request the justices declined without comment.

Peters became a flashpoint in Colorado's 2020 election integrity debates after her office experienced a security breach involving voting equipment. She faced charges related to her handling of the incident and was convicted. While she has completed her prison sentence, the rejection of her appeal means the conviction stands.
