# Democrats' Socialist Wing Pushes Party Further Left on Immigration, Israel

Colorado Democrats should take note: their national party is lurching toward positions that will alienate mainstream voters and invite Republican attacks on border security and public safety.

The Democratic National Committee's Resolutions Committee last week approved a resolution calling on Congress to draft legislation abolishing Immigration and Customs Enforcement (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, Democratic Socialists of America-endorsed candidates are challenging pro-Israel moderates in primary races, signaling an internal civil war over foreign policy and immigration (Fox News Politics).

Republicans argue these moves expose Democrats' radical flank. "Abolishing ICE" means gutting interior immigration enforcement—a position that hands conservatives a potent 2024 message on border chaos and sanctuary policies. Colorado Republicans can hammer Democratic incumbents on whether they support dismantling federal immigration agencies.

Democrats counter that ICE has engaged in controversial enforcement tactics, but the party's embrace of abolition rhetoric—rather than reform—plays directly into GOP messaging that Democrats prioritize open borders over law enforcement.

For Colorado, this matters: the state has seen significant illegal immigration flows, and Denver's sanctuary policies remain contentious. Expect Republicans to tie Colorado Democrats to the national party's immigration radicalism in upcoming races.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation distances itself from the DNC resolution or doubles down on it.
