# Colorado County Pays $10M for Detention Center Death—Taxpayers Foot the Bill

Huerfano County taxpayers are on the hook for a $10.19 million settlement after a man died in county custody following what the lawsuit describes as a physical altercation with an officer and subsequent denial of medical care (9News Denver).

According to the complaint, Michael Burch was thrown into a metal bench during an incident at Huerfano County Detention Center, then denied medical attention for a week before his death in his cell (CPR News).

The settlement underscores a persistent problem: when government facilities fail in their duty of care, taxpayers—not the officials responsible—absorb the cost. Conservatives argue that accountability mechanisms should focus on individual misconduct rather than blanket liability that punishes entire communities. The lack of proper medical protocols and oversight at a county-run detention facility raises serious questions about management and training.

This case also highlights why jail reform must emphasize operational competence and staff accountability, not just litigation settlements that drain local budgets. Huerfano County residents deserve answers about what systemic failures allowed this to happen and what concrete changes are now in place.

Watch for whether county leadership implements meaningful reforms or whether this becomes another expensive lesson learned too late.
