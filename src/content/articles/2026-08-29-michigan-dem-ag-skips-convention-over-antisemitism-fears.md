# Michigan Democrats' Israel Rift Shows Leftist Party Fracturing Over Antisemitism

Nessel cited fears of antisemitism and personal safety after pro-Palestinian activists heckled Rep. Haley Stevens, D-Mich., during the party's April convention for voicing support for Israel (Fox News, Just The News). The harassment of a fellow Jewish Democrat over her pro-Israel stance reveals the depth of the rift consuming Democratic ranks.
