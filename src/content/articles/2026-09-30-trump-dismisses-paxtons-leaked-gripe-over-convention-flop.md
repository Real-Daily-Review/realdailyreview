# Trump Dismisses Paxton's Convention Complaint as GOP Fractures Over Strategy

Donald Trump is pushing back hard against leaked audio of Texas Republican Ken Paxton griping that the GOP's midterm convention tanked his polling numbers. "The convention was amazing," Trump told reporters (Washington Examiner), despite Paxton's reported grumbling that "everybody's numbers dropped" (Daily Caller).

The spat exposes a growing rift within Republican ranks over convention strategy heading into 2026. Paxton, the GOP's Texas Senate nominee facing Democrat James Talarico, apparently believed the party's marquee event failed to move the needle—a damaging admission if true, since conventions are supposed to energize the base and boost candidate profiles.
