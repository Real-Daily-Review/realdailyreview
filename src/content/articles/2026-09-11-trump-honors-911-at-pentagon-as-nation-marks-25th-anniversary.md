# Trump Honors 9/11 at Pentagon; Vance Joins Former Presidents at Ground Zero

President Donald Trump marked the 25th anniversary of 9/11 with a solemn ceremony at the Pentagon, while Vice President JD Vance attended the World Trade Center memorial alongside former presidents. Trump's remarks emphasized national unity in the aftermath of terror, stating: "In those harrowing days after the attacks, we saw the authentic spirit of our nation. We were all New Yorkers that day."

The dual observances reflected the administration's commitment to honoring first responders and victims of the deadliest terrorist attack on American soil. Trump's Pentagon appearance underscored the military's central role in the post-9/11 response and the ongoing defense of the homeland.

For Colorado, the 9/11 anniversary carries particular weight: the state lost residents in the attacks and has contributed significantly to military and intelligence operations in the two-decade war on terror. Colorado Springs hosts U.S. Space Force Academy graduates and NORAD operations—institutions born from post-9/11 security priorities.

Conservatives view such commemorations as essential reminders of why strong national defense, border security, and counterterrorism capabilities remain non-negotiable. The ceremony stood in contrast to progressive narratives that often downplay American exceptionalism or military sacrifice.
