# Knicks End 53-Year Championship Drought With Finals Victory Over Spurs

The New York Knicks claimed their first NBA title since 1973, defeating the San Antonio Spurs 94-90 in Game 5 of the Finals on Saturday night, according to 9News Denver and The Daily Wire.

The Knicks mounted a comeback after trailing by 10 points in the opening quarter, ultimately securing the series victory in San Antonio. The championship marks the franchise's first Finals win in over five decades—a drought that had tested the patience of one of the NBA's most storied franchises.

The victory capped a remarkable playoff run for New York, which entered the postseason as underdogs in a competitive Eastern Conference. The Spurs, despite their championship pedigree, couldn't hold off the Knicks' late-game execution.
