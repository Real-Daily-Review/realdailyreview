# Federal Judge Dismisses Case Against Wrongfully Deported Man

A federal judge has dismissed criminal charges against Kilmar Abrego Garcia, a Salvadoran man wrongfully deported last year, finding the prosecution was vindictive and selective. U.S. District Judge Waverly Crenshaw ruled Friday that the Justice Department pursued the case to punish Garcia for challenging his mistaken deportation, according to NPR and PBS NewsHour. The charges—which included human smuggling allegations—were dropped on grounds that the prosecution was designed to retaliate against Garcia rather than serve legitimate law enforcement purposes, CBS News reported.

Garcia's case became a focal point in debates over the administration's immigration enforcement efforts. The judge's decision represents a significant legal victory for Garcia, who had challenged what he characterized as a wrongful deportation. According to The Hill, the ruling marks "an enormous victory" for the defendant in a case that drew attention to potential overreach in federal prosecution.

The dismissal underscores tensions between immigration enforcement and due process protections. The Justice Department has not yet publicly responded to the ruling.

Watch for any potential appeal by the Justice Department or further developments in Garcia's civil claims related to his deportation.
