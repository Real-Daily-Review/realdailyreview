# Tony Romo Arrested on DUI Suspicion in Milwaukee

Former Dallas Cowboys quarterback and CBS NFL analyst Tony Romo was arrested Thursday night in Milwaukee on suspicion of operating a vehicle while intoxicated, marking a significant fall from grace for the high-profile sports personality.

According to reports from 9News Denver and The Daily Wire, Romo was stopped by officers on a highway after performing poorly on field sobriety tests. Wisconsin charges the offense as Operating While Intoxicated (OWI)—the state's equivalent to a DUI. The arrest represents Romo's first offense of this kind.

Romo, who transitioned from his NFL career to become CBS's lead game analyst, has since been released pending a court appearance scheduled for September 21, according to Just The News.

The incident underscores ongoing concerns about impaired driving nationwide, a public safety issue that transcends partisan lines. Colorado law enforcement similarly prioritizes DUI enforcement, with strict penalties for first-time offenders including license suspension, fines, and potential jail time.

The case will proceed through Wisconsin's court system, where Romo faces the standard legal process for OWI charges. Watch for developments at his September 21 court date and any potential impact on his broadcasting role at CBS Sports.
