# Government-Run Healthcare Scheme Masks True Cost, Conservatives Warn

Progressive Democrats are pushing single-payer healthcare while downplaying its staggering price tag—a bait-and-switch Colorado voters should reject.

Michigan Democratic Senate nominee Abdul el Sayed recently tried to reassure voters about his support for government-run healthcare, but the numbers tell a different story. According to the *Washington Examiner*, nonpartisan and even left-leaning estimates peg single-payer costs at more than $3 trillion annually—on top of existing federal spending already running trillions in the red.

Conservatives argue this represents classic progressive deception: sell voters on "free healthcare" while hiding the massive tax increases required to fund it. *National Review* warns against allowing government to nationalize entire industries, noting the predictable inefficiency and waste that follow government takeover of private sectors.

**The Colorado angle:** If Democrats gain ground nationally on single-payer, Colorado's business community and taxpayers face potential federal mandates forcing participation in a bloated government system. State employers and workers would bear the cost through higher taxes and reduced choice.

Republicans counter that market-based reforms—price transparency, interstate insurance competition, and reducing regulatory barriers—deliver better care at lower cost without government rationing.

Watch for whether Colorado Democrats echo el Sayed's healthcare rhetoric in 2026 state races—and whether they'll be honest about the tax bill.
