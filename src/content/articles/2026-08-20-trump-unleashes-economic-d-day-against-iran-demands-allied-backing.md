# Trump Launches 'Economic D-Day' Against Iran—Demands Allied Support

Trump is escalating pressure on Iran's regime with what he calls the "most crushing economic operation ever," threatening severe sanctions against any nation or business propping up Tehran's economy (Fox News, Washington Examiner). The president declared "economic warfare" on Wednesday, warning that countries aiding Iran will face "tremendous economic consequences" and backing away from peace talks that have dragged on for nearly six months (Just The News).

The administration is targeting Iran's shadow economy with an unprecedented sanctions campaign, demanding that U.S. allies join the effort (The Daily Wire). Trump framed the move as a response to Iran's refusal to negotiate, signaling that diplomatic overtures have exhausted themselves.

**The Colorado angle:** Energy markets could feel ripple effects. Tighter Iran sanctions historically tighten global oil supplies, potentially affecting gas prices at the pump and energy sector stability—matters that hit Colorado consumers and the state's oil and gas workforce directly.

**What to watch:** Whether European and Asian allies actually comply with Trump's sanctions demands, or whether they prioritize trade relationships with Iran over U.S. pressure.
