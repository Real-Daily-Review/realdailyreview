# Democratic Senate Candidates' Troubling Associations Raise Questions About Judgment

Democratic Senate candidates are facing scrutiny over their ties to figures with criminal histories and fringe ideologies—a pattern that should concern voters nationwide, including Colorado Democrats watching their party's direction.
