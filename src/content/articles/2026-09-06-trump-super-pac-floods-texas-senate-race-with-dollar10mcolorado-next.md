# Trump Super PAC Floods Texas Senate Race—But Colorado Republicans Should Watch the Playbook

Donald Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is dumping $10 million into Ken Paxton's Texas Senate race against Democrat James Talarico, signaling Trump's willingness to spend heavily in competitive GOP primaries (Fox News, Just The News). The Texas attorney general and Talarico are running neck and neck, according to recent polling, making the seat a potential bellwether for midterm dynamics.

The cash injection underscores Trump's grip on Republican fundraising and his strategy of backing loyalists in high-stakes races. MAGA Inc.'s firepower—and Trump's dual headlining of both nights of the GOP convention in Dallas—demonstrates the former president's continued leverage over party resources and messaging.

**For Colorado conservatives**, this matters. If Trump's super PAC model proves effective in Texas, expect similar plays in competitive Colorado races, particularly in Senate and House contests where margins are tight. Republicans should monitor whether MAGA Inc. targets Colorado Democrats or backs primary challengers to sitting GOP members deemed insufficiently loyal. The Texas playbook—heavy ad spending, Trump's personal endorsement, convention-stage dominance—could reshape Colorado's 2026 election landscape.
