# UK Police Arrest 5 Men Near U.S. Air Base on Terror Suspicion

British authorities have arrested five men near RAF Fairford, a Royal Air Force base used by the United States for military operations, on suspicion of preparing a terrorist act.

Police declared a "major incident" in the Whelford area near the base and evacuated nearby properties as bomb disposal units responded to the scene (9News Denver, Washington Examiner). The arrests come as RAF Fairford serves as a key staging point for U.S. defensive operations in the Middle East, including strikes authorized by the UK against Iran.

Details on the specific charges and nature of the suspected explosives remain limited, though multiple outlets confirm the men face explosives-related offenses. Police have not yet released the identities of those detained or provided a timeline for charges.

The incident underscores ongoing security concerns at military installations hosting U.S. forces abroad. RAF Fairford has long been a critical hub for American air operations in the region, making it a potential target for hostile actors.
