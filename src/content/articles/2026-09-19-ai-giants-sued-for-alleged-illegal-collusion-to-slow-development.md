# AI Giants Face Antitrust Lawsuit Over Alleged Development Slowdown

A federal lawsuit accuses four major AI companies—Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX's xAI, and Google—of illegally colluding to slow artificial intelligence development, according to filings in U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California (Washington Examiner).

The proposed class-action suit, filed by four consumers paying for ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or Gemini subscriptions, alleges the companies violated antitrust law through a coordinated "pacing" agreement. The complaint centers partly on a September 12 essay by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei calling for measured AI advancement (9News Denver).

**The conservative angle:** If true, this represents exactly the kind of corporate collusion that free-market advocates warn against—competitors secretly agreeing to limit innovation and consumer choice. Rather than competing to deliver faster, better AI tools at lower prices, these firms allegedly prioritized regulatory appeasement and market control. This undermines the competitive dynamism that should drive the tech sector and benefits consumers.

The suit raises questions about whether Big Tech is using "safety" rhetoric as cover for anticompetitive behavior—a pattern conservatives have flagged in other industries captured by regulatory capture.
