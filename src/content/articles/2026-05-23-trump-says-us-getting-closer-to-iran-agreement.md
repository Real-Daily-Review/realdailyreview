# Trump Administration Signals Progress in Iran Negotiations

President Trump said the U.S. is "getting a lot closer" to an agreement with Iran, according to CBS News, as officials and negotiators discuss a process to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

The diplomatic overture marks a shift in approach. According to NPR, three months into the conflict with Iran, the Trump administration has alternated between strike threats and diplomatic engagement, with neither strategy producing a clear resolution so far.

U.S. officials briefed on the conversations have indicated talks are underway on the mechanics of reopening the strategic waterway, a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments. The comments suggest the administration may be prioritizing negotiation over military escalation at this juncture.

The timing and substance of any potential agreement remain unclear. Watch for official statements from the State Department or Trump administration officials clarifying the scope of negotiations and any timeline for resolution.
