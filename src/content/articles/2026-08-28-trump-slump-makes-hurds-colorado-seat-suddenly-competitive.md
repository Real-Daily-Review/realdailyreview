# Trump Slump Opens Door for Hurd's Reelection Challenge in Colorado's 3rd

Rep. Jeff Hurd entered 2026 expecting a comfortable reelection in Colorado's 3rd Congressional District, but sagging Trump approval ratings have suddenly made his seat competitive, according to reporting from the *Colorado Sun* and CPR News.

Hurd, facing unexpectedly tough headwinds, is now actively distancing himself from the president—a tactical shift that underscores how national political currents can reshape even seemingly safe races. The congressman's strategy reflects a broader Republican calculus: in districts where Trump's numbers have weakened, GOP incumbents are betting that separation from the White House plays better than loyalty.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Hurd's Trump-skepticism resonates with 3rd District voters or whether it signals deeper erosion of Republican support in a traditionally conservative region. The race will test whether Trump's unpopularity can flip a Republican seat or whether Hurd's local standing holds firm. Taxpayers and conservative voters in the district should demand clarity on where candidates stand on spending restraint, energy development, and Second Amendment protections—not just Trump approval ratings.
