# LIV Golf's Collapse Leaves PGA Tour Victorious—But the Sport Forever Changed

Saudi-backed LIV Golf is imploding under mounting losses, lawsuits, and unpaid bills, marking a stunning defeat for the upstart league that promised to shake up professional golf. According to the *Washington Examiner*, while mainstream sports media and the PGA Tour celebrate the demise, LIV Golf's brief existence accomplished something conservatives should recognize: it exposed the PGA Tour's own hypocrisy and forced the stuffy establishment to compete on merit rather than gatekeeping.

LIV Golf's aggressive player recruitment and no-cut format challenged the PGA's monopoly and challenged the sport's stale, risk-averse culture. The league's collapse doesn't erase that disruption—it simply means the PGA Tour, facing real competition, had to adapt or die. That's capitalism working.

For Colorado golf enthusiasts and business-minded conservatives, the lesson is clear: even failed ventures can reshape markets and force incumbents to innovate. The PGA Tour's eventual merger discussions with LIV Golf's backers underscore that competitive pressure, not regulatory protection, drives improvement.

Watch whether the consolidated golf landscape maintains the competitive energy LIV Golf forced into the sport, or whether the PGA reverts to its old gatekeeping ways now that the threat has vanished.
