# Trump Unveils New Air Force One—A Qatari Gift While Boeing Delays

President Trump has introduced a refurbished Boeing 747-8i as the temporary new Air Force One, a gift from Qatar that will serve until Boeing delivers purpose-built presidential jets in 2028. The aircraft, unveiled Friday at Joint Base Andrews, features expanded size, luxury upgrades, and a refreshed red, white, and blue paint scheme—and will lead a July 4 flyover of Washington, D.C.

The move sidesteps a costly delay in Boeing's contracted replacement aircraft. Rather than wait years for new jets or absorb massive taxpayer costs for expedited production, the Trump administration accepted Qatar's donation as a practical interim solution (The Daily Wire, Washington Examiner).

**The conservative case:** Accepting a foreign gift to fill a capability gap beats throwing billions at Boeing to accelerate delivery. It's fiscally prudent and avoids rewarding defense contractors for schedule slips. However, some conservatives may question the optics of relying on a Middle Eastern nation's aircraft for presidential transport, particularly given Qatar's geopolitical complexities.

The arrangement keeps Air Force One operational without new federal spending while the aerospace industry catches up—a rare example of government efficiency.

Watch whether Boeing faces pressure to accelerate the 2028 delivery timeline, and whether Congress questions the terms of Qatar's gift.
