# Democrats Pivot to Oversight While Affordability Pledges Fade

Democrats are preparing to abandon their 2024 affordability messaging now that they control Congress, pivoting instead toward aggressive regulatory oversight—even as their own immigration agenda threatens to undermine the cost-of-living gains they're claiming credit for.

According to Washington Examiner chief political correspondent Byron York, Democrats appear ready to "declare victory" on affordability despite mixed results. York noted gas prices have fallen from $4.30 to $3.72 per gallon, but Democrats are now shifting focus to regulatory expansion rather than sustaining relief.

That pivot reveals the real agenda. Breitbart reports Democrats are prioritizing amnesty and immigration bills that conservatives argue would suppress wages and spike rents—directly contradicting their affordability platform. Meanwhile, the Daily Caller notes a backlog of nuclear reactor permits piled up under the Trump administration, with Democrats now positioned to take over Nuclear Regulatory Commission oversight.

**Colorado angle:** Energy-dependent Colorado businesses and workers should brace for tighter nuclear and energy regulation under Democratic oversight. If Democrats follow through on amnesty promises while controlling Congress, Colorado's already-strained housing market and labor costs could face additional upward pressure—undoing any affordability gains.
