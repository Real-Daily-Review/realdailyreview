# Trump Threatens Trade War With Spain Over NATO Spending Shortfall

President Trump is escalating pressure on NATO allies to meet defense commitments, threatening to end all trade with Spain after the country refused to hit the 5% defense spending target required of member nations. "Spain is a wasted cause," Trump declared at a NATO summit press conference with Secretary General Mark Rutte, according to Fox News and the Washington Examiner.

Trump's hardline stance reflects a long-standing conservative argument: American taxpayers shouldn't subsidize European defense while allies shirk their obligations. Republicans have consistently demanded NATO members pull their weight financially rather than relying on U.S. military backing.

**The Colorado angle:** Energy-dependent Colorado has stakes in transatlantic trade relationships. Any broad trade restrictions could ripple through Colorado's agriculture, manufacturing, and energy sectors—though the full economic impact remains unclear.

Democrats and European allies will likely argue that Trump's trade threats destabilize the Western alliance at a critical moment, but conservatives counter that accountability and reciprocal burden-sharing are non-negotiable.

Watch whether Trump follows through on the trade threat or uses it as leverage to force Spain and other lagging NATO members to increase defense spending commitments.
