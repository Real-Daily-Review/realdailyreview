# Democratic Base Embraces Socialism as Party Drifts Left, Alarming Moderates

A CBS News/YouGov poll has exposed a stark ideological divide within the Democratic Party—and it should worry Colorado voters who value free enterprise. According to the survey, 58% of Democrats hold a more positive view of socialism than capitalism, while half view capitalism negatively (Washington Examiner, Breitbart).

The numbers reveal how far the Democratic base has drifted from mainstream American economics. Even Sen. John Fetterman (D-PA), hardly a conservative, called the findings "lunacy," signaling alarm among party moderates about the socialist tilt.

**Why Colorado should care:** This ideological shift directly threatens Colorado's economy. The state's energy sector, agriculture, and small-business community depend on market-driven policies, not government-directed socialism. If Democrats continue embracing anti-capitalist rhetoric, Colorado's working families—from oil and gas workers to ranchers to tech entrepreneurs—face pressure for punitive taxes, regulatory overreach, and government control of industry.

Republicans argue the poll proves Democrats have abandoned the free-market principles that built American prosperity. Conservatives warn that socialist-leaning policies—price controls, wealth taxes, nationalized industries—destroy jobs and innovation.
