# EU's Top Diplomat Warns Europe: Don't Appease Putin's Sabotage Campaign

Europe's chief diplomat—a woman who lived under Soviet oppression—is sounding the alarm that Russia's intimidation strategy will only escalate if the West capitulates. Kaja Kallas, the EU's top diplomat, told European leaders that Moscow's sabotage and disinformation campaign is designed to fracture support for Ukraine, and giving in would invite worse aggression (Fox News).

The warning comes as Russia and China doubled down on anti-American rhetoric at the UN General Assembly, with Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Vice President Han Zheng both defending Iran against U.S. policy and siding against American interests on Cuba (Washington Examiner). The coordinated messaging underscores a hardening axis of authoritarian powers working to undermine Western resolve.

For Colorado, the stakes matter: energy security, defense manufacturing, and military readiness all hinge on NATO stability and a strong American posture abroad. Weakness invites aggression; appeasement rewards it. Republicans argue that strength—not accommodation—deters authoritarian expansion, a lesson Kallas learned firsthand under Soviet rule.
