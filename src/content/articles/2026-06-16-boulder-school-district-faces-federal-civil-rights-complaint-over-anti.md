# Boulder School District Faces Federal Civil Rights Complaint Over Antisemitic Harassment

A Jewish middle-schooler in Boulder endured two years of severe antisemitic harassment and physical assault while district officials sat idle—a failure that now has the Anti-Defamation League filing a federal civil rights complaint.

According to 9News Denver and CPR News, the student faced repeated threats, harassment, and physical violence. The Daily Caller reports the abuse escalated to a peer fashioning a Chromebook charging cord into a lasso in an apparent strangulation attempt—yet administrators failed to intervene adequately.

The ADL characterizes the district's inaction as a clear violation of the student's civil rights. This case raises hard questions about school safety and administrative accountability that Colorado parents across the state should demand answers on: Why did Boulder Valley officials allow this to persist for two years? What safeguards exist to protect vulnerable students from harassment and assault?

The complaint underscores a broader conservative concern: when schools prioritize ideology over student safety and fail to enforce basic discipline, families suffer. Colorado taxpayers fund these districts and deserve transparency on how administrators handled this case and what systemic failures enabled it.

Watch whether the district faces federal sanctions and what policy changes—if any—Boulder Valley implements to prevent future failures.
