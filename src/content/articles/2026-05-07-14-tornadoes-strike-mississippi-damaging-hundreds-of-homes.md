# Mississippi Tornadoes Leave Hundreds of Homes Damaged, Multiple Injured

Fourteen tornadoes swept across Mississippi, collapsing hundreds of homes, downing trees, and knocking out power lines across multiple counties, according to CBS News.

A dozen people were injured at a trailer park in Bogue Chitto, a rural community in Lincoln County, said Scott Simmons, a spokesperson for the Mississippi Emergency Management Agency, according to PBS NewsHour. Survivors described harrowing moments during the storms, with some crawling under furniture and holding onto their children as the tornadoes struck.

The scale of damage across the state remained under assessment as emergency officials surveyed affected areas. Power outages affected residents across multiple counties as infrastructure sustained significant damage from the severe weather.
