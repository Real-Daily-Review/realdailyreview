# Trump's South Carolina Hedge: Double Endorsement Muddies GOP Governor Race

Donald Trump's decision to endorse both Republican candidates in South Carolina's gubernatorial runoff—Lt. Gov. Pam Evette and Attorney General Alan Wilson—undercuts the decisive backing that typically amplifies a frontrunner's momentum. Trump claimed he "couldn't pick between" the two "highly respected" candidates, but the move signals weakness in his ability to settle intra-party contests decisively (Washington Examiner).

The dual endorsement represents a notable reversal. Trump had previously backed Evette, making the Friday announcement a public retreat that could embolden Wilson's campaign and confuse GOP voters seeking clear direction. As the Daily Caller noted, Trump's framing—"with either one you can't go wrong"—reads more like a diplomatic dodge than the forceful endorsement his base expects.

For Colorado Republicans watching national GOP dynamics, this matters: Trump's influence on primary outcomes remains potent but not absolute. When the former president hedges, it opens space for candidates to build independent coalitions and for state parties to reassert their own judgment.

The real test comes in voter turnout and which candidate can convert Trump's tepid support into actual votes. Watch whether Evette's initial Trump backing holds or whether Wilson capitalizes on the president's refusal to choose.
