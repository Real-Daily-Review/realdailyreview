# Trump's Iran Deal Collapse Sparks Conservative Criticism Over Capitulation

President Trump's sudden reversal on Iran negotiations has drawn sharp rebuke from across the right, with conservatives arguing he abandoned core positions to secure a face-saving truce agreement.

National Review reports that Trump capitulated on "all the key issues of contention" entering the conflict, effectively adopting Iran's negotiating stance. The criticism stings because Trump had campaigned on strength against Tehran and withdrawal from the Obama-era nuclear deal.

Colorado Sun columnist Mike Littwin frames the reversal as desperation, noting that Trump's team—including Vice President JD Vance—has resorted to misleading claims about the agreement's terms to justify the shift.

For Colorado conservatives, the Iran reversal raises questions about Trump's negotiating credibility heading into his second term. Coloradans who backed Trump on foreign policy strength and energy independence—particularly regarding sanctions on Iranian oil—now face uncertainty about whether the administration will hold firm on other commitments.

Republicans must reckon with whether this signals a pattern of retreat on stated principles when political pressure mounts. Watch whether conservative media and GOP lawmakers demand clarity on what Trump actually secured versus what he surrendered.
