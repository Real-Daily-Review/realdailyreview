# Republican Lawmakers Demand WNBA Crack Down on Dangerous Play Against Caitlin Clark

Republican lawmakers are calling out the WNBA for failing to protect star player Caitlin Clark from what they characterize as unsafe, targeted physicality on the court. According to 9News Denver, GOP representatives have urged WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert to enforce stricter player safety standards after multiple incidents involving Clark drew scrutiny.

National Review argues the league's treatment of Clark—the Indiana Fever's transformative rookie—suggests institutional resistance to her presence, describing her as "treated like a virus that the league's antibodies seek to reject." The Daily Caller reports that officiating inconsistencies have fueled the controversy, with the issue now reaching Capitol Hill.

Republicans contend the WNBA is either unwilling or unable to enforce equal protection standards, allowing physical play against Clark that would draw flagrant fouls if directed at other players. They argue this undermines the league's credibility and player safety protocols.

Democrats and WNBA officials have not publicly responded to these specific allegations in the sourced material, though the league's silence itself has drawn conservative criticism as tacit acceptance of double standards.

**Watch for:** Whether Commissioner Engelbert issues new safety directives or whether GOP pressure translates into concrete rule changes before the season concludes.
