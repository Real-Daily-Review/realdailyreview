# French Singer Patrick Bruel Denies Sexual Assault Allegations; Toronto Police Officers Arrested in Barcelona

French singer Patrick Bruel is facing a wave of sexual assault allegations in France, including a rape claim from TV and radio presenter Flavie Flament, who alleges the assault occurred in 1991, according to BBC World. Bruel has denied the accusations.

In a separate incident, three off-duty Toronto police officers were arrested in Barcelona last week in connection with a sexual assault, according to The Guardian. Police in Catalonia say the alleged assault involved a sex worker in a taxi in the Ciutat Vella neighborhood during the early hours of May 13. The arrests add to scrutiny facing the Toronto police force, which is already under public pressure.

The two cases underscore ongoing allegations of sexual misconduct involving public figures and law enforcement across multiple jurisdictions. Bruel's denials and the Barcelona arrests remain under investigation.
