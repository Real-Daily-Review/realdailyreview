# Broncos' Henley Trade Collapses at Deadline

Denver's hopes for a defensive upgrade evaporated Wednesday when the Broncos' reported trade for Chargers linebacker Daiyan Henley fell apart, leaving the team scrambling just hours before the NFL trade deadline closed.

The deal, which had been announced Tuesday, never materialized—a last-minute reversal that underscores the volatility of deadline day negotiations. Neither the Broncos nor Chargers disclosed specific reasons for the collapse, but the failed transaction leaves Denver's linebacker corps unchanged heading into the stretch run.

For Broncos fans and the organization's front office, the breakdown represents a missed opportunity to bolster a defense that's been inconsistent this season. The team had apparently identified Henley as a potential difference-maker, only to watch the acquisition slip away.

The failed trade highlights the unpredictability of NFL deadline maneuvering—deals that appear locked can unravel over contract details, medical clearances, or last-minute hesitation from either side.
