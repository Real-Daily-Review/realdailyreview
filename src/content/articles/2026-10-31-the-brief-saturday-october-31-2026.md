## Avian Flu Hits Another Colorado Dairy

State health officials confirmed avian flu at another Colorado dairy operation, adding to a string of detections that have rattled the state's agricultural sector. Officials say pasteurized milk remains safe for consumers. For Colorado ranchers and dairy producers already navigating regulatory pressure and thin margins, every new outbreak raises the stakes — and the question of whether state and federal agencies are doing enough to protect the industry without burying it in red tape.

## Polish President Storms High Court in Constitutional Showdown

## Tennessee Execution Survivor Breaks Silence

Christa Pike, who survived a lethal injection in Tennessee last month, says she is in constant pain but grateful for support she has received. For Colorado conservatives, the issue hits close to home — Colorado abolished the death penalty in 2020 under Democratic Governor Jared Polis, over the objections of victims' families and law enforcement advocates who argued the state surrendered a critical tool of justice.

## Two Helicopters Collide Near Oregon Airport, 3 Dead

Colorado has a robust general aviation community, and any accident involving training aircraft draws attention to airspace safety protocols and whether federal aviation oversight is keeping pace with increased air traffic around smaller regional airports across the Mountain West.
