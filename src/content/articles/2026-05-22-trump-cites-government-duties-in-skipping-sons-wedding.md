# Trump Skips Son's Wedding Due to Government Duties

President Trump will not attend his son Donald Trump Jr.'s wedding this weekend, citing official responsibilities in Washington, D.C.

"I feel it is important for me to remain in Washington, D.C., at the White House during this important period of time," Trump said Friday, according to CBS News. The Hill reported Trump attributed his absence to "circumstances pertaining to Government," adding he "very much wanted to be" present for his son's marriage to Bettina Anderson.

Trump Jr. was previously married to Vanessa Trump, with whom he has five children, according to CNBC.

The president did not elaborate on which specific government matters required his presence during the weekend event.

Watch for any additional statements from Trump or his family regarding the wedding or the government circumstances he cited.
