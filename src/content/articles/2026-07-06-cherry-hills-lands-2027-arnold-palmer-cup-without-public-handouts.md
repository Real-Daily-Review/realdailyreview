# Cherry Hills to Host 2027 Arnold Palmer Cup—A Win for Colorado Golf and Tourism

Colorado's prestige in collegiate golf is on the rise: Cherry Hills Country Club has secured hosting rights for the 2027 Arnold Palmer Cup, one of the nation's premier mixed-gender collegiate tournaments (KDVR, 9News).

The event represents a significant draw for Denver-area tourism and hospitality. Cherry Hills, a storied venue in the Cherry Hills Village enclave south of Denver, will welcome the nation's top college golfers to compete in a format that pairs men and women—a distinctive feature that has made the Arnold Palmer Cup a marquee event in amateur golf.

The tournament's return to Colorado underscores the state's standing as a golf destination and reflects strong private-sector investment in world-class sporting events. Unlike government-subsidized stadium deals or tax-increment financing schemes that burden Colorado taxpayers, this event relies on private club resources and market-driven sponsorship.

For the Denver metro area, the 2027 cup means hotel bookings, restaurant revenue, and visibility on the national sports calendar—economic activity generated without a dime of public subsidy or regulatory mandates.

Conservatives should watch whether local or state officials attempt to extract public funding, tax breaks, or regulatory concessions from Cherry Hills or tournament organizers in the name of "economic development."
