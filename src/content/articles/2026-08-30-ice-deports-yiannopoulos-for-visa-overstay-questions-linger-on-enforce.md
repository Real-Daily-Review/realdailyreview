# Milo Yiannopoulos Deported After ICE Arrest for Visa Overstay

British political commentator Milo Yiannopoulos has been deported to the United Kingdom following his arrest by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents, according to reporting from *Just The News* and *Breitbart*. Yiannopoulos was apprehended in New Orleans, Louisiana on Thursday night after overstaying his visa, *Breitbart* reports.

The deportation marks a sharp turn for the controversial right-wing commentator, who has maintained a visible presence in American conservative media circles. While some conservative outlets have questioned whether political infighting—specifically involving activist Laura Loomer—played a role in the enforcement action (*Daily Caller*), the core issue remains straightforward: visa violation.

The case underscores a broader tension within immigration enforcement. Conservatives have long argued that the federal government must enforce immigration law uniformly and without political favoritism. Yet the circumstances surrounding Yiannopoulos's arrest—and questions about whether internal conservative disputes influenced DHS action—raise concerns among some on the right about whether enforcement is being applied selectively rather than consistently.

For Colorado taxpayers watching federal immigration policy, this episode illustrates how enforcement decisions can become entangled with political considerations, a risk that cuts both ways depending on who controls the agencies.
