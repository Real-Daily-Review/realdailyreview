# Unaffiliated Doctor Challenges Far-Left Democrat in Colorado's 1st District

A Denver physician has qualified for the November ballot as an independent challenger to Democrat Melat Kiros in Colorado's 1st Congressional District, signaling voter unease with the party's hard-left direction on Israel and antisemitism.

Shimon Blau, a first-time candidate, gathered enough signatures to secure ballot access after expressing alarm over Kiros' primary victory last month. According to the Colorado Sun, Blau—who is Jewish—cited Kiros' controversial statements on Israel and her refusal to label the 2025 Boulder firebombing of a Jewish community rally as antisemitic as reasons for entering the race.

Kiros won the Democratic primary to succeed retiring U.S. Rep. Diana DeGette, a seat Democrats have held for decades. The emergence of an unaffiliated challenger suggests fractures within the party base over progressive stances on Middle East policy and antisemitism—issues that have roiled Democratic politics nationally.

Conservatives see Blau's candidacy as evidence that even in deep-blue Colorado districts, voters reject the progressive left's equivocation on Jewish safety and Israel. Republicans argue the Democratic Party's tolerance for anti-Israel rhetoric and failure to condemn antisemitic violence alienates moderate voters.
