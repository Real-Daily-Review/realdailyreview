# Senate Republicans Demand Jack Smith Answer for Trump Prosecutions

Senate Judiciary Committee Chair Chuck Grassley (R-IA) has invited former Special Counsel Jack Smith to testify on September 22, signaling Republican determination to scrutinize the federal prosecutor's handling of cases against President Donald Trump.

Smith, who led the Justice Department's prosecutions of Trump on classified documents and January 6th charges, has become a prime target for GOP oversight now that Republicans control the Senate. Grassley's invitation reflects conservative concerns that Smith's office operated with political bias and weaponized federal law enforcement against a political opponent—a core complaint from Trump allies and Republican lawmakers.

Republicans argue the special counsel's office pursued aggressive charges that many legal analysts viewed as legally questionable, particularly the classified documents case. They contend Smith's tenure exemplifies the kind of prosecutorial overreach that erodes public trust in institutions and threatens equal justice under law.

Democrats have defended Smith's independence and the legitimacy of his investigations, but with Republicans now holding the Senate gavel, that defense will face hostile questioning.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this hearing matters: it tests whether Republican-led Congress will actually hold accountable the federal bureaucrats who weaponized the Justice Department. Watch whether Smith accepts the invitation and what specific documents Grassley demands about the Trump cases.
