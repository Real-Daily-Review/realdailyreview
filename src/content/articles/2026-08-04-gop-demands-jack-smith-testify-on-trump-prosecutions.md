# GOP Demands Answers on Jack Smith's Trump Prosecution

Republicans are demanding accountability from former special counsel Jack Smith over his handling of the federal cases against President Trump. Sen. Chuck Grassley (R-IA) has invited Smith to testify before the Senate Judiciary Committee on Sept. 22, according to the *Washington Examiner* and *Just The News*, as GOP lawmakers investigate what they view as weaponized prosecutions.

Smith became a prime Republican target after the Trump indictments, which conservatives argue represented a politicized abuse of federal power. Republicans now control the Senate and are using their majority to examine Smith's conduct and the Justice Department's independence under the Biden administration.

The invitation reflects broader GOP efforts to scrutinize the special counsel's office and its prosecutorial decisions. Conservatives argue the cases against Trump exemplified government overreach and selective enforcement—concerns that resonate across Colorado's Republican base, where skepticism of federal authority runs deep.

Democrats have defended Smith's work as independent and legally sound, but with Republicans controlling the Senate Judiciary Committee, the hearing will likely feature sharp criticism of the prosecution's scope and timing.

Watch for whether Smith accepts the invitation and what specific documents or communications Republicans demand regarding the investigation's origins and direction.
