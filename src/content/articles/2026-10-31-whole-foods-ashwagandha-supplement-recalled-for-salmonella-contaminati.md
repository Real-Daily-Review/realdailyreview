# Whole Foods Ashwagandha Supplement Recalled Over Salmonella Risk

Colorado consumers who bought stress-relief supplements at Whole Foods or Amazon need to check their medicine cabinets immediately. The 365 Whole Foods Market brand ashwagandha capsules—marketed to reduce stress and improve sleep—are being recalled due to potential salmonella contamination (9News Denver, New York Post).

The recall affects capsules sold through both Whole Foods stores and Amazon, meaning thousands of Colorado shoppers may have purchased the product. Salmonella contamination poses serious health risks, particularly for children, elderly individuals, and those with compromised immune systems.

This incident underscores a broader conservative concern: the supplement industry operates with minimal FDA oversight compared to pharmaceuticals. While conservatives generally oppose heavy-handed regulation, product safety failures like this demonstrate why basic quality controls matter. The voluntary recall system relies on companies policing themselves—a market mechanism that works until it doesn't.

Colorado residents should verify batch numbers and dispose of affected products safely. Those experiencing symptoms after taking the supplement should contact their healthcare provider.

Watch whether this recall prompts calls for stricter supplement regulation—and whether Colorado lawmakers attempt to impose state-level rules that could burden legitimate manufacturers and drive up costs for consumers seeking natural health alternatives.
