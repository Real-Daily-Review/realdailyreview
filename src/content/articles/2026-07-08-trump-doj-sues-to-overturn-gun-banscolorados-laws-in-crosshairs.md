# Trump DOJ Launches Aggressive Second Amendment Offensive—Colorado Gun Owners Should Pay Attention

The Trump Justice Department is mounting an unprecedented legal assault on firearm restrictions across the country, filing over a dozen lawsuits challenging assault weapons bans and handgun rosters that have strangled Second Amendment rights for years (Fox News Politics).

This matters for Colorado. The state's own assault weapons ban and magazine restrictions—passed by Democrats in 2013 over fierce Republican opposition—could face federal scrutiny under this DOJ strategy. Colorado gun owners and manufacturers have long argued these laws are unconstitutional infringements that don't meaningfully reduce crime while punishing law-abiding citizens.

Republicans argue that the Second Amendment protects an individual's right to bear arms without government interference, and that state-level bans contradict Supreme Court precedent. Democrats counter that reasonable regulations save lives and that the Constitution permits common-sense restrictions.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this DOJ push represents a rare opportunity to challenge laws that have cost the state business investment and burdened gun owners with compliance costs. The outcome could reshape Colorado's regulatory landscape.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature and governor attempt to defend the state's gun restrictions in federal court—and whether they'll spend taxpayer dollars fighting the Trump administration's legal challenge.
