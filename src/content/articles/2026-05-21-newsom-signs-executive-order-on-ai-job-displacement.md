# California Governor Signs Executive Order on AI Job Displacement

California Gov. Gavin Newsom signed an executive order Thursday directing state agencies to evaluate policies supporting workers displaced by artificial intelligence, according to The Hill and the Washington Examiner.

The move comes as industry leaders themselves have warned that AI could significantly impact the labor market. Newsom's order positions California at the forefront of the national debate over AI regulation and workforce protections, the Examiner reports, as the technology rapidly reshapes industries including media and customer service.

The executive order does not specify which support policies will be evaluated or implemented, nor does it detail a timeline for agency assessments. State officials have not yet announced concrete measures to address potential job losses.

The order reflects growing concern among policymakers about AI's labor market effects, even as the technology continues to advance rapidly across sectors. California's move may influence how other states approach AI workforce policy.
