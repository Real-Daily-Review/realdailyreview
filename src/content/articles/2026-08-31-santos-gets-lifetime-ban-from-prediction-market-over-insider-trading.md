# George Santos Gets Lifetime Ban From Prediction Market After Insider Trading Scheme

Disgraced ex-Congressman George Santos has become the first person ever permanently banned from online prediction market Kalshi after the platform determined he engaged in market manipulation and insider trading (Washington Examiner). Santos, who was expelled from Congress in 2023 after a fraud conviction and seven-year prison sentence, placed a series of large bets in February 2026 on whether he would attend President Trump's State of the Union address—then allegedly manipulated those contracts to profit from his own attendance decision. Kalshi imposed a $71,356 penalty alongside the lifetime ban.

The scheme represents exactly the kind of self-dealing that erodes public trust in markets and government alike. Santos fabricated his entire public persona—inventing a bogus Wall Street background to win office as a Republican (9News Denver)—then leveraged inside knowledge of his own movements for financial gain. It's a textbook case of why prediction markets, while useful tools for aggregating information, require robust safeguards against bad actors.

The Santos saga underscores a broader conservative concern: bad actors exploit systems designed for honest actors. Republicans and conservatives have long warned that regulatory capture and fraud flourish when government and markets lack transparency and accountability.

Watch whether Kalshi's enforcement action prompts Congress to tighten rules around prediction market participation by public officials.
