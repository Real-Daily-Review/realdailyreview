# Glenwood Springs City Council Sues to Shut Down ICE Detention Facility—Dodging Federal Authority

A left-leaning Colorado city council is using state courts to do what it won't do directly: shut down an Immigration and Customs Enforcement holding facility operating within its borders.

Glenwood Springs' City Council voted 6-1 Thursday to sue the property owner of the ICE detention center in state court, according to 9News Denver. The Colorado Sun reports that council members are banking on this legal strategy to close the facility while sidestepping a direct confrontation with federal immigration authorities.

**The conservative concern:** This is lawfare dressed up as local governance. Rather than respect federal immigration enforcement authority, progressive city leaders are weaponizing state courts to obstruct ICE operations. Conservatives argue that immigration enforcement is a federal responsibility, and Colorado municipalities shouldn't use litigation to undermine it.

The lawsuit targets the property owner, not ICE directly—a legal maneuver designed to exploit state landlord-tenant or zoning rules. If successful, it sets a dangerous precedent: any Colorado city council hostile to federal law can simply sue private parties into compliance with its ideological preferences.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** This case will test whether cities can effectively nullify federal authority through creative state litigation. Taxpayers should watch whether this lawsuit succeeds and whether other Colorado municipalities follow suit, turning the state into a patchwork of anti-federal sanctuary jurisdictions.
