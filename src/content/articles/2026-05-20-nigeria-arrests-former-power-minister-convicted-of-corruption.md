# Nigeria Arrests Former Power Minister Convicted of Corruption

Nigerian authorities have arrested a former power minister who had been in hiding following a corruption conviction, according to BBC World. The minister was sentenced to 75 years in jail in absentia earlier this month.

The arrest marks a significant moment in Nigeria's anti-corruption efforts, though Deutsche Welle notes broader questions about who faces prosecution in the country's anti-corruption campaign. The case underscores tensions between high-profile enforcement actions and questions about the consistency and scope of corruption prosecutions across Nigeria's government.

Details on the specific charges, the circumstances of the arrest, or the minister's current status remain limited in available reports. Watch for developments on whether the former official will appeal the conviction or provide testimony that might implicate other officials.
