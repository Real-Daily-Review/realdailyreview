Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her Democratic Socialist allies are scrambling to distance themselves from the wreckage of their woke policy experiments—but conservatives say they shouldn't be allowed to memory-hole their radical rhetoric.

When the left's national figures retreat from their positions without genuine accountability, it emboldens Colorado Democrats to double down on similar experiments locally.
