# Iran's Weapons Threat Exposes America's Self-Loathing Left

America faces a genuine national security crisis—and the progressive internet's first instinct is to blame the United States. Reports of Iranian plans for dirty bombs, chemical, and biological weapons have triggered the predictable response from the left: America deserves it.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, President Trump detailed Iranian efforts to develop chemical and biological weapons after U.S. sanctions closed off nuclear pathways. "Iran is looking at gases, looking at bio, because we've shut them out with the nuclear," Trump told Fox News. "Nuclear is much harder, and they're looking" at alternatives.

The *National Review* notes that online critics have rushed to frame American deterrence as provocation—the reflexive posture of what it calls "useful idiots" who blame U.S. policy rather than Iranian aggression. This isn't serious foreign policy debate; it's moral preening that weakens American resolve.

**The Colorado angle**: Colorado hosts critical national defense infrastructure and a significant military presence. Threats to U.S. security directly affect our state's economy, workforce, and strategic importance.

Conservatives argue that strength and clear deterrence prevent wars; appeasement invites them. The left's instinct to apologize for American power in the face of genuine threats from hostile regimes is not just naive—it's dangerous.

Watch whether Congress demands a full briefing on Iranian weapons capabilities and whether Democrats push back against Trump's hardline stance.
