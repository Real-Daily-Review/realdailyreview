# Colorado Voters Narrowly Back Youth Sports and Medical Restrictions

Colorado voters narrowly approved two ballot measures Tuesday that impose limits on transgender youth participation in sports and access to gender-affirming surgeries—a rare win for social conservatives in a state trending blue.

Proposition 134 restricts which youth sports categories transgender athletes can join, while Proposition 135 bans gender-affirming medical procedures for minors. Both measures passed with slim margins, according to CPR News.

Conservatives argue these measures protect fairness in women's sports and prevent irreversible medical interventions on minors who cannot legally consent to other procedures. They contend parental rights and biological reality should guide policy on youth athletics and healthcare.

Democrats and progressive groups opposed both measures, framing them as discriminatory and harmful to vulnerable youth. They argue existing safeguards adequately protect young people seeking medical care.

The narrow victories underscore Colorado's political divide. While the state has swung Democratic in recent cycles, Tuesday's results show significant voter concern over gender ideology in schools and youth programs—an issue resonating across the political spectrum.

**What to watch:** Whether the state legislature attempts to overturn or narrow these voter-approved measures through litigation or legislative action in the 2027 session.
