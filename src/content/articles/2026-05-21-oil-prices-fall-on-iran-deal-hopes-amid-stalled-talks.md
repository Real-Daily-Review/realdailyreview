# Oil Prices Fall on Iran Deal Hopes, but Uncertainty Remains

Oil prices have declined as investors bet on a potential U.S.-Iran agreement, though negotiations have stalled since the two countries agreed to a fragile ceasefire last month, according to CNBC.

The tentative optimism masks real downside risk. GasBuddy's head of petroleum analysis warned that gas prices could spike next week if the U.S. and Iran fail to reach a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments, The Hill reported.

The dynamic reflects how tightly energy markets are tied to geopolitical outcomes. Investors are pricing in a successful negotiation, but analysts caution that without a formal agreement, prices could reverse sharply as fuel inventories deplete at gas stations nationwide.
