# Bernie's Billionaire Hypocrisy Exposes Left's Wealth-Envy Agenda

Bernie Sanders has become a millionaire—yet continues railing against billionaires as threats to democracy. Complete Colorado notes the irony: Sanders shifted his populist wrath upward once he joined the wealth club himself, revealing the left's wealth-envy crusade as selective moral theater rather than principled economics.

This matters for Colorado because Sanders-style socialism still drives policy in Democratic strongholds like Boulder and Denver. Progressive politicians invoke "oligarchy" rhetoric to justify tax hikes, regulatory expansion, and wealth redistribution—even as they accumulate personal fortunes through book deals and speaking fees.

Republicans argue the real problem isn't billionaires existing; it's government picking winners and losers through crony capitalism and regulatory favoritism. When politicians attack wealth creation while enriching themselves, they expose the game: power, not principle.

Meanwhile, National Review reports President Trump is taking a hardline on Venezuelan oil nationalization, framing it as protecting American energy independence—a contrast to the left's reflexive hostility toward domestic energy development that keeps Colorado oil and gas workers employed.

**Watch for:** whether Colorado Democrats will distance themselves from Sanders-style billionaire-bashing or double down as midterm pressure mounts.
