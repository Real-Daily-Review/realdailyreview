# Trump Touts Record Employment; Democrats Float Third Impeachment

President Trump claims more Americans are working than at any point in U.S. history, crediting his administration's economic policies and pushing back against what he calls media refusal to report the gains. "The American People elected me to deliver HISTORIC Economic Results," Trump said, noting his responsibility to report achievements the "Fake News refuses" to cover (Just The News).

Meanwhile, House Democrats are signaling appetite for a third impeachment. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) told NBC's *Meet the Press* that impeaching Trump again would amount to "setting a standard" should Democrats regain House control after November's midterms (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative take:** Republicans see Trump's employment claims as validation of pro-growth, deregulatory economics—policies Colorado energy and agriculture sectors have benefited from under reduced federal overreach. Democrats' third-impeachment talk, conservatives argue, exposes the left's weaponization of impeachment as a partisan tool rather than a serious constitutional remedy. For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the contrast is stark: one party focused on economic results; the other on endless investigations and political revenge.

**Watch:** Whether Colorado's delegation—split between Republican and Democratic representatives—breaks ranks on any future impeachment vote.
