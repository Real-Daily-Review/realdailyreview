# Trump Resumes Military Operations Against Iran After Regime Escalates Threats

President Trump has formally notified Congress of resumed U.S. military operations against Iran, restarting the War Powers Act clock after months of relative calm collapsed under Iranian aggression (The Daily Wire). The administration cited Iranian attacks on commercial vessels in the Strait of Hormuz as the trigger for renewed operations beginning July 7.

The timing is significant: Iran has reportedly intensified assassination plots against Trump himself, according to intelligence assessments (Washington Examiner). The regime's desperation appears tied to Trump's decision to terminate the Obama-era memorandum of understanding with Tehran—a move conservatives have long championed as necessary to counter Iranian regional ambitions and nuclear threats.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Trump's decisive posture—scrapping failed diplomatic frameworks and responding militarily to Iranian provocations—restores American deterrence after years of weakness. Democrats and the left typically counter that military escalation risks broader conflict and prefer diplomatic channels, though Iran's recent behavior undercuts that argument.

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets and oil prices could face upward pressure if Middle East tensions spike further, affecting gas prices at the pump for Colorado drivers and transportation costs for state businesses.

Watch whether Congress challenges Trump's War Powers notification or whether Democrats attempt to constrain his Iran authority through legislation.
