# Missouri GOP Maps Blocked Days Before Election—A Warning for Colorado Redistricting Fights

Missouri's Supreme Court just handed Democrats a last-minute victory by blocking Republican-drawn congressional maps with ballots due in days—a cautionary tale for Colorado conservatives watching our own redistricting battles.

The Missouri court unanimously blocked the GOP maps ahead of November's midterms, prompting President Trump to blast the ruling as "ridiculous" (Fox News). Missouri Attorney General Catherine Hanaway immediately launched an appeal to the U.S. Supreme Court, but faces a brutal timeline: ballots must be finalized by Tuesday under state law and mailed shortly after (Just The News).

Republicans argue the ruling represents judicial overreach—courts substituting their own maps for legislatively drawn ones based on partisan grounds. The timing, they contend, is deliberately disruptive, forcing last-minute chaos that benefits Democrats.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's Independent Congressional Redistricting Commission drew our maps in 2021, but Democrats have already signaled appetite for court challenges if future maps don't suit their interests. This Missouri precedent shows how state courts can weaponize redistricting disputes to flip outcomes weeks before elections.

Conservatives should watch whether the U.S. Supreme Court intervenes in Missouri—and whether Colorado Democrats cite this ruling to challenge future maps in state court.
