# Connecticut Man Arrested 40 Years After Virginia Murder; Mango Tycoon's Son Faces Scrutiny in Hiking Death

A Connecticut man has been arrested in connection with the 1986 murder of 22-year-old Roberta Walls, whose body was discovered in a field in Virginia Beach on May 15, 1986, according to CBS News. The arrest comes four decades after the killing, marking a significant development in a decades-old cold case.

In a separate high-profile case, Jonathan Andic, son of Isak Andic—founder of Mango, one of Europe's largest clothing retailers—has been arrested following his father's fatal fall, according to the BBC. Jonathan denies involvement in the death, which authorities are investigating as either a murder or accident. The circumstances surrounding the incident remain under investigation.

Both cases underscore the persistence of law enforcement in pursuing cold cases and high-profile deaths. DNA evidence and modern investigative techniques have increasingly enabled authorities to solve decades-old crimes, while high-profile family cases continue to draw international attention.

Watch for developments in the Virginia Beach murder case as prosecutors present evidence, and for clarification on the nature of Isak Andic's death as the investigation into Jonathan Andic's involvement proceeds.
