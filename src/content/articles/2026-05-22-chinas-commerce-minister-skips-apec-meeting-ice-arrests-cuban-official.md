# China's Commerce Minister Absent From APEC Trade Meeting; ICE Arrests Cuban Official's Sister

China's Commerce Minister Wang Wentao skipped the opening of an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation trade meeting Friday, with Beijing citing "urgent official business," according to CNBC. International trade representative Li Chenggang chaired the session instead. The absence comes as China has called for expanded regional cooperation.

In a separate development, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrested Adys Lastres Morera, sister of a Cuban business official, one day after the State Department revoked her lawful permanent resident status, the Washington Examiner reported. Secretary of State Marco Rubio and ICE Homeland Security Investigations announced the arrest Thursday in Miami as the administration escalates pressure on Cuba, characterizing her as posing a threat to U.S. interests.

The timing of Wang's absence—whether routine or diplomatically significant—remains unclear from available reporting. The Cuban arrest reflects the administration's hardline stance on the island nation, though specific details about the threat assessment were not disclosed.

Watch for any official Chinese explanation for Wang's absence and further developments in U.S.-Cuba enforcement actions.
