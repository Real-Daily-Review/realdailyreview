# Colorado Schools Face Safety and Accountability Crisis

Colorado's public schools are under mounting scrutiny as lawsuits and criminal cases expose serious failures in student protection and staff oversight.

A former student at Addenbrooke Classical Academy has filed suit against the school and officials, alleging they ignored repeated racial harassment during her sophomore year—a damning indictment of administrative negligence (KDVR). Meanwhile, the Colorado Bureau of Investigation arrested a former Montrose school counselor on charges of distributing controlled substances to a minor, raising hard questions about background checks and institutional safeguards.

These cases underscore a conservative concern: public institutions entrusted with children's safety are failing at their core mission. When schools cannot prevent harassment, vet staff properly, or maintain basic security, they forfeit the trust and tax dollars Coloradans invest in them. The lawsuits will likely prove costly—and rightly so if negligence is proven.

Republicans and education reformers argue these failures demand accountability, transparent investigations, and real consequences for administrators who look the other way. The left typically responds with calls for more funding and training, but Colorado taxpayers deserve to know: where did existing resources go, and why weren't they deployed to protect kids?

**Watch for settlement amounts and whether school boards implement meaningful disciplinary action against negligent officials.**
