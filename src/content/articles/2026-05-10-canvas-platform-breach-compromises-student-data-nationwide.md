# Canvas Learning Platform Suffers Major Data Breach Affecting Millions of Students

A significant cyberattack has compromised student data across universities and K-12 districts nationwide, according to NPR National. Canvas, a widely used classroom management tool, was taken offline by the breach this week, disrupting educational institutions relying on the platform for coursework, grades, and student records.

The exact scope of compromised information and the identity of those responsible remain unclear from available reports. Canvas serves as a critical infrastructure tool for educational institutions, making the breach a potential concern for student privacy and institutional operations.

The incident highlights vulnerabilities in edtech platforms that handle sensitive student data. Schools and universities using Canvas are likely to face questions about data security protocols and notification procedures for affected students and families.

Watch for Canvas's timeline for restoring full service and any official statements detailing what specific data categories were accessed during the breach.
