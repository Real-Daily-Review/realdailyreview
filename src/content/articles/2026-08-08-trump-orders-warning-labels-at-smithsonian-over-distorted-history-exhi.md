# Trump Orders Warning Labels at Smithsonian History Museum Over 'Distorted' Exhibits

President Trump is taking direct aim at what conservatives say is left-wing revisionism in America's premier history museum.

Trump signed an executive order directing temporary warning signs outside the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History, cautioning visitors that some exhibits may present a distorted account of the nation's past (Washington Examiner). The order follows a White House report released on July 4 questioning whether the museum has drifted from its original mission.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that major cultural institutions have been captured by progressive ideology that downplays American achievements and overemphasizes grievances. Trump's move signals that conservatives intend to challenge what they view as institutional bias in how America's story gets told to the next generation.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** This reflects a broader national battle over education and cultural narratives that directly affects Colorado schools, universities, and how state institutions present history and civics. Parents and taxpayers increasingly demand transparency about ideological slant in publicly funded institutions.

The real test comes next: whether Congress will fund or restrict the Smithsonian's operations based on how it responds to these warnings, and whether other states follow Colorado's lead in auditing their own cultural and educational institutions for similar bias.
