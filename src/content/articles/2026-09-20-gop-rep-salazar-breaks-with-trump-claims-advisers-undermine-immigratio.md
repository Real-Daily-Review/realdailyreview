# GOP Rep. Salazar Breaks with Trump on Immigration, Claims Advisers Undermine President

A Florida Republican is publicly challenging President Trump's immigration stance—and claiming some of his advisers are steering him wrong. Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-FL), who represents a heavily Hispanic Miami district, told CBS's "Face the Nation" that certain Trump advisers "are compromising his legacy" on immigration policy (Breitbart). 

Salazar, who released an advertisement last week attacking Trump's anti-immigration positions, said the President was "very receptive" and "very courteous" when they spoke afterward (Washington Examiner). She framed the conversation as constructive, suggesting Trump himself may be open to moderating his hardline stance if the right voices reach him.

The split highlights tension within the GOP over immigration strategy heading into 2026. While Trump has made restrictive immigration central to his platform, some Republicans—particularly those representing Hispanic-majority districts—worry the approach costs the party votes and alienates potential supporters.

**The conservative case:** Trump's core supporters argue strict immigration enforcement is essential to border security and protecting American workers. Advisers pushing moderation risk diluting the mandate voters gave Trump. Salazar's ad, by contrast, suggests a more business-friendly, immigration-accommodating approach that some conservatives view as capitulation.

**Watch next:** Whether Trump's team responds publicly and whether other GOP members in purple districts follow Salazar's lead in breaking ranks on immigration.
