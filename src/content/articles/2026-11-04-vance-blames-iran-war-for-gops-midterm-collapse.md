# GOP Reckons With Iran War Backlash After Midterm Drubbing

Vice President JD Vance delivered a blunt post-mortem Wednesday: Republicans lost control of Congress because voters rejected the Iran war, not Trump's core agenda. "The reason we lost is the war," Vance said, noting that President Trump himself acknowledged the connection (The Daily Wire). GOP operatives close to the White House echoed the assessment, with one telling The Daily Wire that the conflict was "the chief cause" of the midterm collapse.

The candid analysis marks a sharp break from neoconservative war hawks who had pushed military escalation. Conservative outlets like the Daily Caller framed the result as a "reality check" for interventionists who promised quick victory without domestic political cost.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado voters, like Republicans nationwide, punished the party over foreign entanglement. The state's swing districts—critical to GOP hopes—turned blue partly on war-weariness, suggesting that Colorado's independent streak rejected open-ended military commitments abroad.

Republicans now face a reckoning: whether to chart a restraint-focused foreign policy aligned with Trump's 2024 messaging, or risk repeating 2026's losses. Conservatives should watch whether the party's 2028 platform embraces non-interventionism or reverts to establishment hawkishness.
