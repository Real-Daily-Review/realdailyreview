# Senate Democrats Block Protections While Abandoning Veterans

Senate Republicans are calling out Democratic obstruction on two fronts: Sen. Ted Cruz vowed to advance the Northeast Lobsterman Protection Act after Sen. Susan Collins accused a top Democrat of blocking the bill (Fox News), while a Purple Heart Vietnam veteran who attended President Trump's March 2025 Joint Address as Sen. Jon Ossoff's personal guest is now accusing the Georgia Democrat of abandoning veterans entirely in his re-election campaign (Breitbart).

The dual offensive illustrates a Republican messaging strategy heading into the 2026 cycle: Democrats talk a good game on supporting working Americans and military service members, but their legislative record tells a different story. Collins' complaint signals Democratic resistance to industry-specific protections, while the Georgia veteran's accusation—coming from someone Ossoff himself invited to a presidential address—carries particular sting.

For Colorado conservatives, the pattern matters. When Democrats block targeted protections for workers and industries, they're signaling willingness to sacrifice regional economies for broader ideological goals. Veterans' issues, too, resonate across the Mountain West, where military service runs deep.

Watch whether Republicans can translate these specific accusations into sustained campaign messaging, or whether Democrats successfully reframe the narrative before 2026.
