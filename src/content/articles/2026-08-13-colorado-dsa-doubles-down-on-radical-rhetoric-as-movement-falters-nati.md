# Colorado DSA Movement Faces Credibility Crisis as National Momentum Stalls

Colorado's Democratic Socialist Alliance is doubling down on radical rhetoric even as the movement crashes into electoral reality nationwide.

According to *Complete Colorado*, Colorado DSA activists are embracing increasingly extreme language that echoes Bolshevik and Maoist ideology—a rhetorical turn that underscores the movement's drift toward vengeful class warfare rather than pragmatic governance. Meanwhile, *National Review* reports that in Wisconsin, Democratic Socialist candidate Francesca Hong suffered a crushing primary defeat to moderate David Crowley, signaling voter rejection of the DSA's radical agenda outside deep-blue strongholds.

The contrast matters for Colorado. While the state's progressive wing has gained ground in Denver and Boulder, DSA-backed candidates pushing wealth confiscation, energy rationing, and gun control face an uphill climb in purple and red regions—from the Western Slope to El Paso County. Colorado voters in swing districts have shown they'll punish candidates who sound more interested in revolutionary rhetoric than kitchen-table issues: jobs, housing costs, and school safety.

Republicans argue the DSA's explicit embrace of socialist ideology and its contempt for property rights and free enterprise disqualify its candidates from mainstream Colorado politics. The movement's failure in Wisconsin suggests that message is resonating.
