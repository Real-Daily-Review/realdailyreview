# New Lyons Center Honors Christian Glass, Offers Youth Space

Sally and Simon Glass have converted an old diner in Lyons into a community art center dedicated to their son, Christian Glass, who was killed by police in 2022. The center provides a gathering space for teens and young adults to create art, connect, and participate in community activities while memorializing their son.

The Glass family's initiative reflects a growing trend of private citizens stepping in to address local needs—in this case, youth engagement and mental health support—without waiting for government programs or taxpayer-funded initiatives. The center operates as a community-driven project, allowing Lyons residents to invest directly in their young people's wellbeing through private philanthropy rather than bureaucratic channels.

According to coverage from both the Colorado Sun and KDVR Fox31 Denver, the space has already begun hosting youth activities and serves as a meaningful tribute to Christian Glass while filling a genuine gap in Lyons' recreational and creative offerings for young people.

The center demonstrates how private enterprise and family-led community action can address local priorities more efficiently than government expansion—a model conservatives argue should be replicated across Colorado communities facing similar youth engagement challenges.

Watch whether the center attracts additional private donors and whether it becomes a model other Colorado towns adopt for youth programming without expanding the public budget.
