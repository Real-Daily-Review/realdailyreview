# Trump Administration Escalates Iran Sanctions as Democrats Pursue Impeachment Theater

The Trump administration is tightening the economic noose around Iran with "Operation Economic Outcast," a Treasury Department campaign to isolate Tehran from global markets and punish foreign banks and companies doing business there, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced Monday (The Daily Wire). The move follows Trump's earlier military campaign against Iran and signals a hardline approach to one of America's most destabilizing adversaries.

Meanwhile, Democrats are doubling down on impeachment theater. Rep. Al Green introduced articles of impeachment against Trump citing deaths allegedly caused by ICE agents, according to Just The News. Republicans argue these prosecutions represent selective lawfare—the kind of allegations federal prosecutors would not normally pursue under standard legal standards, National Review notes.

**The Colorado angle:** Trump's Iran sanctions could affect energy markets and Colorado's oil and gas sector, which benefits from reduced global competition and higher commodity prices. Conversely, Democrats' impeachment push wastes congressional time and resources that could address border security and immigration enforcement—issues directly impacting Colorado communities.

Conservatives should watch whether Senate Republicans hold firm against impeachment pressure while monitoring how Iran sanctions affect domestic energy independence and Colorado's energy workforce.
