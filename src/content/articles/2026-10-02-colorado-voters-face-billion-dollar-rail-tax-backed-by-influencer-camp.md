# Front Range Rail Tax: Colorado Voters Face Billion-Dollar Boondoggle Dressed Up as Progress

Colorado taxpayers are being asked to bankroll a massive rail project that even its own boosters admit few will actually ride. The Front Range rail initiative—branded as the "Colorado Connector"—is heading to the ballot backed by a slick, taxpayer-funded propaganda campaign that includes paid Instagram influencers and targeted mailers, according to CPR News.

Complete Colorado identifies the core problem: voters face a choice between "blowing billions on a train few will ride and keeping that money in their own pockets." The project exemplifies how progressive infrastructure dreams routinely ignore basic economics and ridership realities.

The campaign's reliance on paid social-media influencers and glossy mailers to sell the project reveals the uncomfortable truth—the Colorado Connector can't win on merit alone. When government has to hire Instagram personalities to convince Coloradans to tax themselves, something is deeply wrong with the pitch.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue the state should reject this wealth transfer disguised as transit innovation. The money would be better spent on roads, water infrastructure, or returned to families and businesses struggling with Colorado's rising cost of living.

Watch whether voters see through the influencer campaign and reject this tax grab at the ballot box.
