# Trump's AG Nominee Blanche Rejects Democratic Epstein Narrative at Confirmation Hearing

Todd Blanche, Trump's attorney general nominee, flatly rejected Democratic claims Wednesday that his administration has stonewalled on Jeffrey Epstein files, calling the narrative "false" during his Senate confirmation hearing.

Sen. Dick Durbin (D-IL) and other Democrats demanded Blanche personally meet with 10 Epstein victims within 30 days and answer questions about transparency on the convicted sex offender's crimes. Blanche countered that while he could meet with survivors, he couldn't directly approach those represented by lawyers—a standard legal protocol Democrats glossed over.

According to the Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire, Blanche dismantled what he characterized as a coordinated Democratic attack, asserting the Trump administration has already made Epstein-related documents available. Republicans argue Democrats are manufacturing a transparency crisis where none exists, using victim advocacy as political cover to attack a Trump nominee.

The confirmation hearing underscores the partisan divide over judicial and executive appointments. While the Epstein case itself has no direct Colorado angle, Blanche's confirmation will shape federal law enforcement priorities affecting Colorado's federal courts and prosecutorial resources.
