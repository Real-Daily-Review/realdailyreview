# Co-Pilot's Stabbing Attack on Israel-Bound Flight Leaves Four Americans in Danger

A FlydubaiI flight carrying 174 passengers—including four Americans—narrowly avoided catastrophe when a co-pilot allegedly stabbed the captain and attempted to crash the aircraft en route to Tel Aviv. The plane plunged nearly 14,000 feet before crew members regained control, averting what could have been a mass-casualty terror attack.

President Trump told reporters at the White House that he and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu discussed the incident "at length" by phone. The State Department confirmed the presence of American passengers and said it is coordinating with local authorities to provide consular assistance.

The attacker's identity and motive remain unknown, though the incident echoes post-9/11 aviation security concerns. The rapid crew response prevented a potential disaster that could have killed hundreds.

For Colorado families with loved ones traveling internationally, this incident underscores the ongoing threat of aviation terrorism and the critical importance of robust security protocols—and the need for government to prioritize passenger safety over political correctness in threat assessment.
