# Massachusetts Mayor Arrested for $1.5M COVID Loan Fraud—A Reminder of Government Handout Abuse

Federal authorities arrested Lawrence, Massachusetts Mayor Brian DePena on Friday for allegedly stealing $1.5 million in COVID-19 small-business relief funds and funneling them to his political campaign and personal debts, according to the Washington Examiner and Daily Caller. DePena, 61, faces wire fraud and money laundering charges after the FBI investigation revealed he diverted pandemic relief money to pay campaign expenses, personal taxes, and roughly $880,000 in property mortgage payments.

This case exemplifies what conservatives have long warned about: massive government spending programs create fertile ground for fraud and corruption. When Washington floods the economy with emergency relief with minimal oversight, bad actors exploit the chaos. The COVID loan programs, while well-intentioned, lacked the guardrails necessary to prevent abuse—a predictable outcome of rapid government expansion.

Colorado taxpayers should note that similar relief programs flowed through state and local governments here. While there's no indication of comparable schemes in Colorado, this Massachusetts case underscores why conservatives demand stricter accountability and sunset provisions on emergency spending.

Watch for how federal prosecutors handle this case and whether it prompts serious congressional review of pandemic-era program safeguards—or whether Democrats simply move on to the next spending initiative.
