# Fauci Pleads Fifth as Rand Paul Demands Answers on COVID Origins, Gain-of-Function Funding

Dr. Anthony Fauci repeatedly invoked his Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination during a contentious Senate hearing with Sen. Rand Paul, refusing to answer questions about his diaries and gain-of-function research funding in China ahead of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Paul, chairing the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee, pressed Fauci on documents that exposed his private sentiments during the crisis. When Fauci's lawyer repeatedly interrupted the questioning, Paul had him removed from the hearing room, telling him: "Another word and you're gone" (Daily Caller).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Fauci's silence and legal maneuvering constitute an admission of wrongdoing. Paul has demanded a public apology over the gain-of-function research funding and called for accountability on decisions that shaped pandemic policy affecting millions of Americans—including Coloradans who lost jobs, businesses, and freedoms under lockdowns and mandates.

Democrats have largely defended Fauci's record, framing the hearing as partisan theater rather than legitimate oversight.

Coloradans who endured some of the nation's strictest COVID restrictions should watch whether Congress pursues criminal referrals or whether this hearing marks the end of accountability efforts.
