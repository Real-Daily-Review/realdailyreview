# Deadly Weld County Crash Kills Three; Alcohol Suspected

A rollover crash in northern Colorado Saturday claimed three lives and underscores the ongoing danger of impaired driving on state roads.

Colorado State Patrol responded to the incident in Weld County, where three passengers were ejected from a pickup truck. Two others were injured in the collision. Investigators are examining whether alcohol impairment played a role in the crash, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver.

The fatality count reflects a persistent public safety challenge across Colorado highways. While the investigation remains ongoing, troopers have already identified alcohol as a potential factor—a reminder that impaired driving enforcement remains critical to protecting Coloradans on the road.

State Patrol has not yet released the names of the victims or detailed circumstances surrounding the crash. The agency typically completes its full investigation within weeks.

Conservatives and safety advocates alike emphasize that tough enforcement of DUI laws and personal responsibility behind the wheel are essential to reducing preventable deaths on Colorado roads.
