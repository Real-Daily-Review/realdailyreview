# NSA Failure: Spied on Americans While China Breached Our Networks

The National Security Agency has become a domestic surveillance machine that missed its actual job—stopping foreign adversaries—and the Trump administration is demanding accountability.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, recent disclosures reveal a damning pattern: Chinese state-linked campaigns penetrated American and Canadian networks undetected for years, with at least one operation remaining active since September 2023. Meanwhile, the NSA was busy monitoring Americans' communications, violating constitutional limits on government power.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** The NSA has inverted its mission. Rather than defending the nation against genuine threats like Chinese cyber operations, the agency expanded its domestic surveillance apparatus, collecting data on law-abiding citizens without warrant or justification. This is precisely the kind of government overreach the Fourth Amendment was designed to prevent.

Republicans and constitutional conservatives argue the NSA's failures demand structural reform—or abolition. If a $15 billion-plus agency cannot detect years-long Chinese intrusions but excels at warrantless domestic spying, it has forfeited its legitimacy and its budget.

For Colorado taxpayers and businesses reliant on cybersecurity, the implications are stark: federal agencies tasked with protecting critical infrastructure have proven incompetent at their core mission while eroding civil liberties at home.

Watch whether Congress demands a full accounting of NSA domestic surveillance programs and whether the Trump administration moves to defund or restructure the agency.
