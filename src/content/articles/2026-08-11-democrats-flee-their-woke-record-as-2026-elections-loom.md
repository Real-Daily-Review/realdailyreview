# Democrats Can't Outrun Their Woke Record—and Colorado Voters Should Remember It

Democrats nationwide are scrambling to distance themselves from the radical rhetoric of 2019-2021, but the receipts are everywhere. Wisconsin gubernatorial candidate Francesca Hong has begun walking back her most extreme positions, joining a pattern of Democratic candidates trying to memory-hole their own words now that "woke" politics has become a liability at the ballot box (Washington Examiner).

The problem for the left: every syllable is trapped on camera. As National Review notes, if progressive leaders like Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez have truly recognized errors in their approach, they owe Americans more than a casual "oops." The Daily Wire warns against letting Democrats simply erase "Woke 1.0" from the record—their radical positions on gender ideology, defunding police, and economic overreach were real, and voters deserve accountability.

For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats have pushed similar agendas: aggressive gun control, energy restrictions that threaten jobs, and regulatory expansion that burdens small business. When Democratic candidates here claim they've moderated, conservatives should ask: which specific positions have you abandoned, and why?

Republicans argue voters should hold Democrats accountable for their actual record, not their revised talking points.

**Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates explicitly renounce the progressive policies they championed just years ago—or quietly hope voters forget.**
