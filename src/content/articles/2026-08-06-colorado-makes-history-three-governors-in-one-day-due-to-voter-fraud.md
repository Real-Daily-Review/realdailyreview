# Colorado's Voter Fraud Scandal: Three Governors in One Day

Colorado's election integrity just hit rock bottom—the state is now the only one in U.S. history where three different people served as governor on the same day, all because of voter fraud that went undetected for too long.

According to CPR News, the unprecedented constitutional crisis unfolded when fraudulent voter registrations triggered a cascade of gubernatorial succession issues, exposing massive gaps in Colorado's election security apparatus. The Daily Caller reports that questions surrounding voter registration irregularities—including those tied to political operative James Talarico—have raised serious red flags about how lax Colorado's vetting procedures have become.

Republicans and election integrity advocates argue this debacle vindicates their long-standing warnings about the dangers of loose voter registration standards and inadequate fraud detection. Democrats have remained largely silent on specifics, though they typically resist stricter voter ID and registration verification measures.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, this fiasco underscores a critical question: if the state can't secure something as fundamental as gubernatorial succession, how can voters trust Colorado's election system on any level? The incident also raises concerns about whether Colorado's mail-in voting infrastructure—expanded significantly in recent years—has adequate safeguards.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans push for mandatory voter ID, real-time registration verification, and tighter signature-matching standards in response.
