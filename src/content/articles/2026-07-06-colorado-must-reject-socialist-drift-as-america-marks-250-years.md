# Colorado Must Reject Socialist Drift as America Marks 250 Years of Freedom

Colorado conservatives face a stark choice: embrace the free-market principles that built America, or slide toward the socialist experiments gaining traction among young progressives nationwide.

As the nation marks 250 years of independence, National Review warns that today's Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) push the same failed collectivist ideology that has impoverished nations worldwide—yet with even less understanding of economics than their predecessors. The left's push for expanded government control over energy, healthcare, and the economy directly threatens Colorado's oil and gas workers, small businesses, and property owners already burdened by state-level progressive taxation.

Meanwhile, conservatives point to America's actual success story: free enterprise and limited government. The Daily Caller argues nuclear energy—not government-mandated renewables—represents the future of American power independence. That matters for Colorado, where energy policy increasingly favors expensive wind and solar mandates over reliable, affordable baseload power that supports jobs and affordable electricity.

Those who've escaped socialist regimes, as National Review notes, understand what Americans risk losing: economic freedom, property rights, and opportunity.

Colorado taxpayers should watch closely as Democrats push further climate regulations and energy mandates that will spike utility bills and kill energy-sector jobs.
