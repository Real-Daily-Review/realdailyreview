# Trump Pushes Senate GOP to End Daylight Saving Time Before Recess

President Trump is pressing Senate Republicans to pass the Sunshine Protection Act before lawmakers leave Washington, according to the Washington Examiner and Just The News. The legislation would make daylight saving time permanent nationwide, eliminating the twice-yearly clock changes that have frustrated Americans for decades.

Trump called out Sen. Tom Cotton (R-Ark.) on social media, claiming the senator is blocking passage "for reasons unknown" (Just The News). The push reflects growing conservative support for ending the disruptive practice—a rare area of bipartisan agreement, though Republicans and Democrats differ on the permanent standard.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado businesses, ranchers, and families would feel the impact of a permanent time shift. Mountain Time operations coordinating with both coasts and Mexico would face new scheduling complexities. Energy consumption patterns could shift, affecting Colorado's power grid and utility costs.

Conservatives generally support the Sunshine Protection Act as a common-sense fix eliminating government-mandated inconvenience. The real debate centers on *which* permanent time to adopt—permanent daylight saving or permanent standard time—with agricultural and outdoor recreation interests divided.

Watch whether Senate Republicans unite behind Trump's push or whether procedural objections and regional concerns keep the bill stalled through recess.
