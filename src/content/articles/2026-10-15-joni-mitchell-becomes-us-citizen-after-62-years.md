# Music Legend Joni Mitchell Becomes U.S. Citizen After Six Decades

After 62 years living in the United States, Canadian-born folk icon Joni Mitchell has officially become an American citizen, marking the end of a remarkable personal journey that began in the 1960s.

Mitchell, who shaped American popular music for generations while maintaining Canadian citizenship, completed her naturalization process and is now a U.S. citizen. The move comes after decades of cultural contributions to the American music landscape, from her groundbreaking songwriting to her influence on rock, folk, and pop genres.

"A journey that began 62 years ago," as the *Daily Caller* noted, underscores Mitchell's deep roots in American society despite her Canadian origins. The timing of her citizenship decision—after more than half a century—reflects her enduring connection to the country where she built her legendary career.
