# Federal Student Aid Fraud Drains Millions While Colleges Look Away

Millions in federal student aid dollars are vanishing into a sophisticated scam targeting college campuses nationwide, with fraudsters exploiting lax institutional oversight and federal verification gaps to pocket taxpayer money through fake enrollments.

According to Fox News, fraud experts are sounding alarms about "ghost students"—fabricated enrollees used to siphon federal aid funds that never reach legitimate educational purposes. The scheme has accumulated a multimillion-dollar price tag, yet many colleges have done little to tighten controls or report suspicious activity.

The scam exposes a fundamental problem conservatives have long highlighted: federal student aid programs lack adequate safeguards and accountability. When Washington dumps billions into higher education without rigorous verification, bad actors exploit the system—and Colorado taxpayers foot the bill through federal taxes funding these programs.

The Washington Examiner notes that colleges must also respect due process and factual accuracy in their own investigations, underscoring how institutional failures compound when universities bypass proper procedures alongside inadequate fraud prevention.

**What to watch:** Pressure Congress to demand real accountability from colleges receiving federal aid, including mandatory fraud audits and clawback provisions for institutions that fail to prevent ghost-student schemes.
