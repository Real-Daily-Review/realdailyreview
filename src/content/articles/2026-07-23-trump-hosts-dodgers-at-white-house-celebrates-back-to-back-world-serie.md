# Trump Hosts Dodgers at White House, Celebrates Back-to-Back World Series

President Donald Trump welcomed the Los Angeles Dodgers to the White House on Thursday, celebrating the team's consecutive World Series championships with characteristic enthusiasm. Trump praised the organization and players for their "historic" achievement, calling them "champions" and "unbelievable" (Washington Examiner).

The president singled out superstar Shohei Ohtani for particular acclaim, comparing him favorably to Babe Ruth and describing him as "the best pitcher and the best hitter at the same time" (New York Post). Trump also revealed he had made a World Series "bet" with Canada's prime minister and gave the team a tour of the Rose Garden.

The White House visit underscores Trump's continued engagement with American sports and popular culture—a contrast to the previous administration's more distant relationship with major league baseball. The Dodgers' back-to-back titles represent one of baseball's rarest achievements, and the team's visit marks a traditional celebration of athletic excellence at the executive level.
