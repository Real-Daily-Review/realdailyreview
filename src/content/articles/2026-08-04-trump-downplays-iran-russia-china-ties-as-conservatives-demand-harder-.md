# Trump Faces Pressure to Take Harder Line on Iran as Russia, China Quietly Aid Tehran

Trump is downplaying intelligence reports that Russia and China are covertly supporting Iran, even as conservatives and former officials push for more aggressive U.S. action against the regime.

According to Fox News, Trump says he'll ask Putin directly about satellite imagery allegations showing Russian and Chinese assistance to Iran. But the president's measured approach contrasts sharply with hawkish voices in his own party. Former Vice President Mike Pence told Fox News it's time for Trump to "unleash the Armed Forces" to "finish the job" in Iran (Breitbart).

The Washington Examiner's editorial board argues that holding Iran accountable is both morally justified and strategically necessary. Iran has attacked Israel through proxies—Hamas, Hezbollah, and the Houthis—and launched direct strikes on the region. Conservatives contend that deterrence requires credible military readiness, not diplomatic restraint when adversaries are actively arming America's enemies.

The core conservative argument: allowing Russia and China to prop up Iran unchecked invites further escalation and emboldens hostile regimes. Trump's willingness to negotiate directly with Putin, while pragmatic in theory, risks appearing weak if Moscow continues aiding Tehran without consequence.
