# Secretary of State Rubio Draws Fire From Left Over Venezuela Policy Role

Marco Rubio's aggressive stance on Venezuela is drawing fierce criticism from progressive activists who view his influence over the country's oil revenue and sanctions regime as a threat to their anti-interventionist agenda, *National Review* reports.

According to *The Daily Wire*, Rubio has assumed an outsized role in shaping U.S. Venezuela policy from the State Department, including overseeing sanctions and managing how the country's oil revenue flows. The left argues this represents dangerous interventionism; conservatives counter that Rubio is simply protecting American interests and supporting democratic opposition to the Maduro regime.

**The Colorado angle:** Energy markets matter here. Venezuela's oil production directly affects global crude prices and domestic energy policy. Colorado's oil and gas sector—already under pressure from Biden-era regulations—watches Venezuela developments closely. If Rubio's sanctions strategy destabilizes Venezuelan oil supplies, it could reshape energy markets that Colorado producers depend on.

Republicans defend Rubio's approach as principled opposition to authoritarian socialism. Democrats and progressive groups claim he's overstepping diplomatic norms and pursuing regime-change policy by other means.

Conservatives and Colorado taxpayers should monitor whether Rubio's Venezuela strategy influences domestic energy policy or triggers retaliatory moves that affect U.S. oil markets and Colorado's energy workforce.
