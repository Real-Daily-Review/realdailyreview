# Trump Keeps Bessent at Treasury, Sidelines AI Czar Role

President Trump has ruled out Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent for a newly created "Super Intelligence Czar" position, according to reporting from the Washington Examiner and Just The News. Trump confirmed Friday that Bessent will remain focused on his Treasury duties rather than lead the administration's artificial intelligence initiative.

The decision leaves the AI czar role—part of Trump's broader push to establish an "AI Force"—unfilled for now. The move signals Trump's intent to keep his economic team intact as the administration navigates tariff policy, federal spending, and tax strategy heading into 2025.

Meanwhile, Trump confirmed two additional meetings with Chinese President Xi Jinping before year's end, following Xi's recent state visit to Washington. The repeated high-level engagement underscores ongoing U.S.-China negotiations on trade, technology, and geopolitical competition.

**Colorado angle:** Treasury decisions on tariffs, energy policy, and federal land management directly impact Colorado's economy, ranching interests, and energy sector. Bessent's continued focus on Treasury means sustained attention to how federal fiscal policy affects state taxpayers and business climate.

**What to watch:** Whether Trump's AI Force initiative gains traction without a dedicated czar, and how Treasury policy under Bessent shapes Colorado's competitive position on energy and business regulation.
