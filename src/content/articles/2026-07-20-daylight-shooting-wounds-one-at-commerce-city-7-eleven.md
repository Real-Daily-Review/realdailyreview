# Daylight Shooting at Commerce City 7-Eleven Leaves One Critical

A broad daylight shooting at a Commerce City gas station Sunday underscores Colorado's growing public safety crisis in urban areas. One person was critically injured in the incident outside a 7-Eleven, marking another violent crime in the Denver metro region (KDVR Fox31).

The shooting occurred in broad daylight—a stark reminder that Colorado communities are increasingly unsafe, even during daytime hours when families and workers expect relative security. Commerce City, part of the greater Denver area, has seen rising violent crime rates that local law enforcement and residents blame on soft-on-crime policies, inadequate prosecution, and bail reform measures that keep dangerous offenders on the street.

Meanwhile, a separate shooting in Asheville, North Carolina left two dead and seven injured. Investigators believe that shooting was not random and are working to identify those responsible (9News).

These incidents highlight a national pattern: violent crime persists despite progressive promises that gun control and social programs alone would reduce violence. Conservatives argue that Colorado needs tougher prosecution, swift incarceration of violent offenders, and support for law enforcement—not more restrictions on law-abiding gun owners.
