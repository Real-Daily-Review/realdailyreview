# SpaceX Launches Enhanced Starship on Test Flight

SpaceX successfully launched its most powerful Starship rocket yet after postponing the initial attempt, according to multiple outlets. The mega rocket completed its 12th test flight (PBS NewsHour), marking a significant step in development of the vehicle designed to carry astronauts to the moon and eventually Mars.

The launch came two days after SpaceX CEO Elon Musk announced plans to take the company public (NPR), adding momentum to the milestone. NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman said the flight brings Starship "one step closer to the moon" (NPR).

The enhanced version of the Super Heavy-Starship rocket is central to NASA's Artemis program for returning humans to the lunar surface (CBS News). Perfecting the massive vehicle will be critical to those plans, according to the network.

The launch followed a scrub the previous day, when SpaceX halted takeoff before liftoff (CNBC). The company proceeded with the second attempt on Friday.
