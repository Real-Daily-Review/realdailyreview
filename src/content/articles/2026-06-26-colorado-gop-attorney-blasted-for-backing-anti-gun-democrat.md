# GOP Attorney's Gun-Control Stance Sparks Backlash From Party Faithful

A Republican attorney general is facing fire from fellow conservatives for aligning with Colorado's anti-gun Democratic AG Jay Jones on Second Amendment restrictions, according to the Daily Caller. The GOP official defended his position in heated remarks to party members, saying he's caught "in the middle" of competing pressures—a claim that has drawn sharp criticism from gun-rights advocates within Republican ranks.

The dispute underscores a growing fault line within Colorado GOP circles over how aggressively to oppose progressive gun-control measures. Conservatives argue that Republicans should present a unified front against Democratic efforts to restrict firearm rights, particularly in a state where energy workers, ranchers, and rural voters depend on Second Amendment protections for both livelihood and personal safety.

Meanwhile, former Attorney General Bill Barr is pushing Senate Republicans to confirm Todd Blanche as the next U.S. Attorney General, per the Washington Examiner, arguing Blanche is best positioned to lead the Justice Department. Barr's endorsement carries weight among Senate GOP members weighing confirmation votes.
