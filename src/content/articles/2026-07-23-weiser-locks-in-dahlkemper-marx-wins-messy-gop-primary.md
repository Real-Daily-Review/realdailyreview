# Colorado Democrats Lock in Weiser-Dahlkemper Ticket as GOP Settles Contentious Primary

Colorado Democrats have formalized their 2026 gubernatorial slate, with Attorney General Phil Weiser naming Jefferson County Commissioner Lesley Dahlkemper as his running mate (Colorado Sun, CPR News). Dahlkemper, an early Weiser supporter, will serve as lieutenant governor if the ticket prevails in November.

Meanwhile, Republicans are moving forward after a bruising primary. Victor Marx, a first-time political candidate and nonprofit leader, narrowly won the GOP nomination over veteran state Sen. Barb Kirkmeyer (Denver Post). Marx's victory—by roughly 2,000 votes—came after an unconventional campaign and amid questions about his controversial personal history. The race remained too close to call for days as election officials processed deficient ballots.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans face a general election challenge with a first-time nominee carrying baggage, while Democrats present a unified front. Weiser's record as attorney general—including aggressive regulatory actions and progressive litigation priorities—will be central to the fall campaign. Conservatives should expect Weiser to campaign on his office's environmental and labor enforcement record, which business groups have criticized as overreach.

**What to watch:** Whether Marx can consolidate GOP support and articulate a clear conservative platform on taxes, energy development, and government overreach before the general election heats up.
