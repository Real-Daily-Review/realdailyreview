# Paul Pelosi Faces Hit-and-Run Charge in California

Nancy Pelosi's husband, 86-year-old Paul Pelosi, has been charged with hit-and-run after allegedly striking a parked vehicle in Napa County and leaving the scene, according to the Napa County Sheriff's Office (Fox News).

The incident occurred Friday around 2:30 p.m. in Yountville. Authorities ruled out DUI involvement, but the sheriff's office has submitted a driver re-evaluation referral to the California DMV (Fox News). Pelosi has reportedly apologized to the vehicle's owner (Daily Caller).

This marks another traffic incident for the former House Speaker's husband. The case comes years after a 2022 DUI arrest in the same county that drew national attention.
