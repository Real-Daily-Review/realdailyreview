# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Left's Medical Overreach Agenda

A Michigan Democratic Senate nominee is arguing that doctors—not elected officials—should have unchecked authority to perform gender-transition procedures on minors, a position that reveals the Left's strategy to shield radical medical interventions from democratic accountability.

Abdul El-Sayed, touting his medical credentials at a Michigan State University rally, told students that physicians should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments without government interference (Fox News, The Daily Wire). His argument sidesteps the core conservative concern: whether experimental procedures on minors should proceed without parental consent laws, legislative guardrails, or independent medical review.

**Republicans counter** that parents—not activist doctors or Democratic politicians—should retain authority over their children's medical decisions. Conservatives argue that gender-transition drugs and surgeries for minors remain scientifically contested, with long-term effects unknown, making legislative caution reasonable rather than "interference."

**Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have pushed similar "gender-affirming care" policies in schools and Medicaid. If El-Sayed's framing gains traction nationally, expect Colorado progressives to cite it when defending expanded access to puberty blockers and cross-sex hormones for minors without parental notification.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature attempts to codify El-Sayed-style protections for gender procedures in state law—effectively locking parents out of their own children's medical decisions.
