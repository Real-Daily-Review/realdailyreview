# Colorado Conservatives Brace for 'Woke 2.0' as Left Regroups Ahead of 2026

Progressive ideology isn't retreating—it's repackaging itself for a comeback, and Colorado voters should expect a fresh wave of cultural and political overreach if Democrats gain ground in the 2026 midterms.

According to National Review and the Daily Caller, what conservatives call "Woke 1.0"—the explicit racial grievance politics and corporate DEI mandates of recent years—is morphing into a subtler but potentially more dangerous iteration. Rather than disappearing, the movement is adopting new tactics and rhetoric, obscuring its core agenda behind softer language and strategic repositioning.

The Washington Examiner warns that Democratic operatives are actively trying to convince voters that the woke era is "dead and buried," even as party leaders prepare to resurrect progressive priorities. Conservatives argue this rebranding is a calculated political move: admit the excesses of the last cycle, then quietly advance the same agenda under different names.

For Colorado specifically, this matters. The state's Democratic legislature and governor have already pushed progressive education curricula, environmental regulations that threaten energy jobs, and gun-control measures. A resurgent national left emboldened by 2026 gains could accelerate similar overreach at the state level.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans effectively expose and counter this rhetorical shift before the midterms—or whether voters get fooled by the repackaging.
