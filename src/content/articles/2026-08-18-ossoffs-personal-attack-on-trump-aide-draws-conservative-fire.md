# Georgia Democrat's Personal Attack on Trump Aide Draws Fire From Right

Georgia Senator Jon Ossoff's weekend rally attack on President Trump's executive assistant Natalie Harp—a cancer survivor—has drawn sharp criticism from conservative outlets for what they call a calculated, personal smear.

Speaking in Atlanta, Ossoff didn't directly accuse Trump of an affair but made a pointed reference to Trump wanting to "build his ballroom and travel with Natalie," letting the audience draw its own conclusion (Washington Examiner). The Daily Wire reports Ossoff made the remark while accusing Trump of lying the nation into war with Iran, dragging Harp into an unrelated political attack.

**Conservative take:** Right-leaning commentators argue Ossoff's tactic exemplifies Democratic double standards on feminism and decency. The Washington Examiner notes the senator's approach was "calculated"—insinuating impropriety without stating it directly, a rhetorical move conservatives say allows Democrats to attack women while claiming moral high ground. The Daily Wire quotes critics calling Ossoff a "scumbag weasel" for targeting a cancer survivor.

Democrats have not publicly defended Ossoff's specific language, though the broader argument from the left would likely frame any Trump criticism as fair political speech.

**What to watch:** Whether national Democratic leaders distance themselves from Ossoff's personal attack or remain silent—a test of whether party standards apply equally to both sides.
