# Democratic Socialist Cori Bush's Comeback Bid Collapses in Missouri Primary

Missouri voters rejected the hard-left socialist wing of the Democratic Party on Tuesday, as incumbent Rep. Wesley Bell decisively defeated former Rep. Cori Bush in the 1st Congressional District primary—a sharp rebuke to the Democratic Socialists of America's influence within the party.

Bush, a former member of the progressive "Squad," had hoped to reclaim her seat after losing to Bell in 2022. Despite backing from DSA-aligned groups and momentum from other socialist candidates performing better than expected in recent cycles, Bush's comeback attempt fell flat. Bell's victory marks the second consecutive primary win against the socialist challenger, signaling that even in a heavily Democratic district, voters are tiring of the far-left agenda.

The result matters beyond Missouri. It suggests that Democratic primary voters—at least in competitive districts—are moving away from the radical socialism that defined Bush's tenure. Her positions on defunding police, open borders, and massive government spending proved toxic even among Democratic base voters.
