# Trump Shrinks Utah Monuments, Opening Path for Energy Development

President Trump has signed an executive order reducing Bears Ears and Grand Staircase-Escalante national monuments by roughly 90 percent, according to Fox News, cutting protected land from 3 million acres to 300,000. The move removes drilling restrictions and clears the way for coal and uranium extraction.

Republicans argue the downsizing returns land-use decisions to states and private owners, unlocking economic opportunity in rural Utah communities and reducing federal overreach. Conservatives say monument designations under the Antiquities Act have been abused to lock up resource-rich terrain without congressional approval.

Democrats and environmental groups counter that the reductions threaten irreplaceable geological and cultural sites, including sacred Native American lands, and accelerate fossil fuel development as the nation should pivot toward clean energy.

**The Colorado angle:** Utah's energy sector decisions ripple across the Mountain West. If uranium and coal production expand in Utah, it could affect Colorado's own energy markets, mining operations, and the regional push-back against federal land restrictions that constrain Colorado ranchers and energy workers.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly Republicans—will push similar monument reviews on Colorado federal lands, and whether the Biden-era land-lock policies face legal challenges in the courts.
