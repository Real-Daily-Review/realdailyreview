# Denver in Running to Host 2028 Democratic Convention—But Conservatives Should Count the Costs

Denver has made the cut as one of three finalist cities to host the 2028 Democratic National Convention, joining Boston and Philadelphia (Colorado Sun, CPR News, KDVR). Party officials visited the three contenders—along with Atlanta and Chicago—to evaluate logistics, fundraising capacity, and other hosting factors, with a final decision expected later this year (Just The News).

**The conservative angle:** While Denver boosters tout the economic benefits and national profile, taxpayers should scrutinize what hosting actually costs. Democratic conventions draw massive security requirements, infrastructure demands, and disruption to city services. The 2024 convention in Chicago required substantial public resources and police deployment. Denver would face similar burdens—road closures, overtime costs, and regulatory compliance—all underwritten by Colorado taxpayers and the city budget.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue that the glamour of "hosting" a major party convention masks the real bill: security, permits, traffic management, and cleanup typically fall on local government and residents, not the Democratic Party. The promised private fundraising and hotel revenue rarely offset public expenditures.

**Watch next:** Conservatives should demand full transparency on Denver's bid costs and any public subsidies or tax breaks offered to secure the convention.
