# Independent Doctor Challenges Far-Left Democrat in Colorado's First Congressional District

A Denver physician just qualified for the November ballot to challenge Democrat Melat Kiros in Colorado's First Congressional District—and his entry signals serious fractures in the Democratic primary coalition.

Shimon Blau, a Jewish doctor, said he was "saddened and alarmed" by Kiros' primary victory last month (Denver Post). Complete Colorado reports that Blau's candidacy offers voters concerned about Kiros' "antisemitism" allegations and "democratic socialism" policy positions a concrete alternative.

Kiros won the Democratic primary in CD1, a heavily Democratic Denver-based seat, but her victory apparently triggered enough buyer's remorse among moderate Democrats and independents to fuel Blau's independent run. His entry complicates the general election calculus: a three-way race could splinter the Democratic vote, though Republicans have historically struggled in this district.

**The conservative angle:** Blau's challenge exposes how far left Colorado Democrats have drifted—so far that even Democratic-leaning voters are bolting. Whether Blau can actually peel away enough votes to make the race competitive remains unclear, but his campaign will force Kiros to defend her record on antisemitism and socialist-leaning policies throughout the fall.
