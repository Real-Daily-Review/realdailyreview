# Trump, Cohen Reunite on Air—But Colorado Voters Should Focus on What Matters Here

Former President Trump sat down with Michael Cohen, his onetime fixer-turned-witness, for a radio interview this week—a stunning public reconciliation after years of legal warfare. According to the Washington Examiner, Trump praised Cohen for retracting his 2024 testimony in the hush money trial and argued the justice system had been "weaponized" against him.

The interview marked an unusual moment: Cohen, who testified against Trump both in court and before Congress, now appears to be walking back his previous statements. Trump used the platform to relitigate his legal battles and claim prosecutorial overreach.

**Here's what matters for Colorado:** While national media obsesses over Trump-Cohen drama, Coloradans should be watching their own state's priorities—energy policy, water rights, property taxes, and Second Amendment protections. This radio spectacle, however theatrically interesting, doesn't move the needle on issues that directly affect Colorado families and businesses.

Republicans argue the weaponization of the justice system is real and dangerous; Democrats counter that Trump faced legitimate legal accountability. Either way, Colorado conservatives should demand their elected officials focus on state-level governance, not cable-news feuds.
