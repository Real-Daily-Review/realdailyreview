# Democrat Haley Stevens Breaks Ranks Over El-Sayed's Israel Stance

A prominent Michigan Democrat has publicly withdrawn her support for Abdul El-Sayed, signaling deepening fractures within the party over antisemitism and anti-Israel rhetoric.

Haley Stevens, a former Democratic Senate candidate, cited El-Sayed's "hatred of Israel" as her breaking point, according to National Review. Stevens emphasized that her decision was her own, telling the New York Post that fellow Democrats' swift condemnation of her move "reveals something deeply troubling about what is missing in today's Democratic Party."

National Review reports that Stevens is far from alone—many Democrats harbor serious reservations about El-Sayed but have remained silent, suggesting the party's tolerance for anti-Israel activism runs deeper than public statements suggest.

Stevens's defection underscores a widening rift between mainstream Democrats and the party's progressive wing on Middle East policy. While the left has increasingly embraced pro-Palestinian activism, traditional Democratic supporters of Israel find themselves marginalized and pressured into conformity.

The episode exposes how the Democratic Party punishes dissent on identity politics and foreign policy, even from its own members. For Colorado Democrats watching national trends, this signals that the party's center of gravity has shifted further left on issues that once commanded broad consensus.

Watch whether other Democrats follow Stevens's lead or whether party discipline silences further defections.
