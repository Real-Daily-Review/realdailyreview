# Trump's Iran Conflict Threatens to Overshadow Economic Agenda Ahead of Midterms

President Trump is doubling down on military action against Iran even as the five-month conflict threatens to derail his core economic message before November's midterm elections. Trump vowed the U.S. will "win" and predicted Iranian resistance will "peter out," but acknowledged the fighting could stretch longer than anticipated (Fox News).

The escalating commitment raises a critical problem for Republicans: the prolonged conflict is consuming political oxygen and resources that could spotlight Trump's tariff strategy and economic record—his signature strength with voters. National Review argues the Iran war has caused Trump to "forfeit" his defining issue of economic stewardship, a dangerous miscalculation with midterms looming.

For Colorado, the stakes are real. Energy markets remain volatile amid Middle East tensions, affecting gas prices at the pump and the state's oil and gas sector. Defense contractors and military families across Colorado also face uncertainty as the conflict drags on.

Republicans must decide whether sustained Iran operations energize their base or become a liability that allows Democrats to shift debate away from inflation, border security, and government spending—issues favoring the GOP.
