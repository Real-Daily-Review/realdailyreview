# Maine Democrats' Graham Platner Problem Exposes Party's Moral Hypocrisy

Democrats are scrambling to defend an indefensible Senate candidate in Maine. Graham Platner, the party's nominee, carries serious baggage—including Nazi-inspired tattoos and a troubling behavioral history—yet prominent Democrats are urging voters to elect him anyway.

Bill Maher told Maine voters to support Platner "for two reasons," according to the *New York Post*, while Hunter Biden mounted a bizarre defense, challenging critics to reveal their own phone contents rather than address Platner's actual record (*Daily Wire*). The *Denver Post* editorial board, hardly a conservative outlet, called out the Democratic Party's "moral double standard," noting Platner carries "more baggage than Denver International Airport on Thanksgiving weekend."

This is what happens when party loyalty trumps principle. Democrats spent years lecturing Republicans about character and fitness for office. Now they're asking voters to overlook Nazi imagery and disturbing behavior because Platner has a D next to his name.

The contrast is stark: Republicans faced relentless media pressure to distance themselves from controversial figures. Democrats? They're asking voters to ignore the red flags entirely. It's a stunning collapse of the moral authority Democrats claimed to possess.

Colorado voters should watch whether national Democratic leaders—including those with Colorado ties—follow suit and endorse Platner, or whether they'll finally acknowledge that standards should apply equally to both parties.
