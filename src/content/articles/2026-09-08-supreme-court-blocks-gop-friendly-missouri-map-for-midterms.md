# Supreme Court Blocks GOP-Friendly Missouri Map, Hands Democrats a Midterm Gift

Justice Brett Kavanaugh has rejected an emergency bid by Missouri Republicans to use a redrawn congressional map favoring the GOP 7-1 in November's midterms, according to Fox News and Just The News. The state's highest court had previously ruled that voters must approve the new map via ballot measure, effectively blocking its use this cycle.

The practical result: Missouri Republicans must now compete under the old map—which still favors them 6-2—rather than the more advantageous 7-1 configuration they'd already used in their primaries. Kavanaugh issued no comment on the denial, leaving conservatives frustrated over what they see as judicial interference in a state's redistricting authority.

Republicans argue the decision undermines their legitimate efforts to redraw districts based on updated census data. The Washington Examiner notes the timing creates chaos: Missouri used the new map for primaries but now must pivot to the old one for the general election.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado's own congressional maps remain contentious, this ruling signals how courts—even conservative-leaning justices—may constrain GOP redistricting efforts nationwide, potentially affecting Republican gains in swing states heading into 2024.
