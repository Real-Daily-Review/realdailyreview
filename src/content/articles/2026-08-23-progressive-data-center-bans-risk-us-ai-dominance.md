# Data Center Hysteria Spreads as States Strangle AI Infrastructure

Progressive governors are strangling data center development with restrictive policies, even as centrist Democrats warn the nation risks ceding AI dominance to China.

Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro (D) signed an executive order Tuesday restricting data center development, prompting Sen. John Fetterman (D-PA)—hardly a Republican—to push back hard on social media. "AI supremacy and energy dominance underpins our national security," Fetterman said, rejecting what he called "doomsdaying" over artificial intelligence (Washington Examiner).

The pattern is spreading. National Review notes that despite "vast quantities of ink" spent addressing Americans' anxiety about data centers, the hysteria persists—and policy keeps tightening.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's energy sector and grid capacity could become attractive to data center operators fleeing blue-state restrictions. But state regulators and environmental activists are already eyeing similar restrictions here, threatening to repeat Pennsylvania's mistake.

Republicans and pro-growth conservatives argue that blocking data center infrastructure hands economic advantage and national security leverage to China and other adversaries. The left's reflexive opposition to large-scale energy projects—even those powering AI innovation—prioritizes environmental symbolism over American competitiveness.

Watch whether Colorado lawmakers attempt their own data center restrictions, and whether energy companies push back by relocating operations to freer states.
