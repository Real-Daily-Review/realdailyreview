# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Broader Left-Wing Push Against Parental Rights

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's full-throated defense of pediatric sex-change procedures—without government safeguards—exposes the progressive playbook that could reshape Colorado healthcare policy if similar candidates gain power here.

Abdul El-Sayed, running for U.S. Senate in Michigan, argued Friday that doctors, not government, should decide whether children undergo gender-transition treatments (Fox News, The Daily Wire). He invoked his medical credentials to dismiss concerns about state oversight, telling a Michigan State University crowd that physicians should have final say on the matter.

Republicans and conservatives argue this position strips parents of authority over their own children's medical decisions and ignores the irreversible nature of such procedures. They contend that minors cannot meaningfully consent to life-altering treatments and that government has a legitimate role protecting vulnerable youth from experimental medicine.

Colorado conservatives should note the ideological trajectory: El-Sayed's stance mirrors arguments advanced by progressive activists and medical boards in states like California and Colorado, where Democratic legislatures have moved to shield gender clinics from parental oversight and state regulation. If similar candidates gain traction in Colorado elections, expect renewed pushes to expand "gender-affirming care" access for minors while limiting parental notification and consent requirements.

Watch for whether Colorado Democrats embrace or distance themselves from this framing ahead of 2024 state races.
