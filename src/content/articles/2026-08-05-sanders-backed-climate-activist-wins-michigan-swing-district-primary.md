# Far-Left Activist Wins Michigan Primary, Sets Up Showdown Over House Control

A Bernie Sanders-backed climate activist just became the Democratic nominee in a pivotal swing district—and Republicans see an opening to flip a seat that could decide House control.

William Lawrence, co-founder of the Sunrise Movement, defeated establishment favorite Bridget Brink, former U.S. Ambassador to Ukraine, in Michigan's 7th Congressional District Democratic primary (Fox News, New York Post). Lawrence also beat veteran Matt Maasdam in the crowded race.

The district, centered on Lansing, is a genuine battleground. Lawrence will face Republican incumbent Tom Barrett in what observers expect to be one of the cycle's most consequential House races (Just The News).

**The conservative take:** Republicans argue Lawrence's victory signals Democrats' leftward lurch on energy and climate policy—positions that could alienate working-class voters in industrial Michigan. The Sunrise Movement has pushed aggressive climate mandates that threaten manufacturing jobs and energy independence, priorities that resonate in districts like this one. Establishment Democrats' loss to a Sanders-endorsed activist suggests the party's base is pulling away from centrist candidates.

For Colorado conservatives, this race matters: similar primary dynamics are reshaping Democratic energy and climate platforms nationwide, directly affecting how Colorado's Democratic delegation votes on oil, gas, and mining policy affecting the state's economy.

Watch whether Lawrence's nomination energizes Republicans in other swing districts—or whether his climate-first agenda proves toxic in general election territory.
