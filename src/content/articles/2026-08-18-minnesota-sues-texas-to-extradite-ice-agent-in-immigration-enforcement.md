# Minnesota Sues Texas Over ICE Agent Extradition—Another Blue-State Attack on Immigration Enforcement

Minnesota's Democratic Attorney General Keith Ellison is suing Texas Gov. Greg Abbott to force extradition of ICE agent Christian Castro, who faces charges in Minneapolis related to an alleged assault during a federal immigration enforcement operation (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire). Castro was charged by Hennepin County prosecutors with multiple felony counts stemming from the Trump-era deportation crackdown.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and immigration hawks contend that blue-state prosecutors are weaponizing the criminal justice system against federal immigration enforcement. Constitutionally, states must honor extradition requests for criminal defendants (Just The News), yet Abbott's refusal signals a broader conservative resistance to what they view as politically motivated prosecutions of ICE officers doing their jobs. Conservatives argue that targeting immigration agents discourages enforcement and emboldens sanctuary-city politics.

**Democrats counter** that the charges reflect genuine criminal misconduct unrelated to policy disagreements—a distinction Ellison's office likely emphasizes.

**Colorado angle:** This case echoes tensions between Colorado's Democratic leadership and federal immigration enforcement. If Abbott prevails in resisting extradition, it could embolden other Republican governors and complicate interstate law enforcement cooperation.

Watch whether Abbott's defiance holds and whether other GOP-led states follow suit in protecting federal agents from blue-state prosecution.
