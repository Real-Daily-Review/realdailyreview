# Americans Want Out of Iran; AI Job Fears Rising

Voters are sending Washington a clear message: end the Iran conflict now, and get serious about AI's threat to American workers.

A CBS News/YouGov poll shows 78% of Americans believe the U.S. should end the conflict with Iran immediately, while 79% doubt any peace agreement will produce pro-Western Iranian leadership (Washington Examiner, Breitbart). The bipartisan consensus reflects war fatigue and skepticism of nation-building promises that have defined two decades of Middle East policy.

Meanwhile, 70% of Americans express concern that artificial intelligence will eliminate jobs, according to polling cited by Just The News. Those with less education report the highest anxiety—a warning sign for policymakers pushing AI adoption without addressing workforce displacement.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's tech corridor and defense contractors have stakes in both issues. AI development accelerates across Denver and Boulder, while Colorado workers in manufacturing, logistics, and service sectors face real displacement risks. Republicans should demand that any federal AI policy includes retraining provisions and doesn't subsidize corporate automation while workers bear the cost.

Conservatives argue Washington must stop treating military interventions as open-ended commitments and focus on protecting American jobs from technological disruption—not through Luddite policies, but through market-driven retraining and education reform.
