# Prime Video Launches TikTok-Style 'Clips' Feed to Compete With Netflix, Disney

Amazon is adding a vertical short-form video feed to Prime Video, joining Netflix and Disney Plus in adopting TikTok-like discovery features for streaming content.

The new "Clips" feed, accessible via a carousel on the app's homepage, will display scrollable snippets from shows and movies available on the platform, according to The Verge. Users can watch brief clips and jump directly to full titles, or rent or purchase content from the feed. Prime Video says the experience will personalize based on viewing history, showing "something new" with each visit.

The move represents an expansion of Prime Video's previous short-form video efforts. The platform previously offered a TikTok-style feed featuring NBA game highlights, but the Clips feed broadens this to include general entertainment content across its catalog, according to TechCrunch.

The feature aligns with a broader industry trend. Netflix and Disney Plus have both introduced similar vertical video feeds in recent months as streaming services compete for user engagement and seek new ways to surface content discovery amid market saturation.
