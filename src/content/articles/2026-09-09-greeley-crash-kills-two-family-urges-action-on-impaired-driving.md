# Colorado Family Grieves After Impaired Driver Kills Father in Greeley Crash

A preventable tragedy in Greeley has renewed focus on the dangers of impaired driving—and raises hard questions about who's behind the wheel.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, a Greeley family is mourning after a crash claimed two lives. The daughter of one victim is now urging drivers to reconsider getting behind the wheel while impaired, a straightforward message that resonates across political lines.

The incident underscores a broader concern conservatives have raised: Colorado's roads face real threats from drivers who have no business operating vehicles. While impaired driving remains a persistent public safety issue regardless of the driver's background, The Daily Wire has reported on similar cases involving previously deported illegal immigrants driving stolen vehicles under the influence—a convergence of failures in immigration enforcement, vehicle theft prevention, and DUI enforcement that compounds the tragedy.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Colorado needs stronger enforcement of existing laws, particularly around repeat DUI offenders and vehicle theft rings, rather than more regulations that burden law-abiding drivers. They also point to the need for consistent immigration enforcement to keep dangerous individuals off Colorado roads.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado lawmakers will push for tougher penalties on repeat DUI offenders and stolen vehicle rings, or if they'll pursue broader restrictions affecting all drivers.
