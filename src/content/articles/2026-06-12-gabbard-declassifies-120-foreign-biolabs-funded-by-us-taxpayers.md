# Gabbard Declassifies Biolab Funding: 120 Labs in 30 Countries on U.S. Dime

Outgoing Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard has released declassified documents revealing the federal government has funded over 120 biological laboratories across 30-plus foreign countries—a disclosure that vindicates skeptics who questioned the narrative pushed by mainstream media and federal agencies.

According to Gabbard's announcement, "the information surrounding the existence, history, locations and funding of these US funded biolabs has been intentionally covered up by powerful people falsely claiming that they do not exist" (Just The News). The New York Post reports the labs were funded directly by U.S. taxpayers, raising immediate questions about oversight, purpose, and justification for the spending.

The revelation underscores a broader conservative concern: unelected bureaucrats operating shadowy programs with minimal transparency or accountability. When officials and their media allies actively deny the existence of taxpayer-funded operations abroad, it erodes public trust and suggests institutional resistance to oversight.

Colorado taxpayers deserve to know exactly how their federal tax dollars are being spent overseas and why such programs operate under a veil of secrecy. This isn't about conspiracy—it's about government transparency and fiscal responsibility.

Watch for congressional Republicans demanding a full accounting of biolab locations, funding amounts, and stated missions.
