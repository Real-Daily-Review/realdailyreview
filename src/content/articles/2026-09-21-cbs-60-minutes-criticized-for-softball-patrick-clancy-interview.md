# Media Soft-Pedals Patrick Clancy Interview While Conservatives Demand Real Accountability

CBS's "60 Minutes" fumbled a chance to press Patrick Clancy on hard questions during his recent sit-down about his wife Lindsay's killing of their three children, drawing sharp criticism from conservative media outlets and true-crime analysts.

Briana Whitney, a contributor to the "Crime Junkie Podcast," told the New York Post that the interview was "the worst interview I've seen in a long time," noting that Clancy "wasn't really asked anything by the host of actual substance." According to the Daily Caller, Clancy claimed he forgave his wife despite her actions—but the "60 Minutes" segment failed to probe the deeper questions surrounding the case.

The Daily Wire argues that progressive voices who spent the summer attacking Clancy have gone silent now that sympathetic media coverage has emerged, suggesting selective outrage based on ideology rather than principle.

**The Conservative Take:** Right-leaning outlets contend that mainstream media outlets like CBS prioritize narrative management over rigorous journalism, particularly when cases involve complex family dynamics or mental health issues. Critics say "60 Minutes" should have pressed Clancy on specifics rather than allowing him an unchallenged platform.
