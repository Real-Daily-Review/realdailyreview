# Democrats Nominate Pro-Israel Speaker While Pushing Massive Entitlement Expansion

House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker this week—a choice that exposed the party's rhetorical gap between its anti-Israel wing and its actual leadership priorities. Despite months of progressive posturing on Middle East policy, Democrats backed Jeffries, whose pro-Israel record is well-documented, according to the *Daily Caller*.

But here's what matters for Colorado taxpayers: Jeffries is simultaneously championing a massive expansion of federal entitlements, which *National Review* characterizes as "reckless in the extreme." That means more federal spending, higher deficits, and pressure on states like Colorado to match or subsidize new federal programs—ultimately landing on your tax bill.

Republicans argue the contradiction is telling: Democrats will abandon their loudest activists on foreign policy to maintain party unity, but they won't abandon their appetite for government spending. The entitlement push, conservatives say, ignores the fiscal reality that unfunded promises eventually require tax hikes or cuts elsewhere.

For Colorado, this signals continued federal spending pressure and potential mandates that could strain the state budget or force lawmakers to choose between tax increases and program cuts.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly Democrats—will publicly distance themselves from Jeffries' entitlement agenda or quietly fall in line.
