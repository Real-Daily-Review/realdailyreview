# ABC Reportedly Ending Jimmy Kimmel's Late-Night Show in 2027

Late-night television continues its slow collapse as ABC reportedly plans to shutter *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* when the host's contract expires in May 2027, marking the end of his 24-year run on the network.

According to Page Six (via *The Daily Wire*), ABC will not renew Kimmel's deal, with the upcoming season expected to be the show's final year. *Breitbart* reports that network insiders have confirmed the decision, though ABC has publicly denied the claims.

The move reflects broader industry headwinds: viewership for late-night programming has cratered as cord-cutting accelerates and younger audiences abandon traditional television. Kimmel's show, once a reliable platform for Democratic talking points and celebrity activism, has struggled to maintain relevance in a fractured media landscape.

For conservatives, the decline of legacy late-night is a welcome shift. These programs have long functioned as unofficial Democratic messaging operations, using comedy as cover for partisan advocacy. Fewer eyeballs on that content means less cultural gatekeeping by Hollywood's progressive establishment.

ABC's denial notwithstanding, the rumor signals what many have observed: network television's stranglehold on American entertainment is finally loosening.
