# Colorado Voters Face Tax-Refund Extinction While Weighing Redistricting Reform

Colorado taxpayers may never see another TABOR refund, according to *Complete Colorado*, as Proposition NN threatens to permanently redirect surplus revenue away from residents' pockets and into state coffers. The measure represents a fundamental assault on the Taxpayer Bill of Rights—the constitutional guardrail that has returned billions to Colorado families since 1992.

Meanwhile, voters will also decide Initiative #256, which would impose new restrictions on congressional redistricting to prevent partisan gerrymandering. Supporters argue the measure creates neutral guardrails; Republicans counter that unilateral redistricting rules could disadvantage conservative representation if Democrats retain control of the legislature.

**The conservative case:** Prop NN eliminates the mechanism that forces government to live within its means. Rather than spending restraint, Democrats are betting voters won't notice the slow death of tax refunds buried under campaign noise about education and healthcare. Initiative #256, while facially neutral, hands redistricting authority to commissions that may prove hostile to Republican mapmakers—a risk Colorado conservatives should weigh carefully.

Taxpayers should watch whether Prop NN passes and, if so, how quickly the state legislature moves to lock in permanent spending increases.
