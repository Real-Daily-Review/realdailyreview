# Trump Opts for Old Air Force One Over New $400M Aircraft—Here's Why

President Trump is flying the aging Air Force One back to Washington after the NATO summit in Turkey rather than using the newly completed presidential aircraft, citing a practical reason: he wants American troops stationed abroad to tour the new plane at a U.K. air base.

According to Fox News, Trump explained the decision by noting the new Air Force One will head to Mildenhall Air Force Base in the United Kingdom following the NATO gathering, allowing service members to see the upgraded aircraft firsthand.

The Washington Examiner, however, notes the move has sparked questions. The new plane—reportedly a converted Qatari airliner—carries a $400 million price tag, and Trump's explanation "raised more questions than answers" about why the aircraft wasn't immediately operational for presidential use.

The decision underscores ongoing questions about the Air Force One modernization program's timeline and readiness. For Colorado taxpayers footing the bill through federal spending, the delayed deployment of a $400 million asset raises legitimate concerns about project management and whether the aircraft will deliver value commensurate with its massive cost.
