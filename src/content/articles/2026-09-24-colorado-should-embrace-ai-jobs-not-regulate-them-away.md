# Colorado Should Seize AI Opportunity—Not Regulate It Into Oblivion

Colorado has a chance to lead on artificial intelligence workforce development instead of joining the progressive panic brigade. The Colorado Sun and others have floated the idea of AI-enabled apprenticeships—pairing young workers with real jobs under clear standards—a market-driven approach conservatives can support. Complete Colorado notes that Magellan Strategies' recent survey shows solid reasons for Colorado to bet on AI rather than fear it, citing workforce development and economic opportunity.

But here's the rub: while Colorado could attract data centers and AI talent, the left's instinct is regulation and control. Democrats nationally have pushed AI "safety" frameworks that risk stifling innovation and handing competitive advantage to China, as The Daily Wire warns. America's dependency on foreign supply chains—rare earths, pharmaceuticals, steel—shows what happens when we abandon domestic capability.

Colorado's energy infrastructure, water resources, and business-friendly pockets make the state attractive for AI development. Apprenticeships that match workers with employers—without heavy-handed government mandates—could fill real labor gaps while keeping costs down and opportunity open.

**Watch whether Colorado lawmakers resist the urge to over-regulate AI development and instead let market forces and voluntary apprenticeships drive growth.**
