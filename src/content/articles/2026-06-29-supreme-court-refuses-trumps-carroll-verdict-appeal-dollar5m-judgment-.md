# Supreme Court Blocks Trump's Last Appeal in Carroll Verdict

The U.S. Supreme Court has refused to hear President Trump's challenge to a $5 million civil verdict in the E. Jean Carroll sexual abuse and defamation case, leaving the judgment to stand without explanation in a brief, unsigned order typical of the Court's practice (9News Denver).

Trump's legal team had sought review of the lower court's decision, but the High Court declined to intervene. According to the New York Post, Trump now faces the obligation to pay the full $5 million to the former advice columnist.

The case originated from Carroll's allegations and subsequent defamation claims after Trump denied her account. The civil verdict—distinct from any criminal proceedings—represents a significant legal loss for the president as he navigates multiple ongoing legal challenges.

**Colorado angle:** While this case centers on federal courts and New York law, it underscores broader questions about litigation costs and presidential accountability that resonate with taxpayers nationwide who foot portions of executive legal defense bills.

Conservatives should monitor whether Trump pursues alternative remedies or whether this verdict influences the trajectory of other pending cases against him.
