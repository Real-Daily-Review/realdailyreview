# Michigan Democrat's Refusal to Condemn Antisemitic Ally Exposes Far-Left Radicalism

Abdul El-Sayed, Michigan's Democratic Senate nominee, has doubled down on his alliance with Hasan Piker, an online personality with a documented record of antisemitic and anti-American rhetoric—a choice that reveals how far left the Democratic Party's bench has drifted.

According to newly circulated video footage reported by *The Daily Wire*, El-Sayed flatly refused to condemn Piker for any of his inflammatory remarks, instead defending their months-long campaign partnership. *National Review* notes El-Sayed campaigned extensively with Piker despite his well-known views, suggesting either willful blindness or ideological alignment.

Republicans argue this episode exposes the Democratic Party's tolerance for extremism when it advances their political interests. El-Sayed's refusal to distance himself from a self-described Marxist influencer with antisemitic views undercuts Democratic claims to oppose bigotry and hate speech.

Democrats have not offered a substantive defense of El-Sayed's position, instead remaining largely silent on the controversy.

For Colorado conservatives, this serves as a cautionary tale: similar radical elements have gained influence in Colorado Democratic circles, from environmental absolutists blocking energy development to gun-control activists reshaping state policy.
