# Trump Rallies GOP Base in Ohio Push; Colorado Republicans Watch Playbook

Former President Donald Trump hit the campaign trail in Ohio Saturday, urging voters to "pretend I'm on the ballot" as he stumped for Republican Senate incumbent Jon Husted and gubernatorial candidate Vivek Ramaswamy (Washington Examiner). The rally in Vandalia drew former NFL coach Jon Gruden, who touted his Ohio roots to energize the Republican base ahead of midterms.

Trump framed the election as a choice for a "golden age" (Fox News Politics), part of a 10-state tour designed to mobilize GOP voters in competitive races. Republicans argue the campaign represents a critical moment to reclaim legislative power and reverse what they see as Biden administration overreach on inflation, energy policy, and border security.

Democrats counter that Trump's continued dominance of GOP messaging pulls the party rightward and away from kitchen-table issues affecting working families.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado Republicans are watching Trump's midterm strategy closely as the state remains a purple battleground. GOP candidates here will likely adopt similar messaging on inflation and energy independence—critical issues for Colorado's oil and gas workers and rural communities.

**Watch next:** Whether Trump's rally momentum translates to GOP turnout in swing states like Colorado, where Senate and gubernatorial races could reshape state policy on taxes, land use, and energy development.
