# Quick Response Stops Commerce City Grass Fire Before Major Damage

Denver-area fire crews demonstrated effective emergency response Sunday when they rapidly contained a grass fire near Tower Road in Commerce City that briefly forced evacuations.

The fire, which sparked Sunday evening, prompted an evacuation order affecting the area near 112th and Tower Road for approximately 30 minutes while firefighters worked to suppress the blaze. According to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31, crews quickly stopped the spread and lifted the evacuation order without reports of major structural damage or injuries.

The incident underscores both the wildfire risk facing the Denver metro area and the importance of well-trained, adequately funded emergency response teams. Colorado's Front Range communities face increasing exposure to grass and brush fires, particularly during dry seasons—a reality that demands reliable local fire services and clear evacuation protocols.

The rapid containment prevented what could have escalated into a more serious threat to residential and commercial property in the Commerce City corridor. As Colorado's population continues to grow along the Front Range, maintaining robust emergency infrastructure and response capacity remains critical for protecting taxpayers' lives and property.
