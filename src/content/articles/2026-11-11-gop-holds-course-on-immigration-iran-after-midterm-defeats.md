# Republicans Double Down on Immigration, Iran Pressure After Midterm Rout

Senate Republicans have kept their leadership intact despite historic electoral losses, with John Thune retaining the minority leader post (Breitbart). Meanwhile, key GOP figures are signaling no retreat on core conservative priorities: Rep. Markwayne Mullin pledged "zero change" on immigration enforcement, noting the Department of Homeland Security is deporting roughly 3,600 people daily (Fox News Politics).

On foreign policy, the Trump administration launched Task Force Iron Gate to tighten economic pressure on Iran by choking off Tehran's land borders—a move experts warn could trigger escalation (Fox News Politics).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that maintaining tough immigration enforcement and Iran containment reflects voter demands for border security and American strength abroad, not electoral weakness. Holding leadership steady signals resolve rather than capitulation.

**Democrats counter** that doubling down on contentious policies after midterm losses ignores voter rejection of GOP priorities and risks further isolation.

**Colorado angle:** Immigration enforcement directly affects Colorado's agriculture, construction, and energy sectors, where labor dynamics remain contested between business interests and restrictionist conservatives.

Watch whether Senate Republicans under Thune can actually block Biden administration policies or whether minority status forces genuine strategic recalibration.
