# Russia Readies Winter Assault on Ukraine Energy Grid—Putin Betting Cold Will Break Kyiv's Will

Putin is betting that a brutal winter will fracture Ukrainian resolve, but history suggests authoritarian strongmen routinely misread their opponents' capacity to endure.

Russia is preparing "massive strikes" against Ukraine's energy infrastructure as winter approaches, Putin announced openly—a calculated strategy to freeze civilians into surrender. The Russian leader has explicitly instructed his armed forces to target power plants and grid systems in retaliation for Ukrainian strikes on Russian territory.

This is Putin's gamble: that Ukrainians, facing blackouts, frozen pipes, and humanitarian crisis, will pressure their government to capitulate. It's a rational calculation from a dictator's perspective—but it ignores what Ukraine has already demonstrated. After nearly two years of invasion, Ukrainian civilians have endured relentless bombardment, displacement, and loss. Cold and darkness, while brutal, have not yet broken their will to resist.

Conservatives should note the broader lesson: authoritarian regimes consistently underestimate the resolve of free peoples defending their homeland. Ukraine's resistance, funded substantially by American aid, underscores why deterring aggression early is cheaper than managing prolonged conflict.

Watch whether the Biden administration and Congress sustain Ukraine aid commitments through winter, or whether war fatigue and domestic political pressure begin eroding bipartisan support.
