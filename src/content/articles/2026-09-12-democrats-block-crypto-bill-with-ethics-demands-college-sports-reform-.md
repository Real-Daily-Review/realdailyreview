# Senate Crypto and College Sports Bills Face Democratic Obstruction

Democrats are weaponizing ethics demands to block Republican-backed crypto legislation, even as the Senate races to pass bipartisan college sports reform before the August recess ends.

The Trump administration's Clarity Act—aimed at clarifying crypto regulation—faces a critical 60-vote threshold in the Senate (Fox News). Democrats are demanding ethics rules tied to the President's reported $1.4 billion digital currency holdings be attached to the bill, effectively holding crypto clarity hostage to progressive compliance demands. Republicans argue this is a transparent delay tactic designed to kill pro-growth policy.

Meanwhile, Sen. Ted Cruz (R-TX) is pushing hard for passage of the Protect College Sports Act, which would establish uniform rules for athlete compensation and name, image, and likeness (NIL) deals (Washington Examiner). Cruz faced a rowdy crowd at ESPN's College GameDay while advocating for the measure, underscoring the cultural divide over how college athletics should operate.

For Colorado, these bills matter: the state's universities and athletes stand to benefit from clearer NIL frameworks, while Colorado's growing crypto and blockchain sector needs regulatory certainty to compete nationally.

**Watch:** Whether Democrats successfully filibuster crypto reform or if Republicans can peel off enough Democratic votes to pass both bills before Congress returns from recess.
