# Social Media Influencers Andrew and Tristan Tate Arrested in Miami on Rape, Trafficking Charges

Federal authorities arrested controversial social media influencers Andrew and Tristan Tate in Miami on Saturday, according to the U.S. Marshals Service, following an outstanding extradition warrant from the United Kingdom.

The dual U.S. and British citizens face serious charges including rape, human trafficking, and child pornography allegations. According to *The Daily Wire*, Andrew Tate was charged last year by the Crown Prosecution Service with 10 counts of crimes, including rape and human trafficking related to three women. *Just The News* reports the specific charges against both brothers remain under seal pending formal announcement.

The Tate brothers have cultivated massive followings across social media platforms, though their content has drawn criticism for controversial messaging. The arrest marks a significant development in an ongoing international investigation.

Details regarding extradition proceedings and the timeline for their appearance before a U.S. magistrate remain unclear at this writing.
