# Florida Democrats Hold Ground as Donalds Wins GOP Nod for Governor

Florida's primary results Tuesday show Democrats clinging to shrinking turf while Republican Rep. Byron Donalds decisively won the GOP gubernatorial primary with 48.7% of the vote, setting up a November matchup against former Republican-turned-Democrat David Jolly (Washington Examiner).

In the state's few remaining Democratic strongholds, incumbents Jared Moskowitz (District 25) and Debbie Wasserman Schultz (District 20)—both representing Miami-area seats—advanced to the general election (Just The News). Both districts, situated north of Miami, remain among Florida's last reliably blue territory after recent redistricting reshaped the state's political map.

Donalds' commanding primary performance—nearly double his nearest competitor, Lt. Gov. Jay Collins, who garnered 24.8%—signals unified Republican backing heading into the general. Republicans argue the party's consolidation around Donalds reflects voter appetite for conservative governance and opposition to Democratic policies on inflation, border security, and government overreach. Democrats counter that Jolly's switch to their party and his Republican credentials position him as a moderate alternative.
