# Republican Pushes Declaration of Independence Bill as Socialist Candidates Gain Ground

Rep. Matt Van Epps is forcing a conversation conservatives need to have: as America approaches its 250th birthday, socialist candidates are gaining traction in Democratic primaries, and the foundational principles of 1776 are under siege.

Van Epps's bill reaffirms the Declaration of Independence—a direct response to the leftward lurch of the Democratic Party. Republicans argue that reasserting the Declaration's core tenets of individual liberty, limited government, and natural rights is essential pushback against progressive candidates openly embracing socialist economics and expansive state power.

For Colorado, this matters. The state has seen its share of progressive legislative overreach—from energy regulations to gun restrictions to tax hikes justified by government "investment." A reaffirmation of Declaration principles would serve as a constitutional north star against further regulatory creep and the erosion of property rights, energy independence, and Second Amendment protections.

Democrats likely view this as performative—a symbolic gesture rather than substantive policy. But conservatives understand that ideas precede politics. When the left openly challenges the Declaration's vision of individual liberty and limited government, reasserting those principles in statute form is not ceremonial; it's defensive.

Watch whether Colorado's Republican delegation co-sponsors this bill and how state Democrats respond—their answer will reveal whether they've fully embraced the progressive agenda or still claim fidelity to America's founding documents.
