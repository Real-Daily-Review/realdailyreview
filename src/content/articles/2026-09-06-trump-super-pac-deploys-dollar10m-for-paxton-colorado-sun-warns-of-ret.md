# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Race; Colorado Sun Warns of 'Retribution'

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is flooding $10 million into the Texas Senate race to rescue Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton against Democrat James Talarico, according to FEC filings reported by The Daily Wire. The financial infusion marks the first major commitment by Trump's independent expenditure committee in a competitive general election this cycle.
