# Denver DA Drops Charges Against Fired Officer—Another Win for Accountability

Denver's district attorney is moving to dismiss all charges against a former Denver Police Department officer fired in July following allegations of assault, misconduct, and false reporting, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The officer, Gabriel Lucero, was charged in connection with an alleged altercation at a nightclub. Despite the criminal charges, DPD terminated him months ago—a rare instance of the department holding one of its own accountable without waiting for the courts.

The DA's decision to drop the case raises questions about prosecutorial judgment and case strength. While details remain sparse, conservatives have long argued that Colorado's criminal justice system suffers from inconsistent enforcement and weak prosecution of certain cases. This dismissal underscores that concern: either the evidence didn't support conviction, or the DA's office mishandled the initial charging decision.

For Denver taxpayers, the outcome is mixed. Lucero's firing protects the department's credibility and removes a problematic officer from the force. But the dropped charges leave unanswered questions about what actually happened and whether justice was served—or whether prosecutorial overreach led to charges that should never have been filed.
