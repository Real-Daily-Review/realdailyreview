# Thanksgiving Storm Hammers East Coast; Colorado Spared Worst of Weather System

A massive storm system battered the East Coast over Thanksgiving, dumping record rainfall across New York City and surrounding regions while knocking out power to nearly a million customers, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver and the New York Post. The deluge forced organizers to postpone the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade for the first time ever—a striking reminder of how severe weather can disrupt even America's most iconic traditions.

While Colorado largely dodged the worst impacts this time, the storm underscores a broader reality: extreme weather events are becoming more frequent and unpredictable, straining infrastructure and emergency response systems nationwide. For Colorado, that means state and local officials should be stress-testing water management systems, power grids, and emergency protocols before the next major system arrives.
