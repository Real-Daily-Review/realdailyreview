# Military Leadership Signals Caution on Iran Escalation as Options Narrow

The top U.S. military commander is privately seeking an "off-ramp" from potential Iran conflict, citing dangerously limited military options—a stark warning that even within the Trump administration, there's recognition that unchecked executive war-making creates strategic vulnerability.

According to the Washington Examiner, Joint Chiefs of Staff Chairman Gen. Dan Caine is discussing concerns about escalation options in private conversations, signaling the military brass isn't comfortable with the current trajectory. National Review argues the problem runs deeper: relying on a single advisor for military counsel is "irresponsible" and leaves the president exposed to groupthink and miscalculation.

**The conservative case:** Republicans have long warned against open-ended military commitments and nation-building adventures. This moment underscores why robust military counsel—not echo chambers—matters. Limited options often mean limited good choices; sound strategy requires honest debate, not cheerleading.

**What Colorado taxpayers should know:** Any Iran escalation would drain resources from domestic priorities and potentially draw Colorado-based military installations into extended operations.

Watch whether Congress demands transparency on military planning and whether the administration broadens its advisory circle before any irreversible decisions are made.
