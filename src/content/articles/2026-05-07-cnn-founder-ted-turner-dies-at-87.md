# CNN Founder Ted Turner Dies at 87

Ted Turner, the media entrepreneur who founded CNN and built a cable television empire, died Wednesday at 87, according to PBS NewsHour.

Turner transformed the media landscape by creating the first 24-hour news network, fundamentally changing how billions consumed news globally, PBS reported. Starting with a single television station in Atlanta, he expanded into a sprawling cable empire that included CNN, TBS, and TNT, according to CBS News.

Known for his risk-taking approach and outspoken public persona, Turner became a celebrity figure in his own right. He was married to actress Jane Fonda, further cementing his place in popular culture.

Turner's legacy centers on his vision for continuous news coverage—a model that became standard across the industry and shaped modern journalism. His willingness to challenge conventional broadcasting norms established him as a visionary in television history.

No immediate details on cause of death were provided in the available reports.
