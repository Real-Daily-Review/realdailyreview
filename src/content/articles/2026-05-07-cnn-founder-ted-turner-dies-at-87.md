# Ted Turner, CNN Founder, Dies at 87

Ted Turner, the visionary entrepreneur who founded CNN and transformed global news consumption, died Wednesday at age 87, according to PBS NewsHour and CBS News.

Turner revolutionized the media landscape by creating the first 24-hour news network, fundamentally changing how billions accessed information worldwide. Beyond CNN, he built a cable empire that included TBS and TNT, establishing himself as a major force in American broadcasting.

The Atlanta-born risk-taker was known for his bold business moves and outspoken public persona. His influence extended beyond media: Turner became a celebrity in his own right, including a high-profile marriage to actress Jane Fonda.

His legacy encompasses not just the networks he built, but the structural shift he created in how news was delivered and consumed globally. CNN's round-the-clock format became the industry standard, influencing news operations worldwide.
