# Democrats Scramble to Dump Maine Nominee as Assault Allegations Mount

Democrats are in full retreat from their Maine Senate nominee Graham Platner after fresh sexual assault allegations surfaced, leaving the party scrambling to find an exit strategy less than four months before Election Day.

According to National Review and the Daily Caller, prominent Democrats including Elizabeth Warren and Bernie Sanders have begun distancing themselves from Platner as the allegations pile up. The Daily Wire reports that while Maine Democrats technically have time to replace him on the November ballot, the mechanics are complicated—requiring his withdrawal and a party process to nominate a successor.

Republicans see the chaos as emblematic of Democratic hypocrisy on #MeToo accountability. When allegations target their own candidates, Democrats have historically demanded immediate resignations from Republicans; now facing the same standard, they're scrambling for procedural loopholes rather than principle.

The timing is brutal for Democrats. With the general election four months away, a replacement nominee would enter the race at a disadvantage, forced to rebuild name recognition and donor networks while Republicans maintain momentum.
