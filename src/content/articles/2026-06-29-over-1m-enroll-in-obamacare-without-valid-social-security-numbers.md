# Obamacare Plagued by Fraud as Over 1 Million Enroll Without Valid Social Security Numbers

The Affordable Care Act's enrollment system is hemorrhaging credibility: RFK Jr. and Dr. Oz report that more than 1 million people without Social Security numbers have enrolled in Obamacare, according to the Daily Caller. Federal authorities have also indicted eight illegal migrants in Kentucky for using stolen Social Security numbers to work—a symptom of broader identity-theft schemes exploiting the healthcare law's lax verification protocols.

Republicans argue the fraud exposes what they've long warned about: government healthcare programs lack the security controls to prevent abuse. The system's inability to verify enrollee identity before dispensing benefits represents reckless stewardship of taxpayer dollars and national security vulnerabilities.

Democrats have not yet responded publicly to the specific fraud allegations, though they typically defend the ACA's enrollment accessibility.

**Colorado angle:** Coloradans enrolled in the state's Connect for Health Colorado marketplace should demand answers about whether their personal data and tax credits are being protected. The state's exchange operates under federal oversight—taxpayers deserve clarity on whether Colorado residents' information is being compromised by the same verification gaps plaguing the national system.

Watch for congressional Republicans to demand a full audit of ACA enrollment records and tighter identity-verification requirements before the 2026 enrollment season.
