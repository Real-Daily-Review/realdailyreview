# Trump Administration Keeps Iran Deal Text Under Wraps as Scrutiny Mounts

The Trump administration is refusing to release the full text of its Iran agreement, citing "delicate diplomatic" concerns—a dodge that's drawing fire even from some Democrats frustrated with the deal itself.

Vice President Vance has promised publication "in the near future," but the continued secrecy fuels legitimate questions about what exactly was negotiated and whether Americans are getting the full story (National Review). Rep. Yassamin Ansari (D-AZ), no Iran hawk, called the deal "abysmal" on CNN while acknowledging the need to "cut our losses" and end the conflict (Breitbart).

Conservatives argue that transparency is non-negotiable when it comes to foreign policy agreements affecting American security and interests. Keeping the terms hidden prevents Congress and the public from properly evaluating whether the administration got a fair deal or simply kicked a problem down the road. The administration's vague invocation of diplomatic sensitivity rings hollow—if the agreement is sound, sunlight should strengthen it, not weaken it.

The longer the text stays classified, the more it looks like the administration has something to hide. Colorado voters and their representatives deserve to know what commitments their government is making abroad.

Watch whether Congress demands the full text through formal channels or whether the administration finally makes good on its transparency promises.
