# Boebert, Laubacher Clash Over Tariffs, Inflation in Heated Debate

Lauren Boebert and Democrat Eileen Laubacher traded sharp attacks Monday night in their only televised debate for Colorado's 4th Congressional District seat, with moderator Kyle Clark frequently intervening as the two candidates talked over each other (Colorado Sun).

The debate covered economic ground conservatives care about: tariffs, inflation, and diesel prices (Denver Post). Boebert, the Republican incumbent, has consistently opposed Biden-Harris inflation policies and backed pro-growth, America-First trade positions. Laubacher, the Democratic challenger, predictably blamed Republican tariff proposals for price pressures—the standard left-wing argument that trade restrictions harm consumers, despite evidence that unchecked inflation under Democratic spending has driven Colorado's cost of living into crisis.

The 4th District, which spans Colorado's Eastern Plains and includes energy-dependent communities, has felt inflation's bite acutely. Diesel prices, agricultural input costs, and energy sector uncertainty directly impact ranchers and small-business owners who form the district's backbone. Boebert's record defending oil and gas development and opposing regulatory overreach resonates here; Laubacher represents the progressive energy-transition agenda that threatens rural Colorado livelihoods.

The debate's contentious tone reflects the stakes: control of a swing seat in a Republican-leaning district where economic messaging will decide the race.

**Watch for:** early voting patterns and whether rural turnout favors Boebert's energy and inflation messaging over Laubacher's climate-focused platform.
