# White House Fraud Task Force Targets 750,000 Fake Obamacare Enrollees, Saving Taxpayers $2.2 Billion

Vice President JD Vance's White House Fraud Task Force is eliminating approximately 750,000 fraudulent Obamacare enrollments, according to announcements this week. The action is expected to save American taxpayers $2.2 billion.

The crackdown represents a direct challenge to the Obama-era healthcare law, which conservatives have long criticized as bloated and prone to waste. Republicans argue that the Affordable Care Act has been riddled with fraud and improper payments since its inception—a reality the Biden administration largely ignored. By targeting these fraudulent accounts, the Trump administration is doing what the previous administration refused to do: hold the program accountable and protect taxpayer dollars.

The scale of the problem underscores a core conservative principle: government programs, especially those built on expansive federal authority, attract waste and abuse. When bureaucrats control access to benefits with minimal verification, fraud inevitably follows.

For Colorado, where roughly 200,000 residents rely on ACA coverage, the question becomes whether legitimate enrollees will face disruptions while the fraud purge proceeds—and whether state officials will cooperate fully with federal audits.
