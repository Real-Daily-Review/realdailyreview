# Colorado Amendment 87 Backers Can't Get Their Story Straight—and Voters Should Notice

Colorado voters face a credibility problem with Amendment 87: proponents are struggling to present the measure's true impact accurately, raising serious questions about what they're actually selling.

According to *Complete Colorado*, supporters of the tax-change amendment have deployed misleading claims that obscure the proposal's real scope and consequences. The outlet reports that Colorado media outlets have also painted an incomplete picture, failing to give voters the full financial and policy details needed for an informed decision on what amounts to a significant tax restructuring.

Conservatives argue that when amendment backers can't present their own measure honestly, taxpayers should be deeply skeptical. The pattern of incomplete disclosure suggests proponents know the unvarnished details would face stronger opposition—a classic tell in ballot-measure campaigns.

Colorado voters deserve transparency on how Amendment 87 would affect their wallets, state revenues, and business climate. The fact that supporters are relying on selective framing rather than straightforward numbers is itself a red flag.

Watch whether Colorado's major newsrooms will press for full disclosure of Amendment 87's fiscal impact before Election Day—or whether the incomplete coverage continues.
