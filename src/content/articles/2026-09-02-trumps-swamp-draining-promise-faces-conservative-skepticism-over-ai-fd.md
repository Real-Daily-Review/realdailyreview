# Trump's AI Ambitions and FDA Pick Spark Conservative Swamp-Draining Doubts

President Trump's second-term moves are drawing fire from his own right flank over alleged government cronyism and institutional capture.

The Daily Caller reports Trump is building what critics call an "AI empire" through government partnerships, raising questions about whether the administration is truly dismantling the revolving door between Washington and corporate interests—a centerpiece of his "drain the swamp" messaging.

Meanwhile, Trump's FDA nomination has drawn conservative scrutiny. The Washington Examiner's opinion section argues the pick contradicts his "Make America Healthy Again" platform, which promised to challenge industries profiting from the status quo and reform broken institutions. Instead, conservatives say, the nomination signals a return to establishment capture.

**The Colorado angle:** AI data-center expansion and FDA regulatory decisions directly affect Colorado's tech sector, agricultural interests, and pharmaceutical manufacturing. Coloradans who voted for Trump expecting genuine institutional reform—not corporate-friendly cronyism dressed in populist rhetoric—are watching closely.

**What's next:** Monitor whether Trump's cabinet appointments and executive orders on AI regulation and FDA reform actually challenge entrenched interests or simply shuffle the same players into new positions.
