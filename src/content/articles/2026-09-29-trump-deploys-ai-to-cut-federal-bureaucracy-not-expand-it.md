# Trump Launches AI Chatbot to Cut Through Federal Bureaucracy

The Trump administration is using technology to do what Congress won't: simplify government. On Tuesday, President Trump unveiled America.gov, an AI-powered chatbot designed to help citizens navigate federal services without drowning in red tape—a practical alternative to expanding the bureaucracy itself.

"We do not surrender to fears about what technologies will do to us," Trump said, framing the tool as a confidence play in American innovation rather than regulatory caution (Just The News). The chatbot allows users to research public services and access information more intuitively than the current labyrinth of government websites.

For Colorado taxpayers and small-business owners, the pitch is straightforward: fewer hours wasted hunting for permit requirements, benefit eligibility, or compliance rules. Rather than hiring more federal staff to answer phones or process inquiries, the administration is deploying AI to compress the friction between citizens and the services they've already paid for.

The move reflects a conservative instinct—use technology to shrink the footprint of government rather than expand it. No new agencies. No new hires. Just smarter tools serving existing functions.

Conservatives should watch whether this model extends to other agencies, particularly those regulating energy development, water rights, and land use—areas where Colorado businesses face Byzantine approval processes that could benefit from streamlined access to rules and timelines.
