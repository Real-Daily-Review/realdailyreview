# Trump Treasury Blocks Billions in Fraudulent Payments—Including to Dead People

The Trump administration's Treasury Department is cracking down on federal fraud with real results. The "Do Not Pay" program screened over 1.1 billion payments worth $3.7 trillion and caught 13,500 improper payouts to deceased individuals, according to Fox News and the Daily Caller.

This is exactly the kind of government accountability conservatives have demanded for years. Taxpayer money flowing to dead people is the poster child for wasteful bureaucracy—and it's preventable. The Treasury's approach of stopping fraud "before money goes out the door" (Daily Caller) represents basic fiscal discipline that should have been standard practice long ago.

The scale is staggering: $3.7 trillion screened means the federal government finally has visibility into its own payment systems. Colorado taxpayers, already burdened by state and federal taxes, deserve to know their money isn't being siphoned into phantom accounts.

Republicans have long argued that government waste and fraud are features of bloated federal agencies, not bugs. This program proves that when you actually *look* for the problem, you find it—and you can stop it. The question now is whether this vigilance extends to other forms of improper federal spending and whether similar safeguards get applied across all government programs.

Watch whether Congress demands this level of fraud prevention become permanent policy across all federal agencies.
