# Breaking Bad Actor RJ Mitte Dies at 34

A Colorado connection to Hollywood: RJ Mitte, the 34-year-old actor best known for playing Walter White Jr. in AMC's "Breaking Bad," has died. According to reports from 9News Denver and Just The News, Mitte was found at his home in Brownsville, Texas.

Beyond his role in the acclaimed crime drama, Mitte was recognized as a disability rights activist—he lived with cerebral palsy and used his platform to advocate for representation in entertainment. His representative confirmed the death, though specific details about the cause remain limited in initial reporting.

Mitte's career extended beyond "Breaking Bad," which concluded in 2013 after five seasons. The show became a cultural phenomenon and launched the careers of several cast members. His work as an advocate brought attention to disability inclusion in Hollywood, an issue that has gained traction in recent years as the entertainment industry faces pressure to diversify casting and storytelling.

The loss marks another chapter in the legacy of one of television's most acclaimed dramas, which remains widely watched and discussed more than a decade after its finale.

Watch for additional details from his family and representatives in coming days.
