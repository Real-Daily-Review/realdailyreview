# Criminal Arson, Not Climate Hysteria, Behind Major Wildfire Outbreak

A 37-year-old Arizona man with a violent criminal history—including a manslaughter conviction for killing his own father—has been arrested for arson in connection with the largest of three wildfires currently burning in Washington State, according to 9News Denver and Just The News. Aaron Farinacci was identified after a tip led police to a man acting suspiciously near grass along a roadside.

The arrest undercuts the progressive narrative that climate change drives wildfire seasons. In France this summer, authorities arrested nearly 400 people in connection with devastating wildfires, Breitbart reports, suggesting human criminal conduct—not atmospheric conditions—bears primary responsibility for major fire outbreaks.

For Colorado, the lesson is clear: while the state faces genuine wildfire risk from drought and forest management failures, blaming "climate change" distracts from the real culprit—criminal arson and negligence. Colorado taxpayers and property owners should demand that law enforcement prioritize prosecution of arsonists and that land managers focus on forest thinning and defensible space rather than chasing climate-policy panaceas that do nothing to stop a man with a lighter.
