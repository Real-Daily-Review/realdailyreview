## Colorado Takes a Step Toward Separating State from Party

A new push in Colorado argues the state government has no business granting special recognition or privileges to political parties — and conservatives should be paying close attention. Complete Colorado calls the move "courageous," and they're right: taxpayer-funded machinery should not be propping up party infrastructure on either side. For Colorado conservatives who've watched the state's Democratic supermajority use every lever of government to entrench its own power, this is a reform worth watching and supporting.

## Arapahoe County Investigates Senior Living Facility

Arapahoe County officials are investigating All Saints Home Care and asking former residents to come forward. Details are still emerging, but when a government-licensed care facility draws a formal investigation, the questions are always the same: Who was supposed to be watching, and why weren't they? Colorado families who trusted this facility with their most vulnerable loved ones deserve fast, transparent answers — not bureaucratic foot-dragging.

## Boulder Reservoir Swim Beach Closed Over Algae Bloom

The swim beach at Boulder Reservoir is temporarily shut down due to a potentially harmful algae bloom, with officials asking visitors to keep dogs out of the water as well. Algae blooms are a recurring headache along Colorado's Front Range waterways. Coloradans should keep an eye on whether local water managers are being straight about the scope and duration of the closure — and whether the usual suspects use it as a pretext to push new regulatory schemes on agriculture and ranching upstream.

## NYC's Mamdani Threatens to Arrest Israeli PM Netanyahu

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani says his administration is exploring whether the city can arrest Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu over an International Criminal Court warrant during the UN General Assembly. This is radical-left grandstanding at its most dangerous. The ICC has no jurisdiction over U.S. soil, and a sitting mayor threatening to detain the leader of America's closest Middle East ally is a direct assault on U.S. foreign policy. Colorado's congressional delegation should be on record condemning this immediately.

## Hawaii Judge Attacks SCOTUS, Vows Defiance

A Hawaii state Supreme Court justice publicly blasted the U.S. Supreme Court's conservative majority, accusing it of reviving discredited historical rulings and announcing Hawaii's intent to defy the high court. Republicans and conservatives have a clear answer: this is nullification, plain and simple — the same doctrine the left spent 150 years condemning when it suited them. If a state can simply opt out of Supreme Court rulings it dislikes, the rule of law is finished. Colorado's own progressive legal community will be watching Hawaii's playbook closely.
