# Hickenlooper Dodges Answers on TB Crisis at Aurora ICE Detention Center

Sen. John Hickenlooper refused to answer questions about tuberculosis cases at the GEO Group-operated Aurora ICE detention facility during a recent visit, even as state health officials imposed a deadline for the private contractor to hand over investigation records (Colorado Sun, CPR News).

The standoff highlights a familiar pattern: when government contracts go wrong, politicians dodge accountability. The detention center, which houses immigrants awaiting deportation proceedings, has faced a public health investigation into TB transmission. Rather than demand transparency, Hickenlooper—a Democrat who once championed "responsible" governance—clammed up.

Republicans and conservatives argue this exemplifies why private contractors operating government facilities need real oversight, not political theater. If conditions are safe, release the data. If they're not, fix them and explain why taxpayers should trust the arrangement. Instead, Coloradans get silence from their senator and finger-pointing between ICE and GEO Group.

The Colorado angle cuts deeper: Aurora residents deserve to know if a major detention facility in their backyard poses a public health risk. That's not partisan—it's basic accountability.

Watch whether state health officials actually enforce their deadline or whether political pressure causes the investigation to quietly fade.
