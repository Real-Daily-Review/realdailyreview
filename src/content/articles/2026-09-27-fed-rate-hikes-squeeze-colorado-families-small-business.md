# Federal Reserve Rate Hikes Squeeze Colorado Families and Small Business

The Federal Reserve's aggressive interest-rate campaign is hitting Colorado working families and small businesses where it hurts most: the wallet.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, higher rates have made mortgage payments harder to afford, car financing more expensive, and business borrowing costlier for Colorado entrepreneurs trying to invest and hire. The pain is real for families stretched thin on housing costs in Colorado's competitive real estate markets and for small-business owners already battling labor shortages and supply-chain headwinds.

Conservatives argue the Fed is swinging too hard at inflation without accounting for the collateral damage to Main Street. While price stability matters, critics say the central bank should weigh whether rate hikes are the blunt instrument this economy needs—especially when working families are already struggling with the cost of living.

The Fed faces a genuine dilemma: tame inflation without crushing growth. But Colorado taxpayers and business owners shouldn't bear the full cost of Washington's monetary policy miscalculations.

Watch whether Colorado's business community and Republican lawmakers push back on further rate hikes, or whether the Fed pauses to assess the damage already done.
