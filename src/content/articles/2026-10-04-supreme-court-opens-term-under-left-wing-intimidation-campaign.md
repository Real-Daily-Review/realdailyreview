# Supreme Court Faces Intimidation Campaign as New Term Opens with Major Cases

The Supreme Court is opening its new term under siege—facing record-low approval ratings, coordinated intimidation efforts, and a coordinated left-wing campaign to delegitimize the institution itself. Justice Samuel Alito warned this week that a "concerted" effort to pressure justices threatens the Court's independence and future (Washington Examiner).

The new term brings high-stakes cases on gun rights, voter citizenship, and religious liberty—issues that directly affect Colorado conservatives and Second Amendment advocates. Republicans argue the Court must rule on these cases based on constitutional text and original meaning, not political pressure or mob intimidation.

Democrats and progressive activists have escalated attacks on the Court since the Dobbs decision overturned Roe v. Wade, with some calling for Court-packing, term limits, and justice removal. The left claims the Court has lost legitimacy by ruling against progressive preferences (Fox News).

Conservatives counter that an independent judiciary—insulated from public pressure campaigns—is essential to the rule of law. When justices rule based on the Constitution rather than what activists demand, that's not a legitimacy crisis; it's the system working as designed.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado's Democratic delegation and state officials join calls to pack or restructure the Court, and how Colorado voters respond to threats against judicial independence.
