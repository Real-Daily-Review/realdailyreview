# Michigan Democrat's Far-Left Primary Win Signals Radical Shift in Party

Michigan Democrats just nominated Abdul el Sayed for Senate—a DSA-aligned activist who wants to abolish institutions and claims "capitalism is the thief of sex"—and Republicans are already making him the face of the party's lurch toward socialism.

El Sayed defeated Rep. Haley Stevens in Wednesday's primary, emerging as the Democratic nominee in a crucial swing state. According to the *Washington Examiner*, the GOP wasted no time: campaigns, party committees, super PACs, and allied organizations launched coordinated television ads and messaging to portray him as representative of the Democratic Party's radical shift leftward.

National Review notes el Sayed does not disclaim the Democratic Socialists of America's politics and maintains a lengthy list of institutions he'd like abolished. The *Daily Wire* reported that on his 2024 podcast, el Sayed argued capitalism negatively affects relationships and sexual health—rhetoric that underscores how far mainstream Democratic candidates have drifted.

President Trump attacked el Sayed during a Wednesday Las Vegas speech, calling him "full of s***" and repeating previous claims that el Sayed "hates Jews," per the *Washington Examiner*.

**The Colorado angle:** If a radical socialist can win a Michigan Democratic primary, Colorado Democrats—already governing from the left on energy, taxes, and gun control—may feel emboldened to nominate further-left candidates in 2026 state races.
