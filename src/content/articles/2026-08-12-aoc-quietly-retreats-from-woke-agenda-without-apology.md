# Progressive Darling AOC Quietly Distances Herself From Woke Agenda She Championed

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, the face of the Democratic Party's progressive wing, appears to be abandoning the very agenda she spent years promoting—without offering Colorado taxpayers or the nation a straight answer about what went wrong.

According to *National Review*, AOC and her allies have begun distancing themselves from policies they once championed, yet they've offered little more than an implicit "oops" to Americans who suffered the consequences. *The Daily Caller* notes the awkward silence: if progressives now recognize errors in their approach, what exactly was wrong with the policies they pushed so aggressively?

For Colorado, this matters. The progressive agenda AOC championed—from aggressive climate mandates to regulatory overreach—has directly shaped state policy under Democratic leadership. Colorado taxpayers funded experiments in green energy mandates, housing regulations, and expanded government programs that progressives now seem to be quietly retreating from.

The conservative argument is straightforward: if progressive policies were misguided enough to abandon, they were misguided enough to have opposed in the first place. Republicans warned about the costs and unintended consequences; progressives dismissed those warnings as obstruction.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats will acknowledge their own role in advancing failed progressive policies—or whether they'll simply rebrand and move forward without accountability.**
