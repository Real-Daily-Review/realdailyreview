# Police Pursuit Ends in Crash on Highway 7 After Denver Kidnapping

A high-speed police chase in the Denver area Friday morning ended in a serious crash on Colorado State Highway 7, leaving a 17-year-old victim critically injured, according to authorities.

The pursuit began after a suspect allegedly carjacked and kidnapped the victim in Denver. Law enforcement initiated the chase, which ultimately resulted in a collision that closed Highway 7 and sent the young victim to the hospital with serious injuries (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

The incident underscores ongoing public safety challenges in the Denver metro area, where carjackings and violent crime have drawn scrutiny from residents and law enforcement alike. While police pursued the suspect aggressively to apprehend the perpetrator, the crash highlights the dangerous calculus officers face during high-speed chases in populated areas—balancing the need to stop criminals against risks to innocent bystanders and victims.

Details on the suspect's identity, charges, and the victim's current condition remain limited. The Colorado State Patrol and Denver Police Department are investigating the circumstances surrounding both the kidnapping and the pursuit.

Coloradans should monitor whether prosecutors pursue felony charges and what this case reveals about crime trends in the Denver corridor.
