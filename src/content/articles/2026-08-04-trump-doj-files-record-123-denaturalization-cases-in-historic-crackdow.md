# Trump DOJ Targets Naturalized Citizens in Record Denaturalization Push

The Trump Justice Department has filed 123 civil denaturalization complaints since taking office—the largest such effort in recorded history—signaling a sharp pivot toward stripping citizenship from naturalized Americans accused of fraud or serious crimes.

The DOJ filed complaints against 25 naturalized citizens from 17 countries in just two weeks, alleging offenses ranging from attempted murder to marriage fraud (Fox News). Officials declared the campaign is "only the beginning," suggesting a sustained enforcement agenda ahead.

Conservatives argue denaturalization enforcement targets a genuine problem: citizenship granted to individuals who obtained it through deception or concealment of criminal histories. Republicans view this as restoring integrity to the naturalization process and protecting American citizens from those who gamed the system.

Democrats and immigration advocates counter that mass denaturalization efforts risk due-process violations and create chilling effects on immigrant communities, though no Colorado-specific impact has been reported yet.

**Colorado angle:** No Colorado cases have been identified in the initial filings, but the policy could affect naturalized citizens statewide who face DOJ scrutiny.
