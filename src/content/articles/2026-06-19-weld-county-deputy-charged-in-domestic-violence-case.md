# Weld County Deputy Charged in Domestic Violence Case

A Weld County sheriff's deputy faces serious charges—including assault and stalking—stemming from a domestic violence incident in May, according to reporting from KDVR Fox31 Denver and 9News Denver.

Deputy Alex Porter has been placed on unpaid administrative leave pending investigation, the sheriff's office confirmed. The charges underscore a persistent challenge for law enforcement agencies: accountability when officers themselves cross the line.

Colorado conservatives and taxpayers have legitimate reason to scrutinize how local law enforcement handles internal misconduct. While most deputies serve honorably, cases like this demand swift, transparent investigation and appropriate consequences—not cover-ups or union-protected delays that erode public trust in institutions that depend on it.

The Weld County Sheriff's Office has moved to suspend Porter without pay, a step that signals the agency is taking the allegations seriously rather than circling wagons. That's the standard Coloradans should expect: zero tolerance for officers who abuse their badge and the public's trust.

Watch whether the sheriff's office follows through with full transparency on findings and whether charges stick—or whether bureaucratic delays and legal maneuvering allow accountability to slip away.
