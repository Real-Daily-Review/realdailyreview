# DSA's Constitutional Assault Signals Danger for Colorado Conservatives

The Democratic Socialists of America are abandoning rhetorical restraint and moving openly against constitutional limits on government power—a shift Colorado Republicans should take seriously as DSA-aligned activists gain influence in Congress and city halls nationwide.

According to the Washington Examiner, the DSA is "tactically retreating" from its most extreme rhetoric while doubling down on attacks against constitutional constraints. With DSA figures now heading to Congress and allies like Zohran Mamdani winning mayoralties in major cities, the group is positioning itself closer to Democratic mainstream politics than ever before.

The threat extends beyond ideology. DSA's constitutional assault targets the very framework that protects property rights, limits regulatory overreach, and preserves individual liberty—core concerns for Colorado energy workers, ranchers, and small-business owners already battling state-level progressive regulations.

National Review notes that a colorblind Constitution remains essential to conservative jurisprudence, yet the left increasingly rejects constitutional originalism in favor of malleable interpretations that justify expansive government power. This matters in Colorado, where progressive majorities in Denver and Boulder already push race-based policies and regulatory schemes that conservatives argue violate equal protection principles.

Watch for DSA-backed candidates and ballot measures targeting Colorado's 2026 elections—particularly those framing constitutional limits as obstacles to "equity" and "climate justice."
