# Democrats Flip-Flop on Trans Sports as GOP Readies Election Weapon

Colorado Democrats are watching their national party scramble to reposition on transgender athletes—a vulnerability Republicans say exposes years of progressive overreach on a kitchen-table issue that resonates across swing districts.

South Carolina Democratic Senate nominee Annie Andrews now opposes biological males competing in women's sports, reversing her 2022 stance (Fox News). Republicans argue this represents transparent flip-flopping ahead of November, not genuine conviction. The shift signals Democrats recognize the political liability of their permissive transgender policies, particularly in competitive races.

For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats have championed expansive transgender policies in schools and sports, positions that clash with voters in rural counties and suburban Denver districts where Republicans are competitive. The national party's retreat suggests Democratic operatives see these issues as electoral anchors.

Republicans argue Democrats are simply trying to have it both ways—maintaining progressive activist support while appearing moderate to swing voters. Conservatives counter that the real issue is Democratic unwillingness to defend their own policies on the merits, instead adopting convenient positions when polls tighten.

The Washington Examiner notes Republicans possess significant messaging ammunition heading into midterms, though some GOP candidates remain hesitant to deploy it aggressively.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt similar rhetorical repositioning in state races, or double down on their existing transgender-friendly record.
