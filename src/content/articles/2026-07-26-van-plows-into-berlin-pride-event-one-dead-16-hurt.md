# Berlin Pride Event Struck by Vehicle; One Dead, 16 Injured

A van plowed into crowds at Berlin's Christopher Street Day LGBT pride parade Saturday evening, killing at least one person and injuring 16 others, according to the Washington Examiner and Breitbart. Police shut down the event hours after it began and evacuated attendees from the scene.

The incident occurred around 10 p.m. local time in Germany's capital. Authorities have not yet identified the driver or disclosed a motive, though initial reports suggest the vehicle was driven intentionally into the gathering. Just The News reported that police ordered all attendees to leave the area immediately following the attack.

Details remain sparse as German law enforcement investigates. The identity and background of the motorist—and whether this was a deliberate act or accident—have not been confirmed by authorities.

This marks another vehicle-ramming incident at a public gathering in Europe, raising questions about event security and crowd protection measures at large outdoor celebrations.
