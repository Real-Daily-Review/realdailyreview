# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Trial Over Protest Shooting; Prop 137 Land Grab Looms

A Colorado judge has ruled sufficient evidence exists to prosecute Brandon Booth, an ex-ICE detention center guard accused of shooting toward female protesters who confronted him about "race traitors" and threatened to locate guards' children, according to the *Colorado Sun*. The case underscores tensions around immigration enforcement facilities and protest activity in the state.

Meanwhile, Colorado voters face Proposition 137 this fall—a land conservation measure that conservatives argue amounts to a taxpayer-funded subsidy for wealthy environmental nonprofits. *Complete Colorado* reports the initiative's primary sponsor, The Nature Conservancy, would benefit directly from the measure, raising red flags about who really profits from conservation spending.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue Prop 137 represents another example of government funneling tax dollars to activist organizations under the guise of environmental stewardship. Rather than genuine conservation, they say it's a backdoor wealth transfer to NGOs with progressive agendas and deep pockets.

The Booth case highlights the volatile intersection of immigration policy and protest culture in Colorado; Prop 137 tests whether voters will approve yet another spending measure benefiting the environmental left.

Watch whether conservative groups mount an effective campaign against Prop 137 before November's election.
