# Oklahoma GOP Primary: Trump-Backed Mazzei Edges Out Rival in Tight Governor's Race

Trump's endorsement still carries weight in Republican primaries—Mike Mazzei's narrow victory over Oklahoma Attorney General Gentner Drummond in Tuesday's GOP gubernatorial runoff proves it. Mazzei, the former Oklahoma Budget Secretary, won with 50.3% of the vote, edging Drummond's 49.7% in what the Washington Examiner called a decisive Trump-backed win.

The race underscores ongoing tensions within GOP primary contests between establishment and Trump-aligned candidates. Drummond, as sitting Attorney General, represented the state's institutional Republican apparatus; Mazzei's Trump endorsement proved the decisive edge in a dead-heat contest.

On the Democratic side, newcomer N'Kiyla Jasmine Thomas defeated Jim Priest in her party's Senate runoff, vowing to impeach Trump if elected—a signal of how polarized 2024 races remain (Fox News Politics).

**The Colorado angle:** While Oklahoma's races don't directly impact Colorado voters, they reflect national GOP dynamics that shape how Colorado Republicans position themselves heading into 2024. Colorado conservatives should watch whether Mazzei's Trump-dependent strategy resonates in general-election swing states, or whether Drummond's more traditional conservative approach gains ground in purple districts.
