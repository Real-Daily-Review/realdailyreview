# News Brief

**New York Police Stop Times Square Stabbing Spree With Lethal Force—Raising Questions About Gun-Free Zones**

Police in New York City fatally shot a knife-wielding woman Monday after she allegedly stabbed two people near Times Square, killing one victim, according to the Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire. The attack occurred around 4:30 p.m. near West 42nd Street and Seventh Avenue; the suspect, identified as 49-year-old Pamela Cisneros, was described as emotionally disturbed. One victim died at the hospital; the other is in stable condition.

NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani praised police for stopping the attack.
