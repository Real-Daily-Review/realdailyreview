# U.S. Adds 115,000 Jobs in April, Beating Forecasts but Signaling Mixed Economic Health

The U.S. labor market added 115,000 jobs in April, significantly outpacing the Dow Jones consensus forecast of 55,000, according to the Labor Department, as reported by PBS NewsHour. Unemployment held steady in April, the report showed.

The stronger-than-expected headline figure masks underlying concerns about economic momentum. CNBC noted that the jobs report contained "several red flags for the economy," suggesting weakness beneath the surface gains.
