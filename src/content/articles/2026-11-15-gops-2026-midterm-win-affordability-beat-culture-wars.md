# Colorado Conservatives Win Midterms on Kitchen-Table Issues, Not Culture Wars

Republicans dominated the 2026 midterms by hammering affordability—food prices, housing costs, and family finances—rather than getting bogged down in secondary cultural battles, according to analysis in the *Washington Examiner*. The winning message wasn't abortion or climate policy; it was whether ordinary Americans could afford to eat, pay rent, and provide for their families.

For Colorado, this signals that GOP candidates and conservative messaging should stay laser-focused on the state's cost-of-living crisis: grocery inflation, skyrocketing property taxes, and energy costs that progressive policies have made worse. Coloradans are tired of ideological posturing. They want relief at the pump and the grocery store.

The lesson for state Republicans: don't let Democrats drag the conversation into abortion or climate hysteria. Keep it on kitchen-table economics where conservatives have the stronger argument. Colorado's energy workers, small-business owners, and middle-class families are hurting under Biden-era inflation and state-level tax-and-spend policies—that's the winning terrain.

Watch whether Colorado GOP candidates in 2026 state races (governor, legislature, congressional seats) actually discipline their messaging around affordability or slip back into culture-war talking points that let Democrats change the subject.
