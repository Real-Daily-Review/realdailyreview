# Democratic Defection: Michigan Rep. Stevens Abandons Party Over Anti-Israel Stance

A sitting Democratic congresswoman has crossed the aisle to endorse her Republican opponent, signaling deep fractures within the left over antisemitism and foreign policy radicalism.

Rep. Haley Stevens (D-Mich.) broke ranks to support her GOP challenger, citing what National Review describes as Abdul El-Sayed's "hatred of Israel" as her breaking point. Stevens, a former Democratic Senate candidate, has become the public face of Democratic anxiety over the party's drift toward anti-Israel activism—a concern that extends well beyond Michigan.

National Review reports that Stevens is "not alone," with numerous Democrats harboring "serious reservations" about El-Sayed's positions. The defection drew comparison to Liz Cheney's endorsement of Kamala Harris, though Stevens's move cuts the opposite direction: away from the Democratic Party entirely.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans see this as vindication of their warnings about progressive antisemitism and the left's hostility toward America's closest Middle Eastern ally. Democrats counter that Stevens represents an outlier, though the breadth of internal Democratic concern suggests otherwise.
