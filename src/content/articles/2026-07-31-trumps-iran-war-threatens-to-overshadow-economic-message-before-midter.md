# Trump's Iran Conflict Threatens to Overshadow Economic Agenda Ahead of Midterms

President Trump is doubling down on military action against Iran even as the five-month conflict threatens to derail his core economic message before November's midterm elections. Trump vowed the U.S. will "win" in Iran, predicting Iranian resistance will "peter out" as American strikes continue—yet acknowledged the fighting could stretch well beyond the election cycle (Fox News).

The escalating conflict poses a political liability. National Review warns that Trump's focus on the Iran war, combined with his tariff agenda, has cost him his signature issue: economic stewardship. Conservatives worry the prolonged military engagement will consume White House bandwidth and media oxygen needed to highlight tax cuts, deregulation, and job growth—the economic wins that energize Republican voters.

For Colorado, prolonged Middle East tensions could spike energy prices and disrupt oil and gas markets just as the state's energy sector seeks regulatory relief. Defense contractors and military families across Colorado may see increased deployment and uncertainty.

Republicans argue Trump must refocus on the economy and deliver tangible wins on inflation and growth before voters head to the polls; the Iran conflict risks becoming a distraction that plays into Democratic messaging about endless wars.

Watch whether Trump pivots messaging in coming weeks to emphasize economic achievements or whether Iran dominance continues consuming the political calendar.
