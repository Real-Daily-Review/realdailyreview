# Biden DOJ's School Board Crackdown Exposed as Partisan Targeting, Internal Memos Show

The Biden Justice Department weaponized federal law enforcement against parents opposing school board policies, according to internal documents released by the Trump administration's Weaponization Working Group. Merrick Garland's 2021 memo directing the FBI to investigate school board threats sparked alarm among DOJ officials themselves, with one calling it an "Anti-MAGA Task Force" (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue the initiative was a pretext to intimidate parents exercising First Amendment rights at public meetings. Rather than addressing genuine threats, the DOJ cast a wide net over ordinary citizens opposing progressive curriculum and COVID policies—chilling free speech and weaponizing law enforcement for political ends.

**Democrats' position:** The left maintains the memo addressed legitimate safety concerns after school boards reported escalating threats and harassment.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado parents have clashed repeatedly with school boards over curriculum, mask mandates, and transgender policies. If federal law enforcement was indeed targeting conservative parents nationwide, Colorado families attending board meetings faced the same chilling effect.

The documents reveal the kind of government overreach conservatives warned about: federal agencies deployed against citizens for political speech. Taxpayers should watch whether Congress holds Garland accountable and whether similar targeting continues under current leadership.
