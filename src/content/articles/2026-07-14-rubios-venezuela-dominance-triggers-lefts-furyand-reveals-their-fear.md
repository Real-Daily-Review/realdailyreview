# Marco Rubio's Venezuela Dominance Draws Left's Fire—and Shows Why They Fear Him

Secretary of State Marco Rubio's aggressive stance on Venezuela has made him public enemy number one for the American left, which recognizes in him a genuine ideological opponent willing to use executive power against socialist regimes.

According to *The Daily Wire*, Rubio has assumed an outsized role in shaping U.S. Venezuela policy from Washington, overseeing sanctions architecture, managing the country's oil revenue flows, and maintaining direct contact with Venezuela's opposition leadership. *National Review* reports that progressive activists have identified the State Department as the critical chokepoint for their anti-American foreign policy agenda—and Rubio as the man blocking it.

The left's counterargument, implicit in their targeting of Rubio, centers on claims that his Venezuela policy is too hawkish and destabilizes the region. Democrats typically argue for diplomatic engagement over sanctions-heavy approaches.

**Colorado angle:** While Venezuela policy may seem distant, Colorado's energy sector and broader economic interests benefit from U.S. strength against socialist regimes that undermine market stability and property rights—principles conservatives defend at home and abroad.

Conservatives should watch whether the left escalates attacks on Rubio's confirmation or whether his Venezuela record becomes a template for how Republicans will handle other hostile regimes under the next administration.
