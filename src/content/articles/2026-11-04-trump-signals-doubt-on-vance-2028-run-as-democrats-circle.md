# Trump Signals Uncertainty Over Vance's 2028 Plans as Democrats Eye White House

Trump's grip on his own ticket is already loosening. Hours after Democrats flipped both chambers of Congress in the midterms, the president told reporters he's unsure whether Vice President JD Vance will mount a 2028 presidential bid—a stunning admission that undercuts party unity heading into the next cycle.

The uncertainty surfaced after Vance posted a lengthy social media statement distancing himself from potential Iran military action, signaling independence from Trump's foreign policy line. Trump acknowledged speaking with Vance but offered no clarity on the VP's future ambitions (Washington Examiner).

Meanwhile, Trump warned that Democrats are fielding "very formidable, talented people" for 2028, though he refused to name them publicly—claiming he didn't want to boost their profiles (The Daily Wire). The comment reveals Trump's concern about the Democratic bench, even as his own administration faces internal questions about loyalty and succession.
