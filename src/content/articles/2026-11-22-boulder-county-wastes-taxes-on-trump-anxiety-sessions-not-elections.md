# Boulder County's Tax-Funded Trump Anxiety Session

Boulder County officials are spending taxpayer dollars on what amounts to group therapy for residents obsessed with Trump, according to *Complete Colorado*. The county's so-called "election champions" program has morphed into a forum for progressive activists to process their political anxieties rather than focus on election administration basics.

This is precisely the kind of government mission creep conservatives warn about. Boulder County residents are footing the bill for what should be neutral, professional election management—not ideological counseling sessions disguised as civic engagement.

Republicans argue that election offices should stick to their core job: running fair, secure elections accessible to all voters, regardless of party. Instead, Boulder County appears to be using public resources to reinforce partisan narratives and soothe progressive angst. That's not election administration; it's taxpayer-funded activism.

Colorado voters across the state should demand accountability from their county clerks and election officials. Elections work best when they're boring, professional, and above partisan theater. Boulder County's approach suggests the opposite is happening in one of Colorado's most left-leaning counties.

Watch whether other Colorado counties follow Boulder's lead—or whether Republican officials push back on this misuse of election office resources.
