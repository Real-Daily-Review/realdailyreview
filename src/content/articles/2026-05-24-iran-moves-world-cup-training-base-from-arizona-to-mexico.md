# Iran Relocates World Cup Training Base to Mexico

Iran's soccer federation has moved its World Cup training camp from Arizona to Tijuana, Mexico, with FIFA approval, according to the Iranian federation president announced Saturday.

The team had been scheduled to train in Tucson, Arizona, but will now establish its base camp in Mexico just south of San Diego, according to CBS News. The Iranian federation received clearance from FIFA to make the switch, PBS NewsHour reported.

Iran was expected to fly from Turkey to Arizona to continue World Cup 2026 preparations before the relocation decision, Al Jazeera reported.

The move comes amid ongoing U.S.-Iran diplomatic discussions. Al Jazeera noted that experts have characterized the current period as involving "delicate diplomacy" between the two nations, with both sides indicating progress in talks.

The decision underscores the complex geopolitical dynamics surrounding Iran's participation in international sporting events held in or near the United States.
