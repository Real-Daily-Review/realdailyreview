# California's High-Speed Rail Disaster Should Warn Colorado Against Wasteful Government Megaprojects

California's high-speed rail boondoggle is burning billions with nothing to show for it—a cautionary tale Colorado policymakers must heed before embracing similar government-led infrastructure fantasies.

The project, championed by Gov. Gavin Newsom and progressive Democrats, has become a poster child for government waste. While California hemorrhages money on rail that remains incomplete, China has laid enough track to circle America three times, according to the Daily Caller. National Review notes the state is simultaneously pursuing aggressive tire regulations that exemplify progressive overreach—proving California's left-wing leadership can't even manage basic transportation infrastructure, let alone regulate it effectively.

Republicans argue that California's failure demonstrates why government should stay out of massive infrastructure bets. Free markets and private investment, conservatives contend, deliver results far more efficiently than bureaucrat-managed megaprojects. The money poured into California's rail could have funded dozens of practical state priorities—schools, roads, water systems—instead of vanishing into a progressive fantasy.

Colorado must resist similar temptations. State Democrats have occasionally floated rail and transit schemes that would drain taxpayer dollars into unproven ventures. The lesson is clear: government-run megaprojects fail; private enterprise succeeds.

Watch for any Colorado Democrat proposals for major government-funded transit or rail projects—and demand a hard accounting of California's failures before a single Colorado tax dollar gets committed.
