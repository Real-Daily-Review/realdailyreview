# Federal Investigation Into Rep. Omar's Immigration Paperwork Deepens as Campaign Capitalizes on Raid

Minnesota Democrat Ilhan Omar's campaign is fundraising off a federal criminal investigation into her immigration history, even as Homeland Security Investigations agents raided her Washington, D.C. home (Fox News). Tim Mynett, Omar's embattled husband, was spotted at the scene as the probe into her naturalization paperwork intensifies.

Omar's campaign immediately weaponized the raid for donations, sending supporters a "Stand with Ilhan Omar" email framing the investigation as "baseless" and "partisan" (The Daily Wire). The move exemplifies how progressives reflexively blame Republicans for law enforcement actions, regardless of merit.

The investigation centers on Omar's immigration and citizenship documentation—serious federal matters that demand scrutiny regardless of political affiliation. National Review notes the complexity: legitimate questions exist about both the investigation's origins and Omar's conduct, making this a case where principled conservatives should demand facts, not tribal loyalty.

Colorado voters should note how Democrats immediately cry "witch hunt" when federal agencies investigate their own, yet demand aggressive prosecution of political opponents. The double standard corrodes public trust in institutions.

Watch whether the DOJ produces concrete evidence or whether this becomes another politicized spectacle that undermines confidence in federal law enforcement.
