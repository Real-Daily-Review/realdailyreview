# Press Release Distributor Blocks 9/11 Jihadist Victims Campaign as 'Inflammatory'

A major press release service has rejected an announcement for a campaign honoring victims of jihadist terrorism ahead of the 25th anniversary of September 11, citing concerns the initiative could be deemed "inflammatory" toward Muslims (The Daily Wire).

PR Newswire, the communications firm handling the distribution, warned organizers that the campaign's messaging risked being perceived as prejudicial. The decision reflects the broader progressive tendency to prioritize sensitivity language over direct acknowledgment of terrorism's ideological roots—a pattern conservatives argue obscures the nature of the threat Americans face.

Republicans contend that commemorating 9/11 victims and naming the specific ideology behind the attacks is neither inflammatory nor bigoted, but rather honest reckoning with history. Conservatives say the decision exemplifies corporate self-censorship and the chilling effect of progressive orthodoxy on public discourse, particularly around national security and terrorism.

The incident underscores ongoing tension between free speech and corporate gatekeeping of political messaging. For Coloradans concerned about national security and unfiltered public debate, this raises questions about who controls the narrative around America's most defining tragedy.
