# Trump Blasts Cornyn Over Federal Judge Blockade; Warns of 'Radical Left' Replacement

President Trump is publicly pressuring Sen. John Cornyn (R-Texas) to stop blocking DOJ lawyer Elliot Gaiser's nomination to a federal judgeship in Dallas, calling the hold a "tremendous disservice" (Fox News). Trump warned that without Cornyn's support, "we're going to have a radical left lunatic get in there" (New York Post).

The dispute highlights a recurring tension within Republican ranks over judicial confirmations. While Trump backs Gaiser's nomination, Cornyn—a senior GOP senator—has apparently raised concerns about the candidate, though his specific objections remain unclear from available reporting.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that federal judgeships are too important to block without transparent, substantive reasons. Allowing vacancies to persist risks activist judges filling the bench through Democratic appointments or interim rulings. Trump's position reflects the GOP priority of securing originalist, constitutionalist judges who will interpret law rather than legislate from the bench.

**Cornyn's position:** The senator has not publicly detailed his objections, but Senate holds are a legitimate procedural tool for vetting nominees.

For Colorado conservatives, this battle underscores why judicial appointments matter: federal courts shape energy policy, water rights, Second Amendment cases, and regulatory overreach affecting the state.

Watch whether Cornyn releases his specific concerns or if Trump's public pressure forces a resolution before the nomination stalls further.
