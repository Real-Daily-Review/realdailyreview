# Democratic Socialists Infiltrating Party Leadership as Establishment Dems Lose Control

House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries's public embrace of Democratic Socialists of America-backed nominees signals the left's complete capture of the Democratic Party—a warning sign for Colorado voters watching their state drift further left.

Jeffries congratulated DSA-endorsed candidates in New York, prompting sharp backlash from the Republican Jewish Coalition and conservative commentators. Byron York of the *Washington Examiner* argues that establishment Democrats are increasingly alarmed as socialists use the party "as a vehicle to get on to ballots, to get money, to get connections, to win" elected office. Bill Maher told JD Vance on air that Democratic socialism is pushing moderate voters away, telling the vice president his "vote is in play."

**The conservative case:** Republicans warn that the Democratic Party's leadership is openly welcoming avowed socialists into positions of power, abandoning any pretense of fiscal responsibility or free-market principles. This matters for Colorado, where progressive Democrats already control the statehouse and have pushed aggressive tax hikes, regulatory expansion, and anti-energy policies. If national Democrats continue normalizing socialism, Colorado's left-wing majority will likely accelerate similar agendas—from wealth taxes to further restrictions on oil and gas development.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado Democratic leaders distance themselves from or embrace the national party's socialist wing heading into 2026.
