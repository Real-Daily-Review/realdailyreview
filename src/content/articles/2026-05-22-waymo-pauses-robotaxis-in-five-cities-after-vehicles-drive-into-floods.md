# Waymo Pauses Robotaxis in Multiple Cities After Vehicles Drive Into Flooded Roads

Waymo has expanded a temporary suspension of its robotaxi service across five U.S. cities after autonomous vehicles repeatedly drove into flooded roads, according to BBC World. The pause now includes Atlanta and San Antonio, where the company is working to prevent its vehicles from navigating through standing water.

A Waymo spokesperson said the company made the decision "out of an abundance of caution," according to BBC World. The expanded suspension suggests the autonomous vehicle operator identified a systemic issue with how its vehicles handle weather-related hazards and road conditions.

TechCrunch reported that Waymo's robotaxi service is suspended in at least four cities as the company addresses the flooding problem. The exact scope of the pause—which cities are affected and how long the suspension will last—remains unclear from available statements.

The incidents highlight a key challenge for autonomous vehicle operators: programming vehicles to recognize and avoid hazardous conditions that human drivers navigate intuitively. Watch for Waymo's next update on what technical changes it implements before resuming service.
