# Democrats Push Electoral College Power Grab—Here's Why It Threatens Colorado

The left is openly plotting to neuter the Electoral College through the National Popular Vote Compact, a scheme that would strip flyover states of their constitutional influence and hand presidential elections to coastal population centers.

MSNBC's Rachel Maddow recently urged Democrats to "swing for the fences" by pressuring states with Democratic control to join the compact, which would award electoral votes to the national popular vote winner—effectively abolishing the Founders' system (New York Post). Complete Colorado notes the left "knows where its power centers reside," and this gambit proves it: Democrats want to make rural America irrelevant.

**Why Colorado should care:** The Electoral College ensures that Colorado's 10 electoral votes matter. Under a pure popular vote system, Denver and Boulder's preferences could drown out the concerns of Western Slope ranchers, energy workers, and rural communities. Colorado's swing-state status gives it outsized influence; the compact would erase that leverage overnight.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue the Electoral College protects federalism and prevents a handful of mega-cities from dictating national policy. It forces candidates to build geographically diverse coalitions—exactly what the Constitution intended.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats attempt to push the state toward joining the compact; any such move would signal they're willing to sacrifice Colorado's political power for partisan advantage.
