# Maine Democrat's Credibility Crisis Won't Stop His Senate Bid

Graham Platner's Democratic primary win in Maine this week proves the left will nominate anyone with a pulse if he checks the right ideological boxes—credibility optional.

The scandal-plagued Maine Democrat, who won his primary essentially unopposed on Tuesday, has built a campaign on empty promises while leaving voters with no reason to trust a word he says, according to *National Review*. The *Washington Examiner* goes further, calling Platner "the socialists' latest con man," comparing his shameless ascent to Caligula making his horse a senator.

Republicans argue Platner's nomination exposes how far the Democratic Party has drifted: they'll back a candidate with a compromised record and zero credibility rather than field someone with actual accomplishments. Conservatives say this is what happens when progressive ideology matters more than character or competence.

Platner now heads toward November's general election with momentum, despite his baggage. Democrats counter that he won the primary fair and square and represents their party's values.

**Colorado angle:** While this race is in Maine, it signals how national Democrats operate—and how Colorado's own Democratic establishment might nominate weak candidates if they align with the progressive agenda.
