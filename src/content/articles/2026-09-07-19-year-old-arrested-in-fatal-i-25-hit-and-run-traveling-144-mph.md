# Teen Arrested in Fatal I-25 Hit-and-Run; Speed Cited as Factor

A 19-year-old has been arrested in connection with a deadly hit-and-run crash on northbound I-25 near Orchard Road in Greenwood Village on August 30, according to police and court documents.

According to an affidavit cited by 9News Denver, the suspect was traveling at 144 mph before the crash. The high-speed incident left at least one person dead and raises serious questions about reckless driving enforcement on Colorado's highways.

The arrest marks a step toward accountability in a case that underscores the dangers of dangerous driving on metro Denver's busiest corridors. KDVR Fox31 Denver reported the arrest, while 9News Denver obtained details from the affidavit showing the extreme speed involved.

Colorado conservatives and law-and-order advocates have long emphasized the need for swift prosecution of violent crimes and reckless endangerment. This case illustrates why consistent enforcement and meaningful consequences matter—especially when negligence results in loss of life.

Taxpayers should watch whether prosecutors pursue maximum charges and whether the courts impose sentences that reflect the severity of fleeing a fatal accident.
