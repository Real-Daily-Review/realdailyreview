# ZZ Top Cancels Colorado Springs Show After Drummer Frank Beard's Death

Frank Beard, the legendary drummer who anchored ZZ Top's blues-rock sound for decades, has died at 77. The band has canceled its Wednesday concert at Pikes Peak Center in Colorado Springs, marking a significant loss for American rock music.

Beard was instrumental in defining ZZ Top's unmistakable groove and rhythm, helping drive the Texas trio through generations of hits. His death removes a foundational pillar from one of rock's most enduring acts.

The Colorado Springs show cancellation will disappoint fans who had tickets to the performance. ZZ Top has not announced whether remaining tour dates will proceed or be rescheduled.

Beard's passing underscores the toll time takes on classic rock acts that defined American popular music. His legacy remains embedded in the band's catalog—a reminder of an era when rock drummers were as essential to a band's identity as lead singers.

Watch for any announcements from ZZ Top regarding the future of their current tour and whether surviving members will continue performing.
