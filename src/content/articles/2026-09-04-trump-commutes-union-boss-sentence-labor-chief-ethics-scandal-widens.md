# Trump Commutes Union Official's Sentence; Cabinet Ethics Scandal Emerges

President Trump has commuted the sentence of former Philadelphia union official John Dougherty, according to his attorney George Bochetto (Fox News). The move marks another use of Trump's clemency power since taking office.

The commutation comes as a separate ethics scandal engulfs a former Trump Cabinet official. The Department of Labor Inspector General found that Lori Chavez-DeRemer, who served as Labor Secretary before resigning in April, maintained an "inappropriately close" relationship with her security detail (Fox News). According to the IG report, Chavez-DeRemer ordered a security agent to throw cash at a stripper during an unplanned, off-the-record stop at an Oregon club featuring partially nude dancers (Just the News).

**The conservative angle:** While Trump's clemency authority is constitutionally sound, Republicans must reckon with ethical lapses among appointees. Chavez-DeRemer's conduct—misusing taxpayer-funded security personnel for personal misconduct—represents exactly the kind of government excess conservatives claim to oppose. Colorado voters and taxpayers deserve Cabinet officials who respect both the law and public trust.

Conservatives should watch whether Trump's team implements stronger vetting and ethics protocols for future appointments, or whether such incidents become a pattern that undermines the administration's credibility on fiscal responsibility and good government.
