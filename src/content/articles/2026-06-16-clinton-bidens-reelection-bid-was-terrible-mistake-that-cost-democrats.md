# Hillary Clinton Blames Biden's Failed Reelection Bid for Democrats' 2024 Collapse

Hillary Clinton is now saying the quiet part out loud: Joe Biden's decision to run for reelection in 2024 was a "terrible mistake" that cost Democrats the White House. Speaking to New Yorker editor David Remnick, Clinton argued that a competitive Democratic primary could have produced a stronger nominee—implying she'd have preferred the party ditch Biden earlier rather than let him drag down the ticket (Daily Caller, New York Post).

The irony is sharp. Clinton, who lost to Donald Trump in 2016, backed Biden's reelection bid. Now she's essentially admitting Democrats' strategy backfired spectacularly. The Daily Wire notes Clinton expressed regret for her endorsement, suggesting an open primary would have given Democrats a better shot at retaining the presidency.

For Colorado conservatives, this infighting among Democrats underscores a broader truth: the left's institutional failures—from inflation to border chaos to regulatory overreach—weren't hidden. Voters saw them clearly. Clinton's hindsight confession won't undo the damage Biden's policies inflicted on Colorado families' wallets, energy jobs, or quality of life.
