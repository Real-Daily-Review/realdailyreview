# Colorado High Court Blocks Democrat Gerrymandering Scheme

Colorado's Supreme Court has shut down a Democratic redistricting plan, keeping the state out of the national gerrymandering wars—at least for now.

The ruling, reported by *Complete Colorado*, represents a win for those opposing partisan map-drawing. Democrats had pushed a redistricting scheme that conservatives and good-government advocates argued would entrench partisan advantage through manipulated district lines.

Republicans have long warned that unchecked gerrymandering—whether by Democrats or Republicans—undermines representative democracy and voter choice. The Colorado court's decision to block the Democratic plan reflects judicial skepticism of naked partisan line-drawing, a principle that should apply equally regardless of which party holds the pen.

The ruling keeps Colorado's congressional and legislative maps from becoming a tool of Democratic electoral engineering, preserving competitive districts and voter representation. However, redistricting battles remain fluid; Democrats may attempt alternative approaches before the next cycle.

Colorado taxpayers and voters should watch whether Democrats challenge this ruling or attempt end-runs around the court's decision through legislative maneuvers.
