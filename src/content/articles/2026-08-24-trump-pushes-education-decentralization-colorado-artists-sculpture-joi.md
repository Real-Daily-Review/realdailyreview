# Trump Pushes School Choice While Colorado Sculptor's Work Adorns Redesigned Rose Garden

The Trump administration is making a deliberate push to return education control to the states rather than Washington bureaucrats—a move conservatives have championed for years. At a Rose Garden event, administration officials emphasized "very deliberate and thoughtful steps" to decentralize education policy, signaling a sharp break from decades of federal overreach in K-12 schooling (Just The News).

Meanwhile, a Colorado sculptor's work has found a home in Trump's redesigned Rose Garden. Rather than adding traditional roses, the White House is installing statuary, including a piece from the Colorado artist, marking a notable recognition for state talent on the national stage (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

**The Conservative Case:** School choice—empowering parents to select schools rather than forcing them into zip-code-assigned government institutions—has long been a cornerstone of conservative education reform. By shifting authority from federal agencies to states and local communities, the administration aims to eliminate progressive-driven curriculum mandates and restore parental rights. Colorado parents and taxpayers should support this decentralization; it means more control over how education dollars are spent and what values are taught.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado lawmakers will seize this opportunity to expand Education Savings Accounts and charter school access, or whether progressive resistance will stall real school choice expansion in the state.
