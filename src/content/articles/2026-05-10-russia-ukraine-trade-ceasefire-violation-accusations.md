# Russia, Ukraine Trade Ceasefire Violation Accusations

Both Russia and Ukraine accused each other of breaking a U.S.-brokered three-day ceasefire that began Saturday, with each side reporting casualties from drone and artillery strikes within 24 hours, according to PBS NewsHour.

Russia claimed Ukraine committed more than 1,000 violations since the truce took effect, according to Al Jazeera English. Moscow said three Ukrainians were killed in strikes over the past day. Ukraine has not yet publicly detailed its own casualty figures or violation counts in response to the Russian allegations.

The ceasefire represents a rare diplomatic intervention in the conflict, though its immediate fragility underscores the deep mistrust between the warring parties. Both sides have routinely accused each other of bad-faith conduct throughout the war.

The three-day window is set to expire, making compliance—or lack thereof—a critical test of whether further negotiations are viable.
