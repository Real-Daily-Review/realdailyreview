# Government Official's Joyride While Americans Stranded Raises Questions About Federal Priorities

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin used a government plane to attend an LSU-Texas football game Saturday—just hours after the FAA lifted a nationwide ground stop that left nearly 7,000 flights canceled and thousands of Americans stranded at airports, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire.

The timing is brutal optics for the Biden administration. A telecommunications failure triggered the Friday shutdown, snarling the nation's air traffic system. By Saturday, as the FAA cleared skies for commercial flights, Mullin boarded a government aircraft for the game.

Mullin defended the trip, but conservatives argue the move epitomizes federal government tone-deafness: ordinary Americans scrambled to reschedule trips and lost money while a cabinet secretary enjoyed taxpayer-funded leisure travel during a crisis aftermath.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado travelers and businesses dependent on reliable air service were among those affected by the outage. State aviation and tourism industries take hits when the FAA's infrastructure fails—and federal officials' casual use of government resources while citizens suffer compounds the credibility problem.

Republicans should demand accountability for both the FAA's operational failures and the judgment lapses at the cabinet level.

Watch whether Congress demands a full accounting of government plane usage during national emergencies and whether the FAA overhauls its telecommunications redundancy systems.
