# Paul Pelosi Faces Hit-and-Run Charge in California

California authorities are investigating Paul Pelosi, 86-year-old husband of former House Speaker Nancy Pelosi, for allegedly striking a parked vehicle in Napa County wine country and leaving the scene (Fox News Politics, 9News Denver). The incident occurred Friday around 2:30 p.m. in Yountville, leaving the parked car with major damage, according to authorities.

The Napa County Sheriff's Office ruled out DUI but is referring Pelosi for a driver re-evaluation to the California DMV (Fox News Politics). Pelosi may face misdemeanor charges related to the hit-and-run allegation.

This marks another traffic incident for Pelosi, who was involved in a DUI case years earlier in the same region (The Daily Wire). The latest investigation comes as questions about aging drivers and road safety continue to draw public attention.

While this incident occurred in California rather than Colorado, it underscores broader concerns conservatives raise about accountability and equal application of the law—particularly when high-profile figures are involved. Taxpayers and voters should monitor whether Pelosi faces the same legal consequences an ordinary Californian would encounter in similar circumstances.
