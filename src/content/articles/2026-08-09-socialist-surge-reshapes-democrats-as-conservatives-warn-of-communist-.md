# Democratic Socialism Gains Ground Among Young Voters—But Conservatives Warn It's a Trojan Horse for Communism

Colorado voters should be alarmed that socialist ideology is reshaping the Democratic Party's platform, particularly on economic policy that would devastate Colorado's energy sector and small businesses.

According to Fox News Politics, socialist primary wins are signaling a leftward shift in Democratic ranks, with 800 pro-Israel students recently joining Senator John Fetterman in Washington—a sign that even traditionally progressive coalitions are fracturing over the party's ideological drift.

But the real danger lies deeper. Complete Colorado warns that "democratic socialism" is merely a gateway to full communism, with the Democratic Socialists of America platform explicitly targeting constitutional checks and balances. Conservatives argue that DSA's economic agenda—wealth redistribution, nationalized industries, and massive tax hikes—would cripple Colorado's oil and gas economy, eliminate private property protections, and strangle the entrepreneurship that built this state.

Republicans counter that voters should reject the false choice between capitalism and socialism. Free markets, not government control, have lifted millions out of poverty and created the prosperity Colorado depends on.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from DSA-backed candidates in 2026, or whether the party fully embraces its socialist wing—a test of whether moderate voters will abandon the left entirely.
