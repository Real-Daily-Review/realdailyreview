# Aaron Rodgers Benched: Steelers Turn to Mason Rudolph for Broncos Showdown

The Pittsburgh Steelers have benched four-time NFL MVP Aaron Rodgers for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos, with backup Mason Rudolph taking the starting role and Rodgers declared inactive (KDVR, 9News). The move marks a stunning reversal for the 43-year-old quarterback in what was expected to be his final season.

The decision blindsided Steelers teammates, according to reports (New York Post). Rodgers, one of the most decorated quarterbacks in NFL history, will not suit up for the divisional matchup in Denver—a significant development for a Broncos team that will now face a less experienced signal-caller.

The benching underscores ongoing questions about Rodgers' fit with Pittsburgh's system and his performance this season. While the Steelers have not detailed the reasoning behind the move, the decision signals the organization's willingness to pivot away from the veteran despite his Hall of Fame credentials.

For Colorado football fans, the Broncos now face a golden opportunity to exploit a Steelers offense operating without one of the league's most accomplished arms—a chance to capitalize on Pittsburgh's quarterback uncertainty.
