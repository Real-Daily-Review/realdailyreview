# Progressive Figures Face Reckoning Over Failed Woke Agenda

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her progressive allies are finally confronting the wreckage of their ideological experiment—but conservatives say a casual "oops" won't cut it.

According to *National Review*, prominent left-wing figures appear to be acknowledging errors in their approach, yet they're offering little more than vague mea culpas to the American people. The outlet argues that if progressives genuinely recognize their mistakes, they owe taxpayers and voters a full accounting of the damage inflicted by their policies.

*Daily Caller* columnist Duke goes further, questioning what comes next: "What, exactly, was wrong with Woke 1?" The implication is sharp—if the left is pivoting away from its failed agenda, it must admit the original vision was fundamentally flawed, not merely in execution but in principle.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans argue that progressive overreach on spending, regulation, and cultural issues has harmed working families and small businesses. From inflation-fueling spending bills to anti-energy mandates, the left's agenda has real consequences. A quiet retreat without accountability lets architects of these failures escape responsibility.

**Colorado Angle:** Colorado taxpayers and energy workers have borne the brunt of progressive climate mandates and spending-driven inflation. Conservatives will watch whether national progressives' apparent reckoning translates into rollback of policies still strangling Colorado's economy and energy sector.

Watch whether this represents genuine policy reversal or merely rhetorical repositioning ahead of 2026.
