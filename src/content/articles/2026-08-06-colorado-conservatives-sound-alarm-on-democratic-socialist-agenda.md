# Democratic Socialism Agenda Threatens Constitutional Limits, Conservatives Warn

Colorado conservatives are sounding the alarm over what they see as a dangerous leftward lurch in Democratic politics—one that goes far beyond traditional socialism into outright communism.

According to Complete Colorado, the Democratic Socialists of America platform would fundamentally undermine constitutional checks and balances, representing what critics call an "opening act for communism." The concern isn't academic: DSA's stated positions on wealth redistribution, government control of production, and elimination of private enterprise represent a direct assault on free-market capitalism and individual property rights that built Colorado's economy.

Republicans argue the Democratic Party has abandoned even the pretense of defending constitutional limits on government power. Fox News reports that former President Trump has directly challenged Democratic socialist candidates, naming Abdul El-Sayed and warning that communism represents "the greatest threat facing the U.S. in its history."

Democrats counter that they support democratic socialism—a mixed economy with robust social programs—not communism, and accuse Republicans of fearmongering. They argue stronger government intervention corrects market failures and protects workers.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the stakes are clear: policies rooted in socialist ideology would mean higher taxes, aggressive regulation of energy development, land-use restrictions, and erosion of property rights that ranchers and entrepreneurs depend on.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from DSA rhetoric ahead of 2026, or double down on the progressive agenda.
