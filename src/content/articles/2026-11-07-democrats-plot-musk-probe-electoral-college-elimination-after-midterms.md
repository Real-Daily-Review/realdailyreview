# Democrats Eye Musk Probe, Electoral College Overhaul After Midterms

Colorado conservatives should brace for a Democratic assault on two fronts: federal overreach into private enterprise and an end-run around the Electoral College that would gut rural and Western states' political power.

Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-Md.) signaled Democrats plan to investigate Elon Musk's cost-cutting at the Department of Government Efficiency, calling DOGE a "catastrophe" (Fox News). Republicans counter that Musk's efficiency push targets genuine waste—not a legitimate target for congressional witch hunts designed to punish a political opponent.

More alarming for Colorado: Democrats are floating plans to abolish the Electoral College, according to The Daily Wire. This would shift presidential power decisively toward high-population urban centers, diminishing Colorado's voice in national elections. The scheme would require a constitutional amendment—a heavy lift, but Democrats' midterm gains have emboldened the Left to pursue structural changes once considered fringe.

Conservatives argue the Electoral College protects federalism and ensures candidates must build geographically diverse coalitions. Eliminating it would mean Denver and the Front Range suburbs could dominate Colorado's entire political weight, while rural counties—energy producers, ranchers, and gun owners—become afterthoughts.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats, facing pressure from the national party, embrace the Electoral College abolition push or resist it as politically toxic in a swing state.
