# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Fight; Colorado Sun Warns of 'Retribution'

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is throwing $10 million behind Texas Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign against Democrat James Talarico, according to new FEC filings reported by *The Daily Wire*. The financial injection marks the first major commitment by Trump's outside group in a competitive general election race this cycle.

Meanwhile, *Colorado Sun* columnist Carman warns that Trump's administration is orchestrating a "campaign of retribution" against Colorado specifically, claiming the state faces federal coercion to surrender constitutional powers. Carman frames the conflict as an existential threat to state sovereignty.

**The conservative counter:** Republicans argue Trump's support for Paxton reflects backing for a Republican attorney general defending conservative priorities—not "retribution." Paxton has championed Second Amendment protections and challenged federal overreach, positions aligned with Trump's base. The framing of federal-state disputes as "coercion" versus legitimate constitutional enforcement depends heavily on one's view of the underlying policy dispute.

For Colorado conservatives, the Paxton race signals Trump's willingness to invest heavily in competitive Senate seats. Watch whether similar resources flow to Colorado's 2026 Senate race and whether the state's Republican nominee can capitalize on Trump's financial backing.
