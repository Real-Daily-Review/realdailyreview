# Bernie Sanders, GOP Find Rare Common Ground on Hospital Price Transparency

Bernie Sanders and Republicans are actually agreeing on something: hospitals need to stop hiding prices from patients. According to the *Washington Examiner*, Congress appears ready to tackle what conservatives call a bloated healthcare industrial complex that feeds on taxpayer dollars through opaque billing and secret negotiated rates.

The issue cuts across ideological lines. Republicans have long argued that government-protected hospital monopolies and insurance gatekeepers exploit patients and drive up costs. Conservatives say price transparency—forcing hospitals to disclose what they actually charge—is a free-market solution that lets patients shop around and compete providers down on cost.

Sanders, predictably, frames it as fighting a "corrupt system," but the mechanism both sides support is deregulation and transparency, not more government control. That's the conservative win here: exposing the racket without expanding federal bureaucracy.

For Colorado, this matters. Hospital systems across the state have faced criticism for opaque pricing. If Congress passes transparency rules, Colorado patients and employers could finally see what they're actually paying—and vote with their feet.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation pushes for teeth in any transparency bill, or whether hospital lobbies water it down with loopholes.
