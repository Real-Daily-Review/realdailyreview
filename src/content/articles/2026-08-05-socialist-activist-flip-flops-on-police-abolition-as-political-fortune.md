# Socialist Colorado Candidate Flip-Flops on Police After 'Defund' Past Catches Up

A Colorado progressive activist who once called to abolish police is now claiming she's "serious about public safety"—a convenient reversal as her political profile rises. Francesca Hong, a self-described socialist chef, made headlines in 2020 for tweets demanding police defunding and abolition, but now insists she's committed to law enforcement (Fox News Politics).

The flip-flop underscores a broader problem conservatives have flagged: progressive candidates nationwide embraced radical anti-police rhetoric during the 2020 unrest, only to backpedal when voters rejected the agenda. Republicans argue that defund-the-police movements destabilize communities and endanger public safety—a concern that resonates in Colorado, where serious crimes including violent crashes and assaults demand functional police departments.

Hong's sudden pivot raises credibility questions. Conservatives say voters deserve clarity: did she abandon her 2020 positions, or is she simply repositioning for electability? Either way, her record suggests ideological flexibility over principle.

Colorado taxpayers and business owners should watch whether local media press Hong on specifics—and whether other progressive candidates in the state face similar accountability for past anti-law-enforcement rhetoric.
