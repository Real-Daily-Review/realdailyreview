# Newsom Vetoes Radical Abortion Sanctuary Bill—Even California Has Limits

California Governor Gavin Newsom just rejected the "Care Without Fear Act," a bill that would have barred future governors from extraditing residents to other states for abortion or transgender procedures (New York Post). The veto signals Newsom's reluctance to let California become a legal sanctuary for out-of-state abortion seekers—a position that has infuriated his own party's progressive wing.

**The conservative read:** Newsom's move exposes the absurdity of the left's sanctuary-state rhetoric. Democrats claim to champion "rule of law" while simultaneously pushing policies that would shield residents from lawful interstate cooperation. By rejecting this bill, Newsom tacitly acknowledges that unlimited abortion expansion and gender-medicine immunity create constitutional and practical problems even progressives can't ignore.

**Democrats counter** that Newsom's veto betrays reproductive rights advocates and transgender activists who see California as a refuge. They argue the governor is positioning himself for a 2028 presidential run by appearing moderate—sacrificing principle for optics (Just The News).

**Colorado angle:** This matters for Coloradans watching how far-left policies metastasize. If California—the progressive flagship—won't go full sanctuary on abortion and gender procedures, it signals even blue-state governors recognize limits to radical social experimentation.
