# Michigan Dems Nominate Climate Activist in Swing District—Colorado Should Note the Pattern

Democrats in Michigan's 7th Congressional District have nominated William Lawrence, co-founder of the Sunrise Movement climate advocacy group, to challenge Republican Rep. Tom Barrett in a crucial swing race (The Daily Wire). Lawrence defeated two rivals in Tuesday's primary, signaling the party's continued embrace of radical environmental activism over moderate governance.

The Sunrise Movement, which Lawrence helped establish, has built its brand on aggressive anti-fossil-fuel activism and opposition to immigration enforcement. This nomination reflects a broader Democratic strategy: elevating ideological purists in competitive districts rather than pragmatic candidates who might appeal to working-class voters concerned about energy costs and border security.

**For Colorado, the implications are stark.** Democrats control the state legislature and governor's mansion, and they've already weaponized climate policy to strangle energy development and hike utility bills. If the Michigan model spreads—nominating activists over moderates—Colorado's 2024 and 2026 races could see similar far-left candidates pushing policies that devastate our energy sector, eliminate good-paying jobs, and drive up costs for families already squeezed by inflation.

Republicans argue Lawrence's nomination proves Democrats have abandoned working families for coastal environmentalists. Conservatives should watch whether Colorado Democrats follow suit by elevating climate absolutists in competitive state and federal races.
