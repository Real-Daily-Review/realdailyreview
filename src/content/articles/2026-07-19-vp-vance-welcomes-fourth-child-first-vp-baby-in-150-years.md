# VP Vance Welcomes Fourth Child, First VP Baby Born in Office Since 1870s

Vice President JD Vance and second lady Usha Vance welcomed their fourth child, Alec Neel Vance, on Sunday morning at Walter Reed Medical Center—marking the first child born to a sitting vice president in more than 150 years, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire.

"Usha and the baby are happy and healthy, and our kids are overjoyed to meet their little brother," Vance said in a statement. Alec joins older siblings Ewan, 9; Vivek, 6; and Mirabel, 4 (Just The News).

The birth is a notable milestone for the Trump-Vance administration. Vance, a former Ohio senator and author of *Hillbilly Elegy*, has been vocal about family values and demographic concerns facing America—themes central to his political identity. The arrival underscores his personal commitment to those principles as the second-highest-ranking official in government.

Both mother and child are reported healthy, and the Vance family has not announced further details about the newborn's arrival or plans for public appearances.

Conservatives and Trump supporters will likely view the birth as a positive symbol for the administration's pro-family messaging heading into the second term.
