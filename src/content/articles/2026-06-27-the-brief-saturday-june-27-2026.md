Colorado Republicans should want their senators on record on this confirmation — accountability starts at the top.

## Texas Mandates Bible Stories in K-12 Schools

Texas became the first state in recent history to require Bible stories as part of its K-12 public school reading curriculum, with the majority-Republican State Board of Education approving the measure. Conservatives argue that Western civilization's foundational texts belong in a serious education — and that the same progressive establishment that has no problem with gender ideology in classrooms suddenly discovers the separation of church and state when the Bible is involved. Colorado's Republican legislators should be watching Austin for a model.
