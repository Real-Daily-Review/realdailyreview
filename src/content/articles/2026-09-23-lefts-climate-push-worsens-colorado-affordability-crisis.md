# The Left's Climate Agenda Is Pricing Out Working Coloradans

The American Left claims to champion affordability while simultaneously pushing climate policies that drive up energy and housing costs—a contradiction that should alarm Colorado families already squeezed by inflation.

According to the Washington Examiner, Democrats are desperate to center the 2026 midterms on "affordability" after the generational inflation of the Biden-Harris years. Yet they continue advancing climate mandates that make energy and construction more expensive. New York's experience illustrates the problem: strict environmental regulations and climate-driven building codes inflate housing and utility costs, pricing out working- and middle-class residents.

**Conservatives argue** this is economic malpractice. Climate policies that restrict energy development, ban natural gas, and impose costly environmental compliance don't reduce prices—they raise them. Colorado's energy sector, which supports thousands of jobs and generates substantial tax revenue, faces similar pressure from progressive regulators who prioritize climate ideology over affordability and economic growth.

**Democrats counter** that long-term climate investments prevent costlier disasters and create green jobs. They frame these policies as necessary for future prosperity.

The real issue: working Coloradans can't wait for theoretical long-term benefits while paying higher heating bills and rent today. Watch whether Colorado Republicans make this affordability-versus-climate contradiction a centerpiece of 2026 messaging.
