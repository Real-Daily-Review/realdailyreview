# Poudre Schools Face Parent Revolt Over Nine Proposed Closures

Poudre School District's plan to shutter nine schools and consolidate others is colliding with furious parents who say the district is abandoning their communities. The proposal, presented to the Board of Education this week, has sparked protests from families worried about longer commutes, disrupted education, and the loss of neighborhood schools (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

The closures reflect a familiar problem: declining enrollment and budget pressure. But conservatives and parents argue the district should first address why families are leaving—and whether bloated administrative overhead, not school buildings, is the real problem. Rather than consolidating, critics say Poudre should cut central office spending and restore fiscal discipline.

Parents are demanding answers on how the district calculated which schools to close and whether community input was genuinely sought. The board faces mounting pressure to justify the plan's rationale and timeline.

**What to watch:** Whether Poudre's board caves to parent pressure or proceeds with closures—and whether Colorado taxpayers demand accountability for how school districts spend money before cutting classrooms.
