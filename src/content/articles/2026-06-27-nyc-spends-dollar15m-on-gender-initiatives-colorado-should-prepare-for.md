# NYC Doubles Down on Gender Ideology While Colorado Watches Its Own Spending

New York City is committing $15 million in taxpayer money to expand sex-change initiatives for children and adults, a move that underscores the progressive left's priorities even as federal scrutiny intensifies.

NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani framed the spending as a defense against federal action, claiming the initiative represents "healthcare as a human right." But conservatives argue the real issue is whether government should be bankrolling experimental gender treatments at all—especially for minors—rather than letting families and the private market decide.

Republicans counter that this spending exemplifies progressive governance: massive outlays on ideological priorities while core services deteriorate. The move also raises questions about parental rights and medical ethics that resonate beyond New York's borders.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't announced similar initiatives, progressive legislators in Denver have pushed gender-affirming care policies in schools and Medicaid. If the state expands taxpayer funding for these programs, expect pushback from conservative lawmakers and parents who argue the money should go to reading, math, and basic infrastructure instead.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats attempt to shield state spending on gender initiatives from federal scrutiny—and whether Republican legislators demand transparency on how much state money already flows to these programs.
