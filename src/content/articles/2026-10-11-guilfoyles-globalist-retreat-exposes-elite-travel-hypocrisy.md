# Guilfoyle's Globalist Junket Exposes Elite Hypocrisy on Taxpayer Dime

Ambassador Kimberly Guilfoyle reportedly bragged about elites bankrolling her luxury travel at an exclusive retreat, claiming "I can deliver the world to you" (Daily Caller). The remarks highlight the disconnect between Washington insiders who lecture ordinary Americans about sacrifice while enjoying lavish perks funded by connected donors.

The incident drew sharp pushback from the State Department. Deputy Secretary Christopher Landau fired back at former Obama ambassador Ivo Daalder's "smug" criticism of Secretary of State Marco Rubio's foreign travel, according to Breitbart, underscoring tensions over how top officials conduct diplomatic missions.

**The conservative concern:** When government elites accept luxury accommodations from wealthy donors—even indirectly—it raises questions about whose interests they truly serve. Colorado taxpayers footing the bill for federal operations deserve officials focused on American interests, not networking with globalist financiers at five-star resorts.

Republicans argue this exemplifies the two-tiered system: ordinary citizens tighten their belts while Washington's connected class lives large. Democrats and their allies typically defend such travel as necessary for diplomacy, though critics say it often amounts to junkets that could be conducted more efficiently.

Watch whether Congress demands transparency on who funds official travel and whether similar arrangements involve Colorado-based officials or federal employees.
