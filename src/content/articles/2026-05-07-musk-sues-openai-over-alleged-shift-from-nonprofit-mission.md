# OpenAI Trial: Musk Alleges Departure From Nonprofit Mission

Elon Musk's high-stakes lawsuit against OpenAI is underway, with testimony focusing on whether the AI startup has abandoned its founding mission to benefit humanity in favor of profit maximization, according to The Verge.

Shivon Zilis, a former OpenAI board member and mother of four of Musk's children, testified Wednesday that Musk had proposed offering Sam Altman a Tesla board seat as part of an effort to integrate OpenAI with his EV company, according to CNBC. The trial has already heard from Musk, his financial manager Jared Birchall, and OpenAI cofounder Greg Brockman.

Musk filed the lawsuit in 2024, contending that OpenAI shifted from its original nonprofit focus toward commercial interests. TechCrunch reports the case may ultimately hinge on whether OpenAI's for-profit subsidiary structure enhances or undermines the organization's stated commitment to ensuring humanity benefits from artificial general intelligence.

The trial could reshape OpenAI's future and influence the trajectory of ChatGPT, its flagship product. Watch for additional testimony on OpenAI's governance structure and whether its business model conflicts with its founding principles.
