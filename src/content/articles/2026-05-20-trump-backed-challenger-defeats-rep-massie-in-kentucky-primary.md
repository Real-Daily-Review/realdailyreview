# Trump-Backed Challenger Defeats Massie in Kentucky GOP Primary

President Trump's endorsee, Ed Gallrein, a retired Navy SEAL and farmer, defeated Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.) in Tuesday's GOP primary, extending Trump's winning streak against Republicans who have openly defied him, according to Decision Desk HQ.

With roughly 72 to 74 percent of votes counted, Gallrein led with 54.4 percent to Massie's 45.6 percent, according to Al Jazeera. The victory marks a major win in Trump's retribution campaign against party members who have opposed his priorities. Massie, a libertarian-leaning lawmaker, had long drawn Trump's ire for his opposition to key administration goals.

Trump celebrated the result, telling reporters that Massie "was a bad guy" and "deserves to lose," according to the Washington Examiner. The president and his allies took a victory lap Tuesday night following the primary outcome.

Massie's defeat represents the latest casualty in Trump's effort to reshape the Republican Party by removing internal critics. The race was notably expensive, according to the Financial Times.

Watch for whether Trump's primary influence continues to hold sway in upcoming Republican contests.
