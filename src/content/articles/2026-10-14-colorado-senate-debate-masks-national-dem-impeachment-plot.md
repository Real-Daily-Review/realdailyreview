# Colorado Senate Debate: Hickenlooper and Baisley Spar on Policy While National Democrats Plot Impeachment

Colorado's U.S. Senate race took a substantive turn this week as incumbent Democrat John Hickenlooper and Republican challenger Mark Baisley clashed over policy in their first debate, according to CPR News. The two focused on issues rather than personal attacks—a stark contrast to the national Democratic playbook.

While Colorado voters weigh the candidates' records, Democrats nationwide are signaling their true agenda. In Georgia, Sen. Jon Ossoff (D) openly stated he would support impeaching President Trump if Democrats win the House majority, Breitbart reported. That admission crystallizes what conservatives have long warned: the left's obsession with removing Trump supersedes governing.

Coloradans should ask Hickenlooper directly: does he share Ossoff's impeachment ambitions? Hickenlooper's record as governor shows he can work across the aisle, but his Senate voting record leans reliably left. Republicans argue his debate civility masks a voting pattern that backs Biden's regulatory overreach, inflation-driving spending, and energy policies that threaten Colorado's oil and gas workers.

Baisley represents a genuine alternative for voters tired of Washington gridlock and progressive experimentation. Watch whether Hickenlooper distances himself from the national Democratic impeachment agenda—or stays silent.
