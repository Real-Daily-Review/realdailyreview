# Colorado Faces Dangerous Heat Wave as Extreme Temperatures Persist Through Monday

Denver and the Front Range are bracing for another brutal day of triple-digit heat, with a Weather Impact Alert and Heat Advisory in effect through Monday as an unusual hot streak grips Colorado.

Sunday night conditions remain partly cloudy and warm, with gusty winds exceeding 30 mph at times, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver. Monday's forecast calls for continued extreme heat—pushing toward 100 degrees—before a "rare hot streak" finally breaks, 9News reports.

The extended heat poses real risks for Coloradans: outdoor workers, ranchers managing livestock, and families without adequate cooling face genuine health hazards. Authorities are urging residents to limit time outside and take precautions.

While weather events themselves aren't partisan, conservatives should note that Colorado's energy grid—increasingly dependent on unreliable renewables mandated by progressive state policy—faces stress during peak demand periods like this. Reliable baseload power from natural gas and coal plants becomes critical when temperatures soar, yet the state continues pushing toward their elimination.

Residents should monitor local forecasts closely and ensure vulnerable family members and neighbors have access to cooling and hydration through the heat event.
