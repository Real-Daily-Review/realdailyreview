# Trump Administration Eliminates Venezuelan Gang Leader in Joint Strike

President Trump announced a U.S. military strike that killed Tren de Aragua leader Niño Guerrero, calling the operation retribution for victims of violent crime linked to the Venezuelan criminal organization. The Daily Caller reports Trump labeled the gang "one of the most bloodthirsty Terrorist Organizations on Planet Earth," while Fox News notes the strike was framed as accountability for those harmed by the group's violence.

The Daily Wire reports the operation marked a rare moment of U.S.-Venezuela cooperation, occurring after former dictator Nicolás Maduro's arrest. Trump shared footage of the strike Friday evening, touting the joint operation as evidence of shifting regional dynamics.

For Colorado, this matters: Tren de Aragua has expanded operations into U.S. cities, including Denver, where the gang has been linked to retail theft, human trafficking, and violent crime. Colorado law enforcement and business owners have flagged the group as a growing public safety threat. The strike signals Trump's willingness to pursue direct military action against transnational criminal organizations threatening American communities—a sharp contrast to the previous administration's more restrained approach to Venezuelan affairs.

Watch whether this operation leads to broader Trump administration pressure on Venezuelan criminal networks operating in Colorado and other U.S. states.
