# White House Counsel Pushes Back Against Democratic Congressional Overreach

The White House is drawing a line against what it calls Democratic abuse of investigative power. White House counsel Will Scharf has warned House Oversight ranking member Rep. Robert Garcia against exceeding congressional authority, even as Senate Democrats—led by Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D-CT)—pursue investigations into Trump administration figures and supporters (Fox News Politics, Washington Examiner).

The DOJ has flagged Blumenthal's probe into White House ballroom donors as potentially intimidating Trump supporters, signaling concern that Democrats are weaponizing committee power for political purposes rather than legitimate oversight.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Congressional investigations should target genuine misconduct, not punish political opponents or their donors. Republicans argue the pattern reflects the left's playbook—use investigative committees to harass, intimidate, and drain resources from political enemies. Scharf's warning suggests the White House won't roll over for what it sees as partisan theater.

Colorado conservatives should note this dynamic: if Democrats control committees, they'll use subpoena power aggressively. Colorado's Democratic delegation—including Sens. Michael Bennet and John Hickenlooper—will likely align with these investigations.

**Watch whether House Republicans reciprocate with their own aggressive probes once they regain committee control, or whether bipartisan norms actually constrain both sides.**
