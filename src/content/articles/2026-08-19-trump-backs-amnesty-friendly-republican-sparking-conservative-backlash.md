# Trump Endorses Florida Republican in Contentious Amnesty Fight

President Trump has endorsed Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar (R-FL) for reelection, but the move has ignited sharp criticism from immigration hawks within the conservative movement.

Salazar, a former news anchor representing Florida's 27th Congressional District, has positioned herself as a leading voice on the Right for what critics call mass amnesty policies. Trump praised her as "doing a truly fantastic job" in a Truth Social post Monday evening, according to *The Daily Wire*.

**The Conservative Split**

Republicans and conservatives are divided on Salazar's immigration stance. Immigration hawks argue she's undermining Trump's core agenda of border security and enforcement-first immigration reform. The *Daily Caller* characterized her position bluntly as "rank amnesty, and everybody knows it," suggesting Trump's endorsement contradicts his own hardline messaging on illegal immigration.

Salazar's supporters counter that she brings valuable perspective as a Cuban-American lawmaker and former journalist representing a heavily Hispanic district where pragmatic immigration positions resonate with voters.

**The Colorado Angle**

While Salazar represents Florida, her influence on national Republican immigration policy directly affects Colorado's border security concerns and labor market dynamics, particularly in agriculture and construction sectors where illegal immigration impacts wages and working conditions.

**Watch Next**

Conservatives should monitor whether Trump's endorsement signals a broader shift toward compromise on immigration enforcement—or remains an outlier in his otherwise restrictionist record.
