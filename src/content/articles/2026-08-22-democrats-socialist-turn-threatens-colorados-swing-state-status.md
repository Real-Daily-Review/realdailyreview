# Democrats' Socialist Drift Signals Danger for Colorado Moderates

Colorado Democrats are swimming against the national tide as their party lurches left. While Democratic leadership like House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries publicly rejects the Democratic Socialists of America agenda, polling shows most Democrats now view socialism more favorably than capitalism (Fox News Politics), a troubling sign for moderate swing-state Democrats who depend on centrist voters.

The shift mirrors a broader pattern: Democrats are nominating increasingly radical candidates even in conservative strongholds. National Review notes the party's embrace of socialist-backed nominees in traditionally Republican states, signaling ideological rigidity over electoral pragmatism.

For Colorado, this matters. The state's purple status depends on suburban and rural voters skeptical of big government. If national Democrats continue championing socialist policies—higher taxes, expanded regulation, government-run healthcare—Colorado's Democratic establishment will face mounting pressure to follow suit or risk primary challenges from the activist left.

Republicans argue the Democratic Party has abandoned working families for utopian fantasies that destroy jobs and liberty. Conservatives say Colorado voters should reject candidates tied to DSA-backed agendas and vote for leaders committed to free markets, low taxes, and limited government.

**Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from national socialism rhetoric—or embrace it and lose swing voters.**
