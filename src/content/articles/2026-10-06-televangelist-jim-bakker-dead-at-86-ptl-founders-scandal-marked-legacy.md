# Televangelist Jim Bakker, Scandal-Plagued PTL Founder, Dies at 86

Jim Bakker, the once-prominent televangelist whose empire collapsed under federal fraud charges, has died at age 86, his second wife Lori announced via Instagram.

Bakker rose to national prominence co-hosting "The PTL Club" with his first wife, Tammy Faye, from 1974 to 1989—a Christian television program that became a cultural fixture. The ministry's spectacular implosion under federal investigation for financial misconduct became a defining scandal of the 1980s evangelical movement, damaging public trust in televangelism broadly.

According to the New York Post, Bakker had mourned the death of his sister, Donna Puckett, 91, just a week before his own passing. Puckett died September 29 in Hollister, Missouri.

Bakker's death marks the end of a complicated legacy: a man who built a massive religious media enterprise, faced serious legal consequences for mismanagement, and spent decades attempting to rehabilitate his public image through continued ministry work.
