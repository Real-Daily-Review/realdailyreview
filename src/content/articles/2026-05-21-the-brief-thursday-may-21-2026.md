## SpaceX Files for Historic IPO, Targeting $1.75 Trillion Valuation

SpaceX has filed for an initial public offering that, if successful, would value the company at $1.75 trillion — surpassing any IPO in U.S. history. The filing outlines ambitions that include asteroid mining alongside its established launch and satellite businesses. Before SpaceX, the largest U.S. IPOs were raised by Alibaba, Facebook, and Visa. The offering is expected to draw intense investor scrutiny given the company's scale and Elon Musk's concurrent leadership of other major enterprises.

## Xi and Putin Meet in Beijing, but the Partnership Shows Strain

Chinese President Xi Jinping received Russian President Vladimir Putin in Beijing with formal ceremony — just days after hosting U.S. President Donald Trump. Analysts are examining whether the relationship, long described as a "no limits" partnership, is shifting as Russia's war in Ukraine deepens Moscow's economic and diplomatic dependence on Beijing, and as Western leaders pursue warmer ties with China. The back-to-back summits underscore Beijing's effort to position itself at the center of multiple competing relationships.

## Carney Turns Trump's Teleprompter Criticism Back on Him

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney drew laughs at a Greater Vancouver Board of Trade event Wednesday by joking about President Trump's past criticism of him over a teleprompter malfunction at the United Nations. The quip signals Carney's continued willingness to engage Trump rhetorically, even as Ottawa and Washington navigate ongoing trade and sovereignty tensions. The remark was light in tone but landed in a charged bilateral environment.

## Ebola Outbreak in DR Congo Involves Rare Strain, Conflict Complicates Response

A new Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo involves a rare strain and is occurring in a region affected by active armed conflict, making containment significantly harder, according to public health officials. Ebola is a severe hemorrhagic fever virus with high fatality rates in uncontrolled outbreaks. The combination of an unusual strain and limited humanitarian access has raised concern among international health authorities.

## Trump Envoy Faces Cool Reception in Greenland; Raúl Castro Indicted

On two separate fronts, the Trump administration pressed assertive foreign policy moves. In Greenland, U.S. envoy Jeff Landry's visit — framed as a mission to make "friends" — has unsettled some Greenlanders, who view American interest in the semi-autonomous Danish territory with suspicion. Separately, federal prosecutors announced charges against former Cuban leader Raúl Castro in connection with the 1996 downing of civilian aircraft flown by Miami-based exiles. President Trump called the indictment "a very important moment" for the Cuban people.
