# Trump, NATO Chief Urge Russia to Avoid Ukraine Escalation

President Donald Trump and NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte met Wednesday night at Mar-a-Lago to push back against Russian aggression in Ukraine. According to a joint statement released Thursday, the two leaders emphasized preventing further escalation of the conflict and discussed threats from both Russia and Iran (Washington Examiner, Fox News).

Trump called the meeting "outstanding," signaling alignment between the administration and NATO leadership on containing the war. The emphasis on de-escalation reflects the Trump administration's stated priority of ending the Ukraine conflict rather than indefinitely funding it—a position that has drawn criticism from some Republicans who want stronger anti-Russia posturing, and from Democrats who fear Trump will pressure Ukraine into unfavorable terms.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado defense contractors and military installations depend on NATO stability and sustained defense spending. Any negotiated settlement that weakens U.S. commitment to European security could affect defense contracts and jobs across the state.

Republicans argue that preventing further escalation serves American interests by avoiding direct confrontation with a nuclear power. Democrats counter that Trump's diplomatic overtures risk emboldening Putin and abandoning Ukraine.
