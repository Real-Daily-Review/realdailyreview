# Cyclist Killed in Fort Collins Semi Crash

A woman riding a bicycle was struck and killed by a semitrailer Monday evening at a construction site in Fort Collins, according to police. The crash occurred at the intersection of East Mulberry Street and Whedbee Street.

Fort Collins Police Services confirmed the fatality but have not yet released details on the circumstances surrounding the collision or whether citations will be issued.
