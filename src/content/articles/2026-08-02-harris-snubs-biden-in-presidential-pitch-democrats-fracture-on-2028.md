# Harris Distances Herself From Biden as Democrats Eye 2028

Kamala Harris is already running from her own boss. When asked recently to name America's most effective presidents, the former vice president conspicuously omitted Joe Biden—instead citing FDR, JFK, and LBJ (Breitbart, Daily Caller). The snub signals Harris knows the Biden-Harris record is a political liability heading into 2028.

The move underscores deepening fractures within the Democratic Party. A Michigan poll shows Pete Buttigieg, Jon Ossoff, and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez polling ahead of both Harris and California Governor Gavin Newsom in a hypothetical Democratic primary (New York Post), suggesting the party's progressive wing is already shopping for alternatives.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: Harris's retreat from Biden's record—inflation, border chaos, regulatory overreach—amounts to a tacit admission that four years of Democratic governance failed. Yet she offers no ideological break from that agenda. Republicans should hammer home that Harris cannot escape her role in Biden's economic and border disasters, no matter how carefully she curates her presidential messaging.

**Watch for:** whether Harris's 2028 campaign doubles down on progressive policy or attempts a centrist rebranding—and whether Colorado voters believe either version.
