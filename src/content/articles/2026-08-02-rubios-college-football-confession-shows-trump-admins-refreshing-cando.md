# Marco Rubio's College Football Fantasy Reveals What Matters to America's Top Diplomat

Secretary of State Marco Rubio offered a refreshingly candid glimpse into life at the top of the Trump administration: given a week off, he'd camp in front of a television from noon Eastern through the West Coast games ending at 2 a.m. (Washington Examiner). "I'd love to sit down and watch every college football game," Rubio said, adding with characteristic humor that his wife would hate the answer (The Daily Wire).

The comment, lighthearted as it sounds, underscores something Colorado conservatives appreciate: Rubio's grounded, relatable approach to power. Unlike the typical Washington elite who lecture Americans about their leisure time while expanding government reach, Rubio owns his passion for college football without apology—a sport that matters deeply to millions of Coloradans and represents the kind of American tradition progressives increasingly target through Title IX overreach and woke athletic department mandates.

Rubio's rising profile within the Trump administration (Washington Examiner) reflects broader conservative momentum heading into 2025. His willingness to speak plainly about what he actually enjoys—rather than performing sanctimony—resonates with voters tired of performative politics.
