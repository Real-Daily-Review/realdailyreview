# Iranian State Media Posts Assassination Threat Against Barron Trump; Secret Service Responds

Iranian state media has circulated a video threatening the life of Barron Trump, complete with a reported $10 million bounty for his location, prompting the Secret Service to confirm it is monitoring the threat. The video emerged following the U.S. assassination of Iran's Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, according to 9News Denver and Just The News, and represents an escalation in Tehran's hostile rhetoric targeting the president and his family.

The Secret Service has acknowledged awareness of the threat and is taking appropriate protective measures. Iranian state media has used similar tactics on multiple occasions since Khamenei's death, circulating content designed to intimidate American leadership.

This incident underscores the real security risks facing American officials and their families in an increasingly volatile geopolitical environment. The brazen nature of the threat—a state actor openly posting assassination content with financial incentives—reflects Iran's willingness to operate without restraint when it perceives American action as an existential challenge.

Coloradans should expect heightened security protocols around all presidential family members and watch whether the Biden-Harris administration takes direct diplomatic or military action in response to Iranian threats.
