# Trump Orders Vaccine Schedule Overhaul as Schools Open

Trump is pushing federal health agencies to rethink childhood vaccine protocols—a move that will ripple through Colorado schools and pit parental choice against public health bureaucracy.

The executive order directs the nation's health department to recommend separating combination vaccines like MMR into individual shots spaced across different visits, and to expand vaccine research generally (Just The News). The Daily Caller reports the order emphasizes "vaccine flexibility," citing post-COVID skepticism: "Americans … are asking questions."

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue parents deserve more control over vaccination timing and combinations, and that federal health agencies lost credibility during COVID lockdowns and mandates. Proponents say the order restores choice without banning vaccines.

**The counterargument:** Public health officials warn that spreading shots across more visits reduces compliance, delays protection, and risks outbreaks—especially as measles cases rise nationally (9News Denver). They contend combination vaccines are safe, tested, and efficient.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado schools must navigate state immunization rules while this federal guidance shifts. Expect tension between school districts, parents demanding flexibility, and state health officials defending current protocols.

Watch whether Colorado's Department of Public Health and Environment adopts the federal recommendations or resists—and how many parents use this as cover to delay or skip shots entirely.
