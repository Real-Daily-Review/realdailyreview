# Trump's FIFA Call Clears US Soccer Star for World Cup Quarterfinal Push

President Trump reportedly picked up the phone and called FIFA's president to challenge a dubious red card that would have benched American striker Folarin Balogun for the U.S. Men's National Team's critical World Cup round-of-16 matchup against Belgium. FIFA reversed course Sunday, suspending Balogun's one-game ban and clearing the three-goal leader to play Monday—a decision that left Belgium's soccer federation visibly irritated (Breitbart). The Washington Examiner confirmed Trump made the direct appeal to FIFA leadership after the controversial call against Balogun for stepping on an opponent's foot in the Bosnia match.

Whether you view this as shrewd executive advocacy for Team USA or as an uncomfortable reminder that international sports bodies bend to political pressure, one thing's clear: Trump unapologetically champions American interests, even in soccer (Daily Caller). Belgium's federation's public astonishment at the reversal underscores how unusual—and effective—the intervention was.
