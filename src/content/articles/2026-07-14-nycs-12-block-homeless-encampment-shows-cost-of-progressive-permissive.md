# NYC's Homeless Crisis Spirals as Progressive Policies Fail

New York City's permissive approach to homelessness is creating sprawling, crime-infested encampments that should alarm Colorado cities considering similar policies.

A massive homeless encampment now stretches 12 blocks across Manhattan's West Side, from 34th to 46th Street near the Intrepid Museum, according to *The Daily Wire*. The encampment has become a magnet for drugs, prostitution, and theft—predictable outcomes of policies that prioritize accommodation over enforcement.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani has offered few concrete solutions, *Fox News* reports, even as complaints mount from residents and businesses. This is the inevitable result of progressive governance: unlimited tolerance for disorder, coupled with spending that fails to address root causes.

**The conservative argument is straightforward:** Permitting sprawling encampments enables crime, destroys neighborhoods, and wastes taxpayer dollars on ineffective services. Colorado cities like Denver have flirted with similar approaches—allowing camps in parks, relaxing enforcement, and expanding social spending without measurable results. The outcome mirrors NYC's failure.

Colorado conservatives and taxpayers should watch closely whether Denver and Boulder follow New York's trajectory or finally adopt enforcement-first policies that prioritize public safety and property rights over progressive ideology.
