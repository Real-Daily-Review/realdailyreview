# NYC Mayor's Office Caught Planning Cozy Iran Meeting—Trump Admin Steps In

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's administration was forced to cancel a scheduled meeting between a senior city official and Iran's UN ambassador after the Trump administration intervened, according to the Washington Examiner and New York Post.

Ana María Archila, commissioner of the Mayor's Office for International Affairs, had planned to meet with Iranian Ambassador Amir-Saeid Iravani at UN Plaza this week alongside two other city officials. The Trump administration shut it down amid escalating Middle East tensions.
