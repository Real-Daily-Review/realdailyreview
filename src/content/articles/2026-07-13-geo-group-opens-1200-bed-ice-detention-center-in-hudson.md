# GEO Group Moving Forward With ICE Detention Center in Hudson Despite Activist Opposition

Colorado is about to get a major immigration enforcement facility—and the left is already mobilizing to block it.

The GEO Group, a private corrections contractor, confirmed plans to convert the shuttered Big Horn facility in Hudson into a 1,200-bed ICE detention center (KDVR). The expansion signals renewed federal commitment to immigration enforcement under current policy priorities.

Activists and progressive groups are mounting resistance, citing concerns over transparency, emergency access, and due process protections for detainees (Colorado Sun). They plan to continue fighting the facility's opening, though the company has already secured approval to move forward.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the detention center represents practical border security infrastructure—outsourced to a private operator rather than expanding federal payroll. Republicans generally support ICE enforcement operations as necessary to enforce immigration law, though some libertarian-leaning conservatives question the efficiency and oversight of private prison contracts.

The facility will bring jobs and economic activity to the Hudson area, though critics worry about the community's capacity to handle emergencies and maintain oversight of detention operations (CPR News).

Watch whether Colorado Democrats attempt legislative action to block or restrict the facility's operations—and whether GEO Group's contract terms include adequate transparency and accountability measures.
