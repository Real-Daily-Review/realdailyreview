# Trump Administration Targets Synthetic Hemp Products in Drug Strategy

The Trump administration is signaling a crackdown on synthetic hemp products containing high levels of THC, according to a National Drug Control Strategy released this week. The White House characterized such products as a "growing concern" that have proliferated in the market, according to The Hill.

The move reflects broader administration priorities on drug policy, though specific enforcement mechanisms or timelines were not detailed in available accounts. Synthetic hemp products—often marketed as legal alternatives to cannabis—have become increasingly available in states where traditional cannabis remains restricted or illegal.

The administration's focus on intoxicating hemp products comes as the broader landscape of cannabis regulation remains fragmented across state and federal lines. The strategy document signals the White House intends to treat these synthetic variants as a policy priority, though details on how enforcement would proceed remain unclear.

Watch for announcements on specific regulatory or legislative proposals the administration may introduce to restrict the sale and distribution of high-THC hemp products.
