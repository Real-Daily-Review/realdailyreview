# Colorado School Faces Lawsuit Over Racial Harassment Failures

A former student at Addenbrooke Classical Academy has filed suit against the school and its officials, alleging they ignored repeated racial harassment during his sophomore year—raising serious questions about institutional accountability and duty of care in Colorado classrooms.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, the lawsuit claims school administrators failed to intervene despite knowing about the harassment. The case highlights a critical gap: when schools become aware of hostile environments targeting students based on race, what legal and moral obligations do they have to act?

Colorado conservatives and taxpayers should demand clarity on what happened here. Public and private schools alike have a fundamental duty to maintain safe learning environments—not through ideological posturing, but through actual protection of students from harassment. The question isn't whether the school should have acted; it's why it didn't.

The lawsuit will likely expose internal communications, policies, and decision-making that either protected or failed this student. Colorado parents across the political spectrum deserve transparency about how their schools handle these situations.

Watch whether Addenbrooke's leadership takes responsibility, what the discovery process reveals about institutional failures, and whether Colorado's education community uses this case to strengthen real accountability measures—not just diversity statements.
