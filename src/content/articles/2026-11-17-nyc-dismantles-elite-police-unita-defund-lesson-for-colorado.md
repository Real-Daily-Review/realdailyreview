# NYC Mayor Dismantles Elite Police Unit—A Warning for Colorado on Defund Ideology

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has officially disbanded the NYPD's Strategic Response Group, an elite counterterrorism unit, in what police unions and law enforcement leaders say is a reckless move that endangers public safety (Fox News, Breitbart).

Police union officials have condemned the decision, arguing the specialized unit was essential for rapid response to serious threats. An op-ed in the New York Post from the unit's founder warns that while Mamdani can reorganize the department, he cannot reorganize away the threats that demand fast, coordinated tactical response.

**The conservative case against this move is straightforward:** Dismantling specialized law enforcement capabilities doesn't eliminate crime or terrorism—it simply leaves cities less prepared to respond. This reflects the broader "defund the police" ideology that has gained traction among progressive Democrats, prioritizing activist demands over public safety.

**Colorado conservatives should take note.** While Colorado hasn't gone as far as New York, progressive cities like Denver have pushed similar anti-police rhetoric and budget cuts. The NYPD decision offers a cautionary tale: when ideology trumps operational necessity, citizens and law enforcement both pay the price.
