# Ohio Rep. Kaptur Hospitalized After Hit-and-Run Crash

Ohio Democratic Rep. Marcy Kaptur, 80, was hospitalized Sunday after a hit-and-run crash near Toledo—a reminder that no one, regardless of political office, is exempt from the dangers of the road.

The incident occurred around 10:45 a.m. local time in Toledo, Ohio's 9th Congressional District, which Kaptur represents. According to police and her office, Kaptur sustained non-life-threatening injuries and was one of two people transported to a nearby hospital for evaluation (Fox News, Washington Examiner). The crash remains under investigation, with authorities working to identify the hit-and-run driver.

Kaptur, a long-serving Democratic congresswoman, was en route to church when the crash occurred (Fox News). Her office has not yet disclosed additional details about the circumstances or her current condition beyond confirming the injuries are not life-threatening.

The incident underscores the ongoing problem of hit-and-run accidents across the country and the need for law enforcement to hold drivers accountable. Colorado drivers should remain vigilant on roadways and report any suspicious vehicle activity to local authorities.
