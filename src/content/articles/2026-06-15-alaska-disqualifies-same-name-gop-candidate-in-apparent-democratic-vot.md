# Alaska Election Official Disqualifies Same-Name GOP Candidate in Apparent Democratic Dirty Trick

Alaska's election director has ruled a Republican challenger ineligible for the Senate primary after determining his candidacy was a calculated scheme to confuse voters—not a genuine bid for office.

Dan J. Sullivan, who shares the name of incumbent Sen. Dan Sullivan (R-AK), was disqualified by Alaska Division of Elections Director Carol Beecher on Monday. According to Fox News and the Washington Examiner, Beecher found that the challenger deliberately chose an identical name and party affiliation to sow confusion at the ballot box, violating Alaska election law requiring candidates to file in "good faith."

Just The News reports that evidence presented to the election division showed the same-name candidate selected his name and party specifically because they matched the sitting senator's—a textbook voter-confusion tactic long associated with Democratic operatives seeking to split opposition votes.
