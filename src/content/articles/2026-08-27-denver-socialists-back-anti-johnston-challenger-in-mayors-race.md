# Denver Socialist Group Backs Anti-Johnston Challenger in Mayor's Race

The Denver Democratic Socialists of America have endorsed Shontel Lewis in her challenge to Mayor Mike Johnston, signaling left-wing pressure on the incumbent from the party's progressive flank (Denver Post). The DSA endorsement could provide meaningful momentum in a Democratic primary, given the group's track record of mobilizing voters in recent election cycles.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives should note the ideological direction this endorsement signals. The DSA represents the furthest-left faction of Colorado Democrats—advocates for expanded government programs, higher taxes, and aggressive regulation. An endorsement from this bloc suggests Lewis may be positioning herself to Johnston's left on spending, housing policy, and municipal control.

Johnston, despite his progressive credentials, has faced criticism from the far left over his handling of homelessness and public safety. The DSA's move indicates dissatisfaction among Denver's socialist wing with the pace of government intervention and spending.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners watching Denver's fiscal trajectory, this race matters: whoever wins will shape tax policy, regulatory burden, and municipal spending for the state's largest city. The DSA's involvement is a reminder that Denver's Democratic establishment faces constant pressure from activists demanding even more government expansion.

Watch whether Johnston responds by moving further left on spending and regulation to reclaim DSA support—or holds his ground.
