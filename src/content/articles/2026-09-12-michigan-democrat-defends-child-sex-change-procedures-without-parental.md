# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Broader Left-Wing Medical Overreach

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's full-throated defense of child sex-change procedures—without parental or government safeguards—exposes the progressive left's willingness to override parental rights and medical caution in the name of ideology.

Abdul El-Sayed, the Democratic nominee, argued Friday at Michigan State University that doctors alone should decide whether children undergo gender-transition treatments, dismissing government "interference" (Fox News, The Daily Wire). He invoked his medical credentials to justify the position.

Republicans and conservatives argue this stance recklessly prioritizes activist ideology over child welfare. They contend that irreversible medical procedures on minors demand parental consent, legislative guardrails, and genuine medical consensus—not deference to individual practitioners operating without oversight. Colorado conservatives have fought similar battles: the state's Democratic legislature has resisted parental-notification requirements and age-verification safeguards that majorities of Coloradans support.

El-Sayed's comments reflect a national Democratic pattern: treating gender ideology as settled medical science while dismissing legitimate concerns about long-term consequences, informed consent, and parental authority. Democrats counter that restricting access amounts to discrimination, but conservatives argue protecting children from experimental procedures is a parent's fundamental right.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats face similar pressure to clarify their stance on child gender procedures ahead of 2024—and whether Republican candidates make parental rights a centerpiece of their campaigns.
