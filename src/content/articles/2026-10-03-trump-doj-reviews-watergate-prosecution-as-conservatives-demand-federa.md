# Trump DOJ Revisits Nixon-Era Federal Overreach as Conservatives Demand Accountability

The Trump Justice Department is reviewing how federal prosecutors handled the Watergate investigation, signaling a broader conservative push to expose what Republicans argue was weaponization of government against political opponents. According to *The Daily Wire*, the DOJ is examining the conduct of the special prosecutor's office that investigated Nixon, part of a larger effort to root out what conservatives say is a pattern of federal agencies targeting conservative leaders.

*National Review* cautions that while Nixon was "flawed but accomplished," he shouldn't become a conservative role model—a warning that the right shouldn't use historical grievances to justify its own overreach.

The review reflects Republican frustration with what they see as selective prosecution and abuse of federal power. Conservatives argue the Watergate-era precedent established dangerous tools for targeting political enemies, tools they claim were later deployed against Trump and his allies.

Democrats have not yet responded substantively to the DOJ review, but the left will likely argue the Watergate investigation was justified and that comparing it to recent prosecutions is a false equivalence designed to delegitimize accountability.
