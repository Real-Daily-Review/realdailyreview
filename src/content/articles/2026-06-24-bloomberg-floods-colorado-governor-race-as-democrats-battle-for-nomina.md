# Bloomberg Cash Floods Colorado Governor's Race as Democrats Scramble for Nomination

A New York billionaire is bankrolling Colorado's Democratic primary, signaling national progressive anxiety over who will lead the state. Michael Bloomberg, the former New York mayor and failed 2020 presidential candidate, is pouring millions into the race (Complete Colorado), while U.S. Sen. Michael Bennet and Attorney General Phil Weiser compete for the Democratic nomination.

Bennet is leaning on his record as an elected official and government insider (Denver Post), while Weiser touts his legal battles against the Trump administration as a campaign credential (Denver Post). Both candidates represent the Democratic establishment's vision for Colorado governance.

**What conservatives should note:** Bloomberg's intervention signals national Democrats view Colorado as winnable but vulnerable—and they're willing to spend heavily to secure it. Weiser's emphasis on Trump-era litigation suggests Democrats plan to make the 2024 election and judicial activism central to their pitch, rather than defending Colorado's actual economic record under Democratic leadership.

Republicans should watch whether this primary battle weakens the eventual Democratic nominee or whether Bloomberg's money unites the left behind a single candidate before November.
