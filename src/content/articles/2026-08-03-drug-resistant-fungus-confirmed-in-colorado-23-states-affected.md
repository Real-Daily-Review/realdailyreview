# Drug-Resistant Fungus Spreads to Colorado as CDC Scrambles

Colorado is now among 23 states battling a deadly drug-resistant fungus that poses particular danger to hospitalized patients with weakened immune systems, according to new CDC data reported by Fox31 Denver and Just The News.

The fungal infection, which healthy people often fight off naturally, becomes life-threatening in hospital settings where patients' immune responses are compromised. Despite containment efforts, cases continue spreading across the country this year.

**The conservative concern:** This outbreak underscores why Colorado hospitals and healthcare providers need regulatory flexibility—not more federal mandates—to respond quickly to emerging threats. Colorado's medical community should be empowered to implement isolation protocols and treatment strategies tailored to our state's specific patient populations without waiting for Washington approval.
