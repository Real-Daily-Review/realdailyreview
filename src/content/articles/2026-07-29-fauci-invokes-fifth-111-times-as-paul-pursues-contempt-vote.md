# Fauci Pleads Fifth 111 Times as Paul Pushes Contempt Vote

Sen. Rand Paul is moving forward with a contempt-of-Congress vote against Dr. Anthony Fauci after the former NIH director invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times during Senate testimony, refusing to answer questions about gain-of-function research funding (Fox News). Paul ejected Fauci's lawyer from the hearing after repeated disruptions, signaling the Kentucky Republican's determination to force accountability on a figure conservatives blame for pandemic lockdowns and regulatory overreach that devastated Colorado's small businesses and energy sector.

Republicans argue Fauci's silence amounts to obstruction and demand transparency on federal funding of potentially dangerous research. Democrats and the establishment media counter that invoking Fifth Amendment rights is legally protected and suggest Paul's pursuit is politically motivated grandstanding.

The contempt vote faces procedural hurdles—the Department of Justice would ultimately decide whether to prosecute—but Paul's push reflects conservative frustration with unelected bureaucrats wielding unchecked power during the COVID crisis. Colorado taxpayers and business owners who suffered under pandemic restrictions should watch whether this accountability effort gains traction or stalls in a divided Congress.
