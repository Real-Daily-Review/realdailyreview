# AI Industry Leaders Call for Slower Development Pace Amid Safety Concerns

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is urging the artificial intelligence industry to pump the brakes—and surprisingly, competitors are listening. In a weekend essay, Amodei warned that AI capabilities are advancing faster than safety measures can keep pace, citing risks including potential internet takeover scenarios within six to 12 months (9News Denver). Both Sam Altman and Elon Musk signaled agreement within hours, according to Breitbart, suggesting rare consensus among tech's biggest players.

Amodei framed the call as prudence rather than panic, telling the Daily Caller he's "become convinced that fully addressing the risks requires even more prudence." The industry self-regulation message stands in sharp contrast to the regulatory push from Washington and progressive states eyeing AI oversight.

For Colorado, this matters: the state hosts growing AI and tech clusters in Denver and Boulder. If the industry voluntarily slows development, it could affect job growth and investment timelines in those hubs. Conversely, if federal regulators use this moment to impose heavy-handed rules—as Democrats have proposed—Colorado's tech workforce and startups could face compliance costs that favor big players like Anthropic over smaller innovators.
