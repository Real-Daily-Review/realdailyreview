# White House National Security Adviser Exits as Chinese Intel Official Buys Building Steps From Oval Office

A top White House national security official is heading for the exit just as reports surface of a Chinese Communist Party intelligence operative purchasing a historic building steps from the White House—raising serious questions about security lapses under the current administration.

According to *Just The News*, the national security adviser to Vice President JD Vance will leave in coming weeks after working closely with Vance on Iran negotiations. The *Daily Caller* reports he told associates, "I'll never be far away," suggesting continued advisory capacity.

More alarming: *Just The News* and the *Daily Caller* report that Philip Qiu and his family foundation—identified as connected to CCP intelligence—purchased the 100-year-old Securities Building on 15th Street in July for $8.4 million. The location puts the property steps from the White House complex.

**The conservative concern is stark:** How does a Chinese intelligence official acquire premium real estate adjacent to the nation's command center? The *Daily Caller* raises the specter of surveillance capabilities—"hidden drone swarms or shipping container missiles"—that such proximity could enable. This isn't theoretical; it's a counterintelligence nightmare.

Republicans and national security hawks will demand answers on vetting failures, CFIUS (Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States) review processes, and whether the administration adequately screened this transaction.

**Watch for:** Congressional Republicans demanding a full accounting of how this purchase cleared any security review—and whether similar CCP acquisitions near federal facilities have gone undetected.
