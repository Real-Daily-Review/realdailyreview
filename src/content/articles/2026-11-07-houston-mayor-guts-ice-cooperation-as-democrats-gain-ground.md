# Texas Mayor Abandons ICE Cooperation as Blue Wave Emboldens Open-Border Left

Houston's Democratic mayor just handed illegal immigrants a get-out-of-jail card—and Colorado should take note of what happens when progressive policies go unchecked.

Days after Texas voters delivered a stunning rebuke to Republican leadership, Houston Mayor John Whitmire signed an order severely restricting local police cooperation with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (Fox News, Daily Wire). The directive bars officers from notifying ICE during nonviolent stops, effectively allowing some illegal immigrants to walk free rather than face deportation proceedings.

The move directly contradicts Gov. Greg Abbott's push for tighter immigration enforcement across Texas. Republicans argue that sanctuary-city policies like Whitmire's undermine border security and create havoc for law enforcement trying to maintain public order. Conservatives warn that when cities prioritize illegal immigrants over citizen safety, crime and social costs spike—burdens ultimately borne by taxpayers.

Democrats counter that cooperation with ICE separates families and disproportionately harms immigrant communities. The left frames such restrictions as humanitarian policy.

**The Colorado angle:** As progressive Democrats gain ground in Colorado politics, similar sanctuary-city proposals could resurface in Denver and Boulder. Local law enforcement cooperation with federal immigration authorities remains contentious here, and Whitmire's order signals how emboldened Democrats become after electoral wins.
