# Denver Police Arrest Second Suspect in Easter Park Shooting

Denver Police have arrested a second suspect in the April Easter Sunday shooting death of 18-year-old Pharrow Ware at Russell Square Park, nearly three months after the fatal incident.

Dazirius Wilford, 18, is being held for investigation of first-degree murder (9News Denver). The arrest follows an earlier apprehension in connection with the case, according to reporting from KDVR Fox31 Denver.

The slow pace of arrests—nearly a quarter-year after the shooting—underscores persistent public safety challenges in Denver's neighborhoods. While police deserve credit for pursuing leads, the timeline raises questions about investigative resources and the broader spike in violent crime that has plagued the city in recent years.

Colorado conservatives have long argued that Denver's progressive criminal justice policies—including reduced bail requirements and early release programs—have failed to deter violent offenders or protect residents. Public safety advocates point to cases like this as evidence that tough-on-crime enforcement and swift prosecution matter.

Wilford's arrest represents progress, but Coloradans should watch whether prosecutors pursue maximum charges and whether the case moves swiftly through the courts—a real test of whether Denver's justice system can hold violent criminals accountable.
