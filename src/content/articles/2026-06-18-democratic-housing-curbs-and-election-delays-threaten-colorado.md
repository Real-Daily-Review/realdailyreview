# Democrats' Housing Restrictions and Election Chaos Should Alarm Colorado Voters

Liberal environmental mandates are strangling affordable housing development while Democrats push voting systems that leave elections in limbo for weeks—two cautionary tales for Colorado.

Democrats claim to champion affordable housing, yet their stringent environmental restrictions make new construction prohibitively expensive and slow. As *National Review* reports, these regulations effectively kill the supply-side solutions that could actually lower housing costs. Colorado's Front Range communities face similar pressures: zoning delays, environmental reviews, and permitting gridlock drive up development costs that get passed directly to homebuyers and renters.

Meanwhile, Democrats are pushing voting procedures modeled on California's chaos. Los Angeles took more than two weeks to count ballots from its June 2 election—a "running joke" that the *Washington Examiner* warns Democrats want to nationalize. Colorado election officials should take note: mail-in voting expansion without robust chain-of-custody protocols and faster counting timelines invites the same confusion and erodes voter confidence.

Republicans argue these policies reflect progressive priorities—environmental activism and voting procedures that favor mail-in ballots—that consistently backfire on ordinary people. Coloradans pay more for housing and wait longer for election results.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado Democrats push similar mail-in voting expansions or environmental restrictions that further constrain housing supply in 2026.
