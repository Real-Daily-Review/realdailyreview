# Colorado Voters Back Trans Sports Ban, But Questions Loom on Implementation

Colorado voters have decisively approved Proposition 134, a measure restricting transgender students from competing on school sports teams aligned with their gender identity rather than biological sex—a rare conservative win in a state trending blue (Denver Post, AP call at 90% counted).

The ballot measure reflects growing national pushback against progressive gender ideology in schools. Conservatives argue that fairness in women's sports demands sex-based eligibility rules, protecting opportunities for biological females. Democrats and progressive advocates counter that the law discriminates against transgender youth and creates enforcement nightmares for school districts already stretched thin.

School districts now face the hard work: implementing eligibility standards, handling disputes, and navigating potential legal challenges. CPR News reports families and administrators are already grappling with unanswered questions about compliance timelines and dispute resolution.

The win comes as Colorado's congressional map remains firmly Democratic-controlled—voters are sending one Republican and seven Democrats to Congress (Denver Post). Republicans had pushed Initiative 256 to impose anti-gerrymandering guardrails on redistricting, but that effort stalled.

**Watch whether Colorado school districts move swiftly to implement Prop 134 or face legal delays that could leave the rule in limbo through the 2027 school year.**
