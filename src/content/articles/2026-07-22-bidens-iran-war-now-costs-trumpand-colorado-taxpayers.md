# Biden's Iran Mess Now Falls to Trump—and Colorado Taxpayers Foot the Bill

The Iran conflict that's now consuming congressional attention and defense dollars originated under the Biden administration, not Trump, according to conservative analysts tracking the escalation (Washington Examiner). As costs mount and Congress debates spending to cover military operations, Democrats are already pivoting blame: Sen. Tammy Duckworth (D-IL) told CNN the deaths of 18 American servicemembers are "Trump's fault" (Breitbart), while Sen. Chris Van Hollen (D-MD) claimed the Trump administration has "no idea what they're doing" in Iran (Breitbart).

The reality conservatives point to is simpler: Biden's weak posture invited Iranian aggression. Now Trump inherits both the conflict and the bill—one that Colorado taxpayers will help finance through federal spending that could otherwise reduce the deficit or fund tax relief.

This matters to Coloradans because every dollar Congress spends on foreign military campaigns is a dollar not returned to working families or invested in border security and domestic priorities. Republicans argue the Biden administration's failed deterrence created this costly mess; Democrats blame Trump for managing it poorly.

Watch for the final congressional spending bill: will Republicans demand fiscal accountability, or will bipartisan war spending pass without serious debate on costs?
