# Burberry, Stellantis Signal Recovery Momentum With Turnaround Plans

Luxury fashion and automotive companies are signaling renewed confidence in their turnaround efforts, with both reporting positive momentum in recent weeks.

Burberry upgraded its earnings outlook after sales returned to growth in the Americas, according to the Wall Street Journal. The company attributed the improvement to its brand revamp strategy, which showed positive signs during the key holiday period.

Separately, Stellantis CEO Antonio Filosa unveiled a five-year strategic plan worth 60 billion euros (approximately $69.7 billion), according to CNBC. The automaker is targeting positive cash flow by 2028 as part of the initiative.

Both moves reflect efforts by major corporations to stabilize operations after recent challenges. Burberry's return to sales growth in a key market suggests its rebranding efforts are gaining traction with consumers. Stellantis' substantial investment commitment signals confidence in its ability to navigate industry headwinds, though the company's path to positive cash flow extends several years into the future.

Watch for quarterly earnings reports from both companies to validate whether these positive signals translate into sustained financial improvement.
