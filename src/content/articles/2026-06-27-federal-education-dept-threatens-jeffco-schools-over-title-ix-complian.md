# Federal Overreach Targets Colorado Schools While Transit Safety Crumbles Elsewhere

The Biden administration's Department of Education is threatening Jefferson County Public Schools with federal enforcement action over Title IX compliance—yet another example of Washington bureaucrats micromanaging local school districts instead of letting parents and elected boards run their own schools.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, the Office for Civil Rights issued a "Letter of Impending Enforcement" against Jeffco schools, alleging Title IX violations. Conservatives argue the federal government has weaponized Title IX enforcement to impose progressive gender ideology on Colorado classrooms, overriding parental rights and local control. Jeffco officials have responded to the letter, but the threat of federal sanctions—and potential funding cuts—illustrates how Washington uses the purse to coerce compliance with its agenda.

Meanwhile, Transportation Secretary Duffy is reviewing the Federal Transit Administration's safety protocols after a brutal killing on a MARTA train (Fox News Politics), raising questions about whether federal agencies can enforce basic public safety before lecturing local schools on ideological compliance.

Colorado taxpayers foot the bill for both federal mandates and local school operations. When Washington threatens to withhold education dollars over contested civil rights interpretations, it's local families who suffer—not distant bureaucrats.

Watch whether Jeffco capitulates to federal pressure or stands firm on local governance and parental input.
