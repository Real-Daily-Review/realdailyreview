# 9/11 Families Push Congress on Victim Support Fund as Deadline Looms

Congress is running out of time to fund a critical compensation program for 9/11 victims before a September 30 deadline—and families are taking their case directly to Republican leadership.

Angela Mistrulli, daughter of a 9/11 victim, and other family members are pressing lawmakers at the Republican convention to fully fund the U.S. Victims of State Sponsored Terrorism Fund (USVSST) before the clock runs out, according to Fox News Politics. The families argue they've been fighting for justice for 25 years and deserve the resources promised to them.

Meanwhile, the broader Middle East policy debate reveals Washington may be stuck in outdated thinking. National Review reports that U.S. foreign policy continues to focus on cross-border networks and regional conflicts, even as the region has fundamentally shifted toward state-sponsored defense industry expansion. The mismatch between Cold War-era strategy and current regional realities suggests policymakers need a strategic reset.

For Colorado, this matters: federal spending priorities and how America allocates resources for both victim compensation and defense shape tax burdens on Colorado families and workers. Republicans should ensure that commitments to 9/11 families are honored without creating new entitlements, while simultaneously demanding that defense spending actually reflects current threats rather than yesterday's playbook.

Watch whether Congress acts before September 30 or lets another deadline slip.
