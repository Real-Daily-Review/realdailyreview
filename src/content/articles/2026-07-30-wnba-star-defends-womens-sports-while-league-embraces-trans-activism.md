# WNBA Star Cunningham Stands Firm on Women's Sports While League Doubles Down on Trans Activism

Sophie Cunningham of the Indiana Fever has ignited a culture war within professional basketball by defending the exclusion of biological males from women's sports—a position Senate Democrats have largely avoided taking publicly. In response, Seattle Storm center Stefanie Dolson wore a "Trans Rights Are Human Rights" shirt Tuesday as part of a league-sponsored Alaska Airlines campaign, signaling the WNBA's institutional embrace of transgender activism over athlete safety concerns.

Cunningham's stance aligns with conservative arguments that women's sports integrity depends on maintaining sex-based categories. Republicans and sports advocates argue that allowing biological males to compete in women's leagues undermines fair competition and erodes opportunities for female athletes—a position Democrats have been reluctant to defend openly, even as progressive organizations push the opposite direction.
