# Federal Immigration Enforcement Intensifies as Criminal Alien Arrests Spike Nationwide

Federal immigration enforcement is ramping up dramatically, with ICE officers arresting hundreds of criminal aliens and preventing serious crimes—a stark contrast to the open-border policies that plagued Democratic-run jurisdictions during the Biden years.

In May alone, ICE-Houston arrested 735 criminal illegal aliens accounting for nearly 1,200 violent convictions, including murderers, rapists, child predators, and gang members from MS-13, Surenos 13, and other organizations (Breitbart). The enforcement push follows documented crime waves in sanctuary cities: Virginia under Rep. Abigail Spanberger recorded 17 serious crimes by illegal immigrants in 2026, with nine in Fairfax County alone (Fox News).

The crackdown includes fatal confrontations. ICE officers shot and killed Lorenzo Salgado Araujo, a Mexican national, during a Houston traffic stop after he allegedly rammed a vehicle (Washington Examiner). Critics argue the Trump administration's "heavy-handed" tactics warrant scrutiny (KDVR), but conservatives counter that enforcing immigration law and protecting citizens from criminal aliens is Washington's basic duty—one Democrats abdicated for years.
