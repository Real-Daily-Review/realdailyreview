# Fauci Diary Claims Raise Questions About Vaccine Rollout Timing

Anthony Fauci's diary entries allegedly reveal he coordinated with left-leaning officials to delay COVID vaccine distribution in 2020, according to reporting by *The Daily Wire*. The entries reportedly show Fauci expressed disapproval of Trump's focus on reopening the economy and the 2020 election, raising conservative concerns about whether public health decisions were influenced by political considerations.

Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. has cited the diary to claim Fauci experienced a pulmonary infarction months after receiving the vaccine, *Just The News* reports.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans argue the diary entries suggest Fauci prioritized political damage to Trump over accelerating vaccine availability to Americans. If accurate, the timeline raises questions about whether the vaccine rollout was delayed for reasons unrelated to public health—a charge that would undermine public trust in federal health agencies and vindicate critics who questioned the politicization of COVID response.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** Coloradans who lost jobs or businesses during lockdowns, and those skeptical of federal health mandates, view this as potential vindication that decisions weren't purely scientific.

Watch for congressional Republicans demanding full transparency on Fauci's communications with Biden administration officials during the vaccine rollout period.
