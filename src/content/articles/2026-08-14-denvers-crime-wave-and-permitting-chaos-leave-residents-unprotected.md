# Denver's Public Safety Crisis Deepens as City Struggles With Violence and Disorder

Denver's crime wave continues unabated, with a fatal crash and a shooting death within hours of each other this week—the latest evidence that the city's progressive leadership has failed to maintain basic public order.

On Thursday, one person was killed in a crash near East 17th Avenue Parkway and Glencoe Street in the Park Hill neighborhood (9News Denver). That same evening, a 19-year-old was shot and killed while sitting in a car in the 4100 block of West Custer Place, with Denver Police still investigating (9News Denver).

Meanwhile, neighborhood residents are fighting back against city permitting decisions they see as reckless. A Denver neighborhood group is actively opposing a license application to open an escort service in their community (KDVR Fox31), highlighting how residents feel abandoned by city hall on quality-of-life issues.

Conservatives argue Denver's permitting and licensing processes have become divorced from neighborhood safety and character. While the city prioritizes progressive regulatory agendas, violent crime persists and residents lose faith in municipal government to protect them or respect their neighborhoods' integrity.
