# Trump Warns GOP: Don't Let Democrats Nuke the Filibuster First

Former President Trump is sounding the alarm that Republicans must end the Senate filibuster before Democrats do—or risk permanent minority status. Trump's warning comes after Michigan Democrat Abdulrahman El-Sayed, whom Trump labeled a "Communist," won his party's Senate primary this week and has signaled support for eliminating the 60-vote threshold (Just The News, Breitbart).

Trump's logic: if Democrats abolish the filibuster first under a future Democratic president, Republicans could face irreversible legislative disadvantage. "It could make me the last Republican President ever," Trump warned, according to Breitbart.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue the filibuster has become a one-way ratchet—Democrats threaten to nuke it whenever it blocks their agenda, while Republicans hesitate to use the same tool. Conservatives say preemptive action protects GOP legislative power. Critics counter that eliminating the filibuster would enable radical swings in policy with each election cycle, destabilizing governance.

**Colorado angle:** Any filibuster change directly affects Colorado's ability to block or advance energy policy, water compacts, and Second Amendment legislation. A weakened filibuster could accelerate progressive environmental mandates or, conversely, enable Republican energy development policies.

Watch whether Senate Republicans actually move to end the filibuster if they retain control—or whether Trump's warning becomes another high-stakes bluff.
