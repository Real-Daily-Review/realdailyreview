# Marxist Activists Block AI Infrastructure While Colorado Faces Data Center Boom

A Virginia grassroots campaign against an AI data center reveals how far-left organizers are weaponizing environmental and community concerns to block critical infrastructure—a playbook Colorado conservatives should watch closely as the state becomes a hub for data center development.

According to Fox News Politics, the opposition campaign in Frederick County, Virginia, is led by activists tied to the Party of Socialism and Liberation, a Marxist-Leninist organization. The effort frames itself around "foreign influence concerns," but Republicans argue this is ideological obstruction dressed in populist language designed to halt private investment and technological advancement.

**The Colorado angle:** As data centers proliferate across Colorado—drawn by cheap power, fiber infrastructure, and business-friendly policies—similar left-wing coalitions could mobilize against future projects. Progressive activists have already targeted energy infrastructure in the state; AI facilities could become the next battleground.

Conservatives counter that blocking data centers means surrendering economic growth, high-wage jobs, and tax revenue to other states. The infrastructure supports everything from AI innovation to cloud computing that powers American competitiveness against China. Regulatory delays and activist obstruction only weaken U.S. technological leadership.

Colorado taxpayers and business leaders should monitor whether similar Marxist-aligned groups attempt to organize against proposed data center projects—and whether state and local officials will prioritize economic development or capitulate to radical activists.
