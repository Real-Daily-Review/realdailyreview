# Colorado Must Demand Border Enforcement Before More Preventable Deaths

Colorado voters should demand their elected officials stop treating illegal immigration as a victimless policy debate—because the bodies are piling up across America.

A Vietnam veteran in Philadelphia faces charges for shooting an illegal immigrant allegedly tied to an international fraud ring targeting homeowners. Meanwhile, an illegal immigrant from India received less than half the maximum sentence after killing three people in a California semi-truck crash (The Daily Wire). In Minnesota, Gov. Tim Walz (D) pardoned an illegal immigrant child molester from Laos—a decision State Department spokesman Tommy Pigott called "completely inexplicable" (Breitbart).

These aren't abstract policy disagreements. They're preventable tragedies that expose the failure of open-border rhetoric.

**Republicans argue** that sanctuary policies and lenient sentencing embolden criminal aliens and endanger American citizens. They point to these cases as evidence that Democratic governors prioritize illegal immigrants over public safety. **Democrats counter** that these are isolated incidents and that immigration enforcement should focus on violent felons, not all undocumented residents.

For Colorado, the stakes are real: our highways, construction sites, and neighborhoods are affected by federal immigration policy failures. Colorado conservatives should watch whether our state's Democratic leadership will demand accountability from Washington or continue enabling the status quo that leaves citizens vulnerable.
