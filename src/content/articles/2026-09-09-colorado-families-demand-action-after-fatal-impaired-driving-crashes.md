# Colorado Families Demand Accountability After Impaired Driving Deaths

A Greeley family is grieving after a fatal crash claimed two lives, reigniting the conversation about impaired driving enforcement and border security failures that put Colorado families at risk.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, the daughter of one victim is urging drivers to reconsider getting behind the wheel while impaired. The tragedy underscores a preventable loss that Colorado law enforcement and prosecutors take seriously.

But the broader picture is more troubling. The Daily Wire reports that a similar fatal crash in North Texas involved a previously deported illegal immigrant driving drunk in a stolen vehicle—a case that raises hard questions about immigration enforcement and repeat offenders.

**Conservatives argue:** When illegal aliens with criminal histories remain in the country and commit violent crimes, it's a failure of federal immigration enforcement and border security. Colorado families shouldn't bear the cost of lax deportation policies. Republicans have long warned that sanctuary policies and weak enforcement create preventable tragedies.

**Democrats counter** that impaired driving is a broader public health issue affecting all demographics, and that focusing on immigration status distracts from universal drunk-driving prevention.

The reality for Colorado taxpayers: whether the driver is a citizen or a deportable alien, impaired driving kills. Stronger enforcement—both of DUI laws and immigration removal orders—matters.

Watch for how Colorado lawmakers respond to calls for tougher penalties on repeat DUI offenders and whether they'll demand federal cooperation on removing criminal aliens.
