# GOP Rep. Massie Revives Hegseth Impeachment Push—But Faces Uphill Battle

A Kentucky Republican is forcing a House vote on impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, signaling internal GOP fractures over military leadership and foreign policy direction.

Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.) reintroduced articles of impeachment against Hegseth this week, citing concerns over Iran war policy and constitutional overreach, according to the *New York Post* and *Washington Examiner*. Massie told *The Post* he believes the resolution has a better chance of passage after the election than before—a telling admission about its current viability.

**The conservative case for scrutiny:** Massie and his allies argue Hegseth poses a threat to constitutional limits on executive war powers. They want rigorous oversight of the Defense Department's direction.

**The Republican establishment's position:** Most GOP leadership backs Hegseth and views the impeachment push as a distraction from party priorities. Few Republicans are expected to vote for removal.

The resolution highlights a real divide within conservative ranks over military interventionism and presidential authority—a debate Colorado's Republican delegation will likely face as defense spending and military readiness remain central to GOP messaging.

Watch whether this gains any traction in committee or if it dies quietly, revealing how much appetite exists for genuine constitutional pushback on executive war powers versus party loyalty.
