# Socialist Candidates Surge in Democratic Primaries as Trump Warns of Federal Intervention

**Far-left activists are winning Democratic endorsements across major cities, signaling the party's leftward drift on crime, borders, and government control.**

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is backing congressional candidate Darializa Avila Chevalier despite resurfaced deleted posts in which Chevalier attacked the U.S., police, and border enforcement (Fox News). Meanwhile, self-described socialist Janeese Lewis George is competing in Washington, D.C.'s Democratic mayoral primary, prompting President Trump to vow federal intervention if she wins (Washington Examiner).

Republicans argue these endorsements expose the Democratic Party's embrace of anti-American radicalism. Conservatives say socialist candidates openly hostile to law enforcement and national sovereignty represent the party's true direction—not a fringe minority. The pattern reflects how progressive activists have captured Democratic primary machinery in major cities.

For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats increasingly align with national progressive orthodoxy on energy policy, gun control, and government expansion. If socialist-backed candidates gain traction in Democratic primaries nationwide, Colorado's Democratic leadership—already among the nation's most left-leaning—will face pressure to match their radicalism or risk primary challenges from the left.
