# Republican Pushes Declaration of Independence Reaffirmation as Socialist Threat Looms

Rep. Matt Van Epps is introducing legislation to reaffirm the Declaration of Independence ahead of America's 250th birthday—a direct response to socialist candidates gaining traction in Democratic primaries, according to Fox News. The move signals Republican concern that foundational American principles are under assault from the left.

As the nation marks its semiquincentennial on July 4th, celebrations are underway across the country. The Washington Examiner reports that foreign allies are preparing commemorative gestures, though those celebrations occur against a backdrop of tariff disputes and uncertainty over Trump administration foreign policy. Meanwhile, cultural institutions like Monticello are drawing visitors to reflect on Jefferson's legacy, and classic American symbols—from Ford vehicles to Union Station—are being featured in patriotic displays, per the Daily Caller.

For Colorado conservatives, Van Epps's bill underscores a broader Republican argument: that progressive ideology increasingly rejects the Constitution's limits on government power and individual liberty. The Declaration's assertion of natural rights and limited government stands in direct opposition to expansive state control—precisely what Colorado taxpayers and business owners have resisted through ballot measures and legislative fights over the past decade.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly in competitive districts—embraces or distances itself from this reaffirmation effort.
