# Violence Plagues Homecoming Celebrations Across Nation

Colorado families should take note: homecoming season is turning into a shooting gallery in cities across America, and the pattern reveals a law-and-order crisis Democrats refuse to address.

Two separate incidents this week left multiple dead and wounded. In Vienna, Georgia, gunfire erupted at a homecoming block party around 1:20 a.m., killing two and hospitalizing 25 others—despite deputies already on scene to monitor the crowd (9News Denver). Meanwhile, outside a Minneapolis homecoming game, Somali gangs opened fire, wounding two teens and forcing event cancellations (New York Post).

**The conservative argument:** These shootings underscore why Second Amendment rights matter and why soft-on-crime policies fail. Democrats in urban centers have gutted police budgets, eliminated bail requirements, and refused to prosecute gang violence aggressively. The result: criminals operate with impunity while law-abiding citizens—and teenagers at school events—become targets.

Republicans argue that restoring order requires three things: fully funded police departments, swift prosecution of violent offenders, and respect for citizens' right to self-defense. Colorado communities hosting homecoming events should demand their local leaders commit to visible law enforcement presence and zero tolerance for gang activity.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership proposes meaningful gang enforcement measures or retreats into the same failed "root causes" rhetoric that has left other cities drowning in violence.
