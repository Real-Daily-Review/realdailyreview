# Trump Demands Investigation Into Commerce Department GDP Revisions

Trump is calling for a federal investigation into the Commerce Department's handling of pre-election GDP data, alleging the agency manipulated figures to boost the administration's political standing ahead of the midterms.

The Commerce Department's Bureau of Economic Analysis revised third-quarter GDP growth upward this week, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Trump, without presenting evidence (*9News Denver*), is demanding an investigation into whether the figures were deliberately falsified before voters went to the polls.

**The conservative concern:** Republicans have long questioned whether federal agencies politicize economic data to serve sitting administrations. If the Commerce Department inflated growth numbers before an election, it would undermine public trust in official statistics—data Colorado businesses, investors, and policymakers rely on for real decisions about hiring, expansion, and capital allocation.

Democrats would likely counter that revised GDP estimates are routine and reflect improved data collection, not manipulation. The timing, however, fuels skepticism among conservatives already wary of government credibility.

For Colorado's economy—dependent on accurate forecasting for energy, agriculture, and tourism sectors—politicized federal data poses real risks to long-term planning and investment confidence.

Watch whether congressional Republicans launch a formal audit of Commerce Department methodology and whether the agency releases internal communications from the revision period.
