# Fauci's Fall: Conservative Reckoning Over COVID's Architect

Anthony Fauci's sudden exile from the left's good graces marks a stunning reversal for the bureaucrat who shaped America's pandemic response. *National Review* reports the celebrity doctor has discovered he was merely "a tool in his political allies' hands"—useful when lockdowns and mandates served progressive ends, disposable once the political winds shifted.

The reckoning is overdue. Fauci's invocation of the Fifth Amendment 111 times before Sen. Rand Paul's (R-KY) homeland security panel, combined with released diary entries, has exposed contradictions conservatives flagged for years: shifting mask guidance, suppression of the lab-leak theory, and regulatory overreach dressed up as science. *Washington Examiner* notes Americans can now assess COVID policy "with 20/20 hindsight"—and the picture is damning.

Colorado taxpayers and business owners paid the price for Fauci-endorsed lockdowns that devastated the state's economy, shuttered schools, and trampled individual liberty. Republicans argue his testimony and documents prove the pandemic response was driven by politics, not epidemiology—a lesson for resisting future government health mandates.

The left's abandonment of Fauci reveals the cynical machinery behind progressive COVID policy: deploy experts selectively, discard them when inconvenient, and move on without accountability.
