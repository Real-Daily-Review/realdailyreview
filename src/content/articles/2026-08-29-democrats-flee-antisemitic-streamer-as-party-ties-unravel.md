# Democrats Scramble as Party's Ties to Antisemitic Streamer Unravel

Colorado Democrats should take note: the national party's credibility problem just got worse. Democratic figures are now publicly distancing themselves from podcaster Hasan Piker after his antisemitic remarks surfaced, but the damage reveals how deeply progressive activists have embedded themselves in Democratic campaigns.

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries and other party figures have condemned Piker's comments, yet the streamer maintains close ties to multiple Democratic candidates and officeholders. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA), positioning himself for a presidential run, has cultivated a media presence that overlaps significantly with Piker's audience and activism circles. When Democratic Senate nominee Abdul El-Sayed tried dismissing Piker as merely "a streamer in California," MSNBC's Katy Tur pushed back—noting that Piker actively campaigns with numerous Democrats across the country (Breitbart).

The pattern is clear: Democratic candidates court activist influencers for reach and energy, then scramble to condemn them when controversy erupts. Republicans argue this exposes the left's real priorities and the company it keeps.

For Colorado voters and taxpayers, the lesson is straightforward: watch which state Democrats maintain ties to national progressive activists and influencers, and demand they answer for those associations directly.
