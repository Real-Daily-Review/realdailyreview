# California's Progressive Failures Offer Colorado a Cautionary Tale

California's sanctuary policies are backfiring spectacularly—and Colorado should take note before following the same path.

ICE officers are conducting enforcement operations at California community colleges, prompting panic among administrators who've grown accustomed to shielding illegal immigrants from federal law (Washington Examiner). Republicans argue that California Democrats have deliberately incentivized illegal immigration through special privileges and sanctuary policies, creating the very conditions that trigger ICE raids. The state's approach treats federal immigration law as optional—a posture that destabilizes communities and invites federal intervention.

Meanwhile, California's bullet train project continues hemorrhaging credibility. After breaking ground in 2015, the state's flagship infrastructure boondoggle now faces legal challenges over route changes (New York Post), adding to years of cost overruns and delays that have become synonymous with progressive megaproject mismanagement.

Conservatives argue these twin disasters—sanctuary chaos and infrastructure incompetence—stem from the same root: government overreach divorced from fiscal reality and rule of law. California throws billions at projects that never materialize while creating legal gray zones that invite federal enforcement actions.

**Colorado takeaway:** As progressive lawmakers push similar sanctuary measures and infrastructure spending sprees, taxpayers should demand hard timelines, real accountability, and respect for federal law—not California's cautionary model.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats attempt to expand sanctuary protections or green-light major transit projects without binding cost controls.
