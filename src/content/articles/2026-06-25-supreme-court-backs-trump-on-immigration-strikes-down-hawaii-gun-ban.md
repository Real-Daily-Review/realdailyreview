# Supreme Court Hands Trump Major Wins on Immigration, Second Amendment

The Supreme Court has delivered three consecutive victories for the Trump administration and constitutional conservatives, striking down progressive immigration policies and affirming gun rights in rulings that will reshape federal enforcement and state gun control efforts nationwide.

In a 6-3 decision, the Court ruled that migrants stopped on the Mexican side of the U.S.-Mexico border who have not physically entered American soil cannot automatically claim asylum protections, giving the administration broad authority to turn back asylum seekers at the southern border (New York Post). The same ideological split upheld Trump's power to end Temporary Protected Status for Haiti and Syria, with the Court finding that the homeland security secretary's determination cannot be challenged in court (Washington Examiner).

On the Second Amendment, the Court struck down Hawaii's sweeping gun law requiring permits to carry firearms on private property—a major victory for gun rights activists and a rebuke to state-level gun control schemes (Washington Examiner). Justice Samuel Alito's 6-3 majority opinion found the law unconstitutional under the Second Amendment.

**Colorado angle:** These rulings reinforce Second Amendment protections that Colorado gun owners have fought to preserve against progressive restrictions, while strengthening border enforcement that affects Colorado's economy and communities.
