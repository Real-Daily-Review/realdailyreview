# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Trial; Conservatives Warn Against Prop 137 Land Grab

A Colorado judge has ruled there's sufficient evidence to prosecute Brandon Booth, an ex-detention center guard accused of shooting toward protesters who made inflammatory remarks about "race traitors" and threatened to locate guards' children, according to the Colorado Sun. The case underscores tensions around immigration enforcement facilities in the state.

Meanwhile, conservatives are sounding the alarm over Proposition 137, a ballot measure that would funnel Colorado taxpayer dollars to wealthy environmental nonprofits under the guise of land conservation. Complete Colorado warns that The Nature Conservancy—the initiative's primary backer—stands to become a major beneficiary of public funds, raising questions about whether this represents genuine conservation or corporate welfare disguised as environmental stewardship.

Conservatives argue Prop 137 exemplifies how progressive ballot measures often benefit well-connected special interests while expanding government spending and reducing local control over land-use decisions. Taxpayers should scrutinize the fine print: who profits, how much state money flows out, and whether Colorado's land management improves or simply enriches activist organizations.

Watch for the final weeks before Election Day, when both the Booth trial developments and Prop 137's funding sources will likely dominate Colorado political discourse.
