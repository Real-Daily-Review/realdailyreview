# Trump Doubles Down on Iran at UN as Colorado Diesel Prices Spike

President Trump defended his Iran military campaign at the United Nations this week, presenting a stark choice: negotiate a deal or face complete economic isolation of Tehran. Speaking to world leaders, Trump touted the military's success in degrading Iranian capabilities, declaring they were "the bully of the Middle East, but they are the bully no more" (Just The News). He called for international economic pressure until Iran halts attacks on commercial shipping, abandons nuclear ambitions, and stops funding terror groups.

The aggressive posture came after Trump warned he may "annihilate" Iran if diplomacy fails, signaling willingness to escalate further. US and Iranian officials met Tuesday following the address, though substantive progress remains unclear.

For Colorado, the timing stings. Diesel prices have skyrocketed in September, hitting trucking companies, farmers, and small businesses hard across the state. Energy-dependent sectors—already battered by regulatory overreach in Denver—face mounting fuel costs tied to Middle East tensions.

Republicans are growing restless with the Iran project, according to polling, raising questions about domestic political sustainability for an extended conflict.
