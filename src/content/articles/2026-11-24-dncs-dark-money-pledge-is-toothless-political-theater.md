# DNC's Dark Money Theater Won't Stop the Cash Flood

The Democratic National Committee is demanding 2028 primary candidates denounce super PAC and 501(c)(4) spending to qualify for debate stages—a performative gesture that exposes the left's hypocrisy on campaign finance.

Here's the catch: the DNC has zero enforcement power. As the *Washington Examiner* reports, the party cannot actually stop dark money from flowing into the race, meaning candidates can sign a pledge and still benefit from unlimited outside spending. It's a rhetorical fig leaf masking the reality that Democrats, like Republicans, rely on the very funding streams they publicly condemn.

Republicans have long argued that campaign finance restrictions are unworkable theater—and this rule proves it. You cannot ban speech through debate stage requirements when the First Amendment protects independent political spending. The DNC's approach amounts to asking candidates to lie about their funding ecosystem while doing nothing to change it.

For Colorado voters, this matters: our state's Democratic primary electorate will hear candidates mouth anti-dark-money platitudes while secretly courting the same donors they're denouncing. It's the kind of political fakery that erodes trust in both parties.

Watch whether any 2028 Democratic candidate actually refuses dark money support—or whether this pledge becomes another broken campaign promise.
