# San Diego Mosque Shooting Leaves Three Dead; Attackers Motivated by Broad Hatred

A shooting at the Islamic Center of San Diego on Monday killed three people, including security guard Amin Abdullah, a father of eight who was hailed for his actions during the attack. According to the BBC, Abdullah was described as "a shining light," while his daughter remembered him as the "absolute best dad in the world" (Al Jazeera).

Two other victims were also killed in the incident, according to CBS News. Investigators are still working to identify all those affected.

The attackers were teenagers whose motivations extended beyond a single target. "These subjects did not discriminate in who they hated," said Mark Remily, special agent in charge of the FBI's San Diego Field Office, according to CBS News. The FBI's characterization suggests the perpetrators were driven by broad ideological hatred rather than a specific grievance.

Details about the attackers' identities and exact motivations remain under investigation. Authorities have not yet released comprehensive information about the full scope of the investigation or potential charges.
