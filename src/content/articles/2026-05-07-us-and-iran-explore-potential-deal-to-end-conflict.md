# U.S. and Iran Explore Potential Deal to End Conflict

Diplomatic efforts are underway to resolve the U.S.-Iran conflict, with Pakistan expressing optimism about an agreement. Pakistani Foreign Ministry spokesperson Tahir Andrabi told NPR that "our hope and expectation is for an agreement sooner rather than later."

According to the Financial Times, Tehran is reviewing a Washington-backed proposal that President Donald Trump says could end the conflict. Trump has expressed optimism about reaching a deal, though Iran has not yet formally responded to the proposal, according to Deutsche Welle.

The potential agreement has already moved markets: oil prices are hovering around $100 per barrel on hopes of a resolution, the Financial Times reports.

PBS NewsHour convened experts to examine the proposal, including Alan Eyre of the Middle East Institute, who served on the Obama administration's negotiating team for the previous Iran nuclear deal, and Miad Maleki, a senior fellow at the Foundation for the Defense of Democracies, to provide additional perspective on the talks.

The negotiations mark a significant diplomatic moment after prolonged tensions between Washington and Tehran. Watch for Iran's formal response to the U.S. proposal and any statements from Tehran on the terms under discussion.
