# Colorado Senate Candidate Graham Platner's Implosion Exposes Democratic Vetting Failure

Graham Platner's spectacular collapse as a Democratic Senate candidate—marked by Nazi-linked tattoos, abuse allegations, and rape claims—reveals how thoroughly the left's vetting process failed Colorado voters.

Rep. Madeleine Dean (D-Pa.) said the decision to reject Platner was "an easy call," citing his Nazi-linked tattoo and abuse allegations that emerged well before the rape claim surfaced (Fox News). National Review documented Platner's downfall as a cautionary tale of Democratic negligence, while The Daily Wire noted that his sexually explicit texts to other women during marriage and Nazi chest tattoo represented a betrayal of his Marine Corps oath—"Semper Fidelis" (always faithful).

What's striking: Democrats nominated a candidate with these red flags already visible. The party's failure to conduct basic background checks on a major statewide nominee raises serious questions about institutional competence heading into 2026.

For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: while Platner's removal from the ballot eliminates one deeply flawed opponent, it also means Democrats will likely replace him with a more polished progressive who shares their tax-and-regulate agenda. Taxpayers should watch whether the Democratic replacement candidate supports the same energy restrictions, gun control measures, and spending increases that have made Colorado less competitive for business.
