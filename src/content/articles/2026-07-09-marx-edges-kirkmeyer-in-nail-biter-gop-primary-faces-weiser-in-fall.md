# Victor Marx Edges Kirkmeyer in Razor-Thin GOP Primary; Now Faces Weiser in November

Colorado Republicans have nominated Victor Marx, a first-time candidate and nonprofit leader, as their gubernatorial standard-bearer—but barely. Marx secured 39.9% of the vote to state Sen. Barb Kirkmeyer's 39.4%, winning by roughly 2,500 votes in a race that remained too close to call for over a week (CPR News, Denver Post). The narrow margin reflects deep divisions within the GOP primary field: only two candidates advanced out of 18 Republican contenders who competed at the state assembly (Complete Colorado).

Marx, whose campaign has drawn scrutiny for his controversial personal history, will now face Democrat Phil Weiser in the general election. Republicans argue Marx's grassroots appeal and outsider status energized base voters tired of establishment politics. Democrats counter that his unorthodox background and thin primary margin signal weakness heading into November.

For Colorado conservatives, the real test begins now: whether Marx can unite a fractured Republican base and articulate a clear conservative agenda on taxes, energy development, and government overreach—or whether Weiser's incumbency advantage proves decisive in a state trending blue.
