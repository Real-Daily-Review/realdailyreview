# Trump Tightens Border: Birth Tourism Ban Takes Effect

President Trump just closed a major immigration loophole that's cost Colorado taxpayers millions in anchor-baby welfare and education costs. The administration ended birth tourism—the practice of foreign nationals entering the U.S. specifically to deliver babies and claim automatic citizenship (The Daily Wire).

The move targets what conservatives have long called a backdoor citizenship scheme. Birthright citizenship, intended to protect freed slaves under the 14th Amendment, has been exploited by wealthy foreign families seeking U.S. citizenship for their children without immigration vetting. Democrats and open-borders advocates argue the ban violates constitutional protections and discriminates against immigrants, but Republicans counter that sovereignty requires controlling who becomes a citizen—not foreign nationals gaming the system.

For Colorado, the impact is real: anchor babies strain emergency rooms, K-12 budgets, and welfare rolls in Denver and other urban centers. Schools and hospitals absorb costs that should fall on federal immigration enforcement, not local taxpayers.

The administration is expected to defend the order in court, where the Left will challenge it as unconstitutional. Watch whether Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature attempts to circumvent federal policy through state benefits or sanctuary measures—a likely next move.
