# Electoral College Under Fire as Left Pushes to Remake Presidential Elections

Colorado conservatives should brace for renewed assault on the Electoral College—the constitutional safeguard that prevents coastal mega-cities from dictating presidential outcomes to the rest of America.

Democrats and progressive activists are intensifying efforts to undermine the Electoral College, which has anchored presidential elections since George Washington. *National Review* warns that the "plot to overthrow the Electoral College must fail," arguing the system preserves federalism and ensures smaller states retain meaningful influence. *Complete Colorado* notes that the left understands its power centers lie in dense urban corridors and sees the Electoral College as an obstacle to consolidating that advantage into permanent national control.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** The Electoral College forces candidates to build geographically diverse coalitions and prevents a handful of high-population states—California, New York, Illinois—from determining every election. It protects the interests of rural America, energy-producing states, and agricultural communities that Democrats increasingly take for granted or actively antagonize through regulation.

**Democrats counter** that the system is "undemocratic" and that a national popular vote would be fairer. They ignore that the Constitution was designed as a federal republic, not a pure democracy, and that eliminating the Electoral College would require a constitutional amendment—a deliberately high bar.

Colorado voters should watch whether state legislators face pressure to join the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact, which would effectively nullify Colorado's electoral votes.
