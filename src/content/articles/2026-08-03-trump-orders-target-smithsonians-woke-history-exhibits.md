# Trump Targets Smithsonian 'Woke' History Push With Executive Orders

President Trump is wielding executive orders to challenge what conservatives call the Smithsonian Institution's ideological drift toward "decolonization" and DEI initiatives at the expense of patriotic American history (Fox News Politics).

The move reflects a broader Republican argument that federal institutions have abandoned balanced historical education in favor of progressive narratives that undermine national pride. Trump's orders directly target how the Smithsonian—a taxpayer-funded institution—curates exhibits and shapes public understanding of American heritage.

Conservatives argue that government-funded museums should present history fairly, not as a vehicle for contemporary political ideology. The Smithsonian's pivot toward examining America's historical failings through a progressive lens, Republicans contend, marginalizes traditional narratives and alienates ordinary Americans who fund these institutions.

Democrats and museum advocates counter that inclusive history—acknowledging colonialism, slavery, and systemic injustice—represents scholarly rigor, not ideology. They argue Trump's orders amount to censorship of legitimate historical inquiry.

For Colorado taxpayers, this battle matters: federal spending on cultural institutions ultimately flows from your wallet. The question is whether those dollars should fund balanced education or ideological messaging.
