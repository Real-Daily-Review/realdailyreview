# Vance Tells Israeli Officials to Accept Trump's Iran Deal or Lose U.S. Support

Vice President JD Vance has bluntly warned Israeli cabinet members to stop criticizing President Trump's interim Iran agreement—or face consequences for U.S. backing. Speaking Thursday, Vance told skeptical Israeli officials to "wake up" and "smell the reality," according to the Washington Examiner and Just The News.

Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir and other senior officials have publicly opposed the 14-point memorandum of understanding signed Wednesday between the U.S. and Iran, citing security concerns. Vance's response: Israel receives American support and should fall in line accordingly.

"The problem for Israel is not Donald J. Trump," Vance said, per the Daily Caller, signaling the administration views dissent from its closest Middle East ally as ungrateful.

The rebuke reflects Trump's transactional approach to foreign policy—aid and alliance come with strings attached. Whether the Iran deal actually constrains Tehran's nuclear ambitions or nuclear weapons timeline remains contested among national security experts, but Vance's message is unmistakable: comply or recalibrate expectations of U.S. commitment.
