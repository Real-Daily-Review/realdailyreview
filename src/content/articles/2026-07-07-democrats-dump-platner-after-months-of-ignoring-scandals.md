# Democrats Dump Maine Nominee After Months of Ignoring Red Flags

Democrats are scrambling to distance themselves from Senate nominee Graham Platner after sexual assault allegations surfaced—but conservatives note the party's sudden moral awakening rings hollow. According to National Review and The Daily Wire, Platner's disqualifying conduct wasn't new: Nazi-adjacent tattoos, social media posts mocking rape victims, and derogatory remarks about rural Americans circulated for months while Democratic leadership looked the other way.

Republicans argue this exposes Democratic hypocrisy: the party tolerated misconduct when it thought Platner could win, then weaponized moral outrage once the political calculus shifted.
