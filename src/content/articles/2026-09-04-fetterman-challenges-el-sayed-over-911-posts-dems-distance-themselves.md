# Pennsylvania Democrats Distance Themselves From Michigan Candidate's 9/11 Controversy

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's deleted 9/11 posts have triggered a rare rebuke from within his own party—a sign that even progressive Democrats recognize some lines shouldn't be crossed.

Sen. John Fetterman (D-PA) challenged Abdul El-Sayed to campaign alongside far-left streamer Hasan Piker on the 25th anniversary of 9/11 and his controversial half-sister, offering $100,000 in matching funds if El-Sayed accepted (The Daily Wire). The challenge amounts to a public dare—Fetterman's way of signaling El-Sayed's liabilities without formally abandoning him.

The Washington Examiner reports that CBS News deleted posts from El-Sayed's social media history related to 9/11, with commentator Bari Weiss accusing the left of "gaslighting" the public about the candidate's record. Conservatives argue the deletions and media silence reveal how progressives handle inconvenient narratives: scrub the record and move on.
