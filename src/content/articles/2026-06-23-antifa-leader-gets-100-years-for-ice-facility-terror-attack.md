# Antifa Cell Leader Gets 100 Years for ICE Facility Terror Attack

Federal prosecutors just handed down a landmark sentence that should remind Americans what happens when left-wing extremism turns violent: Benjamin Hanil Song, an Antifa cell leader, received 100 years in prison Tuesday for leading a July 4, 2025, armed assault on a Texas Immigration and Customs Enforcement facility (Daily Caller, Daily Wire). Song, a former Marine Corps Reservist, allegedly shot a law enforcement officer during the coordinated ambush before evading capture for a week, according to prosecutors.

Seven co-conspirators received decades-long sentences as well, marking a significant federal victory against organized political violence (Just The News).
