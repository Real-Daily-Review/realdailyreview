# RFK Jr. Pushes Food Safety While Colorado Fire Crews Warn Against Turkey Fryer Disasters

Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. is leveraging his "Make America Healthy Again" platform to promote traditional food preparation, teaming up with Indigenous chef Ray Naranjo in a *Washington Examiner* feature on converting Thanksgiving leftovers into turkey tamales. The move signals the Trump administration's interest in food culture and nutrition messaging ahead of the holiday season.

Meanwhile, Colorado fire departments are sounding a more urgent alarm. North Metro Fire Rescue District conducted a dramatic demonstration this week—deliberately setting a deep-fried turkey ablaze—to illustrate the genuine dangers of improper turkey frying techniques, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver. The demonstration underscored how quickly holiday cooking can turn catastrophic.

The contrast is telling: while Kennedy's initiative focuses on heritage recipes and food quality, Colorado's first responders are battling the preventable fires that plague Thanksgiving every year. Deep-frying remains one of the season's leading causes of kitchen fires and burns.

For Colorado families planning holiday meals, the takeaway is straightforward—follow fire safety protocols if you're frying, and if you're not confident in the technique, stick to traditional oven roasting or explore the tamale route Kennedy's promoting.
