# Missouri Senator's VP Bid Implodes in Six Hours

Sen. Eric Schmitt (R-MO) learned a hard lesson about presidential politics: don't leak your ambitions to friendly media and expect them to stay buried.

The Missouri Republican's transparent campaign for the 2028 vice presidency evaporated before lunch. Around 6 a.m., *Axios* published what amounted to a promotional piece touting Schmitt—a senator with just three years of experience—as a serious contender for the second chair. By midday, the effort had collapsed under the weight of its own hubris, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

The episode illustrates a broader problem in Republican circles: the gap between political ambition and actual accomplishment. Schmitt's three-year Senate tenure hardly qualifies him for national ticket consideration, yet he apparently believed a coordinated media rollout could manufacture credibility overnight.

For Colorado Republicans watching the 2028 race take shape, the Schmitt fiasco is a cautionary tale. Voters—and party insiders—reward substance over spin. Premature VP campaigns built on thin résumés and media manipulation tend to backfire spectacularly, as this six-hour disaster demonstrates.

Watch whether other potential 2028 contenders learn from Schmitt's mistake or repeat it.
