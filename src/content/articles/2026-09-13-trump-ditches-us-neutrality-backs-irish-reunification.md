# Trump Breaks U.S. Neutrality on Ireland, Signals Support for Reunification

Trump has abandoned decades of American diplomatic restraint by publicly backing Irish reunification—a move that could complicate U.S. relations with Britain and upset the delicate balance maintained since the Good Friday Agreement.

Speaking in Dublin, the president told reporters he would "love to see" Ireland unified as one nation, according to *Fox News*. The statement marks a sharp departure from longstanding U.S. policy, which has carefully avoided taking sides on Northern Ireland's constitutional status. *The Daily Wire* notes Trump's comments "broke decades of American neutrality" on the issue.

While Trump attempted to soften the remarks—saying "I don't want to cause any problem"—the damage was done. The U.S. has historically maintained strict neutrality on Northern Ireland to preserve the 1998 Good Friday Agreement, which ended decades of sectarian violence and established a power-sharing government.
