# Trump's Proposed Washington Arch Gets Design Approval

A Trump-appointed Commission of Fine Arts approved a modified design Thursday for a 250-foot triumphal arch proposed for Washington, D.C., according to CBS News and PBS NewsHour. The monument would be built on Columbia Island at Memorial Circle on the Virginia side of the Potomac River, between the Lincoln Memorial and Arlington National Cemetery, according to the Washington Examiner.

The arch's height would place it between the Lincoln Memorial and the Washington Monument, which stands about 555 feet tall, PBS reported. An earlier design iteration included lions at the base; the approved version does not, according to CBS News.

The commission's approval marks a significant procedural step, though the project still faces additional regulatory hurdles. President Trump learned of the approval during an Oval Office press conference and reacted positively, according to The Hill.

The decision came despite what CBS News characterized as "overwhelming public opposition" to the project. All commissioners were appointed by Trump.
