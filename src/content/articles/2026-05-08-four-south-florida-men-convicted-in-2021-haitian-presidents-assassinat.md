# Four South Florida Men Convicted in Haitian President's 2021 Assassination

A Miami federal court has convicted four South Florida men of plotting to assassinate Haitian President Jovenel Moïse in July 2021, according to The Guardian. Prosecutors argued during the nine-week trial that the defendants assembled roughly two dozen former Colombian soldiers and supplied them with money, guns, ammunition and tactical vests to carry out the killing at Moïse's Port-au-Prince home.

The assassination of the 53-year-old president triggered a prolonged political crisis in Haiti, according to Al Jazeera. Prosecutors characterized Florida as a central hub in the conspiracy that ultimately resulted in Moïse's death.

The conviction marks a significant development in the case, which has drawn international attention given Haiti's ongoing instability. The trial revealed the scope of the alleged plot, including the recruitment of foreign mercenaries and the logistical support provided by the defendants.
