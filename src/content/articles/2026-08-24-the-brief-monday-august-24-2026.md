## Lakewood Council Moves to Muzzle Its Own Voters

Lakewood's city council is heaping new procedural burdens on the citizens' initiative process — and some residents aren't buying the excuse. They're calling it retaliation, plain and simple, for the April special election in which voters pushed back on the council's zoning agenda. This is textbook government overreach: elected officials using bureaucratic process to punish constituents who had the nerve to exercise their democratic rights. Colorado conservatives should be watching every local council race in Jefferson County with this in mind.

## Karmelo Anthony Denied New Murder Trial

A Texas judge on Saturday rejected convicted killer Karmelo Anthony's bid for a new murder trial. Anthony fatally stabbed Austin Metcalf in the heart at a high school track meet. Judge Michael Chitty's ruling is a win for the rule of law and for the Metcalf family, who have waited long enough for justice. The case became a flashpoint in the national debate over crime, accountability, and the left's reflexive sympathy for defendants over victims — a debate that resonates in Colorado, where soft-on-crime policies continue to drive real consequences for real families.

## Newsom Signs 'Stop Nick Shirley Act'
