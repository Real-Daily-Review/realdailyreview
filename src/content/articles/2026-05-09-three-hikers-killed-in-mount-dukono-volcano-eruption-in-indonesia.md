# Three Hikers Dead After Mount Dukono Erupts in Indonesia

Mount Dukono volcano on Halmahera island erupted, killing three hikers who were in a restricted zone despite prior safety warnings.

The victims included two Singaporeans and one Indonesian national, according to officials cited by The Guardian. The eruption sent an ash cloud approximately 10 kilometers into the air, the BBC reported. No towns or villages were close enough to face immediate danger from the blast.

Officials had previously issued warnings against climbing the active volcano due to safety concerns. The hikers' presence in the no-go zone at the time of the eruption suggests they either ignored or were unaware of the restrictions.

Mount Dukono is one of Indonesia's most active volcanoes. The country sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire, a region prone to significant seismic and volcanic activity.
