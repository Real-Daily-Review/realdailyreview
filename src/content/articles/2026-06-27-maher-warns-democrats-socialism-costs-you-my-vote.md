# Bill Maher Warns Democrats: Keep Pushing Socialism, Lose Moderate Voters

Even Hollywood liberals are jumping ship as the Democratic Party lurches toward socialism. On Friday's "Real Time," comedian Bill Maher told Vice President JD Vance that his vote is "in play" if Democrats continue embracing Democratic Socialists of America ideology—particularly their antisemitism, anti-capitalism, and radical criminal justice positions (Daily Wire, Breitbart).

Maher's warning signals a real fracture in the left's coalition. The HBO host cited the party's "obsession with Israel" and "Jew-hating" rhetoric, alongside demands to abolish prisons and dismantle capitalism, as dealbreakers for moderate Democrats who built the party's postwar consensus.

This matters for Colorado. The state's suburban swing voters—especially in Jefferson, Douglas, and El Paso counties—have grown increasingly skeptical of progressive overreach on energy policy, gun control, and spending. If national Democrats continue alienating pragmatists like Maher, Colorado Republicans have an opening to reclaim purple districts in 2026 and 2028.

Conservatives should watch whether Democratic leadership distances itself from DSA-aligned candidates or doubles down on the socialist wing—that choice will reshape Colorado's electoral map.
