# New York Mayor's Reparations Plan Exposes the Left's Historical Revisionism

New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani's proposal to cut checks to black New Yorkers as reparations for slavery represents exactly the kind of divisive identity politics that undermines merit and personal responsibility—and Colorado taxpayers should take note of where this ideology is headed.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Mamdani's logic "would be historically incoherent and legally perilous," transforming descendants of immigrants into historical perpetrators while casting millions of black New Yorkers as permanent victims. The plan ignores basic facts: most New Yorkers today have no ancestral connection to slavery, yet would foot the bill through taxes.

**What conservatives argue:** This approach abandons individual accountability and replaces it with collective guilt based on race and ancestry. It treats government as a reparations agency rather than a protector of equal rights under law. Republicans contend that such schemes inevitably expand government spending, create new bureaucratic categories of victimhood, and poison civic unity by dividing Americans into perpetual debtor and creditor classes.

**The Colorado angle:** If this reparations fever spreads to state and local governments, Colorado could face pressure to implement similar programs—draining resources from schools, infrastructure, and tax relief while creating legal chaos over who qualifies and who pays.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats begin floating reparations proposals in the 2025 legislative session.
