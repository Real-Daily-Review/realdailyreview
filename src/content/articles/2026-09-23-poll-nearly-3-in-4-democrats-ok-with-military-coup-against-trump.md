# Democrats' Willingness to Back Military Coup Against Trump Exposes Authoritarian Streak

A stunning poll finding reveals that nearly three-in-four Democrats say they'd be comfortable with the military overthrowing President Donald Trump, according to the Daily Caller. The Washington Examiner frames the result as evidence that Democrats embrace military force—just so long as it targets their political opponents.

The poll, conducted by the Center for Strategic Politics, underscores a dangerous double standard on the left. Democrats routinely condemn Trump as a threat to democracy, yet a supermajority appears willing to abandon constitutional order itself if it means removing him from office. That's not defending the Constitution—that's shredding it.

Republicans and conservatives argue this reveals the true authoritarian impulse behind progressive rhetoric. When Democrats claim to champion democracy while simultaneously endorsing military coups, they expose the hollowness of their constitutional principles. The willingness to deploy the armed forces against a duly elected president—regardless of one's opinion of that president—represents a fundamental rejection of the rule of law.

For Colorado voters and taxpayers, this should crystallize a core question: Do you want to entrust power to a political movement that views the military as a tool for removing political enemies? Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation distances itself from this position or remains silent.
