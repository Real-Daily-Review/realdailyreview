# Trump's Thanksgiving Message: Roast, Pumpkin Pie, and a Swipe at the 'Radical Far Left'

President Trump used his Thanksgiving Day platform to deliver a message that mixed patriotic sentiment with pointed criticism of his political opponents. After opening with "HAPPY THANKSGIVING TO ALL AMERICAN PATRIOTS WHO HAVE WORKED SO HARD TO MAKE OUR COUNTRY GREAT!" (The Daily Wire), Trump pivoted quickly into what amounted to a lengthy roast, sparing few targets—including those on the left.

The president and First Lady Melania Trump also participated in their annual Thanksgiving calls to U.S. service members (Washington Examiner), maintaining a tradition that underscores the administration's focus on military appreciation. During those calls, Trump revealed his favorite Thanksgiving dessert: pumpkin pie with vanilla ice cream—a detail that humanized the moment amid broader political messaging.

Trump's Thanksgiving remarks reflected a broader pattern: using high-profile occasions to reinforce his political brand while keeping pressure on what he views as destructive progressive policies. For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the message was clear—the administration continues to position itself as the defender of American values against what it characterizes as radical leftism.
