# Trump Name Removed From Kennedy Center as Federal Judge's Order Takes Effect

A federal judge's order forced the Kennedy Center to remove President Trump's name from the Washington, D.C. arts venue by Friday's deadline, with crews beginning scaffolding work this week (Washington Examiner). Trump's handpicked board at the center had pursued last-minute efforts to keep the nameplate, but the court ruling prevailed (9News Denver).

The removal marks another legal setback for the Trump administration in its opening weeks. The judge's decision rejected renovation plans that would have closed the center for two years—a proposal Trump's board had championed as an alternative to the name removal.
