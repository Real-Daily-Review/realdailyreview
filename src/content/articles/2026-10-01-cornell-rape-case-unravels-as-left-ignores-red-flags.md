# Cornell Case Exposes Left's Rush to Judgment on Serious Allegations

Democrats and progressive activists are treating unproven gang-rape allegations against Cornell University students as established fact—before any trial, conviction, or even complete investigation—revealing how ideology trumps due process on the left.

The case centers on allegations that seven Chi Phi fraternity members drugged and assaulted a woman in 2024. But according to reporting from *Breitbart* and the *Washington Examiner*, additional text messages between the alleged victim and one of the accused men have emerged that appear to undermine key elements of the accusation. Despite these red flags, Democrats and celebrities have already rushed to condemn the accused and demand maximum punishment.

Conservatives argue this reflects a broader pattern: the left treats accusation as conviction when it fits a preferred narrative. The presumption of innocence—a cornerstone of American justice—gets discarded in favor of activist pressure and social media outrage.

The case also highlights how universities become battlegrounds for political messaging rather than institutions committed to fair investigation. Colleges face intense pressure from progressive donors and activists to punish accused students before facts are established.

Republicans and conservatives stress that serious allegations deserve serious investigation—not predetermined conclusions. Both accusers and accused deserve due process, not mob justice dressed up as moral clarity.

Watch whether mainstream media continues amplifying the accusation despite emerging contradictions, or whether journalistic standards reassert themselves.
