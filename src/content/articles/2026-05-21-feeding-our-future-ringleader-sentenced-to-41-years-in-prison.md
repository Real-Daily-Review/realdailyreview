# Feeding Our Future Ringleader Sentenced to 41 Years

Aimee Bock, the convicted mastermind of the $250 million Feeding Our Future fraud scheme in Minnesota, was sentenced to 41 years in federal prison Thursday, according to CBS News and the Washington Examiner.

The 45-year-old former executive director of Feeding Our Future received the longest sentence among the 78 defendants indicted in the case involving a federal program meant to provide free meals to children. Bock orchestrated the scheme, which diverted funds intended for child nutrition assistance.

The sentencing marks a significant milestone in what prosecutors have described as one of the largest pandemic-era fraud cases. The sprawling investigation has resulted in multiple convictions across numerous defendants involved in the fraudulent meal delivery operation.
