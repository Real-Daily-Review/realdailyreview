# Trump Taps UFC Boss Dana White for White House Press Secretary Role

President Trump has offered UFC CEO Dana White the position of White House press secretary, according to announcements Saturday (Washington Examiner, New York Post). Trump praised White as "a violent Fighter and Warrior" suited for the high-pressure role.

White, a longtime Trump ally and UFC executive, would bring an unconventional background to the traditionally media-facing position. The appointment signals Trump's continued preference for loyalists and outsiders over establishment Washington figures—a pattern consistent with his first term staffing decisions.

The move reflects Trump's broader strategy of filling key roles with figures from business and entertainment rather than career political operatives. White's experience managing high-profile athletes and navigating media scrutiny in the combat sports world could translate to managing the White House press corps, though the role demands deep knowledge of policy, constitutional law, and diplomatic messaging.

For Colorado conservatives, this appointment underscores Trump's commitment to reshaping federal institutions with non-traditional leaders who prioritize direct communication over bureaucratic protocol—a refreshing departure from the progressive establishment's preference for credentialed insiders.
