# Meta Settlement Leaves Colorado Taxpayers Footing the Bill—Without Real Accountability

Meta's $17.6 billion settlement with 47 states sounds like a win, but conservatives argue it's a hollow victory that lets Big Tech off the hook while Colorado and other states waste taxpayer money on litigation theater.

The deal, announced last week, requires no admission of wrongdoing from Meta or CEO Mark Zuckerberg and avoids trial altogether (Breitbart). National Review notes the settlement "settles nothing"—it merely renegotiates social media's role in society through financial penalty rather than genuine legal accountability.

**The conservative case:** This is government overreach masquerading as consumer protection. Rather than suing their way to tech regulation, states should let parents and users make their own choices about social media. The $17.6 billion payout doesn't address the real problem—it just extracts cash without forcing structural change or admitting liability. Colorado taxpayers funded this litigation; they deserve to know whether their attorneys general actually won anything meaningful or simply negotiated a settlement that lets Meta continue business as usual.

**What's next:** Watch whether Colorado's share of the settlement actually funds parental controls and digital literacy—or simply disappears into general revenue.
