# Houthis Strike Saudi Airport; 12 Dead, 300+ Wounded in Iran-Backed Terror Attack

Iran-backed Houthi rebels launched a missile attack on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport, killing at least 12 people and wounding more than 300 in the latest assault on critical Saudi infrastructure (Just The News). The attack marks another escalation in the terror group's campaign against the kingdom, following a Wednesday strike that killed a Sudanese national and wounded eight others.

Republicans and national security hawks argue these repeated attacks underscore the failure of the Biden-Harris administration's Middle East strategy and its reluctance to confront Iranian proxy networks decisively. The Houthis, armed and directed by Tehran, have systematically targeted civilian infrastructure while the U.S. has pursued diplomatic overtures to Iran that conservatives say have emboldened regional adversaries.

Democrats counter that military escalation risks wider regional conflict and that diplomatic channels remain essential to de-escalation—though critics note such approaches have yielded minimal results as attacks continue.
