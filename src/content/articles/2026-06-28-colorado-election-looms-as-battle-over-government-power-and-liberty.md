# Colorado Voters Face Stark Choice Between Liberty and Progressive Overreach in Upcoming Election

Colorado's next election represents far more than routine primary voting—it's a referendum on whether the state will continue down the path of progressive government expansion or return to constitutional limits on state power.

The Colorado Sun's Eugene Carman warns that unchecked political power historically leads to tyranny, invoking Ancient Rome's cautionary tale. Republicans counter that Colorado Democrats have already demonstrated the dangers Carman fears: runaway spending, regulatory overreach on energy and water policy, and erosion of Second Amendment protections that have made the state less business-friendly and less free.

Meanwhile, the New York Post argues that Americans should reclaim their authentic founding story—one of individual liberty and limited government—rather than accept the left's revisionist narrative. That message resonates with Colorado conservatives who see progressive activists rewriting state history to justify ever-larger government.

The stakes are concrete: Colorado's energy sector, water rights, gun laws, and tax burden all hang in the balance. Democrats will push further left; Republicans must offer a clear alternative rooted in constitutional governance and economic freedom.

**Watch whether Colorado Republicans can articulate a compelling vision of limited government—or whether they'll simply react to Democratic initiatives.**
