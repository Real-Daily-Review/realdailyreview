# Cost-of-Living Crisis Hits Home: Colorado Families Squeezed While Washington Claims Economy Is Booming

Grocery bills and gas prices are crushing Colorado households faster than wages can keep up, exposing the disconnect between official economic cheerleading and kitchen-table reality.

The *Washington Examiner* reports that across the nation—from Maryland to Main Street America—families face a mounting squeeze at checkout counters and pump handles. The problem is simple: inflation has outpaced wage growth, leaving working Coloradans with less purchasing power despite employment gains touted by the Biden-Harris administration.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and fiscal hawks say progressive spending policies—from stimulus packages to regulatory overreach—fueled inflation that has eroded middle-class savings and retirement accounts. They argue that government-driven cost pressures on energy, food production, and supply chains have made basic necessities unaffordable for ordinary Coloradans, particularly those on fixed incomes or in rural areas dependent on affordable fuel and groceries.

**Democrats counter** that the economy remains fundamentally strong, pointing to low unemployment and GDP growth. They blame supply-chain disruptions and corporate profit-taking rather than government policy.

For Colorado taxpayers, the real question is whether state and local leaders will address cost drivers—energy regulations, zoning restrictions, tax burdens—or simply follow Washington's playbook of denial.
