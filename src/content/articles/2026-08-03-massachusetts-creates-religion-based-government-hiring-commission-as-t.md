# Massachusetts Doubles Down on DEI While Trump Dismantles It Federally

Massachusetts is moving in the opposite direction of the Trump administration's 2026 rollback of federal diversity, equity, and inclusion offices—proposing a permanent, religion-specific government hiring commission. Senate Bill S.2134, titled "An Act promoting the civil rights and inclusion of American Muslims in the commonwealth," would establish an 11-member commission tasked with identifying barriers to Muslim employment in state government and recommending hiring practices tailored to that single religious group.

The move illustrates the widening gap between red and blue states on DEI policy. While conservatives argue that race- and religion-based hiring preferences violate merit-based employment principles and constitute discrimination by another name, Massachusetts Democrats are institutionalizing the opposite approach—creating a permanent bureaucracy dedicated to preferential treatment.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's state government has faced similar DEI expansion over the past four years. Conservatives should watch whether Democratic leadership in Denver follows Massachusetts's playbook or whether the Trump administration's federal pressure creates political cover for Republican legislators to roll back Colorado's own DEI initiatives in hiring and contracting.

Watch for whether other blue states adopt Massachusetts's model—and whether Colorado Republicans use federal momentum to challenge state-level DEI programs.
