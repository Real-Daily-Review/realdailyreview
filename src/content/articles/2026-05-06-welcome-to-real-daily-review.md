Welcome. **Real Daily Review** is a small experiment in fast, balanced daily news. The premise is simple: most readers don't have time for fifteen tabs of competing coverage every morning. They want one short brief that reads in five minutes, links every source, and labels competing perspectives plainly when a topic is contested.

That's what we publish.

## How we work

Stories are drafted by AI from multiple primary and secondary sources, then reviewed before publication. We disclose AI involvement on every article, link every source, and flag when reporting is thin. We don't fabricate quotes. We don't invent statistics. When a topic is genuinely contested, we say "supporters argue X; critics argue Y" and let you weigh in.

## What we'll publish

- **Today's Brief** — one daily digest, weekday mornings. Five minutes, ~600 words.
- **Standalone briefs** — 5–8 short pieces a day on the morning's biggest stories.
- **Explainers** — longer pieces that try to make sense of complicated news (policy bills, court rulings, market events).

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we want to know. Email us at feedback@realdailyreview.com or use [the feedback form](/feedback). Material corrections are appended in-line with date stamps; we don't silently delete or edit a published claim away.
