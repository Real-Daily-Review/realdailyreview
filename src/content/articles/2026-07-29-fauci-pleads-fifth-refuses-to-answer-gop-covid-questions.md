# Fauci Pleads the Fifth, Dodges GOP Grilling on COVID Origins

Dr. Anthony Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment during a Wednesday Senate hearing, refusing to answer questions from Republican lawmakers investigating his role in the coronavirus pandemic response. The move by the former federal infectious disease chief—testifying under subpoena before the Senate Homeland Security Committee—signals he won't cooperate with GOP scrutiny over gain-of-function research funding, lab-leak suppression, and pandemic policy decisions that upended American life.

Republicans argue Fauci's silence amounts to an admission of guilt and obstruction of legitimate congressional oversight. They contend taxpayers funded his agency's grants to foreign labs and deserve answers about pandemic origins and the scientific rationale behind lockdowns and mask mandates that damaged Colorado schools, small businesses, and the economy.

Democrats and Fauci's allies frame the hearing as partisan theater designed to relitigate settled science. They claim the Fifth Amendment is a constitutional right available to all citizens facing potential legal jeopardy.

The hearing underscores lingering conservative frustration over pandemic policies that Colorado families endured—school closures, business shutdowns, and federal overreach—without clear accountability from the officials who championed them (9News Denver, Daily Caller, Just The News).
