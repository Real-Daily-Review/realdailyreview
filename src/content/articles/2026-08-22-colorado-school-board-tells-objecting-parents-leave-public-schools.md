# Colorado School Board Tells Parents Unhappy With Trans Teacher: Leave Public Schools

A Colorado father is raising alarms after school officials suggested families objecting to a transgender teacher should consider private school alternatives—while another board speaker proposed relocating children of parents deemed "bigots." The incident highlights the deepening divide between parents asserting traditional values and progressive school administrators dismissing parental concerns as bigotry.

According to Fox News Politics, the father expressed outrage at being essentially told public education wasn't for families with his views. The same board meeting featured rhetoric suggesting children of dissenting parents could be removed, framing parental objection to gender ideology in classrooms as intolerance rather than legitimate concern.

This reflects a broader Colorado pattern: school boards increasingly dismissing parent input on curriculum and personnel decisions involving gender and sexuality. Conservatives argue public schools—funded by all taxpayers—should reflect community values, not impose ideological conformity. When officials tell parents their concerns are unwelcome, they're essentially telling Colorado families their tax dollars fund institutions hostile to their beliefs.

Meanwhile, a separate tragedy underscores the human cost of institutional failures: Lakewood art teacher Lindsey Barnett was found dead, with her husband charged in her murder (KDVR Fox31). The case raises questions about what warning signs schools and communities may have missed.
