# NYC Dumps $15M Into Gender Ideology While Colorado Watches Its Own Spending

New York City is funneling $15 million in taxpayer money into sex-change initiatives for children and adults, a move that should alarm Colorado conservatives watching similar progressive experiments creep westward.

NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced the spending spree as what he framed as a defense against federal action, claiming "healthcare is a human right" (Daily Caller). The move represents exactly the kind of government overreach conservatives warn about: public dollars bankrolling ideological priorities rather than core services.

Republicans and conservatives argue this spending exemplifies progressive governance run amok—using taxpayer funds to advance contested medical and social agendas rather than address basic infrastructure, public safety, or fiscal responsibility. The federal pushback Mamdani cited reflects growing Republican concern that gender-affirming care for minors lacks sufficient long-term safety data and that such decisions should rest with parents, not government bureaucrats.

Colorado has largely avoided NYC-style gender spending at the state level, but progressive Denver city council members and Boulder activists have pushed similar initiatives. The question for Colorado taxpayers: will state and local officials resist the pressure to follow New York's lead, or will we see similar budget requests dressed up as "healthcare equity"?

Watch whether Colorado Democrats introduce comparable spending proposals in the 2026 legislative session.
