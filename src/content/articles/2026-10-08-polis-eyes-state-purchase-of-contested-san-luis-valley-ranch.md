# State Land Grab Looms Over San Luis Valley Water Rights

Gov. Jared Polis is floating a state purchase of Cielo Vista Ranch in Costilla County—a move that would hand Colorado government control over one of the state's most contested water resources and private land holdings (Colorado Sun). Former U.S. Sen. Ken Salazar, tapped by Polis to broker a "solution," has recommended the state buy into the property at the center of a decades-long dispute.

Conservatives and property-rights advocates should sound the alarm. State acquisition of private ranch land sets a dangerous precedent for government overreach into Colorado's agricultural heartland and water markets. Rather than resolve disputes through market mechanisms and existing water law, Polis's approach centralizes control—and risk—in government hands.

The San Luis Valley, already strained by competing water demands and federal interference, doesn't need another state agency managing its most sensitive resource. Private ownership and negotiation between willing parties—not government purchase—respects property rights and preserves local autonomy.

Taxpayers should watch whether the Polis administration uses state funds (your money) to bankroll this land grab and what strings attached to federal dollars might drive the final deal.
