# Trump Meets Iranian Officials as Tensions Escalate

President Trump is playing hardball with Iran—threatening "annihilation" at the U.N. while simultaneously opening a diplomatic channel with Iranian officials in New York. The mixed messaging reflects Trump's negotiating style: maximum pressure paired with a door left slightly ajar.

According to 9News Denver, Trump defended his Iran strategy at the U.N., arguing he's shown the resolve other leaders lack. Just The News reports that U.S. and Iranian delegations met for three hours in Manhattan, shortly after Trump's stark warning that Iran faces either a deal or destruction.

The approach mirrors Trump's 2020 maximum-pressure campaign, which conservatives credit with forcing Iran to the negotiating table without a costly military escalation. Critics on the left worry the rhetoric risks miscalculation, though the fact that talks are happening suggests both sides see value in dialogue.

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets will watch closely. Any Iran deal affects oil prices and domestic energy policy—critical for Colorado's oil and gas sector, which has faced years of regulatory headwinds from the Biden administration.

Conservatives should monitor whether these talks produce concrete concessions on Iran's nuclear program or devolve into the kind of toothless diplomacy that preceded the 2015 JCPOA.
