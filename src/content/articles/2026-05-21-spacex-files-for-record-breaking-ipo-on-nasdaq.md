# SpaceX Files for Record-Breaking IPO

SpaceX has filed for what could become the largest initial public offering in U.S. history, according to The Verge, formally submitting its S-1 prospectus to the SEC and setting the stage for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker SPCX.

The company generated $18.67 billion in revenue in 2025, with its Starlink satellite internet service accounting for more than $11 billion of that total, according to reporting by The Wall Street Journal cited by The Verge. However, SpaceX posted a loss of over $4.9 billion last year while capital expenditures jumped to $20.7 billion, up from $11.2 billion in 2024.

The potential IPO would surpass previous record-setting U.S. debuts. CNBC noted that Alibaba, Facebook, and Visa have raised historic sums in past offerings, though none reached the scale SpaceX is expected to achieve.

The filing marks a significant milestone for Elon Musk's aerospace and satellite company, which has grown substantially through government contracts and commercial ventures. The Financial Times characterized the move as the opening salvo in a wave of major Wall Street listings.

Watch for the SEC's review process and any regulatory questions that could affect timing or valuation of the offering.
