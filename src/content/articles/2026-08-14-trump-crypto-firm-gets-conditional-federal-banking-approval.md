# Trump-Linked Crypto Firm Gets Federal Banking Nod—But Conditions Apply

Federal regulators have handed preliminary approval to World Liberty Financial, a cryptocurrency venture tied to the Trump family, for a bank charter application reviewed by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. The conditional green light, announced Friday, clears a major hurdle for the firm's banking ambitions, though final approval remains pending.

Democrats will predictably cry foul over conflict-of-interest concerns—a familiar refrain whenever Trump-adjacent businesses navigate federal permitting. Republicans counter that the OCC's preliminary approval reflects standard regulatory procedure and that crypto banking, properly supervised, could unlock innovation in financial services. The question isn't whether Trump's name is on the letterhead; it's whether the firm meets legitimate banking standards.

For Colorado specifically, this matters less than broader crypto policy. Our state has positioned itself as crypto-friendly terrain, and regulatory clarity—whether through traditional banking charters or alternative frameworks—could attract blockchain talent and capital to Denver and Boulder. Conversely, if federal regulators weaponize permitting against Trump-linked ventures, it signals that political connections now trump competence in banking decisions.

Conservatives should watch whether the OCC imposes unreasonable conditions on final approval or whether Democrats in Congress attempt legislative retaliation against the charter.
