# Former Marine Robert Gilman Released After 1,666 Days in Russian Prison

Trump administration secures freedom of U.S. veteran held under disputed charges in Moscow.

Former U.S. Marine Robert Gilman has been released from Russian custody after spending 1,666 days imprisoned on charges his family and American officials dispute as baseless (Fox News Politics). Gilman was detained in 2022 during the Biden administration and reportedly arrived in a catatonic state before his release, according to reports of abusive treatment in Russian custody (9News Denver).

Trump announced the deal as a prisoner exchange—part of a pattern where Russia trades American detainees for Russians held in U.S. custody (Just The News). The release comes as Russia continues holding multiple American citizens, often using them as leverage in diplomatic negotiations.

Sen. Ed Markey (D-MA) held a press conference Tuesday regarding Gilman's homecoming, though details on the administration's negotiating position remain limited (Washington Examiner).

The case underscores a troubling reality: American citizens abroad face real danger from hostile regimes, and their safety depends on effective diplomatic leverage and clear messaging from Washington. Conservatives should watch whether the Trump administration pursues a broader strategy to prevent future wrongful detentions and strengthen protections for Americans overseas.
