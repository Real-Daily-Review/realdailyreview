# Democrats' Socialist Drift Signals Trouble for Colorado's Left

Colorado Democrats are swimming against the national tide as their party lurches toward socialism—a political liability that could reshape state politics for years.

While Democratic leaders like House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries publicly distance themselves from the Democratic Socialists of America agenda, polling data tells a different story. According to Fox News reporting, most Democrats now view socialism more favorably than capitalism, signaling a fundamental shift in the party's base that party brass can no longer control or credibly deny.

This ideological drift matters in Colorado. The state's competitive political landscape—where suburban voters and independent-minded Coloradans decide elections—has little appetite for the socialist experiments gaining traction in Democratic circles. Higher taxes, expanded government control of energy and healthcare, and regulatory overreach poll poorly among the swing voters Democrats need to win statewide races.

National Review notes that even in deep-blue states, Democrats are fielding candidates with openly socialist sympathies, a strategy that alienates moderates and energizes Republican turnout. Colorado's business community, ranchers, and energy workers have already signaled resistance to progressive overreach; a party openly embracing socialism will only accelerate that rejection.

**Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from national party socialism rhetoric—or double down and lose the middle.**
