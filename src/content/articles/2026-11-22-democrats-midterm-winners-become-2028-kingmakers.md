# Democrats Claim Midterm Kingmakers as 2028 Race Takes Shape

Democrats' unexpected 2026 midterm sweep—flipping Texas, South Carolina, and Mississippi—has created a new class of political power brokers eyeing the 2028 presidential race. According to the Washington Examiner, winning Democratic candidates in battleground states are now fielding endorsement requests from 2028 hopefuls rather than seeking them out themselves.

The shift signals a potential realignment in Democratic primary politics, with freshly minted senators and governors from traditionally red states positioned as kingmakers in the coming presidential contest.

Republicans, however, offer a different read on the midterm results. A Washington Examiner opinion piece argues that Trump's delivery on campaign promises—not abandonment of his base—cost Republicans legislative control in 2026. The counterargument: that fulfilling conservative priorities, rather than compromising them, triggered the midterm losses.

For Colorado conservatives, the broader takeaway matters: Democratic momentum in neighboring red states suggests the party's ground game and messaging are resonating beyond traditional blue strongholds. Whether that wave reaches Colorado's 2028 races depends partly on how effectively state Republicans counter the emerging Democratic narrative.
