# Colorado Conservatives Should Reject Left's Race-Based Spin on Murder Conviction

A Texas jury convicted Karmelo Anthony of murder and sentenced him to 35 years—a straightforward application of justice that House Democrats are now weaponizing along racial lines. According to Fox News, Democratic lawmakers are blaming "racism" and an "all-White" jury for the guilty verdict, attempting to reframe a clear-cut murder case into a morality play about systemic bias.

National Review editors argue the jurors "took that task seriously and called Anthony's crime what it was: murder," rejecting the left's attempt to inject race into a case where the evidence spoke for itself.

The fallout has been telling. The Daily Wire reports that a Texas Department of Criminal Justice employee was terminated after posting Facebook remarks about Anthony, while a vocal Anthony supporter was arrested on weapons charges after confronting the victim's family outside the courthouse.

**The Colorado angle:** This case illustrates how progressives weaponize identity politics to undermine the rule of law—a tactic that erodes public confidence in courts and juries statewide. When Democrats prioritize racial narratives over facts, they weaken the legitimacy of the justice system that protects all Coloradans.
