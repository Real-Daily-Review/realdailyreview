# Jack Smith Grilled Over Sweeping Media Surveillance in Trump Probe

Former Special Counsel Jack Smith faced hostile questioning from Senate Republicans on Capitol Hill after revelations that his investigators obtained communications between nearly 50 journalists and government officials during the Trump investigation—a move conservatives say crossed a constitutional line.

Senate Judiciary Chairman Chuck Grassley (R-Iowa) led the hearing, pressing Smith over the scope of his probe and the collection of communications from Republican lawmakers (Fox News). Republicans argue the seizure of journalist records represents an alarming expansion of prosecutorial power that threatens First Amendment protections and chills press freedom. The Daily Wire characterized the investigation as a "witch hunt," reflecting conservative skepticism that Smith's probe was politically motivated rather than legally justified.

Smith has defended his prosecutorial decisions and continues fielding questions from lawmakers as the hearing proceeded. Democrats have largely remained silent on the specifics, though the left generally supported Smith's Trump investigations as necessary accountability.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this case underscores the dangers of unchecked federal power—a cautionary tale about how government agencies can sweep up communications without clear legal boundaries. The precedent set here could affect how future administrations handle political opponents and the press.
