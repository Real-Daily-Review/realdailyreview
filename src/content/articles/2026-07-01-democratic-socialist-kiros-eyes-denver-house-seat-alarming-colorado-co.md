# Democratic Socialist Eyes Denver House Seat, Raising Concerns Over Radical Agenda

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress, alarming Colorado conservatives who worry about the district's leftward drift. According to CPR News, Melat Kiros—a DSA member—is positioning herself as a serious contender in what has long been a reliably Democratic seat. Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue that DSA-aligned candidates typically push for massive government spending, wealth redistribution, and anti-business policies that would harm Colorado's economy and job creators.

The DSA platform traditionally advocates for socialism, wealth taxes, and aggressive regulation—positions that clash sharply with Colorado's entrepreneurial tradition and energy sector. Conservatives warn that electing a self-identified democratic socialist to Congress would embolden the progressive wing already dominant in Denver politics, potentially accelerating tax-and-spend policies that drive businesses and working families out of the state.

Democrats counter that Kiros represents younger voters frustrated with establishment politics and demanding action on climate, healthcare, and inequality. They frame her candidacy as a response to economic anxiety among her constituents.

The race reflects a broader ideological battle for Colorado's urban centers—and whether Denver remains a business-friendly hub or becomes a laboratory for radical left-wing experiments.
