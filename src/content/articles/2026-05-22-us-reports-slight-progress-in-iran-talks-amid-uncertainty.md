# U.S. Reports 'Slight Progress' in Iran Talks as Mediators Engage

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Friday there was "slight progress" in talks with Iran, though significant uncertainty remains about whether negotiations will yield a deal or conflict will resume.

Pakistan's army chief Asim Munir is in Tehran as part of ongoing mediation efforts, according to Al Jazeera. The diplomatic engagement comes as the European Union moves to sanction Iran over what it characterizes as a blockade of the Hormuz Strait.

Rubio's characterization of "slight progress" suggests incremental movement in discussions, but the State Department has not detailed the substance of the talks or specific areas of agreement. The presence of Pakistani military leadership underscores the involvement of regional actors in de-escalation efforts.

The talks remain fragile, with both sides maintaining positions that could lead to renewed military confrontation. Officials have not indicated a timeline for resolution or the likelihood of a comprehensive agreement.

Watch for announcements from the State Department on next negotiation rounds and any statements from Iranian officials responding to Rubio's assessment.
