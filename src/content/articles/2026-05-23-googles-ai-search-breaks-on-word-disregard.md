# Google Search's AI Overviews Malfunction on Word "Disregard"

Google's AI-powered search results encountered a glitch Friday when users searched for the word "disregard," according to TechCrunch and The Verge. The AI Overviews feature—which summarizes search results at the top of the page—initially responded with chatbot-like language such as "Got it. If you need anything else or have a new question later, just let me know!" rather than providing relevant search information, The Verge reported.

By Friday afternoon, Google had removed the AI Overview entirely for that search term, reverting to a standard list of links instead, according to The Verge. The malfunction appears isolated to that specific word, though it highlights ongoing reliability issues with Google's AI search integration launched earlier this year.

The incident is the latest in a series of problems with AI Overviews, which have previously generated nonsensical or inaccurate summaries. Google has not publicly commented on the "disregard" glitch or explained its cause.

Watch for Google's statement on the issue and whether similar malfunctions emerge with other search terms.
