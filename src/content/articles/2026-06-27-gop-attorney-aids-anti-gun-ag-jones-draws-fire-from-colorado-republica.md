# GOP Attorney Caught Between Party and Anti-Gun AG Jay Jones

Colorado Republicans are watching an uncomfortable split in their own ranks over Attorney General Jay Jones—a Democrat whose aggressive gun-control agenda has drawn fire from conservatives statewide, yet one GOP lawyer is actively assisting his office.

According to the Daily Caller, the Republican attorney faced sharp questioning from fellow GOP members about why he's helping Jones, an official Republicans have opposed on Second Amendment grounds. The attorney's response: he's caught "in the middle" of professional obligations and party loyalty.

**The conservative case against Jones is straightforward:** Colorado gun owners and Republicans argue his office has pursued regulatory overreach on firearms, from magazine restrictions to red-flag rules that conservatives say violate due process. Republicans contend Jones represents the progressive playbook on gun control that threatens Colorado's hunting and self-defense traditions.

**The complication:** when individual attorneys work across party lines on specific cases or matters, it muddies the political narrative. Republicans say principle should come first—that assisting an anti-gun attorney general effectively undermines the conservative movement's credibility on Second Amendment defense.

Meanwhile, former Attorney General Bill Barr is pushing Senate Republicans to confirm Todd Blanche as Trump's next AG (Washington Examiner), signaling the national GOP's focus on restoring Justice Department independence from what conservatives view as weaponized prosecution.
