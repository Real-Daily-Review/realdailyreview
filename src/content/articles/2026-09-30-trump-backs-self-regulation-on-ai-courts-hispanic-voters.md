# Trump Pushes Self-Regulation Deal on AI While Courting Hispanic Voters

President Trump is betting on voluntary industry compliance over heavy-handed regulation—a refreshing departure from the left's instinct to regulate first and ask questions later.

On Tuesday, Trump and major tech CEOs signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, a self-regulatory framework for artificial intelligence development (Washington Examiner, Breitbart). The agreement commits companies to "robust internal controls and layers of internal and external review," according to House Speaker Mike Johnson. Rather than imposing federal mandates that could stifle innovation, Trump's approach trusts industry to police itself—a market-friendly alternative to the regulatory overreach progressives typically favor.

Meanwhile, Trump hosted a White House Hispanic Heritage Month celebration Wednesday with hundreds of Hispanic Americans and business owners, signaling a strategic push to solidify Latino support ahead of midterms (Washington Examiner). The dual moves reflect the administration's broader strategy: pro-growth policies paired with outreach to traditionally Democratic-leaning constituencies.

For Colorado, the AI accord matters: the state's tech corridor and innovation economy benefit from regulatory restraint that keeps compliance costs low and entrepreneurship high. Heavy-handed federal AI rules could have chilled investment and job creation across the Front Range.

**Watch whether Congress attempts to override this voluntary framework with mandatory regulations—a key test of whether Republicans can hold the line against the regulatory state.**
