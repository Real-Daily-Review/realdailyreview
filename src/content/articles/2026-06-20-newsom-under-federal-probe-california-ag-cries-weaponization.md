# California's Newsom Faces Federal Probe as State AG Cries 'Weaponization'

California Gov. Gavin Newsom is under federal investigation—and his own administration is already blaming Trump for political persecution.

California Attorney General Rob Bonta claims the Trump administration has weaponized the Department of Justice against Newsom, calling the probe political retaliation (Fox News). The investigation reportedly stems from Biden-era origins, though details remain murky. Notably, U.S. Rep. Adam Schiff has remained silent on the matter despite his prominent California profile.

The controversy intensified after Newsom filed a Freedom of Information Act request seeking details about the DOJ investigation into him and his wife. That move prompted a California legislator to accuse Newsom of violating state code (Just The News).
