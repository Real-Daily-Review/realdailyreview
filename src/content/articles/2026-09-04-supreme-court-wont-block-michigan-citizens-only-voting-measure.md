# Michigan Citizens-Only Voting Measure Blocked; Supreme Court Won't Intervene

Supreme Court Justice Brett Kavanaugh has declined to hear an emergency application to place a citizens-only voting measure on Michigan's November ballot, dealing a blow to election-integrity advocates pushing back against non-citizen voting in American elections.

The Americans for Citizen Voting-Michigan had appealed a deadlocked decision by the Michigan Board of State Canvassers, which removed the proposal from the ballot citing insufficient valid petition signatures (Just The News). The group sought Kavanaugh's intervention to reverse that determination before Election Day.

The rejection underscores a widening divide over election security. Conservatives argue that verifying voter citizenship is a fundamental safeguard against fraud and election manipulation. Republicans have made non-citizen voting a central campaign issue, warning that lax verification invites foreign interference and undermines ballot integrity.

Democrats and their allies dismiss the concern. Former White House lawyer Ty Cobb claimed on MSNBC that non-citizen voting "is not a real scenario" (Breitbart), echoing the left's position that the issue is overblown.

Meanwhile, Maryland's top court cleared a Democrat-backed redistricting measure for the November ballot—a move the New York Post notes represents a Democratic counter to Republican redistricting efforts backed by President Trump.
