# French Couple Held in Custody Over Abandoned Children in Portugal

A French woman and her partner have been remanded in custody pending trial after allegedly abandoning two boys on a roadside in southern Portugal, according to BBC World.

The couple will remain in custody as authorities investigate the circumstances surrounding the children's abandonment. Details about the boys' ages, how long they were left unattended, or how they were discovered were not disclosed in available reports.

The case has drawn attention to child welfare concerns across European borders. Portuguese authorities are handling the investigation, though the woman's nationality suggests potential involvement of French authorities as well.

Watch for updates on the trial date and any additional charges that may be filed against the couple.
