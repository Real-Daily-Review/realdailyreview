# War Secretary Hegseth Demands $350B, Defies Dem Impeachment Push

Pete Hegseth is doubling down. The War Secretary demanded Congress approve $350 billion in military funding while simultaneously vowing to fight off Democratic impeachment efforts (Fox News Politics). The move comes as House Republicans themselves signal a bipartisan impeachment threat may gain traction when Congress reconvenes, with some GOP members blaming Hegseth's Iran war strategy for GOP midterm losses (Fox News Politics).

In a defiant five-minute video posted Thursday outside the Pentagon, Hegseth pushed back against Democratic calls for additional Pentagon oversight, signaling he has no intention of backing down (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue robust military funding is essential to national security and deterrence. Hegseth's $350 billion request reflects the real costs of defending American interests abroad.

**The threat:** The bipartisan impeachment talk—unusual for a Republican-controlled Congress—suggests Hegseth's Iran strategy has fractured GOP unity. Some House Republicans are openly questioning whether his approach damaged the party's electoral prospects.

**Colorado angle:** Defense spending ripples through Colorado's economy via military installations, contractors, and aerospace jobs. Any Pentagon leadership instability could affect defense contracts and federal spending in the state.
