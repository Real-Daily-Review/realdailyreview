# Squad Member Cori Bush's Comeback Bid Collapses in Missouri Rematch

Establishment Democrats have dealt a decisive blow to the far-left insurgency within their own party, with Rep. Wesley Bell (D-MO) defeating former "Squad" member Cori Bush in a Missouri primary rematch Tuesday.

Bush, a DSA-linked progressive who lost her seat two years ago, attempted to reclaim Missouri's deep-blue 1st Congressional District but fell short against Bell, who held a commanding lead with roughly 26% of votes counted. The result marks the second consecutive victory for establishment Democrats over the socialist wing of the party in this particular race.

The outcome signals a potential fracture within Democratic ranks between moderate pragmatists and the radical left. While conservatives have long criticized the Squad's anti-police rhetoric, open-borders advocacy, and socialist economic proposals, this primary defeat shows even Democratic voters are growing weary of the far-left agenda.

For Colorado, the broader lesson is instructive: voters—even in deep-blue districts—reject extreme progressivism when given a clear choice. As Colorado Democrats push further left on energy policy, gun control, and spending, state Republicans should highlight how even Missouri Democrats rejected the Squad's radical blueprint.
