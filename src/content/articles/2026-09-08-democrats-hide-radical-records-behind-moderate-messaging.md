# Democrats' Radical Masquerade: Beware the 'Moderate' Label

Colorado voters should take note: some of this election cycle's most dangerous Democratic candidates are running as moderates while hiding deeply progressive records.

According to the Washington Examiner, several Democratic Senate candidates are employing what amounts to political sleight of hand. While some progressives openly campaign on radical policies, others—including figures like former Ohio Sen. Sherrod Brown and Rep. Chris Pappas (D-NH)—are playing what the outlet calls "a game of smoke and mirrors, hoping voters won't notice their records."

This matters for Colorado. As national Democrats push further left on energy policy, gun control, and spending, candidates masquerading as centrists often vote lockstep with the progressive wing once in office. Voters here have seen this playbook before: a candidate runs as a "reasonable Democrat," gets elected, then votes for every tax hike and regulatory expansion the party leadership demands.

Republicans argue that voters deserve transparency about a candidate's actual voting record and policy positions, not carefully crafted campaign messaging designed to obscure a radical agenda. The strategy reveals Democrats' own doubt that their true positions can win in swing states and competitive districts.

**Watch for Colorado Democratic candidates this cycle who claim moderation—demand they answer directly about their records on taxes, energy development, and gun rights.**
