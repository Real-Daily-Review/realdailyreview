# White House Pressure on Canoeist Case Sparks Abuse-of-Power Accusations

The Biden-Harris White House is pushing the Justice Department to pursue a misdemeanor prosecution against Olympic canoeist David Hearn after federal prosecutors dropped a felony charge related to paddling in the Reflecting Pool—a move that has triggered Republican accusations of political weaponization.

According to Fox News, officials are now exploring the lesser charge following the felony dismissal. The intervention has drawn fire from conservatives who see it as executive branch overreach into prosecutorial independence. House Judiciary Democrats, meanwhile, have launched their own probe (Just The News), with Ranking Member Jamie Raskin demanding communications between Interior Secretary Doug Burgum's office and the White House regarding the case.

Republicans argue the White House pressure exemplifies the kind of politicized justice system conservatives have warned about—where enforcement decisions bend to executive preference rather than legal merit. The fact that prosecutors already moved to drop the felony suggests the case lacked prosecutorial strength, making the White House's push for a lesser charge appear retaliatory rather than principled.

For Colorado conservatives, this case underscores a broader concern: federal agencies answering to political appointees rather than the rule of law. Watch whether the DOJ caves to White House pressure or reasserts prosecutorial independence—a test of whether the Justice Department serves justice or politics.
