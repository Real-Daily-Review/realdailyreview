# FAA Grounds Miami Airport After FedEx Cargo Plane Overshoots Runway

A FedEx cargo plane overran the runway at Miami International Airport Wednesday evening, triggering an FAA ground stop that disrupted travel across the Southeast and potentially rippled through connecting hubs serving Colorado travelers.

The incident occurred around 8:16 p.m. EST, according to the Washington Examiner. The large jet departed the runway surface, prompting the FAA to halt all departures and arrivals at one of the nation's busiest cargo and passenger hubs. No injuries were reported (9News Denver).

The ground stop raised immediate questions about runway safety protocols and aircraft handling procedures—issues that affect Colorado's aviation infrastructure and the reliability of cargo and passenger service through Denver International Airport, which depends on smooth operations at major hub airports nationwide.

While the FAA has not yet released a full incident report, the agency's rapid response prevented potential secondary collisions or injuries.
