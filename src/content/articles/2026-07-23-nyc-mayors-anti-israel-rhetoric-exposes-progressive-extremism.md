# NYC Mayor's Anti-Israel Rhetoric Exposes Progressive Extremism Problem

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's calls to arrest Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu during his UN visit have ignited accusations of antisemitism and exposed how far-left politics now dominates Democratic-controlled cities.

Mamdani, who campaigned on arresting Netanyahu, released a video this week calling the Israeli leader a "war criminal" — rhetoric that Jewish groups and Hamas terror attack survivors say crosses into dangerous antisemitism (Washington Examiner). The mayor later admitted he lacks legal authority to make the arrest, effectively breaking a promise to his far-left base (Breitbart).

Mazal Tazazo, a Hamas attack survivor, directly condemned Mamdani's video as "dangerous," underscoring how progressive anti-Israel activism increasingly conflates legitimate criticism with demonization of the Jewish state (Fox News).

Voters should watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership distances itself from Mamdani's extremism or stays silent — a telling indicator of where the party's center of gravity has shifted.
