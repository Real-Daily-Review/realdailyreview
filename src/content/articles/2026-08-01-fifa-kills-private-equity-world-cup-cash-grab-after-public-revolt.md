# FIFA Drops Private Equity World Cup Scheme After Backlash

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has abandoned plans to sell World Cup profits to private equity investors, reversing course after widespread opposition forced his hand.

In a statement Friday, Infantino acknowledged that the proposal had created divisions "no longer in the interest of the objective set out in the first place" (9News Denver). The scheme would have handed control of lucrative World Cup revenue streams to outside investors—a move that sparked immediate criticism across the political and sports spectrum.

Just The News reports Infantino now plans to "bring the sport back together" and focus on "growing football everywhere, particularly in those countries that mostly need our support."
