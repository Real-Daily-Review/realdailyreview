# South Carolina GOP Senate Runoff Shapes Up as Conservative vs. Establishment Battle

A green energy PAC is spending heavily to defeat Rep. Ralph Norman in South Carolina's Republican Senate runoff—a sign that the race has become a proxy war between conservatives and the climate-agenda left.

Norman faces interim Sen. Darline Graham Nordone, sister of the late Sen. Lindsey Graham, in the runoff after neither candidate secured enough votes to win outright in the primary. Rep. Russell Fry, who placed third, endorsed Graham Nordone, signaling establishment backing for the interim senator (Fox News).

The ideological divide is sharp. Students for Life Action, a national anti-abortion organization, endorsed Norman, reflecting his strong pro-life record (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, green energy interests are targeting Norman with outside spending—a clear attempt to protect a Senate seat from a conservative who opposes the Biden-Harris energy agenda (Breitbart).

**The Colorado angle:** This race matters to Colorado Republicans and energy workers. A Norman victory would strengthen the Senate caucus opposing federal overreach on energy policy and climate mandates that threaten Colorado's oil, gas, and coal industries. A Graham Nordone win could signal GOP capitulation to establishment-friendly candidates who won't fight green energy expansion.

Watch whether Norman's anti-establishment, pro-energy message can overcome the combined weight of establishment endorsements and outside green-PAC spending in a Republican primary.
