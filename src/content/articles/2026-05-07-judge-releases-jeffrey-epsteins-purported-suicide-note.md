# US Judge Releases Jeffrey Epstein's Purported Suicide Note

A New York judge has released a note that Jeffrey Epstein's former cellmate claims he found after the financier's first suspected suicide attempt in jail, according to PBS NewsHour. The handwritten message had been sealed in a courthouse vault for nearly five years as part of an unrelated legal dispute before being made public Wednesday.

Epstein's cellmate previously claimed the note was written by the convicted sex offender following a failed suicide attempt less than a month before his death in custody in 2019, CBS News reported. The note has not been independently verified by news outlets.

The document's release comes as part of ongoing legal proceedings, though the specific reasons for the judge's decision to unseal it remain unclear from available reporting. The Financial Times noted the note was handwritten, but details of its contents have not been disclosed in public reporting.

Epstein died in a Manhattan jail cell in August 2019 while awaiting trial on sex trafficking charges. His death was ruled a suicide, though questions about the circumstances have persisted.
