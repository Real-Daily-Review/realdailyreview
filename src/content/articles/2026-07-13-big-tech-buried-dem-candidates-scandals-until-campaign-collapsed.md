# Big Tech's Selective News Blackout Shields Dem Candidate Until Collapse

Silicon Valley's gatekeepers buried damaging coverage of disgraced Maine Democratic Senate candidate Graham Platner until his campaign imploded anyway—exposing how Apple News and Google News function as political protection rackets for the Left.

According to the Media Research Center, Apple and Google's news aggregators—which command 215 million monthly hits—largely ignored reports on Platner's scandals, giving him artificial credibility until his campaign collapsed last week (The Daily Wire). The platforms' selective suppression of negative coverage illustrates what conservatives have long argued: Big Tech doesn't just censor; it curates reality for progressive candidates.

Platner's downfall has fractured the Democratic Left. While the Democratic Socialists of America pressured allies to abandon him, New York Post reports that Mayor Zohran Mamdani refused to drop his close adviser Morris Katz despite DSA pressure. Meanwhile, progressives have pivoted to backing Maine Senate candidate Troy Jackson, with Bernie Sanders's organizing group already fundraising for him (Washington Examiner).

The scandal underscores a core conservative complaint: unelected tech monopolies wield editorial power without accountability, deciding which candidates deserve oxygen and which deserve silence. Colorado voters should ask whether our own local races receive similar treatment from Silicon Valley's news filters.

Watch whether Colorado media outlets demand transparency from Apple and Google on their news-curation algorithms ahead of 2026.
