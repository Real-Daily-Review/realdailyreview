# Denver Police Death in Custody Sparks Multi-Agency Investigation

Denver police are under scrutiny after a man died following a struggle with officers, reigniting questions about use-of-force protocols and accountability in the city's law enforcement.

According to 9News Denver, the Denver Police Department confirmed a multi-agency investigation is underway. Fox31 reports the incident began with a 911 call reporting a fight in the 2100 block of East Colfax, where a caller reported a man in his underwear attempting to carjack him.

Details remain limited, but the case arrives as Denver—and Colorado broadly—continues grappling with high-profile custody deaths and their political fallout. Police departments across the state face mounting pressure from progressive city councils and activist groups demanding stricter use-of-force rules, body camera mandates, and civilian oversight boards.

Conservatives argue that premature judgment and reflexive anti-police sentiment undermine officer safety and recruitment, while making it harder to attract quality candidates to law enforcement. They also warn that overly restrictive policies can hamstring officers responding to genuine threats—as the initial carjacking report suggests.

The investigation's findings will likely shape Denver's ongoing debate over police reform and whether the city strikes the right balance between accountability and operational effectiveness.

**Watch for:** Whether the investigation's conclusions drive another round of policy changes that could affect how Colorado police respond to violent crime.
