# Pentagon Enforces Discipline: Hegseth Fires General Over Classified Leaks

Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth has fired Army Lt. Gen. Brett Sylvia after the general admitted to leaking classified information about Ukraine and Iran operations to multiple reporters over a one-year period, according to a Pentagon report reviewed by *The Daily Wire*.

Hegseth's swift action underscores a hardline stance on operational security—a sharp contrast to the Obama-Biden era's tolerance for leaks that damaged national interests. The general confessed to sharing war secrets with journalists from separate outlets, a clear breach of the trust placed in senior military leadership.

"Classified information is not personal property," *Daily Caller* reports Hegseth stating, signaling zero tolerance for the kind of institutional leaking that has plagued the Pentagon for years.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans have long argued that career military bureaucrats and intelligence officials weaponize classified information to undermine elected leadership and shape policy through the press. Hegseth's firing sends a message that the Trump administration will hold generals accountable for insubordination—a refreshing departure from the revolving-door discipline of previous administrations.

**What's Next:** Watch whether this sets a precedent for rooting out the broader culture of leaks within the Pentagon that has compromised Colorado-based NORAD operations and other sensitive defense installations.
