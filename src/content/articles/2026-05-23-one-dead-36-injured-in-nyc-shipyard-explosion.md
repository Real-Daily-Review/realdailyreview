# Blast at NYC Shipyard Kills One, Injures Dozens

A fire and explosion at a New York City shipyard on Friday killed one person and injured 36 others, according to NPR, with most of the injured being firefighters and first responders.

The incident occurred at a dock on Staten Island after workers reported two colleagues trapped in a confined space at approximately 3:30 p.m., according to PBS NewsHour. Responding firefighters discovered a fire burning in the basement of a large metal structure at the dock, said Joanne Mariano with the New York Fire Department's press office.

The blast occurred as crews worked to rescue the trapped workers. The New York Fire Department is investigating the cause of the fire and subsequent explosion.

Authorities have not yet released the identity of the person killed or provided details on the severity of injuries sustained by the 36 others affected. Most of those hurt were emergency responders who arrived at the scene.
