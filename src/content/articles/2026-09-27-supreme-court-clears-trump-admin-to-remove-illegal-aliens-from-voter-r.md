# Supreme Court Greenlights Voter Roll Cleanup; Trump Blasts Justices on Redistricting

The U.S. Supreme Court has cleared the way for the Trump administration to remove illegal aliens from voter rolls, a move conservatives argue is essential to election integrity (Daily Caller). The decision affirms that federal authorities are "best suited to the task" of maintaining accurate voter registrations—a position Republicans have championed as fundamental to fair elections.

Meanwhile, President Trump lashed out at the Court over a Missouri redistricting case, claiming the justices blocked a legitimate election victory (Just The News). Trump's criticism reflects conservative frustration with judicial decisions that, in their view, undermine state authority over electoral maps and voter eligibility.

**The Colorado angle:** Election integrity remains a flashpoint in Colorado politics. State Republicans have long pushed for stricter voter roll maintenance and questioned the security of mail-in voting systems. The Supreme Court's ruling on illegal alien removal could embolden GOP efforts to tighten Colorado's voter registration processes—though Democrats control the state legislature and have resisted such measures as unnecessary and potentially discriminatory.

**What to watch:** Whether Colorado's Democratic-led Secretary of State's office cooperates with federal voter roll audits or resists the Trump administration's directives on illegal alien removal.
