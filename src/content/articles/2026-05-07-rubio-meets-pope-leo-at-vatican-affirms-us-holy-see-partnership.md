# Secretary of State Rubio Meets Pope Leo at Vatican

Secretary of State Marco Rubio met with Pope Leo XIV for two and a half hours Thursday at the Vatican, where the two leaders discussed global politics and religious freedom, according to the Washington Examiner. Rubio "underscored the strong relationship between the United States and the Holy See" and emphasized the "enduring partnership" between the two nations, the outlet reported.

The Hill characterized the meeting as an effort by Rubio to "smooth tensions" with the pontiff, though neither outlet detailed the specific nature of any prior disagreements. The Vatican visit represents a high-level diplomatic engagement between the State Department and the Catholic Church on matters spanning international relations and faith-based concerns.

The meeting comes as the U.S. continues diplomatic outreach on multiple fronts, including Iran's review of a new peace plan, according to the Hill. Watch for any official statements from the State Department or Vatican regarding outcomes or agreements reached during the meeting.
