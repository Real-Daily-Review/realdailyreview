# Colorado Seeks $28M in Federal Flood Relief After Back-to-Back Storms

Gov. Jared Polis has requested $28.5 million in federal disaster funds from the Trump administration following devastating back-to-back floods that ravaged southwest Colorado, according to 9News Denver and the Colorado Sun. The storms caused widespread damage across the region, with the total relief request likely to climb as damage assessments continue.

Polis urged President Trump to approve the disaster-aid requests, framing the funding as critical to recovery efforts in affected communities. The governor's office indicated that preliminary damage estimates exceed $28 million, but final figures remain pending.

**The conservative angle:** While disaster relief for legitimate infrastructure damage and emergency response is appropriate, taxpayers should scrutinize how these federal dollars are deployed. Colorado has a track record of using disaster declarations to expand government programs beyond immediate recovery needs. Republicans should demand transparency on how funds are allocated and ensure money goes to rebuilding—not padding state bureaucracies or funding pet projects under the guise of disaster relief.

Watch for how the Polis administration spends these dollars and whether Republicans in Congress demand accountability before approving additional tranches.
