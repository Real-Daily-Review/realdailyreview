# Regional Primary Results Trickle In; Colorado Republicans Watch National Abortion Debate Heat Up

A Florida GOP gubernatorial candidate's push to shutter every abortion clinic in the state signals how sharply the abortion issue is dividing Republican primaries nationwide—a preview of battles likely to intensify in Colorado's 2026 midterms.

James Fishback, running in Florida's GOP primary, has pledged to close all abortion clinics and replace them with crisis pregnancy centers (Fox News). Meanwhile, primary elections continue across the country: Maine held its gubernatorial primary (9News Denver), and California officials are still counting ballots from its June 2 primary, with results potentially taking weeks to finalize (Just The News).

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Republicans remain divided on abortion messaging after the state's 2022 midterm losses, where abortion rights proved decisive with suburban and independent voters. While social conservatives push for stricter bans, pragmatists worry aggressive clinic-closure rhetoric could cost Republicans swing districts in Denver's suburbs and college towns.

Democrats, by contrast, are running unified campaigns centered on abortion access—a strategy that worked in 2022 and will likely dominate 2024 and 2026 messaging statewide.

**Watch:** Whether Colorado GOP primary candidates in 2026 adopt Fishback's hardline approach or chart a more moderate course on abortion—a decision that could determine whether Republicans can recapture the state legislature.
