# Washington State's Transgender Mandate Shows How Far Progressive Overreach Has Gone

Washington state's Human Rights Commission is ordering a women-only nude spa to admit men, citing anti-discrimination law—a stark example of how progressive ideology is steamrolling women's privacy rights in the name of transgender activism (Washington Examiner).

The case against Olympus Spa illustrates a broader pattern: Democratic-controlled states are using government power to override the preferences and comfort of female customers and business owners. Republicans argue this represents regulatory tyranny masquerading as civil rights, where ideological purity trumps common sense and basic decency.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't yet pursued such aggressive transgender mandates in public accommodations, the state's Democratic legislature has steadily expanded gender identity protections. Conservatives worry similar spa cases could land on Colorado's doorstep if the left continues pushing identity politics through state agencies rather than the ballot box.

Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado's civil rights regulators follow Washington's lead—and whether Republican lawmakers will push back with legislation protecting women's spaces and business owners' freedom of association.
