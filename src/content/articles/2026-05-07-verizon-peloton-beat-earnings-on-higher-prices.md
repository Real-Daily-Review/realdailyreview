# Telecom and Fitness Giants Lift Earnings Through Price Increases

Verizon and Peloton both beat Wall Street expectations this quarter, crediting higher prices as a key driver of revenue growth.

Verizon, the New York-based telecommunications giant, posted earnings that exceeded forecasts, boosted by price increases and gains in wireless customer additions, according to the Wall Street Journal. The company's revenue benefited from the higher pricing strategy implemented across its service offerings.

Peloton similarly beat revenue estimates, with CEO Peter Stern telling CNBC that the company views its subscription price increases as "value-driven," suggesting the company believes customers are willing to pay more for its offerings, per CNBC.

Both results underscore a broader corporate trend: major companies are using price hikes to offset cost pressures and improve profitability, even as consumer spending faces headwinds. The earnings suggest that at least some consumers in telecom and fitness sectors are absorbing higher costs without significantly reducing their usage or subscriptions.

Watch whether other major companies report similar success with price increases in coming weeks, or if consumer resistance begins to emerge.
