# Missouri Senator's VP Bid Implodes in Six Hours

Sen. Eric Schmitt (R-MO) learned a hard lesson about ambition and timing when his barely-concealed campaign for the 2028 vice presidency collapsed before lunch.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Axios published a favorable profile of Schmitt early Wednesday morning positioning him as a potential running mate for the next Republican presidential nominee. The piece highlighted the three-term senator's profile and political positioning. By midday, however, the effort had cratered—undone, the *Examiner* notes, by what amounted to political overreach and poor judgment in the timing.

The episode underscores a broader reality in Republican politics: vice-presidential jockeying before a nominee is even chosen looks desperate and divides the party. Schmitt's stumble suggests that GOP insiders and voters alike have little patience for premature positioning, especially when it appears orchestrated through friendly media.

For Colorado Republicans watching the 2028 landscape, the lesson is clear: focus on delivering results in your current role, not angling for higher office before the primary season even begins. Voters and party leadership reward substance over ambition.
