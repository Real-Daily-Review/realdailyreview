# Federal Judge Blocks Abuse of Eminent Domain to Kill Housing Development

A Rhode Island federal court just handed property owners a victory that Colorado conservatives should watch closely: Judge Melissa R. DuBose ruled that the Town of Johnston's seizure of a 31-acre parcel was unconstitutional overreach (Washington Examiner).

The case, SCLS Realty, LLC v. Town of Johnston, centered on a Democratic mayor's attempt to seize private land ostensibly for public use—but the real motive was blocking housing development. The judge saw through it and voided the seizure entirely.

**Why this matters for Colorado:** Local governments across the West routinely abuse eminent domain and regulatory power to block development, raise housing costs, and entrench progressive land-use ideology. Colorado cities and counties have weaponized zoning, environmental reviews, and "public interest" claims to kill projects that threaten their vision of controlled growth. This ruling signals that courts are willing to push back when government naked power-grabs become too obvious.

Conservatives argue that property rights are foundational—government shouldn't seize land to engineer social outcomes or block lawful development. When mayors and city councils use the machinery of government to prevent housing supply and drive up prices, they're harming the very working families they claim to help.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado courts adopt similar reasoning when challenging local land seizures and whether state legislators move to tighten eminent domain abuse statutes.
