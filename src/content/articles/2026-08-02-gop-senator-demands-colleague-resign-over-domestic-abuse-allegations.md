# GOP Senator Calls for Colleague's Resignation Over Domestic Abuse Allegations

Sen. Bernie Moreno (R-OH) is demanding that Rep. Max Miller (R-OH) resign from Congress and seek psychiatric help following domestic abuse allegations from Miller's ex-wife—Moreno's daughter, Emily Moreno.

Miller responded to the allegations by going live on social media Sunday, denying the abuse claims and instead suggesting his ex-wife struggles with "significant mental health challenges," according to the Washington Examiner. Moreno rejected that framing in a lengthy statement, calling his former son-in-law's attempt to discredit his daughter "deeply troubling" and saying Miller needs "serious psychological help" and should step down immediately (Fox News Politics).

Just The News reported that Moreno stated Miller "should not" be serving in Congress, marking a rare public split within GOP ranks over a sitting member's conduct.

The dispute underscores how serious domestic abuse allegations can fracture party unity, even among Republicans who typically close ranks. Miller's strategy of attacking his accuser's mental health—rather than directly addressing the allegations—has backfired spectacularly, drawing condemnation from a sitting U.S. senator and his own party.
