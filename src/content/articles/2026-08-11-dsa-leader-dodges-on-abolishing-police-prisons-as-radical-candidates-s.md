# Socialist Agenda Goes Mainstream as DSA Candidates Surge in Primaries

A Democratic Socialists of America leader's evasive performance on national television this week exposed the radical incoherence at the heart of the movement gaining traction in Democratic primaries. When pressed on NBC Los Angeles about what happens to violent criminals under DSA's platform to abolish police and prisons, Frances Gill, a DSA National Political Committee member, simply dodged—refusing to answer who would catch a rapist or where dangerous offenders would go (Breitbart).

The DSA platform goes far beyond criminal justice. According to *Just The News*, the organization's agenda includes abolishing the U.S. Senate and the presidency itself, alongside eliminating law enforcement and incarceration entirely. These aren't fringe talking points anymore: DSA-backed candidates are surging through Democratic primary elections nationwide.

**Why Coloradans should care:** As progressive activists gain influence in Democratic politics, their radical proposals—defunding police, dismantling prisons, expanding government control—threaten Colorado communities, property rights, and public safety. Denver and other Colorado cities have already flirted with police-defunding schemes that backfired when crime spiked.

Republicans argue the DSA agenda would create chaos: without police or prisons, violent crime goes unpunished and law-abiding citizens become victims. Democrats largely distance themselves from these proposals, but their party's primary electorate is moving left.
