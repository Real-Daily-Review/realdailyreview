# Judge Releases Purported Epstein Suicide Note After Nearly Five Years Sealed

A New York judge has released what is said to be a suicide note written by convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, according to multiple reports. The handwritten note, which had been sealed in a courthouse vault for nearly five years, was made public Wednesday as part of an unrelated legal dispute involving Epstein's former cellmate.

According to PBS NewsHour, the cellmate claimed he discovered the note following Epstein's first suspected suicide attempt in jail—less than a month before his death in custody in August 2019. CBS News reported that the former cellmate had previously made this claim about when and where he found the document.

The note's authenticity remains unconfirmed. The Financial Times described it as "purported," and The Hill characterized it as "alleged," reflecting uncertainty about whether Epstein actually wrote it. The circumstances surrounding its discovery and preservation have not been fully detailed in available reports.

Epstein died in a Manhattan jail cell in 2019 while awaiting trial on sex trafficking charges. His death was ruled a suicide by the New York City medical examiner, though questions about the circumstances have persisted.
