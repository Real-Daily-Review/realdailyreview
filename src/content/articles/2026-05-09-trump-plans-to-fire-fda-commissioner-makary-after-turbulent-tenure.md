# Trump Set to Fire FDA Commissioner Makary After Turbulent Tenure

President Trump is reportedly planning to remove Food and Drug Administration Commissioner Marty Makary, according to multiple outlets, marking the end of a tumultuous period since Makary's appointment last year.

The move comes after months of turmoil at the agency, though sources note the plan is not yet final and could change. Makary's tenure has been marked by significant internal challenges, according to PBS NewsHour, which cited health policy reporting on the commissioner's struggles.

The exact reasons for the potential ouster remain unclear from available reporting, though the decision would represent a major shift in FDA leadership during Trump's administration. Makary, a physician and public health figure, has faced mounting pressure during his time leading the agency.

Watch for official confirmation from the White House and any statement from Makary regarding his potential departure.
