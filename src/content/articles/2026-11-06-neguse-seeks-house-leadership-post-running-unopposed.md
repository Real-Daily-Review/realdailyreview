# Colorado Democrat Neguse Seeks House Leadership Post—Running Unopposed for No. 4 Spot

Colorado Congressman Joe Neguse is positioning himself for a cushy leadership role that would cement Democratic control over the state's congressional delegation for years to come.

Neguse is running unopposed for assistant speaker—the No. 4 job in House Democratic leadership—according to reporting from the Colorado Sun and CPR News. If elected, he would become the youngest lawmaker ever to hold the position, a fact both outlets emphasize as a credential.

The move signals Neguse's ambitions to climb the Democratic leadership ladder and maintain influence within the party caucus. Running unopposed means no internal challenge from fellow Democrats, suggesting broad support among the party establishment.

**What conservatives should note:** Colorado's congressional representation continues tilting further left. Neguse's ascent to national Democratic leadership means more influence over party strategy on taxes, energy regulation, and gun control—issues that directly affect Colorado workers, ranchers, and Second Amendment advocates. Republicans should watch whether the state's GOP can field competitive challengers in 2026 to reclaim seats and block further Democratic consolidation of power.
