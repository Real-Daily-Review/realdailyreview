# D.C. Gym Shooting Exposes Failure of Gun-Control Policies

Another mass shooting in America's most restrictive gun-control jurisdiction has reignited the Second Amendment debate—this time in Washington, D.C., where five people were shot Thursday, including one fatally, near a gym in the Logan Circle neighborhood steps from the White House (9News Denver).

D.C. maintains some of the nation's strictest firearm regulations, yet the shooting occurred in broad daylight in one of the most heavily policed areas of the country. Police said the suspect is dead, but the incident underscores a core conservative argument: gun-control laws disarm law-abiding citizens while criminals ignore them.

**Republicans argue** that D.C.'s decades-long gun restrictions have failed to prevent violence and only ensure that potential victims cannot defend themselves. The Second Amendment protects the right to bear arms precisely in urban centers where crime rates are highest—yet progressive jurisdictions impose the harshest bans.

**Democrats counter** that more federal gun regulations are needed, though they struggle to explain why D.C.'s existing arsenal of restrictions did not stop Thursday's shooting.

For Colorado gun owners, this case reinforces why state-level Second Amendment protections matter: as progressive cities double down on failed policies, Colorado must resist similar overreach.

Watch whether D.C. politicians use this tragedy to push federal gun-control measures that would strip rights from law-abiding Coloradans.
