# California Democrats Push Election Bills While Party Grapples With Misconduct Scandal

California Democrats are advancing a slate of election bills as their legislative clock winds down—even as the party confronts internal turmoil over misconduct allegations against one of its own.

The bills include measures requiring state officials to report federal election interference to California's Department of Justice and Secretary of State, according to *Just The News*. Democrats frame these as safeguards; Republicans argue they represent partisan overreach disguised as election security, potentially weaponizing state agencies against political opponents.

Meanwhile, Rep. Jimmy Gomez (D-CA), facing sexual misconduct allegations, has told members of the Congressional Dads Caucus he won't seek another term as chairman, *Washington Examiner* reports. Gomez is simultaneously running for re-election against a progressive challenger—a sign of how fractured California's Democratic establishment has become.

**The Colorado angle:** While California's internal Democratic chaos may seem distant, Colorado Democrats have adopted similar election-security rhetoric to justify expanded government oversight of voting. Conservatives should watch whether Colorado lawmakers attempt to import California's federal-interference reporting schemes, which could create new bureaucratic layers and opportunities for partisan abuse of state power.
