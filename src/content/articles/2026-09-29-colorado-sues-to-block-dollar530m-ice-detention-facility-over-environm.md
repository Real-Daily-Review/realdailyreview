# Colorado Sues to Block ICE Detention Facility, Citing Skipped Environmental Review

Colorado's Democratic Attorney General Phil Weiser is suing the federal government to halt a planned 1,200-bed immigration detention facility in Hudson, claiming the Department of Homeland Security and ICE bypassed required environmental reviews before contracting with private prison operator GEO Group to reopen a former state prison (9News Denver, CPR News).

The lawsuit targets a $530 million contract and argues that the facility's community impact has not been fully assessed, according to the Colorado Sun. Weiser's office contends DHS violated procedural requirements by failing to complete environmental documentation before moving forward with the remodeling project.

**The conservative take:** Republicans and immigration enforcement advocates argue that environmental review delays are a common tactic used by progressive states to obstruct federal immigration enforcement. They contend that Colorado's lawsuit prioritizes bureaucratic process over border security and that detention capacity is essential for processing illegal immigrants. The GEO Group facility would add needed beds in a region where ICE operations have faced capacity constraints.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether federal courts side with the state's procedural arguments or reject them as obstruction dressed in environmental language—and whether this becomes a template for other blue states to block immigration enforcement infrastructure.
