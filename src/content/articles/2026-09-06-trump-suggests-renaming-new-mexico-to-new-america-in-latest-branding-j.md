# Trump Floats Renaming New Mexico to 'New America'—Another Branding Jab at the Left

President Trump is trolling the progressive establishment again, this time suggesting New Mexico rebrand itself as "New America" in a series of Truth Social posts over the weekend. The White House reposted the image—a map with "Mexico" crossed out and replaced with "America"—days after Trump's executive order renaming the Gulf of Mexico to "Lake America" (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

The move fits Trump's pattern of symbolic confrontation with left-wing naming conventions. Conservatives see it as pushback against what they view as identity politics and globalist framing embedded in official geography. The Left, predictably, dismisses it as performative and divisive.

**Colorado angle:** While New Mexico's rebranding doesn't directly affect Colorado law or policy, the broader message resonates with Colorado conservatives frustrated by progressive cultural dominance in state institutions. If the Biden-Harris administration's naming conventions face rollback at the federal level, pressure may mount on Colorado's Democratic legislature to reconsider its own progressive rebranding initiatives—from public lands nomenclature to state agency language.

Watch whether Trump follows through with executive action on state-level naming or whether this remains rhetorical red meat for his base.
