# Colorado GOP Governor Race Gets Crowded—and Unconventional

Victor Marx's narrow Republican primary win for Colorado governor just got complicated. The ministry leader and first-time politician, whose background centers on exorcism work and spiritual deliverance, now faces competition from Greg Lopez, a former Republican congressman running as an independent after qualifying for the 2026 ballot (Denver Post).

Marx's path to the GOP nomination raised eyebrows even within conservative circles. While he touts casting out evil spirits as his signature achievement, critics have questioned his exorcism practices and unconventional candidacy (9News Denver). Republicans backing Marx argue his outsider status and faith-based platform appeal to voters hungry for anti-establishment candidates. Skeptics counter that his lack of political experience and controversial spiritual claims could alienate moderate voters Colorado Republicans need in a general election.

Lopez's independent bid adds a wrinkle: he splits the non-Democrat vote and could fracture the conservative coalition if Marx struggles to consolidate GOP support. For Colorado taxpayers and business leaders, the real question is whether either candidate will articulate a clear platform on taxes, regulatory reform, and energy policy—or whether this race devolves into personality politics.

Watch whether Marx clarifies his actual policy positions on Colorado's economy, water rights, and Second Amendment protections before the general election heats up.
