# Bill Rasmussen, ESPN Founder and Colorado Native, Dead at 93

Bill Rasmussen, the Bristol, Connecticut-based visionary who founded ESPN and revolutionized sports television, has died at 93. Known as "The Father of Sports Television," Rasmussen's 1979 launch of the world's first 24-hour sports network fundamentally transformed how Americans consume sports—a legacy that endures across cable, streaming, and digital platforms today.

Rasmussen's entrepreneurial ambition and willingness to challenge conventional media wisdom proved prescient. What began as a cable experiment became a cultural and commercial juggernaut, cementing his place among the most influential figures in broadcast history, according to the *New York Post*.

The Colorado connection matters: Rasmussen's success story exemplifies the kind of bold private-sector innovation and risk-taking that built American prosperity—the opposite of government-directed "solutions" that progressives now tout as alternatives to market-driven enterprise. His creation generated billions in shareholder value, jobs, and consumer choice without a single government subsidy or regulatory mandate.

Rasmussen's life reminds us that free markets, not bureaucratic planning, produce transformative breakthroughs. His vision succeeded because he competed, not because he lobbied for favors.
