# Colorado Republicans Blame Democrats for Gas Prices—But Data Tells a Different Story

Republican Gabe Evans is hammering Colorado Democrats over elevated gasoline prices, a familiar campaign tactic in a state where energy costs hit voters' wallets hard. Yet experts and actual price data paint a more complicated picture than the partisan blame game suggests.

Colorado's gas prices have indeed run slightly above the national average in recent weeks, according to the *Colorado Sun*. Republicans argue that Democratic policies—particularly environmental regulations and restrictions on energy development—artificially inflate fuel costs for Coloradans. Evans and conservatives contend that blocking oil and gas projects and imposing stricter emissions rules drive up production costs, which consumers ultimately pay at the pump.

Democrats counter that Colorado's price premium reflects regional market factors, refinery capacity, and transportation costs—not state policy alone. The *Colorado Sun* reports that "experts and the numbers themselves tell a more nuanced story" than Evans's messaging suggests.

Meanwhile, the *CPR News* reports that Democratic leaders in rural Colorado are attempting to rebuild support in regions that have "turned deeper red in recent elections"—a sign the party recognizes its weakness outside urban centers, where energy jobs and agricultural concerns dominate voter priorities.
