# Trump Administration's Iran Deal Draws Fire From Conservative Hawks

The Trump administration's Memorandum of Understanding with Iran is drawing sharp criticism from the right, with conservatives arguing the agreement hands strategic advantage to a hostile regime without securing American interests.

National Review calls the MOU problematic for Americans, while Breitbart Editor-in-Chief Alex Marlow warns that "Iran getting money is never a good thing," questioning whether adequate safeguards were established before funds flow to Tehran (Breitbart). Secretary of State Marco Rubio's notably cool response at the G7 summit—remaining stone-faced while the president joked about the deal—signals internal skepticism among Trump's own foreign policy team (Washington Examiner).

Republicans argue the agreement repeats the mistakes of the Obama-era Iran nuclear deal by providing financial relief to a state sponsor of terrorism without ironclad verification mechanisms or lasting constraints on Iranian nuclear ambitions. Conservatives contend the U.S. surrendered leverage without extracting meaningful concessions.
