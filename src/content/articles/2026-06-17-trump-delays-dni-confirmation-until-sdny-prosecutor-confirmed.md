# Trump Delays DNI Confirmation Fight, Signals Hardball Tactics on Judicial Picks

President Trump is holding Jay Clayton's nomination for Director of National Intelligence hostage until his preferred U.S. attorney for the Southern District of New York gets confirmed first—a power move that underscores his determination to control key law enforcement and intelligence positions.

Trump announced the delay (Fox News, Just The News) after selecting Jamie McDonald for the crucial SDNY prosecutor role. Bill Pulte will remain acting DNI in the interim. The maneuver reflects Trump's willingness to leverage confirmation leverage across agencies to ensure his team controls both intelligence and federal prosecution in New York—traditionally one of the most consequential U.S. attorney offices.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: the SDNY office has jurisdiction over financial crimes, securities fraud, and organized crime cases that can ripple across national markets and affect Colorado-based businesses and investors. A Trump-aligned prosecutor there signals tougher enforcement against corporate malfeasance but also raises questions about politicization of federal law enforcement.

Meanwhile, Democrats nominated their Senate candidate in Alabama (Fox News), a solidly red state where the party faces "long odds" in the midterms—a sign of Democratic weakness in Trump-friendly territory heading into 2026.

Watch whether Senate Republicans confirm McDonald quickly or whether Democrats mount a filibuster fight over the SDNY pick.
