# Texas Senate Race Heats Up as Paxton Questions Talarico's Campaign Trail Absence

Republican Ken Paxton is calling out Democrat James Talarico's conspicuous absence from the campaign trail just weeks before Election Day. At a Trump rally in San Antonio, Paxton led supporters in a "Where is Talarico?" chant, with Trump himself joining the criticism and questioning the Democrat's whereabouts (Washington Examiner). 

Talarico's campaign responded that the Democrat has been sidelined with the flu, but Republicans argue the 11-day absence raises questions about his commitment to voters during a critical stretch of the race (Fox News Politics). With less than a month until Election Day, Paxton and Trump are seizing on the opening to suggest Talarico isn't serious about earning Texans' votes.

The exchange underscores how tight the Texas Senate race has become. Democrats are desperate to flip the seat, but Talarico's extended disappearance—whether due to illness or campaign strategy—hands Republicans a ready-made talking point about Democratic engagement and work ethic.

**Watch for:** whether Talarico returns to full campaign mode and whether this absence becomes a defining narrative in the final weeks before voters head to the polls.
