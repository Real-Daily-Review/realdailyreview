## Beulah Wildfire Leaves Aspen Acres Without Clean Water — Recovery Will Be Long

Water managers in the Beulah area are beginning the painstaking work of assessing utility damage from the Aspen Acres wildfire, with full restoration of clean water service still a long road ahead. Evacuations remain in effect for affected residents. While most other active fires in Colorado have so far spared critical water infrastructure, this situation is a stark reminder of how quickly wildfire can overwhelm rural communities. Colorado's ranchers, rural homeowners, and small water districts — already stretched thin — deserve state resources and a government that gets out of the way and lets recovery happen fast.
