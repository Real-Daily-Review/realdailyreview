# Bear Attack Report in Boulder County Was False Alarm

Colorado wildlife officials cleared the air Friday after initial reports of a bear attack near Ward turned out to be unfounded. The Boulder County Sheriff's Office discovered an unconscious 57-year-old man in his camper at Beaver Reservoir on Friday afternoon, but Colorado Parks and Wildlife (CPW) determined after investigation that no bear was involved in the incident.

The mix-up highlights how quickly outdoor-safety concerns can spread in mountain communities—and how important it is that officials get the facts straight before issuing warnings that could unnecessarily alarm residents and visitors. Boulder County and the Ward area draw hikers, campers, and outdoor enthusiasts year-round, and false alarms about wildlife threats can create unnecessary panic and fuel overreaction.

CPW's swift clarification prevented what could have been an overblown response. Colorado's wildlife management has generally done well balancing public safety with responsible animal stewardship, though conservatives have long argued that excessive environmental regulations sometimes hamstring land management and wildlife control efforts.

The incident underscores why Coloradans should rely on official CPW guidance rather than social-media rumors when wildlife concerns arise.
