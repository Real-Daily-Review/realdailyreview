# Senate Panel Advances Todd Blanche's Attorney General Nomination

Colorado conservatives should welcome the Senate Judiciary Committee's 12-10 vote to advance Todd Blanche's nomination for permanent Attorney General, clearing the way for a full chamber confirmation vote.

Blanche, who has served as acting AG, faced a key hurdle this week when the committee debated his record. A central issue: Blanche, in his capacity as acting Justice Department leader, formally documented that a $1.8 billion fund allegedly set aside to compensate Trump allies no longer exists—a move Sen. Chuck Grassley called overdue, saying it was a "crime" the matter took so long to resolve (Just The News).

The 12-10 party-line vote reflects the narrow Republican Senate majority. Democrats have not publicly outlined specific objections in available reporting, but the tight margin signals partisan division over the nomination.

For Colorado taxpayers and conservatives, Blanche's confirmation matters: the Attorney General oversees federal law enforcement, civil litigation, and regulatory enforcement that directly affects Colorado energy development, water rights, Second Amendment cases, and federal land policy. A permanent AG committed to restrained prosecutorial power and respect for constitutional limits could reshape how federal agencies interact with Colorado's economy and citizens.

Watch for the full Senate vote and whether any Republicans break ranks.
