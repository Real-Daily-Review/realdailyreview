# Colorado Should Demand Answers on Deportation Failures After College Player's Murder

A Nigerian national in the country illegally—shielded from deportation by multiple administrations—has been charged with murdering a UMass Dartmouth football player, according to *The Daily Wire* and Fox News. Chukwunonso David Uzoma-Eze, 19, allegedly stabbed two football players near a Providence, Rhode Island nightclub early Saturday.

The case underscores a critical failure: a deportable alien remained in the U.S. despite clear legal grounds for removal. Republicans argue this reflects the Biden-Harris administration's catch-and-release policies and refusal to enforce immigration law. Conservatives say sanctuary policies and prosecutorial discretion to shield deportable aliens from removal create preventable tragedies and endanger American citizens.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado cities including Denver and Boulder have adopted sanctuary policies limiting cooperation with federal immigration enforcement. As illegal immigration surges and violent crime by deportable aliens makes headlines, Colorado taxpayers should demand their elected officials explain why they're protecting aliens with criminal histories over public safety.

Democrats counter that mass deportation is impractical and that immigration enforcement should target serious criminals—though critics note this case shows even that standard fails when administrations refuse to act.
