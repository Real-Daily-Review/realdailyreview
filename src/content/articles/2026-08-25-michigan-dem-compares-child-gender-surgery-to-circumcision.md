# Michigan Democrat's Transgender Surgery Comparison Exposes Left's Radical Position on Child Procedures

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's attempt to normalize gender transition surgery for minors by comparing it to circumcision reveals how far the progressive left has drifted on protecting children from irreversible medical interventions.

Abdul El Sayed, running against Republican Mike Rogers in November's tightly contested Senate race, made the comparison during a Fox News appearance, according to the Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire. When pressed on his support for transgender procedures on minors, El Sayed equated the surgeries to routine circumcision—a comparison that backfired spectacularly on air.

**Republicans argue** that gender transition surgeries for children are experimental, irreversible, and fundamentally different from standard medical procedures. They contend Democrats are prioritizing ideological commitments over child welfare and parental rights.

**Democrats counter** that they support age-appropriate care and claim restrictions on such procedures harm vulnerable youth—though El Sayed's clumsy framing suggests the party struggles to articulate a coherent position when pressed.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: similar debates over parental notification, school policies on gender identity, and medical autonomy are playing out in our own state legislatures and school boards.
