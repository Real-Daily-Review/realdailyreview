# Georgia Democrats Battle for Safe Seat While Republicans Get Shut Out

Georgia's heavily Democratic 13th District is forcing a runoff between two Democrats—the late Rep. David Scott's daughter, Marcye Scott, and former school board chair Everton Blair—after neither cleared 50 percent in the special election (Fox News, Washington Examiner). The real story: Republicans didn't crack double digits, underscoring just how locked-in this seat is for the left.

This matters for Colorado conservatives watching Democratic strongholds cement control. While Georgia's 13th is safely blue, the runoff dynamic reveals how Democrats are consolidating power in urban and suburban districts nationwide. The August 25 runoff will determine which Democrat takes the seat—a foregone conclusion in a district where Republicans can't gain traction.

For Colorado taxpayers, the lesson is clear: progressive-controlled districts keep electing Democrats who support higher federal spending, regulatory overreach, and policies hostile to energy development and Second Amendment rights. Colorado's own purple districts remain battlegrounds precisely because they haven't calcified into one-party rule like Georgia's 13th.
