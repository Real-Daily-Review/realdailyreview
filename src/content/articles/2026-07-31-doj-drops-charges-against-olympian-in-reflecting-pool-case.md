# Justice Department Drops Charges Against Former Olympian in Reflecting Pool Case

The Justice Department is moving to dismiss charges against former Olympic canoeist David Hearn, accused of damaging the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool in June—a stunning reversal that exposes government incompetence, not criminal wrongdoing.

U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro filed the dismissal motion after new evidence revealed the damage resulted from "flawed installation by the contractor," not vandalism by Hearn (Washington Examiner, Just The News). The indictment was built on faulty premises, wasting taxpayer resources and subjecting an innocent citizen to federal prosecution.

This case illustrates a broader conservative concern: government agencies rushing to judgment without proper investigation, then quietly walking back charges when facts emerge. Hearn faced serious federal charges based on incomplete information—a cautionary tale for any American who might find themselves in the crosshairs of an overzealous prosecution.

While this particular case involves a D.C. landmark rather than Colorado directly, it underscores why conservatives remain skeptical of expanding federal prosecutorial power and why due process protections matter. Colorado taxpayers fund the Justice Department; they deserve an agency that gets facts straight before charging citizens.

Watch whether the contractor faces accountability for the flawed installation that triggered this mess—or if government simply moves on without consequences.
