# Two Teens Killed in I-70 Crash Near Rifle

Colorado's highways continue to claim lives, and Sunday's tragedy near Rifle is a stark reminder of the dangers facing drivers on our interstates.

Two teenagers died in a head-on collision on westbound I-70 just east of Rifle around 2:30 a.m. Sunday, according to Colorado State Patrol. The crash involved three vehicles, including a semi-truck (KDVR Fox31).

Details remain limited as troopers investigate the circumstances. Head-on collisions at that hour raise questions about driver fatigue, road conditions, and whether speed or impairment played a role—factors that consistently emerge in fatal I-70 incidents.

I-70 through western Colorado has long been a corridor of concern for safety advocates and families who've lost loved ones on the stretch. The highway's challenging terrain, heavy commercial traffic, and late-night driving create a volatile mix.

State officials should prioritize data-driven safety improvements—better lighting, clearer signage, and enforcement of reckless driving—rather than reflexive calls for speed restrictions that burden law-abiding commuters and truckers. Coloradans deserve highways that are both safe and free-flowing.

Watch for the State Patrol's final investigation report and whether it reveals preventable factors that could inform genuine safety interventions.
