# Federal Judge Defies Supreme Court on Deportations; AR-15 Ruling Signals Second Amendment Win

A Massachusetts federal judge appointed by President Biden has blocked deportations of Ethiopian nationals despite a Supreme Court ruling clearing the way for removal, underscoring the left's continued resistance to immigration enforcement even after SCOTUS spoke (Fox News Politics).

The judge's decision directly contradicts the high court's authority and reflects what conservatives see as judicial overreach. Republicans argue lower courts have no business second-guessing Supreme Court rulings on immigration policy—a critical issue for Colorado border security and law enforcement resources.

Meanwhile, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit struck down New Jersey's "assault weapons" ban, a decision that has the left scrambling. Slate lamented the ruling, claiming it gives SCOTUS "cover" to find constitutional protection for AR-15s (Breitbart). For Colorado gun owners, this signals potential relief from similar state-level restrictions that progressives have pushed for years.

Conservatives argue the Third Circuit got it right: the Second Amendment protects modern rifles, full stop. Democrats counter that such rulings undermine public safety measures, though their track record on crime prevention remains poor.
