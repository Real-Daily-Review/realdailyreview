# House GOP Fractures Over Spending While Democrats Play Both Sides on Socialism

Speaker Mike Johnson's anti-socialism resolution nearly collapsed this week when House Republicans rebelled over his federal spending record—only to be rescued by two Democrats who broke ranks (Fox News Politics).

The drama exposed a core conservative complaint: Johnson's handling of the budget has angered fiscal hawks within his own party. Republicans argue the Speaker has failed to rein in wasteful government spending, undermining the party's credibility on fiscal restraint.

Meanwhile, the Democratic Socialists of America doubled down, backing Twitch streamer Hasan Piker and attacking the anti-socialism resolution—even as moderate Democrats Jon Ossoff and Jared Moskowitz distanced themselves from the controversial figure (Fox News Politics). The split reveals Democrats' internal struggle: some want to appear mainstream; others openly embrace socialist rhetoric.

Sen. Ron Johnson (R-WI) blamed Democrats for blocking the SAVE America Act, calling it "harmful to America" to obstruct Republican fiscal reform efforts (Breitbart).
