# Israeli Strikes Kill Dozens in Lebanon Despite Ceasefire

Israeli airstrikes killed at least 39 people in Lebanon, according to BBC World, continuing a pattern of violence that has persisted since a ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah took effect on April 17.

The strikes included three drone attacks south of Beirut that killed four people, according to PBS NewsHour, alongside separate southern airstrikes that killed at least 13 more. PBS characterized the incidents as "another escalation" following the ceasefire announcement last month.

The deaths mark a significant breach of the truce, which was meant to halt the conflict between Israeli forces and the militant group. Both sides have continued to trade fire despite the deal's implementation, according to BBC World.

The ceasefire's collapse underscores the fragility of the agreement and raises questions about whether either party intends to honor the terms. The pattern of strikes suggests a return to active hostilities rather than a sustainable peace arrangement.
