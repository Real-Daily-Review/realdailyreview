# Border Security Gaps Leave Colorado Vulnerable as Illegal Crossings Surge

Colorado taxpayers are footing the bill for a wide-open southern border that's failing to stop dangerous criminals from entering the country.

An illegal immigrant trucker released into the U.S. during the Biden administration was arrested months after allegedly killing two people in a California hit-and-run, *The Daily Wire* reports. Indian national Chamkaur Singh, 30, was apprehended only after being spotted behind the wheel in another state—a stark reminder that federal immigration enforcement has collapsed.

The incident underscores why Sen. Rick Scott (R-FL) is now pushing legislation to combat illegal border tunnel crossings, *Breitbart News* reports. Republicans argue the Biden-Harris administration's catch-and-release policies have created a revolving door for criminals and traffickers, leaving states like Colorado exposed to transnational crime networks and the social costs of unvetted arrivals.

**The Colorado angle:** Border chaos fuels drug trafficking into Colorado communities, strains local law enforcement, and inflates healthcare and social service costs. Colorado sheriffs have repeatedly warned that federal border neglect makes their jobs harder and more dangerous.

Democrats defend their immigration policies as "humane" and blame Republicans for opposing "comprehensive reform," but conservatives counter that enforcing existing law and securing the border must come first—not after bodies pile up.
