# Colorado Should Embrace AI Growth, Not Strangle It With Panic

Colorado has a real opportunity to lead in artificial intelligence and data center development—but only if state policymakers resist the urge to regulate first and ask questions later.

Complete Colorado reports that a Magellan Strategies survey shows Coloradans are open to AI growth, yet the state risks falling behind competitors if it adopts the kind of restrictive policies some progressives are pushing nationally. The concern: heavy-handed regulation and artificial "safety" mandates could drive tech investment to Texas, Arizona, or other states with friendlier business climates.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Colorado's competitive advantage lies in its workforce, energy infrastructure, and business-friendly reputation. Strangling AI development with premature regulation would cost jobs and tax revenue at a time when Colorado's budget faces real pressures.

The broader national debate has taken on an almost religious tone—even Pope Leo recently issued an encyclical on AI safety concerns (9News). While legitimate questions about AI governance exist, conservatives warn against letting worst-case-scenario thinking paralyze economic opportunity.

Colorado's energy sector, already under siege from progressive climate mandates, could benefit enormously from AI-driven efficiency gains and data center operations. The state should welcome this growth, not weaponize regulation to appease environmental activists.

Watch whether Colorado's legislature resists calls for AI-specific restrictions that could undermine the state's competitive position.
