# Pennsylvania Governor Signals Restraint on Progressive Agenda—If Democrats Sweep Midterms

Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro is telegraphing a cautious approach to progressive priorities should Democrats secure a state Senate trifecta in Tuesday's midterm elections, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Shapiro indicated he'd prioritize a minimum wage hike over more ambitious left-wing initiatives—a notable retreat from the party's typical playbook when it holds unified control.

Republicans argue this reveals the political fragility of progressive governance: even Democratic governors recognize that sweeping ideological agendas alienate swing-state voters. Conservatives say the calculation underscores how unpopular far-left policies remain in battleground territory, forcing Democrats to choose between their base and electoral viability.

The restraint contrasts sharply with Colorado's own Democratic trifecta, which has aggressively pursued gun control, energy regulations, and tax increases without similar hesitation. Shapiro's hedging suggests Pennsylvania Democrats fear voter backlash—a lesson Colorado Republicans should weaponize in 2026 state races.

A *Breitbart* report notes that a professor who incorrectly predicted Harris would defeat Trump is now forecasting a Democratic midterm win, adding skepticism to establishment polling narratives.

**Watch for:** Whether Shapiro's post-election actions match his pre-election moderation, or whether Democratic pressure forces him left anyway.
