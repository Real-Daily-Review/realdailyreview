## Colorado State Board of Education Races: Don't Sleep on These

Three seats on the Colorado State Board of Education — representing Congressional Districts 1, 3, and 7 — are on the ballot today, all featuring incumbents. This board sets curriculum standards and education policy for every public school in the state. Colorado parents who have watched the board push progressive curriculum frameworks and resist parental transparency measures know exactly what's at stake. Local races like these fly under the radar, but they shape what your children are taught every single day.

## Colorado Students' Deaths Ruled Climbing Accidents — Not Weather

Chaffee County officials have released autopsy results confirming that two Colorado college students who died in a climbing incident were killed by falls — not weather exposure — with deaths described as "instant" and linked to equipment failures. This is a tragedy, full stop, and our condolences go to the families. The finding matters because early speculation pointed to conditions rather than gear failure. Coloradans who recreate in the backcountry know that personal responsibility and proper equipment aren't optional — they're life and death.
