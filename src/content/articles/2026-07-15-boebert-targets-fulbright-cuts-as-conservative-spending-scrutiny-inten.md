# Rep. Boebert Targets Fulbright Funding as Conservative Spending Cuts Gain Traction

Colorado Rep. Lauren Boebert is pushing to slash Fulbright program funding, arguing the international exchange initiative represents wasteful federal spending that should be cut as part of broader deficit reduction efforts. The Colorado Sun's opinion section warns the move could damage a "national security program" that strengthens global partnerships, though the outlet doesn't quantify the program's actual budget or Colorado's specific benefit.

Conservatives argue the Fulbright program, despite its stated diplomatic value, exemplifies the kind of international spending that balloons the federal budget without clear accountability to American taxpayers. Republicans contend that with inflation eroding household budgets across Colorado and the national debt spiraling, even "soft power" initiatives warrant scrutiny.

The left counters that cutting cultural and educational exchanges undermines American influence abroad and isolates the country from global talent pipelines—a framing that glosses over the real cost to Colorado taxpayers footing the bill.

The dispute reflects a broader conservative push to audit federal spending line-by-line. Watch whether Boebert's amendment gains co-sponsors among Colorado's Republican delegation and whether Democrats mount a serious defense of the program's ROI claims with actual data.
