# Foreign Adversaries Weaponizing Anti-Data Center Sentiment, Pentagon Warns

A Department of Defense official has alleged that foreign adversaries are orchestrating psychological operations to turn Americans against data centers and AI infrastructure—a claim that deserves scrutiny given the genuine concerns Colorado communities have raised about energy consumption and land use.

The Pentagon's assertion, reported by Fox News, frames opposition to data center expansion as potential foreign manipulation. But conservatives and Colorado residents have legitimate reasons to question massive data center buildouts: strain on local power grids, water usage in drought-prone regions, and property tax impacts on rural counties.

Meanwhile, a separate national security embarrassment has surfaced. A CIA officer, David Rush, pleaded guilty to wire fraud after the FBI discovered 298 gold bars worth $46 million, $2.1 million in cash, and luxury goods in his home (Just The News). Worse: Rush admitted exposing an intelligence source to a foreign official—a betrayal that undermines the very security apparatus now warning about foreign psyops.

**The Colorado angle:** Data center proposals are advancing across the state, particularly in rural areas. Taxpayers should demand transparency on energy costs and water impacts before accepting Pentagon talking points dismissing all opposition as foreign manipulation.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado regulators will conduct independent environmental and fiscal reviews of proposed data centers, or defer to federal security claims.
