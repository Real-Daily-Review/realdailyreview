# Trump Administration Escalates Iran Sanctions as Democrats Pursue Impeachment Theater

The Trump administration is tightening the economic noose around Iran with "Operation Economic Outcast," a Treasury Department campaign designed to isolate Tehran from global markets and punish foreign banks and companies doing business with the regime. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced the offensive roughly six months after Trump's military campaign against Iran began (The Daily Wire).

Meanwhile, House Democrats are doubling down on impeachment spectacle. Rep. Al Green introduced articles of impeachment against Trump citing deaths allegedly caused by ICE agents, continuing the left's pattern of using constitutional remedies as political weapons rather than genuine accountability measures (Just The News).

Republicans argue the impeachment push is baseless lawfare. National Review notes that federal prosecutors would not normally pursue allegations of this nature, underscoring how Democrats are weaponizing impeachment for partisan gain.

**The Colorado angle:** Trump's Iran sanctions could affect energy markets and gas prices at the pump—a direct hit to Colorado families and businesses already squeezed by inflation. Tighter Iran sanctions typically tighten global oil supplies, potentially raising costs for Colorado consumers and small businesses.

**What to watch:** Whether Treasury's economic pressure on Iran holds firm or faces pushback from multinational corporations seeking Tehran's markets.
