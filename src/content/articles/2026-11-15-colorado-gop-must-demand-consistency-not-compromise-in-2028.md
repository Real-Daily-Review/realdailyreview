# Colorado Republicans Need Backbone, Not Compromise

The 2028 Republican primary is already shaping up as a test of whether the party will nominate a genuine conservative or another establishment squish willing to bend with the political wind.

According to the Washington Examiner, the real measure of a Republican nominee isn't how they perform at donor dinners or in front of friendly crowds—it's whether they can say no to a Republican president, a Democratic governor, or their own party's money men and give the *same reason every time*. That's consistency. That's principle.

For Colorado Republicans, this matters enormously. The state has a Democratic governor and a legislature increasingly hostile to Second Amendment rights, energy development, and property rights. Colorado needs a nominee who won't cave to pressure from either party or special interests when it comes to defending gun owners, protecting water rights, or standing against regulatory overreach.

The lazy assumption in Republican politics—that you can be conservative when it's convenient and pragmatic when it's profitable—has cost the party credibility and elections. Voters can smell a flip-flopper from a mile away.

Watch whether Colorado's Republican establishment starts coalescing around a genuine conservative or settles for another compromiser who'll abandon principle the moment the pressure increases.
