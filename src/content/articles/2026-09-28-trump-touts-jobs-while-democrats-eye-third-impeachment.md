# Trump Touts Record Employment While Democrats Float Third Impeachment

President Trump claims more Americans are working than at any point in U.S. history, crediting his economic policies and pledging to deliver results the "Fake News refuses" to report (Just The News).

Meanwhile, House Democrats are already laying groundwork for potential political warfare. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) told NBC's *Meet the Press* that impeaching Trump a third time would be "setting a standard" if Democrats regain House control after November's midterms (Washington Examiner). The comment signals Democrats view impeachment as a routine political tool rather than the serious constitutional remedy it was designed to be.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the contrast is stark: one side is focused on economic growth and job creation; the other is preparing partisan investigations and impeachment theater. Colorado's energy workers, small business owners, and working families have a direct stake in which vision prevails—pro-growth policy or endless Democratic investigations that distract from kitchen-table issues.

Republicans argue that serial impeachment cheapens the constitutional process and reveals Democratic priorities are political, not governing. Democrats counter that Trump poses unique threats warranting extraordinary measures.

**Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation—particularly in swing districts—distances itself from Khanna's impeachment talk or embraces it heading into 2024.**
