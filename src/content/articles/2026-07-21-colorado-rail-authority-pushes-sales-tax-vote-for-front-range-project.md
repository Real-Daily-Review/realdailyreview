# Front Range Rail Authority Eyes November Sales Tax Push on Colorado Voters

Colorado's rail planners are gearing up to ask Front Range voters for yet another tax increase—this time a one-third penny sales tax to fund an ambitious passenger rail project stretching from Fort Collins to Pueblo (Colorado Sun). The Front Range Passenger Rail District must decide in August whether to put the measure on November ballots, with proposed stations at sports venues including Broncos Stadium and Summit FC facilities (9News Denver).

**The conservative case against it:** Republicans and taxpayers should question whether this is another government boondoggle masquerading as progress. Colorado already carries heavy tax burdens; a new sales tax—even a "small" one-third penny—hits working families, retirees on fixed incomes, and small businesses hardest. Rail projects nationwide routinely exceed budgets and fail to meet ridership projections. Before asking voters to open their wallets, the district should publish detailed cost estimates, ridership forecasts, and proof that private investment won't cover gaps.

Democrats and transit advocates counter that rail reduces congestion, supports climate goals, and spurs development—framing it as "investment" in Colorado's future.

**Watch this space:** Conservatives should demand full financial transparency before August's decision and scrutinize whether this project can survive without perpetual taxpayer subsidies.
