# Virginia Supreme Court Strikes Down Democratic Redistricting Map

Virginia's Supreme Court has nullified a voter-approved congressional redistricting plan that would have benefited Democrats, marking a major setback for the party as Republicans push redistricting efforts across multiple states.

The court's decision, according to PBS NewsHour, struck down maps that voters had approved. Former Vice President Kamala Harris responded Friday, saying Democrats are "rightfully outraged" by the ruling. "The Virginia Supreme Court ignored the will of the people and overturned those democratically chosen maps," Harris wrote, according to The Hill.

The move is part of a broader Republican strategy following a Supreme Court decision that weakened minority voting rights protections. According to NPR, Republicans in four states have moved to redistrict as part of what the outlet describes as Trump's push for favorable maps ahead of the midterms.

The redistricting battle reflects an intensifying "war of mid-decade redistricting" between the parties, as noted by PBS NewsHour. Democrats had sought to use the Virginia maps to offset Republican gains in other states, but the state court's action has reversed those efforts.

Watch for whether other states follow Virginia's lead in revisiting redistricting plans, and how the national Democratic response shapes the 2026 midterm landscape.
