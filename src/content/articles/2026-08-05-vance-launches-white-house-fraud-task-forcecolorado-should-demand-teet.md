# White House Launches Fraud-Fighting Push—Colorado Should Demand Results on Election Integrity

Vice President JD Vance is convening a White House task force to tackle fraud across federal programs, hosting lawmakers Wednesday to discuss legislative reforms and state-level enforcement actions (Fox News Politics). The administration is framing this as a "watershed" moment for cracking down on waste and abuse in government spending.

Conservatives have long argued that federal agencies hemorrhage billions annually through fraud, waste, and mismanagement—money that could stay in taxpayers' pockets or fund legitimate priorities. Vance's push signals the Trump administration intends to make good on its campaign promise to root out government corruption and inefficiency.

For Colorado, this matters. State officials and taxpayers should press the White House to prioritize election integrity, welfare fraud prevention, and energy-sector compliance audits—areas where Colorado has seen documented problems. The task force's success will depend on whether it produces actual legislative teeth or remains performative.

Just The News reports Vance has previously hosted similar roundtables with state attorneys general, suggesting a coordinated federal-state approach (Just The News). That's the right model—but Colorado Republicans must ensure the administration follows through with real enforcement, not just rhetoric.

Watch whether Congress actually passes fraud-fighting legislation, or whether this becomes another unfunded mandate.
