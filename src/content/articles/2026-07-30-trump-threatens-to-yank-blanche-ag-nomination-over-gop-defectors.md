# Trump Pressures GOP Senators Over Blanche AG Nomination

President Trump is threatening to withdraw Todd Blanche's attorney general nomination unless two Republican senators—John Cornyn of Texas and Thom Tillis of North Carolina—leave office, according to *The Daily Wire* and *9News Denver*. Blanche would remain as acting attorney general during any delay.

The standoff centers on a Justice Department fund tied to Trump's IRS settlement. Cornyn and Tillis are demanding written assurances the DOJ will not use the fund before they'll support Blanche's confirmation, *Breitbart* reports. Senate Judiciary Committee Chairman Chuck Grassley postponed Thursday's committee vote pending resolution.

Trump called Blanche "a STAR" and signaled he'll wait out the dissenting Republicans rather than compromise. The dispute reflects deeper GOP fractures over Trump's second term agenda and how far Senate Republicans will go to challenge the president's picks.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: it signals whether Trump can consolidate Republican control of the Justice Department and reshape federal law enforcement without internal GOP obstruction. Taxpayers should watch whether Blanche's confirmation ultimately clears and what concessions—if any—Trump makes to holdout senators.
