# Trump Warns Iran of Severe Consequences Over Reported D.C. Attack Plot

President Trump is taking a hard line after U.S. intelligence reportedly uncovered an Iranian plot targeting Washington, D.C., warning Tehran of "severe consequences" and "unprecedented retaliation" (Fox News). Senior administration officials have relocated to military housing as a precaution, signaling the threat is being treated with urgency.

This marks a sharp departure from the Biden administration's approach to Iran policy. Republicans have long argued that weakness and appeasement invited Iranian aggression—from nuclear ambitions to proxy warfare across the Middle East. Trump's warning reflects the conservative view that American strength and credible deterrence are the only languages hostile regimes understand.

The reported plot underscores why energy independence and a strong military posture matter for Colorado and the nation. A destabilized Middle East drives oil prices higher, hitting Colorado families at the pump and raising costs for ranchers and small businesses already squeezed by inflation. It also reinforces the case for robust defense spending and border security—priorities conservatives say the Biden administration neglected.

Democrats have not yet publicly responded to the specific threat, though the left typically cautions against escalation rhetoric with Iran.

**Watch for:** Whether Congress demands a full intelligence briefing on the plot's scope and whether Trump's warning translates into concrete diplomatic or military action.
