# Socialist Mayors Now Control Four of America's Largest Cities—Here's Why Colorado Should Care

Four of the nation's top 20 cities—Los Angeles, Washington D.C., Seattle, and New York City—are now led by Democratic Socialists of America (DSA)-affiliated mayors, affecting roughly 14 million Americans, according to Fox News and Breitbart reporting on Tuesday's elections.

Los Angeles and D.C. joined Seattle and New York in electing socialist-aligned leadership, a trend conservatives warn signals the mainstreaming of far-left governance in urban America. Republicans argue these mayors will pursue aggressive tax-and-spend agendas, expanded government services, and anti-business regulations that stifle economic growth and drive out productive residents and employers.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado's major cities remain under Democratic but non-socialist leadership, Denver and Boulder lean progressive on taxes, regulation, and land use. Conservative observers worry that socialist policy experiments in coastal megacities—from wealth taxes to aggressive rent control to defunding police—could migrate westward, influencing Colorado Democrats' policy appetite. If major cities embrace socialist economics, pressure will mount on state lawmakers to follow suit on taxes and regulation.

Taxpayers and business owners should monitor whether Colorado Democrats cite these cities as policy models—and whether DSA-style candidates gain traction in upcoming state and local races.
