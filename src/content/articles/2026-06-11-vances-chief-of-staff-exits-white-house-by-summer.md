# Vance's Chief of Staff Exits White House by Summer's End

Jacob Reses, Vice President JD Vance's chief of staff, is departing the Trump administration by the end of summer, according to multiple reports Thursday.

Reses, who assumed the role when Trump returned to the White House in January 2025, informed Vance of his departure plans several months ago after his wife became pregnant with their first child, according to *Just The News*. The exit marks another staffing shift in the second Trump administration as the White House navigates personnel changes.

No immediate replacement has been announced. Vance's office has not yet commented publicly on the transition or timeline for filling the position.

The departure comes as the Trump administration continues implementing its policy agenda across federal agencies. For Colorado conservatives, the focus remains on whether the administration will follow through on promises to reduce federal overreach in energy development, water rights, and land management—areas where federal bureaucrats have long frustrated state officials and private landowners.
