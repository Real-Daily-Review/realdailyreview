# Harris Caught in Donor Double-Game While Biden Stumbled, Ex-Dem Insider Claims

Kamala Harris was running a shadow campaign for the White House while publicly defending Joe Biden's fitness for office, according to former Democratic National Committee insider Lindy Li, who details the alleged deception in her forthcoming book.

Li claims Harris held private calls with wealthy Democratic donors insisting Biden would remain the 2024 nominee—even as she positioned herself as his successor to those same financiers. "It's all charade," Li told the *New York Post*, describing Harris's public loyalty as theater designed to keep donor money flowing while she auditioned for the top job.

The accusation cuts to the heart of Democratic establishment credibility. If accurate, it suggests Harris knowingly misled major donors about Biden's viability while the party's leadership privately acknowledged his candidacy was collapsing. Democrats have framed Biden's exit as a sudden, organic decision; Li's account suggests a coordinated, calculated transition orchestrated weeks in advance.

For Colorado Democrats banking on Harris's authenticity and decisive leadership, the revelation poses a messaging problem heading into 2026. Voters skeptical of political phoniness—a growing bloc in purple Colorado—will note that the party's standard-bearer allegedly couldn't be straight with her own donors.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic leaders distance themselves from Harris or double down on defending her donor strategy as standard political practice.
