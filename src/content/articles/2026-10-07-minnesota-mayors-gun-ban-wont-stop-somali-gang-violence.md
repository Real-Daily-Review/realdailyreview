# Minnesota's Gun-Ban Cure for Gang Violence Ignores the Real Problem

A Somali Democrat mayor's push to ban firearms in response to gang shootings in Minnesota exposes the left's reflexive anti-gun playbook—and offers a cautionary tale for Colorado.

St. Louis Park Mayor Nadia Mohamed (D) is proposing a gun ban as the solution to Somali gang violence that has plagued the Twin Cities, including a 97-casing gunfight captured on CCTV in Bloomington (Fox News). Republicans and conservatives argue this approach gets the diagnosis backwards: criminals don't follow gun bans. The real issue is gang activity, inadequate law enforcement response, and failed integration policies—not legal firearm ownership by law-abiding citizens.

Minnesota's Democratic Attorney General Keith Ellison has remained notably quiet on the gang violence, instead focusing his reelection messaging on framing opponents as bigots (Washington Examiner). Conservatives say this political deflection abandons residents demanding actual public safety.

**The Colorado angle:** This Minnesota playbook—blame guns, ignore criminal behavior, dodge accountability—mirrors arguments Colorado progressives have made for years. As Denver and other blue cities struggle with crime, expect similar calls for gun restrictions rather than enforcement of existing laws or addressing root causes.
