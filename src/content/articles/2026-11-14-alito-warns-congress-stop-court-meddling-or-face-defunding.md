# Supreme Court Justice Alito Warns Congress: Back Off or Face Defunding

Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito has issued a stark warning to Congress: stop meddling with the judiciary, or face the consequences of losing funding for the nation's highest court (Fox News Politics). Alito's remarks come as progressive lawmakers have increasingly called for court-packing schemes and other institutional attacks on the bench following decisions conservatives view as constitutionally sound.

The justice argued that some lower court judges "have gone far astray," signaling frustration with activist rulings from the bench. His warning underscores a growing conservative concern: the left's assault on judicial independence when courts rule against progressive priorities.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado voters delivered a win for Republican stability in the 3rd Congressional District, where Rep. Jeff Hurd defeated Democrat Dwayne Romero in a closely contested race (Denver Post). The district—spanning the Western Slope and southern Colorado—has remained in Republican hands since 2011, a sign that Colorado's rural and exurban voters continue rejecting left-wing overreach.

Conservatives argue that Alito's warning reflects legitimate institutional concerns: Congress shouldn't weaponize the purse to punish courts for unfavorable rulings. Democrats counter that the Supreme Court has strayed from precedent and public opinion.
