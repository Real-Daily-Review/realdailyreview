# New York Sanctuary Chaos Shows Why Border Security Matters to Colorado

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is calling for Immigration and Customs Enforcement to be abolished—a radical position that exposes the real-world consequences of sanctuary policies that Colorado taxpayers should understand.

The dispute centers on ICE's enforcement against illegal immigrants, including a recent shooting of an undocumented migrant. Rather than support law enforcement, Mamdani has accused ICE of targeting immigrants who use children as human shields during criminal activity, according to the Washington Examiner. The Department of Homeland Security fired back, blasting Mamdani and New York Governor Kathy Hochul for shielding over 16,000 illegal migrant criminals from deportation (Breitbart).

**The conservative argument is straightforward:** Sanctuary policies prioritize illegal aliens over public safety and rule of law. When cities refuse to cooperate with federal immigration enforcement, they create havens for criminal activity—and the costs eventually ripple across state lines.

Colorado has its own sanctuary jurisdictions, including Denver and Boulder, which limit cooperation with ICE. While those cities haven't gone as far as New York's rhetoric, the principle is identical: local officials choosing ideology over enforcement.

Republicans argue that abolishing ICE would be catastrophic for border security and public safety. Democrats counter that immigration enforcement is inhumane and that local police shouldn't be deputized as immigration agents.

**Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado's sanctuary cities follow New York's lead in openly defending illegal aliens over citizens.**
