# Democrats Chase Rural Colorado Voters as Cost-of-Living Anger Reshapes Electorate

Democrats are scrambling to reclaim rural Colorado counties they've lost in recent cycles, betting that listening to voter concerns—rather than doubling down on progressive messaging—can reverse their rural collapse.

According to the Colorado Sun, Democratic Party leaders across the state are shifting strategy, emphasizing direct engagement with conservative-leaning voters over ideological positioning. The shift comes as frustration over inflation and living costs reshapes voter sentiment statewide.

A Fox News poll underscores the opening: voters squeezed by rising costs are turning away from Republicans, with Trump's job approval sitting at 39 percent and a 26-point endorsement gap favoring Democrats on economic management.

**The conservative angle:** Republicans argue that Democratic spending policies—not listening tours—created the cost-of-living crisis hammering Colorado families and small businesses. Conservatives say Democrats' answer to inflation has been more government programs and regulatory burdens, not the tax relief and deregulation that could ease pressure on paychecks and energy bills.

Rural Colorado counties have swung sharply Republican over the past decade, driven by opposition to federal land restrictions, energy regulations, and gun control. Democrats' willingness to engage directly with rural voters is noteworthy—but conservatives should watch whether the party actually commits to rolling back the policies that alienated these voters in the first place.
