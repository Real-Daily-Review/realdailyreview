# Waymo Pauses Service as Robotaxis Struggle With Flooded Roads; Uber Restarts Autonomous Testing

Waymo has suspended its robotaxi operations in Atlanta and San Antonio after its vehicles repeatedly drove into flooded roads, according to TechCrunch. The company is working to address the navigation issue that caused the service interruptions in both cities.

Meanwhile, Uber is taking a different approach to autonomous vehicles. The company has relaunched its own self-driving car program—called AV Lab—but with a narrower focus than traditional robotaxi deployment, according to The Verge. Rather than operating as passenger-carrying robotaxis, Uber's vehicles will collect sensor data to support its network of robotaxi partners. The initial pilot involves a single Hyundai Ioniq 5 equipped with cameras, lidar, and radar, though Uber indicated it is not committed to that specific model long-term.

The moves reflect divergent strategies in the autonomous vehicle sector. Waymo is addressing operational challenges in existing markets, while Uber is positioning itself as a data provider and technology partner to other robotaxi operators rather than a direct service provider.

Watch for updates on whether Waymo resolves its flood-detection capabilities and resumes service, and whether Uber's data-collection model expands to additional vehicles or markets.
