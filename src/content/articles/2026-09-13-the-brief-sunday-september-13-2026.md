## Progressive Dark Money Is Buying Colorado Activists — Literally

Out-of-state progressive money is flowing into Colorado to pay "mobilizers" — essentially paid political operatives dressed up as grassroots activists. If you're willing to carry water for the left's agenda, there's a paycheck waiting. ## Colorado Voters Get a Shot at Redistricting Reform

Initiative #256 will ask Colorado voters whether congressional redistricting should be explicitly banned when its purpose is to benefit a political party. Republicans argue gerrymandering — by either party — corrupts representative government, and Colorado conservatives should pay close attention to how this measure is written and who's campaigning against it. A fair map process is the foundation of legitimate elections. Watch for dark money groups to oppose it loudly.

## New York Governor Kneecaps a Key Supreme Court Immigration Case

Gov. Kathy Hochul pardoned a convicted noncitizen, effectively killing a major Supreme Court case that would have clarified bond hearing rights for detained immigrants facing deportation. Democrats counter that the pardon was an act of mercy; conservatives say it was a calculated move to prevent the Court from setting precedent that would strengthen immigration enforcement. Colorado, which has seen its own battles over sanctuary policies and immigration enforcement, should note how blue-state governors are willing to manipulate the legal system to protect illegal immigrants from consequences.
