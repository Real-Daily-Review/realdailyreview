# New York Mayor Orders NYPD Use-of-Force Review After High-Profile Shooting

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has launched a sweeping review of NYPD use-of-force policies following the Win Rozario shooting, but the initiative falls short of his campaign promises, according to Fox News and the Washington Examiner.

Mamdani ordered a comprehensive examination of the department's force protocols over coming months, responding to pressure from progressive activists demanding stricter police accountability measures. However, conservatives note the review stops short of fulfilling key pledges made during his campaign—a pattern familiar to Colorado voters watching how progressive politicians implement (or don't) their radical rhetoric once in office.

Republicans argue that sweeping use-of-force reviews often tie the hands of law enforcement without addressing root causes of crime, making officers hesitant to respond decisively to threats. They contend that such policies can embolden criminals and endanger public safety—concerns that resonate in Colorado communities already grappling with rising crime and calls to defund police.

Democrats counter that comprehensive reviews improve accountability and rebuild community trust in policing.
