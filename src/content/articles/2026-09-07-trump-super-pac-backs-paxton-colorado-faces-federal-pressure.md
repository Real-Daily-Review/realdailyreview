# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Race as Colorado Faces Federal Pressure

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is flooding $10 million into the Texas Senate race to rescue Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton against Democrat James James Talarico, according to new FEC filings reported by *The Daily Wire*. The financial injection marks the first major commitment from Trump's war chest in a competitive general election.

Meanwhile, Colorado faces escalating pressure from the Trump administration over state sovereignty disputes. The *Colorado Sun* reports the administration is demanding Colorado relinquish constitutional powers—or face federal retaliation. The outlet frames this as a "campaign of retribution," though specifics on the disputed powers remain unclear from available reporting.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Trump's support for Paxton reflects commitment to electing America First attorneys general who will challenge federal overreach and progressive state policies. Paxton has positioned himself as a defender of state sovereignty and conservative values.

**The left's position:** Democrats and progressive outlets characterize Trump's federal pressure on Colorado as authoritarian coercion designed to punish states resisting his agenda.

**Colorado angle:** If the administration is indeed targeting Colorado over constitutional disputes, taxpayers and state officials should brace for a prolonged legal and political battle over which powers remain reserved to states under the Tenth Amendment.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership capitulates to federal pressure or mounts a constitutional defense.
