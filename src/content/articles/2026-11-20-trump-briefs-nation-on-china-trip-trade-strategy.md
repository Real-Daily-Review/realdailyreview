# Trump to Brief Nation on China Trip, Trade Talks

President Trump will hold a Friday news conference at the White House to detail what he's calling a "great and highly consequential trip" to China, where he met with President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, according to 9News Denver and the Washington Examiner.

The president announced the briefing on Truth Social while returning to Washington, signaling that trade policy will feature prominently in his remarks. Trump has made reshaping U.S.-China trade relations a centerpiece of his economic agenda, arguing that previous administrations allowed Beijing to exploit American workers and businesses through unfair practices.

For Colorado, Trump's China strategy carries direct implications. The state's agriculture sector—particularly wheat, beef, and specialty crops—depends heavily on export markets, and Chinese tariffs have historically squeezed Colorado ranchers and farmers. Energy development, another pillar of Colorado's economy, also hinges on trade dynamics and regulatory certainty that Trump has promised to restore.

Democrats have criticized Trump's trade approach as economically reckless, warning that tariffs raise consumer prices and invite retaliation. Republicans counter that tough negotiating posture is necessary to level the playing field and protect American manufacturing and workers from Chinese competition.

Watch for specifics on tariff timelines, agricultural relief measures, and whether Trump signals any Colorado-specific economic initiatives during the presser.
