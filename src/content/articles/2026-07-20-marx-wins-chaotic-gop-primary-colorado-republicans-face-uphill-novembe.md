# Colorado GOP Nominates Marx in Chaotic Primary—Conservatives Worry About November

Victor Marx, a nonprofit leader and first-time candidate, narrowly won Colorado's Republican gubernatorial primary over veteran state Sen. Barb Kirkmeyer, edging her by roughly 2,000 votes in a race that remained too close to call for days as deficient ballots were corrected (Denver Post). Marx's victory came after only two of 18 GOP gubernatorial candidates advanced from the state assembly—a sign of deep fracturing within Colorado's Republican Party.

Conservative observers at Complete Colorado warn the primary result amounts to a "death wish" for liberty advocates heading into November. The general election landscape looks grim for Republicans statewide, with Democrats controlling the governorship and much of the state apparatus.

Meanwhile, Colorado's 8th Congressional District—a genuine battleground—is shaping up as a pocketbook fight. Republican Rep. Gabe Evans faces Democrat Manny Rutinel as strategists focus messaging on inflation and cost-of-living pressures hitting Colorado families (Denver Post). That race could determine whether Republicans gain ground in a state trending blue.

**Watch:** Whether Marx can consolidate GOP support and whether the 8th District becomes a proving ground for Republican economic messaging heading into 2026.
