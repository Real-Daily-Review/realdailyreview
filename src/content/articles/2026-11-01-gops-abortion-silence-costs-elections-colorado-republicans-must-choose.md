# GOP Must Reckon With Abortion Politics, Not Dodge It

Republicans cannot win elections by pretending abortion will vanish from the ballot box. According to the Washington Examiner, the party's tendency to sidestep the issue—treating it like an ostrich with its head in sand—has cost Republicans dearly in recent cycles. Abortion remains "the social issue of our day," and voters across the political spectrum are demanding clarity, not evasion.

For Colorado Republicans, this carries real weight. The state has trended blue partly because GOP candidates have fumbled messaging on social issues, allowing Democrats to define the narrative unchallenged. Conservatives argue that Republicans must articulate a principled, persuasive case for their position rather than hoping the issue disappears. Dodging the question only cedes ground to the left's framing.

The Washington Examiner's analysis suggests Republicans face a choice: engage authentically on abortion policy and values, or continue bleeding support among voters who see the party as incoherent. Colorado's purple status means this debate will determine whether the state remains competitive or slides further Democratic.

Watch whether Colorado GOP candidates in 2024 develop a unified, confident message on abortion—or repeat the mistake of silence.
