# Colorado Democrat Primary Roils as Hickenlooper Faces Left-Wing Challenge

Colorado Democrats are battling over their Senate seat as progressive challenger Julie Gonzales attacks incumbent John Hickenlooper as a creature of establishment politics. Gonzales, running in the Democratic primary, argues voters should reject "more of the same, go-along-to-get-along politics" (Denver Post), signaling that even within the state party, Hickenlooper's moderate brand faces pressure from the left.

The challenge reflects broader Democratic infighting over how far left the party should move. Gonzales represents the progressive wing demanding more aggressive action on climate, spending, and regulation—positions that have already reshaped Colorado's legislature and statewide offices. Hickenlooper, a former governor and businessman, has positioned himself as a pragmatist, but that moderation now makes him vulnerable in a Democratic primary where the activist base holds sway.

Republicans should note the irony: while Democrats squabble over who's progressive enough, Colorado's business community and energy sector face mounting regulatory pressure from whichever Democrat wins. Whether Hickenlooper or Gonzales emerges, the general election will pit a Democrat against the Republican nominee—likely ensuring continued left-wing dominance on taxes, gun control, and land-use policy.

Watch whether Gonzales can mobilize young voters and climate activists enough to topple an incumbent, or if Hickenlooper's establishment machine and name recognition hold.
