# North Korea's Escalation Shows Why America Can't Afford Distraction Abroad

North Korea's deepening military entanglement with Russia—including troop deployments to Europe—signals a dangerous realignment that demands clear-eyed American strategy, not the complacency creeping into public consciousness.

According to *National Review*, the DPRK is "further entrenching itself in Russia's war" with "significant" military commitments. Meanwhile, *Washington Examiner* opinion notes that repeated North Korean missile tests have become so routine that they no longer shock South Korean society—a dangerous normalization of provocation.

Conservatives argue this escalation reflects the cost of American strategic confusion. While the Biden-Harris administration has focused resources on Ukraine and domestic progressive priorities, adversaries have exploited the opening. North Korea's willingness to commit forces to Russia's war effort, and its continued ballistic testing, underscore that hostile regimes don't pause when America looks away.

For Colorado, this matters: defense contractors and military installations across the state depend on a coherent national security posture. Distraction abroad, combined with the administration's push to defund and demoralize the military, weakens deterrence and invites further aggression.

Republicans insist the next administration must restore strategic clarity—prioritizing American interests, rebuilding military readiness, and making clear that adversarial coalitions carry costs.
