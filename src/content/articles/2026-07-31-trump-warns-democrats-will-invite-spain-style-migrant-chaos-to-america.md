# Trump Warns of Spain-Style Migrant Surge If Democrats Regain Power

Trump is sounding the alarm that open-border Democrat policies could turn America into another Spain—and Colorado voters should take note of what's at stake in 2026.

During a Cabinet meeting, the president pointed to Spain's recent migrant crisis, where tens of thousands of illegal aliens stormed the African enclaves of Ceuta and Melilla from Morocco, as a cautionary tale (Fox News). Trump warned that similar scenes could unfold here if Democrats return to power.

Stephen Miller, Trump's top immigration aide, was blunt: Democrats will "welcome a migrant invasion" if given the chance (Breitbart). Miller and Trump argue that Democratic open-border rhetoric and sanctuary policies have emboldened illegal crossings and created humanitarian chaos at the southern border.

Republicans contend that Democratic opposition to Trump's border enforcement agenda—including wall construction, interior enforcement, and asylum restrictions—amounts to a blueprint for uncontrolled migration. Democrats counter that Trump's rhetoric is inflammatory and that they support "humane" immigration reform, though they oppose mass deportations and strict border militarization.

For Colorado, the stakes are real: uncontrolled migration strains local services, housing, and job markets in Denver and other metro areas. Conservative taxpayers are already footing the bill for sanctuary city policies and migrant services.

**Watch for:** How Colorado Democrats respond to Trump's 2026 border messaging—and whether GOP candidates make immigration enforcement a centerpiece of state and federal races.
