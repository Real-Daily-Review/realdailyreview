# Trump Weighs Iran Response as Hawkish Allies Push Military Action

Trump is playing diplomatic chess with Iran while his own party's hawks demand he unleash the military. According to Fox News, the president is downplaying intelligence reports that Russia and China are quietly backing Iran against U.S. forces—instead saying he'll ask Putin directly about satellite imagery allegations. Meanwhile, former Vice President Mike Pence told Fox News it's time for Trump to "unleash the Armed Forces" and "finish the job" in Iran (Breitbart).

The Washington Examiner's editorial board argues that holding Iran accountable for its attacks on Israel and U.S. interests isn't just morally justified—it's strategically necessary, given Tehran's pattern of proxy warfare through Hamas, Hezbollah, and the Houthis.

**The conservative split:** Pence and hawkish Republicans see restraint as weakness and demand decisive military action. Trump, by contrast, appears to be signaling he'll pursue diplomacy first—a posture that frustrates those who view Iran's regime as beyond negotiation.

For Colorado, this matters: any major Middle East escalation could spike energy prices, affecting gas pumps and heating costs statewide, while military action could impact defense contractors and veterans in the state.

Watch whether Trump pivots toward military strikes or maintains his diplomatic gambit—and whether Pence's faction can move him.
