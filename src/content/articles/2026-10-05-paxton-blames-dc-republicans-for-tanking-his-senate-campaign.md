# Texas AG Paxton's Senate Bid Unravels Under DC Republican Control

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton is publicly blaming Washington Republican leadership for sabotaging his Senate campaign—a damning admission that the GOP establishment is actively working against a conservative firebrand who has challenged the party's direction.

In leaked audio obtained by Politico and reported by the *Washington Examiner* and *New York Post*, Paxton vented that he has "never been in a race where I felt less control over the campaign" because "it's all coming from DC." He acknowledged his campaign is being run by outsiders, signaling that national Republicans are dictating strategy and messaging rather than letting the Texas AG control his own race.

The dysfunction runs deeper. Senator John Kennedy (R-LA) notably refused to endorse Paxton's character when asked directly if he "has the character to serve" in the Senate, according to the *Daily Caller*—a public rebuke from a fellow Republican that underscores establishment doubts.

For Colorado conservatives, this is instructive: it shows how the national GOP apparatus can marginalize candidates who don't toe the party line, even when those candidates have strong conservative credentials. Paxton's struggle illustrates the tension between grassroots conservative energy and Washington's preference for controlled, predictable candidates.

Watch whether Paxton's campaign implodes entirely or whether Texas voters reject the DC establishment's interference.
