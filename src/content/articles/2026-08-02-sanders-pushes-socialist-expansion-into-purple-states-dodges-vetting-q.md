# Bernie Sanders Pushes Socialist Expansion Into Purple States—But Dodges Vetting Questions

Socialist Sen. Bernie Sanders is openly confident that Democratic socialist candidates can win in politically divided states, not just deep-blue strongholds—a stark reminder of how far left the Democratic Party's activist base has drifted.

Speaking on NBC's *Meet the Press*, Sanders pointed to recent progressive primary victories as proof that socialist candidates can compete nationally. He praised Maine Democratic Senate nominee Troy Jackson's "working-class background and progressive platform," but notably hedged when pressed on whether Democrats had properly vetted Jackson following the implosion of his predecessor, Graham Platner.

**The conservative concern:** Sanders's confidence in expanding socialism into purple states should alarm Colorado voters. If national Democrats embrace socialist economics—higher taxes, government-run programs, regulatory overreach—those ideas will inevitably reach Colorado's competitive districts. Colorado Republicans should prepare messaging around the real-world costs of socialist policy: job losses, inflation, and the erosion of individual liberty.

Sanders's refusal to answer basic vetting questions also exposes Democratic carelessness. Voters deserve to know whether nominees have been properly scrutinized before their names appear on ballots.
