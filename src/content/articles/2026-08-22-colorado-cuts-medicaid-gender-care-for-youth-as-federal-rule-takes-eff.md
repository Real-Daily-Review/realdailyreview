# Colorado Suspends Medicaid Gender-Affirming Care for Youth Under Federal Rule

Colorado is halting Medicaid coverage for gender-affirming care treatments for transgender youth, following a new federal rule that blocks federal dollars from funding such procedures (Colorado Sun). The change will affect hundreds of young Coloradans currently receiving care.

The suspension reflects a broader conservative push to restrict taxpayer-funded gender ideology in schools and healthcare. Republicans argue that federal dollars shouldn't subsidize experimental treatments on minors, while Democrats and progressive advocates contend the restrictions harm vulnerable youth and deny them access to medically necessary care.

Meanwhile, a federal judge has temporarily blocked Colorado's law expanding state health authority over immigrant detention facilities, ruling against unannounced inspections at the GEO Group's detention center (Colorado Sun). The company is now moving to shift Colorado's separate lawsuit—filed by the state attorney general over a tuberculosis investigation—from state to federal court (CPR News). Conservatives say aggressive state overreach on detention facility regulation risks federal preemption; Democrats argue the state has legitimate public health obligations to monitor conditions.

**Watch:** Whether Colorado's attorney general pursues the TB investigation in federal court and how the state responds to further judicial limits on detention-facility oversight.
