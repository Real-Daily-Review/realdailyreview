# Democrats Fold on Tariff Authority, Hand Trump Sweeping Russia Sanctions Win

Congress has passed a sprawling Russia sanctions bill that grants the White House broad tariff powers—a victory Republicans have sought while Democrats reluctantly signed on, according to Fox News Politics.

The legislation, named after the late Sen. Lindsey Graham, sailed through the House with bipartisan support after more than a year of negotiation (9News Denver). The bill targets Russian aggression and sanctions evasion, but its real prize for conservatives is the tariff authority it hands to the executive branch.

**Republicans argue** the measure strengthens America's hand against hostile regimes and gives the president necessary flexibility to protect U.S. interests. **Democrats counter** that the tariff provisions are dangerously broad and could be weaponized against allies, according to Fox News Politics—a concern that underscores their reluctance to support the final package.

For Colorado, the outcome matters: tariff authority could reshape trade dynamics affecting the state's agriculture, energy, and manufacturing sectors. Farmers and ranchers should monitor how the administration deploys these powers, particularly regarding agricultural exports and retaliatory measures from trading partners.

**Watch next:** Whether Trump uses this tariff authority narrowly against adversaries or broadly in ways that roil markets and Colorado's export-dependent industries.
