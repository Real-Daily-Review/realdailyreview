# America 250 Celebration Shouldn't Be Hijacked by Left-Wing Mockery

As Americans mark the nation's 250th birthday, conservatives are pushing back against late-night comedians and progressive voices using the milestone to bash American traditions rather than celebrate them.

Washington Examiner columnist Joe Concha criticized late-night host Jimmy Fallon for mocking the Great American State Fair, comparing fair foods to medical waste. "The public should be able to enjoy America 250 without added 'nonsense' from the Left," Concha wrote, arguing that cultural institutions deserve respect during the nation's anniversary year (Washington Examiner).

Concha also warned that establishment Democrats face a reckoning as socialist voices gain influence within the party. "The socialist genie is out of the bottle," he said on Fox &amp; Friends, predicting that leftward drift will cost Democrats at the ballot box (Washington Examiner).

Meanwhile, conservatives are reclaiming the narrative around American identity. Breitbart contributor Dylan Gwinn emphasized baseball as central to any honest telling of American history and values during the 250th celebration.

For Colorado conservatives, the broader message is clear: progressive elites use cultural moments to demean working-class traditions—state fairs, baseball, Americana—rather than unite around shared heritage. Watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership embraces or distances itself from this anti-American sentiment as the anniversary year unfolds.
