# Biden Judge Frees MS-13 Murderer, Raising Questions About Judicial Overreach on Immigration Enforcement

A federal judge appointed by President Biden has released an MS-13 gang member convicted of murder after ruling that Immigration and Customs Enforcement violated due process by holding him without a required bond hearing for five months, according to Fox News and Just The News.

The suspect, known as "La Bruja" (the witch), is now free in California despite being wanted for murder. Department of Homeland Security Secretary Alejandro Mayorkas condemned the decision, telling Just The News: "This radical activist judge has put American lives at risk by releasing a wanted murderer."

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and DHS officials argue the ruling exemplifies how judicial activism on immigration undermines public safety. They contend that while due process matters, releasing violent criminals on technicalities prioritizes legal procedure over protecting Americans from dangerous offenders.

**The broader concern:** This case reflects a pattern conservatives say shows how Biden-appointed judges are constraining immigration enforcement at the border and interior—a trend that could affect Colorado communities if criminal aliens are released into the state.

Coloradans should watch whether this decision sparks legislative pushback from Republicans demanding clearer timelines for immigration detention and bond hearings that don't result in murderers walking free.
