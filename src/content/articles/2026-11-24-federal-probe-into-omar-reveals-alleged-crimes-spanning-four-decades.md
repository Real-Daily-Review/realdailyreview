# Federal Probe Into Omar Reveals Alleged Crimes Spanning Four Decades

A federal judge unsealed search warrant documents Monday revealing that investigators have been examining Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN) for alleged immigration fraud and other crimes dating back to 1980—over 40 years of potential wrongdoing.

The redacted filings, released under court order, detail the scope of a Homeland Security Investigations raid on Omar's Washington D.C. home, during which agents seized her phone and passport. According to the *Washington Examiner* and *New York Post*, prosecutors allege Omar committed offenses "between 1980 to the present," suggesting a decades-long pattern of alleged criminal conduct.

The unsealed documents offer the first public glimpse into what federal investigators believe they've uncovered. While specifics remain redacted, the breadth of the investigation—spanning more than four decades—underscores the seriousness prosecutors attach to the case.

Omar, a prominent progressive voice in Congress, has not been charged. Her office has not yet responded to requests for comment on the newly released filings.

**What to watch:** Whether additional charges materialize and how Democratic leadership responds to one of their own facing a federal criminal probe of this magnitude.
