# WNBA Star Cunningham Refuses Apology for Defending Women's Sports—and Sales Surge

A professional athlete willing to state the obvious without groveling to the woke mob is apparently newsworthy. Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham sparked predictable outrage from progressive sports media after publicly opposing men competing in women's sports under the banner of "inclusivity." Rather than capitulate, Cunningham doubled down, posting an unapologetic Instagram message: "I said what I said" (The Daily Wire).

The backlash from the left-wing sports establishment was swift and vicious. Yet Cunningham's refusal to bend the knee has resonated with ordinary Americans: her signature pink Adidas basketball shoes sold out online following her comments (Breitbart), suggesting that common sense on sex-based fairness in athletics still commands real support among consumers.

This moment exposes the progressive left's playbook—weaponize social media outrage, demand apologies, and shame dissenters into silence. Cunningham's stand proves that strategy doesn't always work when someone refuses to play along. National Review noted her comments simply stated "the obvious about men playing women's sports under the guise of inclusivity."

Colorado conservatives should watch whether other athletes find courage in Cunningham's example to speak plainly on issues the corporate media treats as third rails.
