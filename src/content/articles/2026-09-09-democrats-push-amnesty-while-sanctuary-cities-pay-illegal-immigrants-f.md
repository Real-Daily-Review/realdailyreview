# Colorado Should Reject the Open-Border Left's Citizenship Giveaway

Democrats are doubling down on amnesty schemes while American taxpayers foot the bill for illegal immigration's chaos.

Texas Democratic Senate nominee James Talarico this week called for a pathway to citizenship for illegal immigrants already in the U.S., claiming he's a "border security Democrat" (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, sanctuary jurisdictions are literally paying illegal immigrants' families with public money: Arlington County, Virginia just voted unanimously to spend $50,000 in taxpayer funds on families whose wage earners were detained by ICE (The Daily Wire).

**Here's what conservatives say:** This is fiscal recklessness masquerading as compassion. Colorado taxpayers already shoulder massive costs for emergency services, education, and social programs serving illegal immigrants. A federal pathway to citizenship rewards lawbreaking, incentivizes more illegal crossings, and undercuts American workers competing for jobs. Sanctuary policies that shield illegal immigrants from federal enforcement—and then compensate their families—invert the rule of law entirely.

Democrats frame amnesty as "humane" and "practical," but Republicans rightly argue it's a magnet for more illegal immigration and a betrayal of citizens who follow the law and pay taxes.

Colorado's legislature has already moved left on immigration enforcement. Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether state Democrats push similar "sanctuary" spending schemes or citizenship pathways that would drain Colorado's budget while rewarding illegal entry.
