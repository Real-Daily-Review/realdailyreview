# Border Enforcement Failures Expose Colorado to Criminal Aliens

Colorado taxpayers and law-abiding residents face mounting risk as federal immigration enforcement collapses under Biden-Harris policies. Recent cases reveal a pattern: illegal aliens with serious criminal histories—including murder suspects, assault perpetrators, and major fraud convicts—are entering the country unchecked or being released into communities.

An Indian national overstayed his visa and defrauded Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce in a $35 million Ponzi scheme before conviction (The Daily Wire). A Guinean national apprehended at the Arizona border in 2023 under the Biden administration now faces assault charges, with authorities suspecting additional victims (Just The News). A man arrested for speeding turned out to be wanted in Honduras for involvement in a 2016 shooting massacre (Just The News).

Republicans and conservatives argue these cases expose the catastrophic failure of border security and interior enforcement. When ICE lacks resources, when catch-and-release policies prevail, and when visa overstays go unmonitored, violent criminals and fraudsters operate freely in American communities—including Colorado.

Democrats have resisted GOP calls for stricter enforcement and border controls, framing immigration restrictions as discriminatory rather than a public safety imperative.
