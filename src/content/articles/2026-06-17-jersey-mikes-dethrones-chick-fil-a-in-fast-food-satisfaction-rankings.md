# Fast-Food Shifts: Jersey Mike's Tops Chick-fil-A in Customer Satisfaction

A new customer satisfaction survey has dethroned Chick-fil-A from its years-long reign atop America's fast-food rankings, with Jersey Mike's Subs now claiming the top spot (9News Denver). The shift marks a notable change in consumer preferences, ending Chick-fil-A's dominant streak after leading all fast-food brands just a year prior.

Meanwhile, McDonald's is capitalizing on patriotic nostalgia ahead of America's 250th birthday by bringing back its discontinued fried apple pies for a limited-time run (The Daily Wire). The move taps into consumer appetite for classic Americana as the nation approaches its milestone celebration.

For Colorado consumers and business owners, these trends underscore a competitive fast-food landscape where customer loyalty remains fluid and brand heritage—whether Chick-fil-A's values-driven positioning or McDonald's Americana appeal—can shift quickly. Local Colorado franchisees in these chains will be watching how national momentum translates to foot traffic and sales in the state's competitive QSR market.
