# Tucker Carlson Abandons GOP, Vows to Build Third Party—Colorado Republicans Should Pay Attention

Tucker Carlson is walking away from the Republican Party and pledging to build a third political force, declaring the GOP part of a "lock-step" establishment that ignores what "really matters"—war and finance (Daily Caller). In an interview with the Columbia Journalism Review, Carlson said he plans to "help build a third party" and called for "a good-faith effort to figure out what benefits the country" (Washington Examiner).

The move marks a dramatic split from mainstream conservatism. Carlson's departure signals deep fractures within right-wing politics over the GOP's direction and priorities.

**Why Colorado conservatives should care:** Third-party movements historically splinter conservative votes in swing states like Colorado, potentially handing elections to Democrats. If Carlson's effort gains traction among disaffected Republicans—particularly younger or populist-leaning voters—it could reshape Colorado's electoral math in 2026 and beyond, especially in tight congressional and statewide races where margins matter.

Republicans defending the party line will argue that fracturing the right-wing vote only empowers the left. Carlson's supporters counter that the GOP has abandoned its base on core issues.
