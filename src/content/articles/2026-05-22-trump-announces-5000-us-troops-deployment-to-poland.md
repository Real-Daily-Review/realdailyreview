# US to Deploy 5,000 Troops to Poland, NATO Chief Welcomes Move

President Trump announced Thursday that the United States will send an additional 5,000 troops to Poland, according to CNBC. The announcement came ahead of a NATO foreign ministers meeting and was welcomed by the alliance's chief, according to BBC World and Deutsche Welle.

The deployment represents a significant military commitment to NATO's top-spending member state. Trump made the announcement on Truth Social, stating: "I am pleased to announce that the United States will be sending an additional 5,000 Troops to Poland."

However, the move has sparked concern among some European allies. According to Al Jazeera, the deployment underscores broader uncertainty about US policy direction, with the announcement described as part of "erratic US policy moves" that leave European NATO partners "increasingly unclear about which areas they should prioritise."

NATO leadership, meanwhile, has framed the troop commitment positively as a demonstration of alliance solidarity and defense readiness. The deployment aligns with discussions about increased NATO defense spending, with NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte noting that the alliance will spend hundreds of billions of dollars on defense.
