# Boulder County's Tax-Funded Trump Anxiety Session Reveals Progressive Panic

Boulder County officials have turned a public "election champions" program into what amounts to taxpayer-subsidized therapy for Trump-obsessed residents, according to Complete Colorado. The presentation reportedly focused less on election integrity and more on processing collective anxiety about the incoming administration—a striking admission of how thoroughly progressive Boulder has surrendered to Trump derangement.

This is what happens when left-leaning counties treat elections as ideological crusades rather than neutral civic functions. Instead of ensuring fair processes and voter confidence across the political spectrum, Boulder's approach weaponizes public resources to comfort one faction while dismissing legitimate concerns from conservatives and Republicans about election administration.

The episode underscores a broader problem: government-funded programs in deep-blue counties increasingly serve as echo chambers for progressive grievance rather than institutions serving all taxpayers. Boulder residents—and Colorado voters statewide—should ask hard questions about how their tax dollars are being spent on what amounts to political activism dressed up as civic engagement.

Conservatives should watch whether other Colorado counties follow Boulder's lead in using public funds for partisan anxiety management rather than actual election administration improvements.
