# WHO Upgrades Ebola Risk to 'Very High' in Democratic Republic of Congo

The World Health Organization has elevated its risk assessment for the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo to "very high" at the national level, citing rapid spread of the virus, according to PBS NewsHour.

WHO chief Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said Friday that the outbreak is "spreading rapidly" across the country. The UN health agency maintains that the risk remains "high" in the wider region but stays "low" globally, according to BBC World.

Provincial authorities in Ituri province—the epicenter of the outbreak—have responded by banning public gatherings in an effort to contain transmission, according to Al Jazeera English.

The upgraded assessment reflects growing concern about the virus's trajectory within Congo's borders, though international health officials have not signaled alarm about global spread at this stage. The move comes as health authorities work to implement containment measures across affected areas.

Watch for updates on case numbers, vaccination campaigns, and whether additional countries in the region raise their own risk assessments.
