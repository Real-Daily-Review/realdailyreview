# California Firefighter Allegedly Faked Death to Dodge Federal Charges

A former California firefighter indicted on federal charges may have staged his own drowning to escape prosecution, the FBI says. Charles Harris allegedly faked his death in a boating accident in April 2023—just one month after facing federal indictment—according to investigators (9News Denver).

The case underscores a troubling reality: when federal enforcement teeth are dull or defendants calculate the odds of conviction as poor, some will go to extreme lengths. Harris's alleged scheme, if confirmed, represents a stark failure of the federal system to deter flight from justice or adequately monitor high-risk defendants.

If Harris was deemed safe enough to remain free pending trial, how did authorities miss warning signs of potential flight?
