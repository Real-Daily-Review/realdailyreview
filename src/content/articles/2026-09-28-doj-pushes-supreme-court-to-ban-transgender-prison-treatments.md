# DOJ Seeks Supreme Court Backing for Prison Transgender Treatment Ban

The Justice Department is asking the Supreme Court to enforce a Bureau of Prisons policy that would end hormone therapy and gender-related accommodations for transgender federal inmates, according to Fox News and Just The News.

U.S. District Judge Royce Lamberth had previously ordered the Trump administration to continue providing such medical care to a class of transgender prisoners. The DOJ's appeal signals the administration's intent to reverse that mandate.

**The conservative argument:** Taxpayer-funded gender treatments in federal prisons represent wasteful spending on a narrow population while Colorado and other states struggle with core corrections priorities—inmate safety, staffing, and rehabilitation programs. Republicans contend that prison policy should prioritize security and cost-effectiveness, not ideological accommodation. The Trump administration's position reflects the view that federal resources should serve the broader inmate population and legitimate penological interests, not specialized medical demands.

**Democrats counter** that denying medical care violates inmates' constitutional rights and basic human dignity, regardless of incarceration status.

The case highlights how federal prison policy—funded partly through Colorado's federal tax dollars—has become a flashpoint in the broader culture war. Conservatives should watch whether the Supreme Court sides with the DOJ or upholds the lower court's mandate, setting precedent for state prison systems nationwide.
