# GOP Faces Midterm Headwinds as Democrats Smell Blood

Speaker Mike Johnson is putting on a brave face, but Republicans heading into the midterms are battling inflation, elevated gas prices, and sagging approval ratings—precisely the economic conditions that typically punish the party in power. Democrats, sensing opportunity, are preparing a fragmented but aggressive response to the Republican National Committee's midterm convention, though party strategists remain divided on how best to exploit GOP vulnerabilities (Washington Examiner).

Johnson insists he remains "very bullish" on Republican prospects despite the headwinds (Fox News Politics), but the math tells a different story. With Election Day two months away, Democrats are openly eyeing Senate and House majorities—a stunning reversal from historical midterm patterns that typically favor the opposition party.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's competitive House seats and the state's purple-state status make it a prime battleground. Inflation and energy costs hit Colorado families and small businesses hard, but so do Democrats' regulatory overreach on oil and gas development. Republicans argue the Biden administration's anti-energy policies have artificially inflated prices; Democrats blame corporate greed and Russia's invasion of Ukraine.
