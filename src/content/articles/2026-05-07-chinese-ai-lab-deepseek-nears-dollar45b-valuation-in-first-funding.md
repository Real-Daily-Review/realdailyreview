# Chinese AI Lab DeepSeek Nears $45B Valuation in First Funding Round

DeepSeek, a Chinese artificial intelligence lab, is approaching a $45 billion valuation in its first investment round, according to TechCrunch and the Financial Times. The valuation reflects investor appetite for the startup, which counts Tencent among firms seeking stakes in the company.

DeepSeek gained prominence in early 2025 after launching a large language model that required significantly less computing power and capital than comparable U.S. models from OpenAI and Anthropic, per TechCrunch. The efficiency of its approach has drawn attention from major investors as the global AI race intensifies.

The fundraising discussions are ongoing, the Financial Times reported. The valuation would mark a rapid ascent for the startup, which has captured investor interest despite intense competition from established American AI firms.

Watch for the final terms of DeepSeek's funding round and whether the $45 billion valuation holds as negotiations conclude.
