# New York AG's Trump Prosecution Faces Credibility Crisis Over Alleged Selective Justice

New York Attorney General Letitia James's office is under fire after a whistleblower alleged the prosecution of Donald Trump was driven by predetermined conclusions rather than evidence—a damning indictment of how progressive prosecutors weaponize the justice system against political opponents.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, the whistleblower compared the prosecutorial approach to medical malpractice: selecting the target first, then manufacturing the case to fit. This mirrors conservatives' long-standing concern that Democratic-led jurisdictions have abandoned impartial law enforcement in favor of partisan vendetta.

The allegation strikes at the heart of rule-of-law arguments: prosecutors exist to follow evidence, not predetermined outcomes. When government lawyers reverse-engineer cases to target political enemies, they undermine public trust in courts and validate Republican warnings about institutional capture by the left.
