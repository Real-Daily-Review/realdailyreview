# Broncos Lock Down Moss with $67M Deal

The Denver Broncos secured cornerback Riley Moss to a four-year, $67 million contract extension, positioning the defensive back as a cornerstone of the franchise's secondary going forward.

The deal carries a $16.75 million annual average, making Moss the NFL's 23rd-highest-paid cornerback overall but the fifth-highest-paid at the No. 2 corner position (9News Denver). The extension signals the Broncos' commitment to building a competitive defense after years of secondary struggles that plagued the team's playoff hopes.

Moss, a key contributor to Denver's defensive scheme, represents management's bet that locking in young talent early prevents bidding wars in free agency. The contract structure allows the Broncos to manage salary-cap flexibility while retaining a player the front office views as essential to their defensive identity.

For Colorado sports fans, the deal underscores the Broncos' willingness to invest in homegrown talent rather than chase aging veterans—a fiscally sound approach that mirrors conservative principles of disciplined spending and long-term planning.
