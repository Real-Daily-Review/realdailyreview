# Harris Courts Socialist Wing of Democratic Party

Kamala Harris is reportedly networking with far-left figures including New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and other socialist leaders within the Democratic Party, signaling her likely 2028 presidential ambitions (Washington Examiner).

The move underscores how far the Democratic Party has drifted leftward. Harris, who lost the 2024 general election, appears to be consolidating support among the party's socialist and communist-aligned faction—a telling indicator of where Democratic primary power now resides.

Conservatives argue this alignment reveals the Democratic Party's true ideological center of gravity. Rather than moderate toward the political center after a national defeat, Harris is embracing the radical wing that has pushed policies like Green New Deal mandates, defund-the-police movements, and open-border immigration schemes that have damaged Democratic electoral prospects.

For Colorado, this matters. The state's Democratic establishment has already adopted many of these far-left priorities—aggressive climate regulations that threaten energy jobs, gun-control measures that erode Second Amendment rights, and spending policies that fuel inflation and cost-of-living crises for working families.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from Harris's socialist courtship or double down on alignment with the national party's radical wing heading into 2026 state races.
