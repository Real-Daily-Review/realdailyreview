# Colorado Governors Face Pressure to Embrace School Choice—and Stop Leaving Education Dollars on the Table

Colorado policymakers are sitting on a massive opportunity to expand education freedom, and conservatives say it's time to act. A new state-by-state analysis from the American Enterprise Institute highlights what governors nationwide risk by delaying school choice reforms—with 52 million students potentially affected by inaction, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

The push centers on Education Savings Accounts (ESAs) and education freedom tax credits, which conservatives argue represent the most significant expansion of education opportunity in a generation. Unlike traditional public-school funding models that lock families into zip-code-assigned districts, ESAs and tax credit scholarships let parents direct education dollars toward private schools, homeschooling, tutoring, and other alternatives.

The *National Review* notes that ESAs pair particularly well with income-tax elimination strategies, offering a market-driven path to school choice that doesn't require massive new government bureaucracies.

For Colorado families—especially those in underperforming districts or rural areas with limited options—school choice reforms could unlock real alternatives without waiting for another round of failed education spending increases. The state's Republican leadership should seize this moment to champion parental control over education dollars rather than ceding that power to Denver bureaucrats.
