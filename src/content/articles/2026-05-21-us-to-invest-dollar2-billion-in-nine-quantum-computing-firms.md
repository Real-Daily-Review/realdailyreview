# U.S. to Invest $2 Billion in Nine Quantum Computing Firms

The U.S. government plans to award $2 billion in grants to nine quantum computing companies and take equity stakes in the firms, according to CNBC. The move aims to bolster the domestic quantum sector, a technology area seen as strategically important.

The beneficiaries include a startup backed by a firm with links to the Trump family, according to Ars Technica and the Financial Times. The equity stake approach marks a shift from traditional grant-making, positioning the government as a shareholder in the companies' success.

Quantum computing stocks rose on the announcement, reflecting investor optimism about the sector's growth prospects and government backing.

The initiative underscores Washington's focus on quantum technology as a competitive priority, though details on the selection criteria and timeline for the investments remain limited in available reports.

Watch for announcements naming all nine recipients and details on the equity terms and governance arrangements.
