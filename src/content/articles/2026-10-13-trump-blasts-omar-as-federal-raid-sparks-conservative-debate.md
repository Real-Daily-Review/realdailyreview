# Trump Rallies Against Omar as DOJ Probe Intensifies

President Trump escalated his criticism of Rep. Ilhan Omar at an Ohio rally, with crowds chanting "deport" hours after federal agents raided the Minnesota Democrat's home. Trump called Omar "a terrible person" and "a terrible congresswoman," though he stopped short of endorsing deportation calls himself (The Daily Wire).

The federal search—details of which remain unclear—has ignited fierce debate even among conservatives. National Review's editors expressed ambivalence, noting they find themselves "rooting against both sides," suggesting concern about both Omar's record and potential DOJ overreach.

**The conservative case against Omar is straightforward:** Republicans have long criticized her anti-Israel rhetoric, her 2019 tweet suggesting pro-Israel advocacy groups push "allegiance to a foreign country," and her pattern of inflammatory statements about American foreign policy. Conservatives argue she represents the progressive wing's hostility toward U.S. allies and traditional American interests.

**The complication:** Some right-leaning outlets worry that aggressive federal prosecution of political opponents—even ones they dislike—sets a dangerous precedent for government power, regardless of which party wields it.

For Colorado conservatives, this serves as a reminder that federal law enforcement independence matters, even when targeting figures you oppose.
