# Colorado's Largest Active Wildfire Swells Past 91,000 Acres as Containment Lags

Colorado's Aspen Acres Fire—now the state's largest active wildfire—has ballooned to over 91,500 acres across Pueblo and Custer counties with containment stuck at just 12 percent, forcing new evacuations in Fremont County and threatening mountain communities from Leadville to the southern Front Range.

About 800 firefighters are battling the blaze, which grew by 900 acres overnight near Leadville, prompting mandatory evacuations southwest of the city (Colorado Sun). The Willow fire near Leadville and the Gold Mountain fire have also disrupted regional life—the Ouray 100 race was canceled due to fire danger.

Officials expressed cautious optimism Saturday morning, citing higher relative humidity and cloud cover as conditions that could help crews gain ground (CPR News). However, progress remains slow: containment edged from 13 percent to 12 percent by Monday, July 6, according to the Alaska Complex Incident Management Team (KDVR).

Residents across Pueblo, Custer, and Fremont counties face devastating losses as evacuation zones expand. The fire underscores Colorado's vulnerability to catastrophic wildfire season—a reality that demands robust forest management and swift response, not bureaucratic delays.
