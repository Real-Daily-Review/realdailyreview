# Denver Could Send Democratic Socialist to Congress

A 29-year-old democratic socialist named Melat Kiros is positioned to represent Denver in Congress, raising concerns among Colorado conservatives about the direction of the state's political representation.

According to CPR News, Kiros, who is aligned with the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA), could soon become Denver's congressional representative. Republicans argue that DSA-backed candidates push policies fundamentally at odds with Colorado's tradition of fiscal restraint and individual liberty—including expansive government spending, wealth redistribution schemes, and regulatory overreach that would burden Colorado's small businesses and working families.

The DSA platform advocates for Medicare for All, aggressive climate regulations, and wealth taxes—all positions that would translate into higher taxes and reduced economic freedom for Colorado taxpayers and job creators. Conservatives warn that a DSA representative in Congress would vote for the kind of progressive legislation that has already made Colorado's Front Range increasingly unaffordable and business-hostile.

Democrats counter that Kiros represents grassroots energy and younger voters demanding action on healthcare, climate, and inequality. They argue her election would amplify Colorado's progressive voice in Washington.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Kiros advances in the primary and whether her DSA platform gains traction among Denver voters—a bellwether for how far left Colorado's urban centers are willing to drift.
