# Trump Weighs Iran Escalation as Ceasefire Collapses

President Trump is signaling readiness to expand military strikes against Iran following the collapse of a ceasefire just two weeks ago, according to the Washington Examiner and Just The News. U.S. Central Command has announced daily bombing operations targeting Iran's ability to threaten commercial shipping in regional waterways.

Trump and Secretary of State Rubio have cited difficulties negotiating with current Iranian regime figures, with Just The News reporting the administration views escalation as a tool to pressure hardliners obstructing a potential peace deal. The Washington Examiner notes concerns about transparency in casualty reporting and the scope of expanded operations.

**The Colorado angle:** Military escalation abroad typically increases defense spending and could affect Colorado's aerospace and defense contractors, particularly those in the Denver metro and along the Front Range. Broader Middle East conflict also carries implications for energy markets and gas prices—a direct hit to Colorado households and businesses already dealing with inflation.

Conservatives generally support Trump's willingness to project strength against Iranian aggression, though questions about operational transparency and long-term strategy remain fair game. Watch for whether Congress demands clearer authorization and oversight of any expanded campaign.
