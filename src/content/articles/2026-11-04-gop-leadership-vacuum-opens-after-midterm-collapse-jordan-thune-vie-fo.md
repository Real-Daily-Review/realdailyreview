# GOP Leadership Scramble Begins as Democrats Seize Control After Midterm Rout

Republicans are fighting over the wreckage after Democrats flipped the House and Senate in the 2026 midterms, with House Speaker Mike Johnson stepping aside and multiple contenders jockeying for minority leader roles.

Johnson announced Wednesday he won't seek House minority leader, clearing the field for a bruising internal battle (Washington Examiner). Rep. Jim Jordan is already working the phones to build support among GOP colleagues, potentially setting up a clash over the House minority leader post (Fox News). Meanwhile, Senate Republicans face their own leadership vacuum: Sen. John Thune has signaled his intention to run for Senate minority leader after the GOP lost at least five seats (Fox News).

The leadership scramble reflects deeper Republican divisions over strategy and direction heading into the 2028 presidential cycle. Conservatives will be watching whether the party charts a course correction toward limited government and fiscal restraint—or doubles down on establishment approaches that failed to hold the line.

The real test: whether GOP leaders use minority status to mount serious constitutional challenges to Democratic overreach on spending, regulation, and executive power, or settle into comfortable opposition.
