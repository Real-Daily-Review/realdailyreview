# Colorado Wildfires Spread as Hot, Dry Conditions Persist

Colorado's wildfire season is intensifying, with multiple large fires burning across the state as forecasters warn of dangerous conditions ahead.

The Aspen Acres fire near Pueblo grew only modestly overnight Friday despite hot and dry conditions expected Saturday, according to CPR News. Firefighters are working to identify hot spots and contain the blaze on its western flank.

Further north, the Willow Fire west of Leadville has scorched more than 5,800 acres with containment at just 33% as of Sunday evening, 9News reported.

**The conservative concern:** Colorado's forest management practices—shaped by decades of environmental restrictions on logging and controlled burns—have left vast tracts of state and federal land overgrown and primed for catastrophic fire. Republicans and land-use advocates argue that aggressive thinning and timber harvesting, long blocked by environmental litigation and federal red tape, would reduce fuel loads and protect both lives and property. Instead, taxpayers foot the bill for increasingly expensive firefighting operations while watching forests burn.

The state's reliance on federal land management compounds the problem. Colorado conservatives argue that state and private landowners should have greater authority to manage forests proactively rather than waiting for crisis response.

Watch for how state and federal agencies respond to calls for expedited forest thinning and whether Colorado's legislature will push back against environmental groups blocking timber management projects.
