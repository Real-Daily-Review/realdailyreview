# Fort Hood Terrorist to Face Firing Squad After Trump Approves Execution

President Trump has approved the execution of Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan, ending a nearly two-decade legal saga that tested American resolve on military justice. Hasan, who killed 13 people in a 2009 rampage on the Texas base, will be executed by firing squad on December 3, 2026, according to *Just The News*. The jihadist claimed his attack was retaliation for U.S. military operations in Iraq and Afghanistan.

The approval marks a decisive break from the Obama-era hesitation that delayed justice for over 16 years. Trump and Defense Secretary Hegseth signed off on both the execution and the firing squad method—a rare choice that underscores the severity of Hasan's crimes and ideology.

Conservatives view the execution as overdue accountability for an act of domestic terrorism committed by a radicalized Army psychiatrist. The case exposed vulnerabilities in military vetting and political correctness that prevented earlier intervention, concerns Republicans have raised repeatedly since 2009.

While the execution is not a Colorado-specific issue, it reflects the Trump administration's willingness to enforce military justice and national security priorities that Colorado defense contractors and military families support.
