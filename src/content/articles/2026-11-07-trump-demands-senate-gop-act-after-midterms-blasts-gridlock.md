# Trump Turns on GOP Senate After Midterms, Demands Lame-Duck Action

President Trump is publicly rebuking Senate Republicans after the midterm elections, demanding the chamber "act" during the lame-duck session and declaring it is "killing our country" by failing to pass his agenda (Washington Examiner). The criticism comes as conservatives assess the electoral results and chart the party's direction forward.

The rebuke signals tension within Republican ranks over legislative priorities and execution. Trump's comments reflect frustration that Senate Republicans have not delivered on key administration goals, even as the party claims midterm success. National Review and the Daily Caller have both raised questions about the state of GOP messaging and voter confidence following the elections.

For Colorado Republicans and taxpayers, the infighting matters: Senate gridlock directly blocks efforts to streamline federal regulation, protect Second Amendment rights, and challenge Biden-era energy restrictions that have hampered Colorado's oil and gas sector. A fractured GOP caucus weakens leverage on water rights, public lands policy, and tax reform that affect Colorado's economy.

Conservatives should watch whether Senate Republicans respond to Trump's pressure with concrete legislative wins during the lame-duck period—or whether internal divisions continue to stall the conservative agenda.
