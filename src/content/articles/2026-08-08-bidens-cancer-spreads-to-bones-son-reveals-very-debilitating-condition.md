# Biden's Health Crisis Deepens as Cancer Spreads

Former President Joe Biden's prostate cancer has metastasized to his bones, causing significant pain, according to his son Hunter Biden in recent interviews. Hunter described the condition as "very painful" and "really sad to watch," revealing that the cancer has progressed beyond initial diagnosis (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31 Denver).

Despite the serious health developments, Biden continues public appearances, though the New York Post reports Hunter characterized the condition as "very debilitating." The disclosure raises fresh questions about presidential fitness and transparency—issues that dominated Biden's final months in office before his 2024 withdrawal from the race.
