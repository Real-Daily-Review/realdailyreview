# Tax Revolt: Voters Reject Big-Government Spending Agenda

Colorado voters are joining a nationwide taxpayer revolt against government overreach and fiscal recklessness, signaling clear rejection of progressive spending schemes at the ballot box.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, voters across the country are sending a unified message: "hands off our wallets." This anti-tax, anti-spending sentiment reflects growing frustration with government expansion and the fiscal burden it places on working families and small businesses—a concern that resonates deeply in Colorado, where property taxes, energy regulations, and regulatory compliance costs already strain household budgets and energy-sector employment.

Republicans argue that Democrats' relentless push for higher taxes and increased government spending fuels inflation, kills job creation, and undermines economic freedom. Conservatives contend that voters understand a simple truth: government doesn't spend money better than families and entrepreneurs do. The left, by contrast, frames spending as "investment" in social programs and climate initiatives—a framing that ignores the real cost to Colorado taxpayers and the state's energy workers facing regulatory headwinds.

This election-cycle message matters for Colorado's 2024 races and state legislative battles over tax policy, energy regulation, and government budgets.

Watch for whether Colorado Republicans capitalize on this anti-spending momentum to challenge proposed tax increases and regulatory expansions in the state legislature.
