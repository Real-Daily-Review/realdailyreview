# AI Billing Chaos: Hospital System Dunning Patient Twice for Same Care

Colorado families already squeezed by healthcare costs now face a new threat: AI-powered billing systems gone haywire. One patient paid their hospital bill in full, only to be slapped with a $27,000 demand months later—all because an AI process within the Epic billing system malfunctioned, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

The patient's October experience reveals a troubling pattern: hospitals deploying automated systems without adequate safeguards, leaving patients to fight phantom charges. When the hospital was contacted, staff blamed the AI process—essentially admitting the system failed to recognize a payment already made.

This matters for Colorado taxpayers and families. Healthcare costs already rank among the top financial stressors for working Coloradans. Adding billing errors powered by untested AI systems only worsens the affordability crisis. Conservatives argue that healthcare markets need *less* regulatory complexity and *more* transparency—not more black-box automation that shifts the burden of error-correction onto patients.

The real problem: hospitals deploying expensive tech solutions without accountability. When AI fails, patients suffer. When patients complain, they're told a machine made the mistake.

**Watch whether Colorado's Attorney General or state legislators demand billing transparency standards—or whether hospitals continue hiding behind "AI errors" to avoid responsibility.**
