# Trump Administration Hikes Citizenship Application Fees by $570

The Trump administration is proposing a steep increase in citizenship application fees—a move that will price out lower-income Americans seeking legal status while eliminating reduced-fee options and waivers entirely, according to 9News Denver.

The $570 fee hike represents a significant barrier to naturalization, effectively making citizenship a luxury good for those who can afford it. Conservatives who champion legal immigration and orderly naturalization processes face a tension here: while stricter immigration enforcement aligns with border-security priorities, jacking up fees on applicants already navigating the legal system may undermine the case for merit-based immigration reform.

The proposal removes fee waivers—assistance for low-income applicants—which could disproportionately affect working families and legal permanent residents in Colorado and across the Mountain West who've followed the rules but lack deep pockets.

Republicans backing the administration argue the higher fees reflect true administrative costs and discourage frivolous applications. Critics counter that pricing citizenship out of reach contradicts the nation's immigration ideals and may actually slow legal assimilation.

Colorado taxpayers and business owners relying on legal immigrant workers should watch whether this fee structure survives public comment and congressional scrutiny—or whether the administration adjusts course to balance enforcement with accessibility.
