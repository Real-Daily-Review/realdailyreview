# Democrats' Post-Election Hiring Spree Signals Partisan Investigations Ahead—Union Boss Warns Party on Worker Loyalty

Democrats are wasting no time weaponizing their election gains, with party operatives already hiring investigators and oversight counsel to target the Trump administration (Washington Examiner). House oversight committee ranking member Robert Garcia has signaled aggressive "anti-corruption" probes—Washington's euphemism for partisan witch hunts.

But cracks are already showing in the Democratic coalition. United Auto Workers President Shawn Fain delivered a blunt warning: a blue-wave victory doesn't guarantee worker loyalty in 2028 without real action on living wages (Fox News Politics). Translation: unions are losing faith Democrats will deliver on economic promises, not just rhetoric.

For Colorado, this matters. Democratic-controlled committees will likely push aggressive labor regulations and wage mandates that could burden small businesses and manufacturers across the state. Meanwhile, the union fracture signals Democrats' progressive base may demand even more aggressive government intervention—higher taxes, stricter labor rules, and expanded regulations that Colorado employers will foot the bill for.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation joins the partisan investigation crusade or pushes back on costly labor mandates that could drive jobs out of state.
