# Pedestrian Killed After Being Struck by Frontier Plane at Denver Airport

A pedestrian was fatally struck by a Frontier Airlines plane during takeoff at Denver International Airport late Friday night, according to airport authorities and the airline.

The person jumped a fence and entered a runway as the aircraft was departing for Los Angeles, PBS NewsHour reported. The plane's takeoff was canceled following the incident, according to CBS News.

The airport and Frontier Airlines confirmed the fatality but did not immediately release additional details about the circumstances or identify the deceased. The incident marks a rare occurrence of a ground-level collision involving a departing aircraft at a major U.S. airport.

Federal authorities typically investigate such incidents. The runway was closed temporarily following the strike, though the airport did not announce extended service disruptions.
