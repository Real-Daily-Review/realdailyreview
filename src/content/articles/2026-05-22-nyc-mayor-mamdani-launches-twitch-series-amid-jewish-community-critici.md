# NYC Mayor Launches Twitch Series Amid Criticism Over Jewish Community Relations

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is launching a Twitch streaming series today to engage directly with residents, according to TechCrunch. The inaugural episode airs at 4 p.m. ET.

The move comes as Mamdani faces criticism from Jewish New Yorkers over his handling of recent protests and community safety concerns. According to the Washington Examiner, critics argue the mayor's statements have been insufficient, pointing to disruptions at schools and houses of worship as evidence that Jewish families feel unsafe navigating public spaces during demonstrations.

The Twitch initiative represents an unconventional approach to municipal communication, using a platform popular with younger audiences to discuss city issues in real time. The format allows for direct questions and responses without traditional media intermediaries.
