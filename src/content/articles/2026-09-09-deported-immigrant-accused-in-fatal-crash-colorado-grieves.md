# Illegal Immigrant Accused in Fatal Wrong-Way Crash; Colorado Family Grieves Greeley Tragedy

Border security failures and sanctuary policies are putting Colorado drivers at risk, as a previously deported illegal immigrant faces charges in a fatal Texas highway crash—while a Greeley family mourns a father killed in a separate impaired-driving collision.

Alexander Nunez, 26, allegedly drove drunk the wrong way on a North Texas highway in a stolen vehicle, striking and killing Kenan Ellis and injuring two cousins, according to *The Daily Wire*. Nunez had been previously deported, raising questions about why he remained in the country and whether federal immigration enforcement gaps enabled the tragedy.

Meanwhile, a Greeley daughter is urging drivers to reconsider impaired driving after losing her father in a local crash, *KDVR Fox31 Denver* reports. The dual tragedies underscore Colorado's vulnerability to both reckless drivers and the compounding dangers when immigration enforcement breaks down.

Republicans argue that sanctuary policies and weak border security create preventable casualties. When individuals with deportation orders remain free to drive stolen vehicles while intoxicated, the cost falls on law-abiding families—in Colorado and beyond. Democrats typically resist linking individual crimes to broader immigration policy, framing such cases as outliers.
