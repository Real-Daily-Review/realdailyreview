# Trump Demands Ukraine Replace Zelenskyy Over Diesel Strikes, Escalates Peace Pressure

President Trump is escalating his push to end the Ukraine war by calling for Zelenskyy's removal, demanding Kyiv halt strikes on Russian oil refineries that he says are driving up global fuel costs.

Trump's demand marks a sharp departure from traditional U.S. support for Ukrainian leadership. According to Fox News, Trump warned that Ukrainian attacks on Russian energy infrastructure are worsening global diesel and fuel supplies—a concern that could ripple into Colorado's energy and transportation sectors if crude prices spike further. The Washington Examiner reports Trump characterized the conflict as a war "that should have never started" and called for "a new leader who can make a deal."

**The conservative case:** Trump argues that prolonging the conflict through continued strikes on Russian energy targets only inflates global fuel prices, harming American consumers and businesses. Ending the war through negotiated settlement—even one unfavorable to Ukraine—is preferable to indefinite conflict and economic pain at the pump.

**The counterargument:** Ukraine and its Western allies contend that striking Russian oil refineries degrades Moscow's ability to fund the war machine and that surrendering leverage now invites further Russian aggression.

For Colorado, the stakes are real: energy prices affect everything from heating costs to agricultural operations to transportation budgets. Watch whether Trump's pressure campaign actually shifts the diplomatic calculus or hardens Ukrainian resolve to continue fighting.
