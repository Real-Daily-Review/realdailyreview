# Colorado Parents, Schools Clash Over Parental Rights in Gender Transitions

Colorado parents are increasingly at odds with schools over who controls information about children's gender identity—a flashpoint that has now drawn federal attention.

A new podcast featuring Colorado parents discussing transgender children aims to normalize the experience, according to 9News Denver. But the initiative underscores a deeper divide: parents and schools often operate under conflicting assumptions about disclosure and consent.

The Trump administration has now weighed in. Just The News reports that the White House reminded state attorneys general that federal law grants parents "the right to know and be informed of the most sensitive and personal information pertaining to their children"—a direct challenge to school policies that allow minors to transition socially without parental notification.

**The conservative argument:** Parents, not bureaucrats, bear legal and moral responsibility for their children's welfare. Schools that conceal gender-identity transitions from parents undermine parental authority and potentially expose minors to irreversible decisions without family input. Republicans argue Colorado schools must prioritize transparency and parental consent.

**The progressive counter:** Advocates say disclosure can endanger LGBTQ+ youth in unsupportive homes and that schools have a duty to protect student safety and autonomy.

For Colorado taxpayers, the stakes are clear: whose values—family or institutional—will guide public education?
