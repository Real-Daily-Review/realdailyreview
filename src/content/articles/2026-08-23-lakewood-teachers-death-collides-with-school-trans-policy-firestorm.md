# Lakewood Teacher Killed; School District Faces Backlash Over Trans Policy Messaging

A Lakewood art teacher's death has shaken the community as her husband faces murder charges—but the incident has also collided with an ongoing school district controversy over how administrators are handling parent objections to a transgender teacher.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, Lindsey Barnett was found dead in her home, and her husband has been charged in connection with her death. A GoFundMe has been established for her children.

Separately, Fox News Politics reports that parents objecting to a trans school employee say they've been told by district officials that "public school may not be for them," while one speaker suggested children of "bigots" could be relocated. A father of three expressed outrage over the messaging, arguing families shouldn't face pressure to leave public schools over policy disagreements.

**The conservative concern:** Parents argue schools should respect legitimate parental input on curriculum and staffing without suggesting dissenting families abandon public education. Critics say the district's framing—implying that objecting parents are bigots—shuts down dialogue rather than addressing substantive concerns about classroom environment and parental rights.

Colorado taxpayers fund these schools and expect administrators to engage respectfully with community concerns, not dismiss them as intolerance.

Watch whether the school board responds to parent complaints with genuine dialogue or doubles down on the dismissive rhetoric.
