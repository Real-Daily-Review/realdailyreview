# Denver Faces Growing Street Violence as Intentional Hit-and-Run Kills Motorcyclist

Denver police have arrested a suspect in what investigators believe was an intentional vehicular attack on a motorcyclist, marking another troubling incident in the metro area's ongoing public safety crisis. According to 9News Denver, the driver deliberately struck the victim's motorcycle, causing a fatal crash that underscores the dangers facing commuters and residents on Colorado's streets.

The arrest comes as the Denver metro grapples with a surge in violent crime, including a separate tragedy in Eaton where three high school graduates were killed in a vehicle crash last month—a loss so profound that the community has organized a fundraiser to support grieving families (KDVR Fox31).

**The conservative concern:** These incidents highlight the failure of progressive criminal justice policies to deter violent offenders or protect law-abiding Coloradans. While Democrats have championed bail reform and reduced prosecution standards, Denver's streets have grown measurably less safe. Taxpayers deserve accountability from elected officials who prioritize criminal rehabilitation rhetoric over victim protection and community safety.

Republicans and law-and-order advocates argue that Colorado needs tougher penalties for violent crime, swift prosecution, and policies that prioritize public safety over ideological experiments in criminal justice.

**Watch next:** Whether Denver's district attorney will pursue maximum charges and whether Colorado lawmakers will revisit bail reform laws that critics say enable repeat violent offenders to remain on streets.
