# Sydney Sweeney Ad Exposes the Real Women's Sports Hypocrisy

Female athletes are outraged over an actress's provocative betting app commercial—but stay silent on transgender athletes competing in women's sports, conservatives argue.

Sydney Sweeney's nude-heavy advertisement for a sports gambling platform sparked backlash from women athletes and progressive groups, with some calling it demeaning to female athletics. Former UCLA gymnast Gracie Kramer posted a training montage in response, implying Sweeney's ad didn't represent "what a woman in sports looks like."

But here's the disconnect conservatives are pointing out: the same activists who condemned Sweeney's commercial have largely remained quiet on the far more substantive issue of biological males competing in women's sports categories—a policy that directly threatens female athletes' opportunities, scholarships, and safety.

The Daily Wire and New York Post note the selective outrage, suggesting progressive critics are more comfortable attacking a Hollywood actress than confronting the transgender athlete debate that divides their own coalition. The Daily Caller argues the real problem isn't nudity in advertising—it's the inconsistent standards applied when ideology enters the conversation.

For Colorado, this matters: state schools and athletic programs face ongoing pressure to adopt inclusive transgender policies that could reshape women's sports opportunities for Colorado's female athletes.

Watch whether Colorado's female athletes and coaches speak up on transgender participation policies with the same energy they've directed at entertainment controversies.
