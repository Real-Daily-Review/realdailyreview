# National Debt Hits $40 Trillion: A Reckoning Colorado Taxpayers Can't Ignore

America's national debt has crossed the $40 trillion threshold—a fiscal catastrophe that will hammer Colorado families and businesses for decades. The Washington Examiner warns the nation is "heading for bankruptcy," while National Review calls it "America's $40 Trillion Nightmare." This milestone arrives as federal spending continues unchecked, with the 25-year War on Terror alone consuming nearly $10 trillion (Just The News).

Republicans and conservatives argue this trajectory is unsustainable. Runaway spending—whether on entitlements, defense, or progressive initiatives—crowds out private investment, inflates the currency, and ultimately forces tax hikes on working Coloradans. Every dollar Washington borrows is a dollar that could have stayed in Colorado households and small businesses.

Democrats have resisted meaningful spending restraint, instead proposing tax increases on corporations and high earners. Conservatives counter that the problem isn't revenue—it's Washington's refusal to prioritize and cut waste. Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and middle-class workers will bear the brunt of inflation and eventual fiscal correction if Congress doesn't act.

The debt now exceeds annual GDP by a dangerous margin. Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation—particularly Republicans—will demand real spending cuts or merely talk tough while voting for more appropriations.
