# China, Russia Deepen Ties as Trump Tensions Rise

Russian President Vladimir Putin arrived in Beijing for a state visit Tuesday, just four days after President Donald Trump departed China, according to The Guardian. Putin was greeted by China's Foreign Minister Wang Yi with an honour guard and a welcome ceremony featuring youths waving both nations' flags.

The timing underscores shifting geopolitical alignments. Putin said relations with China have reached an "unprecedented level," according to The Guardian, as the two countries hold talks amid broader US tensions.

The visit coincides with escalating Iran rhetoric. According to Al Jazeera, Tehran warned the US of "surprises" after Trump threatened military action against Iran and gave it "two to three days" to strike a deal. Iran's warning suggests potential retaliation if conflict resumes, Al Jazeera reported.

The sequence of high-level visits—Trump to China followed immediately by Putin's arrival—signals competing diplomatic efforts to secure regional influence and alliance-building at a moment of heightened international tension.
