# Democrats Can't Escape the 'Defund Police' Mess They Created

Democratic candidates are scrambling to distance themselves from the radical "woke 1.0" agenda they championed just years ago—and it's backfiring spectacularly.

Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez recently declared that "woke 1 was crazy," according to the Daily Caller, even as she tries to memory-hole her own role in pushing the defund-the-police movement and other progressive excesses of 2019–2021. Wisconsin gubernatorial front-runner Francesca Hong is doing the same, walking back positions she once held, the Washington Examiner reports.

**Here's the problem for Democrats:** their rhetoric is permanently recorded. As the Daily Wire notes, every syllable is "trapped in amber like prehistoric bugs, forever available for public dissection." Voters remember when these same politicians called for abolishing police departments, embraced open-borders rhetoric, and championed other radical experiments that damaged public safety and quality of life.

Republicans are rightfully calling out the hypocrisy. Conservatives argue that Democrats can't simply erase unpopular positions once they realize those positions cost them elections. The left's attempt to rebrand itself as "moderate" rings hollow when the receipts are everywhere.

**Colorado conservatives should watch whether our state's Democratic leaders—from Gov. Jared Polis on down—attempt similar rhetorical gymnastics ahead of 2026 midterms.**
