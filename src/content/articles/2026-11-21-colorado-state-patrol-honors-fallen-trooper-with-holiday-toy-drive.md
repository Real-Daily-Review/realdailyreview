# Colorado State Patrol Honors Fallen Trooper's Son With Holiday Toy Drive

Colorado State Patrol is partnering with Cancer Fighters to the Rescue for its annual holiday toy drive benefiting children at Aurora's Children's Hospital—a tribute to Diego Alvarado, the late son of Sergeant Ivan Alvarado (KDVR). The initiative reflects the department's commitment to supporting families facing medical hardship during the season.

The toy drive underscores a broader conservative principle: community organizations and law enforcement stepping up where government often falls short. Rather than waiting for state bureaucrats to fund children's programs, Colorado troopers are mobilizing private charitable partnerships to deliver direct aid to sick kids and grieving families.

This kind of grassroots generosity—driven by personal sacrifice and voluntary association rather than tax-funded mandates—represents the kind of civil society conservatives champion. It builds community bonds, honors fallen officers, and helps families without adding to the state budget or creating another government program.

The Colorado State Patrol's approach demonstrates that meaningful support for vulnerable Coloradans doesn't require expanding government spending or creating new regulatory frameworks. It requires people of conscience taking action.

Watch whether this model gains traction among other Colorado law enforcement agencies and whether private donors step up to match the patrol's commitment.
