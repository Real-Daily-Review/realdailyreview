# Trump Mobile Faces Data Leak as T1 Phone Nears Launch

Trump Mobile is leaking customer addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses through an unsecured website vulnerability, according to reports from The Verge and TechCrunch.

YouTuber Coffeezilla first reported the apparent breach after being tipped off by an anonymous person who discovered the vulnerability. Two YouTubers verified that their own leaked data was authentic, according to TechCrunch. The Verge reports the leak has also revealed actual T1 Phone order numbers, which are significantly lower than viral figures previously claimed.

Trump Mobile has not responded to people alerting the company of the data exposure, according to TechCrunch. The T1 Phone was scheduled to begin shipping last week, according to The Verge.

The incident underscores data security risks for emerging tech ventures. Watch for Trump Mobile's official response and whether the company discloses the full scope of affected customers.
