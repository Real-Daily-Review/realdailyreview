# Colorado Must Reject Socialist Ideology Gaining Ground Among Young Americans

Colorado's youth are increasingly susceptible to Democratic Socialist Association messaging that fundamentally misunderstands American prosperity—a trend conservatives must counter before it takes root in state politics.

National Review warns that today's young socialists, like their predecessors, grasp little about the economic realities that built American wealth. The DSA's growing influence among Gen Z represents a dangerous rejection of the free-market principles that have powered Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small-business economy for generations.

Compounding this ideological drift is what National Review identifies as an "gratitude crisis"—younger Americans taking for granted the constitutional freedoms and economic abundance inherited from the Founders. Unlike previous generations, today's youth increasingly view American capitalism with suspicion rather than appreciation.

The Daily Caller underscores the stakes: America's 250-year energy dominance—built on diverse fuel sources including nuclear power—depends on rejecting anti-growth ideology. Colorado's energy workers, ranchers, and taxpayers have thrived under policies that embrace responsible development, not socialist redistribution.

Conservatives argue that Colorado must inoculate its young people against these collectivist ideas through education emphasizing property rights, individual liberty, and the empirical success of free markets. The state's economic future depends on it.

**Watch for: Whether Colorado schools and universities actively teach the case for capitalism and limited government—or continue allowing progressive ideology to dominate the classroom.**
