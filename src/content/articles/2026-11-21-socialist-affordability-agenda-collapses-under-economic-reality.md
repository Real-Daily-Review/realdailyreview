# Socialist 'Affordability' Agenda Proves Incompatible With Free Markets

New York City's socialist Mayor Zohran Mamdani is pushing an "affordability" agenda that amounts to government price-fixing and regulatory overreach—a cautionary tale for Colorado progressives eyeing similar schemes.

According to National Review, Mamdani's policies conflate affordability with socialism, banning established businesses and attacking companies for simply informing customers of new government-imposed fees. The Washington Examiner notes the mayor is simultaneously dismantling police units and planning to close the city's central jail while reducing incarceration—a public-safety disaster wrapped in progressive rhetoric.

Conservatives argue Mamdani's approach proves the left's fundamental misunderstanding of economics: you cannot mandate affordability through government fiat. Price controls, regulatory mandates, and business restrictions destroy the supply, competition, and innovation that actually lower costs for consumers. When government picks winners and losers, businesses flee, tax bases shrink, and working families pay the price.

Colorado taxpayers should note this playbook. Progressive activists and city councils here have pushed similar "affordability" mandates—from rent control schemes to business licensing restrictions. Each fails for the same reason: government cannot repeal supply and demand.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats attempt to import Mamdani-style policies into state law or local ordinances—and whether Republicans will mount a principled defense of free-market solutions to housing and cost-of-living challenges.
