# Georgia Democrat's Personal Attack on Trump Assistant Draws Fire for Hypocrisy

Sen. Jon Ossoff (D-GA) drew sharp criticism from conservatives this weekend after making a pointed personal jab at President Trump's executive assistant during a rally in Atlanta. According to the Washington Examiner and The Daily Wire, Ossoff suggested Trump would rather "build his ballroom and travel with Natalie" than govern—a calculated insinuation about Trump's relationship with assistant Natalie Harp, a cancer survivor.

Republicans argue the attack exemplifies Democratic double standards on decorum and feminism. The Daily Wire reports Ossoff faced backlash for what critics called a "sleazy cheap shot," while the Washington Examiner notes the senator deliberately left the implication hanging rather than making an explicit accusation—a rhetorical tactic conservatives say reveals the weakness of his actual policy critique.

The broader conservative complaint: Democrats routinely condemn personal attacks and demand civility, yet deploy innuendo and character assassination when it suits their political interests. Ossoff's gambit—attacking not Trump directly but a female staffer to damage the president—undercuts progressive claims to principled feminism, conservatives argue.
