# Colorado Preschool Teacher Marks Transgender Day of Remembrance

A Denver preschool teacher observed Transgender Day of Remembrance this week without her late partner for the first time, according to 9News Denver. Kirsten DeVon Herman previously marked the annual observance alongside her partner, Voss Barclay, before his death.

The day, recognized nationally, honors transgender individuals who have died. Democrats have used the occasion to call for remembrance of those lost to "hatred and violence," with party officials posting statements on social media (Breitbart).

The story reflects a deeply personal moment for Herman and the broader national conversation around transgender issues—a topic that has become increasingly polarized in Colorado politics and beyond. While Democrats frame the day as a memorial to combat violence, conservatives have raised concerns about how progressive activists use such observances to advance broader ideological agendas in schools and public institutions, particularly regarding curriculum and parental involvement in children's education.

Colorado parents and taxpayers should monitor how school districts incorporate discussions of gender identity into classroom instruction and whether parental notification and consent policies remain in place.
