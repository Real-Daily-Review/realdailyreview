# Progressive Education Ideology Sacrifices Individual Students to Racial Agenda

Nikole Hannah-Jones's own daughter just exposed the human cost of progressive school assignment policies—and it's a cautionary tale for Colorado parents watching similar experiments unfold.

The *New York Times* writer, a leading voice for race-based school assignments, reportedly pressured her daughter to attend a lower-performing school to advance racial justice goals. Her daughter pushed back, fought for a better high school, and told her mother: "You put me on the line to help a school, but it didn't work" (New York Post). The National Review notes this isn't isolated—progressives have "long subordinated individual students to the cause of racial justice."

The irony is sharp: Hannah-Jones advocates policies that restrict school choice for ordinary families while apparently reserving choice for her own child. Colorado has seen similar debates over school assignment and equity mandates. Republicans argue that one-size-fits-all progressive education policies harm the students they claim to help, particularly when ideology trumps individual achievement and parental preference.

Conservatives say the lesson is clear: education policy should prioritize each child's needs and family choice, not ideological experiments that treat kids as means to a social end.

**Watch:** Whether Colorado school boards cite Hannah-Jones's equity framework in their own assignment policies—and whether parents demand the same flexibility for their kids that progressive elites claim for theirs.
