# Former Election Official Tina Peters Doubles Down on Conspiracy Claims After Prison Release

Colorado's disgraced former Mesa County Clerk Tina Peters is back peddling election conspiracy theories just weeks after her early release from federal prison, undercutting her claims of remorse and raising fresh questions about the credibility of Colorado Republicans who embraced her baseless fraud allegations.

Speaking Thursday at Douglas County Freedom Fest alongside MAGA podcaster Joe Oltmann, Peters told the crowd she feels remorseful—but not for her own crimes. Instead, she claimed other people "should be in prison but are not," according to 9News Denver, recycling the same debunked election-fraud narrative that landed her in federal custody in the first place.

Peters was convicted of tampering with voting equipment and breach of duty after her 2021 antics in Mesa County became a national symbol of the post-2020 election denial movement. Her early release—granted despite her refusal to accept responsibility—now appears to have simply given her a larger platform to spread the same lies.

**The Colorado angle:** Peters' continued conspiracy mongering embarrasses state Republicans who legitimized her claims and underscores why election integrity depends on election officials who respect the rule of law, not partisan grievance.

Conservatives and election-security advocates should watch whether Colorado GOP leaders distance themselves from Peters or continue enabling her discredited crusade.
