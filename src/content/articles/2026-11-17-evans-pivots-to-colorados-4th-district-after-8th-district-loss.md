# Republican Evans Eyes 4th District Comeback After 8th District Loss

Gabe Evans is already plotting his return to Congress—just not from where voters rejected him. Days after Democrat Eileen Laubacher narrowly defeated Lauren Boebert in Colorado's 4th Congressional District, the Republican announced he'll pursue the open seat in 2028 rather than attempt a rematch in the 8th District, where he lost reelection this cycle (Colorado Sun, CPR News).

Evans' move gives him an early organizational advantage over other potential GOP challengers eyeing the 4th. Laubacher, a retired Navy rear admiral, won by less than 1 percentage point—a razor-thin margin suggesting the district remains competitive (Denver Post).

The timing underscores Colorado Republicans' determination to reclaim ground in a state trending purple. However, the 4th District's narrow 2026 outcome reflects deeper challenges: suburban Denver voters are increasingly skeptical of Trump-aligned Republicans, and Boebert's polarizing profile may have cost her the seat.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado Republicans learn from the 8th and 4th District losses or double down on the same messaging. Meanwhile, Initiative #256—which would ban partisan congressional redistricting—looms as a potential constraint on GOP map-drawing advantages if passed (Complete Colorado).
