# Gas Prices Surge Ahead of Memorial Day Travel Weekend

U.S. gas prices have climbed to their highest levels in nearly four years as millions of Americans prepare to travel for Memorial Day weekend, according to CNBC. The spike is driven by global oil inventory declines and geopolitical tensions, with analysts warning that gasoline, diesel, and jet fuel prices could rise further within four to six weeks.

The surge comes at a particularly sensitive time, as Memorial Day marks the unofficial start of summer travel season. CBS News reports that the price increases are affecting consumers planning road trips during the holiday weekend.

According to CNBC, the price pressures stem partly from the Iran war and its impact on the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global oil chokepoint. The combination of falling inventories and supply concerns has created upward pressure on fuel costs across multiple petroleum products.

Retailers are capitalizing on the holiday weekend with Memorial Day sales on travel-related goods and outdoor equipment, according to The Verge, though higher gas prices may dampen consumer spending on discretionary purchases.

Watch for whether oil inventories stabilize in coming weeks and whether analysts' forecasts of further price increases materialize before summer travel season peaks.
