# Federal Judge Blocks Trump Mail-Ballot Order; Appeals Court Kills Biden Gas-Stove Ban

A federal judge in Boston has blocked the Trump administration from enforcing a new executive order restricting mail-in voting rules ahead of the 2026 midterms, according to Fox News Politics. The ruling prevents the USPS from implementing the changes before the election cycle.

Meanwhile, conservatives scored a major regulatory win: a federal appeals court struck down the Biden administration's gas-stove rule in a 3-0 decision, with Louisiana, Mississippi, Montana, Nebraska, Tennessee, Texas, and Utah prevailing in the challenge (Just The News).

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado voters and energy consumers benefit from the gas-stove ruling. The Biden rule would have threatened home appliances and construction practices across the state, adding costs to builders and homeowners already facing inflation. Republicans argue such executive overreach stifles consumer choice and drives up living costs—a particular concern for Colorado families struggling with housing affordability.

On the mail-ballot front, Republicans contend election integrity requires tighter ballot-access controls, though the Boston court disagreed. Democrats argue expanded mail voting increases participation.

**What to watch:** Whether the Trump administration appeals the voting order or pursues alternative election-security measures, and whether Colorado's energy sector faces new federal restrictions under the incoming administration.
