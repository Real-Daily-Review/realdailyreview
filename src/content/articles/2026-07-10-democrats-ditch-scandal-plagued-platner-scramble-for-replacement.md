# Democrats Ditch Scandal-Plagued Platner, Exposing Party's Rotten Vetting

Maine Democrat Graham Platner suspended his U.S. Senate campaign this week after rape allegations surfaced, forcing Democrats to scramble for a replacement candidate—a humbling collapse that Republicans are weaponizing across midterm races (Fox News Politics).

Platner's exit follows the party's pattern of defending obviously unfit nominees until polling tanks, then abandoning them after primary voters have spoken (Washington Examiner). Trump seized on the chaos, noting Democrats will struggle to find a viable replacement (Fox News Politics).

Republicans are linking Platner's multiple scandals directly to Democratic leadership in key House and Senate races, arguing the party is "completely stained" for backing him through controversies (Fox News Politics). The Daily Wire reports Democrats are now evaluating replacement candidates as Platner prepares to formally withdraw from the ballot.

National Review's assessment: "You hate to see it"—a cutting reference to the Democrats' self-inflicted wound and the damage-control scramble ahead.
