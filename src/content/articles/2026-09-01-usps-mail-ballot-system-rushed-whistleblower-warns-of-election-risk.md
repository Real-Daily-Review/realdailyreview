# USPS Mail-Ballot System Rushed, Whistleblower Warns of Election Chaos

A U.S. Postal Service whistleblower is raising alarms that the agency's hastily built system for tracking mail-in ballots could bungle the midterm elections. According to complaints cited by the Washington Examiner and Just The News, the Federal Ballot Mail Portal and its supporting IT infrastructure are being assembled in a "sloppy and rushed manner" that poses "significant risk" to ballot delivery. The whistleblower warns that a single scanning error in a batch of tens of thousands of envelopes could prevent any ballots from reaching voters—a nightmare scenario for election integrity.

**The conservative concern:** Republicans have long argued that mail-in voting systems lack sufficient safeguards and transparency. This whistleblower complaint validates those worries: when government agencies rush to implement new election procedures without proper testing and oversight, the result is predictable—chaos that could disenfranchise voters and undermine confidence in results.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado conducts mail-in elections statewide. If USPS systems fail, Colorado voters could face delayed or lost ballots, compounding existing concerns about mail-ballot security and chain-of-custody procedures that conservatives have raised for years.

Watch whether Colorado election officials demand assurances from USPS that tracking systems are fully tested before the next election cycle.
