# Russia-China Military Alliance Threatens U.S. Pacific Dominance

A retired Navy admiral is sounding the alarm: Russia is actively helping China close the military gap with America in ways that could reshape Pacific security and Colorado's defense-industrial economy.

Retired Rear Adm. Mark Montgomery warned (Fox News) that Russia is transferring submarine quieting technology to China—a capability that could neutralize one of the U.S. Navy's last decisive undersea advantages. The transfer underscores a deepening Russia-China military partnership that threatens American interests from Taiwan to the broader Indo-Pacific.

Meanwhile, Taiwan is taking its own precautions. The island conducted its annual Han Kuang defense exercises this week, deliberately throttling national internet access for roughly 30 minutes (Washington Examiner) to test communications resilience against a hypothetical Chinese invasion scenario. The drill, which concludes Friday, signals Taipei's serious preparation for potential conflict.

**The Colorado angle:** Defense contractors and aerospace firms across Colorado—from Lockheed Martin to smaller suppliers—depend on sustained U.S. military superiority and Pacific stability. A China-Russia technological edge threatens both defense budgets and the high-skilled manufacturing jobs that support Colorado's economy.

Republicans argue the Biden-Harris administration has been too slow to counter Russian-Chinese military cooperation and should accelerate advanced weapons development and Pacific Fleet modernization.

Watch whether Congress demands accelerated funding for next-generation submarine technology and whether Colorado defense firms secure contracts in the coming defense budget cycle.
