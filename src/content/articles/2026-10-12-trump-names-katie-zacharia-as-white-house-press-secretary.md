# Trump Taps Katie Zacharia as New Press Secretary

Trump has named Katie Zacharia as his new White House press secretary, with her tenure beginning Monday on Columbus Day, according to *Just The News* and *Breitbart*. Trump praised Zacharia as someone who "has been with me, in spirit, from the very beginning," signaling continuity in his communications strategy heading into the second half of his term.

Zacharia will hold her first briefing Tuesday, marking a transition in the administration's public-facing messaging operation. The appointment reflects Trump's preference for loyalists in key communications roles—a pattern conservatives view as essential to countering what they see as hostile mainstream media coverage.

The timing coincides with ongoing Republican efforts to reshape federal agencies and push back against what conservatives characterize as the deep state's resistance to Trump's agenda. A reliable press secretary aligned with the administration's priorities matters significantly to how effectively Trump can communicate directly with Americans over the heads of legacy media outlets.

Coloradans should watch whether Zacharia's tenure brings sharper messaging on energy policy, water rights, and Second Amendment protections—issues where the Trump administration's positions diverge sharply from the Biden-era regulatory regime that hamstrung Colorado's oil and gas sector and Western water development.
