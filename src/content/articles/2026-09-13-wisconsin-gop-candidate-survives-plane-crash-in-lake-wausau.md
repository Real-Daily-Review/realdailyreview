# Wisconsin GOP Candidate Survives Harrowing Plane Crash—But Colorado Republicans Should Remember: Accidents Happen to Good Candidates

Wisconsin Republican Tom Tiffany swam to safety Saturday night after his four-seat Bonanza lost power and ditched in Lake Wausau during an approach to Wausau Downtown Airport. Tiffany, a GOP gubernatorial candidate, and the pilot exited the submerging aircraft and swam to shallow water, where emergency responders picked them up by boat (Fox News, Just The News).

The incident underscores the real risks candidates take while campaigning across sprawling states—a reality Colorado Republicans know well. Tiffany's quick thinking and the pilot's emergency landing skill prevented what could have been a tragedy, and both men escaped without reported serious injury.

For Colorado conservatives, the takeaway is straightforward: Republican candidates often cover vast distances to reach rural voters in places Democrats ignore. That dedication comes with genuine hazards. While Tiffany's escape was fortunate, it's a reminder that GOP candidates pursuing office in less densely populated regions face logistical challenges urban-based Democrats rarely encounter.

Watch whether Tiffany's campaign momentum holds or whether the incident affects his gubernatorial bid—and whether Colorado's GOP learns anything about candidate safety protocols for statewide races.
