# Democratic Frontrunner Signals 2028 Primary Will Embrace Anti-Israel Posturing

Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) deliberately entered a restricted Israeli military zone in the West Bank without authorization, triggering his detainment and generating the exact controversy he sought, according to the Washington Examiner. The stunt reveals how Democrats positioning for the 2028 presidential primary are willing to embrace increasingly aggressive anti-Israel rhetoric to appeal to their party's base.

Conservatives argue Khanna's calculated provocation—visiting a sensitive security zone knowing he lacked clearance—demonstrates the Democratic Party's leftward drift on Middle East policy. Republicans say the incident foreshadows a 2028 primary where candidates will compete by villainizing Israel to win over progressive voters.

The Washington Examiner notes that while Khanna engineered the controversy to boost his anti-Israel credentials with Democratic primary voters, the episode also exposed how easily foreign policy becomes a pawn in domestic political theater. Israel, the outlet argues, must address the extremist settler activity that Khanna exploited for political gain.

For Colorado Democrats and independents watching the 2028 race, this signals the party's establishment is increasingly comfortable with candidates who prioritize anti-Israel positioning over traditional U.S.-Israel alliance commitments.
