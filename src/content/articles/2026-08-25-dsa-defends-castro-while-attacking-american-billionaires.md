# Democratic Socialists Celebrate Castro While Attacking American Wealth

The Democratic Socialists of America are drawing fire for their romanticization of communist tyranny—a troubling sign for Colorado progressives who've embraced DSA-aligned politics in recent years.

DSA national co-chairwoman Megan Romer recently refused to call Cuba a dictatorship, instead claiming Cubans enjoy "democracy in a way that we do not" (Washington Examiner). The group celebrated Fidel Castro's centennial while simultaneously attacking American billionaires, exposing what National Review calls a glaring ideological double standard: the DSA's hatred of wealth stops conveniently at communist dictators.

Romer also opposes U.S. military aid to Ukraine, reflecting the DSA's broader anti-American foreign policy stance.

**The Colorado angle:** DSA-aligned candidates and activists have gained influence in Denver city politics and Colorado's progressive wing. If these national positions—defending communist regimes while opposing American military strength—represent the movement's true north, Colorado voters should know exactly what they're supporting when they back DSA-endorsed candidates locally.

Conservatives argue the DSA's Castro apologism reveals the bankruptcy of socialist ideology: when forced to choose between principle and defending authoritarian regimes, the left picks tyranny. Republicans should press Colorado Democrats on whether they share the DSA's admiration for communist dictatorships.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic leaders distance themselves from the DSA's Cuba stance—or remain silent.
