# Newsom's Death Row Commutations Expose California Democrat's Hypocrisy on Trump

California Governor Gavin Newsom just proved that progressive lecturing about presidential overreach rings hollow when Democrats wield the same power.

Newsom commuted the death sentences of all 548 of California's death row inmates—a sweeping executive action that contradicts his own criticism of Trump for allegedly acting without principle or restraint (Washington Examiner). The move exposes what conservatives argue is rank hypocrisy: Newsom attacks Trump for executive power grabs while deploying identical tactics to advance his own agenda.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans say Newsom's blanket commutations bypass the democratic process and ignore victims' families and the rule of law. This is precisely the kind of unilateral gubernatorial power that should concern anyone worried about executive overreach—regardless of party.

**Democrats counter** that capital punishment is immoral and that Newsom is exercising legitimate gubernatorial clemency authority.

**Colorado angle:** While Colorado abolished capital punishment in 2020, this case illustrates how progressive governors use executive power to reshape criminal justice policy without legislative input. Coloradans should watch whether similar unilateral moves emerge here on parole, sentencing, or law enforcement policy.

Watch whether Newsom's hypocrisy on executive power becomes a liability in his broader political positioning.
