# Colorado School District Tells Parents Unhappy With Trans Teacher: Leave Public Schools

A Colorado father of three says his family faced an extraordinary ultimatum during a school board dispute over a transgender teacher—either accept the district's position or remove their children from public school entirely. According to Fox News, families objecting to the teacher were told by district officials that "public school may not be for them," while another speaker suggested children of "bigots" could be relocated.

The exchange highlights the collision between parental rights and progressive ideology in Colorado classrooms. Republicans and conservatives argue that parents—not bureaucrats—should have final say in their children's education and exposure to contested social issues. They contend that telling families to leave public school if they disagree with gender ideology amounts to coercion masquerading as tolerance.

School districts counter that they must protect all students, including transgender youth, from discrimination. Democrats argue that parental objections to inclusive policies constitute bigotry that harms vulnerable children.

The incident underscores a broader Colorado education battle: who controls curriculum and values taught in taxpayer-funded schools? As more families weigh private school options or homeschooling, public school enrollment and funding face real pressure.
