# DOJ Sues Kansas School District Over Secret Gender Transition Policies

The Trump administration's Justice Department filed a landmark lawsuit against Kansas City, Kansas Public Schools this week, alleging the district maintained policies allowing children to "socially transition" at school while deliberately hiding that information from parents—a direct violation of federal privacy law.

According to Fox News and the Washington Examiner, the DOJ claims the district's practices violated the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Protection of Pupil Rights Amendment (PPRA), both of which require parental notification and consent on matters affecting student welfare.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and the Trump administration contend that parents have a fundamental right to know what's happening to their children at school, and that deliberately concealing gender identity changes from families represents government overreach into parental authority. This case signals the administration's intent to challenge progressive school policies nationwide.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** While this lawsuit targets Kansas, Colorado school districts have faced similar pressure from progressive boards and advocacy groups pushing gender-affirming policies. Conservative parents and taxpayers should watch whether this federal action emboldens Colorado Republicans to demand transparency in their own districts' gender policies—and whether the state's Democratic-controlled legislature moves to shield local boards from federal scrutiny.
