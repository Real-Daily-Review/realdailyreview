# Lakewood Driver Charged in Fatal E-Scooter Crash

A Lakewood man faces vehicular homicide charges after striking and killing a teenager on an e-scooter in late August, authorities say.

Joseph Sutter, 33, was charged following the Aug. 29 crash that killed a 17-year-old rider, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31. The incident underscores ongoing safety concerns around e-scooter use in Colorado's Front Range communities, where the devices have proliferated on city streets with minimal regulation.

Colorado municipalities have struggled to balance the popularity of e-scooters—marketed as eco-friendly transit—with public safety and liability questions. Cities like Denver and Lakewood have implemented speed limits and designated riding zones, yet enforcement remains inconsistent and accidents continue.

Conservatives and taxpayers have raised concerns about the liability exposure cities assume when permitting these devices on public streets without robust safety standards or operator licensing requirements. Questions linger over whether local governments adequately vetted e-scooter companies before allowing deployment, and whether riders face sufficient accountability for reckless use.

The case will proceed through Jefferson County courts. Watch whether Lakewood and other Colorado cities tighten e-scooter regulations or face civil liability suits from the victim's family—a potential wake-up call for municipalities that prioritized progressive transit trends over pedestrian safety.
