# Dhillon Exits DOJ Civil Rights Post as Trump Administration Reshuffles

Harmeet Dhillon has resigned as the Department of Justice's assistant attorney general for civil rights, sources confirmed to Fox News Thursday. Dhillon, who led the Trump administration's investigations into university DEI programs, hate crimes at houses of worship, and election integrity efforts, departs as the DOJ continues internal reorganization.

The resignation marks another staffing shift in Trump's second term. Dhillon's civil rights division had become a flashpoint for conservative priorities—scrutinizing what Republicans view as ideological overreach in higher education and examining election security concerns that Democrats dismiss as baseless.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Dhillon's office correctly targeted the administrative bloat and ideological capture of American universities, particularly their race-based admissions and hiring practices. Her work on election integrity aligned with GOP efforts to tighten voting procedures and investigate irregularities.

**Democrats counter** that her investigations amounted to weaponizing the Justice Department against progressive institutions and voting rights advocates—a familiar complaint from the left about Trump-era DOJ priorities.

For Colorado, the shift matters: state universities have faced GOP scrutiny over DEI spending and admissions policies, and any DOJ civil rights leadership change could affect how aggressively federal investigators pursue those cases.

Watch whether Trump appoints a successor equally committed to dismantling university DEI infrastructure or takes a softer line.
