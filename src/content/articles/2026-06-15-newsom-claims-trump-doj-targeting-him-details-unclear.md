# Newsom Claims Trump DOJ Targeting Him—But Details Remain Murky

California Gov. Gavin Newsom is crying foul, alleging that President Trump has weaponized the Justice Department to investigate him and his wife—a charge that echoes the left's familiar playbook of blaming Republicans for political persecution.

According to Fox News, Newsom claims Trump directly ordered the DOJ probe. However, as 9News Denver reports, the nature of the alleged investigation remains unclear as of Monday morning, and neither outlet has confirmed specifics about what the investigation targets or whether it even exists in the form Newsom describes.
