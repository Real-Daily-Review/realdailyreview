# House Democrats Fracture Over Speaker Vote as Jeffries Faces Rebellion

House Democrats are already eating their own before the new Congress even gavels in, with incoming members refusing to fall in line behind Hakeem Jeffries and party brass allegedly threatening dissenters into submission.

Rep.-elect Don Leonard (D-OH) claims senior Democrats are pressuring him to drop conditions for backing Jeffries as speaker, threatening "intraparty repercussions" if he doesn't comply (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, new House Democrats haven't settled on a speaker candidate at all, signaling deeper fractures than the party's public unity messaging suggests (Fox News).

Jeffries has already staked out a hard-left position, vowing Democrats won't "give a dime" to Trump's deportation efforts and backing impeachment of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth (Fox News). That's the kind of uncompromising stance that plays well with the progressive base but leaves moderate Democrats—especially those representing Trump-won districts like Leonard—exposed back home.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's Democratic delegation will watch this closely. If national Democrats double down on open-border rhetoric and anti-law-enforcement posturing, it could hurt vulnerable Dems in swing districts and energize Republican challengers in 2026.

Conservatives should monitor whether this speaker chaos weakens Democratic leverage in budget negotiations—and whether Colorado Republicans capitalize on Democratic dysfunction heading into the next election cycle.
