# Coast Guard Searches for Missing Medical Jet Off Massachusetts

A Gulfstream G100 medical transport plane carrying six people vanished over the Atlantic early Saturday during a Bermuda-to-Boston flight, prompting an active Coast Guard search operation off the Massachusetts coast.

The Federal Aviation Administration lost contact with the aircraft around 2 a.m. Saturday and immediately alerted the Coast Guard, according to 9News Denver. The Daily Wire reports the search is focused on waters off Nantucket as crews work to locate the missing jet.

Details remain sparse at this hour. The aircraft was operating as a medical transport, suggesting it may have been carrying patients or medical personnel, though specifics on those aboard have not been released. The Coast Guard's response time and coordination with the FAA underscores the established protocols for aviation emergencies, though the remote location and early-morning timing complicate search-and-rescue efforts in open ocean conditions.

No cause for the loss of contact has been determined. Weather conditions and mechanical failure remain under investigation as authorities work to locate the plane and determine the status of those aboard.
