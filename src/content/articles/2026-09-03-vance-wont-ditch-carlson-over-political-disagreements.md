# Vice President Vance Refuses to Abandon Tucker Carlson Over Political Splits

Vice President JD Vance is sticking with his friendship with Tucker Carlson despite the commentator's recent attacks on Trump and other Republican allies—a move that signals the Trump-Vance administration won't fracture over media disagreements.

Speaking at a White House press briefing, Vance rejected calls to distance himself from Carlson, saying: "I'm not going to play the game where I throw friends under the bus because I have political disagreements with them. I think that is a terrible place for the United States to go" (Just The News, Daily Wire).

The statement comes after Carlson called for Trump's removal from office and criticized Mike Rogers—whom Vance had campaigned for—in the same week.

Vance also declined to telegraph military strategy on Iran, telling reporters: "It would be irresponsible for us to write out our strategy and our timeline" (Just The News). The VP's refusal to set public deadlines reflects a more disciplined approach to foreign policy messaging than the previous administration's sometimes-improvised approach.
