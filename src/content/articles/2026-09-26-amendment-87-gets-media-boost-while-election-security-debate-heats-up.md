# Colorado Amendment 87 Faces Media Bias as Voters Head to Polls

Colorado's progressive establishment and allied media are stacking the deck for Amendment 87, according to conservative critics who say incomplete arguments and one-sided coverage are drowning out legitimate taxpayer concerns about the measure.

Complete Colorado reports that state government and mainstream media outlets are tilting heavily in favor of the amendment, presenting selective framing that obscures what conservatives argue are significant fiscal and regulatory costs to Colorado families and businesses. Republicans contend the amendment represents another expansion of government authority and spending without honest debate about trade-offs.

Meanwhile, election integrity remains a live issue as Colorado heads into the voting season. The Denver Post reported that three fraudulent ballots were counted in 2024 after passing signature verification—a reminder that even Colorado's vaunted mail-ballot system has vulnerabilities. Amendment 84, which would require Social Security numbers for voter registration, could tighten those safeguards, though election officials maintain the current system's "many checks and balances" catch fraud before it spreads.

The contrast is stark: while progressives push Amendment 87 with media backing, conservatives argue for election security measures that Democrats resist. Taxpayers should watch whether Amendment 87's true fiscal impact gets honest scrutiny before November.
