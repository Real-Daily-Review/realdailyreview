# Kansas Democrat's Anti-Beef Sermon Signals Radical Environmental Agenda That Could Hit Colorado Ranchers

A Kansas Democrat running for U.S. Senate once urged his megachurch congregation to "repent" for eating beef—a stunning tell about the environmental extremism now driving the party's rural strategy. Adam Hamilton, a pastor-turned-politician seeking Senate, leveraged his pulpit to shame parishioners over cattle consumption in one of America's premier cattle states (Fox News, Breitbart).

The move exposes what conservatives have long warned: the Democratic Party's climate agenda directly targets agriculture, ranching, and energy production—industries that sustain Colorado's rural economy and feed American families. Hamilton's moral crusade against beef consumption mirrors the left's broader push to regulate livestock farming, restrict water rights, and impose carbon restrictions on agricultural operations.

Republicans argue such rhetoric alienates working families and reveals the Democrats' contempt for rural livelihoods. Conservatives counter that sustainable ranching and responsible cattle production are compatible with environmental stewardship—without government-mandated guilt trips or regulatory overreach.

For Colorado, the warning is clear: if Democrats gain Senate power, expect aggressive pushback against the state's ranching heritage, water allocations, and energy independence. Taxpayers and ranchers should monitor whether similar anti-agriculture messaging spreads into Colorado's 2026 Senate race.
