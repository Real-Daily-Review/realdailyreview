# Trump's White House UFC Event Draws Fire From Clinton—And a Swift Conservative Counterattack

Hillary Clinton's swipe at Donald Trump for hosting a UFC event at the White House backfired spectacularly, with conservatives quickly reminding her of her husband's far more damaging White House conduct. Clinton criticized the event as unbecoming of the presidential residence, but critics pointed out that Bill Clinton's documented scandals—including the Monica Lewinsky affair and alleged misconduct within the building itself—set a considerably lower bar for White House decorum (Fox News).

The Washington Examiner's opinion section went further, arguing the event represented a broader "prostitution of the White House," questioning whether mixed martial arts entertainment belongs in the nation's most official residence.

Republicans counter that Trump's hosting of a sporting event—legal, consensual, and public—hardly compares to the Clintons' history. Conservatives argue the selective outrage from the left ignores their own party's far graver White House controversies.

**What Colorado conservatives should watch:** Whether this becomes a broader talking point about presidential decorum and standards, or fades as another partisan skirmish ahead of 2024.
