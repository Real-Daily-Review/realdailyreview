# America's Energy Future Hinges on Nuclear Power, Not Green Mandates

As America marks 250 years of independence, the nation faces a critical choice: embrace proven nuclear energy or double down on unreliable renewables that drain taxpayer wallets. According to the Daily Caller, nuclear power has been America's backbone for generations, yet progressive policymakers continue to strangle the industry with regulation while pushing expensive solar and wind schemes that require constant subsidies.

Colorado, home to major energy operations and thousands of workers in traditional power generation, stands to lose significantly if the Biden-Harris administration's anti-nuclear bias persists. Conservatives argue that nuclear energy is the only realistic path to affordable, reliable baseload power—a position backed by energy economists across the political spectrum. Yet Democrats remain ideologically committed to a green-energy fantasy that ignores physics and economics.

The deeper problem, National Review warns, reflects a broader "gratitude crisis" among Americans who take prosperity for granted. Young socialists and their sympathizers in government fail to understand that American abundance flows from free markets and energy independence, not government mandates (National Review). 

Meanwhile, the rise of DSA-style socialism among younger voters threatens the capitalist foundation that built this country (National Review).

**Watch for:** Colorado legislators' votes on any nuclear-expansion bills or renewable-mandate extensions in the 2027 session—a clear test of whether state Republicans will defend energy workers or capitulate to green ideology.
