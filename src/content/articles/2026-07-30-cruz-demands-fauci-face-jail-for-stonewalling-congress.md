# Fauci Should Face Justice, Cruz Says—Colorado Conservatives Agree on Accountability

Senator Ted Cruz is demanding criminal prosecution of Anthony Fauci, calling the former NIH director "America's most damaging bureaucrat" after Fauci pleaded the Fifth over 100 times during a Senate hearing (Fox News, New York Post). Cruz says Fauci should face contempt charges, perjury prosecution, and jail time for his evasions.

Republicans argue Fauci's refusal to answer questions represents a brazen stonewalling that obstructed legitimate congressional oversight. They contend his pandemic policies—lockdowns, school closures, vaccine mandates—caused massive collateral damage to the economy, education, and individual liberty without scientific justification.

Colorado conservatives have particular reason to care: state businesses suffered under federal COVID restrictions, schools locked down students for months, and workers faced federal vaccine mandates that cost jobs. Fauci's influence extended directly into Colorado health policy and workplace rules that conservatives say were never warranted by the evidence.

Democrats have largely remained silent on Fauci's Fifth Amendment invocations, instead defending his pandemic record as scientifically sound and politically necessary.
