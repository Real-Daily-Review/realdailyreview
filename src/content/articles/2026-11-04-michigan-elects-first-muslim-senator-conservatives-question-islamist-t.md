# Michigan Elects First Muslim Senator Amid Controversy Over Islamist Ties

Abdul El-Sayed, a Detroit Democrat, has won Michigan's U.S. Senate race and will become the first Muslim elected to the chamber, according to projections from *The Daily Wire* and Breitbart. With 75% of ballots counted, El-Sayed led Republican Mike Rogers by over three points.

The victory has drawn sharp scrutiny from conservative outlets over El-Sayed's past associations. *National Review* reports that El-Sayed has repeatedly denied ties to the Muslim Brotherhood, an Islamist movement, following what the magazine characterizes as "a familiar playbook" of denials similar to those used by other figures accused of such connections.

**The conservative concern:** Republicans and right-leaning analysts argue El-Sayed's record and associations warrant serious vetting before he takes office. *The Daily Wire* describes him as a "far-left Islamist Democrat," while critics point to his denials of Muslim Brotherhood ties as evasive rather than transparent.
