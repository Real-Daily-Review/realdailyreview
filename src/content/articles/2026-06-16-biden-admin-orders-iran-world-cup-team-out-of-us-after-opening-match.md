# Iran's World Cup Team Ordered Out of US—Coach Cries Foul

The Biden administration's decision to order Iran's World Cup soccer team out of the United States immediately after their opening match against New Zealand has sparked a diplomatic row that reveals the left's inconsistent approach to international engagement.

Iran's coach Amir Ghalenoei claimed his team was "the most oppressed team in the whole World Cup" after being ordered to return to their Mexico training base following a 2-2 draw in Los Angeles (9News Denver). Breitbart noted the coach's emotional response, characterizing the complaint as playing the victim card.

Republicans counter that national security concerns regarding Iran's government justify heightened scrutiny of Iranian nationals on U.S. soil, particularly given Tehran's hostile posture toward American interests.
