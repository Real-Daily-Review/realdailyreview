# Tom Jackson, Broncos Legend and ESPN Analyst, Dies at 75

Denver lost one of its greatest sports icons Friday with the death of Tom Jackson, the Hall of Fame linebacker who anchored the Broncos' legendary Orange Crush defense and became a fixture on ESPN's "Sunday NFL Countdown" for decades.

Jackson, 75, played 14 seasons with the Broncos beginning in 1973, establishing himself as one of the most dominant defensive players in franchise history. His impact on the field translated seamlessly to the broadcast booth, where he became known for sharp analysis and an unfiltered perspective that made him essential viewing for NFL fans nationwide (KDVR). His longtime on-air partner Chris Berman announced Jackson's death at the start of Sunday's "NFL Countdown" show, honoring Jackson's family's wish to time the announcement with the program Jackson helped define.

Jackson's legacy extends beyond statistics and accolades. He represented the best of Colorado sports culture—a player who earned respect through excellence, intelligence, and no-nonsense professionalism. His transition from gridiron star to trusted media voice showed that character and competence transcend eras.

The Broncos organization and Colorado sports community will feel his absence deeply.
