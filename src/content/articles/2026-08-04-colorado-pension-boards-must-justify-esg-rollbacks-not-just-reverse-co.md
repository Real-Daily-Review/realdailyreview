# Pension Boards Must Justify ESG Rollbacks, Not Just Reverse Course

Colorado public pension trustees racing to strip diversity and climate language from investment policies risk repeating the same fiduciary mistake they made when adding it—only in reverse.

The Washington Examiner warns that removing ESG (environmental, social, governance) and DEI (diversity, equity, inclusion) mandates requires the same rigorous financial documentation that justified their adoption. Trustees cannot simply erase "woke" language without proving the reversal serves pensioners' financial interests.

**The conservative case:** Republicans and pension reformers have long argued that ESG and climate investing distort markets, prioritize ideology over returns, and ultimately harm retirees' nest eggs. Removing these constraints is sound policy—but only if boards document that the shift improves fund performance and reduces risk.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** The state's Public Employees' Retirement Association (PERA) and other public pension systems manage billions in retirement savings for teachers, firefighters, and state workers. Sloppy governance—whether adding or removing ideological overlays—puts Colorado retirees' security at risk.

Trustees must demand independent financial analysis proving that ESG rollbacks enhance long-term returns. Ideology in either direction is a betrayal of fiduciary duty.

Watch whether Colorado pension boards conduct genuine financial reviews or simply swap one set of talking points for another.
