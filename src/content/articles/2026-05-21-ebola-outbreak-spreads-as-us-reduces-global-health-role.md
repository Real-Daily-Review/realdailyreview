# US Public Health Cuts Amid New Ebola Outbreak in Central Africa

A previously undetected outbreak of the Bundibugyo variant of Ebola is spreading through parts of central Africa, with hundreds of cases reported in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, according to The Guardian. The rare variant has no known cure or vaccine.

Health leaders and scientists are racing to contain the virus, but the US has notably reduced its role following sweeping cuts to global and domestic public health efforts, including the dismantling of USAID and cancellation of key scientific research programs, The Guardian reports.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Tuesday that the World Health Organization was "a little late" in identifying the outbreak, telling reporters that the CDC and WHO would lead response efforts. Rubio did not address the US public health cuts directly.

Critics argue the timing of the cuts has hampered the American response to the outbreak. Deutsche Welle has examined whether US aid reductions worsened the situation, though details of that analysis were not provided in available excerpts.

Watch for statements from the CDC and WHO on outbreak containment efforts and any clarification from the State Department on how US public health cuts may affect international disease surveillance and response capabilities.
