# Iran's Brinkmanship Tests U.S. Resolve—Again

Tehran is doubling down on regional aggression while pretending it holds a winning hand. According to *National Review*, Iran's strategic position remains weak despite its saber-rattling, yet the regime continues probing American patience through proxy conflicts and rhetoric. The *Washington Examiner* reports that low-intensity U.S.-Iran skirmishes are becoming the new normal—neither side wants full-scale war, but Iran's anti-American ideology and regional ambitions guarantee continued testing of American resolve.

This matters for Colorado because energy markets remain vulnerable to Middle East instability. Any escalation could spike oil and gas prices, hitting Colorado families at the pump and threatening the state's energy sector workforce. Republicans argue the Biden-Harris administration's weakness on Iran—including the failed nuclear deal and sanctions relief—emboldened Tehran's aggression. Conservatives say only credible American strength deters Iranian adventurism.

President Trump's return to a "maximum pressure" posture signals a tougher line, but sustained commitment will be essential. Watch whether the administration backs rhetoric with sanctions enforcement and whether Colorado's energy industry sees relief from regulatory uncertainty that has hampered domestic production.
