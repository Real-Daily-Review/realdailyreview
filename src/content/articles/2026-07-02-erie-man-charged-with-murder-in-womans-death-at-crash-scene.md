# Man Charged With Murder in Erie Crash Death

A 29-year-old man faces first-degree murder charges following the death of a 28-year-old woman discovered at a vehicle crash scene in Erie early June 21, according to Erie Police and local reports (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

Officers responded to the reported crash around 1 a.m. and found the woman dead outside the vehicle. The incident has been classified as a homicide rather than an accident, marking a significant escalation from initial crash reports.

Details remain limited as the investigation continues. The suspect is in custody facing the first-degree murder charge, though additional specifics about the circumstances, motive, or evidence have not yet been released by authorities.

This case underscores ongoing public safety concerns in Boulder County communities. Residents and local officials will be watching how quickly the district attorney's office moves forward with prosecution and what evidence emerges during preliminary hearings.
