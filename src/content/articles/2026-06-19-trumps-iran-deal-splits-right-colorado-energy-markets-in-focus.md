# Trump's Iran Deal Splits Conservative Opinion—But Coloradans Should Demand Clarity on Oil Markets

President Trump's framework agreement with Iran has divided the right, with a Reagan Institute poll showing Americans evenly split: 39% favor negotiated settlement while 36% back regime change (Fox News).

Trump administration officials frame the deal as pragmatic diplomacy reducing war risk and opening U.S.-Tehran communication channels (Washington Examiner). Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei has backed the agreement, though sources note his initial reservations (Breitbart).

**The conservative split:** Some right-leaning outlets question whether negotiations have truly begun or merely created false confidence. The Daily Wire notes Trump is acting as "pragmatist," though critics worry about the long-term security implications. The Washington Examiner warns of "unthinkable next steps" if the agreement fails to hold.

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets matter here. Any Iran deal affecting global oil supplies directly impacts Colorado's energy sector and gas prices for working families. Colorado oil and gas workers deserve transparency on whether this agreement stabilizes or destabilizes energy markets—and whether it genuinely constrains Iranian nuclear ambitions or simply delays the problem.

Conservatives should demand detailed answers: What enforcement mechanisms exist? Does this strengthen or weaken U.S. leverage in the Middle East?

Watch whether Congress demands a full treaty vote or if Trump bypasses legislative oversight.
