# Democrats Scrub Radical Posts as Swing-District Races Tighten

A Democratic nominee in a competitive House race has deleted hundreds of social media posts revealing his far-left agenda—a sign that even Democrats know their radical positions won't sell in swing districts.

William Lawrence, the Democratic nominee in a closely watched race and former member of Democratic Socialists of America, scrubbed posts calling for abolishing the Senate, eliminating police and prisons, ending fossil-fuel production, opening borders, and dismantling the nuclear family, *The Daily Wire* reports. CNN first uncovered the deletions.

The cleanup reflects a broader Democratic scramble in competitive districts. Rep. Kathy Castor (D-FL), facing a redrawn district now favoring Republicans, has launched ads distancing herself from "extreme partisanship"—a tacit admission that her party's progressive wing has become electoral poison in purple America, per the *Washington Examiner*.

**The conservative takeaway:** Democrats are running from their own platform. Lawrence's deleted posts expose what progressives actually believe when they think no one's watching—abolishing law enforcement, killing energy jobs, open borders, and social engineering. That Castor must rebrand herself as a moderate in a district Democrats once controlled underscores how far left her party has drifted.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado Democrats face similar reckoning in 2024 as districts tighten and voters demand answers on crime, energy policy, and fiscal responsibility.
