# Transportation Secretary Duffy Testifies on Budget as Airline Costs Rise

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy testified before a House Appropriations Committee subpanel Thursday on President Trump's fiscal 2027 budget request for the Department of Transportation, according to The Hill. The White House is seeking $26.6 billion in funding.

The hearing comes as airline travelers face rising ticket costs amid a jet fuel shortage linked to the Iran conflict. The budget proposal includes funding for the Federal Aviation Administration, though specific details on addressing fuel supply disruptions were not outlined in available excerpts.

The testimony occurred as the House prepared to vote on a Senate resolution to limit Trump's war powers, according to PBS NewsHour. Democrats have expressed hope that Thursday's vote could mark a shift, though previous similar resolutions have failed to pass.

The dual focus—on transportation funding and war powers constraints—reflects broader congressional scrutiny of the administration's spending priorities and military authority. The jet fuel shortage's impact on commercial aviation costs underscores how geopolitical tensions are affecting domestic travel economics.
