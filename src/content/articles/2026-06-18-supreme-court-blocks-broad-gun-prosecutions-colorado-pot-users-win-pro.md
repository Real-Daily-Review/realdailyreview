# Supreme Court Deals Blow to Gun Prosecutions; Colorado Gun Owners Should Take Notice

The Supreme Court unanimously ruled that federal prosecutors cannot broadly apply a gun law to all illegal drug users, a decision that directly undermines the case against Hunter Biden and signals major limits on government overreach in Second Amendment cases.

The ruling, reported by Fox News, centered on a Texas man who admitted to regularly using marijuana while keeping a firearm for self-defense. The Court's unanimous decision means the statute cannot be weaponized against every drug user who owns a gun—a critical protection for Colorado gun owners and medical marijuana users who might otherwise face federal prosecution simply for exercising their constitutional rights.

This matters in Colorado: the state has nearly 200,000 medical marijuana cardholders. Under the old prosecutorial theory, any of them could theoretically face federal gun charges. Conservatives argue the ruling restores constitutional sanity to Second Amendment protections, while the Biden administration's selective prosecution of Hunter Biden now looks even more politically motivated.

Meanwhile, Just The News reports that Republican senators are urging Trump's DOJ to challenge Biden's pardon of Dr. Anthony Fauci in court, citing allegations that Fauci funded gain-of-function research.
