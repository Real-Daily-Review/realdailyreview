# Anti-Jewish Bias on Campus Spreads as Colleges Capitulate to Activist Pressure

Progressive college administrators are systematically sidelining Jewish students and organizations, surrendering to activist demands that treat support for Israel as a litmus test for campus belonging.

At City University of New York, a Jewish student group was excluded from a Cultural Arts Night, with administrators citing Title VI compliance—a transparent dodge for ideological discrimination (Fox News Politics). Meanwhile, at Scripps College in California, student leaders removed a Palestinian flag from a campus coffeehouse rather than comply with a basic requirement to display Israeli and U.S. flags alongside it (New York Post), revealing how activist pressure now dictates campus speech codes.

Conservatives argue this represents institutional cowardice masquerading as social justice. When colleges abandon viewpoint neutrality and allow political litmus tests to determine which student groups deserve recognition, they betray their educational mission and expose themselves to legal liability under civil rights law.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado universities—from CU Boulder to Colorado State—have faced similar campus activism around Israel-Palestine issues. Trustees and administrators should take note: capitulating to activist mobs doesn't resolve conflict; it entrenches it and invites costly litigation.

Watch whether federal education officials under the new administration enforce civil rights protections for Jewish students with the same vigor progressives demand for other groups.
