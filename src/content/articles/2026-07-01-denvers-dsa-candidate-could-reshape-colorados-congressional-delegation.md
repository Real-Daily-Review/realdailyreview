# Denver Could Send Democratic Socialist to Congress—Here's What That Means

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could represent Denver in Congress if Melat Kiros wins her race, marking a significant shift leftward for Colorado's capital (CPR News). Kiros, aligned with the Democratic Socialists of America, would bring an explicitly anti-capitalist agenda to Capitol Hill—precisely the kind of big-government ideology that has already burdened Colorado taxpayers with some of the nation's highest cost-of-living increases.

Republicans and conservatives argue that DSA-backed candidates push policies that expand government control over healthcare, energy, and the economy—positions that would accelerate the regulatory burden already strangling Colorado's energy sector and small businesses. The DSA platform opposes fossil fuels outright, threatening the jobs of thousands of Colorado oil and gas workers and the tax revenue that funds schools and infrastructure.

Democrats counter that Kiros represents younger voters demanding climate action and economic justice. They frame her candidacy as a response to inequality and corporate influence.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the stakes are clear: a DSA representative from Denver would likely champion carbon taxes, aggressive climate regulations, and wealth redistribution schemes that hit middle-class Coloradans hardest.
