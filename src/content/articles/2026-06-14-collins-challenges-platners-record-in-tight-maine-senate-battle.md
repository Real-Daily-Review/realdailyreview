# Maine Senate Race Heats Up as Collins Challenges Platner's Record

Senator Susan Collins is drawing a sharp contrast with her Democratic opponent Graham Platner, highlighting his "angry rhetoric" and troubled past as Democrats target her Maine seat (Fox News Politics). The National Review notes that mainstream media outlets have rushed to defend Platner rather than scrutinize his record, a pattern conservatives say reflects partisan bias in political coverage.

Collins, who built her political brand on pragmatism and constituent service, is positioning herself as a moderate voice willing to work across the aisle—a strategy that has worked in Maine's purple electorate. Republicans argue that Platner's inflammatory statements and baggage make him unfit for statewide office, while Democrats counter that Collins represents an out-of-touch establishment Republican disconnected from younger voters' priorities.

The race carries national implications: Senate Democrats, led by Chuck Schumer, have made Collins' seat a top target in their effort to expand their majority. If Collins loses, it would signal that even moderate Republicans face headwinds in 2026, even in states where they've historically competed.
