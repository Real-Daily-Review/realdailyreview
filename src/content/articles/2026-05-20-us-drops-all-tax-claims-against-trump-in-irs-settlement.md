# U.S. Government to Drop All Tax Claims Against Trump

The Trump administration has agreed to permanently bar the federal government from examining or prosecuting President Trump, his sons, and the Trump organization on current tax issues, according to a one-page settlement document posted to the Department of Justice website Tuesday.

Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche announced the government will drop any outstanding tax claims and audits of the president, according to PBS NewsHour. The settlement agreement states the U.S. is "forever barred and precluded" from pursuing such matters, according to NPR.

The move represents a significant expansion of an earlier IRS lawsuit settlement. Blanche also defended the creation of a nearly $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization" fund as part of the broader settlement framework.

The settlement effectively closes the door on any federal tax prosecution of Trump or his family business entities going forward. Watch for potential congressional response and any legal challenges to the settlement's scope.
