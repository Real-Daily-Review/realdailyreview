# Trump Signs Bill Authorizing Medal of Honor Monument on National Mall

President Trump signed legislation Thursday clearing the way for a Monument to Medal of Honor recipients on the National Mall—a straightforward recognition of America's most decorated service members that shouldn't require federal approval but does.

Trump signed the Hershel "Woody" Williams National Medal of Honor Monument Location Act during a ceremony in Arlington, Texas, where he met with 15 living Medal of Honor recipients and toured the National Medal of Honor Museum. The bill authorizes the museum to construct the monument in Washington, DC.

This is uncontroversial stuff: honoring those who've earned the nation's highest military decoration. Unlike many federal projects that balloon in cost or scope, this one simply removes bureaucratic obstacles to commemorate genuine American heroism. It's the kind of legislative lift that shouldn't be necessary—private entities should be able to build monuments without congressional permission—but in today's regulatory state, even honoring heroes requires an act of Congress.

Colorado veterans and their families should support this straightforward tribute to Medal of Honor recipients, many of whom have deep ties to military communities across the state.
