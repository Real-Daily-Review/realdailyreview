# FBI Stops ISIS-Inspired Plot Against New York Capitol—A Reminder of Domestic Terror Threats

Federal law enforcement has disrupted what could have been a catastrophic attack: an alleged ISIS sympathizer arrested for plotting to bomb the New York State Capitol building in Albany.

Jessica Bowie, 35, faces federal charges for attempting to provide material support to ISIS after contacting what she believed was an ISIS facilitator—actually an undercover FBI informant. According to *Just The News*, Bowie had conducted surveillance of the Capitol at least five times and purchased materials for an explosive device, indicating she was in advanced stages of planning.
