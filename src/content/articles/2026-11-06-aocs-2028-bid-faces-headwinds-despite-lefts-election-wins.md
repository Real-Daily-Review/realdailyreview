# AOC's 2028 Ambitions Face Steep Climb Despite Left's Recent Wins

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez may be eyeing a 2028 presidential run, but the New York Democrat confronts serious structural headwinds even as progressives celebrate recent electoral gains, according to *National Review*.

The left has momentum from this cycle's victories, yet AOC's path to the nomination remains fraught. *The Daily Wire* notes that 2028 will be framed—as every election cycle is—as the most consequential in American history, a narrative that typically energizes media coverage and donor bases alike.

For Colorado conservatives, the real story is what comes next: a potential 2028 primary battle between the Democratic Party's progressive wing (represented by figures like AOC) and its establishment center. That internal fight will shape whether Democrats double down on the regulatory overreach, tax-and-spend agenda, and green-energy mandates that have burdened Colorado businesses and energy workers, or pivot toward more moderate ground.

Republicans should watch whether AOC's national profile translates into actual delegate support or remains confined to social media celebrity—and whether Colorado Democrats align with the progressive or establishment lane heading into 2028.
