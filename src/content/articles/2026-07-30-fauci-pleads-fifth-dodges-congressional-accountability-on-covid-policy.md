# Fauci Invokes Fifth Amendment, Dodges Congressional Accountability on COVID Policy

Dr. Anthony Fauci's decision to plead the Fifth before Congress signals the end of any pretense that federal health bureaucrats will answer for lockdowns and vaccine mandates that upended American life.

When subpoenaed to testify, Fauci invoked his constitutional right against self-incrimination rather than defend the policies he championed during the pandemic. Legal experts quoted by the Daily Caller note he was "within his rights," but conservatives argue the move itself is damning: if Fauci believed his guidance was sound, why invoke legal protection?

Commentator Ron Hart (Daily Caller) frames the irony sharply—Americans were pressured to take the shot under Fauci's authority, yet Fauci now refuses to answer questions under oath. Breitbart's John Nolte argues Fauci's silence effectively abandons the "cult-of-Fauci" believers who enforced his mandates, suggesting the doctor fears legal exposure more than he values transparency.

For Colorado, this matters: state and local officials imposed some of the nation's strictest lockdowns and vaccine requirements, citing federal guidance. Fauci's refusal to testify leaves unanswered whether those policies rested on solid science or bureaucratic overreach—and whether Coloradans who lost jobs or freedoms will ever get straight answers.

Watch whether Congress pursues contempt charges or whether this becomes another example of unaccountable government officials escaping scrutiny.
