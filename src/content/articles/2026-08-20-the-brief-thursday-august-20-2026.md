## Colorado GOP Narrows the Governor's Race

Out of 18 Republican gubernatorial candidates, only two survived the state assembly and advanced to the primary ballot. With Democrats holding the governor's mansion and the legislature, conservatives need a candidate who can actually win statewide. The assembly result suggests the party is trying to get serious. Watch who those two candidates are and whether either can consolidate the base quickly.
