# Colorado Faces Smoke from Canadian Wildfires as Democrats Weaponize Border Sovereignty

Colorado and the West are once again choking on hazardous smoke drifting south from Canadian wildfires—a recurring problem that exposes the limits of federal environmental policy and cross-border cooperation. According to *Just The News*, concerns over smoke have now shifted as severe weather threatens other regions, but the underlying issue remains: Canada's forest management failures directly impact Colorado air quality and public health, yet Democrats have little to say about it.

When President Trump suggested Canada become the 51st state, Democratic House members—including U.S. Rep. Linda Sánchez—immediately weaponized the comment, claiming his rhetoric "threatens to leave us alone to face other challenges we cannot overcome on our own," *The Daily Wire* reports. Republicans counter that the real problem isn't Trump's provocative messaging; it's Canada's inadequate wildfire prevention and suppression, combined with Biden-era policies that hamstrung U.S. forest management through regulatory overreach.

Colorado taxpayers and outdoor enthusiasts suffer the consequences: degraded air quality, health risks, and economic losses from tourism and outdoor recreation shutdowns. Yet progressive lawmakers would rather score political points than demand Ottawa take responsibility for its forests.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation—regardless of party—will pressure the federal government to negotiate binding cross-border wildfire management standards or continue accepting Canadian smoke as an inevitable cost of progressive environmental ideology.
