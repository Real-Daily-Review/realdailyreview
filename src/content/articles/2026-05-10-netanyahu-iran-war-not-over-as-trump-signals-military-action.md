# Iran Conflict Enters Second Month as Trump Signals Aggressive Stance

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said the conflict with Iran is "not over" as diplomatic efforts stall, according to CNBC. The war, now in its second month, has driven up oil and gas prices globally.

Netanyahu indicated that President Donald Trump has expressed willingness to take direct military action. According to the Washington Examiner, Netanyahu said Trump told him he wanted to "go in" to Iran to remove enriched uranium from the country, though Netanyahu stopped short of detailing specific military plans.

An Iranian peace offer has been rejected, according to CNBC, leaving little room for near-term de-escalation. The prolonged conflict has had measurable economic consequences, with energy prices climbing in the U.S. and internationally.

The statements from Netanyahu suggest the Trump administration may be considering more direct involvement in the conflict beyond current support for Israel. The comments come as diplomatic channels appear to have narrowed, with Iran's latest proposal dismissed.

Watch for any formal U.S. military announcements or further clarification from the Trump administration on the scope and timing of potential operations targeting Iranian nuclear facilities.
