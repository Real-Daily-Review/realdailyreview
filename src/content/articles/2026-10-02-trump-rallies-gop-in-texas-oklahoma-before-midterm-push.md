# Trump Rallies Republicans in Texas, Oklahoma as Midterms Loom

President Trump is hitting the campaign trail hard in Republican strongholds, barnstorming Texas and Oklahoma to energize GOP voters ahead of November's midterm elections. Trump toured the Peterbilt Motors Company factory in Denton, Texas, before heading to Durant, Oklahoma—roughly 86 miles apart—to tout his administration's economic and trade record at a rally (Washington Examiner, Just The News).

The back-to-back stops underscore Republicans' strategy to defend their congressional majorities while Democrats aim to flip both chambers. Trump's factory visit highlights a core GOP message: his policies support American manufacturing and working-class jobs—a contrast to Democratic regulatory overreach that conservatives argue stifles domestic production.

For Colorado Republicans, Trump's emphasis on trade and economic growth carries weight. Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small businesses have chafed under Biden-era regulations and tax proposals. Trump's pro-business, pro-energy messaging resonates with voters in rural Colorado counties and among oil-and-gas workers facing federal restrictions.

Democrats counter that Trump's trade wars and tax cuts favored corporations over working families, though Republicans dispute that characterization.

**Watch for:** Whether Trump's midterm push translates to turnout gains in swing states and purple districts—and whether Colorado Republicans can capitalize on anti-inflation, pro-energy messaging in November.
