# Georgia Democrats Pick Bottoms; Trump-Backed GOP Candidates Advance

Former Atlanta Mayor Keisha Lance Bottoms won the Democratic primary for Georgia governor, avoiding a runoff and defeating a crowded field that included former DeKalb County CEO Michael Thurmond and Republican-turned-Democrat Geoff Duncan, according to The Hill.

On the Republican side, two Trump-backed candidates advanced in a three-person field after Secretary of State Brad Raffensperger—known for resisting Trump's 2020 election-reversal push—finished last, according to The Hill. NPR notes Georgia is a swing state where both parties are shaping their direction ahead of the fall midterm elections.

Bottoms will face one of the GOP nominees in what is expected to be a closely watched general election in a state that has emerged as a battleground for national politics. The primary results underscore the continued influence of Trump within Republican circles, even as some establishment figures like Raffensperger face primary defeats.
