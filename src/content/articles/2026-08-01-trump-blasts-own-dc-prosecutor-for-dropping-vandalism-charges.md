# Trump Rebukes Top DC Prosecutor Over Reflecting Pool Vandalism Dismissal

Trump is publicly feuding with his own U.S. Attorney over what he calls a capitulation to vandalism—a rare break that signals how seriously the administration views law enforcement at the Lincoln Memorial.

U.S. Attorney for D.C. Jeanine Pirro filed a motion this week to dismiss charges against David Hearn, a former Olympian accused of vandalizing the Reflecting Pool. Trump responded sharply on Truth Social, writing: "I disagree 100% with Jeanine Pirro...on the Reflecting Pool. I don't know what she was thinking?" He called the incident a "pure case of vandalism" (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative angle:** Trump's pushback underscores a core Republican principle—that law enforcement must prosecute vandalism and property destruction consistently, regardless of the defendant's profile or public sympathy. Dismissing charges against a sympathetic figure sends the wrong message about accountability and the rule of law, conservatives argue. The incident reflects broader concerns about inconsistent prosecution standards in the nation's capital.

While the story centers on D.C. politics, it matters to Colorado conservatives who've watched selective enforcement of laws during recent years of civil unrest. Coloradans should watch whether Trump's Justice Department clarifies its prosecution standards for monument vandalism and property crimes nationwide.
