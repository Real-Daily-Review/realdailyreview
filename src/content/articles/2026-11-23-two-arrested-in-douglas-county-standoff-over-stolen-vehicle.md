# Two in Custody After Hours-Long Douglas County Standoff

Law enforcement in Douglas County ended a tense hours-long standoff Monday morning after deputies cornered a vehicle with stolen license plates on Lincoln Avenue near Oswego Street, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The two occupants refused to exit the vehicle, prompting an extended police response that shut down the roadway. Both individuals were eventually taken into custody, the sheriff's office confirmed.

Details on charges, identities, and the circumstances leading to the traffic stop remain limited. The incident underscores ongoing public safety challenges in the Denver metro area's southern suburbs, where property crime—including vehicle theft and plate fraud—continues to strain local law enforcement resources.

Douglas County Sheriff's Office has not released additional information about potential charges or the investigation's status.

Coloradans should monitor whether this case reveals patterns of organized theft rings or repeat offenders, and whether county prosecutors pursue charges aggressively.
