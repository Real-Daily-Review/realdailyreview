# Colorado Angle: Local Psychologist Sentenced for Sexual Assault; Broader Pattern Raises Questions

A former high school psychologist in Lakewood has been sentenced to prison following conviction on sexual assault charges, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver. The case underscores a troubling pattern: individuals in positions of trust and authority over vulnerable populations—children and students—exploiting that access for abuse.

The sentencing comes as national attention focuses on similar cases, including the conviction of Stacie Marie Laughton, a former New Hampshire state representative, who received a 33-year sentence for sexual exploitation of children at a Massachusetts daycare (Fox News). Laughton's case drew particular scrutiny given her high-profile status as a transgender lawmaker.

**The conservative concern:** These cases highlight why vetting, background checks, and accountability in schools and youth-serving institutions matter enormously. Parents entrust educators and counselors with their children's safety—a sacred responsibility. When that trust is betrayed, the consequences are catastrophic.

Colorado taxpayers should demand that school districts and state agencies strengthen hiring protocols, conduct thorough background investigations, and maintain transparent reporting of misconduct allegations. Protecting children must supersede political considerations or institutional reputation management.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado's Department of Education and school boards implement stronger safeguarding measures and whether similar cases surface in state institutions.
