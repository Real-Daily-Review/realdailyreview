# Harris 2028 Dreams Hit Snag as Former Mentor Warns Against Comeback

Democrats are already fracturing over 2028, with Kamala Harris's own political godfather urging her to sit out the race. Willie Brown, the 92-year-old ex-San Francisco mayor who launched Harris's California career, told Fox News and the New York Post that she's "not a communicator" and should abandon White House ambitions (Fox News Politics, New York Post). 

Brown's rebuke signals trouble within Democratic ranks as Pete Buttigieg and California Governor Gavin Newsom position themselves for a primary fight. For Colorado Democrats—already struggling to hold statewide offices against Republican gains—the infighting underscores a party searching for direction after 2024's electoral setbacks.

Republicans see an opening. While Democrats litigate their bench strength, the GOP can focus on Colorado's real issues: water rights, energy independence, and tax relief. Harris's political weakness matters less to Coloradans than whether Democrats will double down on the regulatory overreach and spending that's hammered the state's economy.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic establishment rallies behind a 2028 candidate or remains divided—a split party can't defend its legislative record or block Republican gains in swing districts.
