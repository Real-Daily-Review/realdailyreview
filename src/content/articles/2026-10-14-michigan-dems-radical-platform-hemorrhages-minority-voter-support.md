# Michigan Democrat's Radical Record Alienates Voters Beyond Jewish Community

Abdul El-Sayed, Michigan's Democratic Senate nominee, faces a widening credibility crisis that extends far beyond concerns from Jewish voters over his Israel positions. According to *National Review*, minority voters broadly—including Black voters—harbor serious doubts about the candidate's judgment and respect for their communities. President Trump argued Tuesday (per *Breitbart*) that Black voters reject El-Sayed because he "mocks" and "disrespects them."

The deeper problem: El-Sayed's far-left platform has drifted from Democratic fringe to mainstream party orthodoxy. A *Washington Examiner* opinion piece notes that positions once considered extreme—abolishing ICE, imposing Medicare for All, and effectively disarming Israel—now carry the endorsement of former President Barack Obama himself, who cut an ad for El-Sayed on Monday.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. El-Sayed's radicalism on healthcare, immigration enforcement, and foreign policy previews the agenda Democrats would pursue with unified control. His collapse among minority voters signals that even progressive base voters recognize overreach when they see it.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from El-Sayed's positions heading into 2026, or double down on the same ideological commitments that are fracturing Democratic coalitions nationwide.
