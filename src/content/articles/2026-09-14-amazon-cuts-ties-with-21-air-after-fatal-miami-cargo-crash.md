# Amazon Suspends 21 Air Partnership After Fatal Miami Cargo Crash

Amazon has suspended its partnership with cargo carrier 21 Air following a deadly plane crash off a Miami runway that killed five people last week, according to 9News Denver and the Washington Examiner.

The e-commerce giant's decision came as the National Transportation Safety Board launched its investigation into the crash. Amazon did not immediately disclose whether the suspension was temporary or permanent, or what specific safety concerns prompted the move.
