# AI Giant Anthropic Yanks Advanced Models Offline After Biden Export Controls Kick In

Federal overreach is already strangling American AI innovation before it can compete globally. Anthropic, a major AI developer, has taken its latest models—Fable 5 and Mythos 5—offline after the Biden administration imposed new export controls barring foreigners from accessing the technology (Washington Examiner).

The company had just released these models as its most advanced public offering, but compliance with the new restrictions forced an immediate pullback. The move illustrates how government mandates, however well-intentioned on national security grounds, can hobble American tech companies and hand competitive advantages to foreign rivals who face no such constraints.

Republicans and free-market advocates argue that heavy-handed export controls risk driving innovation overseas and slowing U.S. technological leadership. They contend that American companies should compete on merit in global markets, not be hamstrung by regulatory barriers that foreign competitors don't face. Democrats counter that protecting sensitive AI technology from adversaries is a national security imperative.

For Colorado—home to growing tech and AI sectors—this sets a troubling precedent: federal mandates that limit what companies can build and sell abroad ultimately reduce investment, hiring, and economic opportunity in the state.
