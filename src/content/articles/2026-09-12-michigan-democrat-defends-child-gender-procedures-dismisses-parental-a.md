# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Procedures Signals Broader Left-Wing Medical Agenda

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's full-throated defense of child gender-transition procedures—and his dismissal of government safeguards—exposes the progressive left's willingness to override parental rights and common sense in the name of radical ideology.

Abdul El-Sayed, touting his medical credentials at a Michigan State University rally, argued that doctors alone—not elected officials or parents—should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments (Fox News, The Daily Wire). His position reflects the Democratic Party's increasingly extreme stance on transgender medicine for minors, a sharp departure from the cautious approach taken by major European medical systems that have begun restricting such procedures.

Republicans counter that children lack the maturity to consent to irreversible medical interventions, and that parents—not activist physicians—should retain authority over their kids' healthcare decisions. Conservatives also note that many gender-dysphoric adolescents desist from identifying as transgender after reaching adulthood, raising serious questions about permanent medical intervention.
