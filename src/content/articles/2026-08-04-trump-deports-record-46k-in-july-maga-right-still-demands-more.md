# Trump Administration Deports Record 46,000 Illegals in July—But MAGA Right Turns on DHS Chief Mullin

The Trump administration deported 46,000 illegal aliens in July alone—a record that dwarfs any previous month under any president—yet hardline immigration hawks are attacking Homeland Security Chief Markwayne Mullin over perceived enforcement gaps.

According to the Washington Examiner, ICE arrested 51,000 immigrants that same month, averaging 1,645 daily apprehensions. Over the past 18 months, more deportation orders have been issued by judges than during the entire four-year Biden administration, the outlet reports.

Despite these numbers, Breitbart reports that MAGA supporters are criticizing Mullin, claiming he isn't moving fast enough on the administration's immigration agenda. The infighting reflects tension within Trump's coalition over whether current enforcement levels match campaign promises.

**The Colorado angle:** Illegal immigration strains Colorado's healthcare, education, and social services. Border security and interior enforcement directly affect Colorado communities and taxpayers footing the bill for services to undocumented populations.

Conservatives argue that while deportation numbers are historically strong, the administration must maintain pressure to ensure no illegal aliens slip through enforcement cracks. The left, meanwhile, continues opposing aggressive deportations as inhumane.

**Watch next:** Whether the Trump administration accelerates interior enforcement operations or faces sustained pressure from the right to exceed even July's record pace.
