# Colorado Women Cite Cost of Living—But Ignore Who's Driving It

Colorado women are worried about affordability and inequality, according to a new poll cited by the *Colorado Sun*, but they're not connecting the dots to the progressive policies fueling inflation and housing costs.

The survey found financial strain, healthcare access, and housing affordability topping voter concerns among women heading into the election cycle. Fair enough—those are real problems. But here's what's missing from the conversation: Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature has spent the last decade expanding government, hiking taxes, and imposing regulations that have made the state less affordable, not more.

Republicans argue that the left's answer—more government spending, wage mandates, and housing restrictions—only worsens the cost-of-living crisis. Meanwhile, conservatives point to energy policy overreach, permitting delays, and anti-business regulations as direct culprits in Colorado's housing shortage and rising utility bills.

The *Complete Colorado* editorial board notes that GOP ballot measures are gaining traction precisely because voters are fed up with progressive governance on issues like biological males competing in girls' sports—another symptom of left-wing ideology prioritized over common sense.

Watch whether Republican candidates can effectively tie Colorado's affordability crisis to Democratic policies—or whether the left successfully blames "corporate greed" and avoids accountability.
