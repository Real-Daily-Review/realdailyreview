# Biden Admin Extends Student Loan Giveaway as Debt Burden Grows

The Biden administration has extended the deadline for federal student loan borrowers to enroll in automatic payments and claim a 1% interest rate reduction—now pushing the enrollment window to the end of 2026. Both 9News Denver and the New York Post report the extension allows more borrowers time to lock in the rate cut.

Here's the conservative concern: this is yet another federal subsidy masquerading as relief. While borrowers benefit from lower rates, taxpayers foot the bill through foregone revenue and expanded government lending programs. The administration frames it as "help," but it's fundamentally a wealth transfer from those who paid their debts or skipped college altogether to those carrying federal loans.

Republicans argue these endless extensions and rate reductions delay the reckoning on college costs and bloated university tuition. Instead of addressing why degrees cost six figures, Washington keeps throwing borrowed money at the symptom. Colorado families already struggling with inflation and housing costs shouldn't be subsidizing others' educational choices.

The real question: when does the federal government stop treating student debt as a political tool and start demanding universities justify their pricing?
