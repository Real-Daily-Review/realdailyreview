# Colorado Family Mourns Father Killed in Impaired-Driving Crash; Border Security Failures Again in Spotlight

A Greeley family is grieving after a fatal crash claimed two lives, with a daughter publicly urging drivers to reconsider getting behind the wheel while impaired. The tragedy underscores a broader pattern: a separate incident in North Texas involved a previously deported illegal immigrant accused of killing a young father while driving drunk in a stolen vehicle on the wrong way down a highway (The Daily Wire).

These cases highlight what conservatives argue is a systemic failure at the border and in enforcement. Republicans contend that inadequate immigration enforcement and catch-and-release policies enable repeat offenders to remain in the country, putting American families at risk. Democrats typically counter that impaired driving is a broader public-health issue affecting citizens and non-citizens alike, and that focusing on immigration misses the larger problem of drunk driving prevention.

For Colorado, the Greeley incident is a local reminder of the stakes. Conservatives and taxpayers should demand accountability: stricter DUI enforcement, mandatory ignition interlock devices, and—critically—federal immigration enforcement that actually removes dangerous criminals from the country rather than cycling them back.
