# Jackson Pollock Painting Sets Auction Record at $181 Million

A Jackson Pollock Abstract Expressionist work sold for a record price at Christie's, shattering the artist's previous auction high.

The painting, "Number 7A, 1948," fetched $181.2 million, according to Deutsche Welle, making it Pollock's most expensive artwork ever sold at auction, according to BBC World. The sale marks a significant milestone for the mid-20th-century artist, whose drip paintings have long commanded premium prices in the contemporary art market.

The record underscores continued collector appetite for canonical modernist works, even as broader art market conditions remain uneven. Pollock's works have historically performed well at major auction houses, and this sale demonstrates sustained demand among wealthy buyers for pieces from the Abstract Expressionist movement.

The sale comes as major auction houses navigate a competitive landscape for high-value artworks, with Christie's and rival Sotheby's regularly competing for marquee collections.

Watch for whether other major Pollock works appear on the auction block in coming months, and whether this record influences pricing expectations for other Abstract Expressionist masters.
