# AI Boom Mints Millionaires While Colorado Tech Sector Watches from Sidelines

Anthropic's record $81 billion IPO Thursday—valuing the AI startup at $1.5 trillion—has created instant wealth for thousands of employees, with the average worker's net worth jumping to over $40 million and at least 400 staffers now worth $100 million-plus each (Daily Wire). The company priced shares at $83, above its proposed $70–$78 range, signaling explosive investor appetite for artificial intelligence plays.

The windfall underscores a troubling reality for Colorado's tech economy: while the state has positioned itself as an innovation hub, mega-cap AI wealth is concentrating in coastal enclaves. Colorado's venture capital and startup ecosystem, though growing, remains dwarfed by Silicon Valley's gravitational pull. Local entrepreneurs and investors should be asking why Colorado isn't capturing more of this generational wealth creation.

Meanwhile, the Trump administration is directing record resources where they belong—$44.8 billion to local VA healthcare providers in fiscal 2026, expanding veterans' medical access and channeling billions into regional economies (Breitbart). This is smart government: targeted spending that serves those who served us, with measurable local impact.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether state policymakers will create tax and regulatory conditions competitive enough to attract AI talent and capital—or continue watching billions flow elsewhere.
