# Colorado Republicans Nominate Victor Marx for Governor—Brace for National Ridicule

Victor Marx has secured the Republican gubernatorial nomination in Colorado, edging out rivals by roughly 2,500 votes as of Thursday, according to 9News Denver. The projection sets up a general election matchup that will test whether Colorado Republicans can reclaim the governor's office after years of Democratic control.

Marx's narrow primary victory, however, has already drawn skepticism from Colorado's political establishment. The Denver Post's editorial board warned that a Marx nomination would return Colorado to "late-night comedy" status—a backhanded acknowledgment that the Republican nominee may struggle with mainstream media coverage and suburban swing voters who decide statewide races.

**What conservatives should know:** Marx will face an uphill climb in November against a Democratic incumbent in a state that has drifted blue over the past decade. His thin primary margin suggests the GOP base was divided, which could hamper unity efforts heading into the general election. Colorado Republicans will need to consolidate support among rural voters, energy workers, and Second Amendment advocates while making inroads with Denver metro moderates—a tall order in a state where Democrats have dominated recent statewide races.

**Watch next:** Whether Marx can unite the fractured GOP base and articulate a compelling conservative message on taxes, energy policy, and government overreach before November's general election.
