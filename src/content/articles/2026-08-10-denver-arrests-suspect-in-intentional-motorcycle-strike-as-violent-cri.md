# Denver Faces Rising Violent Crime as Intentional Motorcycle Strike Kills Rider

Denver police arrested a suspect after investigators determined a driver intentionally struck a motorcyclist, resulting in a fatal crash (9News Denver). The deliberate nature of the collision underscores a troubling pattern: Colorado's capital continues struggling with violent crime even as progressive city leadership doubles down on soft-on-crime policies.

Meanwhile, the community is rallying around three Eaton High School graduates killed in a separate vehicle crash last month, with a fundraiser organized to support their families (KDVR Fox31 Denver). These tragedies highlight the human cost of Denver's deteriorating public safety environment.

Conservatives argue that Denver's approach—prioritizing criminal justice "reform," reducing police funding, and declining to prosecute low-level offenses aggressively—has emboldened dangerous actors. Republicans point to rising violent crime statistics as evidence that progressive policies fail to protect law-abiding Coloradans. City officials counter that systemic issues require long-term solutions, not enforcement-focused approaches.
