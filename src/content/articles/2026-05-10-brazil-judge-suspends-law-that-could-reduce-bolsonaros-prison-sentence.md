# Brazil Judge Blocks Law That Could Have Shortened Bolsonaro's Prison Sentence

Brazil's Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes has suspended a law that would have reduced prison sentences, blocking a potential path to shorten former President Jair Bolsonaro's 27-year jail term, according to Al Jazeera.

The suspension is pending further review by the court. De Moraes' action prevents the immediate application of the law, which would have allowed sentence reductions for inmates meeting certain criteria.

Bolsonaro is serving his sentence following convictions related to charges that have kept him barred from political office. The suspended law represented a potential avenue for sentence mitigation, but the judge's decision halts that mechanism for now.

The move underscores ongoing legal battles surrounding Bolsonaro's case and broader questions about Brazil's judicial handling of the former leader's convictions. De Moraes, who has overseen several high-profile cases involving Bolsonaro, will determine the law's fate through the full review process.

Watch for the Supreme Court's final ruling on whether the law will be permanently blocked or reinstated.
