# Trump Cracks Down on Reflecting Pool Vandals as $14M Makeover Faces Fresh Headaches

President Trump is promising swift justice for vandals targeting the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool, even as his ambitious—and costly—renovation of the iconic monument hits fresh snags.

Multiple people, including a former Olympian, have been arrested for allegedly defacing the pool. Trump warned of serious jail time for those responsible, vowing "full enforcement" of consequences (Just The News). The crackdown comes as the administration pushes ahead with broader beautification efforts across Washington, D.C.

But the project itself is proving messy. The Washington Examiner reports Trump's $14 million overhaul—which includes a $6.9 million renovation—has already stumbled: the pool's new "American flag blue" coating began peeling just two weeks after reopening. Some of Trump's other monument projects, including a proposed White House ballroom, have drawn legal scrutiny.

For Colorado conservatives watching federal spending, this serves as a cautionary tale: even well-intentioned infrastructure projects can balloon in cost and deliver subpar results when government manages them. The Reflecting Pool fiasco—expensive, delayed, and now damaged—mirrors the kind of waste taxpayers see in state and local projects closer to home.
