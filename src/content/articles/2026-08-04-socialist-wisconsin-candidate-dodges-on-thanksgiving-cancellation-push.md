# Socialist Wisconsin Candidate Dodges on Thanksgiving Cancellation Push

A Democratic socialist running for Wisconsin governor is refusing to own up to her past calls to cancel Thanksgiving, claiming her "views can evolve" when pressed on the issue during a CNN appearance.

Francesca Hong, a chef-turned-politician, deleted a 2020 social media post calling for the holiday to be scrapped but wouldn't directly address the matter when host Kaitlan Collins confronted her on *The Source* (Washington Examiner). Instead of a straight answer, Hong pivoted to her personal history cooking Thanksgiving meals for her community at age 16—a classic dodge that left the question unanswered.

The non-answer is emblematic of how progressives handle their own radical positions once they go mainstream. Hong's refusal to clarify whether she still supports canceling one of America's most cherished traditions reveals the gap between what the left says in activist circles and what it's willing to defend publicly.

For Colorado conservatives, this is a cautionary tale: similar ideological activists have gained ground in state legislatures and city councils across the West, often softening their rhetoric once in office while advancing the same agenda through regulation and spending. Watch whether Hong's evasion becomes a template for other progressive candidates facing scrutiny on their past statements.
