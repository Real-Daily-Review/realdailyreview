# DHS Targets Immigration Lawyers Filing Bogus Asylum Claims

The Department of Homeland Security is cracking down on immigration attorneys flooding the system with meritless asylum petitions—a move conservatives say is long overdue to restore integrity to a broken immigration process.

According to the Daily Caller, DHS has launched an enforcement action against lawyers filing frivolous claims that clog federal courts and delay legitimate cases. Breitbart reports the crackdown targets attorneys responsible for tens of thousands of false asylum filings for migrants, effectively weaponizing the asylum system to circumvent immigration law.

Republicans argue the asylum process has become a de facto open-border loophole, with activist attorneys gaming the system to keep migrants in the country while cases drag through courts for years. Conservatives contend that meritless filings waste taxpayer resources, overwhelm immigration judges, and undermine the rule of law. They view this DHS action as necessary accountability.

Democrats and immigration advocates likely counter that aggressive enforcement against attorneys chills legitimate legal representation and that systemic delays—not frivolous claims—are the real problem. They may argue the crackdown targets lawyers defending vulnerable migrants.
