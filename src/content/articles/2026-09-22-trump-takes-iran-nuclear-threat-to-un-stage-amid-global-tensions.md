# Trump Pivots to Iran, Gaza at UN as Global Tensions Simmer

President Donald Trump is using his United Nations General Assembly address to confront what conservatives view as critical threats to American security and Middle East stability. According to Fox News, Trump plans to focus on Iran's nuclear ambitions, the Gaza conflict, and structural reforms at the UN itself—signaling a hardline approach to foreign policy after months of diplomatic maneuvering.

The Iran nuclear issue sits at the center of Trump's worldview: conservatives argue the previous administration's deal left Tehran too close to weapons capability and failed to constrain regional aggression. Trump's UN speech comes as tensions with Iran escalate, with The Daily Wire reporting intensifying Iranian protests demanding an end to ayatollah rule—a potential opening for U.S. pressure.

The Gaza component reflects Trump's effort to broker Middle East stability while maintaining strong support for Israel. His UN overhaul agenda signals frustration with what conservatives see as a bloated, ineffective institution that often constrains American interests.
