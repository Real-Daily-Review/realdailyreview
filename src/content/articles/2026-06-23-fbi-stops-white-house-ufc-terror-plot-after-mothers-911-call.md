# FBI Disrupts White House UFC Terror Plot After Mother's Alert

A concerned mother's 911 call prevented what could have been a catastrophic attack on a Trump White House UFC event, with the FBI arresting multiple suspects accused of plotting violence at the Freedom 250 gathering.

According to *The Daily Wire*, the mother of 19-year-old Tycen Proper contacted law enforcement after growing alarmed about her son's activities, triggering an FBI investigation that unraveled the alleged conspiracy. The bureau moved quickly to disrupt the plot before the event proceeded.

The scope of the threat expanded as investigators made additional arrests. *The Daily Wire* reports the Justice Department charged William Lee Spartacus Falkner and Jordan Rincker with conspiracy to commit murder in connection with the scheme. One suspect had drone piloting experience, suggesting the plotters may have contemplated using unmanned aircraft in their attack.

The case underscores both the persistent terrorism threat facing high-profile events and the critical role ordinary citizens—in this instance, a vigilant parent—play in protecting national security. The FBI's swift action prevented potential mass casualties at a major White House gathering.
