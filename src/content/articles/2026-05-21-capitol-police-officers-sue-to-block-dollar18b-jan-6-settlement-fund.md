# Capitol Police Officers Sue to Block Jan. 6 Settlement Fund

Two police officers who defended the U.S. Capitol during the Jan. 6 riot have filed a lawsuit to block payouts from a settlement fund, according to NPR and PBS NewsHour.

The officers are challenging what PBS NewsHour describes as the Trump administration's "anti-weaponization fund," which is valued at $1.8 billion according to PBS. The lawsuit seeks to prevent any disbursements from the fund.

The officers' legal action represents a direct challenge to the administration's approach to Jan. 6-related claims. The specifics of their objections and the legal grounds for the suit were not detailed in the available reporting.

The case will test whether courts will allow the fund to proceed as structured or whether the officers' challenge will succeed in blocking distributions.
