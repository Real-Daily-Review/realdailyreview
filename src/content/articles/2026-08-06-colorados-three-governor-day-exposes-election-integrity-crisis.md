# Colorado's Voter Fraud Crisis: Three Governors in One Day

Colorado's election integrity just hit rock bottom—the state now holds the dubious distinction of being the only one where three different people served as governor on the same day, all because of voter fraud that went undetected until it was too late.

According to CPR News, the historical anomaly stems from fraudulent voter registrations that compromised the legitimacy of a gubernatorial election. The Daily Caller reports that questions surrounding voter registration practices—specifically involving figures like James Talarico—have raised serious red flags about how Colorado's election system failed to catch obvious fraud before ballots were cast and counted.

This is exactly what Colorado conservatives have warned about for years: without robust voter ID requirements, signature verification, and real-time fraud detection, Colorado's elections remain vulnerable to manipulation. Democrats have consistently blocked stricter election security measures, dismissing voter fraud concerns as overblown—yet here we are with a constitutional crisis on our hands.

The three-governor day represents not just an embarrassment but a fundamental breakdown in election administration. Colorado taxpayers deserve answers about which officials failed to catch this fraud, and whether similar schemes have compromised other races undetected.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans demand a full forensic audit of recent elections and push for mandatory voter ID legislation in the 2027 session.
