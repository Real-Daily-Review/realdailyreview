# Student Pilot Lands Plane Solo After Instructor's Mid-Flight Death

A 22-year-old student pilot in Argentina successfully landed a small aircraft alone after her flight instructor jumped to his death during a training flight over the weekend, according to 9News Denver and The Daily Wire.

The instructor, identified as Leandro Bertazzo, 42, reportedly told his student—named Rosario—"You know what you have to do, carry on" before removing his headset, unfastening his seat belt, and exiting the plane over Toledo, Argentina. The student, left alone in the cockpit, managed to land the aircraft safely.

The incident underscores both the critical importance of pilot training fundamentals and the split-second decision-making required in aviation emergencies. While this case involved an instructor's deliberate act rather than mechanical failure, it highlights why rigorous certification standards and thorough emergency protocols exist—and why they work.

The student's ability to execute a safe landing under extreme duress demonstrates the value of proper instruction, even as it raises questions about the instructor's mental state and the screening processes aviation authorities use to clear instructors for duty.
