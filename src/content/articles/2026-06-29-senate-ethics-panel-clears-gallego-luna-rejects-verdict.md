# Senate Ethics Panel Clears Gallego; Luna Fires Back

Arizona Democrat Ruben Gallego has been cleared of sexual misconduct and campaign finance allegations after the Senate Ethics Committee found no evidence to support the complaint filed by GOP Rep. Anna Paulina Luna, according to *Fox News*.

The committee conducted a formal investigation, taking statements from individuals and reviewing federal campaign finance filings and congressional financial disclosures. Despite the thorough examination, the panel determined insufficient grounds to proceed with disciplinary action.

Luna, undeterred by the dismissal, fired back sharply. "Once a creep, always a creep," she declared (*Daily Caller*), signaling Republicans' skepticism of the committee's conclusion and their belief that the investigation fell short.

The case highlights the partisan divide over Senate ethics enforcement. Republicans argue the committee's standards for evidence remain too high, allowing Democratic senators to escape accountability. Democrats maintain the investigation was thorough and the dismissal vindicates Gallego.

While this case involves an Arizona senator, it underscores broader conservative concerns about the Senate's willingness to police its own members—a dynamic that could affect how Colorado voters view their own senators' conduct and the credibility of ethics oversight on Capitol Hill.

Watch whether Luna pursues additional avenues to challenge the committee's decision or if this becomes a rallying point for GOP demands to strengthen Senate ethics enforcement.
