# Wisconsin Socialist's Holiday Crusade Exposes Progressive Overreach

Bernie Sanders won't even endorse her—a telling sign that Wisconsin Democrat Francesca Hong's socialist gubernatorial campaign has veered so far left it's alienating even the Vermont senator. Hong has spent months attacking American holidays as oppressive, capitalist, or personally inconvenient, from Christmas trees to fireworks to Valentine's Day (New York Post). When backlash mounted over her Thanksgiving "cancellation" rhetoric, she walked it back, claiming the holiday is actually her favorite—a transparent flip-flop that reveals how radical her actual positions are.

The Daily Wire notes Hong's "how dare we say Merry Christmas" energy perfectly captures the progressive contempt for American traditions that resonates poorly even with Democratic insiders. Her attacks span at least seven major holidays, suggesting a deeper ideological hostility to American culture itself.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: Hong represents the activist left that increasingly dominates Democratic primary politics. If this ideology gains traction in neighboring states, expect similar culture-war assaults on Colorado traditions, energy development, and individual liberty. Colorado Republicans should watch whether national Democrats distance themselves from Hong's radicalism or quietly embrace it—that answer will tell you everything about where the party's base actually stands.
