# Colorado Voters to Decide on Constitutional "Right to Natural Gas"

Colorado will vote this November on whether to enshrine a constitutional right to natural gas—a direct pushback against progressive efforts to phase out natural gas in homes and businesses across the state.

Initiative 177, brought forward by Advance Colorado, qualified for the ballot after meeting signature requirements, according to the Colorado Secretary of State's office (Denver Post). The measure would prevent state and local governments from banning natural gas sales or use, protecting both consumers' choice and the energy workers and businesses that depend on the industry.

The initiative reflects growing conservative concern that Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature and left-leaning municipalities are moving toward aggressive electrification mandates that would force costly retrofits on homeowners and small businesses. Supporters argue the measure defends property rights and economic freedom against regulatory overreach.

Democrats and environmental groups have not yet mounted a visible public campaign against the measure, though they likely will frame it as an obstacle to climate goals. Conservatives counter that Colorado families and businesses—not government bureaucrats—should decide their own energy sources.

The ballot measure also signals the natural gas industry's determination to fight back against the green-energy agenda that has gained traction in Denver and Boulder. Watch whether the environmental lobby mobilizes significant funding to oppose Initiative 177 in the final months before November.
