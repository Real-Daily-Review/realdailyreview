# Trump Mobile Confirms Data Breach Exposing Customer Information

Trump Mobile has confirmed a data exposure affecting its customers' personal information, according to TechCrunch. The breach included phone numbers and home addresses accessible through a third-party platform.

The company said it is currently evaluating whether customer notification is required under applicable regulations. Trump Mobile did not disclose the number of affected customers or provide additional details about the scope of the exposure.

The incident highlights ongoing security challenges facing smaller telecommunications providers and branded services. The company's response will likely depend on state and federal data breach notification laws, which typically require companies to inform consumers when personal data is compromised.

Watch for updates on whether Trump Mobile issues formal breach notifications and what security measures the company implements to prevent future incidents.
