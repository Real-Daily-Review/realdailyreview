# Democratic Socialism Agenda Threatens Constitutional Limits, Conservatives Warn

Colorado conservatives are sounding the alarm over the Democratic Socialists of America platform, which they argue represents a direct assault on constitutional governance and free-market principles. Former President Trump has escalated the rhetoric, calling communism "the greatest threat facing the U.S. in its history" and specifically targeting DSA-aligned candidates (Fox News Politics).

According to Complete Colorado, the DSA platform would undermine the constitutional system of checks and balances—a foundational protection against concentrated government power. Conservatives argue that "democratic socialism" is merely a stepping stone toward outright communism, not a legitimate policy alternative.

Republicans contend that DSA's agenda—which typically includes massive wealth redistribution, government control of key industries, and erosion of private property rights—would devastate Colorado's energy sector, agricultural economy, and small-business community. The platform's anti-capitalist framework directly conflicts with the limited-government, free-market values that have driven Colorado's prosperity.

Democrats and DSA supporters frame their proposals as addressing inequality and corporate excess, but conservatives reject this framing as cover for authoritarian economics that has failed everywhere it's been tried.
