# Federal Prosecutors Expose Massive Tax and Benefits Fraud by Illegal Aliens

An illegal alien has been sentenced to eight years in federal prison for orchestrating an $89 million payroll tax fraud scheme involving illegal alien construction workers, according to *Breitbart* and the *Daily Caller*. The case underscores what conservatives argue is a predictable consequence of lax immigration enforcement: widespread exploitation of federal tax and benefit systems.

In a separate case, the Department of Justice charged 15 individuals—11 of them illegal aliens—in Massachusetts for stealing $1.4 million in food stamps and disability benefits, *Breitbart* reported.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans and immigration hawks argue these cases vindicate their warnings that sanctuary policies and weak border enforcement create conditions for systemic fraud. When illegal aliens work off-the-books or through fraudulent payroll schemes, they undercut tax compliance, depress wages for legal workers, and drain social safety nets meant for American citizens. Colorado's construction and agriculture sectors—both labor-intensive industries—face similar pressures from illegal employment networks that undercut legitimate businesses and workers.

**What's Next:** Watch whether Colorado state lawmakers push for E-Verify mandates or tighter state-level employment verification, or whether progressive legislators block such measures in the name of "sanctuary" policies.
