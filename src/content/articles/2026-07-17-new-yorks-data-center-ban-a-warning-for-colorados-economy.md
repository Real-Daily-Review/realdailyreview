# Colorado Should Watch New York's Data Center Trap—It Could Happen Here

New York Governor Kathy Hochul just imposed the nation's first statewide moratorium on data center construction, bowing to pressure from socialist Democrats like Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, who is pushing a national ban in Congress (Washington Examiner). The move blocks infrastructure development in the name of environmental activism—a cautionary tale for Colorado.

**Why this matters to Colorado:** Data centers represent high-paying jobs, tax revenue, and economic growth. Colorado's tech corridor and rural communities have attracted data center investment. If progressive activists succeed in strangling this sector nationally, Colorado could lose billions in development and thousands of jobs.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that banning data centers is economic self-sabotage. These facilities drive innovation, create stable employment, and generate tax revenue without the environmental footprint of manufacturing. The left's obsession with blocking infrastructure development—whether data centers, pipelines, or power plants—reflects an ideology that prioritizes activist demands over working families' prosperity.

Democrats counter that data centers consume enormous amounts of water and electricity, straining resources. But conservatives say that's a planning and engineering problem, not a reason to ban an entire industry.

**Watch this:** Colorado lawmakers should reject any attempt to import New York's anti-development playbook and protect the state's competitive advantage in attracting data center investment.
