# Amendment 87 Would Lock Colorado Into Bracket Creep Tax Trap

Colorado voters face a deceptive ballot measure that promises to soak the rich while quietly raising taxes on everyone else through bracket creep—a fiscal time bomb that will hit middle-class Coloradans hard once inflation kicks in.

Amendment 87 would raise income taxes on the top 3% of earners and 5% of businesses, with proponents claiming it generates $2.7 billion annually (Colorado Sun). But conservatives warn the measure contains a fatal flaw: it fails to index tax brackets to inflation. That means as wages rise with the cost of living, more middle-class Coloradans will automatically slide into higher tax brackets without any legislative vote—a stealth tax increase that hits working families hardest.

Complete Colorado reports that bracket creep will transform Amendment 87 into "a long-term tax increase for all Coloradans," not just the wealthy. It's a classic progressive bait-and-switch: tax the rich today, tax everyone tomorrow.

**The conservative case:** This is government fiscal engineering at its worst. Colorado's economy doesn't need another permanent tax burden. Bracket creep punishes wage growth and forces families to pay more simply for keeping up with inflation—a tax on prosperity itself.

**Watch for:** Whether the Colorado legislature, if Amendment 87 passes, will have the political will to fix bracket creep indexing or simply pocket the windfall revenue.
