# Democrats Can't Outrun Their Woke Record—and Colorado Voters Should Remember It

Democrats nationwide are frantically distancing themselves from the radical rhetoric they championed just years ago. According to the *Washington Examiner*, Democratic candidates are "running away from things they said from 2019 to 2021," with Wisconsin gubernatorial front-runner Francesca Hong walking back previous positions. The *Daily Wire* notes that progressive figures like Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez are attempting to "memory-hole" their own recorded statements—a strategy doomed to fail in the age of digital archives.

Republicans argue this retreat exposes the hollowness of progressive ideology. *National Review* asks pointedly: if Democrats have recognized "the errors of their ways," why aren't they offering genuine apologies rather than calculated silence? Conservatives say voters deserve accountability, not amnesia.

For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats have embraced similar woke policies on energy regulation, gender ideology in schools, and government overreach. When progressive candidates nationwide suddenly discover moderation, it signals weakness—and an admission that their agenda doesn't sell to mainstream voters. Colorado conservatives should document every local Democrat's embrace of 2019-2021 radicalism and hold them accountable when they try to rebrand.
