# Squad Democrat's ICE Abolition Bill Draws Fire From Trump Administration

A radical push to dismantle federal immigration enforcement has collided head-on with the Trump administration's border-security agenda. Rep. Delia Ramirez (D-Illinois), a member of the progressive "Squad," introduced legislation to abolish Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and effectively dismantle the Department of Homeland Security—drawing swift condemnation from DHS Secretary Markwayne Mullin.

"Radical leftists are calling to defund the police and abolish DHS. It's NEVER going to happen," Mullin said, according to the New York Post and Fox News Politics.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Abolishing ICE and CBP would cripple border enforcement and interior immigration operations at a moment when illegal crossings remain a flashpoint for voters nationwide. Republicans argue the proposal reflects the Democratic left's ideological commitment to open borders over national security and rule of law.

**Democrats and progressive activists counter** that ICE and CBP have engaged in civil-rights abuses and that immigration enforcement should be reformed or transferred to other agencies—though Ramirez's bill goes further than most mainstream Democratic proposals.

For Colorado, this matters: the state has grappled with sanctuary-city policies in Denver and Boulder while rural counties depend on federal immigration enforcement to manage labor and security concerns.

Watch whether this bill gains co-sponsors or becomes a litmus test for 2026 Democratic primary races.
