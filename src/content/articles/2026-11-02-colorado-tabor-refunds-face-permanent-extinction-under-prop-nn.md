# Colorado Voters Face TABOR Erosion While Weighing Redistricting Reform

Colorado taxpayers are staring down a permanent loss of TABOR refunds—the constitutional guardrail that returns surplus state revenue to citizens instead of Sacramento-style government coffers. According to Complete Colorado, Proposition NN represents a "perfect storm of distraction" that could eliminate refunds for most Coloradans' lifetimes.

The threat comes as voters simultaneously weigh Initiative #256, which would establish new guardrails against partisan congressional redistricting. While anti-gerrymandering reform sounds appealing in theory, conservatives worry the measure could constrain legitimate map-drawing while the state quietly dismantles TABOR protections that have saved Colorado taxpayers billions since 1992.

Republicans argue that losing TABOR refunds means government keeps money that belongs to citizens—a direct transfer of wealth from family budgets to state spending. Democrats counter that stable, predictable revenue allows better long-term planning for schools, roads, and services. But the timing is telling: while voters focus on redistricting fairness, the left advances a stealth tax grab.

For Colorado families already squeezed by inflation and property taxes, this is existential. TABOR has been the only brake on government growth; without it, expect permanent tax hikes disguised as "revenue measures."

**Watch whether the legislature moves to formally repeal TABOR protections before voters fully grasp what's at stake.**
