# Democrats' Embrace of Radical Streamer Exposes Party's Leftward Lurch

Colorado Democrats should answer a hard question: why are party candidates campaigning with a self-described communist who has praised terrorism?

Left-wing streamer Hasan Piker, aligned with the Democratic Socialists of America, has become one of the most sought-after campaign surrogates for Democratic candidates nationwide, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Columnist Joe Concha called out the arrangement bluntly: "Democrats actually campaign with this maniac."

Piker isn't a fringe podcaster making provocative comments from his basement. Multiple candidates have proudly aligned themselves with him and appeared at campaign events alongside him. The *Washington Examiner* reports he holds extreme positions that go well beyond typical progressive politics—positions most mainstream Democrats won't publicly defend.

This matters for Colorado because it signals how far the Democratic Party's center of gravity has shifted. When major party candidates court surrogates with radical ideological commitments, it reveals what the party actually values, even if leadership won't say so out loud.

Republicans argue this reflects Democrats' inability or unwillingness to police their own coalition. Conservatives say Colorado voters deserve clarity: do Democratic candidates agree with Piker's worldview, or are they simply willing to use him for votes?

Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from this alliance or double down on it heading into the next election cycle.
