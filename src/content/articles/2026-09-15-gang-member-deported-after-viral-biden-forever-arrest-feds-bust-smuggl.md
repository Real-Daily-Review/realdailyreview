# Haitian Gang Member Deported After 19-Month Custody; Feds Bust Massive Smuggling Fraud Ring

**Biden-era immigration enforcement finally removes a viral symbol of open-border chaos—but the smuggling epidemic continues.**

A Haitian gang member who became an internet sensation by shouting "Biden forever!" during his 2023 ICE arrest has been deported to Haiti after 19 months in federal custody, *Fox News* reports. Wisteguens Jean Quely Chargles went viral for his defiant tirade—"f*** Trump, Biden forever!"—during the arrest, crystallizing conservative concerns about lax enforcement under the Biden administration.

The deportation comes as federal authorities uncover a sprawling visa fraud scheme tied to illegal alien smuggling. According to *The Daily Wire*, five alleged smugglers attempted to lure nearly 900 illegal immigrants into the United States by fraudulently obtaining visas. The operation underscores how criminal networks exploit America's immigration system while border communities and Colorado taxpayers absorb the costs of processing, detention, and social services.

Republicans argue the 19-month delay in Chargles' deportation exemplifies Biden-era bureaucratic foot-dragging. Conservatives say such cases prove that catch-and-release policies and visa fraud networks operate with near-impunity under progressive administrations that deprioritize enforcement.
