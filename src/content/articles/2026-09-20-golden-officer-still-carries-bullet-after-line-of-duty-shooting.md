# Colorado Officer Still Carrying Bullet After Line-of-Duty Shooting

A Golden police officer wounded in an August 2026 gas station shooting remains hospitalized with a bullet lodged in his body, underscoring the real dangers law enforcement face daily—and the inadequate support systems left behind when officers are injured in the line of duty.

Officer Greg Metzel was shot while responding to an incident at a gas station on West Colfax Avenue. According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, Metzel still carries the bullet inside him and has required ongoing medical care. The MC-1 Foundation stepped in Saturday with a fundraiser in Littleton to support the injured officer—a private charity effort that highlights a troubling gap: Colorado taxpayers and nonprofits are filling holes that government should address for those who put on the badge.

While details remain limited on the suspect and circumstances, the incident reflects a broader pattern: law enforcement officers absorbing the consequences of violent crime while progressive prosecutors and soft-on-crime policies dominate Colorado's criminal justice system. Officers like Metzel deserve comprehensive, state-funded support—not reliance on charitable fundraisers.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado's legislature moves to strengthen officer support programs and whether district attorneys will pursue serious charges against the suspect.
