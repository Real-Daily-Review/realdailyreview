# Cost of Living Crisis Hits Home: Why Washington's 'Booming Economy' Rings Hollow for Colorado Families

While national media and Biden administration officials tout economic growth, ordinary Coloradans are feeling the squeeze at the grocery store and gas pump—and they're not buying the narrative.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, the disconnect between headline GDP numbers and household reality has become impossible to ignore. Americans across the political spectrum report the same problem: wages aren't keeping pace with inflation on essentials. Groceries, fuel, and housing have become luxury expenses for working families who thought they were doing everything right.

Conservatives argue this cost-of-living crisis stems directly from government overspending and the Federal Reserve's monetary policies—decisions made in Washington that hit Colorado ranchers, small-business owners, and wage earners hardest. When the government floods the economy with trillions in stimulus spending, inflation follows. When energy policies restrict domestic production, gas prices spike. When regulatory red tape drives up business costs, those expenses get passed to consumers.

The left counters that supply-chain disruptions and corporate profit-taking are the real culprits, not government policy. But Republicans point out that inflation accelerated *after* massive federal spending packages, not before.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state lawmakers will resist the urge to pile on their own spending and tax hikes—or whether they'll follow Washington's failed playbook.
