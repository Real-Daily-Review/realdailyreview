# House Republicans Pick Jim Jordan as Minority Leader After 2026 Midterm Losses

Jim Jordan has seized the House Republican leadership after Republicans absorbed steep midterm losses, positioning the Ohio firebrand as the party's chief antagonist to the Democratic majority in the 120th Congress.

Jordan defeated House Majority Leader Steve Scalise in a closed-door secret ballot Tuesday, securing 106 votes to Scalise's 73, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Speaker Mike Johnson declined to seek the minority leader role, clearing the field for the contest.

Jordan's victory signals Republicans are choosing confrontation over consensus-building. The Ohio conservative has built his reputation as a relentless investigator and Trump ally—exactly what House Republicans want as they face a Democratic-controlled chamber after what the *New York Post* describes as a "blowout" midterm performance.

For Colorado Republicans, Jordan's ascension matters: the House minority leader shapes messaging on energy policy, water rights, and Second Amendment defense—issues where Colorado conservatives clash with Democratic majorities in Washington. Jordan's combative style could energize the GOP base but may complicate bipartisan negotiations on issues affecting Colorado's economy and natural resources.

Watch whether Jordan's leadership accelerates Republican calls for investigations into Biden-era regulatory overreach—particularly on energy and land-use decisions that hit Colorado hard.
