# AOC's Michigan Endorsement Exposes Deep Dem Divide Over Senate Direction

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez's endorsement of progressive Dr. Abdul El-Sayed in Michigan's Democratic Senate primary is a stark reminder that the left's internal civil war over ideology and control shows no signs of cooling—and Colorado Democrats should take note about where national party momentum is headed.

AOC formally backed El-Sayed, a Bernie Sanders-aligned candidate, directly defying Chuck Schumer and the Democratic establishment's support for moderate Haley Stevens (Fox News, New York Post). The split underscores a fundamental clash: progressives demand ideological purity and aggressive left-wing policy, while establishment Democrats prioritize electability and party unity.

**Why Colorado cares:** This fracture matters for Colorado's 2026 Senate race and state legislative contests. If the progressive wing gains ground nationally, expect similar pressure on Colorado Democrats to embrace higher taxes, aggressive climate mandates, and anti-energy policies that could hurt working families and rural communities. Conversely, if moderates hold the line, Colorado's competitive districts may remain winnable for Republicans.

**The conservative takeaway:** Democrats are openly battling over whether to move further left or recalibrate toward the center. Republicans should watch whether Colorado Democrats follow AOC's lead or Schumer's pragmatism—that choice will define the battlefield.
