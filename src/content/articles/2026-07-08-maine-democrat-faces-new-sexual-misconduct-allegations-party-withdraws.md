# Maine Democrat's Sexual Misconduct Allegations Mount as Party Distances Itself

A Democratic Maine Senate candidate is facing mounting allegations of sexual misconduct that have prompted party leaders and institutions to withdraw support. Graham Platner's ex-girlfriend Lyndsey Fifield accused him Tuesday of repeatedly removing condoms without consent during their 2013-2015 relationship in Washington, D.C. (The Daily Wire). This follows a bombshell report Monday alleging Platner sexually assaulted a different ex-girlfriend—claims that triggered swift Democratic backlash.

The allegations represent serious criminal accusations. Removing condoms without consent, known as "stealthing," violates sexual consent laws in multiple jurisdictions and constitutes sexual assault under many state statutes. Fifield has also previously accused Platner of physical abuse (The Daily Wire).

Democrats have moved quickly to distance themselves from Platner, with party officials and allied institutions rescinding endorsements and support (Washington Examiner). The speed of the Democratic response contrasts sharply with how the party has handled similar allegations against other candidates in recent cycles—a point conservatives note when evaluating claims of selective accountability.

While this story centers on Maine politics, it underscores broader questions about vetting and institutional responsibility within political parties.
