# Midwest Primary Roundup: Trump-Backed Republicans Advance While Dems Reject Socialist

Trump's endorsement muscle is working in the Midwest, even as Democrats show signs of moderating their bench.

In Wisconsin's 7th Congressional District, Michael Alfonso—Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy's son-in-law—won a crowded Republican primary with 48% support, powered by Trump's backing (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, in Minnesota's 1st District, Republican Brad Finstad survived his primary challenge on turf once held by now-VP Tim Walz, as Democrats eye a potential flip (Fox News).

The more telling story: Wisconsin Democrats rejected democratic socialist Francesca Hong in their gubernatorial primary, choosing moderate David Crowley instead (New York Post). That's a sign the left may be recalibrating after 2024's losses.

**The Colorado angle:** These Midwest contests preview 2026 dynamics that could ripple into Colorado's own congressional and statewide races. If Trump-endorsed Republicans continue consolidating primary support while Democrats drift toward moderation, Colorado Republicans should watch whether that momentum translates to recruitment and fundraising for competitive seats here—particularly in CD-3 and CD-8, where GOP gains remain possible.

**Watch:** Whether Alfonso and Finstad can hold these seats in November, and whether Democratic moderation signals a broader retreat from progressive policy nationwide.
