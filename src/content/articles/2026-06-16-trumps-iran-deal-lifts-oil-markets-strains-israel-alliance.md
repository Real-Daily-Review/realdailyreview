# Trump's Iran Deal Rattles Middle East, Lifts US Energy Markets

President Trump announced a landmark interim peace deal with Iran that immediately lifts the U.S. naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz—a move already roiling Middle Eastern stability and reshaping energy markets.

Oil prices plummeted to three-month lows following the agreement (Daily Caller), which requires Iran to abandon its nuclear weapons program and cease funding terrorist activities across the region in exchange for significant sanctions relief. The deal's first phase hinges on an end to fighting "on all fronts," including Lebanon, where Israel and Hezbollah remain locked in fierce combat (Just The News).

That regional complication has already surfaced: Trump expressed displeasure with Israel over weekend strikes on Lebanon that coincided with Iran negotiations (New York Post), signaling potential friction between Washington and its closest Middle East ally.

**For Colorado:** Lower oil prices benefit consumers at the pump, though they pressure domestic energy producers and workers in the state's oil and gas sector—a key economic driver.

Former Trump economist Stephen Moore predicts the deal will trigger a powerful U.S. economic boom (Just The News), though skeptics worry whether Iran will honor long-term commitments. Senate Minority Leader Schumer has demanded Congress be briefed on the agreement's full terms.

**Watch:** Whether Congress forces transparency on sanctions relief terms and whether Israel's Lebanon operations derail the fragile accord.
