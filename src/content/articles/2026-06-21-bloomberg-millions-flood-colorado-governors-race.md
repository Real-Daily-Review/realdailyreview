# Bloomberg's Millions Flood Colorado Governor's Race—Again

A New York billionaire with a history of failed presidential ambitions is now pouring cash into Colorado's 2026 gubernatorial race, according to *Complete Colorado*. Michael Bloomberg, who lost his own White House bid, is betting heavily to shape the Democratic primary and, ultimately, who sits in the governor's mansion.

The influx of out-of-state money underscores a familiar pattern: coastal elites bankrolling Colorado politics to advance their progressive agenda. Bloomberg's track record includes aggressive gun control advocacy and expansive regulatory overreach—positions that clash sharply with Colorado's gun owners, energy workers, and business community.

On the Democratic side, U.S. Sen. Michael Bennet and Attorney General Phil Weiser are competing for the nomination (*Denver Post*). Weiser has built his campaign partly on legal battles with the Trump administration, signaling where his priorities lie: more litigation, more regulation, more government.

Republicans argue Colorado voters should reject out-of-state billionaire interference and demand candidates focused on kitchen-table issues: housing affordability, energy jobs, and keeping government out of Coloradans' lives. The state's energy sector, in particular, faces existential threats from Bloomberg-backed environmental policies.

**Watch for:** whether Colorado Republicans can unite behind a strong general-election candidate before Bloomberg's money overwhelms the airwaves.
