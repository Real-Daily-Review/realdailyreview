# UK Labour Suffers Heavy Local Election Losses; Starmer Stays On

Prime Minister Keir Starmer is defying calls to resign after Labour suffered significant losses in Thursday's local council elections across England, according to the Financial Times. Nigel Farage's Reform UK made early gains in the balloting, which also included elections for devolved governments.

The electoral setback has drawn scrutiny to the government's economic credibility, with bond markets showing signs of strain. According to CNBC, "bond vigilantes" are circling British government debt (gilts) as investors reassess confidence in the administration's fiscal management.

Despite the poor showing, Starmer has resisted pressure from within and outside the party to step down. The results represent a significant challenge to Labour's standing less than a year after winning the general election.

The losses reflect broader voter discontent, though sources do not specify the scale of Labour's council seat losses or Reform UK's exact gains. The timing coincides with concerns about the pound's stability and gilt yields, suggesting markets are pricing in political uncertainty.
