# Bystanders Become Heroes After Private Jet Crashes on Texas Highway

Ordinary citizens proved that American courage still exists when a NetJets-operated Cessna Citation Latitude crashed on a Laredo, Texas highway Tuesday night, sending a fireball across the roadway near the U.S.-Mexico border (The Daily Wire). One person was killed and five others injured in the crash, but the outcome could have been far worse—bystanders rushed into the inferno before emergency crews arrived, pulling survivors from the wreckage (9News Denver).

The incident underscores a reality progressives often overlook: communities don't need government mandates to do the right thing. When lives hang in the balance, Americans step up without waiting for bureaucratic permission or federal coordination. These heroes didn't fill out forms or attend a safety seminar—they acted on instinct and conscience.

The crash also highlights why private aviation matters to Colorado's economy and workforce. Business jets connect our state's energy sector, agriculture operations, and remote communities to critical markets and services. Over-regulation of general aviation—a threat from Washington regulators—would undermine the very infrastructure that keeps Colorado competitive.

Watch for any federal safety reviews that might use this incident to justify new FAA restrictions on private aviation operations.
