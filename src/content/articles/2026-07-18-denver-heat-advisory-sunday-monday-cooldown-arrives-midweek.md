# Denver Faces Dangerous Heat Before Next Week's Cooldown

Colorado's Front Range is bracing for a dangerous heat spike this weekend, with the National Weather Service issuing heat advisories for Sunday and Monday as temperatures remain stubbornly elevated across the Denver metro area.

According to KDVR Fox31, a Pinpoint Weather Alert Day is active for both days, with conditions expected to peak before a significant cooldown arrives midweek. 9News reports that relief is "lurking" but residents must first endure what could be the worst of the heat over the coming days, followed by a "stormy cooldown" next week.

The timing matters for Colorado's energy grid and outdoor workers. Summer heat spikes drive demand for air conditioning, putting pressure on Xcel Energy and other providers—a reminder that reliable baseload power from natural gas and coal remains essential, not a luxury, despite progressive push toward rapid renewable-only transitions. Coloradans working construction, agriculture, and outdoor industries should take precautions during the advisory period.

Residents should monitor updated forecasts closely and ensure vulnerable family members stay hydrated and cool during the heat advisory window.
