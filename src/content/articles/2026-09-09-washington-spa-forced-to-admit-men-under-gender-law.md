# Washington State Forces Women's Spa to Accept Men—A Preview of Progressive Overreach

Washington state's Human Rights Commission is ordering Olympus Spa, a women-only nude facility, to admit men, citing transgender rights law (Washington Examiner). The case illustrates how far progressive governments will push gender ideology at the expense of women's privacy and business autonomy.

Conservatives argue this represents government coercion masquerading as civil rights. Women seeking privacy in intimate settings—a reasonable expectation—are being overruled by bureaucratic mandate. Business owners face legal liability for exercising basic judgment about their clientele.

Democrats and progressive advocates counter that denying service based on gender identity constitutes discrimination under state law, and that transgender inclusion is a matter of human dignity.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's own anti-discrimination statutes have expanded to include gender identity protections. If similar cases reach Colorado courts or the Colorado Civil Rights Division, expect similar conflicts between women's privacy rights and transgender accommodation mandates. Colorado businesses—gyms, spas, shelters—could face comparable legal pressure.

Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature moves to clarify or strengthen gender-identity protections in public accommodations, potentially triggering the same clash between competing rights claims.
