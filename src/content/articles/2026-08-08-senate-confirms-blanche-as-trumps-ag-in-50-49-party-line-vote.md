# Senate Confirms Trump's AG Pick Blanche in Party-Line Vote

Trump's attorney general nominee Todd Blanche cleared the Senate in a 50-49 vote early Saturday, surviving defections from two moderate Republicans but securing the votes needed to lead the Justice Department.

Sens. Lisa Murkowski (R-Alaska) and Susan Collins (R-Maine) joined all Democrats in opposing Blanche's confirmation, according to Fox News and the New York Post. Sen. Bill Cassidy emerged as a last-minute Republican supporter Friday, providing the decisive margin.

Blanche's narrow confirmation underscores the razor-thin Republican Senate majority and highlights ongoing tension between Trump loyalists and establishment moderates. Conservatives argue Blanche's prosecutorial background and commitment to restoring DOJ independence from progressive activism make him the right choice to clean house after years of what Republicans view as weaponized law enforcement under Biden.

Democrats opposed Blanche, citing concerns about his representation of Trump in legal matters, though their arguments failed to sway enough Republicans to block confirmation (9News Denver).

The confirmation comes as Democratic attorneys general, including New Jersey's, have launched civil rights investigations into ICE detention facilities—a sign that progressive states will continue aggressive legal challenges to Trump administration immigration enforcement (Washington Examiner).
