# Milo Yiannopoulos Deported After ICE Arrest for Visa Overstay

British political commentator Milo Yiannopoulos has been arrested by Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents in New Orleans and deported to the United Kingdom following an overstay of his visa, according to Breitbart. The arrest came after questions swirled about whether activist Laura Loomer had triggered the enforcement action, per the Daily Caller.

According to Department of Homeland Security statements cited by Breitbart, Yiannopoulos may face a permanent bar from re-entering the United States—a consequence typically reserved for serious immigration violations or deportations following criminal conviction.

The case underscores a straightforward principle conservatives generally support: immigration law applies equally, regardless of political prominence or media profile. Visa overstays are violations, and ICE enforcement of existing statute is the government doing its job.

What remains unclear from available reporting is whether the deportation reflects standard enforcement protocols or selective targeting. Conservatives should watch whether DHS applies the same rigor to other high-profile visa violators, or whether this case signals political weaponization of immigration enforcement.
