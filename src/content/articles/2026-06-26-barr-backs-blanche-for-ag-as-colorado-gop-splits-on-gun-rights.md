# Barr Backs Blanche for AG—But Colorado GOP Splits on Anti-Gun State AG

Former Attorney General Bill Barr is publicly urging Senate Republicans to confirm Todd Blanche as the next U.S. Attorney General, arguing in a *Wall Street Journal* op-ed that Trump's acting Justice Department chief is the best choice to lead the department (Washington Examiner).

The endorsement carries weight among conservatives who value strong executive leadership and rule-of-law restoration after years of weaponized DOJ prosecutions under Biden.

However, the push exposes fractures within Colorado's Republican establishment. A GOP attorney facing fire from fellow Republicans for supporting Colorado Attorney General Jay Jones—widely viewed by conservatives as hostile to Second Amendment rights—unleashed a sharp defense of his position, citing the political complexity of state-level alignments (Daily Caller).

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado gun owners and Second Amendment advocates have long criticized AG Jones's office for aggressive prosecution of firearms cases and support for gun-control measures. Republicans backing Jones at the state level while supporting Trump's federal AG pick highlights the tension between party loyalty and principle on constitutional rights—a live issue for Colorado's conservative base.

Conservatives should watch whether Blanche's confirmation faces resistance from Senate Republicans concerned about DOJ independence, and whether Colorado's GOP can reconcile support for a pro-gun federal administration with a state AG many view as anti-Second Amendment.
