# Left-Wing L.A. Candidate Celebrates Paint Job as 'Infrastructure'—A Cautionary Tale for Colorado

A Los Angeles Democratic mayoral candidate just handed conservatives a perfect case study in progressive governance theater.

Nithya Raman, a self-described socialist running for L.A. mayor, drew withering ridicule this week after touting the completion of a crosswalk as a major infrastructure victory (Fox News Politics, The Daily Wire). The before-and-after photos told the real story: yellow paint slapped over cracked, unrepaired pavement. The underlying street damage remained untouched.

The neighborhood council had requested the crosswalk in late 2025. What they got was cosmetic work—a photo op masquerading as infrastructure investment.

Republicans and conservatives seized on the moment as emblematic of left-wing governance: announcing wins for basic services, celebrating minimal action as transformative, and avoiding the hard work of actually fixing problems. The Daily Wire noted the contrast between Raman's triumphalism and the visible decay beneath the paint.

**The Colorado angle:** As progressive politicians in Denver and Boulder push similar "infrastructure" narratives—often meaning bike lanes and transit subsidies over road repair—Coloradans should ask hard questions about what their tax dollars actually fund. Are we getting real maintenance, or just painted curbs and press releases?

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic-led legislature uses this L.A. playbook to justify spending on progressive priorities while core infrastructure crumbles.
