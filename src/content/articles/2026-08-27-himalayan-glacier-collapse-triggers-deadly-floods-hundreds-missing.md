# Catastrophic Floods in Nepal, Tibet Kill Hundreds; Americans Among Missing

A glacial collapse in the warming Himalayan region has triggered devastating floods across Nepal and Tibet, leaving hundreds dead and more than 1,300 missing (9News Denver). Videos show the floodwaters swallowing people and destroying buildings and bridges as the disaster unfolded on the Bhotekoshi River and surrounding areas (Just The News).

Death tolls vary by report—ranging from at least 95 to 157 confirmed dead (Breitbart, Just The News)—as rescue efforts continue in the mountainous terrain. Torrential rains and swollen rivers caused massive landslides, sweeping away entire villages and destroying critical infrastructure including roads and bridges (Breitbart).

American tourists are among the hundreds reported missing in the catastrophe (Just The News). Experts attribute the glacial collapse to warming in the region, underscoring the vulnerability of high-altitude communities to climate-driven environmental shifts (9News Denver).

The disaster highlights the real-world consequences of geological instability in densely populated mountain valleys, where infrastructure remains fragile and rescue operations face severe logistical challenges.
