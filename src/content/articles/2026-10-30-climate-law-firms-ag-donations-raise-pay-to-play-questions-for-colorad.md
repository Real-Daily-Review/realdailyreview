# Colorado Democrats' Climate Litigation Funding Raises Pay-to-Play Red Flags

A climate-focused law firm has dramatically increased donations to Democratic attorneys general ahead of the midterm elections, raising serious questions about whether environmental activism is driving state legal strategy—and Colorado taxpayers should care.

According to the Daily Caller, the firm has substantially boosted financial support to Democratic AGs, fueling conservative concerns about what critics call "biased climate litigation." Republicans argue this funding pattern suggests environmental groups are essentially purchasing favorable legal action against energy companies and industries—turning state attorneys general into de facto environmental enforcers rather than impartial legal officers.

For Colorado, this matters directly. Democratic AG Phil Weiser has pursued aggressive climate-related litigation and regulatory positions. If outside money is influencing state legal priorities, Colorado taxpayers and energy workers could face costly lawsuits and regulations driven by activist donors rather than Colorado's actual interests.

Conservatives contend that attorneys general should represent their states' citizens and economies, not serve as litigation arms for well-funded advocacy groups. The funding surge before elections, they argue, suggests quid pro quo expectations—support now, favorable legal action later.

**Watch whether Colorado Republicans demand transparency from Weiser's office about outside funding influence on litigation strategy and whether energy-sector groups push back publicly against what they see as coordinated legal harassment.**
