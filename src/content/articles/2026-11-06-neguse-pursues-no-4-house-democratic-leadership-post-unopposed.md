# Colorado Democrat Neguse Pursues House Leadership Post, Unopposed

Colorado's Joe Neguse is positioning himself for a major power grab in Washington, running unopposed for House assistant speaker—the No. 4 Democratic leadership slot—and would become the youngest lawmaker ever to hold the position (Colorado Sun, CPR News).

The Boulder-area congressman's ascent reflects Democrats' confidence in his political machinery, but conservatives should note what this means: Neguse would help shape the party's legislative strategy and messaging on everything from spending bills to regulatory overreach. His leadership role would amplify Colorado's influence over national Democratic priorities—including the green-energy mandates and tax-hike proposals that have already reshaped Colorado's economy.

Republicans have not mounted a public challenge to his bid, suggesting either a lack of competitive leverage in the Democratic caucus or strategic focus elsewhere. The post enables Neguse to consolidate power within Democratic ranks and position himself for higher office—a trajectory Colorado voters should watch closely given his voting record on energy regulation, gun control, and federal spending.

Conservatives should monitor whether Neguse's new leadership role translates into aggressive pushes for policies that would harm Colorado's energy sector, water rights, or Second Amendment protections at the federal level.
