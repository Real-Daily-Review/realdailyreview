# Los Angeles News Helicopter Crash Kills 3, Including NBC4 Reporter

A news helicopter operated by NBC4 Los Angeles crashed near Chatsworth on Tuesday evening, killing three people aboard, including veteran aerial reporter Eliana Moreno—known on social media as "Eli in the Heli"—and pilot George Marciniw (9News Denver, New York Post). The aircraft went down shortly after 7 p.m. while covering a separate fatal bus crash in the area.

Experienced helicopter pilot Zoey Tur, reviewing audio from the crash, suggested the aircraft may have experienced engine failure, according to reporting from the New York Post. The exact cause remains under investigation by authorities.

Moreno was a longtime fixture in Los Angeles broadcast news, recognized for her distinctive aerial reporting work. Marciniw was described as a veteran pilot with extensive experience in news operations.

The incident underscores the inherent risks journalists and pilots face while covering breaking news in real time, particularly during complex multi-scene emergency operations. Colorado news outlets regularly deploy similar aviation assets for storm coverage and breaking news—a reminder of the operational hazards newsrooms accept in pursuit of immediate reporting.

Investigators will likely focus on mechanical factors, weather conditions, and operational procedures as the National Transportation Safety Board examines the wreckage.
