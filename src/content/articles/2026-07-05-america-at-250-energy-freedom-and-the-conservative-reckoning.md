# America at 250: Energy Freedom and the Stakes of Self-Government

America's 250th birthday is a moment to reckon with what made this nation exceptional—and what threatens to squander it.

As the Washington Examiner noted in a sweeping reflection on the milestone, America's founding experiment in self-government remains rare in human history. Most such experiments collapse under corruption, division, debt, or citizens forgetting what freedom demands. That warning carries weight as Colorado faces its own tests: energy policy, water rights, and regulatory overreach that pit individual liberty against government control.

Energy dominance exemplifies the point. The Daily Caller highlighted how American energy wealth flows directly from economic freedom—the ability to develop resources, take risks, and profit from innovation. Colorado's oil, gas, and coal industries have powered prosperity for generations. Yet progressive policies increasingly restrict that development in the name of climate ideology, threatening jobs and state revenues while Americans remain dependent on hostile foreign regimes for energy.

The Washington Examiner framed America at 250 as a "ledger" of blood, sweat, faith, and generations choosing "the harder right over the easier wrong." That's the conservative case: freedom requires vigilance. It demands citizens and leaders who resist the rot of dependency, debt, and the slow surrender of liberty to bureaucratic control.

Watch whether Colorado's next legislature doubles down on energy restrictions or recognizes that American prosperity—and independence—still depends on the freedom to develop our own resources.
