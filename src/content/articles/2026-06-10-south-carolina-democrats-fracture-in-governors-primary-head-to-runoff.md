# South Carolina Democrats Head to Runoff as Party Splinters Over 2026 Governor's Race

South Carolina Democrats are headed to a June 23 runoff after failing to coalesce around a single candidate in the gubernatorial primary to replace term-limited GOP Gov. Henry McMaster, according to Fox News Politics. The fractured primary underscores deepening divisions within the state party as it seeks to reclaim the governor's mansion in a reliably Republican state.

An ex-pro basketball player emerged from the crowded field to advance to the runoff, signaling the Democratic establishment's struggle to unite behind a coherent message or candidate. Meanwhile, a left-wing billionaire self-funder reportedly spent $200 million of his own money in a failed bid to become governor, only to lose to a Fox News host in what the Daily Caller characterized as a stunning rebuke of progressive money and influence.

The Democratic infighting reflects broader national tensions between the party's establishment wing and its activist base—a dynamic that typically favors Republicans in general elections. South Carolina remains solidly red at the statewide level, and a divided Democratic primary only improves GOP prospects for holding the governorship.
