# Arkansas Governor Leaves Door Open to 2028 White House Run

Arkansas Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders isn't closing the book on a 2028 presidential bid, though she acknowledged her father—former presidential candidate Mike Huckabee—might have reservations about the grueling nature of a national campaign.

"I don't know, given the fact that he knows what that means, I'm not sure he would wish that on me," Sanders told CNN's Dana Bash on Sunday (Washington Examiner). The comment reflects the personal toll a presidential run exacts, even as Sanders, who served as White House Press Secretary during Trump's first term, keeps her options open.

Sanders has built a conservative record as Arkansas governor, but any national ambitions would face the same headwinds confronting all Republican candidates: a crowded primary field, media scrutiny, and the question of whether Trump himself runs again. For Colorado conservatives watching the 2028 landscape, Sanders represents the establishment Republican wing—competent, loyal to Trump during his presidency, but without the outsider appeal that energized the base in 2016 and 2020.

Her measured response on a presidential run suggests she's testing the waters without committing, a familiar posture for potential candidates. Watch whether Sanders builds a national donor network or endorsement infrastructure over the next two years—the real indicator of serious presidential intent.
