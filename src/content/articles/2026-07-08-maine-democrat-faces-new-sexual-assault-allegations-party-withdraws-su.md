# Maine Democrat's Sexual Assault Allegations Mount as Party Distances Itself

A Democratic Maine Senate candidate is facing mounting allegations of sexual misconduct that have prompted party leaders and institutions to withdraw support. Graham Platner's ex-girlfriend Lyndsey Fifield alleged Tuesday that he repeatedly removed condoms without her consent during their 2013–2015 relationship in Washington, D.C., according to reporting by *The Washington Post* (via *The Daily Wire*). This follows a bombshell Monday report alleging Platner sexually assaulted a different ex-partner.

The allegations have triggered swift Democratic damage control. Party officials and allied institutions have rescinded endorsements and support, signaling they view the claims as serious enough to abandon their candidate mid-cycle—a rare move that underscores the severity of the accusations.

Republicans have seized on the story as evidence that Democrats' rhetoric on sexual misconduct rings hollow when their own nominees face credible allegations. The contrast between progressive institutional posturing on #MeToo issues and actual party behavior when a Democratic candidate is implicated has not gone unnoticed on the right.

Fifield has also previously accused Platner of physical abuse, adding weight to the pattern of allegations now public.

**Watch for:** Whether Maine Democrats formally replace Platner on the ballot or attempt to salvage his candidacy despite the mounting evidence.
