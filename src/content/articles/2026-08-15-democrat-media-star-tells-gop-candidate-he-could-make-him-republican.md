# Florida Republican Donalds Draws Praise from Unlikely Ally as GOP Primary Looms

A lifelong Democrat and prominent media personality just handed a significant endorsement to a Republican candidate—a rare crack in partisan walls that signals growing frustration with the left's direction.

Sports commentator Stephen A. Smith told Byron Donalds, a Florida congressman seeking the GOP gubernatorial nomination, that he could actually convert him to the Republican Party. "He's the real deal. Make no mistake about it. I've seen him battling with Democrats," Smith said, according to the New York Post.

Donalds, who is competing in next week's Republican primary against Lt. Gov. Jay Collins, former Speaker Paul Renner, and outsider James Fishback, has built a reputation as a principled conservative willing to challenge Democratic orthodoxy. Smith's comments—reported by both Just The News and the New York Post—underscore how some Democrats and left-leaning figures are openly questioning their party's trajectory.

The endorsement matters because it reflects a broader phenomenon: working-class and middle-class Americans, including those in media, are increasingly skeptical of progressive policies on spending, crime, and cultural issues. Smith's willingness to publicly praise a Republican suggests the Democratic coalition is fracturing at the edges.

**Watch whether Donalds uses this momentum to consolidate support among Florida's business community and working voters ahead of the primary.**
