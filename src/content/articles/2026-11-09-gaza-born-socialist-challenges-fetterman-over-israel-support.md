# Pennsylvania Democrat Faces Primary Challenge From Anti-Israel Socialist

Sen. John Fetterman's support for Israel is drawing a primary challenger from the Democratic Socialists of America—a sign of how sharply the party's base has shifted left on Middle East policy.

Ahmed Moor, a Gaza-born writer and businessman, announced his 2028 primary bid against Fetterman (D-PA), one of the Senate's most vocal Israel defenders. According to *The Daily Wire*, Moor has previously urged anti-Zionist Israelis to sabotage military vehicles, underscoring the ideological gulf between the incumbent and his challenger.

The race reflects a broader Democratic Party fracture. Fetterman, who has consistently backed Israeli security interests, now faces pressure from the party's socialist wing—a constituency that has grown louder on foreign policy since October 2023. Moor's candidacy signals that mainstream Democratic support for Israel is increasingly costly in primary contests.

**The Colorado angle:** While this is a Pennsylvania race, it mirrors tensions within Colorado's own Democratic Party. State progressives have pushed similar anti-Israel rhetoric, and the Fetterman challenge shows how primary voters in blue states are rewarding candidates who break with traditional Democratic foreign policy.

Conservatives should watch whether Fetterman holds his seat or whether the Democratic socialist insurgency gains ground—a test of whether the party's base has fundamentally realigned on America's Middle East alliances.
