# Aurora Police Search for Suspect in Possible Kidnapping

Aurora police are hunting a suspect after a woman was apparently forced into a silver BMW on East Colfax Avenue Tuesday afternoon, marking another serious crime in the metro Denver area.

According to 9News Denver, the incident occurred around 12:30 p.m. near 9898 East Colfax Avenue when witnesses reported seeing a man force a woman into the vehicle. KDVR Fox31 reports the suspect vehicle fled from Aurora police, who are now actively searching for the BMW and the suspect.

Details remain limited as the investigation continues. Police have not yet released suspect descriptions or confirmed the woman's condition or whereabouts.
