# Detroit Strip Club Shooting Kills 3; Denver Sees Own Violence Spike

A deadly shooting at a Detroit strip club early Sunday left three dead and four wounded—yet another reminder that gun violence tied to criminal activity and lawlessness continues unchecked in American cities, even as progressives push gun control that targets law-abiding citizens instead of criminals.

The Detroit incident, which erupted from a fight at an after-hours location at 7:21 a.m., claimed victims in their 20s and 30s, according to Detroit Police Chief Todd Bettison (Washington Examiner). Meanwhile, Denver's own Five Points neighborhood saw two people hospitalized Friday morning in a separate shooting (KDVR Fox31).

**The conservative argument:** While Democrats in Colorado and nationwide demand stricter gun laws affecting lawful gun owners, actual crime—gang violence, drug-related shootings, and criminal assaults—persists in cities with the strictest regulations. Chicago, Detroit, and Denver's crime-plagued neighborhoods prove that disarming honest citizens doesn't stop criminals who obtain weapons illegally. Republicans argue resources should target criminal prosecution, gang enforcement, and bail reform to keep dangerous offenders off streets—not penalize Second Amendment rights.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether Denver leadership addresses root causes—gang activity, drug trafficking, inadequate prosecution—or simply doubles down on gun-control theater that ignores the real problem: criminal behavior.
