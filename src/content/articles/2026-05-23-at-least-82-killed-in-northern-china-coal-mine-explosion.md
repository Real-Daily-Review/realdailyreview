# Chinese Coal Mine Explosion Kills Dozens, Rescue Efforts Underway

An explosion at a coal mine in northern China killed at least 82 people, according to state media reports cited by the BBC. The blast occurred Friday at 19:29 local time. Deutsche Welle reported at least eight confirmed dead with dozens trapped, suggesting casualty figures may still be rising as rescue operations continue. Chinese authorities have not yet released an official statement on the cause of the explosion or the total number of workers present at the time of the incident. Coal mining accidents remain a persistent safety concern in China despite regulatory efforts in recent years. Watch for updated casualty counts and official investigation findings from Chinese authorities in coming days.
