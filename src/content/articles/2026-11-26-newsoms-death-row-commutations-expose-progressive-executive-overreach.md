# Newsom's Death Row Commutations Expose California's Moral Inconsistency

California Gov. Gavin Newsom just commuted the death sentences of all 548 inmates on the state's death row—a sweeping act of executive power that reveals the hypocrisy at the heart of progressive governance.

Newsom has positioned himself as Trump's moral opposite, yet the *Washington Examiner* notes he's wielding the same principle-free executive authority he condemns in the president. By unilaterally overturning capital sentences, Newsom bypassed the democratic process and ignored the will of California voters who have repeatedly supported capital punishment at the ballot box.

Conservatives argue this exemplifies how progressive leaders use government power selectively: they denounce executive overreach when Republicans do it, then deploy identical tactics when it serves their ideological agenda. The commutations also underscore a broader pattern: California Democrats impose their values on citizens through judicial and executive fiat rather than persuasion or legislation.

For Colorado, this matters. As California's progressive policies migrate eastward through Democratic networks, Coloradans should expect similar end-runs around voters' preferences on crime, energy, and gun rights. The Centennial State's Democratic leadership has already shown willingness to ignore voter-approved measures and use regulatory power to advance its agenda.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats cite Newsom's commutations as a model for criminal justice policy—and whether they'll respect voter intent or follow California's pattern of executive overreach.**
