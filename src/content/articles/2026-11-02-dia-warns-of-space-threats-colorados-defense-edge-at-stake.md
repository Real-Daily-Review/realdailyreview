# DIA Report: Space Threats Rising, U.S. Must Strengthen Space Force

Colorado's aerospace and defense sector depends on American military superiority—and a new Defense Intelligence Agency report warns that U.S. interests in space face rapidly growing threats.

The DIA assessment, released last week, underscores the Space Force's mounting strategic importance to national defense. As the War Department's intelligence service, the DIA's analysis carries weight: threats to American space assets are no longer theoretical. They're operational reality.

For Colorado, this matters directly. The state hosts major defense contractors, military installations, and aerospace firms whose prosperity hinges on U.S. technological edge and defense spending. A weakened space posture threatens jobs and economic security across the Front Range and eastern plains.

Conservatives argue robust investment in Space Force capabilities is non-negotiable—not wasteful spending, but essential deterrence. China and Russia have weaponized space strategy; America cannot afford to lag. The alternative is ceding high-ground advantage to adversaries who don't hesitate to exploit it.

Colorado taxpayers and defense workers should watch whether Congress prioritizes Space Force modernization in upcoming defense bills, or whether budget-cutting impulses undermine readiness.
