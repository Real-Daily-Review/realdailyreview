# Chicago Sky Bans Enes Kanter Freedom Over Gender Ideology Dispute

The Chicago Sky has banned former NBA player Enes Kanter Freedom from attending home games, citing him as a "potential threat" after he was ejected Sunday for a confrontation with guard Natasha Cloud (The Daily Wire). Sky owner Michael Alter announced the ban Tuesday, barring Freedom from Wintrust Arena.

According to conservative outlets covering the incident, the clash centered on Freedom's public statements defining biological sex—a position that drew verbal confrontation from Cloud. The Washington Examiner's account suggests Cloud "verbally assaulted" Freedom over his comments, though mainstream reporting has characterized the exchange differently.

**The conservative argument:** Freedom faced ejection and now a ban for expressing a viewpoint on sex and gender that contradicts progressive orthodoxy. Republicans and free-speech advocates argue this demonstrates how corporations enforce ideological conformity under the guise of "safety," silencing dissent on contested social issues. The "potential threat" language, critics contend, conflates speech with danger to justify censorship.

**Democrats and WNBA supporters counter** that Freedom's remarks were disruptive and disrespectful to players and fans, warranting removal from the venue.

For Colorado conservatives, this case exemplifies how woke corporate power polices speech on gender and biology—issues increasingly central to state politics, education policy, and athletic eligibility debates.
