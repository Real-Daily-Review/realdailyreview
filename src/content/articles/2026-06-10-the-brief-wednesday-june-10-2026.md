## House Republicans Pass Immigration Enforcement Funding — On Party Lines

House Republicans pushed through an immigration enforcement funding bill Tuesday without a single Democratic vote. The left's refusal to fund border and interior enforcement isn't a policy disagreement — it's a statement of priorities. For Colorado, where communities from Aurora to Greeley have felt the strain of unchecked illegal immigration firsthand, this vote matters. Republicans argue the bill keeps Americans safe; Democrats apparently disagree. Colorado's congressional delegation split was predictable — and voters should remember it.

## I-70 Shut Down Near Glenwood Springs as Paradise Fire Burns

The Paradise Fire has scorched roughly 20 acres near Glenwood Springs, forcing closure of I-70 — the economic and logistical spine of Colorado. That stretch of highway is a lifeline for mountain communities, ski resorts, freight, and energy workers moving through the Western Slope. Every hour it stays closed costs Colorado businesses real money. State and local emergency managers have their hands full, and Coloradans who depend on that corridor should monitor conditions closely before hitting the road Wednesday.

## Trump-Backed Steve Hilton Advances to California Governor's Race

California's November governor's race is set: Trump-backed Republican Steve Hilton versus Democrat Javier Becerra, who succeeds Gavin Newsom. Hilton's advance is a signal that the post-Newsom California left isn't a foregone conclusion — and that national conservative energy is pushing into blue territory. For Colorado conservatives watching their own state drift leftward under Democrat-dominated Denver politics, a competitive California race is a reminder that no state is permanently lost when the right candidate carries the right message.

## SPLC Doubles Down on Labeling Turning Point USA a "Hate Group"

Turning Point USA has a significant presence on Colorado college campuses. Students and donors should understand exactly what the SPLC is — and what it isn't.
