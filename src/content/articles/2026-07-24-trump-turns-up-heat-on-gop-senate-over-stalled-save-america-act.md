# Trump Pressures GOP Senate on SAVE America Act as Holdouts Resist

President Trump is turning up the heat on Senate Republicans to pass the SAVE America Act, with White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt warning Thursday that his patience is "running out" on the stalled legislation (Daily Caller). Trump told reporters he's "willing" to personally call holdout senators to secure votes, signaling direct White House intervention in the whip operation (Washington Examiner).

Senate Majority Leader John Thune has faced criticism from Trump allies over the bill's slow progress. The tension reflects a broader GOP divide over the legislation's scope and priorities, though sources did not detail the specific provisions at issue or Colorado's stake in the measure.

Republicans backing the bill argue it's essential to Trump's agenda and party unity heading into the election cycle. Skeptical GOP senators have not publicly outlined their objections in these reports, leaving their concerns unclear.

For Colorado Republicans and taxpayers, the key question is whether SAVE America includes provisions affecting energy policy, water rights, or federal land management—areas where Colorado's economy and conservative base have direct interest.
