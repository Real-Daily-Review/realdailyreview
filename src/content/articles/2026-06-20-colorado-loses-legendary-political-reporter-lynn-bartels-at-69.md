# Colorado Loses a Legendary Political Reporter

Lynn Bartels, the tenacious political reporter who spent decades covering Colorado statehouse intrigue and election cycles at the Rocky Mountain News and Denver Post, has died at 69. Bartels earned respect across the political spectrum—a rarity in modern journalism—for her relentless shoe-leather reporting, deep source networks, and ability to break stories that moved Colorado politics (CPR News, 9News Denver). The Colorado Sun called her the "Grand Dame" of Colorado political reporting, noting her infectious personality matched her grit.

In an era when local political coverage has contracted sharply, Bartels represented an older breed of journalist: one who showed up, asked hard questions, and held power accountable regardless of party. Republicans and Democrats alike valued her fairness and accuracy, even when her reporting stung. That institutional knowledge—who's really running what, where the bodies are buried, how deals actually get made in Denver—is increasingly rare as newsrooms shrink and institutional memory walks out the door.

Colorado's political class loses a crucial check on power at a moment when government spending, regulatory overreach, and legislative maneuvering demand exactly the kind of dogged scrutiny Bartels exemplified.
