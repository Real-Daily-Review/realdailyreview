# Trump Administration Ends Biden's Costly Medicare Subsidy—Taxpayers Cheer

The Trump administration is terminating a Biden-era subsidy program that funneled billions in taxpayer dollars to insurance companies under the guise of lowering Medicare Part D prescription costs, ending the handout in 2027.

Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Administrator Mehmet Oz framed the decision bluntly: "The Biden admin gave BILLIONS of taxpayer money DIRECTLY to Big Insurance Companies. This is unacceptable. We are stabilizing the market so this bailout is no longer needed," according to *Just The News*.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans argue the subsidy was corporate welfare dressed up as healthcare reform. Rather than letting market competition drive down drug prices, the Biden administration simply wrote checks to insurers—a classic example of government picking winners and losers while Colorado seniors and taxpayers footed the bill. Ending the program forces the market to function as intended.

**Democrats' Counter:** The left will likely argue the subsidy kept premiums affordable for seniors and that killing it will raise out-of-pocket costs for Medicare beneficiaries—a talking point they'll deploy heading into 2026 midterms.

For Colorado seniors on Medicare Part D and taxpayers tired of corporate bailouts, this reversal offers relief. Watch whether the Trump administration's market-based approach actually reduces prescription costs or simply shifts burden back to beneficiaries.
