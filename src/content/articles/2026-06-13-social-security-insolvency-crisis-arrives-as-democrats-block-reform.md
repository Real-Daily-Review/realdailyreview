# Social Security Hits Insolvency Crisis as Democrats' Decades of Scaremongering Block Reform

Social Security has officially entered insolvency, and Colorado taxpayers and retirees will pay the price for decades of political cowardice on both sides of the aisle.

According to the latest Social Security trustees report, the program's trust fund depletion is no longer a distant threat—it's here. The *Washington Examiner* notes that politicians have dodged meaningful reform for years precisely because bankruptcy seemed safely over the horizon. That excuse is gone.

Democrats bear particular responsibility. An Obama administration alumnus, writing in *National Review*, laments how effectively Democrats weaponized Social Security in campaign messaging, using demagoguery to block any serious entitlement reform. For decades, the left has attacked Republicans proposing sensible adjustments as "cutting benefits," poisoning the political well and leaving the system to collapse under its own weight.

The result: Colorado's workers and seniors face either massive tax increases, benefit cuts, or both—the very outcomes Democrats claimed to prevent through their obstruction.

Republicans have long argued that modest, proactive reforms—raising the retirement age gradually, means-testing benefits for wealthy retirees, or adjusting the payroll tax cap—could have fixed this painlessly. Instead, inaction guarantees a crisis.

Watch whether Congress finally acts on real reform or doubles down on the same political theater that created this mess.
