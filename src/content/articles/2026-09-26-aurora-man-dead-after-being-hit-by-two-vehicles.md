# Aurora Pedestrian Struck by Two Vehicles; Investigation Underway

A 35-year-old man is dead after being hit by two separate vehicles Friday night in Aurora, according to police reports from 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31. Both drivers remained at the scene and are cooperating with investigators, authorities said.
