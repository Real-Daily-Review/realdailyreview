# Former NFL Star Chris Johnson Diagnosed with ALS at 39

Former Tennessee Titans running back Chris Johnson announced Monday that he has been diagnosed with sporadic Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) at age 39, marking a sobering health crisis for the ex-NFL standout who also played for the Arizona Cardinals and New York Jets.

Johnson revealed the diagnosis during an appearance on *Good Morning America*, emphasizing that "I am still me" despite the progressive neurological disease already affecting his voice. According to 9News Denver, Johnson said he was sharing his diagnosis publicly because if it helps "even one person" get diagnosed sooner or gives another family hope, "then it's worth it."

ALS, a degenerative disease affecting nerve cells that control voluntary muscle movement, has no cure. Johnson's decision to go public with his diagnosis underscores the disease's devastating impact on former athletes and the broader population—a reminder that serious health challenges don't discriminate by income or athletic achievement.

The former running back's openness may help raise awareness about ALS symptoms and encourage earlier diagnosis in other patients facing similar struggles.

Conservatives and Colorado residents should monitor how private charitable organizations and medical research—rather than government-dependent programs—respond to support ALS patients and accelerate treatment breakthroughs.
