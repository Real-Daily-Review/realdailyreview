# Makar's Record Deal Signals Colorado's Sports Market Strength

Colorado's Cale Makar just inked the richest contract in NHL history—eight years, $163.2 million with the Avalanche—a vote of confidence in both the star defenseman and Denver's ability to retain elite talent in a competitive market.

The deal underscores what Colorado businesses have long known: the state's quality of life, growing economy, and business-friendly environment attract top performers willing to stay put. Unlike higher-tax states hemorrhaging talent and revenue, Colorado has managed to keep its marquee franchise competitive while maintaining reasonable tax rates that don't drive away wealth.

For the Avalanche organization, the contract reflects Makar's elite status—he's worth every penny as one of hockey's most dominant defensemen. The franchise's willingness to invest heavily signals confidence in the team's direction and Denver's market viability.

The broader takeaway: when government stays out of the way and doesn't overtax businesses and high earners, Colorado thrives. The Avalanche's ability to compete for and retain generational talent proves the state remains an attractive destination for excellence—whether in sports, tech, or energy.
