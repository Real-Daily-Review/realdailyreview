# Spencer Pratt's LA Mayor Bid Tests Online Campaign Strategy

Former reality TV star Spencer Pratt is mounting a Los Angeles mayoral campaign built on social media saturation, edgy humor, and AI-generated content aimed at younger, digitally native voters, according to NPR. Political experts view his approach as a potential template for future online campaigning.

Pratt's strategy centers on flooding social platforms with combative rhetoric and what NPR describes as "AI slop"—a tactic designed to capture attention in crowded digital spaces. The Washington Examiner notes parallels to Donald Trump's 2016 upset victory, suggesting Pratt may be banking on a "silent vote" of supporters who either keep their backing private or remain underestimated by traditional polling.

The campaign reflects a broader shift in how candidates engage voters outside traditional media. Pratt's reliance on internet culture and meme-adjacent messaging targets a demographic often overlooked by conventional political analysis.

Political insiders remain divided on whether viral momentum translates to actual electoral success in a major city race. Watch whether Pratt's online engagement converts to measurable polling gains as the election approaches.
