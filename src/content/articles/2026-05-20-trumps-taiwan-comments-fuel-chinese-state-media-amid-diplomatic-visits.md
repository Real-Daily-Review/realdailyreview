# Trump's Taiwan Comments Create Opening for China Amid Diplomatic Visits

Donald Trump's recent remarks about Taiwan arms sales being a "negotiating chip" with Beijing have given Chinese state media ammunition to exploit, even as the U.S. president met separately with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin this week, according to The Guardian.

During Trump's Beijing visit Thursday, Xi issued a strongly worded statement reaffirming China's claims over Taiwan, which Beijing considers part of its territory despite never having ruled it. While initial assessments suggested the meeting went relatively well for Taiwan's government, Trump's characterization of weapons deals as leverage has unsettled Taipei's leadership.

The contrast between Trump's meetings with Xi and Putin—which occurred days apart—was stark in tone and content, according to CNBC. Taiwan featured prominently in discussions with Trump but was notably absent from Xi's separate talks with Putin, underscoring differing priorities among the three powers.

The Guardian reports that Chinese state media has seized on Trump's shifting remarks to advance Beijing's narrative. Taiwan's government faces uncertainty over whether the U.S. will maintain its longstanding support for the island under Trump's unpredictable approach to cross-strait relations.

Watch for Trump's next statements on Taiwan policy and any formal moves regarding U.S. military aid to the island.
