# Trump-Backed Hilton Advances to California Governor Race as State's Ballot Counting Drags On

California's chaotic election system is on full display: a week after the June 2 primary, officials are still counting ballots in the nation's most populous state, with Trump-backed Republican Steve Hilton now advancing to face Democrat Xavier Becerra, former Biden HHS secretary, in November's gubernatorial general election.

Hilton, a conservative commentator endorsed by President Trump, secured roughly 25 percent of the primary vote. Becerra placed first. National Review has criticized California's election infrastructure as fundamentally broken, pointing to systemic defects evident in concurrent races like Los Angeles's mayoral contest (National Review).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue California's slow ballot-counting process—stretching weeks after Election Day—undermines confidence in results and invites questions about election integrity. The state's open primary system and mail-in voting apparatus have become poster children for progressive governance failures.

**Colorado angle:** While Colorado's mail-in voting system faces similar scrutiny from conservatives, the state counts ballots far more efficiently than California. Coloradans should watch whether Hilton's Trump endorsement and anti-establishment message resonates in deep-blue California—a potential model for how conservative messaging plays in hostile terrain.

Watch whether Becerra's Biden-era baggage becomes a liability as inflation and border policy remain top voter concerns heading into November.
