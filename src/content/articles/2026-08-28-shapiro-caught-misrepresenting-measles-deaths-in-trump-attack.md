# Pennsylvania Governor Caught Misrepresenting Measles Data to Attack Trump

Gov. Josh Shapiro (D-PA) faces credibility questions after allegedly mischaracterizing measles deaths to score political points against the Trump administration, according to conservative outlets Washington Examiner and Breitbart. Both sources report that coroner information contradicts Shapiro's public claims about measles fatalities in Pennsylvania.

The controversy centers on Shapiro's use of health data in what critics characterize as a partisan attack. Washington Examiner argues the governor "struggles to tell the truth" on the issue, while Breitbart reports that coroner records undermine the governor's stated position, suggesting coordinated misrepresentation with sympathetic media outlets.

**The conservative argument:** When Democratic leaders weaponize public health crises for political gain—especially by distorting facts—they erode public trust in legitimate health guidance. Shapiro's alleged misstatement raises questions about whether progressive governors will prioritize accuracy or partisan narrative when communicating with citizens on serious matters.

**What this means for Colorado:** While this story centers on Pennsylvania, it reflects a broader pattern conservatives warn about: Democratic officials using health emergencies as political cudgels. Colorado voters should scrutinize how state health officials under Democratic leadership communicate measles and other public health data—and whether accuracy takes a backseat to partisan positioning.

Watch whether Pennsylvania media outlets demand accountability or let the governor's claims stand unchallenged.
