# Former DU Player Returns to Court After Fatal DUI; Colorado Faces Questions on Accountability

A former University of Denver basketball player is back in college basketball three years after causing a fatal DUI crash that killed Uber driver Kathy Rothman, 42, and seriously injured her passenger in 2023, according to 9News Denver.

Coban Porter's return raises hard questions about consequences and accountability in Colorado's justice system. Rothman was working to support her family when Porter's decision to drive impaired ended her life. Her passenger survived but carries permanent injuries—a reminder that DUI isn't a victimless mistake.

Colorado conservatives have long argued that the state's criminal justice system needs stronger accountability measures and consistent enforcement. While Porter appears to have served his legal obligation, the case underscores why tough DUI laws matter and why Colorado must maintain zero tolerance for impaired driving—especially when it claims lives.

The incident also highlights the broader issue of personal responsibility: athletes and public figures must face real consequences for reckless decisions that destroy families. Rothman's death wasn't an accident; it was preventable.
