# DeGette's Seat in Play as Kiros Surges in Colorado's 1st District Primary

Diana DeGette's 30-year grip on Colorado's 1st Congressional District is cracking—and Democratic insiders are panicking enough to dump nearly half a million dollars into TV ads to save her.

Melat Kiros, a Democratic challenger tapping into anti-incumbent sentiment among party voters, has weaponized social media savvy to mount a credible primary threat that has DeGette's camp "incredibly nervous," according to the *Colorado Sun*. The *Denver Post* reports that pro-choice super PAC Pro-Choice Majority Action alone spent nearly $460,000 on TV ads backing DeGette in the primary's final weeks—a sign of how vulnerable the longtime congresswoman has become.

The race reflects broader Democratic frustration with entrenched incumbents, even in safe blue districts. Kiros has capitalized on that discontent while leveraging digital organizing tools that traditional politicians often underestimate.

For Colorado conservatives, this primary battle is a reminder that even heavily Democratic seats aren't immune to voter fatigue. While both candidates lean left, the infighting and massive outside spending underscore how fractured the Democratic base has become—and how grassroots energy, not establishment money, increasingly drives primary outcomes.

Watch whether Kiros's momentum carries into Election Day and what that means for Democratic turnout in November's general election.
