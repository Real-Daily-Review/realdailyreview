# 9/11 Families Demand Mamdani Stay Away From Memorial

Over 101,000 people have signed a petition urging New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani to skip Friday's 25th anniversary 9/11 memorial ceremony—a stunning rebuke that underscores growing concerns about his fitness for office.

The petition, delivered to City Hall, reflects deep unease among 9/11 victims' families about Mamdani's presence at the solemn observance. Former Mayor Rudy Giuliani, who led the city through the attacks, has joined the chorus of former officials calling on Mamdani to stay away from the event (Just The News).

While sources do not detail Mamdani's specific record or statements that prompted the petition, the scale of the signature drive—over 100,000 strong—signals that New Yorkers and families directly affected by the terror attacks question whether he commands the moral authority the moment demands. The petition itself is a form of democratic pressure: citizens making clear their expectations for leadership during a moment of national remembrance.

This controversy raises a broader question about what qualifies someone to lead a major American city, particularly one forever marked by terrorism.
