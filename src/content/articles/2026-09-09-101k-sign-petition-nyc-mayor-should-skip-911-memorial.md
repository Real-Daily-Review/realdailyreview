# 9/11 Families Demand NYC Mayor Skip Memorial Over Anti-American Record

Over 101,000 families and New Yorkers have signed a petition demanding Mayor Zohran Mamdani stay away from Friday's 25th anniversary 9/11 memorial service, citing his documented hostility toward America's response to the terror attacks (Fox News Politics). Former Mayor Rudy Giuliani, who led the city through the September 11 attacks, joined the chorus of officials calling on Mamdani to absent himself from the ceremony (Just The News).

The petition reflects deep concern among 9/11 families that Mamdani's presence would dishonor victims and first responders. Conservatives argue that elected officials who have opposed America's national security measures and counterterrorism efforts—particularly those who have sympathized with anti-American narratives—have no place at solemn commemorations of the worst terror attack on U.S. soil.

This controversy underscores a broader tension: whether progressive politicians who have criticized American military and intelligence responses to 9/11 can credibly represent communities scarred by that day. The families' message is unambiguous—some wounds run too deep for symbolic unity.
