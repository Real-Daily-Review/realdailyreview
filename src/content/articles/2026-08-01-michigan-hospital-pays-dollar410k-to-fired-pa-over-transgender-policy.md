# Michigan Hospital Settles With Fired PA Over Transgender Policy Clash

A Michigan hospital system has agreed to pay $410,000 to settle a lawsuit filed by physician assistant Valerie Kloosterman, who was terminated over her refusal to comply with the facility's transgender medical policies, according to the New York Post.

Kloosterman sued University of Michigan Health West (UMHW) in October 2022, represented by First Liberty Institute. The organization argued the hospital made no effort to accommodate her religious and conscience-based objections to the policies. The settlement marks a rare legal victory for healthcare workers challenging institutional mandates on gender-affirming care.

The case underscores a growing tension in American medicine: the collision between institutional DEI protocols and employee conscience protections. Conservatives argue that hospitals increasingly prioritize ideological conformity over professional accommodation, forcing workers to choose between their beliefs and their livelihoods. The settlement suggests at least one court recognized merit in that claim.

For Colorado healthcare workers facing similar pressures—particularly in Denver's progressive medical establishment—the precedent matters. It signals that conscience-based defenses can succeed, even against well-funded institutional defendants.
