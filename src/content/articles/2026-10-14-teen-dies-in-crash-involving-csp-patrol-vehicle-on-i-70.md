# Teen Dies in Crash Involving Colorado State Patrol Vehicle on I-70

A 19-year-old died Wednesday morning in a crash involving a Colorado State Patrol trooper's vehicle on Interstate 70 east of Denver, according to 9News and KDVR Fox31. The trooper was responding to an earlier semi-truck crash when the collision occurred.

Colorado State Patrol is investigating the incident, as is standard protocol in officer-involved traffic fatalities. Details about the sequence of events, visibility conditions, and speed remain under review.

This tragedy underscores the inherent risks law enforcement faces during highway response operations—troopers routinely position vehicles in active traffic zones to assist stranded motorists and manage accident scenes. The investigation will determine whether proper safety protocols were followed and whether any mechanical, environmental, or human factors contributed to the fatal outcome.

Coloradans should monitor CSP's findings and any recommendations for improved roadside safety procedures during emergency response operations.
