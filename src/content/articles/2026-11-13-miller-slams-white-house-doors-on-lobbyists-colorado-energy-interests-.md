# Stephen Miller Tightens White House Doors to Lobbyists—Colorado Energy Interests Take Notice

Stephen Miller is making life uncomfortable for Washington's influence peddlers by restricting lobbyist access to Trump White House staff, a sharp reversal from predecessor Susie Wiles' open-door approach (Washington Examiner).

Miller, now chief of staff, has implemented internal restrictions on meetings between White House staffers and lobbyists, according to the Daily Caller. The memo-driven crackdown signals Miller "operates based on policy outcomes" rather than relationship management—a potential headache for special interests seeking favorable regulatory treatment.

**The Colorado angle:** Energy companies, water interests, and agricultural groups that traditionally lobby the White House on federal land policy, water rights, and energy development may face tougher sledding under Miller's gatekeeping. Colorado's oil, gas, and mining sectors—already battling Biden-era regulations—will need to make their case on substance rather than access.

Conservatives argue this approach cuts through the revolving door between government and K Street, potentially reducing regulatory capture and crony capitalism. Lobbyists, predictably, are unhappy.
