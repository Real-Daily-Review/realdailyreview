# DOJ Probes Brooklyn Coffee Shop for Discriminatory Post Against Pro-Israel Congressman

A New York City coffee shop's hostile social media attack on a pro-Israel congressman has triggered a Department of Justice investigation—raising questions about selective enforcement and the limits of political speech in America.

Poetica Coffee in Brooklyn posted on Instagram that it would have refused service to Rep. Dan Goldman, calling him a "genocide enabler" after he visited the shop. The post mocked him with crude language about "genocide juice," according to *Just The News*.

The DOJ's involvement signals federal concern over potential discrimination based on political viewpoint. The coffee shop's owner, Parviz Mukhamadkulov, has a documented history of inflammatory anti-Israel rhetoric online, comparing the Jewish state to Nazi Germany and accusing it of genocide, the *New York Post* reports.

**The conservative concern:** This case exposes the left's weaponization of "woke" politics against political opponents while raising uncomfortable questions about DOJ priorities. If a conservative business owner posted similarly hostile content about a progressive politician, would federal investigators move this quickly? Conservatives argue the investigation, while justified on discrimination grounds, highlights the broader intolerance of progressive activists who believe their moral certainty justifies denying service and dignity to those who disagree.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether the DOJ applies equal scrutiny to discriminatory conduct regardless of the perpetrator's politics—or whether selective prosecution becomes the new normal.
