# Trump, Zelensky Align on Ukraine Aid—But Colorado Conservatives Should Demand Accountability

President Trump and Ukrainian President Zelensky are warming to each other, with the U.S. leader impressed by Ukraine's battlefield performance, according to *National Review*. During a White House meeting, Zelensky pressed Trump for additional missiles to counter Russia's sustained attacks, *Just The News* reports. Trump has previously signaled openness to allowing Ukraine to manufacture Patriot missiles domestically, though Zelensky emphasized the need for immediate shipments.

The shift marks a notable pivot from Trump's earlier skepticism about Ukraine aid. *Washington Examiner* notes the relationship solidified partly through late Sen. Lindsey Graham's diplomatic efforts before his death—Graham had been negotiating a Russia sanctions package with the White House during his final days.

**The conservative case:** Republicans have long questioned open-ended Ukraine spending without clear metrics for victory or accountability. While supporting Ukraine's defense against Russian aggression, conservatives argue Congress must scrutinize every dollar committed and demand transparent reporting on how aid is used. Colorado taxpayers footing the bill deserve answers on whether this assistance advances genuine American interests or becomes another blank check.

Watch whether Trump's warming relationship with Zelensky translates to congressional demands for strict oversight and defined end-goals before additional appropriations pass.
