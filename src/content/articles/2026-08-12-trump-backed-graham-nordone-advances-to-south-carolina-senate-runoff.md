# South Carolina GOP Senate Race Heads to Trump-Backed Runoff

South Carolina Republicans will decide between Trump's pick and an establishment alternative in a high-stakes August runoff for Lindsey Graham's Senate seat.

Sen. Darline Graham Nordone, backed by President Donald Trump, advanced to the August 25 runoff after neither candidate secured a majority in Tuesday's special primary (The Daily Wire). She'll face Rep. Ralph Norman (R-SC), setting up a contest that will define the GOP's direction in the state following the July death of her brother, the late Sen. Lindsey Graham.

Graham Nordone led the crowded field with roughly 30.5% of the vote, according to The Daily Wire, while Norman advanced as the second-place finisher. The race reflects broader Republican tensions: Trump's endorsement proved decisive for Graham Nordone, signaling the former president's continued influence over GOP primary voters even in a state where the late senator held considerable institutional power.

For Colorado conservatives, this South Carolina showdown matters as a bellwether. Trump's endorsement muscle in Republican primaries—demonstrated here—will likely shape Colorado GOP races heading into 2026, particularly in congressional primaries where establishment and populist wings compete.

Watch whether Norman's challenge gains traction in the runoff, or if Trump's endorsement proves insurmountable in a Republican primary electorate.
