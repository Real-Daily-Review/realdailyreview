# Democrats Flip on Trans Sports as GOP Readies Midterm Offensive

Colorado voters should pay close attention: Democrats are quietly abandoning their most radical gender positions just as Republicans prepare to weaponize the issue in November.

South Carolina Democratic Senate nominee Annie Andrews now opposes biological males competing in women's sports—a stark reversal from her 2022 stance. Republicans argue this isn't a principled shift; it's panic-driven flip-flopping (Fox News). The move signals Democrats recognize how toxic their transgender agenda has become with mainstream voters, particularly in swing districts.

The timing matters. A new poll from President Biden's own pollster, Tony Fabrizio, surveyed 37 battleground districts and found Democrats handed Republicans a "loaded weapon" heading into the midterms (Washington Examiner). Yet some GOP candidates still haven't aggressively deployed the issue—a strategic mistake conservatives say could cost them winnable races.

For Colorado, this cuts deep. The state has become ground zero for progressive gender policy experiments in schools and sports. Democrats' national retreat suggests even they know the overreach is politically toxic. Conservatives argue the sudden repositioning proves the left's ideology was never about fairness—it was about power.

**Watch whether Colorado Republicans running statewide will actually use this ammunition, or if they'll repeat the mistake of their national counterparts by staying silent.**
