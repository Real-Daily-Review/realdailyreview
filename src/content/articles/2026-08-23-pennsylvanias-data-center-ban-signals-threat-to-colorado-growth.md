# Data Center Backlash Spreads as States Choke Off AI Infrastructure

Colorado could soon face the same anti-growth hysteria sweeping blue states over data centers and AI infrastructure. Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro (D) just signed an executive order restricting data center development, prompting even centrist Sen. John Fetterman (D-PA) to push back, arguing that "AI supremacy and energy dominance underpins our national security" (Washington Examiner). Yet National Review notes the anti-data-center movement persists despite widespread efforts to address public concerns—suggesting ideology, not facts, is driving the resistance.

**The conservative case:** Data centers represent genuine economic opportunity. They create jobs, generate tax revenue, and position America competitively against China. Fetterman's willingness to break ranks shows even moderate Democrats recognize the folly of regulatory obstruction.

**The left's position:** Environmental groups and progressive activists claim data centers consume excessive water and energy, threatening local resources and climate goals.

For Colorado—a state with abundant hydroelectric power, reasonable electricity costs, and business-friendly regions—data center investment could mean thousands of jobs and billions in economic activity. But if Democratic-led municipalities follow Pennsylvania's playbook and impose restrictive permitting or outright bans, Colorado risks ceding this industry to Texas, Arizona, and other competitor states.

Watch whether Colorado's legislature or local governments move to restrict data center development in 2026.
