# Trump Touts Record Employment as Democrats Float Third Impeachment

Trump claims more Americans are working than ever before, touting economic gains his administration says prove his policies are delivering results. "The American People elected me to deliver HISTORIC Economic Results," Trump said, accusing mainstream media of refusing to report the figures (Just The News).

Meanwhile, House Democrats are already laying groundwork for potential future confrontation. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) told NBC's *Meet the Press* that impeaching Trump a third time would be "setting a standard" if Democrats regain House control after November's midterms (Washington Examiner). Khanna rejected the notion that Democrats should hold back on investigations.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans see Trump's employment claims as vindication of pro-growth, deregulatory policies—a sharp contrast to what they argue were Biden-era labor market distortions. Democrats' talk of a third impeachment signals they're preparing scorched-earth tactics should they return to power, conservatives warn. For Colorado voters, the employment picture matters: strong job growth in energy, agriculture, and small business sectors directly affects state economic health and tax revenues.

**What to watch:** Whether Trump's employment numbers withstand independent scrutiny, and whether Democratic impeachment talk gains traction or remains fringe positioning ahead of 2026.
