# Trump Walks Back Dana White Press Secretary Joke—But UFC Boss Still Heading to White House

President Trump said Sunday he was "only kidding" about offering UFC boss Dana White the White House press secretary job, walking back remarks he'd made just a day earlier (9News Denver, Fox News Politics). White had taken the offer seriously enough to confirm he was flying to Washington for a Monday meeting with the president (The Daily Wire).

The back-and-forth highlights Trump's unpredictable communication style—a trait that energizes his base but keeps Washington guessing. Whether this was a genuine offer rescinded, a test of White's loyalty, or simply Trump's trademark humor remains unclear. What's certain: White is still visiting the White House, suggesting the relationship between the president and the UFC CEO remains intact despite the public reversal.

For Colorado conservatives, the spectacle is a reminder that Trump's unconventional approach to governance and personnel decisions continues to dominate the news cycle—sometimes overshadowing substantive policy debates on energy, water rights, and regulatory reform that directly impact state taxpayers and businesses.
