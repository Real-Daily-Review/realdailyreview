# Trump Administration Pressures Smithsonian Over Ideological Mission Creep

The Trump administration is threatening to slash federal funding for the Smithsonian Institution unless it refocuses on American history and culture instead of progressive activism around race, gender, sexuality, and climate change.

Interior Secretary Doug Burgess and White House domestic policy aide Vince Haley issued the warning, arguing the institution has strayed from its core mission as steward of the nation's heritage. According to *Just The News*, they contend the Smithsonian's promotion of political issues diverts resources and attention from historical preservation and education.

The pressure comes as Smithsonian leadership rushed a board vote to transfer a National Mall building to the controversial National Museum of the American Latino—a move the *Washington Examiner* characterizes as a delaying tactic by "woke leadership" trying to lock in progressive priorities before Trump appointees can reshape the institution's direction.

**The conservative case:** Federal dollars should fund museums that educate Americans about shared history, not advance ideological agendas. The Smithsonian's pivot toward identity politics represents mission drift that wastes taxpayer money.

**What's at stake:** The Smithsonian receives substantial federal appropriations. If the Trump administration follows through on defunding threats, it could force a reckoning over how American cultural institutions spend public money—a model that could ripple to other federally funded agencies and grant programs.

Watch whether Congress intervenes to protect Smithsonian funding or allows the administration to reshape the institution's priorities.
