# Trump Right to Sound Alarm on China, But Missing Bigger Military Threat, Experts Warn

President Trump's recent disclosure that China stole millions of voter records in 2020 has refocused attention on Beijing's election interference—but conservative national security experts argue he's overlooking a more urgent strategic danger.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, while the data breach is concerning, it pales compared to China's broader military and intelligence operations targeting American interests. The outlet reports that Gen. Dan Caine, chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, is prioritizing deterrence against China's expanding military capabilities over other regional threats, including Iran's munitions proliferation.

Conservatives broadly agree China represents America's defining geopolitical challenge. However, some argue the Trump administration should calibrate its response: election interference warrants serious counterintelligence measures, but the real threat lies in Beijing's military buildup, espionage networks, and transnational repression operations that undermine U.S. strategic interests globally.

For Colorado, this matters. Chinese industrial espionage targets American energy, aerospace, and defense contractors operating in the state. Tighter counterintelligence and supply-chain security could reshape how Colorado's tech and manufacturing sectors operate.
