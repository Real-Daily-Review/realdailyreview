# Anti-ICE Activist Arrested After Firebombing NYC Federal Building

An armed man believed to be an anti-ICE activist detonated an incendiary device outside a federal immigration enforcement office in New York City on Monday, injuring at least two bystanders and triggering a terrorism investigation.

According to multiple outlets, the suspect deployed fireworks mixed with flammable liquid outside 26 Federal Plaza, which houses a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement field office. The Daily Wire reports he was armed with two airsoft rifles and "menaced" bystanders during the attack. Just The News identified the device as a "makeshift incendiary device" constructed from fireworks and flammable liquid.

Federal and local law enforcement responded swiftly, and the man is now in custody.
