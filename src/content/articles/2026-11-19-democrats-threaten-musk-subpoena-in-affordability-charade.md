# Democrats' Affordability Crusade Turns to Subpoena Threats Against Musk

Democrats won the 2026 midterms on a promise to tackle affordability—and now they're threatening to weaponize Congress against private citizens who disagree with them.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, incoming Democratic leadership, including Rep. Gallego, has begun threatening Elon Musk with subpoenas as part of what they're framing as an affordability agenda. Republicans and conservatives argue this represents exactly the kind of government overreach voters should fear: using legislative power to intimidate business leaders rather than pursuing actual pro-growth, pro-market solutions that lower costs.

The contrast is stark. While Democrats claim their midterm victory was a mandate for affordability measures, their opening move is regulatory intimidation—not tax relief, not deregulation, not removing barriers to production and competition that actually drive prices down. Conservatives point out that real affordability comes from unleashing free markets, not from congressional witch hunts.

For Colorado, this matters. The state's energy sector, tech workers, and small-business owners should pay attention to how Washington treats successful entrepreneurs. If Democrats are willing to subpoena Musk over policy disagreements, what's to stop them from targeting Colorado's oil and gas operators, agricultural exporters, or other industries that don't toe the progressive line?

Watch whether Republican leadership in Colorado and Congress pushes back publicly against this tactic—or whether they let Democrats set the tone for the next two years.
