# Rockies' Goodman Becomes Unlikely Fundraiser as Cleveland Fans Show Class

Colorado's Hunter Goodman just proved that baseball—and human decency—still matter in an age of cynicism and division.

After the Rockies defeated Cleveland last week, Guardians fans didn't sulk. Instead, they rallied behind Goodman's two-homer performance that clinched the AL Central for their team, donating over $22,000 to Clothes to Kids of Denver, the Rockies' official charity partner (9News). The outpouring flooded in through social media as grateful Cleveland supporters turned disappointment into generosity for a local nonprofit serving underprivileged children across the metro area.

KDVR reports the Denver-based charity has seen "skyrocketing donations" from the Midwest contingent—a reminder that sports fandom, at its best, transcends tribal loyalty and builds community. Goodman's clutch hitting became the vehicle for real charitable impact in Colorado.

The story underscores what happens when fans choose grace over grievance. In a political climate saturated with zero-sum thinking and resentment, Cleveland's response stands out as refreshingly human.

Watch whether Clothes to Kids leverages this momentum into sustained fundraising for the 2025 season—and whether other MLB markets take note of how a single athlete's performance can mobilize donors nationwide.
