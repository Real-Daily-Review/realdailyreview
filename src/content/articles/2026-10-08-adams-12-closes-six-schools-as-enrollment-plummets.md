# Adams 12 Closes Six Schools as Enrollment Collapse Forces Hard Choices

Colorado's Adams 12 Five Star Schools district is shuttering six schools due to plummeting enrollment—a stark reminder that progressive education policies and demographic shifts are hollowing out once-thriving school communities.

The school board voted Wednesday to approve the closures and consolidations, with members visibly emotional about the decision. Parents expressed deep concerns about the impact on their children and the future viability of remaining schools (9News Denver).

This isn't just about numbers. When enrollment craters, per-pupil spending becomes unsustainable, and districts face a choice: cut programs, raise taxes on already-burdened property owners, or close buildings. Adams 12 chose the latter—but the root causes matter.

Colorado conservatives point to years of progressive education spending that prioritized administrative bloat and ideological initiatives over classroom fundamentals and fiscal discipline. Meanwhile, families are voting with their feet, fleeing urban and suburban districts for charter schools, private alternatives, and homeschooling—or simply leaving the state.

The closures will disrupt communities and displace staff, but they also expose a hard truth: you cannot spend your way out of enrollment decline. Districts must cut costs or face insolvency.

**Watch for:** whether Adams 12 uses this crisis to demand more state funding (and higher taxes) or finally right-sizes its bureaucracy to match actual student demand.
