# DC Democrats Target Colorado CD8 as National 'Flip' Priority

Democrats are doubling down on Colorado's 8th Congressional District, adding Democratic candidate Manny Rutinel to the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee's "Red to Blue" list—a signal that national party brass sees CD8 as winnable territory (Complete Colorado). The designation reflects DC Democrats' assessment that the seat, currently held by a Republican, ranks among the country's most competitive races.

Republicans counter that CD8 remains solidly Republican-leaning and that Democratic investment here represents a misallocation of resources in a cycle where Democrats face headwinds nationally. GOP strategists argue the district's conservative lean and voter skepticism of progressive spending make it an uphill climb for Rutinel, regardless of DCCC backing.

The move underscores how both parties view Colorado as a battleground. CD8 spans parts of northern and central Colorado, including areas dependent on energy development and agriculture—sectors where voters have grown wary of Democratic environmental overreach and regulatory expansion.
