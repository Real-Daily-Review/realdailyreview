# Trump Pressures GOP Senators on Election Security Bill After Court Setback

President Trump is ramping up pressure on five Republican holdouts to support the SAVE America Act following a Supreme Court ruling that allows mail-in ballots postmarked by Election Day to be counted after polls close—a decision Trump called a "tremendous loss" (Washington Examiner).

The bill aims to tighten election security requirements, including documentary proof of citizenship for voter registration. Support for citizenship documentation runs deep: Gallup polling shows 83% of Americans back the measure, according to a Washington Examiner opinion piece.

But the push has exposed GOP fractures. While Trump argues the court loss makes the bill more urgent, some Republicans warn the party faces electoral danger if it follows Trump's lead on the filibuster fight needed to pass it. One conservative commentator put it bluntly: Republicans opposing citizenship documentation requirements are "doomed" politically, yet forcing a filibuster showdown could backfire.

For Colorado, election integrity remains a live issue. The state has expanded mail-in voting, and tighter citizenship verification could reshape voter rolls and turnout patterns here—affecting everything from statewide races to congressional contests.
