# America at 250: Conservatives Chart Course for Next Era of Growth

As America marks 250 years, conservatives are asking a harder question than how we got here—how do we build even greater prosperity ahead without surrendering to socialist overreach?

The occasion has sparked reflection across right-leaning outlets on what made America exceptional and what threatens it now. National Review warns that today's young socialists, like their predecessors, fundamentally misunderstand the system that created American abundance. The publication also highlights that only those who've lived under dictatorship can truly appreciate the freedom Americans often take for granted—a pointed reminder as progressive ideology gains ground on college campuses and in Democratic-controlled cities.

Energy independence emerges as a key pillar for the next 250 years. The Daily Caller argues nuclear power must anchor America's future energy strategy, rejecting the green-energy mandates that have hamstrung Colorado's oil and gas workers and driven up electricity costs for families across the state.

The Washington Examiner frames the challenge directly: prosperity doesn't happen by accident. It requires policies that unleash free markets, protect property rights, and reject the regulatory stranglehold progressives have tightened around American business.
