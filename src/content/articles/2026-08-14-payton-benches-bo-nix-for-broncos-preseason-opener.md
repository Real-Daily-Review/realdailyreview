# Broncos Hold Bo Nix Out of Preseason Opener—Smart Move or Overcautious?

Sean Payton is playing it safe with Bo Nix, sitting the Broncos' franchise quarterback out of Denver's preseason opener against Atlanta on Aug. 13, according to CPR News. Nix will instead make his first appearance in the Aug. 21 matchup against Green Bay.

The decision reflects a broader trend in the NFL: protecting young signal-callers from unnecessary preseason risk. Payton's caution makes sense—Nix is the future of the Broncos' offense, and a preseason injury could derail the entire season before it starts. Colorado fans have waited years for a competent quarterback; losing him to a meaningless August game would be maddening.

That said, some argue preseason reps matter for chemistry and rhythm, especially for a young QB learning a new system. Nix needs live snaps to build timing with receivers and get comfortable in Payton's scheme. Sitting him entirely risks rust when the regular season opens.

The broader NFL injury landscape adds urgency to Payton's thinking. The 49ers, for example, suffered another injury on the first drive of their preseason opener (New York Post), underscoring how quickly things can go wrong in August.
