# ICE Upgrades Body Cameras as Border Enforcement Intensifies

Immigration and Customs Enforcement is deploying new automatic body camera technology that activates all nearby cameras when one agent turns theirs on—a move aimed at documenting encounters and countering public scrutiny of enforcement operations.

The upgrade, which began last week according to three ICE sources speaking to *The Daily Wire*, represents the agency's effort to create a fuller record of field interactions. The automatic activation system means agents can't selectively record, potentially reducing disputes over what happened during confrontations.

The timing aligns with intensified focus on border security and migrant movement. Sen. Bernie Moreno (R-OH) is simultaneously pushing Trump administration officials to investigate individuals and organizations financing or organizing migrant caravans heading toward the U.S., calling for criminal prosecutions, financial sanctions, asset freezes, and visa suspensions where applicable, *Breitbart* reports.

For Colorado, stricter enforcement and caravan disruption could affect the flow of migrants into the state and reduce strain on local resources and law enforcement. Republicans argue that documenting enforcement actions protects agents and deters frivolous complaints, while also creating accountability records. The left typically frames such measures as surveillance overreach, though the automatic activation here cuts both ways.

Watch whether Colorado sanctuary cities and immigrant advocacy groups challenge these enforcement measures in court or push back against cooperation with federal immigration authorities.
