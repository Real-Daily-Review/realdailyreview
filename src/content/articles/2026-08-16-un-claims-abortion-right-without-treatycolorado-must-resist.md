# UN Pushes Abortion as "Human Right" Without Treaty Authority—Colorado Should Resist

Unelected international bodies are manufacturing abortion "rights" that no nation ever agreed to, and Colorado conservatives should reject the pressure.

The UN and allied institutions increasingly claim that nations violating international law by restricting abortion—despite the fact that no legally binding international treaty establishes abortion as a human right, according to the *Washington Examiner*. This regulatory overreach by unaccountable global bureaucracies represents exactly the kind of sovereignty threat conservatives have long warned about.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Nations negotiate and ratify treaties through democratic processes. When the UN, World Health Organization, and other international bodies declare obligations that legislatures never consented to, they're imposing policy by fiat. Colorado lawmakers—and Americans broadly—should not defer abortion policy to Geneva bureaucrats who answer to no voters.

**The left's argument:** International human rights frameworks evolve to protect vulnerable populations, and abortion access is essential healthcare.

This matters for Colorado because progressive state leadership has already moved to protect and expand abortion access. If international pressure mounts, expect Colorado Democrats to cite UN declarations as justification for further restrictions on pro-life legislation and conscience protections.

Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation pushes back against UN overreach or quietly accepts these manufactured "obligations."
