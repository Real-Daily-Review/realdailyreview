# Democratic Infighting Erupts in Denver as Progressive Wing Turns on Jeffries

House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries faced a hostile reception in Denver Friday when progressive activists heckled him as a "traitor" during the What's Next Democratic conference, exposing deep fractures within the party's coalition.

Activists demanding a Green New Deal and an arms embargo on Israel repeatedly interrupted Jeffries as he attempted to build support for a potential House speaker bid (Fox News, The Daily Wire). The disruption underscores the growing tension between Democratic establishment figures and the party's activist base—a rift that could complicate Democratic unity heading into the next election cycle.

For Colorado conservatives, the infighting is instructive: it reveals how the progressive wing of the Democratic Party views traditional Democratic leadership as insufficiently radical. Jeffries, a New York congressman working to consolidate power within his caucus, was deemed unacceptable by activists demanding more aggressive climate mandates and foreign policy positions aligned with anti-Israel sentiment.

Republicans have long argued that Democrats are beholden to their activist base rather than moderate voters. This Denver scene validates that concern—Democratic leaders cannot control their own coalition, let alone govern effectively.

Watch whether this progressive pressure forces Democratic candidates in Colorado to adopt more extreme positions on energy policy, climate mandates, and Middle East policy ahead of 2024.
