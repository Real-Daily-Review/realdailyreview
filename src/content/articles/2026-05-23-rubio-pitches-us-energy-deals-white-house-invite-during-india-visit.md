# US Secretary of State Rubio Visits India, Pitches Energy Deals and White House Invite

Secretary of State Marco Rubio arrived in India to advance US energy exports and strengthen bilateral ties, according to BBC World and Al Jazeera English.

Rubio is seeking to sell American energy to India to offset shortfalls created by the Iran conflict, BBC World reports. The visit comes as the US aims to mend relations with New Delhi, with the US-Israel war with Iran looming as a central issue in discussions, according to Al Jazeera English.

During the visit, Rubio extended an invitation to Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi to visit the White House, signaling Washington's interest in deepening the strategic partnership. Energy security and geopolitical alignment appear to be the dual pillars of the diplomatic push.

The timing reflects broader US strategy to leverage energy markets and security partnerships in response to regional tensions. India's energy needs and its geopolitical positioning make it a key player in American efforts to counter Iranian influence while securing new markets for US exports.

Watch for Modi's response to the White House invitation and any joint statements on energy cooperation or security arrangements.
