# Colorado Conservatives Should Care About Israel's Intelligence Role in UK Terror Plot

Israel's warning to Britain about an Iranian-sponsored attack near RAF Fairford underscores why American allies depend on Israeli intelligence—a reality that matters as progressive voices increasingly pressure the Biden-Harris administration to distance itself from Israel.

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu told Fox News that Israel provided advance warning of the Iranian-backed plot, only to face international sanctions afterward (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire). The timing is telling: Israel shares critical counterterrorism intelligence with Western partners, yet faces diplomatic blowback from the global left.

Meanwhile, the IDF released evidence that at least 10 Gaza operatives posed as journalists to dispute claims Israel systematically targets reporters (Just The News). This matters for American credibility abroad—when hostile actors weaponize the press narrative, it complicates legitimate scrutiny of military conduct.

**The Colorado angle:** U.S. military operations and intelligence partnerships directly affect Colorado's defense contractors, Fort Carson, and Schriever Space Force Base. Weakening the U.S.-Israel intelligence relationship weakens American security infrastructure that Colorado workers depend on.

Republicans argue that abandoning Israel's intelligence partnership would cripple Western counterterrorism efforts. Democrats counter that accountability for civilian casualties must come first—but that debate shouldn't obscure the operational reality: Israel's intel saves American and allied lives.

Watch whether the Harris administration continues Biden's policy of conditional aid or pivots further left on Israel.
