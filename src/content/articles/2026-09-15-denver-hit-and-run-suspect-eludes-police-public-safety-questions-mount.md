# Denver Hit-and-Run Suspect Still at Large as Police Expand Search

Denver police are hunting a driver who struck a pedestrian and fled the scene—a stark reminder that Colorado's streets face real public safety challenges that demand actual law enforcement, not the defund-the-police rhetoric that has hamstrung departments across the state.

According to 9News Denver, the incident occurred Sunday when a driver in a light blue, medium-sized SUV hit a pedestrian, briefly stopped, then abandoned the scene, leaving the victim seriously injured. Police have released the vehicle description but have not yet apprehended the suspect.

The hit-and-run underscores a broader concern conservatives have raised: as progressive cities like Denver have cut police budgets and shifted resources away from traditional law enforcement, response times and investigative capacity have suffered. While Democrats argue that reimagining public safety reduces incarceration, Republicans counter that criminals exploit weakened enforcement, leaving law-abiding citizens and victims vulnerable.

The case also connects to a separate pursuit that ended in Larimer County on Sunday night, when a driver fleeing Wyoming law enforcement crossed into Colorado (KDVR Fox31), illustrating how crime doesn't respect state lines and requires coordinated, well-resourced enforcement.

**Watch for:** Whether Denver police secure leads on the SUV and whether city leadership uses this case to justify restoring investigative resources—or doubles down on progressive alternatives to traditional policing.
