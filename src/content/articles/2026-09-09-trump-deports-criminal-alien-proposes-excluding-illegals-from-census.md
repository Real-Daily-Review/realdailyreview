# Trump Admin Tightens Immigration Enforcement, Proposes Census Changes

The Trump administration is moving swiftly on immigration enforcement while simultaneously proposing significant changes to how the federal government counts the nation's population.

Immigration and Customs Enforcement deported Israel Christopher-Ortiz, 19, an illegal immigrant convicted of groping nine girls at a Virginia high school, to El Salvador this week, according to *The Daily Wire*. The Department of Homeland Security announced the deportation on social media, signaling a harder line on criminal aliens in the U.S. illegally.

Separately, the Trump administration has proposed excluding illegal immigrants and those in the country temporarily from the decennial census, *Just The News* reports. The proposal would also eliminate the race and ethnicity question that has appeared on the census since 1790.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans argue that counting illegal aliens inflates representation in Congress and Electoral College votes for sanctuary states like California—effectively rewarding states that harbor illegal immigrants. Removing illegal migrants from the census would reallocate House seats and federal funding based on actual citizenship, a core conservative priority.

**Colorado Angle:** Colorado's population has grown significantly; census accuracy directly affects the state's congressional representation and federal funding formulas for schools, infrastructure, and social programs.

**Watch Next:** Whether Democrats mount a legal challenge to the census proposal, and how Colorado's Democratic-led legislature responds to potential shifts in federal funding allocation.
