# Tropical Storm Lala Batters Hawaii; Power Outages, Deaths Mount

Hawaii is reeling after Tropical Storm Lala swept across the islands Sunday, leaving at least one dead and over 130,000 households without power across the state's three largest islands, according to 9News Denver and The Daily Wire.

The storm, which made landfall Saturday as a Category 1 hurricane on the Big Island before downgrading to a tropical storm, has caused severe damage. Homes have been swept off their foundations, and infrastructure damage is mounting as the system grinds westward.

Gov. Josh Green (D-HI) confirmed the fatality Saturday evening. Emergency response efforts are underway, though the scale of recovery remains unclear as power restoration continues across Oahu, Maui, and Hawaii counties.
