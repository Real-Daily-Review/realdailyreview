# AI Pause Talk Is Political Theater While China Races Ahead

While some politicians grandstand about hitting the brakes on artificial intelligence development, the real competition is happening overseas—and America can't afford to fall behind.

As the *Washington Examiner* notes, the latest push for an AI "pause" is largely performative. Proponents can't even articulate what such a pause would actually mean, which tells you everything about the seriousness of the proposal. Meanwhile, China has no intention of slowing down its AI ambitions, and neither should the United States if it wants to maintain technological and economic dominance.

President Trump understands the stakes. America's competitive edge depends on innovation velocity, not regulatory theater that only hamstrings domestic companies while foreign competitors sprint ahead unchecked.

For Colorado, this matters. The state is home to growing tech hubs and aerospace innovation clusters that depend on American leadership in cutting-edge technology. Regulatory overreach or artificial development slowdowns would only push talent and investment to friendlier jurisdictions—or worse, to China itself.

Conservatives argue that the answer to AI concerns isn't government-imposed pauses but market competition, transparency, and targeted safeguards where genuine risks exist. Blanket development freezes are both unenforceable and economically suicidal.
