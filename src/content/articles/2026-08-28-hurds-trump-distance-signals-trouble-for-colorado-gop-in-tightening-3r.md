# Colorado's 3rd District Tightens as Hurd Distances Himself from Trump

Rep. Jeff Hurd, once positioned for a comfortable reelection, is now scrambling to separate himself from President Trump as his 3rd Congressional District race tightens unexpectedly (Colorado Sun, CPR News). The Republican incumbent, who appeared headed for an easy win at the start of the cycle, is now actively touting disagreements with Trump as the president's national approval ratings have declined in recent months.

The shift signals trouble for Colorado Republicans banking on Trump's coattails. Conservatives should note that Hurd's strategy—distancing from the party's standard-bearer—reflects broader anxiety among establishment Republicans about Trump's standing heading into the general election. Whether this approach resonates with Colorado's swing voters or alienates the GOP base remains an open question.

The 3rd District, which includes much of western Colorado's energy-producing regions and rural communities, has historically leaned Republican. A competitive race here would force national Republicans to defend territory they'd expected to hold, diverting resources and attention from offensive opportunities elsewhere.
