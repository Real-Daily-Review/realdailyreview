# California Democrat's Anti-Israel Posturing Signals Danger for U.S. Allies Everywhere

California Rep. Ro Khanna's latest stunt—backing an amendment to strip all foreign aid to Israel, including defensive weapons like the Iron Dome—exposes the radical anti-Israel wing of the Democratic Party and raises hard questions about American commitment to allies.

Khanna, eyeing a presidential run, announced support for Rep. Thomas Massie's (R-KY) amendment eliminating U.S. military assistance to Israel without apparently reading what he was endorsing (Washington Examiner). National Review called the move an obvious "confrontation to promote his inevitable presidential candidacy"—performative politics at the expense of a democratic ally under genuine security threats.

Conservatives argue that gutting aid to Israel's defensive systems weakens deterrence against Iranian proxies and terrorist groups, emboldening adversaries across the Middle East. The move also signals to Taiwan, South Korea, and Eastern European nations that American security guarantees are negotiable based on domestic political fashion.

For Colorado, this matters: defense contractors and tech firms supporting allied nations operate here. Weakening U.S. credibility abroad undermines Colorado's economy and military readiness.

**Watch whether other Democratic presidential hopefuls embrace this anti-Israel position—it's a bellwether for how far left the party's base has drifted.**
