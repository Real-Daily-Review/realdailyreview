# Colorado Taxpayers Footing Bill for Illegal Immigrants' College Aid While Angel Dads Mourn

While Colorado families struggle with rising education costs, states across the country—potentially including Colorado—are using taxpayer dollars to fund college financial aid for illegal immigrants, even as Angel Dads mark another Father's Day without children killed by crimes committed by illegal aliens (Fox News).

The debate is heating up: Democrats and progressive advocates argue that undocumented students deserve access to higher education regardless of immigration status. Republicans and conservatives counter that state taxpayer money should prioritize American citizens and legal residents—especially when Colorado families already face steep tuition bills and student debt. The principle is straightforward: why should Colorado workers subsidize college for people who entered the country illegally?

Meanwhile, the Department of Homeland Security honored Angel Dads on Father's Day, sharing stories of fathers whose children were killed by crimes committed by illegal aliens and cartel members. These families face an entirely different crisis: the loss of their children to preventable violence.

The contrast is stark. One group of fathers mourns murdered children. Another group of taxpayers watches their money fund benefits for illegal immigrants. Colorado conservatives should demand clarity on whether their state participates in this spending—and push lawmakers to prioritize citizens first.

Watch for which Colorado legislators vote to expand or restrict taxpayer-funded aid to illegal immigrants in the next legislative session.
