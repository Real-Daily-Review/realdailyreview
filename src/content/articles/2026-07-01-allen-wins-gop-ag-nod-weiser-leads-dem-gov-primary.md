# Colorado Republicans Pick Allen for AG; Weiser Leads Dem Gov Primary

El Paso County Prosecutor Michael Allen won the Republican nomination for Colorado attorney general, positioning conservatives to defend the state's top law enforcement post against Democratic incumbent Phil Weiser in November.

Allen, who gained prominence prosecuting the 2022 Club Q shooting in Colorado Springs, emerged from a competitive GOP primary. Republicans argue Weiser has prioritized progressive causes over public safety and constitutional rights—particularly on Second Amendment enforcement and crime prosecution. Conservatives say Colorado needs an AG focused on protecting taxpayers and defending state sovereignty against federal overreach, not advancing left-wing litigation agendas.

Meanwhile, in Colorado's 3rd Congressional District—a sprawling region from Craig to Durango—Republican incumbent Jeff Hurd and Democrat Dwayne Romero won their respective primary races (CPR News). The seat remains competitive turf for November.

On the Democratic side, Attorney General Phil Weiser leads U.S. Senator Michael Bennet in the gubernatorial primary, according to a poll released by Weiser's backers (Denver Post). The poll shows Weiser with momentum heading into the primary election.

The general election matchups will test whether Colorado voters embrace Republican calls for fiscal restraint, energy development, and constitutional governance—or double down on progressive policies that have driven up costs and expanded state regulation.

Watch the November general election spending totals: Colorado's races are shaping up as expensive tests of whether conservative messaging can break through in a purple state trending blue.
