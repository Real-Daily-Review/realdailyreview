# Florida Democrats' Socialist Primary Win Signals Party's Leftward Lurch

Florida Democrats just nominated an avowed socialist for U.S. Senate, a stunning rebuke to the party's establishment and a warning sign for moderate voters nationwide.

Angie Nixon, a state representative and Democratic Socialists of America member, won her primary this week despite—or perhaps because of—a record of inflammatory rhetoric. According to The Daily Wire, Nixon spent months attacking conservative activist Charlie Kirk and his supporters, calling them tools of "white supremacy" and comparing legislation honoring Kirk to "something brought straight by the KKK." This came just six days after Kirk's murder.

Nixon also owns "Cafe Resistance," a bookstore-cafe branded around activist ideology, according to Breitbart. Her primary victory reflects how far left the Democratic base has drifted on economic policy and cultural issues.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Nixon's nomination exposes the Democratic Party's embrace of socialism and identity-based hostility toward conservatives. Her inflammatory language—equating mainstream conservatives with the KKK—reveals the contempt progressives hold for half the country.

**Colorado angle:** While this race is in Florida, Colorado Democrats face similar pressure from their activist base. Watch whether Colorado's Democratic Senate candidates distance themselves from socialism or embrace it—a telling indicator of where the party is headed in a purple state where working families still value economic growth over ideological purity.

**What to watch:** Whether national Democrats embrace or distance themselves from Nixon's candidacy will signal how much the party's socialist wing now controls the agenda.
