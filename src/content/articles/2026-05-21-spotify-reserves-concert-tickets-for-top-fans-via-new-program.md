# Spotify to Reserve Concert Tickets for Top Fans

Spotify is launching a "Reserved" program that will set aside concert tickets for users identified as top fans of touring artists, according to TechCrunch and The Verge.

The streaming platform will determine eligibility by monitoring activity including streams, shares, and other Spotify engagement metrics. Participants must hold a Spotify Premium membership to qualify for the presale access, The Verge reports.

The initiative aims to reduce the friction and stress typically associated with securing tickets for popular acts, offering fans an alternative to standard ticketing processes. Spotify will partner with participating artists and tours to implement the program.

However, The Verge notes that Spotify's methodology for identifying "real fans" remains somewhat unclear, despite the company's stated criteria. The platform has not disclosed additional specifics about how the algorithm weighs different engagement factors or what threshold qualifies a user for Reserved access.

The program represents Spotify's latest effort to deepen artist-fan connections and expand beyond music streaming into the live entertainment ecosystem. Watch for announcements about which artists and tours will participate first, and how fans respond to the eligibility criteria.
