# California High-Speed Rail CEO Exits as Project Hemorrhages Taxpayer Cash

California's High-Speed Rail Authority CEO Ian Choudri resigned Wednesday, citing personal priorities—but his departure won't stop Democratic politicians from pouring more money into one of the nation's most notorious government boondoggles, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

The resignation marks another leadership failure at an agency plagued by cost overruns and misuse of funds. Yet California Democrats show no signs of halting the project, which has become a cautionary tale for wasteful government spending.

**Why Colorado should care:** While Colorado hasn't pursued a similar rail megaproject, state Democrats have championed transit expansion and green-energy infrastructure spending that critics warn could follow California's path of cost inflation and poor oversight. The High-Speed Rail debacle illustrates how progressive infrastructure ambitions often collide with fiscal reality.

Conservatives argue that Choudri's exit exposes deeper structural problems—not leadership gaps, but a fundamental unwillingness by Democratic lawmakers to admit failure and cut losses. Republicans point to the project as proof that government-run transportation ventures lack market discipline and accountability.

Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado legislators cite California's rail fiasco when evaluating future transit or infrastructure proposals—or whether they ignore the lesson entirely.
