# Jury Begins Deliberations in Lindsay Clancy Infanticide Trial

A Massachusetts jury has begun deliberating the fate of Lindsay Clancy, accused of murdering her three young children—Cora, 5; Dawson, 3; and Callan, 8 months—in January 2023. The former labor and delivery nurse admits to strangling all three but claims diminished mental capacity due to postpartum psychosis.

Prosecutors argued during closing statements (Washington Examiner) that the killings were "a choice," not an uncontrollable mental health episode. The judge gave jurors instructions allowing them to consider lesser charges including second-degree murder or manslaughter, departing from the original three counts of first-degree murder that carry life sentences (The Daily Wire).

The case has drawn national attention to maternal mental health and criminal responsibility. Clancy's defense team emphasizes her documented psychiatric struggles, while the state maintains that premeditation and deliberate action distinguish murder from mental illness.
