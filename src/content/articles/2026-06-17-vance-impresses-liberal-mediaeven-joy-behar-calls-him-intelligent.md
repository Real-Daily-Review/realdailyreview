# VP Vance Breaks Through Liberal Media Bubble—Even Joy Behar Impressed

Vice President JD Vance's appearance on ABC's *The View* this week proved that straight talk and genuine engagement can penetrate even the most hostile progressive media environments. Co-host Joy Behar, hardly a Trump ally, publicly called Vance "intelligent" and suggested he run for president—a striking admission that undercuts the left's caricature of the VP as a divisive ideologue.

According to the Washington Examiner and Daily Wire, Behar's comments came during the show's podcast *Behind The Table*, where she acknowledged Vance differed sharply from her expectations. The appearance marked part of a broader media blitz promoting Vance's new book, *Communion: Finding My Way Back to Faith*.

Conservative observers see the moment as significant: Vance's willingness to sit across from hostile hosts—rather than retreat to friendly outlets—demonstrates confidence in his message and appeals to centrist Republicans who've grown weary of partisan tribalism. By refusing to be baited and presenting reasoned arguments, he modeled the kind of persuasion that actually moves persuadable voters.

For Colorado Republicans eyeing 2026 statewide races in a purple state, Vance's playbook offers a lesson: engaging skeptical audiences beats preaching to the choir.

Watch whether Vance continues this media strategy heading into 2028, and whether other GOP figures follow suit in blue-leaning markets.
