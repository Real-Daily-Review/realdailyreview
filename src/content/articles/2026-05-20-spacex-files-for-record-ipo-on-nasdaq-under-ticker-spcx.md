# SpaceX Files for Record-Breaking IPO

SpaceX has formally filed its S-1 prospectus with the SEC, setting the stage for what could be the largest initial public offering in history, according to The Verge. The company plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX.

The filing reveals SpaceX generated $18.67 billion in revenue in 2025, driven largely by its Starlink satellite internet service. However, the company posted a loss of over $4.9 billion last year, The New York Times reports. Capital expenditures surged to $20.7 billion in 2025, up sharply from $11.2 billion in 2024, reflecting aggressive expansion in both satellite and launch operations.

The IPO represents a milestone for Elon Musk's space venture, which has grown from a rocket manufacturer into a diversified aerospace and satellite communications company. Starlink, which provides broadband internet globally, has become a major revenue driver alongside SpaceX's commercial and government launch services.

The company's substantial losses and heavy capital spending underscore the capital-intensive nature of space infrastructure development, even as revenue climbs. Watch for the SEC's review process and any amendments SpaceX files before the offering price and date are announced.
