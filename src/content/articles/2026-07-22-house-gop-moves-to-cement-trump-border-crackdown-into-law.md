# House Republicans Push to Lock In Trump Border Crackdown

House Republicans are advancing legislation to make Trump-era border enforcement permanent—a direct challenge to Democratic open-border rhetoric and a signal that GOP lawmakers won't tolerate another administration's reversal of immigration controls that have reduced crossings.

The Permanent Trump Secure Border Act would criminalize visa overstays, end catch-and-release policies, and tighten asylum eligibility standards. Republicans argue (Just The News) the bill is essential to prevent a future administration from dismantling enforcement gains, even as anti-ICE protests have erupted nationwide.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's border communities and law enforcement have felt the strain of federal immigration policy chaos. Locking in enforcement standards would provide clarity for local agencies and reduce the burden of processing and housing migrants that falls on state and local resources.

Democrats will likely frame this as anti-immigrant hardline politics, but conservatives counter that border security is a core federal duty and that catch-and-release policies reward illegal entry. The bill's passage through committee signals Republican resolve on immigration—a top voter concern heading into 2024.

**Watch next:** Whether this bill gains floor votes and whether any Democrats break ranks to support permanent enforcement measures.
