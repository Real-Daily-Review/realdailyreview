# Ericsson Shares Tumble While SoftBank Surges on AI Momentum

Ericsson's stock fell to the bottom of Europe's Stoxx 600 index as the Swedish telecom-equipment maker reported earnings pressured by higher operating costs and weakness outside its core networks division, according to the Wall Street Journal.

The decline contrasts sharply with gains elsewhere in the tech sector. SoftBank Group shares jumped 20 percent following strong Nvidia earnings results, according to CNBC. The rally reflects investor confidence in artificial intelligence growth, particularly through SoftBank's stake in Arm Holdings, whose chip designs power AI servers and data centers built on Nvidia systems.

The divergence underscores a widening split in technology stocks: companies positioned directly in the AI infrastructure buildout are attracting capital, while traditional telecom equipment makers face headwinds from cost pressures and slowing demand in non-core segments.

Watch whether Ericsson can stabilize earnings and return to growth in its struggling business units, or if the weakness signals broader challenges for legacy telecom suppliers in an AI-dominated market.
