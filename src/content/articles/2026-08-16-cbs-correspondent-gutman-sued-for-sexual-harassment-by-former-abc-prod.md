# CBS Correspondent Gutman Faces Sexual Harassment Lawsuit

Matt Gutman, CBS News' chief correspondent, is being sued by a former ABC News producer over alleged sexual harassment during their time working together at the network.

Former ABC News field producer Samira Said filed a wrongful-termination lawsuit in Los Angeles on Friday, accusing Gutman of making inappropriate sexualized remarks toward her during at least two assignments (The Daily Wire, Breitbart). The suit names Gutman, ABC News, and parent company The Walt Disney Co. as defendants.

The allegations center on conduct that occurred while both worked at ABC News before Gutman's move to CBS. Said's lawsuit characterizes the remarks as sexual harassment, though specific details of the allegations remain limited in initial reporting.

The case adds to ongoing scrutiny of workplace conduct in major media organizations. Disney, which owns ABC News, has faced multiple workplace misconduct allegations in recent years across its sprawling entertainment and news divisions.

Neither Gutman nor CBS News has publicly responded to the allegations at this writing. The lawsuit will likely proceed through California courts, where employment and harassment claims face substantial discovery and potential settlement negotiations.

Conservatives and media watchdogs should monitor whether legacy media outlets apply consistent standards to their own personnel—or whether progressive newsrooms extend the same accountability they demand of others.
