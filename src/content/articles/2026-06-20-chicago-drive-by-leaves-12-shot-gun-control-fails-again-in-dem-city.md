# Chicago Drive-By Shooting Leaves 12 Wounded; Gun Violence Persists in Dem-Run Cities

Another mass shooting has erupted in Chicago, with at least 12 people shot when an SUV pulled up to a crowd on the South Side and two occupants opened fire late Friday, leaving two victims in critical condition, according to police and reports from 9News Denver and the Washington Examiner.

The incident underscores a persistent problem in Democratic-controlled cities: despite some of the nation's strictest gun laws, Chicago continues to experience epidemic-level violence. While gun-control advocates argue for more restrictions, conservatives point out that Chicago's decades of progressive policies—including aggressive gun bans and heavy-handed regulations—have failed to stop criminals from obtaining and using firearms. The real issue, Republicans argue, is not law-abiding gun owners but rather failed urban governance, soft-on-crime policies, and the collapse of law enforcement effectiveness in cities run by the left.

Colorado gun owners should note the contrast: states and cities that respect Second Amendment rights and focus on prosecuting actual criminals—rather than disarming the law-abiding—maintain better public safety outcomes. The Chicago shooting is a cautionary tale about what happens when progressive cities prioritize gun confiscation over crime prevention.
