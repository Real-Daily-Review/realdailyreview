# Denver Crime Wave: Mail Carrier Robbed, Bike Thieves Arrested as Public Safety Deteriorates

Colorado's urban crime problem keeps getting worse, with Denver police arresting suspects in a brazen armed robbery of a postal carrier and Boulder authorities cracking down on an organized theft ring targeting high-end bikes and scooters.

According to 9News Denver, a suspect was arrested after stolen mail was recovered in a dishwasher following an armed robbery of a letter carrier. A second robbery reported October 1 is believed unrelated, suggesting this isn't an isolated incident but part of a broader pattern of street crime targeting essential workers.

Meanwhile, KDVR Fox31 reported that Boulder Police arrested three people and recovered numerous stolen high-end bikes and scooters following an investigation into multiple thefts. The coordinated nature of the bike theft operation points to organized crime rather than petty shoplifting.

These incidents underscore what conservatives have long warned: progressive criminal justice policies—including reduced prosecution, early release programs, and defunded police departments—create conditions where criminals operate with impunity. When mail carriers can't safely do their jobs and residents lose property to organized theft rings, the social contract breaks down.

Colorado taxpayers deserve elected officials who prioritize law enforcement funding, swift prosecution, and consequences for criminals—not catch-and-release policies that embolden repeat offenders.

Watch whether Denver and Boulder leadership will demand accountability from prosecutors and judges, or continue blaming "root causes" while crime spreads.
