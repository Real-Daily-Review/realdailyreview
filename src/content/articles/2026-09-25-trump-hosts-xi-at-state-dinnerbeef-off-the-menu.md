# Trump Hosts Xi at White House State Dinner—But Where's the Beef?

President Trump rolled out the red carpet for Chinese leader Xi Jinping Thursday night with a state dinner that notably skipped one American staple: beef. The menu featured sesame-crusted sea bass and White House honey dessert instead, according to Fox News, raising eyebrows among conservatives who've long viewed trade negotiations with China through a protectionist lens.

The guest list reflected Trump's inner circle and media allies—House Speaker Mike Johnson, Elon Musk, Trump family members including daughters Tiffany and Ivanka, and Fox News hosts Bret Baier, Jesse Waters, and Laura Ingraham attended (Just The News). Trump gifted Xi an ornate bald eagle statue, symbolizing American strength during what the Washington Examiner called a "friendly" day of meetings aimed at settling trade differences.

For Colorado, the dinner's diplomatic tone matters. The state's ranchers and agricultural exporters have been hammered by Chinese tariffs and trade tensions. A warming U.S.-China relationship could ease market access for Colorado beef and agricultural products—or it could signal Trump is softening on trade enforcement that conservatives argue protects American workers and farmers.

Conservatives should watch whether this diplomatic thaw translates into concrete trade wins for Colorado agriculture or becomes another example of globalist compromise.
