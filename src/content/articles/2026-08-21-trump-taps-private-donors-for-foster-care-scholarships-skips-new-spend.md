# Trump Administration Launches Foster Care Scholarship Push

The Trump administration is backing a private-sector scholarship program for foster youth aging out of the system—a targeted approach that relies on corporate donations rather than new government spending.

First Lady Melania Trump announced a $2 million scholarship commitment from IndyCar and Fox Corporation to fund educational opportunities at Indiana University and Purdue University through her "Fostering the Future" initiative, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The White House unveiled the broader program in June, framed as a pathway to financial independence for young people transitioning out of foster care.

The initiative reflects a conservative preference for leveraging private philanthropy and corporate partnerships over expanding federal bureaucracy. Rather than creating another government entitlement or welfare program, the administration is mobilizing private capital to address a genuine problem: foster youth face documented challenges in education and economic stability after aging out of state custody.

The scholarship model avoids the cost and overhead of new federal agencies while creating measurable outcomes tied to educational attainment. It's the kind of public-private collaboration conservatives favor—government identifying a need, then stepping back to let private enterprise and philanthropy solve it.

Watch whether other corporations step up with matching commitments, or if the administration expands the model to other states and universities.
