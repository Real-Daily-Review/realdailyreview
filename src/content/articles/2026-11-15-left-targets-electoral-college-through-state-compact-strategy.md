# Electoral College Under Siege as Left Pushes National Popular Vote Compact

Colorado conservatives should brace for a coordinated assault on the Electoral College—the constitutional safeguard that prevents a handful of high-population urban centers from dictating presidential elections to the rest of America.

According to Complete Colorado, the Democratic Party and its allies understand precisely where their power concentrates and are moving to exploit it. National Review warns that blue-state activists are positioning themselves to execute an "end run around the constitutional system" through the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact, which would award electoral votes to the national popular vote winner once states representing 270 electoral votes sign on.

The stakes for Colorado are direct: the compact threatens to render swing-state voters—including Coloradans—irrelevant in future presidential contests. Instead of candidates competing for Colorado's nine electoral votes, campaigns would focus exclusively on maximizing turnout in California, New York, and Illinois.

Republicans argue the Electoral College was designed precisely to prevent this tyranny of the majority, ensuring that less-populated states retain meaningful influence in selecting the president. The Founders understood that a pure popular vote would marginalize rural America, energy-producing regions, and agricultural communities—the backbone of Colorado's economy and values.

Conservatives should monitor which Colorado legislators face pressure to join this compact and demand they defend the constitutional system that protects Colorado's voice in presidential elections.
