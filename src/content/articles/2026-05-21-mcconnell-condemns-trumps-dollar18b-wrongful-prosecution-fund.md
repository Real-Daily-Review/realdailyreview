# McConnell Blasts Trump DOJ Fund as 'Morally Wrong'

Former Senate Republican Leader Mitch McConnell on Thursday condemned acting Attorney General Todd Blanche's acceptance of a Trump administration proposal to establish a $1.8 billion legal compensation fund for people claiming wrongful prosecution by the Justice Department, according to The Hill.

McConnell called the "Anti-Weaponization Fund" "utterly stupid, morally wrong," per CNBC. Critics across both parties worry the fund could provide money to Trump supporters who attacked police during the January 6, 2021 Capitol riot, according to CNBC.

The fund represents a centerpiece of the Trump administration's effort to overturn prosecutions related to the Capitol breach and other cases the administration views as politically motivated. Blanche's acceptance of the proposal marks a significant step toward implementation, though the fund would likely require congressional approval for full deployment.

The backlash from McConnell—a longtime Trump ally—signals potential Republican fractures over the initiative's scope and beneficiaries. Watch whether Congress moves to block or modify the fund's structure and eligibility criteria.
