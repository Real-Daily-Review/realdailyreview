# Trump Launches Direct-to-Public Media Channel After Press Pool Boycott

President Trump is bypassing hostile media outlets entirely, launching Trump TV on Monday as a direct-to-viewer streaming platform after major news organizations boycotted White House coverage and filed suit over access restrictions.

The White House rolled out the 24/7 channel featuring administration announcements, past moments, and real-time updates—a stark escalation in Trump's long-running battle with what he calls the "Fake News" establishment. The move came after CNN, MS Now, and Politico jointly sued the administration over Trump's decision to ban those outlets from White House grounds last week (Washington Examiner).

Trump has signaled he will fight the lawsuit aggressively, rejecting the media organizations' claims that access restrictions violate press freedoms.

**The conservative case:** Trump's allies argue that hostile media outlets have forfeited credibility through years of biased coverage and that direct communication with voters is a legitimate presidential tool. The Daily Wire notes the channel streams "top past moments, announcements, and the latest and greatest from the administration"—unfiltered by editorial gatekeepers.

**The media's counter:** News organizations argue that White House access is essential to accountability journalism and that banning outlets sets a dangerous precedent for press freedom.
