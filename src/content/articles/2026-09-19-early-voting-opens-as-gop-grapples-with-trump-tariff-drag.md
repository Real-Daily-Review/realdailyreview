# Early Voting Opens as GOP Faces Midterm Headwinds Over Trump Tariffs

Early voting has begun in the 2026 midterm elections, with control of Congress hanging in the balance—Republicans currently hold the majority, but Democrats are positioning for gains (9News Denver). The race comes as internal GOP tensions simmer over President Trump's tariff strategy, which conservatives argue is undermining Republican momentum heading into the election.

**The tariff divide:** The Washington Examiner's opinion section reports that Trump has touted his tariff regime as economically successful, claiming "the most successful two years financially … in the history of the presidency." However, some Republicans worry the tariffs—which function as taxes on imported goods—are dragging down GOP electoral prospects by raising costs for consumers and businesses.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and manufacturing base all depend on competitive supply chains and reasonable trade conditions. Tariff-driven inflation and retaliatory trade measures could squeeze Colorado ranchers, small manufacturers, and energy companies already navigating regulatory headwinds from Democratic state policies.

**What's at stake:** Republicans must decide whether to double down on Trump's trade nationalism or pivot toward free-market messaging that resonates with swing voters concerned about grocery and energy prices.

Watch for how Colorado Republicans—particularly in swing districts—navigate the tariff question as early voting ramps up over the next six weeks.
