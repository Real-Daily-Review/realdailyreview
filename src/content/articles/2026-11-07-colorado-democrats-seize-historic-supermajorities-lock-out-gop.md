# Colorado Democrats Lock in Historic Supermajorities—Brace for Aggressive Agenda

Colorado Republicans face their bleakest legislative outlook in modern history after Democrats secured supermajorities in both the state House and Senate for the first time ever, according to the Colorado Sun and Denver Post. The party also swept all four statewide executive offices, giving Democrats unchecked control of state government heading into 2027.

State Rep. Jennifer Bacon, D-Denver, was elected House Speaker by her Democratic colleagues, replacing term-limited Speaker Julie McCluskie. Democrats flipped more seats than anticipated, building what amounts to a 2-to-1 advantage over Republicans in the Capitol.

**The conservative concern is straightforward:** supermajorities mean Democrats can pass legislation without a single Republican vote. That translates to unfettered authority to raise taxes, expand regulations, accelerate energy transition mandates, and reshape Colorado's business climate—all without meaningful legislative resistance. Conservatives argue this concentration of power invites the kind of overreach that has already driven businesses and residents from California and New York.

Republicans will have minimal leverage to block or amend bills. Expect aggressive pushes on climate mandates affecting Colorado's energy sector, gun control measures, and tax increases targeting businesses and high earners.

**Watch whether Democrats move immediately on controversial legislation or govern cautiously—their next moves will signal how aggressively they plan to use their newfound power.**
