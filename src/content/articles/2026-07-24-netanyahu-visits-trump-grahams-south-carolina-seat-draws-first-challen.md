# Netanyahu to Visit Trump; Graham Seat Race Heats Up

Benjamin Netanyahu will meet President Trump at the White House and attend Senator Lindsey Graham's memorial service in Washington, according to Fox News, as U.S.-Israel operations against Iran continue. The visit underscores the Trump administration's commitment to the U.S.-Israel alliance—a stark contrast to the Biden-Harris years of daylight between Washington and Jerusalem.

Graham's death has already triggered a scramble for his South Carolina Senate seat. Former South Carolina Governor Mark Sanford announced his candidacy this week, telling the Daily Caller that "Lindsey's death is a reminder of how short and precious life is." Sanford, a Republican who served in Congress, will compete in what's expected to be a competitive primary.

Conservative outlets are also pushing Congress to pass new Russia sanctions legislation in Graham's memory. National Review argues the late senator "was right on Ukraine" and that tougher sanctions could accelerate an end to the war—a position that divides Republicans between hawk interventionists and those skeptical of open-ended foreign aid commitments.
