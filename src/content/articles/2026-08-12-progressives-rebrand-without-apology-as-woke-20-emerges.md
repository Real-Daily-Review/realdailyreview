# Progressive Activists Dodge Accountability as 'Woke 2.0' Rebranding Begins

Colorado taxpayers and business owners have watched progressive activists push costly regulations and ideological experiments for years—and now they're simply rebranding without apology.

According to National Review, figures like Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez are pivoting away from the most visible failures of progressive policy without acknowledging the damage done. Rather than owning the errors of their ways, the left is quietly shifting messaging under a new label, effectively asking Americans to forget what came before.

Republicans argue this is classic progressive misdirection: when radical policies fail—whether on energy mandates, spending, or regulatory overreach—the left simply repackages the same ideology with fresher language. The Daily Caller's analysis notes the fundamental problem remains unchanged: the core assumptions driving "Woke 1.0" were flawed from the start, yet activists are moving forward without genuine course correction.

For Colorado, this matters. The state has absorbed years of progressive energy mandates, water restrictions, and regulatory expansion. If national progressive figures are simply rebranding without substantive reform, Colorado can expect more of the same ideological pressure on energy development, land use, and business freedom.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership follows this national rebranding playbook—or finally acknowledges the real costs of progressive experimentation.
