# Black-Market 'Super-Ozempic' From China Floods U.S., FDA Offers Only Warnings

Chinese suppliers are flooding America with retatrutide, an unapproved weight-loss drug far more potent than Ozempic, targeting teenagers through TikTok, medical spas, and mail delivery—while the FDA issues stern letters instead of enforcement action.

The drug, still in clinical trials for obesity and Type 2 diabetes treatment, has no regulatory approval for human use. Yet it's being marketed aggressively to minors seeking quick weight loss, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The FDA's response has been limited to warning letters rather than aggressive interdiction of the Chinese supply chain or criminal prosecution of domestic distributors.

Conservatives argue this exposes a fundamental FDA failure: the agency prioritizes bureaucratic caution over protecting Americans from dangerous foreign pharmaceuticals. While the FDA spends resources on regulatory overreach against legitimate American businesses, it struggles to stop black-market drugs entering the country through mail and social media.

Colorado parents should be alarmed. Teenagers across the state have access to this unapproved compound through their phones, with no medical supervision, no dosage guidance, and unknown contaminants or potency variations. The long-term effects remain unknown.

Watch whether Congress demands the FDA shift resources from domestic regulatory harassment to actual border and supply-chain enforcement against Chinese drug trafficking.
