# White House UFC Terror Plot Leader Is Illegal Immigrant—DACA Recipient

Border security failures just turned deadly serious: the alleged mastermind of a plot to attack the UFC Freedom 250 event at the White House with explosive drones and snipers is an illegal immigrant from Mexico, the Department of Homeland Security confirmed.

Abraham Alvarez, identified by federal authorities as "responsible for planning, organizing, and directing the planned attack," overstayed his visa after entering the country as a child. His B2 visa expired in 2001—yet he remained in the United States. In 2014, the Obama administration granted him deportation relief through the DACA program (Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals), according to *Just The News*.

**The conservative case:** This case exemplifies why border enforcement and immigration vetting matter. A man with no legal status allegedly plotted mass violence at a high-profile White House event. Republicans argue that catch-and-release policies, visa overstay tolerance, and programs like DACA that shield illegal immigrants from deportation create security gaps terrorists can exploit. If federal authorities cannot track or remove someone whose visa expired two decades ago, how can they claim border security is under control?

Democrats typically defend DACA as humanitarian relief for childhood arrivals, but conservatives counter that national security must come first—and that legal status cannot be decoupled from public safety vetting.
