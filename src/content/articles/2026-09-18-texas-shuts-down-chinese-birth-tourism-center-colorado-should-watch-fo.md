# Texas Shuts Down Chinese Birth Tourism Operation—Colorado Should Take Note

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton has forced the closure of an alleged "birth tourism" center in Houston that prosecutors say helped Chinese nationals unlawfully exploit America's immigration system to secure citizenship for their children. The Daily Wire previously investigated the operation, which allegedly ran three homes and an apartment under the name De'Ai Postpartum Care Center.

Birth tourism—where foreign nationals enter the U.S. on visitor visas, give birth, and return home with U.S. citizen children—represents a direct loophole in immigration enforcement that conservatives argue undermines border security and citizenship standards. Paxton's action signals a willingness to prosecute the scheme aggressively rather than treat it as a victimless administrative matter.

For Colorado, the case underscores why state-level enforcement matters. While Colorado hasn't reported similar operations, the state's major metro areas and hospitals could become targets for similar schemes. Republicans argue that without vigilant state attorneys general willing to investigate and prosecute, birth tourism networks will simply relocate to friendlier jurisdictions.

Democrats typically frame immigration enforcement narrowly, focusing on criminal smuggling rather than civil immigration violations. Conservatives counter that any systematic exploitation of citizenship rules—regardless of the nationality involved—warrants aggressive prosecution.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic Attorney General Phil Weiser launches similar investigations into potential birth tourism operations within the state.
