# Tropical Storms Edouard and Lowell Threaten Gulf Coast and Hawaii

Two tropical storms are bearing down on U.S. territory this week, with Tropical Storm Edouard targeting Texas and Louisiana and Tropical Storm Lowell heading toward Hawaii. Texas Gov. Greg Abbott warned that flooding poses the biggest threat from Edouard, which is expected to dump up to 9 inches of rain on the Gulf Coast by Tuesday, according to the New York Post.

The dual storm threat comes as hurricane season peaks, testing state emergency management and federal disaster response protocols. For Colorado, the indirect impact could include energy market volatility—Gulf Coast refineries and petrochemical facilities may face temporary disruptions, potentially affecting fuel prices at the pump statewide.

Abbott's focus on flooding aligns with conservative emphasis on practical preparedness over climate-doom rhetoric. Republicans generally support robust disaster response and recovery funding while opposing wasteful federal spending unrelated to genuine emergency needs.

Coloradans should monitor whether federal disaster aid remains targeted and efficient, or becomes a vehicle for unrelated spending expansions.
