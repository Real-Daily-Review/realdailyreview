# Founding Principles Still Drive American Innovation, Republicans Say

The spirit of limited government that animated the Founding Fathers remains the engine of American prosperity today, according to Republican leaders reflecting on the nation's founding principles.

Sen. Ted Budd (R-NC) argues that the same constitutional skepticism toward "overreach and overregulation" that guided the Framers continues to fuel innovation and economic growth (Breitbart). He points to North Carolina as proof: a state thriving as a "national leader in innovation" by honoring tradition while embracing market-driven progress.

The New York Post notes the Founding Fathers designed a system larger than themselves—one that created "a sense of possibility for more and more people" and expected each generation to defend and extend those principles of liberty.

**The Colorado angle:** This philosophy directly challenges the regulatory expansion Colorado has experienced under Democratic leadership. From oil-and-gas restrictions to water-use mandates to firearms regulations, progressive policymakers have moved steadily away from the Founders' skepticism of government power. Republicans argue Colorado's economy—particularly energy development and agriculture—suffers when bureaucrats prioritize ideology over innovation.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado GOP candidates in 2026 effectively tie state regulatory overreach to national progressive overreach, making the case that limited government isn't nostalgia—it's the formula for jobs and opportunity.
