# Vice President Vance Stands Firm Against 'The View' Hosts on Immigration, Affordability

Vice President JD Vance walked into hostile territory Tuesday on ABC's "The View," where he defended Trump administration policies on immigration and cost-of-living issues against skeptical hosts.

Vance appeared to promote his new book and discuss the Iran peace agreement, but the conversation quickly pivoted to sparring over the administration's record. According to Fox News Digital, Vance told the network beforehand he hoped the hosts would "meet him halfway." That didn't happen.

Instead, Vance faced what The Daily Wire described as "an intensely one-sided interview," with hosts pressing him on immigration, affordability, and other contentious topics. The vice president remained composed throughout, refuting their points methodically, the Daily Caller reported.

Vance's appearance underscores the Trump-Vance administration's willingness to engage directly with mainstream media outlets hostile to its agenda—a contrast to previous Republican administrations that often avoided such forums. For Colorado conservatives, the exchange highlighted core administration priorities: border security and economic relief from inflation—issues that resonate with voters frustrated by Biden-era policies.
