# Greeley Child Kidnapping Suspect Arrested; Community Safety Concerns Mount

A woman has been arrested after kidnapping a three-year-old from a Greeley apartment complex on Monday, according to the Greeley Police Department. Police say the suspect did not know the child, raising alarm among parents about unsecured residential areas and the adequacy of local law enforcement resources to protect vulnerable Coloradans.
