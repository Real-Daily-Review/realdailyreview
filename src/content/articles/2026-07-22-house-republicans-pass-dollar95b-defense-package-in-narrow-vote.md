# House Republicans Pass $95B Defense Package Amid Fiscal Concerns

House Republicans narrowly approved a $95 billion budget blueprint this week—a slim 216-to-214 vote that exposed fractures within the GOP over defense spending levels. The package allocates roughly $73 billion in additional military funding tied to potential Iran conflict operations, according to *Just The News*.

The razor-thin margin reveals conservative unease. Two Republicans voted against the bill despite party pressure, citing concerns about the high price tag. For Colorado taxpayers already burdened by state and federal levies, the question looms: does Washington have spending discipline, or are Republicans simply swapping out progressive social programs for defense bloat?

Republicans argue the Iran deterrent is necessary and that military readiness protects American interests abroad. Conservatives counter that Congress must justify every dollar of new spending—defense included—rather than treat the Pentagon budget as a blank check. The defense establishment's track record on cost overruns and waste gives fiscal hawks legitimate reason to demand accountability.

Colorado's defense contractors and military installations may benefit from the spending increase, but state taxpayers ultimately foot the bill through federal taxes. The vote signals that even with Republican control, fiscal restraint remains elusive in Washington.

Watch whether House conservatives demand detailed accounting on how these billions are spent and whether any funds flow toward wasteful programs rather than genuine readiness.
