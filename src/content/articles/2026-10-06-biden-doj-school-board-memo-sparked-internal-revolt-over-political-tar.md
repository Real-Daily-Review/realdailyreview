# Biden DOJ's School Board Memo Sparked Internal Revolt Over Political Targeting

The Biden Justice Department's 2021 school board memo—ostensibly aimed at addressing threats to educators—triggered alarm from within its own ranks, with DOJ officials warning it risked becoming an "Anti-MAGA Task Force," according to a Trump administration report released Monday.

Attorney General Merrick Garland's directive instructed the FBI to investigate parents at school board meetings, framing them as potential domestic threats. The move sparked internal dissent: career prosecutors and officials flagged concerns that the initiative would weaponize federal law enforcement against political opponents rather than genuine security threats.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and DOJ insiders contended the memo represented a dangerous politicization of federal law enforcement—using the Justice Department to suppress First Amendment-protected speech and intimidate parents exercising their constitutional right to petition government. The "Anti-MAGA Task Force" label reflects the perception that the Biden administration targeted conservative parents specifically.

**Democrats' position:** The administration framed the effort as protecting school employees from violence and intimidation, not suppressing political speech.

**Colorado angle:** Parents across Colorado participated in school board debates over curriculum, mask mandates, and parental rights during this period. If federal law enforcement was indeed targeting conservative activists, Colorado families could have been caught in that dragnet.

Conservatives should monitor whether Congress investigates the full scope of DOJ's school board operations and whether similar politically motivated initiatives exist elsewhere in the federal bureaucracy.
