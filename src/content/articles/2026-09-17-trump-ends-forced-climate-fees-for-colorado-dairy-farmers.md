# Trump Admin Ends Forced Climate Funding for Dairy Farmers

Colorado dairy farmers just got relief from a Biden-era scheme that forced them to bankroll progressive climate activism.

The Trump administration's Department of Agriculture has ended a practice requiring dairy farmers to pay fees funneled toward left-wing climate initiatives under the Dairy Checkoff Program (The Daily Wire). Under Biden, farmers had no choice but to contribute to causes many opposed—a classic example of government coercion disguised as industry self-regulation.

Republicans argue the policy exemplified regulatory overreach: farmers shouldn't be forced to fund ideological causes unrelated to dairy promotion. The checkoff program, originally designed to market American dairy products, had been weaponized to advance the climate agenda. Conservatives say this move restores economic freedom and lets farmers keep more of their earnings.

Colorado's dairy industry, concentrated in the northeastern plains and among the state's largest agricultural sectors, stands to benefit directly. Removing mandated climate-activism fees means more capital stays in farmers' hands—capital they can reinvest in operations, equipment, or their communities rather than seeing it siphoned to activist groups.

The broader pattern matters: House Republicans are simultaneously investigating foreign-funded climate groups like ClientEarth USA for unduly influencing American courts (The Daily Wire), signaling a coordinated push-back against what conservatives view as a captured regulatory system.

Watch whether Colorado's agricultural delegation—particularly in Congress—amplifies this victory and pushes back against other hidden climate mandates baked into farm programs.
