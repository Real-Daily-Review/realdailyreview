# Texas Democrat's Socialist Past Resurfaces—Colorado Should Take Note

A resurfaced 2020 video shows Texas Democratic U.S. Senate nominee James Talarico calling capitalism an "oppressive system" and discussing "dismantling" free-market economics alongside white supremacy and climate change, according to *Just The News*, *The Daily Wire*, and *Breitbart*. The remarks, made at a Sierra Club virtual forum, reveal rhetoric aligned with Democratic Socialists of America talking points—a sharp contrast to Talarico's current bid to broaden appeal against GOP opponent Paxton.

**Why Colorado cares:** While Talarico is running in Texas, his anti-capitalist ideology mirrors arguments gaining traction among Colorado Democrats pushing aggressive climate regulations, energy restrictions, and wealth-redistribution policies that threaten Colorado's oil and gas workers, ranchers, and small-business owners.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Talarico's comments expose the true agenda of progressive Democrats—dismantling the free-market system that has created American prosperity and lifted millions from poverty. His willingness to equate capitalism with white supremacy reveals the radical framing driving climate policy across the Left.

**Democrats' counter:** Talarico's campaign has attempted to reframe his remarks as part of a broader climate discussion, though the video evidence speaks plainly.

Conservatives and Colorado taxpayers should watch whether similar anti-capitalist rhetoric gains ground in upcoming state legislative races targeting energy development and business regulation.
