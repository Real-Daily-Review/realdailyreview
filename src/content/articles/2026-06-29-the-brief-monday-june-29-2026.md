# Real Daily Review — The Brief
### Monday, June 29, 2026

Colorado conservatives head into the week watching the state's Democratic Party tear itself apart over who can be the most radical — while Washington moves to clean up federal program fraud and enforce the laws Democrats have spent years ignoring. Buckle up.

---

## Colorado Democrats Race to the Left on Energy Lawsuits

The race for Colorado Attorney General has turned into a competition over who will most aggressively weaponize the office against energy producers through climate litigation. That's bad news for Colorado's oil and gas workers, royalty-dependent ranchers, and the rural economies that depend on responsible energy development. Republicans argue the AG's office should enforce the law — not serve as a litigation arm for the climate lobby. Colorado conservatives should be paying close attention to who wins this primary.

---

## Obamacare Fraud: Over 1 Million Enrolled Without Social Security Numbers

RFK Jr. and Dr. Oz are sounding the alarm: more than 1 million people without Social Security numbers are reportedly enrolled in Obamacare, according to reporting citing the two officials. "We are going to find you," the warning goes. For Colorado taxpayers already shouldering the costs of a bloated federal health entanglement, this is exactly the kind of systemic fraud conservatives have long argued is baked into government-run health programs. Accountability is overdue.

---

## ICE-Houston Arrests Expose the Cost of Open-Border Policies

ICE officers in the Houston area arrested 735 criminal illegal aliens in May alone — accounting for more than 1,700 criminal convictions, with nearly 1,200 involving violent crimes, including murderers and child predators. Democrats counter that enforcement is cruel. Conservatives say the cruelty is leaving these criminals on the streets in the first place. Colorado, which has its own sanctuary-friendly jurisdictions, should take note of what aggressive federal enforcement actually looks like when the political will exists.

---

## DC's Mayoral Race Is a Preview of the Left's Governing Vision

President Trump says he'll meet with Washington, D.C., mayoral frontrunner Janeese Lewis George — a candidate who has clashed with the administration over ICE enforcement, cashless bail, and sanctuary city policies. Trump reportedly called her a "communist." Whether or not that label sticks, her platform is a clear preview of where the progressive left wants to take urban governance. Colorado's own Denver Democrats should recognize the model — they've been running a version of it for years.

---

## Missouri Tragedy Underscores the Stakes of Responsible Gun Policy

A 10-year-old boy shot and killed a 7-month-old infant in Missouri, according to police, who also arrested a 19-year-old on gun-related charges following questioning. The facts here are still developing. But conservatives will note what this story is actually about: criminal access to firearms and the breakdown of family and community — not law-abiding gun owners. Colorado's Second Amendment community has long argued that punishing responsible gun owners does nothing to stop tragedies rooted in lawlessness and neglect.

---

## What to Watch

Keep your eyes on the Colorado AG Democratic primary — whoever emerges will likely spend the next four years using the office as a weapon against Colorado's energy sector, and that fight will define the state's economic future as much as any legislative session. On the federal side, watch whether the Social Security number enrollment scandal forces a real audit of Obamacare's rolls — Colorado taxpayers have skin in that game. And as Denver's scorched-earth Democratic primary wraps up, ask yourself: if their own voters are disgusted by the money and cynicism, what does that tell you about who's actually running the party?
