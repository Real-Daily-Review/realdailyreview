# National Democrats Target Colorado's 8th District with Cash Push for Rutinel

Washington Democrats are opening their wallets to flip Colorado's 8th Congressional District, adding Democrat Manny Rutinel to the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee's "Red to Blue" program (CPR News). The move signals national party confidence that CD8—already recognized as one of the country's most competitive races—is winnable territory for the left.

Republicans argue the DCCC's investment reflects Democratic desperation to reclaim House control, not genuine grassroots support in the district. The infusion of out-of-state money underscores how national Democrats view Colorado's seat as crucial to their majority strategy, even as they pour resources into a district that has proven resistant to their candidates.

CD8 encompasses parts of northern Colorado, including areas dependent on energy development and agriculture—sectors where conservative policies on permitting, water rights, and land use carry real weight with voters. Democrats' focus on the seat suggests they believe demographic and suburban shifts have created an opening, but Republicans counter that Colorado voters in this district remain skeptical of progressive tax-and-spend agendas.

The race will test whether national Democratic money can overcome local skepticism of the party's energy and fiscal policies.

Watch whether Republican CD8 candidates capitalize on the district's energy economy and highlight the DCCC's backing as evidence of outside interference in Colorado politics.
