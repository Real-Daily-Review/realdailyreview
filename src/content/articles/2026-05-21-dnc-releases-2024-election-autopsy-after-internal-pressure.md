# DNC Releases 2024 Election Autopsy After Internal Pressure

Democratic National Committee Chair Ken Martin released the party's 2024 election autopsy report Thursday, reversing his earlier decision to withhold it following pushback from within the party.

Martin did not endorse the findings, according to The Hill. A disclaimer appears on each page stating the document "reflects the views of the author, not the DNC," according to CNBC. In his announcement, Martin said the report does not meet his standards.

The chair had previously announced he would not release the autopsy, saying he wanted to move forward rather than dwell on the party's losses. However, according to the Washington Examiner, internal pressure from party members prompted the reversal.

The report was designed to identify reasons for the Democratic Party's losses in the 2024 elections. Martin's decision to release it despite his reservations underscores tension within the party over how to assess and learn from the defeat.
