# Biden Border Chaos: Illegal Immigrants Charged in Serious Crimes While Dems Ignore the Pattern

Colorado taxpayers are footing the bill for a border system that releases dangerous criminals into American communities. An illegal immigrant from Guinea, apprehended at the Arizona border in 2023 under Biden administration policy, now faces charges for allegedly kidnapping and raping a teenager—with authorities suspecting additional victims (Just The News). Meanwhile, another illegal immigrant was convicted of a $35 million Ponzi scheme after overstaying his visa and committing prior crimes (The Daily Wire).

Republicans and conservatives argue Democrats have created a reckless catch-and-release system that prioritizes open-border ideology over public safety. Rep. Brandon Gill (R-Texas) blasted Democrats for ignoring these cases while fixating on Austin ICE enforcement, calling victims "collateral damage" of failed immigration policy (Fox News). 

The pattern is damning: visa overstays, criminal histories, and repeat offenses—all enabled by lax enforcement and administrative releases. Colorado's law enforcement and social services absorb costs when these cases land in our state. Democrats counter that isolated cases don't represent broader immigration trends, but conservatives say the Biden administration's deliberate non-enforcement strategy has created predictable chaos.
