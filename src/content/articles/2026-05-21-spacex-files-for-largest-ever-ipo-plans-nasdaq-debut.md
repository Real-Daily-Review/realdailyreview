# SpaceX Files for Record-Breaking IPO

Elon Musk's SpaceX has formally filed its S-1 prospectus with the Securities and Exchange Commission, setting the stage for what could be the largest initial public offering ever, according to The Verge. The company plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker SPCX.

SpaceX generated $18.67 billion in revenue in 2025, driven largely by its Starlink satellite internet service, which contributed more than $11 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal reporting cited by The Verge. However, the company posted a loss of over $4.9 billion last year, with capital expenditures jumping to $20.7 billion—nearly double the $11.2 billion spent in 2024.

The IPO filing marks a significant milestone for the private aerospace manufacturer, which has become one of the world's most valuable startups through government contracts, commercial launches, and its expanding satellite broadband business. SpaceX has not yet disclosed pricing or share allocation details.

Watch for the company's roadshow and pricing announcement in coming weeks, which will determine the final valuation and IPO size.
