# Trump Rallies Ohio Republicans, But Colorado GOP Eyes Midterm Turnout at Home

Former President Donald Trump headlined a Saturday rally in Vandalia, Ohio, where he urged voters to "pretend I'm on the ballot" while campaigning for Republican Senate incumbent Jon Husted and gubernatorial candidate Vivek Ramaswamy (Fox News). Former NFL coach Jon Gruden joined the event, leveraging his Ohio roots to energize the GOP base (Washington Examiner).

Trump's midterm push reflects the Republican strategy of nationalizing elections around his political brand and messaging on a "golden age" of American prosperity and strength. The rally underscores GOP confidence heading into the cycle, though Democrats will argue Trump's continued dominance of party messaging risks alienating independent voters and suburban moderates.

**Colorado angle:** While Trump campaigns in Ohio, Colorado Republicans face their own midterm math. The state has trended purple-to-blue in recent cycles, and GOP candidates will need to replicate Trump's turnout energy without the former president's polarizing effect on swing voters. Colorado's 2024 Senate race and competitive House districts could hinge on whether Republicans can energize Trump voters while holding college-educated suburban support—a balancing act Ohio's GOP may not face as acutely.

**Watch:** Whether Trump's Ohio playbook translates to Colorado's more diverse, educated electorate, or if state Republicans chart a different course.
