# Harris Signals 2028 Run With Attacks on Election Integrity Measures

Kamala Harris is laying groundwork for a potential 2028 presidential bid by positioning herself as the Democratic Party's fighter—while simultaneously attacking commonsense election security measures that Colorado and other states are implementing.

Speaking at the Louisiana Democratic Party's annual gala Friday, Harris called for Democrats to embrace "leaders who have moral conviction and know how to fight" and emphasized "ruthlessness" in party strategy (Washington Examiner). The remarks signal she's testing messaging for a comeback after her 2024 defeat.

More telling: Harris has begun attacking proof-of-citizenship voting requirements, criticizing the SAVE America Act's voter registration safeguards (Breitbart). Republicans and election integrity advocates argue such measures are essential to prevent fraud and protect ballot legitimacy. Colorado conservatives note that voter ID and citizenship verification enjoy broad public support—recent polling shows majorities of Americans, including independents, back these protections.

Democrats counter that citizenship requirements allegedly suppress turnout among eligible voters, though Republicans dispute this framing, noting that legal citizens face no burden proving citizenship.

Harris's dual message—"ruthlessness" paired with opposition to election security—reveals the Democratic playbook heading into 2028: energize the base with combative rhetoric while fighting state-level election integrity reforms.
