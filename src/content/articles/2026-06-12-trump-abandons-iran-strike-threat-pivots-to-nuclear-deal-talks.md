# Trump Pivots on Iran: Deal Talk Replaces Military Threats

President Trump is abandoning military posturing in favor of direct negotiations with Iran—a sharp reversal that raises questions about American leverage in the region.

After threatening Thursday morning to strike Iran "VERY HARD" and seize its Kharg Island oil facility, Trump canceled the operation by afternoon following signals that Tehran would engage in substantive talks (Washington Examiner). A senior Trump administration official told Breitbart News the president is "closing in on a final agreement" that would genuinely prevent Iran from acquiring nuclear weapons—a direct contrast to the Obama-era Iran nuclear deal, which conservatives argue failed to stop Tehran's weapons ambitions.

The whiplash reflects competing pressures: Trump's stated goal of securing a verifiable non-proliferation agreement versus concerns that canceling military action signals weakness. One Washington Examiner analysis suggests Trump's "leverage is spent," while Breitbart frames the negotiations as Trump executing "the art of the deal."

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets watch Kharg Island closely. Any Iran deal affecting global oil supplies could influence Colorado's energy sector and gas prices for state consumers.

**What to watch:** Whether Trump's final Iran agreement includes the verification mechanisms and enforcement teeth that the 2015 JCPOA lacked—or whether this becomes another toothless diplomatic gesture.
