# Border Enforcement Nets 735 Criminal Aliens in Houston; Colorado Should Demand Same Scrutiny

ICE agents in Houston arrested 735 criminal illegal aliens in May alone, according to Breitbart, with those individuals accounting for more than 1,700 criminal convictions—nearly 1,200 involving violent crimes. The arrests included murderers, rapists, child predators, arsonists, drug traffickers, and gang members from MS-13, Surenos 13, 18th Street, and Tango Blast.

The scale of the enforcement action underscores what conservatives have long argued: the southern border remains a conduit for serious criminal activity that federal immigration authorities can and do intercept when resources are deployed. Republicans contend that sanctuary policies in cities like Denver and Boulder actively obstruct this work, shielding criminal aliens from deportation and putting Colorado residents at risk.

Democrats and progressive advocates counter that focusing on criminal deportations obscures the broader immigration debate and that local law enforcement should not be conscripted into federal immigration enforcement. They argue sanctuary policies protect vulnerable immigrant communities from over-policing.

For Colorado taxpayers and families, the question is stark: How many criminal aliens are operating in Colorado's cities and counties because local officials refuse to cooperate with ICE? Conservatives should demand their state and local leaders disclose exactly how many criminal aliens have been released or protected under sanctuary policies—and why.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature moves to strengthen or weaken state-level cooperation with federal immigration enforcement.
