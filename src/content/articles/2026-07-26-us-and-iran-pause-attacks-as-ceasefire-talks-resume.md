# U.S., Iran Step Back From Brink as Ceasefire Talks Resume

The U.S. and Iran have paused military operations for a second consecutive day, signaling a potential de-escalation after days of tit-for-tat strikes that risked broader regional conflict.

According to 9News Denver, both sides have halted attacks amid renewed efforts to negotiate an interim ceasefire deal. The operational pause marks a shift from the rapid escalation cycle that had threatened to draw American forces into a wider confrontation.

Just The News reports the U.S. military is holding off on further strikes, though the specific reasoning behind the pause remains unclear. The development suggests diplomatic channels may be gaining traction after the cycle of Iranian and American military responses.
