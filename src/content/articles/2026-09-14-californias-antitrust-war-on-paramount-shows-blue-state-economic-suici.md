# California's Antitrust Crusade Against Paramount Merger Shows Why Blue States Drive Business Away

California Democrats are weaponizing antitrust law to block the Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery merger, the *Washington Examiner* reports, in what conservatives view as ideologically driven economic self-sabotage that should serve as a cautionary tale for Colorado policymakers.

Democrat-run states are "holding Paramount hostage," according to the *Examiner*, using regulatory obstruction to prevent a major corporate consolidation. The strategy reflects the left's broader hostility toward business consolidation and market-driven deals—regardless of consumer impact or competitive merit.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that aggressive antitrust enforcement by blue-state attorneys general prioritizes ideology over economic growth. When states weaponize regulatory power to block legitimate mergers, they drive capital, jobs, and headquarters to business-friendly jurisdictions. California's approach exemplifies why the state hemorrhages companies and talent to Texas, Florida, and other low-regulation alternatives.

**The Colorado connection:** As Colorado competes for tech, media, and entertainment investment, state lawmakers should resist the temptation to follow California's regulatory playbook. Heavy-handed antitrust enforcement and business-hostile policies make states less attractive to corporate headquarters and job creators.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature attempts similar antitrust or merger-blocking measures that could further damage the state's business climate.
