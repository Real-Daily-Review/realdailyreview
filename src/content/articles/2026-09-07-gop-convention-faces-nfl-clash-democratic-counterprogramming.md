# GOP Midterm Convention Faces Tough Competition—and Democratic Counterprogramming

Colorado Republicans should pay attention: the first-ever GOP midterm convention in Dallas is getting squeezed from both sides—NFL football and Democratic opposition.

Major TV networks remain noncommittal about covering the convention, according to the New York Post, as the NFL season opener directly competes for eyeballs. Meanwhile, Democrats aren't sitting idle. Just The News reports that Democratic operative Talarico is organizing a food drive "counterprogramming" event timed to coincide with the convention's opening day, a transparent attempt to dominate local media coverage and distract from Republican messaging.

Republicans argue the convention represents a critical moment to unify the party ahead of midterm races—including Colorado's competitive congressional and statewide contests. The timing conflict with football, however, threatens to fragment the audience conservatives need to energize voters and donors.

The Democratic counterprogramming move underscores how aggressively the left fights for narrative control, even during opposition party events. Colorado conservatives should watch whether national GOP leadership effectively pivots messaging strategy to cut through the noise and reach voters who'll decide whether Republicans reclaim ground in purple Colorado.
