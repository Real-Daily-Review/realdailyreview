# Marco Rubio's Venezuela Role Draws Left-Wing Fire

Secretary of State Marco Rubio's aggressive stance on Venezuela has made him a prime target for progressive activists who view his hardline approach as a threat to their foreign policy agenda.

According to *National Review*, the left has identified Rubio as "the foremost threat to their project," launching coordinated attacks on his leadership at State. *The Daily Wire* reports that Rubio has assumed an outsized role in shaping Venezuela policy from Washington, overseeing sanctions regimes, managing the country's oil revenue flows, and maintaining direct contact with Venezuela's acting leadership.

Republicans and conservatives defend Rubio's approach as necessary pushback against socialist expansion in the Western Hemisphere. They argue that strong sanctions and active diplomacy are essential tools to counter authoritarian regimes and protect American interests in Latin America.

Democrats and progressive groups counter that Rubio's interventionist stance risks destabilizing the region and undermines diplomatic channels. They contend his influence over Venezuela's economic management exceeds appropriate bounds for a U.S. official.

For Colorado, Venezuela policy carries indirect weight: oil markets, energy prices, and Latin American trade patterns ripple through the state's economy and energy sector.
