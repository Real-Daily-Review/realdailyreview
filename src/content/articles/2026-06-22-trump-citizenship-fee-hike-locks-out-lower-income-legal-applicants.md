# Trump Administration Proposes Steep Citizenship Application Fee Hike

The Trump administration is pushing to raise citizenship application fees by $570 while eliminating reduced-fee options and waivers—a move that will price out lower-income applicants seeking legal status.

According to 9News Denver, the plan targets both citizenship forms and would remove fee-waiver provisions currently available to those who cannot afford full costs. The administration frames the increase as cost-recovery for processing, but critics argue it effectively creates a financial barrier to naturalization.

**The conservative case is split here.** National Review notes the administration is "dismantling decades of woke racial discrimination" in other areas, but the citizenship fee hike raises questions about whether higher barriers serve conservative immigration-enforcement goals or simply burden legal applicants trying to follow the rules. Republicans traditionally support legal immigration pathways—just controlled and orderly ones.

**For Colorado:** The state's growing Hispanic population includes many legal permanent residents weighing naturalization. Higher fees could slow citizenship rates in Denver, Colorado Springs, and agricultural regions dependent on documented workers.

Conservatives should watch whether this policy survives legal challenge and whether it actually reduces applications or simply shifts costs onto working families already navigating the legal system.
