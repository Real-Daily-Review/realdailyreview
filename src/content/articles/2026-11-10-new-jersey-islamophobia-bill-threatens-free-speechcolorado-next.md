# New Jersey 'Islamophobia' Bill Signals Dangerous Assault on Free Speech—Colorado Should Take Note

Democrats in New Jersey are pushing legislation that would criminalize speech deemed "Islamophobic," marking the first state-level attempt to weaponize identity politics against First Amendment protections (Washington Examiner). The bill would allow prosecutors and plaintiffs to use a government-defined standard of "Islamophobia" in criminal cases and civil litigation—a blueprint for chilling speech nationwide.

Conservatives argue this represents exactly the kind of progressive overreach that erodes individual liberty. By codifying subjective offense into law, Democrats create a tool for selective prosecution based on political viewpoint rather than conduct. The bill's expansive definition of prohibited speech—including "severe" statements about Islam—leaves ordinary citizens and commentators vulnerable to legal harassment for legitimate criticism or debate.

Colorado conservatives should watch this closely. If New Jersey succeeds, expect similar bills to land in the Colorado legislature, where Democrats control both chambers. Such laws would threaten Coloradans' ability to speak freely on religion, immigration, national security, and other contentious topics without fear of state-sponsored litigation.

The First Amendment protects unpopular speech precisely because government cannot be trusted to define which ideas are acceptable. This New Jersey gambit proves why.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats introduce copycat legislation in the 2025 session.**
