# Biden DOI Celebrates 'Two-Spirit' Pride Month While Colorado Energy Projects Face Delays

The Biden Department of Interior is using federal resources to promote "Two-Spirit People" Pride Month initiatives—a move conservatives say exemplifies wasteful DEI spending while Colorado's energy sector languishes under regulatory gridlock.

The Daily Caller reports the DOI's Pride Month push as symptomatic of broader ideological priorities that distract from the department's core mission: managing public lands, water resources, and energy development. Republicans argue the federal government should focus on permitting oil, gas, and mineral projects that support Colorado's economy and energy independence, not internal activism campaigns.

National Review frames the issue as part of a larger pattern of progressive institutional capture, questioning whether federal agencies are serving taxpayers or advancing a narrow ideological agenda.

For Colorado specifically, the timing stings. While the DOI celebrates internal initiatives, conservative critics note that federal land-use decisions, water compacts, and energy permits affecting Colorado ranchers, miners, and oil workers face months-long delays. Republicans contend that bloated bureaucracies consumed by DEI mandates move slower on projects that create jobs and generate tax revenue.

Conservatives argue the DOI should redirect resources toward streamlining permitting, not expanding diversity initiatives.

Watch whether Colorado's Republican delegation demands accountability for permit backlogs and whether the 2026 election makes federal land management a campaign issue.
