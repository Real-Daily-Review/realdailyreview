# Peru Plane Crash Kills 13 European Tourists

A sightseeing aircraft operated by Peruvian company Aerodiana crashed into a field near Peru's Nazca Lines on Saturday, killing all 13 European tourists aboard (9News Denver, New York Post). The plane had departed from the city of Ica for a viewing flight of the UNESCO-listed archaeological site when it went down.

Peruvian authorities are investigating the cause. The crash underscores ongoing safety concerns in regional aviation operations across South America, where smaller tour operators sometimes operate with minimal regulatory oversight.
