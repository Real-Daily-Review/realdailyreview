# Trump Claws Back $1B in Federal Spending—Without Asking Congress First

President Trump is using a rarely invoked power to rescind nearly $1 billion in congressionally approved spending, targeting DEI programs, immigration initiatives, and foreign aid—a move that has rattled even some Republicans and raises questions about executive overreach.

The administration's "pocket rescission" allows Trump to withhold funds for 45 days while Congress decides whether to approve, reject, or ignore the clawback (Just The News). The rescission targets $810 million in immigration and refugee programs, foreign debt relief, and business grants (Washington Examiner). This marks only the second pocket rescission in 50 years—both issued by Trump.

**The Republican split:** Sen. Susan Collins, the GOP chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee, blasted the move for lacking "warning or consultation" (9News Denver). Conservatives, however, argue the spending cuts align with Trump's mandate to slash wasteful federal outlays and eliminate progressive initiatives like DEI programs.

**The Colorado angle:** Federal spending cuts could affect Colorado nonprofits and contractors relying on those grants, though the specific Colorado impact remains unclear from available details.

**What to watch:** Whether Congress moves to block or approve the rescission within the 45-day window—and whether this tactic becomes Trump's new playbook for bypassing legislative budget authority.
