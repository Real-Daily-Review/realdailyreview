# Omar's Staff Altered Birth Records—Colorado Voters Should Demand Answers on Election Integrity

Minnesota Democrat Rep. Ilhan Omar's congressional staff reportedly had her birth year changed in official state records from 1981 to 1982 in May 2019, shortly after she took office, according to emails reviewed by the New York Post and reported by Just The News, The Daily Wire, and Breitbart.

The alteration raises serious questions about election integrity and eligibility. The Constitution requires House members to be at least 25 years old. If Omar misrepresented her age to secure citizenship or meet eligibility requirements, that's a fundamental breach of trust—and potentially fraud.

**Republicans argue** this incident exposes how Democratic operatives exploit government systems with impunity. They're demanding answers: Why did Omar's staff change official records? What was the original birth year, and why? Did she lie on her citizenship application or House candidacy forms?

**Democrats have largely remained silent**, treating the story as a right-wing distraction rather than addressing the substance.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. Election integrity isn't partisan—it's foundational. If a sitting congresswoman can alter her official records without consequence, what confidence can Colorado voters have in the integrity of our own elections and vetting processes?

Watch whether Congress demands a formal investigation or whether Democratic leadership continues stonewalling.
