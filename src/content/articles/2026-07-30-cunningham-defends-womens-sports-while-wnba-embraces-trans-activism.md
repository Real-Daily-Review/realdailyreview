# WNBA Star Cunningham Stands Firm on Women's Sports While League Doubles Down on Activism

Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham has drawn fire from progressive activists—and fellow WNBA players—for defending the exclusion of biological males from women's sports, a position conservatives say reflects common sense that the left increasingly refuses to articulate.

Days after Cunningham's comments, Seattle Storm center Stefanie Dolson responded by wearing a "Trans Rights Are Human Rights" shirt as part of an Alaska Airlines-sponsored promotional campaign, according to The Daily Wire. The move signals the WNBA's institutional embrace of transgender activism over competitive fairness.

**The conservative case:** Republicans and sports advocates argue that biological sex differences in strength, speed, and endurance create insurmountable competitive advantages—a reality Cunningham was willing to state plainly. Breitbart notes that Cunningham is "saying what Democrats won't," highlighting how progressive politicians have largely abandoned defending women's sports categories as a distinct category worthy of protection.

**The left's position:** Transgender activists and their allies frame restrictions on trans athletes as discrimination, framing inclusion as a human rights issue rather than a competitive fairness question.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado has no statewide ban on trans athletes in school sports, leaving the question to individual school districts—a patchwork that leaves parents and female athletes uncertain about fairness standards.
