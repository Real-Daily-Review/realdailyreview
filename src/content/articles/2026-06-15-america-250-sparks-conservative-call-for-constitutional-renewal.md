# America 250 Celebration Underway as Nation Marks Founding Milestone

Colorado conservatives should welcome a renewed focus on American exceptionalism and founding principles as the nation marks 250 years since 1776.

The America 250 commemoration is drawing attention from across the right, with *National Review* emphasizing spiritual renewal grounded in "In God we trust," while *The Daily Wire* frames the milestone as an opportunity to reassert American strength and constitutional governance. Both outlets stress that preserving the republic requires vigilance against progressive erosion of founding ideals.

For Colorado, the moment offers a chance to recalibrate state and local policy around constitutional limits on government power—a principle increasingly tested by Denver's progressive legislature and regulatory overreach in energy, water management, and land use. The 250th anniversary provides a natural inflection point for conservatives to challenge expansionist government and demand accountability from elected officials who've drifted from limited-government principles.

The celebration also underscores the importance of defending Second Amendment rights, property rights, and economic freedom—cornerstones of American liberty that Colorado's Democratic majority has repeatedly targeted through gun restrictions, environmental regulations, and tax increases.

Watch for how Colorado's Republican leadership leverages America 250 messaging to build momentum for 2026 elections and challenge progressive governance at the state level.
