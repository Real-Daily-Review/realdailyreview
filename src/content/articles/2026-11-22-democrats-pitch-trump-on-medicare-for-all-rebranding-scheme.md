# Democrats Court Trump on Medicare for All With Rebranding Scheme

Democrats are openly pitching President Trump on a Medicare for All takeover—if he'll let them slap his name on it. Sen.-elect Peggy Flanagan (D-MN) and incoming Michigan Senator Abdul El-Sayed told national media this week that they'll call government-run healthcare "TrumpCare" if the president signs on, according to reporting from the Washington Examiner and Breitbart.

This is a naked attempt to exploit Trump's brand while advancing a progressive policy that would devastate Colorado's healthcare market and job base. Medicare for All means government rationing, price controls, and the elimination of private insurance—a radical restructuring that would displace thousands of Colorado healthcare workers and force patients into a one-size-fits-all federal system.

Republicans and conservatives have long warned that single-payer healthcare destroys innovation, extends wait times, and hands Washington bureaucrats control over life-and-death medical decisions. The fact that Democrats are willing to rebrand their ideology as "TrumpCare" to pass it reveals their cynicism: the policy hasn't changed; only the marketing has.

Colorado taxpayers and small-business owners who rely on competitive private insurance should watch whether Trump entertains this pitch—and demand their representatives in Congress reject any federal healthcare power grab, regardless of whose name adorns it.
