# Fauci Invokes Fifth Over 100 Times as Senate Republicans Demand Answers on Pandemic Response

Dr. Anthony Fauci's refusal to answer basic questions about his role in the COVID-19 response raises serious accountability questions for Colorado taxpayers who funded his agency.

During a contentious Senate Homeland Security Committee hearing, Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times, according to *Just The News*, while his attorney was ejected from the room after disrupting proceedings (*Fox News*). Republican senators, led by Sen. Rand Paul, pressed Fauci on pandemic origins and federal health agency decisions—questions the former NIAID director repeatedly dodged.

The stonewalling prompted Florida's attorney general to launch an investigation into Fauci for "lack of candor" at the hearing. Republicans argue that Fauci's silence suggests consciousness of guilt regarding decisions that shaped lockdowns, school closures, and vaccine mandates affecting millions of Americans. Democrats have largely remained silent on the specifics, instead defending Fauci's pandemic-era authority.

For Colorado, this matters: federal health bureaucrats like Fauci wielded enormous power over state policy, business operations, and individual liberty—all without meaningful congressional oversight or transparency. Coloradans deserve to know whether their tax dollars funded decisions made in bad faith or based on incomplete science.

Watch whether congressional Republicans push for subpoena power to compel Fauci's testimony or whether the Left successfully shields him from further accountability.
