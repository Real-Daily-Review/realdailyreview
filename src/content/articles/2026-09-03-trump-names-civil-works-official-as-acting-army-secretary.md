# Trump Taps Civil Works Official as Acting Army Secretary

President Trump has named Adam Telle, currently assistant secretary of the Army for civil works, as acting Army secretary following Dan Driscoll's departure this week (Washington Examiner). Telle's background in civil works—overseeing Army Corps of Engineers projects, water infrastructure, and land management—gives him direct experience with federal programs that touch Colorado's water rights, dam operations, and public lands.

The move signals Trump's intent to reshape Army leadership around his policy priorities. Conservatives have long criticized the Army Corps for regulatory overreach and environmental activism that stalls infrastructure projects and water development. Telle's civil works portfolio means he understands the tension between federal mandates and state water needs—a critical issue for Colorado farmers, municipalities, and energy producers who depend on Colorado River allocations and Corps-managed reservoirs.

Democrats have not yet publicly challenged the appointment, though Senate confirmation will be required for a permanent secretary role. Republicans view the leadership change as an opportunity to streamline military bureaucracy and prioritize infrastructure efficiency over progressive environmental constraints.

**Watch for:** Whether Telle's confirmation hearing surfaces debates over the Army Corps' regulatory stance on Colorado water projects and Western development.
