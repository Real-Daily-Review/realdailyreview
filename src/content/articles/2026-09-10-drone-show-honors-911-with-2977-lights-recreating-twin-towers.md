# Drone Light Show Honors 9/11 Victims With Powerful Twin Towers Tribute

A striking drone display lit Manhattan's skyline Wednesday night, recreating the Twin Towers with 2,977 individual lights—one for each person killed on September 11, 2001 (9News Denver, The Daily Wire).

The installation was developed collaboratively with the 9/11 community, including victim families, survivors, and first responders, ensuring the tribute honored those most affected by the attacks. The synchronized aerial performance demonstrated how technology can serve remembrance and national unity around shared tragedy.

The display reflects a broader American commitment to memorializing 9/11—a moment that transcended partisan lines and reminded the nation of the stakes of national security and the sacrifice of first responders. For Coloradans, many of whom lost neighbors and colleagues in the attacks or served in the wars that followed, such tributes reinforce why vigilance against terrorism and support for our military and law enforcement remain non-negotiable.

The drone show also underscores the legitimate role of commemorative public events that bring Americans together around shared values—a stark contrast to the divisive identity politics that increasingly fractures our culture.
