# Front Range Rail Tax: Colorado Voters Face Billion-Dollar Boondoggle Dressed Up as Progress

Colorado taxpayers are being asked to bankroll yet another government megaproject—the Front Range rail system—while state officials deploy millions in public money to sell them on the idea.

According to CPR News, the pro-rail campaign has already burned through millions in tax dollars on paid Instagram influencers and targeted mailers promoting the so-called Colorado Connector. That's right: government is using your money to convince you to give them more of your money.

Complete Colorado lays out the conservative case bluntly: voters face a choice between "blowing billions on a train few will ride" or keeping that money in their own pockets. Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue the project exemplifies wasteful government overreach—massive spending on infrastructure most Coloradans won't use, funded by regressive taxation that hits working families and small businesses hardest.

The rail authority's reliance on paid influencer campaigns to manufacture consent reveals the weakness of the pitch itself. If the Colorado Connector were genuinely popular or economically sound, it wouldn't need six-figure social-media campaigns bankrolled by taxpayers.

Democrats and project backers counter that rail investment drives economic development and reduces congestion, but conservatives remain skeptical of government's track record managing transit projects.

Watch whether Colorado voters reject this tax grab—and whether the rail authority doubles down on propaganda spending if the measure fails.
