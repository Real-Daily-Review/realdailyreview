# Fauci's Own Diary Reveals He Privately Doubted the Lab-Leak Dismissal He Publicly Pushed

Anthony Fauci's private diaries, now public, expose a stunning contradiction: the nation's top pandemic official privately entertained the lab-leak theory while publicly dismissing it—and his own notes show the scientists he consulted largely agreed deliberate insertion was possible.

According to *Just The News*, ten of twelve scientists on a call that produced the influential "Proximal Origin" paper believed "deliberate insertion was possible" regarding COVID-19's spike protein. Yet Fauci's public statements consistently downplayed the lab-leak hypothesis, effectively closing off serious scientific debate during the pandemic's critical early months.

The *Daily Caller* reports Fauci's diary entries show him "staring at the furin-cleavage site and talking about deliberate insertion"—private musings that contradict his public posture. The *Washington Examiner* adds that Fauci's writings reveal a man "fixated on his growing celebrity" while grappling with what he believed was a "chaotic, leaderless" pandemic response consumed by internal rivalries.
