# Colorado Pavilion Wins at D.C. State Fair, But Democratic Governors Boycott Patriotic Event

Colorado's pavilion earned "best in show" honors at the Great American State Fair on the National Mall, a celebration of America's 250th birthday—but the event exposed a stark partisan divide over basic patriotism.

According to CPR News, Colorado was among 10 states whose pavilions received top honors at the July event. Yet the Washington Examiner reports that multiple Democratic governors refused to allow their states to participate in what was meant to be a unifying celebration of American federalism and state pride.

Conservatives argue the boycott reveals the left's willingness to weaponize even nonpartisan civic events. Republicans like Dave McCormick and John Fetterman, the Examiner notes, embraced the fair's spirit, while some Democratic governors—notably Pennsylvania's Josh Shapiro—declined participation, treating a patriotic exhibition as politically suspect.

For Colorado, the win underscores the state's ability to showcase its natural resources, outdoor heritage, and economic vitality on a national stage. It's a reminder that when Colorado competes fairly, it wins.

Taxpayers and conservatives should watch whether Democratic-led states continue to retreat from unifying national events—and whether Colorado's leadership uses such platforms to highlight energy independence, water stewardship, and economic freedom.
