# White House Finally Releases Iran Deal Text After Days of Secrecy and Conflicting Claims

The Trump administration has released the text of its memorandum of understanding with Iran, ending days of opacity that fueled legitimate questions about what the U.S. actually agreed to. Vice President JD Vance had promised the text would be public "by Friday at the latest," according to *Just The News*, though the White House initially denied that leaked versions circulating in media outlets were accurate, per *Breitbart*.

A senior Trump official read the agreement to reporters on Wednesday afternoon, as *The Daily Wire* reported, after the administration faced mounting pressure to show its hand. The deal reportedly reopens the Strait of Hormuz "immediately" and creates a framework conditioning Iranian benefits on ending its nuclear weapons program.

Conservatives have rightly demanded transparency. *National Review* and the *Washington Examiner* editorial board both argued that full public disclosure is the only way to settle conflicting claims about the deal's terms. The *Examiner* called the administration's initial delay "scandalous," noting that leaked versions—if authentic—raised serious concerns about what America conceded.
