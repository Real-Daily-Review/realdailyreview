# Alleged Confession Note Exposed in Charlie Kirk Murder Trial

A handwritten letter allegedly written by Tyler Robinson, 23, was accidentally displayed during a Colorado murder trial Thursday—potentially compromising the judicial process in a high-profile case targeting a conservative activist.

The *New York Post* reports Robinson allegedly hid the note under a computer keyboard.
