# New York AG Debate Exposes Stark Divide Between Progressive Prosecutor and Conservative Challenger

New York Democratic Attorney General Letitia James stumbled badly during Thursday's debate when asked to name a former AG she admired—drawing several seconds of silence before pivoting awkwardly (Fox News). Her Republican challenger, by contrast, delivered what observers called a "zinger for the ages," highlighting the philosophical gulf between the two candidates.

The contrast deepened on a second question about recent reading. James cited "Unthinkable," Rep. Jamie Raskin's January 6 memoir, while her GOP opponent named a children's book she reads to her 2-year-old (The Daily Wire). The exchange crystallized competing visions: James's focus on Trump-era grievances versus her challenger's emphasis on everyday family life and practical governance.

For Colorado conservatives watching national politics, the debate underscores how progressive prosecutors like James have weaponized the attorney general's office against political opponents—a cautionary tale as Colorado voters evaluate their own state AG races. James's record of targeting Trump and allies aligns with the prosecutorial overreach conservatives warn against.

Republicans argue James represents a troubling trend of using law enforcement for partisan ends; Democrats defend aggressive prosecution of January 6 participants and Trump associates as defending democracy.

Watch whether this debate gaffe gains traction in New York's competitive AG race—and whether Colorado Republicans cite it as a warning against electing similar-minded prosecutors statewide.
