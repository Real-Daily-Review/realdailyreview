# Colorado Democrats Censure Gov. Polis Over Election Denier's Commutation

Colorado's Democratic Party formally censured Gov. Jared Polis on Wednesday for commuting the sentence of Tina Peters, a former county clerk convicted in connection with a plot to promote false 2020 election claims, according to NPR and The Hill.

Polis reduced Peters' nearly nine-year sentence to 4.5 years, making her eligible for release next month. The state party voted to rebuke the decision during a virtual meeting, with 90 percent of attendees supporting the censure, The Hill reported.

Peters was convicted over her role in efforts to undermine confidence in Colorado's 2020 election results. Her case drew national attention as part of broader legal fallout from post-election conspiracy theories.

The commutation marks a rare break between Polis and his party base. A formal censure, while symbolic rather than legally binding, represents significant internal party discord and signals Democratic frustration with the governor's clemency decision.

Watch for whether Polis responds publicly to the censure and whether the episode affects his standing among Colorado Democrats ahead of future political contests.
