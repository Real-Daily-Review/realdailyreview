# GOP Rep. Gooden Faces House Ethics Review; Calls Probe 'Baseless'

Texas Republican Lance Gooden is under investigation by the House Ethics Committee after a referral from the Office of Congressional Conduct in June 2026, according to Fox News and the Washington Examiner. The committee's public statement, issued jointly by Chairman Michael Guest (R-MS) and ranking member Mark DeSaulnier (D-CA), did not disclose specific allegations.

Gooden has pushed back hard, blasting the investigation as "baseless," per Fox News. The lack of transparency around the allegations has drawn criticism from conservatives who argue the ethics process itself has become weaponized against Republicans. Republicans contend that vague referrals and delayed disclosures undermine due process and allow political enemies to damage lawmakers through innuendo rather than evidence.

Democrats and the ethics panel have not detailed what prompted the review, making it difficult to assess the merits of the case. The committee's silence fuels conservative skepticism about whether the process is being used as a political tool rather than a genuine accountability mechanism.

This case reflects broader Republican concerns about the House Ethics Committee's independence and fairness—concerns that have intensified as both parties face scrutiny. Coloradans should watch whether the committee releases specific charges and evidence, or whether this becomes another example of process-heavy investigations that damage reputations without transparency.
