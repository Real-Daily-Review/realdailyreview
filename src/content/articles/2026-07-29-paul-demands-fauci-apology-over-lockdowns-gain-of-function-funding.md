# Rand Paul Confronts Fauci Over Lockdowns, Gain-of-Function Funding in Heated Committee Hearing

Sen. Rand Paul opened fire on Anthony Fauci this week, declaring the COVID lockdowns could never have been "inflicted" on Americans without the former NIAID director's influence—and demanding an apology over his role in funding gain-of-function research in China (Just The News).

Paul, chairing the Senate Homeland Security Committee, pressed Fauci on decades of policy decisions that conservatives argue devastated the economy, shuttered schools, and trampled civil liberties. Republicans say Fauci's unchecked authority during the pandemic set a dangerous precedent for government overreach that Colorado families and businesses are still recovering from.

The hearing grew contentious when Paul ejected Fauci's lawyer, David Schertler, after repeated disruptions (Daily Caller). Paul also accused Democrats of stonewalling: ranking Democrat Gary Peters had claimed GOP leadership was hiding evidence, but Paul countered that Democrats never used the access code Republicans provided to review the damning documentation (Just The News).

For Colorado conservatives, this hearing underscores a broader concern—that unelected bureaucrats wielded unchecked power during the pandemic, costing jobs, harming children's education, and eroding trust in institutions. Taxpayers should watch whether Congress moves to strip future health officials of emergency powers without legislative oversight.
