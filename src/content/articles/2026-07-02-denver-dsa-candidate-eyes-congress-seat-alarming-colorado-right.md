# Denver Dem Socialist Eyes Congress Seat, Alarming Colorado Conservatives

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress. According to CPR News, Melat Kiros—a Democratic Socialists of America member—is positioning herself as a serious contender for the seat, signaling how far left Denver's political center has drifted.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this matters. A DSA-backed representative would likely push for expanded government spending, higher taxes on businesses and earners, and aggressive regulation—policies that would ripple across Colorado's economy and energy sector. Democratic socialists typically oppose market-driven solutions to healthcare, energy, and housing, instead favoring government control and redistribution.

Republicans argue that electing avowed socialists to Congress accelerates the erosion of free-market principles and individual liberty. They point to DSA positions on wealth taxes, nationalization rhetoric, and opposition to private enterprise as fundamentally at odds with Colorado's entrepreneurial tradition and working families' interests.

Democrats counter that the DSA represents grassroots frustration with inequality and corporate influence, offering a genuine alternative to establishment politics.

The race underscores a broader realignment in Colorado politics: as Denver becomes increasingly progressive, the state's Republican base in rural and suburban areas faces steeper headwinds in statewide contests.

**Watch whether Kiros gains mainstream Democratic Party backing—a signal of how normalized socialist politics have become in Colorado.**
