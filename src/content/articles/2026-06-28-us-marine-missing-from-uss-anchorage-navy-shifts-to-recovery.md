# Marine Missing from USS Anchorage; Navy Shifts to Recovery Operations

A U.S. Marine has gone missing from the USS Anchorage during training operations off the Southern California coast, prompting the Navy to transition from active search to recovery efforts after more than 40 hours of intensive searching (9News Denver, New York Post).

The Marine was reported missing shortly after 1 a.m. Thursday during what officials describe as routine training. The circumstances surrounding the disappearance remain unclear, and the Navy has not released additional details about the incident or the Marine's identity.

The USS Anchorage is a San Antonio-class amphibious transport dock—one of the Navy's largest vessels designed to carry Marines and equipment for expeditionary operations. The shift from search to recovery operations typically indicates authorities have limited hope of finding the service member alive, though the Navy has not formally confirmed this assessment.

This incident underscores ongoing questions about safety protocols aboard Navy vessels and the adequacy of oversight during training exercises. Colorado families with service members deployed should monitor official Navy communications for updates.

Watch for the Navy's formal incident report and any congressional inquiries into training safety standards aboard amphibious vessels.
