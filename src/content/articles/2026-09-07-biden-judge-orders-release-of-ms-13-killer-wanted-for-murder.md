# Biden Judge Frees MS-13 Killer, Raising Questions About Border Security and Colorado Safety

A Biden-appointed federal judge in California ordered ICE to release a machete-wielding MS-13 gang member wanted for murder in El Salvador, citing a procedural violation—a decision that underscores conservative concerns about judicial activism on immigration enforcement and public safety.

The convicted killer, known as "La Bruja" (the witch), had been held by ICE without a required bond hearing for five months before the judge's ruling (Fox News). Under Trump's 2025 designation of MS-13 as a foreign terrorist organization, such releases raise alarms about enforcement gaps.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and immigration hawks argue that judicial second-guessing of ICE detention decisions—even on procedural grounds—prioritizes legal technicalities over protecting Americans from violent criminals. They contend that catch-and-release policies, enabled by sympathetic judges, undermine border security and endanger communities nationwide, including Colorado.

**The broader concern:** Colorado law enforcement and residents have documented MS-13 activity in the state. Violent gang members operating freely across state lines directly threaten public safety and strain local resources already stretched thin.

Conservatives should watch whether similar rulings multiply under current judicial appointments and whether Colorado's congressional delegation demands accountability from federal judges releasing known murderers into American communities.
