# Former CIA Director's Son Arrested on Child Pornography Charges

Kyle Brennan, 40-year-old son of former CIA Director John Brennan, was arrested on federal child pornography charges following an FBI investigation that uncovered dozens of alleged illegal images, according to Fox News and the Daily Caller.

Prosecutors say the FBI's investigation led to the discovery of the material and subsequent arrest. Details on the specific jurisdiction and timing remain limited, though the case appears to be handled in the Eastern District of Virginia.

The arrest marks a significant development given John Brennan's prominent role in the Obama administration and his subsequent media presence as a vocal Trump critic. Brennan served as CIA Director from 2013 to 2017 and has remained a public political figure in recent years.

Federal child exploitation cases carry serious penalties, and the investigation appears to be ongoing through law enforcement channels.
