# Colorado Progressives Crush Moderate Democrats Despite $2.5M Dark-Money Blitz

Colorado's Democratic establishment just got a reality check: their money can't buy moderation anymore. Dark-money groups spent $2.5 million backing moderate Democratic candidates in eight statehouse primaries, and won just two races (Colorado Sun). Progressive challengers swept to victory across metro Denver, unseating sitting lawmakers including state Rep. Sean Camacho in House District 6, who lost to civil rights attorney Iris Halpern.

**What this means for Colorado:** The state legislature is tilting further left. Progressives now control the Democratic primary process, which in Colorado's blue districts amounts to controlling the general election. Expect more aggressive climate regulations, higher taxes on energy production, and stricter gun measures—all policies that hit Colorado's oil-and-gas workers, ranchers, and Second Amendment advocates hardest.

Republicans argue the primary results expose deep fractures in Colorado's Democratic coalition, but conservatives shouldn't celebrate yet. With Democrats holding supermajorities in both chambers, a more ideologically pure caucus means less negotiating room for GOP priorities on taxes, regulation, and individual liberty.

**Watch next:** Whether moderate Democrats can regroup for the general election, or whether the progressive wave carries straight through November.
