# Biden-Era Border Failures Keep Releasing Criminals Into America

The Biden administration's catch-and-release immigration policies have allowed multiple violent criminals to roam free—including a Guinea national accused of assaulting a teenager and a Honduran wanted for a 2016 massacre at a billiard hall, according to *Just The News*. A third case involved an Indian national who overstayed his visa and defrauded Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce in a $35 million Ponzi scheme before his conviction, *The Daily Wire* reports.

These cases expose a pattern: federal authorities apprehend illegal immigrants at the border or during routine traffic stops, only to release them into communities—sometimes without proper vetting or criminal background checks. The Honduran suspect was caught on a speeding violation before ICE discovered his wanted status for a violent crime. The Guinea national was released after 2023 apprehension at the Arizona border, with authorities now investigating whether additional victims exist.

Republicans argue the Biden administration's lax enforcement and sanctuary policies have created a public safety crisis, allowing dangerous criminals to victimize Americans. Democrats have largely resisted calls for stricter enforcement, framing immigration enforcement as inhumane.

Colorado communities—from Denver to the Western Slope—have absorbed significant illegal immigrant populations. Taxpayers should watch whether state and local law enforcement will demand federal cooperation on criminal background checks and deportation of convicted aliens.
