## Abbott Refers Airport Washing Stations to DOJ — Colorado Should Pay Attention

Texas Gov. Greg Abbott has asked the Justice Department to investigate what he calls "discriminatory" Islamic wudu washing stations at DFW and George Bush Intercontinental airports. Republicans argue that public airports — funded by taxpayers of all faiths and none — cannot constitutionally accommodate one religion's ritual practices while ignoring others. Colorado conservatives should watch this closely: Denver International Airport is one of the busiest in the country, and whatever legal precedent gets set in Texas will land squarely on Colorado's doorstep.

While the left pushes DEI mandates and ideological curricula across higher education, Mines remains a reminder that a university can still prioritize rigorous academics and workforce outcomes. Colorado taxpayers who fund higher education deserve more institutions that follow that model.
