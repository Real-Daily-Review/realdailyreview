# Washington Must Deliver Results, Not Just Rhetoric

Washington's appetite for partisan gridlock has worn out its welcome—and Colorado families and small businesses are tired of paying the price. A *Washington Examiner* opinion piece argues that voters have given leaders a mandate to govern, not grandstand, pointing to the 1990s budget deal between President Clinton and Speaker Gingrich as a model for bipartisan problem-solving that actually delivered results.

The piece contends that small business owners meeting payroll and working families stretching paychecks deserve more than campaign promises. They need elected officials willing to translate electoral victories into tangible progress—lower regulatory burden, fiscal discipline, and policies that let Americans keep more of what they earn.

For Colorado conservatives, this resonates sharply. Colorado's energy sector, agriculture, and small-business community have been hammered by federal overreach and regulatory uncertainty. Whether it's water policy, land-use decisions, or energy development, Coloradans need Washington to move beyond talking points and actually govern.

The real test: will Republican leaders in Congress prioritize fiscal restraint and deregulation, or will they cave to the spending lobby and corporate welfare schemes? Watch whether the new Congress cuts wasteful programs or simply reshuffles the deck.
