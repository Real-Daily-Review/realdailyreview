# Texas Democrat Backtracks on Woke Agenda as Senate Race Tightens

A Democratic Senate candidate in Texas is abandoning the progressive positions that defined his political brand—a sign that even blue-state activists know radical gender ideology is a losing issue.

He now says he "missed the mark" on those positions and backs banning transgender surgeries for minors and opposing trans athletes in girls' sports (Breitbart).

The reversal is telling. Talarico's earlier embrace of fringe gender theory—once mainstream within progressive circles—has become electoral poison even in competitive races. Republicans have made gender ideology central to their 2026 messaging, and voters across the country are rejecting it.

Voters in swing districts—particularly in the Western Slope and rural counties—are increasingly skeptical of progressive social experimentation.
