# Senate Republican Tillis Rebukes Trump Over 'Stupid Stuff' Hurting GOP

Sen. Thom Tillis (R-N.C.) publicly criticized President Trump on Friday, warning that the president's embrace of controversial policy ideas is damaging Republican midterm prospects.

Trump had attacked Tillis on Truth Social, calling him a "nitpicker" and "RINO" for questioning proposals including an $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization fund" and 50-year mortgages, according to The Hill. Tillis responded directly, telling the president that "the stupid stuff is killing our chances" in the midterms.

The retiring senator suggested Trump's advisers were providing poor policy guidance ahead of November elections, according to the Washington Examiner. Tillis characterized himself as offering constructive criticism rather than obstruction.

The exchange underscores ongoing tensions within the Republican Party over Trump's policy direction and messaging strategy as the party prepares for the midterm elections. Tillis, who has positioned himself as a centrist voice, has previously criticized Trump on various fronts.

Watch for whether other GOP senators publicly align with Tillis's concerns or whether the party rallies behind Trump's policy agenda heading into the midterms.
