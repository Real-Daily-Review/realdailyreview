# House Republicans Push $95B Spending Plan—But Conservatives Question the Price Tag

House Republicans have unveiled a $95 billion reconciliation package aimed at bypassing Democratic filibuster obstruction in the Senate, with the bulk of funding directed toward military operations against Iran (9News Denver). The Daily Wire reports the plan includes billions earmarked for conservative initiatives ahead of the midterm elections, allowing Republicans to advance measures like the SAVE America Act without needing 60 Senate votes.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue reconciliation is necessary because Democrats have weaponized the filibuster to block Republican legislation. However, fiscal hawks on the right will scrutinize whether a $95 billion spending increase—even for defense—aligns with calls for fiscal restraint and deficit reduction. Some conservatives question whether military spending abroad should take priority over domestic tax relief or reducing the national debt.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado defense contractors and military installations could benefit from increased defense spending, but Colorado taxpayers footing the bill deserve clarity on how this spending reduces the overall federal footprint.

Watch whether House conservatives demand offsets elsewhere in the budget or whether this becomes a template for larger spending packages that abandon fiscal discipline.
