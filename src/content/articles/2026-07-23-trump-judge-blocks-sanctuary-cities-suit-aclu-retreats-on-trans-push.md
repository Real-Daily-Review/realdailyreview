# Trump Judge Blocks DOJ Sanctuary Cities Suit; ACLU Retreats on Trans Bathroom Push

A Trump-appointed federal judge has gutted the Justice Department's lawsuit against Minnesota's sanctuary immigration policies, ruling the state cannot be forced to assist ICE enforcement—a major setback for the administration's crackdown on jurisdictions that shield illegal immigrants from federal authorities (Fox News Politics).

Meanwhile, the ACLU is quietly backpedaling after losing decisively at the Supreme Court on transgender sports bans. The civil rights group now argues that the high court's ruling protecting girls' sports doesn't apply to bathroom access cases, contradicting its own earlier legal theory (Just The News). Conservatives say the ACLU is trying to salvage a losing hand rather than accept the court's logic.
