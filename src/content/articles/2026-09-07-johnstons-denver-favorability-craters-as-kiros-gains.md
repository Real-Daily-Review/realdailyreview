# Denver Mayor Johnston's Favorability Slides as Challenger Gains Ground

Denver Mayor Mike Johnston's political standing is eroding, according to new polling cited by the *Denver Post*, as challenger Melat Kiros gains favorable ratings among voters. While majorities have historically backed Johnston's direction for the city, the trend shows softening support—a warning sign for a sitting executive heading into a competitive race.

The shift reflects voter frustration with Denver's persistent homelessness crisis, crime, and quality-of-life issues that have defined Johnston's tenure. Conservatives argue the mayor's progressive spending approach—including expanded social services and housing initiatives—has failed to deliver results while straining city finances and public safety. Republicans contend that Kiros's more pragmatic, results-oriented platform is resonating with Denver voters tired of ideological experiments.

Johnston's declining numbers mirror a broader pattern in purple-state politics: candidates across the country are now emphasizing cross-party appeal and bipartisan credentials to reach independent and swing voters (per the *Washington Examiner*). In Colorado's capital, that dynamic could reshape the 2027 mayoral race if Kiros successfully positions herself as the change candidate.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Johnston's team can stabilize support or if Kiros's momentum continues—a bellwether for how Denver voters view progressive governance heading into 2026 statewide races.
