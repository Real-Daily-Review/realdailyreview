# Trump Primetime Address on Elections Blocked by Major Networks

President Trump is set to deliver a primetime address Thursday on elections and voting machines, but ABC and NBC have refused to air it—a decision that raises fresh questions about media gatekeeping on politically sensitive topics.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, Trump plans to revisit concerns about past elections, including claims about his 2020 loss that remain unproven. ABC will stream the address on its digital platform and radio, but both ABC and NBC declined broadcast coverage, Just The News reports.

The networks' decision reflects the ongoing partisan divide over election integrity messaging. Republicans and Trump supporters argue that mainstream media outlets systematically suppress conservative viewpoints and election-related concerns, particularly those challenging 2020 outcomes. Democrats and their media allies counter that platforming unsubstantiated election claims spreads misinformation and undermines democratic confidence.

For Colorado voters, this episode underscores a broader concern: how information about elections reaches the public when major broadcasters curate what gets airtime. Colorado has experienced its own election disputes, and conservatives worry that media bias prevents full airing of legitimate election-integrity questions.
