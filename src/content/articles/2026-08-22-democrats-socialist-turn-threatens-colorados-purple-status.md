# Democrats' Socialist Drift Signals Danger for Colorado Moderates

Colorado Democrats are swimming against the national tide as their party lurches left. While Democratic leadership like House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries publicly rejects the Democratic Socialists of America agenda, polling shows most Democrats now view socialism more favorably than capitalism (Fox News Politics), signaling a fundamental shift in the party's base.

This ideological realignment matters for Colorado. The state's purple status depends on moderate Democrats and independents who still believe in market economics. Yet the party's grassroots increasingly embrace socialist policies—higher taxes, government-run healthcare, aggressive regulation—that would devastate Colorado's energy sector, ranching communities, and small businesses.

Republicans argue this socialist surge exposes the Democratic Party's true agenda: expanding government control over the economy and individual choice. Conservatives say Colorado voters should demand clarity from Democratic candidates on whether they embrace or reject socialism, higher taxes, and the regulatory overreach that comes with it.

National Review notes the party is fielding increasingly radical candidates in competitive races, suggesting Democrats are prioritizing ideological purity over electability—a miscalculation that could hand Republicans opportunities in purple states like Colorado.

**Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from socialism or embrace it—their answer will tell you everything about the 2026 midterms.**
