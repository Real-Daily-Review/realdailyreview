# Colorado Gun Buyers Flood Background Check System Before New Training Mandate Kicks In

Colorado gun owners are racing to purchase firearms before a new Democratic-backed safety training requirement takes effect, creating a historic surge in background checks that reveals public resistance to the law.

The Colorado Bureau of Investigation processed 59,452 gun background checks in July—nearly triple the 20,172 checks from July 2025—as Senate Bill 25-003's mandatory training requirement launched August 1 (CPR News). The 195% spike in applications signals that law-abiding Coloradans are front-loading purchases to avoid compliance costs and bureaucratic delays.

**Republicans and gun-rights advocates argue** the training mandate infringes on Second Amendment rights and creates barriers to firearm ownership for working families and low-income Coloradans. They contend the law doesn't address actual criminal behavior—felons don't submit to background checks—while punishing responsible gun owners with new fees and delays.

**Democrats counter** that safety training reduces accidents and improves responsible gun ownership, framing it as a common-sense measure supported by public opinion.

The data tells a different story: Colorado gun owners voted with their wallets, demonstrating they view the law as an unwelcome government intrusion worth circumventing before it took effect.

Watch whether the Colorado legislature faces pressure to repeal or modify SB 25-003 as enforcement costs and compliance complaints mount.
