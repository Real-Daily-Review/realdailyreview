# Trump Orders Strike That Kills Venezuelan Gang Leader Tied to U.S. Crime Wave

President Trump has taken direct action against transnational criminal networks that have ravaged American cities—including Colorado—by ordering a "swift and lethal kinetic strike" that killed Héctor Rusthenford Guerrero Flores, leader of the Tren de Aragua gang, according to multiple reports (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

The Venezuelan-based gang, designated a terrorist organization by the U.S., has been charged with ordering acts of violence and terrorism on American soil. The Justice Department had previously charged Guerrero Flores with directing and facilitating criminal operations in coordination with other cartels (Just The News).

The strike was executed by U.S. Southern Command in coordination with the Venezuelan government, marking a rare moment of hemispheric cooperation against organized crime.

**The Colorado angle:** Tren de Aragua has expanded operations across major U.S. cities, including Denver, where gang activity has spiked in recent years. The group's involvement in human trafficking, drug distribution, and violent crime directly threatens Colorado communities and strains local law enforcement resources already stretched thin by progressive bail and sentencing policies.

Conservatives argue this decisive military action demonstrates Trump's commitment to border security and public safety—priorities Colorado voters rank high, especially as Venezuelan gang activity has become a visible problem in Denver neighborhoods.
