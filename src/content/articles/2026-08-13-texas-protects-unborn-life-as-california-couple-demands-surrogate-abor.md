# Texas Judge Blocks California Couple's Abortion Plan; Surrogate Keeps Newborn

A Texas court has intervened in a surrogacy dispute that highlights the stark divide between blue-state and red-state abortion law. A California couple allegedly demanded their surrogate terminate a pregnancy after prenatal testing revealed a severe heart defect in the fetus. The surrogate, McKenna West, refused and fled to Texas—where she gave birth to baby Gabriel on Wednesday (Fox News Politics).

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton secured a legal victory enabling the child's life-saving heart surgery. West's lawyer argues she is the legal mother under Texas law: "If you give birth to a child, it's your child" (New York Post).

**The conservative case:** This case underscores why pro-life states matter. Texas law protected an unborn child's right to life when California's permissive abortion regime would have ended it. The surrogate's decision to carry the child to term—and Texas's willingness to defend that choice—saved a life.

**Democrats and the left** frame surrogacy disputes as reproductive autonomy issues, typically siding with biological parents' contractual claims over the carrier's bodily autonomy arguments.
