# Electoral College Under Siege: Left Pushes to Scrap Founding System

Colorado conservatives should brace for renewed attacks on the Electoral College—the constitutional safeguard that prevents a handful of high-population coastal states from dictating presidential elections to the rest of America.

Democrats are mounting what National Review calls "the plot to overthrow the Electoral College," recognizing that their power base concentrates in urban centers where progressive policies run unchecked. Complete Colorado notes the left understands exactly where its strength lies and is moving to eliminate the system that forces candidates to build geographically diverse coalitions.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** The Electoral College, unchanged since George Washington, ensures that Wyoming ranchers, Colorado farmers, and rural Americans retain meaningful influence in presidential politics. Without it, candidates would ignore entire regions, cementing coastal-elite control over national policy.

**Democrats counter** that the Electoral College is "undemocratic" because it can produce presidents who lose the popular vote—a feature, not a bug, designed to protect federalism and prevent tyranny of the majority.

For Colorado specifically, this matters: the state's swing status gives it outsized influence under the current system. Abolishing the Electoral College would reduce Colorado's relevance to a footnote in Democratic stronghold California's margin.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats push for National Popular Vote Interstate Compact participation—a backdoor attempt to neuter the Electoral College without a constitutional amendment.
