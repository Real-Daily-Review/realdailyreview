# National Democratic Ties to Communist Activists Raise Questions About Party Direction

A pattern of Democratic candidates maintaining ties to communist-aligned activists is raising alarms among conservatives about the party's ideological drift. Maine's Democratic Senate nominee Troy Jackson co-hosted a June campaign event with Lorri Nandrea, an activist with extensive writing credits at Communist Party USA publications (The Daily Wire). Meanwhile, a North Carolina Democrat House candidate attended a college known for communist sympathies and labor camp operations (Breitbart).

Republicans argue these connections reveal how far the Democratic Party has strayed from mainstream American values. While Democrats dismiss such associations as guilt-by-association attacks, conservatives counter that candidates should be held accountable for the company they keep and the platforms they share.

**Colorado angle:** As Democrats push for expanded government control over energy, water, and land use—policies conservatives warn mirror socialist overreach—these national ties underscore why Colorado voters should scrutinize candidates' actual ideological commitments. The state's energy sector, agriculture, and property rights depend on leaders who reject collectivist thinking.

Watch for whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from these national figures or embrace similar radical allies.
