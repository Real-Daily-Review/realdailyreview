# Rubio Surges Past Vance in Early 2028 Matchup—But Don't Count on Polls Yet

Secretary of State Marco Rubio has overtaken Vice President JD Vance in key early-state polling for 2028, reversing months of Vance's commanding national lead, according to recent surveys cited by the *Washington Examiner*. The shift marks a significant turn in early speculation about Trump's successor.

However, conservatives should pump the brakes on reading too much into current horse-race numbers. The *Washington Examiner* warns that early frontrunner status is notoriously unreliable—citing Rudy Giuliani's 2007 polling dominance before his collapse once voters learned his actual positions. Name recognition and media saturation often drive early polls far more than genuine support.

**What conservatives should watch:** Both Vance and Rubio represent different wings of the Trump coalition. Vance appeals to America First nationalists skeptical of foreign entanglements; Rubio draws establishment Republicans and hawkish foreign-policy conservatives. As the field develops and voters engage more seriously, positions on energy policy, border security, and government spending will matter far more than January 2025 polling.

Colorado conservatives should track where both candidates stand on federal land use, water rights, and energy development—issues that directly affect the state's economy and independence from Washington overreach.

Watch for substantive policy differentiation as 2028 heats up, not poll bounces.
