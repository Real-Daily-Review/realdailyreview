# Socialist 'Affordability' Agenda Proves Incompatible With Economic Reality

New York City's socialist mayor Zohran Mamdani is discovering what conservatives have long argued: you cannot regulate and tax your way to affordability. According to National Review, Mamdani's push to impose new government fees while claiming to champion "affordability" exposes the fundamental contradiction at the heart of progressive policy—socialism and genuine affordability are incompatible.

The Washington Examiner notes Mamdani is simultaneously banning tourist attractions, demagoguing businesses for passing along government-mandated costs to customers, and dismantling police units while reducing incarceration. These policies—hallmarks of the progressive left's approach to governance—consistently produce the opposite of their stated goals.

For Colorado, this serves as a cautionary tale. As progressive politicians in Denver and statewide push similar "affordability" schemes through regulation and spending, taxpayers should expect the same result: higher costs, fewer choices, and less freedom. When government mandates new fees and restrictions, businesses don't absorb them—customers do. Calling it "affordability" doesn't change the math.

Republicans argue that real affordability comes from removing regulatory barriers, lowering taxes, and unleashing free markets—not expanding government control.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt similar rhetoric while pursuing identical policies that will drive up costs for working families.**
