# Colorado State Patrol Trooper Hit by Suspected Drunk Driver on I-70

A Colorado State Patrol trooper is recovering after a suspected drunk driver rear-ended his patrol cruiser during a traffic stop on I-70 near Watkins, putting law enforcement safety back in the spotlight.

According to 9News Denver, the trooper and two others were injured when an SUV struck the patrol vehicle. KDVR Fox31 reported that a Jeep rear-ended the cruiser with enough force to push it into another vehicle, causing serious damage to the squad car's rear end.

The incident underscores the daily risks Colorado law enforcement faces—and raises hard questions about enforcement gaps. Colorado has struggled with DUI enforcement consistency across counties, and incidents like this remind taxpayers why fully staffed, well-equipped State Patrol units matter. Troopers conducting routine traffic stops are vulnerable to exactly these kinds of collisions, yet budget pressures and staffing shortages have hampered recruitment and retention across the state.

The suspected drunk driver's identity and charges have not yet been disclosed by authorities.
