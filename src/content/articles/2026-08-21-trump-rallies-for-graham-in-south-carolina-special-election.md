# Trump Rallies for Graham in South Carolina Special Election

Trump is hitting the campaign trail for Sen. Darline Graham Nordone (R-SC) at a Myrtle Beach rally Friday night, throwing his weight behind the late Sen. Lindsey Graham's sister as she seeks a full term in a competitive special election.
