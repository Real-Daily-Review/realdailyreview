# Barry Melrose, Hockey Legend and ESPN Analyst, Dead at 70

Barry Melrose, the former NHL player, coach, and ESPN hockey analyst who became a fixture of American sports broadcasting, has died at age 70. Melrose stepped away from his analyst role in 2023 after being diagnosed with Parkinson's disease, according to 9News Denver.

Melrose built a storied career across three decades in professional hockey—first as a player, then as a head coach in the NHL, and finally as a respected voice on ESPN's hockey coverage. His distinctive style and deep knowledge of the game made him a recognizable figure to Colorado hockey fans and national audiences alike.

The Parkinson's diagnosis marked the beginning of his withdrawal from public life. He had been a steady presence on ESPN's hockey broadcasts before stepping back to focus on his health.

Melrose's passing represents a loss for Colorado's sports community and the broader hockey world, where he was widely respected for his contributions to the sport both on and off the ice.
