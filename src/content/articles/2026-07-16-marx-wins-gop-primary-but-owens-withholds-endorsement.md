# Victor Marx Wins GOP Nod, But Colorado Republicans' Establishment Stays Cautious

Victor Marx, a first-time political candidate and nonprofit leader, has narrowly won Colorado's Republican primary for governor—but the state's last GOP chief executive isn't ready to back him yet.

Marx edged out state Sen. Barb Kirkmeyer in an unconventional campaign that surprised Colorado's political establishment (Denver Post). However, former Gov. Bill Owens—Colorado's last Republican governor—said he wants to observe Marx's general election performance over the next month before deciding whether to endorse him, contradicting Marx's claims of Owens' backing (Colorado Sun).

The narrow victory, with Marx holding roughly a 2,000-vote lead, underscores divisions within Colorado's Republican Party. Kirkmeyer, a veteran lawmaker, represented the more traditional conservative establishment wing. Marx's win signals grassroots frustration with the GOP's recent electoral struggles in Colorado, where Democrats now dominate statewide offices.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Owens and other establishment Republicans ultimately unite behind Marx or remain on the sidelines—a split could cripple GOP chances against the Democratic nominee in a state Republicans haven't won for governor since 2002.
