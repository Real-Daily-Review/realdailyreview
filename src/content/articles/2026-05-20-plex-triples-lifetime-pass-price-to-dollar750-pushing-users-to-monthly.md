# Plex Triples Lifetime Pass Price to $750, Pressuring Users to Monthly Subscriptions

Plex is raising the price of its lifetime subscription pass to $749.99 on July 1st, tripling the cost from its current $249.99 level, according to The Verge. The streaming service is giving customers until that date to lock in the existing rate before the increase takes effect.

This marks the second major price hike in roughly 14 months. Plex more than doubled the lifetime pass price from $119.99 to $249.99 last March, The Verge reports.

At the new price point, customers would need to subscribe for 11 years at current annual rates to justify the lifetime purchase cost, according to The Verge's analysis. The aggressive pricing appears designed to push users toward Plex's monthly subscription model instead.

Ars Technica reports that Plex has considered eliminating lifetime passes altogether, suggesting the company may be moving away from that business model entirely.

The move reflects a broader industry trend of streaming services shifting away from one-time purchases toward recurring subscription revenue. Plex offers free ad-supported streaming of personal media libraries alongside its paid tiers.

Watch for whether Plex follows through on discontinuing lifetime passes entirely after the July 1st deadline.
