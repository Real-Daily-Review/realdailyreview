# Colorado Weather Brief: Sunshine Today, Mountain Snow Midweek

Denver gets a brief respite from the wet pattern that hammered the state Monday, with afternoon sunshine and highs near 50 degrees expected Tuesday before the next system rolls in from the west.

According to 9News Denver, clearing skies will dominate the Front Range through Tuesday as the Monday storm system moves eastward. KDVR Fox31 reports early showers will clear quickly, leaving sunny conditions for the afternoon commute.

The reprieve is short-lived. Both outlets confirm another storm is already tracking toward Colorado from the Pacific, bringing renewed moisture and significant mountain snow by midweek. Coloradans should expect the pattern to shift back to unsettled conditions as the week progresses.

For ranchers and agricultural operations across the state, the back-to-back moisture is a mixed blessing—helpful for winter snowpack and spring runoff, but complicating field work and livestock management. Mountain communities and ski areas should monitor the incoming system for accumulation totals.

Watch for updated snowfall forecasts as the midweek storm approaches; mountain passes and high country could see substantial accumulation.
