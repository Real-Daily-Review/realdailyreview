# Senate Passes Toothless Iran War Powers Resolution

The Republican-controlled Senate passed a war powers resolution on Iran that sounds tough but lacks any real enforcement mechanism—a classic Washington performance piece that lets Democrats claim oversight while leaving presidential authority essentially intact.

The resolution, per the Daily Caller, declares "the president cannot continue this war of choice," yet includes no binding restrictions on military operations. Breitbart reports the measure formally limits Trump's military authority over Iran, but without teeth, the constraint is largely symbolic.

**The conservative case:** Republicans backing the resolution argue Congress must reassert its constitutional war powers authority. However, critics note the measure fails to actually *stop* anything—it's a messaging vote that allows senators to posture for their base without forcing hard choices or confronting the White House.

**What gets lost:** Colorado voters should ask whether their representatives voted for this resolution to genuinely check executive power or simply to claim they did. A war powers resolution without enforcement is political theater, not constitutional governance.

The real test comes next: will Congress follow up with actual legislation that cuts funding or imposes real limits, or was this a one-off vote designed to satisfy war-weary voters while changing nothing?
