# Biden-Era Iran Deal Leaves Colorado Energy Sector in the Cold

The Obama-Biden administration's new Memorandum of Understanding with Iran hands leverage to a hostile regime while undercutting American energy independence—a direct blow to Colorado's oil and gas workers already reeling from years of federal overreach.

National Review reports the MOU gives "momentum to Iran," ceding negotiating advantage to a state sponsor of terrorism. The deal, struck amid minimal public scrutiny, represents exactly the kind of foreign-policy capitulation conservatives warned against (National Review). Trump proved during his first term that Iran "never lost a negotiation," yet the current administration handed the regime concessions without securing meaningful restrictions on nuclear development or ballistic missiles.

For Colorado, the timing is toxic. As the state's energy sector fights federal leasing restrictions, methane regulations, and Biden-Harris climate mandates, this Iran deal signals Washington's continued hostility toward domestic energy production. While Colorado oil and gas companies compete globally, the administration negotiates away American leverage and props up a competitor.

Republicans argue the deal weakens U.S. deterrence in the Middle East and abandons allies like Israel and Saudi Arabia. Democrats counter that diplomacy prevents conflict—a position that rings hollow to energy workers watching their livelihoods sacrificed for progressive climate ideology.

**Watch for:** Congressional Republican pushback and whether Colorado's Democratic delegation will distance themselves from the deal before 2026 midterms.
