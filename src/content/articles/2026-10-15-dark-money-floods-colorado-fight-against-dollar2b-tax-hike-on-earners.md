# Conservative Money Floods Colorado to Block Massive Tax Hike on Earners

Millions in untraceable funding is pouring into Colorado to oppose Amendment 87, a graduated income tax measure that would hammer earners above $500,000 and small-business owners—and conservatives say that's exactly what voters should demand.

The Colorado Sun and CPR News report that dark-money groups are targeting the amendment, which would generate an estimated $2 billion in its first year by imposing higher tax rates on top earners. Complete Colorado frames the measure bluntly: "class envy disguised as tax policy," noting that Democrats are selling a tax increase as relief for working families.

Republicans argue Amendment 87 would damage Colorado's competitive business climate and drive earners and entrepreneurs to lower-tax states. The measure's supporters claim it targets only the wealthy, but conservatives counter that graduated income taxes inevitably creep downward, eventually hitting middle-class Coloradans.

Legal clouds are also gathering. KDVR reports that a voter lawsuit challenges the Secretary of State's certification of Amendment 87 signatures, raising questions about ballot validity before Election Day.

Meanwhile, election officials are still cleaning up after an unprecedented ballot error that sent roughly 800,000 Front Range voters incorrect ballots containing Issue 7A (Denver Post).
