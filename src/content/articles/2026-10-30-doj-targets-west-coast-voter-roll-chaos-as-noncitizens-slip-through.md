# Feds Probe West Coast Voter Roll Chaos as Noncitizens Slip Onto Ballots

The Department of Justice is subpoenaing California election officials over their refusal to remove up to 20,000 suspected noncitizens from voter rolls, a move that exposes the Left's resistance to basic election security (Washington Examiner). Election expert J. Christian Adams warns that states like California and Washington are deliberately blocking use of the SAVE database—a federal tool designed to verify citizenship—leaving the door wide open for noncitizen voting (Fox News Politics).

This isn't abstract: Colorado election officials must answer whether they're using available verification tools or following the same lax standards as West Coast Democrats. Conservatives argue that citizenship verification is Election 101, not voter suppression. Democrats counter that such measures are unnecessary and burden eligible voters—a position that rings hollow when thousands of noncitizens are actively registered.

A Clinton-appointed federal judge already blocked the DOJ's attempt to prohibit counting ballots tied to citizenship-questioned voters, signaling judicial resistance to enforcement. Republicans say this is precisely why election integrity demands state-level vigilance and transparency.

Colorado taxpayers should watch whether state election officials are proactively using citizenship verification tools or passively accepting the progressive playbook of lax voter roll maintenance.
