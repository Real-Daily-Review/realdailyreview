# Trump Signals Cabinet Purge After GOP Midterm Rout

Republicans absorbed a stinging defeat in the 2026 midterms, and President Trump is already signaling heads will roll. In a preview of an upcoming *60 Minutes* interview, Trump said he expects to fire Cabinet members in the wake of Tuesday's losses (Washington Examiner), though he declined to name targets.

The results have sparked sharp recriminations within conservative ranks. National Review calls it "Trump's Midterm Disaster," while the Daily Caller reports Trump has been mocking his own voters over market volatility and election performance—a move that has angered supporters already reeling from the GOP's losses.

For Colorado Republicans, the midterm bloodletting raises urgent questions about the state party's direction heading into 2028. Colorado has trended purple-to-blue in recent cycles, and a fractured national GOP could further weaken the state party's ability to compete for statewide office and congressional seats.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Trump's Cabinet shake-up signals a strategic reset or further chaos—and whether Colorado Republicans can rebuild messaging discipline before the next cycle.
