# Trump Faces Institutional Limits; Obama Weighs In on 'Obsession'

President Trump is operating under significant institutional constraints that routinely undercut his policy ambitions, according to *National Review*. Meanwhile, former President Barack Obama claimed in a newly released podcast that Trump maintains an "obsession" with him, noting Trump behaves differently in private than on social media—a charge that underscores the ongoing personal friction between the two former executives.

Republicans argue Trump's effectiveness is hampered by bureaucratic resistance, judicial overreach, and congressional limitations that prevent swift implementation of his agenda. Conservatives contend these institutional obstacles—from the administrative state to activist judges—represent exactly the kind of government overreach the Constitution was designed to prevent.

Democrats and their allies in the media, by contrast, frame these constraints as necessary checks on executive power. Obama's comments reflect the left's broader narrative that Trump operates outside democratic norms and requires institutional guardrails.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, the real question is whether Trump can deliver on promises to reduce federal overreach, cut spending, and restore energy independence—all critical to Colorado's economy and energy workers. Watch whether Trump's team can navigate these institutional barriers to actually shrink the federal footprint or whether gridlock wins out.
