# Denver Weather: Monsoon Chaos Gives Way to Dry Pattern

Colorado's Front Range took a beating from back-to-back severe thunderstorms this week, with golf ball-sized hail and downed trees leaving visible scars across the Denver metro area (KDVR). Saturday's storms dumped heavy rain and hail across Denver and the plains (9News), while Thursday's system produced hail drifts reaching up to 3 feet in some neighborhoods—enough to strip leaves clean off trees.

The good news: relief is coming. After several days of intense monsoon activity, the Denver forecast shifts to drier and warmer conditions for the week ahead (KDVR). Sunday will see spottier storm coverage compared to the previous days, though localized severe weather remains possible in the afternoon.

For Colorado homeowners and property owners, the timing matters. Insurance claims for hail damage spike after events like these, and tree removal costs add up fast. Farmers and ranchers across the plains also face crop and livestock exposure during these intense systems—a reminder that Colorado's weather volatility can hit agricultural operations hard.
