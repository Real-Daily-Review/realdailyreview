# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Race; Colorado Sun Warns of 'Retribution'

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is throwing $10 million behind Republican Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign against Democrat James Talarico, according to FEC filings reported by *The Daily Wire*—a show of force signaling Trump's willingness to back embattled GOP candidates in competitive races.

Meanwhile, *Colorado Sun* columnist Carman warns that Trump's administration is waging a "campaign of retribution" against Colorado specifically, claiming the state faces federal coercion to surrender constitutional powers or face consequences. Carman frames the conflict as an assault on state sovereignty.

**The conservative counterpoint:** Republicans argue Trump is simply mobilizing resources to defend Republican Senate seats and counter Democratic spending—standard campaign practice. Conservatives note that defending allies against Democratic challengers is not "retribution" but electoral strategy. The framing of federal-state disputes as "coercion" often reflects disagreement over policy, not constitutional overreach.

**Colorado angle:** If the Sun's warnings reflect real federal-state tensions, Coloradans should understand what specific powers or policies are at stake—whether energy development, water rights, or regulatory authority—before accepting claims of executive overreach.

Watch whether Trump's super PAC commits similar resources to Colorado races, and whether specific federal-state disputes materialize beyond rhetoric.
