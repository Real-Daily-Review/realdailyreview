# UN Pushes Abortion as 'Human Right' Despite No Binding Treaty—Colorado Should Reject Pressure

Unelected international bureaucrats are manufacturing a fake legal obligation to legalize abortion, even though no binding treaty exists to support it (Washington Examiner). The UN and allied institutions increasingly claim nations violate "international law" by restricting abortion, yet this supposed right was never negotiated or ratified by member governments.

This matters for Colorado. Progressive activists regularly cite international pressure to justify expanding abortion access and weakening state protections. If the Biden administration or future Democratic governors treat UN pronouncements as binding policy guidance, Colorado taxpayers could find themselves funding abortion expansion through state health programs—without a vote from elected representatives.

Conservatives argue the UN is attempting an end-run around national sovereignty and democratic process. No supranational body should dictate abortion policy to Colorado voters. Republicans counter that abortion is a moral question properly decided by states and citizens, not Geneva-based organizations accountable to no one.

The left claims international human rights standards should guide domestic policy. Conservatives reject this wholesale: America's Constitution and Colorado's laws—not UN declarations—determine what rights Coloradans have.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature attempts to enshrine "international human rights standards" into state law or funding requirements.
