# Texas Court Slashes Sandy Hook Judgment Against Alex Jones

A Texas appeals court has reduced a $50 million defamation judgment against Alex Jones and InfoWars to $1.5 million over his false claims that the Sandy Hook Elementary School shooting was a hoax, according to 9News Denver and Just The News.

The ruling represents a significant legal win for Jones, though it does not affect a separate $1.25 billion judgment against him in Connecticut. The reduction came after the court found the original award excessive under Texas law.

Jones's Sandy Hook coverage remains one of the most contentious free-speech cases in recent memory. **Conservatives argue the original judgments—totaling over $1.2 billion—represent a chilling effect on speech rights and set a dangerous precedent for weaponizing defamation law against media figures**, even those with inflammatory rhetoric. **The left counters that Jones deliberately spread lies causing immense suffering to grieving families and that accountability matters more than protecting reckless speech.**

The case underscores broader tensions over where courts should draw the line between protecting free expression and holding speakers liable for provably false statements that cause documented harm.

Colorado conservatives should monitor whether similar defamation cases gain traction against state-level media figures or whether this ruling signals courts are reasserting limits on damages awards.
