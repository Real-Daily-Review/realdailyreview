# Harris Signals 2028 Run With Attacks on Election Integrity, Voter ID Laws

Kamala Harris is laying groundwork for a potential 2028 presidential bid by doubling down on Democratic opposition to voter ID requirements and proof-of-citizenship rules—positions that put her at odds with election-security advocates and Republican-led election reforms sweeping the country.

Speaking at Louisiana's Democratic Party gala Friday, Harris called for party leaders with "moral conviction and know how to fight," signaling aggressive positioning ahead of 2024 (Washington Examiner). She also criticized the SAVE America Act's voter registration requirements, framing proof-of-citizenship mandates as obstacles to voting access (Breitbart).

Republicans and election-integrity conservatives argue that voter ID and citizenship verification are basic safeguards against fraud and election interference—not barriers to legitimate voting. Colorado Republicans have pushed similar measures to tighten voter rolls and prevent non-citizen registration, arguing that election security protects every legal voter's voice.

Harris's rhetoric reflects the Democratic Party's broader resistance to election reforms that conservatives see as commonsense protection. Her 2028 positioning suggests Democrats will continue fighting proof-of-citizenship laws that Colorado and other Republican-led states are implementing.
