# U.S. Military Strikes Iranian Oil Tankers After Failed Missile Attack on Navy Warships

The U.S. military responded decisively Saturday after Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched ballistic missiles at American naval vessels in the Persian Gulf, according to U.S. Central Command.

A U.S. aircraft carrier and guided-missile destroyer successfully evaded multiple Iranian attacks with no American personnel injured, CENTCOM reported (Washington Examiner). In response, U.S. forces struck three Iranian crude oil tankers, demonstrating resolve against Tehran's aggression.

The strike underscores the ongoing threat posed by Iranian hostility to American military assets and global energy security.
