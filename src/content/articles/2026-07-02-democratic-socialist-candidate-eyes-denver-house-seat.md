# Democratic Socialist Eyes Denver House Seat, Raising Concerns Over Radical Agenda

A 29-year-old democratic socialist could soon represent Denver in Congress, alarming Colorado conservatives who worry about the district's leftward drift. According to CPR News, Melat Kiros—a Democratic Socialists of America member—is positioning herself as a viable candidate in what has long been a safely Democratic seat.

Republicans and conservatives argue that DSA membership signals support for policies far outside the American mainstream: wealth redistribution, government control of key industries, and anti-capitalist economics that would devastate Colorado's energy sector and small businesses. The DSA's explicit rejection of market-based solutions threatens the energy independence and job creation that fuel Colorado's economy.

Democrats counter that Kiros represents grassroots activism and younger voters demanding bold action on inequality and climate change—though they rarely acknowledge the DSA's radical platform in public messaging.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, this race matters. A DSA representative in Congress would likely push for aggressive federal regulations on oil and gas, carbon taxes, and wealth taxes that would ripple through Colorado's economy. The district's shift toward democratic socialism reflects broader Democratic Party movement leftward, even in traditionally purple Colorado.
