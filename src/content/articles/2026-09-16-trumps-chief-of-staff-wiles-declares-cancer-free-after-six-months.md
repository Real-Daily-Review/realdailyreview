# White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles Announces Cancer-Free Status

White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles announced Wednesday that pathology results from the Mayo Clinic came back clear, six months after her early-stage breast cancer diagnosis.

Wiles, one of President Trump's most trusted advisors, disclosed her diagnosis earlier this year. In a social media post, she expressed gratitude to her medical team and supporters: "Thank you to my family, friends, doctors, and everyone who prayed for me, checked in, and supported me along the way" (Washington Examiner).

The announcement comes as Wiles continues to serve in her role as White House Chief of Staff, managing day-to-day operations at the highest levels of the Trump administration. Her swift return to full duties following treatment underscores her commitment to the position during a consequential period in the administration's agenda.

Wiles has been a key figure in Trump's political operation for years, playing a central role in campaign strategy and White House management. Her health recovery removes uncertainty about continuity in one of the most demanding roles in government.
