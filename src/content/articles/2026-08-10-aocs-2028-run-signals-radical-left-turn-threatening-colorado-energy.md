# Colorado Conservatives Should Brace for AOC's 2028 Presidential Bid

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez's informal announcement that she's running for president in 2028 signals the Democratic Party is doubling down on the far-left agenda that has hammered Colorado's economy and energy sector, according to conservative analysts.

The New York congresswoman, who has championed the Green New Deal and aggressive climate regulations that threaten Colorado's oil, gas, and coal industries, announced her candidacy through social media rather than traditional channels—a move *The Daily Wire* characterized as emblematic of her outsized influence over Democratic messaging despite her relative inexperience.

Conservatives argue that an AOC presidency would accelerate the regulatory assault on Colorado's energy workers and rural communities. Her environmental extremism has already contributed to Biden-era policies that have strangled domestic energy production, costing Colorado jobs and tax revenue while enriching foreign competitors.

Republicans counter that AOC represents the true face of modern progressivism: radical climate ideology masquerading as grassroots activism, financed by coastal elites indifferent to working-class energy workers. Her 2028 run would force Democrats to either embrace or distance themselves from her agenda—neither option politically sustainable in swing states like Colorado.

Watch for how Colorado Democrats respond to AOC's candidacy and whether they'll defend or distance themselves from her energy policies ahead of 2028.
