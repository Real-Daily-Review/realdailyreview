# Treasury Secretary Bessent Relocated to Military Base Over Iranian Assassination Threats

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has been moved to a military base after U.S. officials determined that Iranian assassination plots against American officials have reached an "advanced stage," according to reports from Fox News, the Washington Examiner, and The Daily Wire.

Bessent, who has spearheaded the Trump administration's economic pressure campaign against Iran—including initiatives labeled "Economic Fury" and "Economic Outcast"—became a direct target as Tehran escalates threats against U.S. policymakers. The relocation from his Washington-area residence underscores the real security risks facing officials implementing hardline Iran policy.

This development reflects the stakes of confronting hostile regimes economically and diplomatically.
