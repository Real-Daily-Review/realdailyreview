# California's Collapse: From Economic Powerhouse to Cautionary Tale

California's implosion under progressive governance is now impossible to ignore—and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent just made it official: Texas has become America's economic center of gravity.

Bessent's "tale of two states" comparison cuts to the heart of what conservatives have long argued: failed governance and ruinous energy policy destroy prosperity. Texas thrives on business-friendly regulation and energy independence; California strangles both with green mandates and bureaucratic overreach (Fox News Politics).

The picture darkens further. The Department of Justice is now escalating a probe into California's refusal to open voter rolls for federal audit, with a federal prosecutor asking pointedly: "What are they afraid of?" (New York Post). California's stonewalling on election records—now contested in the Ninth Circuit—suggests either gross incompetence or something worse.

**The Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't descended into California-level dysfunction, the state faces similar pressures. Progressive energy policies threaten Colorado's oil and gas economy. Unchecked regulatory expansion mirrors California's playbook. And election integrity matters here too—Colorado voters deserve transparent, auditable voter rolls.

Conservatives should watch whether the DOJ's California probe yields real accountability, and whether Colorado lawmakers take note of what happens when progressives run unchecked.
