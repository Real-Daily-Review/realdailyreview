# WNBA Ejects Kanter Freedom Over Trans Athletes Dispute

Former NBA center Enes Kanter Freedom was escorted out of a Chicago Sky-Indiana Fever game Sunday after a heated exchange with player Natasha Cloud, reigniting the culture war over biological sex in women's sports.

Freedom, who wore a shirt displaying the dictionary definition of "woman," was ejected late in the third quarter at Wintrust Arena following the confrontation (New York Post, Daily Wire). According to Just The News, Freedom is among former NBA players attempting to become eligible for the WNBA draft while identifying as women—a move that has intensified debate over fairness in female athletics.

**The conservative argument:** Allowing biological males to compete in women's sports undermines the integrity of female competition and erases opportunities for biological women. Freedom's ejection for simply wearing a definitional shirt illustrates how progressive ideology now polices speech and dissent in sports spaces.

**The progressive counter:** Advocates argue that trans inclusion policies, when properly implemented with medical and athletic guidelines, can coexist with fair competition. Cloud and others view Freedom's actions as provocative activism rather than legitimate discourse.
