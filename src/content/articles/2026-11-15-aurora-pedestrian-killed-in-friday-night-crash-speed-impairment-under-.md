# Aurora Pedestrian Killed in Friday Night Crash; Police Investigating Speed, Impairment

Aurora police are investigating a fatal crash Friday night near East Colfax Avenue and Espana Street that claimed a pedestrian's life when a driver struck a man crossing the street, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

Investigators are examining whether speed or driver impairment played a role in the collision. The circumstances surrounding the crash—including whether the pedestrian had the right of way and the driver's condition at the time—remain under review as Aurora police work to determine cause and potential charges.

No additional details about the victim or driver have been released. The incident underscores ongoing traffic safety concerns in the Aurora area, where pedestrian fatalities have drawn periodic scrutiny from local law enforcement and city officials.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether Aurora's city leadership uses this tragedy to justify new traffic enforcement spending or vehicle surveillance measures rather than focusing on driver accountability and enforcement of existing traffic laws.
