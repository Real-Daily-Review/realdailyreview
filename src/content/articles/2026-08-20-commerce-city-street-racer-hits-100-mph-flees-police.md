# Street Racer Hits 100+ MPH in Commerce City, Flees Police

Colorado law enforcement is dealing with yet another reckless driver who thought the rules didn't apply to him. According to the Commerce City Police Department, a street racer exceeded 100 miles per hour and attempted to flee from an officer this week—a dangerous stunt that endangered every motorist and pedestrian in the area.

These high-speed chases represent a broader public safety crisis that progressive prosecutors and soft-on-crime policies have failed to address. When criminals face minimal consequences for reckless endangerment, they have little incentive to comply with law enforcement. Colorado conservatives argue that tough enforcement, meaningful penalties, and support for police—not defunding or hamstringing them—are essential to keeping roads safe.

The incident mirrors similar lawlessness captured on camera across the country, from Los Angeles to Denver's metro area. Street racing and fleeing police aren't victimless crimes; they put families at risk and tie up law enforcement resources that could be deployed elsewhere.

Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado's courts impose serious consequences for this driver and whether state legislators will support law enforcement with the tools and backing they need to deter future recklessness.
