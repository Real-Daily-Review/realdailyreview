# Macklemore's Anti-Israel Rant at Ed Sheeran Concert Sparks Refund Demands

Rapper Macklemore hijacked Ed Sheeran's MetLife Stadium shows to deliver anti-Israel messaging that left paying concertgoers demanding refunds and Jewish advocacy groups calling for his removal.

The "Thrift Shop" rapper launched into what *Breitbart* describes as an "anti-American, pro-Hamas rant" during Friday's performance, prompting immediate backlash from audience members. The Israeli-American Council has since launched a petition demanding Sheeran fire Macklemore as opening act for the remainder of the "Loop Tour," accusing him of using the platform to deliver what the group characterizes as antisemitic propaganda (*The Daily Wire*).

Concertgoers who paid for tickets to see Sheeran—not a political lecture—are now seeking refunds over the unexpected tirade. The incident underscores a broader pattern of left-wing entertainers weaponizing concert stages for activism, often at the expense of paying customers who simply want to enjoy a show.

This raises a straightforward question for venues and headliners: Do you control your stage, or do you let opening acts hijack it for causes unrelated to the performance ticket buyers purchased?
