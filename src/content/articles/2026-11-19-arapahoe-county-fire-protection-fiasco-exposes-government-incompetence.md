# Arapahoe County Fire Protection Snafu Leaves Businesses in Limbo

A massive mailing blunder by Arapahoe County has left 21 non-residential properties scrambling over fire protection coverage—a cautionary tale of government incompetence that Colorado taxpayers should find deeply troubling.

County officials mistakenly notified thousands of Sheridan residents that their fire protection had been revoked, triggering panic before clarifying the error affected only business properties. The bungled communication raises hard questions: How does a county government lose track of which properties it's supposed to protect? Who's accountable when bureaucratic mistakes put commercial operations at risk?

For Colorado business owners already burdened by rising property taxes and regulatory compliance costs, this fiasco adds insult to injury. Fire protection isn't optional—it's foundational infrastructure that government is supposed to handle competently. When counties can't execute basic administrative functions without mass confusion, it undercuts the case for expanding government authority or raising taxes to fund it.

Meanwhile, the Town of Bennett has moved forward with confidence in county services, extending its contract with the Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office for law enforcement (KDVR). The contrast is stark: law enforcement partnership holds steady while fire protection oversight crumbles.
