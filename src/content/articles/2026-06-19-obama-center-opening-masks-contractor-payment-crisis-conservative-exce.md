# Obama Center Opening Exposes Left's Disconnect From Working America

Former President Obama's presidential center debut in Chicago became a showcase of progressive excess—complete with land-acknowledgment rituals, Hollywood celebrities, and mounting contractor disputes—while the former president attacked America's founders as having "fell terribly short" (Fox News).

The Obama Presidential Center, which includes a museum, athletic center, and library branch, faced delays and cost overruns that the White House contrasted with Trump's D.C. renovation projects, positioning the 45th president as "Builder-in-Chief" (Fox News). But the real scandal lurked beneath the star-studded ceremony: according to Breitbart, unpaid subcontractors—many black-owned businesses—claim millions remain owed for construction work.

Conservatives note the irony: Obama's center, built with progressive fanfare and celebrity endorsements, allegedly stiffed minority contractors while the former president lectured Americans about the nation's founding failures. The land-acknowledgment opening ritual drew online ridicule from conservatives who see such gestures as performative wokeness disconnected from actual working-class concerns (Fox News).

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the Obama Center serves as a cautionary tale about government-adjacent megaprojects: cost overruns, delays, and contractors left holding the bag while elites celebrate.
