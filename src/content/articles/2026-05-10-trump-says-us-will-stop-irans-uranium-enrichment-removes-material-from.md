President Trump said the US will not allow Iran to reach enriched uranium, claiming Washington has the nuclear material "surveilled" and will "blow up" anyone who approaches it, according to Al Jazeera.

The rhetoric comes as the Trump administration announced a separate nuclear success: the US Department of Energy removed 13.5 kilograms of highly enriched uranium from a research reactor in Venezuela, The Guardian reported. The administration credited "President Trump's decisive leadership" for the operation.

The Venezuelan uranium seizure represents a fraction of the roughly 408 kilograms of enriched uranium held by Iran, according to The Guardian's reporting. The removal from Caracas marks a tangible nonproliferation achievement, though it underscores the administration's stated focus on preventing nuclear material from reaching hostile actors.

Trump's comments on Iran reflect escalating rhetoric around Tehran's nuclear program. The US has long sought to constrain Iran's uranium enrichment capabilities, a central point of contention in nuclear diplomacy.
