# Charlie Kirk's Family Sues Utah Valley University Over Fatal Security Lapses

Conservative activist Charlie Kirk was assassinated by a rooftop shooter while debating students at Utah Valley University—a tragedy his family now argues was entirely preventable due to the institution's reckless security failures.

According to legal filings reviewed by *Just The News* and *The Daily Wire*, Kirk's family alleges that UVU knew rooftop access posed a documented security risk, yet failed to secure the building from which the shooter fired. The university positioned Kirk to speak in a campus courtyard dangerously close to that same rooftop—a location the family characterizes as a "foreseeable" kill zone.

The lawsuit accuses UVU and the State of Utah of "a series of stunning failures and reckless decisions" that left Kirk defenseless against an entirely preventable attack. The family's legal team argues the university's negligence transformed a routine campus speaking event into a fatal ambush.

This case underscores a broader conservative concern: institutional incompetence and dereliction of duty by government entities and their contractors. When public institutions know about security vulnerabilities and ignore them anyway, the consequences can be deadly—and someone must be held accountable.

Watch whether UVU settles quickly or fights the lawsuit, and whether this case prompts other universities to finally take campus security seriously.
