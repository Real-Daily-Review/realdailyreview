# Trump Signals Iran Nuclear Deal This Weekend; Strait of Hormuz Set to Reopen

President Trump says a U.S.-Iran peace deal could be signed as soon as Sunday, with Tehran agreeing to abandon its nuclear weapons program—a major foreign policy win that could reshape Middle East stability and global energy markets.

According to Trump's Truth Social post (New York Post), the agreement would represent a dramatic reversal of Iran's nuclear ambitions. The administration is preparing to discuss demining the Strait of Hormuz with G7 allies next week (9News Denver), signaling confidence the waterway—critical to global oil shipments—will reopen for commerce immediately after the deal closes.

Trump indicated the U.S. would later retrieve enriched nuclear material from Iranian facilities using advanced military assets, including B-2 bombers (Just The News).

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets could see significant relief. A reopened Strait of Hormuz and stable Iran would ease crude oil prices, benefiting Colorado's energy sector and consumers at the pump. Lower energy costs also support Colorado's broader economy and reduce inflationary pressure on state taxpayers.

Conservatives have long argued that strength-based diplomacy—not appeasement—works with adversaries. If verified, this deal would vindicate that approach and contrast sharply with the Obama-era Iran nuclear accord that Republicans said was toothless.

Watch for Senate confirmation votes on any formal treaty language and whether Colorado's congressional delegation backs the deal.
