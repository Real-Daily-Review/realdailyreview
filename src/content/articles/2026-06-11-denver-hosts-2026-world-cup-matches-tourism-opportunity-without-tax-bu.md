# Denver Gears Up as 2026 World Cup Host City

Denver will host matches during the 2026 FIFA World Cup, putting Colorado's capital on the international sports stage and drawing tourism dollars to the region. The tournament will feature 104 matches across 16 North American cities, with watch parties already planned at Skyline Park and other Denver venues, presented by FOX31 and the Colorado Rapids.

The World Cup represents a rare opportunity for Colorado to showcase its economy and attract visitors without the massive public subsidy burden that often accompanies mega-events. Unlike many stadium deals and sports initiatives that drain taxpayer coffers, watch parties and existing venues like Denver's soccer facilities can absorb fan traffic with minimal government intervention.

Local organizers are positioning the event as a summer draw, capitalizing on Denver's outdoor culture and established sports infrastructure. The Colorado Rapids' involvement signals private-sector leadership in event promotion—the right model for economic development.

Conservatives should monitor whether city or state officials use the World Cup as a pretext for new spending, stadium subsidies, or regulatory mandates on local businesses hosting watch parties. Keep an eye on whether Denver's government stays disciplined and lets the private sector drive the event's success.
