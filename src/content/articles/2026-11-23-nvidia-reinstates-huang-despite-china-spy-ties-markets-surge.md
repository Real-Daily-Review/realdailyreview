# Nvidia Reinstates Huang Despite China Spy Ties—Markets Soar, Democrats Fume

Nvidia's board has reinstated Jensen Huang as president and CEO immediately, a move that sent tech stocks rocketing higher but drew fire from Democrats over national security concerns.

The reinstatement comes after Huang was removed following revelations of an extramarital affair with a Chinese businesswoman under investigation for ties to chip smugglers and Chinese intelligence. According to Breitbart, the woman—Alice Huang—is the center of federal investigations into whether her former company, Megaspeed, helped funnel Nvidia chips into China through underground trade networks. Other probes allege she maintained regular contact with Chinese intelligence operatives.

**Markets loved it.** Breitbart reported that stocks surged Monday: the S&amp;P 500 rose 4 percent, the Nasdaq jumped 6 percent, and Nvidia shares climbed 30 percent. President Trump claimed he "convinced" the board to reinstate Huang, saying the CEO "did nothing wrong" (Daily Caller).

**Democrats aren't buying it.** The Washington Examiner reports Democrats blasted the decision as "reckless," citing unresolved counterintelligence questions and the apparent conflict between Huang's personal conduct and America's semiconductor security interests.

**The conservative take:** Markets vote with their wallets, and investors clearly believe Huang's leadership matters more than the personal scandal. But Colorado tech workers and taxpayers should watch whether federal investigations into chip smuggling proceed—or get buried under market euphoria.
