# New York Socialist Mayor's Heat Wave Power Grab Shows Why Colorado Can't Go Left

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani—a self-described socialist—ordered residents to set air conditioners to 78 degrees during a brutal heat wave, sparking immediate backlash from New Yorkers fed up with government overreach (Fox News, The Daily Wire).

The directive exposed a hard truth: Democrat-run cities with aging infrastructure and anti-energy policies can't deliver reliable power when citizens need it most. Mamdani framed the thermostat mandate as collective sacrifice for the grid, but critics saw it for what it was—government telling people what temperature they can live in their own homes.

Breitbart's analysis cut deeper: New York's power grid is so decrepit it can't handle normal summer demand, yet the state continues blocking energy development and reliable generation capacity. This is the inevitable result of progressive energy ideology meeting reality.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's grid operators and legislators should take note. As the state pursues aggressive renewable mandates and phases out coal generation, demand for electricity will only climb—especially as population grows and summers get hotter. If New York's grid buckles under heat-wave pressure, what happens when Colorado faces similar conditions while running on wind and solar alone?

Watch for whether Colorado's Public Utilities Commission pushes utilities toward more reliable baseload power or doubles down on the green-energy experiment that's already destabilizing grids nationwide.
