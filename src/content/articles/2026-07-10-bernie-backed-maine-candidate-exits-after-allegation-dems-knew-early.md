# Democratic Senate Bid Implodes as Bernie's Endorsement Curse Strikes Again

A Democratic Senate candidate's campaign collapsed this week after a rape allegation surfaced, exposing what conservatives say is a pattern of poor vetting by progressive kingmakers—and raising questions about when Democratic leadership knew trouble was brewing.

Graham Platner, a Maine Democrat backed by Bernie Sanders, announced he would withdraw from the race (Breitbart). The timing is damning: Fox News reports that Democratic leadership pulled over $6.2 million in ad spending roughly a week *before* the allegation became public, suggesting party insiders may have sensed danger ahead.

The implosion has triggered a reckoning within Democratic ranks. Senator John Fetterman, himself a Sanders-backed candidate, demanded the Vermont socialist apologize to Maine voters—marking a sharp break from the progressive wing (Fox News). Republicans are seizing on the moment to highlight what they call Sanders' "toxic endorsement pattern," pointing to past controversies involving other progressive picks like Andrew Gillum.

For Colorado conservatives, the lesson is clear: this is what happens when ideology trumps due diligence. While the race is in Maine, it underscores how national progressive networks operate—and why voters should scrutinize the judgment of politicians who hitch their wagons to far-left endorsers.
