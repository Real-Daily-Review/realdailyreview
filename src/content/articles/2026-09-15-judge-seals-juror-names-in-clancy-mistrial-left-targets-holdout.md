# Judge Seals Juror Names in Clancy Mistrial as Left Targets Holdout Juror

A Massachusetts judge has indefinitely impounded the names of jurors in the Lindsay Clancy triple-murder trial, citing safety and privacy concerns following the September 4 mistrial (The Daily Wire). The move shields the lone holdout juror—who refused to find Clancy not guilty by reason of insanity—from what conservatives argue is a coordinated campaign to intimidate and demonize him for his principled stand.

The holdout juror's brother recently revealed his sibling is a Haitian immigrant, undercutting leftist attempts to paint the juror as a villain for rejecting the insanity defense (The Daily Wire). Judge William Sullivan's indefinite impoundment order came after the jury list was initially sealed for two weeks, signaling judicial recognition that jurors face real threats.

**The conservative argument:** Jurors deserve protection when they follow the law and their conscience, regardless of public pressure. The left's campaign against this juror—attempting to delegitimize his verdict through doxxing and character assassination—represents an assault on jury independence itself. National Review notes that exonerating Clancy would strip moral accountability from a defendant and undermine society's ability to hold wrongdoers responsible.

**What taxpayers should watch:** Whether prosecutors pursue a retrial and whether media and activist pressure on the judiciary continues to erode jury protections that safeguard all defendants' and jurors' rights.
