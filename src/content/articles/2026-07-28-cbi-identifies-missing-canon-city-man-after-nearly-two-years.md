# Colorado Missing Person Case Closed After Nearly Two Years

The Colorado Bureau of Investigation has identified human remains found in Cañon City as Shawn Michael Dixon Sr., a man missing since September 2023—a grim reminder of how long unsolved cases can linger without answers for families.

Dixon was last seen Sept. 12, 2023, at 7 p.m. near Rudd Park in Cañon City after exiting a taxi. The discovery and identification mark a significant development in a case that had gone cold for nearly two years, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The CBI is now investigating the circumstances surrounding Dixon's death. While authorities have not yet released details on cause of death or circumstances, the case underscores ongoing challenges law enforcement faces in solving missing-persons investigations, particularly in smaller Colorado communities where resources may be stretched thin.

Meanwhile, authorities in Commerce City are grappling with a separate case: human remains discovered in a dumpster last week that could take years to identify, KDVR reports. That investigation highlights the backlog pressures facing Colorado's forensic and investigative systems.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether state funding for the CBI and local law enforcement agencies keeps pace with caseload demands—or whether budget constraints continue to slow critical investigations.
