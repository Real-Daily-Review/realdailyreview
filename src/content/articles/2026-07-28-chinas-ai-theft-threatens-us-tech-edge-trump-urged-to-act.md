# Trump Must Crack Down on China's AI Theft, Experts Warn

The U.S. is losing ground in artificial intelligence to China's cut-rate knockoffs—and American competitiveness hangs in the balance.

China is flooding the market with cheaper AI models built through "distillation," a technique that reverse-engineers advanced U.S. AI systems to create competitive alternatives at a fraction of the cost, according to the *Washington Examiner*. The strategy allows Beijing to undercut American innovation while sidestepping the massive R&amp;D investment U.S. companies have poured into frontier AI development.

Conservatives argue this represents intellectual property theft on an industrial scale—the kind of predatory competition that demands immediate action from the Trump administration. Without aggressive enforcement and trade measures, they warn, American tech companies will lose their edge in one of the most consequential technologies of the 21st century.

For Colorado, which hosts growing tech hubs in Denver and Boulder and benefits from U.S. tech sector strength, a weakened American AI industry means fewer high-wage jobs and reduced innovation investment in the state.

**Watch for:** Whether the Trump administration imposes tariffs, export controls, or other trade restrictions on Chinese AI models—and how Colorado's tech workforce responds.
