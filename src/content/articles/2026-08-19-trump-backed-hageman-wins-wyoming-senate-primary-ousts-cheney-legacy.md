# Trump-Backed Hageman Wins Wyoming Senate Nod, Eyes Seat in Deep Red State

Wyoming Republicans have nominated Rep. Harriet Hageman for U.S. Senate, rewarding the Trump-endorsed congresswoman who ousted Liz Cheney in 2022 with a shot at higher office in one of America's most reliably conservative states. Hageman won the GOP primary on Tuesday to succeed retiring Sen. Cynthia Lummis, cementing her status as a leading voice of Trump-aligned Republicans in the Mountain West.

The nomination reflects the GOP's decisive shift away from the Cheney wing of the party—the anti-Trump establishment that once dominated Wyoming politics. Hageman's primary victory signals that Republican voters in the region reward loyalty to the Trump agenda and punish perceived disloyalty, a lesson that reverberated across the West after Cheney's 2022 defeat.

For Colorado conservatives, the Wyoming result underscores a broader realignment: Trump-backed candidates continue winning GOP primaries across the region, suggesting the party's base remains energized by America First messaging on border security, energy independence, and limited government. Wyoming's energy sector—oil, gas, and coal—has particular relevance to Colorado's own resource economy and water rights disputes.
