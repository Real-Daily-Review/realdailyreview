# Charlie Kirk Statue Vandalized Days After Phoenix Unveiling

Within days of honoring a slain conservative leader, the left's intolerance for dissent strikes again—this time with spray paint.

A bronze statue of Charlie Kirk, founder of Turning Point USA, was vandalized with red paint just three days after its unveiling outside the organization's Phoenix headquarters on Thursday, according to *Breitbart* and *The Daily Wire*. The eight-foot sculpture, created by artist Quintin McCann, was installed to mark the one-year anniversary of Kirk's assassination at Utah Valley University.

The defacement underscores a troubling pattern: progressive activists routinely target conservative monuments and figures with vandalism rather than engage in civil debate. Kirk's work promoting constitutional conservatism and limited government made him a lightning rod for the activist left—a reality that persisted even after his death.

While Colorado hasn't seen comparable statue vandalism recently, the state's universities and progressive strongholds have hosted similar incidents targeting conservative speakers and symbols. This incident serves as a reminder of the hostile climate conservatives face in America's cultural institutions.

Conservatives argue that protecting public monuments—and prosecuting vandals—is essential to preserving civic order and free speech. The left's response will reveal whether it condemns property destruction or excuses it as "activism."

Watch whether Phoenix police identify and charge the vandals, and whether national media covers the incident with the same urgency they'd apply to damage at progressive monuments.
