# Pedestrian Fatally Struck by Frontier Plane at Denver Airport

A pedestrian was killed after being struck by a Frontier Airlines flight on the runway at Denver International Airport late Friday night, according to CBS News and The Hill.

Frontier Flight 4345, bound for Los Angeles, struck the individual around 11:19 p.m. as the plane was departing, the Washington Examiner reported. According to The Hill, the pedestrian had scaled the airport fence and run onto the runway before being hit.

The collision caused smoke to enter the aircraft, prompting crew to evacuate the plane and abort takeoff, CBS News reported. Denver firefighters extinguished a brief fire that resulted from the incident, the Washington Examiner said.

The National Transportation Safety Board is investigating the incident, according to The Hill. The flight was canceled following the collision.

Details about the pedestrian's identity and the circumstances that led them onto the runway remain unclear from available reports.

Watch for NTSB findings on how the individual breached airport security and what additional safety measures may be implemented at the facility.
