# Michigan Democrat's Defense of Child Gender Medicine Signals Broader Left-Wing Push

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's full-throated defense of irreversible gender procedures for minors reveals how far the progressive wing has drifted on parental rights and medical ethics—a stance that could reverberate in Colorado's own battles over school policy and parental authority.

Abdul El-Sayed, running for U.S. Senate in Michigan, argued Friday that doctors—not government—should decide whether children undergo sex-change treatments, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire. Speaking at Michigan State University, El-Sayed invoked his medical credentials to dismiss concerns about government oversight of such procedures on minors.

Republicans and conservatives argue this position strips parents of fundamental authority over their children's medical decisions and normalizes experimental treatments on vulnerable youth. They contend that irreversible procedures warrant robust safeguards, not physician discretion alone.

Colorado conservatives should note the parallel: progressive Democrats nationwide are pushing similar frameworks in schools and medical boards, often sidelining parental consent. This aligns with efforts to expand "gender-affirming care" access while minimizing parental notification—a flashpoint in Colorado school board races and state health policy debates.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt El-Sayed's framing in upcoming legislative sessions or school board campaigns on gender medicine and parental rights.
