# Fauci's COVID Defense Crumbles as Diaries Reveal True Role in School Closures

Anthony Fauci's carefully crafted denials about his role in pandemic school shutdowns are collapsing under the weight of his own documented words. The release of Fauci's diaries shows the nation's top infectious disease official actively pushing for school closures—contradicting his repeated congressional testimony that he "never shut anything down" (Washington Examiner).

While Fauci technically didn't wield the executive authority to order closures himself, his influence over policy was decisive. Colorado schools, like those nationwide, followed federal health guidance that bore Fauci's fingerprints, leaving thousands of students academically behind and families scrambling for childcare. The diaries reveal what conservatives have long argued: unelected bureaucrats, not elected representatives, made sweeping decisions that devastated American education and family life.

Republicans argue Fauci's evasions represent the broader problem of unchecked government health authority during emergencies. His semantic games—claiming he didn't "shut down" schools while actively recommending closures—exemplify the credibility crisis plaguing federal health agencies.

The diaries offer no redemption for Fauci's legacy (National Review). Colorado taxpayers and parents should watch whether Congress pursues accountability measures to prevent similar bureaucratic overreach in future health crises.
