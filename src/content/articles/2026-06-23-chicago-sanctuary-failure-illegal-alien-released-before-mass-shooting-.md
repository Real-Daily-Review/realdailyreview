# Chicago Sanctuary Chaos: Illegal Alien Teacher Arrested in Mass Shooting Case After Local Authorities Let Her Walk

Chicago's sanctuary city policies have once again put public safety on the back burner. Immigration and Customs Enforcement arrested Giovanna Mercedes Moreno Occhipinti, a 32-year-old Venezuelan illegal alien and former Illinois teacher, on June 22 in connection with a deadly Tren de Aragua gang shooting that killed three people (Daily Wire, Just The News).

The case exposes a dangerous gap in law enforcement coordination. Moreno Occhipinti entered the U.S. in October 2021 under the Visa Waiver Program and was supposed to depart by January 2, 2022—but never left (New York Post). After the 2024 shooting, Chicago sanctuary politicians released her from custody without notifying ICE, allowing an alleged mass shooting accomplice to remain at large until federal agents caught up.

**Republicans and immigration hawks argue** that sanctuary policies prioritize ideology over public safety, enabling dangerous criminals to evade accountability. They point to this case as proof that local non-cooperation with federal immigration enforcement creates preventable risks for American communities.

**Democrats defend sanctuary policies** as protecting vulnerable immigrant communities from over-policing, though they rarely address cases like this where the policy directly enabled a suspect in a violent felony.
