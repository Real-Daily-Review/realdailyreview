# Bill Maher's Vote Goes Up for Grabs as Democrats Embrace Socialism

Even Hollywood liberals are jumping ship as the Democratic Party lurches toward socialism and anti-capitalism.

Comedian Bill Maher told Vice President JD Vance on Friday's broadcast of HBO's "Real Time" that his vote is "in play" if Democrats continue their drift toward Democratic Socialists of America ideology. Maher cited the party's recent socialist electoral victories and complained that major Democratic leaders—including leading socialists—have declined his interview invitations (Washington Examiner).

The HBO host specifically objected to what he called the DSA direction's "obsession with Israel," antisemitism, rejection of capitalism, and opposition to prisons (Breitbart). "I like to hear that," Vance responded, according to the Daily Caller.

Maher's warning signals real fractures in the Democratic coalition. When a prominent liberal entertainer publicly signals he's considering Republicans, it reflects deeper voter anxiety about the party's ideological trajectory—particularly among moderates and Jewish voters alarmed by progressive antisemitism.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have increasingly embraced progressive energy policies, gun control, and spending agendas that mirror national socialist-leaning priorities. If defections like Maher's accelerate, Colorado's purple-state status could tighten further in 2026 and 2028.
