# Trump's SEC Game Visit Stirs Security Concerns, Media Access Debate

President Trump's attendance at the Texas-Tennessee SEC matchup in Knoxville has drawn attention to both the logistics of protecting a sitting president at a major sporting event and the ongoing dispute over media access to Air Force One.

According to 9News Denver, CNN, MSNBC, and Politico—outlets Trump previously banned from White House grounds—have been excluded from traveling on Air Force One for the trip. The three networks have since regained access to the executive complex, but the selective media arrangement underscores the Trump administration's contentious relationship with certain outlets.

Coaches are reportedly concerned about potential disruptions from the presidential visit to what's already shaping up as one of the season's marquee games between No. 1 Texas and No. 14 Tennessee, per the New York Post. Air Force One's low pass over Neyland Stadium added to the spectacle.

**The conservative angle:** While Trump supporters view his attendance as a show of support for college athletics and American tradition, critics—particularly in the media—continue to challenge his media access policies. Republicans counter that news outlets have editorial freedom to cover the president through other means and that security decisions rest with the executive branch.

**Watch next:** Whether the presidential visit becomes a distraction to game play or a smooth ceremonial moment will test how major sporting venues handle executive-level security in the post-2024 environment.
