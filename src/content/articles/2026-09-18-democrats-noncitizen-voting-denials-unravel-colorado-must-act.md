# Democrats' Denials on Noncitizen Voting Crumble Under Scrutiny

Colorado voters deserve election integrity—yet Democrats have spent years dismissing concerns about noncitizens casting ballots as conspiracy theory.

According to the Washington Examiner, Democrats have "consistently denied that illegal immigrants were voting in elections," often mocking and ridiculing those who raised the issue as "delusional." That posture is now collapsing under evidence that the problem is real and measurable.

**The conservative argument is straightforward:** Election security requires verifying voter eligibility. When Democrats reflexively dismiss noncitizen voting as a phantom problem, they obstruct commonsense safeguards like citizenship verification at registration and the ballot box. Colorado, a swing state with significant immigration flows, cannot afford lax election administration.

**Democrats counter** that noncitizen voting is statistically rare and that stricter verification measures create barriers for eligible voters. They frame tighter ID requirements as voter suppression.

The gap between Democratic rhetoric and reality matters. If officials denied a genuine vulnerability for years, what other election-security blind spots exist? Colorado's election officials must prioritize transparency: publish data on noncitizen registration attempts, audit voter rolls regularly, and implement robust citizenship checks.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch whether Colorado's Democratic-controlled legislature moves to strengthen election integrity measures—or doubles down on dismissing the problem.
