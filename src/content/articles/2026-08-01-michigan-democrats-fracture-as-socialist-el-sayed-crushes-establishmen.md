# Michigan Democrats in Chaos as Socialist El-Sayed Surges Past Establishment Pick

Michigan's Democratic primary is fracturing as progressive firebrand Abdul El-Sayed widens a double-digit lead over establishment favorite Rep. Haley Stevens in the final days before the primary election, according to multiple polls compiled by the New York Times (Washington Examiner).

The collapse of Democratic unity is stark. Despite endorsements from establishment figures like Sen. Jeanne Shaheen (Breitbart), only 57 percent of Stevens' own supporters say they'd back El-Sayed in November if he wins the primary (Breitbart)—a warning sign that the party's leftward lurch is alienating its moderate base.

El-Sayed's surge reflects the Democratic Party's vulnerability to its progressive wing. Republicans see an opening: nominating a self-described socialist in a competitive Senate race hands the GOP a potent general-election weapon, especially in a state where working-class voters have grown skeptical of the left's economic agenda.

For Colorado conservatives, this Michigan chaos offers a preview of what happens when Democrats allow their radical flank to drive the agenda. Colorado's own Senate race in 2026 could mirror this dynamic if progressive activists push moderate Democrats further left—potentially creating an opening for Republicans to reclaim ground lost in recent cycles.
