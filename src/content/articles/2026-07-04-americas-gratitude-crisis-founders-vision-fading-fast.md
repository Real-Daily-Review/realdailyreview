# Americans Forgetting What Made the Country Great

As the Fourth of July approaches, a troubling cultural shift is becoming impossible to ignore: Americans are losing sight of the blessings that built this nation. While 9News Denver asked Coloradans to share their good news heading into the holiday weekend, National Review's broader assessment cuts deeper—today's citizens have become dangerously comfortable taking freedom and prosperity for granted in ways our Founders never did.

The contrast is stark. The generation that fought for independence understood scarcity, sacrifice, and the fragility of liberty. They knew what they were building and why it mattered. Today's Americans, by contrast, enjoy unprecedented material comfort and freedom yet spend their energy complaining about systems that have delivered both.

This matters for Colorado and the nation. When citizens lose gratitude for constitutional protections—free speech, property rights, Second Amendment freedoms—they become susceptible to trading them away for promises of government solutions. A population that forgets why limited government and individual liberty matter is a population ripe for progressive overreach.

The real question isn't whether Americans have good news to celebrate; it's whether we'll remember *why* we have anything to celebrate at all.

Watch whether this cultural drift accelerates pressure on Colorado voters to embrace bigger government as the "solution" to problems government itself often creates.
