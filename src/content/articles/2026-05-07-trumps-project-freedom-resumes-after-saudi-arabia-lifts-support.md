# Trump's 'Project Freedom' Resumes After Saudi Arabia Lifts Support

President Donald Trump's stalled energy initiative "Project Freedom" is moving forward again after Saudi Arabia withdrew its objections, according to the Financial Times. The kingdom had suspended support for the project, prompting Trump to halt it temporarily. That changed following a call Wednesday between Trump and Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, who lifted Riyadh's suspension.

The Washington Examiner reported the initiative could resume later this week, though neither outlet detailed the project's specific aims or scope. The timing coincides with Trump hosting Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva at the White House on Thursday to discuss bilateral trade.

The episode underscores the diplomatic dimensions of Trump's energy agenda and Saudi Arabia's continued leverage in shaping U.S. policy priorities. It remains unclear what concessions, if any, were made to secure the kingdom's renewed backing.

Watch for formal announcements on Project Freedom's restart and any details on how the initiative will proceed.
