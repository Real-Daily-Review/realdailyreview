# AOC's Woke Pivot Exposes Progressive Movement's Reckoning

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her progressive allies are quietly distancing themselves from the radical agenda they championed just years ago—and conservatives aren't letting them forget it.

The New York congresswoman and her Democratic Socialist allies appear to be recognizing that their earlier "Woke 1.0" push—characterized by aggressive identity politics, regulatory overreach, and anti-energy zealotry—has become a political liability. But as National Review notes, a simple "oops" won't cut it. Conservatives argue the left owes Americans accountability for policies that damaged the economy, energy independence, and rational governance.

The Daily Caller and Daily Wire both highlight the hypocrisy: progressive Democrats can't simply memory-hole their own rhetoric. Every radical proposal—from Green New Deal extremism to defund-the-police movements—remains on the record, captured in speeches, votes, and social media posts that define their actual record.

For Colorado, this matters. The state's energy sector, ranchers, and working families bore the brunt of progressive environmental mandates and regulatory schemes. If Democrats are now retreating from those positions, conservatives should demand explicit repudiation and policy reversal—not just a strategic rebranding.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from the radical wing of their party ahead of 2026 elections, or double down on the agenda that's already cost them credibility.
