# Intel Community Accused of Hiding Chinese Election Interference

Trump is calling out what he says is a "shadow government" within the intelligence establishment—and newly declassified documents appear to back up the claim that federal spooks worked to suppress evidence of Chinese meddling in the 2020 election.

According to Fox News, the President accused intelligence officials Thursday of operating outside democratic oversight to conceal the scope of Beijing's interference efforts. Just The News reports that declassified records now detail how these officials allegedly suppressed information about Chinese threats to U.S. elections.

The allegation cuts to a core conservative concern: unelected bureaucrats in the intelligence community wielding power without accountability. Republicans have long warned that the "deep state" operates with minimal congressional oversight and zero transparency to voters. If intelligence officials deliberately hid foreign interference data, that's not just a procedural failure—it's a direct assault on election integrity and democratic legitimacy.

Colorado voters should care about this because election security directly affects how our state's electoral votes are counted and whether foreign adversaries can manipulate our outcomes. The question isn't academic: Did federal agencies prioritize protecting their institutional reputation over protecting American elections?

Watch for congressional Republicans demanding full transparency on what intelligence officials knew about Chinese interference and when—and whether anyone faces consequences for the alleged cover-up.
