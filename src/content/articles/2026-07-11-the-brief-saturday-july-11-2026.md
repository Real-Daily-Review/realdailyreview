## Beulah Burns: Aspen Acres Fire Leaves Town of 800 in Ruins

The Aspen Acres Fire has carved a path of destruction through Beulah and surrounding communities, with aerial analysis from private firm Vexcel Data estimating 780 structures destroyed across Pueblo and Custer counties — a number that exceeds incomplete official county tallies and includes homes, barns, garages, and sheds. Many homes in the town of roughly 800 residents are simply gone. This is a gut-punch to rural Colorado, and state and local officials need to be moving fast and cutting red tape — not adding it — to get these families back on their feet.

## Trump Kills Weaponized Wildlife Rule That Strangled Energy and Land Use

The Trump administration is rescinding a decades-old Endangered Species Act rule that Interior Secretary Doug Burgum says expanded federal authority far beyond its original mandate, restricting energy production, infrastructure projects, and private land use across the country. Conservatives say the rule had become a bureaucratic weapon used to block pipelines, drilling, and development on private property. For Colorado's energy workers, ranchers, and landowners who've watched federal regulators use ESA designations to lock up productive land, this rollback is long overdue.
