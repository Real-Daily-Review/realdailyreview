# Poudre School District Eyes Closure of 9 Schools as Enrollment Plummets

Colorado's second-largest school district is preparing to shutter nine schools, a stark admission that progressive education policies and demographic shifts have left the system bloated and unsustainable.

The Poudre School District's Comprehensive Planning Committee recommended the closures and consolidations, with a final decision scheduled for October 27 (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31). The district has not publicly disclosed the specific schools or detailed financial justification in available reports, raising questions about transparency in a decision that will upend families and communities across northern Colorado.

The closures reflect a broader crisis: enrollment decline has left districts nationwide—and Colorado specifically—with excess capacity and rising per-pupil costs. Rather than address bloated administrative overhead or inefficient spending, school boards often default to closing buildings and forcing consolidation on families.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether the district uses this restructuring to cut administrative costs and redirect savings to classrooms, or simply shuffles the same bloated budget across fewer schools while taxpayers foot the bill for the transition.
