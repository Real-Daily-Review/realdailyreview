# Trump Administration Tightens Economic Noose on Iran; Bessent Launches 'Operation Economic Outcast'

The Trump administration is escalating its maximum-pressure campaign against Iran with a sweeping economic offensive that could reshape global energy markets and reshape Colorado's oil and gas sector.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced "Operation Economic Outcast" on Monday, a coordinated effort to sever Iran's access to global financial systems and penalize any government, bank, or company maintaining business ties with Tehran (The Daily Wire). The initiative follows Trump's earlier military operations against Iranian targets and represents a hardline approach to constraining Tehran's regional influence and nuclear ambitions.

For Colorado energy producers, tighter Iran sanctions could tighten global oil supplies and potentially support domestic energy prices—a win for state oil and gas workers and the industry that generates substantial tax revenue for Colorado schools and infrastructure. However, aggressive sanctions can also trigger retaliatory economic disruptions and geopolitical volatility that ripple through energy markets unpredictably.

Conservatives argue maximum-pressure sanctions force adversaries to negotiate from weakness and protect American interests abroad. Critics worry about unintended economic consequences and the risk of escalation.

**Watch:** Whether Bessent's campaign successfully isolates Iran economically or triggers countermeasures that destabilize energy markets Colorado depends on.
