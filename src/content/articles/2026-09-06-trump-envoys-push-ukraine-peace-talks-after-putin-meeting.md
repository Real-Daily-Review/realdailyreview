# Trump Envoys Push Ukraine Peace Talks After Putin Meeting

The Trump administration is making a serious diplomatic push to end the Ukraine war—and Colorado taxpayers should pay attention to how much more of their money gets sent overseas before any deal materializes.

Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff, leading Trump's peace effort, met with Vladimir Putin in Moscow for over three hours before heading to Kyiv to meet Volodymyr Zelenskyy (Fox News, Just The News, Breitbart). The back-to-back talks signal a coordinated attempt to revive negotiations aimed at winding down the four-year conflict.

**The conservative case:** Republicans have long argued that endless Ukraine aid drains American resources while Europe sits on the sidelines. Trump campaigned on ending "forever wars" and redirecting that spending toward domestic priorities—infrastructure, border security, and tax relief for working families. Kushner and Witkoff's shuttle diplomacy reflects that commitment to a negotiated settlement rather than indefinite military support.

**Democrats counter** that abandoning Ukraine emboldens Putin and destabilizes Europe, but conservatives argue that's precisely why a negotiated end—not perpetual aid—serves American interests better.

Colorado has sent billions in federal tax dollars to Ukraine aid packages. A genuine peace deal could free up those resources for Colorado infrastructure, water projects, and economic priorities that directly benefit state taxpayers.

Watch whether these talks produce concrete ceasefire terms—or whether they become cover for another round of aid requests.
