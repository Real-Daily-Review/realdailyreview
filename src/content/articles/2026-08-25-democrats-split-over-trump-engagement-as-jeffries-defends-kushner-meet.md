# Democrats Fracture Over Trump Engagement as Jeffries Defends Kushner Sit-Down

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries is catching flak from his own party after meeting privately with Jared Kushner, exposing deep Democratic divisions over whether to negotiate with or obstruct the Trump administration.

Jeffries defended the Monday meeting in a video statement, insisting "no one from the Trump Cartel is getting a pass" (Just The News). But fellow Democrats aren't buying it. Rep. Jamie Raskin (D-MD) escalated pressure on Kushner just days later, sending a letter demanding documents related to his dual roles in the Trump orbit—a move the *Washington Examiner* notes puts Democrats' "conflicting approaches to Trump's inner circle on display."

The tension reveals a core Democratic problem: some want dialogue and potential compromise; others demand total accountability and resistance. Jeffries has even bragged about Democratic obstruction efforts against Trump policies (*Fox News*), undercutting any credibility his outreach might carry.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats, who control the state legislature, will face similar pressure from their progressive base over whether to work with Republican Gov. Tina Peters or treat her administration as an adversary. Watch whether state Democratic leaders follow Jeffries' pragmatism or Raskin's scorched-earth approach—it'll signal how cooperative or combative Colorado's divided government will be.
