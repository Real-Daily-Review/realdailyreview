# Omar Fraud Saga Won't Die—And Colorado Republicans Are Watching

Minnesota Democrat Ilhan Omar faces mounting pressure over alleged ties to individuals involved in a massive fraud scandal, but constitutional reality may shield her from the forced resignation Trump and GOP allies are demanding.

Fox News reports that while Trump claims Omar "must resign," the Constitution grants Congress sole power to remove its own members—a power the Republican-controlled House has shown little appetite to exercise. The Daily Wire's exclusive interview with Minnesota Republican State Rep. Kristin Robbins reveals Omar was "thick as thieves" with people caught up in the fraud scheme, with Robbins noting six unanswered messages to Omar's office about the connections.

Republicans argue the scandal exposes Omar's judgment and raises serious questions about her vetting of associates. Democrats have largely remained silent, offering no substantive response to the fraud allegations or Omar's apparent lack of transparency.

The broader issue: Omar represents the progressive wing's credibility problem. Colorado Republicans watching this story see a cautionary tale about what happens when identity politics and ideological purity trump basic accountability. If House Democrats won't police their own members over credible fraud allegations, voters should ask what else they're willing to overlook.

Watch whether House leadership—or Omar herself—ever addresses those six unanswered messages.
