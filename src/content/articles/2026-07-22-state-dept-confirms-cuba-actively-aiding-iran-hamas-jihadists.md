# State Department Report: Cuba Actively Aiding Iran, Hamas, and Jihadist Groups Across Western Hemisphere

The State Department has finally put on record what conservatives have long argued—that Communist Cuba is a direct threat to American security and regional stability, actively abetting Iran, Hamas, and other jihadist organizations throughout the Western Hemisphere.

According to the department's report, "Cuba: The Capital of 21st Century Communism," the island nation is deliberately spreading hostile influence across the region in coordination with America's enemies. As *Breitbart* reports, the State Department charged Cuba with helping these groups extend their reach and operational capacity in the Western Hemisphere—a reality that undercuts any argument for normalizing relations with Havana.
