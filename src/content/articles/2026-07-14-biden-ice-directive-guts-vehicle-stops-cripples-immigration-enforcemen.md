# ICE Halts Vehicle Stops Nationwide—Gutting Immigration Enforcement After Two Shootings

The Biden administration has ordered ICE agents to stop conducting vehicle stops across the country, a sweeping retreat from immigration enforcement that will cripple the agency's ability to apprehend illegal aliens in Colorado and beyond.

The directive, confirmed by multiple sources including Fox News and The Daily Wire, came days after fatal shootings involving ICE agents in Texas and Maine. Under the new policy, agents can only stop vehicles if the occupants have serious or violent criminal histories—effectively ending routine immigration enforcement on Colorado's highways and in Denver metro areas where illegal immigration has surged.

**Republicans and immigration hawks argue** this policy handcuffs law enforcement at a time when border crossings remain elevated and sanctuary jurisdictions already obstruct federal immigration work. The move signals capitulation to anti-police activism rather than a reasoned response to two isolated incidents.

**Democrats and the Biden administration counter** that the policy prioritizes officer and public safety while focusing resources on the most dangerous offenders.

For Colorado, this means fewer ICE checkpoints on I-25 and I-70, reduced deportations of criminal aliens, and weakened interior enforcement in a state already struggling with illegal immigration's impact on schools, hospitals, and social services.
