# Former NBA Star Enes Kanter Freedom Declares for WNBA Draft, Challenges League's Eligibility Rules

Former NBA player Enes Kanter Freedom declared himself a prospect for the 2027 WNBA Draft on Friday, directly challenging the league to apply its eligibility guidelines consistently. "After careful consideration and reviewing the current eligibility guidelines, I'm officially declaring myself a @WNBA prospect," Freedom posted, claiming he "meets every requirement necessary" under existing rules.

Freedom emphasized he was not "here to mock, make fun of, or disrespect any community or personal choices," but rather demanding the WNBA apply its standards "equally to everyone" (Just The News). The move comes amid growing conservative scrutiny of transgender athlete policies in professional sports leagues—a flashpoint that has energized Republican voters nationwide.

The declaration puts the WNBA in a public bind: either enforce eligibility rules uniformly or openly acknowledge selective application based on identity politics. Conservative outlets (Daily Wire, Daily Caller, Breitbart) framed the move as exposing the logical inconsistency of progressive sports policies.

Colorado conservatives should watch whether the WNBA clarifies or changes its eligibility rules in response—and whether national Republican candidates use this moment to highlight how progressive ideology is reshaping women's sports.
