# Democrats' Moral Collapse on Display as Party Backs Troubled Maine Candidate

Colorado Democrats should take note: their national party is abandoning principle for power in Maine.

The Democratic Party is preparing to nominate Graham Platner for U.S. Senate despite documented red flags that would disqualify any Republican candidate from mainstream media scrutiny. According to the *Denver Post*, Platner carries "more baggage than Denver International Airport on Thanksgiving weekend," including a Nazi-inspired tattoo and a troubling behavioral history (Denver Post). Yet party leadership is moving forward anyway.

Hunter Biden's defense of Platner—essentially a "show me your phone" deflection—reveals the moral bankruptcy at the heart of modern progressivism. When confronted with genuine misconduct, Democrats don't defend the conduct; they attack the critic. This is the same playbook used to shield Biden family corruption for years.

Republicans argue this exposes the left's selective outrage: Democrats lecture conservatives about character and decency while nominating candidates with serious character problems. The *Daily Wire* notes Biden's defense strategy amounts to whataboutism—a tacit admission that Platner's record is indefensible on its own merits.

Colorado voters should watch whether state Democratic leaders distance themselves from this national embarrassment or double down on the party's credibility crisis.
