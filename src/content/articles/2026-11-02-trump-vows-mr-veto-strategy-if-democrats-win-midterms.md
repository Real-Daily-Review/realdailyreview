# Trump Ramps Up Midterm Push as Veto Threat Looms Over Congress

Trump is barnstorming the country ahead of the midterms, signaling he's ready to block Democratic legislation if the left flips Congress. The former president reversed course on campaign appearances this week, rallying supporters in Iowa on Sunday and warning he'll become "Mr. Veto" should Democrats gain control of the House and Senate (Washington Examiner).

The stakes for Colorado are real. A Democratic Congress would likely push carbon taxes, gun restrictions, and federal land grabs that would hammer Colorado energy workers, ranchers, and Second Amendment advocates. Conversely, a Republican-held Congress could advance Trump's deregulation agenda—critical for Colorado's oil and gas sector and water rights in the West.

Trump's veto threat underscores the gridlock Republicans expect if Democrats win. Without congressional majorities, Trump couldn't advance tax cuts, energy development, or border security measures that conservatives prioritize. Democrats would face the same roadblock pushing their spending and regulatory agenda (Daily Caller).

Polling headwinds complicate the picture. NBC data cited by the Daily Caller shows Trump disapproval and economic concerns weighing on Republican momentum—terrain Democrats will exploit in competitive races.

**Watch for:** Colorado's Senate and House races in 2026, where energy policy and water rights will define the battleground.
