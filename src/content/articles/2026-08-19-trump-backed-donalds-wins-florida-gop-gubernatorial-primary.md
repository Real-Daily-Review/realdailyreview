# Trump-Backed Donalds Wins Florida GOP Gubernatorial Nod, Setting Up General Election Test

Trump's endorsement proved decisive in Florida's Republican primary for governor. Rep. Byron Donalds (R-FL), backed by the former president, cruised to victory in the GOP nomination race to succeed Gov. Ron DeSantis, according to The Daily Wire. With 90% of votes counted, Donalds secured approximately 48% of the vote, well ahead of his primary opponents in what observers described as a contentious contest testing Florida's conservative movement's direction.

The primary also saw businessman Jim Schwartzel defeat Trump-endorsed appointee Catalina Lauf in a separate House race to succeed Rep. Byron Donalds in Florida's 19th District—a reliably Republican seat covering Cape Coral and Naples (Just The News, Fox News Politics). That race drew national attention as a "circus-like primary," with Schwartzel's victory suggesting Trump endorsements don't guarantee primary success in every contest.

Donalds now faces a general election showdown to lead the nation's third-most-populous state. His nomination reflects continued Trump influence over Florida Republican politics, though the competitive primary underscores fractures within the state's conservative coalition over the party's future direction.
