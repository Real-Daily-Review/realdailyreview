# Democrats Can't Escape the 'Woke 1.0' Albatross Around Their Necks

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and fellow progressive Democrats are scrambling to distance themselves from the radical positions they championed just years ago—but the receipts won't let them.

According to the Washington Examiner, AOC has begun "triangulating against fellow Democratic Socialists" and distancing herself from her own 2020 positions, even as she eyes higher office in 2028. The problem: voters remember. Democratic candidates nationwide are now running away from positions they once owned—defunding police, abolishing ICE, and using tax dollars to fund gender surgeries for illegal immigrants—positions that polling shows alienate mainstream voters.

The Daily Wire notes that progressive rhetoric, "captured by cameras," remains "forever available for public dissection." Democrats can't memory-hole their own words.

This matters for Colorado. As the state tilts purple, voters here will watch whether Democratic candidates—particularly those running statewide—have genuinely abandoned the far-left agenda or are simply hiding it until after Election Day. Colorado taxpayers and business owners should demand clarity: Are these candidates rejecting woke policy, or just the label?

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic establishment demands explicit repudiations of "Woke 1.0" from their nominees, or quietly hopes voters forget.
