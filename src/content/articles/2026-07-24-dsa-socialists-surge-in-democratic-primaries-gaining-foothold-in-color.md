# Democratic Socialists Gaining Ground in Colorado and Beyond

Colorado Democrats are watching their party shift sharply left as Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) candidates rack up primary wins across the country. According to the *Washington Examiner*, DSA-backed candidates have defeated established Democratic incumbents or claimed open seats in New York, Colorado, and Washington, D.C., signaling a troubling trend for moderate Democrats and a warning sign for Colorado voters concerned about radical policy.

The DSA's electoral momentum reflects the progressive wing's growing control over Democratic primary contests. These candidates typically champion higher taxes, expanded government programs, and aggressive climate regulations—policies that would hit Colorado's energy sector, small businesses, and working families hard.

While the DSA's attempt to unseat a Democratic incumbent in Arizona's Tuesday primary fell short, the group's success elsewhere demonstrates that traditional Democrats are losing ground to the socialist left. For Colorado conservatives, this underscores why Republican candidates must clearly articulate free-market alternatives and warn voters about the real-world costs of DSA-style governance: job losses in energy, skyrocketing taxes, and regulatory overreach.

Taxpayers should monitor which Colorado Democrats embrace DSA-backed policies in 2024—and hold them accountable at the ballot box.
