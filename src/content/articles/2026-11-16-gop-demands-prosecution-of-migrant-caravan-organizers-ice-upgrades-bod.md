# GOP Senator Demands Crackdown on Migrant Caravan Organizers as ICE Tightens Accountability

Senator Bernie Moreno (R-OH) is pushing the Trump administration to investigate and prosecute individuals and organizations financing migrant caravans heading toward the U.S. border, according to Breitbart. Moreno is calling for criminal prosecutions, financial sanctions, asset freezes, and visa suspensions against caravan facilitators—a direct challenge to the networks Democrats and open-border advocates say provide humanitarian aid to migrants.

Republicans argue that organized caravan operations represent coordinated efforts to circumvent U.S. immigration law and overwhelm border resources. Conservatives contend that tracing funding and prosecuting organizers is essential to disrupting what they view as a deliberate strategy to flood the southern border.

Meanwhile, ICE has rolled out new automatic body camera technology that activates all nearby cameras when one agent's camera turns on, *The Daily Wire* reports. The move aims to increase transparency during field operations—a response to public scrutiny and hostile encounters.

For Colorado, immigration enforcement remains a flashpoint: the state has absorbed significant migrant populations in recent years, straining local services and sparking fierce debates over sanctuary policies in Denver and Boulder.

**Watch for:** Whether the Trump administration actually pursues prosecutions against caravan organizers and whether Colorado cities resist federal immigration enforcement efforts.
