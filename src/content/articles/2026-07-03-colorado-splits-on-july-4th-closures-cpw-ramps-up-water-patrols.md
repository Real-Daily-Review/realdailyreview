# Colorado Businesses Split on July 4th Holiday Schedule; CPW Ramps Up Water Enforcement

Colorado's holiday weekend is shaping up with a patchwork of closures and openings as July 4th falls on Saturday this year. According to 9News Denver, some—but not all—Colorado businesses are observing the holiday on Friday, July 3rd, leaving consumers to check ahead before heading to banks, post offices, and retail locations.

Meanwhile, Colorado Parks and Wildlife is using the long weekend as an enforcement opportunity. KDVR Fox31 reports CPW is cracking down on boating under the influence (BUI) across the state's waterways, warning that officers will be actively patrolling lakes and rivers to catch intoxicated boaters.

The mixed holiday schedule reflects the practical reality that businesses operate under different rules: federal holidays don't automatically close private enterprises, and Colorado employers retain discretion over their own observance. Conservatives note this flexibility allows market forces and individual business judgment to prevail rather than one-size-fits-all mandates.

CPW's enforcement push, while reasonable public safety work, underscores the regulatory presence Colorado water recreationalists face during peak season—a reminder that even weekend fun comes with government oversight.

Taxpayers should watch whether CPW's holiday enforcement translates into sustainable year-round water safety messaging or becomes another revenue-generation tool through citations.
