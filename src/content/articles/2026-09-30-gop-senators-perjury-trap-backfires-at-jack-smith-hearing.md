# GOP Senator's Failed Perjury Trap at Jack Smith Hearing Exposes Weak Preparation

A Republican senator's botched attempt to catch special counsel Jack Smith in perjury during a Senate Judiciary Committee hearing has become a cautionary tale about sloppy opposition research. Sen. Eric Schmitt (R-MO) accused Smith of lying about his whereabouts, claiming he was in Atlanta during a specific date—only to have the accusation collapse when Schmitt confused an NBA game with a college women's basketball game, according to the *Washington Examiner*. 

The moment drew audible reactions from colleagues, including Sen. Katie Britt (R-AL), whose stunned expression went viral on social media, the *New York Post* reported.

Schmitt doubled down afterward, insisting he made "not a mistake," but the damage was done. The incident undercuts Republican credibility on the substantive criticisms of Smith's investigation into former President Donald Trump—a probe conservatives argue was politically weaponized against a political opponent.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Republicans should note how easily a poorly executed line of questioning can backfire and overshadow legitimate concerns about prosecutorial overreach.
