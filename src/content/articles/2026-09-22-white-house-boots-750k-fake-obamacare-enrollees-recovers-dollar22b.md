# White House Fraud Task Force Boots 750,000 Fake Obamacare Enrollees, Saves $2.2B

Vice President JD Vance's White House Task Force to Eliminate Fraud is yanking 750,000 fraudulent Obamacare recipients from the rolls, recovering $2.2 billion in taxpayer money that was being siphoned into a broken system (Fox News, The Daily Wire).

The action targets what conservatives have long argued is rampant abuse baked into the Affordable Care Act's enrollment process. Vance told Breitbart News that fraud is "definitely making health care more expensive"—a reality progressives have downplayed for years while defending the law's expansion.

Centers for Medicare &amp; Medicaid Services chief Dr. Mehmet Oz underscored the stakes, saying "Fraud will destroy Obamacare" if left unchecked. The crackdown reflects the Trump administration's stated commitment to rooting out government waste and protecting the integrity of federal programs.

For Colorado taxpayers, this matters: every dollar recovered in fraud prevention is a dollar not subsidizing fake claims or inflating premiums for those actually paying into the system. The state has seen its share of Medicaid and ACA enrollment irregularities over the years.
