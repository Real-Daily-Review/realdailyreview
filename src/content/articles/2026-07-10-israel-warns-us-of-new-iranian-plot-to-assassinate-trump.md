# Israel Warns U.S. of New Iranian Assassination Plot Against Trump

Israel has shared fresh intelligence with the United States indicating Iran has developed a new plot to assassinate President Trump, according to reports from the Wall Street Journal and corroborated by CNN. The warning represents the latest in a years-long pattern of Iranian threats against the president and underscores the persistent security challenges facing the administration.

The intelligence sharing comes as diplomatic efforts in the Middle East show signs of movement. Lebanon has reportedly accepted an American invitation for direct talks with Israel over the Hezbollah conflict, with negotiations set for Rome next week (Breitbart). The dual developments—heightened assassination threats and tentative peace overtures—illustrate the volatile regional dynamics the Trump administration must navigate.
