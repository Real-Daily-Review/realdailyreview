# Conservative FBI Deputy Director Andrew Bailey Steps Down After Year

FBI Deputy Director Andrew Bailey, a conservative favorite tapped by the Trump administration to help reform the bureau, announced his departure Monday, citing family reasons and a return to Missouri (The Daily Wire). Bailey's exit marks another leadership shuffle at an agency conservatives have long criticized for perceived institutional bias and overreach—particularly following the bureau's handling of January 6th investigations and the Hillary Clinton email probe.

Bailey's one-year tenure represented a rare conservative voice inside the FBI's leadership structure. His departure raises questions about the administration's ability to sustain reform efforts at an agency that has resisted scrutiny from Republican oversight committees.

The move comes as conservatives nationwide remain focused on FBI accountability. Colorado Republicans, like their counterparts across the country, have demanded transparency into the bureau's Colorado field office operations, particularly regarding election security protocols and civil liberties protections.

**What to watch:** Whether the Trump administration appoints another reform-minded deputy director or if the FBI reverts to institutional resistance against conservative-led oversight efforts.
