# Colorado Politics Lost a Legendary Reporter—But Her Legacy of Accountability Endures

Lynn Bartels, the relentless political reporter who spent decades holding Colorado's power brokers accountable, has died at 69. Bartels, a fixture at the Rocky Mountain News and Denver Post, earned her reputation as the "Grand Dame" of Colorado politics through dogged reporting, deep sources, and an infectious personality that made even tough interviews feel like conversations with a friend.

Her death marks the end of an era when local political journalism—the kind that forced transparency and exposed backroom dealing—still thrived in Colorado newsrooms. Bartels covered everything from legislative shenanigans to gubernatorial races with the kind of grit that made politicians nervous and voters informed. CPR News and the Colorado Sun both noted her unmatched ability to land scoops and her encyclopedic knowledge of state politics.

In an age when local newsrooms have shrunk and political coverage has grown softer, Bartels represented something increasingly rare: a reporter who believed government officials worked for the people, not the other way around. Whether you agreed with her conclusions or not, you knew she'd done the work.

Colorado's political class will miss having someone in the room who actually knew where all the bodies were buried.
