# Trump Secures G7 Backing on Iran Deal, AI Framework

President Trump closed the three-day G7 summit in France with a diplomatic win: fellow leaders threw their support behind his tentative agreement with Iran to open the Strait of Hormuz and extend a ceasefire, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver and Breitbart.

The agreement marks a significant foreign policy achievement for the Trump administration, though neither the White House nor Iran has publicly released the full text of the deal. That opacity has drawn scrutiny from those demanding transparency on terms affecting global energy markets and U.S. interests.

The summit also addressed artificial intelligence regulation—a growing concern for American tech competitiveness and innovation. Trump's approach favors lighter-touch governance over the heavy-handed EU regulatory model, positioning the U.S. to lead the AI economy rather than be hamstrung by bureaucratic overreach.

**Colorado angle:** Energy markets tied to Middle Eastern stability directly affect Colorado's oil and gas sector and energy prices for state consumers. A more stable Persian Gulf could ease crude prices, benefiting Colorado's energy industry and households.

**What to watch:** Whether the full text of the Iran agreement surfaces and what concessions the administration made—transparency matters when deals affect American energy independence and Colorado jobs.
