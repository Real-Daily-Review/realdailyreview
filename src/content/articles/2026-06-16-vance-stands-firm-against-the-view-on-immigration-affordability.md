# VP Vance Stands Firm Against 'The View' Hosts on Immigration, Affordability

Vice President JD Vance walked into ABC's "The View" on Tuesday and refused to back down from conservative principles, sparring with the show's hosts over immigration, cost-of-living, and other flashpoint issues while promoting his new book.

Vance approached the appearance with humor, joking that he was entering "enemy territory" as he sat among the liberal hosts. According to Fox News Digital, Vance told the network beforehand he hoped the hosts would "meet him halfway" during the segment. That didn't happen—instead, the Daily Wire reports the vice president faced an "intensely one-sided interview" where he had to refute point after point from the panel.

The Daily Caller noted Vance "remained calm" while defending Trump administration positions on border security and economic policy, two issues that directly affect Colorado families struggling with housing costs and concerned about illegal immigration's impact on wages and job availability.

The appearance underscores the administration's willingness to engage hostile media rather than retreat to friendly outlets—a contrast to the previous administration's approach. For Colorado conservatives watching inflation and border chaos, Vance's willingness to defend unpopular truths on a hostile stage matters.
