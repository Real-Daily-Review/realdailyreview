# Trump Threatens Iran Strikes; Energy Markets Watch Closely

President Trump is threatening additional military strikes on Iran while simultaneously pushing to reopen the Strait of Hormuz—a move that could have immediate consequences for Colorado energy markets and gas prices at the pump.

According to 9News Denver, Trump has signaled he will order a halt to Iran strikes once parameters are reached for a deal to end the conflict. But the administration is escalating rhetoric in the meantime, with the U.S. pushing hard to restore shipping through one of the world's most critical oil chokepoints.

The strategy raises questions about Trump's economic priorities. National Review argues the Iran conflict is distracting from Trump's core strength: economic stewardship, noting that the administration's simultaneous pursuit of tariffs and military action abroad risks undermining domestic growth.

**The Colorado angle:** Disruptions to Middle Eastern oil supplies drive up energy costs nationwide. Colorado's energy sector and consumers already face headwinds from regulatory uncertainty; prolonged geopolitical tension could spike heating and transportation costs for families and businesses across the state.

Republicans generally support Trump's hardline Iran stance as necessary deterrence, though some conservatives worry extended military commitments abroad drain resources from domestic priorities and economic growth.

Watch whether the administration achieves a negotiated settlement quickly—or whether escalation continues to roil energy markets heading into winter.
