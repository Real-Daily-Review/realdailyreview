# WNBA Star Cunningham Stands Firm on Women's Sports While League Embraces Trans Activism

Sophie Cunningham of the Indiana Fever has become the rare professional athlete willing to state plainly what most Democrats won't: biological males should not compete in women's sports. Her position sparked immediate blowback from fellow WNBA players, including Seattle Storm center Stefanie Dolson, who responded by wearing a "Trans Rights Are Human Rights" shirt during a league promotional campaign (The Daily Wire).

Cunningham's stance reflects growing concern among conservatives and many ordinary Americans that progressive ideology is eroding fairness in women's athletics. Republicans argue that allowing biological males to compete against women undermines decades of Title IX protections and gives unfair physical advantages that no amount of hormone therapy fully eliminates.

The WNBA's response—featuring players in pro-trans messaging during official promotions—illustrates how corporate America and professional sports leagues have become vehicles for progressive activism rather than neutral platforms. Dolson's shirt, worn as part of an Alaska Airlines-sponsored "Arrivals" campaign, was less about supporting trans rights and more about publicly shaming a woman athlete for defending her own sex-based category (Breitbart).

Conservatives say Cunningham deserves credit for speaking truth when institutional pressure runs the opposite direction. Colorado voters and taxpayers should watch whether this debate spreads to state-level youth sports policy, where similar battles are brewing.
