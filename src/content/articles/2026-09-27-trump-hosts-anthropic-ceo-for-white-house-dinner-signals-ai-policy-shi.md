# Trump Extends Olive Branch to AI CEO After Months of Tension

President Trump is reportedly hosting Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for a private White House dinner Sunday, signaling a potential thaw in their long-running dispute over artificial intelligence policy and Pentagon access.

The dinner invitation comes after months of friction between Trump and Amodei, who previously resisted giving the Defense Department unfettered access to Anthropic's AI systems. Trump personally extended the invitation after Amodei missed last week's state dinner for Chinese President Xi Jinping (The Daily Wire reports).

The move reflects Trump's pragmatic approach to Silicon Valley: while his administration has barred CNN, MSNBC, and Politico from White House press access—a decision Trump aide Todd Blanche defended by noting that "being in the White House is a privilege, not a right"—the president appears willing to negotiate directly with tech leaders on national security and AI governance.

For Colorado, this matters. The state hosts significant AI research and tech infrastructure; how Trump resolves disputes with major AI firms will shape regulatory and investment climate for local tech workers and startups.

Conservatives should watch whether this dinner produces concrete commitments on Pentagon AI access or signals Trump is softening on tech-sector demands that conflict with national defense priorities.
