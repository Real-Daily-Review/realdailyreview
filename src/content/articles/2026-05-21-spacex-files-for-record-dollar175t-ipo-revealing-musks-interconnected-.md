# SpaceX Files for Historic IPO, Revealing Musk's Sprawling Business Web

SpaceX has filed for what could become the largest initial public offering ever, with a potential valuation of $1.75 trillion, according to Al Jazeera. The filing, made public weeks ahead of an expected June launch, positions Elon Musk as CEO, CTO, and chairman—roles that underscore his central control over the aerospace company.

The 330-page document reveals extensive financial entanglement across Musk's business empire. xAI, his artificial intelligence venture, burned $6.4 billion in 2025 while planning major expansion of its Grok product, according to TechCrunch. The filing mentions Tesla 87 times, xAI 356 times, and X 267 times, alongside references to the Boring Company and Neuralink—illustrating what The Verge describes as "ways in which Elon Musk's companies interact and overlap with each other, shuffling money around in ways that are often difficult to keep track of."

The Verge flags Musk himself as a risk factor for investors, given the interconnected nature of his holdings and his outsized influence across multiple ventures. The filing comes weeks after Musk lost a court case to OpenAI, a competitor to xAI, according to the Washington Examiner.

Watch for the IPO's pricing and launch timeline, expected this summer, and how markets react to the disclosed financial relationships between SpaceX and Musk's other companies.
