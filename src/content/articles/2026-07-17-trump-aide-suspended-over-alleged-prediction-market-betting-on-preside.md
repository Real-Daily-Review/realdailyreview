# Trump White House Staffer Placed on Leave Over Alleged Prediction Market Betting Scheme

A Trump administration teleprompter operator has been suspended without pay after federal investigators discovered he allegedly placed bets on prediction markets tied to the president's speeches—a potential conflict of interest that raises serious questions about information access and insider trading concerns.

According to *Just The News*, Kalshi's surveillance systems flagged suspicious betting patterns on markets involving Trump that deviated from normal behavior. When the company investigated the accounts, it discovered the user was a federal employee. The firm's enforcement chief notified federal regulators of the findings (*9News Denver*).

The case highlights a troubling vulnerability: a White House staffer with advance knowledge of presidential remarks could theoretically profit from prediction markets betting on what Trump would say. This isn't a partisan issue—it's a fundamental problem of government employees exploiting privileged information for personal gain.

The investigation underscores why strict ethics rules and transparency matter, regardless of which party controls the White House. Coloradans should expect their tax dollars to pay employees who serve the public interest, not their own wallets.

Watch whether federal authorities pursue insider-trading charges and what new safeguards the White House implements to prevent similar schemes.
