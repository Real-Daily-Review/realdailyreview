# America 250 Celebrations Heat Up—Literally—As Nation Marks Founding

A historic heat dome is turning America's Semiquincentennial festivities into a test of endurance, with Washington D.C. ranking among the hottest places on Earth during the milestone weekend. According to The Daily Wire, only the Sahara Desert, China's Gobi Desert, parts of the Middle East, and America's Desert Southwest are hotter than the nation's capital as celebrations ramp up.

Despite the sweltering conditions, the commemorations are proceeding. Pope Leo XIV, the first U.S.-born pontiff, received the National Constitution Center's Liberty Medal in Philadelphia, declaring "May God bless America!" (Daily Caller). President Trump is scheduled to speak at Mount Rushmore, continuing the America 250 observance across the country (9News Denver).

For Coloradans, the extreme heat serves as a reminder of summer conditions that strain the state's power grid and water resources—issues that demand reliable energy production and smart water management, not ideological restrictions on fossil fuels or overregulation of agriculture. The heat also underscores why Colorado's outdoor economy and tourism depend on stable, affordable electricity.
