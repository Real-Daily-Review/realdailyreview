# Fort Hood Killer Set for Public Execution as Pentagon Opens Firing Squad to Livestream

The Pentagon will allow Americans to witness the execution of Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan via livestream, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced—a rare move that underscores the gravity of the 2009 massacre that killed 13 soldiers and wounded dozens more.

A survivor shot seven times by Hasan plans to witness the firing squad execution but issued a stark warning: don't turn justice into spectacle. His message cuts to the heart of a deeper question about how Americans should reckon with mass murder on military soil.

The decision to livestream reflects a shift toward transparency in capital punishment, though it remains extraordinarily rare. Hasan, a former Army psychiatrist, has long been a symbol of the military's struggle with internal security and radicalization—issues that conservatives argue demand unflinching scrutiny of ideology and vetting procedures within the ranks.

The public execution also raises questions about military justice and deterrence that resonate beyond Fort Hood.
