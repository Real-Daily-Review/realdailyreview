# Lakewood Community Honors Fallen Officer Nick Graham

Colorado's law enforcement community gathers Thursday to honor Lakewood Police Agent Nick Graham, killed in the line of duty—a stark reminder of the dangers officers face daily and why backing the blue matters in an era of anti-police rhetoric.

According to KDVR Fox31, the memorial for Graham has grown exponentially as the law enforcement community prepares to bury one of its own. 9News Denver is providing live coverage of the funeral procession and memorial service Thursday, along with details on road closures and how residents can attend or donate in Graham's honor.

This tragedy underscores a fundamental conservative principle: law enforcement officers put their lives on the line to protect our communities, families, and property rights. While progressive activists across the country push to defund police and strip officers of qualified immunity, Colorado families know the truth—we need strong, well-supported law enforcement.

The outpouring of support for Graham and his family reflects the values most Coloradans hold: respect for those who serve, solidarity with the thin blue line, and commitment to the rule of law that keeps our state safe.

Watch for how Colorado's political leadership responds—whether they'll use this moment to reaffirm support for law enforcement or continue entertaining anti-police activists.
