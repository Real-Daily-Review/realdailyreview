# Obama Center Opens Amid Questions About Costs, Biden's Stumble

The Barack Obama Presidential Center opened Thursday in Chicago with A-list fanfare—Bruce Springsteen, Stevie Wonder, and three former presidents in attendance—but the ceremony highlighted two problems for Democrats: ballooning project costs and an awkward moment that underscored concerns about President Biden's fitness.

The White House seized on the Obama Center's delays and rising expenses to contrast Trump's track record as a builder, *Fox News* reported. Republicans argue the project exemplifies Democratic mismanagement of public resources and cost overruns—a sharp contrast to Trump's efficiency messaging heading into 2024.

The ceremony itself became a metaphor for Democratic dysfunction. Biden was left standing alone on stage after remarks ended, awkwardly waving as the crowd dispersed, *New York Post* noted. The moment reignited questions about his mental acuity that have dogged his presidency.

A lighter note: former President George W. Bush rekindled his friendship with Michelle Obama by gifting her Altoids—a callback to a viral moment from nearly a decade prior, *Washington Examiner* reported.

For Colorado conservatives, this serves as a cautionary tale about government project management and Democratic leadership heading into the 2024 election cycle—watch whether Republicans effectively weaponize the Obama Center's cost overruns in messaging about fiscal responsibility.
