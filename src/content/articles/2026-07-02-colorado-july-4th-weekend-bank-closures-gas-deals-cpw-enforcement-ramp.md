# Colorado Braces for July 4th Weekend: Banks Close, Gas Prices Drop, CPW Ramps Up Enforcement

Colorado taxpayers and holiday travelers face a patchwork of closures and enforcement this Fourth of July weekend as government agencies prepare for an expected surge in road and water traffic.

With Independence Day falling on Saturday, many Colorado banks and post offices will shut down Friday, July 3rd, forcing residents to plan ahead for financial transactions (9News Denver). Meanwhile, roughly 61 million Americans are expected to hit the roads, and Colorado drivers can catch modest gas savings through holiday promotions, though energy prices remain elevated by historical standards (9News Denver).

Colorado Parks and Wildlife is intensifying "BUI" (boating under the influence) enforcement across state waterways this weekend, deploying officers to catch intoxicated boaters (KDVR Fox31). While public safety messaging around impaired boating is reasonable, conservatives should note the broader pattern: government agencies expanding enforcement operations during holidays, often with minimal advance notice to the public.

The holiday weekend also highlights Colorado's energy situation—gas prices remain a pinch on family budgets despite temporary discounts, underscoring the impact of Biden administration energy policies on everyday Coloradans.
