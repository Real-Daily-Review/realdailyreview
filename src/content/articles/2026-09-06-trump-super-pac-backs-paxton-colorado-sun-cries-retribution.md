# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Fight; Colorado Sun Warns of "Retribution"

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC has committed $10 million to rescue Republican Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's Senate campaign against Democrat James James Talarico, according to new FEC filings reported by The Daily Wire. The financial injection marks the first major deployment of Trump's war chest in a competitive general election race this cycle.

Meanwhile, Colorado Sun opinion writer Carman warns that Trump's administration is mounting a "campaign of retribution" against Colorado specifically, claiming the state faces coercion to surrender constitutional powers or face federal retaliation. Carman frames the conflict as an assault on state sovereignty.

Republicans and Trump allies counter that the administration is simply enforcing existing law and holding states accountable for policies they view as unconstitutional—a position conservatives argue is legitimate executive authority, not "retribution." The framing dispute reflects deeper disagreement over federal-state power and whether Trump's actions constitute lawful governance or political weaponization.

For Colorado, the stakes involve water rights, energy policy, and state autonomy—issues where Republican leaders have clashed with Biden-era federal overreach. Conservatives worry progressive Colorado officials will capitulate to federal pressure rather than defend state interests.
