# Rubio Eyes 2028 While GOP Regroups After Midterm Losses

Secretary of State Marco Rubio is signaling potential presidential ambitions at a Federalist Society keynote, even as Republicans grapple with midterm setbacks that have left the party searching for direction.

Rubio's speech comes as conservative legal circles and GOP operatives scrutinize his remarks for clues about a 2028 run (Fox News Politics). The timing is notable: Republicans are under pressure to articulate a coherent agenda after disappointing election results, and Rubio—a seasoned conservative voice on foreign policy and judicial philosophy—represents one potential path forward for the party's establishment wing.

At the event, Rubio's daughter drew attention with her poised public remarks, prompting the Secretary to joke about her natural speaking ability (New York Post). While a lighter moment, it underscores how GOP figures are rebuilding their public profiles in the post-midterm environment.

**For Colorado conservatives:** Watch whether Rubio or other potential 2028 candidates address energy policy, water rights, or Second Amendment protections—issues that will define Republican messaging in Western swing states. The GOP's ability to articulate a winning platform on kitchen-table economics and limited government will determine whether 2028 becomes a genuine reset or another missed opportunity.
