# Rand Paul Hammers Fauci Over Self-Promotion During COVID Crisis

Senator Rand Paul is escalating his criticism of Dr. Anthony Fauci, accusing the former White House medical advisor of lobbying for a $1 million prize while directing the federal government's COVID-19 response. According to Fox News, Paul claims Fauci pressured NIH colleagues to nominate him for the Dan David Prize—a stunning conflict of interest that underscores what conservatives view as the self-serving nature of the pandemic bureaucracy.

The Kentucky Republican's attacks intensified after newly released diary excerpts from Fauci surfaced, prompting Paul to mock the doctor on social media for what he characterized as narcissistic self-aggrandizement (Washington Examiner). The diary entries have reignited partisan battles over federal COVID management, a flashpoint that continues to divide Republicans and Democrats.

**The conservative argument:** Paul and his allies contend that Fauci's conduct reveals how unaccountable federal health officials prioritized personal accolades over transparent science during a national crisis. They argue this behavior exemplifies why government agencies need stronger oversight and term limits.

**Democrats' position:** The left largely dismisses Paul's attacks as performative partisan theater designed to undermine public health institutions.
