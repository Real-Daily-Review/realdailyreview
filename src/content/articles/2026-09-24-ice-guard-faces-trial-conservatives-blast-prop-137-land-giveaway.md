# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Trial Over Protest Shooting; Prop 137 Land Deal Draws Conservative Fire

A Colorado judge has cleared the way for prosecution of Brandon Booth, an ex-ICE detention center guard accused of shooting toward female protesters who confronted him about "race traitors" and threatened to locate guards' children, the Colorado Sun reports. The ruling Wednesday found sufficient evidence to proceed to trial.

Separately, conservatives are mounting a sharp challenge to Proposition 137, Colorado's ballot measure on land conservation. Complete Colorado's editorial board warns that the initiative would funnel taxpayer dollars to wealthy nonprofits—chiefly The Nature Conservancy, the measure's primary backer—under the guise of environmental stewardship. Critics argue this represents another example of government-subsidized activism that benefits special interests while Colorado families foot the bill.

The Booth case underscores tensions around immigration enforcement and protest tactics, while Prop 137 reflects a broader conservative concern: that Colorado's progressive legislature and ballot initiatives increasingly transfer public resources to left-aligned organizations without adequate scrutiny or accountability.

Taxpayers should watch whether Prop 137 gains traction in November and how the Booth trial unfolds—both signal where Colorado's political center is heading.
