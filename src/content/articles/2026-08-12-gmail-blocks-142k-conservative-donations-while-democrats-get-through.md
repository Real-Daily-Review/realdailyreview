# Gmail's Spam Filter Crushes Conservative Donations—and Nobody's Stopping It

Google's Gmail has quietly buried roughly 142,000 conservative campaign donations by flagging WinRed fundraising emails as spam, while allowing Democratic ActBlue messages through at a dramatically higher rate. According to security researcher Brian Krebs, WinRed donation links hit spam traps at roughly four times the rate of ActBlue mail in late July 2025 (Washington Examiner).

Google frames the filtering as legitimate security practice. But conservatives argue this represents unchecked corporate power over political speech and fundraising—a silent thumb on the scale that disadvantages Republican candidates and causes without transparency or accountability.

For Colorado Republicans and conservative donors, the implications are stark: your contributions may never reach campaigns you intend to support, while Democratic rivals face no such obstacles. This isn't a glitch; it's a systemic bias baked into one of the world's most powerful tech platforms.

The real question isn't whether Gmail's filters work—it's whether a private company should unilaterally decide which political messages reach voters' inboxes. Colorado conservatives should demand transparency from Google on its filtering criteria and push state and federal lawmakers to investigate whether tech giants are tilting the electoral playing field.
