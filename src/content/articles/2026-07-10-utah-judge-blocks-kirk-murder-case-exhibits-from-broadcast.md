# Judge Restricts Broadcast of Evidence in Charlie Kirk Murder Case

A Utah judge has barred media outlets from broadcasting exhibits in the Tyler Robinson preliminary hearing—the man accused of assassinating conservative activist Charlie Kirk on September 10, 2025—after a damning letter allegedly written by Robinson was mistakenly aired Thursday (Washington Examiner). The restriction came after video testimony from Lance Twiggs, Robinson's former lover and roommate, was presented in court; Twiggs reportedly expressed regret over his involvement in the case (Breitbart).

The judge delayed a decision on whether Robinson will stand trial until at least September, extending the proceedings nearly a year after Kirk's death (The Daily Wire). The Kirk family has described the emotional toll of the prolonged legal process.

**The Colorado angle:** Kirk, a prominent conservative media figure and founder of Turning Point USA, was based in the state and had significant influence on Colorado Republican politics and youth activism. His death sent shockwaves through conservative circles nationwide.

Republicans and conservatives have closely monitored this case as a high-profile attack on a leading voice in the movement. The delayed trial timeline raises questions about judicial efficiency and the handling of sensitive evidence in politically charged cases.

**Watch next:** Whether the judge's September ruling will finally move the case toward trial or trigger additional delays that further extend the Kirk family's ordeal.
