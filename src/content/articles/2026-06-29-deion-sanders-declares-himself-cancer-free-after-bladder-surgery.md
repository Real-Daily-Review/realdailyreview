# Deion Sanders Says He's Cancer-Free After Bladder Surgery

Colorado football coach Deion Sanders announced he considers himself cancer-free a year after undergoing surgery to remove his cancerous bladder, telling reporters he feels like his younger self again. "I'm just thankful," Sanders said, according to the Daily Caller.

Sanders, who took over as head coach of the University of Colorado Buffaloes, had kept his health battle largely private before going public with the diagnosis. His recovery marks a significant personal milestone as he continues rebuilding the Buffs football program.

The news comes as Sanders has been working to restore Colorado football to national relevance after years of decline. His return to full health removes a major distraction from his coaching duties and signals stability for a program that has struggled with consistency and leadership turnover.

Conservatives and Colorado sports fans should watch whether Sanders' renewed energy translates into on-field success and whether the university's significant investment in his coaching tenure pays dividends for the state's flagship athletic program.
