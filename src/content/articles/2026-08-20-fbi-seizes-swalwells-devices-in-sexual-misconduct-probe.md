# FBI Seizes Swalwell's Devices in Sexual Misconduct Probe

Former California Rep. Eric Swalwell's political career is collapsing under the weight of federal criminal investigation—a stark reminder that no amount of partisan grandstanding shields Democrats from accountability.

FBI agents confronted Swalwell at San Francisco International Airport on Saturday and seized his cell phone and other electronic devices as part of an ongoing sexual misconduct investigation, according to sources cited by Fox News and The Daily Wire. The seizure marks an escalation in the federal probe into allegations that prompted Swalwell to resign from Congress in April and abandon his gubernatorial campaign earlier this year.

Swalwell, a vocal Trump critic and former House Intelligence Committee member, had faced renewed scrutiny over his time in office and fresh allegations from multiple women. The airport confrontation and device seizure signal federal investigators are treating the matter as a serious criminal matter, not mere political theater.

The case underscores a broader reality: when Democrats face real legal jeopardy—rather than the partisan witch hunts they routinely manufacture against Republicans—the machinery of federal law enforcement moves swiftly.
