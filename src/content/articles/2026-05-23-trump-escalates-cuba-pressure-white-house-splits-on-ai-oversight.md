# Trump Escalates Cuba Pressure as White House Splits on AI Oversight

The Trump administration is intensifying pressure on Cuba, with the US warning that a peaceful agreement is unlikely, according to BBC World. Havana has rejected the US position as a "fraudulent case" designed to justify military intervention.

Separately, President Trump's abrupt decision to withdraw an executive order on artificial intelligence testing has revealed internal White House divisions over technology policy, according to The Hill. The move underscores disagreement within the administration about how to regulate AI development without impeding innovation.

The Cuba situation remains unresolved, with both sides at odds over the nature of negotiations. The AI order reversal signals ongoing tension between officials favoring stricter tech oversight and those prioritizing industry growth.

Watch for whether the administration clarifies its stance on both Cuba diplomacy and AI regulation in coming weeks.
