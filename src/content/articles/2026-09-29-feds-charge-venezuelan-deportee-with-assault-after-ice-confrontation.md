# Federal Charges Filed Against Venezuelan Deportee After ICE Confrontation

Border enforcement just got a courtroom test: federal prosecutors have charged a Venezuelan national with assaulting an officer after he allegedly resisted during an ICE traffic stop in Texas that ended with an agent firing his weapon.

According to a federal complaint, Wilber Rafael Garces-Perez, 28, attempted to escape Immigration and Customs Enforcement following a lawful traffic stop. The stop came after a judge issued a final removal order against Garces-Perez, meaning ICE had clear legal authority to apprehend him for deportation (9News Denver, Just The News).

The incident underscores a core conservative argument: immigration enforcement requires officers to have real authority and the ability to execute removal orders without obstruction. When deportable aliens resist lawful detention, the consequences can escalate quickly—and the legal system must back up agents doing their jobs.
