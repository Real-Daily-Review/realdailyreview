# Michigan Democrat's Endorsement Flip Exposes Left's Radical Drift

A sitting Democratic congresswoman just endorsed a Republican for Senate—and her own party is furious about it. Rep. Haley Stevens (D-MI) crossed party lines Sunday to back former Rep. Mike Rogers (R-MI) over Democratic nominee Abdul El-Sayed, telling Michigan voters the choice was clear. Stevens attended church with Rogers in Detroit and announced her support via social media, reportedly telling fellow Democrat Rep. Debbie Dingell just hours earlier that the endorsement rumor was false.

The move triggered a meltdown among Democratic leadership. Chuck Schumer and Hakeem Jeffries unleashed on Stevens, while radical left-wing voices erupted online (Fox News reports). El-Sayed's campaign, meanwhile, saw a $2.4 million single-day fundraising surge—though the nominee himself begged reporters to stop asking about the endorsement, urging them to focus on other topics (The Daily Wire).

**The conservative takeaway:** When a Democrat of Stevens' stature abandons her own nominee two days before Election Day, it signals El-Sayed represents something too far left even for mainstream Democrats. Stevens' willingness to cross party lines over principle—and the Democratic establishment's fury in response—reveals how thoroughly progressives have captured the party apparatus.
