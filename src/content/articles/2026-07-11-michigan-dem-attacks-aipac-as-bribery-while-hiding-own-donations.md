# Michigan Democrat's AIPAC Hypocrisy Exposes Left's Foreign-Money Double Standard

A Michigan Senate candidate backed by Bernie Sanders and AOC is crying "legalized bribery" over pro-Israel donations while staying mum about his own funding sources—a move that exposes the progressive left's selective outrage over money in politics.

Abdul El-Sayed, running in Michigan's Democratic primary with endorsements from Sanders and Ocasio-Cortez (Washington Examiner), has attacked AIPAC spending against his campaign as foreign influence (Fox News). Yet he's remained silent on other donations flowing into his race, raising obvious questions about which money he actually objects to.

The hypocrisy is instructive. Progressives routinely denounce "dark money" and corporate donations when they oppose their candidates—but embrace the same funding streams when aligned with their agenda. El-Sayed's complaint about AIPAC specifically, while ignoring other outside spending, suggests his real objection isn't principle; it's politics.

For Colorado conservatives, this is a reminder: don't expect consistency from the left on campaign finance reform. They'll invoke "democracy" and "foreign influence" selectively, depending on whose interests are threatened. The same activists demanding disclosure and limits on conservative-aligned groups will defend or ignore comparable spending from their own side.

Watch whether national Democrats distance themselves from El-Sayed's AIPAC criticism or double down—that'll tell you everything about their actual commitment to transparency.
