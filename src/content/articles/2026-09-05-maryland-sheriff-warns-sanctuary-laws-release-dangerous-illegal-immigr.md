# Democrats' Sanctuary Policies Embolden Illegal Immigration While Law Enforcement Fights Back

Colorado should pay close attention to how Democratic sanctuary policies are creating chaos in other states—because similar measures could land here next.

A Maryland sheriff is sounding the alarm over the state's Community Trust Act, which he argues prioritizes illegal immigrants over public safety by potentially forcing jails to release dangerous individuals flagged by Immigration and Customs Enforcement (Fox News). Meanwhile, federal authorities arrested 380 Haitian illegal immigrants in Springfield, Ohio, after a federal protection program expired (Washington Examiner), underscoring the scale of the problem when enforcement lapses.

Republicans argue these sanctuary laws tie the hands of local law enforcement and create safe havens for criminals. Sheriffs and ICE officials contend that releasing deportable aliens—especially those with criminal records—endangers communities and undermines federal immigration authority.

Democrats counter that such policies protect vulnerable immigrant communities from deportation and build trust between immigrants and local police. They frame enforcement crackdowns as inhumane.

**The Colorado angle:** Progressive lawmakers in Denver and Boulder have pushed similar sanctuary measures. If Colorado adopts comparable policies, taxpayers could face the same public safety and fiscal costs Maryland and Ohio are experiencing—from detention expenses to crime prevention.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats introduce new sanctuary legislation in the 2025 session and how Republican lawmakers respond.
