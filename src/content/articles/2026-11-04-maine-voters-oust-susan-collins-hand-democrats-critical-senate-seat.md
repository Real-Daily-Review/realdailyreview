# Maine Senate Seat Falls to Democrats as Collins Loses After 30 Years

Republicans lost a critical Senate seat Tuesday when Maine voters rejected Susan Collins, ending the moderate GOP incumbent's three-decade run in Washington. Democrat Troy Jackson's victory narrows the Republican Senate majority and signals trouble for the GOP in blue-leaning states where Collins had managed to survive previous cycles.

Collins, a five-term senator and one of the few Republicans still competitive in a state carried by Kamala Harris in 2024, conceded to Jackson on Tuesday night. The Washington Examiner reports Jackson's win hands Democrats their 53rd Senate seat—a major pickup in what Republicans hoped would be a defensive year.

The loss underscores a harsh reality for Republicans: even moderate, long-serving GOP senators face headwinds in increasingly Democratic states. Collins had carved out a reputation as a dealmaker willing to cross party lines, but that positioning proved insufficient against Democratic turnout and national messaging.

**The Colorado angle:** While Maine's Senate race is distant, Colorado Republicans should note the pattern. Moderate positioning hasn't insulated GOP senators in blue-trending states—a cautionary tale as Colorado's own political lean continues leftward.
