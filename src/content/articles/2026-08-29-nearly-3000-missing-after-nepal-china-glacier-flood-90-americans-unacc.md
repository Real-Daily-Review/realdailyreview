# Nepal-China Floods Leave Nearly 3,000 Missing; 90 Americans Unaccounted For

A catastrophic flash flood triggered by a glacier avalanche along the Nepal-China border has left nearly 3,000 people missing as rescue operations entered their fourth day. According to 9News Denver and the Washington Examiner, about 90 Americans remain unaccounted for in the disaster zone.

Survivors are arriving at relief centers with nothing but the clothes on their backs. Some village residents have begun returning to assess what remains of their homes, though many communities lie buried under debris from the deluge. Authorities continue recovery efforts, transporting survivors to safety as families wait anxiously for word of loved ones.

The scale of the disaster underscores the unpredictable dangers posed by high-altitude glacial systems in remote mountain regions. For American families with relatives in Nepal—a popular trekking and tourism destination—the unfolding tragedy highlights the risks of travel in geologically unstable areas.

The State Department and relevant agencies should prioritize identifying and assisting U.S. citizens caught in the disaster zone.
