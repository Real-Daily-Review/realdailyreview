# Trump Rebrands AI as 'Super Intelligence'—But Colorado Should Focus on Real Policy

President Trump signed an executive order Tuesday officially renaming "artificial intelligence" to "Super Intelligence" (SI), arguing the term "artificial" undersells the technology's actual power and capability. Trump teased the change during a state visit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and formalized it at a White House event, claiming the rebrand reflects accuracy rather than mere marketing (Just The News, Daily Wire).

The move follows Trump's meeting with top AI leaders focused on balancing innovation with regulation—a signal the administration intends to keep the sector competitive rather than strangled by bureaucratic red tape. Trump's rationale: the technology is "very powerful" and "very brilliant," so calling it "artificial" is a misnomer.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans have long argued that heavy-handed AI regulation stifles American innovation and hands competitive advantage to China. A lighter regulatory touch aligns with that philosophy. However, the rebrand itself is largely symbolic—what matters for Colorado's tech sector, energy industry, and job creators is whether Trump's broader AI policy actually removes barriers to development and deployment.

**What to Watch:** Whether this rebranding translates into concrete deregulation that benefits Colorado's growing tech corridor and energy sector, or remains a rhetorical flourish while bureaucratic obstacles remain in place.
