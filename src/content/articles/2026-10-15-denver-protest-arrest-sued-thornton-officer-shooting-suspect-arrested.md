# Denver Protester Sues Over Arrest; Thornton Officer Shooting Suspect Nabbed

A No Kings protester is suing Denver police, claiming he was tackled while complying with orders to leave last October's demonstration (9News Denver). Meanwhile, law enforcement scored a win in the metro area: police arrested a suspect in the shooting of a Thornton officer Sunday night after community tips led investigators to him near Riverdale Road on Tuesday, with bail set at $1 million (KDVR Fox31, 9News Denver).

The protest lawsuit highlights the tension between First Amendment rights and police authority—a debate conservatives and civil libertarians often find common ground on when government overreach is alleged.
