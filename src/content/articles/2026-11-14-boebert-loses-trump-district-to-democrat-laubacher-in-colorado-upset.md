# Lauren Boebert Loses Trump-Friendly District to Democrat Laubacher

Colorado Republicans suffered a stunning setback Tuesday when voters ousted Rep. Lauren Boebert in the heavily pro-Trump 4th Congressional District, handing the seat to retired Navy rear admiral Eileen Laubacher.

Boebert's loss is particularly sharp for the GOP: according to the Colorado Sun, only one other Republican incumbent nationwide lost in a district where President Trump performed better in 2024. The 4th—spanning the Eastern Plains and Douglas County—had been considered safely Republican terrain.

Laubacher, a Democrat, campaigned as a "no drama moderate" (CPR News), directly contrasting herself with Boebert's combative, high-profile style. The strategy resonated with voters in a district that has trended suburban and exurban, where Boebert's firebrand conservatism and personal controversies apparently cost her crucial support.

For Colorado conservatives, the loss signals vulnerability in what should be a reliably Republican seat. Boebert, a Trump ally seeking a fourth term, underperformed the presidential ticket—a warning sign that candidate quality and local dynamics still matter, even in red districts. Republicans will need to recalibrate messaging and candidate selection in Colorado's purple-trending suburbs if they hope to reclaim this seat in 2028.

Watch whether the GOP primary in CD-4 produces a more disciplined, locally-focused candidate who can rebuild trust with Eastern Plains and Douglas County voters.
