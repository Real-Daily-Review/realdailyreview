# US Doctor With Ebola Evacuated to Germany as Congo Outbreak Spreads

An American doctor who contracted Ebola in the Democratic Republic of the Congo has been flown to Germany for treatment, along with his wife and four children, according to The Guardian. The physician, identified as Dr. Peter Stafford, is being treated as the World Health Organization warns of the "scale and speed" of the outbreak in the central African nation.

The DRC outbreak has spread into urban areas, a development health officials view with concern. Authorities have reported at least 134 suspected deaths and more than 500 cases of the hemorrhagic Bundibugyo virus, which currently has no approved treatments or vaccines available. The outbreak has been declared a public health emergency.

Stafford's family members are being monitored for symptoms as a precautionary measure, though it remains unclear whether any have shown signs of infection. Germany's decision to accept the American patient and his family reflects international cooperation on the outbreak response, though details on the treatment protocol remain limited.
