# SpaceX Files for Historic IPO That Could Value Company at $1.75 Trillion

SpaceX has filed for what could become the largest initial public offering ever, with a potential valuation of $1.75 trillion, according to Al Jazeera. The filing, made public weeks ahead of an expected June launch, positions founder Elon Musk to serve simultaneously as CEO, CTO, and chairman of the board, according to TechCrunch.

The IPO filing reveals significant financial entanglements across Musk's business empire. The document mentions Tesla 87 times, his AI company xAI 356 times, and his social media platform X 267 times, according to The Verge. The filing also discloses that xAI lost $6.4 billion in 2025 while planning major expansion of its Grok AI product, per TechCrunch.

The filing underscores Musk's centrality to SpaceX's operations and strategy. The Verge notes that Musk himself is listed as a risk factor in the offering documents, reflecting concerns about the company's dependence on his leadership and the complex financial flows between his various enterprises.

The IPO comes weeks after Musk lost a court case against OpenAI, a competitor to xAI, according to the Washington Examiner.

Watch for the formal IPO launch expected in June and how regulators assess the financial interconnections between SpaceX and Musk's other companies.
