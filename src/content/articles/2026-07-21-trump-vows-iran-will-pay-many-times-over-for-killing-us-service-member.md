# Trump Vows Forceful Response After Iran-Linked Attack Kills U.S. Service Members

President Trump is signaling a hard line after reports that two American service members were killed in Jordan in an attack linked to Iranian proxies—a stark contrast to years of what conservatives call the Obama-Biden approach of measured, ineffectual retaliation.

Trump pledged Monday that Iran will pay "many times over" for each American killed, according to Breitbart. The Washington Examiner's editorial board argues that Iran's rulers have long gambled they could wage proxy war against the U.S. without serious consequences, arming militias, launching drones, and expanding missiles while betting Washington would respond with "limited, carefully calibrated retaliation." That bet, conservatives say, must end now.

**The conservative case:** Deterrence requires credible, overwhelming force. Half-measures and diplomatic theater only embolden Tehran to strike again. American blood demands more than words.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** While this is primarily a national security issue, Colorado hosts significant military installations and personnel. Any sustained conflict with Iran could affect defense contractors, military families, and energy markets—crude prices often spike on Middle East tensions, hitting Colorado consumers at the pump.

Watch whether Trump follows through with action that genuinely degrades Iranian military capacity or settles for symbolic strikes that leave Tehran emboldened for the next round.
