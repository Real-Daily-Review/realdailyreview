# Senate Advances Measure to Limit Trump's Iran War Powers

The U.S. Senate advanced a War Powers Resolution on Tuesday aimed at curbing President Donald Trump's authority to wage war on Iran, marking a rare rebuke of the president, according to Al Jazeera English.

The legislation seeks to force Trump to withdraw from the Iran conflict, as growing numbers of Republicans broke ranks with the administration to support the measure, PBS NewsHour reported. The shift includes Sen. Bill Cassidy, who flipped his position after a primary loss to back the resolution.

The vote reflects mounting pressure within Congress to end U.S. military action against Iran. The resolution represents an unusual display of bipartisan concern over executive war powers, though the sources do not specify the final vote count or next procedural steps.
