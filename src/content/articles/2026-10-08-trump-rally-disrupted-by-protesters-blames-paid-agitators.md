# Trump Rally Disrupted by Protesters in San Antonio; Trump Blames Paid Agitators

Donald Trump's campaign swing through Texas hit a snag Wednesday when multiple protesters repeatedly interrupted his San Antonio rally for Republican Senate nominee Ken Paxton, forcing the president to pause his speech multiple times as the crowd booed the hecklers (Washington Examiner, Just The News).

Trump blamed the disruptions on "paid protesters" working against Republicans, according to the New York Post. The president was particularly pointed in his criticism, describing some demonstrators as "bad looking people" and making personal remarks about their appearance (Just The News). One protester notably shouted down an "angel mom" speaking about her murdered son—a moment Trump used to underscore his argument that the disruptions were orchestrated by "enemies of our nation."

The San Antonio rally was part of Trump's multi-state tour shoring up Republican candidates in competitive races, with additional stops scheduled in Nebraska, New York, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Ohio (Just The News).

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether these coordinated protest efforts signal a broader Democratic strategy to disrupt Republican rallies heading into key elections, and how it affects voter turnout and enthusiasm in targeted states.
