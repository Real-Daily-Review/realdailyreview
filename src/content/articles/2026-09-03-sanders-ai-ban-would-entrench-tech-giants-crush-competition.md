# Bernie Sanders Pushes AI Ban That Would Hand Tech Giants a Regulatory Gift

Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-VT) and Rep. Greg Casar (D-TX) introduced the "Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act" this week, seeking to prohibit development of advanced AI systems they claim could escape human control. But conservatives warn the proposal would entrench existing tech monopolies while crushing innovation from smaller competitors and startups.

The Washington Examiner reports Sanders frames the measure as protection against "AI oligarchs," yet critics argue the opposite outcome is likely. The Daily Wire notes that heavy-handed AI restrictions typically benefit entrenched players like OpenAI and Google—firms with compliance budgets and legal teams to navigate Byzantine regulations—while smaller firms and Colorado's emerging tech sector face crushing compliance costs.

Republicans counter that government bans on emerging technology are blunt instruments that stifle American competitiveness against China and Russia, which won't self-impose such restrictions. Rather than prohibition, conservatives argue for transparent safety standards, liability frameworks, and market competition to drive responsible AI development.

Colorado's growing tech and software communities—from Denver startups to Boulder's innovation hubs—could face significant headwinds if federal AI restrictions become law, raising costs and slowing the state's position in the high-tech economy.
