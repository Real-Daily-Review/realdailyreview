# Former GOP Whip Jon Kyl, Arizona Conservative Leader, Dead at 84

Jon Kyl, the former Senate Minority Whip and Arizona Republican who shaped conservative policy for nearly three decades, has died following a battle with dementia.

Kyl was first sworn into the Senate in 1995 and retired in 2013, but returned to serve in 2018 after the death of Sen. John McCain. During his tenure, he became a reliable conservative voice on fiscal discipline, national security, and limited government—positions that earned him respect across the GOP caucus and made him a key legislative strategist.

His death marks the passing of a generation of Senate Republicans who built their careers on principled conservatism rather than media spectacle. Kyl's legislative record reflected Colorado-aligned priorities: he opposed wasteful spending, defended Second Amendment rights, and pushed back against regulatory overreach from the federal government.

Conservatives in Colorado and across the West lose a mentor figure who understood that governing requires both principle and pragmatism—a balance increasingly rare in modern politics.

Watch for how Arizona Republicans fill the leadership void Kyl leaves behind and whether his successor will maintain the same commitment to fiscal conservatism that defined his career.
