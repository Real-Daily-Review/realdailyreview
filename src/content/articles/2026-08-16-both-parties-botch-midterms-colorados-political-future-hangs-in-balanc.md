# Colorado Republicans See Midterm Opportunity as Both Parties Stumble

Democrats squander a historic chance to expand congressional control while Republicans fail to capitalize on voter frustration—a dynamic that could reshape Colorado's political landscape in 2024.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, both major parties appear determined to snatch defeat from the jaws of victory in the midterm cycle. Democrats entered the election season with structural advantages—yet internal divisions and messaging failures have undercut their position. Republicans, meanwhile, have struggled to present a coherent alternative agenda that resonates beyond base grievances.

For Colorado specifically, this dysfunction matters. The state has trended purple in recent cycles, with Democrats controlling the governorship and state legislature while Republicans hold competitive congressional seats. A national Republican resurgence—or Democratic collapse—could reshape Colorado's delegation and state-level power dynamics.

Conservatives argue that both parties' inability to execute reflects deeper problems: Democrats' embrace of unpopular progressive policies (inflation-fueling spending, energy restrictions, regulatory overreach) and Republicans' failure to articulate a clear pro-growth, limited-government agenda that appeals to independent voters and small-business owners.

The real question for Colorado taxpayers: Will either party offer a serious platform on energy independence, water rights, fiscal responsibility, and Second Amendment protections—or will 2024 be another cycle of wasted opportunity?
