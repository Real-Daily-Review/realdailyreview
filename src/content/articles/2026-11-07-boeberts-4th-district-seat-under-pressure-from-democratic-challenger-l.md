# Colorado's 4th District Tightens as Boebert Faces Unexpectedly Strong Democratic Challenge

Lauren Boebert's House seat in Colorado's 4th Congressional District is in genuine jeopardy, with political newcomer Eileen Laubacher running ahead of the incumbent Republican in early polling (9News Denver). The race has become one of Colorado's most competitive House matchups, defying historical patterns in a district Republicans have held for over a decade.

Laubacher, a Democrat, is mounting an aggressive challenge on kitchen-table issues—healthcare costs, housing affordability, and economic opportunity—while Boebert emphasizes her Second Amendment advocacy, border security, and opposition to Biden-era regulatory overreach. Republicans argue Boebert's record of fighting federal overreach and defending gun rights aligns with 4th District values; Democrats counter that Laubacher represents fresh leadership focused on practical solutions over partisan rhetoric.

Meanwhile, Republican Jeff Hurd held the line in the competitive 3rd Congressional District (Denver Post), defeating Democrat Dwayne Romero in a race that included Colorado's Western Slope and southern regions—traditionally Republican terrain since 2011.

The 4th District result will signal whether conservative messaging on energy development, water rights, and gun rights still resonates with rural and exurban Colorado voters, or whether Democratic gains in suburban areas are reshaping the state's political map.
