# Maine Senate Race Sets Up 2026 Battleground—But Colorado Conservatives Should Focus Closer to Home

Susan Collins has locked up the Republican nomination for Maine's Senate seat unopposed, setting up a high-stakes general election against Democrat Graham Platner, an oyster farmer who won the Democratic primary with 74.7% of the vote (Washington Examiner). The race is shaping up as a potential Senate majority decider in 2026.

Collins' allies are already previewing aggressive campaign strategy, with the New York Post reporting they're preparing what they describe as "the end for Graham Platner" in what's expected to be one of the most closely watched Senate races of the cycle. The battle will likely turn on whether Maine voters prioritize Collins' moderate record or embrace Platner's Democratic platform.

Republicans argue Collins' six-term tenure and cross-party credibility make her the safer choice for Senate stability. Democrats counter that Platner represents fresh energy and alignment with their party's priorities on healthcare, climate, and economic policy.

**The Colorado angle:** While Maine's Senate race will dominate national GOP attention and fundraising, Colorado Republicans should watch whether resources flow here instead—particularly if the state's 2026 Senate race tightens or if GOP efforts to flip the chamber face headwinds elsewhere.
