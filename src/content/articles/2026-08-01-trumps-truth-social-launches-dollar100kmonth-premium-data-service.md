# Trump's Truth Social Launches Premium API Service—Democrats Cry Foul

President Trump's Truth Social is monetizing influence with a new premium data service that gives paying clients real-time access to top-account posts via application programming interface (API), launching August 1 at $100,000 per month (Washington Examiner). The move mirrors similar offerings from X and Meta, letting subscribers tap high-value content feeds for research, trading, or media monitoring.

Democrats are already attacking it. Sens. Adam Schiff and Elizabeth Warren have criticized the service (Just The News), though their specific objections remain unclear—whether they object to the price point, data access itself, or Trump profiting from his platform.

**The conservative take:** This is straightforward capitalism. Private companies monetize their data all the time. If wealthy clients want premium access to market-moving information, that's a legitimate business model. The real question is whether Truth Social can execute—the platform has struggled with user growth and technical stability since launch.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters less directly than national tech policy, but it signals how Trump's post-presidency ventures are evolving beyond social media into data services and infrastructure plays.

Watch whether Democrats push for regulatory restrictions on Truth Social's data offerings—or whether they're simply grandstanding ahead of 2024.
