# Diesel Export Ban Splits GOP, Leaves Colorado Energy Workers in Limbo

Record diesel prices are fracturing the Republican coalition as President Trump and GOP candidates push a temporary export ban—a move that energy experts and Big Oil say won't solve the problem and could backfire on Colorado's refining sector.

The Daily Caller reports that diesel prices have hit record highs even as crude oil prices fall, driven by refinery constraints tied to the Iran conflict and Russian strikes on Ukrainian infrastructure. Trump and Republican candidates are backing an export ban as a quick fix, but industry analysts argue the policy is a blunt instrument that misses the real issue: refining capacity and margins, not exports themselves.

"The blunt tool of banning diesel exports definitely doesn't work," the Daily Caller quotes critics saying. The Washington Examiner notes this stance is straining the GOP's traditional alliance with Big Oil, which opposes the ban.

For Colorado—home to significant refining operations and energy jobs—an export ban could squeeze margins at local refineries without lowering pump prices for drivers. Republicans who support the ban are betting on populist appeal; those aligned with industry warn it's economically counterproductive.

Conservatives should watch whether Trump follows through on the ban and how Colorado refiners respond if the policy takes effect.
