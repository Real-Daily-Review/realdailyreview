# Trump Taps Dallas for First-Ever GOP Midterm Convention

President Trump is shaking up Republican strategy by calling the party's first-ever midterm convention for September 9-10 in Dallas, signaling an aggressive push to energize the base ahead of the 2022 elections.

The move breaks with tradition. Historically, the Republican National Committee has held conventions only in presidential election years. By convening midterm delegates in Dallas—a reliably conservative stronghold—Trump is attempting to unify the party around a cohesive message and candidate slate before voters head to the polls.

For Colorado Republicans, the convention offers a high-profile platform to showcase the state's competitive races and conservative priorities. Colorado has emerged as a battleground in recent cycles, with Republicans targeting Senate and House seats held by Democrats. A midterm convention could amplify messaging on inflation, border security, and energy independence—issues that resonate with Colorado voters and directly affect the state's economy and energy sector.

The convention also signals Trump's continued influence over GOP direction and candidate selection, a dynamic that will shape which Republicans emerge as frontrunners in Colorado's 2022 primaries and general elections.

Watch whether Colorado Republicans use the Dallas platform to coordinate strategy on energy policy and water rights—two issues where the state's conservative base expects aggressive pushback against Biden administration overreach.
