# Obama Presidential Center's $470M Safety Net Still Missing as Contractors Go Unpaid

While the Obama Foundation touts its gleaming new presidential center in Chicago, taxpayers are left holding the bag—again. The Foundation has yet to establish a promised $470 million reserve fund designed to shield the public from future financial liability, even as subcontractors report being owed millions for work already completed, according to Fox News.

The safety net was supposed to be a guardrail against the center becoming a drain on public resources. Instead, the Foundation's failure to fund it raises a familiar question: who pays when progressive pet projects run into trouble?

Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue the situation exemplifies why government-backed ventures require ironclad accountability mechanisms from day one. Without the reserve fund in place, taxpayers could face unexpected costs if the center encounters financial distress—a risk the Foundation accepted when it promised the protection in the first place.

The Foundation has not publicly explained the delay or timeline for establishing the fund. Meanwhile, contractors waiting for payment face their own cash-flow crises, shifting the burden downstream to small businesses and workers.

**Watch for:** Whether the Obama Foundation finally funds the reserve and how much additional taxpayer exposure emerges before it does.
