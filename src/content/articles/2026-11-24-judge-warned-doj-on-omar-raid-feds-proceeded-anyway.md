# Federal Judge's Concerns Over Omar Raid Expose DOJ Overreach Debate

Unsealed court documents reveal a federal judge warned the Department of Justice that its search warrant for Rep. Ilhan Omar's Washington home risked violating the Speech and Debate Clause—a constitutional protection for lawmakers—before agents executed the raid anyway (Fox News).

Judge Matthew Sharbaugh's concerns, now public in a 169-page redacted filing, show internal DOJ debate over the constitutional limits of the investigation (Breitbart). Homeland Security Investigations seized Omar's phone and passport during the raid, which court records indicate stems from a decades-long investigation scope.

**The conservative concern:** When a federal judge explicitly warns the DOJ that a search warrant may violate constitutional protections for members of Congress, and the government proceeds regardless, it signals troubling prosecutorial overreach. Republicans argue this exemplifies how the Biden-Harris Justice Department weaponized law enforcement against political opponents, even when judicial skepticism was on the record.

Democrats and Omar's allies have framed the investigation as routine, but the unsealed documents tell a different story—one where constitutional guardrails were questioned before being ignored.
