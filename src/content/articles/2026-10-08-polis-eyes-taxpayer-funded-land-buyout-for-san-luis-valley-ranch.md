# Polis Floats Land Buyout for San Luis Valley Ranch Dispute

Gov. Jared Polis is pushing Colorado taxpayers toward another land acquisition to resolve a long-running dispute over Cielo Vista Ranch in Costilla County, according to a report from former U.S. Sen. Ken Salazar, whom Polis appointed to broker a solution (Colorado Sun).

**The conservative concern:** Government land purchases in Colorado's agricultural heartland raise red flags about creeping state control of private property and water rights. The San Luis Valley is already home to significant state and federal land holdings; adding more public acreage reduces private ownership, limits ranching operations, and typically means less local tax revenue.

Republicans and property-rights advocates argue that land disputes should be resolved through negotiation and market mechanisms—not taxpayer-funded buyouts that reward litigation and set precedent for future government land grabs. Polis's administration has shown a pattern of expanding state land holdings under the guise of conservation and conflict resolution.

The specifics of Salazar's recommendation remain unclear from available reporting, but the principle is straightforward: Colorado should be wary of using public funds to purchase private land, especially in rural counties already struggling with tax bases eroded by government ownership.
