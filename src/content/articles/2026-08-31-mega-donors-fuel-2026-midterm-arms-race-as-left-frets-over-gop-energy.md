# Big Money Floods 2026 Midterm Race as Left Warns GOP Faces Enthusiasm Gap

Mega-donors are already writing massive checks for the 2026 midterm elections, according to the Daily Caller, signaling an early arms race that will test whether Republicans can hold Congress against a well-funded Democratic machine.

The funding surge comes as progressive voices worry aloud about Republican momentum. Cenk Uygur, cofounder of The Young Turks, told the Washington Examiner that President Trump has "taken all the energy and excitement" out of the Republican base—a telling admission from the left that Trump's return has energized conservatives heading into the cycle (Washington Examiner).

For Colorado conservatives, this matters enormously. The state remains a battleground where outside money floods Senate and House races. Big-dollar donors will likely target Colorado's competitive districts and statewide races, making 2026 a test of whether grassroots Republican enthusiasm can match Democratic donor networks.

Republicans argue that genuine voter enthusiasm—not just donor cash—will determine 2026 outcomes. Conservatives counter that the left's reliance on mega-donor networks reveals their disconnect from working-class voters.
