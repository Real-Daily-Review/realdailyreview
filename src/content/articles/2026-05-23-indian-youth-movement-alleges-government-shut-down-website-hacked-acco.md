# Indian Youth Movement 'Cockroach Janta Party' Says Government Took Website Offline

A new online protest movement in India has gone viral by adopting a cockroach as its mascot to voice frustration over rising unemployment, according to the Financial Times. The "Cockroach Janta Party" has gained traction among young people expressing discontent with job scarcity in the country.

The movement's founder told Al Jazeera that the Indian government took the group's website down and hacked its Instagram page and creator's personal account. The allegations suggest official efforts to suppress the online activism, though the government has not publicly responded to the claims.

The cockroach symbolism reflects the movement's messaging that young people are being treated as pests by the establishment, according to reporting on the campaign. The viral nature of the protest underscores broader economic anxieties among India's youth demographic.

Watch for official government statements addressing the allegations of website takedowns and account hacking, as well as whether the movement sustains momentum or faces further digital disruptions.
