# Progressive Activists Dodge Accountability as 'Woke 2.0' Rebranding Begins

Colorado taxpayers and business owners should brace for round two of progressive overreach—this time with better PR.

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and her allies appear to be quietly pivoting away from the most unpopular aspects of their agenda without admitting error or taking responsibility for the damage already done. Rather than owning the failures of their first wave of radical policies—from inflation-driving spending sprees to regulatory strangleholds on energy development—the left is simply repackaging the same ideology under new branding.

National Review notes that if progressive leaders have truly "recognized the errors of their ways," they owe Americans far more than a casual "oops." The Daily Caller's analysis underscores the dodge: what exactly was wrong with Woke 1.0 that necessitates a version 2.0?

For Colorado, this matters. The state has already absorbed years of progressive policies that have hammered energy workers, driven up housing costs, and expanded government control over water and land use. A rebranded progressive agenda—one that learns to hide its radicalism better—poses a fresh threat to Colorado's economy and individual liberty.

Conservatives and taxpayers should watch closely for which failed policies resurface under softer language in the coming months.
