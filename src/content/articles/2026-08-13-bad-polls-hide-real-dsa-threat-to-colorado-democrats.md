# Democratic Socialist Surge May Be Polling Mirage, But Colorado Democrats Should Still Worry

Faulty polling may have inflated the apparent strength of Democratic Socialists of America candidates in recent weeks, according to analysis from the *Washington Examiner*, but the underlying shift toward radical left politics within Democratic ranks remains real—and poses a direct threat to Colorado's business-friendly moderates.

Polls showing DSA candidates "poised to make major progress" in their bid to reshape the Democratic Party appear to have overstated their actual support, the *Examiner* reports. Yet conservatives argue the real story isn't polling error—it's that the Democratic Party's center of gravity has drifted so far left that socialist candidates can credibly compete for party leadership at all.

For Colorado, this matters. The state's Democratic establishment has already embraced aggressive climate regulations, gun control measures, and tax hikes that have reshaped the state's business climate. A further lurch toward DSA-style politics would likely mean pressure for wealth taxes, energy sector restrictions, and expanded government control over Colorado's economy and land use.

Republicans and conservatives say Colorado voters should recognize that even "failed" DSA campaigns signal where Democratic leadership is headed: toward policies that punish work, discourage investment, and expand government power over private enterprise.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from or embrace DSA-backed positions on energy, taxes, and regulation heading into 2024.
