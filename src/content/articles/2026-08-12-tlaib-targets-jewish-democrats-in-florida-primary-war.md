# Tlaib's Primary War on Jewish Democrats Exposes Left's Internal Fracture

Rep. Critics argue the timing and targeting are no accident, pointing to Tlaib's well-documented hostility toward Israel and Jewish lawmakers who support U.S. aid to the Jewish state.

The Michigan congresswoman's intervention in Florida primaries reveals a deepening rift within the Democratic Party between its progressive, anti-Israel wing and establishment moderates. Republicans see this as proof that the Democratic left is willing to cannibalize its own to advance an ideological agenda—a stark contrast to party unity rhetoric.
