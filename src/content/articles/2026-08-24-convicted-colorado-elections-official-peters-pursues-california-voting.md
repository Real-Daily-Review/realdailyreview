# Former Colorado Elections Official Tina Peters Eyes California Voting Job—No Legal Barrier Stops Her

Colorado's disgraced ex-Mesa County Clerk Tina Peters, whose sentence was recently shortened, faces no parole restrictions preventing her from working in elections again—and she's now being courted for a voter registrar position in California's Shasta County, according to the *Denver Post*.

Peters has spent her reduced sentence touring conspiracy conferences and meeting with Trump in the Oval Office, the *Colorado Sun* reports. Shasta County Clerk Clint Curtis confirmed the job offer in a recent radio appearance, though details remain sparse.

The development raises alarm among election-integrity advocates. Peters was convicted of crimes related to her handling of Mesa County voting equipment and conspiracy theories about the 2020 election. Her early release and subsequent pivot toward elections work underscore conservative concerns about accountability—and questions about whether someone convicted of election-related misconduct should hold voting authority anywhere.

Governor Jared Polis has remained silent on any contacts with Trump regarding Peters, the *Denver Post* notes, leaving unclear what role, if any, state leadership played in this trajectory.

**Watch:** Whether California election officials proceed with hiring Peters, and whether Colorado Republicans call for federal or state safeguards preventing convicted election officials from accessing voting systems.
