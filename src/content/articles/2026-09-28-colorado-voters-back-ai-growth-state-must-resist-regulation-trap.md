# Colorado Should Embrace AI Growth, Not Strangle It With Panic

Colorado has a genuine opportunity to lead in artificial intelligence and data center development—but only if state policymakers resist the urge to regulate first and think later.

A new Magellan Strategies survey shows Colorado voters are open to AI expansion, according to *Complete Colorado*. That's the right instinct. While national figures from Pope Leo to various politicians warn of AI safety risks, conservatives argue that premature regulation and artificial restrictions will simply cede Colorado's competitive advantage to Texas, Arizona, and other states already courting tech investment.

The real concern isn't AI itself—it's whether Colorado government will impose costly mandates, environmental restrictions, or data-privacy rules that sound good in theory but kill job creation and business growth in practice. Energy demands from data centers are real, but Colorado's power grid can handle responsible expansion without the kind of heavy-handed regulations progressives typically favor.

Republicans and business leaders argue Colorado should focus on smart permitting, workforce development, and letting market forces guide AI investment. The state's tech corridor is already competitive; strangling it with precautionary regulation would be economic malpractice.

Watch whether the Colorado legislature moves to impose AI-specific regulations or data-center restrictions in the next session—and whether business groups push back hard enough to stop them.
