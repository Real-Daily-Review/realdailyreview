# DOJ Pursues First Alien Terrorist Removal Court Case Against Afghan with ISIS Ties

The Justice Department is moving to deport an Afghan national through the newly operational Alien Terrorist Removal Court—the first case of its kind—after the suspect allegedly supported a family plot to commit terrorism on American soil, according to *Just The News* and the *Daily Caller*.

FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed the case involves a foreign national who "supported a plot by ISIS-sympathizing family members to commit an act of terrorism in America." The alleged scheme reportedly targeted Election Day operations, underscoring the national security stakes of vetting foreign nationals entering the U.S.

The case marks a significant test of the Trump administration's stricter immigration enforcement posture and its willingness to use specialized legal mechanisms to remove security threats. Republicans have long argued that the federal government failed to adequately screen Afghan evacuees during the chaotic 2021 withdrawal, leaving potential security risks in the country.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado communities have absorbed significant numbers of Afghan refugees since 2021. This deportation case will likely intensify debate over refugee vetting standards and resettlement practices affecting the state.

Conservatives should monitor whether this court becomes an effective tool for removing terrorist-connected aliens, or whether legal challenges slow the process.
