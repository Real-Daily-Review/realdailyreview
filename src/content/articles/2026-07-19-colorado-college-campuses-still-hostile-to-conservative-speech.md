# College Campuses Still Hostile to Conservative Speech—and Colorado Students Are Paying the Price

Political conformity on American college campuses remains a real problem, with conservative and libertarian students routinely facing ostracism for their beliefs. According to the Washington Examiner, the issue is straightforward: students across the ideological spectrum—whether communist, socialist, liberal, libertarian, or conservative—have a right to hold and express their political views without fear of social exile.

For Colorado families paying tuition at CU Boulder, Colorado State, and other state universities, this matters. Taxpayer-funded institutions should protect intellectual diversity, not enforce progressive orthodoxy. When conservative students self-censor or apologize for their beliefs to avoid campus backlash, the university fails its core mission: fostering open inquiry and debate.

The problem isn't that liberals exist on campus—it's that dissenting voices face real consequences. Conservative students shouldn't have to hide who they are or what they think to survive four years of higher education.

Colorado conservatives and parents should demand that state university leadership commit publicly to protecting viewpoint diversity and holding faculty accountable for creating genuinely inclusive intellectual environments—not just the progressive kind.
