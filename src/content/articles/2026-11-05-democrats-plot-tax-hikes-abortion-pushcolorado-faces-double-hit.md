# Democrats' Midterm Win Sets Stage for Tax Hikes, Abortion Push—Colorado Taxpayers in Crosshairs

Colorado conservatives face a two-front threat after Democratic midterm victories: aggressive federal tax increases and a renewed push to nationalize abortion access.

Democrats are signaling plans to resurrect billions in proposed tax hikes that would raise federal income tax rates for some Americans, according to *The Daily Wire*. While the party frames this as "relief for lower-income families," Republicans argue the proposals would hit middle-class earners and small-business owners hard—particularly damaging in Colorado's competitive energy and tech sectors, where high earners already shoulder substantial state tax burdens.

Simultaneously, *National Review* reports Democrats intend to pursue a nationwide right to abortion should they gain legislative ground. Pro-lifers worry this signals a coordinated assault on state-level restrictions, including Colorado's own abortion policies.

**The conservative case:** Tax increases during economic uncertainty throttle job creation and investment. Colorado's business community—from oil and gas to agriculture—cannot absorb federal rate hikes on top of state-level progressive taxation. On abortion, conservatives argue Democrats seek to override state sovereignty and impose a one-size-fits-all federal mandate that ignores Colorado voters' preferences.

**Watch:** Whether Democrats move aggressively on tax legislation in the next Congress and how Colorado's delegation responds to protect state taxpayers and energy workers from federal overreach.
