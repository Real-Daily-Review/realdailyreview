# Ebola Outbreak in DR Congo Complicates World Cup Preparations

An Ebola outbreak in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo has killed Red Cross volunteers and disrupted containment efforts, even as the country prepares for World Cup hosting duties.

Red Cross workers are thought to have contracted the virus before the outbreak was officially identified, according to BBC World. The situation worsened when a treatment tent was set on fire for the second time in one week, prompting 18 suspected Ebola patients to escape, a local hospital director told PBS NewsHour.

The outbreak has drawn international attention to health security concerns. The United States, which will cohost the 2026 World Cup with Mexico and Canada, warned the DR Congo national team to isolate due to Ebola fears, according to Al Jazeera English. However, DR Congo said it will not change its World Cup preparations in response to the warning.

The fires at the treatment facility underscore challenges facing outbreak response efforts in the region. Authorities have not publicly attributed the fires to any specific cause.
