# ABC News Quietly Deletes, Then Revises Trump Story—Raising Questions About Editorial Standards

ABC News deleted a report on Don Jr.'s business dealings within 24 hours, then republished a revised version without clear public explanation, according to *Daily Caller* and *Just The News*. The initial removal drew fire from political commentators who questioned whether the network had scrubbed the story due to factual problems or editorial pressure.

*Daily Caller* reported that ABC claimed the original article was "false," though the network's handling—deleting first, explaining later—fueled conservative criticism of mainstream media accountability. *Just The News* noted the revised version appeared without transparent disclosure of what changed or why the original was yanked.

The incident underscores a broader conservative concern: major newsrooms operate with minimal transparency when correcting or removing stories, leaving readers guessing whether editorial decisions reflect journalistic standards or political considerations. When outlets delete stories quietly rather than issuing clear corrections upfront, it erodes public trust and invites skepticism about their editorial independence.

Colorado voters and taxpayers should watch whether local and national media apply the same scrutiny to Democratic figures' business dealings—or whether the standard shifts based on partisan preference.
