# Front Range Rail Tax: Colorado Voters Face Billion-Dollar Boondoggle Funded by Taxpayer Propaganda

Colorado voters are being asked to bankrupt themselves for a train nobody will ride—and the rail lobby is spending millions in taxpayer money to sell them the bill of goods.

The so-called Colorado Connector project is pushing a Front Range rail tax that would siphon billions from Colorado families and businesses. But before voters decide, they're being bombarded with what CPR News calls a controversial "education" campaign, complete with paid Instagram influencers and targeted mailers—all funded by tax dollars meant for transportation, not political messaging.

Complete Colorado identifies 10 reasons to reject the scheme, starting with the fundamental question: why should Colorado taxpayers subsidize a train that ridership projections suggest few will actually use? The rail authority's own data suggests the project won't generate the promised economic returns or reduce congestion meaningfully.

Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue this is classic government overreach: politicians and special interests using public money to manufacture consent for a massive spending program. They say Colorado families should keep their money in their own pockets, not watch it disappear into another bloated transit project that benefits Denver elites and construction contractors.

Democrats counter that rail investment is essential for Front Range growth and emissions reduction—but won't explain why taxpayers should fund the propaganda campaign selling it.

Watch whether Colorado voters reject this tax grab or fall for the influencer-fueled messaging blitz.
