# UK Counterterror Police Probe Explosives Arrests Near US-Allied Air Base

British counterterrorism police have taken over an investigation into multiple arrests for suspected explosives offenses near a Royal Air Force base that hosts U.S. operations, according to 9News Denver and Just The News.

Police declared a "major incident" and evacuated properties in the Whelford area following the arrests, though specific details about the suspects and their alleged activities remain limited. The RAF base in question is used jointly by British and American military personnel, making the incident relevant to U.S. national security interests.

British authorities have not yet disclosed the identities of those arrested or the nature of the suspected explosives. The handoff to counterterrorism specialists signals official concern about potential terror-related activity rather than routine criminal charges.
