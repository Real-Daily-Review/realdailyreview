# Colorado's Gun Ban Takes Effect July 1—Second Amendment Advocates Cry Foul

Colorado Democrats are moving forward with a sweeping ban on 3D-printed firearms set to take effect July 1, 2026, marking another erosion of Second Amendment rights in a state that once led on gun ownership (9News Denver). The law also expands protections for conversion therapy survivors and tightens marijuana regulations.

Republicans and gun-rights advocates argue the 3D-printer ban is unenforceable theater that punishes law-abiding Coloradans while criminals ignore the rules. They contend the state is using regulatory overreach to chip away at constitutional rights through incremental restrictions—a pattern conservatives say reflects Denver's progressive supermajority's hostility toward gun owners.

Meanwhile, Jefferson County is implementing new wildfire mitigation rules July 1 that will restrict construction and home improvement projects in high-risk areas (KDVR Fox31). While fire prevention is broadly supported, property owners worry the regulations could limit development rights and increase compliance costs without clear evidence the rules prevent major fires.

Conservatives should monitor whether Colorado's gun ban survives legal challenge and whether wildfire rules expand statewide—potentially setting precedent for further land-use restrictions across the state.
