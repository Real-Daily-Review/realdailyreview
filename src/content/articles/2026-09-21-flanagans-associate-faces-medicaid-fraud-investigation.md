# Minnesota Democrat's Circle Faces Medicaid Fraud Probe

Minnesota Democratic Senate candidate Peggy Flanagan is drawing unwanted scrutiny after a longtime associate became the subject of a major state investigation into Medicaid fraud at her autism services nonprofit.

The woman, featured prominently beside Flanagan in a viral hijab video where the candidate defended Minnesota's Somali community as "part of the fabric of the state," operates Milestone Development Centers. State investigators are now examining the facility's Medicaid billing practices, according to Fox News and Just The News.

The timing is awkward for Flanagan's campaign. The original video positioned her as a champion of immigrant communities—a core Democratic talking point. Now that same association links her to potential fraud allegations involving taxpayer dollars meant for vulnerable children with autism.

Republicans argue the case illustrates how progressive politicians' identity-focused messaging often obscures accountability questions about the organizations and individuals in their inner circles. Conservatives note that Medicaid fraud—particularly in social services—represents real money diverted from intended beneficiaries, and that vetting associates matters in public life.

Democrats have not yet responded substantively to the investigation's scope or timeline.

Watch whether Minnesota's attorney general pursues charges and whether national media covers the story with the same intensity they gave the original hijab video.
