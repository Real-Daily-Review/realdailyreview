# State Considers Buying Into San Luis Valley Land Dispute—At Taxpayer Expense

Gov. Jared Polis is eyeing a state purchase of part of Cielo Vista Ranch in Costilla County to resolve a years-long land battle, according to a report from former U.S. Sen. Ken Salazar, whom Polis appointed to broker a solution (Colorado Sun).

Here's the conservative concern: **Colorado taxpayers could foot the bill for a land dispute that private parties should resolve themselves.** State acquisition of private ranch land sets a troubling precedent for government land grabs dressed up as "solutions." Before Polis commits public money, Coloradans deserve answers: How much will this cost? Why is government the answer? What happened to property rights and private negotiation?

The San Luis Valley, already strained by water scarcity and agricultural pressures, doesn't need state ownership of productive ranch land. It needs clarity on water rights and respect for existing property claims—not another government land purchase that removes acreage from private stewardship and the tax base.

Conservatives should watch whether Polis moves forward with a purchase and at what price—and whether the legislature rubber-stamps this without demanding fiscal accountability.
