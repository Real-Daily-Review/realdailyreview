# AI Giant Anthropic's $1.5 Trillion IPO Shows Free Markets Still Work—If Government Stays Out of the Way

Anthropic, the AI company behind ChatGPT rival Claude, raised a record $81 billion in its stock market debut Thursday, pricing shares at $83 each and valuing the five-year-old startup at $1.5 trillion (Breitbart). The offering topped Elon Musk's SpaceX fundraising and signals explosive investor appetite for artificial intelligence—a sector where American innovation still leads the world.

The real story: this is capitalism working. Founded just five years ago, Anthropic now employs over 5,300 workers, with at least 400 employees becoming hundred-millionaires overnight (Daily Wire). The average employee's net worth jumped to over $40 million. That's wealth creation through genuine innovation, not government handouts.

Republicans and conservatives should note the contrast: while the Biden-Harris administration spent years threatening AI regulation and pushing climate-obsessed energy mandates that threaten Colorado's economy, American entrepreneurs built a $1.5 trillion company. No subsidies. No green-energy boondoggles. Just smart people solving hard problems in a free market.

The danger ahead: watch whether incoming administrations resist the urge to regulate AI into mediocrity or tax away the incentives that made this boom possible. Colorado's tech corridor benefits when Washington lets innovation breathe.
