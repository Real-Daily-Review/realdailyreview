# White House Fraud Task Force Boots 750K Fake Obamacare Enrollees, Saves $2.2B

Vice President JD Vance's White House Task Force to Eliminate Fraud is yanking 750,000 fraudulent Obamacare recipients off the rolls, recovering $2.2 billion in taxpayer money that should never have left federal coffers in the first place.

According to Fox News and The Daily Wire, the anti-fraud initiative identified and is eliminating hundreds of thousands of bogus enrollments bleeding the system. Vance told Breitbart News that fraud is "definitely making health care more expensive"—a reality progressives rarely acknowledge when defending the Affordable Care Act's sprawling bureaucracy.

CMS chief Dr. Mehmet Oz warned plainly: "Fraud will destroy Obamacare." The statement cuts to the heart of the problem: when government programs lack real accountability, bad actors exploit them, costs spiral, and honest taxpayers foot the bill.

This action exposes what conservatives have long argued: massive federal health programs attract fraud like a magnet. The Obama administration's lax enrollment verification created the conditions for this waste. Colorado taxpayers—already burdened by state and federal taxes—deserve to know how many fraudulent enrollees were operating in their state and how much their premiums subsidized the scam.

Watch whether Democrats in Congress demand details on how the task force identified fraud or whether they quietly accept the cleanup.
