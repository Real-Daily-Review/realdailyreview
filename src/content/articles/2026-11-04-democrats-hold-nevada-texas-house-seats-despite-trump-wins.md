# Democrats Hold Competitive Western Seats Despite Trump Tailwinds

Democrats notched narrow victories in two competitive House races this week—Nevada's 3rd District and Texas's 35th—signaling that Trump's 2024 coattails may not extend uniformly down-ballot, even in districts he won.

Rep. Susie Lee (D-NV) defeated Republican Marty O'Donnell in the Las Vegas-area seat, which backed Trump in the presidential race. Separately, Democrat Johnny Garcia won Texas's 35th Congressional District, a Trump-targeted seat redrawn by Republicans in a 2022 redistricting push (Fox News).

**The conservative takeaway:** Republicans invested heavily in both races expecting Trump momentum to carry House candidates across the finish line. The defeats suggest either redistricting maps didn't deliver as promised, or Democratic incumbents in purple districts retain structural advantages—turnout, name recognition, and constituent services—that insulate them from national headwinds.

For Colorado, the lesson cuts both ways. Democrats hold several competitive House seats here (Jena Griswold's old 8th District, now represented by Yadira Caraveo). If national Republican energy can't flip lean-Democratic seats in Nevada and Texas, Colorado's purple districts may prove equally sticky for the GOP in 2026.
