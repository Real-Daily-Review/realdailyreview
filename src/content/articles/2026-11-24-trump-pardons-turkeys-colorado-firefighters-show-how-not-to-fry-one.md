# Trump Pardons Turkeys as Colorado Fire Crews Warn Against Kitchen Disasters

President Trump will pardon two turkeys—named "Feathers" and "Freedom"—at the White House's annual Thanksgiving ceremony Tuesday at 2 p.m., continuing a tradition that at least gets the birds' names right, unlike most government naming conventions (Washington Examiner).

Meanwhile, back in Colorado, North Metro Fire Rescue District is taking a more practical approach to Thanksgiving safety. The district hosted a live demonstration Tuesday showing residents the right way—and spectacularly, the wrong way—to deep-fry a turkey. The wrong-way demo went up in flames, illustrating just how dangerous improper turkey frying can be (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

The contrast is telling: while Washington celebrates symbolic gestures, Colorado firefighters are doing the real work of protecting families from preventable disasters. Deep-frying mishaps cause thousands of house fires annually, and first responders here are taking time to educate residents before the holiday rush.

For Coloradans planning Thanksgiving dinner, the lesson is clear—follow safety protocols or risk burning down your home. For taxpayers, it's a reminder that local fire departments deliver genuine value by preventing emergencies rather than just managing them after the fact.
