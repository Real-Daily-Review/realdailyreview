# Garth Brooks Adds Two More Denver Shows as Demand Crushes Ticket Sales

Country music legend Garth Brooks is expanding his Denver run this September, adding two shows to his "Blame It All on My Roots" tour at Ball Arena due to overwhelming fan demand, according to announcements Friday morning (KDVR, 9News Denver).

The additional performances bring Brooks' total Denver engagement to four shows, with tickets priced equally across all seating. The move reflects robust consumer appetite for live entertainment in Colorado's metro area—a bright spot for the state's hospitality and events sector as venues compete for major touring acts.

For Colorado taxpayers and business owners, the expanded run signals healthy demand in the entertainment economy. Ball Arena benefits from extended booking, local hotels and restaurants see increased foot traffic, and the state collects sales tax revenue from ticket and concession sales. It's a straightforward market success story: demand drives supply, venues respond, and the local economy wins without government subsidy or intervention.

The shows underscore Denver's position as a major touring destination, competing effectively with other Western markets for premium entertainment bookings.
