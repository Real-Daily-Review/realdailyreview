# Dolly Parton Estate War: Nephew Ousted Over Alleged Threats to Legacy

Dolly Parton's estate has moved to remove her nephew Bryan Seaver from any role in managing her affairs, accusing him of threatening violence and attempting to seize control of the country music icon's legacy. According to court filings reported by *Just The News* and *9News Denver*, Parton's manager filed a restraining order against Seaver, claiming he threatened to destroy the estate and harm those protecting it. Seaver, who owns the security company that protected his aunt and was personally chosen by Parton to announce her death from cancer last month, has been terminated from his position.

The Daily Caller reports that estate representatives are "dismayed by the unexpected and, so far, unexplained actions" from Seaver, signaling a sharp family rift over control of one of country music's most valuable legacies. The legal battle raises questions about fiduciary responsibility and the protection of Parton's wishes—a cautionary tale for any high-net-worth individual concerned about family disputes derailing their estate plan.

While this story centers on a Tennessee-based estate, it underscores why Colorado families with substantial assets should ensure their wills, trusts, and succession plans are ironclad and clearly communicated to prevent similar courtroom warfare.

Watch for further court filings that may reveal the full scope of Seaver's alleged misconduct and whether criminal charges follow.
