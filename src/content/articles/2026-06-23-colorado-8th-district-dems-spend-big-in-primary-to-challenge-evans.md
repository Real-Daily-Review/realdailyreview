# Colorado's 8th District Primary Becomes Cash Battleground as Democrats Spend Big to Challenge Evans

Colorado's Democratic primary for the 8th Congressional District is turning into a spending war, with candidates Shannon Bird and Manny Rutinel pouring resources into their race to earn the right to face Republican incumbent Rep. Gabe Evans in the general election.

According to CPR News, candidates across Colorado's tight primaries are "raising – and spending – a lot of money" as they compete to advance. The 8th District race, which spans northern suburbs, agricultural land, and oil fields stretching to Greeley, has become one of the state's fiercest primary contests (Denver Post).

**The conservative concern:** Democrats are flooding this swing district with primary spending that signals a serious general-election challenge to Evans. The 8th District's mix of suburban voters and energy-dependent communities makes it a perennial battleground. Republicans should expect the Democratic nominee to arrive at the general election well-funded and battle-tested.

Conservatives argue that excessive campaign spending—even when it reflects genuine voter engagement—often correlates with promises of bigger government and higher taxes once candidates take office. The real question for Colorado taxpayers: which Democrat nominee will demand the most aggressive climate regulations on the oil and gas operations that sustain the district's economy?

Watch whether the Democratic nominee pivots left on energy policy or tries to appeal to the district's working families and energy workers.
