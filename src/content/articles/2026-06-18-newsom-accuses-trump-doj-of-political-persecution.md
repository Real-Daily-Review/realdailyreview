# Newsom Cries Foul as Trump DOJ Investigates California Governor

California Governor Gavin Newsom is accusing President Trump of weaponizing the Justice Department against him, claiming federal agents have knocked on doors of family friends and former employees demanding records. Newsom alleges the administration is "abusing the grand jury process" in what he frames as political retaliation (Washington Examiner).

**The conservative take:** Republicans have long warned that progressive prosecutors weaponized the DOJ under Biden to target Trump. If Newsom's claims prove accurate, it would represent a dramatic reversal—but conservatives note the irony: Newsom spent years supporting aggressive federal investigations into Trump and his allies. What's sauce for the goose, they'd argue, is sauce for the gander. The question isn't whether investigating political opponents is acceptable; it's whether Democrats will finally acknowledge the danger of a politicized Justice Department now that they're on the receiving end.

Colorado conservatives should note this plays into broader concerns about executive overreach and selective prosecution—issues that could affect how federal power is wielded over energy development, water rights, and land-use decisions affecting our state.

**Watch:** Whether congressional Republicans demand transparency on the Newsom investigation and whether Democrats suddenly rediscover their commitment to DOJ independence.
