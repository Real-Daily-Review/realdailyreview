# DC Delivery Tax Shows How Progressive Cities Punish Consumers—a Warning for Colorado

Washington, D.C.'s new delivery tax is the latest example of how left-wing politicians squeeze working families while claiming to help them. According to the Washington Examiner, D.C. Mayor Muriel Bowser's tax hike on food and package deliveries is hitting consumers directly—the opposite of what progressives promised. Similar tax-and-spend schemes are unfolding in New York City and Seattle, where aggressive taxation is driving businesses out of state.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and fiscal watchdogs warn that delivery taxes are regressive—they hurt low-income families most, since they rely more heavily on delivery services. These taxes don't reduce costs; they raise them. When politicians claim new taxes fund social programs, they're really funding government bloat while pricing ordinary people out of convenience.

**The Colorado angle:** As progressive cities double down on taxes, Colorado remains a relative haven for business and affordability. But state Democrats are watching these models closely. If similar delivery or service taxes gain traction in Denver or Boulder, Colorado families and small businesses will pay the price—and online retailers may shift operations out of state, costing jobs and tax revenue.

Coloradans should watch whether state lawmakers propose copycat delivery taxes or other regressive levies targeting working families.
