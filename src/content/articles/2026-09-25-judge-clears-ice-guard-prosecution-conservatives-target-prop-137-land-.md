# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Trial; Conservatives Warn Against Prop 137 Land Grab

A Colorado judge has ruled sufficient evidence exists to prosecute Brandon Booth, an ex-detention center guard accused of shooting toward protesters who made inflammatory remarks about "race traitors" and threatened to locate guards' children, according to the Colorado Sun. The case underscores tensions around immigration enforcement facilities and public safety.

Meanwhile, conservatives are mounting a sharp challenge to Proposition 137, Colorado's ballot measure that would funnel taxpayer money to land conservation efforts. According to Complete Colorado, the initiative would effectively subsidize wealthy nonprofits—particularly The Nature Conservancy, the measure's principal backer—under the guise of environmental stewardship.

**The conservative case:** Taxpayers shouldn't finance private organizations' land acquisitions, no matter how they're marketed. Prop 137 represents another backdoor wealth transfer to the nonprofit-industrial complex while expanding government's footprint in land-use decisions that affect Colorado ranchers, energy development, and property rights.

Democrats and environmental groups counter that conservation protects Colorado's water supply, outdoor economy, and natural heritage—but conservatives argue those benefits don't justify forcing Coloradans to bankroll NGOs with their own agendas.

Watch whether Prop 137 gains traction with suburban voters before the November election, and whether conservative groups can effectively message the taxpayer cost.
