# Zuckerberg's $300M Yacht Ignores Stranded Alaska Boat; Cruise Ship Saves Day

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg's $300 million superyacht was the closest vessel to a stranded 21-foot skiff in Southeast Alaska waters—but refused to answer distress calls, leaving a small cruise ship to perform the rescue instead.

According to vessel-tracking data cited by *Just The News*, the *Wilderness Legacy* cruise ship sailed past Zuckerberg's stationary mega-yacht en route to help the disabled skiff, which had run out of fuel between Petersburg and Juneau. *The Daily Wire* reports that when the cruise ship captain announced Zuckerberg's yacht had "refused" assistance, passengers erupted in boos.

The incident underscores a broader conservative critique: while Big Tech billionaires lecture Americans about social responsibility and "doing good," their personal conduct often tells a different story. Zuckerberg has faced sustained criticism over Meta's censorship practices, data harvesting, and influence over public discourse—issues that resonate with Colorado conservatives skeptical of tech oligarchs' unchecked power.

The rescue itself succeeded without incident, but the optics damage is real. For a CEO already under fire for Meta's role in suppressing conservative speech and election-related content, the image of a $300 million vessel ignoring a maritime emergency reinforces perceptions of elite indifference.
