# Trump Declassifies Election Files, Pushes Voter ID as Senate Weighs SAVE Act

Trump has released declassified intelligence alleging significant election vulnerabilities, including claims that China compromised voter rolls in at least 18 states and that the FBI uncovered criminal activity in Michigan's 2020 election before the Biden administration allegedly shut down the probe (Breitbart).

In a Thursday primetime address, Trump called for mandatory voter ID and citizenship verification measures—cornerstones of the SAVE America Act now facing a Senate deadline. According to Fox News, Trump framed the declassified documents as evidence of "shocking vulnerabilities" in America's election infrastructure.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue these revelations justify tighter election security measures. Voter ID requirements and citizenship verification are standard safeguards that Colorado conservatives have long championed. The alleged foreign interference and domestic fraud claims, if substantiated, underscore why election integrity reforms aren't partisan theater but essential protection for ballot legitimacy.

**What's at stake for Colorado:** As a swing state with mail-in voting, Colorado has faced scrutiny over election procedures. Stricter voter verification could reshape how the state administers ballots—a flashpoint between election security advocates and those who prioritize ballot access.

Watch whether the Senate passes the SAVE Act before the deadline and whether Colorado Democrats signal support or resistance to federal voter ID mandates.
