# Trump Skips Press Conference in China; Conservatives Push for Activist Pressure

President Trump concluded his China trip without holding the customary press conference to address reporters—a notable departure from standard presidential protocol on foreign visits, according to CPR News. While Trump met with Xi Jinping and other officials, he declined to take formal questions before departing.

Conservative voices are seizing on the diplomatic opportunity. National Review argues Trump should use his leverage with Beijing to press for the release of democracy activist Joshua Wong, who was sentenced for meeting with American lawmakers. The outlet frames Wong's imprisonment as "an affront to the U.S." and calls on Trump to make the case a centerpiece of U.S.-China relations.

The absence of a press conference raises questions about what was—and wasn't—discussed during high-stakes talks with China's leadership. For Colorado conservatives concerned about Beijing's economic aggression, intellectual property theft, and human rights abuses, the diplomatic silence is frustrating. Republicans argue the U.S. must use every negotiating tool to hold China accountable on both trade and freedom.

**Watch for:** Whether Trump addresses the press conference gap and Wong's case upon returning to U.S. soil, and what concrete outcomes emerge from the Xi meeting on trade and strategic competition.
