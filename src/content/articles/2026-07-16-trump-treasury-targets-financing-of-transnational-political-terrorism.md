# Trump Administration Targets Financing Behind 'Transnational Political Terrorism'

The Trump administration is moving aggressively to choke off money flowing to what it calls "transnational political terrorism"—a direct challenge to the left's international funding networks. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent will unveil financial crackdown plans Thursday at the State Department, where Secretary of State Marco Rubio is hosting delegations from over 60 countries to coordinate a global response to what officials frame as far-left violence and extremism.

The initiative signals the administration's intent to weaponize Treasury's considerable financial enforcement powers against overseas funding sources for radical movements. Democrats will likely cry foul, claiming the administration is conflating legitimate political opposition with terrorism to justify surveillance and asset seizure. Conservatives counter that shutting down money pipelines to violent extremists—whether domestic or international—is core national security work the Obama-Biden administrations neglected.

For Colorado, the stakes involve how aggressively federal law enforcement pursues funding trails connected to Antifa cells and radical environmental groups that have targeted energy infrastructure and private property across the state. Taxpayers should watch whether Treasury's enforcement actions include transparency reports on which organizations face scrutiny and whether due process protections remain intact.
