# Colorado GOP Primary Poised to Crown Victor Marx as Gubernatorial Nominee

Victor Marx, a Marine veteran, is positioned to win the Republican gubernatorial primary on June 30, according to polling cited by the *Washington Examiner*, setting up a general-election clash against a fractured Democratic field.

Marx leads GOP rivals State Sen. Barbara Kirkmeyer and state Rep. Scott Bottoms in the race to flip Colorado's governorship back to Republican control—a critical objective for conservatives seeking to reverse years of Democratic dominance in state policy, from energy regulation to gun restrictions to tax-and-spend governance.

Meanwhile, the Democratic primary reveals internal weakness. As cartoonist Jim Morrissey observed in the *Colorado Sun*, U.S. Sen. Michael Bennet and Attorney General Phil Weiser are running nearly identical campaigns, suggesting the Democratic bench lacks meaningful ideological distinction. That convergence could signal vulnerability in the general election if Republicans nominate a candidate capable of articulating a clear conservative vision for Colorado's future.

The stakes are high: Colorado's next governor will shape policy on energy development, water management, Second Amendment protections, and tax policy for years to come. Republicans view this cycle as their best opportunity in a decade to reclaim the executive branch.

Watch the June 30 primary results and whether Marx can consolidate GOP support and articulate a credible contrast to the Democratic nominee by fall.
