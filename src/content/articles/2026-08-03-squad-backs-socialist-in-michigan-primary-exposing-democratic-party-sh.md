# Squad's Far-Left Endorsement Exposes Democratic Socialism Creep in Mainstream Politics

Michigan's Democratic Senate primary reveals how thoroughly progressive activists have captured the party's establishment. Every member of the radical "Squad" has endorsed Abdul El-Sayed, a self-described democratic socialist, against moderate Rep. Haley Stevens—signaling that the Democratic Party's center of gravity has lurched left (Fox News Politics).

El-Sayed, who distances himself from the Democratic Socialists of America label while accepting Squad backing, represents the faction pushing government-run healthcare, wealth redistribution, and aggressive climate mandates that would devastate Colorado's energy sector and rural economy. Stevens, by contrast, represents the old Democratic establishment—moderate by today's standards, but still a tax-and-regulate Democrat.

For Colorado conservatives, this Michigan race matters. It demonstrates that the Squad's ideology—open borders, defund-police rhetoric, green-energy zealotry, and anti-fossil-fuel absolutism—now commands veto power over Democratic nominations. If El-Sayed wins, expect similar pressure on Colorado Democrats to adopt harder-left positions on energy policy, gun control, and spending.

Republicans should watch whether Colorado Democrats distance themselves from Squad-style socialism or embrace it ahead of 2024.
