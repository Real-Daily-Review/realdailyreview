# Democrats Eye Tax Hikes, Abortion Push After Midterm Wins

Colorado taxpayers face a potential federal tax assault if Democrats capitalize on midterm gains to resurrect their spending agenda.

According to The Daily Wire, Democratic victories could revive billions in proposed tax increases, targeting federal income tax rates for some Americans while directly challenging President Trump's tax-cut framework. Democrats have campaigned on expanding tax credits for lower-income families, but conservative analysts warn the party's broader proposals would impose significantly higher levies on middle and upper-income earners—a direct hit to Colorado's business owners and professionals.

Republicans argue that tax hikes during economic uncertainty would stifle growth and job creation. They point to Trump's tax cuts as proof that lower rates drive prosperity, not government spending.

Beyond taxes, National Review reports that pro-life advocates should brace for Democrats' push to reinstate a nationwide right to abortion—a cultural flashpoint that will energize both bases heading into 2026 and 2028.

For Colorado specifically, these federal moves matter: higher income taxes reduce capital available for state investment and business expansion, while cultural battles over abortion shape voter turnout in swing districts critical to statewide races.
