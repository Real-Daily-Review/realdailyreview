# Colorado Weather Alert: Storms Rolling In Thursday, More Severe Risk Friday-Saturday

Denver-area residents should batten down the hatches—afternoon thunderstorms are moving in today, with a more serious severe weather and flood threat expected Friday through Saturday.

The National Weather Service is flagging a limited severe weather and flood risk for the Denver metro area this afternoon, according to 9News. Conditions will cool considerably as the system moves through; KDVR reports temperatures will stay below average and "comfy" compared to recent heat.

The more significant threat materializes Friday and Saturday, when additional rounds of showers and storms are expected across Colorado. Residents in flood-prone areas—particularly those in burn scar zones from recent wildfires—should monitor conditions closely. Colorado's mountain communities and Front Range foothills face elevated runoff risk if heavy rain develops.

For Colorado families and businesses, severe weather means potential property damage, power outages, and disrupted operations. Ranchers and agricultural operations should secure livestock and equipment. Homeowners in high-risk zones should review drainage and ensure gutters are clear.

**Watch for National Weather Service updates Friday morning—the weekend forecast will determine whether this becomes a significant weather event or a routine summer storm system.**
