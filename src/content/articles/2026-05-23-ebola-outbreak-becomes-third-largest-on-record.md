# Ebola Outbreak Becomes Third Largest on Record as Cases Surge

An Ebola outbreak has reached third-largest status in recorded history, according to Ars Technica, with deaths climbing to 177 and nearly 750 confirmed cases. Health authorities have elevated the outbreak's risk level amid signs of rapid spread.
