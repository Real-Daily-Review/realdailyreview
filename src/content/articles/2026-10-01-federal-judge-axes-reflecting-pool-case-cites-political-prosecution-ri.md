# Federal Judge Dismisses Reflecting Pool Case, Citing Risk of Political Prosecution

A federal judge has permanently dismissed charges against former Olympian David Hearn in a Lincoln Memorial vandalism case, ruling that the circumstances presented an "extraordinary possibility" of politically motivated prosecution. Judge Todd Edelman's decision—issued with prejudice, preventing the Justice Department from refiling—marks another embarrassment for federal prosecutors pursuing what conservatives view as selective enforcement.

Hearn faced accusations of damaging the Reflecting Pool's coating. But Edelman's language suggests the case lacked merit from the start. National Review frames the dismissal as evidence of "Trump lawfare against political enemies and scapegoats," while Fox News emphasizes the judge's permanent bar on refiling.

The ruling underscores a broader conservative concern: the weaponization of federal law enforcement against political opponents and ordinary citizens caught in the crosshairs of ideological prosecution. Colorado taxpayers should note that such cases consume Justice Department resources and court time that could address actual crimes.

While this case doesn't directly implicate Colorado, it reflects the kind of prosecutorial overreach conservatives warn about—federal power unchecked by judicial skepticism. Watch whether similar dismissals mount as judges increasingly scrutinize the DOJ's case selection and motivations.
