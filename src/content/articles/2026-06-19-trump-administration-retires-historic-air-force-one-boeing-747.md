# White House Marks End of Historic Air Force One Era

President Trump's administration is retiring the iconic Boeing 747-200 that has served as Air Force One for over three decades, marking the end of an era in presidential aviation. The aircraft, which has transported U.S. presidents across the nation and globe, will be replaced as part of ongoing modernization efforts.
