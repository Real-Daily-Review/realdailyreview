# Denver Councilwoman Lewis Challenges Johnston in 2027 Mayor's Race

Denver City Councilwoman Shontel Lewis is mounting a challenge to Mayor Mike Johnston in the 2027 mayoral race, signaling a leftward shift in the city's Democratic primary. Lewis, who earned endorsement from the local Democratic Socialists of America during her council run (Denver Post), has been openly critical of Johnston's administration while serving on City Council (9News Denver).

Johnston, in his first term, has not yet filed for reelection. Lewis appears positioned to capitalize on recent DSA victories in Denver, suggesting the race could pit Johnston's moderate approach against a more aggressively progressive challenger.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this Denver primary matters: the city's spending, regulatory, and homelessness policies set the tone for Colorado's largest metro area and often influence state-level debates. A Lewis victory could accelerate the leftward momentum already evident in Denver's city government—meaning higher taxes, more regulation, and continued expansion of government programs that drain resources from productive residents and businesses.

Watch whether Johnston defends his record or pivots left to compete for DSA-aligned voters, and whether business groups and moderate Democrats coalesce around either candidate.
