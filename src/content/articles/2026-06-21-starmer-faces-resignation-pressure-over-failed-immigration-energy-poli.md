# British PM Starmer Faces Resignation Pressure Over Failed Immigration, Energy Policy

British Prime Minister Keir Starmer is buckling under pressure to resign following electoral losses and mounting criticism from within his own Labour Party, with President Trump publicly declaring the leftist leader's political fate (Washington Examiner).

Starmer's government has hemorrhaged support after significant losses in local elections across England, Scotland, and Wales last month (Just The News). The Labour leader now faces a career-defining choice: step down or fight an internal challenge from rival Andy Burnham (Breitbart).

Trump seized on Starmer's vulnerability, announcing on Truth Social that the British PM "will resign" and specifically blasting him for failures on "two very important subjects—IMMIGRATION AND ENERGY" (Washington Examiner). The President's critique underscores a broader conservative concern: progressive governments that ignore border security and strangle energy development face electoral collapse.

Starmer's mishandling of immigration and his hostility toward North Sea oil development—policies that mirror the green-energy radicalism and open-border rhetoric of the American left—have proven politically toxic even in traditionally Labour strongholds. The lesson for Colorado Democrats and national progressives is stark: voters reject governments that prioritize climate ideology over affordable energy and border chaos over security.
