# National Democrats Target Colorado's Red Districts in 2026 Midterm Blitz

Colorado's Republican congressional delegation is in the crosshairs of a major Democratic spending push ahead of 2026. The Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC) has added the state's 3rd and 4th districts—currently held by Rep. Lauren Boebert and Rep. Jeff Hurd—to its official target list, signaling that national Democrats believe they can flip Colorado entirely blue (Colorado Sun).

The move reflects Democrats' broader strategy to reclaim House control by pouring resources into traditionally Republican seats. Fox News reports the DCCC is targeting 12 additional deep-red districts nationwide as part of the effort.

**What conservatives argue:** Republicans counter that this represents out-of-state interference and a sign of Democratic desperation. Colorado's 3rd and 4th districts have elected conservatives repeatedly; the DCCC's targeting suggests Democrats lack confidence in homegrown candidates and must import national messaging and cash.

For Colorado taxpayers and energy workers, the stakes are real. Democratic control of these seats would likely mean more votes for federal regulations on oil and gas development, higher energy costs, and stricter environmental mandates that could hurt Colorado's energy economy.
