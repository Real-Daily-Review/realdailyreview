# Fatal Crash on US 85 in Commerce City; Driver Faces Charges

A deadly collision between a motorcycle and vehicle closed southbound US 85 at East 112th Avenue in Commerce City Wednesday morning, leaving one rider dead and prompting criminal charges against the driver involved.

According to Commerce City Police, the crash occurred in the early morning hours. The driver is expected to face charges stemming from the incident (KDVR Fox31), though specific charges and details remain under investigation.

Highway 85 southbound was shut down at the crash site as authorities worked the scene and gathered evidence. The closure disrupted commuter traffic through the area during morning rush hours.
