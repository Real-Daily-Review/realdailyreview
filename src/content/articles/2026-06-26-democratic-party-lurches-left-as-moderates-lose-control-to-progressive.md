# Democratic Party Fractures as Moderates Lose Ground to Progressive Wing

Colorado voters should pay attention: the national Democratic Party is tearing itself apart, and the winners are the hard-left activists who've seized control of the party machinery.

Sen. Elissa Slotkin (D-Mich.) recently admitted what conservatives have said for years—the Democratic Party's "old models are no longer working" and needs new leadership (Fox News). But here's the problem: the party isn't moving toward the center. It's sprinting left.

According to the Washington Examiner, Tuesday's primaries revealed a takeover by what critics call "DINOs"—Democrats in name only. The slate of candidates now winning Democratic nominations openly champion socialized medicine, rent control, and wealth taxes. These aren't fringe positions anymore; they're mainstream in Democratic primaries. The hard-left Democratic Socialists already control New York, Chicago, and Seattle, with Los Angeles and Washington in their sights.

For Colorado, this matters. As the state's Democratic establishment drifts further left on energy policy, gun control, and spending, moderate Democrats face a choice: align with the socialist wing or get primaried. That means Colorado's next generation of Democratic leaders will likely be more radical on oil-and-gas regulation, Second Amendment restrictions, and tax hikes than their predecessors.

**Watch whether Colorado's moderate Democrats—if any remain—will publicly break with their party's progressive turn, or quietly fall in line.**
