# Democratic Socialists Gain Ground in Colorado, Raising Concerns Over Far-Left Influence

The Democratic Socialists of America are consolidating power in Colorado and beyond, alarming conservatives who see the movement as a threat to free-market governance and individual liberty.

According to the Washington Examiner, the DSA has notched recent victories in New York, Colorado, and Washington, D.C., emboldening its leadership and raising fundamental questions about the organization's ideology. The Examiner notes that "a good case can be made" that DSA leaders are communists—a charge that underscores conservative concerns about the group's ultimate aims.

**What conservatives argue:** Republicans and limited-government advocates worry that DSA influence within Democratic circles will accelerate calls for wealth redistribution, expanded government control over energy and industry, and regulatory overreach that would harm Colorado's business climate and energy sector. The group's track record of supporting aggressive climate mandates and wealth taxes poses particular risks to Colorado's oil, gas, and mining economies.

**The Colorado angle:** DSA victories in the state signal growing leftist pressure on Colorado's Democratic establishment, potentially shifting state policy toward higher taxes, stricter regulations, and anti-energy positions that could cost jobs in rural communities dependent on resource development.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether DSA-aligned candidates and policies gain traction in the 2024 state legislative races and whether they push for tax hikes or regulatory expansions targeting Colorado's energy and business sectors.
