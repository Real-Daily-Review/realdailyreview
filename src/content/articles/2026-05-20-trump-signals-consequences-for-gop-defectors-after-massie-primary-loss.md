# Trump Celebrates Massie's Primary Loss; Greene Blames Epstein Files Push

President Donald Trump said Wednesday that it "doesn't work out well" when GOP members vote against him, following Rep. Thomas Massie's (R-Ky.) primary defeat on Tuesday, according to PBS NewsHour. Trump celebrated primary night wins and indicated GOP leadership "will be all right."

Former Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-Ga.) offered a different explanation for Massie's loss. Greene blamed his campaign defeat on his push to release files related to disgraced financier Jeffrey Epstein, writing on X: "Releasing the Epstein files was our demise. But it was worth every single bit because now everyone knows the truth," according to The Hill.

The competing narratives reflect tensions within the Republican Party over both loyalty to Trump and Massie's legislative priorities. Massie has been a vocal critic of certain GOP positions and has pushed for transparency on the Epstein documents.

Watch for how Trump uses primary results to enforce party discipline heading into the general election cycle.
