# DOGE's Early Stumbles Hand Opening to Vance as Efficiency Push Faces Reality

Elon Musk's Department of Government Efficiency arrived with fanfare but quickly collided with bureaucratic reality—and that gap may be reshaping the Trump administration's power structure.

The *Washington Examiner* reports that DOGE's initial boldness in targeting government waste and fraud generated early excitement before encountering the grinding resistance of entrenched federal agencies. The stumbles have inadvertently elevated Vice President JD Vance, who now appears positioned to lead a more sustainable efficiency effort.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: DOGE's mandate to slash wasteful spending and root out fraud directly affects how federal dollars flow to the state—from bloated agency overhead to regulatory compliance costs that burden Colorado businesses, ranchers, and energy producers. If the efficiency push falters or becomes merely performative, Colorado taxpayers foot the bill for federal bloat.

Republicans argue that genuine government efficiency requires sustained political will and structural reform, not just a high-profile audit. The left typically resists such efforts, framing them as threats to essential services—though conservatives counter that waste and fraud are precisely what prevent those services from working well.

**Watch whether Vance's approach produces measurable cuts to federal spending or devolves into another Washington talking point.**
