# Tennessee Honors Dolly Parton With Nashville Airport Rename

Tennessee Republican Gov. Bill Lee announced Friday that Nashville International Airport will be renamed after country music legend Dolly Parton, who died this week at 80 following a brief cancer battle. Lee called the move a fitting tribute to Parton's "indelible legacy" and her decades of cultural impact across Tennessee and beyond (The Daily Wire). The proposal came after consultation with Parton's team and represents a bipartisan recognition of the late icon's outsized influence on American music and philanthropy.

Parton, a Tennessee native, left an enduring mark on country music and charitable giving throughout her life. The airport rename stands as a permanent public honor—a relatively uncontroversial decision that reflects broad appreciation for her contributions to the state's cultural identity.
