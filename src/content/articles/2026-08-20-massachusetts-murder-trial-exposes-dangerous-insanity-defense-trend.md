# Colorado Conservatives Should Reject the Insanity Defense Trend Sweeping Social Media

A Massachusetts murder trial is exposing a dangerous cultural rot: the weaponization of mental illness to excuse infanticide. Lindsay Clancy strangled her three children to death. Her defense doesn't dispute the killings—instead, her lawyer argues she shouldn't face criminal responsibility because she suffered postpartum psychosis (The Daily Wire).

Hundreds of supporters, dressed in coordinated pink, staged a vigil outside Plymouth Superior Court, treating a triple murder as a sympathetic cause. Social media has turned the case into a "same, Lindsay" trend on TikTok, with online activists manufacturing narratives divorced from reality (The Daily Wire).

Here's what conservatives must understand: excusing homicide based on mental illness—however genuine—erodes personal accountability and the rule of law. Postpartum depression is real and deserves treatment. Murder is still murder. The insanity defense exists for genuine incapacity; it should not become a cultural cudgel to absolve parents of responsibility for their children's deaths.

This Colorado angle matters: as progressive prosecutors and defense bars expand mental-health defenses, Coloradans should demand that courts distinguish between treatment and exoneration. Sympathy for mental illness cannot mean abandoning justice for victims.

Watch whether Colorado courts follow Massachusetts in treating infanticide as a mental-health issue rather than a criminal one.
