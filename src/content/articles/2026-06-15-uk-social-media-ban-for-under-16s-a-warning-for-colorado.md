# UK Moves to Ban Under-16s From Social Media—What Colorado Should Learn

Britain's leftward lurch toward tech censorship offers a cautionary tale for Colorado parents and policymakers watching government overreach accelerate across the Atlantic.

UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced Monday that his government will ban children under 16 from using TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, and other social media platforms starting early next year (9News Denver, The Daily Wire). The legislation is expected to reach Parliament before Christmas, aligning Britain with Australia's aggressive regulatory posture on tech platforms.

**The conservative concern:** While protecting minors from addictive algorithms has merit, this represents precisely the kind of government-mandated content control that should alarm Americans. Once governments claim authority to ban platforms for children, the regulatory machinery expands—and definitions of "harmful" shift with political winds. Britain's approach treats parental judgment as secondary to state decree.

**The Colorado angle:** As progressive legislatures nationwide push similar restrictions, Colorado lawmakers should resist the temptation to import British-style tech bans. Instead, empower parents through transparency and parental controls—not government prohibition. The free market and parental choice, not bureaucratic mandates, should govern what Colorado kids access online.

Watch whether Democratic-led states adopt UK-style social media bans, and whether Colorado conservatives mount effective pushback before such legislation lands in Denver.
