# Spain's Border Crisis: A Preview of Open-Borders Chaos Democrats Embrace

Colorado conservatives should take note: Spain's socialist government just handed us a real-world case study in what happens when progressive open-borders ideology meets reality.

Tens of thousands of military-aged migrants this week stormed Spain's North African territories of Ceuta and Melilla (Just The News). The chaos was so severe that France deployed four mobile force units, aircraft, and drones to its border with Spain to contain the spillover (The Daily Wire).

The Washington Examiner draws the obvious parallel: this is what Democratic Socialists' mass-amnesty and open-borders agenda would produce in America. Spain's socialist Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez implemented exactly the permissive policies the American left champions—and the result is humanitarian chaos, security threats, and neighboring countries forced to militarize their borders.

Spain claims most migrants have since returned to Morocco, but the damage is done. The incident exposes the fantasy at the heart of progressive immigration policy: that open borders can be managed without consequences. They can't.

For Colorado, the lesson is clear: border security isn't a talking point—it's a prerequisite for sovereignty and public safety. When Democrats push amnesty and sanctuary policies, they're betting Americans won't notice what's already failing in Europe.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation acknowledges Spain's crisis or doubles down on open-borders rhetoric anyway.
