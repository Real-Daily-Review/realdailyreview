# Front Range Braces for Dangerous Heat Wave This Weekend

Colorado's Front Range is about to get hammered by a dangerous heat wave, with the National Weather Service issuing heat advisories for Sunday and Monday starting at noon (9News Denver). Both 9News and KDVR Fox31 have issued weather alert days as sunny, hot conditions settle over the Denver metro area and surrounding regions.

The timing couldn't be worse for outdoor workers, ranchers managing livestock in the heat, and families planning weekend activities. Heat advisories mean dangerously high temperatures that pose real health risks—especially for elderly Coloradans and those working in agriculture and construction.

This is a reminder that Colorado's weather can turn on a dime, and residents need to take precautions: stay hydrated, limit outdoor activity during peak heat hours, and check on vulnerable neighbors. For ranchers and farmers across the state, heat waves like this can stress water supplies and livestock, adding pressure to an already tight water year.

Coloradans should monitor National Weather Service updates closely and heed all heat advisory warnings through Monday evening.
