# Sky Surveillance on Independence Day: Feds Deploy Drones to Police Fireworks

Colorado communities celebrating Independence Day this summer may find federal surveillance drones overhead—all in the name of stopping illegal fireworks.

The Federal Aviation Administration has loosened regulations allowing cities and counties to rapidly deploy "Drone as First Responder" (DFR) initiatives, according to *The Daily Wire*. Public safety agencies nationwide are now using the accelerated approval process to launch drone-based enforcement operations during Fourth of July celebrations, ostensibly to spot illegal fireworks and other violations.

**The conservative concern is straightforward:** Washington is expanding surveillance infrastructure under the guise of public safety, cutting through bureaucratic review that once provided oversight. Fewer regulatory hurdles mean less transparency and fewer checks on government drone deployment—a troubling precedent for Colorado communities already wary of federal overreach.

For Colorado specifically, this matters. Rural counties and mountain towns that value privacy and limited government intrusion now face federal drone surveillance during one of America's most cherished holidays. The FAA's streamlined approval process bypasses the kind of local input that Coloradans expect when federal agencies operate in their airspace.

Conservatives should watch whether Colorado municipalities adopt these DFR programs and what safeguards—if any—local officials impose on federal drone operations.
