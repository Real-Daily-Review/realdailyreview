# Trump Escalates Iran Standoff as Strikes and Counterstrikes Mount

President Trump is taking a hardline stance against Tehran after the U.S. military completed fresh strikes on Iranian targets, with the commander-in-chief warning that more action may be necessary (9News Denver). Iran responded by launching ballistic missiles at American bases in Jordan, prompting Trump to declare that Tehran could be "totally wiped out as a country" if attacks continue (Breitbart).

The escalating tit-for-tat raises stakes for American servicemembers and allies in the Middle East. The Daily Caller reports that Iran may be signaling an "off-ramp" to negotiations, though Trump's public warnings suggest he's keeping military options on the table rather than rushing to the negotiating table.
