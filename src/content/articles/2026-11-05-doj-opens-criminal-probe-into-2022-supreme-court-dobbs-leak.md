# DOJ Finally Pursues Criminal Probe Into Supreme Court Leak—Four Years Late

The Justice Department has launched a criminal investigation into the 2022 leak of the Supreme Court's draft *Dobbs* opinion overturning *Roe v. Wade*, with U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro's office overseeing the inquiry (Washington Examiner). Federal prosecutors have issued grand jury subpoenas and sought information from Supreme Court staff, according to Fox News and The Daily Wire, after Chief Justice John Roberts' internal investigation failed to identify the leaker.

Conservatives have long viewed the leak as a brazen attempt to intimidate the Court and sway justices before the final ruling. The criminal probe represents a rare accountability measure—though critics note the four-year delay raises questions about prosecutorial priorities under the Biden administration.

For Colorado, the *Dobbs* decision restored abortion regulation to the states, allowing Colorado voters and lawmakers to set their own policy. The leak's origin remains a symbol of institutional breach that conservatives argue undermined the Court's independence and the integrity of the judicial process.

Watch whether federal prosecutors actually pursue charges or whether this investigation, like the internal one before it, dissolves without naming a culprit.
