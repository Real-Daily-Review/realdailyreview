# DC Gym Shooting Leaves One Dead, Four Wounded; Gunman Dead After Standoff

A shooting rampage near the White House Thursday morning ended with one dead, four wounded, and the suspect dead after a standoff—a stark reminder that violent crime persists in America's capital despite strict gun laws that progressives tout as models for the nation.

James Stuart, 46, a building manager at the Metropole condominium complex, was shot and killed inside Vida Fitness by Matt Lambert, 55, according to police (Washington Examiner). Lambert had been involved in a dispute with Stuart over building management issues. Four others were wounded, including one in critical condition, before Lambert barricaded himself on the seventh floor of an apartment building and sustained a fatal gunshot wound to the head (The Daily Wire).

Notably, Lambert's attorney claimed the suspect "was doing very well" and had planned to start a new job the following week (New York Post)—raising questions about what triggered the sudden violence and whether warning signs were missed.

The incident underscores a hard truth: Washington, D.C., already has some of the nation's strictest gun regulations, yet remains plagued by shootings. Conservatives argue this demonstrates that gun control alone cannot prevent determined criminals from obtaining weapons or committing violence—and that law-abiding citizens deserve the right to defend themselves.
