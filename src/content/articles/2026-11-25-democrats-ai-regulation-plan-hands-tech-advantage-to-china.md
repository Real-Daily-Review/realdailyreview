# Democrats' AI Overreach Threatens U.S. Tech Edge While China Races Ahead

Incoming congressional progressives are pushing federal AI regulation that would hobble American innovation while leaving Chinese competitors untouched—a recipe for surrendering tech dominance to Beijing.

According to the Washington Examiner, Democrats are championing new federal agencies, government licensing schemes, and expanded litigation against AI developers. Rep.-elect Scott Wiener (D-CA) has led efforts to regulate AI in California, setting a template for national overreach.

**Republicans argue** this regulatory approach is backwards. While Democrats burden U.S. companies with bureaucratic red tape and lawsuit exposure, Chinese AI firms operate freely under Beijing's state direction—giving them a structural advantage in speed, cost, and scale. The result: American innovation slows, investment capital flees, and China leapfrogs the West in artificial intelligence capability.

Conservatives warn that federal AI licensing and new regulatory agencies would entrench incumbent tech giants while crushing startups and smaller competitors. Colorado's growing tech sector—from Denver startups to Front Range software firms—would face compliance costs that smaller players cannot absorb.

The real risk isn't unregulated AI; it's *regulated American* AI competing against *unregulated Chinese* AI. That's a losing bet for U.S. competitiveness and national security.
