# Nvidia CEO Under Fire Over Alleged Chinese Intelligence Entanglement

Nvidia's board has launched an independent investigation into whether CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by Chinese intelligence through an alleged romantic relationship with a woman connected to Beijing's spy apparatus (Washington Examiner). The company retained former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim and a major Wall Street law firm to probe the matter.

Details are emerging about Alice Huang, the alleged Chinese operative at the center of the controversy, and her connections to Megaspeed, a significant Nvidia customer (Breitbart). The New York Post reports Huang boarded Air Force One months before the scandal erupted, raising questions about access and vetting.
