# Canvas Learning Platform Hit by Cyberattack, Data Breach Threatened

Canvas, the learning management system used by thousands of U.S. schools and universities, went offline following a cyberattack that exposed student data, according to CBS News.

The hacking group ShinyHunters claimed responsibility for the breach, which compromised student names, email addresses, ID numbers, and messages, according to The Verge. ShinyHunters posted a message on the downed platform stating the group had breached Instructure, Canvas's parent company, and accused the company of ignoring their contact attempts and performing only "security patches" in response.

The group threatened to release the affected schools' data unless they negotiate directly with the hackers, The Verge reported. Canvas users attempting to access the system Thursday encountered messages from ShinyHunters rather than normal login screens.

Instructure has not yet released a public statement addressing the scope of the breach, the number of affected institutions, or its response timeline. The incident marks a significant disruption to educational operations, as Canvas is widely relied upon for course management, assignments, and student communications across American higher education and K-12 systems.

Watch for Instructure's official disclosure of affected schools, details on the breach scope, and whether institutions negotiate with or report the hackers to law enforcement.
