# Texas Democrats Double Down on DEI After Election Gains—A Warning for Colorado

Texas Democrats are wasting no time pushing DEI programs back into universities after recent electoral wins, introducing bills to repeal the state's 2023 law banning the controversial initiatives. The move signals Democrats' determination to resurrect identity-politics spending regardless of public backlash.

This matters for Colorado. Our universities and public institutions face similar pressure from progressive activists to expand DEI bureaucracies and hiring mandates—costly overhead that diverts resources from merit-based education and hiring. Colorado taxpayers should ask: how much are CU, CSU, and DU spending on DEI administrators, and what measurable outcomes justify it?

Republicans argue DEI programs waste taxpayer money on ideological indoctrination rather than academic excellence. They point to Texas's 2023 law as proof voters reject this approach. Democrats counter that diversity initiatives promote inclusion and combat discrimination—though they offer little evidence these programs improve educational outcomes or lower costs.

Meanwhile, Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) insists Democrats can simultaneously investigate Trump and push an "affordability agenda," claiming accountability for past foreign policy would somehow lower prices. Conservatives say this conflates partisan investigations with economic policy and ignores that government spending—not Trump-era decisions—drives inflation.

**Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature moves to expand DEI mandates or block similar transparency measures that Texas Republicans enacted.**
