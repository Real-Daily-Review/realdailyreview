# Fox News Parts Ways with Maria Bartiromo After 12+ Years

Fox News has abruptly ended its relationship with veteran business anchor Maria Bartiromo, marking a significant shake-up at the network's business division.

Bartiromo, who hosted "Mornings with Maria" and "Maria Bartiromo's Wall Street" on Fox Business Network, departed effective immediately, according to a Fox News Media statement. She had served as the network's global markets editor since joining in 2014 from CNBC, where she built her reputation covering Wall Street and financial markets.

"We thank Maria for her work over the last 12 ½ years and wish her all the best on her next chapter," Fox News said in a terse press release that offered no explanation for the sudden departure.

The exit comes as Fox News continues internal restructuring under new leadership. Bartiromo had been a fixture on Fox Business for over a decade, providing market analysis and business news to conservative-leaning viewers who rely on the network for financial coverage aligned with free-market principles.

Her departure leaves a notable gap in Fox's business programming lineup at a time when markets remain volatile and Colorado investors—particularly those in energy, agriculture, and small business—depend on reliable financial journalism.

Watch for announcements about her replacement and whether Fox Business will maintain its pro-growth, market-friendly editorial approach.
