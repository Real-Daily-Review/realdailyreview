# New York Swing Districts Heat Up Over Immigration, ICE Enforcement

Republican Rep. Mike Lawler is hammering his Democratic opponent on sanctuary city policies and lax bail reform in a heated battle for a New York swing House seat that could determine chamber control. In a fiery debate, Lawler fact-checked Democrat Cait Conley over ICE enforcement and New York's sanctuary laws—core issues driving voter frustration in competitive districts (Fox News Politics).

The clash reflects a broader Republican strategy: making Democratic soft-on-immigration stances a liability in purple districts. Republicans argue that sanctuary city policies and weak bail reform endanger public safety and undermine federal immigration enforcement. Democrats counter that their approach protects vulnerable communities and that Republicans overstate the crime connection.

**The Colorado angle:** While this race is in New York, similar dynamics are playing out in Colorado's competitive districts. Immigration enforcement, public safety, and the costs of sanctuary policies resonate with Colorado voters concerned about border security and responsible law enforcement. As Democrats double down on progressive criminal justice policies, Republicans see an opening to reclaim suburban and swing voters.

Conservatives should watch whether Lawler's immigration-focused messaging gains traction in other swing races—it could signal a winning playbook for 2026 midterms nationwide.
