# Trump Administration Moves to Counter Chinese Influence at Panama Canal

The Trump administration is taking direct action to limit Chinese control over the Panama Canal—a strategic chokepoint the legacy media has largely ignored while fixating on domestic political theater, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Within its first six months in office, the administration has begun reshaping Western Hemisphere geopolitics by pushing back against Beijing's growing influence over one of the world's most critical shipping routes.

Conservatives argue this represents overdue pushback against years of strategic neglect. China's expanding footprint in Latin America—including stakes in canal operations and regional infrastructure—poses a direct threat to U.S. interests and hemispheric security. Republicans contend that previous administrations failed to adequately counter this encroachment, allowing adversaries to gain leverage over American commerce and military mobility.

The move signals a broader Trump-era shift toward great-power competition and away from the globalist consensus that treated the canal as merely a commercial asset rather than a strategic national security asset.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether Congress backs the administration's Panama strategy with funding and diplomatic support, or whether establishment Republicans and Democrats prioritize trade relationships over security concerns.
