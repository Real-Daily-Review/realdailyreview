# Colorado Child Found Safe After CBI Alert for Missing 4-Year-Old

The Colorado Bureau of Investigation's swift action this week demonstrates why law enforcement coordination matters—a 4-year-old girl reported missing after being picked up from school by her non-custodial father has been found safe.

According to 9News Denver, the girl, Vera Spencer, was taken from school last Friday by her father, triggering an Endangered Missing Person Alert issued Wednesday by CBI (KDVR Fox31 Denver). The alert mobilized statewide resources and public awareness, leading to her recovery.

The case underscores a critical vulnerability in Colorado's custody and school safety protocols. Non-custodial parents retaining access to children at educational facilities—even without legal authority—represents a gap that schools and law enforcement must address more rigorously. While the outcome here was positive, the incident raises legitimate questions about verification procedures at pickup and parental rights enforcement.

CBI's rapid response and coordination across agencies proved effective, but conservatives should ask harder questions: Are Colorado schools adequately vetting who collects children? Are custody orders being enforced consistently? The state's family court system and school districts need clearer accountability standards.

Watch for whether this incident prompts legislative or administrative action to tighten school pickup protocols and strengthen custody enforcement mechanisms statewide.
