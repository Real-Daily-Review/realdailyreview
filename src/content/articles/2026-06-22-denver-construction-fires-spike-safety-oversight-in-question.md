# Construction Site Fires Plague Denver Metro as Safety Questions Mount

Denver's construction boom is facing a dangerous new threat: a spike in fires at homes under construction across the metro area. A Friday morning blaze in the Highlands neighborhood completely destroyed a house under construction—the second such fire in that area within a week, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver. Separately, a fatal house fire broke out Sunday evening in Aurora, claiming at least one life (9News Denver).

The pattern raises serious questions about site security, material storage, and building code enforcement during the construction phase. Colorado's red-hot housing market has accelerated development across the Front Range, but inadequate oversight of construction sites—particularly overnight security and fire prevention protocols—may be creating dangerous gaps.

Conservatives and taxpayers should demand answers: Are local building departments conducting adequate inspections? Are contractors properly securing sites and storing flammable materials? Are Denver and Aurora fire marshals adequately staffed to monitor the surge in construction activity? Lax enforcement or understaffing could expose homebuyers and neighborhoods to preventable tragedy while driving up insurance costs for everyone.

Watch for whether city officials acknowledge systemic gaps in construction-site oversight or deflect blame onto contractors.
