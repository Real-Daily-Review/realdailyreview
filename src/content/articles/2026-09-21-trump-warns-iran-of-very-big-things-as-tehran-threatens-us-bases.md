# Trump Signals Hardline Iran Strategy as Tehran Escalates Threats

President Trump is taking a muscular approach to Iran negotiations, warning Sunday that he's weighing options ranging from economic pressure to military action. Speaking to Fox News, Trump said he's in "deciding mode" on Iran, considering "wiping Iran out, letting them rot economically or making a deal," while noting that Tehran's officials are "hiding like rats" and making negotiations difficult (Daily Wire, Breitbart).

The warning comes as Iran's military threatened "sustained, effective and painful" attacks on U.S. bases and interests across the Middle East if Washington strikes again. Trump countered with his own message: "very big things" are coming.

**The Colorado angle:** Energy markets will watch closely. Any escalation in the Middle East typically spikes oil prices, affecting Colorado gas prices and energy sector stability. Additionally, Trump's willingness to use military leverage could reshape U.S. energy independence strategy—critical for Colorado's oil and gas workers and the state's economy.

Conservatives argue Trump's direct posture and willingness to consider all options—including military force—is the only language Tehran understands after years of failed diplomatic overtures. The left typically counters that military threats escalate regional instability, though Iran's own threats undercut that argument here.
