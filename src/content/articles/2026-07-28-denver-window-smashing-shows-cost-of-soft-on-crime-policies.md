# Denver Business Owner Frustrated After Window Smashed; Crime Concerns Mount

Denver's downtown core continues its slide into disorder as a business owner watches helplessly while a man caught on camera hurls a rock through her storefront window—apparently for no reason at all (KDVR Fox31 Denver). The incident underscores what conservatives have warned for years: progressive soft-on-crime policies and inadequate enforcement are turning Colorado's capital into a magnet for petty vandalism and street chaos.

Republicans and law-and-order advocates argue that Denver's permissive approach to quality-of-life crimes—combined with catch-and-release prosecution policies—emboldens repeat offenders and destroys the business environment. When storefront owners can't protect their property without constant surveillance, the social contract breaks down.

The business community, already reeling from inflation and rising operating costs, now faces the added burden of security upgrades and insurance claims. That's money that could go to payroll or expansion instead.

Democrats typically counter that root causes—homelessness, mental health crises, poverty—demand compassion and social services rather than enforcement. But conservatives say that's a false choice: you can address underlying issues *and* maintain public order through consistent consequences.

Watch whether Denver's city leadership responds with actual enforcement or doubles down on the failed approach that created this mess in the first place.
