# Trump Officials Split on Nvidia CEO Huang Espionage Claims

A brewing controversy over Nvidia founder Jensen Huang's alleged ties to Chinese interests has divided the Trump administration, exposing tensions over national security versus tech industry relationships.

National Review raised the core conservative concern: why is the U.S. allowing Nvidia to sell advanced AI chips to China at all? The outlet questioned whether Huang should face scrutiny over the arrangement (National Review). The espionage angle centers on Huang's alleged connection to a Chinese businesswoman, framed by some as a potential intelligence operation.

But David Sacks, Trump's former AI Czar and current co-chair of the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology, is pushing back hard. Sacks told Breitbart the "spy narrative" against Huang is "collapsing already" and doesn't withstand scrutiny.

The split matters for Colorado's tech and defense sectors, which depend on stable U.S.-China tech policy and semiconductor supply chains. If the Trump administration cracks down on Nvidia's China sales, it could reshape Colorado's AI and defense-tech ecosystem—or it could signal the administration won't aggressively restrict tech exports despite national security concerns.
