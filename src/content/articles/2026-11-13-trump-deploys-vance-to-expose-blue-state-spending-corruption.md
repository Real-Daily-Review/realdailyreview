# Trump Taps Vance to Hunt Corruption in Blue-State Spending Schemes

President Trump is enlisting Vice President JD Vance to investigate alleged corruption and waste in Democratic-run states and cities—a move that could expose how billions in taxpayer dollars vanish into government programs, according to *Fox News Politics*. Trump specifically named Minnesota, California, and Illinois as targets for Vance's oversight.

The directive reflects conservative frustration with unchecked spending in progressive strongholds. Republicans argue that Democratic-led governments have become laboratories for fiscal mismanagement, with bloated bureaucracies and failed social programs draining public coffers while delivering poor results for residents.

For Colorado, this matters. While the state leans purple, Denver's progressive city government and Colorado's Democratic legislature have expanded spending on homelessness initiatives, green-energy mandates, and regulatory overhead—all areas where conservatives say accountability is lacking. If the Trump-Vance team successfully documents waste in similar blue-state programs, it could provide ammunition for Colorado Republicans pushing back against similar spending proposals locally.

*National Review* notes potential daylight between Trump and Vance on policy priorities, suggesting the VP role carries real risk if the two diverge on major issues.

**Watch for:** Whether Vance's corruption probe produces concrete findings that Colorado Republicans can cite when opposing state spending increases or new regulations.
