# AOC's 2028 Presidential Ambitions Face Steep Odds, Conservative Analysts Say

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez's potential 2028 presidential run confronts significant headwinds despite recent Democratic electoral gains, according to conservative outlets analyzing the left's post-election landscape.

National Review notes that while progressives celebrated this week's results, AOC faces considerable obstacles to securing the Democratic nomination or winning a general election (National Review). The New York congresswoman represents the party's far-left flank—a position that energizes progressive activists but alienates moderate Democrats and independent voters in swing states critical to winning the presidency.

The Daily Wire frames 2028 as a consequential election that will "dramatically" reshape America's direction, underscoring conservative concerns about which candidate and party will control federal policy on taxes, regulation, energy, and the size of government (The Daily Wire).

For Colorado specifically, a 2028 matchup involving AOC or another progressive standard-bearer could intensify battles over federal land management, water policy, and energy development—issues where the left's environmental agenda directly conflicts with Colorado's ranching, agriculture, and energy sectors.
