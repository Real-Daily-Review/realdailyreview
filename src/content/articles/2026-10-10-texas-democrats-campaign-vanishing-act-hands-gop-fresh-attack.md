# Texas Democrat's Campaign Vanishing Act Raises Questions as Election Looms

A Democratic Texas Senate candidate's mysterious two-week disappearance from the campaign trail has handed Republicans fresh ammunition in a critical race. James Talarico went dark for roughly 12 days before resurfacing at Austin City Limits, prompting sharp attacks from Trump and Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton on the stump (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

Talarico's campaign offered limited explanation for the absence, citing a health diagnosis without specifics. The timing—deep in the homestretch of a competitive Senate race—fueled heavy speculation about what prompted the sudden retreat from public view.

Republicans seized on the gap. Paxton and Trump used Talarico's absence to question his fitness and commitment to the race, turning the candidate's silence into a campaign liability. Democrats have not mounted a vigorous public defense of the disappearance, instead keeping details close.
