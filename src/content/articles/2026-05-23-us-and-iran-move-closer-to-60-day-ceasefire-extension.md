# U.S. and Iran Edge Toward Ceasefire Extension Deal

President Trump said the U.S. is "getting a lot closer" to an agreement with Iran, according to CBS News, as officials and negotiators discuss terms that would extend the current ceasefire by 60 days.

The proposed deal centers on gradual reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments, according to mediators cited by the Financial Times. U.S. officials briefed on the conversations confirmed discussions around a process to reopen the waterway, CBS News reported.

The negotiations represent a significant diplomatic effort to stabilize tensions in the region following recent escalations. Both sides appear to be working through a structured framework, though specific timelines and implementation details remain under discussion.

Watch for official statements from both governments confirming the deal's terms and any announcements about when the 60-day extension would take effect.
