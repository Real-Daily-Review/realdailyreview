# U.S. Strikes Iran After Trump Says Ceasefire Collapsed

The Trump administration launched military strikes against Iran after claiming the regime violated a ceasefire agreement, escalating tensions in the Middle East and raising questions about the durability of any negotiated settlement with Tehran.

According to the Daily Caller, Trump asserted that Iran broke the terms of the ceasefire, prompting the U.S. response. The Daily Wire reported that the strikes came as Iranian protests intensified, with citizens demanding an end to ayatollah rule—a sign of internal instability within the regime.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that strength and credible deterrence are the only language hostile regimes understand. Allowing Iran to violate agreements without consequence invites further aggression and undermines American credibility with allies. Trump's willingness to respond swiftly, supporters contend, demonstrates resolve after years of what they view as the Obama-Biden administration's appeasement.

**Democrats counter** that military escalation risks wider regional conflict and that diplomatic off-ramps should remain open, though specifics from Democratic responses are not detailed in these reports.
