# Trump Administration Tightens Economic Screws on Iran; Democrats Fire Back With Impeachment Articles

The Trump administration is escalating economic pressure on Iran through "Operation Economic Outcast," a Treasury Department campaign designed to isolate Tehran from global markets and punish foreign entities doing business there, according to The Daily Wire. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced the initiative roughly six months after Trump launched military operations against Iran.

Meanwhile, Democrats are responding with political theater. Rep. Al Green introduced articles of impeachment against Trump, citing deaths allegedly caused by ICE agents over the past year, per Just The News. Republicans argue these impeachment efforts represent partisan overreach divorced from constitutional grounds—a continuation of what National Review calls "lawfare" that federal prosecutors would not normally pursue.

**The Colorado angle:** Trump's Iran sanctions could affect energy markets and gas prices at the pump for Colorado drivers, while his immigration enforcement stance aligns with conservative voters' border security priorities.

**What to watch:** Whether Senate Republicans hold firm against Democratic impeachment efforts, and how Iran sanctions impact global oil supplies and Colorado's energy sector.
