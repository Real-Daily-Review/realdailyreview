# Erie Mass Shooting Kills 9, Including Suspected Gunman

A mass shooting in Erie, Pennsylvania, Friday night claimed nine lives—eight victims and the suspected gunman—in what authorities are calling a "deeply disturbing" tragedy (9News Denver). Among the dead was a 45-year-old pregnant woman days away from giving birth, according to the New York Post.

Police responded to gunfire at a residence on Perry Road on Erie's East Side around 4:53 p.m. The shooter's identity has not been released.
