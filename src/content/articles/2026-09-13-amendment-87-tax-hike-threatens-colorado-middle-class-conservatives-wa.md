# Amendment 87 Would Trap Colorado in a Tax-Hike Spiral, Conservatives Warn

Colorado voters face a deceptively marketed tax grab disguised as "soak-the-rich" populism. Amendment 87 promises $2.7 billion annually by raising taxes on the top 3% of earners and 5% of businesses (Colorado Sun), but conservatives argue the real danger lies in bracket creep—the mechanism that will eventually ensnare middle-class Coloradans in higher tax brackets without legislative action.

**Republicans counter** that once the tax structure is in place, inflation automatically pushes ordinary workers into higher brackets, turning a targeted levy into a permanent middle-class tax increase. Complete Colorado warns that Amendment 87 "will result in a long-term tax hike for all Coloradans," not just the wealthy.

The amendment's proponents claim the math works: tax the top earners, cut taxes for everyone else. But conservatives see a familiar trap—government gets a new revenue stream, inflation does the rest, and suddenly working Coloradans pay more while politicians claim they never raised taxes.

For Colorado taxpayers already squeezed by housing costs and energy prices, this is another assault on take-home pay disguised as progressive fairness.

Watch whether Colorado voters reject this bracket-creep scheme or embrace it—the outcome will signal whether the state is serious about fiscal restraint or resigned to perpetual tax expansion.
