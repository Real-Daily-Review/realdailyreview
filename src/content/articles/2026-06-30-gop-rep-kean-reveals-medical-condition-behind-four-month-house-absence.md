# GOP Congressman Tom Kean Jr. Breaks Silence on Four-Month Medical Absence

A New Jersey Republican finally explained why he vanished from Congress for nearly four months—and it wasn't a publicity stunt.

Rep. Tom Kean Jr. revealed in an emotional House floor speech that a serious medical condition sidelined him from legislative duties, according to *Fox News*. The congressman, who had been mysteriously absent since early spring, disclosed that he "did not believe that this would result in a long-term stay," per the *Daily Caller*, suggesting the illness was more severe than initially anticipated.

Kean's extended absence raises practical questions about congressional accountability and constituent representation. While the congressman is entitled to medical privacy, voters in his district deserve clarity about whether their representative can fulfill his duties. Extended absences—whether medical or otherwise—create legislative vacuums that affect committee work, constituent services, and floor votes.

The GOP has generally been supportive of Kean's recovery, treating the matter as a personal health issue rather than a political liability. Democrats have not publicly weaponized the absence, suggesting bipartisan recognition that serious illness transcends partisan lines.
