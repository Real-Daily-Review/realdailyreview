# Bodycam Footage Exposes Disturbing Reality of 6-Year-Old School Shooter

Newly unsealed bodycam footage from the 2023 Richneck Elementary shooting in Virginia reveals a chilling portrait of a young child with alarming behavioral and language problems—raising hard questions about school safety, parental responsibility, and why warning signs went unheeded.

The footage, released this week and reported by the Daily Wire and Just The News, shows the 6-year-old shooter cursing at police and teachers immediately after shooting teacher Abby Zwerner. According to the bodycam record, the child admitted to stealing the gun from his mother, declaring "My mom had that! I stole that from my mom!" (Just The News). The Daily Wire notes that 16 other students and the wounded teacher fled the classroom while the shooter was being restrained.

**The conservative takeaway:** This incident indicts multiple failures—parental negligence in firearm storage, school administrators who reportedly knew of behavioral red flags, and a broader culture unwilling to address the root causes of childhood violence: family breakdown, lack of discipline, and absent accountability. Republicans argue this case demonstrates why gun-safety laws must focus on prosecuting negligent parents and securing weapons at home, not restricting law-abiding Coloradans' Second Amendment rights.

The left typically responds by calling for stricter gun regulations, ignoring the personal responsibility angle entirely.

**Watch for:** Whether Virginia pursues charges against the mother for criminal negligence—a precedent Colorado lawmakers should monitor closely.
