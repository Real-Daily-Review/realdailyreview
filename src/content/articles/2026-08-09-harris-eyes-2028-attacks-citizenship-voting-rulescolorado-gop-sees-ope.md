# Harris Signals 2028 Run, Attacks Citizenship Voting Rules—Colorado Republicans See Opening

Former Vice President Kamala Harris is laying groundwork for a potential 2028 presidential bid, telling Democrats they must embrace "leaders who have moral conviction and know how to fight" at Louisiana's party fundraiser (Washington Examiner). The rhetoric signals Harris intends to position herself as the Democratic Party's fighter—a sharp contrast to her 2024 campaign collapse.

But Harris is also doubling down on positions that alienated swing-state voters. She's publicly attacked proof-of-citizenship voting requirements, railing against the SAVE America Act's voter registration provisions (Breitbart). Republicans argue citizenship verification is basic election integrity; Democrats claim it suppresses turnout.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's election system already requires citizenship verification at registration, though the state allows same-day registration. Harris's opposition to citizenship checks puts her at odds with election-security measures most Coloradans support—a vulnerability in a state where independents and moderate Democrats have grown skeptical of progressive orthodoxy on voting access.

Harris's "ruthlessness" framing also signals the left intends to escalate partisan rhetoric heading into 2028, abandoning any pretense of unity messaging.

**Watch:** Whether Colorado Democrats embrace Harris's hardline stance or distance themselves from her unpopular positions ahead of 2026 midterms.
