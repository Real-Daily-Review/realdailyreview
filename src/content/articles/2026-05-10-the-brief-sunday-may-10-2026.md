A major data breach rattles education technology, a surprise jobs report complicates the economic picture, and international headlines range from Brazil's courts to the West Bank.

## Canvas Data Breach Exposes Millions of Student Records

A cyberattack this week compromised the personal data of millions of students across universities and K-12 districts that use Canvas, one of the country's most widely used classroom management platforms. The breach took the service down and raised immediate concerns about the security of sensitive student information held by ed-tech providers. Canvas is used broadly enough that the incident is expected to affect institutions across multiple states, though the full scope of the compromise is still being assessed.

## Spirit Airlines Crowdfunding Campaign Tops $337 Million in Pledges

A content creator known for a viral stunt flying Spirit Airlines for 24 consecutive hours has launched a crowdfunding campaign to purchase the now-defunct carrier, drawing more than $337 million in pledged funds. Analysts say the campaign, while attention-grabbing, has raised questions about its viability — and that Spirit's collapse continues to reshape the budget airline market, with several competitors positioned to absorb its former routes and passengers.

## April Jobs Report Beats Expectations, Complicating Economic Outlook

U.S. nonfarm payrolls for April came in stronger than forecast, surpassing the Dow Jones consensus estimate of 55,000 new jobs. The better-than-expected figure offers a measure of reassurance about labor market resilience but also adds complexity to expectations around Federal Reserve policy, as a robust jobs picture may reduce pressure on the central bank to cut interest rates in the near term.

## Brazilian Court Blocks Law That Could Have Shortened Bolsonaro Sentence

Brazilian Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes suspended application of a law that could have reduced former President Jair Bolsonaro's 27-year prison sentence, pending further judicial review. The move prevents Bolsonaro from immediately benefiting from the legislation while the court examines its broader implications. Bolsonaro was sentenced following his conviction on charges related to an alleged coup attempt after his 2022 electoral defeat.

---

## Palestinian Family Says Settlers Forced Exhumation of Father's Body

A Palestinian family in Jenin says Israeli settlers compelled them to exhume their father's body just hours after his funeral. The account, if confirmed, would represent a significant escalation in settler activity in the occupied West Bank. The incident has not yet been independently verified, and Israeli authorities have not publicly responded to the family's account.
