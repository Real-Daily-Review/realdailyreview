# News Brief: NBC Helicopter Crashes in Los Angeles; Three Dead

A television news helicopter working for NBC Los Angeles crashed in a fireball Tuesday night while covering a fatal bus accident in the Chatsworth area, killing all three people aboard (New York Post).

The aircraft went down in a parking lot near the original accident scene. An NBC news anchor reported losing contact with the crew during the incident. According to veteran helicopter pilot Zoey Tur's analysis of crash audio, the disaster appeared to involve engine failure (New York Post).

The crash underscores the inherent risks of aerial news gathering—a practice that, while dramatic and ratings-friendly, puts pilots and crews in harm's way chasing breaking stories. The incident raises questions about whether news organizations adequately weigh safety protocols against competitive pressure to get footage first.
