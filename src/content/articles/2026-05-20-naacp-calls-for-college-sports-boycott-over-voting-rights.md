# NAACP Calls for College Sports Boycott Over Voting Rights

The NAACP is urging Black athletes, alumni, fans and the general public to boycott athletic programs at public universities in states that are redistricting longtime Black lawmakers, according to PBS NewsHour. The boycott call follows a Supreme Court voting rights decision that has drawn criticism from voting rights advocates.

Justice Ketanji Brown Jackson weighed in on the ruling, warning that the court risks being perceived as political at a time when public confidence in the institution is already low. According to PBS NewsHour, Jackson said the court must be careful to avoid appearing partisan.

The NAACP's boycott targets college sports programs in Southern states, linking the athletic institutions to broader redistricting efforts that the organization views as threatening Black political representation. The organization is mobilizing multiple constituencies—from athletes themselves to fans—to apply economic pressure on universities.

The voting rights decision at the center of the controversy has become a focal point for debate over the Supreme Court's role in election law and voting access. Jackson's public comments underscore tensions between the court's legitimacy and its recent rulings on voting rights.
