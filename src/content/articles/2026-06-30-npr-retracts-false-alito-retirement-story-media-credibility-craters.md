# NPR's Botched Alito Retirement Hoax Exposes Mainstream Media's Credibility Crisis

NPR accidentally published—then hastily retracted—a false report Tuesday claiming Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito was retiring, igniting a firestorm that exposed the Left-leaning outlet's editorial sloppiness at a critical moment for the Court.

NPR pulled the piece within hours and posted an editor's note acknowledging the error, but the damage was done: the fake bombshell had already seeded doubt about the Court's stability and fueled speculation about the conservative majority's future.
