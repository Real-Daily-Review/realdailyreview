# FBI Probe Into Omar Marriage Claims Shelved Under Biden; Conservatives Demand Answers

A federal investigation into whether Minnesota Democratic Rep. Ilhan Omar married her brother—launched by the Obama administration in 2016—was shut down by a Biden-appointed attorney in 2021, according to Fox News and the Daily Caller. Obama-era investigators reportedly uncovered evidence suggesting the marriage was fraudulent, but the probe was terminated without public explanation under the Biden Justice Department.

Republicans argue the abrupt closure raises serious questions about political favoritism and selective law enforcement. "Why would a legitimate investigation into potential immigration fraud be killed?" conservatives ask, pointing to the pattern of progressive prosecutors declining to pursue cases against Democratic allies.

The Daily Caller also reported that Omar's alleged ex-husband describes himself as a "gay man of the East African diaspora" in academic research, adding another layer to the disputed marriage narrative that Omar has consistently denied.

**Colorado angle:** While Omar represents Minnesota, the case underscores conservative concerns about DOJ politicization—an issue affecting how federal law enforcement treats Colorado businesses, energy companies, and gun owners under Democratic administrations.

Taxpayers and conservatives should watch whether House Republicans demand a formal DOJ review and whether newly empowered Republican leadership pursues oversight hearings on investigative closures.
