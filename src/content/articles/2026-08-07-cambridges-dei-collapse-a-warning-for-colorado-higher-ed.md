# Cambridge's DEI Reckoning Shows Why Merit Still Matters

Prestigious institutions willing to abandon standards for diversity checkboxes inevitably collapse from within—and Cambridge University's implosion over fabricated professor Jason Arday proves the point.

Arday, who became Cambridge's youngest Black professor at 36, was exposed as academically unqualified and a serial deceiver. The university tolerated his manifest inadequacies because DEI ideology trumped basic vetting. National Review notes the institution "was willing to look the other way" when confronted with "manifest inadequacies and deceptions for DEI reasons."

**The Conservative Case:** Conservatives argue that prioritizing demographic boxes over credentials undermines institutional credibility and harms the very groups DEI purports to help. When unqualified people advance through identity politics rather than merit, it delegitimizes their achievements and damages public trust. The Washington Examiner's assessment is blunt: "Cambridge has destroyed itself through DEI."

**The Broader Lesson:** This isn't merely a British academic embarrassment. American universities—including those educating Colorado's next generation of leaders—operate under similar DEI mandates. When Colorado families pay tuition or taxes supporting higher education, they deserve assurance that professors earned their positions through competence, not compliance with ideological quotas.

Watch whether Colorado's university system leadership acknowledges the Cambridge cautionary tale or doubles down on DEI expansion.
