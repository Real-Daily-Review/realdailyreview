# Iran Diplomacy Continues as Regional Toll Mounts

Diplomatic efforts to prevent escalation between the US and Iran are intensifying, with Pakistan's army chief traveling to Tehran as part of a broader mediation push, according to the Financial Times. The visit by Asim Munir joins ongoing efforts by Qatar and other intermediaries to broker a deal and avert a resumption of full-scale conflict.

Tehran says diplomacy continues but has not yet reached an agreement with Washington, according to Al Jazeera. The diplomatic activity comes against a backdrop of mounting casualties in the region: Israeli attacks on Lebanon have killed at least 3,111 people since March 2, with 9,432 injured, according to Lebanon's Health Ministry cited by Al Jazeera.

The mediation efforts underscore international concern about the conflict's trajectory. Pakistan's involvement signals broader regional interest in de-escalation, though the absence of a deal suggests significant gaps remain between the parties.

Watch for announcements from Tehran or US officials on whether negotiations produce concrete progress toward a ceasefire framework.
