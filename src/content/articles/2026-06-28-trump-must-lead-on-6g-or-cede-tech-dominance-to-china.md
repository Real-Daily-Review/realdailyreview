# Trump Must Seize 6G Leadership Before China Does

American technological dominance in wireless networks is slipping—and Colorado's tech corridor and rural broadband future depend on the next administration getting 6G right.

The United States faces a critical window to lead in 6G development, the next-generation wireless technology that will define connectivity for decades (Washington Examiner). While 5G enabled today's mobile networks, 6G will power emerging technologies expected to reshape how Americans work, communicate, and do business.

The stakes are stark: China and other competitors are already moving aggressively into 6G research and deployment. Without decisive American leadership—backed by market-friendly policy and infrastructure investment—the U.S. risks ceding technological and economic advantage to hostile powers.

For Colorado specifically, 6G leadership matters. Rural broadband gaps persist across the state, and closing them requires next-generation infrastructure. Colorado's growing tech hubs in Denver and Boulder depend on cutting-edge connectivity to compete globally. Energy development and agriculture—cornerstones of Colorado's economy—also rely on reliable, advanced wireless networks.

Conservatives argue the Trump administration should prioritize 6G through deregulation, private-sector innovation incentives, and strategic infrastructure partnerships—not bloated government programs. The focus must be speed to market and American technological superiority, not bureaucratic mandates.

**Watch whether the incoming administration backs 6G development with deregulation and private investment, or retreats into regulatory caution while China pulls ahead.**
