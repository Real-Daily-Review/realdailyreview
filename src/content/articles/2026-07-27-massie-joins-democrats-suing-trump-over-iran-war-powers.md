# Trump Faces Rare GOP Rebellion Over Iran Military Action

Rep. Thomas Massie, a consistent Trump critic from Kentucky, has joined Democrats in an unprecedented legal challenge to the White House over alleged violations of the War Powers Resolution regarding Iran operations (Fox News Politics). Massie and Democratic lawmakers are pushing a resolution to sue President Trump and Secretary of Defense Hegseth, claiming the administration overstepped constitutional authority without proper congressional approval.

The move highlights fractures within Republican ranks on executive war powers—a constitutional tension that predates Trump but remains unresolved. Conservatives are divided: some argue the President must act decisively to counter Iranian threats, while constitutional hawks like Massie contend that even Republican presidents cannot bypass Congress on military deployment decisions.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's delegation, including Republican Senators John Hickenlooper and Michael Bennet, will face pressure to clarify their stance on war powers oversight. How Colorado's House members vote on any formal resolution could signal whether they prioritize presidential deference or constitutional checks—a test for 2026 primary and general election positioning.
