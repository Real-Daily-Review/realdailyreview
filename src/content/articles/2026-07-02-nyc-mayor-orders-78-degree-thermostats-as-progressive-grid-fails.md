# NYC Mayor's 78-Degree Thermostat Order Exposes Progressive Energy Mismanagement

New York City's socialist mayor Zohran Mamdani told residents to set air conditioners to 78 degrees during a heat wave—a directive that sparked immediate backlash as a symbol of progressive government overreach and failed energy policy.

Mamdani framed the order as collective sacrifice to protect the power grid, claiming the city's infrastructure was "working overtime" to keep residents cool. Critics, however, saw it differently. *Fox News* and *The Daily Wire* reported that New Yorkers and commentators blasted the mandate as "communism," arguing that citizens shouldn't be told how to cool their own homes. *Breitbart* columnist John Nolte pointed to a deeper problem: Democrat-run New York's aging power grid can't handle basic summer demand, yet residents are expected to suffer rather than invest in infrastructure upgrades.

The incident illustrates a pattern conservatives warn about: progressive cities prioritize regulation and rationing over energy production and grid modernization. Instead of building capacity—whether through natural gas plants, nuclear power, or other reliable sources—left-wing mayors impose restrictions on citizens' comfort and freedom.

Colorado taxpayers and energy workers should note the contrast: states embracing responsible energy development and grid investment don't need to ration air conditioning. Watch whether Colorado's Democratic leadership follows New York's playbook or prioritizes grid reliability over green mandates.
