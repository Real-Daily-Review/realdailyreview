# Colorado Faces Dangerous Heat Wave This Weekend—Prepare Now

Denver braces for near-record temperatures this weekend as a dangerous heat event grips the Front Range. The National Weather Service has issued Pinpoint Weather Alert Days for Saturday and Sunday, with highs approaching record values across the region, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver and 9News Denver.

The heat comes on the heels of Friday evening storms along the I-25 corridor, with additional thunderstorm activity possible Saturday. Residents should expect sweltering conditions that could stress the power grid and strain Colorado's water resources—a critical concern as the state continues managing drought conditions and competing water demands.

Heat waves like this underscore why Colorado needs reliable, affordable energy infrastructure. Conservatives argue that Colorado's aggressive push toward renewable-only electricity leaves the grid vulnerable during peak demand periods. Reliable natural gas and coal plants provide the baseload power necessary to keep air conditioning running when Coloradans need it most. The state's energy transition must prioritize grid stability and affordability for working families, not ideological mandates that risk blackouts.

Coloradans should stock water, check on elderly neighbors, and ensure air conditioning systems are functioning. Keep an eye on whether Colorado's grid handles the demand surge without rolling blackouts or emergency measures.
