# Trump Digs In on SAVE Act as House Faces Standstill

President Trump is refusing to budge on the SAVE America Act, threatening to halt legislative progress until Congress passes it—and GOP holdouts are backing his play by blocking all other votes.

Trump flatly rejected compromise options, including using the reconciliation process to pass portions of the bill, according to the *Washington Examiner*. He canceled signing a housing bill Wednesday to pressure lawmakers, signaling this is non-negotiable.

House Republicans are enforcing the blockade. The *Daily Caller* reports GOP members are threatening a complete legislative shutdown: "Not one piece of their legislation will pass" until SAVE gets done. This hardball tactic has frozen the chamber.

Democrats are predictably crying foul. Sen. Elizabeth Warren (D-MA) claimed Trump is "trying to subvert democracy" by leveraging the housing bill, per *Breitbart*—the standard progressive response when Republicans use leverage to advance their agenda.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado taxpayers and businesses need clarity on what SAVE actually contains and how it affects state interests. Without details in these reports, conservatives should demand transparency on whether this bill expands federal overreach or genuinely restrains government.
