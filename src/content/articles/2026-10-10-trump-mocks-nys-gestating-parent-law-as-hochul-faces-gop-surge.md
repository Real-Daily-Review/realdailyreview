# Trump Blasts New York's 'Gestating Parent' Law as Hochul Faces GOP Challenge

President Trump hammered Democratic Gov. Kathy Hochul's progressive agenda Friday at a Syracuse rally, mocking New York's recent surrogacy law that replaces "mother" with "gestating parent" and "paternity" with "parentage hearings" (New York Post). Trump called the measure emblematic of "radical left lunatic" governance, while backing Republican Nassau County Executive Bruce Blakeman's long-shot gubernatorial bid against Hochul (Washington Examiner, Just The News).

The surrogacy law exemplifies the cultural overreach conservatives warn about: redefining biological reality through legislative fiat. Republicans argue such language games distract from real governance—inflation, crime, energy policy—while alienating working families who reject progressive social engineering.

**Colorado angle:** While New York battles identity politics, Colorado Republicans should watch how Hochul's defeat (or survival) shapes national momentum heading into 2026. Blue-state backlash against progressive excess could embolden Colorado GOP challengers to Dem incumbents, particularly on education curriculum fights and parental rights—issues where Colorado parents increasingly align with Trump's cultural critique.
