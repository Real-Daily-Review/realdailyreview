# Democrats' Midterm Win Sets Stage for Tax Hikes, Abortion Push on Colorado Voters

Democrats' strong midterm performance has emboldened the party to pursue a dual agenda that threatens Colorado taxpayers and pro-life voters: sweeping tax increases and a nationwide abortion-on-demand push.

According to *The Daily Wire*, Democratic victories could revive billions in proposed tax hikes, targeting federal income tax rates and putting President Trump's tax-cut agenda squarely in Democrats' crosshairs. While Democrats frame this as "relief for lower-income families," the proposals would impose significantly higher taxes on earners across the board—a direct hit to Colorado's middle class and small-business owners already squeezed by state-level spending.

Meanwhile, *National Review* warns that emboldened Democrats will pursue a reinstated nationwide right to abortion, overturning state protections Colorado pro-lifers have fought to preserve. This represents a fundamental clash over life, federalism, and whose values shape policy.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado voters narrowly rejected a pro-life ballot measure in 2024, yet the state remains divided on abortion. A Democratic push for federal abortion-on-demand legislation would bypass Colorado's democratic process entirely. Combined with federal tax increases, Colorado families and energy workers face a coordinated assault on their wallets and values.
