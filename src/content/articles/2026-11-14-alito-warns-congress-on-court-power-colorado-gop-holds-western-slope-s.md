# Supreme Court Justice Alito Warns Congress Off Court-Packing; Colorado GOP Holds Western Slope Seat

Supreme Court Justice Samuel Alito is sounding the alarm about congressional overreach, warning that lawmakers have the power to defund the high court and cripple its operations—a stark reminder that the left's court-packing ambitions carry real constitutional risks (Fox News Politics).

Meanwhile, Colorado Republicans secured a crucial win on the Western Slope. GOP Rep. Jeff Hurd defeated Democrat Dwayne Romero in the competitive 3rd Congressional District race, preserving Republican control of a seat the party has held since 2011 (Denver Post). The district spans Colorado's rugged western counties and southern regions—energy-dependent territory where conservative governance on oil, gas, and water issues resonates with voters.

Alito's warning comes as progressive Democrats have openly discussed expanding the Supreme Court and stripping its authority to block left-wing legislation. Republicans argue such moves would destroy judicial independence and weaponize the court for partisan gain. Conservatives say Alito's statement underscores why defending the Court's institutional integrity matters—especially as the left continues threatening institutional reforms whenever rulings don't align with progressive goals.

The Hurd victory signals that Western Slope voters still favor Republican stewardship on energy development and fiscal restraint over Democratic regulatory expansion.
