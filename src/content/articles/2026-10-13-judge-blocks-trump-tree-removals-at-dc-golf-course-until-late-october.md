# Federal Judge Blocks Trump Admin Tree Removals at DC Golf Course

A Biden-appointed federal judge has temporarily halted the Trump administration's tree-cutting operations at East Potomac Golf Links in Washington, D.C., extending a pause until October 26 to allow government officials to explain the work's necessity.

Judge Ana C. Reyes issued the temporary restraining order after the administration removed hundreds of trees over a holiday weekend without apparent notice to environmental or preservation stakeholders (Washington Examiner). Just The News reports the one-day pause was meant to give the government time to locate a key Trump official for questioning about why the massive tree-cutting occurred on the holiday.

The dispute centers on whether the administration properly justified the removal and followed required environmental review procedures. The Trump administration maintains the work was necessary; critics argue it lacked transparency and proper process.
