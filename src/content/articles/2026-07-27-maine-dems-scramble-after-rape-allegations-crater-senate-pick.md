# Maine Democrats Scramble After Rape Allegations Crater Senate Candidate, Bernie Sanders Shrugs

Maine Democrats have crowned Troy Jackson, a fifth-generation logger and former state Senate president, as the replacement nominee to face Republican Susan Collins in what's now a toss-up race—but the party's desperation is showing. Jackson inherits a seat left vacant after Graham Platner, the original Democratic nominee, dropped out following rape accusations from a former girlfriend.

The GOP is already mobilizing. A Republican group has vowed to spend $42 million against Jackson, characterizing him as "like Graham Platner but worse" and citing what they describe as "volatile personality" and "anger issues with women" (Daily Caller).

Most damning: Sen. Bernie Sanders, who aggressively backed Platner, told CBS's *Face the Nation* he has no regrets about that endorsement—even after the sexual assault allegations torpedoed the candidacy. Sanders gave Jackson an "excellent" chance despite the chaos (Washington Examiner, Breitbart).

The episode exposes Democratic vetting failures and the party's willingness to circle wagons around troubled candidates. For Colorado conservatives watching Senate races nationally, this is a reminder that progressive movements often prioritize ideology over accountability—and that voters notice when the left's stated values on women's safety evaporate under political pressure.
