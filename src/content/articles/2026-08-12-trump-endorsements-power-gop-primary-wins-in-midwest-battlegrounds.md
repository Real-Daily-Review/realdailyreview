# Trump Endorsement Powers GOP Primary Wins in Midwest Battlegrounds

Republican candidates backed by President Trump are winning contested primaries in traditionally competitive districts, signaling strong party unity heading into the general election cycle. Brad Finstad survived a three-way primary in Minnesota's 1st Congressional District—Tim Walz's former seat—while Michael Alfonso, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy's son-in-law, claimed Wisconsin's 7th District GOP primary with 48% of the vote, powered by Trump's endorsement (Washington Examiner).

The wins underscore Trump's continued grip on Republican primary voters and suggest the party is consolidating around establishment-backed candidates rather than fracturing into competing factions. Both districts are competitive general-election targets, with Democrats eyeing potential flips in 2024.

**The Colorado angle:** While these races unfold in the Midwest, Colorado Republicans should note the playbook—Trump endorsements are moving primary votes decisively. As Colorado heads toward its own primary season, expect similar dynamics in competitive House and Senate races where Trump's backing could prove decisive among GOP voters.
