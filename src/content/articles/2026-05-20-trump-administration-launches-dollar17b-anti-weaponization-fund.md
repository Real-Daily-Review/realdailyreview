# Trump Administration Creates $1.7B "Anti-Weaponization" Fund for IRS Settlement

Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche announced creation of a fund exceeding $1.7 billion as part of a settlement in President Trump's lawsuit against the IRS over the leaking of his tax returns, according to CBS News.

The Justice Department describes the fund as compensation for people who "suffered weaponization and lawfare" under the Biden administration. Blanche faced bipartisan questioning about the initiative during his first Senate hearing in the role, according to PBS NewsHour, drawing backlash from lawmakers over the fund's structure and purpose.

The announcement marks a significant step in Trump's stated effort to address what his administration characterizes as politicized use of federal agencies. However, the fund's scope—who qualifies for compensation and how disbursements will be determined—remained subjects of Senate scrutiny.
