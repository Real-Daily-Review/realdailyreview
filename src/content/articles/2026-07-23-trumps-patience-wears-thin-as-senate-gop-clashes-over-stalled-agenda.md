# Senate GOP Tensions Flare Over Trump's Stalled Agenda

White House Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt warned this week that President Trump's patience is "running out" with Senate Republicans over delays on the Save America Act—a key Trump priority—prompting a sharp rebuke from Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD), who told Leavitt to "get on the phone and get the votes, right?" (Washington Examiner).

The exchange underscores growing friction between Trump's team and congressional Republicans over legislative pace. Leavitt's public pressure tactic signals frustration with Senate GOP foot-dragging on Trump's agenda, while Thune's clapback suggests the White House should focus on vote-counting rather than public complaints.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: Senate Republicans represent the last institutional check on Biden-era regulatory overreach and spending expansion. If Trump's legislative priorities stall, Colorado's energy sector, water rights, and Second Amendment protections remain vulnerable to continued federal encroachment.

Separately, Leavitt confirmed Trump will not intervene in the extradition of the Tate brothers to the UK, despite their dual citizenship (Just The News).
