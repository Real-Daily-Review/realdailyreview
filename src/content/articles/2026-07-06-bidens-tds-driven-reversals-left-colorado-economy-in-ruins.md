# Biden's Cognitive Decline and Policy Reversals Fuel Conservative Criticism

Joe Biden's presidency has devolved into a cautionary tale of ideological overreach masquerading as governance—and Colorado taxpayers are footing the bill for the consequences.

According to the Washington Examiner, the Biden White House—driven by what conservatives call "Trump derangement syndrome"—systematically reversed policies that were functioning and imposed measures so extreme they would have shocked even progressive administrations. Republicans argue the administration abandoned the moderate platform Biden campaigned on, instead allowing staff obsessed with undoing Trump-era decisions to dictate policy regardless of economic or practical merit.

Conservatives point to energy policy as Exhibit A: Biden's war on domestic oil and gas production has destabilized energy markets, spiked costs for Colorado families and businesses, and handed geopolitical advantage to hostile regimes. His regulatory assault on mining, water development, and land use has strangled Colorado's economy while enriching trial lawyers and environmental activists.

The core conservative critique: Biden's team prioritized ideological revenge over competent governance. A cognitively declining president became a vessel for staff-driven extremism—policies that harmed working Americans and surrendered American strength abroad.

Taxpayers should watch whether the incoming administration can reverse the most damaging Biden edicts before they calcify into permanent bureaucratic infrastructure.
