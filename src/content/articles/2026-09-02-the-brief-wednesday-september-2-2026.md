## Initiative 234: A Reading Standard That Could Bury Colorado in Lawsuits

Initiative 234, which would tie certain policy or funding thresholds to an 8th-grade reading level, sounds straightforward — but conservatives and legal analysts warn the four-syllable word most associated with it may be "litigation." Defining what constitutes an 8th-grade reading level in a legally enforceable way is far harder than it sounds, and Colorado taxpayers could end up footing the bill for years of courtroom battles. Voters should read the fine print before pulling the lever on a measure that may create more government and legal chaos than it solves.
