# Colorado Voters Keep Electing Democrats—Then Voting Down Their Agenda

Colorado's electorate is sending a clear message: elect Democrats to statewide office, but don't trust them with unchecked power. According to *Complete Colorado*, Coloradans have developed a pattern of ticket-splitting paired with aggressive use of citizen initiatives to block Democratic policy priorities—essentially voting for Democrats while simultaneously voting against Democratic ideas.

This reflects a fundamental skepticism among Colorado voters about progressive governance. Rather than trust the legislature to restrain itself, voters have repeatedly turned to ballot measures to impose limits Democrats won't accept voluntarily.

Meanwhile, Democrats are pushing back against scrutiny of their sanctuary immigration policies. Boulder District Attorney Michael Dougherty dismissed a GOP-led congressional report on Colorado's sanctuary practices as a "political stunt" (*9News*), rather than engaging substantively with Republican concerns about public safety and immigration enforcement.

Republicans argue that sanctuary policies undermine law enforcement and create liability for Colorado communities. Democrats counter that such policies protect vulnerable populations and that federal immigration enforcement is not a local responsibility.

The pattern is instructive: Colorado voters like Democratic candidates but distrust Democratic policy. Watch whether the 2026 ballot will again feature citizen initiatives designed to constrain Democratic legislative overreach.
