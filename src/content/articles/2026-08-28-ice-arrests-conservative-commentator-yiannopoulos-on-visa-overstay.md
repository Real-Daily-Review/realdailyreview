# ICE Arrests Milo Yiannopoulos on Visa Overstay; Deportation Ordered

Immigration enforcement just caught a high-profile conservative commentator at the gate—and it raises questions about how the system treats political figures differently.

The Department of Homeland Security confirmed that Milo Yiannopoulos, a polarizing right-wing commentator who gained prominence in the 2010s, was arrested by ICE at Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport on Thursday (Washington Examiner). DHS stated that Yiannopoulos entered the U.S. legally in 2019 via New York City but overstayed his visa (Just The News). A federal judge has ordered his immediate deportation.

Yiannopoulos, who fell from public prominence after 2017, briefly interned for then-Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene (R-Ga.) before his arrest. The case has drawn attention in conservative circles, with some questioning the timing and circumstances of his detention.

**The conservative angle:** While immigration law enforcement is legitimate, conservatives should scrutinize whether enforcement is applied consistently across the political spectrum—or whether high-profile figures face selective pressure based on ideology. Visa overstay cases are common; the question is whether this one received unusual priority.

Coloradans concerned about immigration enforcement consistency should monitor whether similar cases involving left-wing figures receive comparable attention from federal authorities.
