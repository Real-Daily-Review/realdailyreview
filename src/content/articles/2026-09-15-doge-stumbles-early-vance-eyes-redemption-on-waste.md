# DOGE's Early Stumbles Hand JD Vance a Chance to Prove Conservatives Right on Waste

Elon Musk's Department of Government Efficiency promised bold action against federal waste and fraud—then immediately disappointed conservatives expecting real results.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, DOGE generated significant early enthusiasm among Republicans and taxpayers hungry for genuine government accountability. The initiative, staffed by Musk as a special government employee, aimed to expose the bloat and mismanagement that conservatives have long argued plagues Washington.

But the effort quickly hit headwinds. The *Examiner* notes that DOGE's initial momentum stalled, raising questions about whether the Trump administration can deliver on its core promise to cut wasteful spending—a message that resonated with Colorado voters frustrated by federal overreach and runaway budgets.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. Federal spending drives inflation that hits working families and small businesses hard. Bloated agencies also expand regulations that hamper energy development, agriculture, and water management—sectors critical to Colorado's economy.

Vice President JD Vance now has an opening to revive the anti-fraud mission and prove that Republicans, unlike Democrats, will actually cut government rather than expand it. Taxpayers should watch whether DOGE produces real savings or becomes another abandoned campaign promise.
