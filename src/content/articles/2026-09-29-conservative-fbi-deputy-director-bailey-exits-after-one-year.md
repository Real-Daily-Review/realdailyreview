# Conservative FBI Deputy Director Andrew Bailey Steps Down After Year Under Trump

FBI Deputy Director Andrew Bailey, a conservative favorite tapped by the Trump administration to help reform the bureau, is stepping down after just one year on the job, citing family reasons and a desire to return to Missouri (The Daily Wire). Bailey announced his departure Monday via social media, thanking Trump administration officials for their confidence in him during his tenure.

Bailey's exit marks another leadership shuffle at an FBI that conservatives have long criticized for perceived institutional bias and overreach—particularly regarding January 6th investigations and the handling of the Hunter Biden laptop story. Republicans have pushed for wholesale reform of the bureau's leadership and culture, arguing it has become politicized under Democratic influence.

The timing raises questions about continuity in Trump's effort to reshape federal law enforcement. Conservatives have viewed Bailey as a key figure in that mission, making his departure a potential setback for those seeking meaningful change at the agency.
