# Trump Orders Vaccine Schedule Review as Schools Reopen

President Trump has signed an executive order directing a revamp of federal childhood vaccine recommendations, citing calls for "more flexibility" in vaccination protocols (Daily Caller). The move comes as schools nationwide begin their fall sessions amid an active measles outbreak.

The order signals the administration's willingness to challenge the CDC's standardized vaccine schedule. Supporters argue parents deserve discretion in timing and selecting vaccines for their children. "Americans are asking questions, especially post COVID," the Daily Caller reports, reflecting conservative skepticism of one-size-fits-all federal health mandates.

Public health officials warn that deviating from established vaccination schedules risks outbreaks of preventable diseases. They contend the current CDC recommendations are based on decades of safety data and epidemiological research.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado schools must navigate state immunization requirements while federal policy shifts. If the executive order weakens vaccination compliance, Colorado could face measles or whooping cough clusters in unvaccinated student populations—straining rural clinics and school budgets already stretched thin.

Conservatives argue parents and doctors, not bureaucrats, should customize health decisions. Critics counter that herd immunity depends on consistent vaccination rates.
