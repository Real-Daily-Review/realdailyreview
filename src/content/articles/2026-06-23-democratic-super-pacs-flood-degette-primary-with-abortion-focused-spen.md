# Democratic Super PACs Flood Colorado Primary With Abortion Cash for DeGette

Colorado Democrats are opening their wallets wide to protect Rep. Diana DeGette's seat in a primary challenge that reveals how abortion politics—not local issues—now drives party spending priorities.

Pro-Choice Majority Action, a Democratic super PAC, dropped nearly $460,000 on TV ads backing DeGette in the final weeks of her primary fight against challenger Melat Kiros, according to the *Denver Post*. The spending surge underscores how national Democratic groups view abortion access as their top electoral weapon heading into 2026, even in safely blue districts where the real contest happens in primaries.

This mirrors a national trend. Maryland's Democratic primary to replace retiring Rep. Steny Hoyer has attracted $12.5 million in outside spending, the *Washington Examiner* reports—making it one of the priciest House primaries on record.

Republicans argue this spending frenzy reveals Democratic desperation: rather than campaign on kitchen-table issues like inflation, energy costs, or water security that matter to Colorado families, the left is betting everything on single-issue mobilization. Conservatives counter that primary spending wars drain resources that could otherwise fund general-election competitiveness.

For Colorado taxpayers, the real question is whether DeGette—or any incumbent—will actually address the state's energy workforce challenges, water scarcity, or regulatory overreach once safely re-nominated.
