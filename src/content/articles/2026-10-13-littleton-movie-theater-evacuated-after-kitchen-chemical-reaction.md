# Littleton Movie Theater Evacuated After Kitchen Chemical Reaction

South Metro Fire Rescue responded Monday night to an evacuation at Alamo Drafthouse Cinema in Littleton after a small chemical reaction occurred in the kitchen around 8:13 p.m., according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31.

The incident prompted authorities to clear the theater, though details on the cause and severity of the reaction remain limited. No injuries were reported in initial accounts. Fire crews investigated the scene to determine what triggered the chemical interaction and ensure the facility was safe for reoccupancy.

Alamo Drafthouse, which combines dining and cinema, operates kitchens that handle food preparation chemicals and cleaning supplies—standard equipment that, when mishandled or improperly stored, can create hazardous reactions. The evacuation underscores the importance of workplace safety protocols and proper chemical handling in food-service environments.

The theater's response time and emergency services' swift action prevented what could have escalated into a more serious incident. Authorities have not indicated whether violations or negligence played a role.

Coloradans should monitor whether state workplace safety inspectors conduct a follow-up review and what findings emerge about kitchen safety standards at the facility.
