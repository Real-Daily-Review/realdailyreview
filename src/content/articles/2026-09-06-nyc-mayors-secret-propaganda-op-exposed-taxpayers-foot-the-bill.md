# NYC Mayor's Secret Propaganda Machine Exposes Taxpayer-Funded Abuse

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's administration operated a covert propaganda pipeline funneling messages to online influencers through encrypted channels—a scheme the city's own top lawyer essentially admitted violated the law, according to the New York Post. Chief Counsel Ramzi Kassem acknowledged Friday that City Hall took "corrective actions" after discovering the encrypted Signal chat operation, suggesting the illegal propaganda apparatus may have been funded by taxpayers.

The revelation underscores a broader pattern of progressive-led government abuse: Democratic administrations weaponizing public resources to manufacture consent and manipulate public opinion rather than earn it through honest governance.

Meanwhile, California's legislature doubled down on rewarding illegal immigration by authorizing taxpayer funding for college tuition for undocumented residents—a policy that conservatives argue incentivizes further illegal border crossings and punishes law-abiding citizens footing the bill.

**Colorado angle:** While Colorado hasn't yet adopted California's tuition subsidy scheme, progressive state lawmakers have repeatedly pushed similar policies. Taxpayers should watch whether Denver or Boulder city councils attempt similar propaganda operations or whether the state legislature resurrects proposals to fund higher education for illegal immigrants—both moves that would drain Colorado's already-strained budget.
