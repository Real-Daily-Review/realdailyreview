# NYC Mayor's 9/11 Ceremony Attendance Sparks Giuliani Pushback Over Controversial Ties

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani plans to attend Friday's 25th-anniversary 9/11 remembrance ceremony at Ground Zero, drawing sharp criticism from former Mayor Rudy Giuliani and ex-Gov. George Pataki, who have publicly urged him to stay home.

Giuliani told Newsmax the decision "offends me," arguing the ceremony exists "for the families and for America to remember historically." He cited his personal losses and close ties to 9/11 families as the basis for his objection (Just The News).

The Daily Wire reports Mamdani faces scrutiny over "awkward ties to terrorist sympathizers," though Mamdani has proceeded undeterred by the public pressure from two prominent New York Republicans.

Conservatives argue that attendance at solemn national commemorations carries symbolic weight—particularly for public officials whose backgrounds invite legitimate questions about their judgment and associations. The controversy reflects broader conservative concerns about who gets to claim space in America's collective memory of terror attacks and national tragedy.

**What to watch:** Whether other New York Republicans amplify calls for Mamdani's exclusion, and whether the mayor's attendance becomes a flashpoint in New York's 2025 political cycle.
