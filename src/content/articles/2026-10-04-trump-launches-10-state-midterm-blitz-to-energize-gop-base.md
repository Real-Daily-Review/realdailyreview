# Trump Barnstorms Ohio, Eyes 10-State Midterm Sprint

Trump is taking his "golden age" message on the road—and Colorado Republicans should pay attention to what works in swing states.

At a Saturday rally in Vandalia, Ohio, the former president urged voters to "pretend I'm on the ballot" while stumping for GOP gubernatorial candidate Vivek Ramaswamy and incumbent Sen. Jon Husted (Washington Examiner). Former NFL coach Jon Gruden joined the effort, lending star power to the Republican ticket in a state Democrats have been targeting.

Trump announced a 10-state tour ahead of the midterms, signaling the GOP's all-hands-on-deck strategy to energize the base and reclaim momentum. The rallies focus on economic messaging—Trump's "golden age" framing—and direct voter mobilization in competitive races.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Trump's grassroots energy and focus on kitchen-table issues (inflation, border security, energy independence) resonate with voters tired of Democratic overreach. The tour targets states where GOP candidates can flip seats and block further progressive legislation.

**What Colorado conservatives should watch:** Whether Trump's midterm playbook—personal candidate endorsements, economic populism, and high-energy rallies—translates to Colorado's competitive Senate and House races, where energy policy, water rights, and Second Amendment protections remain top-tier voter concerns.
