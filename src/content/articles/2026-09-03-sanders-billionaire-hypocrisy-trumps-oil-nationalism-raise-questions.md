# Bernie's Billionaire Hypocrisy Meets Trump's Oil Power Play

Bernie Sanders' crusade against billionaires rings hollow now that the Vermont socialist has joined their ranks himself—a contradiction Complete Colorado highlights as Sanders pivots his rhetoric toward attacking the ultra-wealthy while sitting comfortably in his own multi-million-dollar portfolio.

Meanwhile, President Trump is making waves internationally by leveraging U.S. government power over Venezuelan oil assets, a move National Review characterizes as "the American nationalization of Venezuelan oil"—essentially using state authority to control foreign energy resources in ways that blur the line between market capitalism and government intervention.

For Colorado, these twin stories matter. Sanders-style wealth attacks fuel the progressive tax-hike agenda that threatens Colorado businesses and energy workers. Trump's Venezuelan oil play raises questions about how far government should reach into markets—even adversarial ones—and whether such moves set precedent for regulatory overreach at home.

Republicans argue that real economic growth comes from free markets and property rights, not government seizure or wealth confiscation. The left counters that concentrated wealth and foreign adversaries require state action.

**Watch whether Colorado Democrats use either story to justify new tax proposals or energy regulations targeting Colorado oil and gas producers.**
