# DNC Chair Ken Martin Faces Resignation Pressure Over Election Autopsy

Democratic National Committee Chair Ken Martin is facing intensifying calls to resign following the release of the party's autopsy report on the 2024 presidential election, according to The Hill and Washington Examiner.

Martin had promised to release the autopsy after winning the DNC chairmanship in early 2025, but delayed the report until this week, initially claiming the party had already absorbed its lessons. The delayed release and what critics describe as an incomplete analysis have triggered backlash from within Democratic ranks.

The timing compounds existing pressure on Martin's leadership. The autopsy was intended to provide a comprehensive examination of what went wrong in the 2024 race, but the report's scope and depth have drawn criticism from party members who expected a more thorough accounting.

Democrats are now openly calling for Martin's ouster, viewing the delayed and incomplete autopsy as emblematic of leadership failures during a critical moment for the party. The controversy underscores broader Democratic frustration over the 2024 outcome and questions about the party's direction.

Watch for whether Martin faces a formal leadership challenge at the DNC or announces his own departure.
