# Israel Allegedly Sought to Eliminate Iran's Lead Negotiators During Peace Talks

U.S. officials reportedly feared Israel was attempting to shoot down aircraft carrying Iran's chief negotiators, Abbas Araghchi and Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, during ongoing peace discussions, according to the New York Post and Daily Caller.

The alleged plot prompted the Biden administration to warn Tehran through third-party intermediaries of the perceived threat to the Iranian delegation. The reports suggest the operation, if carried out, would have represented a dramatic escalation aimed at derailing diplomatic efforts.

Israel has not publicly commented on the allegations. Iranian officials have not confirmed receiving U.S. warnings, though Tehran has historically accused Israel of sabotage and assassination attempts against its nuclear and military officials.

The incident underscores deepening tensions between Israel and Iran even as both nations engage in formal negotiations. It also raises questions about the Biden administration's diplomatic strategy in the Middle East and whether U.S. warnings to Iran signal a shift in how Washington manages Israeli-Iranian hostilities.

Conservatives should monitor whether Congress demands transparency on what intelligence prompted the U.S. warning and whether the administration prioritized diplomatic process over Israeli security concerns.
