# Trump Rebrands AI as "Super Intelligence," Signs Self-Regulation Accord with Tech Giants

President Trump has officially renamed artificial intelligence to "Super Intelligence" via executive order, signaling his administration's preference for industry self-regulation over heavy-handed government mandates.

Trump teased the rebranding during a state visit with Chinese President Xi Jinping before formalizing it Tuesday at the White House. The move accompanied the signing of the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, a voluntary commitment from major tech CEOs to implement "robust internal controls and layers of internal and external review" for frontier AI models (Washington Examiner, Breitbart).

The accord represents Trump's market-friendly approach: letting innovation flourish while asking industry to police itself rather than imposing sweeping federal regulations. This contrasts sharply with the Biden-Harris administration's push for aggressive AI oversight and the progressive left's calls for strict government guardrails on the technology.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's growing tech sector and workforce stand to benefit from a regulatory environment that encourages innovation over bureaucratic friction. The state's AI and software companies won't face the compliance burden that stricter federal rules would impose.

**What to watch:** Whether this voluntary framework actually delivers meaningful safeguards or becomes a fig leaf for industry while Congress eventually pushes for mandatory regulation anyway—watch for Democratic pushback and calls for statutory AI rules.
