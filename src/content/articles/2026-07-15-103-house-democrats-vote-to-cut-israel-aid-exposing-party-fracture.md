# House Democrats Fracture Over Israel Aid as 103 Back Massie's Cut

House Democrats are eating their own over Middle East policy, with over 100 members breaking ranks to support a Republican amendment that would have stripped $3.3 billion in security aid to Israel.

Rep. Thomas Massie's (R-KY) amendment failed 314-104 on the House floor, but the real story is the Democratic civil war it exposed. According to the Washington Examiner, 103 Democrats voted with Massie to eliminate all U.S. aid to Israel, while party leadership—including Hakeem Jeffries and Katherine Clark—opposed the cut (Fox News). Only one Republican, Massie himself, voted yes.

The vote reveals how thoroughly the progressive wing has captured Democratic foreign policy on Israel. Republicans argue the party is fracturing between its traditional pro-Israel establishment and a growing anti-Israel left flank that prioritizes Palestinian activism over American strategic interests in the Middle East.

For Colorado, this matters: the state's Democratic delegation will face pressure from activists demanding they side with the anti-Israel faction, while moderate Democrats worry about alienating Jewish voters and hawkish allies. The amendment's failure doesn't settle the fight—it guarantees fiercer battles ahead.
