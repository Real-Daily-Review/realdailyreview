# Independent Doctor Challenges Socialist Democrat in CD1 Race

A Denver physician has qualified for Colorado's First Congressional District ballot as an independent challenger to Democrat Melat Kiros, signaling voter unease with the party's hard-left direction in a competitive seat.

Shimon Blau, a Jewish doctor, filed to run after Kiros won the Democratic primary last month. According to the *Denver Post*, Blau said he was "saddened and alarmed" by her victory. *Complete Colorado* notes that Blau's entry offers voters concerned about Kiros' "antisemitism" allegations and "democratic socialism" policy positions an alternative to the Democratic nominee.

The race now features a three-way contest: Kiros representing the progressive wing of the Democratic Party, Blau as an independent moderate, and the Republican nominee. For Colorado conservatives, the split Democratic vote could improve GOP chances in a district Democrats have held but that remains competitive in midterm cycles.

Blau's independent candidacy reflects growing fractures within Colorado's Democratic coalition—a sign that even some Denver-area Democrats reject the party's socialist-leaning agenda. Republicans should monitor whether Blau's campaign gains traction among moderate voters and whether his presence depresses Kiros' turnout in November.
