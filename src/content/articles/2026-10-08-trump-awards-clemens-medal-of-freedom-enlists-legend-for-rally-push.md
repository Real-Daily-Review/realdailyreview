# Trump Taps Clemens for Medal, Baseball Legend Introduces San Antonio Rally

President Trump announced that Hall of Famer Roger Clemens will receive the Presidential Medal of Freedom, with the legendary pitcher introducing Trump at a San Antonio rally as the president continues a multi-state campaign swing through key Republican strongholds.

Clemens, who spent over two decades in Major League Baseball with the Yankees, Red Sox, and other franchises, took the stage in Texas to introduce the president. The honor reflects Trump's continued effort to court sports figures and cultural icons—a strategy he's branded as embracing his role as "Sports Fan in Chief" (Daily Wire).

The announcement comes as Trump barnstorms Nebraska, New York, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Ohio, shoring up Republican candidates ahead of critical elections. The San Antonio event underscores the president's focus on energizing the GOP base in competitive regions.

For Colorado conservatives, Trump's rally circuit matters: the president's ground game and endorsements directly influence Republican turnout and candidate viability in swing districts across the state. Colorado Republicans will be watching whether Trump's star power translates to momentum in races where margins remain tight.
