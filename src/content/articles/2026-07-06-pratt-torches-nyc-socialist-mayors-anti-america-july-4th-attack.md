# Spencer Pratt Torches NYC Socialist Mayor's Anti-America July 4th Rant

Former Los Angeles mayoral candidate Spencer Pratt unleashed a viral social media firestorm after New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani used Independence Day to attack ICE and criticize American history, calling the socialist politician a "vile, commie" (Fox News, The Daily Wire).

Mamdani's July 4th remarks—which trashed the United States during what should have been a celebration of the nation's 250th anniversary—drew swift backlash from conservatives who see it as emblematic of the left's hostility toward American founding principles and national pride.

Pratt's counterattack resonated across X, reflecting broader Republican frustration with progressive leaders who use national holidays as platforms for anti-American grievance politics rather than unity. Conservatives argue that such rhetoric from elected officials corrodes civic cohesion and reveals the left's contempt for the country's heritage and institutions.

The exchange underscores a widening cultural divide: while Republicans defend American exceptionalism and traditional patriotism, progressives increasingly frame the nation's history through a lens of systemic failure and injustice.
