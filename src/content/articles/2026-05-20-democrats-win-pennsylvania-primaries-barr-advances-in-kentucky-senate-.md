# Democrats Advance in Pennsylvania, Kentucky Primaries; Barr Wins GOP Senate Race

Democrats secured primary victories across Pennsylvania and Kentucky on Tuesday, while a Trump-backed Republican won the Kentucky Senate primary in a race to succeed retiring Mitch McConnell.

In Kentucky, Charles Booker won the Democratic Senate primary, defeating Amy McGrath six years after McGrath narrowly beat him for the same seat, according to Decision Desk HQ. On the Republican side, Rep. Andy Barr, backed by Trump, is projected to win the GOP primary, putting him on track to replace McConnell, the longest-serving Senate leader in history.

Pennsylvania Democrats advanced several candidates in competitive House races. State Rep. Chris Rabb won the Democratic primary in the 3rd Congressional District, spanning parts of west and north Philadelphia, according to Decision Desk HQ. Former firefighter and union leader Bob Brooks won the 7th District Democratic primary and will face freshman Rep. Ryan Mackenzie (R-PA) in November—a matchup in one of the state's swingiest districts. Bucks County Commissioner Bob Harvie, backed by local Democratic Party establishment, defeated Lucia Simonelli in the 1st District primary and will take on incumbent Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick (R-PA).

Watch for whether Democratic gains in Pennsylvania's swing districts translate to November general election victories.
