# Trump's Hedged Bet Pays Off in South Carolina GOP Governor Race

Trump's rare double endorsement in South Carolina's Republican gubernatorial runoff—backing both Alan Wilson and Pamela Evette—didn't damage his political brand, as state Attorney General Wilson decisively won the nomination with roughly 65% of the vote (The Daily Wire).

The dual endorsement was an unusual move. Evette had secured Trump's initial backing after the first primary round, but Wilson surged in runoff polling, prompting the former president to cover both bases rather than flip his support (Just The News). The strategy appears to have worked: Wilson's commanding margin suggests Trump's endorsement—even split—carried enough weight to propel the attorney general past the lieutenant governor.

For Colorado Republicans, the South Carolina outcome offers a lesson in primary dynamics. Trump's willingness to hedge his bets shows he's willing to adapt tactics when frontrunners shift, a calculation that may influence how Colorado GOP candidates court his endorsement in upcoming races.

Republicans argue Trump's endorsement remains a powerful force in GOP primaries, though his decision to back both candidates raised questions about whether splitting support could backfire. The fact that Wilson won decisively suggests it didn't—but the tactic underscores Trump's pragmatism over ideological consistency.
