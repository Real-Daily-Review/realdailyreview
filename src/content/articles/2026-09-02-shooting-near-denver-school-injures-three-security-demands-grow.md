# Denver School Shooting Injures Three; Safety Concerns Mount at Colorado Schools

Another shooting near a Denver school underscores the urgent need for hardened security measures and swift law enforcement response—not more gun-control rhetoric disconnected from reality.

Three people were injured in a shooting near the Denver School of the Arts on Wednesday morning, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver. Details remain limited, but the incident adds to a troubling pattern of violence near Colorado educational facilities.

Democrats typically respond to such incidents by calling for stricter gun laws and ammunition restrictions. Republicans and conservatives counter that law-abiding gun owners aren't the problem; rather, schools need better physical security, armed resource officers, controlled access points, and rapid response protocols. Colorado already has some of the nation's strictest gun laws—yet shootings persist, suggesting enforcement and mental health intervention, not additional restrictions, should be the focus.

The shooting also highlights how Denver's progressive policies on crime and homelessness may be creating conditions that embolden criminal behavior. Weak prosecution and catch-and-release policies undermine public safety.

Colorado taxpayers and parents should demand that school boards prioritize hardened security over woke curriculum experiments, and that law enforcement be fully resourced to protect students and staff.

Watch for how Denver Public Schools and state education officials respond—and whether they'll embrace real security measures or retreat into calls for gun confiscation.
