# Colorado Voters Face Redistricting Guardrails, Rail Tax Hike This Fall

Colorado voters will decide whether to handcuff their own legislature with Initiative #256, which would ban congressional redistricting designed to favor either political party—a direct rebuke of partisan line-drawing that has defined American politics for decades (Complete Colorado).

The measure arrives as Front Range voters also face a hefty passenger rail tax hike, despite the documented failures of Colorado's existing commuter rail systems like RTD (Complete Colorado). Taxpayers are being asked to fund new rail infrastructure while the state's current transit investments languish.

Meanwhile, Colorado's elections infrastructure took a hit when the Secretary of State's Office accidentally exposed confidential voter information for over 130,000 citizens following a website update error (Denver Post). The breach underscores ongoing concerns about government data security and voter privacy.

On the political front, Latino support for President Trump has cratered in Colorado's competitive 8th Congressional District, with 61% disapproving, according to polling by the Colorado Sun—a potential headwind for Republicans in a swing seat.

**Watch closely:** whether Initiative #256 passes and whether Front Range voters approve another tax hike for a transit system with a track record of underperformance.
