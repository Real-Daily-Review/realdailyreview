# ICE Cracks Down on Criminal Aliens While Sanctuary States Block Deportations

The federal government is finally doing its job—arresting and deporting illegal immigrants convicted of serious crimes—while progressive states actively obstruct enforcement.

ICE operations this week netted illegal immigrants convicted of attempted murder, rape, and child sex crimes across multiple states (Fox News). Meanwhile, the Justice Department has sued Maryland over its "Community Trust Act," a sanctuary law that blocks ICE detainers and prevents deportations—arguing it violates federal supremacy (Fox News). The DOJ accuses Maryland of an "active and deliberate effort" to shield criminal aliens from removal.

The crackdown includes an illegal alien convicted of child rape who was previously pardoned by Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz, now deported by ICE (Breitbart). Mexico's government has already threatened legal action after an ICE agent fatally shot an illegal migrant during a traffic stop (Fox News).

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado's own sanctuary policies—including Denver's limits on ICE cooperation—mirror Maryland's approach. Taxpayers here should ask whether their cities are protecting criminal aliens over public safety.

Republicans argue sanctuary laws endanger citizens and undermine the rule of law. Democrats counter that such policies protect immigrant communities from deportation and build trust with law enforcement. The core dispute: whether state governments can nullify federal immigration authority.

Watch whether the Maryland lawsuit succeeds and whether Colorado cities face similar DOJ pressure.
