# Trump Renames Lake Ontario 'Lake America' in Escalating Canada Trade Fight

President Trump signed an executive order Thursday renaming Lake Ontario to "Lake America," a symbolic jab at Canada as trade tensions between the two nations intensify over tariffs and defense spending.

Trump announced the move from the Oval Office, telling reporters that "Canada has been ripping us off for a long time on trade" and criticized Ottawa's military contributions (Washington Examiner, The Daily Wire). The White House declared the name change "effective immediately" via social media.

The rename comes as the Trump administration pressures Canada on trade negotiations and defense commitments.
