# Colorado Family Grieves as Impaired Driving Tragedy Highlights Border Security Failures

A Greeley family is mourning the loss of a loved one in a fatal crash—a preventable tragedy that underscores why Colorado needs stricter enforcement against impaired driving and why border security matters.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, the crash claimed two lives, and the victim's daughter is now urging drivers to reconsider getting behind the wheel while impaired. It's a reasonable plea that resonates across political lines: drunk driving kills, and Colorado's roads deserve better.

But the broader picture is darker. The Daily Wire reports that a similar fatal crash in North Texas involved a previously deported illegal immigrant driving drunk in a stolen vehicle the wrong way down a highway, killing a young father named Kenan Ellis. The suspect, Alexander Nunez, 26, had already been deported—yet somehow remained in the country to cause this tragedy.

**Republicans argue** that lax border enforcement and inadequate deportation follow-through create preventable dangers for American families. When individuals with prior deportation orders remain free to drive drunk and steal cars, it's not a immigration-policy debate—it's a public safety failure.

Colorado taxpayers and families deserve roads where impaired drivers—whether citizens or illegal aliens—face swift, certain consequences. Watch whether state lawmakers push for tougher DUI enforcement and whether federal immigration authorities actually follow through on deportations.
