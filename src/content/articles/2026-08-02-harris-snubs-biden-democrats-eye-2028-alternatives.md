# Harris Distances Herself from Biden as Democrats Eye 2028 Field

Kamala Harris is already running from her own administration. When asked to name America's most effective presidents, the former vice president pointedly omitted Joe Biden—her boss for four years—and instead cited FDR, JFK, and LBJ (Daily Caller, Breitbart). The snub speaks volumes about the political liability Biden has become to Democrats heading into 2028.

Harris's dodge reveals the left's central problem: they can't defend their record. Four years of inflation, border chaos, and energy policy reversals have left Democrats scrambling for distance. Republicans argue Harris's silence on Biden is an admission that his presidency failed working families—and that she was complicit in those failures.

Meanwhile, a Michigan poll shows Democrats are already shopping alternatives. Pete Buttigieg, Jon Ossoff, and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez are testing better than Harris and California Governor Gavin Newsom in a hypothetical primary (New York Post). That's a stunning indictment of the party's current standard-bearer.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: Harris and the Democratic field have backed the same green-energy mandates, gun-control schemes, and regulatory overreach that have hamstrung Colorado's energy sector and threatened Second Amendment rights. Watch whether Harris pivots left or center as primary pressure mounts—her answer will signal how radical the 2028 Democratic platform becomes.
