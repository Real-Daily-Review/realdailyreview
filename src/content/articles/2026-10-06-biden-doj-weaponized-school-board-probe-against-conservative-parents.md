# Biden DOJ's School Board Probe Became Political Weapon, Internal Warnings Show

The Biden Justice Department weaponized federal law enforcement against parents opposing school board policies, according to internal DOJ warnings that went unheeded.

Attorney General Merrick Garland's 2021 memo directing the FBI to investigate threats against school officials metastasized into what Justice Department insiders themselves labeled an "Anti-MAGA Task Force," *Fox News* and the *Washington Examiner* report. One official warned the effort risked becoming a political operation targeting conservative parents rather than addressing genuine criminal threats.

The Trump administration's Weaponization Working Group released findings Monday documenting that Biden DOJ officials flagged the danger early—and were ignored. The memo, ostensibly about school board safety, became a pretext for federal surveillance of parents exercising First Amendment rights at public meetings.

**The conservative argument:** This exemplifies how progressive administrations weaponize federal agencies against political opponents. Parents attending school board meetings to oppose curriculum changes or mask mandates faced FBI scrutiny. Colorado parents who showed up to defend parental rights in education decisions should recognize this pattern: the federal government under Democratic leadership treats conservative activism as a threat requiring investigation.

**What's next:** Watch whether Colorado's congressional delegation demands accountability from the DOJ and whether state education officials commit to protecting parent participation without federal intimidation.
