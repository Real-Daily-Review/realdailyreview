# Speaker Johnson Gambles Defense Bill on Controversial SAVE America Rider

Speaker Mike Johnson is risking passage of a $1.15 trillion defense spending bill by tying it to the SAVE America Act—a move that could derail military funding over a contentious policy dispute. Johnson unveiled the strategy Monday as the House Rules Committee advanced the fiscal 2027 defense package, according to the *Washington Examiner*.

Republicans backing the maneuver argue the SAVE America Act addresses critical priorities that deserve floor consideration. Critics warn the tactic could jeopardize defense appropriations entirely, leaving military readiness vulnerable to gridlock.

The defense bill itself underscores a broader conservative argument: American military strength depends not just on headline weapons systems, but on thousands of specialized manufacturers maintaining supply chain resilience and industrial capacity. That lesson carries weight for Colorado, home to significant aerospace and defense contractors whose survival depends on stable, predictable federal spending.

**The stakes:** If Johnson's gambit fails and the defense bill stalls, Colorado defense workers and contractors face uncertainty heading into fiscal 2027. Conservatives should watch whether Democrats will negotiate or weaponize the defense bill as leverage—and whether Republicans have the votes to pass either measure separately if the combined approach collapses.
