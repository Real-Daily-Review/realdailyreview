# ICE Chief Challenges 'Abolish ICE' Crowd: Change the Law, Don't Demonize Agents

The progressive left's crusade to dismantle Immigration and Customs Enforcement is colliding with reality—even within Democratic ranks. NYC ICE chief Kenneth Genalo fired back this week at far-left politicians demanding the agency's abolition, telling them bluntly: if you want to change immigration enforcement, change the law (Fox News).

The pushback reveals a deepening fault line in the Democratic Party. Rep. Henry Cuellar (D-TX), a centrist who chairs the House Appropriations subcommittee overseeing ICE funding, has resisted the progressive wing's defund-and-abolish agenda, citing his border-district constituents' concerns about enforcement (Washington Examiner). Cuellar's resistance signals that even Democrats representing swing districts recognize the political toxicity of gutting immigration enforcement.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: open-borders rhetoric and anti-law-enforcement posturing undermine border security and fuel illegal immigration that strains state resources, labor markets, and public services. Republicans argue that dismantling ICE would create a lawless immigration system—exactly what the left wants, but what most Americans reject.

The real tell? When Democrats representing competitive districts won't touch the "abolish ICE" agenda, it's a tacit admission that the policy is electoral poison outside progressive strongholds.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation—particularly those in swing districts—distances itself from the abolish-ICE movement ahead of 2026.
