# Socialist Rent-Control Agenda Gains Ground in Democratic Party

A Bernie Sanders–backed democratic socialist just ousted Providence's incumbent mayor on a rent-control platform, signaling how far left the Democratic Party has drifted—and what Colorado Democrats may push next.

David Morales, 27, defeated Providence Mayor Brett Smiley by 8 points in Rhode Island's primary, running hard on "rent stabilization" and "housing justice" (Fox News). State Rep. Zohran Mamdani, celebrating the victory, framed it as proof of a national shift within the party toward socialist economics (Fox News).

**The conservative concern:** Rent control destroys housing supply and drives up costs for renters long-term—a lesson economists across the ideological spectrum understand. Yet Morales's win shows Democrats are embracing failed 1970s-style price controls as mainstream policy.

**Colorado angle:** Progressive activists in Denver and Boulder have already pushed rent-control proposals. If the Democratic Socialist model spreads nationally, expect Colorado's left-wing cities to follow Providence's lead, threatening Colorado's housing affordability and landlord property rights.

Republicans argue that Morales's victory exposes the Democratic Party's embrace of socialism over pragmatism. Conservatives warn voters to watch whether Colorado Democrats adopt similar rent-control schemes in 2026—and to remember that government price-fixing never delivers cheaper housing.

Watch whether Colorado's Democratic legislature moves to preempt local rent-control bans or funds studies on statewide rent stabilization.
