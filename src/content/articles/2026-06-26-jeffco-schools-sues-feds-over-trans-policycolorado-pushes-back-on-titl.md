# Jeffco Schools Authorized to Sue Feds Over Trans Policy Overreach

The Jefferson County Schools board has greenlit the district to file lawsuits against the federal government after the Office for Civil Rights launched an investigation into alleged Title IX violations—a move that exposes the collision between Biden-era gender ideology and parental rights in Colorado's second-largest school district.

The OCR complaint centers on the district's policies regarding overnight accommodations and shared locker rooms for transgender students. Rather than capitulate to federal pressure, Jeffco's board chose litigation, signaling that Colorado parents and school leaders are increasingly willing to fight Washington's gender mandates in court.

Republicans and conservatives argue that Title IX—enacted to protect women's sports opportunities—has been weaponized to erase sex-based distinctions and compromise girls' privacy and safety. They contend the federal government is overstepping its authority by imposing gender ideology on local schools without legislative backing. Democrats counter that Title IX protections must extend to transgender students to prevent discrimination.

The decision reflects growing frustration among Colorado school boards with federal overreach. Jeffco joins a broader pattern of states and districts challenging Washington's interpretation of civil rights law—much like the 25 state attorneys general now suing the EPA over environmental regulations (Washington Examiner).
