# Colorado Descendants of Thomas Jefferson to Address Family Divide at History Museum

Former state Rep. Sal Pace and actor Shannon LaNier—both descendants of Thomas Jefferson—will speak Wednesday at the Colorado History Museum about reconciliation between two branches of the Jefferson family fractured by historical divisions (Colorado Sun).

The event comes as national debate intensifies over how Americans reckon with the Founding Fathers' legacies. While progressives push revisionist framings of early American history, conservatives like Gov. Ron DeSantis argue for historical accuracy and context—noting that figures like Jefferson operated within the realities of their era, not modern moral standards (Breitbart).

For Colorado, the conversation matters. The state's civic institutions increasingly host forums on American history and identity. Conservatives should insist these venues present balanced perspectives: acknowledging historical complexity without surrendering to the left's tendency to weaponize the past against present-day Americans and institutions.

Pace and LaNier's focus on "building bridges" between family branches offers a model—one rooted in understanding rather than condemnation. That's a conversation worth having.
