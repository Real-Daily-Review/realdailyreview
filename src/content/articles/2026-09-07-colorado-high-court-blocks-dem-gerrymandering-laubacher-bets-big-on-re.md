# Democrat Bet Big on Flipping Colorado's Reddest Seat—But State High Court Just Blocked Their Redistricting Scheme

Colorado Democrats are pouring resources into Eileen Laubacher's long-shot bid to flip one of the state's most reliably Republican districts, where the GOP holds a commanding 2-to-1 voter advantage (Colorado Sun). But their broader strategy to reshape the political map just hit a wall: Colorado's high court shut down a Democrat gerrymandering plan, keeping the state out of the national redistricting wars—at least for now (Complete Colorado).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that Democrats' failed redistricting gambit would have redrawn district lines to manufacture Democratic seats, undermining the will of Colorado voters in red strongholds. The court's decision protects the integrity of districts where Republicans have earned decisive majorities through legitimate voter preference, not cartography.

Laubacher's campaign in this ruby-red district relies on money and ground game to overcome structural headwinds—a sign Democrats recognize they cannot simply legislate their way to victory. Conservatives should watch whether this high-court ruling holds firm and whether Democrats attempt end-runs through other redistricting tactics before the next cycle.
