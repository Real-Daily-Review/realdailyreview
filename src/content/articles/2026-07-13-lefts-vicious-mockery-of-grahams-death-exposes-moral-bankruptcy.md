# Colorado Should Reject the Left's Vicious Rhetoric on Display This Week

Senator Lindsey Graham's sudden death at 71 has sparked an ugly display of the left's contempt for political opponents. Comedian Margaret Cho mocked Graham's passing and wished the same fate on Mitch McConnell (Breitbart), while a California-registered nurse faced backlash for social media posts wishing death on both Graham and President Trump (New York Post).

Vice President JD Vance eulogized Graham as "one of a kind figure in our politics," offering the dignity Graham deserved regardless of policy disagreements.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado conservatives should note this pattern. When the left abandons civility and basic human decency toward Republican leaders—even in death—it reveals the stakes of our political moment. Graham, whatever one's views on his record, was a U.S. senator and a man. The gleeful mockery from progressive celebrities and medical professionals exposes a moral bankruptcy that Colorado voters should reject at the ballot box.

Republicans argue the left's dehumanizing rhetoric toward political opponents has poisoned our discourse and made compromise impossible. Democrats rarely condemn such statements from their own side, suggesting tacit approval.

Coloradans should watch whether national Democratic leaders distance themselves from these comments—or remain silent, as they so often do.
