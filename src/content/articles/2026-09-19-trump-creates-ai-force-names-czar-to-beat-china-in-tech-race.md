# Trump Launches 'AI Force,' Names Czar to Keep America Ahead in Tech Race

President Trump is betting big on artificial intelligence—announcing a new "AI Force" and an incoming "AI czar" appointment to keep America competitive against China and position the U.S. as the global AI leader (Fox News, Daily Caller). Trump framed the move as essential: AI could represent up to 25% of GDP and will be "the next Industrial Revolution," he wrote on Truth Social, adding that "only High I.Q. individuals need apply" for the czar role (Just The News).

The announcement mirrors Trump's first-term creation of the Space Force and signals his administration's intent to protect and accelerate domestic AI development rather than impose the regulatory restrictions Democrats favor. While some tech industry titans have urged caution on AI expansion (Daily Caller), Trump's approach prioritizes market-driven innovation and American dominance over the precautionary regulation pushed by progressives.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado's tech and aerospace sectors—already home to significant AI research and defense contracting—could benefit from federal support for AI infrastructure and talent recruitment, though details on funding and implementation remain sparse.

**Watch next:** Whether Trump's AI czar appointment includes Colorado-based tech leaders and what regulatory relief the administration offers to accelerate AI development without Washington micromanagement.
