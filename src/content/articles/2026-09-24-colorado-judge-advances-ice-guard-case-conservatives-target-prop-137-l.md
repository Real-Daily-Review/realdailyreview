# Ex-ICE Guard Faces Trial; Conservatives Warn Against Prop 137 Land Grab

A Colorado judge has ruled sufficient evidence exists to prosecute Brandon Booth, an ex-detention center guard accused of firing at protesters who confronted him about "race traitors" and threatened to locate guards' children, according to the *Colorado Sun*. The case underscores tensions around immigration enforcement facilities in the state.

Meanwhile, conservatives are mounting a full-court press against **Proposition 137**, Colorado's November ballot measure that would dedicate tax dollars to land conservation. *Complete Colorado* warns the initiative amounts to a backdoor subsidy for wealthy nonprofits—particularly The Nature Conservancy, the measure's primary sponsor—at taxpayer expense.

The Prop 137 critique hits a nerve for Colorado conservatives: state government would effectively funnel public money to private environmental groups under the guise of land stewardship. Republicans argue this represents classic progressive mission creep—using government power and taxpayer dollars to advance an ideological agenda while enriching connected special interests.

For Colorado landowners, ranchers, and property-rights advocates, Prop 137 signals another round of regulatory overreach dressed up as conservation. The measure threatens to expand government control over private land use decisions under environmental pretexts.

**Watch for:** Election Day messaging on Prop 137 and whether conservatives can mobilize rural and suburban voters against the land-control measure before November.
