# Atlanta Mayor Blasts DNC for Snubbing South—and Denver Could Be Next Stop for Party Infighting

Atlanta's Democratic mayor is publicly accusing the Democratic National Committee of "abandoning" Black voters and the South after his city lost out on hosting the 2028 convention. The DNC's three finalists—Boston, Denver, and Philadelphia (per *Just The News*)—notably exclude any Southern city, prompting the mayor to argue the party is "unwilling to do the bare minimum" for its base.

This Democratic civil war matters for Colorado. Denver is now in the running to host the 2028 convention, which means hosting costs, security expenses, and disruption to the city. Conservatives should ask: What's the real price tag to Denver taxpayers? The DNC's selection process reveals the party's internal fractures over regional priorities and racial politics—hardly a sign of unified leadership heading into 2028.

Republicans will likely use this footage of Democratic infighting to argue the left is more focused on internal grievances than governing. Meanwhile, Colorado Democrats should prepare for the scrutiny and expense that comes with a national convention on their doorstep.

Watch whether Denver's Democratic leadership commits to the convention bid and what guarantees they'll demand from the DNC on cost-sharing.
