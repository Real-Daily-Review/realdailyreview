# Trump Blasts Cornyn Over Federal Judge Block; Warns of 'Radical Left' Replacement

President Trump is publicly feuding with Republican Sen. John Cornyn over the Texas Republican's blockade of DOJ lawyer Elliot Gaiser's nomination to a federal judgeship in Dallas, calling the move a "tremendous disservice" (Fox News).

Trump argues that Cornyn's obstruction will hand the seat to a "radical left lunatic" instead—a warning that resonates with Colorado conservatives wary of judicial activism on Second Amendment rights, property rights, and regulatory overreach. Federal judges shape everything from gun rights to energy permitting to water law, making judicial appointments a top-tier issue for the Right.

The dispute underscores a broader Republican tension: Trump-backed judicial picks face resistance from establishment GOP senators who prefer their own candidates or harbor other concerns. Cornyn hasn't publicly detailed his objections, leaving the real reason for the block unclear (New York Post).

For Colorado Republicans, this fight matters. Federal judges in the Tenth Circuit—which covers Colorado—have already blocked or delayed conservative energy projects and Second Amendment challenges. A "radical left" judge could accelerate that trend, tightening regulations on oil and gas development and weakening gun rights in the Mountain West.
