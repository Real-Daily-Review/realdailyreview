# Real Daily Review — The Brief

Colorado voters handed Democrats supermajorities in both chambers of the legislature last week — but they also sent a clear message on protecting kids, and Jefferson County taxpayers are pushing back on a school spending grab. Here's what conservatives need to know this morning.

## Protect Kids Colorado Wins Big — Even as Democrats Tighten Their Grip

Protect Kids Colorado is declaring victory on a trio of ballot measures aimed at safeguarding children, a notable conservative win in an otherwise rough election cycle for the right. The irony is hard to miss: Colorado voters approved these protections at the same moment they handed Democrats supermajorities in both the House and Senate. Republicans should be clear-eyed — those supermajorities mean the left now has the runway to push through virtually any legislation it wants in 2027. Coloradans who voted for these measures had better watch what comes next from the Capitol.

## Jeffco School District's $73 Million Tax Hike Hanging by 5 Votes

Jefferson County Public Schools' 5A mill levy override — a $73 million spending measure — is headed for a recount after failing by just 5 votes. A second measure, 5B, is failing by a wider margin. Colorado taxpayers should understand what a mill levy override actually is: a property tax hike. Jeffco homeowners and small business owners are already squeezed by rising costs, and conservatives argue that school districts need to demonstrate fiscal discipline before asking taxpayers to dig deeper. Five votes is razor-thin — this one isn't over.
