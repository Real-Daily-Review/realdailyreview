# Iran's Energy Threats Could Spike US Oil Prices—and Colorado's Gas Pump

Escalating tensions between the U.S., Israel, and Iran over potential military strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure could send shockwaves through Colorado's economy and energy sector. According to *Just The News*, Iran is reportedly threatening to retaliate by targeting American and Israeli energy infrastructure if attacked. Meanwhile, the *Washington Examiner* reports the U.S. and Israel are preparing strikes against Iranian energy facilities as soon as this weekend, following President Trump's signals of readiness for full-scale military action.

**The Colorado angle:** Any disruption to global oil supplies typically drives up gas prices at the pump—hitting Colorado drivers and businesses hard. Colorado's energy sector, already navigating regulatory headwinds from state Democrats, could face additional market volatility. Conservatives argue that energy security demands American energy independence, making Colorado's oil and gas development critical to national resilience rather than a liability to be regulated away.

Republicans counter that weakening U.S. energy production while tensions with Iran escalate leaves America vulnerable to foreign supply shocks and price spikes—exactly the scenario Colorado progressives should fear when they push to restrict domestic drilling.

**Watch:** Whether military action materializes this weekend and how global oil markets respond—Colorado taxpayers and workers should monitor gas prices and energy stock volatility closely.
