# Border Chaos Reaches College Campuses: DOJ Sues States Giving Illegal Aliens In-State Tuition

Colorado taxpayers should brace for a potential legal battle—the Justice Department is cracking down on states offering in-state college tuition to illegal aliens, and Colorado may be next.

The DOJ has now sued four additional states (Arizona, New Mexico, Oregon, and Washington) over policies granting illegal aliens cheaper tuition rates, joining 17 states already in litigation (Breitbart). The legal offensive targets what conservatives argue is a backdoor subsidy rewarding illegal immigration at the expense of American citizens and legal residents paying full freight.

Meanwhile, border enforcement failures continue making headlines. Three illegal aliens arrested in a major Virginia child exploitation sting were released on bond after DHS confirmed they had been previously caught illegally entering the U.S. and released during the Obama administration (New York Post). In a separate Virginia case, a DACA recipient faces over 20 counts of child pornography possession and distribution (Breitbart).

Republicans argue these cases expose the reckless consequences of sanctuary policies and catch-and-release practices. Democrats counter that such cases represent isolated incidents and that immigration enforcement should focus on serious criminals.
