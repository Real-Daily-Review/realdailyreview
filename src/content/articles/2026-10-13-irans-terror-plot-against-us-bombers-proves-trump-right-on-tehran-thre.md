# Iran's Terror Plot Against U.S. Bombers in UK Shows Why America Can't Let Its Guard Down

Britain has officially blamed Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps for a foiled terror plot targeting RAF Fairford, the British airbase hosting American B-1 bombers—vindicating President Trump's earlier assessment that Tehran was behind the scheme. The security threat forced the U.S. to withdraw all 12 bombers from the base, according to *Breitbart* and *Just The News*.
