# Trump Flies Older Air Force One Back to D.C. While New Jet Tours NATO Allies

President Trump is bypassing the newly built Air Force One and instead flying the older aircraft back to Washington, D.C., while the replacement jet heads to Mildenhall Air Force Base in the United Kingdom for NATO troops to tour following the alliance summit (Fox News Politics).

Trump's decision reflects his preference for the legacy aircraft and underscores the ongoing delays and costs associated with the new Air Force One program—a project that has ballooned in expense and timeline over recent years. The move allows the new plane to serve a diplomatic purpose abroad while keeping the president's immediate travel needs met with the proven, existing platform.

Meanwhile, tensions with Iran are heating up again. According to National Review, the Iranian regime appears to be reigniting confrontation with the U.S., potentially testing Trump's resolve after earlier diplomatic overtures. The outlet notes that what Tehran once characterized as "very rational people" in negotiations may now be miscalculating American willingness to tolerate provocation.
