# NYC Mayor Dismantles Elite Police Unit—A Warning for Colorado Cities Tempted by Soft-on-Crime Politics

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has officially disbanded the NYPD's Strategic Response Group, an elite counterterrorism unit, in a move that police unions say endangers public safety. According to Fox News and Breitbart, law enforcement leaders are condemning the decision as reckless.

The unit specialized in counterterrorism and rapid response to high-threat situations. Police union officials argue the disbanding removes critical capabilities from the department and leaves the city more vulnerable to serious crime and terror threats.

If elected officials in Colorado's major metros embrace similar logic, they risk repeating New York's mistakes.
