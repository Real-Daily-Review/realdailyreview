# Iran Weighs U.S. Proposal as Trump Threatens Renewed Strikes

President Trump said his administration has had productive talks with Iran over the last 48 hours and that a deal could be within reach, according to CBS News. Iran's Foreign Ministry is reviewing a U.S. proposal and will relay a response through mediator Pakistan, NPR reported.

However, Trump has injected caution into negotiations. He posted on social media threatening Iran with more attacks if it doesn't agree to a deal, CBS News reported. The BBC noted Trump himself has tempered hopes for an Iran peace deal, even as there are signs of fresh momentum to end the war.

Pakistan, acting as mediator, said it is "endeavouring to convert this ceasefire into a permanent end to this war," according to BBC World. The Hill observed that time has become a weapon for both sides, with each believing it can outlast the other—a dynamic that complicates prospects for agreement.

Iran has not yet formally responded to the latest U.S. proposal. Negotiators are still assessing the terms, according to NPR.

Watch for Iran's formal response to the U.S. proposal and whether Trump's threats accelerate or derail ongoing mediation efforts.
