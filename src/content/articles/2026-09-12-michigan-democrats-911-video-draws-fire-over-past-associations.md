# Michigan Democrat's 9/11 Messaging Raises Questions About Past Associations

A Michigan Democratic Senate candidate's carefully crafted 9/11 tribute video is drawing scrutiny over his past association with commentators who have made inflammatory statements about the terrorist attacks.

Abdul El-Sayed, running to become the first Muslim U.S. Senator, released a somber video Friday honoring 9/11 victims—a marked departure from previous commentary, according to *The Daily Wire*. The timing and tone shift prompted backlash after El-Sayed shared the message alongside content from Hasan Piker, a streamer who has claimed America deserved the 9/11 attacks, *Fox News* reports.

Republicans argue the contrast between El-Sayed's current messaging and his past associations raises legitimate questions about his actual views on American security and the war on terror. Conservatives note that political figures should be held accountable for the company they keep and the rhetoric they amplify, especially on matters as serious as terrorism.

Democrats have not publicly addressed the specific concerns about El-Sayed's associations, though his campaign released the video as an apparent attempt to demonstrate respect for 9/11 victims.
