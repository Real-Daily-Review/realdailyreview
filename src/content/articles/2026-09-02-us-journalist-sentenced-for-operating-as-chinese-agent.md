# American Journalist Sentenced for Serving as Chinese Agent

A U.S. journalist and political commentator has been handed a two-year prison sentence for operating as an undisclosed agent of a foreign government—a stark reminder that Beijing's influence operations extend into American media and commentary.

Thomas Pauken II, who has lived in China since 2010 and worked for several state media organizations there, was convicted of acting as "an agent of a foreign government" while in the United States. According to *Just The News* and the *Washington Examiner*, prosecutors had sought a 33-month term, while the defense argued for probation or a halfway-house sentence. The judge split the difference at 24 months.

The case underscores a troubling reality: foreign adversaries are actively recruiting Americans in media and commentary roles to shape U.S. public opinion without disclosure. Pauken's work in Chinese state media while maintaining a presence in American political discourse exemplifies the kind of covert influence operation that should alarm policymakers and citizens alike.
