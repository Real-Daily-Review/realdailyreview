# Harris's 2028 Hopes Evaporate as Democrats Pivot Left

Kamala Harris's political stock has cratered so fast that she's already yesterday's news in the 2028 Democratic primary race—and that's bad news for moderates hoping to steer the party back toward the center.

According to the *Washington Examiner*, Harris led early Democratic primary polls just weeks ago, but her standing has collapsed as newly reelected Sen. Gavin Newsom (D-CA) and other progressive figures muscle into the field. The shift reflects Democrats' leftward lurch and Harris's inability to build a durable political brand after a disastrous 2024 campaign.

For Colorado Republicans, this matters. Harris's fade signals the Democratic Party is doubling down on the progressive agenda that alienated swing voters nationwide—higher taxes, aggressive climate mandates, and regulatory overreach. If the 2028 Democratic nominee emerges from the party's activist wing rather than its establishment, expect even more radical proposals on energy policy, gun control, and federal land grabs that directly threaten Colorado's economy and way of life.

Harris's collapse also underscores a hard truth: the Democratic bench is thin, and the party's future belongs to its most ideological wing. Colorado voters should watch whether any credible moderate Democrat emerges to challenge the progressive takeover—or whether the party's 2028 nominee will make Harris look like a moderate by comparison.
