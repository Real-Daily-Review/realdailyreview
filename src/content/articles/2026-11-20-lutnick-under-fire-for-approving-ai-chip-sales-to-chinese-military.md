# Commerce Secretary Lutnick Faces Fire Over Nvidia China Export Approval

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is standing firm despite mounting Republican scrutiny over his handling of advanced AI chip exports to Chinese military-linked firms. According to House Foreign Affairs Committee records released Thursday (Breitbart), the Commerce Department approved sales of more than 879,000 advanced AI chips to organizations the Pentagon designated as Chinese military companies—some connected to Nvidia's former CEO through a reported affair with a Chinese executive.

**The conservative concern:** Republicans argue the approvals represent a catastrophic failure of export controls at a moment when U.S. technological dominance over China is a national security priority. The House committee's disclosure suggests bureaucratic negligence or worse—allowing cutting-edge American AI capabilities to reach Beijing's military apparatus while Lutnick allegedly dismissed warnings about the arrangement (New York Post).

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado hosts significant defense contracting and aerospace operations. Weakened export enforcement undermines the security posture these industries depend on and signals regulatory confusion that could chill legitimate tech investment in the state.

Trump has backed Lutnick despite the backlash, but conservatives should watch whether congressional Republicans demand a full accounting of which other sensitive technologies may have slipped through Commerce Department approval under similar circumstances.
