# Trump Cabinet Secretary Faces Court Challenge Over Religious Emails While Another Dodges Iranian Threats

A Biden-appointed federal judge has blocked Agriculture Secretary Brooke Rollins from sending religious messages to USDA employees, ruling that Easter and Christmas emails likely violated the Constitution (The Daily Wire). Judge Trina L. Thompson of the Northern District of California granted a preliminary injunction against messages including "He is risen" and holiday greetings, claiming they crossed the line between personal faith and official government communication.

Conservatives argue the ruling exemplifies judicial overreach into free speech and religious expression. A Cabinet secretary's personal faith statements to staff shouldn't trigger constitutional violations, they contend—especially when employees can simply delete emails. The decision reflects the broader progressive assault on religious speech in public institutions, critics say.

Meanwhile, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent relocated to a military base after Iranian officials reportedly escalated assassination threats to an advanced stage (Fox News). The move underscores the real security threats facing Trump administration officials from hostile foreign actors.

**Colorado angle:** USDA decisions directly affect Colorado ranchers, farmers, and rural communities. Rollins' leadership on grazing permits, water rights, and agricultural policy matters to the state's agricultural economy—and now her ability to communicate freely with staff faces judicial constraints.
