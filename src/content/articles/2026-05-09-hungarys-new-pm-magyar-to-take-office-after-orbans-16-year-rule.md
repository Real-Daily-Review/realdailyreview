# Hungary's New PM to Take Office After Orbán's 16-Year Reign Ends

Péter Magyar is set to be sworn in as Hungary's prime minister following his Tisza party's landslide victory nearly a month ago, ending Viktor Orbán's 16-year grip on power, according to BBC World.

Magyar's government intends to pursue swift systemic changes, Deutsche Welle reports, signaling a sharp departure from the Orbán era. The incoming administration has framed the transition as a "regime change," underscoring the scale of the political shift.

The election result represents a decisive rejection of Orbán's long tenure, though specifics on Magyar's policy agenda remain limited in available reporting. The new government's stated focus on rapid institutional reform suggests potential changes across Hungary's governance structures, though the scope and timeline of those reforms have not been detailed.
