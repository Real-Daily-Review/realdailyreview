# Democrats' Open-Border Policies Have Real Consequences—Ask Virginia

Virginia's Democratic leadership is learning a brutal lesson about the cost of sanctuary policies. After Gov. Abigail Spanberger rolled back the state's 287(g) immigration enforcement agreement with ICE, the Department of Homeland Security says the move created a "hotbed" for illegal immigrant crime—including the arrest of an illegal alien accused of sexually assaulting and murdering a 2-year-old girl in Louisa County (Breitbart).

The case underscores a pattern conservatives have warned about for years: when Democratic officials prioritize sanctuary policies over public safety, Americans—especially the most vulnerable—pay the price. Republicans argue that Spanberger's decision to weaken cooperation with federal immigration enforcement directly enabled criminals to operate with impunity.

Adding insult to injury, Fox News reports that an illegal immigrant whom a top Democrat fought to free is now registered to vote in Connecticut, despite being under a final deportation order since 1997. The man falsely claimed citizenship to register—a "mockery of our immigration laws," as critics put it.

Colorado conservatives should pay close attention: as progressive governors and mayors across the country double down on sanctuary policies, the question isn't whether similar tragedies could happen here—it's whether state and local leaders will prioritize border security and public safety over ideological posturing.
