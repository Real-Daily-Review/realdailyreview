# Trump's Swamp-Draining Promises Collide With Reality in Second Term

President Trump's pledge to dismantle government cronyism is facing early scrutiny from his own conservative base, with critics arguing his administration is repeating the very corporate-capture playbook he promised to end.

The Daily Caller reports Trump is building what amounts to an "AI empire" through government connections—a far cry from the anti-establishment message that fueled his 2024 comeback. Meanwhile, the Washington Examiner's opinion section flags Trump's FDA pick as a betrayal of his "Make America Healthy Again" agenda, suggesting the administration is already compromising with the pharmaceutical and regulatory establishment it vowed to challenge.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters: crony capitalism and regulatory capture directly inflate costs for energy development, agriculture, and small business. If Trump's second term devolves into backroom deals between government and corporate giants, Colorado's independent operators and taxpayers lose leverage against federal overreach.

Republicans and Trump supporters argue the president must deliver on his core promise—real structural reform, not just new faces managing the same broken system. Failure to do so risks fracturing the coalition that elected him.

Watch whether Trump's cabinet picks and early policy moves actually challenge entrenched interests or simply shuffle the deck chairs.
