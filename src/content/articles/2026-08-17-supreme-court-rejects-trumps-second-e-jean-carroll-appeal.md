# Supreme Court Closes Door on Trump's E. Jean Carroll Appeal—Again

The Supreme Court has declined to reconsider its refusal to hear President Trump's appeal of the $5 million civil judgment in the E. Jean Carroll defamation case, marking the second rejection of his legal challenge (Washington Examiner, Just The News).

Trump's legal team had petitioned the justices to reconsider their June decision rejecting his initial appeal. The Court's latest order, published Monday, offered no explanation—a standard practice when the high court denies such requests, which it "almost never" grants (Just The News).

The verdict, handed down by a New York jury, found Trump liable for sexual abuse and defamation related to Carroll's 1990s allegations. Trump has consistently denied the claims and characterized the case as politically motivated.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans argue the case reflects the weaponization of the courts against a political opponent, particularly given the jurisdiction and jury composition in New York—a heavily Democratic state. They contend the civil standard of proof differs markedly from criminal proceedings, and that Trump's legal options have now been substantially exhausted at the highest court level.

The ruling stands, and Trump faces potential additional liability in a separate defamation case Carroll filed. Conservatives should monitor whether this judgment influences other pending litigation or becomes a template for similar civil suits against political figures.
