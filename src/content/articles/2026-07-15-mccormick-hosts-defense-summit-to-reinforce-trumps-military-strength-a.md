# Defense Summit Showcases Trump's Peace-Through-Strength Approach to Military Innovation

Sen. Dave McCormick (R-PA) hosted hundreds of defense contractors at Pennsylvania's Defense and Innovation Summit this week, signaling Republican commitment to strengthening U.S. military capability through private-sector partnerships. The gathering at the Army War College near Harrisburg brought together companies seeking to collaborate with the Department of Defense on next-generation warfighting technologies—a direct alignment with President Trump's "peace through strength" doctrine.

McCormick told Breitbart News the summit exists to "reinforce" Trump's defense agenda, emphasizing that American military superiority depends on robust public-private partnerships and innovation. Republicans argue this approach—leveraging competitive markets and entrepreneurial energy rather than bloated government procurement bureaucracies—delivers better capabilities faster and at lower cost to taxpayers.

The summit reflects conservative thinking on defense: maintain overwhelming military advantage, streamline acquisition processes, and trust industry innovation over Pentagon red tape. This contrasts sharply with progressive calls for defense budget cuts and diversion of resources to social spending.

**What conservatives should watch:** Whether this momentum translates into actual procurement reforms that cut waste and accelerate capability delivery—or if Pentagon bureaucracy continues to bog down the process.
