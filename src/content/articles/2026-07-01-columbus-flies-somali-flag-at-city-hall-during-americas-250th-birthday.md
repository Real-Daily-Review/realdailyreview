# Columbus Flies Somali Flag at City Hall While America Marks 250 Years

As America celebrates its 250th birthday this July Fourth weekend, Columbus, Ohio—the capital of a red state—chose to raise the Somali flag at city hall instead, sparking fierce conservative backlash over misplaced priorities.

The city hoisted the flag to mark Somali Independence Day, a decision that drew immediate criticism from conservatives who see it as tone-deaf symbolism during America's milestone anniversary. "Excuse me?" was the refrain echoing across right-leaning outlets (Fox News Politics, The Daily Wire), with critics questioning why a U.S. city government would elevate a foreign nation's flag over American patriotism during the nation's 250th celebration.

Republicans argue the move reflects a broader pattern of progressive local governments prioritizing identity politics and multiculturalism over national unity and American exceptionalism. Conservatives contend that city resources and official platforms should center American values, especially during a historic moment for the nation.
