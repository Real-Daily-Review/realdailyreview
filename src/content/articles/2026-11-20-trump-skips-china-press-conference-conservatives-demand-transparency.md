# Trump Skips Press Conference After China Talks—Conservatives Want Answers on Dissident Pressure

President Trump concluded his China trip without holding the customary press conference, leaving questions unanswered about his meetings with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin, according to CPR News. The absence of formal questioning is unusual for presidential foreign visits and has drawn scrutiny over what was discussed behind closed doors.

National Review argues Trump should have used the diplomatic moment to press Xi on the release of democracy activist Joshua Wong, imprisoned for meeting with American lawmakers. Conservatives say such high-stakes summits demand public accountability—especially when human rights and U.S. interests abroad are at stake.

The lack of transparency fuels concern among Republicans that critical negotiations over trade, Taiwan, and strategic competition with China may have occurred without the American public hearing directly from the president about outcomes or commitments made. For Colorado businesses dependent on stable U.S.-China trade relations and energy markets, clarity on Trump's negotiating position matters.

Taxpayers and conservatives should watch whether Trump holds that delayed press conference and what details emerge about commitments made to Beijing—particularly on energy policy and supply chains affecting Colorado's economy.
