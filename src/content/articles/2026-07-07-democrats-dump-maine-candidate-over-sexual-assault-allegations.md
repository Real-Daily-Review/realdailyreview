# Democrats Distance Themselves From Maine Candidate Facing Sexual Assault Allegations

Democratic leadership is rapidly abandoning Maine Senate candidate Graham Platner after serious allegations surfaced that he broke into a woman's house and sexually assaulted her, according to reporting from the Daily Caller and Breitbart.

Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer and Senator Kirsten Gillibrand issued a joint statement calling the allegations "incredibly disturbing" and demanding Platner "immediately withdraw" from the race (New York Post). Other prominent Democrats, including Elizabeth Warren, Bernie Sanders, and Ruben Gallego, have similarly distanced themselves from the candidate, the Daily Caller reports.

The speed of the Democratic retreat underscores how toxic the allegations have become for the party heading into the general election. While Democrats have long positioned themselves as champions of victims' rights and accountability, the Platner case tests that messaging when the accused is one of their own.
