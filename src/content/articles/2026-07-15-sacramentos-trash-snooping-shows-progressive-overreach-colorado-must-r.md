# Government Trash Snooping Shows Why Coloradans Should Fear Progressive Overreach

Sacramento is sending city workers to rifle through residents' garbage bins—all in the name of climate compliance. California's SB 1383, passed in 2016 and backed by Governor Newsom, mandates organic waste reduction. Now the state's capital is enforcing it by having government employees inspect private trash, *The Daily Wire* reports.

This is what happens when progressives weaponize environmental policy. Sacramento residents didn't vote for garbage inspectors; they got them anyway through regulatory mandate. The city frames it as climate action. Conservatives see it for what it is: government intrusion into private property and daily life, justified by an unelected bureaucracy's interpretation of a law most voters never debated.

Colorado should take note. As the state leans further left, similar schemes—waste audits, utility monitoring, property inspections—become more likely. Denver and Boulder have already embraced aggressive climate mandates. If Sacramento's trash-snooping spreads westward, Colorado homeowners and renters could face the same invasive oversight.

The lesson: once government claims authority over your waste stream, it claims authority over your choices. And once that precedent is set, it rarely shrinks.

Watch whether Colorado cities adopt Sacramento-style waste enforcement or similar "climate compliance" inspection programs.
