# Trump Escalates Media Feud, Targets NBC's Welker Over Endorsement Claims

President Trump is escalating his war with legacy media, reporting NBC News anchor Kristen Welker to the Federal Communications Commission for what he calls misrepresentation of his 2026 endorsement record. In a Sunday Truth Social post, Trump demanded "rebuke and punishment" after Welker characterized his endorsements as having "mixed results"—a claim Trump disputes, citing a 100% success rate in Senate primaries and 98% in House races (Washington Examiner, Daily Wire).

**The conservative angle:** This clash reflects a broader Republican frustration with mainstream media's treatment of Trump ahead of the 2026 midterms. Conservatives argue the press consistently downplays Trump's political influence and distorts his record to weaken his standing with voters. The FCC complaint, however, faces long odds—the agency has limited authority over editorial content at cable news networks.

**Colorado context:** As Trump's endorsement power shapes Republican primary races nationwide, Colorado Republicans are watching closely. Trump-backed candidates could reshape the state's GOP delegation in 2026, particularly in competitive House districts. How media covers Trump's influence directly affects the narrative Colorado voters absorb about primary contenders.

**Watch next:** Whether the FCC takes any action, or if this becomes another flashpoint in Trump's media accountability push heading into the midterm cycle.
