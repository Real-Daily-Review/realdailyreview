# Trump DOJ Voter Roll Lawsuit Dismissed; Colorado Dodges Federal Data Demand

A federal judge has tossed the Trump administration's lawsuit demanding Colorado hand over unredacted voter registration data, sparing state election officials from surrendering sensitive personal information to Washington bureaucrats.

The Department of Justice claimed the request was part of a broader effort to ensure states properly maintain voter rolls and remove ineligible voters (Just The News). The Trump administration pursued similar cases against other states, including Washington D.C., where a judge also dismissed the DOJ's demand.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that voter roll maintenance is essential to election integrity—but they also recognize the danger of centralizing voter data in federal hands. Handing unredacted rolls to any administration creates privacy risks and sets a precedent for government overreach. Colorado's election system should remain under state and local control, not subject to federal data grabs regardless of which party holds the White House.

**What Colorado taxpayers should watch:** Whether the DOJ appeals this ruling or pivots to other pressure tactics on state election administration.
