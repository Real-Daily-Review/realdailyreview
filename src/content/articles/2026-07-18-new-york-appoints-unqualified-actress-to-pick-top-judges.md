# New York's Judicial Capture: Actress With No Legal Training Tapped to Pick Top Judges

New York just handed judicial selection to a celebrity activist with zero legal credentials, proving progressive politics now trumps competence in the Empire State.

Chief Judge Rowan Wilson appointed actress Cynthia Nixon—best known for "Sex and the City," not courtroom experience—to New York's Commission on Judicial Nomination. Nixon, a failed 2018 gubernatorial candidate and self-described leftist activist, has no law degree, no judicial background, and no business screening candidates for the state's highest court.

**Republicans and conservatives argue** this appointment exemplifies how progressive jurisdictions have abandoned merit-based governance. Placing an unqualified activist on a judicial selection panel signals that ideology matters more than legal expertise or constitutional fidelity. The New York Post editorial board called it a death knell for "rule of law" in the state—replaced by raw political calculation.

**Democrats haven't publicly defended the pick**, but the appointment reflects their broader push to reshape courts through political loyalty rather than judicial qualification.

**Colorado angle**: While Colorado's judicial selection process uses merit commissions with actual legal professionals, this New York debacle shows why conservatives must remain vigilant against similar "reform" efforts that could politicize state courts here.
