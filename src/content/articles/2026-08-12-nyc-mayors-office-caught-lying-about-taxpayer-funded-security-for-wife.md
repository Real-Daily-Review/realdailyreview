# NYC Mayor's Office Caught Fibbing on Taxpayer-Funded Security Detail

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's office initially claimed the NYPD would fund a security detail for his wife's Middle East vacation—until the police department flatly denied it, forcing a humiliating backtrack (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

Rama Duwaji was set to travel to Syria and Lebanon in September with what Mamdani's team suggested would be publicly funded protection. The NYPD quickly corrected the record, stating no such arrangement existed. Mamdani later confirmed his wife would travel without the taxpayer-funded detail.
