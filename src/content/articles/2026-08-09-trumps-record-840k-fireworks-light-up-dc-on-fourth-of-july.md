# Trump's Record Fireworks Display Draws Hundreds of Thousands to National Mall

President Trump's Fourth of July celebration on the National Mall set a new record—840,537 fireworks lit up Washington, D.C., making it officially the largest display in American history, according to Fox News and the New York Post.

Despite weather delays that forced temporary evacuations of spectators from the Mall, hundreds of thousands of Americans stayed to witness the "Freedom 250" show. The event underscored Trump's willingness to use federal resources for a high-profile patriotic display—a stark contrast to the Biden administration's more subdued approach to Independence Day celebrations in recent years.
