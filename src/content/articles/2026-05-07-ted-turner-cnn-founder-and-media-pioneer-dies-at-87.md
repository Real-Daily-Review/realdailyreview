# Ted Turner, CNN Founder and Media Pioneer, Dies at 87

Ted Turner, the media mogul who launched Cable News Network in 1980 and fundamentally reshaped television news, died on May 6 at age 87, according to BBC World and CBS News.

Turner pioneered the modern 24-hour news cycle when he created CNN, transforming how Americans consumed information. Beyond broadcasting, he built a sprawling media and sports empire, owning television and sports franchises while also competing in the America's Cup yacht races.

In his later years, Turner became known for environmental advocacy alongside controversial public statements. He faced Lewy body dementia, a progressive brain disorder, which affected him in his late seventies, according to CBS News.

Turner's career spanned decades of ambitious ventures—from sports ownership to conservation efforts—making him one of the most recognizable figures in American media history. His creation of CNN established a template for continuous news coverage that persists across the industry today.
