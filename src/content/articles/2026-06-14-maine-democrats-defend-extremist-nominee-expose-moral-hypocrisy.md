# Maine Democrats' Platner Problem Exposes Party's Moral Hypocrisy

Democrats are scrambling to defend Graham Platner, their Maine Senate nominee, after his troubling background—including Nazi-inspired tattoos and a history of concerning behavior—became public, undermining years of sanctimony about character and fitness for office.

The contradiction is stark. Hunter Biden, the embattled former First Son, has mounted an aggressive defense of Platner, challenging critics to reveal their own phone contents rather than address the substance of the allegations (The Daily Wire). Meanwhile, comedian Bill Maher has urged Maine voters to elect Platner *despite* calling his issues "scary"—a stunning admission that Democrats are willing to overlook serious red flags for partisan gain (New York Post).

The Denver Post's editorial board, typically aligned with Democratic causes, couldn't ignore the hypocrisy: "Democrats' moral double standard is being exposed in Maine as the party prepares to nominate Graham Platner, an extremist Senate candidate with more baggage than Denver International Airport on Thanksgiving weekend."

For Colorado conservatives, this is instructive. It reveals how the left's lectures on "character" and "decency" evaporate when power is at stake. Democrats spent years attacking Republican candidates over far lesser controversies while now defending a nominee with documented extremist ties.

Watch whether national Democratic leadership—including Colorado's own delegation—distances itself from Platner or doubles down on the defense.
