# Trump Admin Targets School Districts Over Woke Gender Policies—Colorado Schools Should Take Note

The Trump administration is making good on its promise to defund schools pushing radical gender ideology, threatening to withhold federal dollars from Kansas City Public Schools over its transgender policies (Fox News). The move signals a broader crackdown on progressive social engineering in K-12 education—and Colorado districts should pay attention.

Meanwhile, a Wisconsin high school blocked a graduating senior from displaying a Bible verse in her yearbook photo caption, sparking free speech concerns (New York Post). These incidents reflect a pattern: schools increasingly prioritize progressive orthodoxy over parental rights and religious expression.

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Taxpayer-funded schools should educate students in reading, math, and science—not indoctrinate them with contested gender ideology or suppress religious speech. When schools use federal dollars to advance woke agendas, the Trump administration's leverage is appropriate. Republicans argue that parents, not bureaucrats, should guide their children's values.

Democrats counter that such policies protect vulnerable transgender students from discrimination. The left frames these as civil rights issues.

**Colorado angle:** Denver Public Schools and other progressive districts have adopted similar gender policies. If the Trump administration follows through on defunding threats, Colorado taxpayers could see federal education dollars redirected or withheld—forcing local boards to choose between ideology and budgets.
