# Virginia Governor Blocks Education Tax Credit—A Warning for Colorado Parents

Virginia Gov. Abigail Spanberger just showed what happens when teacher unions call the shots: she blocked her state from participating in a new Federal Scholarship Tax Credit and refused to make K-12 resources available to families (Washington Examiner). The reason? Union pressure.

**The conservative argument:** Republicans and education-choice advocates say Spanberger prioritized union interests over parental freedom and student opportunity. The scholarship tax credit gives families more control over education dollars—exactly what limited-government conservatives support. Blocking it punishes Virginia families and signals that progressive Democrats remain beholden to public-sector unions, not taxpayers.

**Democrats counter** that union concerns center on protecting public school funding and preventing resource drain. But conservatives argue this is backwards: expanding choice strengthens education overall and lets parents—not bureaucrats—decide what works best for their kids.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado parents and taxpayers should watch whether similar education-choice initiatives face union opposition here. As school choice expands nationally, expect Colorado's powerful education establishment to fight parental empowerment at every turn. The Spanberger move is a preview of the playbook.

**Watch next:** Whether Colorado lawmakers embrace or block education-choice policies that give families real alternatives to government-run schools.
