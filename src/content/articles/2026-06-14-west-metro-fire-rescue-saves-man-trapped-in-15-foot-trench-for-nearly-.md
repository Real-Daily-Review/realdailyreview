# Colorado Rescue Crews Save Man Trapped in 15-Foot Trench for Nearly a Day

West Metro Fire Rescue crews pulled off a dramatic Saturday night rescue, extracting a man who had been trapped at the bottom of a 15-foot trench in Wheat Ridge—potentially for nearly 24 hours.

A passerby discovered the man in a construction zone near North Crossing Drive and Clear Creek Drive and alerted emergency responders (9News Denver). Fire Rescue crews responded and successfully freed him from the deep trench, according to KDVR Fox31 Denver.

Details on how the man became trapped or his condition following the rescue remain limited. The incident underscores both the dangers of unsecured construction sites and the critical importance of rapid emergency response—a reminder that Colorado's first responders continue to perform life-saving work in challenging conditions.
