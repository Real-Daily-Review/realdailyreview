# AI Industry Calls for Caution as Safety Concerns Mount

Silicon Valley's AI titans are pumping the brakes—at least rhetorically. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei warned that artificial intelligence could pose serious risks within six to 12 months, including the ability to orchestrate internet-wide attacks, according to 9News Denver. Amodei published an essay urging the industry to slow development and prioritize safety measures, a call that drew quick support from Sam Altman and Elon Musk (Breitbart).

The concern centers on whether safety protocols can keep pace with AI capability advances. Amodei told 9News he's "become convinced that fully addressing the risks requires even more prudence," signaling that even industry insiders acknowledge the technology is outpacing guardrails.

**The conservative angle:** While Colorado isn't a major AI hub, this matters for Colorado taxpayers and businesses. Runaway AI development funded by venture capital and government contracts could create systemic risks—from critical infrastructure vulnerabilities to job displacement in sectors Colorado depends on. Republicans should scrutinize whether federal AI regulation becomes a Trojan horse for tech-industry lobbying and crony capitalism disguised as safety.

The industry's self-regulation pledge rings hollow without transparency and real accountability. Watch whether Congress moves toward heavy-handed AI regulation that entrenches Big Tech incumbents while crushing Colorado startups and innovation.
