# Biden Classified Docs Scandal: Audio Tapes Undermine White House Narrative

Newly released recordings of Joe Biden and his memoir ghostwriter expose serious gaps in the president's classified documents defense—and raise fresh questions about accountability that Colorado Republicans say Washington refuses to answer.

Over 2.5 hours of audio obtained by the Oversight Project (per *The Daily Wire*) capture Biden in 2017 discussing documents he kept "downstairs," directly contradicting his public claims of ignorance about classified material stored in his basement and Delaware home. The recordings also reveal halting speech and memory struggles during sessions with ghostwriter Mark Zwonitzer, according to *Fox News Politics*.

Republicans argue the tapes demolish Biden's core defense: that he was unaware of classified documents in his possession. The audio evidence suggests deliberate knowledge—a potential felony that Democrats have largely ignored while pursuing former President Trump on identical charges.

Colorado conservatives note the glaring double standard. If Trump faces indictment for classified document mishandling, why does Biden escape similar scrutiny? The disparity fuels voter skepticism about weaponized justice and selective prosecution—issues that will dominate 2024 messaging in swing states like Colorado.

Watch for whether congressional Republicans demand a special counsel investigation matching the Trump probe's intensity, and whether Colorado's Democratic delegation addresses the credibility crisis.
