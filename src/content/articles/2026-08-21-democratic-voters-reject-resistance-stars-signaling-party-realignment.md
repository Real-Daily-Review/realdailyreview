# Democratic Base Turning Against Resistance Celebrities, Signaling Shift in Party Strategy

Colorado Democrats banking on celebrity-driven activism to drive turnout may be backing the wrong horse.

According to the Washington Examiner, Democratic voters are increasingly rejecting the "Resistance stars" who dominated anti-Trump messaging during his first term. The distinction between "Resistance 1.0"—the activist-celebrity coalition that mobilized against Trump from 2017-2021—and the current "Resistance 2.0" effort reveals a fundamental tactical split within the Democratic Party.

The shift matters for Colorado politics because state Democrats have leaned heavily on national celebrity endorsements and social-media-driven activism to energize younger voters and urban progressives. If that playbook is losing traction with the Democratic base itself, Colorado's left-leaning political infrastructure may need to recalibrate.

Republicans see an opening: while Democrats squabble over messaging strategy, GOP candidates can focus on kitchen-table issues—inflation, energy costs, property taxes, and regulatory overreach—that resonate with Colorado families regardless of celebrity cheerleading.

Watch whether Colorado Democratic candidates distance themselves from national Resistance figures in 2024-2026 cycles, or double down on the celebrity-activist model.
