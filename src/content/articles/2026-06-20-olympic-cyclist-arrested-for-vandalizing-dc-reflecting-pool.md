# Olympic Cyclist Among Those Arrested for Vandalizing D.C. Reflecting Pool

Federal authorities arrested David Hearn, a former Olympic cyclist, and others for attempting to damage the newly renovated Reflecting Pool in Washington, D.C., according to U.S. Park Police. The incident underscores a broader pattern of vandalism targeting federal monuments and public spaces.

President Trump characterized the vandals as attempting to "destroy and demean" the restoration work on the iconic landmark (Breitbart). The arrests come as the pool neared completion of a major renovation project.

Hearn and his co-defendants face property destruction charges. The specific motivations behind the vandalism remain under investigation, though the timing and targeting of federal property suggest a coordinated effort to damage symbols of American heritage and government investment.
