# White House Blocks Democrat Fishing Expeditions—And Conservatives Are Cheering

The Trump White House is refusing to comply with Democrat document demands, and it's a refreshing reminder that the party out of power doesn't get to weaponize congressional authority for political revenge.

White House Counsel Will Scharf sent a letter this week flatly rejecting Democrat requests for documents and information, according to *The Daily Wire* and the *Washington Examiner*. Scharf accused Democrats of "animus" and "pettiness," noting the administration has no obligation to respond to minority-party fishing expeditions.

This matters for Colorado conservatives who've watched Democrats use investigative power as a cudgel. When the left controlled Congress, they launched endless probes into Trump's finances, family, and associates—often yielding nothing but headlines and legal bills for those investigated. Now that Republicans hold the majority, Democrats are preparing their own investigations. The White House is drawing a line: no documents, no cooperation, no legitimacy for partisan witch hunts.

Republicans argue this is basic constitutional pushback against abuse of oversight power. Democrats counter that transparency and accountability demand cooperation. But conservatives see a pattern: the left demands total access when it's convenient, then cries "obstruction" when the tables turn.
