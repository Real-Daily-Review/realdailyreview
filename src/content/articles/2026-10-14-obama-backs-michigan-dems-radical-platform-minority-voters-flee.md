# Michigan Democrat's Radical Agenda Alienates Voters Across the Board

Barack Obama's endorsement of Michigan Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed signals how far left the Democratic Party has drifted—and voters are noticing. El-Sayed's platform includes abolishing ICE, imposing Medicare for All, and disarming Israel, positions that once lived on the Democratic fringe but now carry mainstream party backing (Washington Examiner).

The problem for Democrats: El-Sayed is hemorrhaging support among minority voters who should be his base. President Trump has argued that Black voters reject El-Sayed because he "mocks" and "disrespects them" (Breitbart), while concerns extend beyond Jewish voters to broader questions about the candidate's judgment and loyalties (National Review).

**The Colorado angle:** While this race unfolds in Michigan, it reflects the same progressive overreach Colorado Republicans have fought at home—from gun control measures to energy restrictions to government-run healthcare schemes. When national Democrats embrace fringe policies like ICE abolition and Medicare for All, they signal what Colorado's Democratic establishment might pursue next.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers, this race is a preview: watch whether El-Sayed's implosion among minority voters teaches Democrats a lesson about radical policy, or whether Colorado's left continues pushing the same agenda regardless of electoral consequences.
