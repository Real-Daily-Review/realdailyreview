# Broncos Head to LA Without Chargers' Star Tackle in Week 5 Matchup

Denver's offense gets a potential advantage Sunday as the Broncos travel to face the Chargers—minus Los Angeles' Pro Bowl left tackle Rashawn Slater, who underwent ankle surgery this week.

Slater, injured in Week 4 against Seattle, had a high-ankle sprain repaired via a tight-rope procedure designed to speed his recovery (New York Post). The Chargers placed him on injured reserve, meaning he won't suit up for the divisional clash. For Denver's pass rush, that's an opening: Slater's absence weakens LA's pass protection on the blind side, potentially giving the Broncos' edge rushers cleaner looks at Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert.

The game airs on KDVR Fox31 Denver and kicks off Sunday (KDVR). Both teams enter Week 5 jockeying for AFC West position in what's shaping up as a competitive division race.

The Broncos should capitalize on this injury luck—quality pass rush opportunities don't come often against playoff-caliber opponents, and the window closes once Slater returns from rehab.
