# Commerce Secretary Lutnick Under Fire Over Nvidia Ties, National Security Concerns

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick faces mounting pressure from Republican senators over his handling of Nvidia dealings, with critics arguing he jeopardized national security while his family stood to profit handsomely. According to the Washington Examiner, Lutnick is "quickly losing friends on Capitol Hill" as GOP lawmakers question whether his actions protecting the semiconductor giant—amid CEO Jensen Huang's resignation following an affair with a woman accused of being a Chinese spy—amounted to a conflict of interest. The controversy deepens as reports reveal Lutnick's sons hold approximately $362 million in Nvidia stock.

Republicans argue the situation raises serious red flags about whether Cabinet-level decisions were influenced by personal financial gain rather than America's strategic interests. The timing is particularly damaging: Nvidia's stock has plummeted over 27% in recent months after driving 15% of the S&amp;P 500's growth last year, and Huang's departure compounds concerns about the company's stability and foreign entanglement.

For Colorado—home to critical semiconductor research and defense contractors dependent on supply-chain security—the Lutnick controversy underscores how Washington's revolving door between corporate interests and government oversight threatens national security and market confidence.
