# U.S. Airstrike Eliminates Senior ISIS Commander in Syria

U.S. Central Command confirmed Wednesday that a precision airstrike in Syria last week killed Ali Husayn al-'Ulaywi, a senior ISIS leader, marking another blow against the terrorist organization that once threatened American interests across the Middle East.

CENTCOM's announcement underscores the ongoing U.S. military commitment to degrading ISIS capabilities in Syria, where the group has attempted to reconstitute itself despite losing territorial control. The precision nature of the strike reflects the technical sophistication American forces maintain in counterterrorism operations.
