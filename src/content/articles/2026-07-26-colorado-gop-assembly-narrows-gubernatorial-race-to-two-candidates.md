# Colorado Republicans Narrow Gubernatorial Field at Assembly

Colorado Republicans have winnowed their 2026 gubernatorial primary field down to just two candidates through the state assembly process, a sharp filtering that reflects party establishment influence over grassroots choice. Out of 18 total Republican hopefuls, only two advanced to the primary ballot following the assembly vote, according to *Complete Colorado*.

The assembly system—where party delegates rather than all registered Republicans select nominees—has long drawn criticism from conservatives who argue it concentrates power among party insiders and limits voter input. This year's dramatic culling raises questions about whether the two advancing candidates truly represent the broader Republican base or merely the preferences of Denver-area delegates.

For Colorado taxpayers and limited-government advocates, the real test comes next: Do these finalists offer genuine alternatives to Democrat Jared Polis's tax-and-spend agenda, or will they soft-pedal on the fiscal restraint and regulatory rollback that Colorado's economy desperately needs? The primary will reveal whether Republican voters accept the assembly's picks or demand fresh faces.

Watch whether grassroots conservative groups challenge the assembly outcome or mobilize behind the two nominees heading into the general election.
