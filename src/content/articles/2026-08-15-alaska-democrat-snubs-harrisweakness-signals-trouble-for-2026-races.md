# Alaska Democrat Rejects Harris Endorsement—But Colorado Conservatives Should Note the Real Story

Mary Peltola, the Democrat challenging Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan in Alaska's 2026 Senate race, is publicly rejecting support from Kamala Harris, signaling that even blue-state Democrats recognize the former vice president is toxic baggage in competitive races.

According to the *Washington Examiner* and *New York Post*, Peltola's campaign declined Harris's endorsement and fundraising push, with a spokeswoman stating her focus is "Alaska" and not "endorsements from anyone from the Lower 48." The move underscores Harris's political weakness heading into the midterm cycle—even Democrats in lean-blue states don't want her name attached to their campaigns.

For Colorado conservatives, this matters. If Harris can't help Democrats in Alaska, her ability to energize the party base or raise funds for competitive Senate races nationwide is severely limited. Colorado's 2026 Senate race could pit Democrat-held seats against Republican challengers, and a weakened national Democratic brand helps local GOP candidates.

Republicans argue Harris's unpopularity reflects voter rejection of Biden-Harris inflation, border chaos, and progressive overreach. Democrats counter that Peltola's move is purely strategic—Alaska-focused, not ideological.
