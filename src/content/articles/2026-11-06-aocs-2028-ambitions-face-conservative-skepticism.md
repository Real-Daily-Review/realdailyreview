# AOC's 2028 Ambitions Face Steep Odds Despite Left's Recent Wins

Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez may be eyeing a 2028 presidential run, but conservative analysts say the New York Democrat faces significant headwinds even as the left celebrates recent electoral gains.

According to *National Review*, despite progressive momentum this cycle, AOC confronts real obstacles to a national campaign. The left's narrative of unstoppable electoral power masks deeper structural challenges—particularly in swing states and among working-class voters who've drifted rightward on taxes, regulation, and energy policy.

*The Daily Wire* notes that 2028 will be a pivotal election, with the country's direction hanging in the balance. Republicans argue that if progressives gain the White House, their agenda—higher taxes, aggressive climate regulations, and expanded government control—will reshape America's economy and energy sector in ways Colorado can't escape.

For Colorado specifically, a 2028 AOC candidacy would likely accelerate the left's push for aggressive climate mandates and renewable-energy-only policies that could devastate the state's oil and gas workforce and rural economies. Conservatives warn that progressive presidential ambitions translate into real policy threats to Colorado's energy independence and taxpayer wallets.
