# Trump Elevates Stephen Miller to Chief of Staff Despite GOP Midterm Rout

President Trump has promoted hardline immigration adviser Stephen Miller to White House chief of staff following the Republican Party's significant midterm election losses—a move that has sparked sharp internal GOP divisions.

According to Fox News, House Republicans "expressed shock and anger" at the elevation, with critics questioning the decision in the wake of what National Review describes as "Trump's Midterm Disaster." The promotion signals Trump's intent to double down on his immigration-focused agenda rather than pivot strategy after the party's electoral setback.

Miller, appearing at Mar-a-Lago, pledged his full commitment to the administration's remaining term (The Daily Wire). Conservatives backing the move view it as a reassertion of Trump's core policy priorities—particularly border security and immigration enforcement—areas where the base demands uncompromising leadership.

However, establishment Republicans worry the appointment reflects poor political judgment. They argue the GOP needs to broaden its appeal and address voter concerns beyond Trump's signature issues, not entrench further around a polarizing figure associated with aggressive executive action on immigration.

**Colorado angle:** Miller's elevation likely signals continued federal pressure on sanctuary city policies and immigration enforcement that affects Colorado's urban centers and labor-dependent industries.
