# Judge Dismisses Reflecting Pool Case Against Former Olympian—Citing Political Prosecution Risk

A federal judge has permanently dismissed charges against former Olympian David Hearn in connection with alleged vandalism at the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool, dealing another blow to what conservatives view as weaponized prosecutions under the Biden-Harris Justice Department.

Judge Todd E. Edelman ruled that the circumstances created an "extraordinary possibility" that Hearn could face "meritless, politically motivated prosecution," according to 9News Denver. The dismissal came with prejudice, meaning the Justice Department cannot refile charges—a rare and significant legal outcome.

National Review characterized the ruling as "an indictment of Trump lawfare against political enemies and scapegoats," underscoring conservative arguments that the Biden administration has selectively prosecuted political opponents and January 6th-adjacent figures while ignoring progressive activism.

Hearn was accused of damaging the pool's peeling coating, charges that Fox News Politics noted were now permanently barred from refiling.

The case reflects broader conservative concerns about selective prosecution and the politicization of federal law enforcement. While Colorado has no direct stake in this D.C. case, it mirrors national patterns conservatives argue demonstrate institutional bias—concerns that resonate with Colorado Republicans skeptical of federal overreach.

Watch for whether similar politically motivated cases face judicial dismissal or whether the Justice Department continues pursuing marginal charges against conservative figures.
