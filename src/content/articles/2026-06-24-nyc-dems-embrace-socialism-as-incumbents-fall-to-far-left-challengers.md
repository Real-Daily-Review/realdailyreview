# NYC Dems Lurch Left as Socialist Candidates Oust Incumbents

New York Democrats are doubling down on far-left ideology, with socialist-backed candidates sweeping House primaries this week and knocking out sitting incumbents—a warning sign for moderate Democrats nationwide and a preview of where the party's base is headed.

In New York's 10th District, former City Comptroller Brad Lander, backed by NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani's socialist network, defeated incumbent Rep. Dan Goldman (The Daily Wire). Meanwhile, Jerry Nadler's hand-picked successor won a crowded primary featuring Kennedy scion Jack Schlossberg and other establishment figures (Fox News Politics).

The results expose a fundamental split in the Democratic Party: while some incumbents cling to centrist positioning, grassroots progressives are systematically replacing them with candidates further left. All three Mamdani-endorsed socialist candidates won their primaries, including two who ousted sitting House Democrats.

For Colorado Republicans, this matters. As the national Democratic Party drifts toward socialism and open-borders progressivism, Colorado Democrats—already pushing aggressive climate mandates, gun control, and tax hikes—will face pressure to match their New York counterparts' radicalism or risk primary challenges from their own left flank.
