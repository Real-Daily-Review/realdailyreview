# Judge Sets Summer 2028 Trial for Charlie Kirk Assassination Case

A Utah judge has scheduled jury selection to begin June 21, 2028, in the capital murder trial of Tyler Robinson, the man accused of assassinating conservative activist Charlie Kirk—a decision that leaves the case in legal limbo for nearly two years.

Fourth District Judge Tony Graf rejected both prosecutors' push for a January 2028 start and Robinson's defense team's request to delay into late 2029, according to *Breitbart* and *The Daily Wire*. Prosecutors anticipate a 17-week trial, underscoring the complexity of the case.

The extended timeline raises concerns among conservatives about the pace of justice in a high-profile attack on a prominent right-wing figure. While the judicial process demands thorough preparation, the near-two-year wait reflects the grinding reality of capital cases—and the burden on victims' families and the broader conservative movement during prolonged proceedings.
