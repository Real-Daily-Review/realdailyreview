# Biden Ally's Defense of Nazi Tattoo Candidate Exposes Democratic Hypocrisy on Extremism

A top Biden confidant's defense of Maine Democratic Senate candidate Graham Platner—who sports a Nazi tattoo—has ignited backlash from Jewish groups and Republicans, laying bare the left's selective outrage over extremist symbols.

Ron Klain, a longtime Biden advisor, defended Platner despite mounting accusations that the candidate knew exactly what the tattoo meant. According to *The Daily Wire*, a new accuser—an ex-girlfriend identifying as a left-wing streamer—told the New York Post that Platner said the Nazi symbol reminded him the U.S. "was the evil bad guy overseas." This directly contradicts claims the tattoo was merely a youthful mistake.

Republicans argue the incident exposes Democratic double standards: the party routinely attacks conservatives over alleged ties to extremism while sheltering its own candidates from serious scrutiny. Jewish groups have condemned both Platner's tattoo and Klain's defense.

While this race unfolds in Maine, Colorado Republicans should note the pattern. Democrats nationwide are increasingly willing to overlook radical ideology—from anti-American rhetoric to extremist symbolism—when it serves electoral interests. The contrast with how the left treats conservative candidates accused of far lesser offenses is stark.

Watch whether national Democratic leadership distances itself from Platner or continues circling the wagons.
