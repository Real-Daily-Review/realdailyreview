# Kennedy Center Closure Exposes D.C. Dysfunction—and Wasteful Federal Arts Spending

The Kennedy Center board voted to close the nation's premier performing arts venue over safety and financial troubles, a stunning admission of mismanagement at a federally funded institution that has long symbolized Washington's bloated cultural spending (Fox News). The dispute erupted after a judge blocked Trump's attempt to name the building after himself—a move that exposed deeper rot: the Kennedy Center, despite federal subsidies, faces mounting deficits and infrastructure failures.

Democrats immediately weaponized the closure for political theater. Rep. Joyce Beatty (D-OH) claimed Trump threw a "tantrum" over the naming dispute, while CNN's Chuck Todd called the proposal "gross" (Washington Examiner, Breitbart). But the real scandal isn't Trump's branding ambitions—it's that a federally funded institution can't maintain basic operations.

For Colorado taxpayers, this is a cautionary tale. Federal arts funding flows to D.C. elites while Colorado communities struggle with underfunded schools and crumbling infrastructure. The Kennedy Center's collapse reveals how government agencies squander public money on prestige projects, then demand more taxpayer bailouts when their mismanagement catches up.

**Watch whether Congress uses this crisis to demand accountability—or simply appropriates more federal dollars to paper over decades of fiscal negligence.**
