# OpenAI Launches Cybersecurity AI Model as Sector Heats Up

OpenAI has rolled out GPT-5.5-Cyber, a specialized AI model designed for cybersecurity teams, in limited preview to vetted organizations, according to CNBC. The move comes roughly a month after Anthropic released Mythos, a competing cybersecurity-focused model that has already made an impact in the field.

Anthropic's Mythos has demonstrated early traction, with security researchers at Mozilla reporting that the model has identified a wealth of high-severity bugs in Firefox, according to TechCrunch. The discovery has prompted Mozilla to reassess its cybersecurity approach.

Both models represent a shift toward AI systems tailored for specific professional domains rather than general-purpose tools. OpenAI's limited rollout suggests a cautious approach to deployment, restricting access to established security teams while the company evaluates performance and safety.

The competitive timing underscores how rapidly the AI industry is moving specialized models to market. Anthropic's early success with Mythos in uncovering critical vulnerabilities may have accelerated OpenAI's timeline for its own offering.

Watch for announcements on broader availability for both models and whether other organizations report similar bug-discovery results.
