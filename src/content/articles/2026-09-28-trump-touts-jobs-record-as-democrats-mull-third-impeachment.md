# Trump Touts Record Employment While Democrats Float Third Impeachment

President Trump claims more Americans are working than at any point in U.S. history, crediting his administration's economic policies and pushing back against what he calls media refusal to report the gains. "The American People elected me to deliver HISTORIC Economic Results," Trump said, emphasizing his responsibility to highlight achievements the "Fake News refuses" to cover (Just The News).

Meanwhile, House Democrats are already signaling appetite for renewed impeachment proceedings. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) told NBC's *Meet the Press* that impeaching Trump a third time would amount to "setting a standard" if Democrats regain the House majority (Washington Examiner). Khanna rejected suggestions Democrats should hold back on investigations.

**The conservative take:** Republicans argue Democrats are weaponizing impeachment as a political tool rather than a constitutional remedy. A third impeachment would represent naked partisan overreach—especially if Democrats control the chamber and face no real check on their power. For Colorado voters and taxpayers already wary of government abuse, this signals the left's willingness to weaponize institutions for political gain.

**What to watch:** Whether Colorado's Democratic delegation—including Rep. Joe Neguse—backs a third impeachment push, and how that plays with moderate voters in swing districts heading into 2026.
