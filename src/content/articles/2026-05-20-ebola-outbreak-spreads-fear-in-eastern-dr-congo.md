# Ebola Outbreak Spreads Fear Across Eastern DR Congo

An Ebola outbreak in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo is triggering widespread alarm as cases rise and health authorities struggle to contain the virus.

The health minister has acknowledged that medical teams are falling behind in their response, citing slow detection of cases, according to BBC World. Concern is spreading across the region from Bukavu to Kinshasa, with residents and street vendors expressing fear as confirmed cases mount, Al Jazeera reports.

The outbreak underscores persistent challenges in disease surveillance and rapid response in the DRC, where previous Ebola epidemics have killed thousands. Health officials are working to accelerate case identification and isolation efforts, though the initial lag in detection has allowed the virus to gain ground.

The DRC has experience managing Ebola outbreaks, but each resurgence tests the country's health infrastructure and coordination between local and national authorities.
