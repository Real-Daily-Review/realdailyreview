# Federal Judge Refers Philadelphia DA Krasner to DOJ Over Possible Criminal Conduct

A federal judge has referred Philadelphia District Attorney Larry Krasner to the Department of Justice for possible perjury and false statements, marking a stark rebuke of progressive prosecutorial overreach.

U.S. District Judge Paul Diamond found that Krasner and senior Assistant District Attorney Matthew Stiegler may have broken the law while concealing how their office supported overturning a murder conviction, according to the *Washington Examiner*. Judge Diamond disqualified both men from the case entirely—a rare and serious sanction.

**The conservative case against Krasner's tenure is straightforward:** Progressive DAs who prioritize ideology over justice undermine the rule of law and public safety. Krasner, a self-described "progressive prosecutor," has faced criticism from Republicans and law-and-order conservatives for his office's handling of cases and what critics call selective enforcement that favors defendants over victims.

The referral signals that even federal courts recognize the danger when district attorneys weaponize their offices for ideological ends rather than honest prosecution. This case exemplifies why voters in major cities—including Colorado's urban centers—should scrutinize their own DAs' records on crime, conviction integrity, and transparency.
