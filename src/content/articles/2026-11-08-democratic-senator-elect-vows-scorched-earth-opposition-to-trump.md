# Democratic Senator-Elect Signals Scorched-Earth Strategy Against Trump, Abandoning Civility Norms

A newly elected Democratic senator is openly calling for his party to ditch political decorum and treat President Trump as an "unserious fool," signaling Democrats intend to wage all-out opposition rather than seek common ground.

Abdul El-Sayed (D-MI), speaking on Pod Save America, argued that Democrats must abandon outdated civility and adopt a more combative posture. His remarks come as Republicans eye a potential Senate takeover in 2028—Democrats currently hold at least 54 seats, but the GOP could flip the chamber by sweeping six competitive states and holding the vice presidency.

**The conservative take:** El-Sayed's rhetoric exposes Democratic strategy heading into Trump's second term: scorched-earth partisanship masquerading as principle. Republicans argue this approach—abandoning norms while claiming Trump violated them first—is precisely the kind of hypocrisy that fueled voter backlash in 2024. Conservatives note that Colorado voters, increasingly skeptical of partisan gridlock, rejected similar divisive messaging from state Democrats in recent cycles.
