# Rand Paul Hammers Fauci Over Self-Promotion During COVID Crisis

Senator Rand Paul is renewing his assault on Dr. Anthony Fauci's conduct as White House medical advisor, citing newly released diary entries that show the nation's top pandemic official lobbied colleagues to nominate him for a $1 million prize while directing the federal COVID response (Fox News Politics).

Paul, a consistent Fauci critic, mocked the former NIAID director on social media after diary excerpts surfaced, suggesting Fauci spent evenings chronicling his own greatness rather than focusing on pandemic management (Washington Examiner). The Kentucky Republican claims Fauci pressured NIH colleagues to support his nomination for the Dan David Prize—a prestigious international award—while simultaneously shaping U.S. coronavirus policy.

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue Fauci's self-promotion during a national health emergency reveals misplaced priorities and conflicts of interest. Paul contends the diary entries demonstrate an official more concerned with personal accolades than transparent, accountable pandemic guidance. For Colorado taxpayers funding federal health agencies, the episode underscores concerns about bureaucratic self-dealing and the lack of accountability within unelected health officials.

**Watch for:** Whether additional diary entries surface and whether Republican lawmakers push for formal investigations into Fauci's conduct during his tenure.
