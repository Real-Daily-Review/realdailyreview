# Boulder County Cold Case Closed After 20 Years; Man Pleads Guilty in Angela Wilds Death

Two decades of uncertainty ended this week when John Angerer pleaded guilty to criminally negligent homicide in the 2006 death of Angela Wilds, resolving one of Boulder County's longest-running cold cases (9News Denver, KDVR Fox31).

Wilds' body was recovered in 2006, but the case languished for over 20 years before Angerer's guilty plea brought closure to her family and the community. The plea means no trial will proceed, sparing resources and allowing investigators to move forward on other pending cases.

The resolution underscores both the persistence of law enforcement and the challenges cold cases present to Colorado's criminal justice system. While Boulder County authorities deserve credit for maintaining focus on unsolved homicides, the two-decade gap raises questions about resource allocation and investigative capacity in smaller jurisdictions.

Meanwhile, Boulder County faced separate weather emergencies this week, with U.S. Highway 36 near Lyons closed in both directions due to mudslides triggered by severe hail and storms (KDVR Fox31). The closure disrupted regional traffic and highlighted infrastructure vulnerabilities in mountain corridors.

Coloradans should monitor whether state and federal funding for rural law enforcement and infrastructure maintenance keeps pace with demand.
