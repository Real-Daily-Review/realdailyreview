# Aurora Police Officers Injured in Crash While Responding to Emergency Call

Two Aurora police officers were injured Tuesday when their patrol vehicle was struck while responding to a separate incident with lights and sirens activated, according to 9News Denver and KDVR Fox31. A driver was detained at the scene following the crash.

The officers were en route to an active call when the collision occurred. Details on the severity of their injuries and the circumstances leading to the crash remain limited, though both outlets confirm the patrol vehicle had emergency equipment activated at the time of impact.

This incident underscores the daily risks Colorado law enforcement faces while performing their duties. Aurora police have not released additional specifics about the detained driver or whether charges are anticipated.

Conservatives and law-and-order advocates emphasize the need for public awareness around emergency vehicle protocols and driver accountability on Colorado roads.
