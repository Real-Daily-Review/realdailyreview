# Nintendo Raises Switch 2 Prices Amid Supply Constraints

Nintendo is raising Switch 2 prices globally, citing "changes in market conditions," and simultaneously forecasting a decline in console sales over the next year.

In the U.S., the Switch 2 will jump to $499.99 from $449.99, effective September 1st, according to The Verge. Canada sees a $50 increase to $679.99, while European prices rise €40 to €499.99. Japan's increase takes effect sooner—May 25th—with the Switch 2 moving from ¥49,980 to ¥59,980, according to CNBC.

The price hikes affect a broader range of Switch products in Japan. CNBC attributes the increases to a "memory crunch," suggesting supply-side pressures are driving the decision.

The company's revised sales forecast signals management expects the higher prices to dampen demand. Nintendo has not detailed the specific duration or severity of the anticipated sales decline.

Watch for Nintendo's next earnings report to clarify how aggressively demand falls and whether supply constraints ease in coming quarters.
