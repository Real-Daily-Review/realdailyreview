# Bernie Sanders Pushes Government Control Agenda While Claiming to Fight Corruption

Bernie Sanders is demanding Democrats ban super PACs from primaries and calling for a halt to AI development—moves that reveal the socialist senator's real priority: expanding government power over markets and speech, not fighting corruption.

Sanders is urging Democratic leaders to ban outside spending in party primaries, citing concerns about influence (Fox News Politics). Meanwhile, he's pressuring CEOs at Anthropic, Meta, and OpenAI to pause AI development or face congressional mandates (New York Post). On healthcare, Sanders has found rare common ground with Republicans on price transparency legislation, targeting what the Washington Examiner calls the "multi-tentacled monster" of hospital systems and insurance conglomerates that "grow ever larger by the year as Washington feeds it taxpayer dollars."

Here's the conservative problem: Sanders wants to ban super PACs—a free-speech issue—while demanding government freeze innovation in AI. That's not anti-corruption; that's government overreach dressed in populist language. Colorado taxpayers and businesses should be wary. If Washington moves to regulate AI development or further micromanage healthcare pricing, Colorado's growing tech sector and healthcare providers face new compliance costs and reduced competitiveness.

The one area of agreement—hospital price transparency—deserves scrutiny: conservatives should ensure any legislation actually increases competition rather than creating new regulatory burdens that entrench existing players.
