# Trump Faces Pre-Election Lawsuit Blitz While Voting by Mail

President Trump says he's "not surprised" by revelations that state attorneys general were planning lawsuits against him months before his 2024 reelection victory—a coordinated legal assault that has now materialized into roughly 100 suits since January 2025, according to *Just The News*.

The lawsuit barrage underscores a core conservative complaint: Democratic-led states are weaponizing the courts against a Republican administration. State AGs, predominantly Democrat, have made Trump litigation a priority, raising questions about whether legal action is being driven by partisan animus rather than genuine legal merit.

There's an irony worth noting: Trump has repeatedly criticized mail-in voting as vulnerable to fraud since 2020, yet *Washington Examiner* reports he voted by mail in Florida's Republican primary this year—the second time he's done so in 2025. Trump and his team have apparently adopted a "tactical retreat" on mail-in voting in certain cases, suggesting pragmatism trumps principle when convenient.

For Colorado conservatives, the state AG lawsuit trend matters: Colorado's Democratic leadership has shown willingness to join multistate legal efforts against Republican administrations. Taxpayers should watch whether Colorado participates in these suits and what legal fees rack up on the state dime.
