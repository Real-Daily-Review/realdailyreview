# Trump Defends Iran Strategy at UN, Calls for Economic Pressure on Tehran

President Trump told world leaders at the United Nations that he faces a binary choice on Iran: force a nuclear deal or "annihilate" the regime pursuing weapons of mass destruction. Speaking before the General Assembly, Trump defended his decision to escalate tensions with Tehran, calling for "complete economic isolation of Iran" until it abandons its nuclear program, stops attacking commercial shipping, and ceases funding terrorist organizations (Just The News).

Trump signaled a peace deal could materialize after midterm elections, positioning the confrontation as unfinished business requiring decisive American leadership. The approach reflects his administration's rejection of the Obama-era Iran nuclear deal, which conservatives argue failed to constrain Tehran's regional aggression or ballistic missile development.

Democrats and international observers have criticized the escalation, warning it risks broader conflict and destabilizes global markets. Trump counters that economic pressure and military readiness are the only languages Iran understands, and that previous diplomatic efforts emboldened rather than contained the regime.
