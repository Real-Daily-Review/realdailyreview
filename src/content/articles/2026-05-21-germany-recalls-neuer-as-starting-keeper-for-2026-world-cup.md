# Germany Recalls Neuer as Starting Keeper for 2026 World Cup

Germany has named Manuel Neuer as its first-choice goalkeeper for the 2026 World Cup, reversing course after the veteran Bayern Munich player's international retirement two years ago, according to Al Jazeera and Deutsche Welle.

Neuer, who led Germany to the 2014 World Cup title, had stepped away from international football in 2022. His recall caps an unexpected comeback and displaces Oliver Baumann, who had been established as the primary option during Neuer's absence.

The decision underscores Germany's confidence in Neuer's ability to perform at the highest level despite his age and time away from the national team. His return marks a significant shift in the goalkeeper hierarchy as the team prepares for the tournament in North America.

Germany's coaching staff has not publicly detailed the reasoning behind prioritizing Neuer over Baumann, who has maintained strong form in the Bundesliga. The move signals the federation's belief that experience and proven championship pedigree outweigh continuity with the interim starter.

Watch for Neuer's performance in upcoming World Cup qualifiers and friendlies to gauge whether the gamble pays off before the tournament kicks off.
