# Colorado Driver Arrested After Deadly Idaho Motorcycle Crash; Wolf Killed Over Livestock Losses

A Colorado Springs woman driving a pickup truck struck three Colorado motorcyclists head-on in northern Idaho on Tuesday, killing all three, according to Idaho State Police (9News Denver). Two of the victims were identified as Larimer County sheriff's deputies; the third was another Colorado man (CPR News). The driver, also from Colorado, was arrested at the scene (KDVR Fox31). State police say the woman attempted to pass another vehicle in a no-passing zone when the collision occurred.

In separate news, Colorado Parks and Wildlife killed a wolf after determining it had preyed on 22 sheep, the Colorado Sun reports. The animal, born in 2024 and nicknamed variously "Elijah," "Lucky," or "Freedom" by different observers, had become a symbol in Colorado's ongoing wildlife management debates. Wildlife advocates mourned the killing, while ranchers and rural Coloradans defended the agency's decision to protect livestock from predation.

The motorcycle crash underscores Idaho road safety concerns; the wolf killing highlights the tension between Colorado's environmental left—which romanticizes predator recovery—and ranchers and rural communities who bear the economic cost of livestock losses.
