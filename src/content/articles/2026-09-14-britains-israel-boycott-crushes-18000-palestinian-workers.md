# Britain's Anti-Israel Boycott Backfires on Palestinians—and Exposes Progressive Foreign Policy Folly

Britain's Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband just handed conservatives a textbook example of how progressive virtue-signaling destroys the very people it claims to help. Last week, Miliband announced a ban on goods from Israeli West Bank settlements. Israel's response was swift: closure of its British consulate in East Jerusalem, a diplomatic presence dating to the 19th century.

The collateral damage? Roughly 18,000 Palestinians who depend on wages from those settlements now face joblessness, according to the *Washington Examiner*. London's progressive activists never asked them.

This move also signals deeper trouble for the U.S.-UK alliance. By treating Israel as a pariah alongside Spain and Ireland—and using inflammatory language like "terrorists" and "ethnic cleansing"—Britain is handing the incoming Trump administration reason to recalibrate the "special relationship," the *Examiner* warns.

**The conservative case:** Boycott movements rooted in ideology, not pragmatism, harm ordinary workers and destabilize allies. Economic sanctions should serve strategic interests, not progressive theater. Colorado voters who value strong alliances and realistic foreign policy should note how left-wing foreign policy experiments often backfire on the vulnerable populations they claim to champion.

Watch whether Trump's team formally responds to Britain's diplomatic shift—and whether other U.S. allies reconsider similar boycott measures.
