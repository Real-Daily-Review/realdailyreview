# Intelligence: Iran Seeks to Deploy Dirty Bomb on U.S. Soil

The Biden administration faces a genuine national security threat that demands serious attention: intelligence assessments indicate Iran is actively pursuing a radiological dispersal device—a "dirty bomb"—for potential deployment inside the United States, according to the Washington Examiner.

The Energy Department's National Nuclear Security Administration has raised alarm over the threat. Unlike a nuclear weapon, a dirty bomb doesn't create a massive atomic explosion but instead spreads deadly radiation across populated areas, potentially causing mass casualties and economic devastation.

The intelligence community is reportedly developing intercept strategies to prevent Iranian operatives from smuggling such a device into American territory. This includes enhanced monitoring of ports, borders, and illicit shipping networks that could transport radioactive materials.
