# Gaza Flotilla Detainees Allege Abuse; Israeli Strikes Continue Despite Ceasefire

Activists detained after a Gaza aid flotilla have alleged they were subjected to physical abuse and sexual violence by Israeli forces, according to the BBC. The detainees claim they were slapped, hit, and humiliated while in custody. Israel's prison service has denied the allegations.

Separately, Israeli forces have conducted strikes in central Gaza despite an agreed ceasefire, according to Al Jazeera. Video footage shows attacks on Nuseirat and Bureij refugee camps that injured dozens of people.

The allegations and continued military operations underscore tensions surrounding Israel's detention practices and adherence to ceasefire terms. The abuse claims add to scrutiny of how detainees are treated, while the strikes raise questions about implementation of the ceasefire agreement.
