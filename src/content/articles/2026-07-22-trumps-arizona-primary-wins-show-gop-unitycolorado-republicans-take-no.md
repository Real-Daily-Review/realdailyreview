# Trump's Primary Dominance Signals GOP Realignment—But Colorado Republicans Should Learn the Lesson

Donald Trump's iron grip on Republican primary voters delivered decisive wins for his endorsed candidates in Arizona, including former NFL kicker Jay Feely, who cruised to victory in a House race (New York Post). The results underscore Trump's continued command of the GOP base heading into November—a reality Colorado Republicans cannot ignore as they prepare their own primary battles.

Democrats, meanwhile, managed to avoid a far-left upset in Arizona, suggesting the party's establishment still controls its nominating process despite progressive pressure (Fox News). Republicans argue this contrast reveals a fundamental difference: Trump's GOP is unified around a clear agenda, while Democrats remain fractured between centrists and socialists.

For Colorado conservatives, the takeaway is straightforward: primary endorsements matter, and grassroots energy behind a candidate can overwhelm establishment resistance. Colorado Republicans should study what worked in Arizona—clear messaging, unified backing, and mobilization of Trump's base—as they prepare for 2026 state races and congressional primaries.

Watch whether Colorado GOP candidates actively seek Trump's endorsement or distance themselves from it, and whether that choice correlates with primary success.
