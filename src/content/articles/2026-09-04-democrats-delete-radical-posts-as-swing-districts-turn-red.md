# Democrats Scrub Radical Records as Swing Districts Slip Away

A Democratic nominee in a competitive House race has deleted hundreds of social media posts revealing his far-left agenda—a sign that even Democrats know their base positions are electoral poison in purple America.

William Lawrence, the Democratic nominee in a swing district and former Democratic Socialists of America member, wiped posts calling for abolishing the Senate, eliminating police and prisons, ending fossil-fuel production, opening borders, and dismantling the nuclear family, *The Daily Wire* reports. CNN first surfaced the deletions.

The scrubbing reflects a broader Democratic panic. Rep. Kathy Castor (D-FL), facing a redrawn district now favoring Republicans, has launched ads distancing herself from "extreme partisanship"—a tacit admission that her party's actual record repels swing voters, per the *Washington Examiner*.

**The Conservative Take:** Republicans argue this reveals the Democratic Party's true colors. When candidates must hide their own words to win, it exposes the gap between progressive ideology and what voters will actually accept. Lawrence's deleted posts on energy production and border policy would devastate Colorado's energy sector and rural communities; Castor's retreat signals Democrats know their agenda is unpopular.

The lesson: Democrats are betting voters have short memories. Watch whether Colorado's swing districts—where energy jobs and border security matter—punish candidates who've embraced this radical playbook.
