# ASEAN Leaders Adopt Measures to Cushion Iran War Economic Impact

ASEAN leaders have adopted measures aimed at easing economic strain from the Iran conflict, according to Al Jazeera. The move reflects growing concern across the bloc about energy security and price volatility.

The region faces particular vulnerability given its dependence on Middle Eastern oil. ASEAN currently imports more than half of its crude oil from the Middle East, making it susceptible to supply disruptions and price shocks stemming from the conflict.

The specific measures adopted were not detailed in available reporting, though Deutsche Welle noted the leaders convened to discuss plans addressing the war's broader economic fallout. The summit underscores how regional geopolitical crises ripple through global energy markets and affect nations far from the conflict zone.

ASEAN's response signals the bloc's effort to coordinate a unified approach rather than have member states act independently—a posture consistent with the organization's consensus-driven model.

Watch for details on implementation timelines and whether individual ASEAN members pursue additional bilateral energy agreements to diversify supply sources.
