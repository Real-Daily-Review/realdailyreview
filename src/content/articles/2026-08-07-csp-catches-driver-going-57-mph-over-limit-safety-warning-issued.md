# Colorado Driver Caught Going 57 MPH Over Limit; CSP Issues Safety Warning

Colorado State Patrol caught a driver traveling at nearly double the speed limit this week, prompting a stern public reminder about highway safety. The trooper's enforcement action underscores persistent reckless driving on Colorado roads—a problem that costs taxpayers millions in emergency response, accident investigation, and highway maintenance.

While CSP's warning is straightforward public safety messaging, conservatives should note the broader context: Colorado's roads are increasingly congested due to unchecked population growth driven partly by state policies that haven't kept pace with infrastructure demand. Rather than simply issuing citations, policymakers should prioritize road expansion and maintenance funding over pet progressive projects.

The incident also highlights why Colorado needs to maintain robust law enforcement presence on highways. Adequate trooper staffing and equipment—not bureaucratic oversight boards—keep roads safe and deter dangerous driving.

Coloradans should watch whether the state legislature addresses highway safety through infrastructure investment or merely through citation revenue.
