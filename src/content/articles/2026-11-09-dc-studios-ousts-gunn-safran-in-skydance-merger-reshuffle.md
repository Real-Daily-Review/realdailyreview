# DC Studios Leadership Ousted in Skydance Merger Shake-Up

Hollywood's latest executive musical chairs has nothing to do with Colorado policy—but it's worth noting that entertainment industry consolidation mirrors the corporate concentration concerns conservatives raise about Big Tech and corporate America generally.

Skydance Motion Picture Group announced Monday that James Gunn and Peter Safran are stepping down as co-chief executives of DC Studios after four years (9News Denver). The shake-up follows Skydance's acquisition of the studio. According to Breitbart, Marvel veteran Jeremy Latcham will replace them.

Gunn's tenure at DC was marked by creative turbulence and box-office disappointment—a far cry from his earlier success shepherding Marvel properties. The Daily Caller noted Gunn's parting comment: "Oh, boy. What a four years."

The leadership change signals that Skydance is betting on fresh direction for the struggling DC cinematic universe, which has hemorrhaged audience confidence under Gunn's stewardship. Whether a Marvel veteran can revive DC's fortunes remains an open question, but the move underscores how quickly corporate priorities shift when shareholders demand results.
