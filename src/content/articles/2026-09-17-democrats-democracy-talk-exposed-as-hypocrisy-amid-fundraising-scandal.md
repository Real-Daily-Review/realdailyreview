# Democrats' Democracy Rhetoric Rings Hollow as Corruption Scandals Mount

The American Left's sanctimonious lectures on "protecting democracy" collapse under scrutiny of their own conduct. According to the Washington Examiner, recent evidence has surfaced involving two major scandals centered on Democrats' get-out-the-vote and fundraising operations, including alleged irregularities in foreign donations and oversight gaps in ActBlue fundraising infrastructure.

Republicans argue this exposes the fundamental hypocrisy of Democratic messaging: the party that claims to be democracy's guardian is simultaneously operating networks rife with questionable practices that undermine electoral integrity. Democrats counter that isolated incidents don't reflect systemic problems and that Republican focus on these cases deflects from their own election-related controversies.

For Colorado, this matters. State Democrats have aggressively pushed mail-in voting and ballot collection systems that critics say lack adequate safeguards. If national Democratic fundraising and voter contact operations are compromised by inadequate oversight, Colorado voters deserve assurance that similar vulnerabilities don't exist in our own election infrastructure.

The broader question: Can Colorado trust that Democratic-controlled election administration is genuinely committed to integrity, or is the party's "democracy protection" rhetoric merely cover for partisan advantage?

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether Colorado's election officials—particularly in blue counties—implement meaningful transparency measures in voter contact operations and donation tracking.
