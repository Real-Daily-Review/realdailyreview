# Trump's Endorsement Muscle Gets Tested in Four-State Primary Gauntlet

President Trump and progressive New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani are both putting their political capital on the line Tuesday as voters in New York, South Carolina, Maryland, and Utah head to the polls—a real-time test of whose endorsement actually moves the needle in 2024.

The marquee matchup is South Carolina's gubernatorial primary runoff, where Trump's pick faces off against the establishment favorite. Two fiercely competitive House races in Utah will also reveal whether Trump's MAGA-aligned candidates can overcome traditional Republican infrastructure and donor networks (Fox News, Just The News).

Republicans argue Trump's endorsements reflect genuine grassroots energy and a realignment of the GOP toward populist, anti-establishment candidates. Democrats and establishment Republicans counter that Trump's picks often struggle in general elections and that his influence may fracture party unity heading into November.

For Colorado conservatives, these results matter: they'll signal whether Trump-style primary challenges to incumbent Republicans gain traction nationally—a dynamic that could reshape Colorado's own 2024 primary landscape and influence how state GOP leadership navigates endorsements in competitive races.
