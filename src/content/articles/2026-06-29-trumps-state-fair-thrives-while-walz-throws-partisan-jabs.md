# Trump's State Fair Draws Crowds While Walz Throws Partisan Jabs

President Trump's Great American State Fair is drawing attendees unbothered by partisan noise, even as Minnesota Gov. Tim Walz attempts to undercut the event with cheap shots about his own state fair.

According to Breitbart, fairgoers at Trump's 16-day celebration in Washington, DC, say politics hasn't overshadowed the Americana on display—despite critics claiming the president is using the venue to promote his administration. That's the kind of event discipline conservatives appreciate: a straightforward celebration of American culture without the usual left-wing grievance theater.

Walz, however, couldn't resist. The Minnesota Democrat took to X Monday to snipe at Trump's fair, calling Minnesota's event "America's real state fair" and telling people to "plan accordingly" (Washington Examiner). The passive-aggressive tone is pure Walz—unable to let Trump have a moment without inserting himself into the conversation.

For Colorado conservatives, the contrast is instructive: one side is building something celebratory; the other is tearing it down. Walz's comment reveals the left's reflexive need to politicize everything, even state fairs.

Watch whether Colorado Democrats follow Walz's playbook and attempt to weaponize the Colorado State Fair against Republican events this fall.
