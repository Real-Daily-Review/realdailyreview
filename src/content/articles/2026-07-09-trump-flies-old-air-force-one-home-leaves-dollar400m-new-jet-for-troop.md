# Trump Opts for Old Air Force One Over New $400M Aircraft—Here's Why

President Trump is flying the aging Air Force One back to Washington after the NATO summit in Turkey rather than the newer presidential aircraft, citing a practical reason: he wants American troops stationed abroad to tour the new plane at a U.K. air base (Fox News). The new Air Force One, a $400 million aircraft, will head to Mildenhall Air Force Base in Britain following the NATO gathering, allowing service members to see the upgraded presidential transport.

Trump's decision to stick with the older aircraft for his return journey has sparked questions about the new plane's operational readiness and timing. The Washington Examiner notes the move raised "more questions than answers" about the $400 million investment and its current deployment status.
