# Fauci Diary Claims Raise Questions About Vaccine Rollout Timing, Health

Anthony Fauci's diary entries, according to conservative outlets, suggest the former COVID czar deliberately delayed vaccine distribution to undermine President Trump's 2020 re-election campaign. *The Daily Wire* reports that Fauci coordinated with left-leaning officials to slow the rollout, despite Trump's push to accelerate vaccine availability. The diary allegedly shows Fauci expressing disapproval of Trump's focus on reopening the economy in May 2020.

Additionally, *Just The News* cites Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s claims that Fauci's diary reveals he suffered a pulmonary infarction five months after receiving the vaccine at a press conference.

**The Conservative Case:** Republicans argue these revelations expose how public health decisions were politicized during the pandemic—prioritizing electoral advantage over rapid vaccine distribution that could have saved lives and restored economic normalcy sooner. Conservatives say this vindicates long-standing skepticism about Fauci's independence from political pressure.

The diary entries have not been independently verified by mainstream outlets, and Democrats have not yet responded to these specific claims.
