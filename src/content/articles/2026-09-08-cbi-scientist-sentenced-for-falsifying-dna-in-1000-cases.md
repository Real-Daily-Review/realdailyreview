# Former CBI Scientist Sentenced for Massive DNA Evidence Scandal

A state government lab analyst's decade-long criminal negligence has now cast doubt on hundreds of Colorado criminal cases and exposed serious failures in government oversight.

Yvonne "Missy" Woods, a forensic scientist at the Colorado Bureau of Investigation, was sentenced to 10 years in prison after pleading guilty to four felonies for falsifying and mishandling DNA evidence in more than 1,000 cases (KDVR Fox31). According to CPR News, Woods altered DNA test results without explanation, jeopardizing the integrity of serious criminal prosecutions across the state.

This scandal raises hard questions about government accountability. Woods faced between 8 and 16 years behind bars (9News Denver), and her guilty plea suggests systemic failures in lab oversight, quality control, and internal auditing at a state agency responsible for criminal evidence. Taxpayers funded this lab and trusted it to maintain rigorous standards—trust that was systematically violated.

The Colorado Sun reports Woods was ordered to serve the full 10-year sentence. Her actions have already forced prosecutors and defense attorneys to re-examine hundreds of cases, potentially freeing guilty parties or overturning convictions based on her falsified work.
