# Trump Demands Senate Action After Midterms; GOP Eyes Lame-Duck Push

President Trump is demanding the Republican-controlled Senate act decisively in the lame-duck session, signaling frustration with legislative gridlock that conservatives say has stalled the party's agenda. "The Senate has to act. The Senate is getting nothing passed, and it's just killing our country," Trump told reporters Friday in Florida (Washington Examiner).

The rebuke comes as Republicans assess midterm performance and chart next steps. National Review notes the GOP faces questions about what comes next following the election cycle, with Trump clearly signaling impatience with Senate inaction.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado Republicans and taxpayers are watching whether the Senate will finally move on energy policy, water rights, and regulatory rollback—issues critical to the state's economy and land management. Conservatives argue the Senate's gridlock has blocked pro-growth legislation that could benefit Colorado's energy sector and reduce federal overreach on water and public lands.

Democrats counter that Senate obstruction reflects legitimate oversight and protection of environmental and social priorities, though they hold the minority.

**What to watch:** Whether Senate Republicans use the lame-duck window to pass Trump-backed legislation before the new Congress convenes, or whether gridlock persists—a key test of GOP unity heading into 2026.
