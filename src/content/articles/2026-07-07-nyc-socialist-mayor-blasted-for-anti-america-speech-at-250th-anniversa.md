# Socialist NYC Mayor's Anti-America Speech Draws Conservative Backlash

A New York City socialist mayor used America's 250th anniversary celebration as a platform to trash the nation—and conservatives aren't letting it slide.

NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani delivered what National Review called a cynically distorted critique of the United States during what should have been a patriotic occasion. The speech prompted sharp pushback from Republicans, including former Los Angeles mayoral candidate Spencer Pratt, who took to social media to denounce what he characterized as Mamdani's "vile" anti-American rhetoric (The Daily Wire).

National Review editors argue that Mamdani's framing fundamentally misrepresents immigrant experience in America, noting that immigrants to the U.S. don't hate the country. The outlet also warned that today's young socialists—apparently including Mamdani—"understand just as little as their predecessors did" about the failures of socialist ideology.

**The Colorado angle:** While this story centers on New York politics, it reflects the broader ideological divide playing out in blue-state governance.
