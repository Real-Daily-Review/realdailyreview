# Iran Threatens Regional Escalation as Israel Continues Lebanon Strikes

Iran's Revolutionary Guard warned Wednesday it would extend Middle East conflict "beyond the region" if the U.S. and Israel resume attacks on Tehran, according to CNBC.

The threat comes as Israeli air strikes on southern Lebanon killed at least eight people, according to Al Jazeera. The attacks occurred despite a recently extended ceasefire agreement scheduled to last until early July.

The dual developments signal deepening tensions across the region. Iran's warning represents a significant escalation in rhetoric, suggesting potential retaliation that could draw in actors beyond traditional Middle East boundaries. Meanwhile, the continued Israeli operations in Lebanon indicate active military engagement persists even under ceasefire terms.

The statements underscore the fragility of current regional arrangements and the risk of broader conflict if either side perceives further provocation. Both developments suggest the ceasefire framework may be under strain.

Watch for whether the U.S. or Israel respond to Iran's threat, and whether the Lebanese ceasefire holds through its July deadline.
