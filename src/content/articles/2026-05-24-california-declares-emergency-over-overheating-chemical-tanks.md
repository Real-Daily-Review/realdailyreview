# California Declares Emergency Over Chemical Leak Risk

California Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency Saturday after overheated chemical tanks at an aerospace facility in Orange County threatened to rupture, prompting mass evacuations in the region.

Thousands of residents in Garden Grove have been evacuated as officials warn that a tank containing approximately 7,000 gallons of methyl methacrylate could explode, according to BBC World. Local emergency responders first attempted to cool the overheated tanks on Thursday, but the situation escalated enough to trigger the state-level emergency declaration.

The Washington Examiner reported that around 40,000 people have been told to leave the area as crews work to contain the chemical leak. The emergency response involves efforts to prevent the tanks from reaching critical temperatures that could cause an explosion.

Methyl methacrylate is a volatile chemical used in aerospace manufacturing. The exact cause of the tank overheating has not been detailed in available reports, nor have officials provided a timeline for when residents might return to their homes.
