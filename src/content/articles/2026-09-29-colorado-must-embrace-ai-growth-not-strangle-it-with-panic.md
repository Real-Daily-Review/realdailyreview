# Colorado Should Embrace AI Growth, Not Strangle It With Panic

Colorado has a genuine opportunity to lead in artificial intelligence and data center development—but only if state leaders resist the siren song of regulatory overreach and AI fearmongering.

A new Magellan Strategies survey, reported by Complete Colorado, finds solid reasons for Colorado to bet on AI rather than join the chorus of doomsayers. The state's tech infrastructure, workforce, and business climate position it to capture high-wage jobs and economic growth in one of the fastest-expanding sectors.

Yet the AI panic narrative persists. Even Pope Leo's recent encyclical on artificial intelligence, covered by 9News Denver, emphasized safety concerns and the need to "protect humanity" from AI risks—language that, while theologically framed, echoes the regulatory impulse many progressives use to justify strangling innovation with red tape.

Here's the conservative reality: Yes, AI requires thoughtful oversight. But Colorado shouldn't handicap its own economy with premature restrictions while competitors in Texas, Arizona, and other states welcome data centers and AI firms with open arms. Overregulation kills jobs and investment faster than any hypothetical AI threat.

Colorado taxpayers and workers benefit from a thriving tech sector. State leaders should focus on smart, light-touch guardrails—not the heavy-handed restrictions progressives favor.

Watch whether Colorado's legislature resists calls for AI-specific regulations that could drive companies and capital elsewhere.
