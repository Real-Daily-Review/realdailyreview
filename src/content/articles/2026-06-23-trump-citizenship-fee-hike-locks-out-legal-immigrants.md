# Trump Administration Proposes Steep Citizenship Fee Hike, Eliminates Waivers

The Trump administration is pushing to jack up citizenship application fees by $570 while axing reduced-fee options and waivers entirely, according to 9News Denver—a move that will price out working-class Americans seeking to naturalize.

The fee increase represents a significant barrier to legal immigration pathways, hitting hardest those with modest incomes who already navigate a costly, years-long process. Conservatives who champion merit-based immigration and orderly legal entry should scrutinize whether higher fees serve border security or simply pad federal coffers while discouraging legitimate applicants.

The proposal eliminates fee waivers that currently help low-income applicants afford the process—a policy shift that inverts the stated conservative principle of removing obstacles to legal immigration while securing the border. If the goal is encouraging lawful citizenship over illegal entry, raising costs contradicts that objective.

Colorado's immigrant communities—particularly in Denver and agricultural regions—will feel this squeeze acutely. Legal immigrants already contribute to Colorado's workforce and tax base; pricing them out of citizenship doesn't strengthen either.

**Watch whether Republican lawmakers in Congress push back on this fee structure, or whether the administration doubles down on revenue-raising over immigration reform.**
