# Payton Takes Back Play-Calling Reins as Broncos Offense Sputters

Sean Payton is reasserting control over Denver's offense after delegating play-calling to coordinator Davis Webb proved ineffective through four games. According to KDVR Fox31 Denver and 9News, the head coach will now collaborate directly on game-day calls rather than ceding authority entirely—a tacit admission that the offseason restructuring hasn't delivered results.

The Broncos' offense ranks near the bottom of the NFL, forcing Payton's hand ahead of Week 5's matchup against the Chargers. While Payton retains overall game-planning duties, the shift signals frustration with Webb's early performance and underscores the challenge of building a winning roster in Denver's competitive AFC West.
