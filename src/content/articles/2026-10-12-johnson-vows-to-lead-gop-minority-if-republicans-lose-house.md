# Speaker Johnson Signals He'll Lead GOP Minority if Republicans Lose House

House Speaker Mike Johnson says he's prepared to stay on as Republican leader even if the GOP loses its House majority in November—a signal of confidence that contradicts some polling headwinds facing the party heading into the midterms.

According to *Just The News* and the *Washington Examiner*, the Louisiana Republican told colleagues he expects to be asked to "continue leading this conference" should Republicans fall short. Johnson framed the decision in personal terms, saying he wants to be of "highest and best use to God and my country."

The statement comes as Republicans navigate economic uncertainty and mixed midterm polling. Democrats, predictably, will argue the Speaker's comments reflect GOP anxiety about losing ground. Republicans counter that Johnson's resolve signals party unity and a forward-looking strategy—whether in the majority or minority.

**The Colorado angle:** Colorado Republicans are watching closely. The state's competitive House seats (particularly CD-3 and CD-7) could determine control of the chamber. A Johnson-led minority would shape GOP strategy in those districts heading into 2026.

**What to watch:** Whether Johnson's confidence holds if November polling tightens further, and whether Colorado GOP candidates distance themselves from or embrace national leadership messaging.
