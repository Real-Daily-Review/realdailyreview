# Socialist Candidates Fuel National Debate Over Far-Left Governance

Far-left socialist candidates backed by progressive Democratic leaders are drawing sharp rebukes from Republicans and conservatives nationwide, raising questions about the direction of major American cities.

NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani, himself a self-described socialist, is standing behind congressional candidate Darializa Avila Chevalier despite resurfaced deleted social media posts attacking the U.S., police, and border security (Fox News Politics). Meanwhile, in Washington, D.C., self-described socialist Janeese Lewis George is running for mayor in the Democratic primary—prompting President Trump to vow he would "take back" D.C. with federal control if she wins (Washington Examiner).

Republicans argue these candidates represent a dangerous expansion of socialist ideology into mainstream Democratic politics. Conservatives warn that socialist governance threatens economic freedom, public safety, and constitutional governance. The embrace of such candidates by established Democratic leaders signals the party's leftward drift.

While Colorado has not seen comparable socialist mayoral races, the state's progressive legislature and Democratic statewide leadership have pursued similar regulatory and spending agendas. Coloradans should watch whether national socialist movements influence state and local Democratic candidates in 2024 and beyond—particularly in Denver and Boulder, where progressive politics already dominate.
