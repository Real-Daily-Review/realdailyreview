# Colorado Streaming Subscribers Have Days Left to Claim $50M Disney Settlement

Colorado consumers who paid inflated Disney streaming prices may still qualify for a piece of a $50 million settlement, but time is running out to file claims.

According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, subscribers of certain Disney streaming platforms—including Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN+—can apply for compensation in a lawsuit settlement over pricing practices. The settlement addresses allegations that Disney charged excessive rates to consumers, and eligible Coloradans need to submit claims before the deadline passes.

This settlement reflects a broader consumer pushback against streaming giants' pricing strategies. As platforms like Disney+ have raised subscription costs repeatedly over the past three years, many households have grown frustrated with the cumulative expense of maintaining multiple subscriptions. The settlement provides at least some recourse for those who felt squeezed by Disney's pricing power.

For Colorado families already stretched by inflation and rising costs of living, recovering even partial reimbursement matters. Every dollar returned to taxpayers' pockets is one less the state's households need to earn to maintain their standard of living.

**What to watch:** Coloradans should verify their eligibility and submit claims immediately—settlement deadlines are notoriously short, and unclaimed funds often revert to the defendant or state coffers rather than reaching consumers.
