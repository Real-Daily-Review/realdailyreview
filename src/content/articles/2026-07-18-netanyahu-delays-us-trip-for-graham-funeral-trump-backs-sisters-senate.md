# Netanyahu Postpones US Visit to Honor Graham at Funeral; Trump Backs Sister's Senate Bid

Senator Lindsey Graham's unexpected death last week has triggered a cascade of political developments—and international diplomatic adjustments. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is postponing his U.S. trip to attend Graham's funeral services, scheduled for July 28 in Washington, D.C., and July 29 in South Carolina (The Daily Wire). Graham, a stalwart Israel advocate, commanded respect across the geopolitical establishment; world leaders prompted funeral organizers to delay services to accommodate foreign dignitaries paying respects.

The succession battle is already underway. President Trump endorsed Darline Graham Nordone, the late senator's sister, to run in South Carolina's August 11 special Republican primary—even before she publicly committed to candidacy (The Daily Wire). Trump's early backing signals his influence over GOP primary dynamics remains formidable.

For Colorado conservatives, Graham's passing removes a reliable voice for strong national defense, pro-Israel foreign policy, and fiscal restraint—positions that have shaped Republican strategy for two decades. His successor will shape Senate dynamics on energy policy, military spending, and international trade—issues affecting Colorado's defense contractors and energy sector.

Watch whether Trump's endorsement of Nordone holds sway in the special primary, and whether her eventual Senate record aligns with Graham's hawkish internationalism or charts a different course.
