# Trump's Border Wall Delays Risk Fracturing His Conservative Base

The Trump administration's halting progress on southern border wall construction is testing the patience of the president's core supporters, who view border security as a defining campaign promise (Washington Examiner). After years of shifting timelines and incomplete projects, the ongoing dispute over wall funding and construction priorities is exposing cracks in Trump's political foundation heading into 2025.

For Colorado conservatives, border security remains a top-tier concern. Illegal immigration strains state resources, drives down wages for working-class Coloradans, and complicates law enforcement efforts across the state. Republicans have consistently argued that a completed border barrier is essential to controlling illegal crossings and restoring sovereignty over the southern frontier—not merely a symbolic gesture, but operational necessity.

The Washington Examiner reports that the Trump administration is poised to resume construction on a 30-foot barrier in West Texas, but the stop-and-start pattern has frustrated grassroots conservatives who delivered Trump his 2016 and 2020 victories. Without clear timelines and full funding commitments, skeptics worry the wall will remain an unfulfilled promise rather than a completed project.

**Watch for:** Whether Trump administration officials clarify construction schedules and secure sustained congressional funding to complete border barrier projects before 2026 midterms.
