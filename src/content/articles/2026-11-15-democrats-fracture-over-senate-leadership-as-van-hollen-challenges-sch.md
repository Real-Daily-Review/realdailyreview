# Democrats in Disarray as Van Hollen Challenges Schumer for Senate Leadership

Senate Democrats are tearing themselves apart over who should lead their caucus, with Maryland Sen. Chris Van Hollen mounting a direct challenge to Minority Leader Chuck Schumer's grip on power. Van Hollen claims he has an "open lane" and that senators are defecting from Schumer's camp, while Schumer counters that Van Hollen is spreading "misinformation" about vote counts (Washington Examiner).

The infighting exposes deep fractures in the Democratic caucus heading into 2026. Meanwhile, incoming Mississippi Sen. Scott Colom is playing kingmaker, remaining noncommittal while warning Republicans they'll face electoral "wipeout" in 2028 if they don't embrace government-run healthcare solutions and prop up rural hospitals—a clear signal that Democrats plan to run hard against GOP healthcare messaging.
