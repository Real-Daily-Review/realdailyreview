# Two Democrats Break Ranks to Back GOP Anti-Socialism Resolution

House Speaker Mike Johnson just proved that even Democrats are tired of their party's socialist drift—and it's a warning sign for progressives nationwide.

In a stunning 210-208 vote this week, Reps. Marie Gluesenkamp Perez (D-WA) and Jared Golden (D-ME) joined all House Republicans to advance a resolution denouncing socialism, blindsiding Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries and his caucus (Washington Examiner). The move came as House Republicans threatened to derail Johnson's spending agenda unless the anti-socialism measure got a floor vote (Fox News Politics).

**The conservative case:** Republicans argue that socialism—government control of production, wealth redistribution, and crushing regulation—is fundamentally incompatible with American prosperity and individual liberty. The fact that two Democrats felt compelled to vote their conscience on this issue suggests the party's leftward lurch on spending, climate mandates, and regulatory overreach has become untenable even to moderates.

**Democrats counter** that the resolution is a political stunt designed to tar mainstream progressive policy as "socialism," conflating social programs with authoritarian economics.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado voters, particularly in swing districts and rural areas, have consistently rejected far-left economic policies. This vote signals that even Democratic incumbents in competitive terrain recognize the electoral liability of being tied to socialist rhetoric.

Watch whether this fracture widens—and whether Colorado's moderate Democrats distance themselves from the national party's spending agenda heading into 2026.
