# Jack Smith's 'No Kings' Moment Exposes the Two-Tiered Justice System

Former Special Counsel Jack Smith appeared at a Maryland "Power to the People Fest" wearing a Bruce Springsteen "No Kings" shirt—a choice that didn't escape Trump's notice. Trump fired back on social media, calling Smith a "Deranged" and "Crooked Prosecutor" (Just The News).

The event, headlined by Springsteen, was billed as a get-out-the-vote rally emphasizing "the power everyday human beings have when they come together" to shape the country (Just The News). Springsteen himself railed against ICE and delivered anti-Trump messaging to the crowd (Breitbart).

**The conservative take:** Smith's public activism at a partisan political event underscores what Republicans have long argued—that the prosecutions of Trump were weaponized by a politicized Justice Department, not impartial law enforcement. A special counsel appearing in campaign garb at an anti-Trump rally sends a clear message about whose side he was really on.

For Colorado conservatives, this reinforces the broader concern about federal overreach and selective prosecution. When unelected bureaucrats use their power to target political opponents while openly campaigning against them, it's not justice—it's tyranny dressed in a Springsteen t-shirt.

Watch whether Congress demands answers about Smith's conduct and whether the DOJ finally acknowledges the politicization that plagued the Trump investigations.
