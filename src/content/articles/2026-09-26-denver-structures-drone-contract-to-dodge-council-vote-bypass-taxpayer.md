# Denver Dodges Accountability on Surveillance Drone Deal

Denver police are drafting a contract for Axon surveillance drones deliberately structured to skirt City Council oversight—sitting just under the $500,000 threshold that would trigger a mandatory vote (9News Denver). The maneuver allows city bureaucrats to bypass elected representatives and taxpayer scrutiny on a major surveillance expansion.

This is government by accounting trick. Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue that deliberately structuring contracts to avoid democratic review—even if technically legal—undermines transparency and public trust. Denver residents deserve a full Council debate on whether police drones represent smart public safety or creeping surveillance overreach.

Meanwhile, Denver and Aurora police continue facing real threats on the streets. A Denver motorcycle officer was seriously injured in a crash, and an Aurora K-9 officer was stabbed responding to a call at an apartment complex, prompting an "Honor Run" this Sunday (KDVR Fox31).

The contrast is stark: while officers risk their lives in the field, city hall games the system to expand surveillance powers without public input. Coloradans should demand their elected representatives demand transparency—no more contract gymnastics designed to dodge the voters' representatives.

Watch whether Denver City Council demands a full vote on the drone program or lets bureaucrats slip it through the back door.
