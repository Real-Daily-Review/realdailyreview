# Biden DOJ's School Board Probe Became Political Weapon, Internal Warnings Show

The Biden Justice Department weaponized federal law enforcement against parents opposing school board policies, according to newly released findings that vindicate conservative concerns about politicized prosecution.

Attorney General Merrick Garland's 2021 memo directing the FBI to investigate threats against school board members sparked internal alarm at the DOJ itself. Officials warned the initiative risked becoming an "Anti-MAGA Task Force" targeting political opponents rather than genuine threats, *Fox News* and the *Washington Examiner* reported Monday, citing findings from the Trump administration's Weaponization Working Group.

The revelation confirms what conservatives argued at the time: the Biden administration used federal law enforcement to chill parental speech at school board meetings, many of which involved Colorado parents protesting curriculum changes, mask mandates, and gender ideology in schools. Rather than distinguish between legitimate protest and actual violence, the DOJ cast a wide net that chilled First Amendment activity.

Republicans have long contended that the Biden Justice Department weaponized itself against political enemies. These internal warnings—from Biden's own officials—prove the concern wasn't partisan paranoia but a documented institutional problem.

Colorado taxpayers and parents should watch whether state and local law enforcement agencies face similar pressure to investigate school board critics under the guise of "public safety."
