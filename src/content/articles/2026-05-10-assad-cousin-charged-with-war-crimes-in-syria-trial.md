# Assad Cousin Charged With War Crimes in Historic Syria Trial

Atef Najib, a cousin of former Syrian President Bashar al-Assad, faces at least 10 charges in a landmark trial, according to Al Jazeera and Deutsche Welle. The charges include murder, torture, and responsibility for massacres stemming from Syria's 2011 uprising.

The trial marks a significant moment for Syrian accountability efforts following the regime's fall. Najib held a senior security position during the conflict and is accused of overseeing systematic abuses against civilians who participated in anti-government protests.

The specific charges reflect allegations that Najib bore responsibility for widespread atrocities committed by security forces under his command. His prosecution represents one of the first major judicial proceedings targeting senior figures from Assad's inner circle.

The case underscores ongoing international and domestic efforts to document and prosecute those allegedly responsible for war crimes during Syria's civil war. Watch for developments in the trial proceedings and whether other high-ranking Assad officials face similar charges.
