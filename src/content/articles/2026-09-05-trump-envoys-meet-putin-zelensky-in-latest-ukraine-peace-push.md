# Trump Envoys Head to Moscow and Kyiv in Latest Push for Ukraine Peace Deal

President Trump's diplomatic team is gambling that face-to-face talks with Putin and Zelensky can break the stalemate in Ukraine—but conservatives are split on whether the mission legitimizes Russian propaganda or represents serious peacemaking.

Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff are heading to Moscow this weekend to meet with Putin, followed by a trip to Kyiv, according to *Just The News*. The envoys previously visited Moscow in January; this marks their first Ukraine visit since Trump took office. The stated goal: determine if any negotiated settlement is possible to end the nearly five-year conflict.

The move reflects Trump's campaign promise to end the war quickly. But the *Washington Examiner* editorial board warns the mission risks handing Putin a propaganda win. By meeting with the Russian leader again, Witkoff and Kushner may appear to validate Putin's claims of sincerity while he continues military operations—a concern Ukrainian President Zelensky himself raised.

For Colorado conservatives, the Ukraine question hinges on America's strategic interests and fiscal responsibility. Endless aid commitments drain resources better spent at home; conversely, a botched negotiation could embolden authoritarian aggression globally.

**Watch whether these talks produce concrete movement toward a ceasefire or simply become another diplomatic theater that extends U.S. entanglement.**
