# House Republicans Block NDAA Over SAVE America Act Dispute; Chamber Shutters for July 4

House Republicans have effectively halted legislative business, blocking procedural votes on the National Defense Authorization Act and forcing Speaker Mike Johnson to dismiss members for a week-long July 4 recess. The standoff centers on the SAVE America Act, which a coalition of GOP members refuses to advance without explicit inclusion in the defense bill.

According to the Daily Caller, 14 House Republicans voted against the rule allowing the NDAA to proceed, torpedoing the measure. The Washington Examiner reports this marks the second consecutive week GOP leadership has canceled floor votes due to inability to pass procedural motions—a rare display of intra-party leverage over spending and policy priorities.

Republicans argue the SAVE America Act represents essential fiscal discipline, though specific provisions remain contested within the caucus. The blockade signals deepening fractures over how aggressively the GOP should challenge spending measures, even defense-related ones.

**Colorado angle:** Defense spending impacts Colorado's military installations and aerospace contractors; prolonged legislative gridlock could delay appropriations affecting Fort Carson, Schriever Space Force Base, and related industries.

Conservatives and taxpayers should monitor whether Johnson can broker a compromise or whether hardliners maintain their veto power through the fall legislative calendar.
