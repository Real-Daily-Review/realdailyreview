# Trump Barnstorms Ohio, Eyes GOP Midterm Surge—Colorado Republicans Watch Playbook

Trump is pulling out all the stops to energize Republican voters before the midterms, headlining a rally in Vandalia, Ohio, where he urged supporters to "pretend I'm on the ballot" and deliver wins for GOP candidates Jon Husted and Vivek Ramaswamy (Washington Examiner). The former president is executing a 10-state tour aimed at firing up the base in competitive races, with former NFL coach Jon Gruden joining the effort to stump for Ohio Republicans.

The rally underscores Trump's continued influence over the GOP base—a reality Colorado Republicans cannot ignore as they prepare their own 2024 strategy. Trump's personal brand remains the party's most potent mobilizing force, particularly among working-class and rural voters who powered GOP gains in 2016 and 2020.

Democrats argue Trump's dominance over the party limits its appeal to suburban and college-educated voters, but Republicans counter that his outsider message and economic record resonate with the coalition that wins elections in purple states like Colorado. The question for state GOP operatives: Can they harness Trump's energy without becoming dependent on his endorsement in races where suburban Denver and Boulder voters hold the balance?

Watch whether Trump's tour gains traction in swing states and whether Colorado Republicans invite him to campaign here before 2024.
