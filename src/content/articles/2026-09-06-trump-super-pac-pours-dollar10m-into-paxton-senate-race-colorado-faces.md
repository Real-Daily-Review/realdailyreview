# Trump Super PAC Deploys $10M for Paxton Senate Fight; Colorado Braces for Federal Pressure

President Trump's MAGA Inc. super PAC is flooding $10 million into the Texas Senate race to rescue Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton against Democrat James James Talarico, according to new FEC filings reported by *The Daily Wire*. The massive financial injection signals Trump's determination to defend a key ally in a competitive general election battle.

Meanwhile, Colorado faces its own Trump-administration headwinds. The *Colorado Sun* reports that the state is "under attack by an administration that seeks to coerce the state to relinquish its constitutional powers — or else," citing what critics characterize as federal pressure on state sovereignty.

Republicans argue Trump's support for Paxton reflects the party's commitment to electing conservative attorneys general who will challenge progressive overreach in courts. Democrats counter that Trump's campaign apparatus amounts to a "campaign of retribution" targeting states that resist his agenda.

For Colorado, the tension underscores a broader conservative-versus-progressive battle over federalism: whether states retain authority over energy policy, water rights, gun regulations, and election administration, or whether Washington—under either party—can dictate terms to the states.
