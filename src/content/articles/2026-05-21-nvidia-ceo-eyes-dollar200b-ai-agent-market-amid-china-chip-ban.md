# Nvidia CEO Touts $200B AI Agent Market as China Bans Advanced Chip

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang identified a "brand new" $200 billion market opportunity in CPUs designed for AI agents, according to TechCrunch, signaling the company's next growth frontier beyond data center GPUs.

The announcement comes as China added Nvidia's RTX 5090D V2 to its list of banned goods at customs checkpoints, according to Ars Technica. The ban took effect last Friday while Huang was visiting the country, marking an escalation in restrictions on advanced Nvidia chips destined for China.

The timing underscores mounting geopolitical tensions over semiconductor exports. The U.S. has imposed successive export controls on high-performance chips to China, citing national security concerns. China has responded with its own restrictions on foreign semiconductor technology.

Huang's pivot toward AI agent processors reflects Nvidia's strategy to diversify revenue streams as competition in GPU markets intensifies. The company has dominated AI infrastructure spending, but faces pressure to identify new applications and markets.

Watch for Nvidia's next earnings call and guidance on how export restrictions may impact revenue from China and Asia-Pacific markets.
