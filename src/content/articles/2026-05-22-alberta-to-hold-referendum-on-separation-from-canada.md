# Alberta to Hold Referendum on Separation From Canada

Alberta will hold a referendum next October on whether to remain in Canada or pursue independence, according to Premier Danielle Smith, marking the first time a Canadian province other than Quebec has put separation to voters.

The vote is scheduled for October 19, according to the Washington Examiner. Albertans will decide whether to stay in Canada or authorize a process for a binding independence referendum, the BBC reported.

The move follows months of campaigning by separatist groups in the oil-rich western province, according to CNBC. The referendum represents a significant political moment for Canada, which has faced previous separation movements primarily from Quebec.

The outcome will determine whether Alberta proceeds with formal steps toward independence or reaffirms its place within the Canadian federation. Watch for how federal officials respond and whether the referendum energizes or marginalizes the separatist movement.
