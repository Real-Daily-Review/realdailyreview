# South Africa Faces Renewed Scrutiny Over Xenophobic Violence Claims

South Africa is confronting mounting allegations of xenophobic attacks as neighboring countries escalate pressure for international investigation. Ghana has called on the African Union to dispatch a fact-finding mission to assess the situation, according to BBC World.

The South African government has pushed back against the claims, condemning what it characterizes as "fake videos" of alleged xenophobic attacks, the BBC reported. The denial comes as tensions resurface around a pattern that has recurred periodically in the country.

Deutsche Welle's timeline documents recurring waves of xenophobic violence in South Africa, underscoring that this is not an isolated incident but part of a longer history of such episodes.

The dispute centers on the authenticity and scale of recent incidents. South Africa's dismissal of video evidence contrasts with Ghana's concern sufficient to warrant continental oversight. The conflicting narratives—between Pretoria's denial and regional alarm—reflect broader tensions over how xenophobic violence is documented, verified, and addressed.

Watch for the African Union's response to Ghana's request and whether an independent fact-finding mission proceeds.
