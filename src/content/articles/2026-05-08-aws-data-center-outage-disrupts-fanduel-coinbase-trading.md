# AWS Data Center Outage Disrupts Trading Platforms

An Amazon Web Services data center outage in Northern Virginia has disrupted trading operations at major platforms including FanDuel and Coinbase, according to CNBC. AWS said it is working to resolve the issue, which the company attributed to overheating at the facility. The cloud provider indicated recovery would take hours.

The outage highlights the concentration risk in cloud infrastructure, as both cryptocurrency and sports betting platforms rely on AWS for core operations. No timeline for full service restoration was provided by AWS at the time of reporting.

Watch for updates on the scope of affected services and any statements from FanDuel and Coinbase on customer impact and compensation.
