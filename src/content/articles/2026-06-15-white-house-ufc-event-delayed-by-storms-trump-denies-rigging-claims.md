# White House UFC Event Delayed by Storms; Trump Denies Rigging Claims

The White House UFC Freedom 250 fights scheduled for Sunday evening were pushed back an hour due to approaching thunderstorms, with organizers prepared to enforce 30-minute lightning delays as needed (The Daily Wire).

The event faced additional controversy when social media messages surfaced allegedly showing Eric Trump asking former UFC star Daniel Cormier whether the White House fights were rigged. Trump denied the claims on X, calling the messages "completely fake" and stating he had never contacted Cormier (Washington Examiner). Trump attributed the messages to artificial intelligence generation.

The seven scheduled fights were expected to run approximately four hours. The UFC's weather contingency protocols allowed for operational flexibility while prioritizing fighter and spectator safety.
