# Boulder County Mobile Home Residents Face $42.5M Buyout Deadline as County Eyes Commission Expansion

San Lazaro residents in Boulder County have until December 8 to scrape together $42.5 million—or lose their mobile home park to an outside buyer. The 213 households are racing against the clock after an unidentified bidder made the offer, according to 9News Denver, forcing a community land trust scramble that highlights how Colorado's skyrocketing property values are pricing out working families.

Meanwhile, Boulder County commissioners are pushing to expand their board, claiming growth demands more representation (Denver Post). Republicans should scrutinize whether this is genuine necessity or a power grab—larger boards often mean more spending and slower decision-making. The expansion push comes as Douglas County faces similar pressure.

On the political front, a Lone Tree Democrat running for Colorado's House of Representatives was arrested in May for public intoxication, per Denver Post court records—a reminder that candidate vetting matters in competitive districts.

For Colorado conservatives and taxpayers: watch whether Boulder County's commission expansion passes and what it costs in administrative overhead. Also monitor whether San Lazaro residents can actually compete with deep-pocketed outside investors—a cautionary tale about land-use policy and affordability in blue counties.
