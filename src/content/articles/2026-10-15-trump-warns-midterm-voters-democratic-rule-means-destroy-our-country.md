# Trump Escalates Midterm Warnings: Democrats Will 'Destroy Our Country'

Former President Donald Trump is barnstorming the West ahead of the midterms, warning voters that Democratic control means economic collapse and open borders—a message that resonates with Colorado conservatives alarmed by inflation, immigration chaos, and progressive governance.

At rallies in Arizona and Montana (Just The News), Trump hammered Democratic leadership, telling Arizona voters they were "living in hell" under Democratic rule and warning that if Democrats regain Congress, they would "immediately reopen your borders" and "sell out Arizona to the cartels" (Just The News). He rallied behind Republican candidates including Arizona's Andy Biggs (Fox News, New York Post).

Republicans argue that Democratic policies—from unchecked spending to lax border enforcement—have triggered inflation, energy costs, and security threats that hit working families hardest. Conservatives say the midterms are a referendum on whether voters want to reverse course on taxes, regulation, and immigration enforcement.

Democrats counter that Republicans offer no real solutions and rely on fearmongering instead of policy substance.

For Colorado, the stakes include energy policy (Democrats vs. oil-and-gas development), water rights, and Second Amendment protections—all on the ballot indirectly through congressional races. Watch whether Trump's anti-establishment messaging moves Colorado's independent voters, who will decide if Republicans can flip the state's congressional delegation.
