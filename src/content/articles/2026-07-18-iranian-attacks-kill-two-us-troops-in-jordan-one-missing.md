# Iranian Attacks Kill Two U.S. Troops in Jordan; One Missing

American military personnel are dying in the Middle East because the Biden-Harris administration has failed to deter Iranian aggression.

Two U.S. service members were killed and one remains missing after Iranian ballistic missile and drone attacks targeted a base in Jordan on Friday, U.S. Central Command confirmed Saturday (Fox News, Daily Wire). Four additional troops were medically evacuated to Jordanian hospitals but have since been discharged.

The strikes represent escalating Iranian aggression in a region where U.S. forces are stretched thin supporting partner nations. The Washington Examiner notes the attacks followed a breakdown in ceasefire efforts, underscoring how weakness invites conflict.

Conservatives argue the administration's inconsistent Middle East policy—including the chaotic Afghanistan withdrawal and reluctance to project strength against Iranian proxies—has emboldened Tehran to test American resolve. Republicans contend that clear red lines and credible military deterrence prevent exactly these kinds of attacks.

The identities of the fallen and missing service members have not been disclosed. Colorado families with loved ones deployed to the region are watching closely as tensions with Iran intensify over control of the Strait of Hormuz.
