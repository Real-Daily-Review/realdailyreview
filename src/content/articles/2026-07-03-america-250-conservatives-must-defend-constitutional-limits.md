# America's 250th Birthday: A Moment to Reaffirm Constitutional Limits on Government

As America approaches its semiquincentennial on July 4th, conservatives should use the occasion to recommit to the founding principles that made this nation exceptional—and to demand that government stay within its constitutional bounds.

The milestone arrives amid considerable uncertainty about America's direction. The *Washington Examiner* reports that foreign allies are preparing celebratory gestures even as tariffs, international conflicts, and questions about U.S. foreign policy create headwinds. Meanwhile, the *National Review* notes the historical irony that America's 250th falls on Shabbat, underscoring how the nation's founders repeatedly drew on Judeo-Christian principles and ancient wisdom to construct a limited republic.

For Colorado conservatives, this moment matters. The founders envisioned a federal government constrained by enumerated powers—not the sprawling regulatory state that now dictates energy policy, water management, and land use across the West. Colorado's energy workers, ranchers, and property owners live daily under federal overreach the Constitution never authorized.

Rather than celebrate government expansion, this anniversary should prompt hard questions: Are we honoring the Constitution's limits, or have we abandoned them? Are we protecting individual liberty and free markets, or surrendering both to progressive bureaucrats?

Watch whether Colorado's Republican leadership uses America 250 to articulate a genuine defense of constitutional governance—or merely offers patriotic rhetoric while accepting the status quo.
