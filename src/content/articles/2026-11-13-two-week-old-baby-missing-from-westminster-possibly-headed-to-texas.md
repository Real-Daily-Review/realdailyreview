# Colorado Amber Alert: 2-Week-Old Baby Missing from Westminster

A two-week-old boy is missing from Westminster after being last seen with his father near 128th Avenue and Huron Street, according to an Endangered Missing Alert issued by the Colorado Bureau of Investigation (KDVR Fox31 Denver).

Police believe Zuriel Calloway is with his father, who may be attempting to transport the infant to Texas (9News Denver). The alert was triggered under Colorado's endangered missing child protocol, which allows law enforcement to issue rapid public notifications when a child's safety is at immediate risk.

Authorities have not released additional details about the circumstances of the disappearance or the father's identity. The case underscores the critical role of swift inter-agency coordination and public alerts in locating missing children across state lines.

Coloradans with information are urged to contact the Colorado Bureau of Investigation or local law enforcement immediately.
