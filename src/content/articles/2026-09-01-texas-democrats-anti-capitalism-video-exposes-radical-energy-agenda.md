# Texas Democrat's Anti-Capitalism Rhetoric Resurfaces—A Warning for Energy States Like Colorado

A resurfaced 2020 video shows Texas Democrat U.S. Senate nominee James Talarico calling capitalism an "oppressive system" alongside white supremacy and climate change, according to *The Daily Wire*, *Just The News*, and Breitbart. During a Sierra Club virtual forum, Talarico used language echoing Democratic Socialists of America rhetoric, speaking of "dismantling" capitalism while predicting the Texas oil industry won't survive.

The remarks matter for Colorado. Talarico's anti-energy stance mirrors the progressive playbook already reshaping Colorado's economy: hostility toward oil and gas development, regulatory strangulation of energy workers, and ideological opposition to free-market energy production. If Democrats gain Senate seats in energy-producing states, expect accelerated federal attacks on drilling, mining, and the jobs that sustain rural Colorado communities.

Republicans argue Talarico's 2020 radicalism reveals his true agenda—one he's now softening for a general-election audience. The contrast between his 2020 socialism and his current Senate messaging, they say, shows he'll govern far left if elected. Democrats counter that his views have evolved and that he's focused on mainstream climate concerns.

Conservatives and Colorado taxpayers should watch whether national Democratic candidates continue hiding their anti-energy records while pushing the same destructive policies in Washington.
