# Socialist Surge in NYC Primaries Signals Leftward Lurch for Democrats Nationwide

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani's socialist allies swept Democratic primary races Tuesday night, cementing his status as a kingmaker within the party and signaling a troubling shift toward far-left ideology that could reshape national Democratic politics heading into 2028.

All three Democratic Socialist candidates backed by Mamdani won their races, including Darializa Avila Chevalier—described by Fox News as having "a history of anti-American rhetoric"—who ousted five-term Democratic incumbent Adriano Espaillat in New York's 13th District. Claire Valdez also secured the Democratic nomination in New York's 7th Congressional District (Fox News, Washington Examiner).

Even CNN's Van Jones acknowledged the magnitude of the shift, telling viewers that "The Democratic Socialists are an insurgency" and "The left is on the march" (Breitbart).
