# FIFA Reverses Balogun Ban After Trump Intervention—USMNT Gets Star Forward Back for Belgium Clash

FIFA's sudden reversal of Folarin Balogun's one-game suspension has the U.S. Men's National Team's leading scorer cleared to play Monday against Belgium in the World Cup Round of 16—but the decision came only after President Trump personally called FIFA's president to challenge the controversial red card.

Balogun received the red card for stepping on Bosnia and Herzegovina's Tarik Muharemović during last week's group-stage match. FIFA initially upheld the ban, which would have sidelined the American forward in a critical knockout game. According to the Washington Examiner and Breitbart, Trump intervened directly, calling FIFA leadership to request a review.

FIFA suspended the ban Sunday afternoon, reversing course without detailed public explanation. USMNT legend Landon Donovan endorsed the outcome, telling the New York Post that FIFA "got the decision right," though he acknowledged the optics remained contentious.

The incident raises questions about presidential influence over international sports governance—and whether executive pressure should determine athletic eligibility. While American soccer fans celebrate Balogun's availability for a crucial match, the reversal underscores how even global sports bodies respond to high-level political pressure.
