# Trump Touts Record Employment as Democrats Float Third Impeachment

President Trump claims more Americans are working than at any point in U.S. history, crediting his administration's economic policies and pushing back against what he calls media refusal to report the gains. "The American People elected me to deliver HISTORIC Economic Results," Trump said, emphasizing his responsibility to communicate achievements the "Fake News refuses to do it" (Just The News).

Meanwhile, House Democrats are already signaling appetite for a third impeachment. Rep. Ro Khanna (D-CA) told NBC's *Meet the Press* that impeaching Trump again would amount to "setting a standard" if Democrats retake the House in November's midterms (Washington Examiner). Republicans argue this represents naked political weaponization—using impeachment as a routine partisan tool rather than the grave constitutional measure it was designed to be.

For Colorado, the employment picture matters directly: strong job growth supports state tax revenue and reduces pressure for tax hikes on workers and businesses. But Democrats' impeachment talk signals their 2024 playbook: obstruction and investigation over governing.

**Watch whether Colorado's Democratic delegation—particularly in competitive districts—distances itself from or embraces Khanna's third-impeachment standard.**
