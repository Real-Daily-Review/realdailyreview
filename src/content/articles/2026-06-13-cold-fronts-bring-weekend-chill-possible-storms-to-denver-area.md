# Colorado Weather Brief: Cold Front Brings Weekend Chill, Possible Storms

Denver's warm streak ends this weekend as two cold fronts push through Saturday, bringing below-normal temperatures and scattered showers, according to forecasters at KDVR Fox31 and 9News Denver.

Friday will remain warm and dry across the Front Range, but the pattern shifts sharply Saturday as the first cold front arrives. A second system will follow, dropping temperatures well below seasonal norms and triggering isolated to scattered showers—primarily Sunday—across the Denver metro and surrounding areas.

Coloradans accustomed to the state's volatile spring weather should prepare for the typical up-and-down temperature swings that characterize this season. Outdoor plans should account for the possibility of precipitation and cooler conditions by Sunday.

Watch for updated forecasts as the weekend approaches to confirm storm timing and intensity.
