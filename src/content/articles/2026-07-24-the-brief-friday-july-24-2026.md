# Real Daily Review — The Brief
## Friday, July 24, 2026

The federal government is drawing a hard line on Colorado's sanctuary-state policies, and the state's own governor is sounding the alarm about the radical left eating his party alive. It's a busy Friday for Colorado conservatives — let's get into it.

## DOJ Sues Colorado Over Illegal Immigrant Tuition Benefits

The Justice Department has filed suit against Colorado, accusing the state of unconstitutionally offering in-state tuition and financial aid to students who are in the country illegally — in direct conflict with federal law. This is a fight Colorado taxpayers should have been demanding for years. Democrats in the legislature handed scarce higher-education dollars to people who have no legal right to be here, and now Washington is finally pushing back. Colorado families paying full freight for their kids' college education deserve better than this.

## Polis Warns: Democratic Socialists Are Taking Over Colorado's Left

Even Gov. Jared Polis — no conservative — is publicly decrying the rise of Colorado's "democratic socialist" faction within his own party. For that crowd, as observers note, "justice" is about tearing down the wealthy and weaponizing language to do it. Republicans have been saying this for years: the Colorado Democratic Party has drifted hard left, and the moderates are losing control. When Polis is the voice of restraint in his own party, Colorado's political center of gravity has moved somewhere most Coloradans wouldn't recognize.

## Judges Force Release of 65,000 ICE Detainees

Federal judges across the country have ordered the release of 65,000 detainees from ICE custody, dealing a significant blow to the Trump administration's immigration enforcement operation. The White House has called it a coordinated judicial effort to undermine the president's crackdown on illegal immigration — and it's hard to argue otherwise. For Colorado, where border-driven crime, fentanyl trafficking, and illegal labor competition are real kitchen-table issues, activist judges substituting their immigration preferences for federal enforcement policy is a direct threat to public safety.

## Pentagon Official Placed on Leave After Vowing to 'Undo' Trump Policies

A senior official in the Office of the Secretary of the Navy has been placed on administrative leave after video emerged — released by James O'Keefe — showing him criticizing President Trump and stating his intention to reverse administration policies from within. Conservatives call it exactly what it looks like: a deep-state bureaucrat using a taxpayer-funded position to sabotage a democratically elected president's agenda. The Trump administration is right to act. Colorado has a significant military and defense contractor presence — the people doing that work deserve leadership that follows the chain of command.
