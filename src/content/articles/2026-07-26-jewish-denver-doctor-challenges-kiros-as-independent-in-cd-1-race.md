# Independent Doctor Challenges Kiros in CD-1, Citing Antisemitism Concerns

A Denver physician has qualified for November's ballot as an independent challenger to Democrat Melat Kiros in Colorado's 1st Congressional District, citing concerns over antisemitism and what he characterizes as radical policy positions.

Shimon Blau, a Jewish doctor, told the Denver Post he was "saddened and alarmed" by Kiros' primary victory last month. Complete Colorado reports that Blau's entry gives voters "uneasy with Kiros' antisemitism or 'democratic socialism' policy positions" an alternative to the Democratic nominee.

Kiros, a progressive Democrat who won the CD-1 primary, has faced criticism from conservatives over her stated policy positions. Republicans argue her "democratic socialism" agenda—which typically includes expanded government spending, higher taxes, and aggressive regulation—represents a threat to Colorado's economy and individual liberty. Conservatives view Blau's independent candidacy as a potential opening to reclaim a seat Democrats have held, particularly if concerns about Kiros' record resonate with centrist voters in the Denver-based district.

The race now features a three-way dynamic: Kiros representing the Democratic left, Blau as an independent moderate alternative, and the Republican nominee. Taxpayers should watch whether Blau's independent status and medical credibility can peel away Democratic-leaning moderates uncomfortable with Kiros' progressive platform.
