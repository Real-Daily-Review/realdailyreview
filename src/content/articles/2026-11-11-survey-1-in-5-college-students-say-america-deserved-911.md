# Campus Radicals Celebrate 9/11; One in Five Undergrads Say America 'Deserved' Attacks

Colorado parents sending their kids to out-of-state universities should brace themselves: a new survey reveals that more than one in five college undergraduates believe the United States somehow "deserved" the September 11 terror attacks that killed nearly 3,000 Americans.

The Buckley Institute polled 803 full-time students under 25 at four-year institutions between September 5 and 14, according to *The Daily Wire*. The findings expose what conservatives describe as systematic anti-American indoctrination on campuses—a troubling pattern of hostility toward the nation and its allies, particularly Israel.

This aligns with broader campus trends. The *Washington Examiner* notes that anti-Israel bias at international institutions remains endemic, with discrimination against the Jewish state treated as routine policy rather than exception.

**The conservative concern is clear:** American higher education increasingly teaches students to view their own country with contempt and moral relativism. When universities fail to instill patriotism or basic historical literacy—when they actively cultivate grievance narratives that justify terrorism—they betray their educational mission and their students' futures.

Colorado families investing six figures in tuition deserve to know whether their institutions are educating citizens or breeding resentment. Demand transparency from your state's universities about curriculum content and ideological balance.
