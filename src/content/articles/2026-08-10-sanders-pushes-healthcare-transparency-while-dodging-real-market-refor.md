# Bernie Sanders Finds Rare GOP Ally on Healthcare Transparency—But Don't Mistake It for Principle

Bernie Sanders is demanding Democrats ban super PACs from primaries while simultaneously pushing hospital price transparency legislation that has unexpected Republican support. The Vermont socialist's crusade against "outside spending and corrupt systems" rings hollow when paired with his simultaneous drive for more government control over healthcare pricing.

On price transparency, Sanders and Republicans do align: both want hospitals to disclose costs upfront. Republicans argue this harnesses market competition and lets patients shop for care—a genuinely conservative principle. Sanders, however, frames it as reining in a "multi-tentacled monster" fed by taxpayer dollars, implying the solution is more regulation, not less government involvement.

The super PAC ban demand exposes Sanders' real priority: controlling Democratic primary money while ignoring how government healthcare mandates themselves distort markets and inflate costs. True market reform would mean reducing federal healthcare spending and regulations, not adding transparency bureaucracy on top of an already-bloated system.

**Colorado angle:** State healthcare costs and insurance premiums directly affect working families and small businesses. Price transparency could help, but only if paired with genuine deregulation—not just more government oversight.
