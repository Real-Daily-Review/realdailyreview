# Former Senator Jay Rockefeller, Liberal Icon and Coal-State Democrat, Dies at 89

Jay Rockefeller, the West Virginia Democrat who represented a coal-producing state while championing expansive government healthcare and social welfare programs, has died at 89 at his Washington, D.C. home, according to Fox News and the Washington Examiner. His family did not disclose a cause of death.

Rockefeller served as West Virginia's governor and U.S. senator, building a decades-long political career around healthcare expansion, tax policy, and national security. He embodied a now-extinct breed: the wealthy liberal willing to champion big-government solutions in a resource-rich state dependent on energy development.

His political legacy reflects the Democratic Party's leftward shift on energy policy. Rockefeller represented West Virginia when coal still anchored the state's economy and Democratic senators could openly support the industry. Today's progressive Democrats—who have effectively declared war on coal and fossil fuels—would find little common ground with his pragmatic approach to balancing labor interests with government programs.

Colorado conservatives should note the broader lesson: as the Democratic Party has radicalized on climate and energy, it has abandoned the working-class coalitions that once sustained it in resource states. That realignment continues to reshape American politics.
