# New York Mayor's Hamas Ties Draw Netanyahu's Fire at UN

New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani's cozy relationship with anti-Israel activists has drawn international rebuke—and a direct attack from Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu at the United Nations this week.

Netanyahu didn't mince words, calling Hamas the socialist mayor's "buddies" during a fiery UN General Assembly speech (Washington Examiner). The Israeli leader specifically criticized Mamdani for his scrutiny of Israel and noted the mayor had attempted to have Netanyahu arrested while he attended the world leaders' gathering.

Gov. Kathy Hochul rushed to Mamdani's defense, claiming Netanyahu was "not telling the truth" and accusing the prime minister of smearing the mayor (New York Post). She offered no specifics refuting Netanyahu's characterization.
