# Colorado Woman's Family Sues Over ICE Agent's Fatal Shooting in Minneapolis

A Colorado resident's family is pursuing federal lawsuits after an Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer shot and killed Renee Good during an immigration enforcement operation in Minneapolis, according to reports from the Colorado Sun, 9News Denver, and KDVR Fox31.

The lawsuits—filed by Good's partner, Becca Good, and her brother, Brent Ganger—allege wrongful death and claim a conspiracy to interfere with civil rights (Just The News). The family seeks damages for Good's death and "the immense suffering Renee endured in the final moments of her life," per the Colorado Sun.

Details surrounding the shooting remain limited in available reporting, though the incident occurred during what sources describe as an immigration surge in Minnesota. The case raises questions about ICE operational protocols, use-of-force standards, and federal accountability—issues that resonate across Colorado, where immigration enforcement and federal agency oversight remain contentious political topics.

The lawsuits name the U.S. government and individual ICE officers as defendants. Conservatives and civil libertarians alike have called for transparency in federal law enforcement incidents, though perspectives differ sharply on immigration enforcement priorities.
