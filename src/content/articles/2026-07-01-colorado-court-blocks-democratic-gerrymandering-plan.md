# Colorado High Court Blocks Democrat Gerrymandering Scheme

Colorado's high court has shut down a Democratic gerrymandering plan, sparing the state from becoming another battleground in the national redistricting wars (Complete Colorado). The ruling keeps Colorado's maps out of the partisan manipulation game—at least for now.

Republicans have long warned that Democrats would weaponize redistricting to lock in electoral advantages. This court decision represents a rare check on that impulse, preventing what conservatives argue would have been a brazen power grab disguised as "fair representation."

The timing matters: Colorado Democrats are simultaneously positioning their attorney general primary as a contest over who will most aggressively use the office to target energy producers through climate litigation (Complete Colorado). That dual approach—rigging maps while weaponizing the AG's office against the energy sector—reveals the broader progressive playbook in Colorado.

For taxpayers and energy workers, the gerrymandering block is a win. A redrawn map favoring Democrats could have locked in anti-energy majorities in the legislature for a decade. But conservatives should watch whether Democrats attempt an end-run around the court's decision through other redistricting mechanisms before the next cycle.
