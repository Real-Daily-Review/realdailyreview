# Colorado Senators Stay Silent on Schumer Leadership Fight

Colorado's Democratic senators are dodging questions about whether they'll back Chuck Schumer's bid to remain Senate majority leader as he faces a challenge from Maryland's Chris Van Hollen, according to the *Denver Post*. Michael Bennet and John Hickenlooper won't commit either way—a telling silence that suggests internal party fractures over leadership and policy direction.

The leadership fight has spilled into Hollywood culture wars. Actor Mark Ruffalo called for Schumer's ouster, citing what he characterized as "entrenched religious beliefs and loyalties" influencing his positions—remarks that drew accusations of antisemitism from critics, per *Breitbart*.

For Colorado conservatives, the real story is this: when Democratic senators won't publicly defend their party's leader, it signals weakness and division. Bennet and Hickenlooper are calculating political risk in a state that's trending purple. Their silence reflects broader Democratic anxiety about messaging on Israel, energy policy, and spending—issues where Colorado voters have shown skepticism of the progressive wing.

Republicans should press both senators directly: Do they support Schumer's agenda on taxes, regulation, and energy development? Their evasion is answer enough.
