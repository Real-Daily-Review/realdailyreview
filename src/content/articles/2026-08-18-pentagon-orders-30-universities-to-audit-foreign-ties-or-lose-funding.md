# Pentagon Orders Universities to Audit Foreign Ties or Lose Federal Funding

The Pentagon is cracking down on what it sees as a national security vulnerability: American universities funneling research and taxpayer dollars to adversaries like China, Russia, and Iran.

Thirty universities—including Harvard, NYU, and Georgetown—have been ordered to audit their foreign financial and research ties or risk losing federal funding (Fox News). Schools must review identified foreign collaborations and assess exposure of "sensitive or export-controlled research" (Just The News).

**The conservative case is straightforward:** Taxpayers fund university research through federal grants. That money shouldn't flow to hostile regimes or enable technology transfer to strategic competitors. If universities want Pentagon dollars, they need transparency and accountability on where partnerships lead.

The Pentagon's move reflects growing concern that American academia has become too cozy with foreign actors—particularly China—through joint research ventures, visiting scholars, and undisclosed funding arrangements. This isn't new paranoia; it's overdue enforcement.

**What Colorado should watch:** While the sources don't specify Colorado universities in this audit wave, Colorado State University and the University of Colorado conduct significant federally funded research. If either institution has undisclosed foreign ties, they could face similar scrutiny and funding jeopardy. Coloradans deserve to know whether their tax dollars are being protected or compromised.
