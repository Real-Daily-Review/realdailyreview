# White House Security Breaches Raise Questions About Perimeter Protection

The Secret Service arrested two individuals Monday after separate incidents near the White House, including a car crash and barricade breach that triggered a lockdown (Fox News, Breitbart). The incidents underscore persistent vulnerabilities in the nation's most heavily guarded residence.

Details remain limited, but both arrests followed what authorities characterized as security breaches. One incident involved a vehicle, the other a barrier penetration—the kind of lapses that should concern any administration claiming competence in protecting the president and the seat of government.
