# NYC Mayor Launches Twitch Series, Announces $50 World Cup Lottery

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani is launching a Twitch streaming series today at 4 p.m. ET to engage directly with residents, according to TechCrunch.

The mayor simultaneously announced a lottery offering $50 World Cup tickets to 1,000 New Yorkers, which he promoted on X with a tongue-in-cheek post comparing the ticket price to other NYC experiences like street cart coffee and Staten Island Ferry rides, per the Washington Examiner.

The dual announcements represent an unconventional approach to municipal outreach, combining a gaming platform popular with younger audiences with a sports-access initiative. Mamdani's use of Twitch aligns with efforts by some elected officials to reach constituents on platforms beyond traditional media channels.

The mayor framed the World Cup lottery as a way to make premium sporting events accessible to ordinary New Yorkers at a below-market price point.

Watch for details on how many New Yorkers participate in the Twitch series and whether the World Cup ticket lottery becomes a model for future municipal giveaways.
