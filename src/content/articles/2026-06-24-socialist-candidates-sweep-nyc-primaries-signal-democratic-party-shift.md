# Socialist Wave Sweeps NYC Primaries—Warning Sign for America's Left Turn

Far-left candidates backed by NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani have swept Democratic primaries in New York, defeating establishment incumbents and signaling the progressive wing's growing grip on the party (Fox News Politics). Among the victors: Darializa Avila Chevalier, whose rhetoric National Review characterizes as fundamentally hostile to American principles.

**The conservative concern:** These victories reveal how thoroughly socialist ideology has infiltrated Democratic ranks. When party establishment figures like Hakeem Jeffries lose primary battles to unapologetic progressives, it signals the death of moderate Democrats—and a party increasingly willing to embrace anti-capitalist, anti-American messaging.

**Colorado angle:** Colorado Democrats have their own socialist contingent. As the state's Democratic Party drifts leftward on taxes, energy policy, and gun control, Colorado voters should recognize this national pattern: primary victories by far-left candidates embolden radical agendas that trickle down to state and local races. Colorado's energy sector, gun owners, and taxpayers face mounting pressure from a Democratic Party no longer tethered to centrist restraint.

Republicans argue these primary results expose the true face of modern progressivism—one hostile to free markets, individual liberty, and constitutional governance.

**Watch:** Whether Colorado Democrats embrace or distance themselves from this national socialist surge ahead of 2026 midterms.
