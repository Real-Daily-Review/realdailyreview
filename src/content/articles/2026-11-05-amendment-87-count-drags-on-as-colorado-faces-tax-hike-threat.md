# Colorado Voters Face Tax Hike Uncertainty as Amendment 87 Count Drags On

Colorado taxpayers remain in limbo as the state finishes tallying Amendment 87, a ballot measure that could fundamentally reshape how Colorado taxes income—and how much residents owe the state.

The amendment's final outcome remains unclear, but if it passes, Colorado taxpayers should brace for potential tax increases. According to KDVR Fox31 Denver, the measure could significantly alter the state's tax structure, though specifics on rate changes await the official count.

The timing matters. National Review notes that Democrats are already plotting aggressive tax strategies for 2026 and beyond, signaling that state-level tax hikes may be just the opening move in a broader progressive fiscal agenda. The Daily Wire reports that Democratic victories nationwide have emboldened the party to pursue "sweeping" tax increases—including proposals to raise federal income tax rates and expand government credits—directly threatening President Trump's tax-cut legacy.

For Colorado specifically, conservatives argue that Amendment 87 represents exactly the kind of government overreach that stifles economic growth and punishes working families. Republicans contend that higher state taxes will drive businesses and earners to more tax-friendly states, hollowing out Colorado's middle class.

**Watch for the official Amendment 87 certification and whether Colorado Democrats move to implement additional tax measures in the 2025 legislative session.**
