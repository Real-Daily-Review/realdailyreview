## Colorado Springs Faces Lawsuit Over April Election Timing

Colorado Springs is being sued over its practice of holding municipal elections in April rather than on the November general election date — a dispute that cuts to the heart of how and when Coloradans vote. Republicans argue that off-cycle elections suppress turnout and benefit entrenched political interests, not everyday voters. Anyone who has worked a polling place knows that bipartisan election teams take their jobs seriously; the question is whether the rules governing *when* those elections happen serve the public or the political class.
