# Federal Judge Sentences Would-Be Bessent Assassin to 73 Months

A Colorado connection to national security: a man who plotted to assassinate Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent—Trump's pick to manage federal finances and, by extension, Colorado's energy and tax policy—has been sentenced to over six years in federal prison.

Ryan Michael English, who identifies as transgender, admitted to traveling armed with a knife and Molotov cocktails with intent to kill Bessent during his confirmation process. U.S. District Judge Rudolph Contreras handed down 73 months in prison plus three years supervised release after English pleaded guilty in May (Just The News). English turned himself in to Capitol Police in January 2025.

The case underscores a troubling pattern: politically motivated violence targeting Trump administration officials. Bessent, now Treasury Secretary, oversees federal spending, tax policy, and energy regulation—all critical to Colorado's economy, from oil and gas development to water rights and federal land management.

Conservatives note the incident as another example of extremist political violence from the left, even as mainstream media largely ignored the story compared to coverage of right-wing threats. The sentencing sends a message that assassination plots will face serious federal consequences, regardless of the perpetrator's stated identity.
