# Texas Airport Scraps Woke Facility Plan After Abbott Flexes Gubernatorial Muscle

Texas Gov. Greg Abbott forced Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport to abandon its plan for Islamic foot-washing stations after he threatened to review all state grants flowing to the facility. The airport had proposed installing ablution stations in two Terminal D restrooms—a move Abbott called illegal and discriminatory toward other faiths (Fox News, The Daily Wire).

Abbott's swift action highlights a broader conservative pushback against what critics call government-funded religious accommodation that singles out one faith over others. Republicans argue such facilities represent exactly the kind of identity-politics spending that wastes taxpayer dollars and violates equal-treatment principles. The governor's willingness to weaponize grant reviews sent a clear message: states can and should scrutinize how public money gets spent.

While Colorado hasn't faced a similar airport controversy, the principle matters here. Denver International Airport and other state-funded infrastructure projects operate under similar grant structures. If progressive administrators attempt comparable faith-based facility expansions—whether for Islamic, Christian, Jewish, or other religious purposes—Colorado taxpayers and elected officials should demand the same accountability Abbott demonstrated.

Watch whether other Republican governors adopt Abbott's playbook to audit state grants for ideological spending.
