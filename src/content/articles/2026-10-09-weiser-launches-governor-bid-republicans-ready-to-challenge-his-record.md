# Colorado AG Weiser Hits Campaign Trail as 2026 Governor's Race Takes Shape

Phil Weiser's bid for Colorado governor is officially underway, with the Democratic attorney general appearing at a series of public forums to make his case to voters ahead of 2026.

Weiser sat down with the Colorado Sun's elections editor Jesse Paul to discuss his campaign platform, while also sharing the stage with Republican candidate Victor Marx at a CPR News forum where both candidates received advance notice of questions (CPR News).

Republicans argue Weiser's record as AG—marked by aggressive regulatory enforcement and litigation against energy companies—signals he'll expand government control over Colorado's economy if elected governor. Conservatives worry his administration would accelerate the state's leftward drift on taxes, land use, and energy policy at a time when Colorado's business climate already faces headwinds from progressive policies.

Marx, the Republican challenger, is positioning himself as the pro-growth alternative, emphasizing limited government and economic freedom. His campaign will likely hammer Weiser on his litigation-heavy approach to regulation and his support for policies that conservatives say burden Colorado's oil and gas sector—critical to rural economies and state revenues.

**Watch for:** How Weiser's gubernatorial message differs from his AG record, and whether Marx can effectively tie Democratic state policies to kitchen-table costs for Colorado families.
