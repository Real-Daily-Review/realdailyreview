# Electoral College Under Siege as Blue States Plot Constitutional End-Run

Colorado and other Republican-leaning states face a coordinated assault on the Electoral College through the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact—a scheme that would effectively neuter the constitutional system protecting smaller states' influence in presidential elections.

According to National Review, blue-state activists are positioning themselves to execute an "end run around the constitutional system" by binding states to award electoral votes to the national popular vote winner, regardless of how their own citizens vote. Complete Colorado warns that Democrats understand their power centers are concentrated in urban coastal regions, making the Electoral College a threat to their dominance.

The compact requires states representing 270 electoral votes to activate—a threshold progressives are methodically approaching. Once triggered, Colorado's electoral votes could be pledged to a candidate Coloradans rejected at the ballot box, stripping the state of meaningful leverage in presidential contests.

Conservatives argue the Electoral College is a foundational protection ensuring that presidential candidates must build geographically diverse coalitions rather than simply running up margins in Democratic strongholds. Eliminating it would cement coastal-urban control over national politics and render rural and swing states irrelevant.

**Watch whether Colorado legislators resist pressure to join the compact—or capitulate to progressive pressure and surrender the state's electoral independence.**
