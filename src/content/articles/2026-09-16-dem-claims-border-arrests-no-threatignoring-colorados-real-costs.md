# Washington Dem's 'No Threat' Claim on Border Arrests Ignores Reality of Open-Border Policy

A Washington Democrat is claiming that the majority of recent Department of Homeland Security arrestees pose "no threat to public safety"—a talking point that glosses over the real-world consequences of the Biden-Harris administration's lax enforcement posture.

According to Fox News Politics, the Democrat asserted that for the first time this summer, most DHS arrests involved individuals deemed non-threatening. The statement conveniently sidesteps a central conservative concern: that catch-and-release policies and reduced deportations have emboldened criminal aliens and created security gaps at the southern border.

Republicans argue that characterizing border arrests as "no threat" contradicts ICE and CBP data showing repeat offenders, gang members, and individuals with serious criminal histories repeatedly slip through enforcement cracks. Colorado law enforcement has repeatedly flagged the strain illegal immigration places on local resources, jails, and public safety budgets—costs ultimately borne by Colorado taxpayers.

The left's framing also ignores that "no threat to public safety" doesn't address sovereignty, rule of law, or labor-market impacts on working-class Coloradans competing for jobs.

**Watch for:** Whether Colorado's congressional delegation will demand transparency on how many arrested aliens have prior criminal records or gang affiliations—and push back against federal talking points obscuring border security failures.
