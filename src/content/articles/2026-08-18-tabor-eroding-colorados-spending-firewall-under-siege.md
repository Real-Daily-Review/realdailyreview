# TABOR Under Siege as Colorado Faces New Spending Pressures

Colorado's Taxpayer Bill of Rights—once a firewall against runaway government spending—is being hollowed out by decades of legislative carve-outs and creative accounting, threatening the affordability that drew families and businesses to the state in the first place.

According to Complete Colorado, TABOR's protections have eroded significantly through political maneuvering, leaving taxpayers vulnerable to hidden tax hikes and spending expansions that bypass voter approval. Republicans and fiscal conservatives argue the remedy is straightforward: close loopholes, restore the original intent, and require voter consent for any new revenue measures or spending authority.

Democrats counter that TABOR's rigid constraints prevent necessary investment in schools, infrastructure, and services—a familiar progressive argument that government spending solves problems rather than creating them.

The stakes are concrete for Colorado families already facing rising housing costs, energy bills, and property taxes. Without genuine TABOR enforcement, state lawmakers have cover to expand government without voter permission, shifting the burden onto working Coloradans who built this state on the promise of fiscal restraint.

Conservatives should watch closely for any legislative attempts to further weaken TABOR protections in the next session—and demand that candidates for statewide office pledge to defend the rule, not dismantle it.
