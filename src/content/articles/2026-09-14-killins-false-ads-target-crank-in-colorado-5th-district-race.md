# Colorado Democrats' Attack Ads Caught Peddling False Claims Against Rep. Crank

Democrat Jessica Killin's first campaign ads against U.S. Rep. Jeff Crank in Colorado's 5th Congressional District contain misleading statements, according to the Colorado Sun. Killin is attempting to become the first Democrat to represent the heavily Republican district—a tall order that apparently requires bending the truth to gain traction.

The false advertising underscores a broader pattern: when progressive candidates can't win on policy, they resort to distortion. Colorado voters deserve honest debate about Crank's record and vision, not fabricated attack spots designed to deceive.

Meanwhile, Colorado voters will decide Initiative #256 this fall, which would ban congressional redistricting designed to favor either party (Complete Colorado). Republicans have long argued that partisan gerrymandering—a tactic Democrats have weaponized in states they control—undermines fair representation. This measure offers guardrails against such manipulation.

On the structural front, Colorado's unaffiliated voter bloc continues growing, and party-affiliated voters who skip caucuses are effectively ceding primary power to activists (Complete Colorado). That dynamic shapes who ultimately faces general-election voters like those in the 5th District.

**Watch:** Whether Killin's misleading ads gain traction despite fact-checks, and whether Colorado voters embrace redistricting reform to prevent future partisan line-drawing.
