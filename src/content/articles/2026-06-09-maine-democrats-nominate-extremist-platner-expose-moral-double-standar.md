# Maine Democrats' Extremist Candidate Exposes Party's Moral Bankruptcy

Maine Democrats are about to nominate Graham Platner for Senate—a Bernie Sanders-backed radical whose support for the Green New Deal and extreme policies should disqualify him, even before his personal scandals enter the room.

Sen. Susan Collins (R-Maine) has called Platner "too far-left for Maine voters," while GOP Rep. Laurel Libby warns his green extremism poses "a greater danger" than his well-documented baggage (Fox News). National Review notes Platner has "no record to run on," leaving voters only his word—which he's repeatedly shown untrustworthy.

The real story: Democrats' moral collapse. Even the Denver Post's editorial board acknowledges the party "cannot ignore Graham Platner's red flags and hold the moral high ground," exposing a glaring double standard. When Republicans nominate flawed candidates, the left screams disqualification. When Democrats do it, silence.

Platner's Green New Deal zealotry matters for Colorado too. If elected, he'd push the same job-killing energy regulations and carbon mandates that threaten Colorado's oil, gas, and coal workers. His extremism on climate policy would accelerate the left's war on affordable energy.

Watch whether national Democrats distance themselves from Platner or embrace him—their answer will reveal whether principle or power matters more.
